1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of symbiotic relationship are exemplified by Wading birds and alligators, in which both species benefit from the interaction?
W) Commensalism
X) Mutualism
Y) Ammensalism
Z) Parasitism
ANSWER: X) Mutualism
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following plant structures accounts for the greatest amount of water lost?
W) root hairs
X) lenticels
Y) stomata
Z) cuticle
ANSWER: Y) stomata
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Under non-standard conditions, what equation in electrochemistry relates the electrical potential of an electrochemical cell to the concentrations of the reacting substances and temperature?
ANSWER: Nernst equation
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 3 allotropes of carbon in decreasing enthalpy of formation 1) graphite 2) diamond 3) fullerene
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What quantity is the rotational analog of mass and measures an object's resistance to rotational acceleration?
ANSWER: moment of inertia
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer What phenomenon occurs when an object moves through a medium at a speed greater than the speed of sound in that medium, causing pressure disturbances to overlap and form a sharp front?
ANSWER: shock wave (also accept: sonic boom)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Ozone absorbs harmful radiation in which of the following atmospheric layers?
W) troposphere
X) stratosphere
Y) mesosphere
Z) thermosphere
ANSWER: X) stratosphere
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 gases that are greenhouse gases 1) water vapor 2) carbon monoxide 3) carbon dioxide
ANSWER: 1 and 3
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following CANNOT be the third side of a triangle with sides 4 and 9?
W) 3
X) 6
Y) 10
Z) 12
ANSWER: W) 3
5. BONUS Math Short Answer A point P(2,1) is rotated 90 degrees counterclockwise about the origin and then reflected across the x-axis. What are the coordinates of the image of point P after these transformations?
ANSWER: (-1,-2)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers are studying the solar maximum in 2025, which caused various solar storms. Assuming a perfect solar cycle, what year is expected to be the next solar maximum year?
ANSWER: 2036
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are studying gravity anomalies to understand variations in Earth’s mass distribution. Which of the following regions would have the most extreme gravity anomalies?
W) Sahara Desert
X) Mariana Trench
Y) Great Plains
Z) Central Australia
ANSWER: X) Mariana Trench
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Plant cells do not burst when placed in a hypotonic environment due to the presence of what rigid structure?
ANSWER: Cell Wall
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice During which of the following phases of the cell cycle will a cell double its DNA content?
W) G1
X) S
Y) G2
Z) M

8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following reaction types can a precipitation reaction NOT fall under?
W) single displacement
X) double displacement
Y) neutralization
Z) metathesis
ANSWER: W) single displacement
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What quantity is equal to the sum of the enthalpy of fusion and enthalpy of vaporization of a substance?
ANSWER: enthalpy of sublimation (also accept: heat of sublimation)
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A stretched string has a fundamental frequency of 50 hertz. In hertz, what is the frequency of the fourth harmonic of the string?
ANSWER: 200
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What law states that the force exerted by an ideal spring is directly proportional to the displacement of the spring from its equilibrium position, provided that the elastic limit is not exceeded?
ANSWER: Hooke’s law

X) 1

Z) 100
ANSWER: Z) 100

13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Collierville Science Bowl students are trying to match homologous segments between 2 species. X) Substitution of 3 nucleotides
Y) Insertion of a single nucleotide
