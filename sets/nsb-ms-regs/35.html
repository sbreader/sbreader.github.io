1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Vestigial structures:
W) have no known function in their present owners
X) function in an analogous fashion in all species
Y) evolve homologously until functional
Z) control nervous system functions
ANSWER: W) have no known function in their present owners
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Mallus ioeusis and Mallus sylvestris are the genera and species of what common tree?
ANSWER: APPLE
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the decibel scale most often used to measure?
W) size of bells
X) magnitude of hurricanes
Y) loudness of a sound
Z) brightness of lights
ANSWER: Y) loudness of a sound
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following are the highest of the clouds in the troposphere and are composed entirely of ice crystals:
W) stratus
X) cirrus
Y) cumulus
Z) altocumulus
ANSWER: X) cirrus
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In the Northern Hemisphere, when is the Earth the greatest distance from the sun:
W) winter
X) spring
Y) summer
Z) fall
ANSWER: Y) summer
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The identity element for addition is what number?
ANSWER: 0
4. BONUS Math Short Answer On a math test, 20 students averaged a score of 80% while 10 students averaged 90%. To the nearest whole number, what is the mean score of the entire class?
ANSWER: 83%
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following units is used to measure electric current:
W) voltage
X) ohms
Y) amperes
Z) watts
ANSWER: Y) amperes

ANSWER: 2 AMPERE
