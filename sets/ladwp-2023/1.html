1. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The lifespan of a certain type of lightbulb is normally distributed with mean 2400 hours and standard deviation 80 hours. Which of the following is closest to the percentile of the lifespan of a 2240 hour light bulb?
W) 2
X) 5
Y) 16
Z) 34
ANSWER: W) 2
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In a first order reaction, the reaction concentration versus time curve is what type of function:
W) Exponential decay
X) Logistic decay
Y) Inverse decay
Z) Logistic growth
ANSWER: W) Exponential decay
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What thyroid hormone is constructed from two tyrosine amino acids and four iodine atoms?
ANSWER: Thyroxine (also accept: T4)
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Mickey has four cats and seven treats. She would like to distribute the seven treats to the four cats such that each cat gets at least one treat. In how many ways can this be done
ANSWER: 20
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What period of the geologic time scale immediately precedes the Cambrian and is distinguished by the earlier appearance of macroscopic animal fossils in sedimentary rocks?
W) Ordovician
X) Devonian
Y) Permian
Z) Ediacaran
ANSWER: Z) Ediacaran
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is true of most apicomplexans?
W) They are photosynthetic
X) They cause algal blooms
Y) They are parasites
Z) They are sessile
ANSWER: Y) They are parasites
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Scientists supported by the Department of Energy are using algorithms to optimize the structures of novel materials. Optimization problems are considerably more tractable if the function to be optimized has a second derivative that is non-negative on its entire domain. What is the adjective for this type of function?
W) Convex
X) Surjective
Y) Elliptical
Z) Concave
ANSWER: W) Convex
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice It is believed, upon formation, Earth's moon may have been as 1/10th as far away from Earth as it is today. If the moon was still at this distance, how many times as great would its tidal force on earth be?
W) 10 times
X) 100 times
Y) 1000 times
Z) 10000 times
ANSWER: Y) 1000 times
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer How many different hydrogen emission lines involve levels no higher than n=5?
ANSWER: 10
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Cassidy is riding her bike along a straight path. She accelerates at 1.5 m/s2 for 3 seconds, then decelerates at 0.5 m/s2 until she comes to a stop. In meters, what is her displacement between the beginning and end of this process?
ANSWER: 27
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A more rapidly rotating galaxy is typically more luminous. This association is described by which of the following?
W) Hubble's Law
X) Tulley-Fisher Equation
Y) Period-Luminosity Relationship
Z) Kepler's Second Law
ANSWER: X) Tulley-Fisher Equation
6. BONUS Math Short Answer There are 64 three digit numbers that can be made using the four digits 1, 3, 5, and/or 6. What is the average of these 64 numbers?
ANSWER: 1665/4 (also accept: 416.25)
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Consider the reaction of solid Fe2O3 with gaseous CO form liquid metallic iron and gaseous CO2. Which of the following changes will not shift the equilibrium position of this reaction?
W) Removing CO2
X) Adding CO
Y) Adding Fe2O3
Z) Adding CO2
ANSWER: Y) Adding Fe2O3
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following streams would have the fastest average channel velocity?
W) A stream with a discharge of 30 cubic feet per second and a cross sectional area of 4 square feet
X) A stream with a discharge of 25 cubic feet per second and a cross sectional area of 7 square feet
Y) A stream with a discharge of 40 cubic feet per second and a cross sectional area of 5 square feet
Z) A stream with a discharge of 20 cubic feet per second and a cross sectional area of 2 square feet
ANSWER: Z) A stream with a discharge of 20 cubic feet per second and a cross sectional area of 2 square feet
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Scientists at Oakridge National Lab are studying deciduous plants. Which of the following best describes these plants?
W) They are adaptive to arid conditions
X) They only live for a single year
Y) Their leaves fall off in the autumn
Z) They thrive in cold, icy environments
ANSWER: Y) Their leaves fall off in the autumn
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists at Los Alamos National Lab are studying influenza-like illnesses which are characterized by fever, loss of appetite, nausea, and coughing. Identify all of the following three organisms which can cause influenza-like illnesses 1) Streptococcus pneumoniae 2) Respiratory syncytial virus 3) Measles
ANSWER: All
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of mutations is most likely to result in a frameshift?
W) The substitution of a single base
X) The insertion of a single base
Y) The substitution of multiple bases
Z) The insertion of three bases
ANSWER: X) The insertion of a single base
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following anatomic features of birds is commonly true?
W) Birds have urinary bladders
X) Female birds have only one ovary
Y) Male birds have only one testis
Z) Birds have a three chambered heart
ANSWER: X) Female birds have only one ovary
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In petroleum geology, what term refers to an impermeable rock layer that prevents oil from flowing out of the reservoir rock in a trap?
ANSWER: Cap rock (also accept: Cap)
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three minerals that might crystallize at the same temperature as olivine in a cooling magma body 1) Calcium-rich plagioclase 2) Sodium-rich plagioclase 3) Muscovite
ANSWER: 1 only
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Assuming identical masses and radii, which of the following objects would have the largest moment of inertia about the central axis?
W) Hoop
X) Solid Cylinder
Y) Solid Sphere
Z) Spherical Shell
ANSWER: W) Hoop
11. BONUS Math Short Answer The complex number Z has an angle of 80 degrees. In the interval from 0 to 360 degrees, what is the largest angle in degrees of the fifth root of Z?
ANSWER: 304
12. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Scientists supported by the Department of Energy are studying quantum computers. What is the basic unit of quantum information?
ANSWER: Qubit (also accept: Quantum Bit)
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 3 kg pendulum bob is swinging on a string of length 20 centimeters. As it reaches its bottom-most point, its speed is 2 m/s. In Newtons, what is the tension in the string at this time?
ANSWER: 89.4
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is not true of fixed action patterns?
W) Once started, they must proceed to completion
X) They are learned during an imprinting period
Y) They are triggered by a specific sign stimulus
Z) They are often species-specific behaviors
ANSWER: X) They are learned during an imprinting period
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are performing regression analysis. The unbiased form of linear regression finds a line that minimizes the sums of the squares of what?
ANSWER: Residuals
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for a function or property of a system that does not depend on the path taken to reach a state, but only on the initial and final states?
ANSWER: State function (also accept: State)
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice During what phase of animal development are the G1 and G2 phase skipped, causing each division to result in smaller and smaller cells?
W) Gastrulation
X) Cleavage
Y) Ecdysis
Z) Senescence
ANSWER: X) Cleavage
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer An angle measures 45 degrees and is inscribed in a circle of radius 12. What is the arc length along the circle subtended by the angle?
ANSWER: 6π
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three compounds that are soluble in water 1) Barium Sulfate 2) Silver Iodide 3) Cadmium Sulfide
ANSWER: 0 only
16. BONUS Math Short Answer A spherical balloon is being inflated. If the radius is increasing by 3 cm/s, then in cm3/s, what is the rate that the volume is changing when the radius of the balloon is 5 cm?
ANSWER: 300π

Z) Adiabatic
ANSWER: Z) Adiabatic

36. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at SLAC are studying charged density waves in superconductors. Which of the following is equal to the time derivative of electric charge density?
W) Curl of current density
X) Negative curl of current density

Z) Negative divergence of current density
