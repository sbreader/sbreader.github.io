1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following sources of DNA would be MOST useful for comparing distantly related species of invertebrates?
W) Mitochondrial DNA
X) Hemoglobin alpha gene
Y) lac repressor gene
Z) MADS box gene
ANSWER: W) Mitochondrial DNA
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer In chickens, dwarfism is a Z-linked recessive disease. What is the probability that a daughter of an unaffected female chicken and an affected male chicken is affected?
ANSWER: 100% (also accept: 1)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three properties of a redox reaction that are intensive 1) Standard Gibbs free energy change of reaction 2) Gibbs free energy change 3) Cell potential.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Twenty moles each of hydrogen and iodine gas are reacted in a 500 liter container. At equilibrium, eight moles of hydrogen iodide gas are present. To two significant figures, what is the reaction’s equilibrium constant?
ANSWER: 0.25
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at the Picower Institute have been studying neurotransmitter release in neurons of Alzheimer’s patients. Before being released into the synaptic cleft, neurotransmitters are found in synaptic vesicles docked to the plasma membrane via what group of water soluble proteins?
ANSWER: SNARE
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Chemists at the Suess Group studied protein affinity for iron-sulfur clusters. Which of the following mutations in a protein’s primary structure would most greatly reduce its binding to iron-sulfur clusters?
W) Cysteine to alanine
X) Threonine to isoleucine
Y) Leucine to histidine
Z) Tyrosine to phenylalanine
ANSWER: W) Cysteine to alanine
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following Milankovitch cycles is the shortest on Earth?
W) Axial tilt
X) Axial precession
Y) Eccentricity
Z) Apsidal precession
ANSWER: X) Axial precession
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals is a framework silicate?
W) Olivine
X) Quartz
Y) Muscovite
Z) Amphibole
ANSWER: X) Quartz
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If Caroline gets a good night of sleep, then she has a 50% chance of falling asleep in lecture, but if she pulls an all-nighter, she has a 90% chance of falling asleep. If Caroline has a 20% chance of pulling an all nighter tonight, what is the probability that she falls asleep in lecture tomorrow?
ANSWER: 58% (also accept: 0.58; 29/50)
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Two concentric circles O1 and O2 are drawn with O1 having a smaller radius. Chord AB of circle O2 is drawn such that AB is tangent to O1. Given that AB = 12, what is the difference between the areas of the two circles?
ANSWER: 36π
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice On a plot of temperature versus entropy, which of the following thermodynamic processes corresponds to a vertical line?
W) Isothermal
X) Adiabatic
Y) Isobaric
Z) Isochoric
ANSWER: X) Adiabatic
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer To the nearest whole number, what is the coefficient of performance of an ideal Carnot refrigerator operating with a hot reservoir at 298 kelvin and an internal temperature of 273 kelvin?
ANSWER: 11
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In order to oppose the action of the renin angiotensin aldosterone system, the atria of the heart release what hormone to inhibit Na+ reabsorption?
ANSWER: ATRIAL NATRIURETIC FACTOR (also accept: ATRIAL NATRIURETIC PEPTIDE; ANP; ANF; ATRIAL NATRIURETIC HORMONE)
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three behaviors that are found in palatable species 1) Mullerian mimicry 2) Batesian mimicry 3) Cryptic coloration.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecular geometries for a coordination complex with non-chiral ligands could give both optical and geometric isomers?
W) Linear
X) Tetrahedral
Y) Square planar
Z) Octahedral
ANSWER: Z) Octahedral
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true about tetrafluoroethylene 1) It displays cis-trans isomerism 2) It has a higher boiling point than ethylene 3) It is highly soluble in water.
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Ryan has a proximity sensor that detects the presence of a magnetic field by determining if a current in the device induces a voltage perpendicular to the direction of current. What effect does the sensor exploit?
ANSWER: HALL EFFECT
9. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice For the time-dependent position x(t), which of the following ordinary differential equations of x(t) describes a harmonic oscillator?
W) x′ + 3x = 0
X) x″ + 3x = 0
Y) x′ − 3x = 0
Z) x″ − 3x = 0
ANSWER: X) x″ + 3x = 0

ANSWER: QUARK GLUON PLASMA (also accept: QGP; QUARK SOUP) (do not accept: PLASMA)

12. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice An unfair coin has a probability p of landing heads on each flip. If the coin is flipped three times, the probability of landing three heads is the same as the probability of landing two heads. Which of the following is the value of p?
