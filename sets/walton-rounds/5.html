1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which quantum number determines the orientation of the electrons?
ANSWER: SPIN QUANTUM NUMBER (also accept: S)
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If 44 grams of C3H8 are combusted in excess oxygen, what mass, in grams, of carbon dioxide is produced?
ANSWER: 132
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name of the great circle that represents the sun's apparent path through the sky?
W) Ecliptic
X) Solar Circle
Y) Eclipse Path
Z) Circumstellar
ANSWER: W) Ecliptic
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which of Jupiter's moons is the smoothest celestial body in the Solar System, and its smoothness is evidence that it may harbor life?
ANSWER: EUROPA
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What law predicts the ultraviolet catastrophe, the prediction that as the wavelength a black body emitted grew shorter, the blackbody would produce infinite energy?
W) Wien's distribution law
X) Rayleigh-Jeans Law
Y) Planck's Law
Z) Stefan-Boltzmann Law
ANSWER: X) Rayleigh-Jeans Law
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer A car is moving in a straight line from point A to Point B. The car's velocity at point A is 10m/s. It will start to accelerate at Point A with 5 m/s2, How far it will travel after 10 seconds?
ANSWER: 350 M
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following sets of reactions occurs in the stroma of the chloroplast in plant cells?
W) Calvin cycle
X) Krebs cycle
Y) Fermentation
Z) Decarboxylation
ANSWER: W) Calvin cycle
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What lineage of Cnidarians exist only as polyps as adults. Many build calcium carbonate exoskeletons and have a mutualistic relationship with photosynthetic protists.
W) Hydrozoa
X) Scyphozoa
Y) Cubozoa
Z) Anthozoa
ANSWER: Z) Anthozoa
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer This is a process that occurs in multiple macronutrient cycles: In this step, producers absorb elements from the soil, and they incorporate the nutrients into their tissues.
ANSWER: ASSIMILATION
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, identify which of the following 3 statements are true regarding the nitrogen cycle. 1) Nitrification oxidizes ammonia into nitrite and then nitrate. 2) Nitrogen fixation converts ammonia to atmospheric nitrogen 3) Organic waste is converted into inorganic substances through ammonification.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The first confirmed Bose-Einstein Condensate was created using what cooling technique that uses a namesake phenomenon to change the momentum of the particles?
ANSWER: LASER COOLING (also accept: DOPPLER COOLING)

7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Name this hydrocarbon: C2H4

ANSWER: BORN-HABER CYCLE
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of fluid found in the brain and spinal cord that helps protect the brain and is produced in the choroid plexus?
ANSWER: CEREBROSPINAL FLUID (also accept: CSF)
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What level of protein structure includes alpha helices and beta sheets?
ANSWER: SECONDARY STRUCTURE

ANSWER: DARK ENERGY
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer One explanation for dark energy is that space itself has energy, and as more space comes into existence, there is more energy for the space to expand. What term in Einstein’s field equations accounts for this energy?
ANSWER: COSMOLOGICAL CONSTANT
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What substance is an alloy of copper and zinc and has a golden tinge?

11. BONUS Biology Short Answer During DNA replication, the helix unwinds and one new DNA strand is copied continuously in the direction of unwinding. ANSWER: 12
13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding entropy is not true?
W) The total entropy change during any reversible cycle is zero
X) Entropy remains constant in Carnot Cycle
Y) In some special cases the total entropy changes could be smaller than zero
Z) Entropy provides a quantitative measure of disorder
