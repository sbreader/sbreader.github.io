1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the volume of a sphere of radius "R"?
ANSWER: (4/3)πR3 (also accept: 4/3 π R3)
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the surface area of a sphere of radius "r"?
ANSWER: 4πR2 (also accept: 4π r2; 12.566 R2)
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many degrees are there in π radians?
ANSWER: 180 DEGREES (also accept: 180)
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Using an x-y coordinate axis, the figure represented by the equation x2/36 + y2/16 = 1 is centered about what x-y coordinate point?
ANSWER: (0, 0)
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer An ellipse is represented by the equation x2/36 + y2/16 = 1. What is the length of the major axis of this ellipse?
ANSWER: 12
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Using an x-y coordinate axis, a parabola is given by the equation y = x2. Give the x-y coordinates of the focal point for this parabola.
ANSWER: (0, 1/4)
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Using an x-y coordinate axis, a parabola is represented by the equation x2 = 6y. The vertex of this parabola is at what coordinate point?
ANSWER: (0, 0) (also accept: x = 0, y = 0)
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer For a right triangle, the sin(A) is 3/5. To what value is the tan(A) equal?
ANSWER: 3/4
