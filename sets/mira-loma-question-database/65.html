1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Living keratinocytes in the stratum germinativum of the epidermis synthesize which antimicrobial peptides as part of inflammation?
W) Interferons
X) Defensins
Y) Chemokines
Z) Leukotreines
ANSWER: X) Defensins
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A spring is released from a position of x=-a and comes to rest at x=a, and its speed at x=0 is v. Another spring with the same spring constant is released from a position of x= -2a. What is its maximum speed?
ANSWER: 2v
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In which specific type of acylation reaction are aromatic rings acylated with acyl chloride using a strong Lewis acid catalyst?
ANSWER: FRIEDEL-CRAFTS ACYLATION
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following four spectral types that T-tauri stars can be: F, G, K, M.
ANSWER: ALL OF THEM
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A polynomial of the form (x + y)n is expanded. The largest coefficient is smaller than 30. What is the largest possible value of n?
ANSWER: 6
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Name all of the following four hydrocarbons that biodiesel can be composed of: methyl, ethyl, propyl, butyl.
ANSWER: METHYL, ETHYL, PROPYL
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why a thicker layer of epidermis is found on the palms and soles?
W) They have greater susceptibility to pathogens
X) They are subject to greater pressure
Y) They are more prone to calluses
Z) They are more sensitive to inward water movement
ANSWER: X) They are subject to greater pressure
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An object undergoes a positive change in absolute temperature of T, causing its density to change. If its original density was p and the coefficient of linear expansion is a, what will its density be after heating in terms of p, a, and T?
ANSWER: p/(1+3aT)
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer Name all of the following that is or are true of thermal expansion: Anisotropic materials have different linear expansion coefficients in different directions. In isotropic materials, the volumetric expansion coefficient is almost exactly three times the linear expansion coefficient. Negative thermal expansion of materials such as cubic zirconium tungstate can be described by the thermal expansion equations. In isobaric conditions, the ideal gas law is a special case of the volumetric gas expansion equation.
ANSWER: 1, 2, AND 4
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The ideal gas law most accurately describes...
W) Diatomic gases at low temperature and low pressure
X) Diatomic gases at high temperature and low pressure
Y) Monatomic gases at low temperature and low pressure
Z) Monatomic gases at high temperature and low pressure
ANSWER: Z) Monatomic gases at high temperature and low pressure
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Craters still exist on the moon's surface because...
W) There is no erosion on the moon's surface
X) The moon lacks a strong magnetic field
Y) The moon lacks fluid flow
Z) Bombardment is especially severe on the moon
ANSWER: W) There is no erosion on the moon's surface
11. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The statement that the shortest distance between two points is a straight line is a...
W) Axiom
X) Postulate
Y) Property
Z) Theorem
ANSWER: X) Postulate
12. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which instrument in a wind turbine measures wind speed and transmits wind speed data to the controller?
ANSWER: ANEMOMETER
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name all two of the following four choices that are true of podocytes in the human kidney: They form the inner layer of the Bowman's capsule They have a low surface area to volume ratio They form pores that make the glomerulus permeable They are responsible for osmoregulation in the kidneys
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In order for an object to be in mechanical equilibrium, which two quantities must equal zero?
ANSWER: NET FORCE AND NET TORQUE
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following must be added to a carboxylic acid to produce an ester?
W) A carboxylic acid
X) An ether
Y) An alcohol
Z) A phenol
ANSWER: Y) An alcohol
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which structure is usually composed of felsic or intermediate rock types and is a large group of intrusive bodies, an example of which is the half dome is Yosemite National Park?
ANSWER: BATHOLITH
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the igneous intrusion injected between layers of sedimentary rocks which form the core of several mountain ranges?
ANSWER: LACCOLITH
17. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which type of operator preserves the operations of vector addition and scalar multiplication?
W) Linear
X) Non-linear
Y) Metric
Z) Distribution
ANSWER: W) Linear
17. BONUS Math Short Answer Name all of the following three choices that are true: If A is a linear operator, then A(cu+v)=c*A(u)+A(v) If u and v are orthogonal, then their scalar product is 1 The adjoint of the adjoint of A equals A
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which enzyme in the parenchyma cells of the liver, pancreas, gut, and brain phosphorylates glucose in glycolysis to yield glucose-6-phosphate?
ANSWER: GLUCOKINASE
18. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not true regarding glucokinase?
W) It is inhibited by glucose-6-phosphate
X) It has a lower affinity for glucose that the other hexokinases
Y) It is the only monomeric regulatory enzyme known to display substrate cooperativity
Z) It changes its shape with rising glucose levels
ANSWER: W) It is inhibited by glucose-6-phosphate
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name all of the following pH buffer systems used in the cells of the renal tubules to maintain normal blood pH: bicarbonate, phosphate, protein.
ANSWER: BICARBONATE, PHOSPHATE
19. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why glucose is excreted in urine when a patient is in a state of hyperglycemia?
W) Filtrate pressure in the tubules is too great to facilitate efficient reabsorption
X) Active transport of glucose is too energetically consumptive
Y) The glucose level exceeds the threshold level and there are too few glucose receptors
Z) Glucose receptors of such patients develop resistance to hyperglycemia
ANSWER: Y) The glucose level exceeds the threshold level and there are too few glucose receptors
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer At sufficiently high velocities, fluid flow in a tube becomes turbulent. This onset of turbulence is determined by which dimensionless factor?
ANSWER: REYNOLDS NUMBER
20. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the term for the speed at which materials fall through a fluid?
ANSWER: SEDIMENTATION RATE
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer 2-propanone and 2-propenol are which type of isomers?
ANSWER: TAUTOMERS
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which glacial feature is an aeolian sediment formed by the accumulation of wind-blown silt, sand, and clay?
ANSWER: LOESS
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following three descriptors that describe loess: heterogenous, porous, calcareous.
ANSWER: POROUS, CALCAREOUS
23. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following actions cannot be performed to transpose a matrix A?
W) Write the rows of A as the columns of AT
X) Reflect A by its main diagonal
Y) Rotate A 90 degrees counterclockwise
Z) Rotate A 90 degrees clockwise and mirror the image horizontally
ANSWER: Y) Rotate A 90 degrees counterclockwise
23. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Matrix A is transposed to yield matrix B. Which of the following is not true?
W) When B is transposed, the result is A
X) The determinant of B equals the determinant of A
Y) The inverse of B equals the inverse of A, transposed
Z) The dot product of A and B equals the identity matrix, transposed
ANSWER: Z) The dot product of A and B equals the identity matrix, transposed
24. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which theorem guarantees that in physical sciences, conserved quantities can be derived from symmetries in the natural laws, such as conservation of energy from time translation symmetry?
ANSWER: NOETHER'S THEOREM

W) Neuron
X) Egg
Y) Sperm
Z) Lymphocyte
ANSWER: Y) Sperm
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer Answer the following five questions about meiosis 1) Which division of meiosis is called the equational division? 2) Which division of meiosis is called the reductional division? 3) In which phase of meiosis do homologs separate? 4) Mendel's first principle, segregation, is the result of which phase of meiosis? 5) Mendel's second principle, independent assortment, is the result of which phase of meiosis?
ANSWER: 1) MEIOSIS II; 2) MEIOSIS I; 3) ANAPHASE I; 4) ANAPHASE I; 5) ANAPHASE I (also accept: METAPHASE I)
