1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The Young’s modulus for Sharon’s throat is 430 pascals, while the shear modulus for her throat is 132 pascals. If the length of her throat is 6 centimeters, and giving your answer in pascals, how much pressure is required for her throat to stretch by 5 meters?
ANSWER: 35,833 (also accept: 36,000; 35,800)
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer A ball is launched into the air from ground level at an angle of 30 degrees. The initial velocity of the ball is 16 meters per second.
