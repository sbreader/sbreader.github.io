Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What chemical substance did William Henry Perkin accidentally create instead of quinine?
ANSWER: DYE (also accept: "mauvine")
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following was a crystalline alkaloid that William Henry Perkin tried to synthesize to meet the demands for patients with malaria?
W) aniline
X) quinine
Y) mauvine
Z) crinoline
ANSWER: X) quinine
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The genesis of modern chemistry can be traced back in the past to certain chemical practices known as what?
ANSWER: ALCHEMY
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which scientist, who is known as the "father of chemistry," changed the name of the early practices of alchemy to what it is today?
ANSWER: ROBERT BOYLE
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Alloys with higher carbon content due to their low melting point and cast ability are known as
W) mild steel
X) stainless steel
Y) wrought iron
Z) cast iron
ANSWER: Z) cast iron
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What elemental metalloid is almost necessary to produce gray cast iron?
ANSWER: SILICON
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The domain of materials science that studies the physical and chemical behavior of metallic elements is known as what?
ANSWER: METALLURGY
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Extractive metallurgy, the practice of removing valuable ores from materials usually ends up with a type of waste known as what?
ANSWER: TAILINGS
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What element on the periodic table was the first example of being created through synthesis?
ANSWER: TECHNETIUM
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which scientist is most famously known as the "pioneer" of organic chemistry?
ANSWER: FRIEDRICH WOHLER
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is not an example of an isolated element discovered by Friedrich Wöhler?
W) Yttrium
X) Beryllium
Y) Titanium
Z) Potassium
ANSWER: Z) Potassium
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What are the given names to all six different types of quarks?
ANSWER: UP, DOWN, CHARM, STRANGE, TOP, BOTTOM
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The general term given to quarks held together by a strong force to form composite materials is known as
W) hadrons
X) kaons
Y) baryons
Z) pions
ANSWER: W) hadrons
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is a common "nickname," that is usually used to express the element mercury due to its physical appearance?
ANSWER: QUICKSILVER
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A very corrosive, sublimating and easily poisonous form of mercury is known as what?
ANSWER: MERCURY (II) CHLORIDE
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the mass percentage of carbon in CO2?
W) 37.5%
X) 27.3%
Y) 75%
Z) 45%
ANSWER: X) 27.3%
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The chemical compound that is composed of two oxygen atoms covalently bonded to a single carbon atom is known as what?
ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the identity of mayonnaise?
W) a coalescent solution
X) a hydrogenated oil
Y) a covalent solution
Z) an emulsion
ANSWER: Z) an emulsion
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Emulsions are parts of a general class of two-phase systems known as what?
ANSWER: COLLOIDS
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a metal that unusually tends to occur naturally as an un-compounded mineral?
W) gold
X) copper
Y) silver
Z) platinum
ANSWER: X) copper
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is a common term used to express the loss of electrons in a chemical reaction?
W) reduction
X) oxidation
Y) displacement
Z) replacement
ANSWER: X) oxidation
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What type of compound has two or more hydrogen atoms of the core structure replaced by a functional group?
ANSWER: SUBSTITUTED COMPOUND
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the process to how crude oil is separated into its different components?
W) chemical reaction
X) simple distillation
Y) fractional distillation
Z) settling
ANSWER: Y) fractional distillation
14. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Ammonium Chloride usually forms naturally in what type of region in the world?
W) basins
X) volcanic regions
Y) areas at sea level
Z) underwater
ANSWER: X) volcanic regions
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements has the largest atomic radius?
W) strontium
X) francium
Y) calcium
Z) bromine
ANSWER: X) francium
15. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements has the lowest electro negativity?
W) mercury
X) lead
Y) francium
Z) strontium
ANSWER: Y) francium
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of an element from the actinide series of the periodic table?
W) beryllium
X) gold
Y) cerium
Z) uranium
ANSWER: Z) uranium
16. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of an element from the lanthanide series of the periodic table?
W) californium
X) cesium
Y) cerium
Z) calcium
ANSWER: Y) cerium
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When iron content is high in the substance, sphalerite, what does its new chemical formation turn into?
ANSWER: MARMATITE
18. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements is an example of similar chemical behavior to radium?
W) radon
X) barium
Y) francium
Z) cesium
ANSWER: X) barium
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What was the famous chemical experiment that was done to probe the structure of an atom known as?
ANSWER: GOLD FOIL EXPERIMENT (also accept: Rutherford Experiment)
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In Rutherford's experiment, substances that were composed of two neutrons and protons bonded together into a particle similar to that of the nucleus of a helium atom are known as what?
ANSWER: ALPHA PARTICLES
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the pH level of a substance that is 100 times more acidic than water?
ANSWER: 5
20. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a very acidic pH level?
W) 10
X) 11
Y) 14
Z) 2
ANSWER: Z) 2
21. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer At what point on the Celsius and Fahrenheit scales exactly equal?
ANSWER: -40 DEGREES
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A crystallized substance containing water is most appropriately termed as which of the following?
W) a hydrate
X) an oxide
Y) a hydroxide
Z) a condensation crystal
ANSWER: W) a hydrate
22. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice A hydrate, which has lost water is referred to as which of the following?
W) gas hydrates
X) anhydrous
Y) anhydride
Z) anhydrin
ANSWER: Y) anhydride
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What type of element is the most abundant in the universe?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN
23. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What type of element is the most abundant in the universe after hydrogen?
ANSWER: HELIUM
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice When water at 10°C is heated to 20°C, its internal energy does which of the following?
W) changes by 10°C
X) is doubled
Y) is less than doubled
Z) is more than doubled
ANSWER: Y) is less than doubled
24. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the pH level of water?
ANSWER: 7
25. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In any chemical reaction, a quantity that decrease to a minimum is known as which of the following?
W) free energy
X) entropy
Y) temperature
Z) enthalpy
ANSWER: W) free energy
25. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What are the two necessary parts in a chemical reaction that are needed to state that a reaction did occur?
ANSWER: REACTANTS AND PRODUCTS
