1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A pair of rivers that mark the approximate southern limit of the Ice Age glaciers in North America are the Missouri and which of the following?
W) St. Lawrence
X) Ohio
Y) Delaware
Z) Tennessee
ANSWER: X) Ohio
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following number of years best approximates the length of time since the last ice age in the United States?
W) 1,000,000
X) 1,000
Y) 11,000
Z) 250,000
ANSWER: Y) 11,000
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is molten rock that remains underground called?
ANSWER: MAGMA
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the volcano in the continental United States that erupted in May 1980.
ANSWER: MOUNT ST. HELENS
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Hawaiian Islands are what type of volcano?
ANSWER: SHIELD (also accept: SHIELD VOLCANOES; SHIELD VOLCANO)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The internal structure of crystals was first studied with which of the following?
W) x-rays
X) isotopes
Y) electron microscopes
Z) lasers
ANSWER: W) x-rays
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the world's largest active volcano?
ANSWER: MAUNA LOA (also accept: MAUNA LOA, HAWAII)
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In geological studies, cemented volcanic ash is called:
W) caldera
X) a-a
Y) lava
