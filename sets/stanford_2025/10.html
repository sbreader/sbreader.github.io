4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Suppose a container of volume 2V is partitioned into two equal sections of volume V . One of the partitions is filled with n moles of gas A, and the other partition contains a vacuum. The partition is then removed so that the gas expands irreversibly into the entire container. Which of the following best describes the entropy increase of the system?
W) Independent of n and V
X) Independent of n but dependent on V
Y) Dependent on n but independent of V
Z) Dependent on both n and V
ANSWER: Y) Dependent on n but independent of V
