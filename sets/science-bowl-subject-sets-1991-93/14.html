Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A type of lava characterized by a blocky appearance is termed:
W) Pillow Lava
X) Aa
Y) Vessicular
Z) none of these
ANSWER: X) Aa
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A type of volcano composed of mainly pyroclastics is termed:
W) cinder cone
X) shield cone
Y) composite cone
Z) none of these
ANSWER: W) cinder cone
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice An example of a typical shield volcano is:
W) Washington's Mount Rainier
X) Hawaii's Mauna Loa
Y) Ecuador's Mount Cotopaxi
Z) Japan's Mount Fuji
ANSWER: X) Hawaii's Mauna Loa
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Molten rock beneath the Earth's crust is called:
W) magma
X) liquicite
Y) lava
Z) igneous
ANSWER: W) magma
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The jet-stream lies between which two layers of the earth's atmosphere?
W) thermosphere and ionosphere
X) mesosphere and thermosphere
Y) troposphere and stratosphere
Z) stratosphere and mesosphere
ANSWER: Y) troposphere and stratosphere
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The degree of aridness of a geographic region is generally defined as the ratio of precipitation to:
W) condensation
X) evaporation
Y) transpiration
Z) snowpack depth
ANSWER: X) evaporation
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In the nebular hypothesis, each planet is formed from which of the following?
W) a comet
X) a number of moons
Y) a passing star
Z) a ring of gas
ANSWER: Z) a ring of gas
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is a selenologist?
ANSWER: LUNAR GEOLOGIST (also accept: A SCIENTIST WHO STUDIES THE MOON; MOON GEOLOGIST)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice There are three commonly detectable points of zero polarization of diffuse sky radiation, neutral points, lying along the vertical circle through the sun. Which of the following is NOT one of these points?
W) Arago Point
X) Babinet Point
Y) Brewster Point
Z) Longfellow Point
ANSWER: Z) Longfellow Point
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice An igneous rock which is commonly black, has a glassy texture, and has a conchoidal fracture is which of the following?
W) pelite
X) calcite
Y) obsidian
Z) none of the above
ANSWER: Y) obsidian
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In geological studies, a breccia contains particles that have which of the following shapes?
W) rounded
X) angular
Y) square
Z) spheroidal
ANSWER: X) angular
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the most important chemically-active fluid involved in the formation of rocks?
W) hydrochloric acid
X) sulfuric acid
Y) water
Z) methane
ANSWER: Y) water
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Most natural minerals can be found in what two crystal systems?
ANSWER: ORTHORHOMBIC AND MONOCLINIC (also accept: MONOCLINIC AND ORTHORHOMBIC)
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Diamond and graphite are polymorphs of which of the following elements?
W) magnesium
X) carbon
Y) sulfur
Z) nitrogen
ANSWER: X) carbon
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The streak of a mineral can be obtained most easily by performing which of the following tests?
W) rubbing the mineral on an unglazed white tile
X) putting the mineral into acid
Y) scratching the mineral with a diamond
Z) examining the mineral under a microscope
ANSWER: W) rubbing the mineral on an unglazed white tile
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of minerals are difficult to tell apart because both are always dark in color and almost equally hard?
W) muscovite and orthoclase
X) muscovite and quartz
Y) hornblende and talc
Z) hornblende and augite
ANSWER: Z) hornblende and augite
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A specialized form of concordant igneous intrusion that is characterized by a dome in the country rock and a nearly planar floor is called a:
W) lopolith
X) laccolith
Y) batholith
Z) stock
ANSWER: X) laccolith
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The most common mineral found in igneous rock is:
W) feldspar
X) olivine
Y) muscovite
Z) fluorite
ANSWER: W) feldspar
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The metamorphic rock which forms from pure limestone is:
W) slate
X) gneiss
Y) schist
Z) marble
ANSWER: Z) marble
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which coal is considered a metamorphic rock?
W) anthracite coal
X) bituminous coal
Y) lignite coal
Z) all of these
ANSWER: W) anthracite coal
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for a sloping pile of rocks at the foot of a cliff?
ANSWER: TALUS (also accept: TALUS SLOPE; SCREE)
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The earth's average density is:
W) 2.82 grams per cubic centimeter
X) 4.75 grams per cubic centimeter
Y) 5.52 grams per cubic centimeter
Z) 10.5 grams per cubic centimeter
ANSWER: Y) 5.52 grams per cubic centimeter
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The continental shelves comprise what percentage of the Earth's total land area?
W) 38 percent
X) 28 percent
Y) 18 percent
Z) 8 percent
ANSWER: Y) 18 percent
26. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Continental shelves range in width from:
W) 1,500 to 5,000 kilometers
X) 1,500 to 3,000 kilometers
Y) 0 to 5,000 kilometers
Z) 0 to 1,500 kilometers
ANSWER: Z) 0 to 1,500 kilometers
27. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The transport of energy or matter across the parallels of latitude in a north-south direction or the reverse is called:
W) global transport
X) air transport
Y) meridional transport
Z) polar transport
ANSWER: Y) meridional transport
28. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Orthoclase is what number on Moh's hardness scale?
W) 4
X) 5
Y) 6
Z) 7
ANSWER: Y) 6
29. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Diamond is what number on the Moh's hardness scale?
ANSWER: 10
30. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the chemical formula for calcite?
W) CaCO3
X) CaMgSO4
Y) FeCO3
Z) FeS2
ANSWER: W) CaCO3
31. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The nickname, "Fool's Gold," refers to which of the following?
W) calcite
X) apatite
Y) fluorite
Z) pyrite
ANSWER: Z) pyrite
32. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The oxide of what lightweight metal forms corundum, rubies, and sapphires?
ANSWER: ALUMINUM
33. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the only element which is "mined" from the sea?
W) copper
X) magnesium
Y) uranium
Z) zinc
ANSWER: X) magnesium
34. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which physical phenomenon or principle is most often applied in the search for petroleum reservoirs?
W) magnetic field distortions
X) electromagnetic radiation
Y) natural radioactive decay of minerals
Z) acoustic wave transmission and reflection
ANSWER: Z) acoustic wave transmission and reflection
35. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following names is given to the supercontinent thought to have existed in the Cambrian period?
W) Pangaea
X) Afrasia
Y) Gondwanaland
Z) Europia
ANSWER: Y) Gondwanaland
36. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the oldest period of time lasting from the beginnings of the earth to approximately 570 million year ago?
W) Precambrian
X) Pleistocene
Y) Miocene
Z) Jurassic
ANSWER: W) Precambrian
37. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following time periods includes part of the geologic range of dinosaurs?
W) Mississippian
X) Devonian
Y) Jurassic
Z) none of the above
ANSWER: Y) Jurassic
38. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer This period in earth history is the earliest subdivision of the Paleozoic Era. Many of the oldest known invertebrate fossils occur in this period, the principal types being trilobites, chitinous brachiopods, and primitive graptolites. Name this period.
ANSWER: CAMBRIAN (also accept: CAMBRIAN PERIOD)
39. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice On the Phanerozoic time scale, which of the following geologic periods occurred approximately 65 to 136 million years ago?
W) Cambrian
X) Ordovician
Y) Carboniferous
Z) Cretaceous
ANSWER: Z) Cretaceous
40. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The emergence of land plants is generally accepted as occurring during what period on the geologic time scale:
W) Cretaceous
X) Triassic
Y) Permian
Z) Silurian
ANSWER: Z) Silurian
41. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Angiosperms developed during the:
W) Precambrian period
X) Cenozoic era
Y) Paleozoic era
Z) Mesozoic era
ANSWER: Z) Mesozoic era
42. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice On a topographic map, lines that connect points of the same elevation are termed:
W) benchmarks
X) bathymetric lines
Y) contour lines
Z) isobars
ANSWER: Y) contour lines
43. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Of the following, the color blue on a topographic map indicates what surface feature?
W) vegetation
X) water
Y) photorevision
Z) rock formations
ANSWER: X) water
44. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Of the following choices, the color purple on a topographic map indicates what surface feature?
W) vegetation
X) water
Y) photorevision
Z) rock formations
ANSWER: Y) photorevision
45. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following names is used to describe the zero degree longitudinal line that runs through Greenwich, England?
W) Great Circle
X) Principle Line
Y) Longitudinal Maximum
Z) Prime Meridian
ANSWER: Z) Prime Meridian
46. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Another method of locating points on a map besides the longitude and latitude method which is frequently employed in the western states is known as the:
W) Arc Method
X) Tier and Range System
Y) Square Similar Method
Z) none of these
ANSWER: X) Tier and Range System
47. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A point of known or assumed elevation on a topographic map is termed:
W) benchmark
X) contour line
Y) bathymetric point
Z) none of these
ANSWER: W) benchmark
48. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following terms is used to describe the angular difference between TRUE north and magnetic north on a topographic map?
W) Magnetic field
X) Magnetic intensity
Y) Magnetic inclination
Z) Magnetic declination
ANSWER: Z) Magnetic declination
49. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In geological studies, a fracture in the earth's surface along which movement has taken place is termed:
W) tension fracture
X) gash fracture
Y) fault
Z) none of these
ANSWER: Y) fault
50. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In geological studies, a fault in the earth's surface in which the hanging wall has moved down relative to the footwall is termed:
W) normal fault
X) reverse fault
Y) upthrust fault
Z) none of these
ANSWER: W) normal fault
51. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In geological studies, a reverse fault having a dip less than 10 degrees is known as which of the following types of faults.
W) normal
X) overthrust
Y) strike-slip
Z) none of the above
ANSWER: X) overthrust
52. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The San Andreas fault in California is a classic example of which of the following types of faults?
W) tear fault
X) upthrust fault
Y) dip-slip fault
Z) strike-slip fault
ANSWER: Z) strike-slip fault
53. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Of the following states, the principal bituminous coal producing state in the United States is:
W) Tennessee
X) Illinois
Y) Pennsylvania
Z) West Virginia
ANSWER: Z) West Virginia
54. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Continents have three major features. The oldest areas of each continent are the:
W) folded mountains
X) stable platforms
Y) mountain cores
Z) shields
ANSWER: Z) shields
55. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for a distinctive fossil that existed over a relatively large part of the earth during a short time of earth history?
ANSWER: INDEX FOSSIL
56. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Cordaites were the ancestor of one of the following. Were they primitive:
W) amphibians
X) cone-bearing trees
Y) snakes
Z) sharks
ANSWER: X) cone-bearing trees
57. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The fossils found in the tar pits of Rancho La Brea in Los Angeles included all of the following EXCEPT one. Which one was absent?
W) saber-toothed cat
X) dire wolf
Y) wooly mammoth
Z) giant ground sloth
ANSWER: Y) wooly mammoth
58. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The fossil archaeopteryx is the earliest known example of:
W) mammal
X) fish
Y) bird
Z) snake
ANSWER: Y) bird
59. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the famous scientist who, in 1835, discovered seashell fossils 13,000 feet up in the Andes.
ANSWER: CHARLES DARWIN (also accept: DARWIN)
60. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Large streams that enter a standing body of water produce which of the following geological features at their mouths?
W) deltas
X) load casts
Y) anticlines
Z) synclines
ANSWER: W) deltas
61. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are tidal waves that surge up rivers from narrow estuaries called?
ANSWER: TIDAL BORES (also accept: BORES; BORE; TIDAL BORE)
62. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Cromwell Current is a subsurface current that flows how? Does it flow:
W) west in the Atlantic Ocean
X) east in the Atlantic Ocean
Y) east in the Pacific Ocean
Z) west in the Pacific Ocean
ANSWER: Y) east in the Pacific Ocean
63. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Crinoids are the same as one of the following. Which one is it?
W) algae
X) sea scorpions
Y) sea lilies
Z) lobe-finned fishes
ANSWER: Y) sea lilies
64. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which one of the following seas has the lowest salinity?
W) Baltic
X) Dead
Y) Mediterranean
Z) Red
ANSWER: W) Baltic
65. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to the excessive growth of algae and other primary producers in streams and lakes as a result of an input of soil nutrients? The name begins with the letter "E".
ANSWER: EUTROPHICATION
66. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In an ocean, the upper layer of water with sufficient light penetration to support photosynthesis by aquatic plants is known as the:
W) free zone
X) plant zone
Y) photic zone
Z) primary zone
ANSWER: Y) photic zone
67. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Caves are frequently associated with a certain kind of topography that includes sinkholes, disappearing streams and many limestone outcrops. With which of the following topographies are such features related?
W) arid topography
X) valley and ridge topography
Y) Karst topography
Z) none of these
ANSWER: Y) Karst topography
68. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Most cave deposits form from which of the following minerals?
W) hematite
X) calcite
Y) quartz
Z) none of these
ANSWER: X) calcite
