1. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What hydrocarbon is the primary constituent of natural gas?
ANSWER: METHANE
1. BONUS Energy Short Answer What would be the cost, in cents, if you use a 2000-watt heater for two hours and electricity costs 10 cents per kilowatt-hour?
ANSWER: 40
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice DNA ligase is an enzyme that catalyzes which of the following reactions?
W) Cutting phosphodiester bonds
X) Cutting hydrogen bonds
Y) Forming phosphodiester bonds
Z) Forming hydrogen bonds
ANSWER: Y) Forming phosphodiester bonds
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Neurons have very stereotyped electro-physiological activity that behaves in an "all-or-none" pattern. What is the name of this activity?
ANSWER: ACTION POTENTIAL
3. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the sum, in degrees, of the interior angles of a regular heptagon?
W) 540
X) 720
Y) 900
Z) 1080
ANSWER: Y) 900
3. BONUS Math Short Answer The numerals for the counting numbers are written in order, beginning with 1. What is the 125th digit written?
ANSWER: 7
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A 500-gram object is moving at 4 meters per second. What is its kinetic energy in joules?
ANSWER: 4
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements has the highest first ionization energy?
W) Calcium
X) Phosphorus
Y) Oxygen
Z) Aluminum
ANSWER: Y) Oxygen
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Given that the function f of x has a zero at x = 6. At what x-value must f of (x + 2) have a zero?
W) 3
X) 4
Y) 8
Z) 12
ANSWER: X) 4
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Len takes 12 hours to clean a 40 by 50 room. Ben can clean the same room in 10 hours. To the nearest tenth of an hour, how long would it take them if they worked together?
ANSWER: 5.5
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Pyrite is primarily composed of what two elements?
ANSWER: IRON AND SULFUR (also accept: Fe AND S)
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The ocean conveyor belt is driven by which of the following?
W) Wind and tectonic activities
X) Thermohaline gradients and wind currents
Y) Waves and tides
Z) Tidal phases and the Earth's rotation
ANSWER: X) Thermohaline gradients and wind currents
7. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer By definition, what is the octane number of heptane?
ANSWER: ZERO
7. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice If a wind turbine produces 400 kilowatts of electrical power at one wind speed, how much power, in kilowatts, will be produced if the wind speed decreases to one-half the original value?
W) 20
X) 50
Y) 100
Z) 200
ANSWER: X) 50
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many faces does a quadrilateral prism have?
ANSWER: 6
8. BONUS Math Short Answer A sequence has the property that every term starting with the third is the sum of the previous two terms. If the tenth term is 157 and the eighth term is 60, what is the sixth term?
ANSWER: 23
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What scientist used Cepheid variables in order to propose that recession velocity is related to distance?
ANSWER: EDWIN HUBBLE
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four gasses from most to least abundant in Earth's atmosphere 1) Argon 2) Nitrogen 3) Helium 4) Oxygen.
ANSWER: 2, 4, 1, 3
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the velocity, in meters per second to the nearest tenth, of a wave with wavelength 15 meters and frequency 0.3 hertz?
ANSWER: 4.5
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 300-gram mass is experiencing a constant force of 6 newtons. In meters per second squared, what is its acceleration?
ANSWER: 20
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer During what phase of the cell cycle do cells synthesize new DNA?
ANSWER: S PHASE (also accept: S; SYNTHESIS PHASE)
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Schwann cells are responsible for making what insulatory material in the peripheral nervous system?
ANSWER: MYELIN

W) 12
X) 23

Z) 45
ANSWER: Y) 34

ANSWER: 2, 4, 1, 3 (also accept: CLEAN DIESEL, HYBRID, PLUG-IN HYBRID, ALL-ELECTRIC)

ANSWER: 0.001
