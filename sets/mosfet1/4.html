1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice According to Newton's law of universal gravitation, the gravitational potential energy between two objects is proportional to what power of the distance between them?
W) –2
X) –1
Y) 1
Z) 2
ANSWER: X) –1
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Water travels through a cylindrical hose at a speed of 1.2 meters per second in a section where the radius of the hose is 4 centimeters. The hose then constricts to a radius of 2 centimeters. What is the speed of the water in this section of the hose?
ANSWER: 4.8
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What hair-like projections on a paramecium allows it to move through its environment?
ANSWER: CILIA
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following three carbohydrates by increasing molar mass 1) Lactose 2) Cellulose 3) Glucose.
ANSWER: 3,1,2 (also accept: GLUCOSE, LACTOSE, CELLULOSE)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following four layers of the atmosphere in which temperature increases with increasing altitude 1) Troposphere 2) Stratosphere 3) Mesosphere 4) Thermosphere.
ANSWER: 2,4 (also accept: STRATOSPHERE AND THERMOSPHERE)
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following would result if the density of the universe was greater than the critical density?
W) Continuous expansion of the universe
X) Flat universe
Y) Big crunch
Z) Big freeze
ANSWER: Y) Big crunch
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A coin is flipped 8 times. How many ways are there to get exactly 7 heads?
ANSWER: 8
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Triangle ABC is an obtuse triangle. Identify all of the following three angles, in degrees, that could be the measure of one of the angles in triangle ABC 1) 20 2) 90 3) 110.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3 (also accept: 20 AND 110)
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements has a yellow flame test?
W) Lithium
X) Boron
Y) Strontium
Z) Sodium
ANSWER: Z) Sodium
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The reaction of sodium hydroxide with aluminum foil evolves what flammable gas?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN GAS (also accept: H2)
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at UW Madison are studying the release of energy of a chemical reaction by measuring the amount of heat absorbed and released in water. What type of laboratory technique would most likely be used to measure the energy change?
ANSWER: CALORIMETRY
6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at the NPRE Lab at UIUC are studying nuclear fusion. Which of the following states of matter are the researchers most likely investigating?
W) Solid
X) Liquid
Y) Gas
Z) Plasma
ANSWER: Z) Plasma
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A man pulls a sled with a rope at an angle of 60 degrees to the horizontal with a 5-newton force. In joules, what is the work done by the man if he pulls the sled a distance of 4 meters?
ANSWER: 10 JOULES
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer What phenomenon occurs when the frequency of an applied force is close to a system's natural frequency, causing the amplitude of oscillations to increase?
ANSWER: RESONANCE
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the primary structure of a protein?
W) The interactions between separate polypeptide subunits
X) The three dimensional shape of the protein
Y) The amino acid sequence of the protein
Z) The coiling and folding of a polypeptide due to hydrogen bonds
ANSWER: Y) The amino acid sequence of the protein
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What phylum, whose members often have radial symmetry, do starfish and sea urchins belong to?
ANSWER: ECHINODERMATA
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three mass extinctions in chronological order 1) Late Devonian 2) Permian-Triassic 3) Cretaceous-Paleogene.
ANSWER: 1,2,3 (also accept: LATE DEVONIAN, PERMIAN-TRIASSIC, CRETACEOUS-PALEOGENE)
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following planets is NOT correctly matched to the primary component of its atmosphere?
W) Venus – carbon dioxide
X) Earth – nitrogen
Y) Mars – sulfur dioxide
Z) Jupiter – hydrogen
ANSWER: Y) Mars – sulfur dioxide
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The complex number –3 + 2i lies in which of the following quadrants in the complex plane?
W) First
X) Second
Y) Third
Z) Fourth
ANSWER: X) Second
10. BONUS Math Short Answer If a sphere has a surface area of 36π, then in terms of π, what is its volume?
ANSWER: 36π
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A one-to-one solution of which of the following would NOT produce an effective buffer?
W) Ammonia and ammonium chloride
X) Carbonic acid and sodium bicarbonate
Y) Hydrofluoric acid and potassium fluoride
Z) Perchloric acid and silver perchlorate
ANSWER: Z) Perchloric acid and silver perchlorate
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In a 3-liter container of hydrogen gas at 300 kelvin, the pressure is measured to be 1.2 atmospheres. If the volume of the container is expanded to 5 liters while the temperature stays the same, what would be the new pressure, in atmospheres, of the container?
ANSWER: 0.72
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at UIUC are using acetone to simulate the water-repellant effects of cicada wings. What property describes substances that repel water?
ANSWER: HYDROPHOBIC
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at the University of Chicago have developed "flashlights" to look inside organelles and study how they work. Identify all of the following three organelles that are theorized to have developed from endosymbiosis 1) Golgi apparatus 2) Mitochondria 3) Chloroplast.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3 (also accept: MITOCHONDRIA AND CHLOROPLAST)

W) Inductor
X) LED

Z) Resistor
ANSWER: X) LED

ANSWER: 2 AND 3 (also accept: SPEED AND DISTANCE)
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What enzyme, found in the mesophyll cells of C3 plants, catalyses the carboxylation of RuBP in the Calvin Cycle?
ANSWER: RUBISCO

ANSWER: 1 AND 3 (also accept: VITAMIN A AND VITAMIN D)

ANSWER: MONSOON

ANSWER: 7

ANSWER: 36

ANSWER: Mn(SO4)2

Z) Zinc

ANSWER: PHOTON
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer A piece of aluminum has a density of 2.5 grams per cubic centimeter. If the aluminum thermally expands so that its volume increases by 25% while its mass remains the same, what is the new density of the metal, in grams per cubic centimeter?
ANSWER: 2

ANSWER: 2 AND 3 (also accept: PYRUVATE AND ATP)
20. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is true regarding the phospholipids that make up the cell membrane?
