1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The amount of heat required for melting a substance is called its:
W) heat of liquidation
X) enthalpy of evaporation
Y) enthalpy of vaporization
Z) heat of fusion
ANSWER: Z) heat of fusion
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A Beer’s law standard straight-line curve is a plot of what value on the ordinate and what value on the abscissa?
ANSWER: ORDINATE = ABSORBANCE; ABSCISSA = CONCENTRATION (or molarity)
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer According to the standard model of particle physics, how many leptons are there, including both matter and antimatter?
ANSWER: 12
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer Order the following three substances from the one that will show the largest increase in temperature for each single calorie added to one gram of that substance, to the one that will show the least: lead, water, aluminum
ANSWER: LEAD, ALUMINUM, WATER (also accept: Pb, Al, H2O)

X) -60 millivolts
Y) -45 millivolts
Z) 0 millivolts
ANSWER: W) -70 millivolts
