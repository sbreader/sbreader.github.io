6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A skater pushes off a railing to increase her speed on a horizontal frictionless surface. Treating the skater as a system, it is clear that the 3 external forces, gravity, normal, and force due to the railing, do no work to the skater. Yet, she somehow increases her kinetic energy, which seems to be a violation of the conservation of mechanical energy. Which of the following explains this phenomenon?
W) We must account for all external forces in a system
X) The skater is expanding her internal energy to increase her kinetic energy
Y) The normal force and force due to gravity are non-conservative
Z) Although parts of her body move, her center of mass doesn’t move
ANSWER: X) The skater is expanding her internal energy to increase her kinetic energy
