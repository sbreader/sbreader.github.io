1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an example of systematic error?
W) A scale randomly differs by a few grams for each measurement
X) The Earth is not a perfect sphere when calculating the local acceleration of gravity
Y) A spring does not precisely follow Hooke's law
Z) The molar mass of an element has a small margin of error
