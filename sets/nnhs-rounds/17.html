X) Buffer

Z) Protein dye

ANSWER: MONOAMINE OXIDASES (also accept: MAO)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is FALSE regarding hydrothermal vents?
W) Hydrothermal vents typically form near mid-ocean ridges
X) The color of white smokers results from iron and sulfur
Y) Black smokers are more common than white smokers
Z) Black smokers are hotter than white smokers
ANSWER: X) The color of white smokers results from iron and sulfur
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify which spectral class each of the following three spectral lines belongs to, according to the Harvard classification system 1) Strong TiO lines 2) Strong balmer lines, weak calcium lines 3) Weak balmer lines, medium calcium lines.
ANSWER: 1) M; 2) A; 3) G
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In triangle ABC, D lies on side BC such that AD is an angle bisector, AB = 5, AC = 7, and BC = 8. What is the ratio of the area of triangle DAC to triangle DAB?
ANSWER: 7/5

ANSWER: √21

Z) Addison’s disease leads to decreased bone mass
