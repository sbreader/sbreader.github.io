1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A particle’s Compton wavelength is 257 meters. Assuming that planck’s constant is 6.6 × 10-34 joules per second, and giving your answer in kilograms, what is the particle’s mass?
ANSWER: 8.5 × 10-45
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer An RL circuit has a current of 5 milliamperes and dissipates power at a rate of 37 watts. Assuming that the current of the RL circuit is always constant, what is the voltage of the circuit?
ANSWER: 7.4 MILLIVOLTS
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules has an s-character of 33%?
W) NH3
X) CO2
Y) C2O32-
Z) SO3
ANSWER: Y) C2O32-
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Give the proper name for the coordinated complex with a central atom of iron, 4 CO ligands, and 2 OH minus ligands.
ANSWER: TETRACARBONYL-HYDROXO-IRON(III) ION
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are using structural biology and the Advanced Light Source to study COVID-19 antibodies. What antibody class is responsible for travelling to cells and releasing chemicals that cause an allergic reaction?
ANSWER: IMMUNOGLOBULIN E (also accept: IGE)
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab are conducting research on how cells regulate enzyme activity to maintain a liquid center. Identify all of the following 3 statements regarding enzymes that are true 1) The region where the enzyme binds to the substrate is called the enzyme-substrate complex 2) In catalysis, enzymes change the gibbs free energy for a reaction 3) Noncompetitive enzyme inhibitors impede enzymatic interaction.
ANSWER: 3 ONLY

ANSWER: 2.3
11. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Annabeth tries pulling a pencil apart. Assuming there are only small changes in heat and temperature, and that there is no net change in internal energy in the pencil, which of the following quantities is indirectly proportional to the inward force from the pencil?
W) The force that Annabeth exerts on the pencil
X) The rate of change of the entropy of the pencil
Y) The change in potential energy of the pencil
Z) The pressure on the pencil
ANSWER: X) The rate of change of the entropy of the pencil
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at Brookhaven National Lab built the Booster Synchrotron to accelerate ion and proton beams. Which of the following is a valid reason for why the Booster didn’t use a cyclotron design?
W) Ions/protons in the Booster are travelling at speeds where their frequency isn’t constant
X) Ions/protons are too unstable in a cyclotron
Y) Ions/protons in the booster are traveling at speeds where they can’t be accelerated by electric fields
Z) Ions/protons in the Booster are traveling such that they can’t be controlled by magnetic fields
ANSWER: W) Ions/protons in the Booster are travelling at speeds where their frequency isn’t constant
