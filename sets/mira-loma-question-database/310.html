1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Xanthine, theobromine, caffeine, uric acid, isoguanine. What is common about all of these molecules?
W) They are all soluble.
X) They are all inorganic molecules.
Y) They are all pyrimidines.
Z) They are all purines.
ANSWER: Z) They are all purines.
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The glucose that we can digest is known as amylose, or starch. What is the difference between this and cellulose, another form of glucose?
W) Starch possesses an alpha linkage that cellulose does not.
X) Starch possesses a beta linkage that cellulose does not.
Y) Starch has an extra lone pair that makes it easier to digest.
Z) Starch is a stereoisomer of cellulose.
ANSWER: W) Starch possesses an alpha linkage that cellulose does not.
