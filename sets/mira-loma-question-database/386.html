1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Starting from the vena cava and ending in the aorta, what is the order of the chambers in which blood must flow through the heart
ANSWER: RIGHT ATRIUM, RIGHT VENTRICLE, LEFT ATRIUM, LEFT VENTRICLE
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Starting from the vena cava and ending in the aorta, what is the order of valves in the heart through which blood must flow
W) Tricuspid, Mitral, Pulmonary, Aortic
X) Mitral, Tricuspid, Aortic, Pulmonary
Y) Tricuspid, Pulmonary, Mitral, Aortic
Z) Aortic, Mitral, Pulmonary, Tricuspid
ANSWER: Y) Tricuspid, Pulmonary, Mitral, Aortic
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Lipids are a class of macromolecules defined by
W) Presence of glycerol
X) Poor solvability in water
Y) Presence of fatty acids
Z) High solubility in water
ANSWER: X) Poor solvability in water
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Cholesterol is defined as a
W) Carbohydrate
X) Nucleic Acid
Y) Protein
Z) Lipid
ANSWER: Z) Lipid
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organelles is directly involved in cell locomotion
W) Nucleus
X) Cilium
Y) Mitochondrion
Z) Golgi Apparatus
ANSWER: X) Cilium
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which side of the Golgi Apparatus functions in receiving molecules in vesicles
W) Trans face
X) Cis face
Y) Reticulum face
Z) Ribosomal face
ANSWER: X) Cis face

W) Trypanosomosis
X) Bubonic plague
Y) Influenza
Z) Scarlet fever
ANSWER: W) Trypanosomosis

W) The function of helper T-cells
X) The rate of the heart
Y) The lining of blood vessels
Z) The nervous system
ANSWER: Y) The lining of blood vessels

X) Chlorophyll

Z) Granum

W) Lipids
X) Carbohydrates

Z) Nucleic acids

Z) Enzymes require relatively low amounts of heat
