1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Along with possessing a double membrane, chloroplasts also have what interior membranous system of flattened sacs crucial for the light reactions of photosynthesis?
ANSWER: Thylakoid
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Assuming Mendelian inheritance, which of the following conclusions cannot be drawn if a cross between true-breeding green and yellow flowers of the same species results in all green offspring?
W) A yellow flower from the parental generation would produce only yellow offspring when self-pollinated
X) A green flower from the parental generation would produce only green offspring when self-pollinated
Y) Heterozygous flowers would be green
Z) All flowers in the F2 generation would be green
ANSWER: Z) All flowers in the F2 generation would be green
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many 1 by 3 tiles are required to fully tile a 4 by 6 grid?
ANSWER: 8
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Charlie has two empty jugs with capacities of 2 liters and 5 liters. In one step, he may fill a jug, empty a jug, or pour water from one jug into the other until one is full or the other is empty. What is the minimum number of steps required to obtain exactly 1 liter of water in the 5-liter jug and 0 liters in the 2-liter jug?
ANSWER: 5
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In the ground-state electron configuration of an atom, which of the following subshells would be filled after the 4s orbital is filled?
W) 2p
X) 3s
Y) 3p
Z) 3d
ANSWER: Z) 3d
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following acids is diprotic?
W) Hypochloric acid
X) Perchloric acid
Y) Nitric acid
Z) Sulfuric acid
ANSWER: Z) Sulfuric acid
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three atmospheric layers in which temperature tends to increase as altitude increases 1) Stratosphere 2) Mesosphere 3) Thermosphere
ANSWER: 1 and 3
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following constellations is located towards the center of the Milky Way?
W) Orion
X) Sagittarius
Y) Cassiopeia
Z) Taurus
ANSWER: X) Sagittarius
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What group of subatomic particles, containing baryons and leptons, has half-integer spin?
ANSWER: Fermions
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 20-meter tower is filled with water and a tap is placed 10 meters above the ground on the tower. What is the velocity of the water exiting the tap, to the nearest meter per second?
ANSWER: 14
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Ritwik monitors bacterial growth in culture and notices that once the population reaches a certain size, growth slows and the population declines due to limited resources. What term describes this maximum sustainable population size in a given environment?
ANSWER: Carrying capacity
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice MPF is a cyclin-dependent complex important for triggering a cell's passage beyond interphase. Which of the following phases in the cell cycle would MPF promote entry into?
W) G1 phase
X) S phase
Y) G2 phase
Z) M phase
ANSWER: Z) M phase
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Thomas is currently applying to jobs. If he applies to 3 jobs every week, 2 jobs are automatically rejected every 4 weeks, and the rest of the job applications are left standing, how many job applications will he have left standing after 12 weeks?
ANSWER: 30
7. BONUS Math Multiple Choice A standard six-sided dice is weighted such that each face's probability of being rolled is proportional to its value. What is the expected value of a roll of the dice?
W) 12/3
X) 13/3
Y) 14/3
Z) 15/3
ANSWER: X) 13/3
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three isoelectronic species in increasing order of atomic radius 1) Cl– 2) Ar 3) Ca2+
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules has a square planar molecular shape?
W) Sulfur trioxide
X) Xenon tetrafluoride
Y) Ammonia
Z) Methane
ANSWER: X) Xenon tetrafluoride
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Astronomers compare an object’s apparent and absolute magnitudes using the inverse-square law. Using this relationship, what general class of objects with known luminosities allow determination of cosmic distance measurements?
ANSWER: Standard candles
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of streams flow rarely, mostly after large rainstorms?
W) Intermittent river
X) Ephemeral stream
Y) Perennial stream
Z) Glacial stream
ANSWER: X) Ephemeral stream
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If 6.25% of a radioactively decaying sample remains after 20 days, what is the half-life of the sample, in days?
ANSWER: 5
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer Total internal reflection occurs beyond what angle, at which a light beam is entirely reflected back into its original medium?
ANSWER: Critical angle
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A dog initially salivates only when food is placed in its mouth. After repeatedly hearing a bell just before receiving food, the dog begins to salivate upon hearing the bell alone. Which of the following learning behaviors best explains this phenomenon?
W) Operant conditioning
X) Classical conditioning
Y) Habituation
Z) Imprinting
ANSWER: X) Classical conditioning
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following three plant structures by increasing distance from the stem 1) Leaf blade 2) Node 3) Petiole
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Yunyi picks sticks out of a pile of 15 sticks, each with one of the lengths between 1 and 15, inclusive. He selects the sticks with lengths 2 and 7. How many of the remaining sticks could he select to form a non-degenerate triangle with his other two sticks?
ANSWER: 2
12. BONUS Math Short Answer Given that the log of 2 is 0.301 and the log of 3 is 0.477, what is the log of 75?
ANSWER: 1.875

W) Gibbs free energy
X) Entropy
Y) Enthalpy
Z) Activation energy
ANSWER: Z) Activation energy
13. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Edwin reacts 32 grams of oxygen with 6 grams of hydrogen to form water. Respectively, how many moles of water are formed, and what is the limiting reactant?
W) 2 moles, and oxygen is limiting
X) 2 moles, and hydrogen is limiting
Y) 3 moles, and oxygen is limiting
Z) 3 moles, and hydrogen is limiting
ANSWER: W) 2 moles, and oxygen is limiting

W) Limestone
X) Quartzite
Y) Gypsum
Z) Halite
ANSWER: W) Limestone
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why cold fronts commonly trigger thunderstorms?
W) Cold fronts force warm air downwards, promoting convection
X) Cold fronts force warm air upward, promoting convection
Y) Cold fronts force warm air downwards, inhibiting convection
Z) Cold fronts force warm air upward, inhibiting convection
ANSWER: X) Cold fronts force warm air upward, promoting convection

ANSWER: 2, 3, 1

ANSWER: 4

ANSWER: Aquaporins

ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Min Shin is making cake balls for his birthday. His recipe reads that, for every 2 cups of sugar he uses, he has to use 5 cups of cake mix. If Min Shin can buy any quantity of sugar at a rate of 50 cents a cup, and he has 43 cups of cake mix, how much money, in cents, does he need to spend on sugar?
ANSWER: 860
17. BONUS Math Short Answer Edwin randomly assigns A, B, C, D the values 4, 1, 6, 7 in some order. What is the expected value of the sum AB + CD, where AB and CD are 2 digit positive integers?
ANSWER: 99
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Gaurav is analyzing a sealed vessel containing a mixture of xenon and helium gas. He notices that the total pressure of the vessel equals the sum of the partial pressures of xenon and helium. What chemical law does his finding support?
ANSWER: Dalton's law (also accept: Dalton's Law of partial pressures; Law of partial pressures)
18. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The dissolution of NaCl is endothermic, but this reaction's entropy change is positive. Under which conditions is this process thermodynamically favorable?

X) At low temperatures
Y) At all temperatures
Z) At no temperatures
ANSWER: W) At high temperatures

ANSWER: 1/8

ANSWER: Kepler's 2nd law

ANSWER: 16
