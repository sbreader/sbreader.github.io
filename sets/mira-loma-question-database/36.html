Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name for the structure that joins the two parietal bones in the midline?
ANSWER: sagittal suture
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What connective tissue joins the ribs to the sternum?
ANSWER: costal cartilage
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Respectively, how many true ribs, false ribs, and floating ribs are there?
ANSWER: 7,3,2
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What pigment in skin causes the characteristic yellow color in Asians?
ANSWER: carotene
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What glands secrete sebum, an oily substance that lubricates the skin?
ANSWER: sebaceous glands
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the names of bundles of muscle fibers which compose muscle?
ANSWER: fascicles
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Which lines separate sarcomeres from each other?
ANSWER: Z lines
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which protein, also the longest word in the English language, joins myosin filaments to the ends of the sarcomere?
ANSWER: titin
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The thalamus and hypothalamus all compose which part of the human brain?
ANSWER: diencephalon
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which nucleus in the hypothalamus is the most important of the body's biological clocks, and controls circadian rhythms?
ANSWER: suprachiasmatic nucleus
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What organ system of the body comprises of the skin, and its affliated glands and hair?
ANSWER: integumentary system
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are networks of spinal nerves where fibers interconnect at places such as the brachial, celiac, or lumbar regions?
ANSWER: plexus
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are spaces between Schwann cells on myelinated neurons where an action potential can be revitalized?
ANSWER: node of Ranvier
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for bones that ossified from a cartilaginous model?
ANSWER: endochondral bones
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name for the body of a vertebra, the main portion?
ANSWER: centrum
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of joint joins the metacarpals of the fingers to the phalanges?
ANSWER: condyloid
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Leukocytes are divided into two categories. What are these?
ANSWER: granular and agranular
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer After the foramen ovale closes, after birth, what structure replaces it?
ANSWER: fossa ovalis
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer After the traveling through the Bundles of His, what are the final nerves that conduct an electric signal in the heart?
ANSWER: Purkinje fibers
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Give the three layers of a blood vessel wall, from inside to outside.
ANSWER: tunica intima, tunica media, and tunica adventitia
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Where the two vertebral arteries join at the brainstem, what new artery forms?
ANSWER: basilar artery
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What pulse point can be found on the posterior part of the knee?
ANSWER: Popliteal
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What sphincter separates the esophagus from the stomach?
ANSWER: cardiac sphincter
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What hormone secreted by the adrenal glands can increase sodium reabsorption in the body?
ANSWER: aldosterone
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer After fertilization, what signals to the corpus luteum to increase in size, thus releasing large amount of estrogen and progesterone, which develop and the endometrium and placenta? Without this, the corpus luteum degenerates, causing abortion of the embryo.
ANSWER: hCG (human Chorionic Gonadotropin)
