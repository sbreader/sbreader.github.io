1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 statements that are true 1) A diverging lens always produces a smaller image than object 2) A converging lens always produces a bigger image than object 3) A convergent mirror always produces a virtual image?
ANSWER: 1
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A lens has a focal length of f in water, and a focal length of r in a medium with index of refraction 1.66. The ratio of f/r is closest to which of the following?
W) 1/4
X) 1/2
Y) 1
Z) 2
ANSWER: Z) 2
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers are studying how poplar trees grow on the molecular level so that they can optimize their growth for energy production. Which of the following properties of poplar trees best explains why they are a promising source for bioenergy and biomass?
W) They have very thick trunks
X) They grow very quickly
Y) They produce fruits rapidly
Z) Their roots grow very quickly
ANSWER: X) They grow very quickly
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the specific term for a redox reaction in which the same element is both oxidized and reduced?
ANSWER: DISPROPORTIONATION (also accept: DISMUTATION)
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What two functional groups are the products of alkyl radical disproportionation?
ANSWER: ALKANE AND ALKENE
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A hospitalized patient is experiencing extreme pain in his heels and tailbone due to prolonged pressure on those areas from lack of movement. What is the name of this skin problem?
ANSWER: DECUBITUS ULCER (also accept: PRESSURE ULCER; BED SORE)
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, which of the following are true about the epidermis 1) There are five layers 2) The stratum corneum layer undergoes mitosis 3) It is vascular 4) Merkel and langerhan cells are present in the stratum spinosum layer
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A circle has a sector with central angle θ. Given that the radius of the circle and the area of the sector are both equal to 5, what is the value of θ (in radians)?
ANSWER: 0.4
5. BONUS Math Multiple Choice What is the height of a cone made out of a circular sector with radius 2 and central angle π?
W) 1
X) √2
Y) √3
Z) 2
ANSWER: Y) √3
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the type of mass wasting where unconsolidated material slides downwards along a curved surface?
ANSWER: SLUMP
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about the weathering of silicates?
W) almost all silicates are incredibly resistant to weathering
X) silicates higher up on the Bowen's reaction series are generally more resistant to weathering
Y) silicates higher up on the Bowen's reaction series are generally less resistant to weathering
Z) silicates that have a higher ranking on the Mohs hardness scale are always more resistant to weathering
ANSWER: Y) silicates higher up on the Bowen's reaction series are generally less resistant to weathering
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory are studying how the sun’s rays interact with a 3-D surface, such as those on mountains. What is the term for the fraction of incoming solar radiation that is reflected by a surface?
ANSWER: ALBEDO
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The researchers found that in general, a 3-D surface has higher temperatures than a 2-D surface would. At which of the following times of day would the 3-D effect of solar radiation on a mountain be at its lowest, leading to a relative cooling?
W) Dawn
X) Midday
Y) Evening
Z) Dusk
ANSWER: X) Midday
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of inhibitor is an inhibitor if the Km of an enzyme decreases from 85 micromolar to 60 micromolar when the inhibitor is added to the enzyme solution?
ANSWER: COMPETITIVE
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What name is given to the type of inhibition where the inhibitor binds to the enzyme at the same time as the substrate and whose presence is indicated by a decrease in both v max and k m?
ANSWER: UNCOMPETITIVE
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If sound intensity has units of W/m2, what are it's SI base units?
ANSWER: kg/s3
9. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Snell's Law does not help explain or provide information about which of the following scenarios?
W) Reflection
X) Refraction
Y) Diffraction
Z) Materials with Negative Indices of Refraction
ANSWER: Y) Diffraction
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the chemical formula of the compound sodium arsenite?
ANSWER: Na3AsO3
10. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds is the least stable?
W) Vanadium (I) nitrate
X) Vanadium (II) sulfate
Y) Vanadium (III) nitride
Z) Vanadium (IV) oxide
ANSWER: W) Vanadium (I) nitrate
11. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice If g(x) is the inverse of f(x) and f(x) = ln(x), Which of the following is the value of g’(1)?
W) 1/e
X) e

Z) -1
ANSWER: X) e
11. BONUS Math Short Answer A twenty-sided die with faces labeled with positive integers from 1 to 20 is rolled 4 times. What is the expected value of the sum of the rolls?
ANSWER: 42
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By what factor would the transit depth increase in a system consisting of a planet orbiting around a star if the radius of the planet is multiplied by 6 and the radius of the star is multiplied by 2?
ANSWER: 9

ANSWER: JUAN DE FUCA AND NORTH AMERICAN
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What type of fruit develops from many carpels of one flower?
W) Simple Fruit
X) Aggregate Fruit
Y) Multiple Fruit
Z) Accessory Fruit
ANSWER: X) Aggregate Fruit
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of when an incomplete flower results in the flower being sterile?
W) Grass flowers that lack petals
X) The parts of a sunflower that appear to be the petals
Y) Coconut flower that lacks stamen
Z) Watermelon flower that lacks carpel
ANSWER: X) The parts of a sunflower that appear to be the petals
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are attempting to compute an atomic nucleus, specifically the deuteron. Deuteron is an isotope of which element?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Deuteron is also the nucleus of deuterium, which is widely used as a tracer for analyzing chemical reactions. What compound is formed when deuterium combines with oxygen?
ANSWER: HEAVY WATER (also accept: DEUTERIUM OXIDE)
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In conducting the double slit experiment, Thomas Young wanted which of the following to produce a more visible diffraction pattern?
W) Greater distance between slits
X) Less distance between Slits
Y) More transparent material on either side of slit
Z) Object with high index of refraction between slits
ANSWER: X) Less distance between Slits

X) Uranium-238

16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Assume that there is a cross section of stratas labeled from top to bottom as A, B, and C with a dike going through stratas B and C.
