1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Using the Maclaurin series to 2 terms, compute sine of 1.
ANSWER: 5/6
1. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area enclosed by the polar curve r = 1 – cosine theta?
ANSWER: 3π/2
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Rounding your answer to the nearest integer, calculate the phase velocity, in meters per second, of a wave travelling in a lagoon with water depth of 40 centimeters.
ANSWER: 2 METERS PER SECOND
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Meridiana is the alpha star of the Corona Australis constellation. If it is observed to have a parallax angle of 0.025 arcseconds, calculate the distance to Meridiana in light years. Round your answer to two significant figures.
ANSWER: 130 LIGHT YEARS
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following types of particle detectors uses a superheated liquid?
W) Bubble chamber
X) Cloud chamber
Y) Geiger counter
Z) Scintillation counter
ANSWER: W) Bubble chamber
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true about the characteristic K-alpha X-ray line 1) It is produced when a hole moves from the n = 1 to the n = 2 energy level 2) It is shorter in wavelength than the K-beta line 3) Its wavelength increases as atomic number increases.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Decreased fibrin production can be traced back to a malfunction in what organ?
W) Spleen
X) Adrenal Glands
Y) Liver
Z) Lymph Nodes
ANSWER: Y) Liver
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer The reaction converting prothrombin to thrombin in the liver is dependent on which vitamin?
ANSWER: VITAMIN K (also accept: PHYLLOQUINONE; PHYTOMENADIONE)
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three elements in terms of increasing standard reduction potential 1) Fluorine 2) Oxygen 3) Silver.
ANSWER: 3,2,1
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When bonded to a central metal atom, cyanide ligands can either donate their lone pair on carbon or nitrogen. What type of isomerism is exhibited due to this effect?
ANSWER: LINKAGE ISOMERISM
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory operate the National Ignition Facility, which is currently being used to study high-energy density science. There, they are trying to get a chamber to temperatures of up to 300 million Kelvin in order to give deuterium and tritium nuclei enough kinetic energy to start fusion. What energy barrier must be overcome to get the nuclei close enough to fuse?
ANSWER: COULOMB BARRIER
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab are studying superconductive grains in iron-nickel meteorites. Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding iron-nickel meteorites 1) They are thought to originate from cores of differentiated planetesimals 2) They are the most common type of meteorite 3) The iron-nickel alloy is almost completely present in the taenite and kamacite phases.
ANSWER: 1,3
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer At what x value is the Mean Value Theorem satisfied when applied to f(x) = 10 x2 – 2 x + 1 across the interval x = 0 to x = 8?
ANSWER: 4
7. BONUS Math Short Answer The double factorial is similar to the factorial, but is defined as the product of every other integer. For example, 7 double factorial equals 7 times 5 times 3 times 1. How many trailing zeros does 2020 double factorial have?
ANSWER: 251
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about guyot formation is not true?
W) Guyots are generally volcanic in origin
X) Guyots subside as they move away from the mid-ocean ridge
Y) Guyots become flat topped as they are eroded at the sea surface
Z) Guyots rarely contain coral due to their depth
ANSWER: Z) Guyots rarely contain coral due to their depth
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four stages of the Wilson cycle following a continent-continent collision 1) Subduction of oceanic lithosphere 2) Pre-drift extension 3) Seafloor spreading 4) Opening of shallow ocean basin.
ANSWER: 2,4,3,1
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What shape are the nodal surfaces that correspond to fully destructive interference between two coherent wave sources in 3D space?
W) Spheres
X) Hyperbolic paraboloids
Y) Hyperboloids of one sheet
Z) Hyperboloids of two sheets
ANSWER: Z) Hyperboloids of two sheets
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer A train is headed directly towards an observer with an initial velocity of 360 m/s. As the bell on the train rings for the first time, it begins decelerating at 18 m/s2. The bell rings a total of four times with a uniform spacing of 1 second. Assuming the speed of sound to be 343 m/s, order the four bell rings from FIRST to reach the observer to the last, using 1 for the first ring, 2 for the second ring, and so on.
ANSWER: 2,1,3,4

12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Lab are studying the distribution of the 2+ and 4+ oxidation states of tin in magma bodies. Though tin is not a transition metal, it may exist in both 2+ and 4+ oxidation states due to what effect?
