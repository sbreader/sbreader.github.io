Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true regarding coelacanths?
W) They possess lobed fins
X) They give birth to live young
Y) They have a hollow, fluid-filled notochord
Z) They have a skeleton made of cartilage
ANSWER: Z) They have a skeleton made of cartilage
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three plant physiological processes that are a direct result of ethylene gas exposure 1) Triple response 2) Leaf abscission 3) Fruit ripening.
ANSWER: ALL
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at the Joint Genomics Institute have recently discovered a group of archaea species that steal and take advantage of resources of other species of archaea. This is best characterized as what symbiotic relationship?
ANSWER: PARASITISM
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab have been looking into identifying green alternatives to current commercial jet fuels. Which of the following techniques allows researchers to analyze properties of possible alternative fuels by probing their vibrational modes?
W) Infrared spectroscopy
X) X-ray crystallography
Y) NMR spectroscopy
Z) Mass spectrometry
ANSWER: W) Infrared spectroscopy
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is 72 times 27?
ANSWER: 1944
3. BONUS Math Short Answer Consider an isosceles triangle with vertices A, B, and C where the angle at vertex A is 40 degrees. In degrees, what are all the possible angles at vertex B?
ANSWER: 40, 70, 100 (also accept: in any order)
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A football is thrown directly upward into the air at a speed of 35 meters per second. Assuming that g is 10 meters per second squared, how long in seconds would it take for the football to hit the ground?
ANSWER: 7
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer Curie’s law states that the magnetization of a solid is inversely proportional to what quantity?
ANSWER: TEMPERATURE
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of biogenous sediment is made from the shells of diatoms and radiolaria?
ANSWER: SILICEOUS OOZE
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Astronomers at UC Berkeley recently discovered that variation in the gaseous composition of Saturn’s atmosphere may cause planetary-scale storm events. Which of the following best characterizes Saturn’s primary atmospheric composition?
W) Ammonia and Hydrogen
X) Nitrogen and Oxygen
Y) Hydrogen and Helium
Z) Methane and Ammonia
ANSWER: Y) Hydrogen and Helium
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a strong acid?
W) Hydrogen iodide
X) Hydrogen bromide
Y) Hydrogen chloride
Z) Hydrogen fluoride
ANSWER: Z) Hydrogen fluoride
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three molecules that are exceptions to the octet rule 1) Acetic acid 2) Borane 3) Sulfuric acid.
ANSWER: 2
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the kinetic energy, in joules, of a cart with a mass of 15 kilograms that is traveling in a straight line at a speed of 2 meters per second?
ANSWER: 30
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer The equipotential surfaces surrounding a point charge most closely resemble what 3D shape?
ANSWER: SPHERE
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Eukaryotic cells are defined by the presence of membrane-bound organelles. Which of the following organelles has a double membrane?
W) Golgi apparatus
X) Smooth endoplasmic reticulum
Y) Rough endoplasmic reticulum
Z) Chloroplast
ANSWER: Z) Chloroplast
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the primary function of stomata in plants?
W) Regulation of gas exchange
X) Synthesis of carotenoids
Y) Transport of sugars
Z) Transport of water
ANSWER: W) Regulation of gas exchange
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals is higher on the Mohs hardness scale than quartz?
W) Talc
X) Topaz
Y) Calcite
Z) Apatite
ANSWER: X) Topaz
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three processes that are main causes of sea level rise 1) Thermal expansion of seawater 2) Melting of land ice 3) Melting of oceanic ice.
ANSWER: 1
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules is nonpolar?
W) NH3
X) SF6
Y) CH3F
Z) HCN
ANSWER: X) SF6
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three acids in terms of increasing pKa 1) Acetic acid 2) Sulfuric acid 3) Boric acid.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A right triangle has a hypotenuse of length 16 and an area of 64. If a similar triangle has a hypotenuse of length 6, then what is its area?
ANSWER: 9
11. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the y–intercept of the graph of y = the fraction with numerator x2 + 3x – 4 and denominator x – 1?
ANSWER: 4
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers in the Doudna lab have recently identified similar adaptations across various strains of SARS–CoV–2 that developed independently of each other. What evolutionary pattern best describes this development?
ANSWER: CONVERGENT EVOLUTION
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at the Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are building a high-speed quantum computing network based on the concept that interacting with one particle can influence the quantum state of a different particle arbitrarily far away. What is the name of this phenomenon?
ANSWER: ENTANGLEMENT
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Find all roots of the following parabola: y = 2x2 + 5x + 2.
ANSWER: x = –1/2 AND x = –2
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Let f of x be a differentiable function such that f of –2 equals –4 and f of 2 equals 8. The Mean Value Theorem guarantees that the derivative of f obtains what value between –2 and 2?
ANSWER: 3
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Angela analyzed a biological compound in a mass spectrometer and found that it contained carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, and phosphorus. Which of the following macromolecules would this compound most likely be classified as?
W) Carbohydrate
X) Lipid
Y) Nucleic acid
Z) Protein
ANSWER: Y) Nucleic acid
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following epithelial cell types is best suited for organs that require expansion of surface area, such as the bladder or urethra?
W) Simple squamous
X) Pseudostratified columnar
Y) Stratified cuboidal
Z) Transitional
ANSWER: Z) Transitional
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three fault classifications which correctly describe the San Andreas Fault 1) Transform 2) Strike-slip 3) Normal.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the areas of the lunar surface which contain large amounts of basalt, believed to have been formed by the surfacing of molten rock from the Moon’s mantle?
ANSWER: MARIA (also accept: MARE)
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the work, in joules, required to move a 1-coulomb charge from the surface of a 20-volt sphere to a 10-volt sheet?
ANSWER: –10
16. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following systems has the greatest gravitational potential energy?
W) 500-gram ball held at 10 meters above Earth’s surface
X) 1-kilogram ball held at 10 meters above the Moon’s surface
Y) 5-kilogram ball held at 10 meters above Earth’s surface
Z) 5-kilogram ball held at 10 meters above the Moon’s surface
ANSWER: Y) 5-kilogram ball held at 10 meters above Earth’s surface
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice If the pH of an NaOH solution at 25 degrees Celsius is 12, then what is the pOH?
W) 2
X) 3
Y) 5
Z) 14
ANSWER: W) 2
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Peptide bonds are formed between what two functional groups?
ANSWER: CARBOXYL AND AMINO (also accept: CARBOXYLIC ACID AND AMINE)
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What moon is known as the most volcanically active object in the Solar System?
ANSWER: IO
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Antares is approximately 170 parsecs from the Earth. To the nearest thousandth of an arcsecond, what is the parallax angle of Antares as viewed from the Earth?
ANSWER: 0.006
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at the Lawrence Livermore National Lab are studying the growth of crystals in deuterium and tritium isotopes, which are used as fuel in fusion ignition experiments. Deuterium and tritium are isotopes of what element?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer The UC Berkeley Ultracold Atomic Physics Lab has been exploring the capabilities of a state of matter which can form below a critical temperature in some materials, in which a large fraction of the bosons all occupy the same quantum state. What is the name for this state of matter?
ANSWER: BOSE–EINSTEIN CONDENSATE (also accept: BEC)
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A circuit consists of two 3-ohm resistors in parallel connected in series with a 1-ohm resistor. In ohms and to two significant figures, what is the equivalent resistance of this circuit?
ANSWER: 2.5
20. BONUS Physics Short Answer A block is placed with zero initial velocity at the top of a 10-meter long frictionless inclined plane and has a velocity of 10 meters per second when it reaches the bottom. Assuming g equals 10 meters per second squared, what angle in degrees does the ramp form with the ground?
ANSWER: 30
21. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If f of x equals 2x2 – 5 and g of x equals 3x, what is f of g of 2?
ANSWER: 67
21. BONUS Math Short Answer Consider the following set of integers: 3, 5, 11, 6, and x. If this set has a range of 12, then what is the maximum value for its mean?
ANSWER: 8
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements explains why zinc is used in the galvanization of iron?
W) Zinc is cheaper than iron
X) Zinc is more easily oxidized than iron
Y) Zinc increases the conductivity of iron
Z) Zinc is lighter than iron
ANSWER: X) Zinc is more easily oxidized than iron
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Nickel is industrially refined via the Mond process, in which solid nickel is reacted with carbon monoxide in a sealed container to form nickel tetracarbonyl. In scientific notation and to one significant figure, what is the equilibrium constant of this reaction at 300 kelvins if the partial pressure of CO in the container is 4 atmospheres?
ANSWER: 4 × 10-3
23. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true regarding the phospholipid bilayer?
W) The phosphate head is hydrophilic and the lipid tail is hydrophobic
X) Nonpolar molecules like carbon dioxide must use a transmembrane protein
Y) Large polar molecules such as glucose cannot perform simple diffusion
Z) Large nonpolar molecules such as glycerides must perform facilitated diffusion
ANSWER: X) Nonpolar molecules like carbon dioxide must use a transmembrane protein
23. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the only type of selection that does not lead to descent with modification?
W) Directional selection
X) Disruptive selection
Y) Sexual selection
Z) Stabilizing selection
ANSWER: Z) Stabilizing selection
