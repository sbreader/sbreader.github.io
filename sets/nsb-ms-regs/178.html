1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What famous scientific apparatus demonstrated the Earth's rotation by integrating the effect of the Coriolis force over a long time period?
ANSWER: FOUCAULT'S PENDULUM (also accept: PENDULUM)
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following covalent bonds is most polar?
W) C-N
X) N-O
Y) H-F
Z) F-F
ANSWER: Y) H-F
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals can create metamorphic structures known as snowball structures?
W) Kyanite
X) Muscovite mica
Y) Garnet
Z) Talc
ANSWER: Y) Garnet
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Earth's most severe extinction event, also called the Great Dying, resulted in the extinction of 96% of marine species and 70% of terrestrial vertebrate species. This event marks the boundary between what two geological periods?
ANSWER: PERMIAN AND TRIASSIC
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the name of the force experienced by a charged particle moving through a magnetic field, such that the force is at right angles to both the direction in which the particle is moving and the direction of the applied field?
ANSWER: LORENTZ
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the term used for a compound that contains copper in the +2 oxidation state?
ANSWER: CUPRIC

X) 3.5

W) 3.5 thousand
X) 33 thousand
Y) 350 thousand
Z) 3.5 million
ANSWER: Z) 3.5 million

6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the coldest and densest water in Earth's oceans?
W) Antarctic bottom water
X) North Atlantic deep water
Y) Atlantic intermediate water
Z) Arctic water
ANSWER: W) Antarctic bottom water
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice An igneous rock has a porphyritic texture. Which of the following does this indicate about the cooling history?
W) It cooled in stages, at different levels within the crust
X) It cooled rapidly near the surface
Y) It cooled on the surface and was quenched
Z) It cooled slowly, at one time, at depth
ANSWER: W) It cooled in stages, at different levels within the crust

W) Sodium
X) Aluminum
Y) Phosphorus
Z) Chlorine
ANSWER: Z) Chlorine

ANSWER: 20
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Bauxite is primarily mined to attain what natural resource?
ANSWER: ALUMINUM
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true about the Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter?
W) The transit time from Earth to Mars was about seven months
X) It has a camera that can spot Martian objects that are as small as a dinner table
Y) It discovered carbon dioxide snow falling on Mars

ANSWER: Y) It discovered carbon dioxide snow falling on Mars
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice When a reptile is cold, it will do which of the following?

ANSWER: W) Lie perpendicular to the Sun's rays

ANSWER: HYDRAULIC FRACTURING (also accept: FRACKING)
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a reason why a prion is different from a virus?

Y) Prions do not contain nucleic acids
Z) Prions are always enveloped, while only some viruses are
