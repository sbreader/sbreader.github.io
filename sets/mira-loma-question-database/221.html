Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Defined as having a DNA sequence known as the homeobox, what group of genes are integral is specifying the anterior-posterior axis and segment identity of animals during embryonic development?
ANSWER: Hox genes
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Detectors in nuclear reactors that measure neutron flux level are made of this element, and it is used as a catalyst in the three-way catalytic converter. Very inert against corrosion and chemicals, this metal of the platinum group is often plated on top other rare metals, such as white gold. For four points, name the most expensive precious metal with an atomic number of 45.
ANSWER: Rhodium
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Bob pulls on a sled with a rope angled 60 degrees to the horizontal. The sled, starting from rest, accelerates to 20 meters/second in 4 seconds. If the force Bob applied to the rope was 50 Newtons, what is the mass of the sled, ignoring frictional forces?
ANSWER: 5 kilograms
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following shoreline features would be augmented if a breakwater was built offshore from it?
W) wave-cut platform
X) tombolo
Y) sea arch
Z) sea stack
ANSWER: X) tombolo
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 4 features that are advantages of wind power: 1: produces no greenhouse gases 2: the intermittency of wind 3: renewability of wind 4: global distribution of wind
ANSWER: 1, 3, 4
6. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Triangle ABC is similar to triangle DEF. Which of the following statements must be true?
W) angle CAB is equal to angle FDE
X) angle BCA is equal to angle FED
Y) side BC is equal to side EF
Z) side AC is equal to side FD
ANSWER: W) angle CAB is equal to angle FDE
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following conditions over a day will prevent the blooming of a short-day plant with a critical night length of 9 hours?
W) 14 hours light, 10 hours dark
X) 4 hours light, 1 hour dark, 9 hours light, 10 hours dark
Y) 4 hours light, 5 hours dark, 10 hours light, 5 hours dark
Z) 20 hours dark, 4 hours light
ANSWER: Y) 4 hours light, 5 hours dark, 10 hours light, 5 hours dark
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A gas in a bomb calorimeter has a partial pressure of 100 torr at STP. An inert gas is added such that the total pressure inside the calorimeter rises to 2 atmospheres. Then, the temperature of the calorimeter is increased to 273 degrees Celsius. What is the new partial pressure of the gas?
ANSWER: 200 torr
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A pipe of resting length 10 meters is traveling at 0.6c, relative to the observer. What is the observed length of the pipe, assuming the direction of movement is parallel to the length of the pipe?
ANSWER: 8 meters
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Giovanni Cassini discovered many moons of Saturn. On one of these, he correctly theorized that the anomalous variations of brightness were due to the presence of darker material on one hemisphere. What moon was this, the third largest of Saturn's?
W) Titan
X) Rhea
Y) Iapetus
Z) Dione
ANSWER: Y) Iapetus
11. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is not an intended goal of clean coal technology?
W) carbon sequestration
X) reducing minerals and impurities from coal
Y) steaming flue gases to remove sulfur dioxide
Z) increasing water content of low-grade coals
ANSWER: Z) increasing water content of low-grade coals
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Members of this phylum are bilaterian, unsegmented, and soft-bodied invertebrates. They have no body cavity, no specialized circulatory or respiratory organs, and thus are usually flat in shape to allow greater diffusion. Consisting of animals such as planarians, trematodes, and tapeworms, what group is commonly referred to as the flatworms?
ANSWER: Platyhelminthes
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In vial A, a reaction occurs, and at equillibrium the concentration of substrate 1 is 50 mol/L. Vial B is the same as vial A, but a catalyst is added, which increases the reaction speed by 25%. After the reaction finishes, what is the concentration of substrate 1 in vial B?
ANSWER: 50 mol/L
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A 12 volt battery is connected to a 3 ohm resistor and a 6 ohm resistor in parallel. How much current flows through the 3 ohm resistor?
ANSWER: 4 Amperes
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, indicate which 2 of the following 4 minerals would likely be found in a felsic rock: 1: olivine 2: plagioclase feldspar 3: orthoclase feldspar 4: quartz
ANSWER: 3 and 4
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following locations would be most optimal to operate an Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion system?
W) off the coast of California
X) in the Gulf of Mexico
Y) Sydney Harbor, Australia
Z) Around the Philippine archipelago
ANSWER: Z) Around the Philippine archipelago
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer An arithmetic sequence has a starting term of 2 and a common difference of 5. What is the sum of the first 10 terms of this sequence, noting that the tenth term is equal to 47?
ANSWER: 245
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Hydrogenation of this chemical yields cetyl alcohol, and it is also known as hexadecanoic acid. First discovered in 1840, and later used along with naphtha to produce an incindiary in World War 2, what is one of the most common saturated fatty acids, designated as C16:0?
ANSWER: palmitic acid

W) neutrons
X) neutrinos
Y) electrons
Z) pions

W) top left
X) top right

Z) bottom right
ANSWER: X) top right
