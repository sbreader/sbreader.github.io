2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the types of radiation primarily absorbed and emitted by Earth’s surface, respectively?
W) Longwave, Longwave
X) Shortwave, Shortwave
Y) Longwave, Shortwave
Z) Shortwave, Longwave
ANSWER: Z) Shortwave, Longwave
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three supercontinents in chronological order of formation 1) Pangaea 2) Laurasia 3) Rodinia
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Yazoo streams are most commonly associated with which of the following events?
W) Flooding
X) Volcanic eruption
Y) Glacial retreat
Z) Drought
ANSWER: W) Flooding
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The distance modulus often overestimates stellar distances due to what process that scatters and absorbs starlight through interstellar dust?
ANSWER: Extinction
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three phenomena that are likely to occur due to a thermal inversion 1) Fog formation 2) Thunderstorm formation 3) Vertical mixing
ANSWER: 1 only
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 types of galaxies or galactic nuclei by increasing luminosity 1) Grand Design Spiral Galaxy 2) Quasar 3) Seyfert Galaxy
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What features on the seafloor are products of offset mid-ocean ridge segments, where active strike-slip faulting is no longer taking place?
ANSWER: Fracture zone
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains how a glacier can flow around obstacles even if the ice remains brittle rather than deforming plastically?
W) Ice melts under increased pressure at the obstacle and refreezes afterward
X) The glacier heats up and becomes liquid near the obstacle
Y) The glacier ice fractures and creates space to flow through
Z) Sublimation removes ice near the obstacle, reducing resistance
ANSWER: W) Ice melts under increased pressure at the obstacle and refreezes afterward
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Plerions like the Crab Nebula remain luminous long after their supernova explosion due to energy injected by what rotating compact remnant?
ANSWER: Pulsar (also accept: Magnetar) (do not accept: Neutron Star)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In sedimentary basins, hydrocarbons sometimes accumulate against the impermeable sides of what upward-moving features that are composed of evaporites such as halite?
ANSWER: Salt Dome
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following four planets that can be seen in the crescent phase from Earth 1) Mars 2) Jupiter 3) Saturn 4) Uranus
ANSWER: None
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Oxisols, soils with a deep iron and aluminum oxide layer due to leaching, are most likely to be found in which of the following Koppen climate classifications?
W) A
X) B
Y) C
Z) D
ANSWER: W) A
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Type Ia supernovae often occur in binary systems when a white dwarf surpasses the Chandrasekhar limit in the presence of a companion object. Identify all of the following stellar objects that could be the identity of this companion 1) Red dwarf 2) White dwarf 3) Blue supergiant
ANSWER: All
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why the wet adiabatic lapse rate is lower in magnitude than the dry adiabatic lapse rate?
W) Wet air is less dense than dry air
X) Saturated air has a higher specific heat capacity
Y) Latent heat release during condensation warms the air
Z) The intermolecular forces between water molecules release energy
ANSWER: Y) Latent heat release during condensation warms the air
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Newtonian telescopes are most likely to suffer from what type of aberration?
ANSWER: Spherical aberration (also accept: coma)
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three coastal features that are erosional in origin 1) Spit 2) Terrace 3) Sea stack
ANSWER: 2, 3
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three minerals on Bowen's reaction series that can scratch muscovite 1) Olivine 2) Plagioclase 3) Quartz
ANSWER: All
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Max is standing on Earth at 60 degrees north on the winter solstice. To the nearest tenth of a degree, what is the declination of the Sun at local noon as seen by Max?
ANSWER: 83.5
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A deep, anomalously high seismic velocity zone would be most likely found at which of the following locations?
W) Canadian Shield
X) Mariana Trench
Y) Mid-Atlantic Ridge
Z) Himalayan Mountains
ANSWER: X) Mariana Trench
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true regarding sunspots?
W) They migrate from high to low latitudes during a cycle
X) They are located in the Sun's chromosphere
Y) They are regions of lower temperature than their surroundings
Z) Solar flares tend to occur in regions with dense sunspots
ANSWER: X) They are located in the Sun's chromosphere
