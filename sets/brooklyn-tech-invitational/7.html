Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In human females, the secondary oocyte is released from the follicle during ovulation. During this process, the cell is arrested in which stage of meiosis?
ANSWER: METAPHASE II (do not accept: Metaphase I)
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The shedding of the uterine lining during menstruation is most strongly linked to which of the following processes?
W) division of the second polar body
X) cell cycle arrest of the fertilized egg
Y) degeneration of the corpus luteum
Z) preservation of the mature follicle
ANSWER: Y) degeneration of the corpus luteum
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following four scientists who are correctly matched with their contributions to modern atomic theory. 1. J.J. Thompson, discovery of electrons 2. J.J. Thompson, plum pudding model 3. Niels Bohr, orbitals 4. Ernest Rutherford, structure of nucleus
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3, and 4
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following four elements that would burn red when subjected to a flame test 1) Lithium 2) Calcium 3) Barium 4) Strontium
ANSWER: 1, 2, and 4 (also accept: LITHIUM, CALCIUM, STRONTIUM)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice If a spaceship travels close to the speed of light relative to Earth, which of the following will an observer on Earth measure about the spaceship?
W) The spaceship’s clocks run faster, and its length is longer than at rest
X) The spaceship’s clocks run slower, and its length is shorter than at rest
Y) The spaceship’s clocks run slower, and its length is longer than at rest
Z) The spaceship’s clocks run faster, and its length is shorter than at rest
ANSWER: X) The spaceship’s clocks run slower, and its length is shorter than at rest
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer What experiment, conducted in 1820, demonstrated that an electric current produces a magnetic field, forming the foundation for the field of electromagnetism?
ANSWER: Oersted’s experiment
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following right triangles has an angle that is twice the size of the smallest angle in a right triangle with side lengths 3, 4, and 5?
W) (5, 12, 13)
X) (8, 15, 17)
Y) (7, 24, 25)
Z) (9, 40, 41)
ANSWER: Y) (7, 24, 25)
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Given the Fibonacci sequence with F1 =1, F2 = 1, compute the remainder when F100 is divided by 7.
ANSWER: 3
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the phenomenon immediately before sunrise or after sunset in which the sun glows green-blue?
ANSWER: Green Flash (also accept: green ray)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the optical phenomenon where multiple concentric halos appear around an object due to the interference of sunlight around water droplets?
ANSWER: Glory
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Amy has no history of kidney disease. After she gets hurt at her grueling construction job, she notices that her pee is Coca-Cola brown. Which of the following kidney-damaging conditions does Amy likely have?
W) Glomerulonephritis
X) Goodpasture’s syndrome
Y) Polycystic disease
Z) Rhabdomyolysis
ANSWER: Z) Rhabdomyolysis
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following mechanisms through which rhabdomyolysis can affect kidney function. 1. Formation of plugs in the renal tubules 2. Dilation of blood vessels in the glomeruli 3. Hypercalcemia
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the correct electron configuration for the Manganese 4+ ion?
W) [Ar] 4s2 3d1
X) [Ar] 3d3
Y) [Ar] 3d2 4p1
Z) [Ar] 4s2 4d1
ANSWER: X) [Ar] 3d3
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer N2O is in equilibrium with N2O4 in a transparent cylinder fitted with a piston. Identify all of the following three changes that would increase the number of N2O molecules in the cylinder 1) Depressing the piston 2) Increasing the temperature 3) Adding nitrogen gas
ANSWER: 2 only
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What theorem, demonstrated by Soviet cosmonaut Dzhanibekov using a spinning wingnut in microgravity, explains why rotation around an object’s intermediate principle axis is unstable?
ANSWER: Intermediate Axis Theorem (also accept: Tennis Racket Theorem; Dzhanibekov effect)
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer In Rayleigh scattering, the energy of a scattered photon remains the same as that of the incident photon. In which type of scattering does the photon instead lose or gain energy due to interaction with vibrational modes?
ANSWER: Inelastic scattering (also accept: Inelastic; Raman; Stokes Anti-Stokes)
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the standard argument for proving that the ring of rationals is countable?
ANSWER: DIAGONALIZATION ARGUMENT (also accept: DOVETAILING; FAREY SEQUENCE)
9. BONUS Math Short Answer Which of the following sets are countably infinite? 1. Set of all finite length words over the alphabet 2. Ring of polynomials over the integers 3. Ring of algebraic numbers
ANSWER: 1,2,3 (also accept: ALL)
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of mounts uses a pole star to calibrate its movement?
W) Altazimuth
X) Equatorial
Y) Dobsonian
Z) Fork
ANSWER: X) Equatorial
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following four types of telescopes that use lenses. 1) Cassegrain 2) Newtonian 3) Gregorian 4) Catadioptric
ANSWER: 4 only
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice All chordates display pharyngeal slits or clefts at some point in development. These develop into different structures depending on the species of chordate. Which type of structure cannot be derived from the pharyngeal slits or clefts?
W) Pectorals
X) Trapezius
Y) Tonsils
Z) Gills
ANSWER: W) Pectorals
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following parts of the spine that are derived from the notochord. 1. Intervertebral foramen 2. Annulus fibrosis 3. Nucleus pulposus 4. Anterior longitudinal ligament
ANSWER: 3 ONLY
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Iron minerals can be subdivided into different categories based on their gangue content. Into which of the following categories would you place a mineral that is made up primarily of aluminum oxide?
W) Basic gangue
X) Autofluxing
Y) Acid Gangue
Z) Primary gangue
ANSWER: Y) Acid Gangue
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer For each of the following three processes, identify whether the change in enthalpy is positive or negative. 1. Sublimation of dry ice 2. Condensation of gaseous Astatine to liquid Astatine 3. Decomposition of Ammonium Nitrate
ANSWER: 1) +, 2) –, 3) -
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Air with density 1.2 kg/m3 and viscosity 1.8 × 10-5 Pa*s flows past the wing of an Airbus with a characteristic length of 2 m. The cruising speed of the air relative to the wing is 250 m/s. Giving your answer in scientific notation with a whole number coefficient, what is the approximate Reynolds number of this flow?
ANSWER: 3 × 107
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer The Reynolds number of air flowing past the wing of an Airbus is approximately 3 × 107. Under which kind of flow is this categorized?
ANSWER: TURBULENT
14. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The number 134 is equal to a number with base-10 digits A 8 5 6 1, in that order. Compute A.
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 3
Z) 4
ANSWER: X) 2
14. BONUS Math Multiple Choice A number written in ternary has digits that add up to an even number. Which of the following statements about this number is always true?
W) The number of 1’s in its binary representation is odd.
X) The last digit of its ternary representation is either 2 or 0.
Y) The sum of its digits when expressed in base 10 is even.
Z) The last digit of its binary representation is 0.
ANSWER: Z) The last digit of its binary representation is 0.
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What physical property of atomic states is responsible for natural broadening in spectral lines?
ANSWER: Quantum uncertainty (also accept: Uncertainty)
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What theorem states that the only three independent variables of a black hole are charge, angular momentum, and mass?
ANSWER: No-hair theorem (also accept: Black Hole Uniqueness theorem)
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A baby is brought into the ICU and diagnosed with spina bifida Which of the following processes occurred abnormally during its development?
W) Formation of the meninges
X) Invagination of the neural plate
Y) Differentiation of stem cells into osteoblasts
Z) Production of myelin
ANSWER: X) Invagination of the neural plate
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following three layers of the meninges from innermost to outermost. 1. Arachnoid mater 2. Pia mater 3. Dura mater
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3 (also accept: Pia mater, Arachnoid mater, Dura mater)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In the synthesis of Aluminum Oxide, what is the coefficient of elemental Aluminum?
ANSWER: 4
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The balanced chemical equation for the reaction of oxygen and propane is: C3H8(g) + 5O2(g) → 3CO2(g) + 4H2O(g). If 16 grams of O2 react with excess C3H8, to the nearest gram, how many grams of CO2 will be formed?
ANSWER: 13
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A superconducting ring is cooled below its critical temperature while sitting in a magnetic field. When the external field is removed, a magnet is placed inside of the ring. What effect keeps the magnetic field inside the superconductor from changing?
ANSWER: MEISSNER EFFECT (also accept: MEISSNER-OCHSENFELD EFFECT)
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer A bazooka launches a rocket straight up into the air, where it reaches a height of h. It is then set to fire at an angle of 45 degrees above the horizontal, launching with the same initial velocity as before. In terms of h, what is the maximum height that the rocket will reach in the second trial?
ANSWER: 0.5h (also accept: ½ of h; h/2)
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A smooth function f(x) is defined such that its value at x = 0 and the values of all its derivatives at x = 0 are equal to 1. This function will exhibit what type of growth?
ANSWER: EXPONENTIAL (also accept: ex)
19. BONUS Math Short Answer Consider the differential equation y''-8y'+15y=45. This equation has a non-constant solution of the form y=ekx+c, where k and c are constants. Find the minimum value of k+c.
ANSWER: 6
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice At which point in an orbit will another planet make a right angle with the central star and observer?
W) Greatest elongation
X) Superior conjunction
Y) Inferior conjunction
Z) Quadrature
ANSWER: Z) Quadrature
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Dr. Brickman in New York City notices Star Lemma and finds the declination of Lemma to be -55.3 degrees, and right ascension 23 hours. The coordinates of New York City are 40.7 degrees north and 74.1 degrees west. Assuming that it is noon, calculate the altitude of Star Lemma in degrees.
ANSWER: 75.4 degrees
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What animal phylum is the only major phylum that does not fall under the clade Eumetazoa, on account of having diverged first from the common ancestor of all animals?
ANSWER: PORIFERA (also accept: sponges)
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer Though sponges lack true tissues, they do exhibit cell differentiation. The structure of the cell sponges use for feeding is similar to a group of protists, leading scientists to believe this group is the oldest extant relative of all animals. What is the name of this group of protists?
ANSWER: CHOANOFLAGELLATES (also accept: Choanoflagellata)
22. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In terms of the elementary charge e, what is the electric charge of a strange quark?
ANSWER: NEGATIVE 1/3e (do not accept: 1/3e)
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer In Heisenberg’s Uncertainty Principle, the product of position and momentum uncertainties is greater than or equal to which constant divided by TWO?
ANSWER: Reduced Planck constant (also accept: h-bar) (do not accept: Planck’s constant)
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Random variables X and Y are chosen uniformly and randomly on intervals [0,8] and [0,7] respectively. Providing your answer as a fraction, what is the probability that x+y is less than 11?
ANSWER: 6/7
23. BONUS Math Short Answer Now random variables X and Y are both chosen on interval [0,2]. Providing your answer as a fraction, what is the probability that x*y is less than 1?
ANSWER: (2ln2 + 1)/4 (also accept: (ln(4) + 1)/4; ln(√2) + 1/4)
