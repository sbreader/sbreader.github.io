1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice All elements in a given group of the periodic table have similar:
W) maximum principal quantum number
X) maximum angular-momentum quantum number
Y) valence-shell electron configuration
Z) atomic number
ANSWER: Y) valence-shell electron configuration
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Name a nonmetal element which is a liquid at room temperature:
ANSWER: BROMINE
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which optical phenomenon explains why the sun appears whitish at the zenith and red near the horizon:
W) diffraction
X) scattering
Y) reflection
Z) doppler shift
ANSWER: X) scattering
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer Who is commonly credited in discovering that the buoyant force of a body immersed in a fluid is equal to the weight of the fluid displaced by that object:
ANSWER: ARCHIMEDES
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Encephalitis is an inflammation of what organ?
ANSWER: BRAIN
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer In the classical classification of organisms, three of the five Kingdoms are Protista, Fungi, and Monera. What are the other two?
ANSWER: ANAMALIA, PLANTAE
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice If Venus is between the Earth and the Sun, what configuration is it in:
W) inferior conjunction
X) superior conjunction
Y) quadrature
Z) inferior quadrature
ANSWER: W) inferior conjunction
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Near the end of its life, the Sun will do which of the following:
W) become a red giant and then evolve to a white dwarf
X) become a neutron star
Y) become a blue giant and evolve into a white dwarf
Z) become a brown dwarf
ANSWER: W) become a red giant and then evolve to a white dwarf
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In order, what are the first 5 prime numbers between 50 and 72?
ANSWER: 53, 59, 61, 67, 71
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the average speed, in miles per hour, of a person who travels 120 miles in 3 hours, 40 miles the next 3 hours, and 36 miles the following 2 hours?
ANSWER: 24.5 (also accept: 24 – 25)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the outer 100 km of the solid Earth, which includes the crust and uppermost mantle:
ANSWER: LITHOSPHERE
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a metamorphic rock:
W) marble
X) quartzite
Y) granite
Z) gneiss
ANSWER: Y) granite
7. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Rounded the nearest fifty, one horsepower is equivalent to how many BTU’s per hour?
ANSWER: 2550
7. BONUS Energy Short Answer Rounded to the nearest decimal place, convert 122°F into Centigrade:
ANSWER: 50
8. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What does the acronym ROM stand for?
ANSWER: READ ONLY MEMORY
8. BONUS Math Short Answer The decimal number 10 is equivalent to what octal number?
ANSWER: 12
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for the direct conversion of a solid to a vapor without going through a liquid phase?
ANSWER: SUBLIMATION
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What element is a volatile purple-black solid with a beautiful metallic sheen, is found in minerals such as lautarite and dietzeite, was first prepared in 1811 from seaweed, and is the least reactive halogen?
ANSWER: IODINE (also accept: I)
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If Lisa swings a ball on the end of a string and maintains it in uniform circular motion, what is the most accurate term for the tension force exerted on the rock through the string?
ANSWER: CENTRIPETAL
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer Neglecting friction, what is the acceleration, in meters per second squared, when a force of 2000 newtons is applied to a 10 kilogram object:
ANSWER: 200
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The wing of a bird and the arm of a human are considered:
W) congruent structures
X) analogous structures
Y) homologous structures
Z) vestigial structures
ANSWER: Y) homologous structures
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What bone marrow progenitor cell typically contains a polyploid nucleus of up to 64n.
ANSWER: MEGAKARYOCYTE
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A sidereal hour is made of how many sidereal minutes?
ANSWER: 60
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rounded off to the nearest whole day, what is the sidereal rotation period of the Moon?
ANSWER: 27 DAYS (also accept: 27)
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The number 400 is what percent of 160?
ANSWER: 250
13. BONUS Math Short Answer What are the median, mean and mode of the following set of numbers: 1, 3, 8, 8, 9, 10, 12, 13
ANSWER: MEDIAN = 8.5; MEAN = 8; MODE = 8

W) aquiduct
X) aquifer
Y) aquitard
Z) aquaberm
