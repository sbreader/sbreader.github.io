1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following quantities that are dimensionless. 1) Reynold’s number 2) Viscosity 3) Friction coefficient
ANSWER: 1, 3
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice When waves encounter an obstacle, they tend to bend and curve around corners. What is the name of this phenomenon?
W) Diffraction
X) Diffusion
Y) Dispersion
Z) Refraction
ANSWER: W) Diffraction
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The KT Boundary, marked by its high iridium concentration, stratigraphically marks the end of which period?
ANSWER: CRETACEOUS PERIOD
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four events from most recent to least recent 1) Extinction of the Dinosaurs 2) End of the last Ice Age 3) Krakatoa’s last major eruption 4) Breakup of Pangea
ANSWER: 3,2,1,4
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 compounds that are considered weak Bronsted Lowry acids 1) Acetic Acid 2) Ammonia 3) Nitrous acid 4) Hydrofluoric acid
ANSWER: 1, 3, AND 4
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer For a hypothetical reaction that has a rate constant of 24 Moles per liter seconds, what is the reaction order?
ANSWER: 0
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 compounds that are considered electron carriers in cellular respiration 1) NAD+ 2) FAD 3) O2
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about muscle tissue is NOT true?
W) Acetylcholinesterase is an enzyme used to excite neuromuscular junctions.
X) Tropomyosin wraps around actin and prevents myosin from grabbing it, and causes skeletal muscle contraction.
Y) Muscle tissue is formed from embryonic development through a process called myogenesis.
Z) Cardiac and skeletal muscles are striated because they contain sarcomeres.
ANSWER: W) Acetylcholinesterase is an enzyme used to excite neuromuscular junctions.
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice When the second derivative of a function at a certain point is zero, which of the following best describes the point?
W) Critical Point
X) Maximum
Y) Minimum
Z) Point of Inflection
ANSWER: Z) Point of Inflection
5. BONUS Math Short Answer An ant is walking on the edges of an equilateral triangle. If its average speed was 1 mile per hour for the first edge, 2 miles per hour for the second edge, and 3 miles per hour for the 3rd edge, what is its total average speed?
ANSWER: 18/11
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A block with mass 2 kilograms is attached to a spring with spring constant 200 Newtons per meter. If the block is stretched a distance of 7 meters, what will its instantaneous acceleration be when it is released?
ANSWER: 700
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer How many degrees of freedom does neon gas have?
ANSWER: 3
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following terrestrial planets will have the largest temperature difference between their day and night cycles?
W) Mercury
X) Venus
Y) Earth
Z) Mars
ANSWER: W) Mercury
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the terrestrial planets has the most natural satellites?
ANSWER: MARS
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Researchers at Lawrence Livermore national lab are studying the growth of neutron stars. Which of the following types of supernovae cannot lead to the birth of a neutron star?
W) Type Ia
X) Type Ib
Y) Type Ic
Z) Type II
ANSWER: W) Type Ia
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at Ames national lab are studying the catalytic uses of ferric oxide. What is the name of the most common mineral that is composed of ferric oxide?
ANSWER: HEMATITE
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Companion cells and sieve cells originate from what other plant structure that consists of undifferentiated cells?
ANSWER: MERISTEMS
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The electron carrier protein Ferredoxin aids most heavily in which of the following processes?
W) Photosynthesis
X) Citric acid cycle
Y) Fermentation
Z) Glyoxylate cycle
ANSWER: W) Photosynthesis

X) 36

ANSWER: 210

ANSWER: TORQUE

ANSWER: 3

W) Quartz
X) Mica
Y) Feldspar

X) Thorium
