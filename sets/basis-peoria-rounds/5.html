1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of epithelium lines the upper respiratory tract?
W) Simple squamous epithelium
X) Simple columnar epithelium
Y) Pseudostratified columnar epithelium
Z) Stratified cuboidal epithelium
ANSWER: Y) Pseudostratified columnar epithelium
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer There are three main types of cartilage present in the human body. Identify the following terms. (1) This type of cartilage is found in the rib cage. (2) This type of cartilage is found in the intervertebral disks.
ANSWER: 1) Hyaline cartilage; 2) Fibrocartilage
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In electrochemistry, what law states that the amount of mass deposited at an electrode is directly proportional to the charge of the current passed through it?
ANSWER: Faraday’s first law of electrolysis
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In addition to Faraday’s first law of electrolysis, his second law of electrolysis states that there is a direct relationship between the amount of mass deposited at an electrode and what other quantity at a constant electricity?
ANSWER: Equivalent weight
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What hypothesis states that a gravitational singularity is not observable?
ANSWER: COSMIC CENSORSHIP HYPOTHESIS
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer By number, give all of the following things that are true about charged black holes. 1) they have two horizons 2) black holes with a charge greater than their mass have been observed in nature 3) they must satisfy the Reissner-Nordström equations 4) they cannot become degenerate.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The point (1, 5) is reflected across first the line y = x and then the line x = 1. What are the coordinates of the final point?
W) (-3, -1)
X) (-3, 1)
Y) (3, 1)
Z) (3, -1)
ANSWER: X) (-3, 1)
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the cross product of the two vectors i-2j + 5k and 3i + j-3k.
ANSWER: i + 18j + 7k
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The K-Pg boundary layer contains unusually high quantities of what element, which on Earth, almost exclusively exists within the core?
ANSWER: IRIDIUM
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Mare Moscoviense is a lunar mare, a dark spot on the Moon formed by lava on the Moon’s surface immediately after an asteroid or comet impact. Mare Moscoviense is different from virtually all other lunar mare for what reason?
W) It is the largest mare
X) It is on the Moon’s far side
Y) It is shaped like a star
Z) It is the darkest mare of all
ANSWER: X) It is on the Moon’s far side
6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Scientists at Oakridge National Laboratory are using the Spallation Neutron Source to find contrasts in the molecular structures of different neutrons. To find the contrast in neutron spallation of protium atoms, what should protium be replaced with?
ANSWER: Deuterium (also accept: Heavy Hydrogen)
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Due to decades of use of antibiotics, many bacteria have acquired a resistance to most modern-day antibiotics. Researchers at Oakridge National Lab are using the Spallation Neutron Source to investigate the interaction on a neutron level between what enzyme and the antibiotics?
ANSWER: Beta-lactamase
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer BCG is a relatively uncommon vaccine in the United States. However, in countries where this disease is more prevalent, it is usually administered to infants and small children. Name the disease that this vaccine prevents.
ANSWER: Tuberculosis
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer This group of diseases is caused by infectious proteins called prions. More specifically, this group consists of rare neurodegenerative diseases, such as kuru, mad cow disease, and Creutzfeldt-Jakob Disease. Name this group of fatal diseases, which give the brain a spongy appearance and have the acronym TSE.
ANSWER: Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What type of diagram is used to display the speeds of different particles at specific temperatures?
ANSWER: Maxwell-Boltzmann Distribution
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following would have the lowest standard deviation on a Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution?
W) SO2
X) SeO2
Y) CO2
Z) NO2
ANSWER: X) SeO2
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice For which of the following objects would Gauss’ Law apply?
W) Klein bottle
X) Möbius strip
Y) Röman surface
Z) Torus
ANSWER: Z) Torus
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer Suppose that an infinitely long straight current-carrying wire has a uniform linear charge density λ. Let this wire be on the y-axis of the xy-plane, and let x > 0 be the distance between the y-axis and a point P on the xy-plane. What is the strength of the electric field at the point P?
ANSWER: λ/(2πε0 x)
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Give the derivative of sin(x)/x.
ANSWER: (x cos(x) − sin(x))/x2
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the equation of the normal line to y = x2 at x = √3.
ANSWER: y = −(√3/6)x + 7/2 (also accept: y = −√3x/6 + 7/2)

ANSWER: LARAMIDIA AND APPALACHIA

Y) Ice sheets blocking off the inlets
