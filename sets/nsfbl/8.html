1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following BEST describes the relationship between metallicity and age of stars?
W) Older stars tend to have higher metallicity
X) Younger stars tend to have higher metallicity
Y) Metallicity does not tend to change with age
Z) Metallicity tends to change randomly with age
ANSWER: X) Younger stars tend to have higher metallicity
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the specific name of the point between two binary stars where their Roche lobes meet, and mass can be transferred between the stars?
ANSWER: INNER LAGRANGE POINT (also accept: L1) (do not accept: LAGRANGE POINT)

Y) 4/3
