Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following scientists published The Origin of Species, studying the finches of the Galápagos Islands?
W) Hank Green
X) Alfred Wallace
Y) Ernst von Haeckel
Z) Charles Darwin
ANSWER: Z) Charles Darwin
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following theories have been proven to be false?
W) Natural selection
X) Artificial selection
Y) Acquired characteristics
Z) Inherited characteristics
ANSWER: Y) Acquired characteristics
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What type of relatively strong intermolecular bond is formed in molecules such as hydrogen fluoride and water?
ANSWER: Hydrogen bond
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following scientists named a unit describing the number of atoms present in 12 grams of carbon-12?
W) Nikola Tesla
X) J. J. Thompson
Y) Niels Bohr
Z) Amedeo Avogadro
ANSWER: Z) Amedeo Avogadro
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Naman has fired a cannonball at a 70 degree angle above the horizontal on level ground. At what angle, in degrees, should he fire his second shot so it travels the same distance?
W) 10
X) 20
Y) 30
Z) 45
ANSWER: X) 20
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Newton's 3rd Law says that forces always come in pairs. Which of the following best describes the reactionary force to the Earth's gravity pulling on Naman?
W) The Earth exerting a normal force on Naman
X) Naman exerting a normal force on the Earth
Y) Naman's gravity pulling on the Earth
Z) None of the above
ANSWER: Y) Naman's gravity pulling on the Earth
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which scientist first proposed the theory of plate tectonics?
ANSWER: Alfred Wegener (also accept: Wegener)
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the point on Earth’s surface directly above an earthquake’s focus?
ANSWER: Epicenter
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice 6! * 7! is equal to which of the following?
W) 8!
X) 10!
Y) 13!
Z) 42!
ANSWER: X) 10!
5. BONUS Math Short Answer A triangle has an area of 36 square centimeters and a height of 60 millimeters. In centimeters, what is the length of its base?
ANSWER: 12
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which organelle is found only in plant cells and provides structure to the cell?
ANSWER: Cell wall
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following classes of compounds that are types of lipids 1. Steroids 2. Sphingolipids 3. Phospholipids
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer At 25°C and 1 atm, what is the pOH of a solution with a pH of 3?
ANSWER: 11
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What type of reaction is the following: 2NO + O2 -> 2NO2?
ANSWER: Synthesis
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What fundamental force is responsible for tides?
W) Electromagnetic
X) Gravitational
Y) Strong
Z) Weak
ANSWER: X) Gravitational
8. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice For which of the following discoveries was Albert Einstein awarded his only Nobel Prize?
W) Theory of Relativity
X) Photoelectric Effect
Y) Mass-energy Equivalence
Z) Brownian Motion
ANSWER: X) Photoelectric Effect
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the point in a celestial body's orbit where it is closest to Earth?
ANSWER: Perigee
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four spectral classes from hottest to coldest. 1. O 2. G 3. M 4. K
ANSWER: 1, 2, 4, 3
10. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the name given to the well-known algorithm that finds an element in a sorted list in O(log n) time?
ANSWER: Binary Search
10. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following functions has the highest value at x= π/3?
W) sin(x)
X) cos(x)
Y) tan(x)
Z) √x
ANSWER: Y) tan(x)
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Most nutrient absorption occurs in what part of the digestive system?
W) Esophagus
X) Stomach
Y) Small intestine
Z) Large intestine
ANSWER: Y) Small intestine
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which taxonomic kingdom are humans classified into?
ANSWER: Animalia
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many bonds join the nitrogen atoms in the compound N2?
ANSWER: 3
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which chemist, known as the discoverer of radioactivity, earned a Nobel Prize for her discovery of the elements polonium and radium?
ANSWER: Marie Curie (do not accept: Curie)
14. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Seesaws, scissors, and wheelbarrows are all examples of which of the following simple machines?
W) Inclined Plane
X) Lever
Y) Wedge
Z) Screw
ANSWER: X) Lever
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer Rank the following four objects in terms by increasing momentum. 1. A 9 kilogram ball rolling at 5 meters per second 2. A parked car 3. A 50 kg person crawling at 1 meter per second 4. A 5 gram grenade fragment traveling at 1000 meters per second
ANSWER: 2, 4, 1, 3
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes molten rock beneath Earth’s surface?
ANSWER: Magma
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of rock is characterized by forming from existing rocks, being molded by forces inside the Earth?
ANSWER: Metamorphic
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the remainder when 95 is divided by 6?
ANSWER: 5
16. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the radius of a circle with the equation: x2 + (y-2)2 + 1 = 10?
ANSWER: 3
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term used to describe the process by which DNA is synthesized into RNA?
ANSWER: Transcription
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer What enzyme catalyzes the process of transcription, synthesizing a complementary RNA strand from DNA?
ANSWER: RNA polymerase
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for the amount of time required for a radioactive sample to lose half its mass?
ANSWER: Half-life
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the elements with partially filled d subshells that are found in the middle of the periodic table?
ANSWER: Transition Metals
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer By name or number, rank the following types of radiation by increasing penetration power. 1. Alpha 2. Beta 3. Gamma
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer How far, in meters and to two significant figures, would a particle fired from the ground at an angle of 45° and an initial speed of 5 meters per second travel?
ANSWER: 2.5
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the most abundant gas in Earth's atmosphere?
ANSWER: Nitrogen
20. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following continents does not border the ring of fire?
W) North America
X) South America
Y) Africa
Z) Asia
ANSWER: Y) Africa
21. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the order of the polynomial 2x3-4x + x7-3?
ANSWER: 7
21. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the base 10 number 55 represented in base 8?
ANSWER: 67
