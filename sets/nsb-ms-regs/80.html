Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility are using an electron beam to study the internal structure of protons. What particles, which come in six flavors, are found within protons?
ANSWER: QUARKS
1. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are using molecules such as fluorescein isothiocyanate for experiments that depend on the emission of light. What technique are they performing with this molecule?
W) Electron microscopy
X) Mass spectrometry
Y) Fluorescence microscopy
Z) NMR spectroscopy
ANSWER: Y) Fluorescence microscopy
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Detrital, organic, and chemical are terms used to refer to the origins of what general type of rock?
ANSWER: SEDIMENTARY
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three substances that are minerals 1) Copper metal 2) Coal 3) Synthetic diamond.
ANSWER: 1 only
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Penicillin is able to inhibit bacterial growth without harming human cells because it targets what bacterial structure that animal cells lack?
ANSWER: CELL WALL
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer In the human heart, what other chamber contracts at the same time as the left ventricle?
ANSWER: RIGHT VENTRICLE
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What type of heat transfer process occurs when heat flows from a hot object to a cold object that it is directly touching?
ANSWER: CONDUCTION
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding astronauts in low Earth orbit 1) Their mass is nearly zero 2) Their weight is nearly zero 3) They experience no gravitational force from the Earth.
ANSWER: 2 only
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Using Tycho Brahe's observations, what scientist is credited for discovering the elliptical nature of planetary orbits?
ANSWER: KEPLER (also accept: JOHANNES KEPLER)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of celestial object is the smallest mass object capable of deuterium fusion and is considered to have characteristics of both a planet and a star?
ANSWER: BROWN DWARF
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Two cross-country runners want to finish their 6-kilometer practice run at the same time. The runners run at rates covering one kilometer in 5.5 minutes and 7 minutes, respectively. How many minutes of head start should the faster runner give the slower runner?
ANSWER: 9
6. BONUS Math Short Answer To the nearest whole number, what is the length of the hypotenuse in a right triangle with legs of length 17 and 12?
ANSWER: 21
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Oak Ridge National Lab produces actinium-225 for radiation therapy in which it acts as an alpha emitter. An alpha particle is composed of two protons and how many neutrons?
ANSWER: 2
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Savannah River National Lab are using the multi-component potential theory of adsorption to model the purification of a raw, gaseous mixture normally composed of ethane and methane. What is the common name for this mixture when it is retrieved from underground sources?
ANSWER: NATURAL GAS
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Permian extinction occurred near the end of what geologic era?
ANSWER: PALEOZOIC
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rock strata exposed by the Colorado River at the Grand Canyon are separated by intervals where no net sedimentary deposition occurred. What is the general term for these unrecorded intervals of time in the rock record?
ANSWER: UNCONFORMITY (also accept: DISCONFORMITY) (do not accept: THE GREAT UNCONFORMITY; GREAT UNCOMFORMITY)
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer When two different waves end up in the same location, they undergo what phenomenon that can either be constructive or destructive?
ANSWER: INTERFERENCE
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 3-volt battery is connected to a resistor with resistance of six ohms. In watts, what is the power dissipated by the resistor?
ANSWER: 1.5 (also accept: 3/2; 1 1/2)
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at SLAC National Accelerator Lab are improving the intensity of their Linac Coherent Light Source, or LCLS, laser, that they currently use to determine the structures of biological molecules and crystals. This laser operates in what region of the electromagnetic spectrum?
ANSWER: X-RAY
10. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers at SLAC National Accelerator Lab are studying phase-change materials, which are used in the RAM of computers and smartphones. These materials change from a glassy phase to a crystalline phase with a thermal pulse, and then lock in that phase at a lower temperature. While the crystalline phase is ordered, what term best describes the glassy phase because it is a solid material that lacks long range order?
ANSWER: AMORPHOUS
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the x-intercept of the line with equation 5x – 6y = –60?
ANSWER: –12 (also accept: (–12, 0))
11. BONUS Math Short Answer For the function f of x = x2 + 3, evaluate f(3a)-3f(a) when a = 5.
ANSWER: 144
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In female mammals, the initiation of meiosis only occurs in what organ?
ANSWER: OVARY
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer During birth, oxytocin results in uterine contractions, which eventually results in the production of more oxytocin. Of what type of regulation is this an example?
ANSWER: POSITIVE FEEDBACK (also accept: POSITIVE FEEDBACK LOOP)
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the general term for a process that results in a chemical change?
ANSWER: CHEMICAL REACTION
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer The net torque on an object describes the rate of change of what quantity over time?
ANSWER: ANGULAR MOMENTUM
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the volume of a right rectangular prism with dimensions 3 by 5 by 6?
ANSWER: 90
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Two children have a pile of building blocks. Mark used 1/3 of the blocks to build a house, and Mary used 3/8 of the blocks to build a ship. If 140 unused blocks remained, how many blocks were there to begin with?
ANSWER: 480
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following would be most useful for determining the age of an asteroid?
W) Composition
X) Degree of differentiation
Y) Concentration of radioactive elements
Z) Temperature
ANSWER: Y) Concentration of radioactive elements
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer When the core of a sun-like star runs out of most of its hydrogen fuel, it can grow in size, cool down, and start fusing helium. What is the term for this type of star?
ANSWER: RED GIANT
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What series of chemical reactions are performed by plants to synthesize sugars from water and carbon dioxide?
ANSWER: PHOTOSYNTHESIS
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three trophic levels that directly contribute energy flow to detritivores 1) Primary producers 2) Primary consumers 3) Secondary consumers.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Round 6,157.58 to the nearest hundred.
ANSWER: 6,200
17. BONUS Math Short Answer A hula hoop with a radius of 16 inches is cut into 5 congruent pieces. To the nearest whole number of inches, what is the length of each piece?
ANSWER: 20
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What layer of Earth’s atmosphere is the densest?
ANSWER: TROPOSPHERE
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three outcomes that result from increased dissolution of carbon dioxide into the world’s oceans 1) Seawater will become more acidic 2) Seawater will dissolve more seafloor carbonate 3) Coral reefs will decline.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer You put two solutions with different concentrations of salt in a container, separated by a semi-permeable membrane. Later, you observe that the water level on one side of the container is higher than the other. What effect is responsible for this?
ANSWER: OSMOSIS
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer When only considering the ABO blood group, identify all of the following three types of blood that a person with blood type O could safely receive 1) A 2) B 3) AB.
ANSWER: NONE (also accept: 0 only)
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the term for the electric configuration of an object composed of a positive charge separated by a distance to a negative charge of equal magnitude?
ANSWER: DIPOLE (also accept: ELECTRIC DIPOLE MOMENT)
20. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The carboxyl functional group is MOST associated with which of the following classes of molecules?
W) Alcohols
X) Amines
Y) Ketones
Z) Organic acids
ANSWER: Z) Organic acids
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Some animals, such as frogs and butterflies, undergo a process in which their larval form makes a large transformation in body shape as they become adults. What is the term for this process?
ANSWER: METAMORPHOSIS
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the largest organ in the human integumentary system?
ANSWER: SKIN
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Give one of the linear factors of x2 + 3x – 40.
ANSWER: x-5 (also accept: x + 8)
22. BONUS Math Short Answer A rhombus has diagonals of length 13 and 22. What is its area?
ANSWER: 143
23. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the term for a device that converts radiant energy directly into electrical energy?
ANSWER: PHOTOVOLTAIC (also accept: PV)
23. BONUS Physics Short Answer What solid-state electrical component only allows current to flow in one direction?
ANSWER: DIODE
