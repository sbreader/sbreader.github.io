1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Red algae are multicellular marine organisms from which what growth medium is extracted?
ANSWER: AGAR
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A scientist is testing a water sample from a nearby river for common ions. When he adds sodium hydroxide to the sample and heats it, he notices a very strong, pungent odor. What ion does this water sample contain?
W) Nitrate
X) Ammonium
Y) Sulfate
Z) Phosphate
ANSWER: X) Ammonium
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A scientist cuts a 1 meter long copper wire and calculates its cross-sectional surface area to be 1 × 10-3 m2. He knows the electrical resistivity of copper is 2 × 10-9 Ωm. What is the resistance of the wire?
ANSWER: 2 × 10-6 OHMS
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A river contains 5000 colonies of E. coli per liter. Ten days later it contained 10,000 colonies per liter. The number of colonies had grown exponentially. When would there be 20,000 colonies per liter?
ANSWER: 20 DAYS AFTER THE FIRST TEST
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice An apparent magnitude of zero used to be defined as the magnitude of
W) Proxima centauri
X) Sun
Y) Vega
Z) Betelgeuce
ANSWER: Y) Vega
6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer A scientist takes a water sample and carefully lowers a Secchi disk into the water. She observes how deep the disk can go until it disappears. What is the scientist measuring?
ANSWER: TURBIDITY
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Nitrate leaching into groundwater can cause serious problems like eutrophication. Which of the following is a positively charged soil particle that prevents the leaching of nitrates?
W) Gravel
X) Sand
Y) Silt
Z) Clay
ANSWER: Z) Clay
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Staining chromosomes with the trypsin-giemsa method reveals a series of alternating light and dark bands called what?
ANSWER: G BANDS
9. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which American marine biologist's writings led to a nationwide ban on DDT and other pesticides?
ANSWER: (RACHEL) CARSON
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the most important gas that leads to acid rain?
W) Carbon dioxide
X) Sulfur dioxide
Y) Nitrogen dioxide
Z) Ozone
ANSWER: X) Sulfur dioxide
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which law states that a line joining a planet to the sun sweeps out an equal area over equal periods of time?
ANSWER: KEPLER'S SECOND LAW OF PLANETARY MOTION
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which principle states that any point on a wave serves as the source of secondary waves spread out in all directions?
W) Young's Principle
X) Huygens' Principle
Y) De Broglie's Principle
Z) Rutherford's Principle
ANSWER: X) Huygens' Principle
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In a straight-chain alkane, if there are n carbon atoms, how many hydrogen atoms will there be?
ANSWER: 2N+2
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A farmer has 80 meters of fencing. What is the maximum area that can be enclosed?
ANSWER: 400 METERS SQUARED
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term used to describe two chromosomes that loop out of their territories and interact with each other?
ANSWER: KISSING CHROMOSOMES
16. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the harmonic mean of the following series: 1, 2, 4?
W) 12/7
X) 7/12
Y) 4/7
Z) 7/4

17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which one of the following is not normally present in the soil?
W) Nitrate
X) Phosphorus

18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A copper wire is placed in a solution to form a circuit. The solution acts as a 5 millivolt battery. The electrical resistance of the circuit is 0.01 ohms. What is the amperage of the circuit?
ANSWER: 0.5 AMPERES
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following temperatures of water requires the most heat to raise its temperature by 1 degree Celsius?
W) Cold water
X) Lukewarm water
Y) Hot water
Z) All require the same amount of heat
ANSWER: Y) Hot water
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A star is 1 × 1010 meters away and has a luminosity of 1 × 1020 Watts. In terms of pi, what is the apparent luminosity of the star? Give your answer as a fraction.
ANSWER: 1/(4π) W/m2
21. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which letter of the Greek alphabet represents colloid osmotic pressure, the prime counting function, the product operator, and a mathematical constant?

W) Decreases
X) Stays the same
