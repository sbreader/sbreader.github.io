1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is it called when warm air overtakes and rises above cold air?
ANSWER: OVERRUNNING
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements concerning streams and rivers is not true?
W) A stream that existed before the present topography was created is called an antecedent stream.
X) Foreset beds are generally composed of sand and silt.
Y) The east coast of North America generally lacks deltas because of weak waves and tides.
Z) A superposed stream tends to continue the pattern that it developed earlier rather than adjusting to its surroundings.
ANSWER: Y) The east coast of North America generally lacks deltas because of weak waves and tides.

ANSWER: ROTATION CURVE
