1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the intensity of the transmitted light when natural light is passed through an ideal polarizing filter?
W) 0 times the intensity of the unpolarized light
X) ¼ the intensity of the unpolarized light
Y) ½ the intensity of the unpolarized light
Z) 1 times the intensity of the unpolarized light
ANSWER: Y) ½ the intensity of the unpolarized light
