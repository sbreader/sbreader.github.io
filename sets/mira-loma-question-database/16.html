1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Buckminsterfullereens are spherical structures composed of what element
ANSWER: CARBON
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Carbon dating relies on the principle that over specific periods of time Carbon-14 decays into
W) Carbon-12
X) Boron-10
Y) Nitrogen-14
Z) Nitrogen-12
ANSWER: Y) Nitrogen-14
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the heaviest element to occur naturally on earth?
ANSWER: URANIUM
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What scientist is credited with discovering radioactivity while investigating the phosphorescent of uranium salts?
ANSWER: HENRI BECQUEREL
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Discovered by Pierre and Marie Currie, what element is known as having the largest number of isotopes, all of which are radioactive
W) Polonium
X) Radium
Y) Radon
Z) Francium
ANSWER: W) Polonium
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Arrange the following elements from the most to least amount of electric conductivity, Gold, Silver, Copper
ANSWER: SILVER, COPPER, GOLD
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What element is often recognized as the most chemically reactive element in existence?
ANSWER: FLUORINE
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Among the so-called "inert" noble gases, which of the following has largest number of known compounds
W) Krypton
X) Xenon
Y) Helium
Z) Radon
ANSWER: X) Xenon
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Predict the type of bonding that would occur between an element with an electronegativity of 3.44 and an element with an electronegativity of 2.1
W) Non-polar covalent
X) Ionic
Y) Polar Covalent
Z) Metallic
ANSWER: Y) Polar Covalent
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What metal has the highest electronegativity?
ANSWER: GOLD
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements would not normally form an ion with a charge of +2
W) Sodium
X) Copper
Y) Zinc
Z) Lead
ANSWER: W) Sodium
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What element derives its atomic symbol Sb from its original name "Stibium"?
ANSWER: ANTIMONY
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following alpha-decay series does not end in lead as its final, stable end product
W) Uranium-235
X) Uranium-238
Y) Thorium-232
Z) Neptunium-237
ANSWER: Z) Neptunium-237
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is not the most common isotope of that element as found on earth
W) Uranium-238
X) Oxygen-16
Y) Hydrogen-2
Z) Carbon-12
ANSWER: Y) Hydrogen-2
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Edward Teller constructed the first hydrogen bomb using a combination of deuterium and an isotope of what element?
ANSWER: LITHIUM
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice James Chadwick made his breakthrough discovery of neutrons in 1932 when he bombarded what element with alpha particles
W) Lithium
X) Gold
Y) Zinc
Z) Beryllium
ANSWER: Z) Beryllium
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What well-known acid has become the United State's most produced industrial chemical due to its wide range of uses?
ANSWER: SULFURIC ACID
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What element is the main ingredient in the infamous mustard gas first used in World War I?
ANSWER: CHLORINE
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What element serves as the "break-even" point in nuclear fusion, in which the energy required to achieve the fusion is greater than the energy produced
W) Iron
X) Lithium
Y) Gold
Z) Uranium
ANSWER: W) Iron
10. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What element is lightest element that can only exist on earth through human synthesis
W) Neptunium
X) Technetium
Y) Plutonium
Z) Molybdenum
ANSWER: X) Technetium
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What well-known and eccentric English scientist is credited with the discovery of Hydrogen?
ANSWER: CAVENDISH
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What American scientist is best known for his work in co-discovering transuranium elements such as Neptunium while working with Glen Seaborg, while also leading teams that discovered other elements such as Eistenium?
ANSWER: ALBERT GHIORSO (also accept: GHIORSO)
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What British scientist is credited with the discoveries of many metals such as sodium, potassium, calcium, and magnesium?
ANSWER: SIR HUMPHREY DAVY
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Organize the following elements from oldest date of discovery, to most recent: Calcium, Phosphorous, Hydrogen, Lead
ANSWER: LEAD, PHOSPHOROUS, HYDROGEN, CALCIUM
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the most abundant state-of-matter in the Universe
W) Solid
X) Liquid
Y) Gas
Z) Plasma
ANSWER: Z) Plasma
13. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following does NOT contain plasma
W) Neon signs
X) Stars
Y) Incandescent lighting
Z) Fluorescent lighting
ANSWER: Y) Incandescent lighting
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many electrons are denoted in the following electron configuration: 1s22s22p5
ANSWER: NINE

W) Argon
X) Sodium
Y) Fluorine
Z) Neon
ANSWER: Z) Neon
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Ozone is an important, yet toxic compound of what element?
ANSWER: OXYGEN
15. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The distinctive odors of many thiols can be attributed to the presence of what element
W) Carbon
X) Hydrogen
Y) Sulfur
Z) Oxygen
ANSWER: Y) Sulfur
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a compound
W) Table Salt
X) Silica

ANSWER: Z) Bronze
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The term "rare-earth elements" can often be applied to what collection of elements?
ANSWER: LANTHANIDES

W) The number of protons
X) The charge
Y) The number of electrons
Z) The valence shell
ANSWER: W) The number of protons

X) Potassium

W) Galactose

W) The number of carbons
X) The placement of the carbonyl group
Y) The number of monomers
Z) The chirality

20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A compound containing what toxic element was used by Romans as an artificial sweetener in wine, often theorized as the cause of the sterility and insanity of many emperors?
ANSWER: LEAD
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The well-known artificial sweetener Splenda uses a chemical known as Sacrulose, which is similar to most natural carbohydrates except that it contains what other element?
ANSWER: CHLORINE
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The pure form of what metal found in the Boron group is known for having such a low melting point that it can melt in a person's hand?
ANSWER: GALLIUM
