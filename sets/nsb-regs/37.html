1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the circumference of the circle with equation (x + 3)2 + (y-5)2 = 25?

1. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is the sum of the first three nonzero terms of the Taylor series of sin(x) around 0?
