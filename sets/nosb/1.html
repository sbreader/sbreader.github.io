Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How do most corals spawn?
W) Seasonally timed release of both gametes
X) Continuous release of both gametes
Y) Parent polyps divide upon reaching a certain size
Z) Sporadic unpredictable release of both gametes
ANSWER: W) Seasonally timed release of both gametes
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the distance between two adjacent wave crests or troughs called?
ANSWER: Wavelength
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is one benefit that marine plants and algae provide to a juvenile fish?
ANSWER: Habitat complexity (also accept: food resources; shelter; increased oxygen)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What two factors are primarily responsible for driving global surface and thermohaline ocean circulation?
W) Temperature and density differences
X) Wind and temperature
Y) Wind and density differences
Z) Tides and density differences
ANSWER: Y) Wind and density differences
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What does SONAR stand for?
ANSWER: Sound Navigation and Ranging
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the most likely method of introduction for invasive sessile invertebrate species like zebra mussels?
W) Pet trade smuggling
X) Ballast exchange
Y) As a hitchhiker on a bird
Z) Introduced as a biological control species
ANSWER: X) Ballast exchange
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What environmental phenomenon was responsible for the Great Barrier Reef die-off in 2015-2016?
ANSWER: El Nino (also accept: ocean warming)
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following dissolved gases is most abundant in seawater?
W) Oxygen
X) Carbon dioxide
Y) Argon
Z) Nitrogen
ANSWER: Z) Nitrogen
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the largest estuary in North America?
ANSWER: The Chesapeake Bay
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an adaptation of deep-sea organisms?
W) Slowed metabolism
X) Large eyes
Y) Bioluminescence
Z) Fast continuous swimming speed
ANSWER: Z) Fast continuous swimming speed
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is a submerged, flat-topped seamount called?
ANSWER: Guyot (also accept: tablemount (U.S. Navy nomenclature))
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Shrimp trawlers use what technology to prevent sea turtle mortality by allowing turtles to escape from nets?
ANSWER: Turtle Excluder Devices (also accept: TEDs; excluder devices)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT known to cause tsunamis?
W) Earthquakes
X) Volcanic eruptions
Y) Spring tides
Z) Submarine landslides
ANSWER: Y) Spring tides
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following parameters is not directly studied using satellite measurements?
W) Sea surface temperature
X) Deep sea chemistry
Y) Ocean color
Z) Sea surface height
ANSWER: X) Deep sea chemistry
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Where are offshore oil and gas deposits primarily found?
W) In the oceanic crust
X) In sediment deposits on continental shelves
Y) Beneath mid-ocean ridges
Z) Adjacent to subduction zones
ANSWER: X) In sediment deposits on continental shelves
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which principle states that regardless of variability in salinity varies, the ratios between the amounts of the major ions in the waters of the open ocean are nearly constant?
ANSWER: The Rule of Constant Proportions (also accept: Marcet's Principle; Forchhammer's Principle)
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following would be a reasonable period for a tsunami wave?
W) 1 minute
X) 30 minutes
Y) 5 hours
Z) 12 hours
ANSWER: X) 30 minutes
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which ocean zone is the deepest?
W) Hadal
X) Abyssal
Y) Bathyal
Z) Littoral
ANSWER: W) Hadal
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which oceanographic research tool was used to discover the first hydrothermal vents in the Pacific Ocean off the coast of the Galapagos Islands in 1977?
ANSWER: Alvin (also accept: Human Occupied Vehicle (HOV); Deep Diving Submersible; Deep Ocean Research Submersible (DRV))
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which region of the Earth's interior is liquid?
W) Inner core
X) Outer core
Y) Crust
Z) Mantle
ANSWER: X) Outer core
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT one of the five major ocean gyres?
W) North Atlantic
X) Indian
Y) South Pacific
Z) Southern
ANSWER: Z) Southern
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What highly toxic compound can be released from anoxic bottom waters in conditions of strong vertical mixing?
W) Sulfide
X) Ammonium
Y) Nitrate
Z) Hydroxide
ANSWER: W) Sulfide
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The city of Los Angeles is located on which tectonic plate?
ANSWER: Pacific Plate
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for water with a depleted concentration of dissolved oxygen (about 2 milligrams per liter)?
ANSWER: Hypoxic (hypoxia) (also accept: suboxic)
