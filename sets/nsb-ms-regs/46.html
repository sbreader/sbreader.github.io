Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Simplify the expression x times (2xy2) squared.
ANSWER: 4x3y4
1. BONUS Math Short Answer From a point 20 feet from the base of a tree, the angle of elevation to the top is 60 degrees. To the nearest foot, what is the height of the tree?
ANSWER: 35
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Mushrooms are parts of organisms that are contained in what kingdom of life?
ANSWER: FUNGI (do not accept: FUNGUS)
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What group of airborne chemicals do insects use to communicate with each other?
ANSWER: PHEROMONES
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer When nitric acid and sodium hydroxide react, the products are a salt and what other compound?
ANSWER: WATER
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the oxidation state of hydrogen atoms in ammonia?
ANSWER: +1 (also accept: 1)
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab have successfully simulated an atomic nucleus using a quantum computer. From these calculations, they were able to extract the binding energy of a deuteron, the bound state of what two subatomic particles?
ANSWER: PROTON AND NEUTRON
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Savannah River National Lab are researching novel methods to store hydrogen gas. One such method is a physical storage process in which gas is adsorbed onto glass microbeads. This adsorption creates a heterogenous mixture of hydrogen and glass that is NOT a solution, but does display the Tyndall effect. What is the general name for this type of mixture?
ANSWER: COLLOID
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following geologic structures forms from sediment deposited at the mouth of a river?
W) Fjord
X) Delta
Y) Levee
Z) Berm
ANSWER: X) Delta
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rhyolitic lavas have a chemical composition identical to what intrusive igneous rock?
ANSWER: GRANITE
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The specific gravity of a solid or a liquid is defined as its density compared to that of what substance?
ANSWER: WATER
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer You want to hide your gold in a bottle that you plan to let sink in a freshwater lake. If the bottle has a mass of 105 grams and a total volume of 1 liter, and the gold comes in nuggets of 10 grams each, what is the minimum number of nuggets you need to put in the bottle before it will sink?
ANSWER: 90
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab are studying a nuclear reaction that involves deuterium and tritium as reactants. What general type of nuclear reaction is this?
ANSWER: FUSION (also accept: NUCLEAR FUSION)
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab are studying magnetic reconnection, a process that can occur in what phase of matter?
ANSWER: PLASMA
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Many people suffer from anemia because they lack what metal that is a component of hemoglobin?
ANSWER: IRON
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Microscopic zooplankton can best be classified as which of the following trophic levels?
W) Decomposers
X) First-level carnivores
Y) Herbivores
Z) Tertiary carnivores
ANSWER: Y) Herbivores
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What adjective, meaning Earth-like, is used to refer to the four inner planets?
ANSWER: TERRESTRIAL (also accept: TERRESTRIAL PLANETS)
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding the Moon's formation 1) It was a captured asteroid 2) It formed at the same time as Earth 3) It contains material that used to be part of Earth.
ANSWER: 3
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for the heat required to change the phase of a solid to a liquid?
ANSWER: HEAT OF FUSION (also accept: ENTHALPY OF FUSION)
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Lithium has two stable isotopes and has an atomic mass of 6.941 amu. How many neutrons do most lithium nuclei possess?
ANSWER: FOUR
11. BONUS Math Short Answer Six dogs have an average woofiness of 7. Another 8 dogs have a woofiness of 4. To the nearest tenth, what is the average woofiness of both groups of dogs combined?
ANSWER: 5.3
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What plant vascular system transports nutrients and carbohydrates throughout the plant?
ANSWER: PHLOEM
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice You press down on your arm and feel a pulse beating. From which of the following types of blood vessels is the pulse originating?
W) Vein
X) Artery
Y) Capillary
Z) Lymphatics
ANSWER: X) Artery
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The recoil that follows from the firing of a gun is a direct result of the conservation of what quantity?
ANSWER: MOMENTUM (also accept: LINEAR MOMENTUM)
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To two significant figures, how many grams are in 4 pounds of gold?
ANSWER: 1800
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the surface area of a cube with edges of length 3?
ANSWER: 54
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Solve the following equation for x: (3x – 7) squared = 64.
ANSWER: –1/3 AND 5
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a term for a high area of land that separates one drainage basin from another?
W) Divide
X) Apex
Y) Peak
Z) Block mountain
ANSWER: W) Divide
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of earthquake body waves are longitudinal waves and can therefore pass through liquids and solids?
ANSWER: P WAVES (also accept: P, PRIMARY)
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the sum of the two expressions 2a + 9b and 3a – 8b?
ANSWER: 5a + b (also accept: b + 5a)
16. BONUS Math Short Answer What is 9525% of 24?
ANSWER: 2286
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of cells in mammals serves as the main carriers of oxygen within the circulatory system?
ANSWER: ERYTHROCYTES (also accept: RED BLOOD CELLS, RBC)
17. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The malleus, incus, and stapes are small bones that play a role in which of the following functions?
W) Hearing
X) Balance
Y) Locomotion
Z) Respiration
ANSWER: W) Hearing
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Lawrence Berkeley National Lab scientists have developed ReACT, a new amino acid labeling technique. This technique can be used to study which of the following biomolecules?
W) Proteins
X) Phospholipids
Y) DNA
Z) RNA
ANSWER: W) Proteins
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are studying how defects in the mixtures of multiple metals can change the properties of the mixture. Generally, these researchers are studying solutions defined by metallic bonding character, which are given what name in chemistry?
ANSWER: ALLOYS
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Simplify the following expression: a8 times a3
ANSWER: a11
19. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the smallest positive integer that has a remainder of 3 when divided by 5, a remainder of 0 when divided by 2, and a remainder of 1 when divided by 3?
ANSWER: 28
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What cataclysmic event is necessary for the creation of a neutron star?
ANSWER: SUPERNOVA
20. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a correct pairing of a layer of the Sun's atmosphere and its effective temperature?
W) Photosphere and 6,000 kelvins
X) Photosphere and 10,000 kelvins
Y) Corona and 6,000 kelvins
Z) Corona and 10,000 kelvins
ANSWER: W) Photosphere and 6,000 kelvins
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Astronomers and physicists believe that the universe's expansion is accelerating due to the action of what undiscovered form of energy that composes 73% of the universe's mass-energy?
ANSWER: DARK ENERGY
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer Consider a ball that is thrown at 20 meters per second at an angle of 30 degrees to the horizontal. In meters per second, what is the horizontal velocity of this ball during its flight?
ANSWER: 10√3
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for unicellular organisms that lack a nucleus?
ANSWER: PROKARYOTES
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the principal pigment that plants use to absorb light for photosynthesis?
ANSWER: CHLOROPHYLL
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By definition, mafic rocks are rich in what two elements?
ANSWER: IRON AND MAGNESIUM (also accept: Fe AND Mg)
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer If you see lightning and hear the thunder 15 seconds later, then, to the nearest whole kilometer, how far away was the lightning strike?
ANSWER: 5
