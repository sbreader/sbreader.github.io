Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the earliest known collection of multicellular organisms on Earth?
W) Fulu biota
X) Ediacaran biota
Y) Precambrian biota
Z) Burgess shale
ANSWER: X) Ediacaran biota
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer The Cambrian explosion is believed to have involved the evolution of arthropods. This is partly because fossils of ferns after that time were found to exhibit abnormal growths induced by insects’ egg laying. What is the term used to describe these growths?
ANSWER: Fern galls (also accept: GALLS)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice An alkyne is added to a reaction vessel containing pressurized hydrogen gas and a catalyst consisting of palladium poisoned by traces of lead(II) ions and quinoline. Which of the following is the major product of this reaction?
W) Alkane
X) Cis-alkene
Y) Trans-alkene
Z) Alcohol
ANSWER: X) Cis-alkene
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following methods is LEAST helpful in identifying chiral isomers?
W) X-ray diffraction
X) Raman spectroscopy
Y) Nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy
Z) Quadrupole mass spectrometry
ANSWER: Z) Quadrupole mass spectrometry
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In a helium-neon laser, which specific quantum process is primarily responsible for the creation of coherent photons in the active medium?
ANSWER: STIMULATED EMISSION
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer What Nobel Prize-winning physicist, recognized for his fundamental work in quantum electrodynamics, introduced the diagrams commonly used to represent particle interactions?
ANSWER: RICHARD FEYNMAN (also accept: FEYNMAN)
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the smallest number divisible by all of the integers from 1 to 10, inclusive?
ANSWER: 2520
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Compute the value of the infinite series sum from n = 1 to infinity of n/4n.
ANSWER: 4/9
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What theory best explains the observations of superluminal motion in small particles ejected by black holes?
ANSWER: Special relativity (also accept: special theory of relativity)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What process causes particle jets ejected by stellar objects to emit photons?
ANSWER: Bremsstrahlung (also accept: Braking radiation)
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Eric is unbelievably short, which makes Emma, who is nonchalant and 6 foot 7, wonder how that can be. She decides to check Eric’s spine and finds that, somehow, he has all four main sections of it intact. Order the following 4 sections of Eric’s spine from the closest to his head to the closest to his toes, which is not a long distance. 1. Lumbar 2. Cervical 3. Thoracic 4. Sacral
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1, 4 (also accept: cervical, thoracic, lumbar, sacral)
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which of the 31 spinal nerves has not been assigned a dermatome, on account of not having a dorsal nerve root?
ANSWER: C1 (also accept: first cervical spinal nerve; cervical spinal nerve 1; suboccipital nerve)
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three ionic compounds in order of increasing boiling point. 1) Sodium chloride 2) Cesium chloride 3) Magnesium oxide
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many structural isomers are there for a compound with the formula C7H16?
ANSWER: 9
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A photon strikes a metal surface, ejecting an electron. If the frequency of the photon is increased, what will happen to the kinetic energy of the emitted electron?
W) It increases
X) It decreases
Y) It stays the same
Z) It becomes negative
ANSWER: W) It increases
8. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice In a region of space, the electric field is changing with time, but there is no current. According to Maxwell’s equations, which of the following can still be true?
W) A magnetic field does not exist
X) Electric charge is not conserved
Y) The magnetic field must be zero
Z) A magnetic field can exist due to the displacement current
ANSWER: Z) A magnetic field can exist due to the displacement current
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If sin(x) = ⅓, compute sin(3x)
ANSWER: 23/27
9. BONUS Math Short Answer How many positive integer solutions are there to 1/x + 1/y = 1/6?
ANSWER: 9
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following processes does not contribute meaningfully to climate change?
W) The Milankovitch cycle
X) El Niño
Y) The Wilson cycle
Z) Catagenesis
ANSWER: Z) Catagenesis
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What process is caused by the dissolution of carbon in the ocean, potentially harming marine life by lowering pH?
ANSWER: Ocean Acidification
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the evolutionary trend of a brain forming in organisms with bilateral symmetry?
ANSWER: Cephalization
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding symmetry in body plans is FALSE?
W) Radial symmetry is beneficial for both sessile and planktonic organisms
X) Both protostomes and deuterostomes can exhibit bilateral symmetry
Y) All bilaterians are triploblasts
Z) Not all bilaterians are known to have true tissues
ANSWER: Z) Not all bilaterians are known to have true tissues
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Calomel is an important reference electrode. In a calomel electrode, what salt solution is used to maintain constant ionic strength?
ANSWER: POTASSIUM CHLORIDE (also accept: KCl; SODIUM CHLORIDE; NaCl)
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true concerning elimination reactions. 1) E2 is a concerted reaction 2) E1 is substantially accelerated by strong, unhindered bases 3) Cis-alkenes are often thermodynamically preferred to trans-alkenes.
ANSWER: 1 only
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Weakly Interacting Massive Particles, also known as WIMPS, are theorized to interact with ordinary matter primarily through which two fundamental forces?
ANSWER: Gravitational and Weak Forces (also accept: Gravity and Weak; Gravitational Force and Weak Force)
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer Michael Schumacher accelerates his Formula 1 car to a high speed. To maintain this speed, his engine must overcome aerodynamic drag. The power required for his car to overcome drag scales with what power of the car’s velocity?
ANSWER: THIRD (also accept: CUBE; THREE)
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A repunit is a number where all of the digits are 1, such as 111. What is the smallest repunit that is a multiple of 7?
ANSWER: 111,111 (also accept: 6 1’s)
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Two circles, each of radius 5, lie in perpendicular planes. They intersect in two points, X and Y, thus determining a common chord XY. If XY = 8, compute the distance between the centers of the circles.
ANSWER: 3√2
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about eruptions?
W) The most abundant gas released is CO2
X) Released sulfate aerosols reduce acidification
Y) Explosive eruptions are the leading cause of volcano-related deaths
Z) Sulfate aerosols warm the Earth's surface
ANSWER: Y) Explosive eruptions are the leading cause of volcano-related deaths
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, rank the following 4 types of eruptions from least to most explosive. 1) Surtseyan 2) Hawaiian 3) Plenean 4) Vesuvian
ANSWER: 2, 1, 4, 3
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify ALL of the following 5 genera of bacteria that are classified as spirochetes. 1. Borrelia 2. Vibrio 3. Leptospira 4. Treponema 5. Rickettsia
ANSWER: 1, 3, 4 (also accept: Borrelia, Leptospira, Treponema)
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following genera of potentially pathogenic bacteria is known for causing the pneumonia-like illness Pontiac fever?
W) Legionella
X) Clostridium
Y) Fusobacterium
Z) Chlamydia
ANSWER: W) Legionella
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer If 23.90 grams of water have a heat capacity of 100 joules per Kelvin, how many joules of thermal energy are required to raise the temperature of this water from 290 Kelvin to 295 Kelvin?
ANSWER: 500
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 4 elements by INCREASING density. 1. Gold 2. Calcium 3. Germanium 4. Tin
ANSWER: 2, 3, 4, 1
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A corner cube retroreflector reflects light back to its source regardless of the angle of incidence. What is the minimum number of mutually perpendicular reflective surfaces required to produce this effect?
ANSWER: 3
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer A mass m1 rests on a rough horizontal surface and is connected by a massless string over a frictionless pulley to a hanging mass m2. The coefficient of kinetic friction is μk. If gravitational acceleration is g, and the system is released from rest, what is the acceleration a of the system in terms of m1, m2, μk, and g?
ANSWER: (m2g-μk m1g)/(m1 + m2) (also accept: (g(m2-μk m1))/(m1 + m2))
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the maximum number of sections that can be made from a circle with 5 lines going through it?
ANSWER: 16
19. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the area of a triangle whose vertices are (1, 5), (4, 3), and (7, 11).
ANSWER: 15

W) Primary production
X) Ocean stratification
Y) Fogs
Z) Fishery Landings
ANSWER: X) Ocean stratification
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, name all of the following 4 zones that are not present in polar regions during winter. 1. Pycnocline 2) Thermocline 3) Halocline 4) Thermostad
ANSWER: 1, 2, and 4
21. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Different types of epithelium are specialized for different functions. To which of the following functions is stratified squamous epithelium most specialized?
W) secretion of substances
X) protection from abrasion
Y) movement of mucus
Z) filtration of bodily fluids
ANSWER: X) protection from abrasion
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify ALL of the following 4 structures that are correctly matched to their type of epithelial lining. 1. Bronchi; pseudociliated columnar epithelium 2. Capillaries; simple squamous epithelium 3. Renal tubules; simple cuboidal epithelium 4. Epidermis; simple columnar epithelium
ANSWER: 1, 2, and 3
22. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes what happens when a silicon semiconductor is n-type doped?
W) Additional silicon atoms increase the energy gap between the valence and conduction bands
X) Atoms with more valence electrons than silicon are added, providing free electrons
Y) The material gains positively charged holes that enhance conduction
Z) The crystal structure becomes metallic and loses all semiconducting properties
ANSWER: X) Atoms with more valence electrons than silicon are added, providing free electrons
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following four atoms that are known to be paramagnetic 1) Aluminum 2) Oxygen 3) Copper 4) Calcium
ANSWER: 1, 2, and 4

ANSWER: 17

ANSWER: 23
