Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer C4 plants perform the Calvin cycle in what specialized cells, which are far from stomata?
ANSWER: Bundle Sheath Cells
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer In a hypothetical environment, the trait for yellow stems is recessive, and the trait for green stems is dominant. If 81 percent of the population has yellow stems, what percentage of the population is heterozygous?
ANSWER: 18%
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer An atom of an unknown element undergoes alpha decay to become a mercury atom. What element was the original atom?
ANSWER: LEAD
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the bond order of the N-O bond in nitric oxide?
ANSWER: 2.5
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer When traveling at relativistic speed, your clock may not agree with someone’s clock in a reference frame at rest, what effect in special relativity causes this discrepancy?
ANSWER: TIME DILATION
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer How many numbers completely describe the spacetime position of an event in our universe?
ANSWER: 4
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many distinct sets of 3 vertices can be chosen from a regular hexagon?
ANSWER: 20 (6 choose 3)
4. BONUS Math Short Answer How many prime numbers are less than 100?
ANSWER: 25
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three seismic waves by the order in which they arrive at a seismograph 1) P waves 2) S waves 3) Surface waves
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What rock comprises the majority of Earth’s mantle?
ANSWER: PERIDOTITE
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Brookhaven National Lab are studying how gold nuclei collide at low energies in a particle accelerator. During the collision, what form of matter can momentarily be created, in which the nucleus falls apart into quarks and gluons?
ANSWER: QUARK-GLUON PLASMA
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Brookhaven National Lab are studying how gold nuclei collide at low energies in a particle accelerator. What is the most common type of quark in these nuclei?
ANSWER: DOWN QUARK
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What plant hormone is responsible for mediating the triple response?
ANSWER: Ethylene
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following plant groups are not gymnosperms?
W) Cycadophytes
X) Ginkgophytes
Y) Anthophytes
Z) Gnetophytes
ANSWER: Y) Anthophytes
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the molecular geometry of sulfur hexafluoride?
ANSWER: OCTAHEDRAL
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer An ideal gas expands isothermally from an initial volume of 5 liters to a final volume of 15 liters. What is the final pressure of the gas if the initial pressure is 30 atmospheres?
ANSWER: 10
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What law relates the angle of refraction for light in a medium to the medium’s index of refraction?
ANSWER: SNELL’S LAW
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What general type of fluid flow occurs when the Reynold’s number of the fluid is relatively low?
ANSWER: LAMINAR FLOW
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A parabola is given by the equation y = (x + 1)2 − 5. The parabola is then moved 2 units right and 3 units up. It is then reflected across the x-axis. What are the coordinates of its new vertex?
ANSWER: (1, 2)
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the volume of a frustum with circular bases of radius 6 and 3 and a height of 4?
ANSWER: 84π
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer An area of land is lifted by tectonic forces into a mountain. Over time, this mountain erodes and becomes flat again. Then sediments are deposited onto the land and lithified. What type of unconformity does this process produce in this area?
ANSWER: ANGULAR UNCONFORMITY
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What rare type of stars display strong stellar winds that have blown away most or all of their hydrogen envelopes?
ANSWER: WOLF-RAYET STARS
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Lab have found a way to convert lignin into nylon polymers. What type of polymers, which includes nylon, are made of different monomers combined into one polymer?
ANSWER: COPOLYMERS
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Lab have found a way to convert lignin into nylon polymers. This process involves converting cyclohexanol into cyclohexanone. What gas is released during this reaction?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Zygapophysial joints are plane joints located in what skeletal structure?
ANSWER: Vertebral Column (also accept: Vertebrae)
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following proteins that are located within the I band of a sarcomere (1) Myosin (2) Actin (3) Nebulin (4) Titin.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 4
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the effect in which the ionization of an atom is immediately followed by the absorption of a free electron into the atom?
ANSWER: AUGER EFFECT
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the limit of the derivative of entropy with respect to temperature as temperature approaches zero kelvins?
ANSWER: 0
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What process can be considered as an inverse of beta positive decay, in which an electron is absorbed by a nucleus?
ANSWER: ELECTRON CAPTURE
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer What phase of matter is created by supercooling gases and has been suggested as an explanation for the superfluidity of Helium-4?
ANSWER: BOSE-EINSTEIN CONDENSATE
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the distance between the point (5,6) and the point (-3,-9)?
ANSWER: 17
16. BONUS Math Multiple Choice A quadrilateral is created by choosing 4 random points on a circle and connecting them. What is the probability that this quadrilateral tessellates the plane?
W) 25%
X) 50%
Y) 75%
Z) 100%
ANSWER: Z) 100%
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three solar system objects that have a substantial atmosphere 1) Titan 2) Miranda 3) Callisto
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a glacier that forms in an alpine region and then flows onto a plain, where it quickly spreads out?
ANSWER: PIEDMONT GLACIER
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab have been studying interactions between neutrinos and baryonic matter. Neutrinos are produced by interactions caused by which fundamental force?
ANSWER: WEAK FORCE
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab have been studying interactions between neutrinos and baryonic matter. What hypothetical type of neutrino could explain the dark matter in our universe?
ANSWER: STERILE NEUTRINO
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In the human body, what is the only bone that does not articulate with any other bones?
ANSWER: Hyoid bone
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer The kinetoplastid Trypanosoma Brucei is famously known to cause what disease, which is characterized by severe headaches and fatigue? This disease is spread through fly bites.
ANSWER: African Sleeping Sickness (also accept: Sleeping Sickness; Trypanosomiasis)
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the chemical formula for phenol?
ANSWER: C6H6O
20. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules contains a triple bond?
W) Acetylene
X) Ethylene
Y) Ethane
Z) Propane
ANSWER: W) Acetylene
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What theorem in fluid mechanics, derivable from Bernouill’s principle, gives the velocity of a stream of laminar fluid exiting a container?
ANSWER: TORRICELLI'S THEOREM (also accept: TORRICELLI'S LAW)
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer Fluids often form stable structures such as smoke rings that are examples of what 3 dimensional shape that is a closed loop?
ANSWER: TORUS
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What 3d analog of polar coordinates is defined by a series of xy planes each defined by an r and theta for the function in that plane and a z value for the z coordinate of the function in each plane?
ANSWER: CYLINDRICAL COORDINATES
22. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the name of the spiral formed when the function r = θ is plotted in polar coordinates?
ANSWER: ARCHIMEDEAN SPIRAL
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the boundary that separates the Earth’s mantle and its crust?
ANSWER: MOHOROVICIC DISCONTINUITY
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of a binary system in which both stars are larger than their Roche lobes?
ANSWER: CONTACT BINARY
