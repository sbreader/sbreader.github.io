1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice An endergonic reaction is a reaction that
W) releases free energy into its surroundings
X) absorbs free energy from its surroundings
Y) stores free energy from its surroundings
Z) absorbs acquired energy from its surroundings
ANSWER: X) absorbs free energy from its surroundings
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the colloquial term for the medical condition known as varicella?
ANSWER: CHICKENPOX
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What are physical and chemical agents that cause mutations called?
W) Carcinogens
X) Oncagenes
Y) Metastasis
Z) Mutagens
ANSWER: Z) Mutagens
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What phenomenon prevents normal human cells from reproducing once they cover a solid surface?
ANSWER: DENSITY-DEPENDANT INHIBITION
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer What phenomenon prevents normal human cells from reproducing while suspended in a liquid, or in an area with no solid surface?
ANSWER: ANCHORAGE DEPENDENCE
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Chlorophyll a is what color?
ANSWER: BLUE GREEN
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer Chlorophyll b is what color?
ANSWER: OLIVE GREEN
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the phenomena in which chemical groups of the active site of an enzyme are placed into position that enhance their ability to catalyze a chemical reaction?
ANSWER: INDUCED FIT
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the main electrogenic pump of plants, fungi and bacteria?
ANSWER: PROTON PUMP
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In what phase of mitotic division does the nucleoli disappear?
W) Prometaphase
X) Anaphase
Y) Prophase
Z) Metaphase
ANSWER: Y) Prophase
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Within sickle cell anemia, what occurs between the afflicted proteins?
ANSWER: THEY FORM CRYSTYLLIZING FIBERS
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The DNA sequence where RNA polymerase attaches and initiates the process of transcription is known as what?
W) Codon
X) Transcription initiation complex
Y) Promoter
Z) Transcription Unit
ANSWER: Y) Promoter
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many chiral carbons does the amino acid glycine have?
ANSWER: NONE (also accept: 0)
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following disaccharides contains an alpha 1,2 glycosidic linkage
W) Sucrose
X) Maltose
Y) Lactose
Z) All of the Above
ANSWER: W) Sucrose
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What protein structure involves interactions between backbone constituents?
ANSWER: SECONDARY STRUCTURE
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Hydrophobic Interactions occur in what protein structure?
ANSWER: TERTIARY STRUCTURE
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Spider Silk contains what secondary structure?
W) Alpha Helix
X) Double Alpha Helix
Y) Beta Pleated Sheet
Z) Tri Beta Pleated Sheet
ANSWER: Y) Beta Pleated Sheet
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Within what structures is starch stored within plant cells?
ANSWER: PLASTIDS
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer In what part of the leaf of a green plant, are the majority of chloroplasts found?
ANSWER: MESOPHYLL
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Hemophilia is what kind of disorder?
W) Sex-linked dominant
X) Sex-linked recessive
Y) Autosomal dominant
Z) Autosomal recessive
ANSWER: X) Sex-linked recessive
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer How many chromosomes does a normal human have?
ANSWER: 46
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer The transmission of traits from one generation to the next is called what?
ANSWER: HEREDITY (also accept: INHERITANCE)
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice An individual that reproduces asexually produces a what?
W) Gamete
X) Chromatosphere
Y) Clone
Z) Haploid
ANSWER: Y) Clone
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer A zygote is what type of cell?
ANSWER: DIPLOID CELL
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice An ordered pair or image of one's chromosomes is called what?
W) Genome
X) Chromosomal Image
Y) Karyotope
Z) Homology
ANSWER: Y) Karyotope
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A substrate binds to what in an enzyme?
W) The protein-reaction complex
X) The Active site
Y) The Affected Site
Z) The inhibited complex
ANSWER: X) The Active site
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Beta oxidation yields what product?
ANSWER: Acetyl CoA
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the mechanism that amplifies the response of enzymes to substrates?
ANSWER: COOPERATIVITY
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Non-protein helpers for catalytic activity are called what?
ANSWER: COFACTORS
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the name of a cofactor that is an organic molecule?
W) Allosterol
X) Competitive Substrate
Y) Ribozyme
Z) Coenzyme
ANSWER: Z) Coenzyme

W) Alpha-Succinate
X) Alpha-Ketoglutarate
Y) Acetyl CoA
Z) Carbon Dioxide
ANSWER: X) Alpha-Ketoglutarate
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Enzymes are responsible for lowering what barrier?
ANSWER: THE ENERGY OF ACTIVATION BARRIER
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The stroke volume is
W) The volume of urine pumped into the bladder
X) The volume of blood pumped to the brain during a synapse
Y) The volume of blood pumped by a heart ventricle in a single contraction
Z) The volume of blood pumped by a heart artery in a single contraction
ANSWER: Y) The volume of blood pumped by a heart ventricle in a single contraction
18. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Organisms that can not tolerate substantial changes in external osmolarity are called what?
W) Stenohalines
X) Symbionts
Y) Suspension Feeders
Z) Tastants
ANSWER: W) Stenohalines
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The stage of the cardiac cycle in which a heart chamber contracts and pumps blood is called what?
ANSWER: SYSTOLE
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer The stock of a flower's carpel, with the ovary at the base and the stigma at the top is called what?
ANSWER: Style

W) Systolic
X) Portalic
Y) Systemic
Z) Circadian
ANSWER: Y) Systemic
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer A Protist in which a "hairy" flagellum is paired with a shorter smoother flagellum is called what?
ANSWER: STRAMENOPILE
21. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the enzyme that catalyzes the lengthening of telomeres in eukaryotic cells?
W) Telomerose
X) Telomerase
Y) Telomerese
Z) Telomeryse
ANSWER: X) Telomerase
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer An organism that transmits pathogens from one host to another is called what?
ANSWER: VECTOR
22. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is an organism called when its genome contains a gene introduced from another organism of the same or different species?
W) Mutagenic
X) Genomic
Y) Transgenic
Z) Transletic
ANSWER: Y) Transgenic
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the type of horizontal gene transfer in which phages carry bacterial DNA from one host cell to another?
ANSWER: TRANSDUCTION
23. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Any detectable variant in genetic character is called
W) Phenotype
X) Genotype
Y) Trait
Z) Heridity
ANSWER: Y) Trait
23. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A cell that can give rise to all parts of the embryo and adult, as well as extraembryonic membranes in species is called what?
W) Omnipotent
X) Totipotent
Y) Multipotent
Z) Pluripotent
ANSWER: X) Totipotent
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A spore from a heterosporous plant species that develops into a male gametophyte is called a
ANSWER: MICROSPORE

ANSWER: MIXOTROPH
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A cell that has only one copy of a particular chromosome instead of the normal two, is referred to as what?
ANSWER: MONOSOMIC
25. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A steroid hormone secreted by the adrenal cortex that regulates salt and water homeostasis is called what?
W) Corticoid
X) Corticsteroid
Y) Mineralocorticoid
Z) Halogenocorticoid
ANSWER: Y) Mineralocorticoid
