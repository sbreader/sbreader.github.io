Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The intrinsic contact clotting pathway can be activated by what very common protein?
ANSWER: Collagen
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following individuals would have the most reproductive fitness?
W) Polygynous male
X) Polygynous female
Y) Polyandrous male
Z) Polyandrous female
ANSWER: W) Polygynous male
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Lucas has a trigonal bipyramidal compound in his hands. However, when he observes it, he actually records it as a square planar compound some of the time. He thinks he is hallucinating, until he reads a chemistry textbook, which explains that the interconversion between trigonal bipyramidal and square planar shapes is a result of what mechanism?
ANSWER: Berry pseudorotation (also accept: turnstile rotation)
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Naman is heating some ethane under a constant volume. However, near the critical point of ethane, he now sees that the solution has turned cloudy. What phenomenon, caused by the density fluctuations resulting from the properties of the critical point, has Naman observed?
ANSWER: Critical opalescence
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A rope is tied between two walls such that it has slack and droops down in the middle. In the middle, Ayden applies a downward force, causing the rope to now become a V-shape. Which of the following describes the center of mass as he applies this force?
W) It moves down
X) It remains stationary
Y) It moves up
Z) There is not enough information to solve this problem
ANSWER: Y) It moves up
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Fermat's Principle of Least Time is a special case of what other principle, which says the path an object will take minimizes the integral of the Lagrangian?
ANSWER: Principle of Least Action
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What non-gravitational effect causes small, rotating asteroids to slowly change their orbits due to the uneven re-radiation of absorbed sunlight?
ANSWER: Yarkovsky effect
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the current, prevailing standard model of cosmology that describes a flat, expanding universe composed of matter, cold dark matter, and dark energy?
ANSWER: Lambda-CDM (also accept: Lambda Cold Dark Matter)
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Solve the following expression: lim h->0 ((3+h)2-32)/h.
ANSWER: 6
5. BONUS Math Short Answer An ice cream cone is made up of a cone connected to a hemisphere. If the radius of the hemisphere is 3 and the height of the cone is 8, in terms of pi, what is the volume of this ice cream cone?
ANSWER: 42π
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Some believe that Adolf Hitler had a singular black, shriveled testicle, but the truth agreed upon by scientists and historians alike is that his right testicle remained undescended. What is the name of this condition?
W) Androgen insensitivity syndrome
X) Achondroplasia
Y) Irritable bowel syndrome
Z) Cryptorchidism
ANSWER: Z) Cryptorchidism
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Liam, Lucas, Naman, and Stefan are all grouses partaking in a lek mating arena. Assuming that their rank increases in the order formerly given, a female grouse who prioritizes mating as fast as possible over finding the strongest mate would most likely mate with which individual?
W) Liam
X) Lucas
Y) Naman
Z) Stefan
ANSWER: W) Liam
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which set of rules predicts the covalent nature of ionic bonds based on the charge of the cation and the relative sizes of the ions?
ANSWER: Fajan’s Rules
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about SN1 reactions?
W) It is a single-step reaction
X) Its rate depends only on the concentration of a single reactant
Y) Its rate is directly proportional to the concentration of the nucleophile
Z) Its rate is increased when taking place in polar aprotic solvents
ANSWER: X) Its rate depends only on the concentration of a single reactant
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer When running at very low speeds, Sonic the Hedgehog has a length of 20 centimeters. In centimeters, what is his length when running at 60% of the speed of light?
ANSWER: 16
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer In terms of pi, what is the resonance frequency of a LC circuit consisting of an inductor of inductance 0.5 Henries and two capacitors of capacitance 1 Farad connected in series?
ANSWER: 1/π
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following elements does rhyolitic lava contain high concentrations of?
W) Potassium
X) Iron
Y) Magnesium
Z) Calcium
ANSWER: W) Potassium
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The flow of what cold eastern boundary ocean current is responsible for the high productivity and anchovy rates off the west coast of South America?
ANSWER: Peru current
10. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer While Dr. B’s students are recording their volume data, they want the first item on their list to be the latest inputted, and want to obtain the first item on the list when they access it. What data structure should they use to store the data?
ANSWER: Stack (also accept: Deque)
10. BONUS Energy Short Answer What architecture, modeled after the visual cortex, uses pooling layers to excel at detecting edges, shapes, and objects?
ANSWER: Convolutional Neural Network (also accept: CNN)
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What kinetic equation describes the rate of an enzyme-catalyzed reaction based on substrate concentration and maximum velocity?
ANSWER: Michaelis-Menten Equation
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What white blood cell is most responsible for killing multicellular pathogens?
ANSWER: Eosinophils
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In transition metal compounds, strong distortions sometimes occur that resolve electron degeneracy and make the complex more stable. What is the name of this effect, which elongates or compresses the axial bonds with respect to the equatorial bonds?
ANSWER: Jahn-Teller Effect
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following conformations that a cyclohexane molecule could exhibit 1. Chair 2. Twist-Chair 3. Boat 4. Half-Boat
ANSWER: 1 and 3
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In an RLC circuit, consisting of a resistor, inductor, and capacitor, what order differential equation describes the time evolution of charge in the capacitor?
ANSWER: 2
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer A rocket in deep space ejects mass at a rate of 1,000 kilograms per second, with an exhaust velocity of 3 kilometers per second. If at a certain moment the rocket has a mass of 600,000 kilograms, what will be its acceleration in meters per second squared?
ANSWER: 5
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What process, critical to the cooling in neutron stars and white dwarfs, emits a neutrino through absorption of a lepton, convection away from the core of the star, and then a beta decay?
ANSWER: Urca process
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the massive gravitational anomaly that pulls other galaxies towards it, composed of a supercluster of galaxies hidden by the Milky Way’s “Zone of Avoidance”?
ANSWER: The Great Attractor
15. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Rank the following five operations in order of increasing time complexity 1. Accessing an element in an array 2. Binary search 3. Linear search 4. Heap Sort 5. Bubble Sort
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
15. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the term for the statistical anomaly where a trend disappears and sometimes reverses when broken down into subpopulations?
ANSWER: Simpson’s paradox

W) Ascomycota
X) Basidiomycota
Y) Glomeromycota

W) Haworth projection
X) Newmann projection
Y) Natta projection

X) 10

18. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Liam is playing percussion for an orchestra. While playing the drums, he notes that he can mathematically deduce the shape of the waves released by his drums. This is because radial waves from a 2D drumhead can be modelled with which of the following tools?
W) Bessel Function
X) Spherical harmonics
Y) Schrodinger's equation
Z) Elliptic integrals
ANSWER: W) Bessel Function
18. VISUAL BONUS Physics Short Answer The image shown is an important diagram that is used to model complex quantum interactions. Answer the following two questions regarding this image 1) What is the name of this diagram? 2) What are the two processes occurring in the diagram?
ANSWER: 1) Feynman diagram; 2) Annihilation and pair production
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When lightning or other electrical discharges come into contact with insulating materials, they produce striking fractal-like patterns on the materials. What is the term for these patterns?
ANSWER: Lichtenberg pattern
19. VISUAL BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Answer the following two questions regarding the image 1) What is the name for these markings, commonly formed from the slow cooling of certain alloys? 2) These figures are formed from the interlocking of what two crystals?
ANSWER: 1) Widmanstatten pattern; 2) Kamacite and taenite
20. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the name of the error that occurs when a program tries to use more memory in the call stack than it has been allocated?
ANSWER: Stack Overflow (do not accept: Overflow)
20. BONUS Math Short Answer What theorem in geometry says that a solid 3D ball can be decomposed into a finite number of pieces and then reassembled, through only rigid motions, into two balls identical to the first?
ANSWER: Banach-Tarski Paradox
