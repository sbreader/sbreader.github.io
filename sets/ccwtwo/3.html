1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What dimensionless constant, equivalent to the product of the speed of light in a vacuum and the vacuum permeability divided by twice the von Klitzing constant, quantifies the electromagnetic force between elementary particles?
ANSWER: FINE-STRUCTURE CONSTANT (also accept: FINE-STRUCTURE; ALPHA)
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following are valid units of the Boltzmann constant?
W) Mole per Kelvin
X) Electronvolt per Kelvin
Y) Kelvin per Coulomb
Z) Joules per Mole
ANSWER: X) Electronvolt per Kelvin
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are developing synthetic trees, which could be used as passive water pumps. These trees utilize what process that occurs in the leaves of plants, causing water to travel upwards?
ANSWER: TRANSPIRATION
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory are studying how uranium reacts after being leaked into the environment. What oxidation state is uranium in when it is originally leaked into the environment, which is also the oxidation state of uranyl, a stable uranium compound?
ANSWER: +6 (also accept: URANIUM +6)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following acids is strongest?
W) HBr
X) HBrO
Y) HBrO2
Z) HBrO3

ANSWER: π
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What term describes a point on the surface of the graph of a function where the slopes in orthogonal directions are all zero, but which is not a local extremum of the function?

6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about ocean waves is correct?
W) Ocean waves display longitudinal motion
X) Ocean waves consist of the movement of water brought about by thermohaline circulation
Y) Unusually small ocean waves formed through various weather phenomena are known as swells
Z) Ocean waves are orbital and involve the transfer of energy, not water
ANSWER: Z) Ocean waves are orbital and involve the transfer of energy, not water

7. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory have utilized the cyanobacterium Cyanothece for energy production. What has this cyanobacterium been shown to be the first to do, that makes it so useful for energy production?

X) It can release methane while performing photosynthesis
Y) It can release natural gas while performing photosynthesis

9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why neutron poisons are held in position above the nuclear reaction by electromagnets?
W) In case of traditional safety mechanisms failing due to complete loss of electricity, the neutron poisons would automatically drop into the reaction and avert a meltdown
X) The magnetic field generated by the electromagnet leads to convection of the fast neutrons produced by nuclear chain reactions
Y) In case of tectonic activity leading to a subcritical state, current could be increasing to move the neutron poisons away from the reaction

15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following provides an accurate reason and explanation for why the top quark was the last quark observed experimentally?
W) The top quark has a relatively high mass, so it can be easily confused with larger particles
X) The top quark has a relatively high degree of instability, so it is more difficult to observe with accuracy
Y) The top quark has a relatively high mass, so it requires more energy to produce
