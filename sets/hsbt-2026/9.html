1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Owen is singing to Chinese songs during an earthquake. Identify all of the following three measurements that are typically used to deduce the moment magnitude for an earthquake 1) Peak wave amplitude 2) Average fault slip 3) Rupture area.
ANSWER: 2 and 3
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice David is wandering around a volcano and wants to determine the age of igneous rocks after they have cooled below the closure temperature. Which of the following radiometric dating methods would prove the most useful in determining the age of these rocks?
W) Carbon-14 dating
X) Potassium-Argon dating
Y) Uranium-Lead dating
Z) Rubidium-Strontium dating
ANSWER: X) Potassium-Argon dating
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The most common form of juvenile bone cancer is osteosarcoma. What is the second most common form, also known for its distinct onion skin appearance in X-ray scans?
ANSWER: Ewing’s sarcoma
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, order the following 5 layers of the epiphyseal plate from the closest to furthest from the diaphysis 1) Zone of Hypertrophy 2) Zone of Ossification 3) Zone of Calcification.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 3 hydrogen atoms in order of increasing H-1 NMR shift 1) Hydrogen atom in formaldehyde 2) Hydrogen atom in ethylene 3) Hydrogen atom in benzene.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice How many sigma and pi bonds are there, respectively, in one molecule of pyrosulfuric acid, whose molecular formula is H2S2O7?
W) 10 sigma, 2 pi
X) 10 sigma, 4 pi
Y) 12 sigma, 2 pi
Z) 12 sigma, 4 pi
ANSWER: X) 10 sigma, 4 pi

6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Hawaii is testing wave energy generation as a potential renewable energy source, deriving energy from high-density wave energy flux. Which of the following best describes how wave power scales with wave height and wave period, respectively?
W) Linearly, linearly
X) Linearly, quadratically
Y) Quadratically, linearly
Z) Quadratically, quadratically

7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Mia wants to simulate a slow creeping flow of magma through a porous mixture of crystals. Which law is best suited for this task, which states that the volumetric discharge rate is proportional to the cross-sectional area and inversely proportional to dynamic viscosity?
ANSWER: Darcy's Law
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The calc-alkaline rock series is best found in which of the following geologic settings?
W) Mid-ocean ridges
X) Continental intraplate hotspots
Y) Continental rift zones
Z) Convergent plate boundaries
ANSWER: Z) Convergent plate boundaries
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer C4 plants spatially separate the process of carbon fixation and the calvin cycle in order to limit photorespiration. What is the term to describe the specific morphology of mesophyll and bundle sheath cells around veins in C4 plants?
ANSWER: Kranz anatomy
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Just like the Calvin cycle, the process of C4 carbon fixation also has a name taken after the scientists who discovered it in 1966. What is the alternative name of the C4 carbon fixation process?
ANSWER: Hatch-slack cycle

11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A series RLC circuit contains a resistor of resistance 5 ohms, an inductor of inductance 8 henries, and a capacitor of capacitance 2 farads. What is the Q factor of this circuit?
ANSWER: 2/5
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer Kevin has a solenoid of inductance L. Using the same piece of wire, he adjusts the solenoid such that the area of each coil is 1/4th of the original. What is the new inductance, in terms of L?
ANSWER: L
12. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Second-generation biofuels use non-food crops and agricultural residues to produce energy. These materials contain hard plant cell walls of cellulose and lignin, which creates a significant energy barrier in biofuel production. What term refers to this process, the natural resistance of plant cell walls to breakdown by chemicals, enzymes, or microorganisms?
ANSWER: Lignocellulosic recalcitrance (also accept: Recalcitrance)
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer A wind turbine has a rotor diameter of 80 m and operates in a steady wind speed of 12 m/s. If the wind speed increases to 18 m/s and the rotor diameter increases to 160 m, then, to the nearest integer, by what factor is the power generated by the wind turbine multiplied?
ANSWER: 14
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term refers to the imaginary line traced by the lowest elevation along a watercourse, corresponding to the deepest part of a river channel where the current moves the fastest?

13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Raphael is observing stars in an eclipsing binary and compiles a brightness timeseries to create a light curve for the pair of stars. Given that Raphael also knows the distance to the system via parallax, but does not have access to spectroscopic data, identify all of the following three properties of the binary that he can accurately determine 1) Orbital separation 2) Orbital eccentricity 3) Stellar masses.

14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Separate from allopatric speciation, what is the type of speciation that occurs when two populations live in geographically distinct regions, but continue interacting with each other?
ANSWER: Parapatric
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Gause’s Law states which of the following?
W) Two populations that inhabit the same niche cannot coexist indefinitely
X) Species adapt to limit niche overlap with other species
Y) Speciation is a gradual process
Z) R-selected species are better adapted to exploiting the available resources in an environment
ANSWER: W) Two populations that inhabit the same niche cannot coexist indefinitely

21. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Why is cold, dilute alkaline KMnO4 frequently avoided in modern total synthesis for the conversion of functionalized alkenes to 1,2-diols, compared to osmium- or transition-metal-catalyzed alternatives?
W) It promotes an alternative anti-dihydroxylation pathway, yielding the undesired trans-1,2-diol stereoisomer
X) It suffers from low atom economy and requires cost-prohibitive stoichiometric quantities compared to catalytic osmium
