Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer This American inventor created, among other things, the phonograph and the microphone.
ANSWER: THOMAS EDISON
1. BONUS Energy Short Answer As an improvement to Edison's lightbulb, Lewis Latimer added what other part, which today is usually made of tungsten?
ANSWER: FILAMENT
2. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice A mycologist would most likely study
W) toxins
X) yeasts
Y) microscopes
Z) aromatic hydrocarbons
ANSWER: X) yeasts
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer This first ever antibiotic is usually used in the treatment of Gram-positive bacteria and was extracted from a fungus it was named for. Name the antibiotic.
ANSWER: PENICILLIN
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Classify the following three substances as molecules, formula units, or elements: uranium, water, table salt.
ANSWER: URANIUM – ELEMENT; WATER – MOLECULE; TABLE SALT – FORMULA UNIT
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In an NaCl molecule, a chlorine atom attracts how many neighboring sodium atoms?
ANSWER: 6
4. BONUS Energy Short Answer Through the scientific method, Benjamin Franklin proved that this phenomenon, in which luminous plasma is created by a coronal discharge, was electrical in nature.
ANSWER: ST. ELMO'S FIRE
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a domain of life?
W) Archaea
X) Eukarya
Y) Prokarya
Z) Bacteria
ANSWER: Y) Prokarya
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Charles Darwin was influenced by this British geologist and his theory of the geologic time scale. Name this geologist who was a chief proponent of uniformitarianism, the idea that the way events take place now are the way they have always taken place.
ANSWER: CHARLES LYELL
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Fossils which are used to date the strata they are found in are called what?
ANSWER: INDEX FOSSILS
7. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which inventor, famous for his series of prestigious prizes named for him, gave the world dynamite?
ANSWER: ALFRED NOBEL
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Nitroglycerin, the main component in dynamite, can also be used for which of the following purposes?
W) Caulking wooden slats
X) Dilate blood vessels
Y) Fertilize soil
Z) Preserving foods
ANSWER: X) Dilate blood vessels
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Xylitol is a sugar alcohol found most commonly in what substance?
ANSWER: CHEWING GUM
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer Xylitol gives 2/3 of the energy of what other disaccharide?
ANSWER: SUCROSE
9. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer The study of glands is called what?
ANSWER: ADENOLOGY
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the largest gland in the human body?
ANSWER: LIVER
10. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Anemology is the study of what?
ANSWER: WIND
10. BONUS Energy Short Answer According to the National Weather Service, what is the term for a wind that is 39-54mph in wind speed?
ANSWER: GALE
11. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer How many Nobel Prizes are science-based?
ANSWER: THREE
11. BONUS Energy Short Answer Name the three Nobel Prizes in the sciences.
ANSWER: CHEMISTRY, PHYSICS, MEDICINE (PHYSIOLOGY)
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Members of the order Monotremata are mammals that do what unique action?
ANSWER: THEY LAY EGGS
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Select all of the following that are not monotremes: long-beaked echidna, kangaroo, short-beaked echidna, platypus.
ANSWER: KANGAROO
13. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following shapes is impossible to create using only a protractor and a straightedge?
W) Equilateral triangle
X) Regular heptagon
Y) Regular octagon
Z) Regular 17-sided figure
ANSWER: X) Regular heptagon
13. BONUS Math Short Answer As a regular polygon gains an infinite number of sides, in theory, what shape is created?
ANSWER: A CIRCLE
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The term used to describe the changing of cheap substances to more valuable ones, such as lead to gold, is
W) Chemistry
X) Metallurgy
Y) Alchemy
Z) Proton Subtraction
ANSWER: Y) Alchemy
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer What famous scientist, credited for the discovery of gravity, was also noted for his illicit alchemy experiments?
ANSWER: SIR ISAAC NEWTON
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer L. Acidophilus, the primary bacteria used in the making of yogurt, would fit in what classification kingdom?
ANSWER: MONERA
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Give the complete classification (all 7 levels) for humans.
ANSWER: ANIMALIA CHORDATA MAMMALIA PRIMATE HOMINIDAE HOMO SAPIENS
16. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice How many bytes are in two kilobytes?
W) 1024
X) 2048
Y) 4096
Z) 8192
ANSWER: X) 2048
16. BONUS Energy Short Answer A traditional CD-RW has how many megabytes?
ANSWER: 700 MB
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Gastric acid has approximately what value on the pH scale?
W) 1.0
X) 2.5
Y) 4.0
Z) 6.5
ANSWER: X) 2.5
17. BONUS Energy Short Answer Store-brand antacids are primary composed of what compound?
ANSWER: CALCIUM CARBONATE
18. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What petroleum-related acronym can be used to remember the results of oxidation and reduction?
ANSWER: OIL RIG
18. BONUS Energy Short Answer What does OIL RIG stand for?
ANSWER: OXIDATION IS LOSS, REDUCTION IS GAIN
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following has been proven to improve the heart's overall health?
W) Low-density cholesterol
X) Phytosterols
Y) Lycopene
Z) Lutein
ANSWER: Y) Lycopene
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer Lycopene is primarily found in what fruit?
ANSWER: TOMATO
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The number one killer in the United States is
W) Heart disease
X) Cancer
Y) AIDS
Z) Hemophilia
ANSWER: W) Heart disease
20. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements concerning cancer is least accurate?
W) The body's own blood vessels connect to tumors and feed them.
X) The risk of lung cancer increases exponentially with the use of cigarettes.
Y) Cancerous tumors can be benign or malignant.
Z) Cancer cells have a different strand of DNA than regular cells.
ANSWER: Z) Cancer cells have a different strand of DNA than regular cells.
21. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Electric appliances and tools are grounded in order to
W) complete the electrical circuit
X) prevent the overloading of circuits
Y) balance the voltage in both lines
Z) prevent surface static charge buildup
ANSWER: Z) prevent surface static charge buildup
21. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the typical outlet voltage in the U.S.?
ANSWER: 120 V
22. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms grows by producing additional units of itself that are very similar to the composition of the original?
W) sea sponge
X) sea urchin
Y) spider
Z) termite
ANSWER: W) sea sponge
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer The more specific term for asexual reproduction without fertilization by a male is what?
ANSWER: PARTHENOGENESIS
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Most dental amalgams are made by reacting mercury with an alloy consisting of what two metals?
ANSWER: SILVER AND TIN
23. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Sugar-free chewing gum has been shown to prevent cavities in the mouth because
W) chicle fills up the holes in the teeth
X) the active ingredients in gum kill plaque-causing bacteria
Y) gum stimulates saliva production, reducing the effectiveness of plaque acids
Z) phenylalanine is a natural enamel strengthener
ANSWER: Y) gum stimulates saliva production, reducing the effectiveness of plaque acids
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Sickle cell disease is caused by the changing of glutamic acid to what other amino acid in the chain?
ANSWER: VALINE
24. BONUS Biology Short Answer Phenylketonuria, also known as PKU, is characterized by the lack of what enzyme in the body?
ANSWER: PHENYLALANINE HYDROXYLASE

W) gluons
X) mesons
Y) the Higgs boson
Z) strange quarks
ANSWER: Y) the Higgs boson
