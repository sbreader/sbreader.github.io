Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What metal is used as the filament in modern incandescent light bulbs?
ANSWER: TUNGSTEN
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Name the process during which nuclear energy is released when the nuclei of hydrogen atoms are combined to form helium.
ANSWER: FUSION (also accept: NUCLEAR FUSION)
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the underlying cause of most volcanoes on Earth?
W) Meteor impacts
X) Tidal forces
Y) Plate tectonics
Z) Weather patterns
ANSWER: Y) Plate tectonics
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following solar system objects are made up of mostly dust and ice?
W) Comets
X) Asteroids
Y) Dwarf planets
Z) Jovian planets
ANSWER: W) Comets
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The majority of nuclear fission technology currently utilizes what group of elements on the periodic table?
W) Halogens
X) Lanthanides
Y) Actinides
Z) Rare earth metals
ANSWER: Y) Actinides
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A right triangle has hypotenuse with length 5 times the square root of 5. One of the legs is of length 5. What is the length of the other leg?
ANSWER: 10
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Compute the mean of the following list of 5 numbers: 23, 25, 27, 31, 34.
ANSWER: 28
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the porcupine's primary mode of defense from predators?
W) Throwing quills
X) Poisoned quills
Y) Barbed quills
Z) Musky spray
ANSWER: Y) Barbed quills
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Marsupials are far more abundant and diverse in Australia than in North and South America. Which of the following best explains why only three families of marsupials are found outside the Australian region?
W) Divergent evolution
X) Convergent evolution
Y) Parallel evolution
Z) Antiparallel evolution
ANSWER: W) Divergent evolution
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In a chemical equation, the number of molecules of a given substance is indicated by which of the following?
W) Coefficient
X) Superscript
Y) Subscript
Z) Degree
ANSWER: W) Coefficient
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the best practice to follow to dilute a concentrated acid?
W) Add small quantities of water to the acid sample
X) Calculate and add the exact amount of water to the acid sample quickly, while stirring constantly
Y) Add small quantities of acid to a quantity of water
Z) Calculate and add the exact amount of acid to water quickly, while stirring constantly
ANSWER: Y) Add small quantities of acid to a quantity of water
7. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer In comparison to diesel, biodiesel contains virtually none of what element, the burning of which is known to contribute to acid rain?
ANSWER: SULFUR
7. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following countries produces the greatest percentage of its electricity from nuclear energy?
W) Germany
X) Hungary
Y) Russia
Z) Switzerland
ANSWER: X) Hungary
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer When a sample of uranium undergoes a fission reaction, what type of particle initiates the chain reaction?
ANSWER: NEUTRONS
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A ball is dropped from the top of a 100-foot-tall building. How long in seconds does it take to reach the ground?
ANSWER: 2.5 (also accept: 5/2, 2 ½)
9. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes a hypothesis?
W) A proposed explanation for a narrow set of phenomena
X) Broad explanations for a wide range of phenomena
Y) A plan to measure observable phenomena
Z) An opinion based on observations of phenomena
ANSWER: W) A proposed explanation for a narrow set of phenomena
9. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice What metric prefix represents one quadrillionth?
W) Femto
X) Pico
Y) Atto
Z) Zepto
ANSWER: W) Femto
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Galena is the primary ore of what metal?
ANSWER: LEAD
10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is most responsible for the formation of the Great Lakes?
W) Rain
X) Wind
Y) Glaciers
Z) Meteor impacts
ANSWER: Y) Glaciers
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The regular price for jeans is $35 per pair, but today's sale is ten percent off when you buy two pairs. What is the sale price for four pairs of jeans?
ANSWER: $126
11. BONUS Math Multiple Choice If x varies inversely as the cube root of y and x equals 3 when y equals 27, what is the constant of proportionality?
W) 1/3
X) 1
Y) 3
Z) 9
ANSWER: Z) 9
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What organelle in the cell contains enzymes for digestion?
W) Central vacuole
X) Endoplasmic reticulum
Y) Lysosome
Z) Golgi apparatus
ANSWER: Y) Lysosome
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What fibrous protein makes up fingernails and hair?
ANSWER: KERATIN
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the SI unit of electrical resistance?
ANSWER: OHM
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer What do AM and FM stand for as related to radio transmission?
ANSWER: AMPLITUDE MODULATION AND FREQUENCY MODULATION
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following plant structures is most closely associated with land plants?
W) Vascular tissues
X) Pollen grains
Y) Leaves
Z) Roots
ANSWER: X) Pollen grains
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What disease is caused by Plasmodium protozoans?
ANSWER: MALARIA

W) Satterlee saw
X) Scalpel
Y) Blade
Z) Scissors
ANSWER: W) Satterlee saw
15. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is the SI derived unit of measurement of pressure?
W) Atmosphere
X) Millibar
Y) Millimeter of mercury
Z) Pascal
ANSWER: Z) Pascal
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following planets has an orbital period less than one Earth year?
W) Earth
X) Mars
Y) Venus
Z) Jupiter
ANSWER: Y) Venus
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three landforms that are types of glacial lakes 1) Kettle 2) Drumlin 3) Tarn.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3 (KETTLE AND TARN)
17. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The equation (x + 2) + 8 = x +10 illustrates the use of which of the following properties of whole-number addition?
W) Associative
X) Commutative
Y) Distributive
Z) Identity
ANSWER: W) Associative
17. BONUS Math Short Answer Evaluate the following expression: 27 plus 3 times 4 minus 4.
ANSWER: 35
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Many of the experiments at our National Laboratories need to take place at extremely low temperatures. What is the branch of physics called that deals with the production and effects of very low temperatures?
ANSWER: CRYOGENICS (also accept: CRYONICS)
18. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is the most widely used form of renewable energy worldwide?
W) Solar energy
X) Hydroelectric energy
Y) Wind energy
Z) Natural gas
ANSWER: X) Hydroelectric energy
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If the product of two binomials is h2 + 2h – 120 and one factor is h – 10, what is the other factor?
ANSWER: h + 12
19. BONUS Math Short Answer A circle has an area of 9. What is its circumference?
ANSWER: 6√π
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the phenomenon, often found in plants, in which an organism contains more than two complete sets of chromosomes?
ANSWER: POLYPLOIDY (also accept: POLYPLOID)
20. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Ornamentation often contributes to the mating behavior and choices of animals. Male elks are highly ornamented. Which of the following types of mating system would you expect?
W) Promiscuous
X) Monogamous
Y) Polyandry
Z) Polygyny
ANSWER: Z) Polygyny
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Most of the matter that makes up the Sun is in what phase?
W) Gas
X) Solid
Y) Plasma
Z) Liquid
ANSWER: Y) Plasma
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What ocean layer receives sufficient sunlight for photosynthesis?
ANSWER: PHOTIC ZONE (also accept: EUPHOTIC ZONE)
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In chemical thermodynamics, what term describes the number of states in which a system can be arranged and is often thought to be a measure of disorder?
ANSWER: ENTROPY
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer A manometer is used for measuring what property of a gas?
ANSWER: PRESSURE

ANSWER: 70
