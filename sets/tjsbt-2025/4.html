1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Eshaan is back with his model cell and now he’s analyzing his extracellular matrix. By name or number, identify all of the following 3 proteins that are present within the extracellular matrix 1. Collagen 2. Myosin 3. Fibronectin

1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following options are true regarding the vestibulocochlear cranial nerve?
W) It automatically adjusts the tension of the eardrum in response to sudden changes in pitch, preventing hearing damage without involving any other structures
X) It interprets both pressure and temperature changes in the inner ear
Y) It transmits auditory signals only from low-frequency sounds, while high-frequency sounds are carried by a separate branch of the trigeminal nerve
Z) It detects rotational movements via the semicircular canals and linear accelerations via the utricle and saccule
