Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the hybridization of the central carbon atom in formaldehyde?
ANSWER: sp2
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the three following properties that are state functions 1) Heat 2) Work 3) Enthalpy.
ANSWER: 3 ONLY
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Yeast produces beer and other alcohol products through what general reaction pathway?
ANSWER: FERMENTATION (also accept: ANAEROBIC RESPIRATION)
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following monophyletic groups is NOT classified as being one of the three major biological domains?
W) Bacteria
X) Protista
Y) Eukarya
Z) Archaea
ANSWER: X) Protista
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at the Fischer Lab at UC Berkeley use a combination of organic synthesis and scanning electron microscopy to create new materials from parent molecules such as graphene, which is an allotrope of which element?
ANSWER: CARBON
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at the Space Sciences Laboratory at UC Berkeley are collaborating with Lockheed Martin to develop a model for the evolution of the Sun’s magnetic field. What type of event consists of charged or magnetized particles being emitted due to fluctuations in the Sun’s magnetic field?
ANSWER: CORONAL MASS EJECTION (do not accept: SOLAR FLARE)
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A wheel is rotating at 5 radians per second. In radians per second squared, what angular acceleration must be applied to the wheel to accelerate it to 10 radians per second in 20 seconds?
ANSWER: 0.25 (also accept: 1/4)
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of forces that are conservative 1) Frictional 2) Gravitational 3) Magnetic.
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the slope of the line with the equation 3y + 5x = 12?
ANSWER: –5/3
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the sum of the infinite geometric series with first term 3/5 and second term 1/5?
ANSWER: 9/10
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for localized volcanism due to plumes of hot magma rising from the mantle?
ANSWER: HOTSPOT
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Dylan looks up at the sky and notices that there are multiple layers of clouds at different heights. Order the following three types of clouds from lowest to highest altitude 1) Altocumulus 2) Cirrus 3) Stratus.
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In what phase of the cell cycle does DNA replication occur?
ANSWER: S-PHASE (also accept: S) (do not accept: INTERPHASE)
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Scientists develop a mutant-type rat that has a large decrease in ATP production compared to its wild-type counterpart by preventing the binding of a heme cofactor to a certain protein. Which of the following systems of cellular respiration did the mutation most likely affect?
W) Glycolysis
X) Pyruvate oxidation
Y) Citric acid cycle
Z) Electron transport chain
ANSWER: Z) Electron transport chain
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a coral reef separated from the main coast by a deep channel or lagoon?
ANSWER: BARRIER REEF
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three geologic periods whose ends were marked by mass extinction events 1) Devonian 2) Permian 3) Jurassic.
ANSWER: 1
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer If a piston does 150 joules of work and loses 20 joules of energy as heat, what is its change in internal energy in joules?
ANSWER: –170
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements has the greatest second ionization energy?
W) Sodium
X) Calcium
Y) Carbon
Z) Titanium
ANSWER: W) Sodium
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the log base 4 of 12 times the log base 12 of 20 times the log base 20 of 64?
ANSWER: 3
10. BONUS Math Short Answer A circle of radius 2 root 2 is centered at the origin and has a tangent line at the point (2, 2). What is the slope of this tangent line?
ANSWER: –1
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If a 20-ohm resistor is rated for 5 watts, then in volts, what is the maximum potential difference that can be applied across it?
ANSWER: 10
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer Consider an isolated, charged capacitor. By what factor is the voltage on the capacitor multiplied when the dielectric is swapped out for a dielectric with twice the original dielectric constant?
ANSWER: 1/2
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of inhibition occurs when a molecule binds to a secondary site on an enzyme and changes the conformation of the active site of that enzyme?
ANSWER: ALLOSTERIC (also accept: NONCOMPETITIVE)
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In their famous experiment, Meselson and Stahl grew bacteria with radioactive isotopes to track DNA. Their experimental results proved which of the following models of DNA replication?
W) Dispersive
X) Semiconservative
Y) Conservative
Z) Random
ANSWER: X) Semiconservative
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What phenomenon describes the emission of light from a solid due to heat?
ANSWER: INCANDESCENCE (also accept: BLACK BODY RADIATION)
13. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the steric number of the central carbon atom in methane?
W) 2
X) 3
Y) 4
Z) 5
ANSWER: Y) 4
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer UC Berkeley Bioengineering Professor Seung-Wuk Lee has demonstrated pyroelectricity in the genetically-engineered virus M13. When heated, the virus develops a voltage difference due to the denaturation of coat proteins located on what viral structure?
ANSWER: CAPSID
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A team led by UC Berkeley Professor Imke de Pater recently observed a 3000-mile wide band near Jupiter’s equator with winds of 320 miles per hour. What is the term for these fast-flowing high altitude air currents?
ANSWER: JET STREAMS
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If f(x) = 12x – 10, what is f inverse of 26?
ANSWER: 3
15. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the lowest possible perimeter for a rectangle with an area of 56?
ANSWER: 8√14 (also accept: 8 TIMES THE SQUARE ROOT OF 14)
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Jellyfish are members of what phylum, which is characterized by radial symmetry and the presence of nematocysts?
W) Mollusca
X) Cnidaria
Y) Arthropoda
Z) Annelida
ANSWER: X) Cnidaria
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three molecules that are products of glycolysis 1) Pyruvate 2) NADH 3) ATP.
ANSWER: ALL
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the magnitude of the force, in newtons, on a wire carrying 5 milliamps of current when placed in a magnetic field of 0.1 teslas parallel to the wire?
ANSWER: 0
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer An Atwood machine has 30-kilogram and 15-kilogram masses hanging off its two ends. Using g equals 10 meters per second squared, what is the magnitude of the acceleration of the system, in meters per second squared and to two significant figures?
ANSWER: 3.3
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the average temperature of the universe in kelvins?
W) 300
X) 30
Y) 3
Z) 0.3
ANSWER: Y) 3
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Constantine is monitoring the weather in central Illinois and observes a passing cold front. Identify all of the following three weather events that he likely observed 1) A broad area of light precipitation 2) An increase in dew point 3) A wind shift from south to northwest.
ANSWER: 3 ONLY
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the highest-energy occupied atomic orbital in a ground state phosphorus atom?
ANSWER: 3p
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer On a heating curve, what term is given to the quantity that determines how much energy is required for a phase change?
ANSWER: LATENT HEAT
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Members of the Particle Data Group at the Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are continually characterizing the properties of various elementary particles of the standard model. To do this, they use a particle accelerator which propels a particle in a spiral path. What is the name of this type of particle accelerator?
ANSWER: CYCLOTRON
20. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In an effort to create more efficient biofuel crops, researchers at the Joint Bioenergy Institute are researching ways to optimize plant cell walls. Which of the following sugars is most likely to be found in plant cell walls?
W) Chitin
X) Cellulose
Y) Glucose
Z) Glycogen
ANSWER: X) Cellulose
21. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many times do the graphs of y = x + 2 and y = ex intersect?
ANSWER: 2
21. BONUS Math Short Answer In radians, what is the sum of the internal angles of a regular dodecagon?
ANSWER: 10π
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The transit of what planet was famously timed to test the theory of general relativity?
ANSWER: MERCURY
22. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the principal ore of aluminum?
W) Cryolite
X) Siderite
Y) Bauxite
Z) Corundum
ANSWER: Y) Bauxite
23. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which of Maxwell’s equations most directly prohibits the existence of magnetic monopoles?
ANSWER: GAUSS’S LAW FOR MAGNETISM (do not accept: GAUSS’S LAW)
23. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 1-kilogram block slides down a 45-degree incline without accelerating. What is the minimum possible value for the coefficient of static friction for this system?
ANSWER: 1
