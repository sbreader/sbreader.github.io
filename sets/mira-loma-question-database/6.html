1. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer At what temperature Celsius would the Fahrenheit equivalent be twice as large?
ANSWER: ONE HUNDRED SIXTY DEGREES
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer The average body temperature of a healthy human is approximated at 98.6 degrees Fahrenheit. Provide both the Celsius and Kelvin equivalents for this temperature to the nearest integer
ANSWER: 37 DEGREES Celsius, 310 KELVIN
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following units is NOT an SI base unit
W) Ampere
X) Coulomb
Y) Mole
Z) Second
ANSWER: X) Coulomb
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The term ketone describes organic compounds with what functional group attached
W) Hydroxyl
X) Sulfhydryl
Y) Carbonyl
Z) Carboxyl
ANSWER: Y) Carbonyl
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds will always contain a carboxyl functional group
W) Triglycerides
X) Polysaccharides
Y) Glycerols
Z) Amino Acids
ANSWER: Z) Amino Acids
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer This scientist from the turn of the 19th century is most widely known as Thomas Edison's rival and victor in the "War of the Currents", where he advocated alternating current as opposed to Edison's direct current
ANSWER: NIKOLA TESLA (also accept: TESLA)
4. BONUS Energy Short Answer This scientist, appointed Chancellor of the University of California, Berkeley in 1958, is credited with the co-discovery of several transuranium elements, leading a section of the Manhattan Project, and has also been honored by having an element named after him
ANSWER: GLENN T. SEABORG (also accept: SEABORG)
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The purine nitrogen bases in RNA are
W) Cytosine, Uracil, and Thymine
X) Cytosine and Uracil
Y) Adenine and Guanine
Z) Adenine and Uracil
ANSWER: Y) Adenine and Guanine
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Give the complementary RNA sequence for the DNA sequence ATGCATTC
ANSWER: UACGUAAG
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The definition of the Atomic Mass Unit is based off which of the following
W) The mass of carbon-12
X) The mass of oxygen-16
Y) The mass of water
Z) The mass of carbon-14
ANSWER: W) The mass of carbon-12
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the common name of the isotope of hydrogen that is most naturally abundant
ANSWER: PROTIUM (do not accept: HYDROGEN-1)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Antibodies are classified as
W) Lipids
X) Carbohydrates
Y) Nucleic Acids
Z) Proteins
ANSWER: Z) Proteins
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What organ is the largest in the human immune system
ANSWER: THE SKIN
8. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which is the only SI base unit that has an official definition derived from a single existing prototype
ANSWER: KILOGRAM
8. BONUS Math Short Answer 120 degrees is equivalent to what measurement in radians
ANSWER: 2π/3 (also accept: 2/3 PI)
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The square of a circuit's current times the resistance of the circuit is equivalent to the circuit's
W) Power
X) Electric potential
Y) Work
Z) Velocity
ANSWER: W) Power
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two four ohm resistors in series are parallel to one six ohm resistor which are all connected to a nine volt battery. What is the difference between the combined voltages over the two four ohm resistors compared to the voltage of the six ohm resistor?
ANSWER: ZERO VOLTS
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The classification system devised by Edwin Hubble, often referred to as the "Tuning Fork", is used to classify what astronomical bodies
W) Planets
X) Galaxies
Y) Stars
Z) Black Holes
ANSWER: X) Galaxies
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer According the theories of many leading astronomers, what exists at the center of many, if not all galaxies
ANSWER: SUPERMASSIVE BLACK HOLES (also accept: BLACK HOLE(S))
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a metamorphic rock
W) Gneiss
X) Slate
Y) Limestone
Z) Marble
ANSWER: Y) Limestone
11. BONUS Energy Short Answer What compound is most abundant by mass in granite
ANSWER: SILICA (also accept: SILICON DIOXIDE)
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The large lunar maria found throughout the surface of the moon have been commonly accepted to have been formed by
W) Ancient volcanic eruptions
X) Collisions with space debris
Y) Ancient oceans that have since dried up
Z) The uneven cooling of the moon when it first formed
ANSWER: W) Ancient volcanic eruptions
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Provide the IUPAC name for the chemical compound found in galena
ANSWER: LEAD (II) SULFIDE
13. BONUS Energy Short Answer What scientist, whose name is often associated with the tire industry, is credited with the modern discovery of the process in which to vulcanize rubber
ANSWER: CHARLES GOODYEAR (also accept: GOODYEAR)
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the cube root of negative 729
ANSWER: -9
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following provides an accurate description of a second-class lever
W) The fulcrum is between the load and the force
X) The load is between the fulcrum and the force
Y) The force is between the fulcrum and the load
Z) No such thing exists
ANSWER: X) The load is between the fulcrum and the force
15. BONUS Energy Short Answer A baseball bat would best be classified under which class of lever
ANSWER: CLASS-THREE LEVER
16. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which law of thermodynamics is described as the transitive property of thermal equilibrium
W) Zeroth law
X) First law
Y) Second law
Z) Third law
ANSWER: W) Zeroth law
16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following relationships could not be analyzed using Kepler's third law
W) The orbit of the moon around Earth and the orbit of a satellite around Earth
X) The orbit of the moon around Earth and the orbit of Earth around the sun
Y) The orbit of Earth around the sun and the orbit of Jupiter around the sun
Z) The orbit Titan around Saturn and the orbit of Calypso around Saturn
ANSWER: X) The orbit of the moon around Earth and the orbit of Earth around the sun
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The atmosphere of which planet in the solar system has the greatest concentration of nitrogen

17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which planet in the solar system has the largest moon in relation to its own size
ANSWER: EARTH

ANSWER: FIVE KILOMETERS NORTHWEST (do not accept: FIVE KILOMETERS)
