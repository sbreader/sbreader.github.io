Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Given that there are 12 applicants for 2 identical job spots, how many possible pairs of applicants can be selected?
ANSWER: 66
1. BONUS Math Short Answer Amy, Brian, Collin, and Dylan want to sit in a row of 4 chairs. If Amy and Brian refuse to sit next to each other, and Amy and Collin must sit next to each other, how many ways are there for the 4 people to sit down in the 4 chairs?
ANSWER: 8
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes the side of a sand dune without wind?
ANSWER: SLIPFACE
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, state which of the following are types of sand dunes. (1) Parabolic (2) Littoral (3) Star (4) Barchanoid
ANSWER: 1,3,4
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many unpaired electrons does an iron atom in the ground state have?
ANSWER: 4
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Calculate the number of bonding and antibonding electrons in a diatomic oxygen molecule?
ANSWER: 8 AND 4 (do not accept: 4 AND 8)
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is it called when macrophages leave from the bloodstream into surrounding tissues to fight off infection?
ANSWER: DIAPEDESIS
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the molecule in muscle cells that transfers its phosphate to ADP to make ATP?
ANSWER: PHOSPHOCREATINE (also accept: CREATINE PHOSPHATE; PCr)
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What quantity is calculated using the Shannon index in ecology and accounts for species richness and evenness?
ANSWER: DIVERSITY (also accept: SPECIES)
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice According to the theory of island biogeography, which of the following is true?
W) Extinction is likely to occur in larger islands
X) The closer an island is to the mainland, the fewer species it tends to have
Y) The equilibrium theory uses immigration and extinction rates to determine the diversity of an island
Z) The rescue effect suggests that lower immigration decreases the chance of extinction
ANSWER: Y) The equilibrium theory uses immigration and extinction rates to determine the diversity of an island
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following amino acid changes will affect the structure of the protein the least?
W) Replacing glycine with alanine
X) Replacing proline with arginine
Y) Replacing phenylalanine with tryptophan
Z) Replacing asparagine with glutamine
ANSWER: X) Replacing proline with arginine
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, indicate which of the following are NOT intermediates of the Krebs Cycle. (1) malate (2) α-ketoglutarate (3) acetate (4) benzoate (5) succinyl-CoA
ANSWER: 3,4 (also accept: ACETATE AND BENZOATE)
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer You are below 1500 m in the South Pacific Ocean. The water is cold, and you pick up some sediment. The sediment is fine and glassy. What type of sediment is it?
ANSWER: SILICEOUS OOZE
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer You are on the abyssal plain of an ocean. You pick up a brown lump of metal that has precipitated from the water. What is this formation?
ANSWER: MANGANESE NODULE
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What theory in quantum physics is debunked by Bell’s Theorem?
W) String Theory
X) Quantum Tunneling
Y) The existence of hidden variables
Z) Quantum Entanglement
ANSWER: Y) The existence of hidden variables
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A photon has a frequency of 1.00 × 1014 Hz. To the nearest hundredth decimal place, how many joules of energy does the photon have?
ANSWER: 6.63 × 10-20
9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the sum of the coefficients of the polynomial (x + 2)7?
W) 334
X) 2187
Y) 128
Z) 2338
ANSWER: X) 2187
9. BONUS Math Short Answer In how many ways can a 2 by 8 rectangle be tiled with 1 by 2 rectangles? Assume that configurations obtained by reflecting another configuration across a horizontal or vertical axis of symmetry are distinct.
ANSWER: 34
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the literal meaning of “halogens”?
W) choke-making
X) metal-making
Y) salt-making
Z) ore-making
ANSWER: Y) salt-making
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following four halogens in terms of increasing electron affinity. 1) Fluorine 2) Iodine 3) Chlorine 4) Bromine
ANSWER: 2,4,1,3 (also accept: IODINE, BROMINE, FLUORINE, CHLORINE)
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice If there is an inelastic collision, which of the following is true?
W) Both kinetic energy and linear momentum are conserved
X) Kinetic energy is conserved but linear momentum is not
Y) Linear momentum is conserved but kinetic energy is not
Z) Neither linear momentum and kinetic energy are conserved
ANSWER: Y) Linear momentum is conserved but kinetic energy is not
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer In an elastic collision, object 2 is at rest and has a mass of 3 kg. Object 1 has a mass of 5 kg and collides with a velocity of 5 m/s. What is the final velocity of object 1, in m/s?
ANSWER: 1.25 M/S
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the wavelength, in meters, of a sound wave that has a frequency of 3,500 hertz and a speed of 350 meters per second?
ANSWER: 0.1 METERS
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Given that the tension in a string is 20 N, and it has a mass of 1 kg and a length of 5 m, find the speed of the waves in the string, in m/s.
ANSWER: 10 M/S
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the alternating waves of contraction and relaxation in the smooth muscles lining the canal that enables food digestion even when lying down?
ANSWER: PERISTALSIS
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which leukocyte is the first responder and contains granules that have hydrolytic enzymes and antimicrobial enzymes?
W) Basophil
X) Eosinophil
Y) Neutrophil
Z) Monocyte
ANSWER: Y) Neutrophil
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Three tectonic plates meet at a triple junction. One stops spreading and a filled-graben system occurs. What is the name of this failed arm of a triple junction?
ANSWER: AULACOGEN
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, place the following in increasing amount of stress. (1) Recumbent Fold (2) Fault (3) Open Fold (4) Overturned Fold (5) Isoclinal Fold
ANSWER: 3,5,4,1,2 (also accept: OPEN FOLD, ISOCLINAL FOLD, OVERTURNED FOLD, RECUMBENT FOLD, FAULT)
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What type of radiation emits Helium isotopes?
W) Alpha Radiation
X) Beta Radiation
Y) Delta Radiation
Z) Gamma Radiation
ANSWER: W) Alpha Radiation
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer Radon-226 decays into Radon-222, an alpha particle, and what subatomic particle?
ANSWER: MUON
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following that would burn green when subjected to a flame test 1) Lithium 2) Barium 3) Calcium 4) Boron
ANSWER: 2 AND 4 (also accept: BARIUM AND BORON)
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer Given that the momentum of an electron is 2 × 10-10 kg m / s and rounding your answer to the nearest tenth, find the electron’s de Broglie wavelength.
ANSWER: 3.3 × 10-24 meters
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Two fair coins are tossed and at least one is a head. What is the probability that both coins are heads?
ANSWER: 1/3
17. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the remainder when 3x4 − 3x3 − 20x2 − 12 is divided by x+2?
ANSWER: -20
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What kind of nephrons have short loops of Henle and are most entirely confined to the renal cortex?
ANSWER: CORTICAL NEPHRONS

W) Formation of thrombin
X) Formation of fibrin
Y) Formation of antithrombin
Z) Formation of collagen fibers
ANSWER: X) Formation of fibrin

ANSWER: 150 MILLION
