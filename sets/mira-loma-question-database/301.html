1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In the process of meiosis, a cell is permanently transformed into ______, and cannot be divided again until fertilization.
W) Gonad
X) Diploid Germ Cell
Y) Gamete
Z) Somatic Cell
ANSWER: Y) Gamete
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer In 1858, which man completed the cell theory, by stating that all cells come from preexisting cells?
ANSWER: RUDOLF VIRCHOW
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A population that is known to be "not evolving", is in this type of equilibrium.
ANSWER: HARDY WEINBERG EQUILIBRIUM
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer This term is known to describe random change in the frequency of alleles, caused by the random sampling of a generation's genes during reproduction.
ANSWER: GENETIC DRIFT
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The process of removing an amine group from a molecule is known as
W) Deamination
X) Staudinger Reduction
Y) Amine Alkylation
Z) Amide Reduction
ANSWER: W) Deamination
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Give the name of the compound with a chemical formula of C6H5COH.
ANSWER: BENZALDEHYDE
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name hypothesis that states that the world and all of its biological and physical components are intertwined in a global system of homeostasis?
ANSWER: GAIA HYPOTHESIS
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term, in contrast to apoptosis, that is a form of mass cell death, but due to extensive cellular injury?
ANSWER: NECROSIS
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Glycolysis is the process by which glucose is converted into which compound?
W) ATP
X) NADH
Y) Pyruvate
Z) Glycerol
ANSWER: Y) Pyruvate
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Inside the chloroplasts, the stacked structures known as grana are comprised of this membrane in which light dependent reactions take place.
ANSWER: THYLAKOID
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When cells move towards an area of higher concentration of a specific chemical, then what type of chemotaxis is this?
ANSWER: POSITIVE
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name given to projections from the cell membranes which help the cell move, comprised mainly of cilia and flagella?
ANSWER: UNDULIPODIUM
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Where in the pancreas is insulin produced?
ANSWER: ISLETS OF LANGERHANS
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer When a body is unable to produce necessary amounts of insulin, then the person is diagnosed with which type of diabetes?
ANSWER: TYPE 1
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How many amino acids coded for in the standard genetic code?
W) 20
X) 21
Y) 22
Z) 23
ANSWER: W) 20
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What element does glutamine regularly donate to the body's anabolic processes?
ANSWER: NITROGEN
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name given to the change in growth movement of plants due to gravity?
ANSWER: GRAVITROPISM
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Nyctinasty is the stimuli in plants which causes it to move in response to what?
ANSWER: DARKNESS (also accept: NIGHT)
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Serotonin's main purpose to the human body is to
W) Regulate mood
X) Control muscle contraction
Y) Adjust sleeping habits
Z) Regulate intestinal movement
ANSWER: Z) Regulate intestinal movement
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer When used as a drug, what does norepinephrine do to a person's blood pressure?
ANSWER: INCREASES IT
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer ALS, a form of motor neuron disease, is commonly known as this, after the famous baseball player.
ANSWER: LOU GEHRIG'S DISEASE
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the fifth cranial nerve, which is responsible for facial sensation?
ANSWER: TRIGEMINAL NERVE
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the name of the period of time when the heart relaxes after contraction in preparation for refilling with circulating blood?
W) Atrial Diastole
X) Cardiac Diastole
Y) Ventricular Diastole
Z) Systole
ANSWER: X) Cardiac Diastole
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the technique in which a doctor mechanically widens a narrowed or obstructed blood vessel?
ANSWER: ANGIOPLASTY
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In the process of translation, this genetic unit is decoded to produce a specific polypeptide, based on the genetic code specified by the unit.
W) mRNA
X) tRNA
Y) rRNA
Z) tmRNA
ANSWER: W) mRNA
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer How many aromatic rings do the pyrimidine bases consist of?
ANSWER: 1
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The main protein that makes up connective tissue is
W) Collagen
X) Keratin
Y) Myosin
Z) Elastin
ANSWER: W) Collagen

W) Substrate
X) Tetramer
Y) Inhibitor
Z) Activator

W) Monoploid Number
X) Haploid Number
Y) Ploidy

16. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the structure formed before implantation, and is used to protect the embryoblast, and then will form the placenta?
