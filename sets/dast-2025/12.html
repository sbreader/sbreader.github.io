W) The N=N double bond attacks the electrophilic carbon of the alcohol, kicking off a hydroxide and stabilizing an intermediate to substitution.
X) The resonance stabilized anion formed after the binding of triphenylphosphine can deprotonate and thus intensify the nucleophile, in addition to deprotonating an unstable intermediate formed by the attack of the alcohol on the triphenylphosphine.
Y) DEAD acts as a heterogeneous catalyst, providing a solid surface for the reagents to bind to and facilitating atom transfer.
