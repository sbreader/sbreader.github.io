1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice If the plates of an isolated charged capacitor are pulled apart, then which of the following is true?
W) the potential difference between the plates increases
X) the potential difference between the plates decreases
Y) the potential difference between the plates does not change
Z) the potential difference between the plates first increases, then decreases
ANSWER: W) the potential difference between the plates increases
