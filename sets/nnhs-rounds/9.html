Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Careful measurements have shown that the mass of an atom is less than the sum of the masses of its protons, neutrons, and electrons. What is the term for this difference?
ANSWER: MASS DEFECT
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer What phenomenon causes two uncharged conducting plates to experience a small attractive force when placed extremely close to each other in a vacuum?
ANSWER: CASIMIR EFFECT
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice According to the ABC hypothesis of flower formation, if the C gene was knocked out, what would the order of flower parts from inside to outside be?
W) Carpels, stamens, petals, sepals
X) Carpels, stamens, stamens, carpels
Y) Sepals, petals, petals, sepals
Z) Sepals, carpels, carpels, sepals
ANSWER: Y) Sepals, petals, petals, sepals
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer On trees and other plants that exhibit secondary growth, small, raised areas on the periderm allow for living cells within the plant to exchange gases with the atmosphere. What are these structures called?
ANSWER: LENTICELS
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a concordant pluton that forms from more viscous magma to create a lens-shaped structure?
ANSWER: LACCOLITH
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the minimum wave steepness at which a wave will break?
W) 1/10
X) 1/7
Y) 1/5
Z) 1/4
ANSWER: X) 1/7
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following graphs does NOT pass through the third quadrant?
W) y = x3 + 5
X) y = 2x2 – 3
Y) y = -7x – 2
Z) y = -3x + 4
ANSWER: Z) y = -3x + 4
4. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the sample standard deviation of the set {1, 5, 6}?
ANSWER: √7
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What reaction converts alkenes or alkynes to alkanes through the addition of hydrogen gas?
ANSWER: HYDROGENATION
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer At very low temperatures, helium forms a superfluid, losing all viscosity. What temperature marks the transition between ordinary and superfluid helium?
ANSWER: LAMBDA POINT
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at the Joint Genome Institute are studying the shrub willow, which, like moths and some birds, uses what sex determination system in which the females, instead of the males, are heterogametic?
ANSWER: ZW
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at the Joint Genome Institute are studying the ability of filamentous fungi to degrade the cell wall of plants. Which of the following model organisms did researchers examine for this study?
W) Escherichia coli
X) Saccharomyces cerevisiae
Y) Neurospora crassa
Z) Caenorhabditis elegans
ANSWER: Y) Neurospora crassa
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A pipe with one closed and one open end has a length of 2 meters. In meters, what is the wavelength of the third harmonic?
ANSWER: 8/5
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer According to Grand Unified Theory, which two fundamental forces are the first to merge into one force at high temperatures?
ANSWER: ELECTROMAGNETIC AND WEAK FORCE (also accept: ELECTROWEAK FORCE)
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What type of nephron extends deep into the medulla and is essential for production of urine that is hyperosmotic to body fluids?
W) Peritubular
X) Henle
Y) Cortical
Z) Juxtamedullary
ANSWER: Z) Juxtamedullary
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What division of the autonomic nervous system corresponds with arousal and energy generation response?
ANSWER: SYMPATHETIC
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following planets undergoes retrograde motion?
W) Venus
X) Jupiter
Y) Saturn
Z) Neptune
ANSWER: W) Venus
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of unconformity is characterized by younger sedimentary rock overlaying older metamorphic or igneous rock?
ANSWER: NONCONFORMITY
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A right triangle has legs of length 20 and 21. What is the length of the median to the hypotenuse?
ANSWER: 29/2
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Triangle ABC has a right angle at B, AB = 3, and BC = 4. D is a point on side AC such that BD is an angle bisector, and M is the midpoint of AC. What is the length of MD?
ANSWER: 5/14
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Order the following four elements by increasing first ionization energy 1) Lithium 2) Beryllium 3) Sodium 4) Magnesium.
ANSWER: 3,1,4,2
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Diborane, B2H6, violates the octet rule by forming what kind of bonds between boron and hydrogen atoms?
ANSWER: 3 – CENTER 2 – ELECTRON BOND (also accept: BANANA BOND)
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Lab are studying how rotational superfluids transfer angular momentum. In particular, they were interested in how the fluids were able to transfer angular momentum in the absence of what force?
ANSWER: VISCOUS FORCE (also accept: FRICTION; FLUID FRICTION; INTERNAL FRICTION)
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Lab are studying how rotational superfluids transfer angular momentum. The scientists found that unlike typical fluids, superfluids do not create what small currents, allowing the atoms to remain in place?
ANSWER: EDDY
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the reduced mass of a system that consists of a 3 kilogram and a 7 kilogram mass?
ANSWER: 21/10 KILOGRAMS
13. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A stationary observer hears a siren approaching at a speed of 20 meters per second and hears a pitch of 1800 Hertz. Given that the speed of sound is 340 meters per second, approximately what frequency would a stationary observer hear from the same siren if the siren was moving away at 20 meters per second?
W) 1600 Hertz
X) 1700 Hertz
Y) 1900 Hertz
Z) 2000 Hertz
ANSWER: W) 1600 Hertz
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Beriberi is caused by a deficiency in what vitamin?
ANSWER: B1 (also accept: THIAMINE)
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What phenomenon is characterized by left-right asymmetry of the organs in the chest and abdomen?
ANSWER: SITUS INVERSUS (also accept: KARTAGENER’S SYNDROME)
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What class of minerals typically effervesces when a drop of hydrochloric acid reacts with its surface?
ANSWER: CARBONATES
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is FALSE regarding the Richter scale and the moment magnitude scale?
W) Both scales are logarithmic
X) The Wood-Anderson seismograph is the standard for the Richter scale
Y) The Richter scale is better at estimating the size of large earthquakes
Z) The moment magnitude scale can be calculated from field studies
ANSWER: Y) The Richter scale is better at estimating the size of large earthquakes
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Five fair, six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that the sum of the numbers rolled is a multiple of three?
ANSWER: 1/3
16. BONUS Math Short Answer A square is rotated 45° around its center, forming a new square. The intersection of the two squares is a regular octagon with side length 3. What is the side length of the original square?
ANSWER: 3 + 3√2
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three compounds that have the same empirical formula as formic acid 1) Acetic acid 2) Glucose 3) 1-propanol.
ANSWER: NONE
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer According to MO theory, what is the bond order of the diatomic ion N2-?
ANSWER: 2.5 (also accept: 5/2)
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Livermore National Lab are studying the production of acetaldehyde and hydrogen from ethanol using a silver catalyst at high temperatures. Identify all of the following three changes that would cause the equilibrium to shift towards the products 1) Increasing pressure 2) Adding more hydrogen 3) Adding helium.
ANSWER: NONE
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Livermore National Lab are studying the “neutron skin” found in many nuclei and in neutron stars. What limit gives the upper bound on the mass of a nonrotating neutron star, which, if exceeded, would lead to collapse into a black hole?
ANSWER: TOLMAN-OPPENHEIMER-VOLKOFF LIMIT (also accept: TOV LIMIT)
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What type of matter has a density and temperature between plasma and condensed matter?
ANSWER: WARM DENSE MATTER (also accept: WDM)
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true about PN-junctions 1) Doping a semiconductor with boron will result in a p-type semiconductor 2) A forward biased PN-junction has a built in electric field in the same direction as the battery-induced electric field 3) Doping a semiconductor with arsenic will result in a p-type semiconductor.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In human females, approximately how many days after the beginning of the menstrual flow phase does ovulation occur?
W) 7
X) 14
Y) 21
Z) 28
ANSWER: X) 14
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements about phage replication that are true 1) The host cell dies in the lysogenic cycle 2) Temperate phages exclusively replicate using the lytic cycle 3) A prophage is viral DNA that has been integrated into a bacterial chromosome.
ANSWER: 3 ONLY
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why the sidereal month is shorter than the synodic month?
W) The sidereal month fails to take into account the orbit of the moon
X) The sidereal month fails to take into account the rotation of the earth
Y) The synodic month fails to take into account the orbit of the earth around the sun
Z) The synodic month fails to take into account earth’s axial tilt
ANSWER: Y) The synodic month fails to take into account the orbit of the earth around the sun
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following four rocks by increasing crystallization temperature 1) Olivine 2) Quartz 3) Muscovite 4) Biotite.
ANSWER: 2,3,4,1
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If rotations and reflections are considered equivalent, then how many ways are there to pick two of the six faces of a white cube and paint them blue?
ANSWER: 2

X) 44

Z) 58

W) Neon
