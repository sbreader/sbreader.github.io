Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In eukaryotes, which enzyme proofreads while DNA replication takes place?
ANSWER: DNA POLYMERASE (also accept: DNA POLYMERASE DELTA; DNA POLYMERASE EPSILON)
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the general name for a point mutation in which a single nucleotide change results in a codon that codes for a different amino acid?
ANSWER: missense mutation
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of RNA is most common in the cell?
ANSWER: rRNA
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of these is not a stop codon?
W) UAA
X) UGG
Y) UGA
Z) UAG
ANSWER: X) UGG
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What was the first codon-amino acid pair to be identified? (Give both the codon and the amino acid)
ANSWER: UUU and phenylalanine
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which amino acid would constitute a disulfide bridge?
ANSWER: Cysteine
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the cellular machinery that removes introns?
ANSWER: Spliceosome
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which process is in play when we respond less strongly to repeated stimuli over time?
W) Sensory Adaptation
X) Habituation
Y) Accomodation
Z) Maturation
ANSWER: X) Habituation

W) Learning
X) Possession
Y) Acquisition
Z) Incubation
ANSWER: Y) Acquisition
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer If a reaction with a Delta G of +3.4 kcal/mol is coupled with ATP hydrolysis, what is the net Delta G?
ANSWER: -3.9 kcal/mol
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The strongest known biological oxidizing agent plays a key role in photosynthesis. What is it called?
ANSWER: P680
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What was the phage that Hershey and Chase used in their experiments, and what two radioactive substances did they tag it with?
ANSWER: T2, S35 and P32 (do not accept: Sulfur and Phosphorus)

19. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which cytoskeletal element functions in cleavage furrow formation?
