1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A container is divided into two compartments, A and B, by a membrane permeable to only water. Adding which of the following to compartment A would lead to the greatest decrease in its solute potential?
W) 1.5 molar NaCl
X) 2.0 molar MgCl2
Y) 3.5 molar C6H12O6
Z) 1.0 molar Al2(SO4)3
ANSWER: X) 2.0 molar MgCl2
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What molecular biology technique uses fluorescent nucleic acid probes that hybridize to their complementary sequences?
ANSWER: FISH (also accept: fluorescence in situ hybridization)
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Which one of Euclid’s original postulates, when not used, leads to non-euclidean geometries?
ANSWER: Parallel Postulate (also accept: Euclid’s 5th Postulate)
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the hundreds digit of 11 to the power of 2024.
ANSWER: 8
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The energy of a 1D particle in a box is proportional to what power of the length of the box?
ANSWER: -2
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A sprinkler is designed such that it shoots a uniform amount of water at equal velocity from all angles. Given that there is linear air drag with coefficient b=0.5, after a long time, which of the following describes the change in the area density of the water on the ground as you move radially outward from the center to the edge of the water spot formed?
W) Increases
X) Decreases
Y) Increases then decreases
Z) Decreases then increases
ANSWER: W) Increases
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a geologic structure where the strata have a uniform dip in the same direction and approximately the same angle?
ANSWER: Monocline
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes a volcano that erupts only once across its lifetime?
ANSWER: Monogenetic
5. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the benefit of a prefix sum array?
W) It allows for O(n) queries after an O(1) preprocessing step
X) It allows for O(log n) queries after an O(n) preprocessing step
Y) It allows for O(1) queries after an O(n) preprocessing step
Z) It allows for O(1) queries after an O(n2) preprocessing step
ANSWER: Y) It allows for O(1) queries after an O(n) preprocessing step
5. BONUS Energy Short Answer Akhil is performing an amortized analysis on an algorithm. Which of the following is true about his results? 1) The big O time complexity can be less than the amortized time complexity 2) If he finds the time complexity for processing an entire sequence to be O(n2 log n), the time complexity for a single element is O(log n) 3) He would find the time complexity of appending to a dynamic array to be O(1)
ANSWER: 3 ONLY
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following four gases in terms of increasing a parameter values for the Van Der Waals equation at STP. 1) SO2 2) H2 3) NH3 4) CH4
ANSWER: 2,4,1,3
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer McNair is analyzing a compound with chemical formula C6H14N2O2. Which of the following four sets of functional groups could be present in the compound? 1) One aromatic ring and one ether group 2) One hydroxyl group and one nitro group 3) One ester group and two amino groups 4) One carbon-carbon double bond and one carbon-carbon triple bond

7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Ryan is looking at a leaf cross-section under the microscope. He sees some elongated directly green cells under the waxy cuticle. What is the name for these cells?
ANSWER: Palisade Mesophyll
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What structure in the root endodermis of plants contains suberin, allowing for better control of the transportation of water and inorganic salts between the cortex and vascular bundle?
ANSWER: Casparian Strip
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When a star starts burning hydrogen in its core, it begins its life on the main sequence of the HR diagram. What is the name given to the point where the star first appears on the main sequence?
ANSWER: Zero Age Main Sequence (also accept: ZAMS)
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 statements that are true regarding star clusters 1) Globular clusters generally contain younger stars than open stars 2) Stars in an open cluster are strongly gravitationally bound 3) Open clusters are typically found in the disk of a galaxy.

10. BONUS Physics Short Answer William is playing with balls. They are attached to springs in series, constrained to move in a circle. ANSWER: Normal Modes

ANSWER: 2
12. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice An RLC circuit has resistance 8 ohms, capacitance 10 farads, and inductance 20 henries. Which of the following graphs best describes the current in the system as a function of time?

Y) Sinusoidal and exponentially decaying

13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer If the dry adiabatic lapse rate is less than the atmospheric lapse rate, but the wet adiabatic lapse rate is greater than the atmospheric lapse rate, what atmospheric condition is occurring?
ANSWER: Conditional instability
