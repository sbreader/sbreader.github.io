1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Ryan is trying to solve Nesbitt's Inequality, which states that a/(b+c) + b/(a+c) + c/(a+b) is greater than or equal to 3/2, for positive reals a, b, c. Before applying Jensen's inequality, Ryan sets a + b + c equal to 1, and substitutes it in the denominators of his equation. This is only possible because scaling each of a, b, and c at the same time will have no effect on the inequality itself, because every term is of the same degree. What is the name for such inequalities?
ANSWER: HOMOGENOUS
