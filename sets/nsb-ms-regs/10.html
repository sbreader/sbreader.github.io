1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the square of the binomial x – 11?
ANSWER: x2-22x + 121
1. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the square root of the expression 12 minus 3 plus 4 times 10?
ANSWER: 7
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the primary reason for the movement of mass downslope during erosion?
W) Friction
X) Electricity
Y) Gravity
Z) Buoyancy
ANSWER: Y) Gravity
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The main asteroid belt is located between the orbits of what two planets?
ANSWER: MARS AND JUPITER
3. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following steps should NOT be used in the event of an electrical fire?
W) Extinguish with water
X) Use a carbon dioxide fire extinguisher
Y) Turn off the power source
Z) Call the fire department
ANSWER: W) Extinguish with water
3. BONUS Energy Short Answer What metric prefix is equivalent to one trillion?
ANSWER: TERA
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Dry ice is the solid form of what substance, which is typically found in gaseous phase at standard temperatures and pressures?
ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE
4. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice In the radioactive decay of uranium, what is the final stable element?
W) Thorium
X) Lead
Y) Plutonium
Z) Radium
ANSWER: X) Lead
5. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Utilities distribute electrical power at high voltage from generating plants to industrial and residential customers through a network of wires. Which of the following is the term for these wires?
W) Transmission lines
X) Step-up lines
Y) Grid transformers
Z) Power cables
ANSWER: W) Transmission lines
5. BONUS Energy Short Answer Order the following three states from least to greatest in terms of coal production 1) Pennsylvania 2) California 3) West Virginia.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following processes does not add carbon dioxide to the atmosphere?
W) Photosynthesis
X) Respiration
Y) Combustion
Z) Carbonic acid decomposition
ANSWER: W) Photosynthesis

X) Extinct

X) Two convex lenses

W) Hydrogen
X) Oxygen

Z) Water
