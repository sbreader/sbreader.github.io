1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a scale of earthquake intensity?
W) Saffir-Simpson
X) Fujita
Y) EF-scale
Z) Moment magnitude
ANSWER: Z) Moment magnitude
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for subsoil that remains frozen year round?
ANSWER: PERMAFROST
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the study of spiders?
W) Arachnology
X) Dendrochronology
Y) Planktology
Z) Lepidopterology
ANSWER: W) Arachnology
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a high-altitude cloud type?
W) Altostratus
X) Stratocumulus
Y) Stratus
Z) Cirrocumulus
ANSWER: Z) Cirrocumulus
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is formed when three atoms combine to produce a single unit of a larger substance?
W) Nucleus
X) Mixture
Y) Solution
Z) Molecule
ANSWER: Z) Molecule
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following four quantities that are considered vector quantities 1) Mass 2) Velocity 3) Speed 4) Acceleration.
ANSWER: 2 AND 4
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What infectious particles consist of nucleic acids packaged into a protein coat?
ANSWER: VIRUS
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Eukaryotic and prokaryotic cells both contain:
W) Cytoplasmic organelles
X) Nuclear membrane
Y) Cell membrane
Z) Chromosomal proteins
ANSWER: Y) Cell membrane
5. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What are the two gases used to power the first generation fuel cell cars?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN AND OXYGEN (also accept: H2 AND O2; H AND O)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer To the nearest ten percent, what percent of the sun's energy that hits the Earth is reflected back into space?
ANSWER: 30
6. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the distance of the point (2, 3) from the origin?
W) √5
X) √13
Y) 5
Z) 13
ANSWER: X) √13
6. BONUS Math Short Answer Given the linear function f(x) = 3x + b, if f(-1) = 7, what is f(5)?
ANSWER: 25
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists were able to determine that the outer core of the Earth is a liquid because what seismic waves cannot pass through it?
ANSWER: SECONDARY WAVES (also accept: S-WAVES)
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following features is created by a volcano?
W) Fjord
X) Caldera
Y) Moraine
Z) Esker
ANSWER: X) Caldera
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In a vacuum, all photons have the same:
W) Speed
X) Frequency
Y) Wavelength
Z) Energy
ANSWER: W) Speed
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the efficiency of an engine that consumes 7500 Joules of energy to load a box that requires 4500 Joules of work?
ANSWER: 60%
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What astronomical measurement is analogous to latitude?
W) Declination
X) Right ascension
Y) Conjunction
Z) Superposition
ANSWER: W) Declination
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Halite is composed of what two elements?
ANSWER: SODIUM AND CHLORINE (also accept: Na AND Cl)
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A father's age is twice that of his son. Expressed as a fraction, what is the ratio of the son's age to the father's age?
ANSWER: 1/2
10. BONUS Math Short Answer If x and y are positive, simplify the following expression: the fraction with numerator 4x5y-1 and denominator 3x-4y2.
ANSWER: (4x9)/(3y3)
11. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Most gasoline in the United States is E10, meaning that 10% of its content is made up of what?
ANSWER: ETHANOL
11. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following countries generates the highest percentage of its electricity by nuclear power?
W) Japan
X) France
Y) United States
Z) Russia
ANSWER: X) France
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By what process does a mother cell split into two genetically identical daughter cells?
ANSWER: MITOSIS
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of plant cell tissue is considered most important for structural support?
W) Mesophyll
X) Parenchyma
Y) Collenchyma
Z) Sclerenchyma
ANSWER: Z) Sclerenchyma
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer For what value of x does the graph of the function y = -5x + 15 cross the x-axis?
ANSWER: 3
13. BONUS Math Short Answer What two numbers have a sum of 36 and a product of 323?
ANSWER: 17 AND 19
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of structures both contain linear DNA?
W) Animal cell nucleus and chloroplast
X) Animal cell nucleus and plant cell nucleus
Y) Fungal cell nucleus and mitochondria
Z) Mitochondria and bacteria
ANSWER: X) Animal cell nucleus and plant cell nucleus
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What green pigment gives plants their color?
ANSWER: CHLOROPHYLL
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following variables is most responsible for lake stratification?
W) Wind
X) Biological activity
Y) Temperature
Z) Current
ANSWER: Y) Temperature
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the days during which the Sun is furthest south or north of the equator?
ANSWER: SOLSTICES
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following causes the Coriolis Effect?
W) Mesospheric winds
X) Strong electric field gradients
Y) Tidal forces
Z) The Earth's easterly rotation
ANSWER: Z) The Earth's easterly rotation
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer What term is used to describe the ever-increasing amount of "disorder" in the universe?
ANSWER: ENTROPY
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A cubic centimeter of water has a mass of almost exactly one of which of the following?
W) Milliliter
X) Kilogram
Y) Gram
Z) Fluid ounce
ANSWER: Y) Gram
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the chemical formula of magnesium nitrate?
ANSWER: Mg(NO3)2
18. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following materials yields the greatest percentage reduction in energy to manufacture when it is made from recycled material rather than from raw materials?
W) Aluminum
X) Glass
Y) Newsprint
Z) Plastics
ANSWER: W) Aluminum
18. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the term for the total energy used to manufacture a finished product, including the energy to extract raw materials from nature, plus the energy used in primary and secondary manufacturing activities?
ANSWER: EMBODIED ENERGY (also accept: EMBEDDED ENERGY)
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Water is formed when oxygen and hydrogen undergo what process?
W) Homogeneous mixture
X) Hydrophobic reaction
Y) Chemical reaction
Z) Spontaneous condensation
ANSWER: Y) Chemical reaction
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many bonds exists between two nitrogen atoms in dinitrogen gas?
ANSWER: THREE
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In how many different orders can you play 5 songs once each?
ANSWER: 120
20. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following equations represents a line with slope of 7/5?
W) 5x + 7y – 11 = 0
X) 7x = -5y – 12
Y) 7x – 5y = 14
Z) 7y/5 = x + 4
ANSWER: Y) 7x – 5y = 14
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Sand dollars, sea stars, sea cucumbers, and sea urchins all belong to what phyla?
ANSWER: ECHINODERMATA
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer Rubisco can use oxygen as a substrate in a process known as what?
ANSWER: PHOTORESPIRATION
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A lunar eclipse occurs when:
W) The moon casts a shadow on the earth
X) The earth casts a shadow on the moon
Y) The earth, sun, and moon form a 90° angle
Z) The moon casts a shadow on the sun
ANSWER: X) The earth casts a shadow on the moon
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Alfred Wegener proposed what theory, which suggested that 200 million years ago, a supercontinent named Pangaea existed?
ANSWER: CONTINENTAL DRIFT
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for exceptionally bright stars that are up to 1000 times the sun's diameter?
ANSWER: SUPERGIANTS
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Most of the convection that occurs in the Earth occurs in what layer?
ANSWER: MANTLE
