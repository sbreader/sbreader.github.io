1. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer The word modem is derived from what two words?
ANSWER: MODULATOR-DEMODULATOR (also accept: MODULATE-DEMODULATE; MODULATOR DEMODULATOR)
2. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the binary representation of 15.5 base 10?
ANSWER: 1111.1
3. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Give the FORTRAN field description for the following INTEGER: 6789
ANSWER: I4
4. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the octal representation of 19 decimal?
ANSWER: 23 (also accept: 23 BASE 8)
5. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the binary representation of 65 octal?
ANSWER: 110101
6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the octal representation of A5 hexadecimal?
ANSWER: 245
7. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the octal sum of 064321 octal and 016477 octal?
ANSWER: 103020
8. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following reads a source program one statement at a time and causes the execution of that statement?
W) assembler
X) compiler
Y) lister
Z) interpreter
ANSWER: Z) interpreter
9. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Suppose that a byte is 8 bits. If one character is represented by one byte of storage, then how many different characters could be represented using one byte per character?
ANSWER: 256 (also accept: 28)
10. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer If b=5, c=4, d=2, and e=3 and all are integer variables, what integer value is obtained for the following expression when programming in BASIC? b + c*d**e
ANSWER: 37
11. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Subtract in base 2: 1011-110
ANSWER: 101
12. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Let b(1) = 1, b(2) = 2 and b(3) = 3. What polynomial does the following FORTRAN routine evaluate? F = b(1) DO I = 2,3 F = F * X + b(I) END DO
ANSWER: x2 + 2x + 3 (also accept: F = x2 + 2x + 3)
13. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Place the following computer languages in order from the lowest level (i.e., most primitive and most hardware dependent) to the highest level(i.e.,least primitive and least hardware dependent): microcode, BASIC, assembler-language, machine language.
ANSWER: MICROCODE, MACHINE-LANGUAGE, ASSEMBLER-LANGUAGE, BASIC
14. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Place the following programming languages in chronological order with regard to their first introduction to the computing public: BASIC, ALGOL, Pascal, FORTRAN
ANSWER: FORTRAN, ALGOL, BASIC, PASCAL
15. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the largest positive decimal value which can be represented by six binary digits (bits)?
ANSWER: 63
16. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following terms refers to the use of a computer to create, store, revise, and produce text:
W) teleprocessing
X) word-processing
Y) time-sharing
Z) voice-synthesis
ANSWER: X) word-processing
17. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice A computer program can be written to play tic-tac-toe in a manner such that the program never loses the same game situation more than one time. In which of the following areas of computer science would this program be of interest:
W) operating systems
X) computability
Y) artificial intelligence
Z) optimization
ANSWER: Y) artificial intelligence
18. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following computers would implement the method of processing in which one operation is completed before the next operation is initiated?
W) analog
X) digital
Y) asynchronous
Z) synchronous
ANSWER: Y) asynchronous
19. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following computers represents variables by physical analogies?
W) analog
X) digital
Y) asynchronous
Z) synchronous
ANSWER: W) analog
20. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What is the process of filling internal storage of a computer with information from auxiliary or external storage?
W) compile
X) link
Y) load
Z) assemble
ANSWER: Y) load
21. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice The algebra concerned with two-state variables was named for which of the following persons?
W) George Boole
X) Blaise Pascal
Y) Issac Newton
Z) Albert Einstein
ANSWER: W) George Boole
22. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Place the following in order from the smallest unit of memory to the largest: block, byte, bit, word
ANSWER: BIT, BYTE, WORD, BLOCK
23. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is characteristic of the first generation of digital computers?
W) vacuum tubes
X) transistors
Y) semi-conductors
Z) chips
ANSWER: W) vacuum tubes
24. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What are the 4 major divisions in a COBOL program?
ANSWER: IDENTIFICATION, ENVIRONMENT, DATA, PROCEDURE
25. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What are graphs of mathematical sets called?
ANSWER: VENN DIAGRAMS
26. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Of the following men, who co-authored the first book on programming?
W) Maurice V. Wilkes
X) John von Neumann
Y) Thomas J. Watson
Z) J. Presper Eckert
ANSWER: W) Maurice V. Wilkes
27. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer The CPU consists of what two units:
ANSWER: ARITHMETIC/LOGIC UNIT AND CONTROL UNIT (also accept: ALU AND CONTROL UNIT)
28. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the material from which most micro-electronic chips are made?
ANSWER: SILICON
29. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer How many nibbles are in a byte?
ANSWER: TWO (also accept: 2)
30. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the 10's complement of 45?
ANSWER: 55
31. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice A COBOL source program has 4 divisions. Which of the following divisions contains the equipment configuration?
W) Identification
X) Environment
Y) Data
Z) Procedure
ANSWER: X) Environment
32. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the radix of an octal number?
ANSWER: 8
33. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What FORTRAN symbol represents a BLANK field?
W) the letter X
X) the letter I
Y) the letter E
Z) the letter F
ANSWER: W) the letter X
34. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Give the FORTRAN field description for the following: -123.45
ANSWER: F7.2
35. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer In a FORTRAN program, I is an integer. What is the value of I after the following FORTRAN statements are executed? I=1 I = 2.5 * I I=I+1
ANSWER: 3
36. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the numerical value of I defined by the following FORTRAN statement: I = (1*2*3)**3
ANSWER: 216 (also accept: I = 216)
37. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT characteristic of an operating system. An operating system:
W) is a resource manager
X) is a library of utility programs
Y) defines the "user interface"
Z) shares the hardware among users
ANSWER: X) is a library of utility programs
38. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice The offsetting of the tremendous variation in I/O speeds for various devices by the use of a device of intermediate speed is known as:
W) unbundling
X) channeling
Y) spooling
Z) loading
ANSWER: Y) spooling
39. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice The name given to a special-purpose computer devoted to handling I/O independently of the main processor of the computer system is:
W) loader
X) channel
Y) track
Z) bundler
ANSWER: X) channel
40. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following techniques is a hardware technique used in high performance computer systems to exploit certain types of parallelism in instruction processing?
W) pipelining
X) networking
Y) multiprocessing
Z) multitasking
ANSWER: W) pipelining
41. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice In the CPU, the "instruction register" holds the instruction which:
W) was previously executed
X) is CURRENTLY being executed
Y) is the next instruction to be executed
Z) contains no instruction information
ANSWER: X) is CURRENTLY being executed
42. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer The term COBOL is an acronym for what?
ANSWER: COMMON BUSINESS ORIENTED LANGUAGE
43. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Second generation computers replaced vacuum tubes with what electronic device?
ANSWER: TRANSISTOR
44. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer The following equation is found in a program written in BASIC: y = 2-A*B What is the first mathematical manipulation carried out by the computer?
ANSWER: MULTIPLICATION (also accept: MULTIPLICATION OF A AND B)
45. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer The following equation is found in a program written in BASIC: y = 6-A*B/C What is the SECOND mathematical operation that will be carried out by the computer?
ANSWER: DIVISION (also accept: DIVISION BY C)
46. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer The following equation is found in a program written in BASIC: y = A*(d-2). What mathematical operation will the computer perform first?
ANSWER: SUBTRACTION (also accept: D-2)
47. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the ones complement of the following 4-bit string: 1101
ANSWER: 0010
48. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Determine the two's complement of the following 4 bit string: 0010
ANSWER: 1110
49. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Write the FORTRAN expression: a + b*c in Reverse Polish.
ANSWER: bc*a+ (also accept: abc*+)
50. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What does the acronym LISP stand for?
ANSWER: LIST PROCESSING
51. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the only data structure in the programming language LISP?
ANSWER: A LIST
52. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer The floppy diskette is used as an external storage device by most microcomputers. In a single-sided, double-density 5-and 1/4 inch diskette, storage locations consist of 40 narrow concentric ring-shaped areas. What is the standard term for the 40 ring-shaped areas?
ANSWER: TRACKS
53. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Give the value of the largest integer that can be written as an 8-bit binary number.
ANSWER: 255 (also accept: 28-1)
54. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer In BASIC, one might commonly begin a loop with the statement: FOR I = 1 TO 5 What statement would be used to end this loop?
ANSWER: NEXT I (also accept: NEXT)
55. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer The data-transfer rate through a modem is often described in terms of this four-letter name. Give me this name.
ANSWER: BAUD

60. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What are lines of computer code that are transmitted to your computer without your knowledge and are capable of such things as slowing down your operations, filling up memory space and distorting your programs called?
ANSWER: VIRUSES (also accept: VIRUS; WORMS; TROJAN HORSE)
61. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer You are programming in BASIC on your PC. What statement should be the last line of your program?
ANSWER: END

ANSWER: -5 TO +4 (also accept: -5 TO 4)
63. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer You are programming in BASIC on your PC. You would like to close all open files and force all file buffers in memory to write to disk. What command would you use?
ANSWER: RESET (also accept: CLOSE)
64. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer When programming in BASIC, what statement reinitializes the sequence of random numbers that are generated by the RND function?
ANSWER: RANDOMIZE
65. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer When programming in BASIC you encounter the following lines of code: V = FIX(-5.5) Print V When you run the program, what value will the computer print for V?

66. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What 3-letter statement is used in a BASIC program to allocate storage space for an array?

67. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer A BASIC program executes the following statements in order: A = 1; B = 2; IF A > 1 THEN A = 10; IF B > 1 THEN A = 100; A = A + 2*B. What is the final value of A?
