4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Consider the function f(x), which has negative slope and is concave down in the closed interval [-2, 5]. The value of the integral of f(x) from -2 to 5 as approximated by a left Riemann sum is:

Z) An underestimate for large steps and an overestimate for small ones
