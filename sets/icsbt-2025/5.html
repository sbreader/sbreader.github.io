1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A toy submarine moves very slowly horizontally through a very large sealed fish tank with an incompressible fluid. The propeller of the submarine pushes a stream of the fluid with speed v. If the pressure of the fluid in the tank is P, which of the following describes the pressure in the stream?
W) Less than P/2
X) Less than P but greater than P/2
Y) Greater than P
Z) Equal to P
ANSWER: Z) Equal to P
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer As temperature approaches infinity what law states that the heat capacity at constant pressure and volume of solids approaches a constant value?
ANSWER: Dulong-petit Law
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals commonly has trace amounts of uranium and thorium, which helps it play an important role in radioactive dating?
W) Andalusite
X) Dolomite
Y) Zircon
Z) Aragonite
ANSWER: Y) Zircon

ANSWER: 2,3,1

9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice You hook up circuits A and B to an alternating voltage source. You then attach an audio amplifier of high input impedance in parallel to the circuits. Circuit A has a resistor in series with a capacitor, and Circuit B has a resistor in series with an inductor. Which of the following best describes the function of circuits A and B?
W) Circuit A is a low-pass filter and Circuit B is a high-pass filter
X) Circuit A is a high-pass filter and Circuit B is a band-pass filter
Y) Circuit A is a high-pass filter and Circuit B is a low-pass filter
Z) Circuit A is a band-pass filter and Circuit B is a high-pass filter
ANSWER: Y) Circuit A is a high-pass filter and Circuit B is a low-pass filter
