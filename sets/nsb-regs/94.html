1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is 1152?
ANSWER: 13,225
1. BONUS Math Short Answer On a calculus pop quiz, 15% of the students scored 6 points, 40% scored 7 points, 35% scored 8 points, and the rest scored 9 points. What is the average score, rounded to the nearest tenth, on the quiz?
ANSWER: 7.4
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What gas law exclusively states that, at constant volume, the pressure of an ideal gas is directly proportional to its absolute temperature?
ANSWER: GAY-LUSSAC'S LAW
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A certain hydrocarbon has the chemical formula C5H10. Identify all of the following three classes of molecules that it could be 1) Alkene 2) Cyclo-alkane 3) Cyclo-alkene.
ANSWER: 1, 2
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Princeton Plasma Physics Lab scientists are studying the rotational profile in the convective zone of the Sun. This rotational gradient contrasts with the uniformly-rotating zone underneath, which is dominated by what type of heat transfer?
ANSWER: RADIATION (also accept: RADIATIVE)
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers from Georgia State University used a supercomputer at Oak Ridge National Lab to develop an integrative model of the transcription preinitiation complex. What is the most common eukaryotic promoter DNA element over which the preinitiation complex forms?
ANSWER: TATA BOX (also accept: GOLDBERG-HOGNESS BOX)
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A circuit is connected to an eight-volt battery which puts out a current of four amperes. In ohms, what is the overall resistance of this circuit?
ANSWER: 2

ANSWER: 9

X) Boreal forest

Z) Tundra

ANSWER: STREPTOCOCCUS (do not accept: STREPTOCOCCI)

W) Scandium
X) Arsenic
Y) Molybdenum
Z) Tin
ANSWER: X) Arsenic

18. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at SLAC National Accelerator Lab are studying what happens when a material is suddenly demagnetized. If a metallic wire were wrapped around the material being demagnetized, what would occur in the wire?

Z) A direct current would be induced in the wire
