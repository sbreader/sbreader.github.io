1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the expected bond order of the bridging three-center B-H-B bonds in diborane?
ANSWER: 0.5
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following would be the expected spontaneity of the dihydroxylation of ethylene into ethylene glycol?
W) Spontaneous at all temperatures
X) Spontaneous at low temperatures
Y) Spontaneous at high temperatures
Z) Nonspontaneous at all temperatures
ANSWER: Y) Spontaneous at high temperatures
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Peptide Tyrosine Tyrosine, commonly known as PYY hormone, acts primarily against which hormone in the human body?
ANSWER: Ghrelin
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which section of the small intestine is known for being one of the parts of the human body that secrete the most PYY hormone?
ANSWER: Ileum
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the main mechanism of formation for tropospheric ozone?
W) H2O splits into H2 and O by ionosphere-to-ground electrolysis, then O and O2 recombine into ozone
X) NO2 splits into NO and O by high energy radiation, then O and O2 recombines into ozone
Y) O2 molecules combine by high energy radiation, releasing ozone and O
Z) O2 splits into two O radicals by high energy radiation, then the O radicals combine with O2 molecules into ozone
ANSWER: X) NO2 splits into NO and O by high energy radiation, then O and O2 recombines into ozone
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Evaluate the derivative with respect to x of 2 sin(x) + x2.
ANSWER: 2x + 2 cos(x)
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the definite integral from 0 to π/10 of cos5(10x) sin(10x) dx.
ANSWER: 0
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the process of muon-catalyzed fusion?
W) High energy muons strike hydrogen gas, which is enough energy to break the Coulomb barrier between different molecules, leading to fusion of the hydrogen
X) A stream of muons strikes solid hydrogen, replacing the electrons within the frozen hydrogen, which significantly reduces the electromagnetic repulsion and leads to fusion of the nuclei
Y) A powerful magnetic field cycles high energy muons in a torus, which replaces the electrons within the hydrogen molecules and leads to fusion of the nuclei
Z) Hot hydrogen plasma is bombarded with muons, which drives out the electrons by repulsion, leading to fusion of the nuclei
ANSWER: X) A stream of muons strikes solid hydrogen, replacing the electrons within the frozen hydrogen, which significantly reduces the electromagnetic repulsion and leads to fusion of the nuclei
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A binary star system has two stars of 4 solar masses and 6 solar masses. If the distance between the stars is 4 AU, then to two significant figures, find the period of the stars, in years.
ANSWER: 2.5 years
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a characteristic seen in the alimentary canal but not the gastrovascular cavity?
W) Digestion
X) One opening
Y) Stepwise digestion
Z) Radial symmetry
ANSWER: Y) Stepwise digestion
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Gingivitis, a non-destructive disease that causes inflammation of the gums, is caused mainly by what type of bacterial consortium?
ANSWER: Biofilm (also accept: Plaque)
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What phenomenon occurs when electrons in a bonding sigma orbital delocalize into adjacent antibonding sigma or pi orbitals, resulting in a shortening of the sigma bond length?
ANSWER: Hyperconjugation
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Give the expected magnetism of F2-, F2+, and F22+ respectively.
ANSWER: F2- is paramagnetic, F2+ is paramagnetic, F22+ is paramagnetic
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The minerals ejected by most undersea hydrothermal vents are mostly:
W) phyllosilicates
X) carbonates
Y) inosilicates
Z) sulfides
ANSWER: Z) sulfides
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Pillow basalts of the Crescent Formation in western Washington have been metamorphosed by deep-sea hydrothermal activity, then later uplifted onto land. Which of the following metamorphic facies would most likely be encountered in this low grade metabasalt?
W) Zeolite to low-greenschist
X) Amphibolite
Y) Hornfels

13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT characteristic of tidal tails?
W) They form from the tidal interaction of two galaxies
