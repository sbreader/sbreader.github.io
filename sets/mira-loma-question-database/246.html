1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The plasma membrane is built from...
W) Lipids and carbohydrates
X) Lipids and proteins
Y) Lipids and lipoproteins
Z) Lipids and water
ANSWER: X) Lipids and proteins
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The lipids in the plasma membrane are chiefly phospholipids like...
W) Phosphatidyl ethanolamine and lipoproteins
X) Phosphatidyl ethanolamine and cholesterol
Y) Lipoproteins and sphingolipids
Z) Lipoproteins and cholesterol
ANSWER: X) Phosphatidyl ethanolamine and cholesterol
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer All G-protein coupled receptors each span the plasma membrane how many times?
ANSWER: 7
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Receptors of peptide hormones and odors are what kind of receptors?
ANSWER: G-PROTEIN COUPLED RECEPTORS
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which one of the following statements about transmembrane proteins is false?
W) The portion within the lipid bilayer is hydrophobic
X) The portion within the lipid bilayer is usually an alpha helix
Y) The portion projecting out of the lipid bilayer is hydrophilic
Z) The portion projecting out of the lipid bilayer is a lipoprotein
ANSWER: Z) The portion projecting out of the lipid bilayer is a lipoprotein
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer The portion of transmembrane proteins projecting out of the lipid bilayer is usually a...
ANSWER: GLYCOPROTEIN
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How are peripheral membrane proteins usually attached to the lipid bilayer?
W) Covalently to phospholipids
X) Covalently to protruding portions of integral membrane proteins
Y) Non-covalently to phospholipids
Z) Non-covalently to protruding portions of integral membrane proteins
ANSWER: Z) Non-covalently to protruding portions of integral membrane proteins
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the main purpose of glycoproteins in the plasma membrane?
ANSWER: CELL RECOGNITION
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not true of cholesterol?
W) Its alcohol group faces outward
X) It reduces membrane fluidity at moderate temperature
Y) It increases membrane fluidity at low temperatures
Z) It can serve as a receptor when bonded to a protein
ANSWER: Z) It can serve as a receptor when bonded to a protein
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of lipids preserves membrane fluidity?
ANSWER: UNSATURATED
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In a transmembrane protein, which terminus is located on the extracellular side of the plasma membrane?
ANSWER: N-TERMINUS
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a function of membrane proteins?
W) Enzymatic activity
X) Gene expression
Y) Intercellular joining
Z) Extracellular attachment
ANSWER: X) Gene expression
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not required for bioluminescence?
W) Energy
X) Luciferin
Y) Luciferate
Z) Luciferase
ANSWER: Y) Luciferate
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The Law of Conservation of Energy is described by which law of thermodynamics?
ANSWER: 1st LAW OF THERMODYNAMICS
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which law of thermodynamics explains why organisms cannot cycle their energy infinitely?
ANSWER: 2nd LAW OF THERMODYNAMICS
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the case in which a protein's function at one site is affected by the binding of a regulatory molecule to a separate site?
ANSWER: ALLOSTERIC REGULATION
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which enzymatic phenomenon is portrayed by the following example: when oxygen binds to the first oxygen-binding site on a hemoglobin molecule, the affinity for oxygen on the other three sites increases?
ANSWER: COOPERATIVITY
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Complete the analogy: negative feedback is to feedback inhibition as positive feedback is to…?
ANSWER: ALLOSTERIC ACTIVATION
10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following conditions does not denature enzymes?
W) Radiation
X) Low pH
Y) High pH
Z) High temperature
ANSWER: W) Radiation
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not one of the three main kinds of cellular work?
W) Transport work
X) Biological work
Y) Chemical work
Z) Mechanical work
ANSWER: X) Biological work
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the coupling of exergonic reactions with endergonic ones?
ANSWER: ENERGY COUPLING
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of metabolic processes is not correctly paired with an example?
W) Metabolism; digestion of lipids by pepsin
X) Catabolism; fermentation
Y) Anabolism; protein synthesis
Z) Energy transformation; phosphorylation
ANSWER: W) Metabolism; digestion of lipids by pepsin
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following terms is not correctly paired with its definition?
W) Fermentation; partial degradation of sugars without oxygen
X) Glycolysis; complete degradation of sugars
Y) Alcohol fermentation; conversion of pyruvate to ethanol with release of CO2
Z) Lactic acid fermentation; conversion of pyruvate to lactic acid without release of CO2
ANSWER: X) Glycolysis; complete degradation of sugars
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following enzymes is not correctly paired with its function in glycolysis?
W) Hexokinase; phosphorylation of glucose at the sixth carbon
X) Phosphofructokinase; phosphorylation of fructose at the first carbon
Y) Isomerase; one-way conversion of dihydroxyacetone phosphate to glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate
Z) Pyruvate kinase; phosphorylation of ADP by dephosphorylation of phosphoenolpyruvate
ANSWER: Y) Isomerase; one-way conversion of dihydroxyacetone phosphate to glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate

ANSWER: GLYCERALDEHYDE-3-PHOSPHATE; 1, 3-BISPHOSPHOGLYCERATE; 3-PHOSPHOGLYCERATE; 2-PHOSPHOGLYCERATE; PHOSPHOENOLPYRUVATE

ANSWER: 6, 6, 3, 3, 3, 3
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer Give the number of carbonyl groups in each of the following intermediates of glycolysis: glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate; 1, 3-bisphosphoglycerate; 3-phosphoglycerate; pyruvate; lactic acid.
