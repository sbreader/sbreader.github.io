1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the maximum luminosity of a star in hydrostatic equilibrium?
ANSWER: EDDINGTON LUMINOSITY (also accept: EDDINGTON LIMIT)
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following types of scattering occurs when light bounces off of particles much smaller than the wavelength of the radiation and has a cross section proportional to the inverse fourth power of wavelength?
W) Compton
X) Thomson
Y) Raman
Z) Rayleigh
ANSWER: Z) Rayleigh
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Julian starts on an arbitrary vertex of a cube with side length 6. What is the minimum distance Julian must travel if he wants to reach the diagonally opposite vertex and can only traverse the surface of the cube?
ANSWER: 6√5
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Lucas painstakingly spent 1015 years counting molecules in gas samples, and realized that if any two gases are at the same temperature and pressure, their molar volumes are identical. What law did he rediscover?
ANSWER: Avogadro's law
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following three biomes in terms of increasing species richness 1) Savanna 2) Taiga 3) Chaparral
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A 12-volt battery is connected to a 2 Ohm resistor and an initially uncharged 0.5 Farad capacitor in series. After a very long time, what is the power dissipated through the resistor in Watts?
ANSWER: 0
7. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following could be a cyclic quadrilateral?
W) A parallelogram that isn't a rectangle
X) A nonisoceles trapezoid
Y) A kite that isn't a rhombus
Z) A rhombus that isn't a square
ANSWER: Y) A kite that isn't a rhombus
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Standard reduction potentials are all measured with respect to what electrode, which consists of platinum in contact with hydrogen ions and bathed in a hydrogen gas at 1 atmosphere?
ANSWER: Standard hydrogen electrode
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Filip is investigating the oxygen dissociation curves for hemoglobin and myoglobin. Which of the following statements would not be true?
W) Myoglobin has a higher affinity for oxygen than hemoglobin
X) Myoglobin's dissociation curve is a rectangular hyperbola, while hemoglobin's is sigmoidal
Y) Both myoglobin and hemoglobin exhibit cooperativity
Z) Myoglobin is a monomer, while hemoglobin is a tetramer
ANSWER: Y) Both myoglobin and hemoglobin exhibit cooperativity
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The breakup of which of the following supercontinents roughly 700 million years ago formed Gondwana and the Panthalassic Ocean?
W) Columbia
X) Nuna
Y) Rodinia
Z) Pangaea
ANSWER: Y) Rodinia
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice For which of the following wavelengths of light will glass have the highest index of refraction?
W) 300 nanometers
X) 400 nanometers
Y) 500 nanometers
Z) 600 nanometers
ANSWER: W) 300 nanometers
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What group consists of just one element A such that A times A equals A?
ANSWER: trivial group
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What constant in the Arrhenius equation represents the frequency of properly oriented collisions?
ANSWER: Pre-exponential factor (also accept: frequency factor)
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Arrange the following three stages in embryonic development from first to last 1) Blastula 2) Morula 3) Gastrula
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which equation in astrophysics, derived from the conservation of mechanical energy, is used to find the velocity of a planet in an elliptical orbit around its host star?
ANSWER: VIS-VIVA EQUATION
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Ethan is playing with a soccer ball. If the ball is a hollow sphere with uniform density, what is the ratio between the moment of inertia about its center and the moment of inertia about an axis tangent to its surface?
ANSWER: 2/5
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Lucas has an infinite collection of garden gnomes, labeled 1, 2, 3, and so on. For a garden gnome labeled n, its height in meters is given by 1/n2. If Lucas stacks all his gnomes on top of each other, in meters, what will be the total height of the gnome stack?
ANSWER: (π2)/6
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following gases would have the least deviation from the ideal gas law?
W) Hydrogen chloride
X) Water vapor
Y) Argon
Z) Dichloromethane
ANSWER: Y) Argon
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Lucas is playing Stardew Valley, and on day 22, his farm is affected by heavy rains! Identify all of the following ions that would be leached from the soil by the rain 1) Nitrate 2) Magnesium ion 3) Carbonate
ANSWER: 1 and 3
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following glacial features would be the most well-sorted?
W) Drumlin
X) Kame
Y) Lateral moraine
Z) End moraine
ANSWER: X) Kame
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 distance measuring techniques by decreasing maximum distance at which they function 1) Parallax 2) Hubble's Law 3) Type 1a Supernova
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer A heat engine has an efficiency of 0.3. If it expels 980 Joules into a cold reservoir, how many Joules of work does it perform?
ANSWER: 420
23. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the sum of the alternating infinite series 1-1/2+1/3-1/4+1/5-1/6+...?
ANSWER: ln(2)
24. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 3 substances in order of increasing boiling point 1) diatomic oxygen 2) diatomic hydrogen 3. formaldehyde
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer Tardigrades, known for their high resistance to stress, can enter a state of dormancy when their habitats dry up. What is this adaptation called?
ANSWER: Anhydrobiosis
26. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A pendulum is placed on an elevator, which accelerates from rest to a constant upward velocity. Which of the following best describes what happens to the period of oscillations of the pendulum?
W) It increases
X) It decreases
Y) It increases, then decreases
Z) It decreases, then increases
ANSWER: Z) It decreases, then increases
27. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a method used to solve differential equations?
W) Separation of variables
X) Laplace transform
Y) Variation of parameters
Z) Simpson's rule
ANSWER: Z) Simpson's rule
28. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds is the most acidic?
W) CaO
X) K2O
Y) ClO2
Z) Al2O3
ANSWER: Y) ClO2
29. BONUS Biology Short Answer Yajur is growing a bean plant. In the plant's root nodules, what molecule is responsible for binding oxygen and regulating its concentration to protect nitrogen-fixing enzymes?
ANSWER: Leghemoglobin
30. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three changes that would likely occur to a mafic melt as a result of crustal assimilation 1) Increase in magma temperature 2) Increased crystal settling 3) Increased polymerization
ANSWER: 2
31. BONUS Physics Short Answer Filip is on a relativistic train when he sees lightning strike both ends of his train at the same time. From the perspective of Shayan, who is on the ground, which end of the train is struck first by lightning?
ANSWER: The back
32. BONUS Math Short Answer A right triangle has integer side lengths. If one side of the triangle has length 15, what is the largest possible value of another side of the triangle?
ANSWER: 113
33. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following atomic orbitals that can form pi bonds. 1) s 2) p 3) d
ANSWER: All
34. BONUS Biology Short Answer For oxygenated blood coming from the pulmonary vein, order the following heart valves it will encounter from first to last 1) Tricuspid valve 2) Aortic valve 3) Bicuspid valve
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
35. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer If the apparent magnitude of a star increased from 5 to 10, and the absolute magnitude remains constant, by what factor will the parallax angle of the star change?
ANSWER: 0.1
36. BONUS Physics Short Answer Shayan makes a converging lens out of glass with an index of refraction of 4/3. If the radius curvature of one face is 6 cm, while the radius curvature of the other face is 7 cm, to one significant figure and in centimeters, what is the focal length of the lens?
ANSWER: 10
37. BONUS Math Short Answer Lucas realizes that he has too many gnomes! He keeps gnomes that are labeled with numbers divisible by 2 or 3, and gets rid of all the others. If Lucas stacks his remaining gnomes on top of each other, in meters, what is the new total height of the gnome stack?
ANSWER: (π2)/18
38. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three molecular orbitals in diatomic nitrogen in increasing energy 1) sigma 2s 2) sigma 2p 3) sigma 2p star
ANSWER: 2 1 3
39. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In the first step of glycolysis, hexokinase adds a phosphate group to glucose to make glucose-6-phosphate. Which of the following is most likely the purpose of this step?
W) It increases the solubility of glucose
X) It irreversibly commits glucose into glycolysis
Y) It allows glucose to pass through the mitochondrial membrane
Z) It traps glucose in the cell
ANSWER: Z) It traps glucose in the cell
40. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer When two water masses mix, they often form a new water mass that is denser than either of the initial water masses. What is the term for this process?
ANSWER: CABBELING
