Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is adding ammonia to copper. By name or number, rank the following three colors he will observe by increasing associated ammonia concentration given that the ammonia concentration is nonzero 1) Colorless 2) Red-Brown 3) Dark Blue
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is true of the most dominant form of protonated nitric acid, H2NO3+?
W) It is weaker than sulfuric acid
X) It has one positive formal charge, which resides on the nitrogen atom
Y) It readily decomposes to release water in solution
Z) It has one N-H bond in its structure
ANSWER: Y) It readily decomposes to release water in solution
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What name is given to the zwitterion intermediate formed after the cleavage of the ozonide intermediate in ozonolysis?
ANSWER: Criegee Intermediates (also accept: Criegee Zwitterion)
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of phrases correctly characterizes thermal [2+2] cycloadditions?
W) symmetry allowed and electronic transitions allowed
X) symmetry forbidden and electronic transitions allowed
Y) symmetry allowed and electronic transitions forbidden
Z) symmetry forbidden and electronic transitions forbidden
ANSWER: X) symmetry forbidden and electronic transitions allowed
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What set of rules are used to calculate the shielding constant by assigning values to electrons based on their shell and to calculate the effective nuclear charge in atoms?
ANSWER: Slater's Rules
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for a compound with a defect structure in which a cation vacancy is paired with an interstitial cation of the same element, common in silver halides?
ANSWER: Frenkel Defect
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term is given to the energy level where an electronic state has a 50% probability of being occupied by an electron?
ANSWER: Fermi level
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav adds Potassium perchlorate and Lead Nitrate to Beaker A and only Lead Nitrate to Beaker B. When he adds Sodium Iodide to both beakers, he notices that the rate of precipitation in beaker A is lower. What is the name of this effect in which the addition of an inert salt can affect the rate of a chemical reaction?
ANSWER: Kinetic Salt Effect
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the chemical process where a typically soluble substance is precipitated out of a solution along with another, primary precipitate by adsorption, inclusion, or occlusion?
ANSWER: Coprecipitation
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the sum of the coefficients of the balanced redox reaction between the permanganate anion and the oxalate anion to form Mn2+ and carbon dioxide in acidic conditions?
ANSWER: 43
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Aldehydes and Ketones undergo what process during electron ionization mass spectrometry in which the parent radical cation splits, creating an alkene and an enol radical cation?
ANSWER: McLafferty rearrangement
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Aside from using a Tollens' Test, another common test for the detection of aldehydes is Fehling's Test. Which of the following colors would mark the presence of an aldehyde when performing Fehling's Test?
W) Blue
X) Red
Y) Green
Z) Orange
ANSWER: X) Red
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer CaF2 takes on what crystal structure, which is characterized by a face-centered cubic arrangement of calcium ions with fluoride ions filling all the tetrahedral interstitial sites?
ANSWER: Fluorite
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements does not adopt a close-packed structure?
W) Magnesium
X) Tungsten
Y) Aluminum
Z) Gold
ANSWER: X) Tungsten
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What rule states that no normal modes of vibration can be both Infrared and Raman active in a molecule possessing a center of symmetry?
ANSWER: Rule of mutual exclusion
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following reactions is likely -1 order with respect to one of its products?
W) Thermal decomposition of calcium carbonate
X) Metal-Catalyzed decomposition of NH3
Y) Beta minus decay of Carbon-14
Z) chelation of Zn2+ ions by EDTA
ANSWER: X) Metal-Catalyzed decomposition of NH3
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following options accurately compares selenic acid to sulfuric acid?
W) Stronger Acid, Stronger Oxidizing Agent
X) Weaker Acid, Stronger Oxidizing Agent
Y) Stronger Acid, Weaker Oxidizing Agent
Z) Weaker Acid, Weaker Oxidizing Agent
ANSWER: X) Weaker Acid, Stronger Oxidizing Agent
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three compounds by increasing length of the carbon oxygen bond 1) Carbon Dioxide 2) Urea 3) Carbonyl Sulfide
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Abhinav performs a certain reaction on aniline and finds that even though amino groups are ortho-para directors, the yield of the meta product is higher than the ortho product. Which of the following was most likely the reaction Abhinav performed?
W) Friedel Crafts Alkylation
X) Nitration
Y) Halogenation
Z) Friedel-Crafts Acylation
ANSWER: X) Nitration
10. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Abhinav treats a nitrile with acidified water and then with SOCl2. How does the highest IR stretching frequency change throughout these reactions?
W) increases
X) decreases
Y) increases, then decreases
Z) decreases, then increases
ANSWER: Y) increases, then decreases
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the brittle, high-melting alloys formed by combining a highly electropositive metal with a more electronegative main-group metal or metalloid?
ANSWER: Zintl Phases
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three molecules which are Raman active 1) H2 2) N2 3) CO2
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The isomerization of cyclopropane to propene can be explained using what model which explains how gas-phase unimolecular reactions can follow apparent first-order kinetics at high concentrations but second-order kinetics at low concentrations?
ANSWER: Lindemann Hinshelwood Model (also accept: Lindemann Mechanism)
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following quantum numbers which are required to find the angular momentum wave function 1) principal 2) azimuthal 3) magnetic 4) spin
ANSWER: 2, 3
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is studying water and oxygen difluoride and finds that oxygen difluoride has smaller bond angle than water. This is explained by what rule, which states that more electronegative atoms prefer orbitals with less s-character?
ANSWER: Bent's Rule
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three molecular orbitals by increasing number of nodal planes present 1) Pi bonding 2) Phi bonding 3) Pi antibonding
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Trimethyl Borate would be used in which of the following coupling reactions?
W) Stille
X) Negishi
Y) Suzuki
Z) Heck
ANSWER: Y) Suzuki
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three compounds by increasing stability at room temperature 1) 1,2-dioxane 2) 1,3-dioxane 3) 1,4-dioxane
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following energy levels is not present in an n type semiconductor?
W) Donor Band
X) Conduction Band
Y) Valence Band
Z) Acceptor Band
ANSWER: Z) Acceptor Band
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three geometries by increasing Jahn Teller distortions 1) Octahedral 2) Square Planar 3) Tetrahedral
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What model describes the adsorption of gas or liquid molecules onto a flat solid surface to form a single layer of uniform coverage?
ANSWER: Langmuir Adsorption Model (also accept: Langmuir Model)
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer While Hund's Rule is typically thought of as one rule, it is actually a set of rules. Which of these rules states electrons fill degenerate orbitals singly with parallel spins before any pairing occurs to maximize spin?
ANSWER: Hund's Rule of Maximum Multiplicity
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term is used to describe different crystalline or structural forms of the same mineral such as calcite and aragonite?
ANSWER: polymorphs
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following statements which are true about the sphalerite and wurtzite structures 1) They have the same coordination number 2) They have the same packing efficiency 3) They have the same empirical formula
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
