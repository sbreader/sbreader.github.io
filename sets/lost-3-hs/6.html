1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Akshay, who lives on top of a skyscraper, is late to his AP Astrology class. His AP Astrology class is in the ground floor of a building down the street from Akshay, but he doesn't want to waste time going down the elevator and walking on the street. Instead, he quickly constructs a slide that can take him directly from his apartment to his class. What shape will allow Akshay to arrive at his Astrology class in the quickest time?
ANSWER: CYCLOID (also accept: BRACHISTOCHRONE)
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer To two significant figures and in scientific notation, what is the momentum of a singular 132-nanometer photon?
ANSWER: 5.0 × 10-27
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Adam and Akshay are playing a game where they take turns rolling a fair 6-sided die. Adam wins if he rolls a 1 or 2. Akshay wins if he rolls a 3, 4, 5, or 6. Given that Adam starts, what is the probability that Adam wins?
ANSWER: 3/7
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Let S be a square with side length 3. What is the area of the region of points within 1 of the boundary of S in the plane of S?
ANSWER: 20+π
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In addition to auxins, what other plant hormone promotes apical dominance and is also responsible for attracting mycorrhizal fungi?

Z) Strigolactones

3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Aurélien Tchouameni is performing live-cell immunofluorescence, and must perform a wash between primary antibody incubation and secondary antibody incubation. Which of the following best explains why phosphate-buffered saline, and not water, must be used as the wash buffer?
W) Diluting highly concentrated PBS is more cost-effective than producing DI water
X) PBS has a pH of 7.4, making it isotonic to mammalian cells
Y) Water will dissolve the hydrophilic nitrocellulose membrane, preventing binding of the secondary antibody to primary antibody
Z) The phosphate ions in PBS crosslink the primary antibody to the target antigen, preventing detachment during subsequent washes
ANSWER: X) PBS has a pH of 7.4, making it isotonic to mammalian cells
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The brilliant Chinicius is studying the staggered form of ethane so he can ace his college midterm. In this form, which of the following is the measure of the dihedral angle between hydrogen atoms on adjacent carbons?

6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about the motion of a projectile launched in the upward direction when air friction is NOT negligible?

10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes a trans-neptunian object?
