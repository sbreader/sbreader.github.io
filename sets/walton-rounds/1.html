1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What law states that the current in a conductor is directly proportional to voltage and inversely proportional to resistance?
ANSWER: OHM'S LAW
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A bicycle wheel spins on its axis at a constant rate but has not yet made a complete rotation. Which of the following statements is correct?
W) The angular displacement is zero.
X) The moment of inertia is zero.
Y) The angular acceleration is zero.
Z) The angular velocity is zero.
ANSWER: Y) The angular acceleration is zero.
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What membrane protein facilitates the diffusion of water across the cell membrane?
W) aquaporin
X) rhodopsin
Y) nuclear pore complex
Z) ion pump
ANSWER: W) aquaporin
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following four structures from beginning to end in which air passes through during inhalation. 1) Nasal Cavities 2) Bronchioles 3) Trachea 4) Alveoli
ANSWER: 1,3,2,4
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Livermore National Lab has helped to discover many very heavy, transuranic elements. Which of the following elements is NOT transuranic?
W) Protactinium
X) Americium
Y) Meitnerium
Z) Flerovium
ANSWER: W) Protactinium
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What chemical is commonly used as nail polish remover and is the simplest ketone?
ANSWER: ACETONE
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What two monosaccharides together form lactose?
ANSWER: GLUCOSE AND GALACTOSE
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What outer layer of the sun is usually only visible during a solar eclipse?
ANSWER: CORONA
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name of number, identify all of the following three factors that do NOT contribute to acid precipitation? 1) Fossil fuel combustion 2) Sulfur dioxide emissions 3) Nitrogen oxide emissions
ANSWER: NONE
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the sum of the first 6 positive integers?
ANSWER: 21
6. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the natural logarithm of -1? Give an exact answer in terms of i.
ANSWER: PI*I (also accept: PI TIMES I)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How do cyanobacteria differ from green unicellular algae?
W) cyanobacteria reduce sulfur compounds
X) cyanobacteria have no nuclei
Y) green algae contain lipids
Z) green algae produce cell membranes
ANSWER: X) cyanobacteria have no nuclei
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Give the phenotypic ratio that is most commonly associated with the Mendelian dihybrid cross, assuming independent assortment.
ANSWER: 9:3:3:1
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the only series in the hydrogen emission spectrum with visible wavelengths called?
ANSWER: BALMER SERIES
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A disk is rotating thirty (30) revolution per minute, what is its angular velocity in radians per second?
ANSWER: π rad/s
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice For each mole of glucose oxidized by cellular respiration, how many moles of carbon dioxide are released in the citric acid cycle?
W) 2
X) 4
Y) 6
Z) 32
ANSWER: X) 4
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice An unknown solution having a pH of 3.5 was titrated with 0.1 M NaOH. Analysis of the resulting titration curve showed a single equivalence point at pH 7. Therefore, which of the following could be the unknown solute in the initial solution?
W) HF
X) NH3
Y) LiOH
Z) HCl
ANSWER: Z) HCl
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the most abundant element in the universe?
W) Oxygen
X) Aluminum
Y) Helium
Z) Hydrogen
ANSWER: Z) Hydrogen
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In what part of the water cycle is water vapor converted into liquid water?
ANSWER: CONDENSATION
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What are the roots of the polynomial x2-6x+9?
ANSWER: 3
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer This type of bond is usually formed when two atoms which are bonding have an electronegativity difference that is below 0.5.
ANSWER: NON-POLAR COVALENT BOND
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rounded to the nearest tenth, what is the bond angle in methane in degrees?
ANSWER: 109.5 DEGREES
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Researchers at Brookhaven National Lab are studying how greenhouse gases are exchanged in the carbon cycle and terrestrial ecosystems. Which of the following is NOT a greenhouse gas?
W) Methane
X) Water Vapor
Y) Sulfur Dioxide
Z) Carbon Dioxide
ANSWER: Y) Sulfur Dioxide
13. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers at the Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics have recently created the Wendelstein 7-X. What energy source was the Wendelstein 7-X designed to harness?
ANSWER: FUSION
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the amplitude of a wave, in meters, if the wave is represented by the equation y = 5sin(2π(2x-3)), where x and y are in meters?
ANSWER: 5
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer A car is driving on a straight highway at 30 m/s. The driver hits the brakes until the car stops with a constant acceleration of -7 m/s2. To the nearest meter, how far does the car travel after hitting the brakes?
ANSWER: 129
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following volcanoes are composed of mafic igneous rocks? 1) Composite 2) Shield 3) Cone
ANSWER: 1,2,3 (also accept: ALL)
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice If our sun one day collapses into a black hole, what would happen to earth's orbit?
W) Start moving toward the black hole in a straight line
X) Start moving toward black hole in a spiral path
Y) Maintain the same orbit as now
Z) Be expelled from the solar system
ANSWER: Y) Maintain the same orbit as now
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is an alkaline earth metal?
W) Vanadium
X) Actinium
Y) Strontium
Z) Antimony
ANSWER: Y) Strontium
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A molecule has 50 single bonds, 15 double bonds, and 13 triple bonds. How many sigma and pi bonds does it have, respectively?
ANSWER: 78 SIGMA BONDS AND 41 PI BONDS
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name given to the mechanism by which information encoded in DNA is made into a complementary RNA copy?
ANSWER: TRANSCRIPTION
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the non-protein-coding sequences in the pre-mRNA called?
ANSWER: INTRONS
18. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at Berkeley National Lab are studying how the Higgs boson may decay into two muons. Muons fall into which of the following categories on the standard model?
W) Quarks
X) Leptons
Y) Bosons
Z) Mesons
ANSWER: X) Leptons
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Berkeley National Lab are using a combination of optical microscopy and synchrotron infrared beams to study symbiosis in aquatic ecosystems. What organism lives symbiotically with coral polyps and provides them with energy as well as giving them their color?
ANSWER: (PHOTOSYNTHETIC) ALGAE

ANSWER: 1 CM X 1 CM
19. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the units digit of 32020?
ANSWER: 1

W) chitin
X) silica
Y) lignin
Z) collagen fibers
ANSWER: W) chitin

W) Golgi apparatus
X) Endoplasmic Reticulum
Y) Mitochondria
Z) Cytosol
ANSWER: Z) Cytosol

ANSWER: CRITICAL ANGLE

ANSWER: 30 °
