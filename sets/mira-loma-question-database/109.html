1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following processes is a physical change?
W) Burning gasoline
X) Subliming dry ice
Y) Electrolyzing water
Z) Rusting iron
ANSWER: X) Subliming dry ice
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following relations is generally correct between different types of matter?
W) Elements can always be physically separated from compounds.
X) A solution must undergo chemical reaction to become a mixture.
Y) Pure substances can be physically extracted from homogeneous mixtures.
Z) Compounds are not considered pure substances.
ANSWER: Y) Pure substances can be physically extracted from homogeneous mixtures.
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Whose law states that if the mass and volume of an ideal gas are kept constant, then the pressure is directly proportional with the temperature?
ANSWER: Joseph Gay-Lussac
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Gay-Lussac is also known for all of the following innovations EXCEPT for?
W) the recognition of Iodine as an element
X) the discovery of the Law of Partial Volumes
Y) improvement of the burette
Z) the codiscovery of boron
ANSWER: X) the discovery of the Law of Partial Volumes
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which model of the atom best disproved Thomson's Plum Pudding Model at the time, through bombarding gold foil with alpha particles?
ANSWER: Rutherford model
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice According to the Rutherford model, an atom has a very dense core known as a nucleus. Roughly approximately the density of nuclear material, approximately how much mass would a meter cubed of this substance have?
W) 1 ton
X) 10 thousand tons
Y) 100 million tons
Z) 100 trillion tons
ANSWER: Z) 100 trillion tons
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following interactions is BEST considered intramolecular?
W) Covalent bonds
X) London Dispersion Forces
Y) Hydrogen bonds
Z) Dipole-Dipole interactions
ANSWER: W) Covalent bonds
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Using the terms "the ionic bond", "the covalent bond", "measured dipole moment of ", "calculated dipole moment of", and "100%", state the equation used to find percent ionic character of a bond.
ANSWER: (measured dipole moment of the covalent bond / calculated dipole moment of the ionic bond) x 100%
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice To calculate the electron arrangement in unbonded atomic orbitals, one can effectively use all of the following guidelines except which?
W) aufbau principle
X) Madelung's rule
Y) Heisenberg uncertainty principle
Z) Hund's rule
ANSWER: Y) Heisenberg uncertainty principle
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Using the generally correct guidelines, give the shorthand electron configuration for element number 33, Arsenic.
ANSWER: [Ar] 4s2,3d10,4p3
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Firewalking on red hot coals is possible because of all BUT which of the following scientific explanations?
W) Coal incandesces at a low temperature, so the bed of coals is not relatively hot.
X) The layer of coals in a coalbed is very shallow, so the total heat capacity is low.
Y) Human feet have a large specific heat capacity so they heat up very slowly.
Z) The Leidenfrost effect allows water droplets on the walker's feet to vaporize and provide a gaseous, insulatory layer that minimizes heat transfer.
ANSWER: W) Coal incandesces at a low temperature, so the bed of coals is not relatively hot.
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which formulation of the second law of thermodynamics MOST directly states: "Heat generally cannot flow spontaneously from a material at lower temperature to a material at higher temperature"?
W) Kelvin Statement
X) Joule Statement
Y) Clausius Statement
Z) Helmholtz Statement
ANSWER: Y) Clausius Statement
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Also known as Gillespie-Nyholm Theory, what "crude", though structurally accurate model predicts the molecular geometries of covalent molecules based upon the extent of electrostatic forces between valence electrons?
ANSWER: VSEPR (Valence Shell Electron-Pair Repulsion) Theory
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Using VSEPR theory, which of the following would the BEST predicted geometry for a central atom of a molecule with steric number of five, and one lone pair of electrons?
W) trigonal bipyramidal
X) linear
Y) T-shaped
Z) see-saw
ANSWER: Z) see-saw
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer This renowned scientist spent his later years advocating a higher intake of vitamins and wrote "How to Live Longer and Feel Better". He was the first person to win unshared Nobel Prizes in two different fields: First was Chemistry, in 1954, for elucidating the nature of chemical bonds and the structure of molecules, especially his orbital hybridization theory and his study of the tetravalency of carbon. Then was Peace, in 1962 for advocating nuclear disarmament. He is commonly known for his work on electronegativity and his namesake electronegativity scale. FFP, name this person who is sometimes called "the father of molecular biology".
ANSWER: Linus Pauling
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Pauling heavily influenced the discovery of the DNA, but did not share credit for the project. Which of the following other collaborators did receive credit for the 1962 Nobel Prize in Physiology/Medicine?
W) Rosalind Franklin
X) Oswald Avery
Y) Maurice Wilkins
Z) Erwin Chargaff
ANSWER: Y) Maurice Wilkins
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What type of chemical reaction involves two compounds that exchange ions or bonds to form different compounds, such as NaCl(aq) + AgNO3(aq) → NaNO3(aq) + AgCl(s)?
W) metathesis
X) substitution
Y) analysis
Z) synthesis
ANSWER: W) metathesis
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The principle of this type of metathesis is that an alkene double bond is cleaved and redistributed alongside the redistribution of an alkylidene under the presence of a catalytic metal. Also known as transalkylidenation, what organic reaction was the subject of the 2005 Chemistry Nobel Prize?
ANSWER: olefin metathesis
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In 1864, Peter Waage and Cato Guldberg pioneered the development of this field by formulating the law of mass action. It investigates factors that can influence the speed and characteristics of chemical reactions, along with yielding information about the reaction mechanism and transition states. FFP, what is the term for the study of rates of chemical processes?
ANSWER: (chemical) kinetics
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer List five out of the six major factors that can significantly affect the reaction rate.
ANSWER: Temperature, Pressure, Concentration, Nature of reactant, Physical state, Catalysts (also accept: similar answers)
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following chemicals is not in its elemental standard state?
W) C (solid)
X) O (gas)
Y) Al (solid)
Z) Hg (liquid)
ANSWER: X) O (gas)
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What would be the standard notation for the standard enthalpy of formation? Describe in detail.
ANSWER: delta capital-H subscript lowercase-f degree-sign
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about semiconductors is FALSE?
W) Silicon and Germanium are very commonly used semiconducting materials.
X) In semiconductors, the energy levels of the empty molecular orbitals overlap with those of the filled molecular orbitals.
Y) A Silicon network doped with Arsenic can produce a n-type semiconductor.
Z) In a p-n junction, when the anode of a battery is connected with the n-type region and the cathode is connected with the p-type region, current is heavily impeded.
ANSWER: X) In semiconductors, the energy levels of the empty molecular orbitals overlap with those of the filled molecular orbitals.
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for an energy that pertains to electrons in a semiconductor, which is the chemical potential μ that appears in the electrons' Fermi-Dirac distribution function, the probability that there is an electron in a particular single-particle state?
ANSWER: Fermi level
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following methods would increase the amount of a gas dissolved in a liquid?
W) Decreasing the pressure of the gas above the solution
X) Decreasing the concentration of the gas
Y) Decreasing the temperature of the gas
Z) Adding a different, inert gas
ANSWER: Y) Decreasing the temperature of the gas
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Using any of the following terms, formulate the most concise interpretation of Raoult's Law in an ideal case: Atmospheric pressure, Vapor pressure of solution, van't Hoff factor, Mole fraction of solvent, Temperature, Moles of solute, Vapor pressure of pure solvent, Boltzmann constant.
ANSWER: Vapor pressure of solution = Mole fraction of solvent x Vapor pressure of pure solvent

W) Milk
X) Coffee
Y) Bleach
Z) Soap
ANSWER: Z) Soap

15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the most common way of producing relatively pure nitrogen gas?
W) Liquefaction of air
X) Heating nitrogen-containing minerals
Y) Using the reverse Haber process
Z) Transmutation of Oxygen through alchemy
ANSWER: W) Liquefaction of air
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The natural way of producing nitrogen gas is a step of the nitrogen cycle, where certain bacteria convert organic nitrates back into nitrogen through what general process?
ANSWER: denitrification
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements are TRUE about zeroth-order reactions?
W) Zeroth-order reactions progress at a rate of zero.
X) The plot needed to give a straight line is ln(concentration) versus time.
Y) The half life is approximated best by: initial concentration/2k.
Z) The Haber Process is a well-known example of a zeroth-order reaction.
ANSWER: Y) The half life is approximated best by: initial concentration/2k.
16. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following factors is NOT present in the Arrhenius equation, which determines the rate constant for reactions?
W) Activation energy
X) Concentration of reactants
Y) Frequency factor
Z) Temperature
ANSWER: X) Concentration of reactants
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In the reaction CaCO3(s) <-> CaO(s) + CO2(g), if more CaCO3(s) is added, how will the equillibrium change?
W) More CO2(g) is formed.
X) CaO(s) is removed.
Y) The temperature will increase.
Z) The equillibrium will not change.
ANSWER: Z) The equillibrium will not change.
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer State the Pressure equillibrium constant for the reaction 2H2O(g) <-> 2H2(g) + O2(g).
ANSWER: Kp = (PH2)2 x PO2 / (PH2O)2
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Some substances will produce acidic solutions when dissolved in water. Examples include SO2(g), NO2(g), and CO2(g). What is the general term for these covalently-bonded oxygen-containing compounds?
ANSWER: acidic oxides
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following acids from largest to smallest pKa value: H2SO3, HClO3, HClO4, HNO2.
ANSWER: HNO2, H2SO3, HClO3, HClO4
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A Downs cell is used to electrolyze what common salt to form pure metal?
W) Sodium Chloride
X) Magnesium Oxide
Y) Aluminum Oxide
Z) Iron (III) Oxide
ANSWER: W) Sodium Chloride
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Give the anode and cathode reaction, respectively, for the simplest Downs cell. Use proper notation and balance electrons.
ANSWER: Anode: 2Cl-(aq) -> Cl2(g) + 2e-; Cathode: 2Na+ + 2e- -> 2Na(s)
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What function does a semipermeable membrane play in osmosis?
W) Stop both solute and solvent particles from passing through
X) Stop only solute from passing through
Y) Stop only solvent from passing through
Z) Allows both solute and solvent to pass through
ANSWER: X) Stop only solute from passing through
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Using any of the following factors, formulate the simplest equation for the calculation of osmotic pressure on solution concentration: osmotic pressure, temperature, volume, Boltzmann constant, Gas constant, molarity of solution, molality of solution, van't Hoff factor.
ANSWER: Osmotic pressure= van't Hoff factor x molarity of solution x temperature x gas constant
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following aqueous systems is an example of an effective buffer solution?
W) HCl and NaCl
X) KOH and K2O
Y) H2SO4 and HSO4-
Z) NH3 and NH4Cl
ANSWER: Z) NH3 and NH4Cl
21. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When the ratio between the acid and the conjugate base (or vice versa) is known, what equation, can be effectively used to find the pH of a buffered solution?
ANSWER: Henderson-Hasselbalch Equation
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer This quantity is calculated as the difference between the enthalpy of a system and its entropy multiplied by temperature. Indicative of the total entropy change of the universe, what concept is paramount in spontaneity calculations, and is symbolized by the letter capital G?
ANSWER: Gibb's free energy
22. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice If the entropy change of a reaction is negative for a system, and if the enthalpy change is also negative, when will the reaction become spontaneous?
W) At all temperatures.
X) At high temperatures.
Y) At low temperatures.
Z) Never.
ANSWER: Y) At low temperatures.

25. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Given that the H-H bond energy is 432kJ/mol, the Cl-Cl bond energy is 239kJ/mol, and the H-Cl bond energy is 427kJ/mol, find the standard enthalpy of formation of HCl(g). You may round to the nearest ten.
