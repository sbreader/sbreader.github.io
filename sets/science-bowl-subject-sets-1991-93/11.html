1. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer How many bits are there in a four-byte long word?
ANSWER: 32
2. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer A programming language is named after this woman. What is her name?
ANSWER: ADA LOVELACE (also accept: ADA; AUGUSTA ADA LOVELACE; LOVELACE)
3. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer How is the number -5 (base 10) represented in 4-bit two's complement binary arithmetic?
ANSWER: 1011
4. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the 2's complement of the 4-bit binary number 1010?
ANSWER: 0110
5. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Express the number 33 (base 8) in base 16.
ANSWER: 1B
6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is another name for a base 16 number?
ANSWER: HEXADECIMAL (also accept: HEX)
7. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer How much is 1A (Hex) plus 2B (Hex)?
ANSWER: 45 (HEX) (also accept: 45; 69 DECIMAL)
8. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the binary result when 0111 binary is subtracted from 1000 binary?
ANSWER: 0001 (also accept: 1)
9. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the decimal range of unsigned integers given an 8-bit word?
ANSWER: 0 TO 255
10. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the hexidecimal difference of 3A minus 2B?
ANSWER: F (also accept: 0F)

X) compiler
