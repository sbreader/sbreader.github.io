1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer When performing hydroboration oxidation on a terminal alkyne, a vinyl alcohol is formed, which forms what other functional group after isomerization?
ANSWER: ALDEHYDE (also accept: CARBONYL) (do not accept: KETONE)
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Arrange the following three mechanistic steps of the Hoffman degradation from earliest to latest 1) Isocyanate formation 2) Amide bromination 3) Decarboxylation.
ANSWER: 2,1,3
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following regions of the Sun’s atmosphere has the highest average temperature?
W) Inner corona
X) Outer corona
Y) Photosphere
Z) Chromosphere
ANSWER: X) Outer corona
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements regarding the El Niño Southern Oscillation that are true 1) In an El Niño year, there is a positive SST anomaly in the eastern Pacific 2) In an El Niño year, there is suppressed upwelling in the eastern Pacific 3) In a La Niña year, the thermocline in the eastern Pacific is anomalously deep.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer 7 identical seats are evenly spaced around a circular table. If rotations are indistinct, how many ways are there to arrange 7 people in these seats?
ANSWER: 720
3. BONUS Math Short Answer Let point O be the center of a unit circle. Points A, B, C, and D are in clockwise order on the circle such that angles AOB, BOC, and COD are all 30 degrees. What is the area of polygon ABCDO?
ANSWER: 3/4
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following amino acids can form disulfide bonds with itself?
W) Tyrosine
X) Phenylalanine
Y) Methionine
Z) Cysteine
ANSWER: Z) Cysteine
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three clades of plants that are considered non-vascular 1) Lycopodiophyta 2) Marchantiophyta 3) Pinophyta.
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer On a stress-strain graph, a Hookean solid follows a straight line in the elastic region. However, when a non-Hookean solid is stretched and then released, the curve that it takes when released is different from the curve it takes when initially stretched. What is the term for this effect?
ANSWER: HYSTERESIS
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer A ball is dropped from a height of 30 meters and repeatedly bounces on the ground with a coefficient of restitution of 2/3. What is the limit of the total distance that the ball travels?
ANSWER: 78
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are investigating the kinetics of various methods of uranium isotope separation. What corrosive acid is produced when uranium hexafluoride is hydrolyzed?
ANSWER: HYDROFLUORIC ACID (also accept: HF)
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Fermilab are constructing liquid argon chambers in order to better detect hypothetical sterile neutrinos. While sterile neutrinos only interact with the gravitational force, active neutrinos are able to interact with the weak nuclear force because they have a value of +1/2 for what quantity?
ANSWER: WEAK ISOSPIN (do not accept: ISOSPIN)
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true concerning viruses 1) The lytic cycle is found in temperate phages 2) The lysogenic cycle is always deadly 3) Prophages never leave the host's genome while proviruses do.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the coefficient of performance of a Carnot refrigerator with a hot reservoir of 600 Kelvin and a cold reservoir of 500 Kelvin?
ANSWER: 5
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A uniform wood block with side lengths of 10, 6, and 4 meters is falling through a fluid with constant density and with its biggest side face down, reaching a terminal velocity of v. It spontaneously rotates so that its smallest side is face down, reaching a new terminal velocity. In terms of v, what is the new terminal velocity?
ANSWER: v√10/2 (also accept: v ROOT 10 OVER 2)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding carbonates in the ocean is false?
W) Aragonite is more soluble than calcite
X) Bicarbonate is the dominant carbonate-containing anion in the ocean
Y) The formation of calcium carbonate acts as a net carbon dioxide sink
Z) The calcite compensation depth is at a depth of 4000 to 4100 meters in all of the major oceans
ANSWER: Z) The calcite compensation depth is at a depth of 4000 to 4100 meters in all of the major oceans
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the altitude angle, in degrees, of the sun at noon on the summer solstice for an observer at 30 degrees north?
ANSWER: 83.5
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 statements regarding the one-dimensional particle in a box model that are true 1) The particle can have exactly 0 potential energy 2) The particle can never escape the box 3) The particle's potential energy is directly proportional to its mass.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
10. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Electrospray ionization mass spectrometry is used to determine the molar mass of an unknown organic compound. Which of the following could be the molecular formula of the unknown molecule?
W) CH4O
X) C2H6O
Y) C2H2
Z) CH4
ANSWER: W) CH4O
11. BONUS Math Short Answer Let x = root 2 over 2 + i times root 2 over 2. What is the magnitude of the imaginary component of the sum from k = 1 to 26 of x to the k power?
ANSWER: 1 + ROOT 2 OVER 2
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab have been using electrospray ionization mass spectrometry to study the lipid surface proteins of lung tissues in order to understand various diseases, such as the lack of surfactant production in infants that are born early. What is the term for this disease where the lack of surfactant production causes emphysema in infants born prematurely?
ANSWER: RESPIRATORY DISTRESS SYNDROME

ANSWER: FOURIER SERIES (also accept: FOURIER TRANSFORM)

Y) All of his sons are unaffected
