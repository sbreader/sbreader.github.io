1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In Nanodrop spectrophotometry, a certain ratio of wavelength absorbance is used to determine the purity of a nucleic acid sample and proper removal of protein contaminants. What is the name for this ratio?
ANSWER: 260 to 280 Ratio (also accept: 260 over 280 ratio, 260/280 ratio, A260/A280 ratio, A260 over A280 ratio, A260 to A280 ratio) (do not accept: 260 to 230 ratio)
1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice One of the most common protocols for RNA extraction involves use of the TRIzol reagent. In this protocol, TRIzol serves which of the following functions?
W) Elution of the RNA pellet
X) Isolation of RNA in the aqueous phase
Y) Denaturation of DNA
Z) Lysing of the sample cells
ANSWER: Z) Lysing of the sample cells
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following 260 to 280 ratios would generally qualify an RNA sample as pure?
W) 0.8
X) 1.0
Y) 1.2
Z) 2.0
ANSWER: Z) 2.0
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which approximation is used in quantum mechanics to separate the electronic and vibrational wave functions into isolated components?
ANSWER: BORN-OPPENHEIMER (also accept: BO)
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the sum of the coefficients in the combustion of Dodecane?
ANSWER: 89
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which physicist led the construction and operation of Chicago Pile-1, the world’s first nuclear reactor, achieving the first self-sustaining nuclear chain reaction?
ANSWER: Enrico Fermi (also accept: Fermi)
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Milo and Mary sneak onto a bus headed to Brookhaven National Laboratory and observe the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider accelerating gold nuclei close to the speed of light. If a gold nucleus moves at 0.866c and has a rest mass of m0, by what factor is its relativistic mass increased?
ANSWER: 2 (also accept: 1.99)
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Eric, Emma, Asher, Mary, and Milo go see a movie. If Emma and Mary must be next to each other and Asher and Milo must be separated by at least 1 person, and assuming a row holds exactly 5 seats, in how many ways can the five of them be seated in a row?
ANSWER: 24
4. BONUS Math Short Answer How many lattice points are strictly inside the triangle bounded by the positive axes and 2x+3y=300?
ANSWER: 7351
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What diagram organizes galaxies morphologically, by placing ellipticals on a central stem and splitting spirals into barred and unbarred branches?
ANSWER: Hubble tuning fork (also accept: Hubble tuning fork diagram)
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Milo’s got the sniffles. When he blows his nose, he notices that his sputum is green. What immune system enzyme is the main contributor to the green color of his sputum?
ANSWER: Myeloperoxidase (also accept: MPO)
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the primary function of the immune system enzyme myeloperoxidase?
W) Hydrolysis of fatty acids
X) Release of granzymes from killer T cells
Y) Production of hypohalous acids
Z) Protection from oxidative stress
ANSWER: Y) Production of hypohalous acids
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following allotropes of phosphorus consists of chains of covalently-linked P4 tetrahedra?
W) White phosphorous
X) Red phosphorous
Y) Black phosphorous
Z) Violet phosphorous
ANSWER: X) Red phosphorous
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Emma is doing titration. She finds that it takes 50.0 milliliters of 0.10 molar Magnesium Hydroxide, Mg(OH)2, solution to neutralize 200 milliliters of a Nitric Acid, HNO3, solution to the equivalence point. Calculate the molarity of HNO3, rounded to the second decimal place.
ANSWER: 0.05 M
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What fundamental set of partial differential equations governs the motion of viscous fluids by describing how they interact with forces such as pressure and friction?
ANSWER: Navier-Stokes equations
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer Giving your answer in volts, what is the Hall voltage of a 0.5 mm thick conductor carrying 2 Amps in a 1 Tesla magnetic field, when the carrier density times the elementary charge is 8000, assuming the magnetic field is applied perpendicular to the surface?
ANSWER: 0.5 (also accept: ½; 2/4)
9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following statements has an inverse statement that is false?
W) If matrix A is invertible, then its determinant is nonzero
X) If f(x) has a local extremum at x = c, then f’(c) equals zero
Y) If set A is a subset of set B, then the intersection of A and B is equal to A
Z) If a triangle is equiangular then it is equilateral
ANSWER: X) If f(x) has a local extremum at x = c, then f’(c) equals zero
9. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the sum of all positive integers n such that n! ends with exactly 10 zeros.
ANSWER: 235
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Pilots track humidity in the air because it negatively affects air density, hindering lift generation. Which of the following correctly explains why increased humidity leads to decreased air density?
W) Humid air increases atmospheric pressure
X) Water vapor molecules are lighter than nitrogen and oxygen molecules
Y) Increased humidity causes a drop in temperature
Z) Water vapor attracts oxygen molecules
ANSWER: X) Water vapor molecules are lighter than nitrogen and oxygen molecules
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A researcher flies with a pilot to study how temperature changes with altitude. What happens to the temperature in each layer as the plane passes through the troposphere, lower stratosphere, and upper stratosphere, respectively?
ANSWER: DECREASES, REMAINS CONSTANT, THEN INCREASES (do not accept: IN ANY OTHER ORDER)
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following parts of the brain is known to contain dopaminergic neurons?
W) Ventral tegmental area
X) Red nucleus
Y) Periaqueductal grey
Z) Caudate nucleus
ANSWER: W) Ventral tegmental area
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Winnie is reading a bedtime story to Manar. Her brain needs to receive and process the words on the page and verbalize them. Order the following parts of Winnie’s brain from first to last in terms of where the visual information will go. 1. Broca’s area 2. Angular gyrus 3. Wernicke’s area 4. Visual cortex 5. Motor cortex
ANSWER: 4, 2, 3, 1, 5 (also accept: VISUAL CORTEX, ANGULAR GYRUS, WERNICKE’S AREA, BROCA’S AREA, MOTOR CORTEX)
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Name all of the following 3 statements that are true for isothermal reversible expansions of ideal gases 1) All heat flow into the system is converted into work 2) The gas increases in pressure 3) This process can be part of the Carnot cycle.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3 (also accept: ALL BUT 2)
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Categorize each of the following four examples of isomerism as either structural isomerism or stereoisomerism 1) geometrical 2) ionization 3) polymerization 4) optical
ANSWER: 1, STEREOISOMERISM; 2, STRUCTURAL ISOMERISM; 3, STRUCTURAL ISOMERISM; 4, STEREOISOMERISM
13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following properties about neutron star mergers and black holes can not be measured using an interferometer?
W) Mass
X) Gravitational wave intensity
Y) Internal structure
Z) Size
ANSWER: Y) Internal structure
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer In a tokamak, the magnetic flux through any circular loop of plasma remains constant as the radius increases. Giving your answer in Teslas, what will the toroidal magnetic field strength be at radius 3 meters from the center axis of the torus, if the toroidal magnetic field strength at 2 meters is 18 Teslas?
ANSWER: 12
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 3 infinite series that diverge 1) The sum of the reciprocals of natural numbers without the digit '1'. 2) The sum of sin(n)/n over the naturals. 3) The sum of the reciprocals of all twin primes.
ANSWER: NONE
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Find all positive integers n, less than 17, for which n! + (n + 1)! + (n + 2)! is an integral multiple of 49.
ANSWER: 5, 12, 14, 15, 16
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following would NOT be true of a blackbody when temperature increases?
W) The area under the blackbody curve increases
X) Peak wavelength decreases
Y) Radiation intensity increases
Z) Total energy decreases
ANSWER: Z) Total energy decreases
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What process in black holes releases thermal energy and allows for them to be classified as blackbodies?
ANSWER: HAWKING RADIATION
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is most indicative of cancer in sample tissue?
W) Increase in typical fibroblasts
X) Epithelial-mesenchymal transition
Y) Downregulation of Bcl2
Z) Smaller nucleoli
ANSWER: X) Epithelial-mesenchymal transition
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Epithelial-mesenchymal transition is a sign of metastasis, but is also an essential process in wound healing. However, injury to internal organs can cause an abnormal buildup of scar tissue. Organ dysfunction resulting from this buildup is grouped under what category of diseases?
ANSWER: FIBROTIC (also accept: FIBROSIS)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many angular and radial nodes, respectively, are there in a hydrogen 3s orbital?
ANSWER: 0 ANGULAR, 2 RADIAL (also accept: 0 AND 2)
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Provide both the oxidation and reduction half-reactions for the chemical reaction that occurs when Calcium Oxide reacts with elemental Chlorine.
ANSWER: Oxidation: 2O2- → O2 + 4e- Reduction: Cl2 + 2e- → 2Cl-
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In classical mechanics, the rotational inertia of an object about a given axis is equal to which derivative of the rotational kinetic energy with respect to the angular velocity?
ANSWER: SECOND (also accept: SECOND DERIVATIVE)
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer An object is released from rest on planet Hoth, experiencing an air drag force. The velocity as a function of time is given as v(t) = (20t)/(4+5t). What is the acceleration due to gravity on planet Hoth?
ANSWER: 5m/s2

ANSWER: Chloroform (also accept: trichloromethane; TCM)

TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 statements that are true regarding Carnot engines operating between two thermal reservoirs 1) Increasing the temperature of the hot reservoir increases maximum efficiency 2) Efficiency depends only on the working substance type 3) Operating in reverse acts as a refrigerator 4) Leaving the hot reservoir unchanged while the cold reservoir is halved exactly doubles the efficiency
