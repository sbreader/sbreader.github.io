1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements is not essential to living animals?
W) Nitrogen
X) Zinc
Y) Nickel
Z) Copper
ANSWER: Y) Nickel
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The color of a piece of very hot steel varies with its temperature. Which of the following colors indicates the highest temperature?
W) Yellow
X) Orange
Y) Reddish orange
Z) Dark red
ANSWER: W) Yellow
2. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice The random movement of dust particles in a room is an example of
W) Inelastic collisions.
X) Elastic collisions.
Y) Brownian motion.
Z) Vortexes.
ANSWER: Y) Brownian motion.

W) in the most ancient class of echinoderms.
X) detritus feeders.
Y) the only echinoderms with sharp spines.
Z) sessile organisms.
ANSWER: W) in the most ancient class of echinoderms.
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The part of the nervous system many echinoderms use for balance consists of
W) radial nerves.
X) statocysts.
Y) light-sensitive cells.
Z) nerve rings.
ANSWER: X) statocysts.
