Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer An organic chemist conducts a series of experiments to determine the identity of an unknown compound. First, mass spectrometry reveals that the compound has a molecular formula of C4H8. This does not uniquely determine the identity of the compound since multiple compounds have the same molecular formula but a different arrangement of atoms. What is the term for these kinds of compounds?
ANSWER: ISOMERS (also accept: STRUCTURAL ISOMERS; CONSTITUTIONAL ISOMERS)
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 structures in terms of increasing distance from Saturn 1) A ring 2) B ring 3) Titan.
ANSWER: 2,1,3
3. BONUS Math Short Answer Rich numbers must start in a 4 and have either 13, 16, or 19 digits. Poor numbers must start with a 5 and have 16 digits. To the nearest integer, how many times as many rich numbers are there compared to poor numbers?
ANSWER: 1001
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab have been studying the moisture feedback between soil and the atmosphere. What is the term given to the collective process where water vapor is transferred from soil, plants, and other land sources to the atmosphere?
ANSWER: EVAPOTRANSPIRATION (do not accept: EVAPORATION; TRANSPIRATION)
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Hemophilia A is an X linked recessive disease that results from a defective allele for a clotting factor. Assuming that the diseased allele is in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, if the probability of a given male having hemophilia is 0.3, what is the frequency of the wild type allele?
ANSWER: 0.7
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A baseball player tosses a ball up in the air, and it eventually falls back down to his glove. Which of the following best describes the sign of the power of gravity acting on the ball?
W) Always positive
X) Always negative
Y) Positive, then negative
Z) Negative, then positive
ANSWER: Z) Negative, then positive
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A photon with wavelength 300 nm strikes a piece of metal, and the energy of the ejected electron is 3/5 of the energy of the photon. What is the maximum wavelength, in nm, that another photon can have and still be able to eject an electron from the same piece of metal?
ANSWER: 750
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer One common method for synthesizing alpha-beta unsaturated carbonyl compounds is aldol condensation. Aldol condensations yield an aldehyde with what other specific functional group?
ANSWER: ALCOHOL (also accept: HYDROXYL)
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A random positive divisor of 22020 is chosen. What is the probability that it is odd?
ANSWER: 1/2021
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Brookhaven National Laboratory have been using neutral current interactions to study neutrino flavor oscillations and their mechanism of transformation. Neutral current interactions arise from the weak force. Identify all of the following 3 particles that mediate the weak force 1) W+ boson 2) Z0 boson 3) Higgs boson.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Ames Lab are investigating the magnetic properties of manganese bismuth alloys, which may arise due to their high anisotropy. High anisotropy is characteristic of what state of matter, which has both molecular ordering and fluidity and is commonly found in TV displays?
ANSWER: LIQUID CRYSTAL
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Order the following 3 isotopes from least to greatest neutron to proton ratio 1) oxygen-16 2) oxygen-18 3) fluorine-19.
ANSWER: 1,3,2
15. BONUS Math Short Answer A regular hexagon has side length 1, with two sides parallel to the x axis. The hexagon is dilated by a factor of 2 in the x direction. What is the new area of the hexagon?
ANSWER: 3√3 (also accept: 3 ROOT 3)
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Mars orbits the sun with an average distance of 1.5 AU. To the nearest integer, how many times as far away is Mars from Earth when it is at its furthest point from Earth than during its closest approach to Earth?
ANSWER: 5
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding periodic trends among the alkali metals 1) Bond strength to chlorine increases monotonically down the group 2) Ionization energy increases monotonically down the group 3) All alkali metals exhibit a red flame test.
ANSWER: NONE
17. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following organic compounds is not a heterocycle?
W) Benzene
X) Pyridine
Y) Tetrahydrofuran
Z) Furan
ANSWER: W) Benzene
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Compute the last two digits of 52020.
ANSWER: 25
19. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A proton and an electron are both traveling north at a speed of 300 m/s when they enter a 20 tesla magnetic field directed eastward. Assuming the proton and electron do not interact with each other, which of the following best describes the paths of the particles?
W) The paths are circular and the radius of the proton’s path is larger
X) The paths are circular and the radius of the proton’s path is smaller
Y) The paths are helical and the radius of the proton’s path is larger
Z) The paths are helical and the radius of the proton’s path is smaller
ANSWER: W) The paths are circular and the radius of the proton’s path is larger
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 2 kilogram block lies stationary on a ramp inclined 60° to the horizontal. To two significant figures, what is the minimum coefficient of static friction between the block and the ramp?
ANSWER: 1.7
21. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Tyler the avid Hawaiian zoologist comes up on an organism at the beach with only two germ layers. Which of the following phyla could it belong to?
W) Porifera
X) Cnidaria
Y) Echinodermata
Z) Placozoa
ANSWER: X) Cnidaria
22. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at the Joint Genome Institute are studying the effects of mechanical stress on the relationship between nitrogen fixing bacteria and plants. Which of the following genera are they most likely studying?
W) Staphylococcus
X) Vibrio
Y) Clostridium
Z) Rhizobium
ANSWER: Z) Rhizobium
