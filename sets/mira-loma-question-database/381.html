1. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which numbering system is based off of eight symbols?
W) hexadecimal
X) decimal
Y) octal
Z) binary
ANSWER: Y) octal
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Five people weigh a 5 gram object on a scale and obtain readings of 7.2 grams, 7.1 grams, 7.3 grams, 7.2 grams, and 7.1 grams. This indicates the scale is
W) accurate
X) precise
Y) both accurate and precise
Z) neither accurate nor precise
ANSWER: X) precise
