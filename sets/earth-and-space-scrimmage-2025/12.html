Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Spectral distortions in the cosmic microwave background are due to what specific type of scattering, by which photons gain energy through collisions with high energy electrons?
ANSWER: Inverse Compton Scattering
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three periods during which the universe was opaque 1) Grand unification epoch 2) Dark ages 3) Inflation
ANSWER: 1 and 3
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Imbrications are most likely to be observed in which of the following types of rocks?
W) Conglomerate
X) Marble
Y) Obsidian
Z) Sandstone
ANSWER: W) Conglomerate
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Fluctus clouds, which are short-lived and resemble the shape of breaking waves, result from what type of instability?
ANSWER: Kelvin-Helmholtz instability
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Bowen's ratio describes the ratio of sensible heat flux to latent heat flux in an environment. Order the following 3 environments from lowest to highest Bowen's ratios 1) Open ocean 2) Temperate forest 3) Desert Playa
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following statements that are true regarding the Madden-Julian oscillation 1) It propagates westward in the Pacific and Indian oceans 2) Its effects are more prominent over cold water 3) It operates on a shorter timescale than El Nino Southern Oscillation
ANSWER: 3 only
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following statements that are true regarding cotidal maps 1) In the Southern Hemisphere, all cotidal lines rotate counterclockwise 2) There is exactly one amphidromic point in each ocean 3) The number on the cotidal line indicates the period between high tides
ANSWER: None
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the formation of boudinage?
W) Tensional forces break competent layers of rock into segments
X) Tensional forces break incompetent layers into segments
Y) Compressional forces break competent layers of rock into segments
Z) Compressional forces break incompetent layers into segments
ANSWER: W) Tensional forces break competent layers of rock into segments
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following species which lunar maria would be enriched in relative to the lunar highlands 1) Silica 2) Alumina 3) Calcium
ANSWER: 3 only
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 terrestrial planets in increasing strength of their Polar Vortices 1) Venus 2) Earth 3) Mars
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following fault types can not be properly displayed on a stereonet?
W) Backthrust
X) Growth
Y) Listric
Z) Oblique
ANSWER: Y) Listric
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Minerals such as zircon are particularly good for radiometric dating as their isotopic ratios are rarely disturbed by partial melting. This is because they have a high value of what quantity?
ANSWER: Closure temperature
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following tectonic environments primarily produces melts as a result of a significant lowering of the solidus?
W) Mid-ocean ridge
X) Volcanic hotspot
Y) Island arc
Z) Igneous intrusions
ANSWER: Y) Island arc
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Torino scale is used to categorize risk associated with which of the following natural phenomena?
W) Asteroid impact
X) Coronal mass ejection
Y) Tsunami
Z) Nuclear winter
ANSWER: W) Asteroid impact
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What proposed candidate for dark matter in the galactic halo is theorized to be composed of low-luminosity objects such as brown dwarfs and neutron stars that have yet to be observed?
ANSWER: MACHOs
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The amount of time required to exhaust a main sequence star’s core hydrogen supply is most closely proportional to which of the following powers of the star’s luminosity?
W) 7/2
X) -7/2
Y) 5/7
Z) -5/7
ANSWER: Z) -5/7
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Due to its lack of an intrinsic magnetic field, Venus lacks which of the following in comparison to Earth?
W) Bow shock
X) Ionosphere
Y) Van Allen belts
Z) Magnetosphere
ANSWER: Y) Van Allen belts
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Planet X, an ideal blackbody, has been discovered to have twice the radius of Earth while orbiting 180° ahead of earth in the same orbital track. In terms of Earth’s emission power P and considering the fact that Earth is not a perfect blackbody, which of the following best describes the power of planet X?
W) Between 1 and 2 P
X) Between 2 and 3 P
Y) Between 3 and 4 P
Z) Between 4 and 5 P
ANSWER: Z) Between 4 and 5 P
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A pumice is formed with vesicles from gases trapped inside the lava upon solidification. A carbon-rich fluid later flows through the vesicles and precipitates calcite. These calcite inclusions are best described as which of the following?
W) Amygdules
X) Phenocrysts
Y) Oligoclase
Z) Porphyroblast
ANSWER: W) Amygdules
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In hydrology, what equation characterizes the conservation of mass between the bed and transported sediments in a stream?
ANSWER: Exner equation
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Einstein–Rosen Bridge theory predicts the existence of what class of theoretical objects?
W) Quark stars
X) Magnetic monopoles
Y) Planet nine
Z) Wormholes
ANSWER: Z) Wormholes
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The distance predicted by the D Sigma relation most deviates from the true distance for which of the following objects?
W) Rapidly Rotating E0 Galaxy
X) Slowly Rotating E0 Galaxy
Y) Rapidly Rotating E5 Galaxy
Z) Slowly Rotating E5 Galaxy
ANSWER: Y) Rapidly Rotating E5 Galaxy
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In a thunderstorm, entrainment of air with which of the following properties would yield the greatest decrease in buoyancy?
W) Warm and dry
X) Warm and moist
Y) Cold and dry
Z) Cold and moist
ANSWER: Y) Cold and dry
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four bedforms that can be observed on a stream bed by increasing flow velocity that they indicate 1) Planar cross-bedding 2) Planar cross-lamination 3) Planar lamination
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Under which of the following conditions is anatexis most likely to occur?
W) Low pressure, low temperature
X) Low pressure, high temperature
Y) High pressure, high temperature
Z) High pressure, low temperature
ANSWER: Y) High pressure, high temperature
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 4 minerals by increasing thermodynamic stability at crustal pressure and temperature 1) sanidine 2) leucite 3) microcline
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The rossby number describes the ratio of inertial forces to the coriolis force in the atmosphere. Order the following systems by increasing magnitude of their Rossby number 1) midlatitude cyclone 2) Hadley circulation 3) tornado vortex
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 forms of precipitation in increasing radar reflectivity 1) Rainbursts 2) Light rain 3) Snow
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three changes to a star that, if increased alone, would increase the radius of its Stromgen sphere 1) Surface gravity 2) Surface temperature 3) Radius
ANSWER: 2 and 3
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Along with surface radiation cooling, what process is thought to primarily drive the cooling of neutron stars and white dwarfs through the emission of neutrinos?
ANSWER: Urca process
