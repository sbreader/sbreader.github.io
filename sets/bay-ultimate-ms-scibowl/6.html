1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In the sodium potassium antiporter, also known as the NaK pump, how much Na+ and K+ are transported per ATP?
W) 3 Na+, 2 K+
X) 3 Na+, 1 K+
Y) 2 Na+, 3 K+
Z) 1 Na+, 1 K+
ANSWER: W) 3 Na+, 2 K+
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the hormone most associated with the acid growth hypothesis in plants?
ANSWER: IAA (also accept: Auxins, indole-3-acetic acid)
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes specific gravity?
W) The gravitational force at a certain point in space
X) The force exerted by a body of water on a submerged object
Y) The fifth dimension described in Einstein's Theory of Special Relativity
Z) The ratio of the density of a material to that of water under standard conditions
ANSWER: Z) The ratio of the density of a material to that of water under standard conditions
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following reactions removes a halogen and a hydrogen atom from adjacent carbon atoms in a haloalkane?
W) Dehydrogenation
X) Dehalogenation
Y) Halogenation
Z) Dehydrohalogenation
ANSWER: Z) Dehydrohalogenation
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The planet Radon is in a circular orbit around Proxima A at a radius of R. If R is quadrupled and the mass of Radon is quadrupled, by what factor is the orbital period of the system multiplied by?
W) 1
X) 4
Y) 8
Z) 16
ANSWER: Y) 8
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer A circuit consists of a 3-Volt battery connected to a 4-Ohm resistor. What is the power dissipated by this circuit in Joules per second?
ANSWER: 9/4 (also accept: 2.25)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following rocks is classified as igneous?
W) Limestone
X) Shale
Y) Basalt
Z) Chert
ANSWER: Y) Basalt
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What portion of the Earth's interior is composed of the crust and the upper mantle and is contrasted with the asthenosphere?
ANSWER: Lithosphere

W) log(9/8)
X) log(10)
Y) log(27)
Z) log(208)
ANSWER: Y) log(27)

ANSWER: 2211
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which of the following are names for models of enzymatic activity? 1. Fluid mosaic 2. Lock and key 3. Induced fit
ANSWER: 2 and 3
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term to describe a population with constant allele and genotype frequencies?
ANSWER: Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements has the highest ionization energy?
W) Lithium
X) Sodium
Y) Potassium
Z) Rubidium
ANSWER: W) Lithium
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 atoms which violate the Aufbau principle. 1. Titanium 2. Chromium 3. Copper 4. Zinc
ANSWER: 2, 3
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Naman is studying economics and wants to understand the elasticity of demand. However, instead of finding an elasticity graph with price and quantity, his graph has stress on the y-axis and strain on the x-axis. What is the term for the slope of this graph?
ANSWER: Young's Modulus (also accept: Elastic Modulus)
8. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the difference between transverse and longitudinal waves?
W) Transverse waves require a medium while longitudinal waves don't
X) Longitudinal waves require a medium while transverse waves don't
Y) Transverse waves oscillate parallel to the wave while longitudinal waves oscillate perpendicular to the wave
Z) Longitudinal waves oscillate parallel to the wave while transverse waves oscillate perpendicular to the wave
ANSWER: Z) Longitudinal waves oscillate parallel to the wave while transverse waves oscillate perpendicular to the wave

X) Quartz

11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of bond is formed between two amino acids, linking the amine group of one amino acid to the carboxyl group of the other and producing one water molecule?
