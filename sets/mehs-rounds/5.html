1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A falling object is no longer experiencing any acceleration. If the gravitational force on the object is 600 newtons, giving your answer in newtons, what is the drag force on the same object?
ANSWER: 600
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer How many quantum numbers are used to describe an electron confined in a two-dimensional electron trap?
ANSWER: 3
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In a reaction mechanism, a substance first appears in the reactants side of a chemical step, then is used up later. Which of the following terms best describes this substance, which does not appear in the rate law?
W) Intermediate
X) Oxidizing agent
Y) Catalyst
Z) Grignard r eagen t
ANSWER: Y) Catalyst
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding Bronsted-Lowry acids and bases 1) A Bronsted-Lowry base is a proton donor 2) The chloride ion is the conjugate base of hydrochloric acid 3) The ammonium ion is a weak acid.
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab are researching how changes in environment conditions can lead to an increase in tree death and possibly stunt the regrowth. What type of succession is demonstrated when trees in a forest regrow after a forest fire?
ANSWER: SECONDARY SUCCESSION
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at SLAC National Accelerator Lab are conducting research on the early universe by studying signs of inflation. Order the following 4 events from the EARLIEST to the LATEST in the universe’s evolution 1) Galaxy formation 2) Star formation 3) Reionization 4) Accelerated expansion.
ANSWER: 2,1,3,4 (also accept: STAR FORMATION, GALAXY FORMATION, REIONIZATION, ACCELERATED EXPANSION)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of metamorphism occurs around intrusive igneous rocks and forms when a magma body enters the upper part of the crust, converting sedimentary rocks into metamorphic rocks?
ANSWER: CONTACT METAMORPHISM
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three sources where terrigenous sediments could originate from 1) Volcanoes 2) Grinding by glaciers 3) Chemical deposits.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
