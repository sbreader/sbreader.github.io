1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the systematic name for baking soda?
W) Sodium Biphosphate
X) Calcium Carbonate
Y) Sodium Hydrogen Carbonate
Z) Disodium Phosphate
ANSWER: Y) Sodium Hydrogen Carbonate
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If Raul has a block of solid carbon dioxide, what is the common name of the substance he has?
ANSWER: DRY ICE
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the bond that gives water its high specific heat?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN BOND (S)
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many hydrogen bonds can the oxygen atom of one molecule of water form?
ANSWER: 2
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following will NOT replace Hydrogen in a Single Displacement Reaction?
W) Magnesium
X) Sodium
Y) Potassium
Z) Copper
ANSWER: Z) Copper
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Elements from which group on the Periodic Table can replace any ion below it in a Single Replacement Reaction?
ANSWER: HALOGENS (also accept: 7A)
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer If Julio has one molecule of hexane, how many more Hydrogen atoms does he have than Carbon atoms?
ANSWER: 8
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Is C2H4an alkane, alkene or alkyne?
ANSWER: ALKENE
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What was the name of the scientist who developed the infamous oil drop experiment?
W) John Dalton
X) Robert Millikan
Y) Niels Bohr
Z) Max Planck
ANSWER: X) Robert Millikan
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In Rutherford's Gold Foil Experiment, what was the name of the particle that bounced back after being fired at the gold foil?
ANSWER: ALPHA PARTICLE
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the common name for C2H6O2?
ANSWER: ETHYLENE GLYCOL
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of a reaction that will occur at any given time without the input of energy?
ANSWER: SPONTANEOUS REACTION
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice When there are too few neutrons and too many protons, what type of radioactive decay ensues?
W) Alpha Decay
X) Beta Decay
Y) Positron Emission
Z) Gamma Decay
ANSWER: Y) Positron Emission
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If a radioactive substance with a half-life of 40 years decays for 80 years what percentage of the original sample will remain?
ANSWER: 25%
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the measure of energy required to completely separate a mole of solid ionic compound into its separate ions?
ANSWER: LATTICE ENERGY
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer As lattice energy increases, what happens to the ionic bond?
ANSWER: IT STRENGTHENS (also accept: IT BECOMES STRONGER)
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Nonmetals tend to do what with electrons when they form bonds?
W) Give Them Up
X) Gain Them
Y) Turn Them Into Positrons
Z) Do Nothing With Them
ANSWER: X) Gain Them
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What happens to ionization energy as you go towards the top right of the periodic table?
W) Increases
X) Decreases
Y) Stays the Same
Z) Is Variable
ANSWER: W) Increases
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the conjugate pair of HCl?
ANSWER: CHLORIDE ION (Cl-)
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name given to the strong acid or base of known identity and concentration which is added to an unknown acid or base solution in an acid-base titration?
ANSWER: TITRANT
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following affect both the frequency of collision, as well as the energy of collisions?
W) Concentration of Reactants
X) Nature of Reactants
Y) Catalysts
Z) Temperature
ANSWER: Z) Temperature
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Catalysts increase the rate of reaction by lowering which energy?
ANSWER: ACTIVATION ENERGY
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the oxidation state for any Alkali Metal involved in a compound?
ANSWER: +1
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer At what electrode does Oxidation occur in an electrochemical cell?
ANSWER: ANODE
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the part of the vessel that neutralizes excess charge buildup in a redox reaction?
ANSWER: SALT BRIDGE
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In which type of electrochemical cell must energy be supplied for a reaction to occur?
ANSWER: ELECTROLYTIC CELL
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer An ester is essentially which 2 other functional groups put together?
ANSWER: KETONE AND ETHER
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In an esterification reaction, an organic acid reacts with what generic organic compound to produce an ester and water?
ANSWER: ALCOHOL
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What section of the electromagnetic spectrum is situated in the 100- 400 nanometer wavelength range?
W) Near Infrared
X) Far Infrared
Y) Visible Light
Z) Ultraviolet
ANSWER: Z) Ultraviolet
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In which type of reaction is a bond broken as a molecule absorbs a photon of light energy?
ANSWER: PHOTODISSOCIATION (also accept: PHOTO-LYSIS/-DECOMPOSITION)
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In an acidic solution, what color will a litmus test show?
ANSWER: RED (also accept: PINK/MAGENTA)
16. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In a flame test, a violet flame would mean which of the following gases was being tested?
W) Potassium
X) Barium
Y) Sodium
Z) Calcium
ANSWER: W) Potassium
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the formula for Sodium Azide?
ANSWER: NaN3
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Balance the following Chemical Equation: Fe + Cl2 = FeCl3
ANSWER: 2, 3, 2
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer When a liquids viscosity reaches zero, then it transforms into what separate phase of matter?
ANSWER: SUPERFLUID
18. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice A substance is considered superconductive when it has no inner magnetic field and also when what value is at exactly zero?
W) Temperature
X) Pressure
Y) Electrical Potential
Z) Electric Resistance
ANSWER: Z) Electric Resistance
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice For a molecule to be considered aromatic, it must obey which of the following rules?
W) Hess's Law
X) Hückel's Rule
Y) Hammond-Leffler Postulate
Z) Henry's Law
ANSWER: X) Hückel's Rule
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Hückel's Rule states that a molecule is aromatic only when it follows what expression, which must equal the number of π electrons?
ANSWER: 4n+2
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The Wave Model describes electrons in what singular body, moving in orbitals?
ANSWER: CLOUDS
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which principle states that no two electrons can occupy the same quantum state simultaneously?
ANSWER: PAULI EXCLUSION PRINCIPLE
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Who discovered the electron?
ANSWER: J.J. THOMSON
21. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the electron configuration for Carbon?
ANSWER: 1s2 2s2 2p2 (also accept: [He] 2s2 2p2)
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A molecule of Carbon in the form of a tube, hollow sphere, or ellipsoid is classified under what general allotropic term?
ANSWER: FULLERENE
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A fullerene that is spherical is classified under what specific class of fullerene?
ANSWER: BUCKYBALL(S)
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What part of a solution is usually present in greater amounts?
W) Liquid
X) Gas
Y) Solute
Z) Solvent
ANSWER: Z) Solvent
23. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Milk is an example of what type of liquid solution?
ANSWER: COLLOID
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Any substance that contains free ions that causes the substance to become electrically conductive is known as what?
ANSWER: ELECTROLYTE
24. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which type of electrolyte is specifically a gel in a lattice framework?
ANSWER: DRY ELECTROLYTES
25. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice If an ion contains unpaired electrons, what type of ion is it?
W) Radical
X) Charged
Y) Neutral
Z) Cation
ANSWER: W) Radical
25. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When metal cations and nonmetal anions are attracted to each other, they form what type of chemical substance?
ANSWER: SALT
