1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer This phenomenon limits the functioning of transistors today. Name this phenomenon that is also observed in alpha decay of nuclei.

1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Alpha decay is used in smoke detectors, which use Americium-241 as an alpha particle source. What other isotope, the decay product of Americium would also be found in smoke detectors?
ANSWER: NEPTUNIUM-237
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What object is the most geologically active body in the solar system, due to its proximity to the planet it orbits?
ANSWER: IO
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The interior of Jupiter is mostly composed of what peculiar, conductive liquid.
ANSWER: METALLIC HYDROGEN
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer At constant volume and a constant number of particles, the inverse of temperature can be expressed as the partial derivative of what quantity with respect to internal energy?
ANSWER: ENTROPY

ANSWER: 4.2 × 10-23

ANSWER: SMOOTH ENDOPLASMIC RETICULUM (also accept: ER)

7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at the IceCube Observatory study cosmic rays by detecting and analyzing the millions of particles that are formed in the upper atmosphere. These cosmic rays notably produce what unstable isotope from interactions with nitrogen in the upper atmosphere?
ANSWER: CARBON-14
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Ames National Labs plans on opening the first Dynamic Nuclear Polarization NMR spectrometer, which will be able to overcome standard NMRs weakness of intrinsically low sensitivity. Which of the following best describes how this will be overcome?
W) The spectrometer will use shorter wavelengths of light as well as increasing the intensity, resulting in higher resolution
X) Much more powerful magnets will be used to align the atomic nuclei, resulting in a much more powerful response from atomic nuclei
Y) Microwaves will be used to excite unpaired electrons which will transfer spin polarization to the nuclei, resulting in stronger responses
Z) Specific wavelengths of light will be used to raise the energy level of electrons in the atoms, these excited state atoms give a stronger response in NMR
ANSWER: Y) Microwaves will be used to excite unpaired electrons which will transfer spin polarization to the nuclei, resulting in stronger responses

12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at Argonne National Lab are studying myosin in tarantulas using APS. Which of the following accurately compares how myosin is activated in tarantulas versus how it is activated in humans.

13. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes why population III (three) stars have been predicted to have very large masses.
W) The higher metallic content leads to higher density cores that are able to attract and hold more matter in the star
X) Population III stars form in areas that are very rich in hydrogen and helium, so the stars are able to grow larger
