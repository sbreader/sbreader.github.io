1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A charged particle moves at a constant velocity into a large region of space containing a constant magnetic field. Which of the following is not a possible flight path for the charged particle?
W) A straight line
X) A circle
Y) A spiral
Z) A non-circular ellipse
ANSWER: Z) A non-circular ellipse
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Rank the following three temperatures from lowest to highest: 1)-41° F, 2)-41° C, 3) 241° K
ANSWER: 2,1,3
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory are sequencing the bacteria Catenulispora acidiphila to obtain specific enzymes from the bacteria that can be used in future production of biofuels. What is the name for the group of enzymes that these scientists are looking for, which catalyzes the breakdown of the most abundant organic compound on Earth?
ANSWER: CELLULASE
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer The bacteria that these researchers used, Catenulispora acidiphilia, belongs to what gram-positive phylum of bacteria that are noted for their economic importance?
ANSWER: ACTINOBACTERIA

X) HSO4-

Z) CO32-

ANSWER: 6

ANSWER: −19/49
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Evaporites are a type of ocean sediment that form when evaporation leads to seawater becoming saturated with minerals, which then begin to precipitate. Which of the following categories of ocean sediment classifications do evaporites fall under?
W) terrigenous sediments
X) biogenous sediments
Y) hydrogenous sediments
Z) turbidites
ANSWER: Y) hydrogenous sediments
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is true of calcareous ooze, a common biogenous sediment?
W) calcareous ooze forms in waters deeper than 4500 meters where higher levels of carbon dioxide completely dissolve organic material
X) calcareous ooze forms when organisms living in the deep ocean die and bacteria decomposes their remains
Y) calcareous ooze is a slush slightly more viscous than seawater filled with microscopic particles of dead organisms
Z) calcareous ooze forms when organisms living near the surface of the ocean die and their remains are partially dissolved by seawater
ANSWER: Z) calcareous ooze forms when organisms living near the surface of the ocean die and their remains are partially dissolved by seawater
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice An international team of scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory are studying a species of fungi that is unique for its possession of lignases. Which of the following best explains the effect that these fungi would have on coal formation?
W) Decrease
X) Slightly Increase
Y) Greatly Increase
Z) No Effect
ANSWER: W) Decrease
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The introduction of this species about 300 million years ago is believed to be a likely cause of the decrease in coal formation after what geologic time period, which is named for the large coal deposits that were formed during this time?
ANSWER: CARBONIFEROUS (do not accept: MISSISSIPPIAN OR PENNSYLVANIAN)
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all the following 4 traits that are characteristic of monocots 1) One cotyledon 2) Netlike veins 3) Taproot root system 4) Scatter vascular tissue

W) Monocots
X) Dicots
Y) Basal Angiosperms

9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The orbital period of an object orbiting around a much larger body is T, and the semi-major axis of the orbit is R. In terms of R, what could the semi-major axis be increased to in order to multiply the period by 27?
ANSWER: 9R (do not accept: 9)
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer For a planet, what quantitative effect does a doubling in radius while keeping the mass constant have on the density to gravitational acceleration ratio?
ANSWER: HALVES (also accept: doubling the radius halves the ratio of density to gravitation acceleration)

11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In terms of pi, what is the lateral surface area of a cone with base diameter 8 and height 3?
ANSWER: 20π
11. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the theorem that states that the product of the distances from the intersection points of a line and a given circle to a given point remains constant for all lines through the given point that intersect the given circle?
ANSWER: POWER OF A POINT
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes how an elliptical galaxy forms?
W) forms as a spiral galaxy ages and rotation starts to slow, eventually morphing into an elliptical galaxy
X) forms as two or more smaller spiral galaxies collide with each other and merge to become an elliptical galaxy
Y) forms as the bulge width of a disk galaxy decreases while the halo lengthens due to a weakening of gravitational forces, creating an elliptical galaxy
Z) forms as a section of a large disk galaxy is cut off due to a massive cataclysmic event, possibly forming an elliptical galaxy
ANSWER: X) forms as two or more smaller spiral galaxies collide with each other and merge to become an elliptical galaxy
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the collective name for the two spiral galaxies located in the constellation Coma Berenices characterized by their long tails and which are likely to merge with each other within the next billion years?
ANSWER: MICE GALAXIES (also accept: NGC 4676)

14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at the Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Laboratory are developing new imaging tools named PhytoPET and PhytoBeta, which allow for better imaging of plants. ANSWER: PHOTOSYNTHESIS

Given that these imaging tools detect positron emissions, what isotope of carbon is used in this research?
ANSWER: CARBON-11
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Objects A and B both have mass 18 kg. They are sliding directly toward each other on a frictionless surface. A has speed 78 m/s while B has speed 80 m/s. The collision between them is perfectly inelastic. What is the final speed of object B?
ANSWER: 1 m/s

X) It affects both the forward and reverse reaction rate

ANSWER: X) It affects both the forward and reverse reaction rate

W) The more electronegative material.
X) The less electronegative material.

Z) The material with a smaller coefficient of kinetic friction.

21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a long, narrow, rigid structure that points outwards at a 90 degree angle from the shore and is designed to prevent the erosion of beaches by trapping sand particles before they are carried out to sea by longshore currents?
W) flood barrier
X) tackifier
Y) groin

21. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Tidal resonance is a phenomenon that can occur in semi-enclosed bodies of water when the frequency of the tides coming in and out match with the seiche of the body of water. Which of the following best explains what seiche is?
W) a standing wave found in a body of water that creates a natural rocking motion within the semi-enclosed area
X) an occasional influx of water caused by the build up and subsequent release of energy from various natural sources in the form of a wave heading away from shore
Y) a surface current caused by differences in the salinity of water found near and far from the shore
Z) a surface current caused by differences in temperature between colder waters found deeper in the semi-enclosed body of water and warmer waters found closer to the surface
ANSWER: W) a standing wave found in a body of water that creates a natural rocking motion within the semi-enclosed area
