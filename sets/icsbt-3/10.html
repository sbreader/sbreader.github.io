1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Shreyas is a long-chain fatty acid. Long-chain fatty acids such as himself must enter the mitochondrial matrix before undergoing beta-oxidation, but Shreyas is not able to directly cross the inner mitochondrial membrane. Instead, Shreyas must use what transport system to move their fatty acyl groups across the inner membrane?
ANSWER: Carnitine shuttle
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What destructive contagious disease caused by the bacterium Erwinia amylovora is known to primarily attack the family Rosaceae, including fruits such as apples and pears?
ANSWER: Fire blight
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Akhil is learning about pericyclic reactions and wants to carry out a reaction with 1,3-butadiene and acetylene. What conformation must the 1,3-butadiene adopt to react?
W) s-cis
X) s-trans
Y) cis
Z) trans
ANSWER: W) s-cis (do not accept: cis)
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What rules, stemming from conservation of orbital symmetry, can help Akhil find out if a reaction is thermally or photochemically allowed?
ANSWER: Woodward-Hoffmann rules
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Strong horizontal temperature gradients in the midlatitudes contain available potential energy that can be converted into the kinetic energy of growing cyclones. What instability drives this conversion?
ANSWER: Baroclinic instability

ANSWER: 2, 3, 1

TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Einstein famously said "God doesn't play dice with the universe" in reference to his theory of local realism, which is the idea that information cannot transfer faster than light, and that the "spooky action at a distance" of entanglement is just an illusion. 7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice An ideal gas is compressed and loses heat to its surroundings. Assuming this process is polytropic, which of the following best describes the value of the polytropic index?
W) Less than zero

Y) Between 1 and 5/3
Z) Between 5/3 and infinity

7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A quantized box is separated by a thin, impermeable barrier into two equal compartments. Each side of the box has six possible particle locations, of which two are occupied by a xenon atom with the rest empty. The barrier is suddenly removed and the atoms move freely in the box. In terms of the Boltzmann constant kB, what is the change in the gas molecules' total entropy when the barrier is removed?
ANSWER: kB ln(22/5)

Y) Alkaline

8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice An ionized nebula exhibits strong forbidden [O III] emission at relatively low electron density. As the electron density increases beyond the critical density of the transition, the forbidden line becomes weaker relative to an appropriate permitted recombination line. Which of the following best explains this behavior?
W) The nebula becomes completely neutral, eliminating all oxygen ions
X) Collisional de-excitation begins to dominate over spontaneous radiative decay of the metastable upper level
Y) The transition becomes electric-dipole allowed as the electron density increases
Z) Higher electron density prevents oxygen ions from occupying excited states under all conditions
ANSWER: X) Collisional de-excitation begins to dominate over spontaneous radiative decay of the metastable upper level

11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Raymond recently bought a new Rolex that caused him to go into debt. Because of this, he decided to become an evolutionary biologist. While Raymond was at work, he was trying to figure out how long ago two distinct species diverged from a common ancestor. To do so, he counts the number of genetic mutations in both the DNA and RNA samples. This technique, which is similar to his Rolex counting time, is commonly known as what?
ANSWER: Molecular clock
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice During a Mitsunobu reaction at a stereogenic alcohol center, which combination of changes is expected?
W) Retention of configuration and replacement of OH
X) Inversion of configuration and replacement of OH

Z) Elimination to an alkene only
ANSWER: X) Inversion of configuration and replacement of OH
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Archean sedimentary sulfides commonly exhibit nonzero Δ33S values that cannot be explained by ordinary mass-dependent isotope fractionation. The widespread disappearance of these signatures later in Earth's history provides evidence for a major change in atmospheric composition. Which of the following best explains both the original isotopic signatures and their eventual disappearance?
W) Sulfur-33 was produced by radioactive decay until Earth's mantle became chemically differentiated
X) Biological sulfate reduction became impossible after oxygen concentrations increased
Y) Ultraviolet-driven atmospheric sulfur photochemistry produced mass-independent fractionation, which was suppressed as atmospheric oxygen and ozone concentrations increased
Z) Plate tectonics progressively eliminated all sulfur-bearing minerals from Earth's crust
ANSWER: Y) Ultraviolet-driven atmospheric sulfur photochemistry produced mass-independent fractionation, which was suppressed as atmospheric oxygen and ozone concentrations increased
