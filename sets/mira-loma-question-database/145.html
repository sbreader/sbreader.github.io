1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In an isothermal process, change in internal energy is equal to what, in reference to heat added and work?
ANSWER: ZERO
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Adding an inert gas into a gas-phase equilibrium at constant volume will result in an equilibrium shift in which direction?
W) the equilibrium will shift towards the side with fewer moles of gas
X) the equilibrium will shift towards the side with more moles of gas
Y) the equilibrium will not shift
Z) it depends on the gas
ANSWER: Y) the equilibrium will not shift
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify the following particle: this particle would be the only scalar elementary particle in nature. This particle, according to the standard model, consists of two charged and two neutral component fields. Both of the charged fields are Goldstone bosons, which explain the interaction with the massive gauge bosons. This boson has no spin and no angular momentum and explains the difference between the massless photon and the massive gauge bosons and is the particle of interest for the Large Hadron Collider.
ANSWER: HIGG'S BOSON
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer Name this subset of fermions that do not interact via the strong interaction, have antiparticles that are only opposite in charge and number, and contain the set of neutral particles known as neutrinos.
ANSWER: LEPTONS
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which second messenger is a classic example of lipid signaling, created by the cleaving phosphatidylinositol bisphophate by phospholipase C and causes the release of calcium ions from the cytoplasm?
ANSWER: INOSITOL TRIPHOSPHATE
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which endocrine gland resides within the sphenoid bone, produces many of the tropic hormones in the human body, and is also called the hypophysis?
