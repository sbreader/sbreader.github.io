Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Charlie has a rectangular pool in his backyard, with sides of lengths 12 and 8. He wants to add a concrete sidewalk of width 2 around the pool. When he is finished, what is the total area of the sidewalk?
ANSWER: 96
1. BONUS Math Short Answer If the graph of y equals the second-degree polynomial p of x contains the 3 points (0, 0), (4, –24), and (6, 0), what is p of x?
ANSWER: 3x2-18x (also accept: y = 3x2-18x)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many total electrons does an atom of neutral beryllium possess?
ANSWER: 4
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 6-ohm and an 8-ohm resistor are connected in parallel, and then this circuit is connected in series to a 5-ohm resistor. In ohms, what is the equivalent resistance of this circuit?
ANSWER: 59/7 (also accept: 83/7)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Milky Way and many other spiral galaxies have a linear structure passing through the galactic center that then spawns spiral arms. What is the term for this type of spiral galaxy?
ANSWER: BARRED SPIRAL (also accept: SB; SPIRAL BARRED)
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three stellar arrangements that would produce a continuous spectrum 1) A black body 2) A star that is obscured by a cloud of gas 3) A cloud of gas that is excited by radiation from a nearby star.
ANSWER: 1 only
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Scientists supported by the Department of Energy are studying vibrational energy levels in molecules. What region of the electromagnetic spectrum are they using to probe these energy levels?
W) Visible
X) Ultraviolet
Y) Radio
Z) Infrared
ANSWER: Z) Infrared
4. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Scientists supported by the Department of Energy are studying piezoelectricity, which is a phenomenon typically demonstrated by what type of material?
W) Liquids
X) Gases
Y) Ceramics
Z) Plasmas
ANSWER: Y) Ceramics
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What gene elements are removed from mRNA through the process of splicing?
W) Poly-A tails
X) Promoters
Y) Exons
Z) Introns
ANSWER: Z) Introns
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Kate is constructing a cladogram that includes angiosperms, conifers, ferns, and mosses. Which of the following is most likely to be an ancestral trait of this cladogram?
W) Flowers
X) Stems
Y) Vasculature
Z) Seeds
ANSWER: X) Stems
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The rate at which a factory packs boxes is proportional to the square of the number of people working. If one person packs 8 boxes an hour, how many boxes will 3 people pack in one hour?
ANSWER: 72
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term used to describe compounds with the same chemical formula but different arrangements of atoms?
ANSWER: ISOMERS
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The maximum amount of elemental barium allowed in drinking water by the EPA is 2 milligrams per liter. Barium has an atomic mass of 140 grams per mole. In millimoles and to two significant figures, what is the maximum amount of barium allowed in one liter of drinking water?
ANSWER: 0.014
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Ferro-magnetic minerals in rocks can record important details of Earth’s magnetic field in the past. When these rocks are metamorphosed at high temperatures, they can lose this record. What is the name of the temperature at which this record of Earth’s magnetic field is lost?
ANSWER: CURIE TEMPERATURE (also accept: CURIE POINT; CURIE)
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Place the following three seafloor regions in order of increasing ocean water depth 1) Abyssal plain 2) Oceanic ridge 3) Oceanic trench.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab are part of a collaboration to study quark-gluon plasma. Which of the following statements is true of gluons?
W) They are leptons
X) They are fermions
Y) They are bosons
Z) They are mesons
ANSWER: Y) They are bosons
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer As a part of the DUNE collaboration, Fermilab scientists are searching for neutrinos that only interact with other matter via gravity. What is the term for these neutrinos?
ANSWER: STERILE NEUTRINOS (also accept: INERT NEUTRINOS; STERILE)
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the openings on plant leaves that allow gas exchange with the atmosphere?
ANSWER: STOMATA (also accept: STOMA)
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer Place the following three neurons in chronological order of activation during a reflex arc 1) Interneuron 2) Motor neuron 3) Sensory neuron.
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The point (–5, 3) is reflected through the line y = x and then reflected through the y-axis. What are the coordinates of the resulting point?
ANSWER: (–3, –5) (also accept: x = –3 AND y = –5)
11. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area of a circle that circumscribes a rectangle with sides of lengths 12 and 14?
ANSWER: 85π
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The Archimedes Principle is most related to which of the following concepts?
W) Pressure
X) Energy
Y) Efficiency
Z) Buoyancy
ANSWER: Z) Buoyancy
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer If a mercury barometer reads 760 mm, then, to the nearest whole meter, what value would a water barometer read at the same pressure?
ANSWER: 10
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer NOx and SOx compounds play a significant role in acid rain formation. What are the two primary acids formed by these two groups of compounds?
ANSWER: SULFURIC ACID; NITRIC ACID
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three rock types that are foliated 1) Marble 2) Quartzite 3) Anthracite.
ANSWER: 0 only
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Lab are developing lithium-ion battery cathodes that contain manganese instead of what element found in vitamin B12?
ANSWER: COBALT
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at SLAC National Accelerator Lab are studying stishovite. What process is thought to produce stishovite on the surface of Earth?
W) Meteorite impact
X) Lightning strike
Y) Low-grade metamorphism
Z) High-grade metamorphism
ANSWER: W) Meteorite impact
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The MAP kinase pathway is a signaling cascade pathway where successive proteins become activated. Activation of these proteins is mediated by the addition of what inorganic functional group?
ANSWER: PHOSPHATE (also accept: PO43-)
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three factors that are density-dependent limiting factors to population growth 1) Disease 2) Predation 3) Competition.
ANSWER: 1 and 2 and 3
16. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the largest integer n such that 7 to the n th power divides 50 factorial?
ANSWER: 8
