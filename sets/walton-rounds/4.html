1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name this organ which contains nephrons which may be classified as either juxtamedullary or cortical.
ANSWER: KIDNEY
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following bones is not located in the human wrist?
W) Scaphoid bone
X) Sphenoid bone
Y) Lunate bone
Z) Trapezium bone
ANSWER: X) Sphenoid bone
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The activity series of the chemical elements is experimentally derived through observations of which of the following?
W) Solubility in water
X) Electronegativity
Y) Specific gravity
Z) Reduction potential
ANSWER: Z) Reduction potential
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The standard reduction potential of what element’s electrode is defined to be zero at all temperatures?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN (also accept: H)
3. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice In how many ways can Safi choose 4 of his 8 friends to give a single AirPod to each?
W) 24
X) 70
Y) 252
Z) 1680
ANSWER: X) 70
3. BONUS Math Short Answer How many people must be in a room to ensure that at least two of them have the same birthday? Assume it is not a leap year.
ANSWER: 366
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of these is not a type of rock?
W) Metamorphic
X) Sedimentary
Y) Igneous
Z) Cyleptic
ANSWER: Z) Cyleptic
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals is not associated with the correct rating on Mohs scale of hardness?
W) Quartz-7
X) Calcite-3
Y) Corundum-9
Z) Fluorite-5
ANSWER: Z) Fluorite-5
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What term describes the final speed of an object free falling once resistance prevents further acceleration?
ANSWER: TERMINAL VELOCITY
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer A block of mass 2 kg is initially at rest at the top of an inclined plane which makes an angle of 30 degrees to the horizontal and is 5 feet tall. The block then slides down the ramp. Assuming no friction and the magnitude of g is 10 meters per second squared, what is the magnitude of the force that pulls the block down the ramp?
ANSWER: 10 N
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the correct reason that car exhaust contains nitrogen oxides?
W) Gasoline contains nitrogen impurities.
X) The high temperatures cause nitrogen gas to react with oxygen gas.
Y) Organic matter gets caught in the engine.
Z) Car exhaust does not actually contain any nitrogen oxides.
ANSWER: X) The high temperatures cause nitrogen gas to react with oxygen gas.
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the correct explanation for why element burning in stars cannot produce elements heavier than iron?
W) More energy goes into making elements heavier than iron than is released.
X) No star possesses enough gravity to produce iron.
Y) Any star that has enough mass to produce heavier elements does not live long enough.
Z) Iron has the lowest binding energy per nucleon of all the elements.
ANSWER: W) More energy goes into making elements heavier than iron than is released.
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Gibbs free energy is directly related to enthalpy and which other thermodynamic quantity that measures the level of disorder in a system?
ANSWER: ENTROPY
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following represents the equation for Gibbs Free Energy?
W) ΔG = ΔH-TΔS
X) ΔG = TΔS-2ΔH
Y) ΔG = ΔH*T-ΔS
Z) ΔG = (ΔH*T*ΔS)/2
ANSWER: W) ΔG = ΔH-TΔS
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following animals is believed to have existed earliest?
W) Bats
X) Elephants
Y) Giant tortoise
Z) Jawless fish
ANSWER: Z) Jawless fish
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following plasmid DNA sequences is palindromic?
W) 5'-CTTGCGTA-3'
X) 5'-ATGAAGTA-3'
Y) 5'-CTAGCTAG-3'
Z) 5'-CATGGTAC-3'
ANSWER: Y) 5'-CTAGCTAG-3'
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a Hadron?
W) pion
X) neutron

Z) hyperon
ANSWER: Y) neutrino
9. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice The position of a ball thrown up can be modeled by the equation x(m) = -3t2 + 6t + 2. What is its velocity after 1 second?
W) 2 m/s
X) -1 m/s
Y) 1 m/s
Z) 0 m/s
ANSWER: Z) 0 m/s
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Define the n-tornado to be the product of the first n primes. What is the value of the 4-tornado divided by the 2-tornado?
ANSWER: 35
10. BONUS Math Short Answer 3 coins are flipped simultaneously. What is the probability that two of the coins show heads and the other shows tails?
ANSWER: 3/8
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the galaxy which contains the most recently imaged black hole.
ANSWER: MESSIER 87 (also accept: M87)
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is true for Saturn and Jupiter?
W) Both rotate faster than the Earth.
X) Both rotate slower than the Earth.
Y) Their periods of rotation are linked to their period of revolution.
Z) Only one rotates rapidly while the other rotates very slowly.
ANSWER: W) Both rotate faster than the Earth.
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a function of smooth endoplasmic reticulum?
W) Toxin degradation in the liver
X) Calcium ion storage
Y) Hormone synthesis in brain cells
Z) Hemoglobin synthesis
ANSWER: Z) Hemoglobin synthesis
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following cells certainly does not have plasmodesmata?
W) A fish cell
X) A flower cell
Y) An algal cell
Z) A tree cell
ANSWER: W) A fish cell

ANSWER: WATER, HEAT, ELECTRICITY
13. BONUS Energy Short Answer What term refers to the semipermeable membrane that acts as an electrical insulator and is placed between hydrogen and oxygen gas in the fuel cell?
ANSWER: PROTON-EXCHANGE MEMBRANE (also accept: POLYMER-ELECTROLYTE MEMBRANE)

ANSWER: 40%
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Young’s and Davisson-Germer’s double slit experiments BEST shows which quantum phenomenon?
W) Heisenberg uncertainty
X) Particle nature of light
Y) Quantum tunneling
Z) Wave nature of light
ANSWER: Z) Wave nature of light
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer A ball is thrown horizontally off a cliff at 5 m/s. At the same time, another ball is dropped off a cliff of equal height but 15 meters away from the first. After how many seconds will the two balls hit each other?
ANSWER: 3 SECONDS

X) Nitrogen
Y) Carbon
Z) Hydrogen
ANSWER: W) Oxygen
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A solid copper sphere has a total charge of plus one Coulomb. Where on the sphere does the charge reside?
W) The charge is evenly spread throughout the sphere
X) The charge is at the center of the sphere
Y) The charge is on the outer surface of the sphere
Z) The charge lies outside of the sphere
ANSWER: Y) The charge is on the outer surface of the sphere
