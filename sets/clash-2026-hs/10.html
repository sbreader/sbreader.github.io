1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In the canonical ensemble, what is the quantity that represents the sum over all possible microstates of a system, weighing each state by its likelihood, and from which all thermodynamic properties can be derived?
ANSWER: Partition function
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer For a fluid with a constant bulk modulus and constant density, the speed of a longitudinal wave is initially 300 meters per second. If we increase the pressure change in the moving fluid by a factor of 2, what is the new speed of a longitudinal wave in the fluid?
ANSWER: 300
2. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Triple integrals are scary! However, we can break apart each triple integral into 3 steps, by integrating each variable individually. Which of the following theorems describes this result?
W) Fubini's theorem
X) Leibniz's theorem
Y) Stokes' theorem
Z) Pappus' theorem
ANSWER: W) Fubini's theorem
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Evaluate the definite integral from 0 to 2π of cos2(x) in respect to x.
ANSWER: π
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the special type of C4 plant tissue arrangement where photosynthetic cells are arranged in a ring around vascular bundles, allowing for separation of carbon fixation steps?
ANSWER: Kranz anatomy
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer In nickel affinity chromatography, what heterocycle outcompetes histidine for binding with Nickel and is also found within histidine molecules?

4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice While removing a volatile organic solvent by rotary evaporation, Diego Brando applies a strong vacuum immediately before the flask begins rotating. Which of the following problems is most likely to occur?

ANSWER: Shapiro time delay (also accept: Gravitational time delay)
