1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following sediment characteristics would typically have the highest porosity?
W) Sorted and Angular
X) Sorted and Rounded
Y) Unsorted and Angulalr
Z) Unsorted and Rounded
ANSWER: X) Sorted and Rounded
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Highly unstable atmospheric conditions are most associated with which of the following types of precipitation?
W) Sleet
X) Freezing Rain
Y) Hail
Z) Snow
ANSWER: Y) Hail
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Rank the following three cells by increasing size of their rough ER 1) B cell 2) Erythrocyte 3) T cell.
ANSWER: 2,3,1
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three groups of algae that have sporopollenin 1) Green algae 2) Brown algae 3) Red algae;
ANSWER: 1
3. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the function f(x) = 21/x, defined for all nonzero x?
W) increasing for all nonzero x
X) decreasing for all nonzero x
Y) increasing for all negative x, decreasing for all positive x
Z) decreasing for all negative x, increasing for all positive x
ANSWER: X) decreasing for all nonzero x
3. BONUS Math Short Answer Identify all of the following three numbers that could be even 1) The sum of two odd numbers 2) The ratio between an even number and an odd number 3) An odd number raised to the power of an even number.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following ions has the smallest ionic radius?
W) Aluminum 3 plus
X) Calcium 2 plus
Y) Fluoride 1 minus
Z) Sodium 1 plus
ANSWER: W) Aluminum 3 plus
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Given that the bond dissociation energy for an oxygen-oxygen double bond is 495 kilojoules per mole, then in kilojoules per mole, what is the standard enthalpy of formation for O2?
ANSWER: 0
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A rocket is travelling in an elliptical orbit around the Sun. In order to maximize the resulting increase in its semimajor axis, where should it apply an impulse tangential to its orbit?
W) Perihelion
X) Aphelion
Y) An endpoint of the latus rectum
Z) A covertex
ANSWER: W) Perihelion
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer When water is moving at 2 meters per second through a pipe of radius 6 meters, it has a pressure of 80 kilopascals. If it now moves to a part of the pipe at the same height with radius of 3 meters, and assuming the water is incomprehensible with a uniform density of 1000 kilograms per cubic meter, what is the new pressure, rounded to three significant figures and expressed in kilopascals?
ANSWER: 50
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Joint MIT-Harvard Medical school scientists have been studying the sinoatrial node in the heart in hopes of developing noninvasive pacemakers. In which chamber of the heart is the SA node located?
W) Left atrium
X) Left ventricle
Y) Right atrium
Z) Right ventricle
ANSWER: Y) Right atrium
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The Surendranath Group is studying the conversion of gaseous oxygen to water vapor by a catalytic solid surface. What type of catalysis is this an example of?
ANSWER: HETEROGENEOUS
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following sedimentary rocks is non-clastic?
W) Siltstone
X) Travertine
Y) Conglomerate
Z) Breccia
ANSWER: X) Travertine
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the creation of andesitic magmas?
W) Decompression melting of dry peridotite at a mid-ocean ridge, followed by fractional crystallization of olivine and pyroxene
X) Partial melting of lower continental crust during continental collision, with assimilation of mantle-derived basaltic melts
Y) Direct melting of subducted oceanic basaltic crust at high pressures in the mantle transition zone, with little interaction with the mantle wedge
Z) Partial melting of hydrated peridotite in the mantle wedge above a subducting slab, followed by assimilation of continental crustal material
ANSWER: Z) Partial melting of hydrated peridotite in the mantle wedge above a subducting slab, followed by assimilation of continental crustal material
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Choanoflagellates are considered the sister group to animals because they resemble the feeding cells of what animal phylum?
ANSWER: PORIFERA (do not accept: SPONGES)
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Derek is isolating in vivo dog chromosomes, but he finds that there is a double strand break. Identify all of the following three biological repair mechanisms that are able to repair double strand breaks 1) Nucleotide excision repair 2) Base excision repair 3) Mismatch repair.
ANSWER: NONE
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at the Weiss Group discovered rare iron (III) phosphite deposits on the Martian surface. What is the chemical formula of iron (III) phosphite?
ANSWER: FEPO3
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Climate scientists at MIT are studying tipping point events that could accelerate climate change. What weakening system of ocean currents in the North Atlantic Ocean is the focal point of their research because its collapse could disrupt global climate patterns?
ANSWER: ATLANTIC MERIDIONAL OCEAN CURRENTS (also accept: AMOC)
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A thermodynamic process does 560 joules of work on the enviornment, while 360 joules of heat are transferred to the system. What is the change in internal energy of the environment during the thermodynamic process?
ANSWER: 180
10. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following bonds has the most covalent character, assuming atoms are in their most common oxidation states?
W) Sodium and chlorine
X) Sodium and iodine
Y) Aluminum and chlorine
Z) Aluminum and iodine
ANSWER: Z) Aluminum and iodine
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Ginger observes a neutrino and determines that it is an electron neutrino, but later, she finds that the same neutrino has become a tau neutrino. What behavior is the neutrino exhibiting?
ANSWER: FLAVOR OSCILLATION (also accept: NEUTRINO OSCILLATION)
12. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Jenny connects an AC voltage source to a series RC circuit and measures the voltage across the capacitor. Which of the following best describes the amplitude of this voltage at low and high AC frequencies, respectively?
W) Low, low
X) Low, high
Y) High, low
Z) High, high
ANSWER: Y) High, low
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Tim writes the positive integers from 1 through 100, and calculates their mean by adding them all together and dividing by 100. However, unbeknownst to him, he forgot to include one of the integers. If his final result is an integer, then what number did Tim forget?
ANSWER: 50
13. BONUS Math Short Answer If x2 − y2 = 24 and x − y = 8, then what is the value of xy?
ANSWER: -55/4
14. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Phoebe has a cylinder of uniform density rolling without slipping down an incline. Which of the following options is false regarding this situation?
W) The ramp must have nonzero coefficient of kinetic friction
X) The cylinder would travel faster if it were sliding down a frictionless incline without rolling
Y) The fastest point on the cylinder is the point furthest from the incline
Z) The speed of the cylinder is independent of the coefficients of friction
ANSWER: W) The ramp must have nonzero coefficient of kinetic friction
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer Cyan is in a rocket that leaves the surface of the Earth at one-third its escape velocity. What is the ratio between the kinetic energy of the rocket and the total mechanical energy of the rocket when it first departs from Earth?
ANSWER: 1/8
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer During fertilization, the cortical reaction is triggered by the entry of what ion into the egg?
ANSWER: CA2+ (also accept: CALCIUM)
