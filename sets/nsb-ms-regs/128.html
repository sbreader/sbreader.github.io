Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for a period of time marked with a rapid die-off of many species of organisms?
ANSWER: MASS EXTINCTION (also accept: EXTINCTION EVENT; BIOTIC CRISIS)
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following animals typically moves around through propagating rhythmic contractions known as peristalsis?
W) Shark
X) Squid
Y) Earthworm
Z) Armadillo
ANSWER: Y) Earthworm
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following SI units can be used to measure weight?
W) Kilogram
X) Joule
Y) Watt
Z) Newton
ANSWER: Z) Newton
2. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following quantities measures the amount of friction between two layers of a fluid moving at different velocities?
W) Ram pressure
X) Surface tension
Y) Refraction
Z) Viscosity
ANSWER: Z) Viscosity
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What major rock type is produced when magma cools and solidifies?
ANSWER: IGNEOUS (also accept: INTRUSIVE IGNEOUS; PLUTONIC)
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Earth’s tectonic plates are made up of lithosphere. Which of the following best describes lithosphere?
W) The top half of the crust
X) The crust
Y) The crust and the upper mantle
Z) The crust and most of the mantle
ANSWER: Y) The crust and the upper mantle
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Scientists at Savannah River National Lab are analyzing microbial activity through staining cells with DAPI, which marks regions rich in adenine and thymine. Which of the following organelles will be stained the most?
W) Cell wall
X) Endoplasmic reticulum
Y) Nucleus
Z) Cytoplasm
ANSWER: Y) Nucleus
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Los Alamos National Lab are synthesizing radium-223 for cancer therapy. What type of decay does thorium-227 undergo to become radium-223?
ANSWER: ALPHA (also accept: ALPHA DECAY)
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Solve the following equation for x: 4x+3 = 1/64.
ANSWER: –6
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the square root of the least common multiple of 16 and 18?
ANSWER: 12
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What celestial object was created as the result of a collision involving the Earth approximately 4.5 billion years ago?
ANSWER: THE MOON (also accept: EARTH'S MOON)
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three solar system bodies that have dunes 1) Titan 2) Mars 3) Venus.
ANSWER: 1 and 2 and 3
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at the Joint Genome Institute are studying infectious agents best described as simple protein coats surrounding a DNA or RNA payload. What are these infectious agents known as?
ANSWER: VIRUSES
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists supported by the Department of Energy are developing new methods to synthesize tagatose, an isomer of glucose that is nearly as sweet as sucrose. What is the chemical formula of tagatose?
ANSWER: C6H12O6
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Charles Darwin suggested that evolution in the wild occurs by what process, in which environmental conditions determine what individuals will produce the most offspring?
ANSWER: NATURAL SELECTION
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What structure controls the size of the pupil in the human eye?
ANSWER: IRIS
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What vector quantity includes both the speed of an object and its direction of motion?
ANSWER: VELOCITY
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of radioactivity that when emitted by an atom, results in a change in its atomic number but NOT a change in its atomic mass number 1) Alpha 2) Beta 3) Gamma.
ANSWER: 2 only
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What detrital sedimentary rock contains a significant proportion of rounded granules, pebbles, cobbles, or boulders cemented by a fine-grained matrix?
ANSWER: CONGLOMERATE
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three characteristic that are true about Earth's magnetic field 1) It is uniform across Earth's surface 2) It originates in the inner core 3) The magnetic poles are stationary.
ANSWER: 0 only
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the slope of a line perpendicular to the line with equation y = –6x + 4 at the point (2, –8)?
ANSWER: 1/6
11. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area of the triangle formed by the x-axis, the y-axis, and the line passing through the point (0, 24) with slope –3?
ANSWER: 96
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Brookhaven National Lab researchers are using quadrupole magnets to focus electron beams. How many ordinary bar magnets are assembled to form a quadrupole?
ANSWER: 4
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Savannah River National Lab scientists are studying immune functions in the context of hyperglycemia. This is a condition in which there is an excess of what substance in the blood?
ANSWER: SUGAR (also accept: GLUCOSE; DEXTROSE)
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What molecule is the initial substrate in the process of glycolysis?
ANSWER: GLUCOSE (also accept: DEXTROSE)
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following populations of cells is likely to be most dramatically reduced in the brain of a patient with Parkinson's disease?
W) Leukocytes
X) Astroglia
Y) Motor neurons
Z) Dopaminergic neurons
ANSWER: Z) Dopaminergic neurons
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In 2019, the Event Horizon Telescope captured the first photo of what type of stellar remnant?
ANSWER: BLACK HOLE
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What planet in the solar system has a mean orbital radius of 228 million kilometers?
ANSWER: MARS
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What instrument is used to measure atmospheric pressure?
ANSWER: BAROMETER
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer In degrees Celsius, at what temperature will the temperature as measured in degrees Fahrenheit be double that in Celsius?
ANSWER: 160
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A 75-foot rope is stretched across 75 feet. How many cuts would be required to cut it into 5-foot sections?
ANSWER: 14
16. BONUS Math Short Answer If an 8-by-13 rectangle has its linear dimensions increased by 50%, what is the area of the new larger rectangle?
ANSWER: 234
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Although urine is stored in the bladder, it is produced in what organ?
ANSWER: KIDNEY
17. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following pathways harvests the largest number of ATP molecules per molecule of glucose?
W) Glycolysis
X) Aerobic respiration
Y) Lactic acid fermentation
Z) Alcohol fermentation
ANSWER: X) Aerobic respiration
18. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Galvanometers and ammeters are used to most directly measure which of the following quantities?
W) Current
X) Voltage
Y) Resistance
Z) Power
ANSWER: W) Current
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer A battery is connected to a circuit with an overall resistance of 6 ohms, causing a 4-ampere current to flow through the circuit. In volts, what is the voltage output of the battery?
ANSWER: 24
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Evaluate the quotient 7/3 divided by 1/6.
ANSWER: 14
19. BONUS Math Short Answer If the ratio of a sphere’s volume to its surface area is 36, what is the radius of the sphere?
ANSWER: 108
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the ecological term for the consumption of one animal by another?
ANSWER: PREDATION
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three orders of mammals that form a placenta during embryonic development 1) Monotremata 2) Marsupialia 3) Eutheria.
ANSWER: 2 and 3
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What physical quantity regarding an electric circuit is defined as the product of the current through the circuit and the voltage drop across the circuit?
ANSWER: POWER
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer Consider a table resting on the surface of the Earth. Which of the four fundamental forces is responsible for resisting the pull of the gravitational force on the table?
ANSWER: ELECTROMAGNETIC (also accept: EM)
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the physical weathering phenomenon in which water trapped in a crack freezes, expands, and forces the crack to grow?
ANSWER: FROST WEDGING (also accept: FREEZE WEDGING)
22. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following geologic periods is closest to when insects, seed bearing plants, and tetrapods first lived simultaneously on land?
W) Cambrian
X) Devonian
Y) Cretaceous
Z) Triassic
ANSWER: X) Devonian
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The temperature at 8 AM was 68 degrees Fahrenheit. If the temperature rose 4 degrees Fahrenheit per hour, what was the temperature, in degrees Fahrenheit, at 2 PM?
ANSWER: 92
23. BONUS Math Short Answer Three apples and two oranges cost 120 cents. Four apples and one orange cost 115 cents. How many cents would two apples and three oranges cost?
ANSWER: 125
