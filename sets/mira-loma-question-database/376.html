1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which Astronomer formulated the famous three laws of planetary motion?
ANSWER: Johannes Kepler
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the most famous comet that flies every 75-76 years?
ANSWER: Halley's Comet (also accept: Comet Halley)
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following define a solar wind?
W) A bubble in the interstellar medium
X) A flow of plasma from the Sun
Y) Ices such as water, ammonia and methane
Z) Dust and other particles
ANSWER: X) A flow of plasma from the Sun
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name the four inner planets. ( Not in order)
ANSWER: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name of an object that has struck Earth's surface?
W) Meteor
X) Meteoroid
Y) Asteroid
Z) Meteorite
ANSWER: Z) Meteorite
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What two planets consist of no moons?
ANSWER: Mercury and Venus
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer How long approximately did the Solar System form?
ANSWER: 4.6 Billion Years ago
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following astronomers was known for observing a supernova with the naked eye?
W) Johannes Kepler
X) Tycho Brahe
Y) Galileo Galilei
Z) Aristotle
ANSWER: X) Tycho Brahe
