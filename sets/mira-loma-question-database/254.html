Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Find the equivalent capacitance of ten capacitors of 25 microfarads connected in series.
ANSWER: 2.5 microfarads
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Mesons are found in which GROUP of elementary particles?
ANSWER: Hadrons
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A pendulum with period T and length L has its length increased by a factor of four. By what factor will its period change?
ANSWER: Two
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The mechanical advantage of an inclined plane can be given as what trigonometric function of the angle from the ground?
ANSWER: Cosecant
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The angular momentum of a counterclockwise rotating disc will always be at what angle with respect to the position vector?
ANSWER: 90 degrees
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer An abnormal accumulation of blood around an internal injury is commonly called what?
ANSWER: Hematoma
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Nematodes are covered by a tough outer "skin" called what?
ANSWER: Cuticle
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer As opposed to punctuated equilibrium, what other model of speciation states that species diverge over time as they acquire new adaptations?
ANSWER: Gradualism
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer 3TC, a drug designed to combat HIV, targets what enzyme to inhibit HIV from copying its genetic material?
ANSWER: Reverse transcriptase
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer This member of the oxime family is used to treat nerve gas by reversing the binding of organophosphorus compounds to acetylcholinesterase. Name the molecule.
ANSWER: Obidoxime
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The cosine and sine functions do not have a limit when extended out to infinity. The graphs of these two functions are said to do what?
ANSWER: Oscillate
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Which of the four conic sections is a type of another conic section?
ANSWER: Circle
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer This "prince of mathematicians" hailed mathematics as "the Queen of the sciences." Name this mathematician who, in 1796, discovered the structure of the heptadecagon.
ANSWER: Carl Friedrich Gauss
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Methanal is another name for what toxic aldehyde?
ANSWER: Formaldehyde
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Name these compounds that are byproducts of in the manufacture of organichlorides and in the incineration of such substances as PVC, readily entered into the human body through the ingestion of food.
ANSWER: Dioxins
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Alloys that return to their original form after reaching a threshold temperature are called what?
ANSWER: Shape memory alloys
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the metals extracted from the following three ores: beryl, molybdinite, scheelite
ANSWER: Beryllium, molybdenum, tungsten
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The phenomenon by which one side of a mountain is lush and green while the other receives no rain and suffers perennial drought is called what?
ANSWER: Rain shadow
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A glacier that is formed by one or several valley glaciers at the base of a mountain is called what?
ANSWER: Piedmont glacier
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Melting glaciers deposit till, the load the glacier has been carrying. What is an alternate name for till?
ANSWER: Boulder clay
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer This graduated piece of glassware is commonly used in the laboratory to do titrations at very precise levels. Name this instrument.
ANSWER: Buret
23. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer This earliest known unit of length is equal to 18 inches, the approximate length of the human forearm from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow.
ANSWER: Cubit
24. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The unit most commonly used for measuring the intensity of ultraviolet light is what?
ANSWER: Finsen unit
25. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer An air temperature of -5°F and a wind of 25mph cause a wind chill factor of what?
ANSWER: -31
