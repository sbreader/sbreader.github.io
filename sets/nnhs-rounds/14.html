1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What theory of physics proposes that every fermion and boson will have a corresponding partner from the other group whose spin differs by exactly 1/2?
ANSWER: SUPERSYMMETRY (also accept: SUSY)
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice An observer travels at 0.8 times the speed of light away from a source emitting light with a wavelength of 240 nanometers. What wavelength light will the observer detect?
W) 48 nanometers
X) 80 nanometers
Y) 540 nanometers
Z) 720 nanometers
ANSWER: Z) 720 nanometers
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What eye condition, often found in older people, is characterized by the loss of elasticity of the lens?
ANSWER: PRESBYOPIA
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following four steps of long term potentiation in chronological order 1) Mg2+ is displaced from NMDA receptors 2) The presynaptic neuron releases glutamate 3) The postsynaptic membrane is depolarized 4) Stored AMPA receptors are inserted into the postsynaptic membrane.
ANSWER: 2,3,1,4
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice When the first miners arrived at a particular volcano tuff cone on the island of Oahu, they mistakenly thought it contained diamond, leading to the name Diamond Head. What mineral did Diamond Head actually contain?
W) Talc
X) Gypsum
Y) Calcite
Z) Zircon
ANSWER: Y) Calcite
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Super Kamiokande detects what particle to monitor for the presence of supernovae?
ANSWER: NEUTRINO
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In 2 dimensions, what is the kissing number?
ANSWER: 6
4. BONUS Math Multiple Choice A cube is formed by connecting the centers of adjacent faces of a regular octahedron of side length 12. What is the ratio of the volume of the cube to the volume of the octahedron?
W) 2/9
X) √2/6
Y) √6/8
Z) 1/3
ANSWER: W) 2/9
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is FALSE regarding Grignard reagents?
W) Undergoes electrophilic attack of ketones
X) Contains an ionic carbon-magnesium bond
Y) Used in the preparation of alcohols
Z) Rapidly reacts with water to produce alkanes
ANSWER: W) Undergoes electrophilic attack of ketones
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What reaction, similar to the Clasien condensation, produces 5 or 6 membered cyclic β-keto esters?
ANSWER: DIECKMANN CONDENSATION
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory have discovered a connection between the gut microbiome and memory in mice. In particular, scientists believe that a certain three-carbon product of fermentation is involved in this connection. What is this molecule?
ANSWER: LACTATE (also accept: LACTIC ACID)
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory are studying the protein XPG, which is involved in nucleotide excision repair. Individuals with a mutation in this protein typically exhibit what condition, characterized by extreme sensitivity to the sun and increased risk of skin cancer?
ANSWER: XERODERMA PIGMENTOSUM
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What method, used in old quantum theory, found the allowed states of a particle by forcing the angular momentum to be a multiple of reduced Planck’s constant?
ANSWER: BOHR-SOMMERFELD QUANTIZATION
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer In string theory, what object generalizes the notion of a point particle to higher dimensions?
ANSWER: BRANE
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Both the swallowing center and the vomiting center can be found in which of the following parts of the brain?
W) Hypothalamus
X) Thalamus
Y) Pons
Z) Medulla oblongata
ANSWER: Z) Medulla oblongata
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following four cell types that a lymphoid stem cell could differentiate into 1) T cell 2) Natural killer cell 3) Basophil 4) Erythrocyte.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Eddington valve mechanism, which causes the pulsations of Mira variable stars, primarily results from the ionization of what element?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What two atmospheric processes are responsible for precipitation on Earth?
ANSWER: BERGERON PROCESS AND COLLISION-COALESCENCE PROCESS
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is equal to the partial derivative with respect to x of the function f of x comma y equals sine of the quantity xy plus cosine of the quantity xy at the point (1, 2)?
W) sin(2) + cos(2)
X) 2sin(2) + 2cos(2)
Y) - sin(2) + cos(2)
Z) -2sin(2) + 2cos(2)
ANSWER: Z) -2sin(2) + 2cos(2)
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What theorem states that over any commutative ring, every square matrix satisfies its own characteristic equation?
ANSWER: CAYLEY-HAMILTON THEOREM
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What diagram is used to show the relative stability of different oxidation states of an element?
ANSWER: FROST DIAGRAM
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains how aqua regia dissolves platinum?
W) Hydrochloric acid oxidizes platinum, which is stabilized by nitrate ions
X) Nitric acid oxidizes platinum, which then forms a complex ion with chloride ions
Y) Platinum forms a complex ion with nitrate ions, which is then stabilized by chloride ions
Z) Platinum forms a complex ion with chloride ions, which is then stabilized by nitrate ions
ANSWER: X) Nitric acid oxidizes platinum, which then forms a complex ion with chloride ions
12. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Researchers at the National Energy Technology Laboratory are optimizing coal sources with consumer needs and identifying key coal properties for end products. By removing the extraneous matter, they are using what general process on the raw coal, improving its quality?
ANSWER: BENEFICIATION
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers at the National Energy Technology Laboratory are studying solid oxide fuel cells, or SOFCs. Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding SOFCs 1) The electrolyte is typically a ceramic 2) Air is typically delivered to the anode 3) Fuel reacts catalytically with the hydrogen, releasing electrons.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What motion is caused by the change in the second Euler angle of a rotating body?
ANSWER: NUTATION (do not accept: PRECESSION)
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer What term is given to birefringent crystals that induce quarter-cycle phase differences on incident polarized light?
ANSWER: QUARTER WAVE PLATE
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Patients with cystic fibrosis have defects in ion channels known as cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator proteins. What ion do the functional form of these proteins transport?
ANSWER: CHLORIDE (also accept: Cl -)
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following hormones inhibits the secretion of hydrochloric acid from parietal cells in the stomach?
W) Gastrin
X) Acetylcholine
Y) Somatostatin
Z) Histamine
ANSWER: Y) Somatostatin

X) The universe would expand at an accelerating rate

ANSWER: TETRAMETHYLSILANE (also accept: TMS; Si(CH3)4)
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of crystalline solids 1) Ionic solids that have similar cation and anion radii will have a coordination number of 8 2) Magnesium oxide adopts a face-centered cubic structure 3) Zinc sulfide has a coordination number of 6.

ANSWER: PAIR DISTRIBUTION FUNCTION TECHNIQUE (also accept: PDF)

23. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about oxymercuration-demercuration is FALSE?
W) The mercurinium ion undergoes electrophilic attack, leading to Markovnikov addition
X) Sodium borohydride reduces the mercury rearrangement, removing the mercury atom
Y) The mercuric cation acts as a strong electrophile and is attacked by the π bond of the alkene
Z) It produces an OH group at the more substituted carbon
ANSWER: W) The mercurinium ion undergoes electrophilic attack, leading to Markovnikov addition
