1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Duan Seek is disappointed to find that Titan’s atmosphere is so thick that virtually no surface imaging is possible. Which of the following gases is the dominant constituent of Titan’s atmosphere?
W) Nitrogen
X) Methane
Y) Carbon dioxide
Z) Hydrogen
ANSWER: W) Nitrogen
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Haru is floating in the asteroid belt and notices a gap in the distribution of the semi-major axes of the asteroids. What is the name of these features in the asteroid belt caused by orbital resonances with Jupiter?
ANSWER: Kirkwood gaps
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Select all of the following 3 that play a role in the cell mediated immune response 1) Antigen presenting cells 2) Cytotoxic T cells 3) NK cells.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What cells decompose bone tissue by releasing lysosomic enzymes?
W) Osteoblasts
X) Osteoclasts
Y) Rank-L Cells
Z) Capethesin-K Cells
ANSWER: X) Osteoclasts
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Sam is studying the relative strength of attractive and repulsive forces between molecules. Rank the following three intermolecular forces in order of increasing strength 1) London Dispersion Forces 2) Dipole-dipole forces 3) Ion-dipole forces.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 3 elements in terms of increasing third ionization energy 1) Aluminum 2) Magnesium 3) Barium.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice If Sola ran the first two miles of a race at 30 miles per hour, and ran the next two miles of a race at 10 miles per hour, which of the following is equal to his average speed across the entire race, in miles per hour?
W) 15
X) 17.5
Y) 20
Z) 24
ANSWER: W) 15
4. BONUS Math Short Answer The whole science bowl squad of 8 people decides to sit on a round table. However Min Shinian is sick, so Ethan da' Gee does not want to sit next to Min Shinian. Everyone else is okay with sitting next to Min Shinian. Assuming rotations and reflections are considered identical, how many ways are there for the squad of 8 people to sit?
ANSWER: 1800
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In a pipe with only one side closed, the natural frequency of a wave is 200 Hz. What is the second overtone frequency of this wave?
ANSWER: 1000 Hz
5. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A particle has velocity exactly equal to the escape velocity of the Earth. What shape is the trajectory of this particle?
W) Ellipse
X) Hyperbola
Y) Parabola
Z) Helix
ANSWER: Y) Parabola
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion, or OTEC, is a potential source of renewable energy that acts as a heat engine, using the temperature gradient between warm surface water and cold deep water to generate electricity. What is the theoretical maximum efficiency of an OTEC system operating between a warm surface layer of 300 K and a cool deep layer of 270 K?
ANSWER: 10%
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice During the formation of bituminous coal from sub-bituminous varieties, continued heat and pressure remove moisture and volatile organic compounds. Which of the following best describes volatile organic compounds?
W) High vapor pressure, high water solubility
X) High vapor pressure, low water solubility
Y) Low vapor pressure, high water solubility
Z) Low vapor pressure, low water solubility
ANSWER: X) High vapor pressure, low water solubility
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Ryan loves inhaling tasty salty water. At which of the following latitudes would the ocean generally have the highest salinity?
W) 0 degrees
X) 30 degrees
Y) 60 degrees
Z) 90 degrees
ANSWER: X) 30 degrees
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three glacial formations by increasing distance from the peak of a mountain 1) Terminal moraine 2) Medial moraine 3) Cirque.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The organisms that form mushrooms are predominantly of what group of fungi?
ANSWER: Basidiomycota (also accept: Basidiomycytes)
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Bob is eating pears and notices a gritty texture in the flesh of his pear. What non-living short plant cells might Bob be noticing with incredibly thick cell walls filled with lignin surrounding a hollow lumen?
ANSWER: Sclereids (also accept: Stone Cells)
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Under a flame test, which of the following atoms emits photons of the longest visible wavelength?
W) Potassium
X) Copper
Y) Calcium
Z) Sodium
ANSWER: Y) Calcium
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following 4 acids in terms of increasing acidity 1) HCl 2) HBr 3) HBrO 4) HBrO2.
ANSWER: 3, 4, 1, 2
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many digits are in the decimal expansion of 2103 * 597?
ANSWER: 99
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Ethan da' Gee has four pairs of red socks, five pairs of blue socks, and six pairs of green socks. How many socks must Ethan da' Gee pick out of his box of socks without looking to guarantee that he has a pair of every color of socks?
ANSWER: 24
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The electric potential of two positive point charges with magnitude q at a distance d from each other is V. In terms of V, what is the electrical potential of four positive point charges with magnitude q arranged in a tetrahedron with side length d?
ANSWER: 6V
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer Atman can exert a constant power of 60 watts on his bike when biking at a constant speed. Assuming quadratic drag, in watts, how much power must he exert to bike twice as fast?
ANSWER: 480
12. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice The capacity factor refers to the ratio of the actual energy produced by a plant to the maximum energy it could have produced if ran at full continuous capacity. Which of the following energy sources and technologies generally has the lowest variation in its capacity factor?
W) Geothermal
X) Wind
Y) Wave power
Z) Hydroelectric
ANSWER: W) Geothermal
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer A nuclear power plant has a nameplate capacity of 1,200 MW. During a standard 30-day month, the plant undergoes a scheduled 5-day refueling and maintenance outage, during which its output is 0 MW. For the remaining 25 days, it operates continuously at 90% of its full nameplate capacity. To the nearest percent, calculate the monthly capacity factor of this nuclear power plant.
ANSWER: 75%
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Watch out! Ichiro uses his matrix powers to dodge a rapidly flowing mixture of water and volcanic debris rushing down the slopes of a volcano. What term is used to describe this volcanically-associated mudflow?
ANSWER: Lahar
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three intrusive igneous features that would be considered concordant 1) Dike 2) Sill 3) Laccolith.
ANSWER: 2 and 3
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following 3 steps in corresponding order of the central dogma 1) DNA Replication 2) Transcription 3) Translation.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer If you have an AB- father and a B+ mother, what is the probability that hemolytic disease of the newborn occurs?
ANSWER: 0
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice According to the kinetic molecular theory of gases, under which of the following sets of conditions would a sample of nitrogen gas behave the most ideally?
W) 100 K and 10 atm
X) 100 K and 0.5 atm
Y) 500 K and 10 atm
Z) 500 K and 0.5 atm
ANSWER: Z) 500 K and 0.5 atm
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A zeroth-order reaction starts with an initial reactant concentration of 0.2 M. Given that the rate constant of this reaction is 3.0 × 10-3 M s-1, calculate the initial half-life of the reaction to two significant figures.
ANSWER: 33 seconds
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A boat goes 30 miles downstream in 2 hours and returns upstream in 3 hours. What is the speed of the current?
ANSWER: 2.5 mph
16. BONUS Math Short Answer Mori has a collection of green, black, and blue marbles. The probability of drawing a green marble is 2/3, and the probability of drawing a black marble is 12.5%. What is the smallest number of marbles that Mori could have in her collection?
ANSWER: 24 marbles
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Neutrinos were proven to have mass by what mechanism, first discovered by Takaaki Kajita and Arthur McDonald?
ANSWER: Neutrino oscillation
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer To one significant figure and in joules per kelvin, what is the change in entropy of one mole of helium gas undergoing free expansion from a volume of 2 L to a volume of 4 L?
ANSWER: 6
18. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Rank the following three stages in the movement of electricity through the grid system by increasing average voltage 1) Transmission lines 2) Distribution lines 3) Generation facilities.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A dunkelflaute refers to a period of prelonged weather that significantly reduces the energy-generation ability of solar and wind systems. In which of the following northern hemisphere seasons would dunkelflaute periods be most common in Hawaii?
W) Summer
X) Fall
Y) Winter
Z) Spring
ANSWER: Y) Winter
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Rohan is having a swim in the core of a star. In lower mass stars, the core first becomes degenerate before helium ignition via the triple alpha processes. When this ignition occurs, what is the name of the runaway thermonuclear event that results?
ANSWER: Helium flash
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following three water masses by increasing average density 1) North Atlantic Deep Water 2) Mediterranean Intermediate Water 3) Antarctic Bottom Water.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In what form is nitrogenous waste disposed of in saltwater fish?
W) Urea
X) Uric Acid
Y) Ammonia
Z) Nitrite
ANSWER: Y) Ammonia
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following 3 steps of PCR in decreasing temperature 1) Denaturation 2) Annealing 3) Extension.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Daniel is looking at electrons in an atom and is analyzing their quantum numbers. Which of the following sets of quantum numbers is not possible for an electron in manganese?
W) n = 2, l = 1, ml = 0
X) n = 2, l = 2, ml = -1
Y) n = 3, l = 1, ml = 1
Z) n = 3, l = 2, ml = -1

W) Wire

ANSWER: 25
