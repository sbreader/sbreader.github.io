1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following developmental stages from earliest to latest: morula, gastrula, zygote, blastula.
ANSWER: ZYGOTE, MORULA, BLASTULA, GASTRULA
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which period of the Paleozoic Era of the Phanerozoic Eon began approximately 542 million years ago with a massive radiation of life?
W) Cambrian
X) Permian
Y) Silurian
Z) Ordovician
ANSWER: W) Cambrian
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer During which period did animals appear?
ANSWER: EDIACARAN
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The Permian Period ended approximately how many million years ago?
W) 120
X) 250
Y) 440
Z) 870
ANSWER: X) 250
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice During which period did early vascular plants diversify?
W) Ordovician
X) Devonian
Y) Carboniferous
Z) Silurian
ANSWER: Z) Silurian
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer During which period did reptiles originate and amphibians dominate?
ANSWER: CARBONIFEROUS
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following epochs is not part of the Neogene Period?
W) Pleistocene
X) Miocene
Y) Oligocene
Z) Pliocene
ANSWER: Y) Oligocene
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In the gastrula stage, the central cavity which gives rise to the alimentary canal is the...
W) Alimentary tube
X) Coelom
Y) Archenteron
Z) Blastopore
ANSWER: Y) Archenteron
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not true?
W) Protostomes have spiral and determinate cleavage
X) Deuterostomes include echinoderms and chordates
Y) In deuterostomes, the anus develops from the blastopore
Z) In deuterostomes, the mesoderm splits from the endoderm
ANSWER: Z) In deuterostomes, the mesoderm splits from the endoderm
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In coelomates, the coelem is found between the...
W) Endoderm and ectoderm
X) Endoderm and mesoderm
Y) Mesoderm and ectoderm
Z) Layers of the mesoderm
ANSWER: Z) Layers of the mesoderm
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a protostome?
W) Rotifer
X) Echinoderm
Y) Arthropod
Z) Flatworm
ANSWER: X) Echinoderm
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the embryonic membrane that is derived from the trophoblast of the blastocyst, and forms the fetal portion of the placenta?
ANSWER: CHORION
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In humans, what is the purpose of the yolk sac?
W) Nutrient storage
X) Formation of blood cells
Y) Elimination of waste
Z) Gas exchange
ANSWER: X) Formation of blood cells
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which inherited or infectious transmissible spongiform encephalopathy is a degenerative neurological disorder characterized by memory loss, personality changes, and hallucinations, and classified into sporadic, variant, and familial?
ANSWER: CREUTZFELDT-JAKOB DISEASE
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer The 1985 epidemic of bovine spongiform encephalitis in England appears to have originated from cattle feed that was contaminated by brain tissue from sheep infected with which infectious prion disease? It was the first transmissible spongiform encephalopathy to be studied.
ANSWER: SCRAPIE
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following are found in prion diseases, but are also implicated in Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, and Huntington's disease?
W) Neurofibrillary triangles
X) Amyloid deposits
Y) Prions
Z) Aluminum
ANSWER: X) Amyloid deposits
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which disease was once found among a tribe in Paupa New Guinea whose rituals included eating the brains of their recently deceased members of their tribe?
W) Kuru
X) Variant Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease
Y) Gerstmann-Sträussler-Scheinker disease
Z) Fatal familial insomnia
ANSWER: W) Kuru
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Using words or numbers, arrange the following four steps of embryonic development in order from earliest to latest: differentiation, patterning, cleavage, growth.
ANSWER: CLEAVAGE, PATTERNING, DIFFERENTIATION, GROWTH
10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The gray crescent divides the zygote into which two poles?
W) Animal pole and herbal pole
X) Anterior pole and posterior pole
Y) Animal pole and vegetal pole
Z) Ventral pole and dorsal pole
ANSWER: Y) Animal pole and vegetal pole
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which cluster of cells in the gastrula, found at the rim of the blastopore, is a cluster of mesoderm cells that develop into the notochord and induces the ectoderm above to form neural tissue?
ANSWER: SPEMANN ORGANIZER
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of cleavage occurs in some birds, fish, reptiles, and monotremes, in which cytokinesis does not divide the cell completely?
ANSWER: MEROBLASTIC
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not an explanation for cell differentiation?
W) Cytoplasmic determinants are distributed unevenly within the cells
X) Cells induce nearby cells to regulate gene expression
Y) Cytoplasmic determinants alter gene expression
Z) Induction alters the chromosomal structure of nearby cells
ANSWER: Z) Induction alters the chromosomal structure of nearby cells
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which extraembryonic layer protects the embryo in a sac filled with amniotic fluid?
W) Yolk sac
X) Chorion
Y) Amnion
Z) Allantosis
ANSWER: Y) Amnion
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which lineage of mammals has many species in Australia but only one in North America whose young are born very immaturely and complete development in their mother's pouch?
W) Marsupials
X) Eutherans
Y) Monotremes
Z) Amniotes
ANSWER: W) Marsupials
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Because they enclose embryos in the amnion, reptiles, birds and mammals are called...
W) Vertebrates
X) Amphibians
Y) Tetrapods
Z) Amniotes
ANSWER: Z) Amniotes
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following terms refers to homologous genes that are found in different species due to speciation?
W) Orthologous genes
X) Paralogous genes
Y) Analogous genes
Z) Specific genes
ANSWER: W) Orthologous genes
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which of the following terms refers to homologous genes that result from gene duplication and are found in more than one copy in the same genome?
ANSWER: PARALOGOUS GENES

W) Isopods
X) Decapods
Y) Copepods
Z) Brachiopods
ANSWER: Y) Copepods

X) Riboflavin
Y) Niacin

X) Iron and copper
Y) Sulfur and copper

Y) Thermogen
Z) Antithermic

W) Radiation
X) Conduction
Y) Convection
Z) Sublimation

ANSWER: THYROXINE (T4)

X) Transcription of the regulatory gene
Y) Activation of the repressor
Z) Synthesis of tryptophan

X) 200 thousand

Z) 20 million
