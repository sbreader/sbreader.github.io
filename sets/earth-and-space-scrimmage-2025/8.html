Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Vishnu observes a star with apparent magnitude of 3. If this star’s surface temperature is doubled, its radius is multiplied by a factor of 10, and its distance is quadrupled, what is the star’s new apparent magnitude to the nearest integer?
ANSWER: -2
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 exoplanet detection mechanisms by decreasing number of exoplanets detected to date 1) Transits 2) Radial velocity 3) Microlensing
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three rocky planets that would have their Gutenberg seismic discontinuity, or the equivalent, closer to the surface than Earth in proportion to their radii 1) Mercury 2) Venus 3) Mars
ANSWER: 1 only
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following stellar classes by increasing B-V index 1) A 2) F 3) O
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Strong northerly winds along the west coast of the United states has which of the following effects on sea surface temperature and sea level near the coast, respectively?
W) Increase, increase
X) Increase, decrease
Y) Decrease, increase
Z) Decrease, decrease
ANSWER: Z) Decrease, decrease
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of eruptions that are more likely to occur with mafic rather than felsic magma 1) Strombolian 2) Effusive 3) Pelean
ANSWER: 1 and 2
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three ions from most to least abundant in seawater 1) Calcium 2) Magnesium 3) Sulfate
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding bimodal volcanism 1) It is most commonly associated with convergent boundaries 2) The eruptions involve felsic magma derived from partial melting of the continental crust 3) The eruptions involve mafic magma derived from the upper mantle
ANSWER: 2 and 3
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The mixing of hot mafic magma into a felsic magma chamber likely produces which of the following zoning patterns in the resulting crystals?
W) Normal
X) Reverse
Y) Oscillatory
Z) Homogeneous
ANSWER: X) Reverse
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes a subsurface fault that cannot be noticed from the surface?
ANSWER: Blind
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Hercules–Corona Borealis Great Wall is considered the largest known structure in the universe. It belongs to what class of large scale structures that is composed of superclusters of galaxies?
W) Large quasar group
X) Supercluster complex
Y) Filament
Z) Void
ANSWER: Y) Filament
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice According to Kirchhoff’s Laws, which of the following conditions will produce an emission spectrum?
W) A hot solid
X) A hot diffuse gas
Y) A hot, dense gas obscured by a cool, diffuse gas
Z) A hot solid obscured by a hot, dense gas
ANSWER: X) A hot diffuse gas
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes how temperature changes within a glacier from surface to bed?
W) Uniformly increases
X) Uniformly decreases
Y) Increases then decreases
Z) Decreases then increases
ANSWER: Z) Decreases then increases
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three epochs in increasing average O18/16 ratio in ice cores 1) Eocene 2) Pleistocene 3) Holocene
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Winds tend to align with geostrophic flow above what layer near the Earth’s surface due to a decrease in friction?
ANSWER: Planetary boundary
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best serves as an index mineral for high-pressure metamorphism in carbonate rocks?
W) Chlorite
X) Aragonite
Y) Malachite
Z) Azurite
ANSWER: X) Aragonite
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true regarding an air parcel rising over a mountain?
W) The parcel brings increased rainfall to the windward side
X) The parcel hinders adiabatic wind development on the leeward side
Y) The dew point increases as the parcel ascends
Z) The parcel will have higher temperatures at the same altitudes after it descends the mountain
ANSWER: X) The parcel hinders adiabatic wind development on the leeward side
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following four mafic igneous rocks by decreasing cooling rate 1) Basalt 2) Gabbro 3) Diabase 4) Scoria
ANSWER: 4, 1, 3, 2
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three distance-measuring methods that would be more appropriate to apply to actively star-forming galaxies compared to those with little star formation 1) Tully-Fisher relation 2) RR Lyrae Variables 3) Hubble’s law
ANSWER: 1 only
10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice On the Uranian moon Miranda, coronae are thought to originate from which of the following solid forms of which of the following substances rather than lava?
W) Ammonia
X) Hydrocarbon
Y) Methane
Z) Water
ANSWER: Z) Water
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following observations would support that an erupted lava was not strongly superheated relative to its liquidus?
W) Presence of quenched glass in lavas
X) Presence of phenocrysts in quenched lava
Y) Abundant xenoliths in lava
Z) High viscosity of erupted lavas
ANSWER: X) Presence of phenocrysts in quenched lava
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A radar echo return pulse is received from the edge of a thunderstorm 10 microseconds after transmission. To the nearest tenth of a kilometer, what is the distance to the thunderstorm?
ANSWER: 1.5
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following stellar processes is most likely to produce a blue loop as seen on the HR diagram?
W) A newborn O-type star rapidly undergoes hydrogen burning
X) A red giant's envelope sharply decreases in opacity
Y) A red dwarf undergoing convective mixing
Z) A white dwarf experiencing helium shell flashes
ANSWER: X) A red giant's envelope sharply decreases in opacity
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Pancake domes on Venus tend to be extremely radar-bright. Due to their high dielectric constants, surface deposits consisting of which of the following groups of minerals best explains this observation?
W) Halide
X) Sulfide
Y) Sulfate
Z) Carbonate
ANSWER: X) Sulfide
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following three ocean currents in decreasing average flow velocity 1) Gulf stream 2) Canary current 3) Equatorial countercurrent
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
13. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following sediment sizes generally requires the lowest flow velocity to erode?
W) Gravel
X) Sand
Y) Silt
Z) Clay
ANSWER: X) Sand
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following isotopes was NOT formed during Big Bang nucleosynthesis?
W) Deuterium
X) Helium-3
Y) Lithium-7
Z) Oxygen-16
ANSWER: Z) Oxygen-16
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Advai loves looking at big planets. As a result of interference from dust and gas, planets located in which of the following galaxy types would be the hardest for Flora to observe with the Spitzer Space Telescope?
W) Elliptical
X) Spiral
Y) Irregular
Z) Starburst
ANSWER: Z) Starburst
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Although most clouds form with the rising of air, which of the following clouds is generally derived from the sinking of air?
W) Lenticular
X) Congestus
Y) Mammatus
Z) Fractus
ANSWER: Y) Mammatus
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following soil orders by increasing temperature in the environment they’re found in 1) Gelisols 2) Oxisols 3) Mollisols
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
