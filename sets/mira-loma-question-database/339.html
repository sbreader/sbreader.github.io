Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the general term used to describe a heritable feature?
W) trait
X) character
Y) genetic cross
Z) hybridization
ANSWER: X) character
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Mendel primarily used which plant for his tests?
ANSWER: PEA PLANT
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following generations is described as being the hybrid offspring of the true-breeding parents?
W) P generation
X) F1 generation
Y) F2 generation
Z) dominant generation
ANSWER: X) F1 generation
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer In Mendel's research with pea plants, what was the color he found to be recessive?
ANSWER: WHITE
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer The concept of the separation of alleles into separate gametes is the basis of which law?
ANSWER: LAW OF SEGREGATION
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Organisms with two different alleles for a character are said to be what?
W) homozygous
X) dihybrid cross
Y) pleiotropic
Z) heterozygous
ANSWER: Z) heterozygous
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Complete the following statement. An organism's genetic makeup is to genotype, as an organism's appearance is to what?
ANSWER: PHENOTYPE
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the diagram that is designed to show the genotype of an organism that has a dominant trait?
ANSWER: TESTCROSS
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer The concept of the independent segregation of each pair of alleles during gamete formation is the basis of which law?
ANSWER: LAW OF INDEPENDENT ASSORTMENT
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Consider a situation where the phenotypes of the heterozygote and the dominant homozygote can not be told apart from one another. Which of the following terms best describes this situation?
W) incomplete dominance
X) complete dominance
Y) codominance
Z) independence
ANSWER: X) complete dominance
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer The ability of a gene to affect an organism in multiple ways is known as what?
ANSWER: PLEIOTROPY
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A gene at one locus alters the phenotypic expression of a gene at a second locus. What genetic condition does this statement describe?
W) neurofibromatosis
X) haemophilia
Y) epistasis
Z) phenylketonuria
ANSWER: Y) epistasis
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the common term for what is commonly referred to as the converse of pleiotropy?
ANSWER: POLYGENIC INHERITANCE
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which theory states that Mendelian genes have specific loci on chromosomes and that it is the chromosomes that undergo segregation and independent assortment?
ANSWER: CHROMOSOME THEORY OF INHERITANCE
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer When working with Drosophila flies, Morgan discovered that the eye color for these flies was carried exclusively on which chromosome?
ANSWER: X-CHROMOSOME
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Genes located on a sex chromosome are known as what?
ANSWER: SEX-LINKED GENES
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer Consider a situation in which, out of 1500 total offspring, 500 are recombinants. What would the frequency of recombination be rounded to the nearest percent?
ANSWER: 33%
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a chromosomal system?
W) X-Y system
X) haplo-diploid system
Y) X-0 system
Z) X-1 system
ANSWER: Z) X-1 system
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer A genetic map based on recombination frequencies is known as what?
ANSWER: LINKAGE MAP
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In 1990, a British research team found a single gene that is required for the development of testes known as what?
ANSWER: SRY
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What sex-linked recessive trait is characterized by the absence of a protein required for blood clotting?
ANSWER: HEMOPHILIA
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not an alternation of the chromosome structure?
W) deletion
X) duplication
Y) replacement
Z) inversion
ANSWER: Y) replacement
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer The inactive X chromosome in each cell of a female condenses into a compact object known as what?
ANSWER: BARR BODY
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes why Down syndrome occurs?
W) There is one less chromosome 21
X) There is one less chromosome 20
Y) There is an extra chromosome 21
Z) There is an extra chromosome 20
ANSWER: Y) There is an extra chromosome 21
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following describes Klinefelter syndrome?
W) An extra Y chromosome in a male
X) An extra Y chromosome in a female
Y) An extra X chromosome in a male
Z) An extra X chromosome in a female
ANSWER: Y) An extra X chromosome in a male
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The first evidence that genes are specific molecules was found in 1928 by who?
ANSWER: FREDERICK GRIFFITH
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the phenomenon that is characterized by a change in phenotype due to the assimilation of external genetic material by a cell?
ANSWER: TRANSFORMATION
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Viruses that infect bacteria are known as what?
ANSWER: BACTERIOPHAGES (also accept: PHAGES)
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Where does the replication of a DNA molecule begin?
ANSWER: ORIGIN OF REPLICATION
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the name of the enzyme that works at the crotch of the replication fork and untwists the double helix of the DNA?
W) ligase
X) primase
Y) helicase
Z) polymerase
ANSWER: Y) helicase
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer What DNA repair mechanism fixes mistakes when DNA is copied?
ANSWER: MISMATCH REPAIR
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Sometimes urine appears black due to which chemical?
ANSWER: ALKAPTON
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer What nitrogenous base is found in deoxyribose, but not in ribose?
ANSWER: THYMINE
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What base is exclusively found in RNA?
ANSWER: URACIL
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term given to the synthesis of RNA under the direction of DNA?
ANSWER: TRANSCRIPTION
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term given to the process of the synthesis of a polypeptide?
ANSWER: TRANSLATION
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer The mRNA is usually modified prior to leaving the nucleus. What is this process known as?
ANSWER: RNA PROCESSING
21. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How many of the 64 codons function as "stop signals"?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 3
Z) 4
ANSWER: Y) 3
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer The mRNA base triplets are called what?
ANSWER: CODONS
22. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which enzymes pry the two strands of DNA apart and hook together the RNA nucleotides?
W) helicase
X) primase
Y) ligase
Z) RNA polymerase
ANSWER: Z) RNA polymerase
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer The entire stretch of DNA that is transcribed into a single RNA molecule is known as what?
ANSWER: TRANSCRIPTION UNIT
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What does the "P" in "P site" stand for?
ANSWER: PEPTIDYL-T-RNA
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer What does "r" stand for in "rRNA"?
ANSWER: RIBOSOMAL
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A functional ribosome consists of how many subunits?
ANSWER: 2
24. BONUS Biology Short Answer What three special base triplets act as signals to stop translation?
ANSWER: UAA, UAG AND UGA
25. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the final stage of translation?
W) elongation
X) initiation
Y) translocation
Z) termination
ANSWER: Z) termination
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer Clusters of ribosomes trailing along the same mRNA are known as what?
ANSWER: POLYRIBOSOMES
26. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What process is characterized by the removal of a large portion of the molecule that is initially synthesized during transcription?
ANSWER: RNA SPLICING
26. BONUS Biology Short Answer Intervening sequences are more commonly referred to as what?
ANSWER: INTRONS
27. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following describes the molecular basis of sickle-cell disease?
W) Point mutation occurs and thymine is replaced by cytosine in hemoglobin.
X) Point mutation occurs and cytosine is replaced by guanine in hemoglobin.
Y) Point mutation occurs and thymine is replaced by adenine in hemoglobin.
Z) Point mutation occurs and thymine is replaced by guanine in hemoglobin.
ANSWER: Y) Point mutation occurs and thymine is replaced by adenine in hemoglobin.
27. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the affects lytic and lysogenic reproductive cycles have on the host cell?
W) The host cell dies in lytic and in lysogenic it does not.
X) The host cell dies in lysogenic and in lytic it does not.
Y) The host cell dies in both, lysogenic and lytic reproduction.
Z) The host cell remains alive in both, lysogenic and lytic reproduction.
ANSWER: W) The host cell dies in lytic and in lysogenic it does not.
28. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer RNA molecules that function as catalysts are commonly referred to as what?
ANSWER: RIBOZYMES
28. BONUS Biology Short Answer Chemical changes in only one nucleotide in a single gene are commonly referred to as what?
ANSWER: POINT MUTATIONS
29. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The protein shell that encloses the viral genome is called what?
ANSWER: CAPSID
29. BONUS Biology Short Answer Nonviral cancer causing agents are commonly referred to as what?
ANSWER: CARCINOGENS
30. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Bacteriophages exhibit how many different reproductive cycles?
W) 0
X) 1
Y) 2
Z) 3
ANSWER: Y) 2
30. BONUS Biology Short Answer Viruses that rely on lytic cycles for reproduction are described as being what?
ANSWER: VIRULENT
