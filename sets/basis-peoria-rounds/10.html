1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer This condition is an opacification of the lens of the eye that leads to a hindrance in vision and needs surgical removal. It commonly develops in elderly individuals and slowly over time. What is the name of this condition?
ANSWER: CATARACT
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer This hormone is produced by the Anterior Pituitary Gland and causes a woman’s breasts to enlarge as well as produce milk after a baby is born. What is the name of this hormone?
ANSWER: PROLACTIN
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following gases will deviate most from the ideal gas law?
W) Hydrogen
X) Helium
Y) Water
Z) Nitrogen
ANSWER: Y) Water
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Name the effect that causes a real gas to cool when it expands after passing through a throttle. This technique is employed in commercial refrigerators to liquefy gases.
ANSWER: JOULE-THOMSON EFFECT
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a “fictitious force”?
W) Centrifugal force
X) Electromagnetic force
Y) Euler force
Z) Coriolis force
ANSWER: X) Electromagnetic force
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the centripetal acceleration, in meters per second squared, of an object with orbital period π and radius of curvature 1?
ANSWER: 4
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the image of the equation y=x+5 when reflected across the x-axis.
ANSWER: y = -x-5
4. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the image of the line 3x+2y=5 when reflected across the line y=x. Give your answer in slope intercept form.
ANSWER: y = −2/3 x + 5/3
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a neutron star with a rotational period of less than 40 milliseconds?
ANSWER: MILLISECOND PULSAR
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a neutron star with an especially strong magnetic field, the strongest known in the universe?
ANSWER: MAGNETAR
6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the name of the law that provides a theoretical maximum limit for the efficiency of a wind turbine?
ANSWER: Betz’s Law
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer Betz’s Law states that the maximum efficiency of a wind turbine cannot exceed the Betz limit. What integer value is closest to the Betz limit?
ANSWER: 59%
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which bone contains the foramen magnum, which is a large opening that allows for the passage of blood vessels and the spinal cord into the skull?
ANSWER: Occipital Bone
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer The brain case consists of numerous bones fused together. This region is where the frontal, parietal, temporal, and sphenoid bones meet. What is the name of this region, which is the weakest point in the human skull?
ANSWER: Pterion
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number identify all of the following five elements that are solids at room temperature. 1) Carbon 2) Mercury 3) Fluorine 4) Helium 5) Lead
ANSWER: 1 AND 5
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for a property of substances to exhibit variations in physical properties along different molecular axes.
ANSWER: ANISOTROPY
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What was the first experiment that disproved the corpuscular theory of light?
ANSWER: DOUBLE-SLIT EXPERIMENT
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the term for the incident angle at which no light is reflected?
ANSWER: BREWSTER’S ANGLE
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Solve the following expression: the absolute value of (x-3) is ≥ 7
ANSWER: x ≥ 10 or x ≤ −4
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the inflection points of the following equation y=x4-x3
ANSWER: 1/2, 0
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Carboniferous period is sometimes classified as 2 separate periods. What are the names of these separate periods?
ANSWER: MISSISSIPPIAN AND PENNSYLVANIAN
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The Carboniferous period saw the creation of vast reserves of coal within the earth. At the same time, a great quantity of another substance was also being produced, this time in the atmosphere. What substance is this?
ANSWER: OXYGEN
12. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following biomasses are not a source of energy?
W) Amorphous solids
X) Switchgrass
Y) Algae
Z) Black liquor
ANSWER: W) Amorphous solids
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the process in which organic materials are heated in the absence of oxygen?
ANSWER: Pyrolysis
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following epidermal layers is the most superior?
W) Stratum lucidum
X) Stratum granulosum
Y) Stratum basale
Z) Stratum corneum
ANSWER: Z) Stratum corneum
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer This disease is the most common skin cancer in the U.S. Primarily caused by extensive exposure to UV light, this cancer affects mitotically active cells in the deepest layer of the epidermis and gradually grows upwards towards the superior layers, forming an uneven bump on the skin surface. Name this disease.
ANSWER: Basal cell carcinoma
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The 1972 Nobel Prize in Physics was awarded to Bardeen, Cooper, and Schrieffer for their BCS theory which explains what physical phenomenon using Cooper pairs.
ANSWER: SUPERCONDUCTIVITY
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Superconductivity was first observed by Dutch physicist Heike Kamerlingh Onnes in 1911 when he cooled this metal to a temperature of 4 K. Name this metal that is also famous for its poisoning.
ANSWER: MERCURY
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What was the first calibrated temperature scale?
W) Fahrenheit
X) Celsius
Y) Rankine
Z) Rømer
ANSWER: Z) Rømer
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer At what temperature does the average of the temperature in degrees Celsius and degrees Kelvin equal the temperature in degrees Fahrenheit?
ANSWER: 131
16. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Assuming the Fibonacci sequence starts with 1, 1, 2, What is the sum of the squares of its first five terms?
W) 26
X) 32
Y) 36
Z) 40
ANSWER: Z) 40
16. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the sum of the reciprocals of all the prime numbers?
ANSWER: INFINITY
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer 3C 273 is an object that is very faint in the night sky, having an apparent magnitude of only 12.9. It looks exactly like a star through amateur telescopes as well as through larger telescopes. However, 3C 273 emits a very large quantity of radio waves, which is not expected for a star. What object is 3C 273?
ANSWER: QUASAR (also accept: ACTIVE GALAXY)
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Quasars are extremely bright galaxies that emit radiation in all directions. Sometimes, however, they also display focused jets that expel matter and radiation out of the galaxy at relativistic speeds. If these jets point directly at Earth, they look significantly brighter, and are classified under a new name. What is the name for these extreme quasars?
ANSWER: BLAZARS
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following elements from lowest RMS velocity to highest RMS velocity, if they are all at a constant temperature of 30 degrees Celsius 1) Nitrogen 2) Carbon 3) Oxygen 4) Argon
ANSWER: 4, 3, 1, 2 (also accept: Argon, Oxygen, Nitrogen, Carbon)
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A container is filled with 3L of Neon at 30 degrees Celsius at 1.5 atmospheres. Another container is filled with 3L of Xenon at 30 degrees Celsius at 2.5 atmospheres. Knowing these conditions, which element’s atoms will have a lower RMS velocity?
ANSWER: Xenon
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer This term is used to explain the fundamental contraction of muscle fibers. What is the name of this model, which describes how thin filaments slide past thick filaments after the release of calcium into the sarcoplasm.
ANSWER: Sliding Filament Model (also accept: Sliding Filament Theory)
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following events in the order that they occur during the muscle contraction process. (1) Calcium ions bind to troponin. (2) Acetylcholine is released into the sarcolemma and binds to receptors. (3) Action potential travels down T-tubules to the sarcoplasmic reticulum, triggering the release of calcium ions. (4) Myosin heads attach to actin and perform a power stroke. (5) Sodium ion channels open, creating an action potential in the sarcolemma.
ANSWER: 2,5,3,1,4
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is true of the relationship between temperature and conductivity for a semiconductor?
W) Semiconductors can only conduct electricity at one temperature.
X) As temperature rises, conductivity rises.
Y) As temperature rises, conductivity falls.
Z) Temperature doesn’t affect conductivity.
ANSWER: X) As temperature rises, conductivity rises.
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which type of semiconductor is germanium doped with arsenic?
ANSWER: N-TYPE
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What law states that the electromotive force is equal to the negative of the derivative with respect to time of the magnetic flux?
ANSWER: FARADAY’S LAW
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer By number, list all of the following that are true about Maxwell’s equations. 1) Ampere’s original law was given an addition by Faraday 2) Gauss’s law for magnetism is not one of the original four equations 3) Magnetic monopoles are forbidden by Gauss’s law of magnetism 4) Two of Maxwell’s equations are named after Gauss.
ANSWER: 3 AND 4
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Water flows out of a pump. The amount of water flowing out of the pump at time t is given by t2 + 1. What is the rate at which the amount of water flowing out of the pump is changing at t = 2?
ANSWER: 4
22. BONUS Math Short Answer Water flows out of a pump. The amount of water flowing out of the pump at time t is given by t2 + 1. How much total water flows out of the pump from t = 0 to t = 3?
ANSWER: 12
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Mendocino Triple Junction is the border between the San Andreas Fault and what subduction zone?
ANSWER: CASCADIA
23. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Cascadia subduction zone is the boundary between the North American Plate and what other plate?
W) Scotia
X) Nazca
Y) Pacific
Z) Juan de Fuca
ANSWER: Z) Juan de Fuca
