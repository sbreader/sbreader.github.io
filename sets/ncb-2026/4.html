Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The tendency of heavy elements, such as thallium and lead, to prefer lower oxidation states is explained by what effect, in which s-electrons of the outermost shell of heavy p-block elements are less likely to participate in bonding?
ANSWER: Inert Pair Effect
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The Cannizzaro reaction is the reaction of two moles of an aldehyde to form one mole of a carboxylic acid and one mole of an alcohol. What is the name for this type of reaction, where one species is both oxidized and reduced?
ANSWER: Disproportionation
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What effect refers to the scattering of light by particles in a colloid or fine suspension, making the light path visible?
ANSWER: Tyndall Effect
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What rule states that every orbital in a subshell will be occupied by one electron before starting to pair electrons?
ANSWER: Hund’s Rule
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice An Acid Chloride is reacted with lithium dimethylcuprate. Which of the following describes the final product?
W) Primary Alcohol
X) Secondary Alcohol
Y) Ketone
Z) Carboxylic Acid
ANSWER: Y) Ketone
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What principle states the transition state of an endothermic reaction will resemble the products?
ANSWER: Hammond Postulate
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Alyssa wants to prepare a buffer solution. Which of the following sets of reagents could she not use to prepare a buffer?
W) Pyridine and Hydrochloric Acid
X) Pyridine and Hydrofluoric Acid
Y) Sodium Hydroxide and Hydrofluoric Acid
Z) Sodium Hydroxide and Perchloric Acid
ANSWER: Z) Sodium Hydroxide and Perchloric Acid
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three elements by increasing ionization energy 1. Carbon 2. Nitrogen 3. Oxygen
ANSWER: 1,3,2
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements will have electronegativity closest to that of Beryllium:
W) Lithium
X) Sodium
Y) Aluminum
Z) Magnesium
ANSWER: Y) Aluminum
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer When benzene is reacted with Sodium Metal and methanol, with Ammonia acting as a solvent, it forms 1,4-cyclohexadiene. What is the name for this reaction?
ANSWER: Birch Reduction
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Superconductivity conventionally arises from the ability of electrons to form pairs, making use of lattice vibrations. What is the name of these pairs?
ANSWER: Cooper Pairs
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three elements by increasing ionization energy 1. Krypton 2. Fluorine 3. Neon
ANSWER: 1,2,3
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The decline in atomic radius in the lanthanide series due to the poor shielding effect of the 4f orbitals is known as what?
ANSWER: Lanthanide Contraction
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Many liquids, including water, can be lowered below their freezing points in a phenomenon known as supercooling. Supercooled water is stable provided it is subjected to no more than small disturbances due to the fact that it experiences what type of equilibrium state?
ANSWER: Metastable
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term is used to refer to any reaction in which two molecules undergo addition accompanied by the loss of a small molecule such as water, carbon dioxide, or nitrogen gas?
ANSWER: Condensation
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three elements by increasing reduction potential 1. Platinum 2. Palladium 3. Nickel
ANSWER: 3,2,1
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What principle dictates the order in which atomic orbitals are filled by electrons?
ANSWER: Aufbau Principle
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the 3 following ions that are colorless in an aqueous solution 1) Sc3+ 2) Zn2+ 3) Co2+
ANSWER: 1,2
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What are the molecular and electron geometries, respectively, of xenon difluoride?
ANSWER: linear and trigonal bipyramidal (do not accept: trigonal bipyramidal and linear)
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A straight chain alkene has molecular formula C15H18. How many double bonds must it have?
ANSWER: 7
