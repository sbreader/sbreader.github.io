Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Physicists most commonly call a mass moving in a straight line path as having:
W) angular or rotational momentum
X) straight momentum
Y) translational momentum
Z) second order momentum
ANSWER: Y) translational momentum
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer On an electric schematic, what does the symbol of a horizontal line interrupted by a saw tooth shaped line represent?
ANSWER: RESISTOR
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a final product of the overall cell reaction in a hydrogen fuel cell:
W) heat
X) electric power
Y) carbon dioxide
Z) water
ANSWER: Y) carbon dioxide
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which element has the following ground-state electron configuration: 1s2 2s2 2p6
ANSWER: NEON (also accept: Ne)
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following accounts for the largest decline in death rate in the USA between the years 1900 and 1990:
W) accidents
X) cancer
Y) cardiovascular disease
Z) infectious disease
ANSWER: Z) infectious disease
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer What accessory organ allows bony fish to remain at a certain depth in the water without swimming?
ANSWER: SWIMBLADDER
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Astronomers usually detect the electromagnetic emissions of a single neutron star in what single form:
W) cosmic radiation
X) microwaves
Y) radio waves
Z) infrared waves
ANSWER: Y) radio waves
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What moon of Neptune orbits in a retrograde direction?
ANSWER: TRITON (do not accept: TITAN; a moon of Saturn)
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the square root of 256?
ANSWER: 16
5. BONUS Math Short Answer A farmer wishes to make a straight barbed wire fence 80 feet long. He wants to have a fence post every 8 feet. How many fence posts does he need?
ANSWER: 11
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which is NOT true about the concentration of methane gas in the atmosphere:
W) it is generated by biological activity related to rice cultivation
X) it is generated as a byproduct of the digestive processes of cattle
Y) it is generated as a byproduct of refrigerants and aerosols
Z) it has an atmospheric lifetime of about 10 years
ANSWER: Y) it is generated as a byproduct of refrigerants and aerosols
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the names of the tides that occur when the sun’s tidal bulges and the moon’s tidal bulges are at right angles to each other?
ANSWER: NEAP TIDES
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What percentage of the world's ice is in Antarctica?
W) 10 percent
X) 25 percent
Y) 50 percent
Z) 90 percent
ANSWER: Z) 90 percent
7. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice If each of the following woods were seasoned to an equally low water content, which would release the most heat per cord when burned?
W) White Oak
X) Red Maple
Y) White Birch
Z) Mountain Aspen
ANSWER: W) White Oak
8. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an example of systems software:
W) file management tools
X) linkers
Y) loaders
Z) word processing software
ANSWER: Z) word processing software
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the largest WAN in existence today?
ANSWER: THE INTERNET
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A figure skater who, while spinning in place, pulls her arms in to increase her rotational speed, is most closely exhibiting which of the following:
W) conservation of angular momentum
X) centrifugal force
Y) satellite motion
Z) centripetal acceleration
ANSWER: W) conservation of angular momentum
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What cycle most directly describes the working cycle of a heat engine operating as an ideal engine of maximum thermal efficiency?
ANSWER: CARNOT CYCLE
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The use of electricity to decompose molten sodium chloride into its component elements is an example of:
W) electrolysis
X) galvanization
Y) a voltaic cell
Z) electroplating
ANSWER: W) electrolysis
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Convert the molecular structure of the hydrocarbon C5H12 into its straight-chain Condensed Structural Formula:
ANSWER: CH3CH2CH2CH2CH3
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Physicians give patients immuno-suppressing drugs after an organ transplant to prevent:
W) infection
X) a graft verses host response
Y) rejection of the transplant
Z) viral infections
ANSWER: Y) rejection of the transplant
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer In many arthropods, the segments of the body are grouped into 3 distinct parts. Name all three parts:
ANSWER: HEAD, THORAX, AND ABDOMEN
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Most astronomers believe that these celestial bodies are at the center of quasars and are the main reason for their large energy emissions:
ANSWER: BLACK HOLES
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer These doughnut shaped zones of atomic particles consists of electrons and protons captured by the Earth’s magnetic field from the solar winds and are called:
ANSWER: VAN ALLEN BELTS
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What form of geometry is one in which the parallel postulate of Euclidean Geometry does NOT hold:
ANSWER: NON-EUCLIDEAN GEOMETRY (also accept: HYPERBOLIC GEOMETRY; BOLYAI-LOBACHEVSKY GEOMETRY; RIEMANNIAN GEOMETRY)
13. BONUS Math Short Answer If the length of the cycloid is 4 times the diameter of a rotating circle, then the area under the arch traced out by that cycloid is how many times the area of the rotating circle?
ANSWER: 3 TIMES
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A low-gradient stream would most likely form a:
W) V-shaped valley
X) waterfall
Y) meander
Z) straight channel
ANSWER: Y) meander
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The angle of insolation on the Earth’s surface is most direct at which latitude:
W) 0°
X) 90° North or South
Y) 66.5° North or South
Z) 23.5° North or South
ANSWER: W) 0°
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice At small apertures or high f stops, a single lens reflex camera will have which of the following properties:
W) have more light gathering capacity
X) generally require fast shutter speeds for all films in normal daylight
Y) have more depth of field
Z) have less depth of field
ANSWER: Y) have more depth of field
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Juglans nigra is the genus and species of what common name tree, whose wood is prized for its qualities in furniture making:
ANSWER: BLACK WALNUT (also accept: WALNUT)
16. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer A term coined by Jack Niles in the early 1970’s to describe an office where workers can work at home on a computer and transmit data and documents to a central office via telephone lines, is:
ANSWER: TELECOMMUTING
16. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the largest possible 8 bit binary number?
ANSWER: 11111111 (also accept: 8 ONES)
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The atom’s nucleus is held together by which of the following:
W) strong interactions of the quark and gluon constituents of the atom’s nucleus
X) strong interactions of the quark and lepton constituents of the atom’s nucleus
Y) weak interactions of the quark and lepton constituents of the atom’s nucleus
Z) strong interactions of the lepton and gluon constituents of the atom’s nucleus
ANSWER: W) strong interactions of the quark and gluon constituents of the atom’s nucleus
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer If F equals electric force, and q and q’ are the charges on two particles, the expression F is proportional to q times q prime divided by distance squared is what law?
ANSWER: COULOMB’S LAW
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following metals react violently with water to produce hydrogen gas:
W) sodium
X) zinc
Y) platinum
Z) silver
ANSWER: W) sodium
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What colorless liquid was originally produced from the distillation of wood and is often referred to as wood alcohol:
ANSWER: METHANOL (also accept: METHYL ALCOHOL; CH3OH)
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following does NOT directly function in the cytoskeleton:
W) microtubules
X) microfilaments
Y) spectrin
Z) actin
ANSWER: Y) spectrin
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer Vibration of this membrane moves 3 small bones that span the cavity of the middle ear:
ANSWER: TYMPANIC MEMBRANE (also accept: EARDRUM)
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer According to "Set Theory", sets that do NOT contain themselves are typically referred to as:
ANSWER: NORMAL SETS
20. BONUS Math Short Answer If you start with 1 bacterium and double the number every 15 minutes, how many bacteria will you have at the end of 3 hours and 45 minutes?
ANSWER: 32,768
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The ability of rock or soil to transmit water is called its:
W) permeability
X) porosity
Y) viscosity
Z) wetability
ANSWER: W) permeability
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following minerals from the hardest to the softest: Topaz; Corundum; Quartz; Fluorite
ANSWER: CORUNDUM; TOPAZ; QUARTZ; FLUORITE
22. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice The SI prefix for the quantity 1015 is:
W) peta
X) eta
Y) tera
Z) yotta
ANSWER: W) peta
22. BONUS Math Short Answer Order the following from the SMALLEST unit of distance to the LARGEST: mile, rod, feet, furlong.
ANSWER: FEET, ROD, FURLONG, MILE
23. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer By passing current through a wire wrapped around an iron rod, William Sturgeon invented the:
ANSWER: ELECTROMAGNET
23. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of Guglielmo Marconi’s experiments on wireless transmission:
W) in 1901 he transmitted a signal from Newfoundland to England
X) he defied current belief on line-of-sight transmission
Y) his work was a practical application of the work of James Clerk Maxwell
Z) his success was a vindication of his belief in the reflective properties of the ionosphere
ANSWER: Z) his success was a vindication of his belief in the reflective properties of the ionosphere
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true about cholesterol:
W) there is no cholesterol in plants
X) cholesterol is found in meats, milk and eggs
Y) cholesterol is not synthesized in the human body
Z) cholesterol is a component of cellular membranes
ANSWER: Y) cholesterol is not synthesized in the human body
24. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What radioactive isotope of this alkaline earth element formed as a fission product of uranium and is of particular importance because it is assimilated in the body much like calcium:
ANSWER: STRONTIUM (also accept: Sr)
25. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Stems grow longer by creating new cells at their tips, in a region known as the:
W) terminal division section
X) terminal mitotic
Y) shoot apical meristem
Z) apical terminus
ANSWER: Y) shoot apical meristem
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer When the body is in a time of stress, which of the 2 divisions of the autonomic nervous system is primarily directing the response?
ANSWER: SYMPATHETIC SYSTEM
