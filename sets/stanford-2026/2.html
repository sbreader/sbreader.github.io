1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Cholesterol contains a polar hydroxyl group on one end and a largely nonpolar steroid ring on the other. What term best describes molecules like cholesterol which have both hydrophilic and hydrophobic regions?
ANSWER: Amphipathic (also accept: Amphiphilic)
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following three cells from first to last that water will pass through as it exits the leaf during transpiration 1) Mesophyll cells 2) Bundle sheath cells 3) Xylem tracheids.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In parsecs, what is the distance to a star with a parallax of 0.5 arcseconds?
ANSWER: 2
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the silica content of the lava that erupts from a shield volcano?
W) Felsic
X) Intermediate
Y) Mafic
Z) Ultramafic
ANSWER: Y) Mafic
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A block travels along a surface at a constant rate of 2 meters per second while being pushed with a force of 2 newtons. After being pushed for 2 seconds, how much work has been done by the applied force on the block, in joules?
ANSWER: 8
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A circular ring has a counterclockwise current when seen from the top. What is the direction of the magnetic field at the center of the ring?
W) Directly up
X) Directly down
Y) Outward towards the ring
Z) There is no magnetic field
ANSWER: W) Directly up
4. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Scientists at Stanford’s Goldhaber-Gordon Group are using the atomically flat flakes of boron nitride to study the properties of electrons in what material, analogous because of its single layer of hexagonally structured atoms?
ANSWER: Graphene
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Stanford’s Dynamic Design Lab are enhancing vehicle safety by modeling tire-road friction. Identify all of the following three factors that directly affect the force of friction 1) Contact surface area 2) Mass of the object 3) Angle of incline of the road.
ANSWER: 2 and 3
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Given that the polynomial x3 + ax + b has two roots that add to 7, what is the third root of the polynomial?
ANSWER: -7
5. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT an example of the transitive property?
W) If Rumi is taller than Zoey and Mira is taller than Rumi, then Mira is taller than Zoey
X) The set of rational numbers is a subset of real numbers, and the set of integers is a subset of rational numbers, so all integers are real numbers
Y) Sirin is older than Kiana and Bianka is younger than Sirin, so Kiana is older than Bianka
Z) If x is equal to y and y is equal to z, then x equals z
ANSWER: Y) Sirin is older than Kiana and Bianka is younger than Sirin, so Kiana is older than Bianka
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice At the half-equivalence point of titrating a weak acid with a strong base, the concentrations of the weak acid and its conjugate base are equal. Which of the following is the pH at this point?
W) The pKa of the weak acid
X) The pKb of the conjugate base
Y) Fourteen plus the pKa of the weak acid
Z) Fourteen minus the pKa of the weak acid
ANSWER: W) The pKa of the weak acid
