Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the process by which oceanic crust at mid ocean ridges diverges, leading to the formation of new oceanic crust?
ANSWER: Seafloor spreading
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three celestial bodies that are generally in hydrostatic equilibrium 1) Stars 2) Comets 3) Planets.
ANSWER: 1 and 3
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In Pokémon, Azurill are 25% male and 75% female, but their evolution Marill has a 50/50 gender ratio. If all Azurills evolve into Marills and all male Azurills become male Marills, what is the probability that a randomly selected female Azurill evolves into a male Marill?
ANSWER: 1/3
2. BONUS Math Short Answer A group of six friends go to a restaurant together and sit at a circular table. However, Kiana and Mei demand to sit next to each other at the table. If rotations are considered identical, how many ways are there for the friends to arrange themselves?
ANSWER: 48
3. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Stanford’s PULSE Institute are using attosecond light pulses to study molecular dynamics. An attosecond is defined as which of the following fractions of a second?
W) 10-12
X) 10-15
Y) 10-18
Z) 10-21
ANSWER: Y) 10-18
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Stanford’s Paleomagnetism Lab are studying planetary magnetism. Identify all of the following 3 terrestrial planets that possess a global intrinsic magnetic field 1) Mercury 2) Venus 3) Earth.
ANSWER: 1 and 3
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three mechanical quantities that are vectors 1) Acceleration 2) Energy 3) Momentum.
ANSWER: 1 and 3
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer A car accelerates from rest at a rate of 5 meters per second squared. How many meters does the car travel after 10 seconds?
ANSWER: 250
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements has the lowest boiling point?
W) Hydrogen
X) Helium
Y) Neon
Z) Argon
ANSWER: X) Helium
5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Brian multiples a reaction’s temperature by its change in entropy in order to calculate its change in Gibbs free energy. Which of the following units could he use to measure change in entropy?
W) Joule
X) Joule per mole
Y) Joule kelvin
Z) Joule per kelvin
ANSWER: Z) Joule per kelvin
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice E. coli were grown on differing isotopes of what element by Meselson and Stahl to determine the semi-conservative replication of DNA?
W) Nitrogen
X) Oxygen
Y) Phosphorus
Z) Sulfur
ANSWER: W) Nitrogen
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following animal behaviors is innate rather than learned?
W) Imprinting
X) Fixed action pattern
Y) Classical conditioning
Z) Operant conditioning
ANSWER: X) Fixed action pattern
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at UCLA’s Barber Lab are studying how differential air pressure affects convection. Which of the following instruments is used to measure atmospheric pressure?
W) Anemometer
X) Hygrometer
Y) Barometer
Z) Sling psychrometer
ANSWER: Y) Barometer
7. BONUS Math Short Answer Scientists at UCLA’s Sports Research Group are analyzing how age varies with injury rate. If they wish to measure the spread of the data, identify all of the following three measures that can be used 1) Interquartile range 2) Standard deviation 3) Median.
ANSWER: 1 and 2
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The credit card scam ”What would your credit card number be if you multiplied by one and added zero?” works because 1 and 0 are examples of what special values for the operations multiplication and addition, respectively?
ANSWER: Identity
8. BONUS Math Short Answer A right triangle has a leg of length 24 and area 84. What is the length of its hypotenuse?
ANSWER: 25
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following celestial bodies is NOT a moon of Saturn?
W) Titan
X) Enceladus
Y) Mimas
Z) Europa
ANSWER: Z) Europa
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three types of clouds by decreasing altitude of formation 1) Stratus 2) Altocumulus 3) Cirrus.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Huygens’ principle explains what effect that describes how waves bend around corners and other obstacles?
ANSWER: Diffraction
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer In watts, how much power is dissipated by a 100 ohm resistor connected to a 10 volt power source?
ANSWER: 1
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of intermolecular bonding which are observed in an acetone solution 1) London dispersion 2) Dipole-dipole 3) Hydrogen bonding.
ANSWER: 1 and 2
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three changes that would increase the pressure in a rigid container of hydrogen gas 1) Removing one mole of hydrogen and adding one mole of steam 2) Replacing hydrogen with an equal amount of oxygen 3) Heating the container.
ANSWER: 3 only
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three pathways in cellular respiration that can generate ATP through substrate-level phosphorylation 1) Glycolysis 2) Krebs cycle 3) Pyruvate oxidation.
ANSWER: 1 and 2
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Astrocytes induce the formation of which of the following types of junctions in brain capillaries?
W) Gap junctions
X) Desmosomes
Y) Tight junctions
Z) Plasmodesmata
ANSWER: Y) Tight junctions
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What term is given to a device that stores energy in the form of a magnetic field created when current flows through it?
ANSWER: Inductor
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer A large wooden board with volume 50 liters and specific gravity 0.5 is placed in a body of water. What is the maximum excess mass, in kilograms, that the board can support before being fully submerged?
ANSWER: 25
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at the Johns Hopkins Suez Lab are studying the gut virome, which encompasses viruses that infect bacterial hosts. What is the name for viruses that specifically target bacteria?
ANSWER: Bacteriophages (also accept: Phage)
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at the Johns Hopkins Toscano Lab are studying how nitric acid dissociates in cellular conditions. What is the conjugate base of nitric acid?
ANSWER: Nitrate (also accept: NO3-)
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of faulting that occur at the Mid-Atlantic Ridge 1) Reverse 2) Normal 3) Transform.
ANSWER: 2 and 3
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of stars will the Sun never become?
W) Red giant
X) Red supergiant
Y) White dwarf
Z) Black dwarf
ANSWER: X) Red supergiant
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following phyla contain organisms that do not possess any true tissue?
W) Porifera
X) Cnidaria
Y) Platyhelminthes
Z) Nematoda
ANSWER: W) Porifera
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following three locations that newly synthesized bile will pass through from first to last 1) Duodenum 2) Liver 3) Jejunum.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer At low temperatures, tin metal spontaneously transforms from a silvery metal to a brittle grey powder. What term describes these two forms of tin, which are different phases of the same element?
ANSWER: Allotropes (also accept: Polymorphs)
17. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about a reaction coordinate diagram is always true?
W) The activation energy is greater than the overall Gibbs free energy change
X) The activation energy is smaller than the overall Gibbs free energy change
Y) The transition state has a higher Gibbs free energy than the products
Z) The transition state has a lower Gibbs free energy than the products
ANSWER: Y) The transition state has a higher Gibbs free energy than the products
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In a room, there are 7 dogs, 3 cats, and 5 rabbits. What is the probability a randomly selected pet is a rabbit?
ANSWER: 1/3
18. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following functions has a unique global minimum at x = 0?
W) x2 + x
X) |x| − x
Y) ln(1/x)
Z) ex + e−x
ANSWER: Z) ex + e−x
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the process by which massive chunks of ice break away from a glacier to create icebergs?
ANSWER: Calving
19. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which of the following regions of the electromagnetic spectrum is the 21 centimeter emission line located?
W) Infrared
X) Radio
Y) Ultraviolet
Z) Visible
ANSWER: X) Radio
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Uranium-238 undergoes a form of nuclear decay in which it releases a helium nucleus to form thorium-234. What is the name of this type of decay?
ANSWER: Alpha decay
20. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice If 0.5 moles of each of the following acids are dissolved in 10 liters of water, which acid would create the solution with the highest pH?
W) Hydrogen fluoride
X) Hydrogen chloride
Y) Hydrogen bromide
Z) Hydrogen iodide
ANSWER: W) Hydrogen fluoride
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In many desert cacti, stomata open only at night, storing carbon dioxide for sugar production during the day. What modified form of photosynthesis do these desert cacti employ to minimize water loss in hot daytime conditions?
ANSWER: CAM (also accept: Crassulacean acid metabolism)
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer What phenomenon occurs when excess nutrients, often from runoff, enter a body of water and stimulate rapid algal bloom?
ANSWER: Eutrophication
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many terms are in an arithmetic series with first term 0, last term 2025, and common difference 5?
ANSWER: 406
22. BONUS Math Short Answer Triangle ABC is an isosceles triangle with AB = AC. If the altitude from A to BC has length 12, and triangle ABC has area 60, find the perimeter of triangle ABC.
ANSWER: 36
23. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The potential energy stored in a compressed spring is proportional to its displacement from equilibrium raised to what power?
ANSWER: 2
23. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Two conductors with free charges touch briefly. Which of the following necessarily describes how the states of the two conductors change?
W) Their charges swap
X) Their potentials swap
Y) Their charges become equal
Z) Their potentials become equal
ANSWER: Z) Their potentials become equal
