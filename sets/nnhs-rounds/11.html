Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What principle states that every point on a wavefront is the source of new spherical wavelets?
ANSWER: HUYGENS PRINCIPLE (also accept: HUYGENS-FRESNEL PRINCIPLE)
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Polarized light travels from glass, with an index of refraction of n = 1.5, to diamond, with an index of refraction of n = 2.4. No reflection will occur closest to which of the following incident angles?
W) 40°
X) 50°
Y) 60°
Z) 70°
ANSWER: Y) 60°
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What metal is found at the center of the porphyrin ring in chlorophyll?
ANSWER: MAGNESIUM
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer In C4 plants, what enzyme first captures carbon dioxide?
ANSWER: PEP CARBOXYLASE
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which of the following latitudes would you expect to find the most dramatic thermocline?
W) 10 degrees North
X) 20 degrees North
Y) 30 degrees North
Z) 40 degrees North
ANSWER: W) 10 degrees North
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Approximately 325 million years ago, the Alleghenian orogeny formed which modern mountain range?
ANSWER: THE APPALACHIAN MOUNTAINS
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The volume of a cube is decreasing at 4 meters cubed per second. What is the side length of the cube if the side length is decreasing at a rate of 1/3 meters per second?
ANSWER: 2 METERS
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Evaluate the indefinite integral of 2 times the square root of the quantity 1 – x2 d x.
ANSWER: x√(1 – x2) + arcsin(x) + C
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following has the largest atomic radius?
W) Iodine
X) Bromine
Y) Manganese
Z) Yttrium
ANSWER: Z) Yttrium
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the formal charge of nitrogen in thiocyanate?
ANSWER: –1
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Livermore National Lab are studying methane hydrates to better understand the seafloor. Rapid release of methane to seawater can cause what effect, which is caused by changes to the bicarbonate buffer system?
ANSWER: OCEAN ACIDIFICATION
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Livermore National Lab have developed a 3D-printed aneurysm to help improve surgical procedures. One non-invasive treatment for aneurysms is to insert a thin metal tube, causing the area to clot. What is the term for the tube?
ANSWER: CATHETER
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about fluids is FALSE?
W) Laminar flow is characterized by high viscosity
X) Surface tension increases as temperature increases
Y) A fluid that obeys the continuity equation must have a constant density
Z) In the absence of gravity, bubbles adopt a spherical shape to minimize surface tension
ANSWER: X) Surface tension increases as temperature increases
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two waves traveling in the same medium have the same speed, but the second wave has three times the amplitude and twice the wavelength of the first wave. If the first wave has intensity I, then in terms of I, what is the intensity of the second wave?
ANSWER: 9I/4 (also accept: 2.25I)
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What cells located in the gastric gland secretes the components of hydrochloric acid?
ANSWER: PARIETAL CELLS
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly describes deuterostomes?
W) Cleavage is spiral and determinate, blastopore develops into mouth
X) Cleavage is radial and indeterminate, blastopore develops into mouth
Y) Cleavage is spiral and determinate, blastopore develops into anus
Z) Cleavage is radial and indeterminate, blastopore develops into anus
ANSWER: Z) Cleavage is radial and indeterminate, blastopore develops into anus
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of tides occur when the sun and moon’s gravitational effects partially cancel, creating weaker high and low tides?
ANSWER: NEAP TIDES
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding the Bergeron process is FALSE?
W) Supercooled water droplets freeze when they come into contact with freezing nuclei
X) Freezing nuclei are more abundant in the atmosphere than condensation nuclei
Y) At temperatures below 0°C, the relative humidity with respect to ice is more than the relative humidity with respect to water
Z) The Bergeron process cannot sufficiently explain precipitation in the tropics
ANSWER: X) Freezing nuclei are more abundant in the atmosphere than condensation nuclei
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A cone has a height of 12 and the circumference of the base of the cone is 10π. What is the lateral surface area of the cone?
ANSWER: 65π
10. BONUS Math Short Answer How many ways are there to distribute 16 indistinguishable oranges between Alice, Bob and Carol, if each person must receive at least 2 oranges?
ANSWER: 66
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Propanal contains which of the following functional groups?
W) Aldehyde
X) Ketone
Y) Ether
Z) Alcohol
ANSWER: W) Aldehyde
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What principle states that the transition state of a reaction will be closer to the species, product or reactants, that it is closer in energy to?
ANSWER: HAMMOND’S POSTULATE
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab are predicting hurricanes through sensors powered by wave energy. Hurricane strength is typically measured on what scale, which classifies them into five categories based on wind speed?
ANSWER: SAFFIR-SIMPSON SCALE
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying the structure of small crystals. Suppose that a cubic grain of table salt has a side length of 0.1 millimeters, and the density of table salt is 2.2 grams per cubic centimeter. What is the mass, in grams, of 12 grains of table salt?
ANSWER: 2.64 × 10-5
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The Lorentz transformation in special relativity replaced what transformation used in Newtonian physics?
ANSWER: GALILEAN TRANSFORMATION
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer A wave’s angular frequency is given by the relation ω = k2 + 5k + 4, where k is the wavenumber of the wave. At what wavenumbers will the wave’s phase and group velocity be equal?
ANSWER: 2 AND –2
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What host protein displays an antigen fragment on the cell surface that facilitates recognition by T cells?
ANSWER: MAJOR HISTOCOMPATIBILITY COMPLEX (also accept: MHC)
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT characteristic of Down syndrome?
W) Increasing risk of developing Alzheimer’s disease
X) Increased risk of atherosclerosis
Y) Small white spots on the iris
Z) Decreased risk of having high blood pressure
ANSWER: X) Increased risk of atherosclerosis
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A glacier recedes and removes weight from a region of the Earth’s crust. As a result, the crust there rises. This process is described by which of the following terms?
W) Decompression melting
X) Exfoliation
Y) Frost wedging
Z) Isostatic rebound
ANSWER: Z) Isostatic rebound
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Light that hits a ground-based telescope must first pass through miles of turbulent air in Earth’s atmosphere, blurring the image. What is the name of this effect?
ANSWER: SEEING
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In rectangle ABCD, AB = 8 and BC = 5, and point E is selected uniformly at random from side AB. What is the probability that the area of AECD is greater than 30?
ANSWER: 1/2
16. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the greatest common factor of 96-1 and 276-1?
ANSWER: 728
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What law states that the amount of dissolved gas in a liquid is proportional to its partial pressure above the liquid?
ANSWER: HENRY’S LAW
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In x-ray crystallography, what notation system is often used to define planes in crystal lattices?
ANSWER: MILLER INDICES

W) Corundum
X) Schist
Y) Fluorite
Z) Orthoclase
ANSWER: W) Corundum

ANSWER: GRAVITY (also accept: GRAVITROPISM)

W) Loss of ovary function due to disease
X) Loss of ovary responsiveness to LH and FSH
Y) Breakdown of the endometrium
Z) Loss of function of fallopian tubes
