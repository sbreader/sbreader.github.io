2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following three blood vessels from first to last in terms of time needed to reach them from the right ventricle 1) Pulmonary vein 2) Pulmonary artery 3) Hepatic portal vein.
ANSWER: 2,1,3
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following glial cells is derived from hematopoietic stem cells?
W) Microglia
X) Oligodendrocytes
Y) Ependymal cells
Z) Astrocytes
ANSWER: W) Microglia
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Physicists at the Laboratory for Nuclear Science studied the nuclear decay of radium-226 compounds. Free radium-226 ejects five alpha particles and four beta particles to decay into what stable lead isotope?
ANSWER: LEAD-206
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer The Collins Lab has artificially engineered gut bacteria that are resistant to the beta-lactam family of antibiotics. To do this, they genetically edited what enzyme that is responsible for cross linking peptidoglycan?
ANSWER: TRANSPEPTIDASE
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In a sodium-sulfur battery, molten sodium and molten sulfur react to form sodium polysulfide. Which of the following acts as the cathode in this reaction?
W) Molten sodium
X) Molten sulfur
Y) Graphite
Z) Sodium polysulfide
ANSWER: Y) Graphite
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following real gases has a compressibility factor greater than 1 at sufficiently low pressures?
W) Hydrogen
X) Ammonia
Y) Methane
Z) Sulfur hexafluoride
ANSWER: W) Hydrogen
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What phenomenon occurs when adjacent crystals of a mineral share lattice points, causing them to grow in a symmetric manner?
ANSWER: CRYSTAL TWINNING
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Although fluvial sediment transport ends at the mouth of a river, submarine fans may continue to transport terrigenous sediment hundreds of kilometers away from their associated river deltas. Which of the following best explains how submarine fans transport sediment?
W) Remanent river currents on the ocean floor continue to propagate sediment outwards
X) Oceanic currents pick up river sediment and preserve their buoyancy until they eventually settle out
Y) Water-saturated sediment is denser than oceanic water and causes underwater landslides that carry sediment away from the river delta
Z) Earthquakes cause instability in the submarine fan, causing sediment to lurch downslope
ANSWER: Y) Water-saturated sediment is denser than oceanic water and causes underwater landslides that carry sediment away from the river delta
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The amplitude of a damped harmonic oscillator decays with a half-life of 6 seconds. In seconds, what is the half-life of the oscillator’s total energy?
ANSWER: 3
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer An infinitely long straight wire carries a current of 5 amperes. In teslas and rounded to one significant figure, what is the magnetic field 1 centimeter from the wire?
ANSWER: 0.0001 (also accept: 1 × 10-4)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Brian is studying addiction to Genshin Impact, where gamers’ reward system overreleases dopamine. Which of the following regions of gamers’ brains should Brian inhibit to treat this addiction?
W) Pons
X) Ventral tegmental area
Y) Substantia nigra
Z) Hypothalamus

7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true about fluorescence resonance electron energy transfer?
W) It is extremely sensitive to small changes in distance
X) The energy is transferred through a nonradiative process
Y) The radius of interaction is larger than the wavelength of light emitted
Z) Measurements can be used to determine if two fluorophores are within a certain distance of each other
ANSWER: Y) The radius of interaction is larger than the wavelength of light emitted
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The solvolysis of a fully deuterated alkyl iodide shows a large primary kinetic isotope effect. Which reaction mechanism is most likely?
W) SN1
X) SN2
Y) E1

8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer An aqueous solution is prepared containing silver (I) acetate, barium acetate, and lithium acetate. Order the following three solutions in the order they should be added to selectively precipitate each metal 1) Potassium fluoride 2) Sodium chloride 3) Sodium sulfate.
ANSWER: 2,3,1
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A large sphere is travelling at constant velocity in space in zero gravity. Then it slowly enters a region with a constant gravitational field perpendicular to the sphere’s direction of motion, until the entire sphere is eventually contained in the region. Which of the following best describes the motion of the sphere from the instant it first enters the region to the instant it is fully inside the region?
W) Constant linear acceleration, zero angular acceleration
X) Constant linear acceleration, nonzero angular acceleration
Y) Increasing linear acceleration, zero angular acceleration
Z) Increasing linear acceleration, nonzero angular acceleration
ANSWER: Z) Increasing linear acceleration, nonzero angular acceleration
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three systems that have identical momentum and energy eigenstates 1) Free particle 2) Harmonic oscillator 3) Two-dimensional finite potential well.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the most significant contribution of the 1883 eruption of Krakatoa to the global climate over the following year?
W) Dark carbon aerosols absorbed sunlight, raising temperatures
X) Greenhouse gases trapped infrared radiation, raising temperatures
Y) Ash blotted out the Sun, lowering temperatures
Z) Sulfuric acid promoted cloud formation, lowering temperatures
ANSWER: Z) Sulfuric acid promoted cloud formation, lowering temperatures
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of volcanic eruption does not occur due to movement of magma, but rather groundwater coming in contact with magma and flashing to steam?
ANSWER: PHREATIC ERUPTION (also accept: STEAM BLAST ERUPTION; ULTRAVULCA-NIAN ERUPTION)
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Experimental physicists at MIT study phase transitions in magnetic materials by measuring the ratio of magnetization to magnetic field intensity. What quantity are they measuring?
ANSWER: MAGNETIC SUSCEPTIBILITY (do not accept: SUSCEPTIBILITY)

ANSWER: TOMOGRAPHY (also accept: COMPUTED TOMOGRAPHY; CT; CT SCAN)
