1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The left and right hemispheres of the cerebrum are primarily connected by what structure consisting of a thick myelinated axon bundle?

2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Grant is scuba diving in the ocean and notices that if he looks up at the surface, he sees a circular window of sky above him surrounded by the reflected sea floor. Which of the following statements about this scenario is false?

6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Scientists at Stanford’s Hilley Lab are modeling elevation change along faults.
