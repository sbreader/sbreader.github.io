Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What rock mostly makes up the low velocity zone in the mantle?
W) Basalt
X) Kaolinite
Y) Peridotite
Z) Diorite
ANSWER: Y) Peridotite
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of surface seismic wave causes horizontal shifting of the earth’s surface during an earthquake?
ANSWER: Love Waves
3. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What percent of the energy used by humans has a geothermal source?
W) 1%
X) 7%
Y) 15%
Z) 20%
ANSWER: W) 1%
4. BONUS Energy Short Answer Which type of coal contains the most energy?
ANSWER: Anthracite
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In what time period did the first primitive plants appear on land?
W) Devonian
X) Silurian
Y) Carboniferous
Z) Ordovician
ANSWER: Z) Ordovician
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are calcareous mounds built up of layers of cyanobacteria called? These mounds are the earliest known fossils and still exist in Australia.
ANSWER: Stromatolites
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A ball is thrown in vacuum at 30 degrees from directly upwards with an initial velocity of 30 m/s. How far away from the initial location does the ball land? Let g = 10 N/kg, and give an exact answer.
ANSWER: 45√3 METERS (also accept: 45√3; 45 ROOT 3)
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A small ball of mass m is thrown in the absence of gravity at low speed in a fluid with resistance constant a. After how much time has the ball lost 95% of its initial speed? Give your answer in exact, simplest form.
ANSWER: (m/a) ln 20 (also accept: (m/a) ln(20))
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What are the signs of enthalpy and entropy for an equilibrium for which K = 300 at 10 K and K = 0.1 at 1000 K?
ANSWER: BOTH NEGATIVE (also accept: ENTHALPY IS NEGATIVE AND ENTROPY IS NEGATIVE)
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the derivative with respect to x of ln(sin(x))? Give your answer in simplest form.
ANSWER: cot(x) (also accept: cot x; cos(x)/sin(x))
11. BONUS Math Short Answer Find an expression for the integral from -a to a for any real number a of sin(x) + 2.
ANSWER: 4a
