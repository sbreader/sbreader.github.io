Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is measured in Hertz?
W) speed
X) frequency
Y) wavelength
Z) amplitude
ANSWER: X) frequency
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer For projectiles fired at identical speeds but various angles from the ground, neglecting air resistance and the curvature of the Earth, at what angle will the maximum horizontal distance be attained?
ANSWER: 45 DEGREES (also accept: π/4)
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Commensalism is when:
W) both species benefit
X) neither species affects the other
Y) one species benefits while the other is unaffected
Z) both species are harmed
ANSWER: Y) one species benefits while the other is unaffected
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What does the first word designate in the binomial nomenclature system?
ANSWER: GENUS
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of helium?
W) deep sea divers breath a mixture of helium and oxygen
X) breathing helium causes a temporary change in the pitch and quality of one’s voice
Y) helium gas is commonly used by dentists as a mild anesthetic
Z) a mixture of helium and neon gas produced the first gas laser
ANSWER: Y) helium gas is commonly used by dentists as a mild anesthetic
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer It is estimated that each person in the United States consumes an average of 100 pounds of sucrose each year. Which two simple sugars are linked to make a sucrose molecule?
ANSWER: GLUCOSE AND FRUCTOSE (also accept: D-GLUCOSE AND D-FRUCTOSE; ALDOHEXOSE AND KETOHEXOSE)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The apparent speed of the Sun along the ecliptic:
W) is constant
X) varies during the solar year
Y) varies only during winter
Z) varies only during summer
ANSWER: X) varies during the solar year
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the scientific names for the two regions of a sunspot?
ANSWER: UMBRA AND PENUMBRA
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What would a graph of the equation f(x) = |x| look like?
W) it would be shaped like the letter V
X) it would have a sigmoid shape
Y) it would have a parabolic shape
Z) it would have a sine wave shape
ANSWER: W) it would be shaped like the letter V
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Six factorial equals what number?
ANSWER: 720
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In determining the distance of an earthquake to its epicenter, which of the following is the most useful tool:
W) intensity of P-wave
X) intensity of S-wave
Y) difference in intensity between P and S-waves
Z) time interval between P-wave and S-wave arrival
ANSWER: Z) time interval between P-wave and S-wave arrival
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A solid compound whose internal structural pattern is expressed as plane faces that can be seen with the unaided eye is generally known as a:
ANSWER: CRYSTAL (also accept: CRYSTALLINE)
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly describes STP:
W) 4° C at 1 Atmosphere
X) 0° C at 0 Atmosphere
Y) 23° C at 1 Atmosphere
Z) 0° C at 1 Atmosphere
ANSWER: Z) 0° C at 1 Atmosphere
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer What are the 7 colors that Newton used to describe the visible light spectrum:
ANSWER: RED, ORANGE, YELLOW, GREEN, BLUE, INDIGO, AND VIOLET
8. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice A typical CD-ROM can hold a maximum of about how much data?
W) 650 MB
X) 1.5 GB
Y) 2.5 GB
Z) 15 GB
ANSWER: W) 650 MB
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer What does the acronym CPU stand for?
ANSWER: CENTRAL PROCESSING UNIT
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the term for the speed at which an object will overcome the gravitational attraction of the Earth?
ANSWER: ESCAPE VELOCITY
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What kingdom of organisms does a professional Mycologist study?
ANSWER: FUNGI
10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following responses do auxins NOT typically produce in plants:
W) cell elongation
X) fruit maturation
Y) xylem differentiation
Z) pollen tube growth
ANSWER: Z) pollen tube growth
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What molecule is characteristically prevalent in sour milk and tired muscles?
ANSWER: LACTIC ACID
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following has the same electron configuration as neon:
W) Ar
X) F
Y) Cl-
Z) Na+
ANSWER: Z) Na+
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer This powerful telescope weighs about 11 tons, has a primary mirror 7.9 feet in diameter, and orbits about 380 miles above the earth:
ANSWER: THE HUBBLE TELESCOPE (also accept: HUBBLE; HUBBLE SPACE TELESCOPE)
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In the Doppler effect, what is the correct term for the change in the color of light when an object that is emitting light is moving toward the observer?
ANSWER: BLUE-SHIFT
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The converse of this theorem is: if the sum of the squares of two sides of a triangle equal the square of the third side then the triangle is a right triangle. What is the name of the theorem?
ANSWER: PYTHAGOREAN THEOREM
13. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Assuming a year in a dog’s life is equivalent to 7 years of a human’s life, what is 1 hour of a dog’s life approximately equivalent to in a human’s life?
W) 3.5 hours
X) 7 hours
Y) 5 hours
Z) 4.5 days
ANSWER: X) 7 hours
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is primarily involved in the movement of a glacier:
W) running water
X) ambient temperature
Y) topology
Z) gravity
ANSWER: Z) gravity
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the margin where two plates in the lithosphere slide past each other?
ANSWER: TRANSFORM FAULT MARGINS
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Iron pyrite is more popularly called:
ANSWER: FOOL'S GOLD
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer In 1546, Frascatorius, by studying epidemics of plague and syphilis, compiled an impressive body of evidence to show disease was transmitted from person to person and is therefore considered to have founded what branch of science?
ANSWER: EPIDEMIOLOGY
16. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice TCP/IP is an example of:
W) a computing environment
X) a database model
Y) a 4th generation computer language
Z) a networking protocol
ANSWER: Z) a networking protocol
16. BONUS Energy Short Answer What does the acronym LCD stand for?
ANSWER: LIQUID CRYSTAL DISPLAY
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What large machine, which came on line in the summer of 2000 at the Department of Energy’s Brookhaven National Lab, is the world’s highest energy ion collider:
W) Advanced Photon Source
X) CERN supercollider
Y) Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider
Z) Phoenix
ANSWER: Y) Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer According to the standard model of particle physics, what is the name for the three leptons that have no electric charge and little if any mass?
ANSWER: NEUTRINO (also accept: ELECTRON NEUTRINO; TAU NEUTRINO; MUON NEUTRINO)
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true about toxic shock syndrome:
W) it is not known to occur in males
X) it is caused by a bacteria
Y) up to 15% of healthy women harbor the causative germ in their vaginal flora
Z) it is associated with infrequent changes of super absorbent tampons
ANSWER: W) it is not known to occur in males
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer What kind of microscopy does the following describe: Samples are treated with an electron-dense material and then cut into thin layers. While inside the microscope’s column, electrons that are passed through the sample and differentially scattered to show a silhouetted image on a phosphorescent screen:
ANSWER: TRANSMISSION ELECTRON MICROSCOPY (also accept: TEM) (do not accept: SCANNING ELECTRON MICROSCOPY; SEM)
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is true of bases:
W) they give rise to hydrogen ions when dissolved in water
X) they are substances that accept or react with hydrogen ions
Y) they always contain the hydroxide ion in it’s structure
Z) they have a pH below 7
ANSWER: X) they are substances that accept or react with hydrogen ions
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Myoglobin binds oxygen in what tissue in the human body?
ANSWER: MUSCLE
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In the Australian winter night sky, which of the following is true regarding the constellation Orion:
W) it is seen in the western sky
X) it is seen in the southern sky
Y) it is seen in the northern sky
Z) it can never be seen
ANSWER: Y) it is seen in the northern sky
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The Kappa Cygnids and the Northern Delta Aquarids are names for what phenomena?
ANSWER: METEOR SHOWERS
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer As glaciers erode the surface over which they move, sets of parallel linear grooves are sometime worn into the rock surface. These grooves are called:
ANSWER: STRIATIONS
21. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Of the following, which are clouds formed at the highest altitude:
W) altocumulus
X) altostratus
Y) cirrocumulus
Z) nimbostratus
ANSWER: Y) cirrocumulus
22. BONUS Energy Short Answer What two Federal scientific agencies are responsible for the general administration of the human genome project?
ANSWER: DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY AND NATIONAL INSTITUTES OF HEALTH (also accept: DOE and NIH)
23. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which general theory of science allows scientists to quantitatively understand the behavior of molecules, solids, liquids, and both conductors and semiconductors:
W) General Theory of Relativity
X) Gravitational Theory
Y) Special Theory of Relativity
Z) Quantum Theory
ANSWER: Z) Quantum Theory
23. BONUS Physics Short Answer This perceived force, that seems to want to throw a person off a merry-go-round, is often regarded by physicists as a fictitious force:
ANSWER: CENTRIFUGAL FORCE
24. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is water loss through the stomata of a plant called:
W) condensation
X) liquidation
Y) transpiration
Z) exchange
ANSWER: Y) transpiration
24. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which germ layer in mammals is predominantly responsible for forming the nervous system?
ANSWER: ECTODERM
25. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The wavelength of yellow light is 600 nanometers. What is the wavelength in centimeters:
W) 6.0 × 10-9
X) 6.0 × 10-7
Y) 6.0 × 10-5
Z) 6.0 × 10-2
ANSWER: Y) 6.0 × 10-5
25. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for the pH at which an amino acid or protein becomes electrically neutral?
ANSWER: ISOELECTRIC POINT
