Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Certain molecules of the human immune system are designated by the abbreviation MHC. What does MHC stand for?
W) Molecular Histone Complex
X) Major Histocompatibility Complex
Y) Methionine Hormone Catalyst
Z) Methionine Hydrogenase Complex
ANSWER: X) Major Histocompatibility Complex
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the name of the portion of an antigen that an antibody recognizes?
W) Epitope
X) Pathogenic determinant
Y) Heavy chain
Z) Light chain
ANSWER: W) Epitope
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the 5-carbon intermediate of the citric acid cycle?
ANSWER: α-KETOGLUTARATE
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name of the massive flowering of life that followed the Precambrian Era.
ANSWER: CAMBRIAN EXPLOSION
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Arrange the following in order of their appearance in the history of life: angiosperms, nonvascular plants, gymnosperms.
ANSWER: NONVASCULAR PLANTS, GYMNOSPERMS, ANGIOSPERMS
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the common name of the human olecranon process?
ANSWER: ELBOW
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the scientific name of the muscle commonly known as the calf of human leg?
W) Trapezius
X) Gastrocnemius
Y) Quadriceps brachii
Z) Quadriceps femorii
ANSWER: X) Gastrocnemius
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Cells that are capable of developing into all the different cell types in an organism are known as
W) Omnipotent
X) Totipotent
Y) Pluripotent
Z) Deuteropotent
ANSWER: X) Totipotent
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What French scientist proposed the principle species evolve through the use and disuse of body parts?
ANSWER: JEAN-BAPTISTE DE LAMARCK (also accept: LAMARCK)
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What the name for the study of the geographic distribution of a species?
ANSWER: BIOGEOGRAPHY
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The natural selection of traits that are population density-dependent over traits that are not population density-independent is known as
W) Q-selection
X) K-selection
Y) r-selection
Z) b-selection
ANSWER: X) K-selection
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Certain populations will grow logarithmically. As time progress, they will approach a limit where they will display no growth. What is the name of this limit?
W) Zero population growth equilibrium
X) Carrying capacity
Y) J line
Z) Growth cap
ANSWER: X) Carrying capacity
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the most abundant protein on Earth?
W) Insulin
X) Collagen
Y) Sucrase
Z) Rubisco
ANSWER: Z) Rubisco
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the two major types of animal conditioning.
ANSWER: CLASSICAL AND OPERANT (also accept: CLASSICAL CONDITIONING AND OPERANT CONDITIONING)
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What part of the peripheral nervous system causes "rest and digest" responses?
ANSWER: PARASYMPATHETIC NERVOUS SYSTEM (also accept: PARASYMPATHETIC)
10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a common neurotransmitter?
W) Gamma-aminobutyric acid
X) Glycine
Y) Serotonin
Z) Indole acetic acid
ANSWER: Z) Indole acetic acid
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Oogenesis is the process by which one primary oocyte divides into four cells. How many of these cells are viable eggs?
ANSWER: ONE
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the name of the open end of a gastrula?
W) Blastopore
X) Archenteron
Y) Protostome
Z) Dorsal lip
ANSWER: W) Blastopore
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the two types of transport vessels in plants.
ANSWER: XYLEM AND PHLOEM
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a dicot?
W) Corn
X) Grass
Y) Tulips
Z) Roses
ANSWER: Z) Roses
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organelles produces lipids?
W) Smooth endoplasmic reticulum
X) Nucleus
Y) Mitochondria
Z) Plastid
ANSWER: W) Smooth endoplasmic reticulum
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the three components of the central dogma of biology?
ANSWER: DNA, RNA, PROTEIN
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the second stage of mitosis.
ANSWER: METAPHASE
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following most accurately describes meiosis?
W) The division of one cell into two cells in one step
X) The division of one cell into four cells in one step
Y) The division of one cell into two cells in two steps
Z) The division of one cell into four cells in two steps
ANSWER: Z) The division of one cell into four cells in two steps
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What biome covers the greatest amount of the earth's surface area?
ANSWER: TAIGA
15. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following has the greatest net primary productivity?
W) The Pacific Ocean
X) The Great Plains
Y) The Great Barrier Reef
Z) The Andes
ANSWER: W) The Pacific Ocean
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the scientific name of the infection of the bone by bacteria?
ANSWER: OSTEOMYELITIS
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In humans, the atlas is what number cervical vertebra?
W) First
X) Second
Y) Third
Z) Fourth
ANSWER: W) First
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a genetically inherited disease?
W) Phenylketonuria
X) Sickle Cell Anemia
Y) Polio
Z) Juvenile Diabetes
ANSWER: Y) Polio
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the disease in which immune cells destroy the myelin sheaths of axons.
ANSWER: MULTIPLE SCLEROSIS (also accept: MS)
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Strands of DNA wrapped around globules of protein are known as
W) Osteons
X) Histones
Y) Flagellates
Z) Kinetochores
ANSWER: X) Histones
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the diploid number of humans?
ANSWER: 46 (also accept: 2N EQUALS 46)
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following plant hormones causes fruit to ripen?
W) Auxin
X) Gibberellin
Y) Abscisic acid
Z) Ethylene gas
ANSWER: Z) Ethylene gas
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer The part of the plant that is located aboveground is known as what?
ANSWER: SHOOT
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a vertebrate?
W) Shark
X) Roundworm
Y) Platypus
Z) Rotifer
ANSWER: Y) Platypus
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the protist that causes malaria?
ANSWER: PLASMODIUM
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A virus that infects bacteria is known as what?
ANSWER: BACTERIOPHAGE
21. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Koch's postulates are used to determine which of the following?
W) The cause of an infectious disease
X) The mechanism of seed dispersal
Y) The compatibility of blood types in a transfusion patient
Z) The likelihood of a graft-versus-host response
ANSWER: W) The cause of an infectious disease
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In humans, T cells complete their development in what organ?
ANSWER: THYMUS
22. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The colostrum confers what type of immunity to a baby?
W) Passive
X) Active
Y) Secondary
Z) Tertiary
ANSWER: W) Passive
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of alcohol is the drinking alcohol found in liquor?
ANSWER: ETHANOL
23. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the waste product produced by human muscle cells when they undergo anaerobic respiration?
W) Ethanol
X) Butanol
Y) Lactic Acid
Z) Fumarate
ANSWER: Y) Lactic Acid
24. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a kingdom of life?
W) Bacteria
X) Animalia
Y) Plantae
Z) Monera
ANSWER: W) Bacteria
24. BONUS Biology Short Answer Halophiles are members of what domain?
ANSWER: ARCHAEA
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the term in which two pollen grains fertilize one ovule?
ANSWER: DOUBLE FERTILIZATION
25. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes perennial plants?
W) Their life cycles are one year
X) Their life cycles are seasonal
Y) Their life cycles are several years
Z) Their life cycles are biannual
ANSWER: Y) Their life cycles are several years
