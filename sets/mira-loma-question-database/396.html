Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The energy of an object or system is
W) how much work it can do on another object or system
X) how much work it cannot do on another object or system
Y) the amount of time it can do work on another object or system
Z) the amount of time it cannot do work on another object or system
ANSWER: W) how much work it can do on another object or system
1. BONUS Energy Short Answer Energy is the ability to perform
ANSWER: work
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Energy can be measured in
W) force
X) light
Y) work
Z) time
ANSWER: Y) work
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What are the two most basic forms of energy?
W) potential energy and unpotential energy
X) kinetic energy and unkinetic energy
Y) potential energy and kinetic energy
Z) real energy and fake energy
ANSWER: Y) potential energy and kinetic energy
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer As an object starts to move, potential energy is converted into
ANSWER: kinetic energy
4. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is a form of energy?
W) real energy
X) star energy
Y) fake energy
Z) thermal energy
ANSWER: Z) thermal energy
4. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is another name for thermal energy?
ANSWER: heat energy
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the speed of light in m/s to one significant figure?
W) 3 × 103 m/s
X) 3 × 107 m/s
Y) 3 × 108 m/s
Z) 3 × 1012 m/s
ANSWER: Y) 3 × 108 m/s
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer In the equation E = mc2, what symbol represents the speed of light?
ANSWER: c
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Sound and light energy travel in
W) rays
X) waves
Y) vacuums
Z) black holes
ANSWER: X) waves
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Can both sound and light waves travel in vacuums?
ANSWER: No
7. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What is the part of the light spectrum that we can be seen by the naked eye called?
W) x-rays
X) radio waves
Y) visible light
Z) sound waves
ANSWER: Y) visible light
7. BONUS Energy Short Answer Which color of visible light has the lowest frequency?
ANSWER: red
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which type of light wave has the longest wavelength?
W) gamma-rays
X) x-rays
Y) infrared waves
Z) radio waves
ANSWER: Z) radio waves
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer Do FM or AM radio waves have longer wavelengths?
ANSWER: AM radio waves
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which type of light has the highest frequency?
W) gamma-rays
X) x-rays
Y) ultraviolet waves
Z) infrared waves
ANSWER: W) gamma-rays
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer How many meters long is one wave of a gamma-ray?
ANSWER: 10-12 meters
10. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What are three types of fossil fules?
W) gasoline, heat, and energy
X) coal, petroleum, and heat
Y) petroleum, natural gas, and unnatural gas
Z) coal, petroleum, and natural gas
ANSWER: Z) coal, petroleum, and natural gas
10. BONUS Energy Short Answer Burning fossil fuels generates
ANSWER: electricity
11. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What type of energy heats the Earth?
W) electromagnetic energy
X) nuclear energy
Y) potential energy
Z) chemical energy
ANSWER: X) nuclear energy
11. BONUS Energy Short Answer What happens to an atom in order to create nuclear energy?
ANSWER: the nucleus is split into 2 smaller nuclei
12. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What percentage of oil is used in automobiles?
W) 22 %
X) 35 %
Y) 55 %
Z) 75 %
ANSWER: Y) 55 %
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer Automobiles account for what fraction of greenhouse emissions?
ANSWER: 1/3
13. BONUS Energy Short Answer In scientific notation, how many joules are in 1 kN X 1 km?
ANSWER: 1 × 106 J
14. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the unit for power?
W) watt
X) joule
Y) newton
Z) kilometer
ANSWER: W) watt
15. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What is the formula for kinetic energy?
W) Kinetic Energy = 1/2 X mass X speed
X) Kinetic Energy = mass X speed
Y) Kinetic Energy = 1/2 X mass X speed2
Z) Kinetic Energy = mass X speed2
ANSWER: Y) Kinetic Energy = 1/2 X mass X speed2
15. BONUS Energy Short Answer Which has more kinetic energy: heavier objects or lighter objects?
ANSWER: heavier objects
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer How much work is done on a 12,000 newton object when it is lifted a height of 1.5 meters?
ANSWER: 18,000 joules
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer To seven significant figures, how much work is done on a 97,634.00 newton object when it pushed a distance of 0.5000000 meters?
ANSWER: 48,817.00 joules
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Through what equation can mass be turned into energy?
ANSWER: E = mc2
17. BONUS Energy Short Answer The formula E = mc2 is used in what type of reactions?
ANSWER: nuclear reactions
19. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Renewable resources are types of energy that
W) can be replaced in nature in a short period of time
X) cannot be replaced in nature in a short period of time
Y) can be replaced in factories in a short period of time
Z) are usually useless
ANSWER: W) can be replaced in nature in a short period of time
19. BONUS Energy Short Answer What are the 6 types of renewable energy
ANSWER: solar energy, hydropower, wind power, biomass, biofuel, and geothermal energy
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Most living organisms feed on molecules called
W) carboxides
X) hydroxites
Y) sulfates
Z) hydrocarbons
ANSWER: Z) hydrocarbons
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Hydrocarbons release energy when combined with
ANSWER: Oxygen
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is gravitational potential energy
ANSWER: potential energy due to elevated position
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the formula for gravitational potential energy?
ANSWER: GPE = Weight X Height
22. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In the formula for gravitational potential energy, what unit is used for weight?
W) grams
X) centigrams
Y) kilograms
Z) megagrams
ANSWER: Y) kilograms
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer If an object weighs 20 kg and is 20 meters above the ground, what is its gravitational potential energy?
ANSWER: 4000 J (also accept: 3920 J)
23. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In the formula for gravitational potential energy, what unit is used for height?
ANSWER: meters
23. BONUS Physics Short Answer If an object weighs 10 kg and has a gravitational potential energy of 65 joules, how high is it from the ground?
ANSWER: 0.65 meters
24. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice One paper clip has about the energy of
W) a campfire
X) a bonfire
Y) a conventional bomb
Z) an atomic bomb
ANSWER: Z) an atomic bomb
24. BONUS Physics Short Answer The energy of a paper clip can be found by using what equation?
ANSWER: E = mc2
25. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which type of light has the lowest frequency?
W) radio waves
X) x-rays
Y) ultraviolet waves
Z) gamma-rays
ANSWER: W) radio waves
25. BONUS Physics Short Answer How many meters long is one wave of a radio wave?
ANSWER: 103 meters
