Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The largest circular storm in our solar system is on the surface of which planet?
ANSWER: JUPITER
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The comet known as Halley's Comet has an average period of:
W) 56 years
X) 66 years
Y) 76 years
Z) 86 years
ANSWER: Y) 76 years
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which one of the following planets has no moons?
W) Mars
X) Neptune
Y) Venus
Z) Jupiter
ANSWER: Y) Venus
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which planet has the shortest year?
ANSWER: MERCURY
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In kilometers, the earth's average distance from the sun is roughly which of the following distances?
W) 250 million
X) 91 million
Y) 150 million
Z) 350 million
ANSWER: Y) 150 million
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The gravity on the moon is what fraction of the gravity on the earth?
W) 1/3
X) 2/3
Y) 1/6
Z) 1/10
ANSWER: Y) 1/6
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The rocks that enter the earth's atmosphere and blaze a trail all the way to the ground and do not burn up completely are known as:
W) meteorites
X) meteors
Y) asteroids
Z) none of these
ANSWER: W) meteorites
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice 95% of the Martian atmosphere is composed of what substance?
W) Carbon dioxide
X) Nitrogen
Y) Argon
Z) Carbon monoxide
ANSWER: W) Carbon dioxide
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer How many of Jupiter's moons did Galileo see with his first telescope?
ANSWER: FOUR
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the definition of one Astronomical Unit?
ANSWER: THE DISTANCE FROM THE SUN TO THE EARTH (also accept: AVERAGE DISTANCE FROM THE EARTH TO THE SUN; RADIUS OF EARTH'S ORBIT)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the motion called when a planet seems to be moving westward in the sky?
W) Retrograde
X) Parallax
Y) Opcentric
Z) Reverse parallax
ANSWER: W) Retrograde
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In the Copernican system, what is the shape of the planets' orbits?
ANSWER: CIRCLE
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice With which one of the following astronomical objects are meteor showers associated?
W) Asteroids
X) Comets
Y) Planets
Z) None of the above
ANSWER: X) Comets
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A device which would not work on the Moon is:
W) thermometer
X) siphon
Y) spectrometer
Z) spring balance
ANSWER: X) siphon
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Where is the Great Red Spot?
ANSWER: JUPITER
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What astronomical event made the headlines in 1986?
ANSWER: HALLEY'S COMET
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What celestial body is known for its 11 year cycle?
ANSWER: SUN
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are Callisto and Ganymede?
ANSWER: TWO OF JUPITER'S MOONS
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What feature do only Jupiter, Saturn, Neptune and Uranus have in common?
ANSWER: RINGS
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The size of the average meteor is about the size of which of the following objects?
W) a grain of sand
X) a baseball
Y) a basket ball
Z) a car
ANSWER: W) a grain of sand
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When could you see Baily's Beads?
ANSWER: DURING A TOTAL SOLAR ECLIPSE
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Geocentric means around:
W) Jupiter
X) the Earth
Y) the Moon
Z) the Sun
ANSWER: X) the Earth
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer How many moons does Mars have?
ANSWER: TWO
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer During what phase of the Moon can a total visual eclipse of the Moon occur?
ANSWER: FULL MOON
26. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the more common name for the aurora borealis?
ANSWER: NORTHERN LIGHTS
27. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which planet is the Earth's twin size-wise?
ANSWER: VENUS
28. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What belts are composed of charged particles surrounding the Earth?
ANSWER: VAN ALLEN BELTS
29. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are "cool" areas on the surface of the Sun called?
ANSWER: SUN SPOTS
30. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which is the only moon in the solar system with a substantial atmosphere?
ANSWER: SATURN'S TITAN
31. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer True or false: The moon is denser than the earth.
ANSWER: FALSE
32. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Does a comet's apparent size decrease, remain the same, or increase as the comet gets closer to the Sun?
ANSWER: INCREASE (also accept: INCREASES)
33. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Pythagoreans appear to have been the first to have taught that the Earth is:
W) at the center of the Universe.
X) spherical in shape.
Y) orbits around the sun.
Z) flat with sharp edges.
ANSWER: X) spherical in shape.
34. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The light-gathering power of a reflecting telescope depends on which of the following?
W) the area of its objective mirror
X) focal length of its eyepiece
Y) focal length of its objective mirror
Z) the ratio of the focal lengths of objective and eyepiece
ANSWER: W) the area of its objective mirror
35. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Who was the first individual to observe the phases of Venus? He observed Venus' phases with a telescope that he had built.
ANSWER: GALILEO
36. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Who first used Tycho Brahe's observational data on the planet Mars to determine that Mars actually traversed an elliptical orbit, the sun being located at one of the foci?
W) Kepler
X) Galileo
Y) Hipparchus
Z) Aristarchus
ANSWER: W) Kepler
37. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer True or false: Newton taught that the natural state of motion of a body is one of uniform motion in a circular path.
ANSWER: FALSE
38. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Approximately how many times could a beam of light travel around the earth in one second?
W) 3 times
X) 7.5 times
Y) 15 times
Z) 25 times
ANSWER: X) 7.5 times
39. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The diameter of the earth is approximately:
W) 5,000 kilometers
X) 13,000 kilometers
Y) 18,000 kilometers
Z) 25,000 kilometers
ANSWER: X) 13,000 kilometers
40. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The average density of the earth is approximately:
W) half the density of water
X) twice the density of water
Y) five and a half times the density of water
Z) ten times the density of water
ANSWER: Y) five and a half times the density of water
41. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The earth's motion around the sun causes relatively nearby stars to shift against the background of the more distant fixed stars. What term is used to describe this phenomenon.
ANSWER: PARALLAX
42. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What name is given to the situation where the sun crosses the celestial equator around March 21?
ANSWER: VERNAL EQUINOX

W) orange
X) violet
Y) green
Z) red
ANSWER: Z) red
44. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In a reflecting telescope where in the tube is the objective mirror placed?
W) the top to the tube
X) the middle of the tube
Y) the bottom of the tube
Z) the side of the tube
ANSWER: Y) the bottom of the tube
45. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The diameter of the disk of our galaxy, within a factor of two, is how many light years?
ANSWER: 100,000 LIGHT-YEARS (also accept: 100,000)
46. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the principal source of a young star's energy?
ANSWER: FUSION OF HYDROGEN TO HELIUM
47. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the approximate age of our sun?
W) 1 billion years
X) 2 billion years
Y) 5 billion years
Z) 20 billion years
ANSWER: Y) 5 billion years
48. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What does it mean when someone says that comets have eccentric orbits? Does it mean
W) they have open orbits
X) they have nearly circular orbits
Y) their orbits are unpredictable
Z) the sun is far from the foci of their orbits
ANSWER: Z) the sun is far from the foci of their orbits

W) Mercury
X) Venus
Y) Saturn
Z) Mars
ANSWER: Y) Saturn
50. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What planet comes closest to the Earth?
ANSWER: VENUS

X) air pressure
Y) centrifugal force
Z) gravity

X) billions of tiny solid particles

Z) highly reflective cosmic clouds
ANSWER: X) billions of tiny solid particles
53. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer True or false: Canals have been photographed on Mars by recent space probes.
ANSWER: FALSE
54. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Of the following, which is the only planet which CANNOT be seen with the unaided eye?
W) Jupiter
X) Mars
Y) Neptune
Z) Saturn
ANSWER: Y) Neptune
55. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Accretion is:
W) the gradual accumulation of matter in one location usually due to gravity.
X) the process of moon formation for planets.
Y) the process of matter accumulation due to centripetal force.
Z) the disintegration of matter.
ANSWER: W) the gradual accumulation of matter in one location usually due to gravity.

ANSWER: LENGTHENED

W) 500
X) 3.5
Y) 18
Z) 1000

W) it is a huge star which appears black at its center.
X) its gravity is so high that it absorbs its own photons.
Y) it represents lack of matter in a portion of space.
Z) it is a dead planet.
ANSWER: X) its gravity is so high that it absorbs its own photons.
