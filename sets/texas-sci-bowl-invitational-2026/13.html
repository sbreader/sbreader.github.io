1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In the complement system, the lectin pathway is most commonly triggered by the binding of lectins to what carbohydrate found on the surface of microbial pathogens?

4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A satellite in a bound Kepler orbit has semi-major axis a and total mechanical energy E = −GMm/(2a). Using the virial theorem, what is the time-averaged speed ⟨v⟩ in terms of G, M, and a?

4. BONUS Physics Short Answer A bead of mass m slides without friction on a circular hoop of radius R rotating about a vertical diameter with constant angular velocity ω. At what angle θ from the bottom of the hoop (measured from center) is there a stable equilibrium for ω > √(g/R)?
ANSWER: θ = cos-1(g/(Rω2))
