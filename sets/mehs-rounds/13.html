1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An entire glass of wine flows down Tyrell Wellick’s throat at an elevation of 0.9 meters. As the elevation of his throat gradually decreases, and assuming pressure is constant, what other quantity of the fluid flow must increase?
ANSWER: VELOCITY (also accept: SPEED)
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer The molar mass of smoke is 289 kilograms per mole. Assuming the temperature of the room is 8.31 kelvin, and the boltzmann constant is 8.31, and giving your answer in terms of radical 3 meters rounded to the nearest tenth, what is the root mean square speed of the smoke?
ANSWER: 0.5
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following i s the molecular geometry of iodine heptafluoride?
W) Octahedral
X) Pentagonal bipyramidal
Y) Trigonal prismatic
Z) Cubic
ANSWER: X) Pentagonal bipyramidal
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What ring structure does the cyclohexane ring adopt in order to minimize molecular strain?
ANSWER: CHAIR
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab are using x-rays to study the potential of using existing drugs to treat COVID-19. Scientists have determined that several hepatitis C drugs can inhibit the SARS-CoV-2 main protease. What organ does the hepatitis C virus mainly affect by causing inflammation?
ANSWER: LIVER
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab are researching the impact of deep convective circulations on large storm complexes. Identify all of the following 3 statements regarding the circulation and development of thunderstorms that are true 1) Thunderstorms start to dissipate shortly after it reaches the mature stage 2) Multicell thunderstorms form in regions with weak wind speed shear 3) Ordinary cell thunderstorms tend to form in regions with limited vertical wind shear.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Thermohaline circulation drives the ocean conveyor belt. Thermohaline circulation is driven by global density gradients. What two factors together determine the density of seawater?
ANSWER: TEMPERATURE AND SALINITY
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 statements regarding rayleigh waves that are true 1) Rayleigh waves travel as ripples through liquids 2) They are body waves that have a similar wavelength to love waves 3) They have a distinct transverse wave motion that decreases in amplitude as the distance from the surface increases.
ANSWER: NONE OF THEM

ANSWER: 31.5 (also accept: 63/2)

X) Molybdenum
Y) Indium

ANSWER: Z) Tantalum

ANSWER: 2-PENTANAL
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A wire carries a negative charge with a current density in the negative z direction. In what direction does the velocity in the wire move?
ANSWER: POSITIVE Z
7. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A satellite burning at the perapsis of its’ orbit will gain more speed than if it burned at the apoapsis of its’ orbit. This phenomena is best explained by which of the following?
W) Tsiolkovsky rocket equation
X) Orbital decay
Y) Oberth effect
Z) Newton’s law of universal gravitation
ANSWER: Y) Oberth effect
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The pericycle is a cylindrical structure in plants that strengthens the roots and provides protection for the vascular bundles. What two ground tissue types mainly make up the pericycle?
ANSWER: PARENCHYMA AND SCLERENCHYMA
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following calcium-regulating hormones maintains the level of calcium and stimulates both resorption and formation of bones?
W) PTH
X) Calcitriol
Y) Calcitonin
Z) TSH
ANSWER: W) PTH
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of cloud forms in the stratosphere during the winter in northerly latitudes and are best observed during civil twilight due to its bright, iridescent appearance?
ANSWER: POLAR STRATOSPHERIC CLOUD (also accept: NACREOUS CLOUDS)
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer To one significant digit, what is the distance modulus of a star that has an apparent magnitude of 5.4 and an absolute magnitude of 2.4?
ANSWER: 40
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of degree measures satisfies the equation secant of x is equal to 2 over the square root of 3?
W) 30 and 330
X) 60 and 120
Y) 60 and 240
Z) 150 and 210
ANSWER: W) 30 and 330
10. BONUS Math Short Answer A 13 foot ladder is sliding down a wall at a rate of 1 foot per second. How fast is the distance between the ladder and the wall changing, in feet per second, when the base of the ladder is 5 feet from the wall?
ANSWER: 2.4 (also accept: 12/5)
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice If a planet with gravitational acceleration g equals 9 meters per second squared has its radius doubled and its density halved, in meters per second squared, what is the new gravitational acceleration of the planet?
W) 4.5
X) 9
Y) 18
Z) 36
ANSWER: X) 9
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer The maximum value for which quantity can be found by taking one minus the difference in temperature of the hot reservoir and the cold reservoir all over the temperature of the cold reservoir?
ANSWER: THERMAL EFFICIENCY (also accept: EFFICIENCY)
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at Fermi National Accelerator Lab have been studying high-energy proton antiproton collisions. A theoretical reaction of a proton decaying into a positron and electron neutrino violates which of the following conservation laws?
W) Conservation of lepton number
X) Conservation of charge
Y) Conservation of energy
Z) Conservation of baryon number
ANSWER: Z) Conservation of baryon number
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Lab have been studying the role of different types of conductors in the national power grid. To minimize power losses due to resistance, what quantity is maximized in transmission lines which transmit electricity over long distances?
ANSWER: VOLTAGE
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In the extracellular matrix of animal cells, cells are binded to integrins on the membrane by what glycoprotein?
ANSWER: FIBRONECTIN

ANSWER: AUTOLYSIS
