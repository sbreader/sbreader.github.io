1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A square with a side length of 4 is overlapping with a circle of radius 2 whose center lies on one of the square's corners. What is the area of the portion of the square that is not overlapping?

2. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A circle is split into multiple slices. Each slice has an angle measure of 10° or any other multiple of 10. If the amount of slices there are is equal to n, then what is the sum of all possible values of n added together?

X) 624
Y) 666
Z) 743

X) Amebic dysentery
Y) Schistosomiasis
Z) Plasmodia

ANSWER: BORN-HABER CYCLE
