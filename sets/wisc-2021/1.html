1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Object A is dropped off a cliff. Object B is thrown horizontally from the same height with an initial velocity of 4.6 m/s. After 3 seconds, how far apart are the two objects?
ANSWER: 13.8 METERS
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer A cannonball is launched at an angle of 15 degrees to hit an object 10 meters away. If the cannonball exactly hits its target, what was its initial velocity to the nearest meter per second?
ANSWER: 14
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following vitamins which are fat-soluble 1) A 2) D 3) E 4) K
ANSWER: All
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In what form of mimicry is the mimic just as harmful or unpalatable as the original species?
ANSWER: MULLERIAN MIMICRY
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following groups of protists is most closely related to plants?
W) Euglenozoans
X) Golden Algae
Y) Green Algae
Z) Brown Algae
ANSWER: Y) Green Algae
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the most common gas in the atmosphere of Jupiter?
W) Hydrogen
X) Nitrogen
Y) Methane
Z) Ammonia
ANSWER: W) Hydrogen
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Choose all of the following four moons which are larger than Pluto? 1) Ganymede 2) Triton 3) Earth's Moon 4) Io
ANSWER: ALL (also accept: 1, 2, 3, 4; GANYMEDE, TRITON, EARTH’S MOON, IO)
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What point inside a triangle refers to the intersection of the angle bisectors?
W) Centroid
X) Orthocenter
Y) Circumcenter
Z) Incenter
ANSWER: Z) Incenter
4. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area of a triangle with side lengths of 5, 6, and 7?
ANSWER: 6√6 (also accept: 6 ROOT 6)
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following salts will NOT produce a colored solution when dissolved in water?
W) Sodium chromate
X) Copper chloride
Y) Cobalt chloride
Z) Barium hydroxide
ANSWER: Z) Barium hydroxide
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three conditions that would shift the equilibrium to the products in the synthesis of ammonia from hydrogen and nitrogen 1) Increasing the pressure of the container 2) Addition of ammonia 3) Addition of nitrogen.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at the Jet Propulsion Biotechnology and Planetary Protection group have been studying microorganisms on earth that may be able to withstand the high salt concentrations on mars. Most such extreme halophiles are members of what domain?
ANSWER: ARCHAEA
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab are trying to identify which areas on the West coast would be the best spots for harnessing wave energy. Which of the following is not a factor that affects the energy in a wave formed in the open ocean?
W) Wind speed
X) Distance wind blows
Y) Ocean depth
Z) Duration of wind
ANSWER: Y) Ocean depth
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about uniform circular motion?
W) Centripetal acceleration is directly proportional to the radius
X) Velocity remains constant
Y) Centripetal acceleration is directly proportional to velocity
Z) Kinetic energy remains constant
ANSWER: Z) Kinetic energy remains constant
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer Sephiroth has a mass of about 6 kg. If this were the case, how much average power would you need to be exerting over 3 seconds to send him flying at a speed of 3 m/s?
ANSWER: 9 WATTS
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Huntington’s disease, a condition which causes the progressive degeneration of the nervous system, follows which of the following inheritance patterns?
W) X Linked Recessive
X) Mitochondrial
Y) Autosomal Dominant
Z) Autosomal Recessive
ANSWER: Y) Autosomal Dominant
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following DNA elements are not found in eukaryotic DNA?
W) Promoter
X) Operator
Y) Enhancer
Z) Poly A Signal
ANSWER: X) Operator
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What property of the atmosphere is measured by a barometer?
ANSWER: ATMOSPHERIC PRESSURE (also accept: AIR PRESSURE; PRESSURE)
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following metamorphic rocks is paired with the INCORRECT protolith?
W) Slate – shale
X) Marble – calcite
Y) Quartzite – granite
Z) Schist – shale
ANSWER: Y) Quartzite – granite
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The 1961 Ferrari Spider costs about 18 million dollars. If Josh currently has 7.5 million dollars in savings and earns about 700,000 dollars a year, how many years will it take for Josh to have enough money to buy the Ferrari?
ANSWER: 15 YEARS
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the smallest three-digit factor of the number 1,000,001?
ANSWER: 101
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the maximum number of stereoisomers that can exist for a compound with 3 chiral centers?
ANSWER: 8
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The mineral Moissanite has the formula SiC. Which of the following best describes this mineral?
W) Ionic solid
X) Amorphous solid
Y) Network solid
Z) Crystalline solid
ANSWER: Y) Network solid
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at the University of Bonn have synthesized a silicon-containing compound that has an unusual structure. In the compound, each silicon atom is bonded to four ligands in a planar trapezoid arrangement. This structure is unusual because silicon along with four ligands generally forms molecules with what molecular geometry?
ANSWER: TETRAHEDRAL (also accept: TETRAHEDRON)
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The researchers at the University of Bonn are interested in studying compounds containing silicon because silicon is an essential component of electronics, especially semiconductors. By name or number, identify which of the following three elements form an n-type semiconductor when doped on silicon. 1) Phosphorus 2) Antimony 3) Gallium.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2 (also accept: PHOSPHORUS AND ANTIMONY)
13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is an elementary particle?
W) Pion
X) Kaon
Y) Meson
Z) Muon
ANSWER: Z) Muon
13. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about particle physics is incorrect?
W) Fermions have half-integral spin.
X) Interaction with Higgs field is responsible for mass of gauge bosons.
Y) Gluons mediate the strong force.
Z) Photons have a spin of 0.
ANSWER: Z) Photons have a spin of 0.
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Molecules of which of the following classes of macronutrients first enter the lymphatic system after their absorption by the intestinal epithelium?
W) Proteins
X) Carbohydrates
Y) Lipids
Z) Nucleic acids
ANSWER: Y) Lipids
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What term describes the collection of all microorganisms living on and within the human body?
ANSWER: MICROBIOME (do not accept: BIOME)
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Chandrasekhar limit is the upper mass limit for what type of stellar remnant?
ANSWER: WHITE DWARF (also accept: DEGENERATE DWARF) (do not accept: DWARF)
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four planets by INCREASING density 1) Venus 2) Mars 3) Jupiter 4) Saturn.
ANSWER: 4,3,2,1 (also accept: SATURN, JUPITER, MARS, VENUS)
16. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following shapes can not tessellate the plane?
W) Square
X) Parallelogram
Y) Regular octagon
Z) Regular hexagon
ANSWER: Y) Regular octagon
16. BONUS Math Short Answer A cyclic quadrilateral ABCD is inscribed in a circle with center O. If angle AOD measures 40 degrees and angle COD measures 50 degrees, what is the measure of angle D?
ANSWER: 135 DEGREES
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds is nonpolar?
W) Ammonia
X) Boron trifluoride
Y) Sulfur tetrafluoride
Z) Oxalic acid
ANSWER: X) Boron trifluoride
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, order the following three compounds by increasing boiling points 1) Hydrogen sulfide 2) Hydrogen chloride 3) Hydrogen bromide.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1 (HYDROGEN CHLORIDE, HYDROGEN BROMIDE, HYDROGEN SULFIDE)
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at Argonne National Lab are researching a new way to treat dengue virus, a prevalent mosquito-borne illness. To do this they are using what class of proteins, which are large y-shaped compounds used to combat infections
ANSWER: ANTIBODIES (also accept: IMMUNOGLOBULIN; AB; IG)
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at SLAC National Accelerator Lab have found an unexpected rearrangement of atoms in a substrate while trying to boost the efficiency of turning water into hydrogen fuel. This rearrangement occurred on what type of substance which accelerates the reaction rate by lowering the activation energy of a chemical reaction.
ANSWER: CATALYST
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A circuit contains a 3 ohm resistor in series with a 6 volt battery. In watts, how much power is dissipated by the resistor?
ANSWER: 12 WATTS
19. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about circuit components is INCORRECT?
W) An inverter can be used to convert DC to AC.
X) There are voltage drops across resistors.
Y) Diodes are made up of two p-n junctions.
Z) A rectifier converts AC to DC.
ANSWER: Y) Diodes are made up of two p-n junctions.

Y) 10,000 joules
Z) 15,000 joules
