Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists measure the activity of radioactive samples using what SI derived unit?
ANSWER: BECQUEREL (do not accept: CURIE)
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three diatomic compounds in order of increasing bond dissociation energy 1) NF 2) HF 3) HCl.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following ocean currents is a cold current that flows northward?
W) California Current
X) Canary Current
Y) Kuroshio Current
Z) Benguela Current
ANSWER: Z) Benguela Current
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is used as evidence for the fact that Milankovitch cycles are the primary cause of global glacial–interglacial climate cycles?
W) Mesopelagic sediment
X) The Keeling curve
Y) Tree rings
Z) Antarctic ice cores
ANSWER: Z) Antarctic ice cores
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If the natural log of x equals 2 and the natural log of y equals –3, what is the value of the natural log of the quantity x2/y?
ANSWER: 7
3. BONUS Math Short Answer The function f of x equals 3x3 – 2x2 – 9x + 6 has a single rational root. What is its value?
ANSWER: 2/3
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A bullet is shot directly horizontally at 200 meters per second from a height of 20 meters. To the nearest second, how much time does it take for the bullet to reach the ground?
ANSWER: 2
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding the double-slit experiment 1) The double-slit experiment demonstrates the wave nature of light 2) The double-slit experiment relies on the refraction of light 3) The double-slit experiment relies on the diffraction of light.
ANSWER: 1
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at UC Berkeley’s Innovative Genomics Institute have been using CRISPR to develop therapies for sickle cell disease by altering the expression of what protein?
ANSWER: HEMOGLOBIN
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers in the Militzer Group have been mapping phase diagrams for alkaline earth metal oxides at extremely high pressures using molecular simulation. What equation allows them to estimate enthalpies of fusion by calculating the slope of a phase boundary on a phase diagram?
ANSWER: CLAUSIUS–CLAPEYRON EQUATION
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organs found in the ear contain fluids and transmits vibrations into electrical signals?
W) Incus
X) Ossicles
Y) Cochlea
Z) Pinna
ANSWER: Y) Cochlea
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following proteins is anchored to microtubules to create flagellum movement?
W) Myosin
X) Dynein
Y) Collagen
Z) Troponin
ANSWER: X) Dynein
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer After tossing 9 fair coins with heads on one side and tails on the other, what is the probability that one observes at least 5 heads face up?
ANSWER: 1/2 (also accept: 0.5; 50%)
7. BONUS Math Short Answer If y varies directly with the square of x, and y = 13/8 when x = 1/4, then what is the sum of the squares of all possible POSITIVE values of x when y = 104?
ANSWER: 4
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A satellite follows a circular orbit with radius 10,000 kilometers around a planet with mass 5 times 10 to the power of 26 kilograms. How much work in joules is done by the planet on the satellite over a year?
ANSWER: ZERO
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer Jordan attaches a 5-kilogram mass to the end of a massless 2-meter rope and swings it around in a horizontal plane such that it undergoes uniform circular motion. If Jordan is exerting 40 newtons of force on the rope, at what velocity, in meters per second, must the mass be traveling?
ANSWER: 4
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three polysaccharides that are present in fungi but not in plants 1) Chitin 2) Pectin 3) Lignin.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following enzymes is responsible for the elongation of DNA in polymerase chain reactions?
W) Taq polymerase
X) DNA polymerase II
Y) RNA polymerase III
Z) DNA ligase
ANSWER: W) Taq polymerase
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a stratovolcano?
W) Mauna Loa
X) Mount Saint Helens
Y) Mount Rainier
Z) Krakatoa
ANSWER: W) Mauna Loa
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Categorize each of the following rocks as igneous, sedimentary, or metamorphic, respectively 1) Slate 2) Andesite 3) Phyllite.
ANSWER: 1 – METAMORPHIC, 2 – IGNEOUS, 3 – METAMORPHIC
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the formal charge of the central carbon atom in acetone?
ANSWER: ZERO
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To one significant figure and in scientific notation, what is the hydrogen ion concentration in moles per liter of a 2-molar solution of formic acid with a Ka of 8 × 10-4?
ANSWER: 4 × 10-2
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Robert Dudley, a UC Berkeley researcher, has investigated the effect and prevalence of ethanol consumption among certain spider monkey populations in Africa. What type of psychological drug is ethanol?
W) Accelerant
X) Depressant
Y) Inhalant
Z) Hallucinogen
ANSWER: X) Depressant
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists in the Long Group at UC Berkeley have been synthesizing and characterizing rare-earth bimetallic hydride complexes to probe their exotic oxidation states. Identify all of the following three types of bonding that hydride ligands can partake in 1) Sigma 2) Pi 3) Delta.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Speciation occurs when groups of organisms stop interbreeding. Which of the following is a post-zygotic factor that can contribute to speciation?
W) Hybrid sterility
X) Temporal isolation
Y) Behavioral isolation
Z) Habitat isolation
ANSWER: W) Hybrid sterility
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer In forward genetic screens, what type of test is often done to determine whether two mutations are on the same gene?
ANSWER: COMPLEMENTATION TEST
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following elements is most abundant in the Earth’s crust?
W) Carbon
X) Oxygen
Y) Silicon
Z) Iron
ANSWER: X) Oxygen
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which of the following directions on the Hertzsprung–Russell diagram would a blue giant travel as it cools into a red giant?
W) Up
X) Down
Y) Left
Z) Right
ANSWER: Z) Right
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the point on a phase diagram where the solid, liquid, and gas forms of a substance can exist at the same temperature and pressure?
ANSWER: TRIPLE POINT
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three materials that are denser than solid aluminum at 25 degrees Celsius 1) Liquid mercury 2) Liquid water 3) Solid lithium.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the sum from n = 1 to 5 of n cubed?
ANSWER: 225
16. BONUS Math Short Answer Identify all of the following three functions that are even with respect to the line x = 3 1) Cosine of the quantity x – 3 2) Cosine of the quantity x + 3 3) Absolute value of the quantity x + 3.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at the Lawrence Livermore National Lab’s National Ignition Facility often explore conditions of high pressures and temperatures similar to those found in stars and nuclear weapons. Substances with pressures and temperatures beyond their critical points are said to be in what state of matter?
ANSWER: SUPERCRITICAL FLUID
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at the Joint Bioenergy Institute have looked into techniques to enrich plants with essential minerals. Identify all of the following three minerals that are considered essential 1) Calcium 2) Titanium 3) Sodium.
ANSWER: 1
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What quantity is defined as the derivative of angular momentum with respect to time?
ANSWER: TORQUE
18. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice The speed of sound of a material is most closely related to which of the following quantities?
W) Shear modulus
X) Bulk modulus
Y) Young’s modulus
Z) Relative permittivity
ANSWER: X) Bulk modulus
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What molecule is found in bacterial cell walls and is the target of gram staining?
ANSWER: PEPTIDOGLYCAN
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for organisms that feed on dead organic matter via extracellular digestion?
ANSWER: SAPROTROPHS
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the three following compounds in order of increasing covalent character 1) Lithium fluoride 2) Water 3) Hexane.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
20. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Heavy elements such as lead exhibit the inert-pair effect, in which the formation of lead(II) ions is favored over lead(IV) ions. Which of the following properties of heavy elements is most directly related to this phenomenon?
W) Lower electron affinities
X) Larger atom sizes
Y) Lower ionization energies
Z) Poor shielding from d-electrons
ANSWER: Z) Poor shielding from d-electrons
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Given that g is 10 meters per second squared, and that the density of water is 1 gram per cubic centimeter, what is the pressure, in pascals, exerted on a block that is submerged 2.5 meters under water?
ANSWER: 25,000
21. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a unit of energy?
W) Kilowatt hour
X) Newton meter
Y) Ampere volt second
Z) Kilogram meter squared per second
ANSWER: Z) Kilogram meter squared per second
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best defines the difference between meteoroids and meteorites?
W) Meteoroids are smaller than meteorites
X) Meteoroids are meteorites that enter Earth’s atmosphere
Y) Meteorites are meteoroids that collide with the Earth’s surface
Z) Meteoroids are roughly spherical while meteorites are irregularly shaped
ANSWER: Y) Meteorites are meteoroids that collide with the Earth’s surface
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the chemical discontinuity between Earth’s outer core and mantle?
ANSWER: GUTENBERG DISCONTINUITY
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Simplify the following expression: the quantity 3 – i times the quantity 2 + i.
ANSWER: 7 + i (do not accept: i + 7)
23. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following functions f of x is NOT integrable from 0 to 1?
W) Tangent of x
X) Arctangent of x
Y) 1 over x2
Z) Natural log of x
ANSWER: Y) 1 over x2
