Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the most common scientific term for a change in velocity over time?
ANSWER: ACCELERATION
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the mass, in grams, of a 1 kilogram object on a planet with a gravitational force of 3 meters per second squared?
ANSWER: 1,000
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What molecular substance is primarily responsible for moderating wide changes in temperatures on Earth?
ANSWER: WATER
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the process by which water vapor changes directly into rain:
W) sublimation
X) evaporation
Y) transpiration
Z) condensation
ANSWER: Z) condensation
3. BONUS Math Short Answer If 12 gallons of gasoline costs $36.00, what would 1 quart cost, in cents?
ANSWER: 75 CENTS
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a coniferous tree:
W) poplar
X) oak
Y) elm
Z) fir
ANSWER: Z) fir
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following pieces of laboratory glassware would be used to MOST accurately measure 1000 milliliters of water:
W) 1000 milliliter volumetric flask
X) 1000 milliliter beaker
Y) 1000 milliliter Erlenmeyer flask
Z) two, 500 milliliter Erlenmeyer flasks
ANSWER: W) 1000 milliliter volumetric flask
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Found in humans and quite prominent in dogs, what is the name of the conical-shaped tooth used in tearing muscle and other foods and assisting in cutting?
ANSWER: CANINE
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer How many canine teeth do humans typically have in early adulthood?
ANSWER: 4
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following atomic properties most directly determines that elements in the same group of the Periodic Table have similar chemical properties:
W) number of protons
X) number of valence electrons
Y) charge of protons
Z) stability of neutrons
ANSWER: X) number of valence electrons
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the atomic mass rounded to the nearest whole number and the net charge of an ion with 7 protons, 6 neutrons, and 8 electrons?
ANSWER: ATOMIC MASS = 13; NET CHARGE = –1
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is believed to be the process that causes Earth's tectonic plates to move:
W) convection currents in the crust
X) convection currents in the mantle
Y) gravitational influence of the Sun and Moon directly on continental plates
Z) volcanic activity along subduction zones
ANSWER: X) convection currents in the mantle
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which of the following locations would you typically expect the average salinity of surface sea water to be the LOWEST:
W) in the Dead Sea
X) in the waters off Antarctica
Y) in the Chesapeake Bay
Z) at the mouth of the Mississippi River
ANSWER: Z) at the mouth of the Mississippi River
8. BONUS Math Short Answer If on average 6 people drink two-thirds of a gallon of lemonade, how many people will drink 4 gallons?
ANSWER: 36
9. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What molecular substance is typically considered the most critical for life to exist on another planet?
ANSWER: WATER
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name all of the following 4 organisms that are typically filter feeders in an aquatic environment: barnacles; corals; frogs; kelp
ANSWER: BARNACLES; CORALS
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer If the 4 main classes of biological molecules are protein, lipid, carbohydrate, and nucleic acid, which would butter be classified as?
ANSWER: LIPID
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Cellulose is a polymer of what 6-carbon sugar?
ANSWER: GLUCOSE
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Order the following 3 liquids from the one with the LOWEST viscosity to the one with the HIGHEST viscosity at room temperature: pure olive oil; water; pure honey
ANSWER: WATER; PURE OLIVE OIL; PURE HONEY
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Name all of the following 4 choices that are compounds: water; antimony; carbon dioxide; mercury
ANSWER: WATER AND CARBON DIOXIDE (also accept: H2O AND CO2)
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Rocks that are produced when magma cools and solidifies deep below the Earth's crust are called:
W) extrusive igneous
X) intrusive igneous
Y) extrusive metamorphic
Z) intrusive sedimentary
ANSWER: X) intrusive igneous
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Of the following 5 choices, name 2 that are igneous rocks: rock salt; granite; basalt; sandstone; gypsum
ANSWER: GRANITE AND BASALT
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Evaluate the following expression: –6 × 3 + –16 ÷ 4
ANSWER: –22
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Convert the following value into standard scientific notation: 0.00634 × 108
ANSWER: 6.34 × 105
14. BONUS Energy Short Answer Multiply the following 2 values, giving your answer in the correct number of significant figures: 10.1 × 1.201
ANSWER: 12.1
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Geotropism is a response of plants to what external force?
ANSWER: GRAVITY
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Flowering plants are grouped into what 2 classes named after their number of seed leaves?
ANSWER: MONOCOTS AND DICOTS (also accept: MONOCOTYLEDONS AND DICOTYLEDONS)
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the scientific term for a drink that settles and needs to be shaken to redistribute the particles throughout the drink:
W) homogenous
X) solvent
Y) suspension
Z) colloid
ANSWER: Y) suspension
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the ideal mechanical advantage of a single fixed pulley?
ANSWER: 1
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The lithosphere is made up of the uppermost mantle and what other layer?
ANSWER: CRUST (also accept: CONTINENTAL AND/OR OCEANIC CRUST)
17. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Volcanoes and earthquakes occur primarily in which of the following locations:
W) along plate boundaries
X) along continental shelves
Y) at the North and South Poles
Z) around the Hawaiian Islands
ANSWER: W) along plate boundaries
18. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the sum of all odd one-digit prime positive numbers?
ANSWER: 15
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following are sometimes called "big dirty snowballs" because of their composition:
W) comets
X) asteroids
Y) meteorites
Z) dwarf planets
ANSWER: W) comets
19. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true about comets:
W) to Earth-bound observers, comets glow because they are nearing Earth’s atmosphere
X) they typically have elliptical orbits
Y) the Deep Impact probe recently struck a comet
Z) the solid part of a comet is called the nucleus
ANSWER: W) to Earth-bound observers, comets glow because they are nearing Earth’s atmosphere
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the location of the biceps humoris muscle:
W) chest
X) leg
Y) abdomen
Z) arm
ANSWER: Z) arm
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer What general type of human blood vessel returns blood to the heart?
ANSWER: VEIN
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many protons does the heaviest isotope of hydrogen have?
ANSWER: 1
21. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Distillation depends mostly upon what physical property?
ANSWER: BOILING POINT (also accept: CONDENSATION POINT)
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Chalk is a type of:
W) limestone
X) coal
Y) rock salt
Z) basalt
ANSWER: W) limestone
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer On which of the terrestrial planets does the Sun rise in the west?
ANSWER: VENUS
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many U.S. quarters equal 750 U.S. nickels?
ANSWER: 150
23. BONUS Math Short Answer Name all of the following 5 numbers that are composite numbers: 3, 11, 33, 53, 63
ANSWER: 33; 63
24. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Convert 100 milliliters into cubic centimeters:
ANSWER: 100
24. BONUS Energy Short Answer At standard temperature and pressure, how many grams, rounded to the nearest whole number, does the contents in a 12 ounce or 355 milliliter water bottle weigh?
ANSWER: 355
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the most common name for the polymers of DNA and associated proteins that are found in the eukaryotic nucleus?
ANSWER: CHROMOSOME
25. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following characteristics is MOST typical of bacteria:
W) numerous mitochondria
X) no nucleus
Y) numerous cilia
Z) reproduction by budding
ANSWER: X) no nucleus
