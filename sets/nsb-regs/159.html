1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An arch supported only by its own strength will be strongest if the shape of the arch follows what mathematical curve?
ANSWER: CATENARY (also accept: INVERTED CATENARY; HYPERBOLIC COSINE) (do not accept: PARABOLA)
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer A theoretical circuit has an infinite number of resistors placed in parallel. The first resistor has a resistance of one ohm, and each following resistor has twice the resistance of the previous one. In ohms, what is the total resistance across the circuit?
ANSWER: 1/2 (also accept: 0.5)
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In brown fat, thermogenin uncouples the mitochondrial proton gradient from the action of what enzyme?
ANSWER: ATP SYNTHASE
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer T cells undergo a process of central tolerance in order to prevent autoimmunity. In what organ does this process occur?
ANSWER: THYMUS
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs would have the most exothermic reaction?
W) Rubidium and chlorine
X) Potassium and chlorine
Y) Potassium and bromine
Z) Rubidium and iodine
ANSWER: W) Rubidium and chlorine
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What color can hexa-aqua-iron (III) appear to be in aqueous solution?
W) Pink
X) Green
Y) Yellow
Z) Blue
ANSWER: Y) Yellow
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice When Karl Jansky noticed a source of radio interference appearing from all directions in the sky, he measured the daily peak strength of the interference. Which of the following best describes how this peak changed each day?
W) It was one hour earlier each day
X) It was 4 minutes earlier each day
Y) It was 4 seconds earlier each day
Z) It was at the same time each day
ANSWER: X) It was 4 minutes earlier each day
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of fusion that are sustainable in a brown dwarf with 75 times the mass of Jupiter 1) Protium 2) Deuterium 3) Lithium.
ANSWER: 2, 3
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Scientists at SLAC National Accelerator Lab are shooting femtosecond electron pulses at samples and measuring their diffraction signals with time resolution. Which of the following phenomena is best probed by this type of experiment?
W) Protein crystal structures
X) Nuclear spin environments in molecules
Y) Chemical kinetics
Z) Extent of charge-parity asymmetry
ANSWER: Y) Chemical kinetics
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists supported by the Department of Energy are studying the human pathogen Pseudomonas aeruginosa. Identify all of the following three statements that are true of this bacterium 1) It is Gram-negative 2) It is rod-shaped 3) It typically infects the immunocompromised.
ANSWER: ALL
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the absolute value of the product of the two complex numbers 1 + 3i and 2 – i?
ANSWER: 5√2
6. BONUS Math Short Answer What are the coordinates of the image of the point (–7, 6) when it is rotated about the point (1, –4) by 90 degrees clockwise?
ANSWER: (11, 4) (also accept: x = 11 AND y = 4)
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A charge of 2 coulombs is placed at the origin, and a second charge is placed on the x-axis at x = –3. In coulombs, for what value of the second charge is there to be no net force on a test charge placed on the x-axis at x = 1?
ANSWER: –32
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three quantities that, according to the Einstein field equations, affect the curvature of spacetime in a given place and time 1) Energy density 2) Momentum density 3) Mass density.
ANSWER: ALL
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer During mitosis, spindle fibers attach to what protein structures located on the centromeres of chromosomes?
ANSWER: KINETOCHORES
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about vertebrates is false?
W) All reptiles are tetrapods
X) All gnathostomes are vertebrates
Y) All tetrapods are amniotes
Z) All vertebrates are chordates
ANSWER: Y) All tetrapods are amniotes
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer When oxygen is photolyzed, it breaks into two oxygen atoms with unpaired electrons. What is the term for this type of species?
ANSWER: FREE RADICAL (also accept: RADICAL)
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the IUPAC name of the major organic product of the reaction of propene with hydrogen gas in the presence of Raney nickel?
ANSWER: PROPANE
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Marine sediment in the North Atlantic is primarily calcareous ooze, while marine sediment in the North Pacific is primarily pelagic clay. Which of the following best explains why?
W) The North Pacific has a higher pH than the North Atlantic
X) The North Pacific is deeper than the North Atlantic
Y) The North Pacific has a lower pH than the North Atlantic
Z) The North Pacific has more dissolved oxygen than the North Atlantic
ANSWER: Y) The North Pacific has a lower pH than the North Atlantic
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements about the fossil record that are true 1) The earliest evidence of multi-cellular life is in the Paleozoic 2) Shelly fossils first appeared in the Mesozoic 3) Most trilobites went extinct near the end of the Cambrian.
ANSWER: 3
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab are studying the protein that gives coronaviruses the shape they were named for and are their interface with host cells. What is the name for this protein?
ANSWER: SPIKE PROTEIN (also accept: CORONAVIRUS SPIKE PROTEIN; S PROTEIN)
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Lab are studying the structure of antibodies. Identify all of the following three antibodies that are typically monomeric 1) IgG 2) IgM 3) IgE.
ANSWER: 1, 3
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is f'(2) for the function f(x) = x3 ex?
ANSWER: 20 e2
12. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the cross product of vectors i + 3j – 2k and i + 2j?
ANSWER: 4i – 2j – k
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The de Broglie wavelength of an object is equal to Planck's constant divided by what property of the object?
ANSWER: MOMENTUM (also accept: RELATIVISTIC MOMENTUM; LINEAR MOMENTUM)
13. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A positively-charged particle is moving in the vicinity of a long current-carrying wire. For which of the following directions of the initial velocity will the particle be repelled from the wire?
W) Directly towards the wire
X) Directly away from the wire
Y) In the same direction as the current
Z) Opposite the direction of the current
ANSWER: Z) Opposite the direction of the current
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What pigmented compound is the result of hemoglobin degradation and is often found to be elevated in individuals with hepatitis?
ANSWER: BILIRUBIN (also accept: INDIRECT BILIRUBIN; UNCONJUGATED BILIRUBIN)

W) Strong cation exchange chromatography
X) Anion exchange chromatography
Y) Reverse phase chromatography

ANSWER: Z) Gel filtration chromatography

15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Consider the compound cis-1-chloro-3-tert-butylcyclohexane. Identify all of the following three statements that are TRUE of the most stable chair conformation 1) Chlorine is axial 2) Tert-butyl is equatorial 3) All carbon-carbon bonds sit in a single plane.

17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying antibiotic resistance in the bacterial genus Mycobacterium. One strain of Mycobacterium causes a disease in humans in which it replicates within nerve cells or macrophages, eventually causing the loss of the extremities in which it replicates. What is the name of this disease?
ANSWER: LEPROSY (also accept: HANSEN’S DISEASE)
