1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What phase of mitosis is characterized by the separation of sister chromatids to opposite sides of the cell?

X) Metaphase
Y) Anaphase
Z) Telophase

X) The spacecraft is accelerating relative to the apple
Y) The apple is moving at a speed more than the magnitude of v relative to the observer
Z) The apple is moving at a speed less than the magnitude of v relative to the observer
