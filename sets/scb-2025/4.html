1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The PQ interval on an ECG is caused by a delay between the depolarization of the atria, and that of the ventricles. What structure within the intraventricular septum is responsible for this delay of about 0.1 seconds in humans?
ANSWER: AV node (also accept: atrioventricular node)
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Glomerular filtration rate is the rate at which plasma is filtered into the Bowman’s capsule of nephrons. Which of the following three conditions will result in an increase in GFR? 1) Vasoconstriction of afferent arteriole 2) Contraction of mesangial cells 3) Vasoconstriction of efferent arteriole
ANSWER: 3
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds acts as the reducing agent in the reaction between a strip of zinc metal and hydrochloric acid?
W) Zinc
X) Hydrochloric acid
Y) Water
Z) There is no reaction
ANSWER: W) Zinc
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In the typical titration of a weak acid, such as 0.1 molar acetic acid, with a strong base, such as 0.1 molar sodium hydroxide, which of the following is the best description of the characteristic titration curve?
W) At the equivalence point, the pH is primarily controlled by the acetate ion
X) At the equivalence point, the pH drops dramatically
Y) The second equivalence point is primarily controlled by the hydroxide ion
Z) The pH at the equivalence point is almost always 7
ANSWER: W) At the equivalence point, the pH is primarily controlled by the acetate ion

X) 10

ANSWER: 2√2 v

ANSWER: meroblastic (also accept: meroblastic cleavage)
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Chef Grace is preparing an octopus soup for the competitors at SCB. ANSWER: Terminal moraine (do not accept: End moraine or recessional moraine)
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In sedimentary rocks, finer grain sizes generally correspond to a reduction in what property, which refers to the ability of a rock body or group of sediment to allow water to flow through or within it?
ANSWER: Permeability

10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Gauss’s law of magnetism prohibits the existence of what type of object which has a net magnetic flux?
ANSWER: Magnetic Monopole
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer A circular wire of radius 1 is placed in a magnetic field of 2 milliteslas. Over a period of 5 seconds, the magnetic field uniformly decreases to 0. If the wire has a resistance of 5 ohms, what is the current in the wire in terms of pi during this period?
ANSWER: 8π × 10-5 Amps
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Arjun notices a similarity between the lateral lines of fish and the semicircular canals of humans. What gelatinous structures, in which the stereocilia of hair cells are embedded, are present in both of these examples?
ANSWER: cupula (also accept: cupulae)
11. ANSWER: 0.32

Y) Orbital radius decreases, orbital speed decreases

14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The Lucas numbers are very similar to the fibonacci numbers, where each term is the sum of the last two. However the first two terms of the Lucas numbers are 2 and 1 in that order. What constant does the ratio of two consecutive terms approach as the sequence continues to infinity?
ANSWER: The Golden Ratio (also accept: (1+√5)/2)

18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Pluto’s Sputnik Planitia has relatively few craters compared to its surroundings. Which of the following is the primary reason for this phenomenon?

20. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In classical mechanics, two charged particles orbiting each other will have their orbits slowly decay. Which of the following best explains why this occurs?
