1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Mr. Guisti, against all odds, is playing science bowl! When Yajur reads a physics question, Mr. Guisti feels a fight-or-flight response. Which of the following hormones has the greatest rate of increase in its blood concentration during a fight-or-flight response?
W) Epinephrine
X) Adrenocorticotropic hormone
Y) Cortisol
Z) Aldosterone
ANSWER: W) Epinephrine
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer For what value of R are the numerical values of the volume and surface area of a sphere of radius R the same?
ANSWER: 3
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The ability of a superconductor to completely expel magnetic fields is known as what effect?
ANSWER: Meissner effect
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following solutions will work as the best buffer?

Z) 0.2 molar sodium acetate and 0.2 molar hydrochloric acid
ANSWER: Y) 0.2 molar sodium acetate and 0.1 molar hydrochloric acid

W) Autopolyploidy, 28
X) Autopolyploidy, 14
Y) Allopolyploidy, 28
Z) Allopolyploidy, 14
ANSWER: W) Autopolyploidy, 28
