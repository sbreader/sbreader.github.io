Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which Swedish physician and botanist sought to classify life's diversity and developed the binomial system?
ANSWER: CAROLUS LINNAEUS
1. BONUS Energy Short Answer Which Greek philosopher viewed species as fixed and arranged on a ladder of increasing complexity, which he called the scala naturae?
ANSWER: ARISTOTLE
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which Scottish geologist proposed that Earth's geologic features could be explained by gradual mechanisms still operating, and developed the theory of gradualism?
ANSWER: JAMES HUTTON
2. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the study of fossils called?
ANSWER: PALEONTOLOGY
3. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which French scientist developed paleontology?
ANSWER: GEORGES CUVIER
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer To explain his observations, Curvier advocated which principle, which states that events in the past occurred suddenly and were caused by mechanisms different from those operating in the present?
ANSWER: CATASTROPHISM
4. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which principle states that mechanisms of change are constant over time?
ANSWER: UNIFORMITARIANISM
4. BONUS Energy Short Answer Which leading geologist of Darwin's time incorporated Hutton's thinking into his principle of uniformatarianism?
ANSWER: CHARLES LYELL
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Who was the first to recognize that evolutionary change explains patterns in fossils and the match of organisms to their environments, and proposed the principles of use and disuse, and inheritance of acquired characteristics?
ANSWER: JEAN-BAPTISTE LAMARCK
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the change in the frequency of an allele in a population due to random sampling?
ANSWER: GENETIC DRIFT
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When a few individuals become isolated from a larger population, this smaller group may establish a new population whose gene pool differs from the source population. What is this effect called?
ANSWER: FOUNDER EFFECT
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer A sudden change in the environment, such as a fire or flood, may drastically reduce the size of a population. What is this effect called?
ANSWER: BOTTLENECK EFFECT
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the transfer of alleles into or out of a population due to the movement of fertile individuals or their gametes?
ANSWER: GENE FLOW
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of speciation occurs when gene flow is interrupted when a population is divided into geographically isolated subpopulations?
ANSWER: ALLOPATRIC SPECIATION
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which type of speciation occurs in populations that live in the same geographic area?
ANSWER: SYMPATRIC SPECIATION
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the two types of polyploidy.
ANSWER: AUTOPOLYPLOIDY AND ALLOPOLYPLOIDY
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which type of polyploidy occurs when an individual has more than two chromosome sets that are all derived from a single species?
ANSWER: AUTOPOLYPLOIDY
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of polyploidy can occur when two different species interbreed and produce hybrid offspring?
ANSWER: ALLOPOLYPLOIDY
10. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which type of microscopy technique fires an electron and exciting gold electrons on the surface, revealing the image of a specimen's topography?
ANSWER: SCANNING ELECTRON MICROSCOPY
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of microscopy examines the internal ultrastructure of cells by aiming an electron beam through a very thin section of a specimen and producing an electron density plot?
ANSWER: TRANSMISSION ELECTRON MICROSCOPY
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In a transmission electron microscope, what are used to bend the paths of the electrons?
ANSWER: ELECTROMAGNETS
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer The study of cell structure is known as what?
ANSWER: CYTOLOGY
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The study of molecular and chemical processes of cells is known as what?
ANSWER: BIOCHEMISTRY
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of microscopy uses optical modifications to exaggerate differences in density to produce a 3-D image?
ANSWER: DIFFERENTIAL-INTERFERENCE CONTRAST (NOMARSKI)
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which type of microscopy is a fluorescent "optical sectioning" technique that uses a pinhole aperture to eliminate out-of-focus light from a thick specimen?
ANSWER: CONFOCAL
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a part of the endomembrane system?
W) Vacuole
X) Lysosome
Y) Mitochondrion
Z) Golgi apparatus
ANSWER: Y) Mitochondrion
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a function of the endomembrane system?
W) Metabolism and movement of lipids
X) Detoxification of poisons
Y) Synthesis of proteins
Z) Transport of nucleic acids
ANSWER: Z) Transport of nucleic acids
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a type of plastid?
W) Chloroplast
X) Amyloplast
Y) Chromoplast
Z) Xyloplast
ANSWER: Z) Xyloplast
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which enzyme is responsible for the replacement of the primer after synthesis of DNA?
ANSWER: DNA POLYMERASE I
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Give the full name for each of the following sites on a ribosome: E site, P site, A site.
ANSWER: EXIT SITE, PEPTIDYL-TRNA SITE, AMINOACYL-TRNA SITE
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which type of point mutation occurs when one amino acid is changed to another?
ANSWER: MISSENSE
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer A shift in the reading frame causes which type of point mutation?
ANSWER: FRAMESHIFT
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which type of point mutation occurs when translation is terminated prematurely?
ANSWER: NONSENSE
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Who was the first to suggest that genes dictate phenotype through enzymes?
ANSWER: ARCHIBALD GARROD
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which two scientists speculated that in Drosophila, each of the various mutations affecting eye color are due to an absence of an enzyme that plays a role in the synthesis pathway of eye pigments?
ANSWER: GEORGE BEADLE AND BORIS EPHRUSSI
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which two scientists won the Nobel Prize in 1958 for their discovery of the relationship between genes and enzymes?
ANSWER: GEORGE BEADLE AND EDWARD TATUM
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Who deciphered the first codon?
ANSWER: MARSHALL NIRENBERG
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of growth medium contains only agar, inorganic salts, glucose, and biotin?
ANSWER: MINIMAL MEDIUM
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which type of growth medium is a minimal medium supplemented with all 20 amino acids and a few other nutrients?
ANSWER: COMPLETE GROWTH MEDIUM
20. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Neurospora crassa is a...
W) Bacterium
X) Amoeba
Y) Bread mold
Z) Archaean
ANSWER: Y) Bread mold
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The poly-A tail is found on which end of RNA?
ANSWER: 3' END
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer Arrange the following steps in order: RNA splicing, addition of 5' and 3' caps, transcription, translation.
ANSWER: TRANSCRIPTION, ADDITION OF 5' AND 3' CAPS, RNA SPLICING, TRANSLATION
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which type of RNA molecules also function as enzymes?
ANSWER: RIBOZYMES
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which type of RNA regulates the expression of mRNA and plays a role in RNA interference?
ANSWER: MICRO-RNA
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the crossing over of exons between alleles or occasional mixing and matching of these exons between non-allelic genes?
ANSWER: EXON SHUFFLING
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which aquatic biome is a vast realm of open blue water, constantly mixed by wind-driven oceanic currents?
ANSWER: OCEANIC PELAGIC ZONE
24. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which aquatic biome is periodically submerged and exposed by the tides?
ANSWER: INTERTIDAL ZONE
25. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which aquatic biome is formed largely from the calcium carbonate skeletons of corals?
ANSWER: CORAL REEF
25. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a type of wetland?
W) Riverine wetland
X) Fringe wetland
Y) Pelagic wetland
Z) Basin wetland
ANSWER: Y) Pelagic wetland
