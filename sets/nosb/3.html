Packet 3 (2018 Regional Round 3) of National Ocean Sciences Bowl:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following instruments has been used by the U.S. Government for over 150 years to show sea level rise?
W) ADCPs
X) Tide Gauges
Y) Refractometer
Z) LIDAR
ANSWER: X) Tide Gauges
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the feet, or lateral appendages, of a polychaete?
ANSWER: Parapodia
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms are NOT in the phylum Cnidaria?
W) Corals
X) Jellyfish
Y) Hydroids
Z) Sponges
ANSWER: Z) Sponges
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a standing wave in a lake, bay, or estuary?
ANSWER: Seiche
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which ocean has the highest average salinity?
W) Atlantic
X) Pacific
Y) Indian
Z) Southern
ANSWER: W) Atlantic
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is another name for the intertidal zone?
ANSWER: Littoral zone (also accept: Foreshore; Seashore)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Northwest Passage is a sea route between which two regions?
W) Northern Bering Sea and Gulf of Alaska
X) Canadian Maritime Provinces and the U.S.
Y) Arctic Ocean and Baltic Sea
Z) North Atlantic and North Pacific
ANSWER: Z) North Atlantic and North Pacific
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Waves that hit the shore at an angle other than 90 degrees drive what type of current?
ANSWER: Longshore current
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What color light penetrates the deepest in clear ocean water?
W) Blue
X) Red
Y) Green
Z) Violet
ANSWER: W) Blue
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the primary, flammable component of gas hydrates in the ocean?
ANSWER: Methane (CH4)
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Manganese nodules are an example of which type of sediment?
W) Lithogenic
X) Biogenic
Y) Cosmogenic
Z) Authigenic
ANSWER: Z) Authigenic
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms do NOT have calcareous structures?
W) Foraminifera
X) Coccolithophores
Y) Radiolarians
Z) Corals
ANSWER: Y) Radiolarians
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the names of the two land masses that resulted from the Triassic breakup of Pangaea?
ANSWER: Laurasia AND Gondwanaland (also accept: Gondwana)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Coriolis acceleration is directed at what angle to the motion of a moving object in the northern hemisphere?
W) 90 degrees to the left
X) 90 degrees to the right
Y) 45 degrees to the left
Z) 45 degrees to the right
ANSWER: X) 90 degrees to the right
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What three variables determine the speed of sound in water?
ANSWER: Pressure; temperature; salinity (also accept: depth)
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice According to the Law of Constant Proportions, which of the following ratios remains constant?
W) The ratio between dissolved nitrogen gas and argon gas
X) The ratios among the major ions in the open ocean
Y) The ratio between chlorophyll and productivity in the water column
Z) The ratio between biocarbonate and carbonate
ANSWER: X) The ratios among the major ions in the open ocean
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which multicellular animal phylum has the oldest fossils?
ANSWER: Porifera (also accept: sponges)
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following elements is NOT a major component of manganese nodules?
W) Copper
X) Nickel
Y) Cobalt
Z) Argon
ANSWER: Z) Argon
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The density of a water parcel at a given pressure can change due to what two variables?
ANSWER: Temperature and salinity
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following animals has placoid scales?
W) Beaked sea snake
X) Tarpon
Y) Mako shark
Z) Saltwater crocodile
ANSWER: Y) Mako shark
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following fishing gear captures the LEAST amount of bycatch?
W) Gillnet
X) Otter Trawl
Y) Longline
Z) Harpoon
ANSWER: Z) Harpoon
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What term describes how long, on average, a given compound remains in seawater?
ANSWER: Residence time
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name of the submarine trench lying immediately offshore of the west coast of Central America?
W) Middle America Trench
X) Marianas Trench
Y) Atacama Trench
Z) Juan de Fuca Trench
ANSWER: W) Middle America Trench
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Conversion of nitrogen gas to ammonia by cyanobacteria is an example of which biogeochemical process?
W) Nitrification
X) Denitrification
Y) Nitrogen fixation
Z) Anammox
ANSWER: Y) Nitrogen fixation
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the marine algae that is expelled from corals during bleaching?
ANSWER: Zooxanthellae (also accept: dinoflagellate; symbiodinium)
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the layer of the ocean where density rapidly increases with depth?
ANSWER: Pycnocline
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which process do scientists use to age seals?
W) Counting rings on otoliths
X) Biopsying body tissue
Y) Counting growth rings in teeth
Z) Measuring hormonal concentrations
ANSWER: Y) Counting growth rings in teeth
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The term “white smokers” refers to which of the following?
W) Ice-capped volcanoes
X) Hydrothermal vent chimneys made of iron sulfides
Y) River plumes carrying light-colored sediments
Z) Hydrothermal vent chimneys made of barium, calcium, and silicon
ANSWER: Z) Hydrothermal vent chimneys made of barium, calcium, and silicon
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer Some organisms, such as certain species of copepods, swim to the sea surface at night and to depths during the day. What is the term for this process?
ANSWER: Diel vertical migration (also accept: Diurnal vertical migration)
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following causes surface currents to shift at 45 degrees relative to the wind direction?
W) Geostrophic balance
X) The Coriolis effect
Y) Gravity
Z) The pressure gradient
ANSWER: X) The Coriolis effect
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In general, during which season of the year are beaches the narrowest?
ANSWER: Winter
