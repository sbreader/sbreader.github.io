1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the kinetic energy of a 5 gram ball traveling 10 m/s?
W) 0.25 Joules
X) 2.5 Joules
Y) 25 Joules
Z) 250 Joules
ANSWER: W) 0.25 Joules
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer As a ball is falling down from the top of a tower, its gravitational potential energy is transferred into what other type of energy, besides kinetic energy?
ANSWER: THERMAL ENERGY (do not accept: HEAT)
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the integral of cos(x) dx from 1/4 π to 0?
ANSWER: -√2/2 (also accept: -SQUARE ROOT OF 2 OVER 2; -0.707)
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Categorize the following molecules according to the arrangement of electron pairs geometry: carbon dioxide, dihydrogen monoxide, and methane.
ANSWER: LINEAR; TETRAHEDRAL; TETRAHEDRAL
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Astronomers were very ecstatic when LIGO announced its discovery of these types of waves on February 11, 2016. What are these special waves called?
ANSWER: GRAVITATIONAL WAVES
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Gravitational waves form when two massive objects orbit each other and distort the fabric of space-time in such a way that waves form. (1) Name an example of objects that are massive enough to cause gravitational waves to form (2) who first predicted the existence of these waves?
ANSWER: NEUTRON STARS OR BLACK HOLES; EINSTEIN (also accept: PULSARS, EINSTEIN; BLACK HOLES, EINSTEIN; NEUTRON STARS, EINSTEIN)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Some bacteria have enzymes that alter the sequence of DNA, in order to maintain resistance. What is the general name for these enzymes?
W) RNA Helicase
X) Polymerase
Y) Inductase
Z) Endonuclease
ANSWER: Z) Endonuclease
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Without the greenhouse effect, which of the following would be true?
W) Earth’s surface would be 40°C, and the earth would gain more energy
X) Earth’s surface would be 20°C, and the earth would lose more energy
Y) Earth’s surface would be -20°C, and the earth would lose more energy
Z) Earth’s surface would be -40°C, and the earth would gain more energy
ANSWER: Y) Earth’s surface would be -20°C, and the earth would lose more energy
