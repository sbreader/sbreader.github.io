1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the largest positive integer that cannot be formed by summing a multiple of 13 and a multiple of 7?
ANSWER: 71
1. BONUS Math Short Answer Suppose Talon has 4 red daggers, 4 blue daggers, and 4 white daggers. If he wants to guarantee he gets at least 1 red dagger, at least how many daggers must he randomly take?
ANSWER: 9
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is a triprotic acid?
W) Hydrobromic acid
X) Phosphorous acid
Y) Phosphoric acid
Z) Sulfuric acid
ANSWER: Y) Phosphoric acid
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Highly electronegative groups can often cause or increase the magnitude of polarization of nearby bonds. What is the name of this effect?
ANSWER: Inductive effect
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following ores that are principal ores of Iron 1) Magnetite 2) Bauxite 3) Hematite.
ANSWER: 1 and 3
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules serves as the main structural component of fungal cell walls?
W) Cellulose
X) Chitin
Y) Glycogen
Z) Pectin
ANSWER: X) Chitin
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which cellular organelle in eukaryotes is primarily responsible for producing ATP through oxidative phosphorylation?
ANSWER: Mitochondria
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Eli drops a Minecraft-sized anvil onto William’s head as he walks by. If Eli is in a window 100 meters above the ground, and William is 10 meters tall, then, to the nearest second, how long does it take for the anvil to hit William’s head?
ANSWER: 4 seconds
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Three springs connected in series have equivalent spring constant 20 Newtons per meter. What would be the equivalent spring constant in Newtons per meter if you were to connect these springs in parallel?
ANSWER: 180 Newtons per meter
6. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which one of the following is not a possible set of side lengths for an obtuse triangle?
W) 4, 7, 9
X) 8, 11, 14
Y) 10, 12, 16
Z) 20, 21, 29
ANSWER: Z) 20, 21, 29
6. BONUS Math Multiple Choice The graph of which of the following functions has a hole?
W) (x2 + 4)/(x − 2)
X) (x2 − 7x + 12)/(x − 5)
Y) (x2 − 16x + 60)/(x2 − 17x + 72)
Z) (x2 − 18x + 77)/(x2 − 15x + 56)
ANSWER: Z) (x2 − 18x + 77)/(x2 − 15x + 56)
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Deneb, Altair, and Vega form what asterism that appears in the summer of the northern sky?
ANSWER: Summer Triangle
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What series describes the temperature at which certain minerals crystallize when cooled, or melt when heated, also explaining why certain rocks are never found at the same spot?
ANSWER: Bowen’s reaction series
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In the 1700s, it was theorized that all acids had to contain oxygen. Which acid disproves this idea?
W) Acetic acid
X) Sulfuric acid
Y) Hydroiodic acid
Z) Nitrous acid
ANSWER: Y) Hydroiodic acid
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What theory of acids and bases would classify NH4+ as an acid, but would not classify NH3 as a base?
ANSWER: Arrhenius
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What hormone, produced by adipocytes, promotes satiety?
ANSWER: Leptin
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What process describes the uptake of DNA by a bacterial cell from its surroundings?
ANSWER: Transformation
10. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer An algorithm which maximizes the short term reward at each timestep is called what?
ANSWER: Greedy
10. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following could be classified as a greedy algorithm?
W) Breadth-first-search
X) Bubble Sort
Y) Dijkstra’s Algorithm
Z) Bellman-Ford Algorithm
ANSWER: Y) Dijkstra’s Algorithm
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Gauss’s Law for Magnetism is most closely related to which of the following statements?
W) The direction of the magnetic Lorentz force is perpendicular to the velocity of a charge
X) The direction of the magnetic Lorentz force is perpendicular to the magnetic field
Y) There are no magnetic monopoles
Z) The magnetic field from a current-carrying wire is inversely proportional to distance from the wire
ANSWER: Y) There are no magnetic monopoles
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer If the coefficient of static friction is large enough, a block on an inclined plane sloping rightwards will tip over and fall if what point is farther right than its bottom right corner?
ANSWER: Center of Mass
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer At Akhil’s school, there is a Spanish club with 24 students, and a French club with 19 students. If 3 students are part of both clubs, then what is the probability that a student chosen from either club will only be in one club?
ANSWER: 37/40
12. BONUS Math Short Answer Conrad is betting on Valorant games. To decide how much he is going to bet, he rolls a 6, 8, 10, 12, and 20 sided dice, finds their arithmetic mean, and bets 100 times that amount. What is the expected amount of money he will bet?
ANSWER: $ 610

W) Water

Z) Glucose
ANSWER: W) Water
