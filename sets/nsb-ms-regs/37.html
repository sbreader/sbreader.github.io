1. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Approximately what percentage of the energy need in the U.S. is currently met by biomass?
W) 15%
X) 10%
Y) 4%
Z) 1%
ANSWER: Y) 4%
1. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is an advantage of using biomass in the form of corn to produce ethanol?
W) Using corn to produce ethanol means that the U.S. will no longer need to import petroleum-based products
X) Corn-based ethanol is more fuel efficient than gasoline
Y) Corn-based ethanol production has little impact on water supply
Z) Food for cattle is produced when corn grain is converted to ethanol
ANSWER: Z) Food for cattle is produced when corn grain is converted to ethanol
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the shape of the orbits of the planets around the Sun in our Solar System?
ANSWER: ELLIPSE (also accept: ELLIPTICAL)
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What was the first planet discovered with a telescope?
ANSWER: URANUS
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice When looking through a glass of water with a straw in it, why might the straw appear bent and somewhat distorted?
W) Refraction
X) Diffraction
Y) Reflection
Z) Pressure
ANSWER: W) Refraction
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following regions of the electromagnetic spectrum has a longer wavelength than visible light?
W) Ultraviolet
X) Gamma rays
Y) Microwaves
Z) X-rays
ANSWER: Y) Microwaves
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following activities will increase your carbon footprint?
W) Eating a more beef-rich diet
X) Eating a more local vegetarian diet
Y) Keeping your family's car tires inflated to the correct level
Z) Using public transportation like buses and trains
ANSWER: W) Eating a more beef-rich diet
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is least likely to be impacted by climate change?
W) Atmosphere
X) Hydrosphere
Y) Cryosphere
Z) Mantle
ANSWER: Z) Mantle
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following must be true about the product of an integer and one greater than the integer?
W) The product is negative
X) The product is positive
Y) The product is even
Z) The product is odd
ANSWER: Y) The product is even
5. BONUS Math Short Answer The circumference of a circle is 6π. What is its area in terms of π?
ANSWER: 9π
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Melting an ice cube and boiling water are examples of what type of change that does not produce a new substance?
ANSWER: PHYSICAL (also accept: PHASE)
6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Thermodynamics tells us that we cannot reduce entropy in a closed system; it will always increase. Of what does entropy serve as a measure?
W) System size
X) Temperature
Y) Disorder
Z) Flux
ANSWER: Y) Disorder
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What do we call the evaporation of water from plants that occurs chiefly while their stomata are open for the passage of carbon dioxide and oxygen during photosynthesis?
W) Transpiration
X) Chemotaxis
Y) Chemosynthesis
Z) Chlorophyll
ANSWER: W) Transpiration
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms cannot perform photosynthesis?
W) Cyanobacteria
X) Ferns
Y) Mushrooms
Z) Trees
ANSWER: Y) Mushrooms
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the largest prime number less than 9?
ANSWER: 7

W) 4
X) 8
Y) 12

ANSWER: Y) 12
9. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true about incandescent lamps?
W) They have the least expensive initial purchase price
X) They typically can last over one million hours
Y) They provide warm light
Z) They operate without a ballast
ANSWER: X) They typically can last over one million hours
9. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice In most developing countries excluding China and India, which of the following is the primary source of energy for household energy consumption in rural and poor areas?
W) Hydroelectricity
X) Wind
Y) Biomass
Z) Solar
ANSWER: Y) Biomass
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If a heavy box is sitting on a table, not moving at all, what force is counteracting the pull of gravity?
ANSWER: NORMAL FORCE
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer You are standing on a scale in an elevator that is accelerating upwards as it goes from the basement to the roof. How does the reading of the scale compare with that obtained when the elevator was standing still?
ANSWER: GREATER (also accept: HIGHER, LARGER)
11. BONUS Math Short Answer At the oil spill in the Gulf of Mexico, underwater robots dove down in the water 5000 feet. If the robot dove down another 3000 feet, then rose up 6500 feet, where would the robot be in relation to sea level?
ANSWER: -1500 (also accept: 1500 BELOW SEA LEVEL)
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In vascular plants, what is the name of the transport tissue that primarily carries water to the plant?
ANSWER: XYLEM
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What force allows the xylem to transport water against gravity?
ANSWER: CAPILLARY ACTION
13. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is a primary issue associated with harnessing solar power via panels?

Y) Difficult assembly

13. BONUS Energy Short Answer Based solely on wind speeds, rank the following states in order from most to least windy: Florida, Missouri, Nebraska, Arkansas.
