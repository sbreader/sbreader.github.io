1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The ability to store charge is known as…
ANSWER: capacitance
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer Given a solid sphere with a mass M and a radius R, what is its moment of inertia?
ANSWER: 2/5 MR2 (also accept: 0.4 MR2)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The presence of a wide stretch around 3000-3500 wavenumbers in an IR Spectra indicates what type of functional group?
ANSWER: ALCOHOLS (also accept: HYDROXYL; O–H; ALCOHOL)
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The characteristic broad stretch of an alcohol in an IR Spectra is due to what?
W) The large electron shielding of the oxygen atom
X) The hydrogen bonding between the alcohol molecules
Y) The intoxicating effects of alcohols in general
Z) The high volatility of alcohols compared to water
ANSWER: X) The hydrogen bonding between the alcohol molecules
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is most likely to be a restriction site?
W) AGGCCCGG
X) UUUAAUUU
Y) ATCGATCG
Z) AAAATTTT
ANSWER: Z) AAAATTTT
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements best describes the ecological principle of Red Queen dynamics?
W) Two species will both constantly evolve in response to each other in order to gain a competitive advantage.
X) Species that live in colonies have an evolutionary advantage over species that don’t.
Y) In honeybee colonies, the queen is redder than the other bees in order to make identification easier.
Z) In times of extreme stress, the queen bee will become a worker bee and not reproduce.
ANSWER: W) Two species will both constantly evolve in response to each other in order to gain a competitive advantage.
7. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Eigenvalues and eigenvectors refer to which of the following with respect to the transformation of a vector by a square matrix?
W) Magnitude and direction
X) Direction and magnitude
Y) Cardinality and magnitude
Z) Magnitude and cardinality
ANSWER: W) Magnitude and direction
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Nuclear fusion is an important stellar process that is initiated when hydrogen plasma reaches a critical temperature on the order of magnitude of approximately how many kelvin?
W) 107
X) 108
Y) 109
Z) 1010
ANSWER: W) 107
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Low-mass stars, upon consuming their core hydrogen fuel, undergo rapid expansion during an aptly named "helium flash," when the triple-alpha process of helium fusion initiates after the star’s contraction heats the core to a critical temperature on the order of magnitude of approximately how many kelvin?
W) 107
X) 108
Y) 109
Z) 1010
ANSWER: X) 108
