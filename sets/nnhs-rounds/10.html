1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer At high energies, photons will undergo what process, creating an electron and a positron?
ANSWER: PAIR PRODUCTION
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice What is the escape velocity of a 2 million kilogram rocket at the surface of Pluto, which has a mass of 1.3 × 1022 kilograms and a radius of 1.2 × 103 kilometers?
W) 600 meters per second
X) 850 meters per second
Y) 1200 meters per second
Z) 2000 meters per second
ANSWER: Y) 1200 meters per second
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following four essential elements in plants from most to least common by percent mass in dry tissue 1) Carbon 2) Calcium 3) Iron 4) Nitrogen.
ANSWER: 1,4,2,3
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What pouch, used by hydras, consists of a digestive compartment with a single opening?
ANSWER: GASTROVASCULAR CAVITY
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the direction of air movement around an anticyclone and a cyclone respectively, in the southern hemisphere?
W) Clockwise, clockwise
X) Clockwise, counterclockwise
Y) Counterclockwise, clockwise
Z) Counterclockwise, counterclockwise
ANSWER: Y) Counterclockwise, clockwise
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three features that are erosional shoreline features 1) Tombolo 2) Sea stack 3) Sea arch.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of regular polygons CANNOT tessellate the plane, if both polygons have the same side length?
W) Square and octagon
X) Pentagon and decagon
Y) Triangle and square
Z) Hexagon and triangle
ANSWER: X) Pentagon and decagon
4. BONUS Math Short Answer A convex heptagon has all of its diagonals drawn in, and no three diagonals intersect at the same point. How many intersections are there inside the heptagon?
ANSWER: 35
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the longest wavelength in the Lyman series for hydrogen?
W) 90 nanometers
X) 120 nanometers
Y) 360 nanometers
Z) 660 nanometers
ANSWER: X) 120 nanometers
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To the nearest kilojoule, what is the change in Gibbs free energy of the reduction of one mole of silver (I) ion to silver, if Faraday’s constant is 96500 coulombs per mole and the standard reduction potential is +0.80 volts?
ANSWER: –77 (do not accept: 77)
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are using neutron scattering to analyze lipid membranes. In particular, they are focusing on what molecule in the lipid membrane that regulates its stiffness?
ANSWER: CHOLESTEROL
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are creating antidotes to a specific class of poisons known as organophosphates. Many organophosphates are nerve agents that attack what enzyme critical to the degradation of neurotransmitters in a synapse?
ANSWER: ACETYLCHOLINESTERASE
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What statement describes the minimum resolvable detail of an imaging process?
ANSWER: RAYLEIGH CRITERION
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer What type of superconductor does not exhibit a complete Meissner effect and exhibits a phase of mixed ordinary and superconducting properties?
ANSWER: TYPE II
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following plant hormones is NOT involved in the plant’s defense against pathogens or herbivores?
W) Auxins
X) Salicylic Acid
Y) Systemin
Z) Jasmonates
ANSWER: W) Auxins
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What reddish protein is found in root nodules that limits the amount of O2 present to provide proper conditions for nitrogen fixation?
ANSWER: LEGHEMOGLOBIN
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the time it takes for the moon to return to the same angular position from the Sun as seen from the Earth?
ANSWER: SYNODIC PERIOD
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In the fossil record, a thin band of rock, known as the KT boundary, separates the Mesozoic and Cenozoic eras. What element is found in unusually high amounts in this rock layer, and is hypothesized to have been deposited by a meteorite impact?
ANSWER: IRIDIUM
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A committee of four members is selected from a group of n people. For which of the following values of n will adding an additional person to the group exactly double the number of possible ways to choose the committee?
W) 5
X) 6
Y) 7
Z) 8
ANSWER: Y) 7
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the mean and median of the first 6 positive integers that are NOT prime?
ANSWER: MEAN IS 19/3 AND MEDIAN IS 7 (also accept: 19/3 AND 7)
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the VSEPR molecular shape of the tri-iodide ion?
ANSWER: LINEAR
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What number determines the electrostatic potential in a crystal by approximating the ions as point charges?
ANSWER: MADELUNG CONSTANT
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Fermilab are studying quantum computing. One reason that quantum computers are much faster than traditional computers is because quantum bits, or qubits, are able to be in many different states at the same time before they are observed. What is the name of this ability?
ANSWER: SUPERPOSITION
12. BONUS Math Short Answer Researchers at Fermilab are studying quantum computing, which could break modern cryptography schemes due to the ability to factor integers extremely quickly. What is the sum of the prime factors of 1554?
ANSWER: 49
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What theory correctly predicted the anomalous orbit of Mercury?
ANSWER: GENERAL RELATIVITY (also accept: GR; GENERAL THEORY OF RELATIVITY; GTR) (do not accept: RELATIVITY; SPECIAL RELATIVITY)
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer A conducting square with side length 2 meters has a charge of 7 nanocoulombs. To one significant figure, what is the electric field at a point very close to the conducting sheet?
ANSWER: 1000
