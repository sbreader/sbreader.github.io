Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What phenomenon, responsible for the blue glow of nuclear reactors, occurs when a charged particle exceeds the speed of light in a medium?
ANSWER: CHERENKOV RADIATION
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer What thermodynamic effect describes the decrease in temperature of a gas as it is throttled through a valve at constant enthalpy?
ANSWER: JOULE-THOMSON EFFECT
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following structures contains bipolar neurons?
W) Auditory nerve
X) Vagus nerve
Y) Cerebrum
Z) Retina
ANSWER: Z) Retina
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice ER stress occurs under which of the following conditions?
W) Lack of phospholipid to make vesicles
X) Lack of ribosomes to produce polypeptides
Y) Increased unfolded protein level
Z) Decreased unfolded protein level
ANSWER: Y) Increased unfolded protein level
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about quasars is correct?
W) Quasars were more common in the early universe
X) Quasars are stellar objects of very low luminosities
Y) Quasars are Seyfert galaxies
Z) Quasars are yet to be observed
ANSWER: W) Quasars were more common in the early universe
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In what natural process does radiation gradually destroy a mineral’s crystal structure, leaving it amorphous?
ANSWER: METAMICTIZATION (also accept: METAMICTION)
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A circle and a square are drawn in the same plane. What is the maximum number of intersections between the two figures?
ANSWER: 8
4. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the largest possible area of a square that is inscribed in a regular octagon with a side length of 4?
ANSWER: 32 + 16√2
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many different enantiomers does the compound with molecular formula CH2FCl have?
ANSWER: 1
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In catalytic perfection, enzymes catalyze a reaction so efficiently that the rate of the reaction is limited by what process?
ANSWER: DIFFUSION (also accept: SUBSTRATE DIFFUSION; PRODUCT DIFFUSION)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Researchers at Fermilab are looking for weakly-interacting massive particles, or WIMPs, which are a potential candidate of dark matter. Dark matter was originally posited to exist after noticing discrepancies in graphs of which of the following two quantities of a galaxy?
W) Acceleration and rotational velocity
X) Rotational velocity and radius
Y) Radius and gravitational force
Z) Luminosity and distance from the galaxy
ANSWER: X) Rotational velocity and radius
6. BONUS Math Short Answer Researchers at Fermilab are studying quantum mechanics. The researchers are using Hermitian matrices, which are square matrices whose transpose equals its complex conjugate. Identify all of the following four numbers that could be the element in the first row and first column of a Hermitian matrix 1) 1 2) 1/2 + √3/2i; 3)-4; 4) 2i.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A spaceship travels at the escape velocity from Earth. Which of the following best describes the shape of the spaceship’s trajectory?
W) Circular
X) Elliptical
Y) Parabolic
Z) Hyperbolic
ANSWER: Y) Parabolic
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer A heat pump operates at the maximum possible efficiency between two reservoirs of 27 and 77 Celsius. What is the coefficient of performance of this heat pump?
ANSWER: 7
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the primary polysaccharide that makes up the middle lamella?
ANSWER: PECTIN
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What type of epithelial tissue is found on the lining of the upper respiratory tract?
W) Simple squamous
X) Simple cuboidal
Y) Pseudostratified columnar
Z) Stratified squamous
ANSWER: Y) Pseudostratified columnar
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In what layer of the atmosphere does convection primarily occur?
ANSWER: TROPOSPHERE
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the time it takes for Earth to complete one cycle of precession?
W) 11,000 years
X) 26,000 years
Y) 92,000 years
Z) 240,000 years
ANSWER: X) 26,000 years
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following could be the eccentricity of a hyperbola?
W) 0
X) 0.4
Y) 1
Z) 1.7
ANSWER: Z) 1.7
10. BONUS Math Short Answer An ant starts at one corner of a rectangular prism with side lengths of 3, 5, and 9. The ant wants to crawl on the surface of the prism to the opposite vertex. What is the minimum distance the ant has to crawl?
ANSWER: √145
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the hybridization of carbon atoms in graphene?
W) s
X) sp
Y) sp2
Z) sp3
ANSWER: Y) sp2
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What rule states that the entropy of vaporization of most substances is around the same value?
ANSWER: TROUTON’S RULE
12. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying the use of metal organic frameworks to store natural gas. Which of the following gases is the primary component of natural gas?
W) Methane
X) Hydrogen
Y) Carbon dioxide
Z) Hydrogen sulfide
ANSWER: W) Methane
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying the SARS-COV-2 virus. In particular, they are looking at what physiological reaction in humans, where the innate immune system causes an uncontrolled and excessive release of pro-inflammatory signaling molecules?
ANSWER: CYTOKINE STORM
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An alpha particle accelerates across a 5 volt potential difference. In electron volts, what is the kinetic energy of the particle?
ANSWER: 10
13. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A uniform, ideal spring is allowed to hang from its endpoints under the influence of gravity. What is the shape of the spring?
W) Catenary
X) Parabola
Y) Circular arch
Z) Elliptical arch
ANSWER: X) Parabola
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice You find two white roses. After crossing the two flowers, all of the resulting offspring are red, the wild-type phenotype. Which of the following is true?
W) The two mutations are found on the same gene
X) The two mutations are found on different genes
Y) The two mutations are linked with recombination frequency 0.5
Z) An F2 generation would be needed before drawing any conclusion
ANSWER: X) The two mutations are found on different genes
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three groups that have dominant sporophytes 1) Mosses 2) Ferns 3) Angiosperms.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which of the following parts of an HR diagram would one expect to find white dwarfs?
W) Top left
X) Top right
Y) Bottom left
Z) Bottom right
ANSWER: Y) Bottom left
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What kind of trace fossil consists of the petrified remains of dung and is often used to analyze the diet of extinct species?
ANSWER: COPROLITE
16. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following equations is equivalent to the polar equation r = 3sin(θ)?
W) x = 3
X) y = 3
Y) x2 + y2 = 3x
Z) x2 + y2 = 3y
ANSWER: Z) x2 + y2 = 3y
16. BONUS Math Short Answer In radians, what is the phase shift of sin(x) + cos(x)?
ANSWER: π/4
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the acid anhydride of nitric acid?
ANSWER: N2O5 (also accept: DINITROGEN PENTOXIDE)
17. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds is most likely to experience the leveling effect in water?
W) H3O+
X) H2N-
Y) H2S
Z) O2-
ANSWER: X) H2N-
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers at Ames National Lab are studying magnetic topological semimetals at the Center for the Advancement of Topological Semimetals. Which of the following elements is a semimetal?
W) Aluminum
X) Phosphorous
Y) Cadmium
Z) Arsenic
ANSWER: Z) Arsenic
18. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Lab are studying the quality control of graphene, which may have implications for the manufacturing of sensors. What sensor, often used in cameras, consists of an integrated circuit that produces electrical signals from photons incident on its surface?
ANSWER: CHARGE COUPLED DEVICE (also accept: CCD)
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An ideal transformer has 10 coils in the primary winding and 30 coils in the secondary winding. If the source current is 60 amps, then what current does the transformer output?
ANSWER: 20 AMPS
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two speakers are placed a distance of 0.5 meters apart and are facing each other, and both speakers emit sound with a wavelength of 1.0 meters. As a microphone is moved from one speaker to the other, however, the microphone does not observe total destructive interference. What phenomenon is responsible for this effect?
ANSWER: DOPPLER EFFECT (also accept: DOPPLER SHIFT)
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When glucose levels are low in the bacterial cell, cyclic AMP levels are increased. Cyclic AMP binds to what protein that, when bound, serves as an activator for the lac operon?
ANSWER: CATABOLITE ACTIVATOR PROTEIN (also accept: CAP; CYCLIC AMP RECEPTOR PROTEIN; CRP)
20. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Nociceptors most directly sense which of the following?
W) Pain
X) Temperature
Y) Heat
Z) Osmolarity
ANSWER: W) Pain
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a cause of tsunamis?
W) Meteor impact
X) Landslide
Y) Volcanic eruption
Z) Mining
ANSWER: Z) Mining
21. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice According to the Saros cycle, if a lunar eclipse occurred on June 5, 2020, then a lunar eclipse must occur on which of the following days?
W) June 11, 2029
X) June 16, 2038
Y) May 29, 2047
Z) June 12, 2056
ANSWER: X) June 16, 2038
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What type of function is characterized by the statement that distinct elements in its domain map to distinct elements in its range?
ANSWER: INJECTIVE (also accept: ONE-TO-ONE) (do not accept: BIJECTIVE; ONE-TO-ONE CORRESPONDENCE; INVERTIBLE)
22. BONUS Math Short Answer Evaluate the indefinite integral of the fraction with numerator dx and denominator 9 plus 4x squared.
ANSWER: 1/6 tan-1(2x/3) + C
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following properties of nitrogen explains why it has a larger first ionization energy than oxygen?
W) Increased electron shielding
X) Stronger fine structure
Y) Higher energy of antibonding electrons
Z) More stable 2p orbital
ANSWER: Z) More stable 2p orbital
23. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In which of the following types of materials would one expect to find nematic and lyotropic phases?
W) Liquid crystal
X) Plasma
Y) Superconductor
Z) Bose-einstein condensate
ANSWER: W) Liquid crystal
