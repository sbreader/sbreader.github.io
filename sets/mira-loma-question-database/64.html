1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If a conic section with an equation of the form r=ep/(1+ecos(theta)) is graphed on the polar axes, what will be at the pole?
ANSWER: FOCUS
1. BONUS Math Short Answer A conic section on the polar coordinate system has the equation r=6/(1-2cos(theta)). What is the eccentricity of this conic? What is the shortest distance between the focus and the directrix? What is the orientation of the directrix?
ANSWER: (1) 2 (2) 3 (3) Vertical
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer This class of polysaccharides is composed of long chains of glucose monomers with beta-glycosidic linkages and is an essential component of certain medically important fungal pathogens as well as the cell walls of plant cells. The synthesis of these is blocked by the drugs caspofungin and micafungin. Name this class of biomolecules.
ANSWER: BETA-GLUCANS
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which law states that the integral of the magnetic field with respect to the length of a closed path is equal to the permeability of free space times the net current that passes through the surface bounded by the closed path?
ANSWER: AMPERE'S CIRCUITAL LAW

W) Argentite
X) Cuprite
Y) Chalcocite
Z) Coltan

ANSWER: ALL OF THEM
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not true regarding digestive enzymes?
W) Chymosin is produced in infants and cleaves casein
X) Aminopeptidase is zinc-dependent
Y) Carboxypeptidase hydrolyzes proteins at the C-terminus

9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which gas law states that for a gas with constant volume, pressure must be directly proportional to temperature?
ANSWER: GAY-LUSSAC'S LAW
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why all materials are not magnetic even though they have a coiled circuit of electrons?
W) The magnetic field produced by electrons is negligible
X) For every electron there is an oppositely revolving electron
Y) The opposite charge of the proton cancels the magnetic field
Z) The atom does not have a changing magnetic flux
ANSWER: X) For every electron there is an oppositely revolving electron

13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Cyclic AMP does not remain in the cytoplasm of a cell and continue to cause cellular responses in the absence of the hormone because...

X) It is inactivated by dephosphorylation
Y) Phosphodiesterase catalyzes its conversion to AMP
Z) The remaining molecules of protein kinase A are degraded

25. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not true of the nervous regulation of the heart?
W) Baroreceptors in the lungs inhibit the inspiratory center in the medulla
