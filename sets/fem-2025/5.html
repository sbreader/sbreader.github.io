1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which biome, found in California and southern Africa, consists of short shrubs with a consistent climate and infrequent rainfall and is naturally moderated by fires?
ANSWER: Chaparral
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following three sequences by increasing melting point 1) 5’- GAATTC-3’ 2) 5’-GCCAGC-3’ 3) 5’-GCTGAC-3’
ANSWER: 1,3,2
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The Plateau-Rayleigh instability occurs when a falling stream of water breaks up into several smaller droplets. This results from what force acting on the water causing it to minimize its surface area to volume ratio?
ANSWER: Surface tension
2. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice The speed of sound in a fluid is 30 m/s. If the bulk modulus of the fluid is multiplied by a factor of 9 and the density is tripled, which of the following is closest to the new speed of sound in m/s in the fluid?
W) 20
X) 30
Y) 40
Z) 50
ANSWER: Z) 50

X) NH3

Z) BF3
ANSWER: Z) BF3

W) 7.5
X) 8
Y) 8.5
Z) 9
ANSWER: X) 8
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Compute the limit as n approaches infinity of (1 + 2/n)3n.
ANSWER: e6

W) Always increases
X) Always decreases
Y) Increases then decreases
Z) Decreases then increases
ANSWER: W) Always increases

X) 12,000
Y) 24,000
Z) 48,000
ANSWER: Y) 24,000

X) Schwann cells
Y) Oligodendrocytes
Z) Ependymal cells
ANSWER: W) Astrocytes

X) Cytosine

Z) Thymidine

X) It has no dipole moment
Y) It has no internal resistance
