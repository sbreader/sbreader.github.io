1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What geologically active moon orbits in retrograde to its host planet, exhibits large-scale cryovolcanism, and has a characteristic "cantaloupe terrain"?
ANSWER: TRITON
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Triton's unusual size and orbit, as well as its young surface features, suggest that it was not formed in a typical fashion. Which of the following is the currently accepted theory for the formation of Triton?
W) Planetary capture of a Kuiper Belt object
X) Accretion of debris from the break-up of a previous, larger moon
Y) A recent merger of several smaller moons
Z) Coalescence of debris after a Neptunian impact, similar to the Earth's moon
ANSWER: W) Planetary capture of a Kuiper Belt object
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three common laboratory reagents that are NOT primary standards 1. NaOH 2. HCl 3. KMnO4
ANSWER: ALL
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Similarly to Plutonian penitentes, carbon nanotubes can also be synthesized by seeding a host polycarbonate and allowing carbon vapor to deposit in a tube structure. This method, as opposed to the "top-down" technique, is known as what process?
ANSWER: "BOTTOM-UP"

4. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A thin spherical shell with a uniform non-zero surface charge density spins with a constant angular speed about one of its diameters. Which of the following is an accurate, qualitative description of the magnetic field on the inside and outside of the shell, respectively?
W) Zero on the inside; like the magnetic field of a finite piece of current-carrying wire on the outside
X) Zero on the inside; like the magnetic field of a dipole on the outside
Y) Uniform, but not zero, on the inside; like the magnetic field of a finite piece of currentcarrying wire on the outside
