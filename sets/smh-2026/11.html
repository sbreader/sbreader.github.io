1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following phyla that possess centralized nervous systems. 1) Ctenophora 2) Porifera 3) Platyhelminthes
ANSWER: 3
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 cell types in which you would expect to find MHC class 1 molecules 1. Erythrocyte 2. Platelet 3. Cardiomyocyte
ANSWER: 2, 3
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An infinitely long wire is placed along the y-axis and intercepts the origin, with current flowing in the negative y direction. A proton has positive x coordinate and is moving in the positive x direction. In what direction is the force on the proton directed?
ANSWER: negative y
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer Select all of the following three processes that occur during cosmic-ray showers 1) Alpha decay 2) Pair production 3) Bremsstrahlung
ANSWER: 2, 3
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Points A and B are chosen uniformly and independently on the circumference of the unit circle. What is the probability that the triangle formed by points A, B, and the origin is acute?
ANSWER: 1/2
3. BONUS Math Short Answer Given that xy = 3x + 8y for integers x and y, what is the number of possible values of x?
ANSWER: 16
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What name is given the reaction in which reactants of the same element but different oxidation numbers combine to form a product with an intermediate oxidation state?
ANSWER: Comproportionation (also accept: Symproportionation)
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Hriday Meka is a reactive individual, so he is currently studying the rate of chemical reactions. He constructs an Arrhenius plot by graphing the natural log of the rate constant against the inverse of temperature. If this graph were extrapolated to the y-axis, then which of the following quantities would equal to the y-intercept?
W) Activation energy
X) Natural log of activation energy
Y) Pre-exponential factor
Z) Natural log of pre-exponential factor
ANSWER: Z) Natural log of pre-exponential factor
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three phenomena observed on the sun by increasing lifetime. 1) Quiescent prominences 2) Active Prominences 3) Solar flares
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three types of rock by increasing abundance in the earth’s continental crust. 1. Igneous 2. Sedimentary 3. Metamorphic
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The motion of cilia and flagella in sperm cells is primarily driven by what motor protein?
ANSWER: Dynein
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Select all of the following processes that occur during anaphase in animal cells. 1) Motor proteins link microtubules with other microtubules 2) Motor proteins link microtubules with chromosomes 3) Motor proteins cause rings of actin filaments to contract
ANSWER: 1 and 2
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In an adiabatic process, the volume of a monatomic gas changes by a factor of 8. By what factor does the pressure of that fluid change?
ANSWER: 1/32
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer Charles irreversibly adds 340 J of heat into a gas, raising its temperature from 100K to 270K. Assuming constant heat capacity, then, in Joules per Kelvin to the nearest integer, what is the change in entropy of the gas?
ANSWER: 2
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many subsets of the first 10 positive integers have the sum of their elements odd?
ANSWER: 512
8. BONUS Math Short Answer Let a and b be the two roots of x2-x-1 = 0. What is a5 + b5?
ANSWER: 11
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the effect in which polydentate ligands such as oxalate, dien, and EDTA increase the stability of resulting coordination complexes formed when they bond to a metal cation?
ANSWER: Chelation effect (also accept: Chelate effect)
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice According to standard d-orbital splitting models, which statement is true about the complex ion hexacyanoferrate(II) with formula [Fe(CN)6]4-?
W) It is diamagnetic because cyanide is a weak-field ligand
X) It is diamagnetic because cyanide is a strong-field ligand
Y) It is paramagnetic because cyanide is a weak-field ligand
Z) It is paramagnetic because cyanide is a strong-field ligand
ANSWER: X) It is diamagnetic because cyanide is a strong-field ligand
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In the hubble classification scheme, which of the following could be used to denote an intermediate between a barred spiral and elliptical galaxy?
W) Sb zero
X) S zero
Y) E zero
Z) Irr
ANSWER: W) Sb zero
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer If Jupiter has a mass of 2 × 1027 kilograms, find the Schwarzschild radius of Jupiter in scientific notation to one significant figure.
ANSWER: 3
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at South Middle Hollylake are studying photons. If a photon undergoes Compton scattering through an angle of 60 degrees, what is the change in its wavelength in terms of the electron Compton wavelength lambdaC?
ANSWER: lambdaC/2
11. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at SMH are studying radio waves. One stormy night, they leave their radio on, and receive radio waves generated by lightning that travelled through Earth's magnetosphere with frequency peaking at 4000 Hz. Which of the following best explains why these signals are heard as descending tones?
W) Earth's magnetic field causes the waves to lose energy continuously, lowering their frequency
X) Different frequencies travel at different speeds, causing higher frequencies to arrive first
Y) The waves undergo repeated reflections that progressively reduce their frequency
Z) The waves are Doppler-shifted as they travel along curved magnetic field lines toward the receiver
ANSWER: X) Different frequencies travel at different speeds, causing higher frequencies to arrive first

W) Gnetophyta
X) Ginkophyta
Y) Cycadophyta
Z) Coniferophyta

12. BONUS Biology Short Answer In emphysema, the coalescence of alveolar membranes gradually results in fewer but larger alveoli, in turn leading to greater compliance. This increased compliance may be explained by what physical law, which relates wall tension to pressure and radius?
ANSWER: LAW OF LAPLACE
13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the general shape traced out by the momentum-position graph of an oscillating spring-mass system?
W) Ellipse
X) Parabola
Y) Hyperbola
Z) Depends on the spring constant
ANSWER: W) Ellipse

W) 4/3
X) 5/4
Y) 3/2
Z) 7/4

ANSWER: Maxwell's demon
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer Jake is currently in a circular orbit around the Earth. In order to transfer to a higher circular orbit, he first fires his rocket engines to transition to an elliptical path, coasts along for half a period, then fires his rocket engines again to transition to a circular trajectory. What name is given to the orbital maneuver that Jake has just performed?
ANSWER: Hohmann transfer
