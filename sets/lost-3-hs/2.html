However, to their surprise, they find that the time it takes for a laser pulse to travel through the length of the wire and back is less than 60% of the accepted value for the speed of light. Which of the following best explains why?
W) The laser pulse loses energy as it travels, causing photons to gradually lose their kinetic energy
