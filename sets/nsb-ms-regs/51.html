1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A child's seesaw would be considered:

X) a form of inclined plane

ANSWER: Z) a class 1 lever
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 100-watt light bulb is lighted by a 100 volt power source. What is the current, in amperes, running through the bulb?
ANSWER: 1 AMPERE

X) cerebellum
Y) medulla oblongata
Z) hypothalamus
ANSWER: W) cerebral cortex
