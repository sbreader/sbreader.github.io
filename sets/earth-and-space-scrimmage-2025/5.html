1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In RR Lyrae and Cepheid variables, periodic changes in luminosity primarily arise due to which of the following processes?
W) Transitions in fusion processes disrupts hydrostatic equilibrium
X) Partial ionized zones in the stellar envelope trap heat and drive pulsations
Y) Radiation pressure in the envelope periodically overcomes gravity, leading to eruptions
Z) Mass loss by stellar wind weaken gravitational stability
ANSWER: X) Partial ionized zones in the stellar envelope trap heat and drive pulsations
1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following four processes in the water cycle that always involve liquid water 1) Condensation 2) Precipitation 3) Evapotranspiration 4) Deposition
ANSWER: 1 and 3
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following three minerals in decreasing number of planes of cleavage 1) Quartz 2) Muscovite 3) Orthoclase
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following isotopes which may be fused in a pre-main-sequence star 1) Hydrogen-1 2) Hydrogen-2 3) Helium-4
ANSWER: 2 only
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following lifting mechanisms would best explain why clouds preferentially form over a relatively flat island rather than the sea around it?
W) Convective
X) Frontal
Y) Convergence
Z) Orographic
ANSWER: W) Convective
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of unconformity is most likely to form in a region initially underwater, became exposed to air for erosion, then finally resubmerged?
ANSWER: Disconformity
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three lapse rates from lowest to highest magnitude, assuming absolutely stable atmospheric conditions 1) Environmental lapse rate 2) Dry adiabatic lapse rate 3) Moist adiabatic lapse rate.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best compares the salinity and temperature of the Mediterranean Sea relative to the Antarctic Intermediate Water?
W) More saline and colder
X) More saline and warmer
Y) Less saline and colder
Z) Less saline and warmer
ANSWER: X) More saline and warmer
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Flora observes a star and sees titanium oxide lines. Identify all of the following three spectral lines she is also expected to see in the star 1) Neutral hydrogen 2) Neutral helium 3) Neutral calcium
ANSWER: 3 only
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following would most strongly suggest that a sandstone formed in a desert rather than a beach environment?
W) High silica content
X) Low concentrations of oxide impurities
Y) Presence of large-scale cross-bedding
Z) Presence of carbonate cement
ANSWER: Y) Presence of large-scale cross-bedding
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Due to large amounts of interstellar dust surrounding the galactic nucleus, Sagittarius A* can be best observed in what region of the electromagnetic spectrum?
ANSWER: Radio
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Rohit is wandering on a black sand beach in Hawaii. Which of the following minerals will likely have the highest concentration on this beach?
W) Garnet
X) Olivine
Y) Feldspar
Z) Quartz
ANSWER: X) Olivine
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the shape traced out by the Sun’s position in the sky at local noon, throughout a full year?
W) Circle
X) Ellipse
Y) Symmetrical figure 8
Z) Asymmetrical figure 8
ANSWER: Z) Asymmetrical figure 8
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following events would most likely strengthen a sea breeze at noon?
W) An increase in cloud cover over the land
X) A cold ocean current moving closer to shore
Y) A surface low-pressure zone building offshore
Z) A sudden increase in humidity over the ocean
ANSWER: X) A cold ocean current moving closer to shore
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A strong positive gravitational anomaly detected beneath a continental ice sheet most likely indicates the presence of which of the following features?
W) Subglacial lake
X) Magma chamber
Y) Mountain range
Z) Esker
ANSWER: Y) Mountain range
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following factors that would promote the development of a thermocline in a temperate lake. 1) High Solar Heating 2) Strong winds 3) Deep lake basin
ANSWER: 1 and 3
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the rate of universe expansion since the Big Bang?
W) Uniformly increased
X) Uniformly Decreased
Y) Increased then decreased
Z) Decreased then increased
ANSWER: Z) Decreased then increased
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the only moon in the solar system known to have a denser atmosphere than the Earth?
ANSWER: Titan
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following processes that would likely decrease the volatility of an andesitic magma during ascent through the continental crust 1) Mixing with magnesium-rich mantle magma 2) Assimilation of limestone 3) Fractional crystallization
ANSWER: 1 only
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The H-alpha line has a rest wavelength of approximately 660 nanometers. When observing a star, Max notices its H-alpha line has been shifted to 590 nanometers. Ignoring relativistic effects, which of the following best describes the magnitude and direction of the star’s radial velocity?
W) 0.1c, towards Max
X) 0.5c, towards Max
Y) 0.1c, away from Max
Z) 0.5c, away from Max
ANSWER: W) 0.1c, towards Max
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Regular thunderstorms often dissipate quickly as downdrafts from the cloud base cut off the rising source of moisture. In contrast, supercell thunderstorms often last much longer, primarily due to which of the following reasons?
W) The updraft is too strong to be countered by the sinking cold air
X) The cloud in supercell thunderstorms begin with a larger volume of water vapor
Y) The updraft and downdraft are spatially separated within the cloud
Z) Supercell thunderstorms lack downdrafts altogether
ANSWER: Y) The updraft and downdraft are spatially separated within the cloud
