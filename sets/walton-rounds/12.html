1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the enthalpy of a system with internal energy 100 J, pressure of 10 N/m2, and a volume of 100 m3?

W) Pyrazine
X) Dimethylformamide

2. BONUS Math Short Answer Rounded to the nearest tenths place, what is the probability that exactly 4 births are girls if there are 10 births?
ANSWER: 0.2
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Engineers at Monash University in Australia have developed a diagnostic test using glass, teflon film, and a strip of paper to detect fibrinogen concentrations in the blood. Fibrinogen is vital to what process, which occurs following a wound or injury to the blood vessels?
ANSWER: CLOTTING (also accept: COAGULATION)

W) 35 m/s
X) 31.95 m/s

ANSWER: TELENCEPHALON
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is a true statement about Isomers?
