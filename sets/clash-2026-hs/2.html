1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A balloon is rubbed against a wall, causing both objects to become charged. Which of the following subatomic particles are transferred between the two objects, which creates this difference in charge?

Z) Quarks
ANSWER: Y) Electrons
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Why does the angular momentum of a satellite never change, even when gravity is acting on it?
W) The net torque applied is zero
X) The average gravity cancels out on opposite sides of an orbit
Y) The net work done is zero
Z) The gravitational force weakens and balances itself out as distance increases
ANSWER: W) The net torque applied is zero

X) Cnidaria
Y) Mollusca
