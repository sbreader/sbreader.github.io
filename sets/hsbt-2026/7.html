1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Varyan loves using geography to terrorize SciBowl students. At which of the following locations would one most likely find a diurnal tidal pattern?
W) Hawaii
X) North Carolina
Y) Oregon
Z) Louisiana
ANSWER: Z) Louisiana
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of telescopes that suffer from chromatic aberration 1) Galilean 2) Keplerian 3) Newtonian.
ANSWER: 1 and 2
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of modifications on afferent neurons allow you to feel moderate changes in temperature?
ANSWER: Transient receptor potential channels (also accept: TRP channels)
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Multiple sclerosis is an autoimmune neuropathy wherein the myelin sheaths in the central nervous system are systemically damaged. What is the peripheral counterpart to multiple sclerosis?
ANSWER: Guillain-Barré syndrome
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Min Shin was supposed to add ozone to his propene solution, but accidentally adds hot, concentrated permanganate solution instead, and observes gas evolving from the resulting solution. Which of the following is the identity of the gas produced?
W) Oxygen
X) Carbon dioxide
Y) Methane
Z) Carbon monoxide
ANSWER: X) Carbon dioxide
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following four compounds in increasing order of boiling point 1) Isobutane 2) Iso-butanol 3) N-butanol 4) N-butane.
ANSWER: 4, 1, 3, 2
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the ratio of the volume of a unit regular octahedron to the volume of a unit regular tetrahedron?
ANSWER: 4
4. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following 3D vectors is orthogonal to the vector (2, 0, 7)?
W) (5, 1, 2)
X) (-7, 4, 2)
Y) (3, 7, 0)
Z) (6, -1, -7)
ANSWER: X) (-7, 4, 2)
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Gluons arise from a SU(3) gauge theory. How many independent types of gluons are there in the standard model?
W) 1
X) 3
Y) 6
Z) 8
ANSWER: Z) 8
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer One mole of a gas of temperature 300 K is expanded isothermally such that it doubles in volume. In two significant figures, calculate the magnitude of work done on the gas.
ANSWER: 1700 J
6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Traditional silicon-based solar cells require high temperatures for manufacturing and operate at relatively low efficiencies on the commercial scale. However, solar cells made out of what other class of materials, an example of which is cesium tin triiodide, is currently being researched due to their high efficiencies and potential for much cheaper manufacturing costs?
ANSWER: Perovskites
6. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice When calculating carbon footprint, companies often use scope to distinguish between direct, indirect, and value-chain emissions. Which of the following sources of greenhouse gases would be considered a form of scope 2 emissions?
W) Fugitive emissions
X) Decomposition of sold products
Y) Purchased steam and heating
Z) Emissions from third-party shipping companies
ANSWER: Y) Purchased steam and heating
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four soil horizons from the lowest concentration of organic matter to the highest in a typical soil profile 1) A horizon 2) B horizon 3) C horizon 4) O horizon.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1, 4
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Suzuko is observing a star named Hong Jinnnius. If Hong Jinnius suddenly was moved 100 times further away and its intrinsic luminosity simultaneously increased by a factor of 100, how would the apparent magnitude change?
W) It would increase by 5
X) It would increase by 2.5
Y) It would not change
Z) It would decrease by 2.5
ANSWER: W) It would increase by 5
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A specific species of Pigeon called the Passenger Pigeon was once extremely numerous, with its global population numbering in the billions. They went extinct in the early 20th century due to human hunting and habitat destruction, significantly reducing their population to below a critical point where their key reproductive behaviors like communal nesting collapsed. What type of population dynamic pattern did this species exhibit?
ANSWER: Strong Allee effect
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer In order for nitrogenase to function, the oxygen concentration in the surrounding vicinity must be kept very low. What plant molecule performs this task of keeping the Rhizobium deoxygenated?
ANSWER: Leghemoglobin
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three group 16 hydrides by increasing average bond angle 1) H2O 2) H2S 3) H2Se.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Yunyi is studying the compounds 1,3 butadiene and 1,3,5 hexatriene. Which of the following best describes the frontier molecular orbital energies of 1,3 butadiene compared to those of 1,3,5 hexatriene?
W) Higher HOMO, Higher LUMO
X) Higher HOMO, Lower LUMO
Y) Lower HOMO, Higher LUMO
Z) Lower HOMO, Lower LUMO
ANSWER: Y) Lower HOMO, Higher LUMO
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A tangent segment PT is drawn from an external P to a circle, touching it at point T. A secant line from P also intersects the circle at points A and B. Given that PT = 10 and PA = 5, what is the length of chord AB?
ANSWER: 15
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the maximum number of regions formed by 4 chords on a circle?
ANSWER: 11

X) Parabola

Z) Cycloid

ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer A hydroelectric dam has a height of 30 m and a generator efficiency of approximately 85%. Assuming the density of water to be 1000 kg/m3 and the flow rate of water through the dam to be 50 m3/s, to one significant figure and in megawatts, what is the electrical power output for this dam?
ANSWER: 10 MW
