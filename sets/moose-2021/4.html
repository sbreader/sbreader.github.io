Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the theory that states that mitochondria and chloroplasts evolved independently from the rest of the cell?
ANSWER: ENDOSYMBIOSIS
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organelles does NOT contain DNA?
W) Mitochondria
X) Glyoxysome
Y) Chloroplast
Z) Nucleus
ANSWER: X) Glyoxysome
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What long fault line runs through the state of California and occasionally causes large earthquakes in the region, such as one that occurred near San Francisco in 1906?
ANSWER: SAN ANDREAS FAULT
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The Cascades mountain range in northwestern North America was created via the subduction of what minor tectonic plate?
ANSWER: JUAN DE FUCA
3. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Chemical reactions in solar panels produce direct current, but the country's electricity grid uses alternating current to provide electricity. What device is necessary to convert the direct current produced by solar panels into alternating current?
ANSWER: INVERTER
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Batteries can be made from foods such as potatoes and lemons. However, the power actually comes from the crossing of electrons between zinc and copper through the chosen food. Here, the food acts as which of the following components?
W) Anode
X) Cathode
Y) Salt bridge
Z) Container
ANSWER: Y) Salt bridge
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the name for the elementary particles in the nucleus that make up protons and neutrons?
ANSWER: QUARKS
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What term is used to describe the difference between the expected mass of an atom and the actual mass because mass in the nucleus is converted to energy?
ANSWER: MASS DEFECT
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the x intercept of a line with equation 2x + 7y = 56?
ANSWER: (28, 0) (also accept: 28)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following layers of Earth’s atmosphere that get hotter with height 1) Troposphere 2) Stratosphere 3) Mesosphere 4) Thermosphere
ANSWER: 2 and 4
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What layer of Earth's atmosphere blocks most ultraviolet light from the Sun?
ANSWER: STRATOSPHERE
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer An increase in prothrombin time and symptoms such as hematoma and bruising characterize the deficiency of what vitamin, found in both leafy green vegetables and animal-sourced foods?
ANSWER: VITAMIN K (also accept: PHYLLOQUINONE; K)
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 3 elements in terms of increasing electronegativity 1) Phosphorus 2) Tin 3) Barium
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice An unknown element has a 1st ionization energy of about 600 kJ/mol, a 2nd ionization energy of about 2000 kJ/mol, a 3rd ionization energy of about 3000 kJ/mol, a 4th ionization energy of about 6100 kJ/mol, and 5th of about 8300 kJ/mol. Which of the following is this element most likely to be?
W) Calcium
X) Gallium
Y) Silicon
Z) Astatine
ANSWER: X) Gallium
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at SLAC are studying neutrinos and their unique properties. Which of the following values is closest to the number of neutrinos that would be necessary to equal the mass of one electron?
W) 1
X) 100
Y) 1,000
Z) 1,000,000
ANSWER: Z) 1,000,000
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at SLAC are building instruments that can be used to detect when neutrinos collide with atoms. Name all of the following 3 fundamental forces that neutrinos use to interact with matter 1) strong interaction 2) weak interaction 3) electromagnetic force
ANSWER: 2 only
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Log base 3 of x equals log base 27 of 1728. What is x?
ANSWER: 12
9. BONUS Math Short Answer x is inversely proportional to the cube root of y. When x = 4, y = 8. What does x equal when y = 27?
ANSWER: 8/3
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three macromolecules that are insoluble in water 1) Carbohydrates 2) Nucleic acids 3) Lipids
ANSWER: 3 only
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer A car is attempting to scale up a hill with height 1 km; an unknown amount of friction is to be experienced. If the driver intends to not use the gas pedal while on the hill, what is the minimum velocity required of the car at the base of the hill to reach the top? Use g = 10m/s2 and give your answer to the nearest ten m/s.
ANSWER: 140
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the general name for any event that occurs during a syzygy of the Earth, Moon, and Sun?
ANSWER: ECLIPSE
11. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the lowest positive value of x such that x is congruent to 0 (mod 7) and x is congruent to 1 (mod 5)?
ANSWER: 21
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are studying hadronic physics. By name or number, identify all of the following three particles that are considered hadrons 1) electrons 2) neutrons 3) protons
ANSWER: 2 and 3
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What specific type of eclipse will occur when the Moon is between Earth and the Sun and is at or near its apogee?
ANSWER: ANNUAL ECLIPSE
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What specific type of solar event is known to have caused the Carrington Event, the largest geomagnetic storm on record, which disabled telegraph lines across the world in 1859?
ANSWER: CORONAL MASS EJECTION (also accept: CME)
13. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are studying the condensation of pions in the early universe. Which of the following groups of particles includes pions?
W) Fermions
X) Leptons
Y) Baryons
Z) Mesons
ANSWER: Z) Mesons
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Two masses moving with oppositely directed velocities crash into each other. After the crash, the total kinetic energy of the two-mass system remains the same. What is this type of collision known as?
ANSWER: ELASTIC COLLISION (also accept: PERFECTLY ELASTIC COLLISION)
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the boundary that was crossed by Voyager 1 in 2012 and is often considered to be the edge of our solar system?
ANSWER: HELIOPAUSE
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are studying enzyme engineering and its effects on ecosystems. What is the name for the theory which states that when enzymes bind to substrates, one or both will change shape to fit the other?
ANSWER: INDUCED FIT THEORY
15. BONUS Math Short Answer 7 people line up for lottery tickets. If the chance of winning the lottery for each person is 1/100. What is the probability that the first and last person in the line win the lottery, but the third person from the front doesn't?
ANSWER: 99/1000000
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a micronutrient essential to plants?
W) Mercury
X) Magnesium
Y) Iron
Z) Copper
ANSWER: W) Mercury
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the temperature at which an antiferromagnetic material loses its antiferromagnetic properties and becomes paramagnetic?
ANSWER: NEEL TEMPERATURE (also accept: NEEL POINT)
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following four quantities that are vector quantities 1) Weight 2) Mass 3) Momentum 4) Force
ANSWER: 1 and 3 and 4
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are using the DOE's Atmospheric Radiation Measurement Data Center, or ARM, to detect incoming solar radiation that could affect the productivity of ecosystems. What compound in the atmosphere protects Earth from incoming gamma radiation from stars and cosmic events?
ANSWER: OZONE (also accept: O3)
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following can exhibit quaternary structure?
W) Amino acids
X) Proteins
Y) Fatty acids
Z) DNA
ANSWER: X) Proteins
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of cells form the myelin sheath that wraps around axons in the peripheral nervous system?
ANSWER: SCHWANN CELLS
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three types of galactic classification for spiral galaxies from most to least tightly wound 1) Sb 2) Sa 3) Sc
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
20. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice What physical concept best explains that the points crossed by the wavefront of a light wave will oscillate with the same frequency as the source of the original wave, thus creating their own wavefronts?
W) Huygen's Principle
X) Snell's Law
Y) Kirchoff's First Law of Spectroscopy
Z) Kirchoff's Third Law of Spectroscopy
ANSWER: W) Huygen's Principle
21. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice If the voltage differential of a membrane is negative and the concentration of sodium ions inside the membrane is equal to the concentration of sodium ions outside the membrane, in what direction will sodium ions transport occur spontaneously?
W) Outside to inside of the membrane
X) Inside to outside of the membrane
Y) No sodium ion transport will occur spontaneously
Z) There is not enough information to determine the direction of sodium ion transport ANWER:
ANSWER: X) Inside to outside of the membrane
40. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Rickets is a disease caused by a deficiency of what vitamin?
ANSWER: VITAMIN D (also accept: CALCIFEROL; D)
41. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A pendulum is let go from an initial angular displacement of 45 degrees from the vertical. If the radius of the arc swept by the pendulum is L, which of the following best represents the period of the pendulum's complete motion?
W) 2π√(L/g)
X) π√(L/g)
Y) 2π√(g/L)
Z) None of the above
ANSWER: Z) None of the above
42. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many factors does 128 have?
ANSWER: 8
43. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Beth buys a lottery ticket. On the ticket, she has to choose a number between 1 and 20 inclusive, but only one of these numbers is the winning number. Each lottery ticket costs 6, and winning the lottery gives 100. What is the expected profit from each ticket bought?
ANSWER: -1
44. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What body is the most volcanically active object in the solar system?
W) Venus
X) Earth
Y) Io
Z) Callisto
ANSWER: Y) Io
45. BONUS Math Short Answer What are the points of intersection between the graphs of y = x2 and y = x + 6?
ANSWER: (3, 9) AND (-2, 4) (also accept: (-2, 4) AND (3, 9))
46. BONUS Biology Short Answer Preceded by the 5' UTR, the start codon in the domains Eukarya and Archaea codes for what amino acid?
ANSWER: METHIONINE
