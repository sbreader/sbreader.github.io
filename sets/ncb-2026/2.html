1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following 4 bases by increasing basicity 1) F- 2) Cl- 3) Br- 4) I-
ANSWER: 4,3,2,1
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice For which of the following molecular geometries, could the central atom not be dsp3 hybridized?
W) Linear
X) Seesaw
Y) Trigonal Bipyramidal
Z) Octahedral
ANSWER: Z) Octahedral
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What idea is a key concept in chemical kinetics where it is assumed that the concentration of a reaction intermediate stays constant over time because due to it being consumed as quickly as it's formed?
ANSWER: Steady State Approximation
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A 1 mole sample of a hydrocarbon releases 88 grams of carbon dioxide when burned. To the nearest integer, how many grams of carbon are in the 1 mole sample?
ANSWER: 24
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the 3 following reactions that will lead to a racemic mixture 1. E1 2. SN1 3. SN2
ANSWER: 2 only
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In the electrolysis of water, what is the ratio of hydrogen gas formed to the oxygen gas formed?
W) 1 to 1
X) 1 to 2
Y) 2 to 1
Z) 1 to 4
ANSWER: Y) 2 to 1
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice How many sigma and pi bonds respectively are present in one molecule of cyanogen, with formula C2N2?
W) 2,4
X) 3,2
Y) 4,2
Z) 3,4
ANSWER: Z) 3,4
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 hybridizations that may result in a linear geometry 1. dsp3 2. sp3 3. sp
ANSWER: 1,3
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following 3 cubic structures by increasing packing efficiency 1. Face Centered Cubic 2. Simple Cubic 3. Body centered cubic
ANSWER: 2,3,1
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following catalysts would be best suited to aid hydrogenation of alkenes?
W) Collins' reagent
X) Palladium on Carbon
Y) DMSO
Z) Copper hydride
ANSWER: X) Palladium on Carbon
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the oxidation number of oxygen in HOF?
ANSWER: 0
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following 3 compounds by increasing bond length 1) O2+ 2) O2- 3) O2
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following salts will produce a colorless aqueous solution when dissolved in water?
W) Copper (II) Sulfate
X) Potassium Permanganate
Y) Iron (III) Chloride
Z) Sodium Chloride
ANSWER: Z) Sodium Chloride
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Acetone reacts with Iodine in acid catalyzed conditions to replace the alpha proton of Acetone with iodine. This reaction has rate law: k[Acetone][H+] . If the concentration of Acetone is halved, and the pH decreases by 1, by what factor does the rate of the reaction increase?
ANSWER: 5
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the double bond equivalency of the compound with formula C4H6N2?
ANSWER: 3
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds will be significantly more soluble at a lower pH?
W) Mercury (I) Iodide
X) Calcium Fluoride
Y) Silver Chloride
Z) Lead (II) Bromide
ANSWER: X) Calcium Fluoride
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Aryan is performing a titration where 0.1 M Sodium Hydroxide is the titrant and he is determining the concentration of a solution of acetic acid. Which of the following accurately describes the pH of the equivalence point?
W) the pH will be lower than 7
X) the pH will be exactly 7
Y) the pH will be greater than 7
Z) the pH cannot be determined
ANSWER: Y) the pH will be greater than 7
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following reactions will not result in the formation of a precipitate?
W) Barium Nitrate and Sodium Sulfate
X) Copper (II) Nitrate and Potassium Iodide
Y) Lead (II) Nitrate and Sulfuric Acid
Z) Sodium Carbonate and Hydrochloric acid
ANSWER: Z) Sodium Carbonate and Hydrochloric acid
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following types of radiation has the greatest penetrating power?
W) Alpha
X) Beta
Y) Gamma
Z) Neutron
ANSWER: Y) Gamma
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following describes directing effects and reactivity of halogenated benzene rings?
W) ortho-para directors, ring activators
X) ortho-para directors, ring deactivators
Y) meta directors, ring activators
Z) meta directors, ring deactivators
ANSWER: X) ortho-para directors, ring deactivators
