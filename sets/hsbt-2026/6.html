1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Travis is sitting on his porch, drinking lemonade, and notices the appearance of cirrus clouds in the distance. The next day, he wakes up to lowered stratocumulus and stratus cloud layers, and later in the afternoon it begins to rain. What type of front has Travis just experienced?
ANSWER: Warm front
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following stages of stellar evolution would be characterized by thermal pulses alongside helium and hydrogen fusion in concentric shells surrounding a degenerate carbon-oxygen core?

Z) Horizontal branch

3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Through his synthesis experiments, Haru has now formed formic acid, which has a lower pKa than acetic acid. Which of the following best explains why formic acid has a lower pKa than acetic acid?
