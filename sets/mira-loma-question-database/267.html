1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What two hypothetical supercontinents split from Pangaea?
ANSWER: LAURASIA, GONDWANA(LAND)
1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer List the seven most common and basic stellar classes from hottest to coolest.
ANSWER: O, B, A, F, G, K, M
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer List the five dwarf planets in any order.
ANSWER: CERES, PLUTO, MAKEMAKE, HAUMEA, ERIS
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What was the name of the first lander to return images of another planet's surface?
ANSWER: VENERA 9
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer New Horizons is an unmanned NASA spacecraft designed to flyby which solar object in 2015?
ANSWER: PLUTO
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the three satellites of Pluto in any order.
ANSWER: CHARON, HYDRA, NIX
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer How many hectopascals are there in a decibar?
ANSWER: 100 HECTOPASCALS
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer William Herschel is notable for having discovered which planet in 1781?
ANSWER: URANUS
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which Shakesperian play are the moons of Uranus named after?
ANSWER: A MIDSUMMER NIGHT'S DREAM
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer If Ceres is 271.2 million miles away from the sun, how many AUs away from the sun is it? Round your answer to 1 decimal place.
ANSWER: 2.9 AUS
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term used for the step in the origins of a solar system that forms protoplanets from planetesimals?
ANSWER: ACCRETION
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following 4 bodies would have a higher than average (0. 5) albedo? 1: Venus 2: Mars 3: Pluto 4: Jupiter
ANSWER: "1, 3, and 4" (also accept: "Venus, Pluto and Jupiter")
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer If a person is 16 on Earth, approximately how old would they be on Mercury? Round your answer to the nearest ten years.
ANSWER: 70 MERCURIAN YEARS (also accept: 66 MERCURIAN YEARS)
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which 2 of the following 3 constellations can be seen from the Northern Hemisphere sky in November through December?1: Aquarius 2: Scorpius 3: Orion
ANSWER: "1 and 3" (also accept: "Aquarius and Orion")
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the most unique feature of Neptune's moon, Triton?
ANSWER: (ACTIVE) (LIQUID NITROGEN) GEYSERS (also accept: CRYOVOLCANOES)
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Enceladus is the moon of which planet?
ANSWER: SATURN
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which Soviet spacecraft was the first man-made object to hit the moon?
ANSWER: LUNA 2
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the meaning of the initialism "SETI"?
ANSWER: (THE) SEARCH FOR EXTRA-TERRESTRIAL INTELLIGENCE
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of star was the first discovered extrasolar planet orbiting?
W) Quasar
X) Supergiant
Y) Pulsar

ANSWER: 3 × 1011 (also accept: 300 billion; 300000000000)
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In reality, what is the "Evening Star" that is sometimes visible in the west after sunset?
ANSWER: VENUS
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for a very short-lived sunspot?
ANSWER: PORE
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer List any two of the first five asteroids to be discovered.
ANSWER: CERES, PALLAS, JUNO, VESTA (also accept: ASTRAEA)
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the brightest star in the sky as seen from Earth?
ANSWER: SIRIUS
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What planet is the "Mariner Valley" on?
ANSWER: MARS
