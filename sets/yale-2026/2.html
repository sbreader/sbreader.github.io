1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not an assumption of Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium?
W) Gene flow between populations
X) Random mating
Y) Very large population size
Z) No natural selection
ANSWER: W) Gene flow between populations
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer The New Haven gray seal population exhibits two whisker phenotypes: white and gray. White whiskers are dominant to gray whiskers. In a population of 1,000 seals, 40 individuals have gray whiskers. Assuming Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, what is the frequency of the allele for white whiskers?
ANSWER: 0.8 (also accept: 80%; 4/5)

W) Stoichiometric point
X) Triple point
Y) Critical point
Z) Fracture point

8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a valid assumption of the Kinetic Molecular Theory of ideal gases?

X) The average kinetic energy of a gas is proportional to its absolute temperature
Y) The collision between gas particles is always elastic, conserving energy
Z) Individual gas particles don't exert any forces on the walls of the container
