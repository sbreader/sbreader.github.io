1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at the National Renewable Energy Lab have computationally screened hundreds of new nitride compounds in the hope of synthesizing them in the lab. They focused on compounds that are theoretically unstable but last long enough to be stable for practical purposes. What is the term for such compounds?
ANSWER: METASTABLE
1. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab have designed a particular implementation of a controlled-NOT, or CNOT, gate to enable future work in quantum information processing. In general, if the TARGET qubit is ket 1, which of the following is a transformation that can be performed by the CNOT gate?
W) If the CONTROL qubit is ket 0, the target qubit will remain ket 1
X) If the CONTROL qubit is ket 0, the target qubit will become ket 0
Y) It will always remain ket 1
Z) It will always become ket 0
ANSWER: W) If the CONTROL qubit is ket 0, the target qubit will remain ket 1
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In a rotating frame of reference, what fictitious force is proportional to the cross product of the frame’s angular velocity with the velocity of an object?
ANSWER: CORIOLIS
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer Alice, who has a mass of 50 kilograms, runs with a speed of three meters per second and jumps onto a stationary skateboard with mass two kilograms. In meters per second, what is Alice's speed after jumping on the skateboard?
ANSWER: 75/26 (also accept: 2 23/26)
3. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice If f of x equals the square root of (x + 6) and g of x equals the square root of (x – 6), which of following intervals represents the domain of the function f of x divided by g of x?
W) x greater than –6
X) x greater than or equal to –6
Y) x greater than 6
Z) x greater than or equal to 6
ANSWER: Y) x greater than 6
3. BONUS Math Short Answer Starting with a cube, one dimension is increased by 4, another is decreased by 4, and the other is left unchanged. If the new solid has a volume 112 less than that of the original cube, what was the volume of the original cube?
ANSWER: 343
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Reptiles, birds, and mammals all develop from what type of egg with four specialized extra-embryonic membranes?
ANSWER: AMNIOTIC
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice HIV primarily targets CD4-positive lymphocytes. Which of the following best describes the normal function of these cells?
W) Receive presented antigens and stimulate other cells
X) Send signals to infected cells to apoptose
Y) Phagocytose pathogens and debris
Z) Produce antibodies
ANSWER: W) Receive presented antigens and stimulate other cells
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What law states that you can add the enthalpy changes associated with component reactions to determine the enthalpy change of a multi-step reaction?
ANSWER: HESS’S LAW
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Place the following four elements in order of increasing electron affinity 1) Sodium 2) Gold 3) Oxygen 4) Fluorine.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2, 4
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The uppermost, white clouds on Neptune are generally composed of ices of what molecule?
ANSWER: METHANE (also accept: CH4)
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three descriptions of chondrites that are true 1) They contain silicate minerals 2) They formed very early in the solar system 3) They often have an iron core.
ANSWER: 1, 2
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Scientists at the Joint Genome Institute are studying giant viruses like Pandoravirus. How many base pairs are in the genome of Pandoravirus?
W) 2.5 billion
X) 2.5 million
Y) 25,000
Z) 2,500
ANSWER: X) 2.5 million
7. BONUS Energy Short Answer Scientists at Idaho National Lab are developing molten salt reactors that use chloride salts as coolant. Identify all of the following three reasons to use salts in a nuclear reactor 1) High melting point 2) Low corrosivity 3) High electrical conductivity.
ANSWER: 1
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If f of x equals e to the power (4x), what is f triple prime of zero?
ANSWER: 64
8. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the 12th power of the complex number 1 – i?
ANSWER: –64 (also accept: –64 + 0i)
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following describes the stability of an air parcel when the environmental lapse rate is between the moist and dry adiabatic lapse rates?
W) Statically stable
X) Conditionally unstable
Y) Absolutely unstable
Z) Absolutely stable
ANSWER: X) Conditionally unstable
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In the human brain, what structure controls the secretions of the pituitary gland?
ANSWER: HYPOTHALAMUS
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following three organs in a bird in the order through which food will pass 1) Proventriculus 2) Crop 3) Gizzard.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Consider an ideal electric circuit consisting of a capacitor and an inductor. Which of the following types of oscillators would best describe its behavior?
W) Simple harmonic
X) Underdamped harmonic
Y) Overdamped harmonic
Z) Critically damped harmonic
ANSWER: W) Simple harmonic
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer A beam of light emerges from a clear liquid with index of refraction of 1.7 into a vacuum. If the incident angle is 30 degrees, then to the nearest multiple of ten degrees, what will be its angle of refraction?
ANSWER: 60
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A student is using mass spectrometry to identify an unknown sample of rock. Which of the following does mass spectrometry not directly quantify?

X) Isotope abundance
Y) Polarity of the material
