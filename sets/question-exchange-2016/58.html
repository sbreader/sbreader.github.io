Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which phylum of fungi is characterized by bearing spores in groups of four on the basidium?
ANSWER: BASIDIOMYCOTA
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Radiolarians have a shell composed of what compound?
ANSWER: SiO2 (also accept: SILICA)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the main product resulting from the bromination of benzenesulfonic acid?
ANSWER: M-BROMOBENZENESULFONIC ACID (also accept: 3-BROMOBENZENESULFONIC ACID)
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice An unknown powder is added to a solution containing silver nitrate. As a result, a white precipitate is formed. Which of the following substances is most likely to be the nature of the precipitate?
W) AgBr
X) NaNO3
Y) AgCl
Z) AgI
ANSWER: Y) AgCl
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the nuclear binding energy of hydrogen, in electron volts?
ANSWER: 0
6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice In nuclear physics, the collective model of the nucleus is most accurately described by which of the following?
W) Nucleons are spheres packed together in a central nucleus
X) Nucleons are imagined as particles in a fluid which interact strongly and collide with each other frequently
Y) The nucleus exists as a soup of freely interacting quarks, which can be described by statistical mechanics
Z) When the number of nucleons in the nucleus reaches a certain threshold, it is possible for the nucleus to undergo fission
ANSWER: X) Nucleons are imagined as particles in a fluid which interact strongly and collide with each other frequently
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which element makes up the majority of the sun’s visible absorption lines?
ANSWER: IRON
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following characterizes an inferior conjunction of an inferior planet?
W) Planet grows brighter and exhibits prograde motion
X) Planet grows dimmer and exhibits prograde motion
Y) Planet grows brighter and exhibits retrograde motion
Z) Planet grows dimmer and exhibits retrograde motion
ANSWER: Z) Planet grows dimmer and exhibits retrograde motion
9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following 2 dimensional shapes on a coordinate plane cannot be completely described with 3 parameters?
W) Upwards facing parabola
X) Circle
Y) Rotated parabola
Z) Vector in standard position
ANSWER: Y) Rotated parabola
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Compute the sum from n = 1 to infinity of [1/n-1/(n+1)]
ANSWER: 1
11. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer By name or number, order the following three countries in increasing order of oil imports in 2014: China, Canada, Japan.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1 (also accept: CANADA, JAPAN, CHINA)
