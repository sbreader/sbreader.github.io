Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What was the first virus to be discovered and studied?
ANSWER: TOBACCO MOSAIC VIRUS
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which Dutch botanist is credited with originally developing the concept of a virus?
ANSWER: MARTINUS BEIJERINCK
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the protein shell enclosing the viral genome?
ANSWER: CAPSID
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Capsids are built from a large number of protein subunits called what?
ANSWER: CAPSOMERES
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which group of viruses consists of eight different RNA molecules in a helical capsid containing 2 different types of glycoprotein spikes?
ANSWER: INFLUENZA VIRUSES
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Each type of virus can infect cells of a limited variety of hosts, called what?
ANSWER: HOST RANGE
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer When phage DNA enters a bacterium, specific sequences are recognized and spliced by enzymes known as what?
ANSWER: RESTRICTION ENZYMES
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The lytic cycle refers to replication by destroying the host cell. What is the term for the cycle in which replication occurs without destroying the host cell?
ANSWER: LYSOGENIC CYCLE
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which Greek letter refers to a temperate phage that resembles T4, but its tail has only one, short fiber?
ANSWER: LAMBDA
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following family of viruses is not correctly paired with an example?
W) Reovirus; rotavirus
X) Flavivirus; yellow fever virus
Y) Rhabdovirus; rabies virus
Z) Paramyxovirus; influenza virus
ANSWER: Z) Paramyxovirus; influenza virus
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following families of viruses is not correctly matched with its type of genetic material?
W) Poxvirus; dsDNA
X) Coronavirus; ssRNA
Y) Orthomyxovirus; ssRNA
Z) Retrovirus; dsRNA
ANSWER: Z) Retrovirus; dsRNA
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for small circular DNA molecules that replicate separately from the bacterial chromosome?
ANSWER: PLASMIDS
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for DNA molecules formed when segments from two different sources are combined in vitro?
ANSWER: RECOMBINANT DNA
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Enzyme is to substrate as restriction enzyme is to...
ANSWER: RESTRICTION SITE
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Bacterial DNA is protected from restriction enzymes by the addition of what groups to adenines or cytosines?
ANSWER: METHYL GROUP
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the single-stranded region of DNA where a restriction enzyme has cut the DNA?
ANSWER: STICKY END
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which enzyme causes temporary base-pair associations in the sticky ends?
ANSWER: DNA LIGASE
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for a DNA molecule that can carry foreign DNA into a host cell and replicate there?
ANSWER: CLONING VECTOR
10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following are widely used as cloning vectors?
W) Bacterial plasmids
X) RNA transcript
Y) Single-stranded DNA
Z) Methylated DNA
ANSWER: W) Bacterial plasmids
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What does the acronym BAC stand for?
ANSWER: BACTERIAL ARTIFICIAL CHROMOSOME
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Double-stranded DNA synthesized by reverse transcription of mRNA and synthesis of a complementary strand by DNA polymerase is called what?
ANSWER: COMPLEMENTARY DNA (CDNA)
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What was the first bacterial species to have its genome completely sequenced?
ANSWER: HEMOPHILUS INFLUENZAE
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the large-scale study of the structure and function of proteins?
ANSWER: PROTEOMICS
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms has the greatest number of base pairs?
W) Escherichia coli
X) Archeoglobus fulgidus
Y) Arabadopsis thaliana
Z) Caenorhabditis elegans
ANSWER: Y) Arabadopsis thaliana
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms has the greatest number of genes per megabase?
W) Homo sapiens
X) Drosophila melanogaster
Y) Mus musculus
Z) Danio rerio
ANSWER: X) Drosophila melanogaster
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for former genes that have accumulated mutations over a long time and have become nonfunctional?
ANSWER: PSEUDOGENES
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Repetitive DNA is found in...
W) Introns
X) Exons
Y) Heterochromatin
Z) Euchromatin
ANSWER: Y) Heterochromatin
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which American scientist performed experiments on corn and eventually postulated the existence of transposable elements?
ANSWER: BARBARA MCCLINTOCK
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of transposable elements move by means of an RNA intermediate that is the transcript of the retrotransposon DNA?
ANSWER: RETROTRANSPOSONS
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What does the acronym STR stand for?
ANSWER: SHORT TANDEM REPEAT
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of transposable element-related DNA are about 300 nucleotides long, and although are transcribed into RNA, do not code for any protein?
ANSWER: ALU ELEMENTS
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which Greek philosopher concluded that species can be arranged on a scale of increasing complexity which he called the scala naturae?
ANSWER: ARISTOTLE
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Complete the analogy: Curvier is to catastrophism as Hutton is to...
ANSWER: UNIFORMITARIANISM
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which British scholar published an essay concerning the limitations of population growth by famine, disease, and other factors?
ANSWER: THOMAS MALTHUS
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which British scientist proposed a theory of evolution independently of Darwin and published his hypothesis before him?
ANSWER: ALFRED WALLACE
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Complete the analogy: Deeper strata is to older fossils as shallower strata is to...
ANSWER: YOUNGER FOSSILS
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which British geologist wrote Principles of Geology, popularized the theory of uniforitism, and was a close friend of Darwin?
ANSWER: CHARLES LYELL
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which phrase was used by Darwin in The Origin of Species as a replacement for the word "evolution" and describes evolution as mutations that occur in a species over many generations?
ANSWER: DESCENT WITH MODIFICATION
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the process by which humans modify organisms over several generations by selecting and breeding individuals with desired traits?
ANSWER: ARTIFICIAL SELECTION
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the average percent of loci that are heterozygous?
ANSWER: AVERAGE HETEROZYGOSITY
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the differences in the genetic composition of separate populations?
ANSWER: GEOGRAPHIC VARIATION
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer If an individual contains 100 genes, 30% of which contain recessive alleles and 80% of which contain dominant alleles, what is the average heterozygosity of the individual?
ANSWER: 10%
22. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following have the lowest mutation rates?
W) Prokaryotes
X) Fungi
Y) Plants
Z) Animals
ANSWER: W) Prokaryotes
23. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a mechanism that contributes to the shuffling of alleles?
W) Independent assortment of chromosomes
X) Mitosis
Y) Crossing over
Z) Fertilization
ANSWER: X) Mitosis
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for a group of individuals of the same species that live in the same area and interbreed?
ANSWER: POPULATION
24. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Over the course of several generations, what happens to the frequency of a recessive allele in a species?
W) It decreases
X) It remains constant
Y) It increases
Z) It disappears
ANSWER: X) It remains constant
24. BONUS Biology Short Answer Suppose 60% of rabbits carry the dominant allele for fur color and 40% of rabbits carry the recessive allele for fur color. Assuming random mating, how many heterozygous, homozygous dominant, and homozygous recessive rabbits are expected in the next generation?
ANSWER: 48% HETEROZYGOUS, 36% HOMOZYGOUS DOMINANT, 16% HOMOZYGOUS RECESSIVE
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the change in the relative frequency in which a gene variant occurs in a population due to random sampling and chance?
ANSWER: GENETIC DRIFT
25. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of selection is not correctly matched with its definition?
W) Directional selection; favoring variants at one extreme
X) Disruptive selection; favoring variants at both extremes
Y) Stabilizing selection; removing extreme variants from population
Z) Sexual selection; favoring variants of one sex
ANSWER: Z) Sexual selection; favoring variants of one sex
26. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following terms is not correctly matched with its definition?
W) Balancing selection; maintenance of two or more forms in a population
X) Heterozygote advantage; heterozygotes have greater fitness than homozygotes
Y) Frequency-dependent selection; high frequency of a trait favors that trait
Z) Neutral variation; change in genotype with no change in phenotype
ANSWER: Y) Frequency-dependent selection; high frequency of a trait favors that trait
26. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the condition in which individuals of one sex are choosy in selecting their mates from the other sex?
ANSWER: INTERSEXUAL SELECTION
27. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Categorize each of the following as prezygotic barriers or postzygotic barriers; reduced fertility, temporal isolation, mechanical isolation, gametic isolation, hybrid breakdown.
ANSWER: POSTZYGOTIC, PREZYGOTIC, PREZYGOTIC, PREZYGOTIC, POSTZYGOTIC
27. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the broad pattern of evolution over long time spans?
ANSWER: MACROEVOLUTION
28. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not one of the definitions of species?
W) Morphological species concept
X) Ecological species concept
Y) Phylogenetic species concept
Z) Behavioral species concept
ANSWER: Z) Behavioral species concept
28. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the condition in which gene flow is interrupted when a population is divided into geographically isolated subpopulations?
ANSWER: ALLOPATRIC SPECIATION
29. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the two forms of polyploidy?
ANSWER: AUTOPOLYPLOIDY AND ALLOPOLYPLOIDY
29. BONUS Biology Short Answer Polyploidy in the cells of a plant's roots is an example of what type of polyploidy?
ANSWER: AUTOPOLYPLOIDY
30. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for a region in which members of different species meet and mate, producing at least some offspring of mixed ancestry?
ANSWER: HYBRID ZONE
30. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following could not be an effect of the interaction of species in a hybrid zone?
W) Gene flow between species
X) Divergence of a population
Y) Halting of evolution
Z) Re-establishment of gene flow after divergence of a population
ANSWER: Y) Halting of evolution
