1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A solid metal sphere has a positive net charge. How is the charge distributed on the sphere?
W) It is concentrated at the center of the sphere.
X) It is uniformly distributed throughout the sphere.
Y) It is uniformly distributed on the surface of the sphere.
Z) It depends on how the sphere was charged.
ANSWER: Y) It is uniformly distributed on the surface of the sphere.

9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In which of the following circuits would a lightbulb with resistance 10 ohms have the greatest brightness at time t = 0 if the circuit always has one battery with voltage V = 10 volts?

X) A circuit with two resistors in parallel each with resistance 5 ohms and lightbulb in series with one of the resistors
