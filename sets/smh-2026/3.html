1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Down syndrome, also known as trisomy 21, results from what type of error during meiosis?
ANSWER: NONDISJUNCTION
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice At which of the following cell embryonic stages would you expect to find cells with the greatest surface area-to-volume ratio?
W) Fertilized egg
X) Four-cell stage
Y) Blastula
Z) Gastrula
ANSWER: Y) Blastula
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer When Serena uses a microscope to look at pollen grains suspended in water, she sees tiny particles moving in a rapid and jittery pattern. What term is given to this random motion of particles in a medium, caused by molecular bombardment?
ANSWER: Brownian motion
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer According to Wien's law, the peak wavelength of an ideal blackbody is proportional to temperature raised to what power?
ANSWER: -1
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Anti-abundant numbers are numbers in which the sum of their factors are less than 50% that of the original number. What is the largest 2-digit anti-abundant number?
ANSWER: 97
3. BONUS Math Short Answer How many divisors does 7007 have?
ANSWER: 12
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The overall shape of proteins and the bonds between nucleotide pairs in DNA are from what specific type of intermolecular interactions?
ANSWER: Hydrogen bonds
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice A reaction favors the products under standard conditions. Assuming delta H is positive, which of the following best describes the signs of delta G and delta S, respectively?
W) Positive, positive
X) Positive, negative
Y) Negative, positive
Z) Negative, negative
ANSWER: Y) Negative, positive
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What ocean current serves as the southern boundary current for the Indian Ocean, South Atlantic, and South Pacific Subtropical gyres?
ANSWER: ACC (also accept: Antarctic Circumpolar Current)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following that are advantages of reflecting telescopes when compared to refracting telescopes. 1) No spherical aberration 2) no chromatic aberration 3) mirrors can be supported from the back
ANSWER: 2, 3
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer While stacking his signature scallion pancakes, Ken realizes they resemble the stacks of thylakoids within chloroplasts. What is the name for these stacks?
ANSWER: GRANA
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Despite the fact that oxygen’s partial pressure is much lower at high altitudes, oxygen unloading in tissues remains relatively unaffected. This is because erythrocytes begin rapid production of which molecule known to bind hemoglobin and reduce its affinity for oxygen?
ANSWER: 2,3 BPG (also accept: 2,3-DPG; 2,3-bisphosphoglycerate)
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Charles, who weighs 40 kilograms, is moving at a speed of 4 meters per second. He then collides perfectly elastically with Nathaniel, who is at rest and weighs 10 kilograms. In meters per second, what is Charles' final speed?
ANSWER: 2.4
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the coefficient of linear expansion for a rod with length 10 meters at 300 Kelvins and length 10.5 meters at 400 Kelvins? Put your answer in scientific notation and to one significant figure.
ANSWER: 0.0005 (also accept: 5 × 10-4)
8. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following functions has at least 1 imaginary root?
W) y = -1
X) y = x-1
Y) y = x2-1
Z) y = x3-1
ANSWER: Z) y = x3-1
8. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the equation of the line tangent to the function y = x2 + 2x-3 at x = 1.
ANSWER: y = 4x-4
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The pKa of formic acid is approximately 3.75. If a buffer solution of pH 5.75 is created using formic acid and formate, then which of the following is the closest to the ratio of the molarities of formate to formic acid in the solution?
W) 10
X) 100
Y) 1000
Z) 10000
ANSWER: X) 100
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Select all of the following three changes that would increase the rate constant of a reaction 1) Increasing the temperature 2) Increasing the concentration of the reactants 3) Adding a catalyst to the reaction
ANSWER: 1, 3
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three remnant plates of the farallon plate by increasing proximity to the North Pole. 1) Juan de Fuca 2) Nazca 3) Cocos
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Iron meteorites are often composed of a single large metal crystal. What is the term for large crystalline patterns on the cross section of iron meteorites?
ANSWER: Widmanstatten patterns
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer By name or number, organize the following 3 materials by increasing thermal conductivity 1) Air 2) Copper 3) Steel
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
11. BONUS Energy Short Answer By name or number, arrange the following three types of coal by increasing carbon content 1) Bituminous 2) Anthracite 3) Lignite
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Crucial to both the middle lamella and the cortical reaction, which divalent ion may be found at high concentrations within the smooth endoplasmic reticulum?
ANSWER: Ca2+ (also accept: CALCIUM)
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Plants in which of the following Gymnosperm phyla would be most suited to desert conditions?
W) Gnetophyta
X) Ginkophyta
Y) Cycadophyta
Z) Coniferophyta
ANSWER: W) Gnetophyta
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What type of heat transfer does not require a physical medium between the heat source and the heated object, and dominates heat from stars?
ANSWER: Radiation
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer Nathaniel launches Charles out of a cannon at 5 m/s. If the cannon is aimed 30 degrees above the horizontal, how far is he launched, assuming flat ground? Assume g = 10 meters per second squared
ANSWER: 5√3/4
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A rhombus has perimeter 24. What is the maximum possible area of this rhombus?
ANSWER: 36
14. BONUS Math Short Answer In triangle ABC, angle A = 40 degrees and angle B = 70 degrees. Point D lies on AC, and BD = BC. In degrees, what is the angle measure of angle DBA?
ANSWER: 30
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the oxidation state of cadmium in cadmium chloride?
ANSWER: 2+

ANSWER: 1, 2, 3, 4

W) Ephemeral
X) Intermittent
Y) Perennial
Z) Exotic
ANSWER: X) Intermittent

17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer While the sensation of sour and salty foods relies on ion channels, what general class of receptor do all other types of taste rely on?
ANSWER: G-PROTEIN COUPLED RECEPTOR (also accept: METABOTROPIC)
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer What Krebs cycle intermediate does acetyl-CoA combine with to form citrate?
ANSWER: OXALOACETATE

If Nathaniel pushes Harold and Zachary to the right with a force of 20 Newtons, what is the force exerted on Harold by Zachary?
ANSWER: 4
