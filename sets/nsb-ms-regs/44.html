1. BONUS Physics Short Answer What derived SI unit is used to measure electric potential?
ANSWER: VOLT
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is true of virus genomes?
W) They are always composed of DNA
X) They are always circular
Y) They may be composed of either DNA or RNA
Z) They are diploid
ANSWER: Y) They may be composed of either DNA or RNA
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice You are using a light microscope to look at cells stained with a dye that detects chromatin and can see densely-colored regions that you recognize as chromosomes. Which of the following must be true?
W) The cell is undergoing mitosis
X) The genome is diploid
Y) The genome is haploid
Z) The cell is in interphase
ANSWER: W) The cell is undergoing mitosis
