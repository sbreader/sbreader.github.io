1. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Argonne National Laboratory are studying qubits and quantum computing. In order for a quantum computer to function, its qubits must be protected from noise in order to maintain superposition and well-defined phase relations. Y) Measureable

4. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice In a real co-axial cable, the magnetic field can still sometimes be non-zero on the outside, even when the wire is perfectly assembled and the currents flowing in opposite directions have equal magnitudes.
