1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Liam is trying to signal to a cell through a transduction pathway. Which of the following could increase the efficiency of his signal transduction?
W) Peripheral protein
X) Scaffolding protein
Y) Integral protein
Z) Nuclear protein
ANSWER: X) Scaffolding protein
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Ehlers-Dahlos syndrome is a result of the insufficiency of what extremely common protein?
ANSWER: Collagen
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Lucas is attempting to determine whether a reaction will proceed spontaneously. Which of the following entropy-enthalpy pairs will result in a spontaneous reaction?
W) Positive entropy, positive enthalpy
X) Positive entropy, negative enthalpy
Y) Negative entropy, positive enthalpy
Z) Negative entropy, negative enthalpy
ANSWER: X) Positive entropy, negative enthalpy
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or chemical formula, identify the hydrocarbon which is 82% carbon and 18% hydrogen by mass.
ANSWER: C3H8 (also accept: propane)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Two tuning forks have frequencies of 450 Hertz and 400 Hertz. In Hertz, what is their beat frequency?
ANSWER: 50
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Dylan fires a large amount of photons in a vacuum. What is the average value to two significant figures, in SI units, for the frequency multiplied by the wavelength of each of the photons?
ANSWER: 3.0 × 108
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Alvarez Hypothesis was validated when the Chicxulub impact crater was discovered, providing evidence for an impact which caused a mass extinction. Which of the following mass extinctions killed off the greatest percentage of species?
W) Late Ordovician
X) Permian-Triassic
Y) Triassic-Jurassic
Z) Cretaceous-Paleogene
ANSWER: X) Permian-Triassic
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for frost formed on cold objects by the rapid freezing of water vapor in clouds or fog?
ANSWER: Rime (also accept: Rime Ice)
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Solve the expression 172-92.
ANSWER: 208
5. BONUS Math Short Answer A triangle has sides of length 4, 6, 8. What is its area?
ANSWER: √135
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following molecules in the chronological time they affect a bolus of food. 1. Pepsin 2. Salivary amylase 3. Trypsin
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following four gases is more readily bound to by red blood cells compared to diatomic oxygen gas?
W) Nitrogen
X) Argon
Y) Sulfur
Z) Carbon monoxide
ANSWER: Z) Carbon monoxide
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A scientist has a sample of propanol, and wants to turn it into propanal. What is the name of the process by which primary alcohols such as propanol and ethanol are converted into aldehydes?
W) Controlled oxidation
X) Uncontrolled oxidation
Y) Controlled reduction
Z) Uncontrolled reduction
ANSWER: W) Controlled oxidation
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, arrange the following four ions in order of increasing polarizability. 1. F- 2. O2- 3. Cl- 4. S2-
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3, 4
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Austin ties a rope to two walls such that there is some slack, causing the rope to droop in the middle. What is the name of the shape that the rope makes?
W) Parabola
X) Sinusoid
Y) Brachistochrone
Z) Catenary
ANSWER: Z) Catenary
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer What type of mirror counters the effects of spherical aberration?
ANSWER: Parabolic
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the steepest angle at which a granular material can be piled on a slope without slumping called?
ANSWER: Angle of Repose
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Deep in the ice in Antarctica, there is a detector for what "ghost" particle which is often used to locate the direction of a violent cosmic event, such as a supernova?
ANSWER: Neutrino
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What theorem makes it easy to find the roots and powers of a complex number by converting it into its polar form?
ANSWER: De Moivre’s Theorem (also accept: De Moivre’s law)
10. BONUS Math Short Answer By name or number, rank the following 3 sets by increasing cardinality. 1. The integer factors of the numbers 83, 160 2. All real numbers 3. All integers less than 60
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Naman is negging in science bowl practice, when he suddenly gets jumpscared by a harmless hoverfly, which looks like a stinging wasp. This is an example of which of the following types of mimicry, where a harmless species mimics another harmful species for protection?
W) Mullerian mimicry
X) Batesian mimicry
Y) Aggressive mimicry
Z) Automimicry
ANSWER: X) Batesian mimicry
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following phyla is composed entirely of pseudocoelomates?
W) Acanthocephala
X) Platyhelminthes
Y) Annelida
Z) Nematoda
ANSWER: Z) Nematoda
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Naman is making a molecule. Right now, his molecule has a trigonal planar electron geometry. How many lone pairs should he add to the central atom to make the electron geometry trigonal pyramidal?
ANSWER: 1
12. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Lucas is a hydrogen atom with exactly one electron. Meanwhile, Liam is observing Lucas. Which of the following sets of quantum numbers could Liam NOT assign to Lucas's electron?
W) n = 1, l = 0, ml = 0
X) n = 10, l = 0, ml = 0
Y) n = 1, l = 1, ml = 1
Z) n = 10, l = 1, ml = 1
ANSWER: Y) n = 1, l = 1, ml = 1
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Rank the following particles by increasing spin 1. Graviton 2. Higgs boson 3. Photon
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer Which experiment used dual slits and their resulting interference patterns to demonstrate that light had wave properties?
ANSWER: Young’s Double-Slit Experiment

X) Mesoderm

W) Chromatography
X) Light microscopy
Y) Spectroscopy

17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify which of the following are true regarding semiconductors. 1. They have conductivities greater than insulators but lower than conductors 2. As temperature increases, their conductivity increases 3. Doping a semiconductor with carbon creates a p-type semiconductor
ANSWER: 1, 2
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In structural engineering, what is the term for forces that act parallel to a cross-section, often being contrasted with normal forces?
ANSWER: Shear forces
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer Stefan is evil and wants to run an experiment on his dog. He flips a coin but doesn't look at which face landed up. If it lands heads, he will feed his dog, but if it lands tails, he will not feed his dog, making his dog go hungry. Prior to observation of the coin, Stefan knows that it could be either heads or tails, and thus considers the current state of the coin to be a combination of both heads and tails. What is the term for this treatment of a system, which denotes its pre-measurement state with a linear combination of all observable states?
ANSWER: Quantum superposition

W) Horn
X) Cirque
Y) Arête
Z) Drumlins

ANSWER: 1
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A Fibonacci sequence as the first two numbers being 1 and 3. What is the 6th number in this sequence?
ANSWER: 18
20. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Sophie wants to graph the function y = x!. However, this function only exists for whole numbers. What function should she use to make this function continuous among the positive real numbers?
W) Alpha function
X) Beta function
Y) Gamma function
Z) Delta function
ANSWER: Y) Gamma function
