2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Wrinkle ridges are sinuous ridges found in the lunar maria with analogous formation to lobate scarps of Mercury. Which of the following best explains the formation of wrinkle ridges?

X) The basalt contracted when cooling, forming these ridges
Y) Tidal forces from the Earth caused stress, forming these ridges
Z) Tidal forces from the Sun caused stress, forming these ridges
