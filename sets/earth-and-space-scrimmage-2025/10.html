1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In most equatorial regions, the ITCZ seasonally shifts to different hemispheres due to changes in the solar heating angle. However, in the Atlantic ocean, the ITCZ never shifts south of the equator primarily due to which of the following ocean currents?
W) Benguela current
X) Brazil current
Y) Falkland current
Z) Guinea current
ANSWER: W) Benguela current
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 mineral groups in increasing ionic character. 1) Halides 2) Oxides 3) Sulfides
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Most flare stars known are classified as which of the following types of objects?
W) Red dwarf
X) Red giant
Y) Blue supergiant
Z) White dwarf
ANSWER: W) Red dwarf
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following four objects by increasing Eddington limit 1) Blue hypergiant 2) White dwarf 3) Neutron star 4) Black hole
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1, 4
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following changes to a temperate lake system would most favor the deposition of black shale?
W) Increased summer overturning
X) Rapid glacial meltwater influx
Y) Increased input of coarse detrital sediments
Z) Reduced mixing due to wind
ANSWER: Z) Reduced mixing due to wind
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A region with a Koppern climate class of Csb can be best described by which of the following?
W) Temperate, warm summers with wet winters
X) Temperate, cool and wet summers
Y) Tropical, warm summers, wet year round
Z) Semiarid, warm, dry year round
ANSWER: W) Temperate, warm summers with wet winters
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding erosion is NOT true?
W) Water generally sorts sediments better than wind
X) Slumps and mudflows are types of mechanical erosion
Y) Attrition most commonly occur on the bed of a stream
Z) Wind erosion is most effective in arid regions with no vegetation
ANSWER: W) Water generally sorts sediments better than wind
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In the Pacific Ocean, the carbonate compensation depth is generally located in which of the following zones?
W) Epipelagic
X) Mesopelagic
Y) Bathypelagic
Z) Abyssopelagic
ANSWER: Z) Abyssopelagic
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following isotopes is most suitable for radiometrically dating a sample believed to be between 1 and 5 million years old?
W) Carbon-14
X) Potassium-40
Y) Rubidium-87
Z) Uranium-238
ANSWER: X) Potassium-40
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What circulation is essential to the residence time of stratospheric ozone as it involves the rise of air into the stratosphere near the equator and the sinking of air near the poles?
ANSWER: Brewer-Dobson Circulation
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A de-Sitter universe, a solution to the Einstein field equations, is dominated by what quantity that is thought to correspond with dark energy in other models?
ANSWER: Cosmological constant
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following that are true regarding the ergosphere 1) It rotates in the same direction as the black hole 2) An object in the ergosphere always appears stationary to an external observer 3) It is located inside the innermost stable circular orbit
ANSWER: None
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In asteroids, scientists have found a discrepancy between the expected and actual amounts of materials that are thought to make up terrestrial planets’ mantle and crust. What olivine-rich rock is thought to compose most of this missing mantle?
ANSWER: Dunite
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding white dwarves 1) Runaway fusion in their accretion disks often lead to novae events 2) They decrease in radius as additional mass is accreted 3) There cores are always composed of stellar metals
ANSWER: 1 and 2
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Helium, due to its low mass, is constantly escaping from Earth’s atmosphere. However, its concentration in the atmosphere remains relatively constant over time due to replenishment of helium primarily by what process?
ANSWER: Alpha decay
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the favorable periods for convective and frontal storms, respectively?
W) Frontal storms favor colder seasons, while convective storms favor warmer seasons
X) Convective storms favor colder seasons, while frontal storms favor warmer seasons
Y) Both favor colder seasons
Z) Both favor warmer seasons
ANSWER: W) Frontal storms favor colder seasons, while convective storms favor warmer seasons
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer An interstratal karst is characterized by an underground karst landscape overlain by a layer of insoluble rocks. By name or number, identify all of the following pairs of bottom and top beds, respectively, that can produce interstratal karst under optimal conditions 1) Limestone, granite 2) Dolomite, gypsum 3) Chert, basalt
ANSWER: 1 only
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following three layers in a Bouma sequence from bottom to top 1) Parallel-laminated siltstone 2) Normally graded sandstone 3) Rippled sandstone
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why Mercury exhibits a 3:2 spin-orbit resonance rather than tidal locking?
W) High orbital eccentricity leads to variable tidal torques
X) The Yarkovsky effect steadily increases spin
Y) Rigid interior resists tidal deformation
Z) Cumulative meteoroid impacts gradually increase spin
ANSWER: W) High orbital eccentricity leads to variable tidal torques
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following four ionized emission lines that are characteristic of a Wolf-Rayet star’s spectrum 1) Hydrogen 2) Helium 3) Carbon 4) Nitrogen
ANSWER: 2, 3, 4
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true regarding metamorphism?
W) Sillimanite is often found in specimens of gneiss
X) Granulite commonly forms in collisional mountain belts
Y) Crystals under shear stress tend to align parallel to the shear direction
Z) Metamorphism involves ejection of volatiles such as water
ANSWER: Y) Crystals under shear stress tend to align parallel to the shear direction
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Rocks with low competence are most likely to develop which of the following?
W) Normal faults
X) Anticlines
Y) Caverns
Z) Melts
ANSWER: X) Anticlines
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following three structural folds that can appear to violate the law of superposition 1) Asymmetrical 2) Isoclinal 3) Recumbent
ANSWER: 3 only
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three changes to a river would increase the likelihood of an avulsion 1) Increased sediment supply causing rapid channel aggradation 2) Increased flood frequency and magnitude 3) Decreased gradient of the riverbed
ANSWER: All
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Planet X has twice the mass of earth and half the radius. Which of the following is closest to the dry adiabatic lapse rate near the surface of planet X?
W) 6
X) 10
Y) 14
Z) 20
ANSWER: Y) 14
13. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Europa has been discovered to have a magnetic field induced by that of Jupiter’s. Which of the following layers is primarily responsible for the presence of this magnetic field?
W) Icy crust
X) Salty subsurface ocean
Y) Rocky mantle
Z) Iron core
ANSWER: X) Salty subsurface ocean
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The flocculent appearance of some spiral galaxies, as opposed to well-developed arms, indicates which of the following processes is forming stars at an unusually high rate?
W) Density waves propagating through the galactic disk
X) Tidal interactions or mergers
Y) Shockwaves from stellar winds and supernovae
Z) Collapse of residual gas clouds from galaxy formation
ANSWER: Y) Shockwaves from stellar winds and supernovae
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following that are true regarding gamma ray bursts 1) Gamma ray bursts shorter than 2 minutes are classified as short 2) Most gamma ray bursts observed are long gamma ray bursts 3) Long gamma ray bursts are commonly observed in star-forming regions
ANSWER: 2 and 3
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following, if increased while keeping other parameters constant, will increase the velocity of a shallow water wave 1) Mass of the Earth 2) Radius of the Earth 3) Water depth
ANSWER: 1 and 3
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The ternary feldspar miscibility gap shrinks with increasing sodium because increased sodium results in which of the following?
W) Reduced lattice strain
X) Reduced entropy of mixing
Y) Increased lattice strain
Z) Increased entropy of mixing
ANSWER: W) Reduced lattice strain
