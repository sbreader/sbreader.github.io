2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In which phase of mitosis does the cleavage furrow appear?
ANSWER: TELOPHASE
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for an imaginary plane where chromosomes align themselves, perpendicular to the spindle fibers of a dividing cell?
ANSWER: METAPHASE PLATE
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which type of epithelial tissue allows distension inside the urinary bladder?
W) Pseudostratified Columnar
X) Simple Columnar
Y) Stratified Cuboidal
Z) Transitional
ANSWER: Z) Transitional
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of epithelial tissue is allows gas diffusion in the alveoli of the lungs?
ANSWER: SIMPLE SQUAMOUS
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which mechanism of secretion are the lactiferous glands using?
ANSWER: APOCRINE
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of membrane lines close body cavities, such as the periotoneum?
ANSWER: SEROUS MEMBRANE
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What are sensory epithelial cells in the epidermis detecting soft pressure called?
W) Merkel Cells
X) Melanocytes
Y) Keratinocytes
Z) Langerhan's Cells
ANSWER: W) Merkel Cells
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the most abundant epithelium-based cell in the epidermis?
ANSWER: KERATINOCYTES
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which inorganic compound accounts for ⅔ of bone weight?
ANSWER: CALCIUM PHOSPHATE
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What substance, resistant to compression and twisting, forms when calcium phosphate reacts with calcium hydroxide?
ANSWER: HYDROXYAPATITE CRYSTALS
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following do Meissner's corpuscles detect?
W) Pain
X) Deep Pressure
Y) Burn
Z) Soft Touch
ANSWER: Z) Soft Touch
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which sensory receptors for cutaneous sensations are located in the hypodermis?
ANSWER: PACINIAN CORPUSCLES
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What type of bone is the scapula classified as?
W) Wormian Bone
X) Flat Bone
Y) Sesamoid Bone
Z) Irregular Bone
ANSWER: X) Flat Bone
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of bones is almost cuboidal in shape?
ANSWER: SHORT BONE
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How many bones does the appendicular division of an average human adult skeleton contain?
W) 82
X) 119
Y) 126
Z) 140
ANSWER: Y) 126
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer How many bones does the axial division of an average human adult skeleton contain?
ANSWER: 80
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which cranial suture is located between the right and left parietal bones?
ANSWER: SAGITTAL SUTURE
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which cranial suture is located between the two parietal bones and the occipital bone?
ANSWER: LAMBDOID SUTURE
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the five vertebral regions.
ANSWER: CERVICAL, THORACIC, LUMBAR, SACRAL, COCCYGEAL (also accept: in any order)
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer How many bones are in the thoracic region?
ANSWER: TWELVE
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a type of a synovial joint?
W) Condylar joint
X) Suture
Y) Sliding joint
Z) Symphysis
ANSWER: W) Condylar joint
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which carpal bone is only visible when the palm is facing up in a supinate position?
ANSWER: PISIFORM
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a definition of a sarcomere in skeletal muscle tissue?
W) Length of an A band
X) Length between two consecutive Z lines
Y) Distance from the I band to the M line
Z) Distance between the Z line to the M line
ANSWER: X) Length between two consecutive Z lines
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What structure does a train of sarcomeres in skeletal muscle tissue make up?
ANSWER: Myofibril
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the hyaline cartilage located in between the ribs and the sternum?
ANSWER: COSTAL CARTILAGE
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which structure of the sternum ossifies in humans by the age of forty?
ANSWER: XIPHOID PROCESS
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which structure of a motor neuron allows integration and fired action potential?
W) Dendrite
X) Soma
Y) Axon Hillock
Z) Node of Ranvier
ANSWER: Y) Axon Hillock
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer In a neuron, what are groups of ribosomes used for protein synthesis called?
ANSWER: NISSL CELLS (also accept: NISSL BODIES; NISSL GRANULES)
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not one of the twelve pairs of cranial nerves?
W) Trigeminal
X) Vagus
Y) Abducens
Z) Hyperglossal
ANSWER: Z) Hyperglossal
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which cranial nerve is associated with hearing and balance?
ANSWER: VESTIBULOCOCHLEAR
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In osmosis, in which direction does the solvent move?
W) Hypertonic to hypotonic
X) Isotonic to hypotonic
Y) Hypotonic to hypertonic
Z) Hypotonic to isotonic
ANSWER: Y) Hypotonic to hypertonic
19. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What determines the flow of solvent in facilitated diffusion?
W) Pressure gradient
X) Concentration gradient
Y) Amount of energy involved
Z) Type of carrier protein
ANSWER: X) Concentration gradient
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the two nerve plexuses involved in the enteric nervous system?
ANSWER: SUBMUCOSAL PLEXUS, MYENTERIC PLEXUS (also accept: in any order)
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer Specifically, which tract in the human body does the enteric nervous system control?
ANSWER: GASTROINTENSTINAL TRACT
22. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How many lymphatic organs are there in the human body?
W) 500 to 600
X) 100 to 200
Y) 700 to 800
Z) 0 –99
ANSWER: W) 500 to 600
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which lymph duct is present in all humans and drains up to 75% of all lymph?
ANSWER: THORACIC DUCT
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the largest lymphatic organ in the human body?
ANSWER: SPLEEN
23. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a function of the spleen?
W) Phagocytosis-based removal of abnormal blood cells
X) Immune response against blood-borne antigens
Y) Produce urea
Z) Storage of recycled iron from erythrocytes
ANSWER: Y) Produce urea
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which structure in the kidney filters waste from the blood?
ANSWER: GLOMERULUS
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the basic structural and functional unit for urine production?
ANSWER: NEPHRON
