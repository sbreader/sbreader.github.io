1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Angela increases the volume of her music from 60 decibels to 90 decibels. By what factor did the intensity of the music increase?
ANSWER: 1000
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer The frequency of a sound wave is increased from 55 hertz to 110 hertz. Assuming that the intensity of the sound wave was 231 watts per meter squared before the increase, and giving your answer in watts per meter squared, what is the current intensity of the sound wave?
ANSWER: 924
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the electron pair geometry of s ulfu r tetrafluoride?
W) See-saw
X) Trigonal Pyramidal
Y) Tetrahedral
Z) Trigonal Bipyramidal
ANSWER: Z) Trigonal Bipyramidal
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When sodium chloride is added to a dilute solution of hydrochloric acid in water, the ionization of the chloride anion decreases. What is the best explanation for this phenomenon?
ANSWER: COMMON ION EFFECT
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are conducting research on how much wetlands have affected global methane emissions. Which of the following features is NOT a type of wetland?
W) Marsh
X) Bog
Y) Fen
Z) Berm
ANSWER: Z) Berm
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are using the Advanced Light Source to study SARS-CoV-2 antibodies. Identify all of the following 3 statements regarding antibodies that are true 1) Antibodies have a u – shaped structure 2) The antigen – binding site of an antibody has a shape that provides a lock-and-key fit with the corresponding epitope 3) Antibodies directly kill pathogens by interfering with their activity.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of drainage pattern is characterized by streams diverging outward from a central high point?
ANSWER: RADIAL
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 factors that resist against the compression of an interstellar gas cloud 1) The thermal energy of the gas 2) The magnetic field within the interstellar medium 3) Rotation of the universe.
ANSWER: ALL OF THEM
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is the remainder when 3 to the power of 11 is divided by 5?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 3
Z) 4
ANSWER: X) 2
5. BONUS Math Short Answer If a hexagonal prism has a volume of 192 times the square root of 3 and a base length of 4, calculate the lateral surface area of the prism.
ANSWER: 192
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many pi bonds are present in 1-3 butadiene?
ANSWER: 2
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A green alien has set foot outside his flying saucer when he accidentally drops his ray gun. He notes that it falls a distance of one blip in a time of one bloop. If it is still falling at a time of 3 bloops, in terms of blips, what is the total distance the ray gun has fallen in that time?
ANSWER: 9

BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following situations in which an object is being accelerated 1) An object moves with uniform circular motion 2) An object moves in a straight line at constant speed 3) An object travels as a projectile in a gravitational field with negligible air resistance

8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following animals would have a single circulation system?
W) Salmon
X) Salamander

8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following 4 layers in mammalian egg from the outermost layer to the innermost layer 1) Nucleus 2) Zona pellucida 3) Follicle cells 4) Plasma membrane.
ANSWER: 3,2,4,1
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the point where 3 plate boundaries intersect?
ANSWER: TRIPLE JUNCTION

ANSWER: ADVECTION (do not accept: ADVECTION-RADIATION)

W) 0
X) 1

ANSWER: Y) 2
10. BONUS Math Short Answer How many diagonals are formed from a regular 13 sided polygon?
ANSWER: 65
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The speed of sound travelling through a substance is 340 meters per second. The density of the substance is 0.5 kilograms per meter cubed. Giving your answer in gigapascals, what is the bulk modulus for the substance?
ANSWER: 5.78 × 10-5 (also accept: 57,800 pascals)
11. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following states that the predictions of quantum physics match the predictions of classical physics at large enough quantum numbers?
W) Correspondence principle
X) Heisenberg uncertainty principle
Y) Pauli exclusion principle
Z) Confinement principle
ANSWER: W) Correspondence principle
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab have been studying the properties of the Bose-Einstein condensate. A Bose-Einstein condensate can be composed of which of the following particles?
W) Electron
X) Quarks
Y) Photon
Z) Muon
ANSWER: Y) Photon
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Argonne National Lab have been conducting research into making computer chips based on a 3 nanometer transistor design. What quantum phenomena poses the main challenge to scaling down the transistors used in computer chips?
ANSWER: QUANTUM TUNNELING
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice From which of the following types of embryonic germ layers is the epithelial lining of the digestive tract, respiratory, and excretory systems derived?
W) Ectoderm
X) Mesoderm
Y) Endoderm
Z) Blastocoel
ANSWER: Y) Endoderm

ANSWER: AMNION
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the phenomenon that occurs at polar latitudes and is characterized by an unexpected rise in temperature in the stratosphere?
ANSWER: SUDDEN STRATOSPHERIC WARMING

X) Weak nuclear force

18. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice An animal experiences an acid-base imbalance in the arterial blood that results in acidosis. To increase pH toward normal, which direction would the ventilation rate be changed and what would be the corresponding change in arterial pressure of CO2?
W) Ventilation rate increases, arterial pressure of CO2 increases
X) Ventilation rate increases, arterial pressure of CO2 decreases
Y) Ventilation rate decreases, arterial pressure of CO2 increases
Z) Ventilation rate decreases, arterial pressure of CO2 decreases
