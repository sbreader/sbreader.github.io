Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term is used by geologists for any naturally occurring inorganic solid that possesses an orderly crystalline structure and a well-defined chemical composition?
ANSWER: MINERAL
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the 4 following choices which are characteristics used to classify minerals. 1: naturally occurring 2: orderly crystalline structure 3: generally organic 4: well-defined chemical composition
ANSWER: 1, 2, 4 (also accept: ALL BUT 3)
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the study of space and the contents of the universe?
ANSWER: COSMOLOGY
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following 4 choices that are key factors in the removal of cocoons around rapidly rotating protostars: 1: angular momentum 2: stellar wind 3: radiation pressure 4: strong magnetic field
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is TRUE of minerals and their physical properties?
W) most minerals have a metallic luster
X) as the colors of a mineral vary with different samples, their streaks vary also
Y) mineralogists use the term "habit" to describe the shape of crystals or aggregate of crystals
Z) tabular crystal habits resemble bunches of grapes
ANSWER: Y) mineralogists use the term "habit" to describe the shape of crystals or aggregate of crystals
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following 5 terms is/are not used to describe luster? 1: submetallic 2: shiny 3: vitreous 4: aqueous 5: tabular
ANSWER: 2, 4, AND 5
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer On the Mohs scale of hardness, between what two minerals will a streak plate fall?
ANSWER: ORTHOCLASE AND QUARTZ
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Using the terms "sheets", "cube", "rhombohedron", and "octahedron", what type of cleavage do the following minerals form? calcite, fluorite, muscovite, halite
ANSWER: RHOMBOHEDRON, OCTAHEDRON, SHEETS, CUBE
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the age of the universe?
W) between 1 billion and 5 billion years old
X) between 5 billion and 10 billion years old
Y) between 10 million and 20 million years old
Z) between 10 billion and 20 billion years old
ANSWER: Z) between 10 billion and 20 billion years old
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four minerals from highest specific gravity to least: galena, magnetite, gold, quartz
ANSWER: GOLD, GALENA, MAGNETITE, QUARTZ (also accept: 3, 1, 2, 4)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What general term is used to describe the tendency of a mineral to break along planes of weak bonding?
ANSWER: CLEAVAGE
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following gemstones is formed by the mineral beryl?
W) opal
X) amethyst
Y) tourmaline
Z) emerald
ANSWER: Z) emerald
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the path that the sun appears to follow each year as the earth orbits the sun?
ANSWER: ECLIPTIC
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What two constellations does the Sun enter during the equinoxes?
W) Aries and Virgo
X) Libra and Cancer
Y) Cancer and Capricorn
Z) Aries and Libra
ANSWER: Z) Aries and Libra
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What two elements are most abundant in dark silicates?
ANSWER: IRON AND MAGNESIUM
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a factor that contributes toward the conversion of a metamorphic rock into a sedimentary rock?
W) uplift
X) deposition
Y) weathering
Z) crystallization
ANSWER: Z) crystallization
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What two constellations does the Sun pass through during the solstices?
ANSWER: CANCER AND CAPRICORN
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What percentage of the mass of the Sun is the smallest mass possible for any star?
W) 12 %
X) 34 %
Y) 8 %
Z) 16 %
ANSWER: Y) 8 %
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What rock, which is composed of amphibole and plagioclase feldspar, crystallizes at depth and is the coarse-grained equivalent of andesite?
ANSWER: DIORITE
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 4 minerals in order of crystallization, from the highest temperature to the lowest according to Bowen's reaction series: biotite mica, pyroxene, olivine, amphibole
ANSWER: OLIVINE, PYROXENE, AMPHIBOLE, BIOTITE MICA (also accept: 3, 2, 4, 1)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is electromagnetic radiation left over by the Big Bang called? The discovery of this type of radiation is considered a landmark test for the model of the Big Bang.
ANSWER: COSMIC BACKGROUND RADIATION
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following that are processes that can alter the chemical composition of magma. 1: magma mixing 2: assimilation 3: crystal settling 4: magmatic differentiation
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3, AND 4
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What modified version of the Big Bang theory states that the universe underwent a rapid period of expansion shortly after the Big Bang, accounting for its large size and uniform nature?
ANSWER: INFLATIONARY THEORY
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the formation of one or more secondary magmas from a single parent magma called?
ANSWER: MAGMATIC DIFFERENTIATION
13. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is INCORRECT regarding glacial erosion?
W) in abrasion, glacial meltwater penetrates cracks and joints along rock floor and pries the rock loose as it expands
X) the products of abrasion are collectively called rock flour
Y) the differential erosion of rock by ice depends on the rate of glacial movement, as well as the thickness of the ice
Z) glacial flow patterns can be reconstructed through glacial striations
ANSWER: W) in abrasion, glacial meltwater penetrates cracks and joints along rock floor and pries the rock loose as it expands
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What two terms are analogous to latitude and longitude on the celestial sphere?
ANSWER: DECLINATION AND RIGHT ASCENSION
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following 4 statements that is/are TRUE of landforms created by glaciers: 1: prior to glaciation, river valleys are generally V-shaped because the rivers are above their base levels, and are thus downcutting 2: hanging valleys can occur when the amount of water flowing into a main stream in the form of tributaries is larger than the main stream itself 3: cirques, which originally exist as irregularities in the mountainside, are subsequently enlarged through frost wedging and plucking 4: several cirques clustered around a single focus can form an arête
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer From what reference point is right ascension measured eastward from?
ANSWER: VERNAL EQUINOX
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term given to objects with masses less than 8% of the Sun, shining only dimly?
ANSWER: BROWN DWARFS
16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice After nebular contraction stops and energy is released, the evolving star becomes a
W) Cepheid variable
X) an asymptotic giant branch star
Y) main-sequence star
Z) nova
ANSWER: Y) main-sequence star
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the general term given to pairs of stars which orbit around their common center of mass?
ANSWER: BINARY STARS
17. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following objects is born when nebulae contract under the influence of gravity?
W) planet
X) star
Y) nebula
Z) comet
ANSWER: X) star
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Lick telescope on Mount Hamilton in California is a 90-cm refractor, while the telescope constructed by Galileo in 1610 was a 3-cm refractor. How many more times as much is the light-gathering power of the Lick telescope compared to Galileo's telescope?
ANSWER: 900 TIMES
18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following objects would MOST likely produce an emission line spectrum?
W) a hot, dense gas
X) a cool, transparent gas
Y) a hot, transparent gas
Z) a cool, transparent gas in front of a hot blackbody
ANSWER: Y) a hot, transparent gas

W) inferior conjunction
X) superior conjunction
Y) greatest eastern elongation
Z) greatest western elongation
ANSWER: Y) greatest eastern elongation

20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Indicate by number all of the following 3 statements that is/are TRUE of CCDs, or charge-coupled devices: 1: it is a thin piece of silicon divided into arrays of pixels 2: pixels on CCDs are represented by n-doped MOS capacitors 3: more than 75% of all photons falling on a CCD are recorded

X) diffraction

W) Vega
X) Procyon

22. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is INCORRECT regarding faults?

23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term given to the type of coastline in which there is a drop in sea level or an uplift of the coastal land mass? An example of this type of coastline is exhibited in the Hudson Bay region of Canada.
