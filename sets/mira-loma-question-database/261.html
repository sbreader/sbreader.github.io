1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the SI unit for weight?
W) gravity
X) pound
Y) newton
Z) kilogram
ANSWER: Y) newton
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer If an astronaut lands on a planet A with three times the mass and twice the radius of Earth, what is her weight a on planet A as a multiple of her Earth weight e?
ANSWER: a = (3/4) e
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the SI unit for electric potential?
W) joule
X) volt
Y) coulomb
Z) watt
ANSWER: X) volt
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer All points on the surface of a charged conductor are at the same potential when they are in what kind of equilibrium?
ANSWER: ELECTROSTATIC
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer For a series combination of capacitors, the magnitude of the charge must be what on all the plates?
ANSWER: THE SAME
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer In what combination of resistors is the algebraic sum of the individual resistances always greater than any of the individual resistance?
ANSWER: SERIES
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What Frenchman is credited with the discovery of electromagnetism and the circuital law named after himself?
W) Faraday
X) Oersted
Y) Edison
Z) Ampère
ANSWER: Z) Ampère
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer According to the loop rule, the sum of the potential differences across all the elements around any closed circuit loop must be zero, as proposed by which scientist?
ANSWER: KIRCHHOFF
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer At 293 K, what is the approximate speed of sound in m/s?
ANSWER: 343

X) 10 W/m2

ANSWER: W) 1 W/m2
