Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Discrepancies in experimental measurements of rotational motion and theoretical plots of galaxies undergoing rotational motion led to the proposal of what substance?
ANSWER: DARK MATTER
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Tim throws a beach ball vertically upward, and it falls in the presence of air resistance. At what moment in its fall is the magnitude of the ball's acceleration greatest?
W) When it first leaves Tim's hand
X) When it reaches the apex of its trajectory
Y) The moment before it hits the ground
Z) The acceleration is constant throughout the ball's flight
ANSWER: W) When it first leaves Tim's hand
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists are studying how an increase in nitrogen in an environment affects the decomposition of dead plant materials by fungi and microbes. What is the term for how fungi obtain their nutrients?
ANSWER: SAPROPHYTIC NUTRITION (also accept: SAPROPHYTIC FEEDING)
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The research shows that an increase in nitrogen in an environment results in a decrease in the decomposition of dead plant materials. Which of the following events would result in a decrease in decomposition in an environment?
W) A decrease in nitrogen fixing bacteria.
X) An increase in agricultural runoff.
Y) A nuclear meltdown occurs nearby.
Z) An decrease in acidic rain.
ANSWER: X) An increase in agricultural runoff.
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the type of reaction in which two chemical species are joined together with the aid of a metal catalyst?
ANSWER: COUPLING REACTION
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the effect in which light is scattered by the particles in a colloid?
ANSWER: TYNDALL EFFECT
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following BEST describes why hydras are unique compared to other hydrozoans?
W) They exist only in the polyp stage
X) They exist only in the medusa stage
Y) They can exist in both the medusa and polyp stage
Z) They exist in a third stage that is different from polyp or medusa
ANSWER: W) They exist only in the polyp stage
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The phylum cycliophora contains only one species. What bizarre property does this species have that makes it unique?
W) It is born without a mouth
X) Females are born pregnant
Y) They have a lophosphere
Z) It will break its own bones to intimidate enemies
ANSWER: X) Females are born pregnant
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a common name given to a type of limacon?
W) Concave Limacon
X) Dimpled limacon
Y) Cardioid
Z) Convex limacon
ANSWER: W) Concave Limacon
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the arclength of the graph of y = √(4 − x2) from x = 0 to x = 2.
ANSWER: π
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What would an increase in the concentration of oxygen-18 in ocean sediments from a specific time period signify about that period?
W) sea levels were higher than usual
X) sea levels were lower than usual
Y) there was an ice age in that period
Z) that period had warmer temperatures than usual
ANSWER: Y) there was an ice age in that period
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 4 organisms that were present during the Tertiary Period 1) Trilobites 2) Ammonites 3) Marsupials 4) Birds.
ANSWER: 3,4 (also accept: MARSUPIALS AND BIRDS)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at the DOE BioEnergy Science Center are analyzing how well a bacterium, Clostridium phytofermentans converts different types of biofuel into ethanol. What is the name for the catabolic process that is generally used to convert biofuel to ethanol?
ANSWER: FERMENTATION
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In this experiment, differences in ethanol production are based on the compounds found in the biomass. There was a significant difference in ethanol production of biomass that came from different individuals of the same species. Which of the following best explains the reason for this difference?
W) There were subtle genetic differences between individuals.
X) Some of the individuals were drier than other individuals, affecting the composition of the biomass.
Y) There was human error in the experiment, resulting in the significant difference.
Z) The difference was simply due to random chance.
ANSWER: W) There were subtle genetic differences between individuals.
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the solution that is typically added after the addition of the crystal violet dye in order to strengthen the bonds between the dye and the bacterial cell membrane?
ANSWER: LUGOL’S IODINE (also accept: AQUEOUS IODINE; STRONG IODINE)
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 4 diseases that are caused by bacteria 1) African sleeping sickness 2) Botulism 3) Typhoid fever 4) Toxoplasmosis.
ANSWER: 2,3
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An ideal monatomic gas at pressure 4.0 × 104 Pa undergoes an isobaric process that raises its volume from 2.0 × 10-3 m3 to 2.0 × 10-2 m3. How many joules of work does the gas do on its environment?
ANSWER: 7.2 × 102 (also accept: 720)
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer The radiation emitted by an ideal blackbody triples. What would be the associated change in temperature?
ANSWER: 31/4 (also accept: fourth root of 3)
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Consider the following equilibrium reaction, FeO(solid) + CO(gas) ↔ Fe(solid) + CO2 (gas), where ΔH equals –10 kilojoules. Which of the following would have a similar effect on the equilibrium as increasing the temperature?
W) Decreasing the volume
X) Adding carbon monoxide
Y) Adding a catalyst
Z) Adding carbon dioxide
ANSWER: Z) Adding carbon dioxide
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What type of covalent bond is formed by two electrons from the same atom?
ANSWER: DATIVE (also accept: DIPOLAR; COORDINATE)
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What function counts the number of natural numbers less than a given positive integer n that are relatively prime to n?
ANSWER: EULER’
11. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the limit of the fraction with numerator x3 + 2x2 + 7x-30 and denominator x2-4x + 4 as x approaches 2
ANSWER: 8
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes a fold where the oldest rock is on the top and located at the center of the fold?
W) synformal syncline
X) synformal anticline
Y) antiformal syncline
Z) antiformal anticline
ANSWER: X) synformal anticline
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the process that likely created Earth’s first primitive atmosphere by releasing large amounts of water vapor, carbon dioxide, and sulfur dioxide?
ANSWER: OUTGASSING (do not accept: VOLCANIC ERUPTIONS)
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following general structures of viruses does the tobacco mosaic virus possess?
W) Helical
X) Icosahedral
Y) Viral envelope
Z) Bacteriophage
ANSWER: W) Helical
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The CRISPR Cas9 system utilizes the natural immune system of bacteria against bacteriophages. Which of the following parts of the bacterial genome is made up of parts of the DNA of bacteriophages that tried to infect the cell in the past?
W) Leader
X) Spacer
Y) Repeat
Z) Cas9 gene
ANSWER: X) Spacer
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Research at the Relativistically Heavy Ion Collider (RHIC) has developed a quark-gluon plasma soup that imitates matter in the early universe through Heavy Ion collisions. The spins of all the particles in the plasma soup will all be pointed in a random direction, but the average spin should have the same direction as what quantity of the system?
ANSWER: ANGULAR MOMENTUM
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer The fluid created at RHIC sets a new record for what quantity that has to do with the swirling motion in a fluid and that identifies the local spinning motion near some point in a continuum.
ANSWER: VORTICITY
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If a source moving above the speed of sound emits a sound, what is the term for the shock wave that is formed?
ANSWER: SOUND BARRIER
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer Calculate the voltage in the center of a square with point charges +2 Coulombs, +3 Coulombs, +4 Coulombs, +5 Coulombs at the vertices, clockwise around the center, and a side length of √2 meters. Approximate the Coulomb constant as 9.0 × 109.
ANSWER: 1.26 × 1011 V
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 4 conditions that must be present in order for an artesian system to form 1) An elevation greater than 75 meters 2) An inclined aquifer with one end pointed towards the surface 3) An aquitard above and below an aquifer 4) An average rainfall of at least 85 centimeters per year.
ANSWER: 2,3 (also accept: AN INCLINED AQUIFER WITH ONE END POINTED TOWARDS THE SURFACE AND AN AQUITARD ABOVE AND BELOW AN AQUIFER)
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the lowered water table located around a well caused when large quantities of groundwater are extracted from the well, the result of a process known as drawdown?
ANSWER: CONE OF DEPRESSION
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term describes a compound that can act as an acid or a base?
ANSWER: AMPHOTERIC
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By number, identify all of the following that are Lewis acids 1) BF3 2) Fe3+ 3) SiF4
ANSWER: ALL (also accept: 1, 2, 3)
18. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the remainder when x3 − 3 x2 + 4 x − 2 is divided by the quantity x − 3?
ANSWER: 10
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In the endothermic dissociation of H2CO3 into H+ and HCO3-, which of the following would cause the pH to increase?
W) Increasing the temperature
X) Adding sodium bicarbonate
Y) Adding sodium chloride
Z) Adding carbon dioxide
ANSWER: X) Adding sodium bicarbonate
19. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the strongest acid?
W) H2SO4
X) HNO3
Y) HBr
Z) HClO3
ANSWER: Y) HBr
20. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the time constant for a RC circuit with resistance 2 ohms, and capacitance 5 microfarads?
W) 2.5 × 10-6
X) 3 × 10-5
Y) 7 × 10-6
Z) 1 × 10-5
ANSWER: Z) 1 × 10-5
20. BONUS Physics Short Answer The time before a capacitor in an RC circuit reaches nearly maximum charge is known as the transient period. What is the term for the time after the circuit has become almost fully charged?
ANSWER: STEADY STATE PERIOD
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the equivalent terms for latitude and longitude, respectively, when dealing with coordinates in the celestial sphere?
ANSWER: DECLINATION AND RIGHT ASCENSION
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the declination and right ascension of the Sun at the vernal equinox? Include units in your answer.
ANSWER: 0 DEGREES (DECLINATION) AND 0 HOURS (RIGHT ASCENSION) (also accept: 0 DEGREES AND 0 MINUTES; 0 DEGREES AND 0 SECONDS)
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of depressant decreases central nervous system activity, can disturb memory and judgment, and decreases anxiety?
ANSWER: BARBITURATES (also accept: TRANQUILIZERS) (do not accept: DEPRESSANTS)
22. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Lysergic acid diethylamide, which is also known as LSD, can be classified as what form of drug?
W) Hallucinogen
X) Stimulant
Y) Depressant
Z) Narcotic
ANSWER: W) Hallucinogen
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the value of i-15?
ANSWER: i
23. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the length of the latus rectum of the graph defined by 4 y2 = x − 16.
ANSWER: 1/4 (also accept: 0.25)
