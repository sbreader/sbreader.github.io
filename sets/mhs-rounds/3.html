2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice According to Steno’s Laws of Stratigraphy, if a layer of granite is approximately 100,000 years old, a layer of bedrock underlying it would be which of the following relative to the granite, assuming no unconformities?

X) Older than the granite
Y) Younger than the granite
Z) Impossible to tell
ANSWER: X) Older than the granite
