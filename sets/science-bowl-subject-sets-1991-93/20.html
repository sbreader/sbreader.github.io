Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Using a rectangular x-y coordinate system, give the equation for a circle whose radius is 5 units and whose center is at the point (4,-4).
ANSWER: (x-4)2 + (y + 4)2 = 25 (also accept: (x-4)2 + (y + 4)2 = 52)
2. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice In a rectangular x-y coordinate system, the equation x2/16 + y2/25 = 1 represents which of the following?
W) parabola
X) hyperbola
Y) ellipse
Z) circle
ANSWER: Y) ellipse
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Using an x-y coordinate system, in what direction does the parabola represented by the equation y = -x2 open?
ANSWER: DOWN (also accept: OPENS DOWN; DOWNWARD; IN THE MINUS Y DIRECTION)
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In an orthogonal x-y coordinate system, in which direction does the parabola represented by the equation y2 = x open?
ANSWER: TO THE RIGHT (also accept: RIGHT; OPENS TO THE RIGHT; IN THE +X DIRECTION)
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In an orthogonal x-y coordinate system, a parabola is represented by the equation x2 = 6y. What is the X coordinate of the focus of this parabola?
ANSWER: 0 (also accept: X = 0)
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Using an x-y coordinate axis, a parabola is represented by the equation x2 = 8y. What is the equation for the directrix of this parabola?
ANSWER: y = -2
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A curve is given by the equation: (y-5)2 = (x + 4) What is the vertex of this curve?
ANSWER: (-4, 5)
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the sum of the internal angles of a pentagon?
ANSWER: 540 DEGREES (also accept: 540)
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer To what value is the product of the sin(A) and the csc(A) equal?
ANSWER: 1
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The cos(-A) is equal to which of the following?
W) cos(A)
X) -cos(A)
Y) -cos(-A)
Z) none of the above
ANSWER: W) cos(A)
11. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The csc(-A) is equal to which of the following?
W) csc(A)
X) -csc(A)
Y) -csc(-A)
Z) none of the above
ANSWER: X) -csc(A)
12. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The cos(180 deg + A) is equal to which of the following?
W) cos(A)
X) -cos(A)
Y) cos(-A)
Z) none of the above
ANSWER: X) -cos(A)
13. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The cos2(A/2) is equal to which of the following expressions?
W) (1/2)[1 + sin(A)]
X) (1/2)[1 + cos(A)]
Y) 2 sin(A) cos(A)
Z) none of the above
ANSWER: X) (1/2)[1 + cos(A)]
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer For what value of the angle "A" is the cosine of A equal to the cotangent of A?
ANSWER: 90° (also accept: 90 DEGREES; π/2; PI/2 RADIANS; 90° + n·180°; (n + 1/2)π)
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Given that the log[base 10](50) = 1.699, what is the log[base 10] of 500?
ANSWER: 2.699
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Given that log[base 10](2) = .30 and that the log[base 10](3) = .48, what is the log[base 10](6) equal to?
ANSWER: .78 (also accept: 0.78)
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer When added, the two complex numbers (5 + 5i) and (1 + 1i) are equal to what?
ANSWER: 6 + 6i
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer When the two complex numbers (5 + 5i) and (1 + 1i) are multiplied, what is the result?
ANSWER: 10i
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The complex number "i" when cubed is equal to what?
ANSWER: -i
20. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A heptagon has:
W) 5 sides
X) 7 sides
Y) 9 sides
Z) 11 sides
ANSWER: X) 7 sides
21. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice For a given perimeter length, the geometric shape containing the greatest area is the:
W) circle
X) square
Y) cycloid
Z) right triangle
ANSWER: W) circle
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the value of zero factorial?
ANSWER: 1
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the time derivative of the cos(U), if U is a function of time?
ANSWER: -sin(U)(dU/dt) (also accept: -sin(U)[dU/dt]; -sin U dU/dt)
24. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is 10 factorial divided by 8 factorial equal to?
ANSWER: 90
25. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In a triangle, what is the name given to the intersection of the perpendicular bisectors of the sides?
ANSWER: CIRCUMCENTER
26. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the period of the function "3 sin(X)"?
ANSWER: 2π (also accept: 2 PI; 360 DEGREES; 360°)
27. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many arrangements are there of 7 objects taken 3 at a time?
ANSWER: 210
28. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The polar equation r = 6θ/π represents which of the following graphs?
W) cardioid
X) three-leaved rose
Y) Archimedian-spiral
Z) limacon
ANSWER: Y) Archimedian-spiral
29. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Name the famous mathematician/physicist who was born on Christmas day (December 25th) in 1642.
ANSWER: ISAAC NEWTON (also accept: NEWTON)
30. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Give a trigonometric function, the absolute value of which varies ONLY between 1 and infinity, which is POSITIVE in the first quadrant and NEGATIVE in the fourth quadrant.
ANSWER: COSECANT
31. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A pyramidal solid has a base which is an octagon. What is the TOTAL number of faces on this solid?
ANSWER: 9
32. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the decimal value of the Roman Numeral CCLII?
ANSWER: 252
33. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If y = 5x-2 and x = 3t, what is the derivative of y with respect to t?
ANSWER: 15
34. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The natural logarithm of A is 2. The natural logarithm of B is 3. What is the natural logarithm of the quantity A times B?
ANSWER: 5
35. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the total number of diagonals in a hexagon?
ANSWER: 9
36. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many faces does an icosahedron have?
ANSWER: 20
37. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What are the first four terms of the series expansion of sin x near x=0?
ANSWER: x-x3/3! + x5/5!-x7/7!
38. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A square matrix which has all zeros above the main diagonal is called:
W) upper triangular
X) singular
Y) lower triangular
Z) invertible
ANSWER: Y) lower triangular
39. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the limit for the sin x as x approaches zero?
ANSWER: 0
40. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the sum of the binary numbers 1011 and 111, in binary and decimal?
ANSWER: 10010 (BINARY) AND 18 (DECIMAL) (also accept: 10010 AND 18)
41. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the SMALLEST prime number greater than 23?
ANSWER: 29
42. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the probability of flipping a coin 3 times and getting: first a head, then a tail, and finally a head?
ANSWER: 1/8
43. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A third degree polynomial with real coefficients can NOT have which of the following: 3 real roots; 1 real root and 2 complex roots; or 2 real roots and 1 complex root?
ANSWER: 2 REAL ROOTS AND 1 COMPLEX ROOT
44. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Evaluate log10(2) + log10(5)?
ANSWER: 1 (also accept: LOG BASE 10 OF 10)
45. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many degrees are contained IN EACH INCLUDED ANGLE of an octagon?
ANSWER: 135 DEGREES
46. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the area of the square inscribed in a circle of unit radius?
ANSWER: 2
47. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What are the four roots of the equation x4 = 16?
ANSWER: 2, -2, 2i AND -2i
48. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Cos 38° = ?
W) sin 38 deg
X) -sec 38 deg
Y) sin 52 deg
Z) 1 / cos 52 deg
ANSWER: Y) sin 52 deg
49. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A computer sells for $1200 after a profit of 20% on the original investment. What is the store's profit in dollars?
ANSWER: $200
50. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The rate of absenteeism for 45 male employees during one month was 15%. For the 60 female employees absenteeism was 5%. What was the overall rate of absenteeism?
ANSWER: 9.29% (also accept: 9.3%; 9.29; 9.3)
51. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The temperature readings on three successive days are +10 deg, -6 deg, and +7 deg. What does the reading on the fourth day have to be if average temperature for the four days is +5 deg.
ANSWER: +9 DEGREES (also accept: 9; 9 DEGREES)
52. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The average of six consecutive EVEN numbers is 21. What is the average of the first five of these numbers?
ANSWER: 20
53. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The sum of four NON-ZERO integers is even. The product of the four integers is also even. How many of the four numbers could be even? Be certain to give all possible numbers of even integers.
ANSWER: 2 AND 4
54. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If 60 > x > 20, what are the limits of (250-x)?
ANSWER: 190 < (250-x) < 230 (also accept: GREATER THAN 190 AND LESS THAN 230; BETWEEN 190 AND 230)
55. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A rope is cut into 3 equal parts. The first part is cut in 6 equal pieces; the second part is cut in 8 equal pieces; and the third part is cut in 10 equal pieces. If each of the resulting pieces has an integral length, what is the minimum length of the rope?
ANSWER: 360 UNITS (also accept: 360)
56. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The sum of any three consecutive odd numbers is always evenly divisible by which of the following numbers: 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6? Be certain to list all correct answers.
ANSWER: 3
57. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the sum of two supplementary angles equal to?
ANSWER: 180 degrees
58. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The angles of a triangle are in the ratio of 3:4:5. How many degrees are in the smallest angle?
ANSWER: 45 degrees
59. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the smallest perimeter, in meters, a triangle can have if the triangle has unequal sides and each side is evenly divisible by 11?
ANSWER: 99 METERS (also accept: 99)
60. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A circle is inscribed inside a square. If the length of the square's side is 6 inches, what is the ratio of the perimeter of the square to the circumference of the circle?
ANSWER: 4 : π (also accept: 4/π; 24 : 6π; 4 TO PI)
61. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A circle is inscribed inside a square. What is the ratio of the perimeter of the square to the circumference of the circle?
ANSWER: 4/π (also accept: 4 : π; 4 TO PI)
62. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A pendulum swings through an arc of 10π inches and an angle of 36 degrees. How long is the pendulum?
ANSWER: 50 INCHES (also accept: 50)
63. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If it cost $3 to paint the plane surface of a hemisphere, what will it cost to paint the hemisphere's curved surface?
ANSWER: $6
64. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A dog is tied with a 9-foot rope to a point where two fences meet. If the dog can roam an area of 9π square feet, at what angle do the fences meet?
ANSWER: 40 DEGREES
65. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The ratio of eligible women to men voters in a certain town is 2:3. If 2/3 of the women and 1/2 of the men voted in the last election, what fraction of the people in the town voted?
ANSWER: 17/30
66. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A child is counting the change in her piggy bank. She is amazed to find that she has an equal number of nickels, dimes, and quarters. If she has $6 altogether, how many coins does she have?
ANSWER: 45 COINS (also accept: 45)
67. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice In hexadecimal, F equals what?
W) 5
X) 10
Y) 15
Z) 16
ANSWER: Y) 15
68. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer "X" number of women can build a house in 30 days. If the number of women working on the project is increased by "Y", how many days will it take to build the house?
ANSWER: 30X/(X+Y)
69. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Jim lives 4 miles directly north of the train station. Barbara lives directly west of the station. If the straightline distance between Barbara and Jim's house is 2 miles less than the distance walking by the train station, how far west of the train station does Barbara live?
ANSWER: 3 MILES (also accept: 3)
70. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Find the slope of the equation y = x2 + 5x + 4 at x = 0.
ANSWER: 5
71. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which great mathematician is responsible for writing the Elements, the first logical axiomatic approach to mathematics?
W) Archimedes
X) Euclid
Y) Hippocrates
Z) Heron
ANSWER: X) Euclid
