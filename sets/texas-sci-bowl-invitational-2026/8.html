1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A patient experiences significant blood loss, leading to low blood pressure and decreased blood flow to the kidneys. The RAAS system is activated. Which of the following is most likely to happen as a result of RAAS activation?

X) Increased urine volume
Y) Increased ADH
Z) Decreased aldosterone
ANSWER: Y) Increased ADH
