1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The replacement of methionine's methyl group with a hydrogen atom forms an analogue of what thiol-containing amino acid?
ANSWER: CYSTEINE
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer During an annual checkup with your primary physician, one of the routine checkups is to look for swollen bumps around the neck. These structures are examples of what tissues that are responsible for housing lymphocytes?
ANSWER: LYMPH NODES
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What enzyme that carries it's own RNA molecule as a template has reverse transcriptase activity and is responsible for the replication of ends of chromosomes?
ANSWER: TELOMERASE
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In Guillain-Barre syndrome, the immune system mistakenly attacks the myelin sheath of peripheral nerves. This disease is analogous to what autoimmune disease of the central nervous system?
ANSWER: MULTIPLE SCLEROSIS
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer During translation termination, the release factor facilitates the release of the newly synthesized polypeptide by attaching it to what molecule?
ANSWER: WATER
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Occludins and claudins are major proteins in what type of cell junction that functions to prevent fluid leaking from one space to another?
ANSWER: TIGHT JUNCTION
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the best explanation of why fermentation is less efficient than cellular respiration?
W) Oxygen acts as a catalyst, speeding up the rate of cellular respiration
X) Fermentation does not require the input of glucose
Y) Glycolysis is less efficient at producing ATP than oxidative phosphorylation
Z) Regenerating NAD+ is faster in cellular respiration
ANSWER: Y) Glycolysis is less efficient at producing ATP than oxidative phosphorylation
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer As opposed to the theory of continuous evolution, what theory proposed that species have long periods of stability interspersed with short periods of rapid evolutionary change?
ANSWER: PUNCTUATED EQUILIBRIUM
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Allen's CD8 proteins are dysfunctional. Which of the following immune cells would be most impacted by this change?
W) Helper T cell
X) Cytotoxic T cell
Y) Plasma B cell
Z) Memory B cell
ANSWER: X) Cytotoxic T cell
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following sugars is nonreducing?
W) Sucrose
X) Lactose
Y) Glucose
Z) Galactose
ANSWER: W) Sucrose
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Rank the following three viral genome types by likelihood of rapid evolution via mutation 1) ssRNA 2) dsDNA 3) ssDNA
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In an ecosystem, producers have a total of 1,000,000 J of energy. How many joules of energy do all tertiary consumers have in total?
ANSWER: 1000
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer While some plants are short day plants, and some plants are long day plants, tomatos and corn belong to what third group of plants that do not display photoperiodism?
ANSWER: DAY-NEUTRAL
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following modes of reproduction is not asexual?
W) Fission
X) Spawning
Y) Parthenogenesis
Z) Budding
ANSWER: X) Spawning
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Electrical synapses are composed of which of the following types of cell junction?
W) Plasmodesmata
X) Tight Junctions
Y) Desmosomes
Z) Gap Junctions
ANSWER: Z) Gap Junctions
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Hemoglobin utilizes iron as its primary oxygen acceptor, in molluscs, the pigment hemocyanin utilizes what other transition metal for this function, giving it its characteristic blue color?
W) Zinc
X) Copper
Y) Cobalt
Z) Cadmium
ANSWER: X) Copper
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a density independent factor?
W) Predation
X) Competition
Y) Disease
Z) Temperature
ANSWER: Z) Temperature
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In a C3 plant, which of the follwing sets of conditions would maximize carbon fixation?
W) High CO2, High O2
X) High CO2, low O2
Y) Low CO2, high O2
Z) Low Co2, Low O2
ANSWER: X) High CO2, low O2
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Temperate phages, like the lambda phage, can choose between the lytic cycle and what other viral replicative cycle?
ANSWER: LYSOGENIC
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In fishes and amphibians, what system allows them to sense the direction and velocity of water currents flowing over its body?
ANSWER: LATERAL LINE
