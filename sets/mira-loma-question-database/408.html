1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What planet contains the most moons?
ANSWER: Saturn
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following atmospheres is the second from the bottom?
W) Stratosphere
X) Troposphere
Y) Thermosphere
Z) Mesosphere
ANSWER: W) Stratosphere
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What of the following rocks is an igneous rock?
W) Oolite
X) Slate
Y) Gossan
Z) Latite
ANSWER: Z) Latite
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the value of x if the equation log[base 3][x]= -4?
ANSWER: x= 1/81
6. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following defines a triangle with 2 equal sides?
W) scalene
X) isosceles
Y) obtuse
Z) right
ANSWER: X) isosceles
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Which of the following terms is defined as a triangular array of the binomial coefficients in a triangle?
ANSWER: Pascal's Triangle
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the slope of the equation, 3x+5y=45?
ANSWER: - 3/5
9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Natural log equation: ln e5 is equal to which of the following?
W) 10
X) 7
Y) 5
Z) 2.5
ANSWER: Y) 5
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What term is defined as a wave that vibrates at right angles to the direction of its propagation?
ANSWER: Transverse Wave
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following terms is used to measure the difficulty of stopping a moving body or system?
W) velocity
X) momentum
Y) force
Z) inertia
ANSWER: X) momentum
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Laws made from scientist, Isaac Newton, are known as what?
ANSWER: Newton's Laws of Motion
13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following instruments is used for detecting and measuring small electric currents?
W) galvanometer
X) ammeter
Y) potentiometer
Z) wattmeter
ANSWER: W) galvanometer
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What term is defined as an object's ability to do work due to its position?
ANSWER: Potential Energy
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following terms is where proteins are made?
W) ribosomes
X) cytoplasm
Y) vacuole
Z) mitochondria
ANSWER: W) ribosomes
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the last stage of mitosis?
W) Grow Phase
X) metaphase
Y) Cytokinasis
Z) Finaphase
ANSWER: Y) Cytokinasis
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which system in the human body deals with the release of bodily fluids?
ANSWER: Urinary System
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is something an animal and plant cell both have?
W) cell wall
X) large, centered vacuole
Y) chloroplasts
Z) mitochondria
ANSWER: Z) mitochondria
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Who is considered as the father of biology?
ANSWER: Aristotle
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best defines an organelle?
W) a structure that carries out specific activities in a cell
X) an extensive system of internal membranes that move proteins and other substances through the cell
Y) a set of flattened, membrane bound sacs that serve as the packaging and distribution center of the cell
Z) cellular structures in which proteins are made
ANSWER: W) a structure that carries out specific activities in a cell
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What are the names of the bottom two series of the periodic table?
ANSWER: Lanthanides and Actinides
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The element chromium is represented by what symbol?
W) Cd
X) Ch
Y) Cr
Z) Co
ANSWER: Y) Cr
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term best defines the process in which a substance rapidly combines with oxygen, producing heat or light?
ANSWER: Combustion
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following terms best describes coffee?
W) homogeneous mixture
X) heterogeneous mixture
Y) compound
Z) homogeneous solution
ANSWER: W) homogeneous mixture
25. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is a property that non-metals do share?
W) not lustrous
X) mainly gases at room temperature
Y) bond with polyatomic ions
Z) bond with other nonmetals
ANSWER: Y) bond with polyatomic ions
