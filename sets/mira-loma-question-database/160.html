Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What molecule is usually termed the "molecular unit of currency" because of its predominance in intracellular energy transfers?
ANSWER: Adenosine Triphosphate (ATP)
2. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice In 2009, what energy source generated the most electricity in the United States?
W) coal
X) hydroelectric
Y) natural gas
Z) nuclear
ANSWER: W) coal
2. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Coal-fired power plants most often convert the thermal energy from burning fossil fuels into electricity through what method?
W) the piezoelectric effect
X) the thermoelectric effect
Y) the triboelectric effect
Z) electromagnetic induction
ANSWER: Z) electromagnetic induction
3. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What law in thermodynamics states that, for any state function, the enthalpy change for a process is independent of the path taken?
ANSWER: Hess's Law
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Hess's Law most often uses the Enthalpy of Formations of substances to determine the total enthalpy change in a reaction. To the nearest whole number, what is the enthalpy of formation of O2 gas in kJ/mol?
ANSWER: 0
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In what type of physical collision is energy completely conserved?
W) perfectly inelastic
X) imperfectly inelastic
Y) elastic
Z) combination
ANSWER: Y) elastic
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer To solve a elastic collision problem, what physical law other than the conservation of energy must be used?
ANSWER: Conservation of (Linear) Momentum
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the thermodynamic cycle with the highest possible efficiency?
ANSWER: Carnot cycle
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer If a Carnot cycle has a heat reservoir at 500 K and a cold reservoir at 200 K, what is the efficiency of the system?
ANSWER: 60%
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In a nuclear reaction, what is the term for the amount of mass which has been converted to energy?
ANSWER: mass defect
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer In a nuclear reaction, 1 × 10-8 kg of mass has been converted to energy. In SI units to one significant figure, estimate the amount of energy released.
ANSWER: 9 × 108 J
7. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice A kiloton is a measurement of nuclear energy approximately equal to which of the following amounts?
W) 4.184 × 106 J
X) 4.184 × 1012 J
Y) 4.184 × 1018 J
Z) 4.184 × 1024 J
ANSWER: X) 4.184 × 1012 J
7. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice The largest nuclear weapon ever tested, the Tsar Bomba, had an energy yield of approximately what?
W) 0.5 kt
X) 500 kt
Y) 50 Mt
Z) 5 Gt
ANSWER: Y) 50 Mt
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In a system undergoing simple harmonic motion, the total amount of energy of the system will
W) increase over time
X) decrease over time
Y) always stay the same
Z) immediately decrease to zero
ANSWER: Y) always stay the same
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A pendulum bob is released at rest from at a vertical height of 5 meters relative to the base of the pendulum's swing. In SI units to the nearest whole number, calculate the maximum velocity of the bob during the course of its swing.
ANSWER: 10 m/s
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer To one significant figure, estimate the ambient energy loss per second for the average human body.
ANSWER: 100 Joules/second (100 Watts)
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The majority of cellular energy expenditure is used by which of the following operations?
W) ion pumping
X) generation of proteins
Y) ingestion and digestion
Z) cellular reproduction
ANSWER: W) ion pumping
10. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the term denoting the amount of energy needed to raise the temperature of a unit mass of a substance a unit temperature?
ANSWER: specific heat (capacity)
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer Given that the specific heat of Aluminum is 900 J/K/kg, how much energy will it take to raise 50g of Aluminum metal from 20 degrees Celsius to 35 degrees Celsius?
ANSWER: 675 J
11. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice A star generates most of its energy from which of the following processes?
W) chemical combustion
X) gravitational friction
Y) nuclear fission
Z) nuclear fusion
ANSWER: Z) nuclear fusion
11. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following choices gives the best estimation for the total power output of the Sun?
W) 1016 W
X) 1026 W
Y) 1036 W
Z) 1046 W
ANSWER: X) 1026 W
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The 0-point for gravitational potential energy can be set arbitrarily in a problem. However, in physics calculations regarding orbital mechanics, which of the following locations is most commonly set as the 0-point?
W) the surface of the body in question
X) the center of the body in question
Y) the Roche lobe of the body in question
Z) a location an infinite distance away
ANSWER: Z) a location an infinite distance away
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer By subtracting the gravitational potential energy at the surface of a planet from that of a location infinitely far away, an energy value is obtained. If this energy is converted to a kinetic velocity, what significant orbital constant is obtained?
ANSWER: escape velocity
13. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What quantity is defined as enthalpy of a system subtracted by the entropy times the absolute temperature of the system?
ANSWER: Gibbs Free Energy
13. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice If the Gibbs free energy change of a process is negative, the process can be termed
W) exothermic
X) endothermic
Y) exergonic
Z) endergonic
ANSWER: Y) exergonic
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In the core collapse of a supernova, the vast majority of the energy released is in the form of
W) gamma rays
X) neutrinos
Y) fission products
Z) heat
ANSWER: X) neutrinos
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Core-collapse supernovae are fueled by the gravitational potential energy that is released when the star implodes. In Type Ia supernovae, where a white dwarf surpasses the Chandrasekhar limit, what powers the supernova explosion?
ANSWER: carbon deflagration (rapid fusion)
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What Earthquake measurement scale quantifies the amount of seismic energy released during the quake?
W) Modified Mercalli
X) Moment Magnitude
Y) Richter
Z) Surface wave Magnitude
ANSWER: Y) Richter
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer On the Richter scale, how many times more energy will magnitude 6.0 earthquake have compared to a magnitude 3.0 earthquake?
ANSWER: about 31,600 (104.5)
16. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Compared to a gram of carbohydrate, a gram of protein contains
W) thrice as many calories
X) twice as many calories
Y) the same amount of calories
Z) half as many calories
ANSWER: Y) the same amount of calories
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many calories per gram do alcohols contain?
ANSWER: 7
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the general name for a device that can measure the heat change of a chemical reaction?
ANSWER: calorimeter
17. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following calorimeters is a constant-volume calorimeter that can withstand the large pressures of reaction?
W) bomb calorimeter
X) adiabatic calorimeter
Y) isothermal titration calorimeter
Z) constant-pressure calorimeter
ANSWER: W) bomb calorimeter
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What term is defined as the differential energy change over time?
ANSWER: (instantaneous) power (do not accept: average)
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer A car engine exerts a force of 1000 N as the car travels at 30 m/s. What is the instantaneous power of this engine, in proper SI units?
ANSWER: 30000 W
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What base unit of energy, equal to 1.602 × 10-19 J, is often used in nuclear physics, and is defined as the energy required to propel a certain charge through a certain electric potential?
ANSWER: electron-Volt
19. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following quantities best represents the energy released during the fission of one atom of Uranium-235?
W) 2 eV
X) 20 keV
Y) 200 MeV
Z) 2 TeV
ANSWER: Y) 200 MeV
20. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What practice of alternative energy uses the temperature difference that exists between the deep and shallow waters of the ocean to run a heat engine?
ANSWER: Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion (OTEC)
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What thermodynamic cycle that converts heat to work supplies 80% of all the electricity in the world, and is the fundamental basis for the steam engine?
ANSWER: Rankine cycle
21. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice The Rankine cycle is often known as the practical Carnot cycle, because their TS diagram resemble each other. However, the Rankine cycle mainly differs from Carnot's because the isothermal steps are replaced by what other steps?
W) adiabatic
X) isovolumetric
Y) isentropic
Z) isobaric
ANSWER: Z) isobaric
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In chemistry, what quantity is defined as the heat required to break Avogadro's number of a type of molecule into their individual atoms?
ANSWER: Bond energy
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In measuring the bond strength of ionic solids, what quantity is defined as the enthalpy of formation of the ionic compound from gaseous ions?
ANSWER: Lattice energy
23. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the net ATP gain when one molecule of glucose is metabolized through glycolysis?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 4
Z) 8
ANSWER: X) 2
23. BONUS Energy Short Answer Rounded to the nearest whole number, about how many times more efficient is aerobic respiration over anaerobic respiration?
ANSWER: 19 (also accept: 18-20)
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Stars can release energy through nuclear fusion until what isotope, where the energy released is insufficient to hold up the star against gravity?
W) Carbon-12
X) Silicon-28
Y) Iron-56
Z) Lead-208
ANSWER: Y) Iron-56
24. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Supernovae can create elements much heavier than Iron through the r-process. What is the heaviest element, with a mass of 254, that a supernova has enough energy to produce?
ANSWER: californium
25. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is an imperial unit of energy that is the amount needed to heat one pound of water one degree Fahrenheit? It is still used in power, steam generation, and HVAC industries.
ANSWER: British Thermal Unit (BTU)
25. BONUS Physics Short Answer Assuming that one pound is 454 grams and one degree Fahrenheit is 5/9 degrees Celsius, estimate to two significant figures the conversion of one BTU into (lowercase) calories.
ANSWER: 250 calories
