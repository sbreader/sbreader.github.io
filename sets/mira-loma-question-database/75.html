Source packet

1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is a class of proteins that play a key role in the innate immune system, and are single, membrane-spanning, non-catalytic receptors that recognize structurally conserved molecules derived from microbes?
ANSWER: Toll-like receptors
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer This effect can be described as vacuum fluctuations cause a particle-antiparticle pair to appear close to the event horizon of a black hole. Caused by such quantum effects, what is the term for thermal radiation that escapes a black hole and results in 'black hole evaporation'?
ANSWER: Hawking radiation
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the scale, from 0 to 8, which measures the strength of eruptions logarithmically, similar to the Richter scale for earthquakes?
ANSWER: Volcano explosivity index (VEI)
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 3 sets of quantum numbers (n, l, ml, ms) that are proper and can exist: 1: 1, 1, -1, ½ 2: 4, 2, 0, 0 3: 2, 0, 1, -½
ANSWER: none of the above
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In mathematics, whose formula for pi states that 1 –1/3 +1/5 –1/7 +1/9-….. = pi/4?
ANSWER: Leibniz
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Using the Gregory-Leibniz series or otherwise, find the infinite sum of 1 + ¼ + 1/9 + 1/16 + …. + 1/n2.
ANSWER: pi2 / 6
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, indicate which 2 of the following 4 materials would make the best control rod in a nuclear reactor. 1: boron 2: graphite 3: uranium 4: hafnium
ANSWER: 1 and 4
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, indicate which 2 of the following 4 materials make up the majority of human bone. 1: iron oxide 2: hydroxyapatite 3: collagen 4: elastin
ANSWER: 2 and 3
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer Also known as unipolar transistors, what type of device relies on an electric field to control the shape and hence the conductivity of a channel for one type of charge carrier in a semiconducting material?
ANSWER: field-effect transistors
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Assuming the luminosity-mass exponential ratio is 3, what is the approximate main-sequence lifetime of a star of 10 solar masses?
ANSWER: 100 million years
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Proteins containing this prosthetic group probably evolved for electron transfer in primitive sulfur-based photosynthesis pathways in ancestral cyanobacteria before the appearance of molecular oxygen. What is this term for this molecule, composed of a large heterocyclic organic ring with an iron atom contained in the center?
ANSWER: heme
10. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Hemoglobin is one of the most famous proteins with the heme group. Which kind of heme is present in hemoglobin and myoglobin, and is the most common type?
W) Heme a
X) Heme b
Y) Heme c
Z) Heme o
ANSWER: X) Heme b
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Which polar shape, with an equation r2 = 2a2(cos2theta), is a plane curve for which the product of the distances between the point and the two foci equals a2, and was first described by Jakob Bernoulli?
ANSWER: lemniscate
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Recently, the Fukushima I nuclear accident, caused by the 2011 Sendai Earthquake, has been compared to the Chernobyl disaster. At the peak of its issues, the maximum radiation levels reached 1 Sv/hour. Approximately, in Sv/hour, what were the highest measured radiation levels surrounding the Chernobyl disaster?
W) 0.3
X) 3
Y) 30
Z) 300
ANSWER: Z) 300
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer To prevent thyroid cancer as a result of radiation, what chemical is usually distributed to people living around places with high radiation levels?
ANSWER: Potassium iodide
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Mediated by enzymes like HAT or HDAC, what process involves the neutralization of lysine groups on the histones in order to convert heterochromatin to euchromatin?
ANSWER: acetylation
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What physical law in fluid dynamics relates the pressure drop in a fluid flowing in a long cylindrical pipe, and can be used to calculate blood pressure drop in the human?
ANSWER: Poiseuille equation
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Supernovae with no hydrogen spectral lines are often termed Type I supernovae. By name or number, indicate all of the following 4 types of supernovae that are considered Type I supernovae: 1: core-collapse 2: stripped core-collapse 3: white dwarf exceeding the Chandrasekhar limit 4: pair-instability
ANSWER: 2 and 3
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Pair instability supernovae occur when supermassive stars produce so much energy they generate pairs of antiparticles. Which antiparticle pair is most commonly generated?
W) protons and antiprotons
X) electrons and positrons
Y) neutrinos and antineutrinos
Z) W+ and W- bosons
ANSWER: X) electrons and positrons
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following 1 L solutions could neutralize the most sodium hydroxide solution, assuming the concentration of NaOH is the same for all scenarios?
W) 5M sulfuric acid
X) 6M hydrochloric acid
Y) 3M phosphoric acid
Z) 2M acetic acid
ANSWER: W) 5M sulfuric acid
17. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the name of the test that is the method to determine whether series with the (-1)n converge, based on if the other portion monotonically decreases?
ANSWER: alternating series test (Leibniz test)
18. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following units would be the most suitable for measuring the charge left on a regular triple A battery?
W) Volts
X) Ohms
Y) Joules
Z) Ampere-hours
ANSWER: Z) Ampere-hours
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer How many Joules of energy can be extracted from a 12 volt battery with 1 Ampere-hour of charge left on it? Assume 100% efficiency.
ANSWER: 43,200 J
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for a short (3-60 amino acids long) peptide chain that directs the transport of a protein to the endoplasmic reticulum for further processing, and was discovered by Gunter Blobel?
ANSWER: signal peptide
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer When a torque is applied perpendicularly to the axis of rotation of a gyroscope, the result is a rotation about its axis perpendicular to both the torque and the angular momentum. This wobbling along its axis is also known as what motion?
ANSWER: precession
20. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice This is a pivoted support that allows the rotation of an object about a single axis. A set of two of these, one mounted on the other with pivot axes orthogonal, may be used to allow an object mounted on the innermost gimbal to remain immobile (like a gyroscope) regardless of the motion of its support.
W) resolver
X) Cardan shaft
Y) Canfield joint
Z) gimbal
ANSWER: Z) gimbal
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of channel tends to occur in river with high slope and large sediment load, like alluvial fans or deltas, where many small channels are separated by temporary islands or bars?
ANSWER: braided stream
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following formulas must represent a saturated, noncyclic hydrocarbon?
W) C2H2
X) C4H14
Y) C7H16
Z) C10H20
ANSWER: Y) C7H16
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many isomers of heptane, C7H16, are there? Include enantiomers in your count.
ANSWER: 11
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In complex analysis, what is the term for a function defined on the complex plane that is periodic in two directions (a doubly periodic function) and at the same time is meromorphic?
ANSWER: elliptical function
24. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What law describes the spectral radiance of unpolarized electromagnetic radiation at all wavelengths emitted from a black body at absolute temperature T, a generalization of Wien's displacement law?
ANSWER: Planck's Law
24. BONUS Physics Short Answer Calculate the peak wavelength of electromagnetic radiation, in nm, emitted from a black body at temperature 1450 K.
ANSWER: 2000 nm
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 4 barriers to reproduction that are usually considered pre-zygotic: 1: behavioral differences 2: temporal isolation 3: hybrid infertility 4: gametic incompatibility
ANSWER: 1,2, and 4
