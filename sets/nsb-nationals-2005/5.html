Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The reaction of hydrocarbons with oxygen to produce carbon dioxide, water and heat is called:
W) fission
X) fusion
Y) endothermic
Z) combustion
ANSWER: Z) combustion
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the only known substance for which there is no triple point:
ANSWER: HELIUM
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the name of the device that is made by combining two basic types of solid state rectifiers allowing the device to both rectify and amplify a current?
ANSWER: TRANSISTOR
2. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following was one of the first direct indications that the speed of light could not be exceeded:
W) Faraday’s law of induction
X) Einstein’s theory of Special Relativity
Y) the Lorentz-Fitzgerald contraction
Z) Planck’s constant
ANSWER: Y) the Lorentz-Fitzgerald contraction
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice An example of negative feedback control is when:
W) the end product of a metabolic pathway inhibits an earlier reaction
X) the initial enzyme of a metabolic pathway inhibits a later reaction
Y) the end product of a metabolic pathway stimulates an earlier reaction
Z) the initial product of a metabolic pathway inhibits the next reaction
ANSWER: W) the end product of a metabolic pathway inhibits an earlier reaction
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the inner surface of the myocardium that is lined with a layer of endothelial cells?
ANSWER: ENDOCARDIUM
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The hydrogen envelope that surrounds the comet’s nucleus derives its hydrogen most directly from:
W) gases escaping from the melting comet’s ice
X) hydrogen captured from interstellar space
Y) breakdown of water by ultraviolet light
Z) hydrogen in the solar winds
ANSWER: Y) breakdown of water by ultraviolet light
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What do Astronomers call the point in the sky that is directly overhead?
ANSWER: ZENITH
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following does NOT yield a periodic non-terminating decimal:
W) 5/6
X) 1/27
Y) 1/18
Z) 7/8
ANSWER: Z) 7/8
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What 3 consecutive numbers add up to 45?
ANSWER: 14, 15, AND 16
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which is the best reason for proposing that a boulder lying in an area that was once glaciated is an erratic:
W) it is of a different composition than the underlying rock
X) it is rounded and worn
Y) it is larger than any surrounding rocks
Z) it is located near a glacial lake
ANSWER: W) it is of a different composition than the underlying rock
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for groundwater emerging naturally at the ground surface, where the water table intersects the surface:
ANSWER: SPRING (also accept: SEEP)
7. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is most specifically designed to measure low temperatures:
W) alcohol thermometer
X) pyrometer
Y) cryometer
Z) mercury thermometer
ANSWER: Y) cryometer
8. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following would be a measurement of a typical printer’s resolution:
W) 0.25 millimeters
X) 300 dpi
Y) 640 X 480
Z) 200 RSU
ANSWER: X) 300 dpi
8. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice The ARPANET was an example of a:
W) telephone network
X) LAN
Y) WAN
Z) email address
ANSWER: Y) WAN
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The ideal fuel for fuel cell use is:
W) compressed natural gas
X) reformulated gasoline
Y) hydrogen
Z) methanol
ANSWER: Y) hydrogen
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the branch of chemistry that deals with the relationship between electricity and chemical reactions?
ANSWER: ELECTROCHEMISTRY
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a consequence of the theory of electromagnetic radiation as formulated by J. C. Maxwell:
W) electric fields can cause magnetic disturbance
X) electromagnetic radiation consists of an electric and magnetic wave
Y) light is a form of electromagnetic radiation
Z) bright red light ejects no electrons from a metallic surface whereas dim violet light can
ANSWER: Z) bright red light ejects no electrons from a metallic surface whereas dim violet light can
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer All elements with an atomic number greater than what number are unstable?
ANSWER: 83
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Mendel did most of his original experimentation using this type of plant:
ANSWER: PEA
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following from the part of the digestive tract nearest to the beginning to the part nearest to the end: jejunum, ileum, esophagus, duodenum
ANSWER: ESOPHAGUS, DUODENUM, JEJUNUM, ILEUM
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A meteor that reaches the surface of the Earth is more accurately called a:
ANSWER: METEORITE
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice At a location half-way from the Equator to the North Pole, which of the following correctly describes the stars apparent motion:
W) they move in circles parallel with the horizon
X) they rise and set vertically
Y) they remain stationary
Z) they rise and set at an angle to the horizon
ANSWER: Z) they rise and set at an angle to the horizon
13. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice In a simple random sample:
W) response bias will be small
X) non-response bias will be small
Y) any two samples of the same size will have the same chance of being drawn
Z) each subject is asked every question and random responses are allowed
ANSWER: Y) any two samples of the same size will have the same chance of being drawn
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Every even integer greater than 2 can be written as the sum of two primes. Give the respective two pairs of prime numbers that when added give 8 and 12:
ANSWER: 5 AND 3 FOR THE NUMBER 8; AND 7 AND 5 FOR THE NUMBER 12
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the professional name for the person who studies fossils?
ANSWER: PALEONTOLOGIST
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following latitudes and dates would coincide with the longest duration of insolation:
W) 23.5° South on September 21
X) 50° North on June 21
Y) 50° South on March 21
Z) 0° on June 21
ANSWER: X) 50° North on June 21
15. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice In the tempering of steel tools, the process of rapidly cooling the tool is called:
W) quickening
X) quenching
Y) drawing
Z) case hardening
ANSWER: X) quenching
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the general name of the unusual infectious agents that causes spongiform encephalopathy?
ANSWER: PRIONS
16. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice A double sided, double layered DVD designed under today’s specifications, has a top storage capacity of:
W) less than 6 gigabytes
X) greater than 16 gigabytes
Y) 8 to 10 gigabytes
Z) 12-14 gigabytes
ANSWER: X) greater than 16 gigabytes
16. BONUS Energy Short Answer In optical resolution terminology, dpi is often used. What does dpi stand for?
ANSWER: DOTS PER INCH
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Out of the following choices, which is the correct name for N2O3:
W) dinitrogen oxide
X) dinitrogen dioxide
Y) dinitrogen trioxide
Z) dinitrogen tetroxide
ANSWER: Y) dinitrogen trioxide
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Convert 673 Kelvin into degrees centigrade:
ANSWER: 400° C
18. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In stars, the fusion of hydrogen typically releases energy as:
W) photons and friction
X) photons and neutrinos
Y) photons and mesons
Z) photons and gravitons
ANSWER: X) photons and neutrinos
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer Assuming friction is negligible, if a lever has a mechanical advantage of 5, how much effort in newtons is needed to lift a 10 newton load?
ANSWER: 2 NEWTONS
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the term for when a segment of a chromosome rotates 180 degrees and rejoins the same chromosome:
W) deletion
X) inversion
Y) translocation
Z) duplication
ANSWER: X) inversion
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer Germs that cause fever usually act on this section of the brain, which is sometimes referred to as the body’s thermostat:
ANSWER: HYPOTHALAMUS
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Light from distant galaxies comes mostly from which of the following:
W) low mass stars
X) high mass stars
Y) ionized hydrogen nebula
Z) large globular clusters of stars
ANSWER: X) high mass stars
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which planet in our solar system has the most circular orbit?
ANSWER: VENUS
21. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In 1700 AD, this person showed that a series of 0's and 1's could be used to denote a number:
ANSWER: GOTTFRIED LEIBNIZ (also accept: LEIBNIZ)
21. BONUS Math Short Answer If perfect numbers, except 6, can be expressed by the sum of the cubes of an unbroken sequence of consecutive odd integers, what are the consecutive odd integers which, when cubed, give the perfect number 28?
ANSWER: 13 + 33
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is responsible for the changing of the seasons in North America:
W) the Earth's orbit is elliptical
X) the Sun moves through space
Y) the Earth's axis is not perpendicular to the ecliptic
Z) the Earth's axis precesses
ANSWER: Y) the Earth's axis is not perpendicular to the ecliptic
22. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following most closely approximates the weight of a 1999 United States nickel:
W) 1.9 grams
X) 2.9 grams
Y) 5.9 grams
Z) 8.9 grams
ANSWER: Y) 5.9 grams
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a buffer system:
W) H2CO3 and water
X) H2CO3 and NaHCO3
Y) NH3 and N2
Z) NaCl and KCl
ANSWER: X) H2CO3 and NaHCO3
23. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Convert -40°F into Centigrade:
ANSWER: -40° C
24. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following particles has not yet been observed but is generally theorized to exist:
W) mesons
X) gluons
Y) fermionic hadrons
Z) gravitons
ANSWER: Z) gravitons
24. BONUS Physics Short Answer With regard to the speed at which sound travels in a specific medium, at standard temperature and pressure, order the following materials from the SLOWEST to the FASTEST: steel, wood, air, water.
ANSWER: AIR, WATER, WOOD, STEEL
25. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A newborn baby’s immunity, which is acquired from the mother, is often referred to as:
W) active immunity
X) passive immunity
Y) bistander immunity
Z) physical immunity
ANSWER: X) passive immunity
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer Parkinson’s disease is caused by a lack of this neurotransmitter in the brain:
ANSWER: DOPAMINE
