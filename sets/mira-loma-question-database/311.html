Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name seven of the top ten most abundant elements in the human body.
ANSWER: Choose 7: OXYGEN, CARBON, HYDROGEN, NITROGEN, CALCIUM, PHOSPHOROUS, POTASSIUM, SULFUR, SODIUM, CHLORINE
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A goiter is an enlargement of what gland caused by Iodine deficiency?
ANSWER: THYROID
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which of the halogens makes up the most of the human body?
ANSWER: CHLORINE
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the second most common isotope of carbon, composing of a little less than 1% of the carbon that exists?
ANSWER: CARBON-13
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What element does radioactive Carbon-14 decay to produce?
ANSWER: NITROGEN
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for random redistributions of electrons in molecules causing brief moments of polarity capable of attracting other molecules?
ANSWER: Van der Waals Interactions
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Morphine is a powerful opiate that functions by imitating the molecular shape of what class of neurotransmitters?
ANSWER: ENDORPHINES
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many moles of water are required to make one mole of glucose through photosynthesis?
ANSWER: 6 MOLES
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the maximum number of hydrogen bonds that a water molecule can form with neighboring water molecules?
ANSWER: 4
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What two properties of water allow for plants to transport what against the force of gravity?
ANSWER: ADHESION AND COHESION
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The density of ice is about what percent less than water?
ANSWER: 10 PERCENT
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Acid precipitation is caused primarily by the presence of what two types of chemicals in the atmosphere?
ANSWER: SULFUR OXIDES AND NITROGEN OXIDES
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In degrees Celsius, what is the average temperature of the human body?
ANSWER: 37 C
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the body taking advantage of when a human sweats to keep cool?
ANSWER: EVAPORATIVE COOLING
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the approximate pH level of human gastric juices?
ANSWER: 2
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What term is used to define the sphere of water molecules around a dissolved ion?
ANSWER: HYDRATION SHELL
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the pH of a solution that has a hydroxide concentration of 10-11?
ANSWER: 3
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Most biological fluids are relatively what on the pH scale?
ANSWER: NEUTRAL
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The carbonic acid-bicarbonate reaction is an example of what kind of reactions used by the body to maintain a steady pH?
ANSWER: BUFFERING REACTIONS
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Rain that drops below what pH is classified as acid rain?
ANSWER: 5.6
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What was the first recorded organic compound synthesized in a lab?
ANSWER: UREA CO(NH2)2
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Compounds with the same formula, but different structures are referred to as what?
ANSWER: STRUCTURAL ISOMERS
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Compounds with the same formula and covalent partnership, but different spatial arrangements are referred to as what?
ANSWER: GEOMETRIC ISOMERS
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Compounds that are essentially mirror images of each other are called what?
ANSWER: ENANTIOMERS
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Propanal can be distinguished as an example of what type of organic compound?
ANSWER: ALDEHYDE
26. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What compound can be defined as the simplest ketone?
ANSWER: ACETONE
27. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Thiols are a type of organic compounds distinguished by the presence of what functional group?
ANSWER: SULFHYDRYL (SH)
28. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name three of the six most common functional groups in biochemistry.
ANSWER: Three of: HYDROXYL, CARBONYL, CARBOXYL, AMINO, SULFHYDRYL, PHOSPHATE
29. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What class of macromolecules does not include polymers?
ANSWER: LIPIDS
30. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In order for monomers to be able to join together, they must first undergo what reaction?
ANSWER: DEHYDRATION
31. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Meaning "To break with water" in Greek, what reaction is used to separate polymers into smaller pieces?
ANSWER: HYDROLYSIS
32. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Around how many monomers commonly exist?
ANSWER: Between 40 and 50
33. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What common disaccharide is created from the dehydration of glucose and fructose?
ANSWER: SUCROSE (TABLE SUGAR)
34. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What common storage polysaccharide in plants is composed entirely of glucose monomers?
ANSWER: STARCH
36. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for granules within the cellular structure of plants used to store starch?
ANSWER: PLASTIDS
37. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What polysaccharide is used by animals for the storing of glucose?
ANSWER: GLYCOGEN
38. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What carbohydrate is described as the most abundant organic compound on Earth?
ANSWER: CELLULOSE
39. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Cellulose is a polysaccharide composed of what monomer?
ANSWER: GLUCOSE
40. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What common polysaccharide is similar to starch, however it can be distinguished by the fact that glucose monomer has a nitrogen-containing appendage?
ANSWER: CHITIN
41. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the main property shared by the compounds classified as lipids?
ANSWER: HYDROPHOBIC (LITTLE OR NO AFFINITY FOR WATER)
42. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Steroids are classified under what type of macromolecule?
ANSWER: LIPIDS
43. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A triglyceride with one or more double bonds can also be called what?
ANSWER: UNSATURATED
44. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the major function of triglycerides?
ANSWER: STORAGE OF ENERGY
45. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Phospholipids, unlike triglycerides, only contain how many fatty acids?
ANSWER: 2
46. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What common steroid is key in the production of many of the important hormones needed in the body, but too much in the blood may contribute to atherosclerosis?
ANSWER: CHOLESTEROL
47. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The word protein comes from the Greek word, "proteios", meaning what?
ANSWER: FIRST PLACE
48. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Polymers of amino acids are referred to as what?
ANSWER: POLYPEPTIDES
49. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In order for a molecule to be considered an amino acid, it must possess what two functional groups?
ANSWER: AMINO, CARBOXYL
50. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the R group in the amino acid Glycine?
ANSWER: H (ONE HYDROGEN)
51. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What amino acid contains the R group, CH3?
ANSWER: ALANINE
52. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term used for proteins that assist in the proper folding of other proteins?
ANSWER: CHAPERONINS (CHAPERONE PROTEINS)
53. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the two types of nucleic acids?
ANSWER: DEOXYRIBONUCLEIC ACID (DNA) AND RIBONUCLEIC ACID (RNA)
54. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Protein synthesis occurs in what part of the cell?
ANSWER: RIBOSOMES
55. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name for the monomers that make up a nucleic acid polymer?
ANSWER: NUCLEOTIDES
56. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The presence or lack of what determines whether a part of the endoplasmic reticulum is smooth or rough?
ANSWER: RIBOSOMES
57. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the complementary DNA strand for AGGTCCG?
ANSWER: TCCAGGC
58. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is defined as the totality of an organisms chemical reactions?
ANSWER: METABOLISM
59. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for a reaction that absorbs free energy from its surroundings?
ANSWER: ENDERGONIC
60. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What locomotion organelle is present in some animal cells?
ANSWER: FLAGELLUM
