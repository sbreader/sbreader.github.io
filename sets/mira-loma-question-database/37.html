Packet 37 (AnishERSC Week 1Part 2) of Mira Loma Question Database:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which agent of erosion is the single most important agent sculpturing Earth's land surface?
ANSWER: Water
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the lowest point at which a stream can erode its channel called?
ANSWER: Base level
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the type of river in steep, narrow valley that results when the land around a meander is uplifted?
ANSWER: Incised meander
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the specific name for the type of abrasion caused by streams?
ANSWER: Grinding
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of load do most streams carry their sediment in?
ANSWER: Suspended load
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A tributary stream that cannot flow into the main river because of natural levees often flows parallel to the river until it can breach the river. What is this type of tributary stream called?
ANSWER: Yazoo tributary
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the abandoned bend that is formed when a meander forms a cutoff and isolates a bend?
ANSWER: Oxbow lake
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of drainage pattern is generally found around isolated volcanic cones and domal updrifts?
ANSWER: Radial drainage pattern
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of drainage pattern occurs in areas marked by alternating bands of resistant and less resistant rock?
ANSWER: Trellis
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Within 2%, how much of Earth's liquid water is groundwater?
ANSWER: 94% (also accept: anywhere from 92% to 96%)
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the upper limit of the zone of saturation called?
ANSWER: Water Table
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the near-surface zone that occurs when water is soaked into the ground but does not travel far due to molecular attraction as a surface film on soil particles?
ANSWER: Belt of soil moisture
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Impermeable layers that stop or hinder water movement, such as clay, are called
ANSWER: Aquitards
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term is used to describe any situation where groundwater rises in a well above the level where it was initially encountered?
ANSWER: Artesian
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of glacier is similar to ice sheets but covers smaller area such as uplands and plateaus?
ANSWER: Ice Cap
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the process in which magma cools and solidifies?
ANSWER: Crystallization
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When valley glaciers emerge from mountain valleys, the advancing ice spreads out to form a broad sheet, creating this type of glacier.
ANSWER: Piedmont Glacier
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer At what depth of ice, in meters, will glacial ice lose its brittleness and start to flow?
ANSWER: 50 meters
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the process by which ice breaks off of the end of a glacier and forms icebergs?
ANSWER: Calving
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the sinuous, sharp ridges that usually surround valley glaciers called?
ANSWER: Arêtes
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for a lake that often occupies a cirque basin after the whole glacier has melted?
ANSWER: Tarn
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Sediment that is laid down by glacial meltwater and is usually sorted according to size and weight is called?
ANSWER: Stratified Drift
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When two advancing valley glaciers come together to form a single ice stream, which type of moraine is formed?
ANSWER: Medial Moraine
26. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of moraine is now a prominent feature in the Northeast, where the moraine stretches from eastern Pennsylvania to Cape Cod, Massachusetts, including a large part of Long Island?
ANSWER: End Moraine
27. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which glacial feature occurs when glacial meltwater washes sediment into openings and depressions in the wasting terminus of the glacier, resulting in steep-sided hills?
ANSWER: Kames
28. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In which epoch did most of the major glacial episodes occur?
ANSWER: PLEISTOCENE
29. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer During brief periods of rainfall in desert climates, streams may flow across alluvial fans and into the basin. What type of lake does this phenomenon create?
ANSWER: Playa lake
30. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer After enough erosion has occurred, mountains can be reduced to large bedrock knobs surrounded by a sediment filled basin. What are these bedrock knobs called?
ANSWER: Inselbergs
31. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are extensive blankets of silt often deposited by wind called?
ANSWER: Loess
32. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the classic example of an ancient fossil that was used primarily by Alfred Wegener to support his supercontinent theory?
ANSWER: Mesosaurus
33. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of plate boundaries are considered to be conservative?
ANSWER: Transform fault boundaries
34. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of boundary is formed between the North American and the African plate?
ANSWER: Divergent boundary
35. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of boundary is formed between the Australian-Indian plate and the Eurasian plate, thus creating the Himalayas?
ANSWER: Convergent boundary
36. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the generic name for the composition of the magma that often results in violent explosions, such as the one seen at Mt. St. Helens?
ANSWER: Andesitic (Intermediate)
37. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name one of the two volcanic arcs located in the Atlantic Ocean.
ANSWER: Lesser Antilles arc (also accept: Sandwich Islands)
38. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which scientist from the University of Toronto discovered the true nature of transform faults in 1956, and said that the Earth was made up of individual plates?
ANSWER: J. Tuzo Wilson
39. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the prominent linear breaks in the oceanic crust, where transform faults join the two segments of an oceanic ridge called?
ANSWER: Fracture Zones
40. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the rising mantle material that is often located under islands like the Hawaiian Islands called?
ANSWER: Mantle plume
41. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the composition of lava flows that contain magnetite, a key "fossil compass"?
ANSWER: Basalt
42. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Rocks that formed millions of years ago and contain a record of the direction of the magnetic poles at the time of their formation are said to possess this property?
ANSWER: Paleomagnetism
43. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which one of the forces that drives plate motion results from the drag of a subducting slab on the adjacent mantle, inducing mantle circulation where both plates are pulled towards the trench?
ANSWER: Slab suction
44. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer How many zones of convection does the "layer cake" model of mantle convection have?
ANSWER: Two
45. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the specific name given to mountains such as the Andes which are produced in part by volcanic activity associated with the subduction of oceanic lithosphere?
ANSWER: Continental Volcanic Arcs
