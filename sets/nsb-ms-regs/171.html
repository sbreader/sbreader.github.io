1. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a type of heat pump?
W) Air-to-air
X) Radiant
Y) Geothermal
Z) Water source
ANSWER: X) Radiant
1. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the name of the device that uses hydrogen as a fuel and creates an electric current with the main waste product being water?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN FUEL CELL (also accept: FUEL CELL)
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Whan an integer is divided by 8, what are the smallest and largest possible remainders, respectively?
ANSWER: SMALLEST = 0, LARGEST = 7
2. BONUS Math Short Answer What are the sum of the roots and the product of the roots, respectively, for the following quadratic equation: 4x2 – 12x – 7 = 0?
ANSWER: SUM = 3; PRODUCT = –7/4 (also accept: –1¾, –1.75)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following phase changes releases heat?
W) Melting
X) Boiling
Y) Sublimation
Z) Freezing
ANSWER: Z) Freezing
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many neutrons does a single atom of carbon-13 possess?
ANSWER: SEVEN
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following are the two parts of sunspots?
W) Aphelion and Parahelion
X) Central Umbra and Penumbra
Y) Crest and Trough
Z) Static and Dynamic
ANSWER: X) Central Umbra and Penumbra
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the phenomenon by which light from a distant object can be bent by a massive intervening object?
ANSWER: GRAVITATIONAL LENSING
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Some cells are known to secrete signaling molecules that then bind to receptors on the same cell. What is the term for this type of signaling?
ANSWER: AUTOCRINE
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer How many molecules of ATP are produced during the citric acid cycle?
ANSWER: ONE
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer An isosceles trapezoid has an interior angle measuring 155 degrees. What is the degree measure of its smallest interior angle?
ANSWER: 25

ANSWER: 3

8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the coefficient of the ac term in the trinomial expansion of (6a + 3b – 5c) squared?
ANSWER: –60
8. BONUS Math Short Answer 53 teams play in a single-elimination tournament; that is, each game is played by two teams, and the losing team is eliminated. Some teams have byes in the first round and don't have to play, but, after the first round, each remaining team plays every round until they are eliminated. How many games are played in the first round?
