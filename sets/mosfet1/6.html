1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In fluid mechanics, what principle states that a pressure change on one part of the fluid tends to be equally transmitted throughout the entire fluid?
ANSWER: PASCAL'S PRINCIPLE (also accept: PASCAL'S LAW)
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the first postulate of special relativity?
W) All laws of physics are the same in all inertial reference frames
X) The universe is expanding
Y) Charged particles experience a force when traveling perpendicular to a magnetic field
Z) Mass and energy are equivalent
ANSWER: W) All laws of physics are the same in all inertial reference frames
