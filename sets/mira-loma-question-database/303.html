Packet 303 (SciBowl Q s- EarthSci REDDY) of Mira Loma Question Database:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name of the current epoch we live in?
W) Holocene
X) Pliocene
Y) Neogene
Z) Miocene
ANSWER: W) Holocene
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the period of time in which a large number of impact craters are believed to have formed on the moon?
ANSWER: LATE HEAVY BOMBARDMENT (also accept: LUNAR CATACLYSM/LHB)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the special sub-category name given to igneous rocks that are fine-grained?
ANSWER: EXTRUSIVE
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What classification of rock is Breccia?
ANSWER: SEDIMENTARY
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice When 2 rock units slide past one another, what is the name of the type of fault that is created?
W) Thrust
X) Normal
Y) Strike-Slip
Z) Slip and Slide
ANSWER: Y) Strike-Slip
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the theory created by Henry Hess regarding the formation of oceanic crust through volcanic activity?
ANSWER: SEAFLOOR SPREADING
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the pipe that brings magma up from the large magma chamber, to the crater, and then out through the vent at the top?
ANSWER: CONDUIT
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name the type of volcano which is characterized as a flat, linear crack in the Earth, through which lava emerges.
ANSWER: FISSURE VENT
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name given to a boundary in which 2 plates slide towards each other?
W) Transform
X) Divergent
Y) Convergent
Z) Subduction
ANSWER: Y) Convergent
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to waves which are the first to appear on a seismogram?
ANSWER: P- WAVE (also accept: PRESSURE/PRIMARY/COPMRESSIONAL WAVE)
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the layer of the Earth directly above the asthenosphere?
ANSWER: LITHOSPHERE
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A rock that is considered felsic has which element in high concentration?
ANSWER: SILICON
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice When work is done on or by a gas, but there is no heat transfer with the environment, then the heating or cooling of the gas is known as
W) Adiabatic
X) Endothermic
Y) Fluid
Z) Tropical
ANSWER: W) Adiabatic
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the evolution or functioning of soils know as?
ANSWER: PEDOGENESIS
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The composition of the Earth is primarily made of what layer, with roughly 2/3 of the Earth composed of it?
ANSWER: MANTLE
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which compound constitutes about half of the mantle?
ANSWER: SILICON DIOXIDE (also accept: QUARTZ)
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The crust is separated from the mantle by what boundary?
W) Asthenosphere
X) Moho
Y) Gutenberg Discontinuity
Z) Lehmann Discontinuity
ANSWER: X) Moho
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What was the name of the "universal sea" that covered the Earth during the time Pangaea existed?
ANSWER: PANTHALASSA
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to deformed arrangements of stratified rocks on the Earth's surface?
ANSWER: FOLDS
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer When the 2 sides of a fold angle away from each other, what type is the fold classified as?
ANSWER: ANTICLINE
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Abrupt periods of time during glacial advance when the velocity is increased are known as
W) Surges
X) Upheavals
Y) Ablations
Z) Creeps
ANSWER: W) Surges
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Any glacier that ends in the sea is classified as what type of glacier?
ANSWER: TIDEWATER GLACIER
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer How many more times greater is a magnitude 8 earthquake than a magnitude 5 earthquake?
ANSWER: 1000 (TIMES GREATER)
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The process known as orogeny refers to the generation of what natural landform?
ANSWER: MOUNTAINS
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A rock that cooled within the Earth, during the rock cycle, is classified as what type of igneous rock?
W) Zeolitic
X) Mafic
Y) Extrusive
Z) Intrusive
ANSWER: Z) Intrusive
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The core of the Earth is thought to be largely composed of what element?
ANSWER: IRON
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What layer of the atmosphere shields the Earth from the charged particles of the solar winds, and is created by Earth's magnetic field?
ANSWER: MAGNETOSPHERE
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the boundary between the atmosphere and outer space?
ANSWER: KARMAN LINE
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The movement of water through the atmosphere is known as what process?
W) Advection
X) Subsurface Flow
Y) Canopy Interception
Z) Evapotranspiration
ANSWER: W) Advection
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The process of speleogenesis is the creation of what natural landform?
ANSWER: CAVES
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the process that clouds go through when precipitation is created in them?
ANSWER: BERGERON PROCESS
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A group of clouds in the shape of a leaf or a spoked wheel is defined as what type of common cloud field?
ANSWER: ACTINOFORM
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which type of cloud is created from the intense heating of the air from the surface?
W) Nimbostratus
X) Stratocumulus
Y) Cirrus
Z) Pyrocumulus
ANSWER: Z) Pyrocumulus
18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which sedimentary rock is most likely to be changed to slate during regional metamorphism?
W) Breccia
X) Conglomerate
Y) Dolostone
Z) Shale
ANSWER: Z) Shale
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the 2 most abundant elements found in the Earth by mass?
ANSWER: OXYGEN AND SILICON
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name the first eon recorded in Earth's history.
ANSWER: HADEAN
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which mineral is white or colorless, has a hardness of 2.5, and splits with cubic cleavage?
W) Calcite
X) Halite
Y) Pyrite
Z) Mica
ANSWER: X) Halite
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer When rock units undergo horizontal extension, what type of fault is created?
ANSWER: NORMAL
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of rock is schist?
ANSWER: METAMORPHIC
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the layering within metamorphic rocks called?
ANSWER: FOLIATION
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Compared to dull and rough rock surfaces, shiny and smooth rock surfaces are most likely to cause sunlight to
W) Reflect
X) Refract
Y) Be Absorbed
Z) Scatter
ANSWER: W) Reflect
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What mineral characteristic describes how a mineral breaks when broken contrary to its natural cleavage?
ANSWER: FRACTURE
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the area where there is an upwelling of abnormally hot rock within the Earth's mantle?
ANSWER: MANTLE PLUME
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What volcanic feature is usually formed after the collapse of land during a volcanic eruption?
ANSWER: CALDERA
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which factor has the greatest influence on the weathering rate of Earth's surface bedrock?
W) Local Air Pressure
X) Angle of Insolation
Y) Age of the Bedrock
Z) Regional Climate
ANSWER: Z) Regional Climate
24. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the geologic era that we live in today?
ANSWER: CENOZOIC
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In general, the probability of flooding decreases when there is an increase in the amount of
W) Precipitation
X) Infiltration
Y) Run Off
Z) Snow Melt
ANSWER: X) Infiltration
25. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name the geologic time period when many coal beds were laid down throughout the Earth.
ANSWER: CARBONIFEROUS
26. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the general name given to fragmental material [produced by a volcanic eruption?
W) Ash
X) Lapili
Y) Tephra
Z) Volcanic Blocks
ANSWER: Y) Tephra
26. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the classification name of a volcano that is tall, conical and layered?
ANSWER: STRATOVOLCANO (also accept: COMPOSITE VOLCANO)
