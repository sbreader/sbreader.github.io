1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What auto-immune disease, caused by the production of autoantibodies to the receptors for Thyroid Stimulating Hormone, results in hyperthyroidism and is often characterized by the protrusion of the eyes?
ANSWER: Graves' Disease
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Sometimes, a patient undergoing thyroid removal surgery will experience post-operative hypocalcaemia. Which of the following best explains this rare condition?
W) The body may be unable to compensate for the increased calcitonin after surgery.
X) The thyroid is a major storage area for calcium. Removal may decrease blood calcium.
Y) The parathyroid glands may be accidentally removed, leading to a drop in PTH.
Z) The lack of thyroxine may cause bones to uptake more calcium from the blood.
ANSWER: Y) The parathyroid glands may be accidentally removed, leading to a drop in PTH.
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer This subatomic particle mediates the strong nuclear force between the nucleons of atom. One form of this particle is composed of one up quark coupled with one anti-down quark. What is the general name for this lightest meson?
ANSWER: pion

21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following explanations best explains why the lapse rate of air masses drops significantly lower above a certain elevation?
