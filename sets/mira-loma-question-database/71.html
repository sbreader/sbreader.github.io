1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What small regulatory protein with a mass of 8.6 kilodaltons uses the catalysis of a ligase enzyme to tag certain proteins for destruction in proteosomes?
ANSWER: ubiquitin
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following statements that are true about proteosomes: 1: Proteosomes are found in all Eukaryotes and Archaens 2: The proteosome's shape is barrel-like, containing four stacked rings around a central pore. 3: The proteosomal degradation pathway is used for processes in the cell cycle and the regulation of gene expression
ANSWER: all of them
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Minkowski space differs from Euclidean space in its inclusion of what major dimension that is often utilized in relativity?
ANSWER: time
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of object is Cygnus X-1, which is part of a binary system that releases a large quantity of X-ray radiation?
ANSWER: black hole
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Where does the energy that generates the X-ray radiation of an X-ray binary system originate from?
W) Hydrogen fusion in the accreted fuel
X) Carbon deflagration of accreted carbon residue
Y) Occasional collisions between the two objects generates heat
Z) Kinetic energy released by gravity
ANSWER: Z) Kinetic energy released by gravity
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many sigma and pi bonds, respectively, are in one molecule of acetylene, which is the simplest alkyne, with a chemical formula of C2H2?
ANSWER: 3 sigma, 2 pi
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What theorem in probability shows the relation between two conditional probabilities that are the reverse of each other?
ANSWER: Bayes' Theorem
5. BONUS Math Multiple Choice A school has 60% boys and 40% girls. All boys wear jeans, while half of the girls wear jeans and the half wears skirts. An observer sees a person in the distance wearing jeans. What is the probability that this person is a girl?
W) 0.2
X) 0.25
Y) 0.4
Z) 0.75
ANSWER: X) 0.25
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following properties describes NONE of the processes of the Carnot cycle?
W) adiabatic
X) isentropic
Y) isothermal
Z) isobaric
ANSWER: Z) isobaric
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following sex-determination systems, commonly seen in hymenopterans, will an unfertilized egg develop as a male, and a fertilized egg develop as a female?
W) XO system
X) XY system
Y) ZW system
Z) haplodiploid system
ANSWER: Z) haplodiploid system
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What phenomenon explained the 'missing neutrinos' of several neutrino detectors, which predicted that neutrinos varied into other flavors?
ANSWER: neutrino oscillations
8. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following particle statistics models describes the energy of single particles in a compact system of identical particles that all obey the Pauli exclusion principle?
W) Maxwell-Boltzmann
X) Bose-Einstein
Y) Fermi-Dirac
Z) Braid
ANSWER: Y) Fermi-Dirac
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In glaciology, what a term describing partially compacted neve, a type of snow that has been left over from past seasons and has recrystallized into a hard substance that looks like wet sugar?
ANSWER: firn
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following substances would have the largest vapor pressure at STP?
W) propane
X) butane
Y) ammonia
Z) water
ANSWER: W) propane
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The heat of vaporization of water is approximately 40.65 kJ/mol. With this knowledge, approximate the entropy change of one mole of boiling water at one atmosphere, to one significant figure.
ANSWER: 0.1 kJ/K
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the polar equivalent of a Cartesian point located at (2, 2), using degree measurement and simplest radical form?
ANSWER: (2√2, 45 degrees)
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Via substrate-level phosphorylation, how many total (non-net) molecules of ATP does one molecule of glucose produce during glycolysis?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 4
Z) 6
ANSWER: Y) 4
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer Through oxidative phosphorylation, how many ATP, rounded to the nearest half-number, can one molecule of NADH and one molecule of FADH2 produce, respectively?
ANSWER: NADH is 2.5 and FADH2 is 1.5
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In an inducible operon such as the lac operon, what is the term for a protein such as CAP which detaches the repressor of a target gene?
ANSWER: activator
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In the regulation of the lac operon, CAP itself must be activated by which of the following molecules that is present in a low-glucose environment?
W) cAMP
X) AMP
Y) ATP
Z) NAD+
ANSWER: W) cAMP
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In the photoelectric effect, what the term for the minimum amount of energy needed to remove an electron from a solid to a point immediately outside the solid surface?
ANSWER: work function
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice When attempting to determine the location of an earthquake, how many separated seismographs are usually needed to pinpoint the exact location?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 3
Z) 4
ANSWER: Y) 3

W) 2
X) 10
Y) 100
Z) 1000
ANSWER: Z) 1000
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What type of covalent chemical bond, that is seen in quadruple bonds, is the overlap of the 4 lobes of electron orbitals in d subshells?
ANSWER: delta bonds
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What non-deterministic technique for determining the number of positive or negative real roots of a polynomial gives upper bounds for each value dependent on the signs of the coefficients?
ANSWER: Descartes' rule of signs
17. BONUS Math Short Answer Factor the binomial (x3 + y3) as a product of two polynomials with real coefficients.
ANSWER: (x+y)*(x2-xy+y2)
18. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In the most common method used in electrical power distribution grids, what is the phase angle that separates each line of alternating current?
W) 60 degrees
X) 90 degrees
Y) 120 degrees
Z) 180 degrees

19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer If the hypothamalus detects an imbalance in osmotic pressure, what neurohypophysial peptide hormone will it release to regulate kidney function?
ANSWER: ADH (also accept: antidiuretic hormone; vasopressin)
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What theorem in fluid dynamics, which is an application of Bernoulli's principle, relates the speed of a fluid flowing out of an opening to the height of the fluid above the opening?
ANSWER: Torricelli's law
20. BONUS Physics Short Answer The fluid flows at 10 m/s inside of a pipe is kept at constant pressure. If the medial elevation of the pipe rises 2 meters, what is the new velocity of the fluid, to one significant figure?
ANSWER: 8 m/s

W) top left
X) top right
Y) bottom left
Z) bottom right
ANSWER: X) top right
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice If the reactant quotient of a reversible reaction is less than the equillibrium constant, which of the following situation will occur?
W) The reaction will progress to the right
X) The reaction will progress to the left
Y) The reaction will not proceed
Z) The reaction quotient cannot be less than the equillibrium constant
ANSWER: W) The reaction will progress to the right
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 4 actions that would shift the equillibrium of the following reaction to the right: N2 + 3H2 <-> 2NH3 + heat 1: Decreasing the volume of the reaction chamber 2: Increasing the temperature 3: Increasing the concentration of hydrogen gas 4: Adding an iron oxide catalyst
ANSWER: 1 and 3
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the sum of an infinite series whose initial terms are: 1/3, 1/9, 1/27, 1/81, and so on?
ANSWER: 1/2
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following forms of tidal power generation utilizes the potential energy difference between high and low tides, and are essentially dams across the width of an entire tidal estuary?

X) Tidal barrage
Y) Static tidal generator
Z) Dynamic tidal generator

24. BONUS Physics Short Answer If the tidal range of the tide at a location is 10 meters, the density of sea water is 1000 kg/m3, and the area of the water surface of the tidal range is 106 meters squared, approximate the potential energy contained within this water, in SI units to two significant figures.

BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 3 traits that are typical of protostomes: 1: At the eight-cell stage, their cleavage is radial and indeterminate. 2: Their coeloms form schizocoelously, where solid masses of mesoderm split to form it. 3: The anus develops from the blastopore.
