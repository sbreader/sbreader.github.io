Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the type of electron flow where only photosystem I is used and photosystem II is omitted?
ANSWER: CYCLIC ELECTRON FLOW (also accept: CYCLIC PHOSPHORYLATION)
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer These are proteins that provide an innate defense against viral infections. Virus-infected body cells secrete these proteins. What are they?
ANSWER: Interferons
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The volume of a fixed amount of gas at constant pressure is directly proportional to the Kelvin temperature. What is this law also known as?
ANSWER: CHARLES'S LAW (also accept: CHARLES' LAW; CHARLES LAW)
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When the pressure exerted by escaping molecules equals that exerted by molecules of the atmosphere, what happens in a liquid?
ANSWER: IT BEGINS TO BOIL (also accept: IT BOILS; BOILING)
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the unit used to measure the ozone layer?
ANSWER: Dobson Unit
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the only silicate mineral consisting entirely of silicon and oxygen?
ANSWER: Quartz
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the state of matter where a gas is cooled to a very low temperature, in which atoms "condense"; they no longer display random motion in all directions, and progressively enter the same quantum state?
ANSWER: Bose-Einstein Condensate
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer This is a quantum mechanical phenomenon in which the quantum states of two or more objects have to be described with reference to each other, even though the individual objects may be spatially separated. What is it?
ANSWER: QUANTUM ENTANGLEMENT (also accept: ENTANGLEMENT)
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the partial derivative of 3x2 with respect to y?
ANSWER: 0
10. BONUS Math Short Answer In a normal distribution, approximately what percent of data falls within 2 standard deviations of the mean?
ANSWER: 95% (also accept: 95; 95.4%)
11. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which type of coal is popular for heating homes because it lacks dust and soot and burns for longer periods than other types of coal?
ANSWER: anthracite
