1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A merry-go-round has a radius of 3 meters and spins once per second. What is the ratio of the linear velocities of someone standing on the edge of the merry-go-round to someone standing halfway between the edge and center?
ANSWER: 2
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer In general relativity, what is the name of the partial differential equations connecting the metric to the mass and energy distribution and describing the curvature of spacetime?
ANSWER: Einstein field equations (also accept: EFE; field equations; Einstein’s equations)
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Cardiac sarcoma is a cancerous tumor found in what organ?
ANSWER: HEART
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer By number or name, identify all of the following four conditions that are hereditary 1) septicemia 2) fragile X syndrome 3) cystic fibrosis 4) sickle cell disease.
ANSWER: FRAGILE X SYNDROME, CYSTIC FIBROSIS, AND SICKLE CELL DISEASE (also accept: 2, 3, 4; ALL BUT 1)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the process that uses molten cryolite to produce aluminum?
ANSWER: HALL-HEROULT PROCESS
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Consider the silver electroplating of a spoon. Which of the following is true?
W) The spoon is the anode
X) The silver is the cathode
Y) Silver ions are reduced
Z) Silver ions are oxidized
ANSWER: Y) Silver ions are reduced
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice For a complex number r, which of the following ranges correctly describes the geometric sequence that converges, starting with the first two terms 1 and r?
W) |r| > 1
X) |r| >= 1
Y) |r| < 1
Z) |r| <= 1
ANSWER: Y) |r| < 1
4. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the derivative of xx?
ANSWER: xx(1 + ln(x)) (also accept: xx + xx*ln(x); xx + ln(xxx))
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the class of exoplanets which are gas giants that orbit very close to their star?
ANSWER: Hot Jupiters (also accept: Hot Saturns)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What object is created when a red giant sheds its outer layers?
W) Black hole
X) White Dwarf
Y) Red Dwarf
Z) Black dwarf
ANSWER: X) White Dwarf
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A certain runner can reach a maximum speed of 50 m/s. Which best describes what would happen if this runner tried to run across a lake?
W) The runner would immediately fall into the lake
X) The runner would run on the lake while slowing down, and then falling in
Y) The runner would be able to run across the lake
Z) None of the above
ANSWER: Y) The runner would be able to run across the lake
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer A system consists of blocks A and B with Mass M and 2M respectively, which are connected by a light string. It is pulled up a ramp with a coefficient of friction 0.3 by an external force, F. Assuming the external force acts directly on block A, what is the Tension in the string in terms of F?
ANSWER: 2/3 F
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Members of the phylum Arthropoda are distinguished by their exoskeleton. Of which of the following substances is their exoskeleton primarily composed?
W) Glucose
X) Chitin
Y) Cellulose
Z) Starch
ANSWER: X) Chitin
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms are NOT considered members of the phylum Arthropoda?
W) Centipedes
X) Snails
Y) Horseshoe Crabs
Z) Insects
ANSWER: X) Snails
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is true for cyclohexane?
W) The boat form is energetically favorable because of the proximity of the hydrogen atoms
X) The chair form is energetically favorable because of the proximity of the hydrogen atoms
Y) The boat form is energetically favorable because the hydrogen atoms are further apart
Z) The chair form is energetically favorable because the hydrogen atoms are further apart
ANSWER: Z) The chair form is energetically favorable because the hydrogen atoms are further apart
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What type of Newman projections are the most stable?
ANSWER: STAGGERED (also accept: ANTI CONFORMATION)
9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following sets of side lengths forms the triangle with the largest area?
W) 5, 10, 10
X) 10, 10, 10
Y) 15, 10, 10
Z) 19, 10, 10
ANSWER: Y) 15, 10, 10
9. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is the probability that 3 vertices randomly chosen on a regular hexagon form a right triangle?
W) 30%
X) 40%
Y) 50%
Z) 60%
ANSWER: Z) 60%
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Cassini division is located between which two rings of Saturn?
ANSWER: A and B
10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following would give the best estimate of the mass of an exoplanet given its maximum redshift and the mass of its star?
W) Angular Velocity
X) Orbital Eccentricity
Y) Angle of inclination
Z) Orbital Period
ANSWER: Y) Angle of inclination
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A uranium nucleus emits an alpha particle. Assuming charge is conserved, the resulting nucleus is of what element?
ANSWER: Thorium
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer A tree that is 3 meters tall is viewed from a distance of 25 meters. Assuming the cornea-to-retina distance of an ideal eye is 2 centimeters, how tall is the image of the tree on the observer’s retina in centimeters?
ANSWER: 0.24 centimeters
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the layer of the heart that is composed of cardiac muscle tissue.
ANSWER: MYOCARDIUM (do not accept: PERICARDIUM; EPICARDIUM; ENDOCARDIUM)
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which term is used to describe an elevated heart rate?
W) Tachycardia
X) Cardiac arrest
Y) Bradycardia
Z) Heart block
ANSWER: W) Tachycardia
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the substituent group that consists of benzene?
ANSWER: PHENYL GROUP (also accept: PHENYL RING)
13. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following aromatic compounds is most typically used in explosives?
W) Naphthalene
X) Anthracene
Y) Phenanthrene
Z) 3,4- Benzpyrene
ANSWER: Y) Phenanthrene
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the value of the binary number 0.101010… (zero point one zero repeating) in simplest fraction form in base 10?
ANSWER: 2/3
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Compute the area bounded by the graph of |x| + |y| = 4
ANSWER: 32
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Immanuel Kant’s original nebular hypothesis for the formation of the solar system was unable to explain which of the following?
W) Why particles stuck together
X) The formation of planets
Y) The shape of the sun’s disk
Z) None of the above
ANSWER: X) The formation of planets
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the weathering process caused by sand and gravel as it is transported, in either running water or a glacier?
ANSWER: Abrasion
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the name of the experiment that found that the speed of light is not affected by the motion of the source relative to the observer?
ANSWER: Michelson-Morley experiment
16. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice At what depth beneath the surface of a body of water is the pressure equal to twice the atmospheric pressure?
W) 10 meters
X) 20 meters
Y) 30 meters
Z) 40 meters
ANSWER: W) 10 meters
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Penicillin is an antibiotic. With which of the following processes does it interfere to prevent bacterial infection?
W) Formation of colonies
X) Binary fission
Y) Anaerobic respiration
Z) Cell wall synthesis
ANSWER: Z) Cell wall synthesis
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Bacterial meningitis involves the infection of the meninges. With what organ are the meninges associated?
ANSWER: BRAIN (also accept: SPINAL CORD)
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following polymers is known for the strength of its threads?
W) Nylon
X) Bakelite
Y) Cellulose
Z) Teflon
ANSWER: W) Nylon
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Consider ethylene molecules that undergo the process of addition polymerization. When the free radical initiates the process, which bond does it break?
ANSWER: PI BOND
19. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Suppose the function f(x) = 2x3-15x2+24x-7 has a domain of [0,6]. What is the range of f(x)?
W) [-7,4]
X) [-7,29]
Y) [-23,4]
Z) [-23,29]
ANSWER: Z) [-23,29]
19. BONUS Math Short Answer Given that the golden ratio equals (1+√5)/2, compute the golden ratio raised to the 6th power.
ANSWER: 9 + 4√5
20. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Most of Earth’s internal heat is created by:
W) Radioactive Decay
X) Volcanism
Y) Its magnetic field
Z) Earthquakes
ANSWER: W) Radioactive Decay
21. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In a circuit, a parallel combination of six 1.6 kilo-ohm resistors is connected in series with a parallel combination of four 2.4 kilo-ohm resistors. If the source voltage is 24 volts, what will be the percentage of total current in one of the 2.4 kilo-ohm resistors?
W) 10 percent
X) 15 percent
Y) 20 percent
Z) 25 percent
ANSWER: Z) 25 percent
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the name of the optical phenomenon where the refraction of light rays creates an image that is either distorted or displaced?
ANSWER: Mirage
