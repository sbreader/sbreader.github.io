1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In eukaryotes, the Kozak sequence is the initiation site for what process?
ANSWER: TRANSLATION
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer In a population of rats, brown fur color is dominant to black fur color. If 36% of the rats have black fur, then assuming that this population follows Hardy-Weinberg genetics, what percentage of the rat population is heterozygous for fur color?
ANSWER: 48%
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following aqueous solutions would have the HIGHEST boiling point?
W) 1 Molal Acetic Acid
X) 1 Molal Sulfuric Acid
Y) 1 Molal Phosphoric Acid
Z) 2 Molal Oxalic Acid
ANSWER: X) 1 Molal Sulfuric Acid
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following four types of radioactive decay in which the daughter nuclide’s atomic number is lower than that of the original nuclide I. Alpha II. Beta III. Gamma IV. Positron
ANSWER: I and IV (ALPHA AND POSITRON)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Most power plants model heat energy transfer through which cycle, which better models the phase change of water from liquid to vapor than the Carnot cycle?
W) Otto Cycle
X) Rankine Cycle
Y) Brayton Cycle
Z) Dual Cycle
ANSWER: X) Rankine Cycle
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer 6 moles of neon gas expands from 15 L to 30 L under 4 atmospheres. To two significant figures, how much work is done on the gas?
ANSWER: 6100 J (also accept: 6.1 × 103 J)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of variable stars would you expect to be MOST chemically similar to the Sun?
W) Type I Cepheid
X) Type II Cepheid
Y) RR Lyrae
Z) Wolf Rayet Star
ANSWER: W) Type I Cepheid
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer 21 centimeter radiation lines emitted by neutral hydrogen are useful for calculating what quantity, defined as the relationship between the orbital speeds of stars in a galaxy versus their radial distance from the galaxy’s center?
ANSWER: ROTATION CURVE (also accept: RADIAL VELOCITY)

W) ⅛
X) ¼
Y) ⅜
Z) ½
ANSWER: Y) ⅜

W) 0
X) 1
Y) 2
Z) 3
ANSWER: X) 1
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Los Alamos National Lab are using the Energetic Electrostatic Analyzer (EESA) to take measurements of the charged particle populations that exist in the terrestrial ionosphere and magnetosphere. What equation is used to interpret the readings from an electrostatic analyzer?
W) Poisson’s equation
X) Gauss’s law
Y) Lorentz force law
Z) Divergence Theorem
ANSWER: W) Poisson’s equation

BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are using neutron scattering to find magnetic correlations in semiconductors, with the hopes of developing high-performance thermoelectric materials. W) Ferromagnetic Phase
X) Ferrimagnetic Phase
Y) Paramagnetic Phase

ANSWER: ALL

Y) RNA contains uracil

12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory are creating and improving quantum machine learning models to help optimize the computing power of these devices. ANSWER: QUANTUM ENTANGLEMENT

14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer You’re given the following set of reactions A + B ⇌ AB (Fast) AB + A → A2 + B (Slow) A2 + B → C + D (Fast) Given that the rate constant for this reaction is k, what is the rate law for this reaction?
