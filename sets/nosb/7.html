Packet 7 (2018 Regional Round 7) of National Ocean Sciences Bowl:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following species forms monogamous pairs for life?
W) Striped parrotfish
X) French Angelfish
Y) Manta Ray
Z) Napoleon Wrasse
ANSWER: X) French Angelfish
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer As sediment laden freshwater mixes with seawater, what process aids in the rapid deposition of fine grained sediments?
ANSWER: Flocculation
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the typical Redfield Ratio of carbon to nitrogen to phosphorus in marine phytoplankton?
W) 16: 1: 1000
X) 100: 20: 5
Y) 106: 16: 1
Z) 3: 17: 250
ANSWER: Y) 106: 16: 1
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice An ocean becomes cut-off from the global ocean, and the seawater begins to evaporate. Which of the following minerals will NOT form in the resulting evaporite deposit?
W) Halite
X) Muscovite
Y) Gypsum
Z) Calcite
ANSWER: X) Muscovite
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Earth's rotation leads to currents on the western side of ocean gyres being stronger than the rest of the gyre. What is the name given to this process?
ANSWER: Western Intensification
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a component in a jellyfish sting?
W) Nematocysts
X) Porins
Y) Bioactive lipids
Z) Herpetotoxin
ANSWER: Z) Herpetotoxin
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is one potential human application of cone snail venom?
ANSWER: Pain medication (also accept: epilepsy medication)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A long, gradual bend or recess in the shoreline that forms a large, open bay describes what landform?
W) Spit
X) Estuary
Y) Bight
Z) Peninsula
ANSWER: Y) Bight
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which statement is NOT true regarding temperature distribution at the surface of the open ocean?
W) On average, isotherms run east-west
X) Near coastal boundaries, isotherms may divert in a north-south direction
Y) Surface temperatures are often higher along the eastern boundaries of the oceans due to upwellings
Z) The distribution of sea surface temperature corresponds closely with the input of short- wave radiation
ANSWER: Y) Surface temperatures are often higher along the eastern boundaries of the oceans due to upwellings
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the primary symptom of sea star wasting disease?
ANSWER: Melting limbs (also accept: disintegrating limbs)
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which underwater scenario is using an AUV preferable to using an ROV?
W) Collecting samples from the seafloor
X) Working in highly turbid environments
Y) Working in an unexplored area collecting visual data
Z) Large scale high resolution seafloor mapping
ANSWER: Z) Large scale high resolution seafloor mapping
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following species is solely nocturnal?
W) Lobster
X) Barnacle
Y) Salmon
Z) Tuna
ANSWER: W) Lobster
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the lowest maximum sustained wind speed needed for a tropical cyclone to be classified as a hurricane?
ANSWER: 74 mph (also accept: 64 knots)
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which is not a source of marine sediment that would be restricted to a particular latitude range?
W) Glacial sediment
X) Volcanic debris
Y) Wind-blown dust
Z) Carbonate sediments
ANSWER: X) Volcanic debris
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Peru-Chile Trench is located at the edge of which lithospheric plate?
W) Juan de Fuca
X) Cocos
Y) Nazca
Z) Scotia Plate
ANSWER: Y) Nazca
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Often called the “splash zone,” what is another name for the region above the high tide line that is covered by water only during extremely high tides or storm surges?
ANSWER: Supralittoral zone (also accept: supratidal zone; spray zone)
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Why do the Christmas Island crabs migrate?
W) To eat
X) To breed
Y) To sleep
Z) To swim
ANSWER: X) To breed
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer After only ten minutes of a constant wind, windrows, or linear convergences, form on the ocean surface parallel to the wind. What is this circulation called?
ANSWER: Langmuir circulation (also accept: Langmuir cells)
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What effect – often caused by topographic features like mountains – causes precipitation on the windward side, but dryness on the leeward side?
ANSWER: Rain-shadow effect
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which event is thought to have preceded the flow of the Antarctic Circumpolar Current, approximately 30 million years ago?
W) An ice sheet collapsed at high latitudes
X) The Isthmus of Panama closed
Y) The Tasmanian Seaway closed
Z) The Drake Passage opened
ANSWER: Z) The Drake Passage opened
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer During which geologic era did Pangea break up and the Atlantic Ocean begin forming?
ANSWER: Mesozoic
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which is NOT a conservative tracer of seawater?
W) Salinity
X) Temperature
Y) Density
Z) Dissolved oxygen
ANSWER: Z) Dissolved oxygen
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the primary component in the trail of mucus secreted by slugs and snails?
ANSWER: Water
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which of the following marine environments would you find graded beds resulting from turbidity currents?
W) Center of ocean gyres
X) Southern Ocean
Y) Along the shoreline
Z) Just below continental rise
ANSWER: Z) Just below continental rise
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a mineral produced by organisms in the ocean?
W) Apatite
X) Corundum
Y) Chert
Z) Aragonite
ANSWER: X) Corundum
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The California Current, Eastern Tropical Pacific region, and the coast of Namibia are all examples of what?
ANSWER: Upwelling zones
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Arctic winter warming events are winter days when air temperatures peak above which value in degrees Celsius?
W) -30
X) -20
Y) -10
Z) 1
ANSWER: Y) -10
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer What mammal has the densest fur?
ANSWER: Sea otter (also accept: Enhydra lutris)
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice If the concentration of the sodium ion was found to be 11 grams per liter at the sea surface, what would you expect it to be at 1000m?
W) 20 grams per liter
X) 5 grams per liter
Y) 0.011 grams per liter
Z) 11 grams per liter
ANSWER: Z) 11 grams per liter
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A 2016 study published in Nature states that with increasing greenhouse gases in the atmosphere, clouds have shifted in which two directions?
ANSWER: Poleward and higher in the atmosphere
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Data collected by NASA’s SeaWiFS satellite is used to make estimates of ocean productivity by measuring the color produced by what biomolecule?
ANSWER: Chlorophyll (also accept: Chlorophyll-A)
