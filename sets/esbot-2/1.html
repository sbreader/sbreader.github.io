1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Kaiwen is repairing a brake system on a car. To prevent overheating of the brake pads, he attaches a heat sink to them with a contact area of 0.05 square meters. If the wheel of the car is spinning at 60 radians per second and the brakes apply a torque of 150 Newton-meters, how much power in Watts must the heat sink absorb to avoid overheating?
ANSWER: 9000

3. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is logically equivalent to the saying, “If a student attends Enloe High School, then they are a nerd”?

6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Kaiwen is studying the effectiveness of different radioactive isotopes in nuclear bombs.
