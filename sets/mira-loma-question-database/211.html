1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following disorders is a dominantly inherited disorder?
W) Albinism
X) Cystic Fibrosis
Y) Achondroplasia
Z) Sickle-Cell Disease
ANSWER: Y) Achondroplasia
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which model of flower formation identifies how three classes of genes direct the formation of the four types of flower organs?
ANSWER: ABC MODEL
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In plants, what is the name of the barrier made of suberin and located in the transverse and radial walls of each endodermal cell?
ANSWER: CASPARIAN STRIP
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which part of the human body contains nerve cells that function as a thermostat, responding to body temperatures outside the normal range by activating mechanisms that promote heat loss or gain?
ANSWER: HYPOTHALAMUS
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Constant exposure to strontium ions can lead to anemia and leukemia since strontium ions can replace which of the following ions?
W) Calcium ions
X) Potassium ions
Y) Iron ions
Z) Sodium ions
ANSWER: W) Calcium ions
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In a balanced chemical equation in equilibrium, the left side has 4 moles of molecules and the right side has 3 moles of molecules. In which direction will the equilibrium shift if the pressure on the system is decreased?
ANSWER: TOWARDS THE LEFT
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following diatomic molecules has the highest average bond energy?
W) H2
X) F2
Y) O2
Z) N2
ANSWER: Z) N2
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which famous physicist studied Carnot's theory and from it, developed the idea of the second law of thermodynamics?
ANSWER: KELVIN (also accept: WILLIAM THOMSON)
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following properties is not a property of an isolated conductor?
W) The electric field is zero everywhere inside the conducting material
X) Any excess charge on lresides entirely on the surface
Y) The electric field just outside a charged conductor is perpendicular to the conductor's surface
Z) On an irregularly shaped conductor, charge accumulates where the radius of curvature of the surface is the largest
ANSWER: Z) On an irregularly shaped conductor, charge accumulates where the radius of curvature of the surface is the largest
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The symptom in a person with advanced arteriosclerosis is produced by an effect. This symptom occurs when the artery is constricted as a result of the accumulation of plaque on its inner walls. Name this effect or principle that causes vascular flutters.
ANSWER: BERNOULLI EFFECT
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Name the method of transforming electrical energy into mechanical energy.
ANSWER: PIEZOELECTRIC EFFECT
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The average separation between galaxies is about how many times their diameter?
ANSWER: 20 (also accept: anywhere from 10 to 30)
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to small nebulae that fluctuate in brightness. This object is named after the two astronomers who discovered it?
ANSWER: HERBIG-HARO OBJECT
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the effect that spreads out the observed spectral lines due to impacts between atoms?
ANSWER: COLLISIONAL BROADENING
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is an incorrect statement and metamorphic rocks?
W) High grade of metamorphism results in large crystal size
X) Gneiss is the result of low-grade metamorphism
Y) Low grade of metamorphism results in low coarseness
Z) Schist is abundant in micaceous minerals
ANSWER: X) Gneiss is the result of low-grade metamorphism
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the rock at the frontier between igneous and metamorphic rocks called?
ANSWER: MIGMATITE
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are wind-faceted pebbles having several curved or almost flat surfaces that meet at sharp angles called?
ANSWER: VENTIFACTS
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the slope of the line normal to the following curve at x equals 2: 3x2 + x + 2
ANSWER: -(1/13)
21. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Joe's age is twice Bob's age. If the sum of the squares of their ages is 80, what are their ages?
ANSWER: BOB IS 4. JOE IS 8
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Given 6 people are standing in a line, how many possible ways can they be arranged?
ANSWER: 720
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What was the approximate magnitude of the earthquake that happened in Gujarat, India in January, 2001?
ANSWER: 8
