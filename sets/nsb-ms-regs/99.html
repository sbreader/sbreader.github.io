1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following laws most directly limits the ability of a simple machine to an efficiency of 100% or lower:
W) Hooke's law
X) law of equal proportions
Y) the first law of motion
Z) the law of conservation of energy
ANSWER: Z) the law of conservation of energy
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer To the first decimal place, what is the ideal mechanical advantage of a lever with a resistance arm of 200 centimeters and an effort arm of 3.4 meters?
ANSWER: 1.7
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following characteristics is most commonly associated with a tsunami wave as opposed to a normal ocean wave in the open ocean:
W) period of 5 seconds
X) wavelength of 300 miles
Y) speed of 50 miles per hour
Z) motion of only the uppermost layer of water
ANSWER: X) wavelength of 300 miles
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a true tidal wave:
W) tsunami
X) tidal bore
Y) rip current
Z) tidal range
ANSWER: X) tidal bore
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Divide x5 by x3 and simplify:
ANSWER: x2
4. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following does the prefix "endo" mean, as in the word endoscope:
W) under
X) dark
Y) inside
Z) invisible
ANSWER: Y) inside
4. BONUS Energy Short Answer Arrange the following 3 choices in order of INCREASING speed: 3 feet per minute; 5 yards per minute; 30 yards per hour
ANSWER: 30 YARDS PER HOUR; 3 FEET PER MINUTE; 5 YARDS PER MINUTE
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following anatomical structures do sharks NOT possess:
W) gill covers or opercula
X) lateral lines
Y) cartilaginous skeletons
Z) livers
ANSWER: W) gill covers or opercula
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What scientist is sometimes considered the father of genetics because of his extensive studies on the patterns of inheritance?
ANSWER: GREGOR MENDEL (also accept: MENDEL)
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the mass of an electron:
W) about the same as a proton
X) about ½ as much as a proton
Y) about 1/1,800th of a proton
Z) electrons have no mass
ANSWER: Y) about 1/1,800th of a proton
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 10,000-newton car is on the top floor of a parking garage 25 meters above the ground. Relative to the ground, what is the gravitational potential energy, in joules, of the car?
ANSWER: 250,000 (also accept: 2.5 × 105)
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Streams running off the terminus of glaciers have a high bed load and easily erodible banks, typically forming which of the following types of stream patterns:
W) meandering
X) oxbow
Y) braided
Z) back swamp
ANSWER: Y) braided
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is similar to a delta but forms when a steep mountain stream spreads out from a mountain canyon onto a flat, broad valley:
W) alluvial fan
X) levee
Y) playa
Z) flood plain
ANSWER: W) alluvial fan
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Solve the following equation for x: 15x + 8x – 3x = 120
ANSWER: 6
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following BEST describes the SI unit called the Newton:
W) a unit of power
X) a unit of force
Y) a unit of work
Z) a unit of energy
ANSWER: X) a unit of force
9. BONUS Energy Short Answer Rounded to the nearest whole number, how many meters per second is equal to 40 kilometers per hour?
ANSWER: 11
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many phalanges are in a human thumb?
ANSWER: 2
10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following terms is most often used to describe a biochemical reaction where the forward reaction is at the same rate as the reverse reaction:
W) isothermic
X) isotropic
Y) equilibrium
Z) homeotropic
ANSWER: Y) equilibrium
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Alkali metals all have the same:
W) number of electrons in their outer shells
X) atomic radii
Y) electronegativities
Z) melting points
ANSWER: W) number of electrons in their outer shells
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following liquids at room temperature and 1 atmosphere would have the GREATEST surface tension:
W) distilled water
X) ethanol
Y) methanol
Z) acetone
ANSWER: W) distilled water
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following most likely formed a U-shaped valley:
W) glacial erosion
X) mountain stream erosion
Y) wind erosion
Z) deforestation
ANSWER: W) glacial erosion
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following gives glacial meltwater a quality which is commonly described as glacial milk:
W) ice crystals in the water
X) the abundance of trapped air
Y) large amounts of suspended material
Z) the abundance of cyanobacteria
ANSWER: Y) large amounts of suspended material
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Name all of the following 3 statements which are always TRUE 1) a square is a regular polygon 2) an equilateral triangle is equiangular 3) a scalene triangle has 2 sides of equal length
ANSWER: 1 AND 2

X) hypsometer
