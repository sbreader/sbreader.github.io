1. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice How many bits are there in a single byte
W) 6
X) 10
Y) 12
Z) 8
ANSWER: Z) 8
1. BONUS Energy Short Answer How many bytes in a single kilobyte
ANSWER: 1024
2. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Who cofounded Microsoft alongside Bill Gates?
ANSWER: Paul Allen
2. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the name of the operating system used by Microsoft and is still used today
ANSWER: Windows
3. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What part of a computer is directly accessible by the CPU and is used to store immediately needed data
ANSWER: Random Access Memory (also accept: RAM)
3. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the part of the computer that holds most of the internal memory and is mostly always greater in space than the RAM?
ANSWER: Hard Disk
5. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What is the numerical value of the prefix "nano"
W) .000001
X) .0001
Y) .00000001
Z) .000000001
ANSWER: Z) .000000001
5. BONUS Energy Short Answer What prefix with a numerical value multiplied by the numerical value of the prefix "micro" would equal the numerical value of the prefix "nano"
ANSWER: milli; .001
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name two of the three people who won the Nobel Prize in 1945 for the discovery and development of penicillin
ANSWER: Name two of the following three: Fleming, Florey and Chain
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What kind of bacteria did this penicillin kill
ANSWER: Staphylococci bacteria (STAFF-UH-LUH-COKE-I)
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What two substances are often used to fill thermometers?
ANSWER: Alcohol and Mercury
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the first and last name of the person the Nobel Prize is named after
ANSWER: Alfred Nobel

ANSWER: Johannes Gutenburg
