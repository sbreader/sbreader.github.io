1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Simon has an infection. Only the memory B and T cells with the right antigen receptors for the specific pathogen that Simon has are proliferated, resulting in a targeted adaptive immune response. What is the name for this process?
ANSWER: CLONAL SELECTION
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Counterintuitive but evolutionarily favorable, the left side of the brain receives information and controls the right side of the body, and vice versa. This is due to what phenomenon that occurs at the medulla oblongata, where the majority of nerve fibers cross over?
ANSWER: DECUSSATION
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In the urea cycle, what intermediate is cleaved by argininosuccinase to produce arginine and what four-carbon dicarboxylic acid that links the urea cycle to the TCA cycle?
ANSWER: FUMARATE
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice SH2 domains are highly conserved protein modules that allow proteins to dock onto other proteins to initiate intracellular signaling. These domains specifically recognize and bind to which of the following modified amino acid residues?
W) Phosphorylated serine
X) Phosphorylated tyrosine
Y) Acetylated lysine
Z) Methylated arginine
ANSWER: X) Phosphorylated tyrosine
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Astrocytes primarily supply energy to neurons in which of the following forms?
W) Glucose
X) Pyruvate
Y) Acetyl-CoA
Z) Lactate
ANSWER: Z) Lactate
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In the central nervous system, glutamate is converted to GABA by glutamate decarboxylase. Which of the following vitamins serves as the cofactor for glutamate decarboxylase?
W) Riboflavin
X) Pantothenic Acid
Y) Pyridoxine
Z) Biotin
ANSWER: Y) Pyridoxine
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What highly energetically efficient ancient pathway, common in acetogenic bacteria, fixes carbon dioxide into Acetyl CoA?
ANSWER: WOODLJUNG-DAHL PATHWAY
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer In the methionine cycle, the demethylation of S-adenosylmethionine eventually leads to the formation of what non-proteinogenic, sulfur-containing amino acid, which can be further converted into cysteine or remethylated back to methionine?
ANSWER: HOMOCYSTEINE
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Dr. Robert runs an X-ray on his patient's lung, noticing that air has traveled into the pleural space, causing the lung to collapse. What is this phenomena known as?
ANSWER: PNEUMOTHORAX
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Jacquevion suspects that he has Hashimoto's disease. To try to increase his thyroid hormone synthesis, he seasons his steak with 15 pounds of iodized salt and gobbles it up. However, when he goes to test his thyroid hormone levels the next day, he notices that the concentrations are lower than they previously were. This is an example of what effect, where high intake of iodine temporarily inhibits thyroid peroxidase, and thus inhibits T4 and T3 synthesis?
ANSWER: WOLFF-CHAIKOFF EFFECT
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The Pyruvate Dehydrogenase Complex requires five distinct cofactors for its activity. Identify all of the following three molecules that serve as cofactors for this complex 1) Thiamine pyrophosphate 2) Lipoate 3) Flavin adenine dinucleotide
ANSWER: All
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer In the termination of GPCR signaling, what family of cytosolic proteins binds to the phosphorylated C-terminus of the receptor, physically sterically hindering further G-protein activation and facilitating clathrin-mediated endocytosis?
ANSWER: ARRESTIN
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Neutrophils are able to perform margination because of specific lectins that allow them to bind to the endothelial wall. What are the names of these lectins?
ANSWER: SELECTINS
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice During the baroreceptor reflex, information about arterial blood pressure from the walls of the carotid sinus is transmitted to the medulla oblongata by which of the following cranial nerves?
W) Trigeminal
X) Abducens
Y) Glossopharyngeal
Z) Accessory
ANSWER: Y) Glossopharyngeal
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an example of a phenolic, which is a class of plant secondary metabolites?
W) Tannins
X) Flavonoids
Y) Lignin
Z) Carotenoids
ANSWER: Z) Carotenoids
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Often containing uncommon amino acids such as mugineic acid, what class of compounds is commonly released by grasses into soil to chelate iron and increase the capacity of iron cation exchange?
ANSWER: PHYTOSIDEROPHORES
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Diabetic Retinopathy, surely not at all caused by diabetes mellitus, leads to the damage of retinal blood vessels and leakage of fluid from those vessels. Which of the following signaling proteins, secreted in an attempt to repair the retinal damage, instead leads to choroidal neovascularization and a positive feedback loop of worsening vessel leakage?
W) Hypoxia-Inducible Factor alpha
X) Fibroblast growth factor 2
Y) Vascular endothelial growth factor
Z) Stromal-derived factor 1
ANSWER: Y) Vascular endothelial growth factor
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three hormones that are elevated in Diabetes insipidus 1) Insulin 2) ANP 3) Aldosterone
ANSWER: 3 only
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The carnitine shuttle is most involved in which of the following pathways?
W) Fatty acid synthesis
X) Beta oxidation
Y) Urea cycle
Z) Cori cycle
ANSWER: X) Beta oxidation
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer A Lineweaver-Burke plot is only one of many ways to linearize the Michaelis Menten plot. What other plot linearizes the curve by plotting V0 against V0 / [S]?
ANSWER: EADIE-HOOFSTEE PLOT (also accept: EADIE PLOT; HOFSTEE PLOT)
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A significantly negative Tajima's D value indicates which of the following?
W) Balancing selection
X) Population bottleneck
Y) Recent selective sweep
Z) Neutral selection for the allele
ANSWER: Y) Recent selective sweep
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer William is studying a gene with two alleles, A and a. In a population, the genotype frequencies are 0.1 AA, 0.4 Aa, and 0.5 aa. If the recessive phenotype has a selection coefficient of 1, then what is the frequency of the A allele in the next generation?
ANSWER: 0.16
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Analagous to the companion cells of angiosperms, what term describes the cells in gymnosperms that provide nutrients to phloem?
ANSWER: ALBUMINOUS CELLS (also accept: STRASBURGER)
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What specific type of accessory fruit derives their fleshiness from the receptacle or floral tube?
ANSWER: POMES
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following traits is a defining feature of fungi that are glomeromycetes?
W) Septate Hyphae
X) Arbuscular Mycorrhizae
Y) Conidiophores
Z) Basidium
ANSWER: X) Arbuscular Mycorrhizae
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A biologist identifies an echinoderm that is very similar to a starfish, but notices that it's central body disk branches off from the arms, giving it a 'brittle' appearance. To which of the following classes of echinoderms does this organism belong to?
W) Asteroidea
X) Holothuroidea
Y) Crinoidea
Z) Ophiuroidea
ANSWER: Z) Ophiuroidea
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Stimulation of the SA and AV nodes, activation of the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system, and secretion of ghrelin are all mechanisms dependent on the presence of what type of adrenergic receptor?
W) alpha-1
X) alpha-2
Y) beta-1
Z) beta-2
ANSWER: Y) beta-1
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What blood clotting factor induces primary hemostasis, carries factor VIII, and is associated with a namesake disease?
ANSWER: VON WILLEBRAND FACTOR
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What process describes the rapid interconversion of alpha to beta glucose?
ANSWER: MUTAROTATION
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name for the organelle composed of a large cytoplasmic ribonucleoprotein whose function is still unknown and conserved across almost all eukaryotic cells?
ANSWER: VAULTS
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In Manhatten plots, single nucleotide polymorphisms are graphed from each chromosome to show their association with genetic diseases. This type of plot is frequently used for what observational studies where genetic variants are associated with certain traits?
ANSWER: GENOME WIDE ASSOCIATION STUDIES (also accept: GWAS)
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Under neutral coalescent theory, the expected time to the most recent common ancestor of two alleles is proportional to which of the following?
W) Mutation rate
X) Effective population size
Y) Recombination frequency
Z) Linkage disequilibrium
ANSWER: X) Effective population size

ANSWER: STROBILI

Y) Root, Exarch
Z) Root, Endarch

W) Fungi
X) Bacteria

19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In population dynamics, what describes a group of spatially separated populations of the same species which interact at some level?
ANSWER: METAPOPULATION

W) Vavilovian
X) Wasmannian
Y) Batesian
Z) Muellerian

20. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Upon the binding of acetylcholine to nicotinic acetylcholine receptors in the adrenal medulla, chromaffin cells are stimulated to produce catecholamines. What is the ratio of epinephrine to norepinephrine secreted by chromaffin cells when stimulated?
W) 1:4
X) 1:2
Y) 2:1
Z) 4:1
ANSWER: Z) 4:1
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What statistical test is used to detect and measure the amount of adaptive evolution within a species by comparing the amount of nonsynonymous to synonymous polymorphisms within a species to other species?
ANSWER: MCDONALD KREITMAN TEST
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer While monoamine oxidase degrades catecholamines within the presynaptic terminal, catecholamine degradation in non-neuronal tissues like the liver and kidney is primarily carried out by what other enzyme?
ANSWER: COMT (also accept: CATECHOL-O-METHYLTRANSFERASE)
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Some plants are responsible for releasing chemicals into the environment that inhibit the germination and growth of other plants. What is this phenomena known as?
ANSWER: ALLELOPATHY
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer Atticus the Agriculturalist, heavily inspired by Mendel, is cross-pollinating his pea plants. The parental generation includes 2 peas heterozygous for both pod size and color, and when crossed, produces a large F1 generation. If 2 pea plants from the F1 generation are randomly selected and crossed, what is the probability that the resulting pea in the f2 generation will have a recombinant phenotype?
ANSWER: 3/8
