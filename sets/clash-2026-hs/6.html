1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What physical law, derived from the conservation of mass and momentum, states that the maximum power efficiency of a wind turbine is approximately 59.3%?
ANSWER: Betz's law
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the image created by a concave lens?
W) Virtual and inverted
X) Virtual and upright
Y) Real and inverted
Z) Real and upright
ANSWER: X) Virtual and upright
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer By name or number, order the following 4 means of a set of distinct real numbers by increasing value 1) Geometric mean 2) Harmonic mean 3) Arithmetic mean 4) Quadratic mean
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3, 4
2. BONUS Math Short Answer For the differential equation dy/dx = 3x2*y, find the particular solution such that y(0) = 1.
ANSWER: y = ex3
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following 3 ear diseases by increasing distance from the pinna of the region affected 1) Meniere's disease 2) Otitis media 3) Otitis externa
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice When a plant closes its stomata, the movement of which of the following ions is most directly responsible for the osmotic movement of water into the guard cells?
W) Ca2+
X) Mg2+
Y) Na+
Z) K+
ANSWER: Z) K+

X) 10 kilograms

Z) 10000 kilograms
ANSWER: Z) 10000 kilograms

ANSWER: 3

ANSWER: 2 and 3
