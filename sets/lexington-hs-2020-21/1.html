1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The decay of uranium 238, which has an atomic number of 92, to lead 206, which has an atomic number of 82, has 14 steps. Which of the following correctly gives how many steps in the decay pathway are alpha decay and beta minus decay, respectively?
W) 6 alpha, 8 beta minus
X) 7 alpha, 7 beta minus
Y) 8 alpha, 6 beta minus
Z) 9 alpha, 5 beta minus
ANSWER: Y) 8 alpha, 6 beta minus
