Packet 283 (Sci Bowl 12 #4 ERSC) of Mira Loma Question Database:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the Precambrian fossils which are the remains of the material deposited by algae, found in distinctive layers of calcium carbonate?
ANSWER: STROMATOLITES
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The pelagic, benthic, and abyssal ocean life zones classify parts of the ocean by what factor?
ANSWER: DEPTH
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following most accurately estimates the maximum spring tidal range?
W) 10 meters
X) 15 meters
Y) 17 meters
Z) 19 mete
ANSWER: Y) 17 meters
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What tidal pattern is characterized by two high tides and two low tides each tidal day?
ANSWER: SEMIDIURNAL
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What class of fog is characterized by cool air moving over warm water, causing evaporation from the water surface?
ANSWER: STEAM FOG
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer If the Bergeron Process is to cold clouds, then what process is to warm clouds?
ANSWER: COLLISION-COALESENCE PROCESS
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In what direction does the jet stream flow?
W) East to West
X) West to East
Y) North to South
Z) South to North
ANSWER: X) West to East
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the process in which the position of a cold front advances faster than the warm front and begins to lift the warm front?
ANSWER: OCCLUSION
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What wind speed, in miles per hour, must a tropical storm reach to be considered a hurricane?
ANSWER: 74
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which meteor shower, associated with the Halley's comet, occurs around May 3rd through 5th?
ANSWER: ETA AQUARIDS
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the problem that occurs with refractive telescopes, due to the fact that when focused on red light, blue and violet light are out of focus?
ANSWER: CHROMATIC ABERRATION
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What diagram, named after the scientist that created it, is used to model the sunspot cycle?
ANSWER: MAUNDER BUTTERFLY DIAGRAM
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the reaction which converts 4 hydrogen nuclei in to the nucleus of a helium atom?
ANSWER: PROTON PROTON CHAIN
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In which corner of the HR diagram would the brightest, hottest stars be found?
ANSWER: UPPER LEFT
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The star Proxima Centauri would be found closest to what corner of an HR Diagram?
ANSWER: BOTTOM RIGHT (also accept: LOWER RIGHT)
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice If the initial mass of the interstellar cloud is 6 times the mass of the Sun, what would be the terminal state of the star created from this cloud?
W) White Dwarf
X) Black Hole
Y) Neutron Star
Z) Planet
ANSWER: W) White Dwarf
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In what constellation is the Crab Nebula found?
ANSWER: TAURUS
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What galaxy type is the most abundant, making up 60% of all galaxies?
ANSWER: ELLIPTICAL
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of moraine is produced when an ice front occasionally stabilizes during retreat, and deposits a layer of till?
ANSWER: RECESSIONAL END MORAINE
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What Philippine volcano, which erupted in June 1991, injected nearly 25 million tons of sulfur dioxide into the atmosphere, and provided scientists with the opportunity to study the climactic effects of a major volcanic eruption?
ANSWER: MOUNT PINATUBO
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of a large body of air characterized by the sameness of temperature and humidity?
ANSWER: AIR MASS
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of a dune formed with scalloped rows, oriented at right angles to the wind?
ANSWER: BARCHANOID
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the lifting of air which results when cool air acts as a barrier over which warmer air rises?
ANSWER: FRONTAL WEDGING
