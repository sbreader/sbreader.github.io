1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The Alpha Magnetic Spectrometer, or AMS, is an experiment aboard the ISS conducted by CERN that detects antimatter found in cosmic rays. The amount of matter significantly outnumbers the number of antiparticles in our current universe because of an asymmetry in the production of matter and antimatter during the big bang. If, however, equal amounts of matter and antimatter were produced during the big bang, the universe would be filled exclusively with what particle?
ANSWER: PHOTONS
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the reason dark matter is given its name?
W) It is the matter that composes a perfect black body
X) It is radiation that is released after a supernova which creates a black dwarf
Y) It doesn’t interact with normal matter and especially light
Z) It is a hypothetical particle just like the Higgs boson but its function is not known yet
ANSWER: Y) It doesn’t interact with normal matter and especially light
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about R-selected species is not true?
W) R-selected species generally have type III survivorship curves
X) The population of R-selected species generally remains stable and close to the carrying capacity
Y) R-selected species are found in environments with little competition
Z) Density dependent inhibition is not a significant threat to populations of R-selected species
ANSWER: X) The population of R-selected species generally remains stable and close to the carrying capacity
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is correct about a country with a crude birth rate of 20 and a crude death rate of 14?

X) It has a 0.6 percent population growth rate.

Z) The population growth rate depends on net immigration along with birth and death rates, so it can’t be determined with the given data.
ANSWER: Z) The population growth rate depends on net immigration along with birth and death rates, so it can’t be determined with the given data.
