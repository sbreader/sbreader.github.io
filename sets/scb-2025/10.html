8. BONUS Biology Short Answer In most cells, methionine serves as the initial amino acid. In bacteria, a separate tRNA is used for regular methionine, and a separate one as the initiator tRNA. ANSWER: Formyl
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Venus has a large isotopic ratio of deuterium to protium, roughly 100x that of Earth. Which of the following is a potential reason for this?
W) Venus formed closer to the Sun and thus accreted more refractory elements.
X) Venus experienced substantial water loss earlier in its history.
Y) Venus’s thick atmosphere produces strong greenhouse warming through carbon dioxide.
Z) Venus’s slow retrograde rotation leads to weak Coriolis forces and reduced global circulation.
ANSWER: X) Venus experienced substantial water loss earlier in its history.
