Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following properties, if increased, would correspond to an increase in the density of air 1) Dew Point 2) Temperature 3) Pressure
ANSWER: 3 only
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In the “drip tectonics” model, drops of the lithosphere delaminate from the base of continents primarily due to the rock's transformation into what metamorphic facies?
ANSWER: Eclogite
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following properties of the Sun most directly causes the apparent movement of sunspots over a solar cycle?
W) Magnetic pole reversals
X) Differential rotation
Y) Centripetal force from rotation
Z) Surface convections
ANSWER: X) Differential rotation
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four solar system bodies by increasing surface escape velocity 1) Moon 2) Pluto 3) Mercury 4) Venus
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3, 4
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Pancake ice is a precursor for the formation of which of the following?
W) Permafrost
X) Sea ice
Y) Alpine glaciers
Z) Pingos
ANSWER: X) Sea ice
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of sedimentary basins generally has the highest concentration of carbonate minerals?
W) Continental rift
X) Passive margin
Y) Intracontinental
Z) Foreland
ANSWER: X) Passive margin
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The occurrence of earthquakes as a result of stress build-up and release cycles in Earth’s crust is best described by what theory?
ANSWER: Elastic rebound theory (Stick Slip)
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A magma with granodioritic composition will likely erupt to form what extrusive igneous rock?
ANSWER: Dacite
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Rogue planets are planetary-mass objects that do not orbit a star and instead drift freely through interstellar space. Which of the following detection methods is most commonly used to find these objects?
W) Microlensing
X) Transit photometry
Y) Radial velocity
Z) Direct imaging
ANSWER: W) Microlensing
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 conditions under which forbidden atomic transitions would be likely to occur 1) High Density 2) High Temperature 3) Low Collision Rate
ANSWER: 3 only
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Cinder Cone volcanoes are primarily composed of rocks of which of the following igneous texture?
W) Phaneritic
X) Vesicular
Y) Aphanitic
Z) Porphyritic
ANSWER: X) Vesicular
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following terms is given to the period in a tidal cycle where the tide is neither advancing nor retreating?
W) Ebb
X) Flood
Y) Slack
Z) Amphidromic
ANSWER: Y) Slack
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true regarding winter solstice in the Northern Hemisphere?
W) The Earth is near its perihelion
X) Antarctica receives 24 hours of daylight
Y) Sun reaches its lowest daily maximum altitude in the sky
Z) It is June in the Southern Hemisphere
ANSWER: Z) It is June in the Southern Hemisphere
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A parcel of air has a temperature of 300 kelvin and a dew point of 280 kelvin. Assuming the dry adiabatic lapse rate is 10 Kelvin per kilometer and the dew point lapse rate is 2 Kelvin per kilometer, what is the lifting condensation level in kilometers?
ANSWER: 2.5
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following regions in a spiral galaxy possesses stars and star clusters but minimum dust and gas?
W) Disk
X) Halo
Y) Central bulge
Z) Nucleus
ANSWER: X) Halo
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Most of the ejecta rays in the Moon’s southern hemisphere originate from what specific feature on its surface?
ANSWER: Tycho Crater
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the brightest stars usually observed in elliptical galaxies, in comparison to the sun?
W) Hotter and larger
X) Hotter and smaller
Y) Cooler and larger
Z) Cooler and smaller
ANSWER: Y) Cooler and larger
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding Seyfert galaxies 1) Their luminosity is primarily due to accretion by central black holes 2) They exhibit the broadest spectral lines when viewed edge-on 3) Most Seyfert galaxies appear like regular spirals when viewed in x-ray
ANSWER: 1 only
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In mylonites, which of the following group of minerals commonly form lenticular, elongated crystals that can be used to identify shear direction?
W) Garnet
X) Mica
Y) Feldspar
Z) Quartz
ANSWER: X) Mica
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following four deposits in increasing distance from the volcano typically reached 1) Lava flow 2) Pyroclastic flow 3) Lahar 4) Ash fall
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2, 4
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Over which of the following ocean currents is sea smoke least likely to occur?
W) Agulhas current
X) Brazil current
Y) Somali current
Z) Gulf Stream
ANSWER: Y) Somali current
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Flora walks across an outcrop, encountering 3 layers in the order of A, B, C, B, A. Flora also discovers that layer C is from the Cretaceous period, while layer A is from the Triassic period. Which of the following structures could she have encountered?
W) Dome
X) Monocline
Y) Syncline
Z) Normal fault
ANSWER: Y) Syncline
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Saturn’s moon Phoebe is a captured Kuiper Belt object. Which of the following is least accurate regarding the orbit of Phoebe around Saturn?
W) High inclination
X) High eccentricity
Y) Small semi-major axis
Z) Retrograde motion
ANSWER: Y) Small semi-major axis
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Binary star systems that can only be identified through Doppler shifts would be best classified as which of the following?
W) Astrometric
X) Eclipsing
Y) Spectroscopic
Z) Visual
ANSWER: Y) Spectroscopic
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes a Bok globule?
W) An emission nebula where star formation is taking place
X) A dark nebula undergoing gravitational collapse
Y) A cold interstellar medium with extremely low density
Z) A congregation of planetesimals in the protoplanetary disk
ANSWER: X) A dark nebula undergoing gravitational collapse
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer According to the no-hair theorem, arrange the following three black holes in increasing number of non-zero quantities 1) Reissner-Nordstrom 2) Schwartzschild 3) Kerr-Newman
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following that are indications of young tropical soil 1) Low cation exchange capacity 2) Well defined horizons 3) Presence of E horizon
ANSWER: 1 only
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice An annular drainage pattern is most likely to be associated with which of the following geologic features?
W) Syncline
X) Rift valley
Y) Volcano
Z) Basin
ANSWER: Z) Basin
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals or mineral groups primarily composes kimberlite?
W) Quartz
X) Diamond
Y) Plagioclase feldspar
Z) Olivine
ANSWER: Z) Olivine
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following statements regarding cyclogenesis that are true according to the polar front theory 1) In the open wave stage, the cold front moves faster and “catches up” to the warm front 2) The occluded front first forms in the dissipation stage 3) The occluded front spirals anticyclonically around a low pressure center
ANSWER: 1 only
