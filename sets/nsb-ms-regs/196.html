1. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following describes the lines represented by the equations 5x + 3y = 15 and –10x – 6y = 60?
W) They are parallel
X) They are skew
Y) They intersect at exactly one point
Z) They are the same line
ANSWER: W) They are parallel
1. BONUS Math Short Answer Nelly bought a mixture of two types of her favorite candies, costing 40 cents and 50 cents per ounce, respectively. The cost of 50 ounces of her mixture was $21.80. How many ounces of the 50-cent candy were in the mixture?
ANSWER: 18
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following instruments is best for measuring humidity?
W) Hygrometer
X) Barometer
Y) Anemometer
Z) Hydrometer
ANSWER: W) Hygrometer
2. BONUS Energy Short Answer The diamond-shaped NFPA Symbol on chemical containers includes 4 smaller diamonds, representing 4 hazard areas. Name 2 of them.
ANSWER: ANY 2 OF: HEALTH, FLAMMABILITY, INSTABILITY, AND SPECIAL HAZARDS (also accept: ANY 2 OF: HEALTH, FIRE, REACTIVITY, AND SPECIAL HAZARDS)
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the main structural material found in a skate's skeleton?
ANSWER: CARTILAGE
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer The keel is a broad surface for muscle attachment that protrudes from what bone in a bird's body?
ANSWER: STERNUM
4. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following electricity generation methods would work best as a peaker plant?
W) Coal-fired
X) Natural gas-fired
Y) Nuclear
Z) Geothermal
ANSWER: X) Natural gas-fired
4. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice In a standard lithium-ion battery, from what substance is the anode made?
W) Carbon
X) Lithium cobalt oxide
Y) Lithium iron phosphate
Z) Copper
ANSWER: W) Carbon
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The rings of Saturn are mostly made of which of the following substances?
W) Iron
X) Ice
Y) Rock
Z) Gas
ANSWER: X) Ice
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four reservoirs of Earth's freshwater from the smallest to the largest 1) Ice caps and glaciers 2) Groundwater 3) Atmosphere 4) Lakes.
ANSWER: 3, 4, 2, 1 (also accept: ATMOSPHERE, LAKES, GROUNDWATER, ICE CAPS AND GLACIERS)
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is it called when all forces acting on an object are balanced?
ANSWER: EQUILIBRIUM (also accept: TRANSLATIONAL EQUILIBRIUM)
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Three resistors of 2, 3, and 4 ohms, respectively, are wired in parallel, and the resulting combination is in series with a 5-ohm resistor. What is the equivalent resistance, in ohms, of the entire circuit?
ANSWER: 77/13 (also accept: 5 12/13)
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a geographical region characterized by hills, plateaus, and mountains?
W) Continental shelf
X) Altitude
Y) Highland
Z) Cliff
ANSWER: Y) Highland
7. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the term used to describe an experiment in which neither the subjects nor the experimenters know which subjects are in the control and treatment groups?
ANSWER: DOUBLE-BLIND
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following traits is NOT a characteristic of modern birds?
W) Feathers
X) A three-chambered heart
Y) A beak with no teeth
Z) A lightweight but strong skeleton
ANSWER: X) A three-chambered heart
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Book lungs would most likely be found in what class of animals?
ANSWER: ARACHNIDA
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice As you walk, which of the following forces is the most directly responsible for propelling you forward?
W) The normal force of the ground on your foot
X) The force of your foot pushing on the ground
Y) The friction between your foot and the ground
Z) The reaction force of the ground on your foot
ANSWER: Z) The reaction force of the ground on your foot
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer A speck of dust sits on the edge of a 12-inch diameter LP record. If the record rotates at 45 rotations per minute, what is the linear speed, in inches per minute, of the speck of dust?
ANSWER: 540π
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the degree of the product of the three binomials x + 2, x4 + x2, and x2 + 3?
ANSWER: 7
10. BONUS Math Short Answer A bag contains 9 different mini candy bars. How many different sets of 3 of them can be selected?
ANSWER: 84
11. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Compact fluorescent lightbulbs should be recycled because they contain what harmful element?
ANSWER: MERCURY (also accept: Hg)
11. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Research at Argonne National Laboratory has been done on nuclear fuel recycling. After a nuclear fuel rod has been removed from a reactor, which of the following is closest to the percentage of the unused uranium that is still present in the rod and could be recycled?
W) 25
X) 50
Y) 75
Z) 95
ANSWER: Z) 95
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which of Hubble's four categories of galaxies does not have a distinct spiral or elliptical shape?
ANSWER: IRREGULAR
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of plate tectonic boundaries is associated with shallow earthquakes, but not with volcanism?
W) Convergent
X) Divergent
Y) Passive
Z) Transform
ANSWER: Z) Transform
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The Rutherford Experiment was actually carried out by Hans Geiger and Ernest Marsden at the request of Rutherford. Which of the following statements is NOT true about this work?
W) Rutherford inferred the gold electrons were responsible for deflecting the alpha particles by small angles
X) Rutherford inferred the gold nuclei were responsible for deflecting the alpha particles by large angles
Y) The deflected alpha particles were detected as points of light on a fluorescent screen
Z) Approximately 1 in 20,000 alpha particles was deflected by 90 degrees or more
ANSWER: W) Rutherford inferred the gold electrons were responsible for deflecting the alpha particles by small angles
13. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice An object is placed between the focal point and curvature center of a concave mirror. How does the image compare to the object?
W) Upside down and smaller
X) Upside down and larger
Y) Right side up and smaller

ANSWER: X) Upside down and larger
