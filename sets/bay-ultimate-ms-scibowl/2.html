Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Naman is a living organism (hopefully), and thus has some characteristics that he shares with all other life. Which of the following is not necessarily a characteristic of life?
W) Breathes oxygen
X) Responds to stimuli
Y) Composed of cells
Z) Reproduces
ANSWER: W) Breathes oxygen
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Lucas is attempting to study the relationship between the height of trees and the amount of water given to the trees daily. What are the independent and dependent variables in this experiment, respectively?
ANSWER: Amount of water, height of trees
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Liam’s new favorite restaurant is Chicken Meets Rice, where he has become addicted to the curry they have to offer. Unfortunately, he finds that the curry also makes him fart. He further finds that there is an inverse relationship between the volume of his fart and the pressure that it exerts. Which of the following gas laws relates volume to pressure?
W) Charles' Law
X) Boyle’s Law
Y) Dalton's Law
Z) Gay-Lussac’s Law
ANSWER: X) Boyle’s Law
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the number of nitrogen atoms present in 1 mole of nitrogen gas?
W) 6.022 × 1022
X) 3.011 × 1023
Y) 6.022 × 1023
Z) 1.204 × 1024
ANSWER: Z) 1.204 × 1024
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A certain scientist noticed a flaw in Ampere's Law. He noted that though the equation correctly predicted the magnetic field surrounding a circuit, it predicted that the magnetic field around a capacitor would be 0, a statement that was experimentally proven to be false. In order to remedy this, he added an extra term to Ampere's Law to reflect this phenomenon. What is the name of this scientist?
ANSWER: James Clerk Maxwell (also accept: Maxwell)
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer By name or number, rank the following scientists, from least to most nobel prizes won. 1. Albert Einstein 2. Marie Curie 3. Isaac Newton
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice According to Kepler's laws, which of the following shapes do all planetary orbits possess?
W) Circular
X) Elliptical
Y) Parabolic
Z) Hyperbolic
ANSWER: X) Elliptical
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the time period, occurring from roughly 541 to 486 million years ago, where the Earth experienced a sudden namesake "explosion" of biodiversity?
ANSWER: Cambrian
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Evaluate the following expression: e2 ln 5.
ANSWER: 25
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Ollie, Kevyn, Jon, and Naor must stand in line to get a fresh cup of coffee. How many different ways can they stand in a line?
ANSWER: 24
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Lucas the organelle is involved in the transportation of materials from inside the cell to outside the cell. Which of the following organelles could Lucas not be?
W) Vesicle
X) Golgi apparatus
Y) Cell membrane
Z) Chloroplast
ANSWER: Z) Chloroplast
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Aliens come to Earth and dissect a human to understand its body. Where would the aliens need to look around to find the thyroid gland?
W) The neck
X) The chest
Y) The abdomen
Z) The brain
ANSWER: W) The neck
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Liam is playing around with a balloon containing an unknown gas, when he accidentally inhales the gas inside. Suddenly, his voice becomes super high-pitched. Which of the following could be the identity of the gas that he inhaled?
W) Helium
X) Carbon Dioxide
Y) Propane
Z) Argon
ANSWER: W) Helium
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the simplest alkane, with chemical formula CH4?
ANSWER: Methane
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the momentum, in kilogram-meters per second, of a ball with a mass of 5 kg that is moving at a constant velocity, travelling 40 meters in 10 seconds?
ANSWER: 20
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer In shot-put, athletes hurl stones with forces of around 300 Newtons. If this force is constant and exerted over 0.2 seconds, ignoring gravity, what will be the momentum, in kilogram-meters per second, of the stone when it releases from the athlete’s hand?
ANSWER: 60
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Halfway during class, Liam’s stomach suddenly erupts with the largest fart known to mankind. One of his classmates says it must be the largest volcano in the solar system. However, this is not true, because the largest volcano in the solar system is called what name?
ANSWER: Olympus Mons
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the furthest part of our solar system, which many long period comets come from?
ANSWER: Oort Cloud
10. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer 1 Byte is equal to how many Bits?
ANSWER: 8
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Which law states that the negation of (A AND B) is logically equivalent to the negation of A OR the negation of B?
ANSWER: DeMorgan’s Law
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Kiran is a world-famous botanist who needs to buy lots of lamps in order to help grow plants for his next exhibition. Which of the following lamp-light colors would be worst?
W) Purple
X) Red
Y) Green
Z) Blue
ANSWER: Y) Green
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following taxonomic groups from most broad to most specific. 1. Phylum 2. Class 3. Genus 4. Order
ANSWER: 1, 2, 4, 3
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Lucas is trying to pluck electrons off of atoms using tweezers. However, being sleep deprived from writing BUMS packets, he doesn’t have the energy necessary to remove any electrons. What quantity describes the minimum energy required to remove the highest-energy electron from a neutral atom?
ANSWER: Ionization Energy
13. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the relationship between the sizes of sodium and its cation?
W) The cation is larger
X) The cation is smaller
Y) The cation is the same size
Z) The relationship depends on the isotope of sodium
ANSWER: X) The cation is smaller
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Naman loves computer science, and attempts to model a particle that moves in a random, jiggling fashion. What is the name of this motion?
ANSWER: Brownian Motion
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer Given that atmospheric pressure is 101 kilopascals, then in Newtons, how much force is exerted by the atmosphere on a square stool with side lengths of 0.4 meters?
ANSWER: 16160
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of minerals can be metamorphosed into marble?
W) Quartz and Calcite
X) Dolomite and Halite
Y) Quartz and Halite
Z) Dolomite and Calcite
ANSWER: Z) Dolomite and Calcite
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What will happen if a mineral with a specific gravity less than one is placed in water?
ANSWER: It will float
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Michelle needs to find a job, so she applies to 1 job each day. Every week, she notices that she gets rejected from 5 jobs and gets interviews from the rest. After the 17th interview, she accepts the job. To the nearest week, how long in weeks did it take her to find a job?
ANSWER: 9
16. BONUS Math Short Answer Liam’s favorite number is 67, and he wishes to factorize it. What are the positive integer divisors of the number 67?
ANSWER: 1 and 67
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Liam is attempting to identify big backs in order to justify his own love of eating. While studying microbiology, he notices that just like him, macrophages eat a lot. In order for macrophages to eat, however, they must utilize their plasma membranes to consume other particles and pathogens. This is an example of what type of endocytosis?
ANSWER: Phagocytosis
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Lucas eats 100 pounds of fish every day. Assuming that 1 pound of fish contains approximately 1 kJ of energy, and that Lucas is one trophic level above fish, how much energy does he gain from eating fish every day in joules?
ANSWER: 10,000
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term given to atoms that have the same number of protons but differing numbers of neutrons?
ANSWER: Isotopes
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Lucas is drawing the Lewis Dot structure of XeF4, or xenon tetrafluoride, when he realizes that he has given the central xenon atom 12 electrons. This violates what guideline that states that atoms require 8 electrons to fill their valence shells?
ANSWER: Octet Rule
19. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following quantities is not conserved in an inelastic collision?
W) Linear Momentum
X) Angular Momentum
Y) Kinetic Energy
Z) Mass-Energy
ANSWER: Y) Kinetic Energy
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer A ball is launched from a cannon at an angle of 30 degrees to the horizontal and lands on the ground 100 meters from the cannon. If another ball is placed in the cannon, which of the following three launch angles would result in the second ball traveling farther than the first once it hits the ground? 1. 29.5 2. 30.5 3. 31.5
ANSWER: 2 and 3
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three layers of the atmosphere by increasing distance from Earth's surface. 1. Mesosphere 2. Thermosphere 3. Troposphere
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, order the following elements in order of increasing abundance in Earth’s atmosphere. 1) Oxygen 2) Hydrogen 3) Nitrogen.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
21. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Kiran is once again struggling with math, but has learned perfect squares. This time, his teacher assigns him square root practice. Which of the following is the closest to the square root of 50?
W) 9
X) 8
Y) 7
Z) 6
ANSWER: Y) 7
21. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the values of x for when the polynomial x2-2x-8 equals 0.
ANSWER: -2 and 4
