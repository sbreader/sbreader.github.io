1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The ice sheet that covered northeastern United States came from which of the following locations?
W) Labrador center in eastern Canada
X) Keewatin center in central Canada
Y) Cordilleran center in the Rocky Mountains
Z) Greenland
ANSWER: W) Labrador center in eastern Canada

W) Syncline
X) Composite cone
Y) Anticline
