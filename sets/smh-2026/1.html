1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The posterior pituitary’s release of ADH increases water reabsorption in the collecting duct by stimulating the insertion of what type of channels?

W) Animals
X) Viruses
Y) Plants
Z) Bacteria
ANSWER: X) Viruses

ANSWER: -8 m/s
