Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the second-most-abundant element in the universe?
ANSWER: HELIUM
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What unit of astronomical distance is approximately 206,000 astronomical units?
ANSWER: PARSEC
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Mammals are named after glands that produce what substance?
ANSWER: MILK
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice To what group of plants do sequoias belong?
W) Conifers
X) Cycads
Y) Angiosperms
Z) Ginkgos
ANSWER: W) Conifers
3. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The equation 3 times 4 = 4 times 3 illustrates which of the following properties of multiplication?
W) Associative
X) Commutative
Y) Distributive
Z) Identity
ANSWER: X) Commutative
3. BONUS Math Short Answer A rectangle's long sides are three times the length of its shorter sides. If its perimeter is 48, what is its area?
ANSWER: 108
4. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Scientists at the National Energy Technology Lab are studying the use of anhydrous, nonvolatile solvents for carbon capture. What specific compound is being captured?
ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE (also accept: CO2)
4. BONUS Energy Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab have used electron energy gain spectroscopy using a monochromated beam to directly observe the atomic vibration energies. What kind of device did they create?
ANSWER: THERMOMETER
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the largest and most prominent region of the human brain, which is thought to be responsible for voluntary activities?
ANSWER: CEREBRUM
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the granular material inside the nucleus that consists of DNA bound to protein?
ANSWER: CHROMATIN
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the general term for a device used to measure atmospheric pressure?
ANSWER: BAROMETER
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The isotope carbon-14 has six protons and eight neutrons. If it undergoes beta-minus decay, how many neutrons will the decay product have?
ANSWER: 7
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying how groundwater moves through fractured rock using neutron scattering experiments. What is the term for an underground water-permeable layer composed of porous or fractured rock?
ANSWER: AQUIFER
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer Jefferson Lab researchers are imaging alpha particles to better understand the nucleus of what element?
ANSWER: HELIUM (also accept: He)
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Humans are part of what phylum?
ANSWER: CHORDATA (do not accept: CHORDATES)
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What part of cellular respiration oxidizes the original carbon atoms from glucose and releases them as carbon dioxide?
ANSWER: KREBS CYCLE (also accept: CITRIC ACID CYCLE, TRICARBOXYLIC ACID CYCLE)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of these would lead to the formation of a deep ocean trench?
W) Compressional tectonic forces
X) Extensional tectonic forces
Y) Volcanic activity
Z) Hydrothermal activity
ANSWER: W) Compressional tectonic forces
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following three reservoirs of Earth's water supply from largest to smallest 1) Ground water 2) Lakes 3) Glaciers
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In the rock cycle, what is the adjective for the type of rock that forms as magma cools and solidifies?
ANSWER: IGNEOUS (also accept: INTRUSIVE IGNEOUS) (do not accept: EXTRUSIVE IGNEOUS)
10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the ratio of the thickness of the mantle to that of the outer core?
W) 1 to 1
X) 2 to 1
Y) 5 to 1
Z) 10 to 1
ANSWER: W) 1 to 1
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following substances is most likely to be able to combust?
W) Octane
X) Silicon dioxide
Y) Sodium chloride
Z) Iron three oxide
ANSWER: W) Octane
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice A chemist might use filter paper and a funnel when she wants to do which of the following separations?
W) Liquid-liquid
X) Solid-liquid
Y) Solid-solid
Z) Liquid-gas
ANSWER: X) Solid-liquid
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the greatest common factor of the 3 numbers 2, 8, and 13?
ANSWER: 1
12. BONUS Math Short Answer Consider the function f of x = x2 – 3x. What is the polynomial that is equal to the composite function f of f of x?
ANSWER: x4 – 6x3 + 6x2 + 9x
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What type of energy is associated with an object's motion?
ANSWER: KINETIC ENERGY
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer A ball starts rolling down a hill at a velocity of 4 centimeters per second. After 15 seconds, the ball is now rolling at 49 centimeters per second. In centimeters per second squared, what is the average acceleration of the ball over this interval?
ANSWER: 3
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the adjective for the type of external symmetry that a dragonfly demonstrates?
ANSWER: BILATERAL
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer Mammals, like dogs and humans, fall into a group that possess a unique organ formed from tissues from both the mother and the embryo during pregnancy. What is the term for this organ?
ANSWER: PLACENTA
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The asteroid belt lies just within the orbit of what planet?
ANSWER: JUPITER
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Approximately where on a Hertzsprung-Russell diagram could you find a star like our Sun?
W) Near the center
X) Top right
Y) Top left
Z) Bottom left
ANSWER: W) Near the center
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab are using lithium oxide as a coating inside a tokamak to reduce plasma instabilities. What is the chemical formula of lithium oxide?
ANSWER: Li2O
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are using the MAJORANA DEMONSTRATOR to study the symmetry between subatomic particles and their antiparticles. What is the commonly used term for the collision and destruction of a particle and its antiparticle?
ANSWER: ANNIHILATION
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the area of a circle with diameter 16?
ANSWER: 64π
17. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the smallest positive integer that has a remainder of 1 when divided by 5 and a remainder of 3 when divided by 7?
ANSWER: 31
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Isotopes of the same element differ in the number of which of the following particles?
W) Protons
X) Neutrons
Y) Electrons
Z) Positrons
ANSWER: X) Neutrons
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer When filling up a bicycle tire, Zoe presses down on her pump, compressing it from 4 liters to 3.5 liters by applying a constant external pressure of 1.2 atmospheres. In liter-atmospheres, what is the magnitude of the work Zoe did on the gas inside the pump?
ANSWER: 0.6 (also accept: 3/5)
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a chunk of interplanetary debris that lands on the surface of the Earth?
ANSWER: METEORITE (do not accept: METEOR, METEOROID)
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The greenhouse effect on Venus is so strong because its atmosphere is primarily composed of what gas?
ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE (also accept: CO2)
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the sum of 2/3 and 3/4?
ANSWER: 17/12 (also accept: 1 5/12)
20. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the volume of a sphere inscribed in a cube with edge length 12?
ANSWER: 288π
21. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A diatom is an example of what?
W) Bacterium
X) Fungus
Y) Protist
Z) Archaean
ANSWER: Y) Protist
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the location at which one neuron transmits an impulse to another neuron via chemical signaling?
ANSWER: SYNAPSE
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Water has a high specific heat capacity due in part to its strong intermolecular interactions known as what?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN BONDS (also accept: H BONDS)
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer A meter stick has a mass of 1-kilogram that is uniformly distributed through the stick. Margaret ties a 1-kilogram rock to one end of the stick. In centimeters, at what distance from the rock is the center of mass of the system?
ANSWER: 25
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the degree measure of the supplement of 43 degrees?
ANSWER: 137
23. BONUS Math Short Answer Mary left home at 8:00 a.m. and drove until 2:45 p.m. If the distance she traveled is 351 miles, what was her average speed, in miles per hour, during the trip?
ANSWER: 52
