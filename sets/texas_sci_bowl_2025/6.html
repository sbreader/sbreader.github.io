2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Why do SN1 reactions occur faster with tertiary alkyl halides than with secondary or primary alkyl halides?

X) Tertiary alkyl halides have carbocations stabilized by three alkyl groups.
Y) Tertiary alkyl halides have weaker carbon-halogen bonds.

ANSWER: X) Tertiary alkyl halides have carbocations stabilized by three alkyl groups.

4. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about far-field multi-slit experiments is true?
W) It is assumed that the distance between the slits is much smaller than their width.
X) The interference pattern intensity is linearly proportional to the number of slits.
Y) The interference pattern is created due to the path length differences between the light sources.
Z) The Young-Laplace equation governs the interference pattern.
ANSWER: Y) The interference pattern is created due to the path length differences between the light sources.

5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes light transduction in photoreceptors?
W) In the presence of light, sodium channels open, depolarizing the photoreceptor and increasing its release of glutamate.
X) In the presence of light, potassium channels close, depolarizing the photoreceptor and increasing its release of glutamate.
Y) In the presence of light, sodium channels close, hyperpolarizing the photoreceptor and decreasing its release of glutamate.
