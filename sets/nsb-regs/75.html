1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A deficiency of what trace element can result in goiter?
W) Sodium
X) Chlorine
Y) Potassium
Z) Iodine
ANSWER: Z) Iodine
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a symptom or sign of a person suffering from diabetes mellitus?
W) Increase in protein breakdown
X) Presence of sugar in urine
Y) Decrease in the amount of urine production
Z) Increased levels of blood glucose
ANSWER: Y) Decrease in the amount of urine production

X) √e
Y) e
Z) 10

W) Oil
X) Coal
Y) Natural gas
Z) Nuclear
ANSWER: Z) Nuclear

X) As a liquid under cryogenic conditions
Y) Absorbed in metal hydrides
Z) Adsorbed in ceramic zeolites

16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice You find a chunk of granite that you conclude, via radiometric dating, is 3.2 billion years old. The granite also contains inclusions of quartzite. Which of the following is a reasonable conclusion based on this data?
W) The quartzite likely formed less than 3.2 billion years ago
X) Because the quartzite is contained in the granite, it also formed 3.2 billion years ago
Y) The quartzite likely formed more than 3.2 billion years ago
