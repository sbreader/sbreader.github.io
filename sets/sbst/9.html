BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding the thermodynamics of ideal gases 1) Constant-pressure heat capacity is always greater than constant-volume heat capacity 2) Molar enthalpy change only depends on temperature 3) Molecules always have higher heat capacity than atoms under the same conditions.
