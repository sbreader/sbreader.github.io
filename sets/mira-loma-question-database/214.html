1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the best reason why the iron core of an electrical transformer is usually composed of laminated rather than solid iron?
W) The lamination provides greater insulation of the magnetic field.
X) Lamination reduces the strength of eddy currents in the core.
Y) Lamination is a more structurally stable.
Z) Lamination reduces electrical resistance in the core.
ANSWER: X) Lamination reduces the strength of eddy currents in the core.
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In nuclear reactions, what is the term for the discrepancy between total mass of the nucleons comprising a nucleus and the combined mass of a nucleus?
ANSWER: mass defect
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following characteristics best describes an integral protein in a cell membrane?
W) hydrophobic
X) hydrophillic
Y) amphipathic
Z) polarity is not a significant factor is determining integration into a cell membrane
ANSWER: Y) amphipathic
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Although it is a bright second-magnitude star, it is only the third brightest in its constellation. Also known as the Amazon star and Latin for "female warrior", what is the common name for Gamma Orionis?
ANSWER: Bellatrix
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following shore landforms is NOT a depositional feature?
W) sea stack
X) baymouth bar
Y) tombolo
Z) spit
ANSWER: W) sea stack
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Assuming that each of your fingers can either be held up or down, how many unique combinations can you make with your two hands?
ANSWER: 1023
7. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What mathematical theorem of communications states that to avoid aliasing of a pulse amplitude modulated signal, the sampling frequency must be at least twice as large as the largest information frequency?
ANSWER: Shannon-Nyquist sampling theorem
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice When an electron travels at a 30 degree angle to a magnetic field in absence of other forces, the acceleration experienced by the electron is "a". At what acute angle would the acceleration experienced be 2a?
W) 0 degrees
X) 45 degrees
Y) 60 degrees
Z) 90 degrees
ANSWER: Z) 90 degrees
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What element, which is the first element to experience significant sigma-pi mixing in its diatomic form making it paramagnetic with a bond order of two, is very reactive with an electronegativity of 3.44?
ANSWER: oxygen
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which enzyme of the citric acid cycle uses FAD as a prosthetic group to add hydrogen atoms to ubiquinone?
W) Isocitrate dehydrogenase
X) alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase
Y) Succinate dehydrogenase
Z) Malate dehydrogenase
ANSWER: Y) Succinate dehydrogenase
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes a mixed semidiurnal tide?
W) Two equal high tides and two equal low tides daily
X) Two equal high tides and two unequal low tides daily
Y) Two unequal high tides and two equal low tides daily
Z) Two unequal high tides and two unequal low tides daily
ANSWER: Z) Two unequal high tides and two unequal low tides daily
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Find the dot product between the zero vector and the vector <5,-3,0>.
ANSWER: 0
14. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In an analog amplitude-modulated wave, what is the term that is mathematically equal to twice the largest frequency deviation of the sidebands?
W) bandwidth
X) modulation constant
Y) channel capacity
Z) Q-factor
ANSWER: W) bandwidth
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What principle of general relativity equates gravitational and inertial mass, saying that the gravitational "force" as experienced locally is actually the same as the pseudo-force experienced by an observer in a non-inertial frame of reference?
ANSWER: equivalence principle
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In what type of mimicry will a deadly, aposematic prey mimic a less dangerous animal, such as true Coral Snakes mimicking False Coral Snakes?
W) Batesian
X) Mullerian
Y) Mertensian
Z) Aggressive
ANSWER: Y) Mertensian
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Jupiter emits more energy than it receives from the Sun because of the Kelvin-Helmholtz mechanism. Which of the following best describes the action of this mechanism?
W) Helium condensing in its atmosphere
X) Radioactive materials in its core
Y) Gradual gravitational compression of its outer layers
Z) Intense reflectivity of the Sun's light
ANSWER: Y) Gradual gravitational compression of its outer layers
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In what form of river or coastal erosion does the material of the bed load erode itself to create smaller fragments downstream?
ANSWER: attrition
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer I am thinking of a two-digit positive integer which is divisible by 3. It has five proper factors, which add up to 3 more than the number. The numbers above and below it are both prime. Its square is equal to 324. What number is it?
ANSWER: 18
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What SI unit is used to measure solid angle, or the two-dimensional angular spans in three-dimensional space?
ANSWER: steradian
22. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice On a diagram of electric fields, the equipotential lines are
W) always perpendicular to the field lines
X) always parallel to the field lines
Y) spaced between the field lines
Z) only present in the immediate vicinity of a point charge
ANSWER: W) always perpendicular to the field lines
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A 5L sample of an ideal gas at a pressure of one bar is compressed to a new volume of 2L. How much work, in joules, was done on the gas in this process?
ANSWER: 300
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In performing a Southern blot, what material is placed directly on the gel to absorb its DNA fragments for later visualization?
ANSWER: nitrocellulose membrane (also accept: Nylon)
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Because geometric albedo compares an object's reflectivity with a diffusely reflecting surface, some planetary bodies actually have an albedo of over 1. Which of the following moons, which has a surface of mostly water ice, has a Bond albedo of 0.99 and a geometric albedo of 1.4?
W) Titan
X) Enceladus
Y) Titania
Z) Triton
ANSWER: X) Enceladus
