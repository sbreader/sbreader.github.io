1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A 60-kilogram block experiences a constant force of 300 Newtons for 4 seconds. What is the impulse experienced by the block?
ANSWER: 1200 KILOGRAM-METERS PER SECOND
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Block A is launched from the ground at a speed of 10 meters per second at an angle of θ, while Block B is launched from the ground at a speed of 5 meters per second vertically upward. Neglecting air resistance, which of the following statements is true regarding the speeds of the blocks when they both reach a height h?
W) Block A will only have a faster speed when θ is less than 30°
X) Block A will only have a faster speed when θ is greater than 30°
Y) Block A will always have a faster speed
Z) Block B will always have a faster speed
ANSWER: Y) Block A will always have a faster speed
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What hormone, secreted by the duodenum in response to acidity, causes the pancreas to secrete bicarbonate?
ANSWER: SECRETIN
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer In addition to secreting hydrochloric acid, the parietal cells of the stomach also secrete intrinsic factor. Intrinsic factor aids in the absorption of what cobalt-containing B vitamin?
ANSWER: VITAMIN B12 (also accept: COBALAMIN)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following planets is NOT visible to the naked eye?
W) Mercury
X) Venus
Y) Saturn
Z) Neptune
ANSWER: Z) Neptune
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Only two planets in the solar system experience retrograde motion, Venus and Uranus. Both planets likely experienced what event, resulting in this kind of motion?
ANSWER: CATACLYSMIC COLLISION (also accept: COLLISION WITH A PROTOPLANET)
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the smallest integer greater than 1 that leaves a remainder of 1 when divided by 2, 3, 4, and 5?
ANSWER: 61
4. BONUS Math Short Answer A positive integer factor of 120 is chosen at random. What is the probability that the chosen factor is even?
ANSWER: 3/4
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is considered a Lewis acid but NOT a Brønsted-Lowry acid?
W) Boron trichloride
X) Carbon dioxide
Y) Ammonium
Z) Hydrochloric acid
ANSWER: W) Boron trichloride
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What method produces alloys by mixing finely powdered metals, and then pressing them into a mold?
ANSWER: SINTERING
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are studying potential applications of starch in the production of biofuels. Starch is composed of what two polysaccharides?
ANSWER: AMYLOSE AND AMYLOPECTIN
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are using supercomputing to study potential drugs in the fight against the SARS-CoV-2 virus. In particular, they are looking for compounds that will bind to what transmembrane protein on the virus?
ANSWER: SPIKE PROTEIN (also accept: S PROTEIN)
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following quantities represents how compressible a material is?
W) Young’s modulus
X) Shear modulus
Y) Longitudinal modulus
Z) Bulk modulus
ANSWER: Z) Bulk modulus
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer Beta-minus decay is mediated through the release of what virtual particle?
ANSWER: W- BOSON (also accept: W MINUS BOSON) (do not accept: W BOSON; W+ BOSON)
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What instrument is used to measure the blood pressure of a patient?
ANSWER: SPHYGMOMANOMETER
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the pH of blood and the affinity of hemoglobin for oxygen during exercise?
W) Blood pH decreases; hemoglobin’s affinity for oxygen increases
X) Blood pH decreases; hemoglobin’s affinity for oxygen decreases
Y) Blood pH increases; hemoglobin’s affinity for oxygen increases
Z) Blood pH increases; hemoglobin’s affinity for oxygen decreases
ANSWER: X) Blood pH decreases; hemoglobin’s affinity for oxygen decreases
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes the two points where the moon crosses the ecliptic?
ANSWER: LUNAR NODES
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four substances that occur in the metamorphism of mafic volcanics by increasing metamorphic grade 1) Granulite 2) Zeolite 3) Greenschist 4) Amphibolite.
ANSWER: 2,3,4,1
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is an antiderivative of sec(x)?
W) sec(x)tan(x)
X) sec2(x)
Y) ln|sin(x) + cos(x)|
Z) ln|sec(x) + tan(x)|
ANSWER: Z) ln|sec(x) + tan(x)|
10. BONUS Math Short Answer How many ways can a parent give 8 indistinguishable cookies to 3 kids, if each kid must receive at least one cookie?
ANSWER: 21
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the degree of unsaturation of the compound 2-pentanone, which has the molecular formula C5H10O?
ANSWER: 1
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Approximately how many times larger is the radius of the nucleus of gallium compared to the radius of the nucleus of hydrogen?
W) 2
X) 4
Y) 8
Z) 16
ANSWER: X) 4
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are studying solar-energy materials. In the materials, the researchers found a way to slow phonons, which are vibrations that transport which of the following quantities?
W) Heat
X) Sound
Y) Current
Z) Voltage
ANSWER: W) Heat
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are researching the effects of mercury on the ecosystem. Mercury, by itself, is not harmful to the environment. What mercury-containing compound, that is generated by microbes in the presence of mercury, causes the most harm to ecosystems?
ANSWER: METHYLMERCURY (also accept: CH 3 Hg)
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What general term refers to a property that is different when measured along different directions?
ANSWER: ANISOTROPIC
13. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice According to Poiseuille’s equation, volumetric flow rate is proportional to what power of radius?
W) -2
X) -1
Y) 2
Z) 4
ANSWER: Z) 4
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In what section of the chloroplast do the light reactions of photosynthesis occur?
ANSWER: THYLAKOID (also accept: THYLAKOID MEMBRANE)
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice When a plant is exposed to 14 continuous hours of darkness, it flowers. If the plant is exposed to a flash of red light during this 14 hour period, it will not flower. Which of the following terms best describes the photoperiod of the plant?
W) Short day
X) Long day
Y) Day neutral
Z) Seasonal
ANSWER: W) Short day
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The ionosphere is a result of the interaction between the upper atmosphere and what type of electromagnetic radiation produced by the sun?
ANSWER: ULTRAVIOLET (also accept: UV)
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Vega, Altair and Deneb are part of what asterism?
ANSWER: SUMMER TRIANGLE
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Identify all of the following three equations that pass through the point (1, 3) 1) x2-y2 = -2 2) 2 x + 4 y = 13 3) xy + yx = 4.
ANSWER: 3 ONLY
16. BONUS Math Short Answer A triangle has two vertices at (-1, 2) and (3, 4), and its centroid is located at (2, 5). What are the coordinates of the triangle’s third vertex?
ANSWER: 4, 9
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three mixtures that exhibit the Tyndall Effect 1) Salt water 2) Milk 3) Whipped cream.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What region in IR spectroscopy from 1450 to 400 inverse centimeters contains an unusually large number of peaks and is often used to identify compounds?
ANSWER: FINGERPRINT REGION
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Lab have devised a method to create micro and nanoscale mechanical oscillators with excellent frequency stability. Most current oscillators use a quartz crystal as the resonating element. Quartz exhibits what effect, which produces an electric charge in response to mechanical stress?
ANSWER: PIEZOELECTRICITY (also accept: PIEZOELECTRIC EFFECT)
