1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three soil horizons in order of increasing soil profile development 1) Entisols 2) Inceptisols 3) Oxisols
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Huronian Glaciation was a 300-million-year period during which the entirety of Earth was buried under glaciers. Which of the following most likely triggered the Huronian Glaciation?
W) The depletion of atmospheric methane via the Great Oxygenation
X) The formation of a supercontinent over the South Pole
Y) A series of volcanic eruptions forming a large igneous province
Z) Solar changes causing a sudden worldwide drop in oceanic salinity
ANSWER: W) The depletion of atmospheric methane via the Great Oxygenation
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A reversible reaction, A goes to B has forward rate constant of 0.8 inverse seconds, and reverse rate constant of 0.2 inverse seconds. If the system is allowed to reach equilibrium, what fraction of the molecules will be present as B?
W) 0.20
X) 0.25
Y) 0.75
Z) 0.80
ANSWER: Z) 0.80
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three ketone reactions by increasing optimal pH by which it should be performed 1) Acetal formation 2) Imine formation 3) Cyanohydrin formation

5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Stanford’s Moler Group are studying the properties of quantum materials. When a temperature gradient is applied across a conductor, what phenomenon describes the generation of an electric potential due to charge carriers diffusing from the hot to cold side?
ANSWER: Seebeck effect
5. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Radiologists at UCLA are building neural networks to analyze CT scans. In their model, what operation is used when a small matrix of weights slides across the image to extract features like edges and textures?
W) Pooling
X) Backpropagation
Y) Convolution
Z) Normalization
ANSWER: Y) Convolution

7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Vivek wants to look at 3D images of a live onion root cell as it undergoes mitosis. Which of the following types of microscopy should he employ?

8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is true regarding an air parcel with a temperature of 18°C at an altitude of 10 kilometers, given that the surface temperature is 26° C and the moist adiabatic lapse rate is 5° C per kilometer?
W) It is absolutely stable
X) It is conditionally stable
Y) It is absolutely unstable
Z) There is no way to determine stability without the dry adiabatic lapse rate
ANSWER: W) It is absolutely stable

12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at UCLA have discovered hydrogen sulfide in exoplanets, helping them discern between planets and brown dwarfs. Which of the following features are NOT characteristic of brown dwarfs?

14. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following processes violates conservation of baryon number?
