1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The difference between eukaryotic and prokaryotic cells, is that prokaryotic cells lack what organelle?
W) Cell membrane
X) DNA
Y) Nucleus
Z) Ribosomes
ANSWER: Y) Nucleus
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following biological molecules make up the main part of the bilayer and hydrophobic region of the plasma cell membrane?
W) Oligosaccharides
X) Carbohydrates
Y) Phospholipids
Z) Proteins
ANSWER: Y) Phospholipids
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Two to the 4th power divided by 2 to the 3rd power equals 2 to what power?
ANSWER: 1st (also accept: 1)
2. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Nick, Anna, and Sherry go on a vacation and agree to split the costs evenly. During the trip, Nick paid $105, Anna paid $125, and Sherry paid $175. In order to share the costs evenly, Nick gave Sherry x dollars and Anna gave Sherry y dollars. What is x + y?
W) 20
X) 30
Y) 40
Z) 50
ANSWER: Y) 40
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following defines a pure substance that cannot be broken down by any chemical means?
W) Compound
X) Element
Y) Solid
Z) Solution
ANSWER: X) Element
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many lone pairs of electrons exist in a single water molecule?
ANSWER: TWO
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In what two seasons do lakes undergo turnovers?
ANSWER: SPRING AND FALL (also accept: SPRING AND AUTUMN)
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The deepest known point of the oceans is found in which of the following?
W) Atlantic Ocean
X) Pacific Ocean
Y) Dead Sea
Z) North Sea
ANSWER: X) Pacific Ocean
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A keystone species is:
W) Required for maintaining the health of an ecosystem
X) Necessary for the survival of another species
Y) The last to go extinct after a disturbance
Z) The hardiest species in an ecosystem
ANSWER: W) Required for maintaining the health of an ecosystem
5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The property of metalloids which makes them useful in computer chips is their:
W) Brittle structure
X) Varying ability to conduct electricity
Y) Somewhat reactive nature
Z) Hardness
ANSWER: X) Varying ability to conduct electricity
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about spiral galaxies?
W) They emit mainly in the ultraviolet range of energy
X) They are usually similar to our galaxy
Y) They originate from supernova explosions
Z) They typically contain a few thousand stars
ANSWER: X) They are usually similar to our galaxy
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following stars can someone in North America see year-round?
W) Sirius
X) Rigel
Y) Betelgeuse
Z) Polaris
ANSWER: Z) Polaris
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is a group of organic compounds that is often added to foods to enhance flavors such as strawberry or banana and are also found in sweet-smelling flowers?
W) Amines
X) Esters
Y) Alcohols
Z) Halogenated hydrocarbons
ANSWER: X) Esters
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer To the nearest whole number, how many minutes does it take for the earth to rotate through one degree of longitude?
ANSWER: 4
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What greenhouse gas is the simplest hydrocarbon?
ANSWER: METHANE
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the process of comparing a property of an object to a well-defined and agreed-upon referent?
W) Generalizing
X) Measurement
Y) Graphing
Z) Scientific Investigation
ANSWER: X) Measurement
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What law or principle relates the applied force to the amount of elongation in a spring when these quantities are linearly related?
ANSWER: HOOKE'S LAW
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer You spend a total of $21.43 at the store, but want to return an item with marked price $5.00. If the sales tax is 7%, how much will you receive back for the item?
ANSWER: $5.35
10. BONUS Math Short Answer From a 7-cup package, a cat eats 1 and 3/5 cups of food on Tuesday and 2¾ cups on Wednesday. How many cups are left in the package?
ANSWER: 2 and 13/20 (also accept: 2.65)
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Female members of what order of animals contain pouches used to complete development of their young?
ANSWER: MARSUPIALIA
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What structures, composed of flexible tissue, connect two or more bones together at joints?
ANSWER: LIGAMENTS
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of map or chart is commonly used in navigation to eliminate distortion?
W) Sextant
X) Latitudinal Chart
Y) North-up Map
Z) Mercator Projection
ANSWER: Z) Mercator Projection
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the SI base unit for measuring temperature?
ANSWER: KELVIN (do not accept: ANY ANSWER WITH "DEGREE" IN IT)
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When a group of living organisms and non-living components in an environment interact as a system, what is the system called?
ANSWER: ECOSYSTEM
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What dark areas occur on the Sun due to intense magnetic activity?
ANSWER: SUNSPOTS
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is common to both bacteria and viruses?
W) Metabolism
X) Ribosomes
Y) Nucleic acids
Z) Cell division
ANSWER: Y) Nucleic acids
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What molecule is released from muscles as a waste product, transported to the lungs in blood, and exhaled as a gas?
ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE (also accept: CO2)
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Under what conditions does the behavior of real gases deviate most from that predicted by the ideal gas law?
W) Low Pressure, Low Temperature
X) High Pressure, Low Temperature
Y) Low Pressure, High Temperature

ANSWER: X) High Pressure, Low Temperature

ANSWER: 7, 9

ANSWER: (x + 2)/(x – 3)
17. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer When creating electric current using steam, the electric generator is attached to what type of equipment that catches the steam to rotate the generator?
ANSWER: TURBINE
17. BONUS Energy Short Answer What type of alternative energy production relies on energy provided by the Moon rather than energy provided by the Sun?
ANSWER: TIDAL (also accept: TIDAL POWER, TIDAL ENERGY)
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Place the following 3 polyhedra in increasing order of the number of faces 1) tetrahedron 2) dodecahedron 3) octahedron.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2 (also accept: TETRAHEDRON, OCTAHEDRON, DODECAHEDRON)
18. BONUS Math Short Answer Triangle ABC is similar to Triangle DEF. If AB = 4, DE = 10, and EF = 15, what is the length of BC?
ANSWER: 6

X) An increase in the mass number by four

ANSWER: Y) A decrease in the atomic number by two
23. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Clean coal research is currently NOT focused on which of the following?
W) Improving the purity of coal ore

Z) Reusing carbon dioxide generated when burning coal
ANSWER: W) Improving the purity of coal ore
