1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which English polymath coined the term for the smallest living biological structure?
ANSWER: ROBERT HOOKE
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the two types of cells?
ANSWER: PROKARYOTIC AND EUKARYOTIC
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the four nitrogen bases that make up DNA?
ANSWER: ADENINE, THYMINE, GUANINE, CYTOSINE
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The nucleobase guanine pairs with which other nucleobase in DNA?
W) adenine
X) thymine
Y) cytosine
Z) uracil
ANSWER: Y) cytosine
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the five main nucleobases is found only in RNA?
W) uracil
X) thymine
Y) guanine
Z) adenine
ANSWER: W) uracil
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Uracil replaces which of the following in RNA?
W) adenine
X) guanine
Y) thymine
Z) cytosine
ANSWER: Y) thymine
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is an irregular bone?
W) fibula
X) vertebra
Y) humerus
Z) carpals
ANSWER: X) vertebra
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The shoulder joint is the part of the body in which the humerus attaches to which bone?
W) scapula
X) clavicle
Y) ulna
Z) fibula
ANSWER: W) scapula
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Messenger RNA, carries the coded message from the nucleus to which cellular structure?
ANSWER: RIBOSOME
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the entire library of genetic instructions that an organism inherits called?
ANSWER: GENOME
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer The international scientific research project whose primary goal was to determine the sequence of chemical base pairs which make up DNA is known as what?
ANSWER: HUMAN GENOME PROJECT
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the most common from of feedback regulation?
ANSWER: NEGATIVE FEEDBACK
10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In a biological process regulated by positive feedback, the end product does what to its production?
W) slows it down
X) speeds it up
Y) does nothing to it
Z) stops monitoring it
ANSWER: X) speeds it up
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which protein is a fingernail composed of?
ANSWER: KERATIN
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the whitish crescent shape at the base of the nail plate known as?
W) nail groove
X) lunula
Y) eponychium
Z) hyponychium
ANSWER: X) lunula
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the diffusion of a solvent through a selectively permeable membrane called?
ANSWER: OSMOSIS
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Osmosis is what type of process?
ANSWER: PASSIVE PROCESS
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which naming system is used to classify species?
ANSWER: BINOMIAL NOMENCLATURE
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Order the following from most general to most specific. Order, phylum, class, kingdom
W) order, class, kingdom, phylum
X) class, kingdom, phylum, order
Y) kingdom, phylum, class, order
Z) order, phylum, kingdom, class
ANSWER: Y) kingdom, phylum, class, order
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Of the three domains, which are composed of prokaryotic organisms?
ANSWER: BACTERIA, ARCHAEA

W) phylum and class
X) class and order
Y) genus and species
Z) family and kingdom
ANSWER: Y) genus and species
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the hardest substance in the human body?
W) keratin
X) enamel
Y) cartilage
Z) marrow
ANSWER: X) enamel

ANSWER: MUSCLE TO BONE

17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which famous scientist proposed an evolutionary mechanism for descent with modification?
ANSWER: CHARLES DARWIN
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the process by which heritable traits that make it more likely for an organism to survive become more common in a population over successive generations called?
ANSWER: NATURAL SELECTION
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Organisms that make their own food are called
ANSWER: AUTOTROPHS

X) cerebrum
