1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which of the following 3 are modified plant stems? 1) Tubers 2) Stolons 3) Rhizomes
ANSWER: all
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which clade isn't a vertebrate?
W) Petramyzontida
X) Mammalia
Y) Actinista
Z) Cephalachordata
ANSWER: Z) Cephalachordata
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the electron configuration of the fluoride ion (F 1-)?
ANSWER: 1s2 2s2 2p6
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer NH3 has what type of molecular geometry?
ANSWER: Trigonal pyramidal
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A ball is being spun in circles with a string. What happens to centripetal acceleration when the length of the string is doubled?
W) quadrupled
X) doubled
Y) unchanged
Z) halved
ANSWER: Z) halved
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Magma that is at 680 degrees Celsius is most likely
W) Felsic
X) ultramafic
Y) mafic
Z) intermediate
ANSWER: W) Felsic
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Compared to oceanic crust, continental crust is which of the following?
W) Thicker and denser
X) Thicker and less dense
Y) Thinner and denser
Z) Thinner and less dense
ANSWER: X) Thicker and less dense
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice In 2009, most of the United States' biomass came from
W) wood and wood waste
X) biofuels
Y) garbage and landfills waste
Z) just ethanol
ANSWER: W) wood and wood waste
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Nitrogen is approximately what percent of the gases in Earth's atmosphere?
W) 1%
X) 23%
Y) 59%
Z) 78%
ANSWER: Z) 78%
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is name of the lateral meristem that adds secondary phloem and xylem?
ANSWER: vascular cambium
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the following plant partitions of a tree from innermost to outermost: cork cambium, periderm, primary xylem, secondary phloem
ANSWER: primary xylem, secondary phloem, cork cambium, periderm (3,4,1,2)
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following 4 which fall under the category tephra. 1) volcanic ash 2) lapilli 3) volcanic bomb 4) volcanic block
ANSWER: all
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer The average cost of the kilowatt-hour is 12 cents. If you read for five hours with a 100-W light bulb, how much does it cost?
ANSWER: 6 cents
