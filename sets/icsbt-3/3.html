1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer As a plant grows in desperation and finally reaches a light source, it begins to slow down stem elongation, expands its leaves, and increases chlorophyll production. What is the name of this process?
ANSWER: De Etiolation (do not accept: Etiolation)
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three events that characteristically occur during prometaphase 1) The nuclear envelope breaks down 2) Spindle microtubules attach to kinetochores 3) Chromosomes begin active movement toward the metaphase plate
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3 (also accept: All)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which of the following 4 compounds are considered weak acids? 1) Hydrocyanic Acid 2) Nitric Acid 3) Chloric Acid 4) Nitrous Acid

W) Left-Front
X) Left-Back
Y) Right-Front
Z) Right-Back
ANSWER: Y) Right-Front

W) Depleted in silica, lower temperature
X) Depleted in silica, higher temperature
