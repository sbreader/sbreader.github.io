1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice If you stick a thermometer into a frozen slurry of lemonade as it is melting, you will find that the reading on the thermometer is:
W) Slowly falling
X) Not changing, as long as there is ice
Y) Slowly rising
Z) Quickly rising
ANSWER: X) Not changing, as long as there is ice
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer At incredibly low temperatures near 0 Kelvin, atoms coalesce into what substance?
ANSWER: BOSE-EINSTEIN CONDENSATE
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of an igneous rock?
W) Flint
X) Obsidian
Y) Anthracite
Z) Chalk
ANSWER: X) Obsidian
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice You discover an igneous rock with a course-grained texture and the appearance of a mass of intergrown crystals of similar size. At which of the following locations did magma most likely solidify?
W) In the ocean
X) On the surface
Y) Far below the surface
Z) No more than 100 feet below the surface
ANSWER: Y) Far below the surface
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why most food chains have fewer than five organisms in them?
W) Energy is too complex to cycle through multiple steps
X) The amount of energy at the producer level can only support a limited number of consumers
Y) There is only enough room on Earth for a certain number of animals, and they are divided into food chains
Z) Ecosystems can only support a certain number of organisms, and they are divided into food chains
ANSWER: X) The amount of energy at the producer level can only support a limited number of consumers
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice An organism's way of life, including its habitat, food, predators, and other organisms it competes with for resources make up an organism's:
W) Ecosystem
X) Niche
Y) Biome
Z) Food pyramid
ANSWER: X) Niche
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice If convection currents in the mantle stopped, which of the following would likely happen?
W) The number of earthquakes would increase
X) The number of earthquakes would stay the same
Y) Earthquakes would get stronger
Z) The number of earthquakes would decrease
ANSWER: Z) The number of earthquakes would decrease
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In what state of matter are convection currents unable to transfer heat?
ANSWER: SOLID
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the rigid segments into which the Earth's crust is divided.
ANSWER: PLATES (also accept: TECTONIC PLATES)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Earth's crust accounts for about what percentage of the Earth's volume?
W) 1%
X) 5%
Y) 10%
Z) 15%
ANSWER: W) 1%
6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer If the temperature change of a pot of water is 180 Kelvin, what is the temperature change in degrees Celsius?
ANSWER: 180
6. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following household appliances uses the least amount of energy over the course of a year?
W) Refrigerator
X) Water heater
Y) Air conditioner
Z) Dishwasher
ANSWER: Z) Dishwasher
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the name for the vector quantity defined as the rate of change of position?
ANSWER: VELOCITY
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer In 12 seconds, a car goes from 0 to 60 miles per hour. In miles per hour per second, what is its average rate of acceleration?
ANSWER: 5
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An ice cube is placed in a perfectly insulated box with an air temperature of 60° Fahrenheit and covered. Sometime later, the box is opened and the ice cube has completely melted. How will the air temperature in the box have changed?
ANSWER: IT WILL BE LOWER
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer According to the laws of entropy, all useful forms of energy are converted into what form of energy?
ANSWER: HEAT
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Shortly after its formation, Earth's moon was:
W) Closer to Earth than it is now
X) Farther from Earth than it is now
Y) The same distance from Earth as it is now
Z) Oscillating between further from and closer to Earth
ANSWER: W) Closer to Earth than it is now

Y) Igneous rock
Z) Rock similar in composition to a metallic meteor

Z) It will not be different
ANSWER: X) It will be higher
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Ariah is holding a bowling ball and Robert is holding a tennis ball on the roof of their school. Ariah and Robert drop these objects off the roof at the same time. Neglecting air resistance, what will happen?
W) Ariah's bowling ball will hit the ground first due to its greater mass
X) Robert's tennis ball will hit the ground first due to its faster acceleration
Y) The tennis ball and the bowling ball will hit the ground at the same time
Z) The result can't be determined without knowing the ball's density
ANSWER: Y) The tennis ball and the bowling ball will hit the ground at the same time
