2. BONUS Math Short Answer A quadratic in x passes through the points (0, 4), (1, 5), and (2, 8). What is the y-coordinate of the intersection of the quadratic with the line x = 10?
ANSWER: 104
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A fire hydrant can supply 25 liters of water per second at a pressure of 400 kilopascals. In kilowatts, how much power is carried by the water?
ANSWER: 10
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab have been studying hundreds of secondary metabolite compounds within plant species all over the world to better understand their production. Many of these compounds have been found to permanently inhibit the germination of nearby seeds. What family of plant hormone is likely inhibited by these metabolites?
ANSWER: GIBBERELLINS
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer The hormone leptin is released by what class of connective tissue cells?
ANSWER: ADIPOSE CELLS
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying the difference in reaction rates between molecules of gamma-lithium aluminate based on whether the lithium atom has 3 or 4 neutrons. What is the term for this effect?
ANSWER: KINETIC ISOTOPE EFFECT
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An ideal spring has a force constant of 100 N/m and a relaxed length of 50 cm. One end of the spring is attached to a ceiling, and a 5 kg weight is hung from the other end. To the nearest cm, what is the stretched length of the spring?
ANSWER: 99
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding photoperiodism 1) Day neutral plants do not display photoperiodism 2) Photoperiodism is mediated by cryptochromes only 3) A short day plant with a critical period of 6 hours will flower if subjected to 24 hours of light interrupted by darkness every five minutes.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Evaluate the expression: log base 2 of 8 + log base 8 of 2.
ANSWER: 10/3 (also accept: 3 1/3)
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer To the nearest meter, what is the maximum possible horizontal range of a projectile fired from flat ground with an initial velocity of 10 meters per second?
ANSWER: 10
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying the trans-Pacific propagation of aerosols. What is the dominant global wind circulation at 45 degrees North latitude that carries aerosols across the Pacific?
ANSWER: WESTERLIES (also accept: ANTI-TRADE WINDS; PREVAILING WESTERLIES)
17. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab have been researching new methods to reduce the atmospheric concentration of carbon dioxide, one of which involves injecting carbon dioxide into subsurface aquifers. What is the term for this general process?
ANSWER: CARBON SEQUESTRATION (also accept: CARBON DIOXIDE REMOVAL; CDR)
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The heating curve of a substance plots heat absorbed on the x axis and temperature on the y axis. What quantity is represented by the slope of this curve?
W) Heat capacity
X) One over heat capacity
Y) Enthalpy of vaporization
Z) Enthalpy of sublimation
ANSWER: X) One over heat capacity
18. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In one reaction, propene is treated with boron trihydride and tetrahydrofuran, followed by hydrogen peroxide and sodium hydroxide. In another reaction, acetone is treated with lithium aluminum hydride. What is the relationship between the major products formed in each reaction?
W) Structural isomers
X) Diastereomers
Y) Enantiomers
Z) Identical
ANSWER: W) Structural isomers
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer What glycoprotein does thrombin cleave to cause the formation of platelet plugs?
ANSWER: FIBRINOGEN (do not accept: FIBRIN)
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer An infinite geometric sequence starts with first term 128 and second term 64. How many terms in this sequence are integers?
ANSWER: 8
20. BONUS Math Short Answer Regular octagon ABCDEFGH has side length 1. What is the area of the square ACEG?
ANSWER: 2 + √2 (also accept: 2 PLUS ROOT 2)
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two spaceships are flying directly towards each other, with an observer in the middle of their paths. Relative to the observer, each spaceship is traveling at a speed of 5/6 c. What is the speed of the second spaceship relative to the first spaceship in terms of c?
ANSWER: 60c/61
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Indicating proper charge, what is the oxidation state of the phosphorus atom in phosphorous acid?
ANSWER: +3 (also accept: 3+) (do not accept: 3)
23. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice How many distinct carbon-oxygen bond lengths exist in the iron(III) complex coordinated by three oxalate ligands?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 3
Z) 6
ANSWER: X) 2
