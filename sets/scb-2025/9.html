1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In nonmetallic compounds, the difference in energies between the HOMO and LUMO can be used to predict the chemical reactivity and stability of a compound. What quantity in metals, analogous to the HOMO-LUMO difference for nonmetals, represents the range where no electrons can exist, and is measured as the difference between the conduction band and valence band?
ANSWER: Band gap
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following four reactions which are expected to be exothermic 1) The attraction of Mg2+ and O2- ions to form solid magnesium oxide 2) The dissociation of solid magnesium oxide to Mg2+ and O2- ions 3) The formation of the O2- ion through the addition of electrons to monoatomic oxygen 4) The formation of the Mg2+ ion through the release of electrons from magnesium

7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains the difference between metasomatism and contact metamorphism?
W) Metasomatism involves changes in chemical composition from fluid percolation while contact metamorphism involves the conduction of heat from magma
X) Contact metamorphism involves changes in chemical composition from fluid percolation while metasomatism involves the conduction of heat from magma
Y) Metasomatism alters rocks primarily through directed pressure while contact metamorphism is driven by circulating hydrothermal fluids

8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice A concentration cell is set up using 2 different containers A and B containing Cu2+ ions and copper electrodes. If the concentration of Cu2+ ions in container A is 0.6 molar, and the concentration of Cu2+ ions in container B is 0.2 molar, then which of the following statements regarding the flow of electrons in the cell is true?
W) Electrons will flow from A to B
X) Electrons will flow from B to A
Y) Electrons will not flow
Z) The reduction potentials must be known to determine
