4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following reasons best describes why birds are able to sit on a power line without being electrocuted, while humans can die from standing on the ground near a lightning strike?
W) The outer coating of power lines protects the birds’ feet, but there is no protective coating on the ground
X) Humans are much less resistive than birds, causing more current to flow through them
