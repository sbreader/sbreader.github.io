Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice When considering atoms, the letter Z stands for which of the following?
W) Mass Number
X) Atomic Number
Y) Element
Z) Atomic Mass
ANSWER: X) Atomic Number
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is an indicator commonly used in a titration?
W) Diphenylamine
X) Viologen
Y) Diphenylbenzidine
Z) Thymolphthalein
ANSWER: Z) Thymolphthalein
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the point in a titration at which the acid is completely reacted with or neutralized by a base called?
ANSWER: EQUIVALENCE POINT (also accept: STOICHIOMETRIC POINT)
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which gas law describes how gasses tend to expand when heated?
W) Charles' Law
X) Boyle's Law
Y) Avogadro's Law
Z) Dalton's Law
ANSWER: W) Charles' Law
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the equation of state for a fluid composed of particles of non-zero size and pairwise attractive interparticle force, and is based on a modification of the ideal gas law?
ANSWER: VAN DER WAAL'S EQUATION
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Name one of the two scientists who formulated the equation that is a means of calculating lattice energy of a crystalline ionic compound.
ANSWER: MAX BORN (also accept: ALFRED LANDE)
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which famous scientist offered a theoretical explanation of the emission spectrum of a hydrogen atom?
W) Albert Einstein
X) Max Planck
Y) Enrico Fermi
Z) Niels Bohr
ANSWER: Z) Niels Bohr
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What physical constant represents the limiting value of the highest wavenumber of any photon that can be emitted from the hydrogen atom?
ANSWER: RYDBERG CONSTANT
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which one of the following elements has the highest electron affinity value of the given set?
W) Lithium
X) Lead
Y) Nitrogen
Z) Iodine
ANSWER: Z) Iodine
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which group on the periodic table has the most negative electron affinity values?
ANSWER: HALOGENS (also accept: GROUP 7A)
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice If the difference between the electronegativity of two elements is 3.5, then the bond between these elements can be said to be
W) Strongly Ionic
X) Strongly Covalent
Y) Weakly Ionic
Z) Weakly Covalent
ANSWER: W) Strongly Ionic
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What happens to the electronegativity of an element as the oxidation number of that element increases?
ANSWER: INCREASES
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the non-SI unit that is typically used to express dipole moments?
ANSWER: DEBYE
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What are the attractive forces that arise as a result of temporary dipoles induced in the atom or molecules called?
W) Ion-Dipole Forces
X) Induced Dipole Forces
Y) Polarized Forces
Z) Dispersion Forces
ANSWER: Z) Dispersion Forces
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Within 10 kilojoules per mole, what is the energy of a hydrogen bond?
ANSWER: 40 KILOJOULES PER MOLE (also accept: any answer from 30 KILOJOULES PER MOLE to 50 KILOJOULES PER MOLE)
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which law states that the solubility of a gas in a liquid is proportional to the pressure of the gas over the solution?
W) Henry's Law
X) Raoult's Law
Y) Duhring's Rule
Z) Dalton's Law
ANSWER: W) Henry's Law
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What order is the reaction, SO2Cl2(g) → SO2(g) + Cl2(g), classified as?
W) Zero-Order Reaction
X) First-Order Reaction
Y) Second-Order Reaction
Z) Pseudo First-Order Reaction
ANSWER: X) First-Order Reaction
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A certain first-order reaction has a rate constant of 6.93 × 10–4seconds-1. Given that the natural logarithm of 1 is 0, the natural logarithm of 2 is 0.693 and the natural logarithm of 3 is 1.10, find the half life of the reaction.
ANSWER: 1000 SECONDS (also accept: 1.00 × 103 SECONDS)
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice If the concentration equilibrium constant is greater than the reaction quotient in a reaction, which of the following is true?
W) The system proceeds from right to left to reach equilibrium
X) The system is at equilibrium
Y) The system proceeds from left to right to achieve equilibrium
Z) The system cannot reach equilibrium
ANSWER: Y) The system proceeds from left to right to achieve equilibrium
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which law, first proposed by Cato Guldberg and Peter Waage, relates the concentration of reactants and products at equilibrium?
ANSWER: LAW OF MASS ACTION
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the strongest acid that can exist in an aqueous solution without reacting with water?
ANSWER: H3O+ (also accept: hydronium ion)
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice If the base ionization constant is greater than the acid ionization constant for a system, which of the following is true?
W) At equilibrium, there will be more hydroxide ions than hydrogen ions
X) At equilibrium, the solution will be neutral
Y) At equilibrium, there will be more hydrogen ions than hydroxide ions
Z) At equilibrium, the solution will become more acidic
ANSWER: W) At equilibrium, there will be more hydroxide ions than hydrogen ions
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the term used that describes a reaction of an anion with a cation of a salt, with water?
ANSWER: SALT HYDROLYSIS
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Ions of which element give off a violet flame when conducting a flame test?
W) Sodium
X) Potassium
Y) Lithium
Z) Copper
ANSWER: X) Potassium
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is one of the most significant non-biological ways in which nitrogen fixation occurs naturally on Earth?
W) Haber-Bosch Process
X) Combustion
Y) Cyanobacteria
Z) Lightning
ANSWER: Z) Lightning
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What are the two final results of nitrogen fixation through lightning?
ANSWER: NITROUS ACID AND NITRIC ACID (also accept: HNO2 AND HNO3)
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is true for a reaction that proceeds spontaneously only at high temperatures?
W) Delta H is negative and delta S is positive
X) Delta H is positive and delta S is negative
Y) Delta H is positive and delta S is positive
Z) Delta H is negative and delta S is negative
ANSWER: Y) Delta H is positive and delta S is positive
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Predict qualitatively the sign of the entropy change expected for the following reaction: Ag+(aq) + Cl–(aq) → AgCl(s)?
ANSWER: NEGATIVE
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the electrode at which oxidation occurs called?
W) Triode
X) Anode
Y) Cathode
Z) Diode
ANSWER: X) Anode
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which animal had Luigi Galvani been observing when he realized that the muscles of the dead animal twitched when struck by a spark?
ANSWER: A FROG
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following categories for the methods of metal purification does the Mond Process, used to refine nickel, fall under?
W) Distillation
X) Electrolysis
Y) Zone Refining
Z) Leeching
ANSWER: W) Distillation
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What are semiconductors that contain acceptor impurities called?
ANSWER: P-TYPE SEMICONDUCTORS
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the term used to describe the ability of a chemical element to form long chain-like structures via covalent bonds called, such as those prominently displayed in carbon compounds?
W) Catenation
X) Concatenation
Y) Polymeration
Z) Procatenation
ANSWER: W) Catenation
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an example of a chelating agent?
W) EDTA
X) Monodentate ligand
Y) Bidentate ligand
Z) Polydentate ligand
ANSWER: X) Monodentate ligand
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What term is used to specify the number of donor atoms surrounding a central atom in a complex ion?
ANSWER: COORDINATION NUMBER
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following sources for the average yearly radiation doses for Americans is the least significant?
W) Cosmic rays
X) Air travel
Y) Medical and dental X-rays
Z) Nuclear waste
ANSWER: Z) Nuclear waste
23. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What term is used to describe molecular fragments having one or more unpaired electrons that usually have short lives and are highly reactive?
ANSWER: RADICALS (also accept: FREE RADICALS)
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Suppose you are given an aromatic compound that consists of a benzene ring with two bromine groups positioned so that they are opposite of each other. What is the name for this compound?
W) o-dibromobenzene
X) m-dibromobenzene
Y) p-dibromobenzene
Z) n-dibromobenzene
ANSWER: Y) p-dibromobenzene
24. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Name the aryl group that consists of two benzene rings fused together.
ANSWER: NAPTHALENE
25. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which category of polypropylene includes polypropylenes that do not pack well and are generally rubbery, amorphous, and relatively weak?
W) Isotactic
X) Syndiotactic
Y) Atactic
Z) Ditactic
ANSWER: Y) Atactic
25. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Name the popular synthetic polymer invented in 1938, which is a fluorocarbon solid that is commonly used as a non-stick coating on pans.
ANSWER: TEFLON (also accept: POLYTETRAFLUOROETHYLENE; PTFE)
