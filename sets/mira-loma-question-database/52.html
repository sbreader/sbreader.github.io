142. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice What is the term for the imaginary current that runs through a charging capacitor, which allows for Ampere's Law to hold, as long as Maxwell's correction is in place?
W) stray current
X) displacement current
Y) magnetic current
Z) potential current
ANSWER: X) displacement current
143. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer On a Pressure-Volume curve, what quantity does an enclosed area symbolize?
ANSWER: work
143. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Symbolized by gamma, what is the term for the ratio of specific heats Cp/Cv, which is applied in PV problems in ideal cases?
ANSWER: adiabatic index (also accept: isentropic expansion factor)
144. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are nucleotides lacking a 3'-hydroxyl group on their deoxyribose sugar, which are used in Sanger's method of DNA sequencing?
ANSWER: dideoxynucleotides
144. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is an alternate DNA sequencing method based on chemical modification of DNA and subsequent cleavage at specific bases, published shortly after Sanger's?
ANSWER: Maxam-Gilbert method
