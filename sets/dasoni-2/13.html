1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following hormones that somatostatin inhibits 1) Vasopressin 2) Thyroid-stimulating hormone 3) Growth hormone
ANSWER: 2 and 3
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A vertically displaced parcel of air oscillates in a statically stable atmosphere. What is the name of this frequency of oscillation?
ANSWER: Brunt–Väisälä frequency
3. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following theorems states that if H is a subgroup of G, the number of distinct left cosets of H in G is equal to the number of elements of G divided by the number of elements of H?
W) Euler's theorem
X) Lagrange's theorem
Y) Gauss's theorem
Z) Laplace's theorem
ANSWER: X) Lagrange's theorem
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Beta oxidation of odd-chained fatty acids produces what final product instead of acetyl-CoA, which allows remaining carbon atoms to be oxidized to carbon dioxide or utilized in glucose production?
ANSWER: Propionyl-CoA
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In retrosynthetic analysis of organic reactions, what term is given to the hypothetical idealized reagents which are charged fragments of the target molecule created from a disconnection, and which could be combined in a reaction to synthesize the final product?
ANSWER: Synthons
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Instead of writing questions, Rahib has been studying Barrovian metamorphism within mountain ranges. Order the following three orogeny periods of the Appalachian mountains from oldest to most recent. 1) Acadian 2) Taconic 3) Alleghian.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not an eicosanoid derivative?
W) Leukotrienes
X) Prostaglandins
Y) Thromboxanes
Z) Endothelins
ANSWER: Z) Endothelins
8. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following values does the hypervolume of a n-dimensional unit hypersphere approach as n approaches infinity?
W) 0
X) 1
Y) 1/e
Z) 1/π
ANSWER: W) 0
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following organisms that have crops in order to aid digestion 1) Earthworm 2) Grasshopper 3) Songbird
ANSWER: All

ANSWER: Foehn Wall
11. BONUS Math Short Answer What are the first two digits of 10011001?
ANSWER: 27

X) Zeolites

ANSWER: Z) Penetration twins
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Lovastatin, a commonly prescribed drug for patients with familial hypercholesterolemia, reduces cholesterol synthesis by inhibiting what enzyme which catalyzes its rate-limiting step?
ANSWER: HMG-CoA reductase
14. BONUS Math Short Answer The Langevin function is defined by L(x) = coth(x)-1/x. What is the derivative of this function evaluated at x = 0?
ANSWER: 1/3
