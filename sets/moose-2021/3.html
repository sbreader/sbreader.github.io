Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is an endocrine gland?
W) Salivary gland
X) Pineal gland
Y) Lacrimal gland
Z) Mammary gland
ANSWER: X) Pineal gland
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What hormone activates the breakdown of glycogen into glucose?
ANSWER: GLUCAGON
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What power of 10 most accurately describes the approximate number of stars in the Milky Way?
W) 109
X) 1010
Y) 1011
Z) 1012
ANSWER: Y) 1011
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Lab are studying superconductors. The properties of superconductors come from loosely bound pairs of electrons with opposite spins, usually called what?
ANSWER: COOPER PAIRS
3. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Oak Ridge National Laboratory is working with Youngstown State University to research battery technology. One possible way to make batteries cheaper is to remove what expensive key element that is often mined in conditions that violate human rights?
ANSWER: COBALT (also accept: Co)
3. BONUS Math Multiple Choice 4 coins are flipped. If one of them is guaranteed heads, what is the probability that 3 heads are flipped?
W) 1/8
X) 1/4
Y) 4/15
Z) 1/2
ANSWER: Y) 4/15
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the molarity, in moles per liter, of a 100 mL solution containing 0.5 moles of glucose, or C6H12O6?
ANSWER: 5
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of front will usually create the most precipitation and is often found in the middle of large storms?
ANSWER: OCCLUDED
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A set of 2021 real numbers has a mean of 8 and a standard deviation of 3, and it follows a normal distribution. What is the probability, to the nearest percent, that a randomly chosen number from the set lies between 5 and 11?
ANSWER: 68%
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice An increase in the plant hormone auxin in a plant cell will cause which of the following?
W) Mitosis
X) Differentiation
Y) Elongation
Z) Plasmolysis
ANSWER: Y) Elongation
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following conditions is typically NOT associated with cyclones in the United States?
W) Low pressure
X) Strong updrafts
Y) Clockwise rotation
Z) Divergence aloft
ANSWER: Y) Clockwise rotation
6. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Lawrence Livermore National Lab are studying ways to improve efficiency of vehicles powered by hydrogen fuel cells. These vehicles usually have inefficient hydrogen storage due to which of the following properties of hydrogen?
W) High reactivity
X) Low density
Y) Small atomic radius
Z) Diatomic nature
ANSWER: X) Low density
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the number of valence electrons in the neutral oxygen atom?
ANSWER: 6
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer In pea plants, the tall gene is dominant to the short gene. A homozygous dominant plant for this gene is crossed with a homozygous recessive plant. What percent of the F2 generation will be short?
ANSWER: 25 (also accept: 25%)
8. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Scientists at Los Alamos National Lab are developing improved technology for hydrogen fuel cells. These fuel cells are a clean alternative to fossil fuels because they only emit what compound as waste?
ANSWER: WATER (also accept: H2O)
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer If Person A yells at 80 decibels and Person B yells at 100 decibels, what is the ratio of loudness of Person A to Person B?
ANSWER: 1 TO 100 (also accept: 1/100; 1:100)
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of inheritance would best classify a disease that is more common in males, where heterozygous females carry the trait but do not display it?
ANSWER: X-LINKED RECESSIVE
9. BONUS Math Multiple Choice The sum of 3 numbers is 12. The first number is divisible by 3. The second number is a perfect square. The third number is divisible by 4. If all three numbers are distinct, positive integers, which of the following numbers is not one of the three?
W) 1
X) 3
Y) 5
Z) 8
ANSWER: Y) 5
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the point at which the solid, liquid, and gas phases of a substance can coexist?
ANSWER: TRIPLE POINT
10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice By definition, chinooks are winds that create hot, dry conditions due to the rain shadow effect in which of the following locations?
W) Central Asia
X) Eastern Europe
Y) Western South America
Z) Western North America
ANSWER: Z) Western North America
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the area of the triangle bounded by the lines: y = –2x + 6, y = 0, and x = 0?
ANSWER: 9
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The Moon is estimated to have up to a billion metric tons of what substance that can be split to produce rocket fuel?
ANSWER: ICE (also accept: WATER)
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Interactions between the atmosphere and charged particles from the Sun produce what effect commonly seen near Earth's poles?
ANSWER: AURORA
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Substances with conchoidal fracture are rarely considered minerals because they tend to break which of the following requirements?
W) Definite chemical composition
X) Orderly crystalline structure
Y) Naturally occurring
Z) Inorganic
ANSWER: X) Orderly crystalline structure
13. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Although nuclear-powered spacecraft are currently the best option for long-term space missions, nearly every operating spacecraft and satellite uses what technology to produce energy directly from the Sun?
ANSWER: PHOTOVOLTAICS (also accept: SOLAR PANELS)
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Farmer Tom has a field that needs to be fenced. His field is in the shape of a five point star. If all sides of the star are equal, and each has length 20 feet, what is the minimum number of posts he will need if the distance between posts cannot exceed 4 feet?
ANSWER: 50
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Sapphire and ruby are two gemstone forms of what common mineral?
ANSWER: CORUNDUM
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following groups from MOST to LEAST reactive 1) Alkali Earth Metals 2) Alkali Metals 3) Transition Metals 4) Noble Gases
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3, 4
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer An isosceles triangle has angle ABC equal to 30 degrees. What are all the possible measures of angle BCA? 1) 30 2) 45 3) 75 4) 120
ANSWER: 1 and 3 and 4
15. BONUS Energy Short Answer Approximately two-thirds of the natural gas in the United States is produced via what controversial process that contaminates water, pollutes air, and can even create small earthquakes?
ANSWER: FRACKING
16. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer A DOE project named ULTIMATE seeks to find materials that can withstand temperatures above 1300 degrees Celsius. These materials will be used to capture what energy source that makes up more than a third of the electricity produced nationwide?
ANSWER: NATURAL GAS
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice If the average lifespan of a red blood cell is 4 months, which of the following is closest to the amount of time the cell spends in interphase?
W) 10 days
X) 30 days
Y) 70 days
Z) 110 days
ANSWER: Z) 110 days
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following that are present in most higher plant cell mitosis 1) Cell Plate 2) Centrioles 3) Cytokinesis
ANSWER: 1 and 3
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Cepheid variables are a common tool to find distances to nearby galaxies. What two characteristics of Cepheid variables allow us to directly determine their distance from us?
ANSWER: PULSATION PERIOD (also accept: LUMINOSITY)
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What nuclear mechanism best describes how the Sun fuses hydrogen into helium?
ANSWER: PROTON-PROTON CHAIN REACTION (also accept: PROTON-PROTON CHAIN; P-P CHAIN)
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer As indicated on the phase transition diagram of water, how many phase transitions does water undergo at STP over the open interval of 0 degrees Celsius to 100 degrees Celsius?
ANSWER: 0
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the name for any material that has no electrical resistance?
ANSWER: SUPERCONDUCTOR
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer In a pedigree for an autosomal recessive trait, what would a half shaded circle mean in terms of gender and condition?
ANSWER: A WOMAN WHO IS A CARRIER (also accept: FEMALE CARRIER)
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What law in genetics states that during meiosis, when homologous chromosomes divide to form haploid cells, this happens randomly?
ANSWER: THE LAW OF INDEPENDENT ASSORTMENT
40. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which plant hormone is responsible for phototropism?
ANSWER: AUXIN
41. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Simplify the following expression: (x2 + 6x + 5)/(-x-5)
ANSWER: -x-1
42. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which two elements on the periodic table are liquid at room temperature and pressure?
ANSWER: MERCURY AND BROMINE (also accept: BROMINE AND MERCURY; Hg AND Br)
43. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the specific name for the most common type of galaxies, which have spherical or ovoid shapes and usually are satellite galaxies?
ANSWER: DWARF ELLIPTICAL
44. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a colligative property of a solution?
W) Freezing point depression
X) Boiling point elevation
Y) Osmotic pressure
Z) Latent heat of fusion
ANSWER: Z) Latent heat of fusion
45. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following must be true of a neutral atom?
W) The atomic number is the same as the number of neutrons in the atom
X) The number of neutrons is the same for every atom of that element
Y) The atomic number is the same as the number of electrons in the atom
Z) If the element is a transition metal, it cannot have a stable isotope
ANSWER: Y) The atomic number is the same as the number of electrons in the atom
46. BONUS Math Multiple Choice According to Descartes' Law of Signs, how many positive and negative roots could the equation x5 + 7x4 – 18x3 – x2 – 8x + 4 = 0
W) POSITIVE: 3 OR 1, NEGATIVE: 2 OR 0
X) POSITIVE: 2 OR 0, NEGATIVE: 3 OR 1
Y) POSITIVE: 3 OR 1, NEGATIVE: 3 OR 1
Z) POSITIVE: 1 OR 0, NEGATIVE: 5 OR 3
ANSWER: X) POSITIVE: 2 OR 0, NEGATIVE: 3 OR 1
