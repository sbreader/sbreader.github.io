1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice (All Bio questions) Which of the following is a disaccharide?
W) Glyceraldehyde
X) Ribose
Y) Fructose
Z) Lactose
ANSWER: Z) Lactose
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What kind of sugar is used in glycogen?
ANSWER: Alpha glucose
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What type of bonds do not hold together tertiary structure?
W) Disulfide bridges
X) Hydrophobic Interactions
Y) Polar Covalent Bonds
Z) Ionic Bonds
ANSWER: Y) Polar Covalent Bonds
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the pyrimidines?
ANSWER: Cytosine, Thymine, Uracil
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of channel proteins facilitates the passage of water molecules through the cell?
ANSWER: Aquaporins
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In diffusion, substances diffuse down the region along which the density decreases, which is called what?
ANSWER: Concentration gradient
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice If a cell with a cell wall is placed in a hypotonic solution, what will happen to it?
W) It will become turgid.
X) It will lyse.
Y) It will stay the same state.
Z) It will become plasmolyzed.
ANSWER: W) It will become turgid.
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of channel proteins open or close in response to a stimulus?
ANSWER: Ion channels
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the study of how energy flows through living animals called?
ANSWER: Bioenergetics
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the study of cells called?
ANSWER: Cytology
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Glucose is converted into glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate and dihydroxyacetone phosphate by what process?
ANSWER: Glycolysis
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What enzyme catalyzes the transformation of Dihydroxyacetone Phosphate into G3P?
ANSWER: Isomerase
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What protein complex makes ATP from ADP and inorganic phosphate?
ANSWER: ATP Synthase
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What organisms cannot survive in the presence of oxygen?
ANSWER: Obligate anaerobes
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the incorporation of carbon into organic compounds in the Calvin cycle called?
ANSWER: Carbon fixation
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What hydrocarbons have colors of yellow and orange and broaden the spectrum of colors that drive photosynthesis?
ANSWER: Carotenoids
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In what stage of mitosis does the nuclear envelope fragment?
W) Prophase
X) Prometaphase
Y) Metaphase
Z) Anaphase
ANSWER: X) Prometaphase
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the noncoding segments of nucleic acids that lie between coding regions called?
ANSWER: Introns
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When all of the voltage-gated sodium channels are opened, a massive change in membrane voltage occurs called what?
ANSWER: Action potential
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the adaptation that enables fast conduction in narrow axons?
ANSWER: Myelin Sheath
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of immunity is found in all animals and is active immediately upon infection and is the same whether or not the pathogen has been encountered previously?
ANSWER: Innate immunity
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the most abundant phagocytic cells in the body?
ANSWER: Neutrophils
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What type of cells detect pathogens due to fragments of molecules characteristic of a set of pathogens?
W) Toll like receptors
X) Macrophages
Y) Eosinophils
Z) Dendritic cells
ANSWER: W) Toll like receptors
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What proteins provide defense against viral infections and are secreted by virus-infected body cells?
ANSWER: Interferons
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Where is histamine stored?
ANSWER: Mast cells
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of cells recognize and eliminate diseased cells in vertebrates due to the dection of class I MHC molecules?
ANSWER: Natural killer cells
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following has a two-chambered heart?
W) Turtles
X) Snakes
Y) Lizards
Z) Fish
ANSWER: Z) Fish
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What chamber of the human heart pumps blood to the capillaries of the legs?
ANSWER: Left ventricle
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What cluster of autorhythmic cells keeps the rate and timing of contractions of cardiac muscle cells?
ANSWER: Sinoatrial node
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer If blood squirts backward through a defective valve, what sound does this produce?
ANSWER: Heart murmur
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What process quickly makes many copies of target segments of DNA molecules?
ANSWER: PCR
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer What technique is used to insert recombinant DNA into eukaryotic cells through applying electrical pulses to a solution containing cells?
ANSWER: Electroporation
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are phages capable of both the lytic cycle and the lysogenic cycle within a bacterium called?
ANSWER: Temperate phages
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What principle states that the frequencies of alleles and genotypes in a population will remain constant from generation to generation?
ANSWER: Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer What kind of bonds holds primary structure of proteins together?
ANSWER: Peptide bonds
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What disaccharide is formed from a combination of glucose and fructose?
ANSWER: Sucroses
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer What organelles do animals use to digest macromolecules?
ANSWER: Lysosomes
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the three binding sites for tRNA?
ANSWER: E site, P site, A site
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer What cells are capable of differentiation into many different cell types?
ANSWER: Pluripotent cells
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are chemical changes in a single base pair of a gene called?
ANSWER: Point mutations
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are substitutions that change one amino acid to another one called?
ANSWER: Missense mutations
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are physical and chemical agents that cause mutations?
ANSWER: Mutagens
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are fruits that result from a single flower with more than one separate carpal?
ANSWER: Aggregate fruits
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are adaptations in plants for growing in darkness called?
ANSWER: Etiolation
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of plant steroids promote xylem differentiation and slow leaf abscission?
ANSWER: Brassinosteroids
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the minimum metabolic rate of a nongrowing endotherm at rest?
ANSWER: Basal metabolic rate
24. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the metabolic rate of a fasting, nonstressed ectotherm at rest at a particular temperature?
ANSWER: Standard Metabolic Rate
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is a physiological state in which activity is low and metabolism decreases?
ANSWER: Torpor
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer What hormone is produced in the hypothalamus and manages the osmoregulatory function of the kidneys?
ANSWER: ADH
