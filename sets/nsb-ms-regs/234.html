1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What alkaline earth metal, commonly used in X-ray-transparent windows, is lighter than aluminum but stiffer than steel?
ANSWER: BERYLLIUM
1. BONUS Energy Short Answer Oak Ridge National Lab operates the High Flux Isotope Reactor, which can produce rare radioisotopes. What particles have a high flux in this reactor?
ANSWER: NEUTRONS
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the largest quotient that can be formed using two of the following five numbers: –40, –2, 1, 8, 16?
ANSWER: 20
2. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the y-intercept of the line that contains the points (-2, 3) and (3, 10)?
ANSWER: 5.8 (also accept: 29/5, 5 4/5)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer By definition, what is the magnitude of a unit vector?
ANSWER: ONE
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer In meters per second and to one significant figure, what will be the magnitude of the final velocity when a 1000-kilogram mass traveling at 20 meters per second collides perfectly inelastically with a stationary mass of 3000 kilograms?
ANSWER: 5
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The reactions that the cell uses to harvest energy from sugars can be classified as what?
W) Decomposition
X) Synthesis
Y) Redox
Z) Combustion
ANSWER: Y) Redox
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer In a biology lab, what machine is used to sterilize glassware via the application of very hot steam?
ANSWER: AUTOCLAVE
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Joe's average score on his first four exams was 80. If his average after the fifth exam was 82, what did he score on the fifth test?
ANSWER: 90
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the coefficient of the ab2 term in the binomial expansion of (5a – 6b) cubed?
ANSWER: 540
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Wolframite is an ore for what transition metal?
ANSWER: TUNGSTEN
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is true regarding ocean tides and currents?
W) Tides are the horizontal movement of ocean water and currents are vertical movements
X) Tides are constant while currents are variable
Y) Both tides and currents are influenced by gravitational pull
Z) Tides and currents vary with the moon phases
ANSWER: Y) Both tides and currents are influenced by gravitational pull
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If an object is moving in uniform circular motion, there is a force vector pointing towards the center of the circle. What is the name of this force?
ANSWER: CENTRIPETAL FORCE (do not accept: CENTRIFUGAL FORCE)
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer How much work, in joules to the nearest integer, is done by gravity when a 2-kilogram ball falls 12 meters to the ground?
ANSWER: 235
8. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the most common type of geothermal plant?
ANSWER: FLASH STEAM
8. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice The capacity factor for a power plant is defined as the amount of electric energy generated over a given time period, divided by the amount of electric energy that would have been generated had that plant operated at 100% for the entire time period. Which of the following would be a typical capacity factor for a solar photovoltaic power plant?
W) 5%
X) 20%
Y) 50%
Z) 80%
ANSWER: X) 20%
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What are the smallest and largest integers that satisfy the following absolute value inequality: the absolute value of (x – 5) is less than 3?
ANSWER: 3 AND 7
9. BONUS Math Short Answer How many different (that is, non-congruent) isosceles triangles that are not equilateral have the length of each side being an integer between 3 and 6 inclusive?
ANSWER: 11
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In the water-gas shift reaction, what gas reacts with water?
ANSWER: CARBON MONOXIDE
10. BONUS Energy Short Answer The radioisotope thermoelectric generators used by NASA to power spacecraft primarily use what radioactive element as a heat source?
ANSWER: PLUTONIUM
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the two types of vascular tissue found in plants?
ANSWER: XYLEM AND PHLOEM
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What human organ produces glucagon?
ANSWER: PANCREAS
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of stellar event created the Crab Nebula?
ANSWER: SUPERNOVA
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Geologically, detritus consists of chunks and smaller grains of rock broken off other rocks by physical weathering and erosion. What is the term for a fragment of detritus?
W) Sediment
X) Clast
Y) Particle
Z) Breccia
ANSWER: X) Clast
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many lone pairs does a single molecule of water have?
ANSWER: TWO
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the oxidation number of sulfur in the sulfate ion, SO42-?
ANSWER: +6
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the percentage of mass in our Solar System belonging to the Sun?
W) 80
X) 90
Y) 95
Z) 99
ANSWER: Z) 99
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Chandrasekhar limit sets a theoretical maximum on which of the following properties of a white dwarf star?
W) Luminosity
X) Mass
Y) Age
Z) Temperature
ANSWER: X) Mass
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What organ system in the human body is responsible for coordinating activities of the body via hormones?
ANSWER: ENDOCRINE
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of tissue covers the surface of the vertebrate body and is derived from all three germ layers?
ANSWER: EPITHELIUM
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Just before the totality of a solar eclipse, the Moon's rough surface allows small patches of sunlight to break through. What is this effect known as?
W) Diamond ring effect
X) Bailey's beads
Y) Clavius spots
Z) Humboldt dots
ANSWER: X) Bailey's beads
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the planets of the Solar System has the longest orbital period?
ANSWER: NEPTUNE
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What allotrope of carbon is similar to graphite, but consists of only a single layer of atoms?
ANSWER: GRAPHENE
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer In order for nuclear fusion to occur, the repulsion from what fundamental force must be overcome?
ANSWER: ELECTROMAGNETIC
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the perimeter of a right triangle with legs of length 1 and 3?
ANSWER: 4 + √10
18. BONUS Math Short Answer A 5-digit palindrome has 3 different digits and is divisible by 3. What is the largest such number?
ANSWER: 98,589
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the name of the thermodynamic law that describes why the isothermal mixing of two ideal gases is spontaneous?
ANSWER: SECOND LAW OF THERMODYNAMICS (also accept: THE LAW OF ENTROPY; SECOND)
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The strongest known hydrogen bond occurs between the fluoride ion and what molecule?
ANSWER: HF (also accept: HYDROGEN FLUORIDE)
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the slope of the line with equation 3x + 7y = –21?
ANSWER: –3/7
20. BONUS Math Short Answer In a particular year, if April 17 is on a Friday, on what day of the week is October 29?
ANSWER: THURSDAY
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the small, fingerlike projections in the small intestine that increase the overall surface area?
ANSWER: VILLI (also accept: MICROVILLI)
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the adjective for the type of muscle contraction that involves the muscle remaining the same length throughout the contraction?
ANSWER: ISOMETRIC
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Lakes and rain on Titan are primarily composed of what compound?
ANSWER: METHANE
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In oceanography, what does the abreviation PSU stand for?
ANSWER: PRACTICAL SALINITY UNIT
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for RNA-based viruses that produce DNA once they infect their host?
ANSWER: RETROVIRUS
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer What kind of tropism in plants occurs as a result of touch?
ANSWER: THIGMOTROPISM
