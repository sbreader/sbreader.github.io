1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following options best describes the spatial separation of the light-dependent reactions and the Calvin cycle in photosynthesis?
W) The light-dependent reactions take place in the stroma and the Calvin cycle takes place in thylakoids
X) The light-dependent reactions take place across the inner chloroplast membrane and the Calvin cycle takes place in the stroma
Y) The light-dependent reactions take place across the thylakoid membrane and the Calvin cycle takes place in the stroma
Z) The light-dependent reactions take place across the outer chloroplast membrane and the Calvin cycle takes place in the intermembrane space
ANSWER: Y) The light-dependent reactions take place across the thylakoid membrane and the Calvin cycle takes place in the stroma
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is true regarding RNA splicing?
W) Splicing is the process of removing exons from the primary transcript

Z) RNA splicing takes place in the cytosol
ANSWER: Y) RNA splicing can lead to multiple different polypeptide chains from a single gene
