Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of cells that are myeloid cells I. T Cell II. Neutrophil III. B Cell
ANSWER: II ONLY (also accept: NEUTROPHIL)
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Before proofreading, which of the following is closest to the error rate for DNA polymerase?
W) 1 in Ten Million
X) 1 in Hundred Thousand
Y) 1 in Thousand
Z) 1 in Ten
ANSWER: X) 1 in Hundred Thousand
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following cations will form a colorless aqueous solution?
W) Co2+
X) Ni2+
Y) Cu2+
Z) Zn2+
ANSWER: Z) Zn2+
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice If an aqueous solution of potassium sulfate has a freezing point of −2.24 degrees Celsius, and given that the Kf of water is 1.86, which of the following is closest to the molality of the solution?
W) 0.4
X) 0.6
Y) 1
Z) 4
ANSWER: W) 0.4
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following four fermions that have a spin greater than ½ I. Up quark II. Top quark III. Electron IV. Muon neutrino
ANSWER: NONE
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer A free neutron decays into a proton and what other particle, which then decays into an electron and an electron neutrino?
ANSWER: W– BOSON (also accept: W BOSON) (do not accept: W+ BOSON)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Kimberlite is a host matrix for what mineral on the Mohs hardness scale?
ANSWER: DIAMOND
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following phenomena is correctly matched with its typical direction of travel?
W) Madden-Julian Oscillation; Eastward
X) Rossby Waves; Eastward
Y) Walker Circulation; Eastward
Z) Kelvin Waves; Westward
ANSWER: W) Madden-Julian Oscillation; Eastward
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Identify all of the following three infinite series that will converge, given that n starts at 1 I. 10n/n! II. n!/n50 III. (n+1)/(n2+1)
ANSWER: I ONLY
5. BONUS Math Multiple Choice A month with 31 days has the same number of Mondays as Wednesdays. Which of the following days of the week CANNOT be the first of the month?
W) Monday
X) Thursday
Y) Friday
Z) Saturday
ANSWER: Z) Saturday
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Argonne National Laboratory are studying the applications of molten salt reactors. Molten salt can be used as which of the following in this reactor?
W) Both fuel and coolant
X) Fuel only
Y) Coolant only
Z) Neither fuel nor coolant
ANSWER: W) Both fuel and coolant
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are studying neutron flux using the High Flux Isotope Reactor. In a core collapse supernova, a star’s neutron flux becomes very high and leads to what nucleosynthetic process that is responsible for creating about half of the elements heavier than iron?
ANSWER: RAPID NEUTRON CAPTURE PROCESS (also accept: R-PROCESS)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What hormone is inhibited by dopamine production in the arcuate nucleus?
W) Prolactin
X) Testosterone
Y) Adrenocorticotropic Hormone
Z) Growth Hormone
ANSWER: W) Prolactin
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer In amino acids and nucleic acids, a hydrogen atom can be shifted to form a structural isomer. What is the term for this type of isomer?
ANSWER: TAUTOMER
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three molecules that are nonpolar, but contain polar covalent bonds I. Carbon Dioxide II. Hydrogen Cyanide III. Water
ANSWER: I ONLY
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A Tollen’s Test is used to distinguish between aldehydes and ketones. A positive test results in the formation of a carboxylate ion and the precipitation of what metal, forming its namesake “mirror”?
ANSWER: SILVER
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The thermodynamic work required to add one electron to a solid state body is known as what?
ANSWER: FERMI LEVEL
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer A convex mirror has a focal length of 8 meters and an object is placed at a distance of 12 m in front of the mirror. Answer the following two questions I. Will the image appear in front of the mirror or behind the mirror? II. How far away from the mirror will the image appear, in meters?
ANSWER: IN FRONT OF THE MIRROR, 24
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes what will happen to a star’s central temperature and density, respectively, if the star begins to produce too much energy?
W) Increased central temperature, increased density
X) Increased central temperature, decreased density
Y) Decreased central temperature, increased density
Z) Decreased central temperature, decreased density
ANSWER: Z) Decreased central temperature, decreased density
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Select all of the following conditions ideal for the formation of a Thorne-Zytkow object I. Low mass red giant II. High mass neutron star III. Crowded globular cluster
ANSWER: I and III
11. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the units digit of the sum of the first 10 factorials?
W) 1
X) 3
Y) 5
Z) 7
ANSWER: X) 3
11. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Brian has two older twin brothers. If the product of all three of their ages is 243, then what is the sum of their ages?
W) 15
X) 18
Y) 21
Z) 24
ANSWER: Y) 21
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Fermilab are studying how particle decays violate CP symmetry. The violation of CP symmetry was first discovered because of the decay of what particle?
ANSWER: KAON (also accept: K MESON)
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Los Alamos National Lab recently observed a merger event between two neutron stars, which produced an unusual gamma ray burst. This event is known as what, which can also occur between a neutron star and a black hole?
ANSWER: KILONOVA
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Arin and Adrian have become beekeepers! They place a dish of sugar water in an open field where a worker bee is foraging for food. The bee eventually finds the dish, so they mark the bee and note that it returns to the same location for sugar water repeatedly. What type of learning does this example show?
W) Operant Conditioning
X) Classical Conditioning
Y) Habituation
Z) Imprinting
ANSWER: W) Operant Conditioning
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of plants that are angiosperms I. Gingko II. Sunflower III. Cycad
ANSWER: II ONLY
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three orbitals that have at least 2 angular nodes. I. 2s II. 3s III. 4p
ANSWER: NONE
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When the left-handed and right-handed form of an enantiomer is mixed in equal proportion, what type of mixture forms?
ANSWER: RACEMIC MIXTURE
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What type of current is the input for a rectifier and the input for an inverter, respectively?
ANSWER: AC and DC (do not accept: DC and AC)
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer What type of diode superconducts electrical current in one direction but is resistive in the other direction?
ANSWER: JOSEPHSON DIODE
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice During which of the following geologic time periods was Earth’s atmospheric oxygen content the highest in history?
W) Cambrian
X) Pennsylvanian
Y) Cretaceous
Z) Neogene
ANSWER: X) Pennsylvanian
16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice On which of the following landmasses would you NOT expect to find evidence of the late Paleozoic ice age?
W) Australia
X) Africa
Y) South America
Z) North America
ANSWER: Z) North America
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Let f(x) be the sum of the positive divisors of x excluding x itself. What is f(f(6))?
ANSWER: 6
17. VISUAL BONUS Math Short Answer Observe the square inscribed in the semicircle. If the area of the square is 20, then what is the area of the semicircle?
ANSWER: 25π/2
18. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory are studying how to track explosions using rugged tracers. They are studying how to incorporate what semiconductor particles into rugged tracers, which are a few nanometers in size and have different properties than larger particles due to quantum mechanics?
ANSWER: QUANTUM DOTS
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory are studying fusion reactors. To produce high pressure plasma, laser energy is converted into X-rays inside of what structure, whose walls are in radiative equilibrium with the radiant energy inside of it?
ANSWER: HOHLRAUM
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of receptor is associated with innate immunity and works by recognizing structurally conserved molecules from microbes?
ANSWER: TOLL-LIKE RECEPTOR (also accept: TLR)
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the cells in the testis that are responsible for growth and spermatogenesis?
ANSWER: SERTOLI CELLS
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify ALL of the following three quantities that must be greater than 0 for the sublimation of a compound at constant temperature I. ΔG II. ΔH III. ΔS
ANSWER: II AND III
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of batteries that are rechargeable I. Dry Cell II. Nickel-Metal Hydride III. Lead-Acid Battery
ANSWER: II AND III
21. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following RL circuits will have the GREATEST time constant?
W) Inductance of 4 henries and a resistor of ½ ohms
X) Inductance of 5 henries and a resistor of ⅓ ohms
Y) Inductance of 1 henry and a resistor of 3 ohms
Z) Inductance of 2 henries and a resistor of ⅙ ohms
ANSWER: X) Inductance of 5 henries and a resistor of ⅓ ohms
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer A certain fermion has a Fermi momentum of P, a Fermi energy of E, and a rest mass of M. Another fermion has a rest mass of 4M and a Fermi energy of 9E. What is the Fermi momentum of the second fermion, in terms of P?
ANSWER: 6 P
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three types of galaxies from LEAST to GREATEST maximum mass I. Elliptical II. Irregular III. Spiral
ANSWER: II, III, I
22. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A Kerr black hole possesses which of the following sets of charge and spin properties?
W) Charge and spin
X) Charge and no spin
Y) Spin and no charge
Z) No spin and no charge
ANSWER: Y) Spin and no charge
23. BONUS Math Short Answer A triangle whose side lengths have ratios 3 : 4 : 5 is inscribed in a circle with radius 1. What is the area of the triangle?
ANSWER: 24/25
