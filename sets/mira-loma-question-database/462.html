Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not an example of the use of chemotaxis?
W) Movement of a cell away from phenol
X) Movement of red blood cells in uniform direction
Y) Movement of sperm towards an egg
Z) Movement of bacteria towards nutrients
ANSWER: X) Movement of red blood cells in uniform direction
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the whip-like structure that allows cell movement?
ANSWER: FLAGELLUM
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Where do the majority of chemical reactions within a cell take place?
ANSWER: CYTOPLASM
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer The cytoplasm is composed primarily of what compound?
ANSWER: WATER
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals is not essential for the body?
W) Zinc
X) Palladium
Y) Magnesium
Z) Molybdenum
ANSWER: X) Palladium
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which mineral is required by the thyroid gland to synthesize its hormones T3 and T4?
ANSWER: IODINE
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By what process does one cell divide into two diploid cells?
ANSWER: MITOSIS
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In which stage of mitosis does chromatin condense into chromosomes?
W) Metaphase
X) Interphase
Y) Prophase
Z) Cytokinesis
ANSWER: Y) Prophase
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Gram positive bacteria turn what color after a Gram stain?
W) Violet
X) Green
Y) Blue
Z) Red
ANSWER: W) Violet
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following bacteria is Gram negative?
W) Staphylococcus aureus
X) Streptococcus pneumoniae
Y) Escherichia coli
Z) Listeria monocytogenes
ANSWER: Y) Escherichia coli
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which organ plays the largest role in the formation of a blood clot?
ANSWER: LIVER
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a function of the liver?
W) Serves as a site for the hemolysis of red blood cells
X) Stores glycogen
Y) Produces urea
Z) Detoxifies foreign proteins
ANSWER: Z) Detoxifies foreign proteins
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which two systemic veins enter the heart?
ANSWER: SUPERIOR AND INFERIOR VENA CAVA
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Arrange the blood pressures of the following arteries from lowest to highest: ascending aorta, abdominal aorta, pulmonary arteries, common iliac artery.
ANSWER: PULMONARY ARTERIES, COMMON ILIAC ARTERY, ABDOMINAL AORTA, ASCENDING AORTA
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The network of vessels that return lymph back to the heart belong to what system?
ANSWER: LYMPHATIC SYSTEM
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organs is part of the lymphatic system?
W) Heart
X) Liver
Y) Spleen
Z) Thyroid gland
ANSWER: Y) Spleen
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Leukocytes are produced from which of the following structures?
W) Heart
X) Yellow bone marrow
Y) Plasma
Z) Red bone marrow
ANSWER: Z) Red bone marrow
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a type of leukocyte that originates directly from red bone marrow?
W) Plasma cells
X) Monocytes
Y) T cells
Z) Basophils
ANSWER: Y) T cells
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Megakaryocytes develop into what type of blood cell?
ANSWER: PLATELETS (also accept: THROMBOCYTES)
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the condition in which there is a decreased platelet count?
ANSWER: THROMBOCYTOPENIA
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for a flat tendon?
ANSWER: APONEUROSIS
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In which location can an aponeurosis be found?
W) Below the brain
X) Around the neck
Y) On the top of the head
Z) On the cheek
ANSWER: Y) On the top of the head
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What feature of the sphenoid bone supports the pituitary gland?
ANSWER: SELLA TURCICA
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Double vision can be attributed to which of the following problems concerning the pituitary gland?
W) Nerve supply suppression
X) Tumor of anterior lobe
Y) Inactivity of anterior lobe
Z) Inactivity of posterior lobe
ANSWER: X) Tumor of anterior lobe
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The body contains how many cavities?
ANSWER: 5
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which cavity encloses the spinal cord?
ANSWER: SPINAL CAVITY
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How does a sponge reproduce?
W) Sexually
X) Asexually
Y) Both sexually and asexually
Z) Sponges don't reproduce
ANSWER: Y) Both sexually and asexually
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer In which phylum do sponges belong?
ANSWER: PORIFERA
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which one of the following is not a neurotransmitter?
W) Acetylcholine
X) Cholinesterase
Y) Norepinephrine
Z) Histamine
ANSWER: X) Cholinesterase
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer In which disease do autoantibodies destroy the acetylcholine receptors in muscle fibers?
ANSWER: MYASTHENIA GRAVIS
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The 6th cervical nerve is commonly abbreviated to what name?
ANSWER: C6
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The cauda equina is closest to the root of which of the following nerves?
W) L5
X) C8
Y) T10
Z) C1
ANSWER: W) L5
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What percent of the total leukocytes in the body are basophils?
W) 55-70%
X) 3-8%
Y) 0-2%
Z) 0.5-1%
ANSWER: Z) 0.5-1%
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the most abundant protein in blood plasma?
ANSWER: ALBUMIN
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following characteristics of a patient's electrocardiogram indicates a heart attack?
W) Elevation of ST segment
X) Absence of QRS complex
Y) Absence of all waves
Z) Extra R waves
ANSWER: W) Elevation of ST segment
18. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following can be used to prevent heart attacks?
W) Ibuprofen
X) Asprin
Y) Relenza
Z) Sodium acetate
ANSWER: X) Asprin
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a part of a bacterium?
W) Pili
X) Plasmid
Y) Proteasome
Z) Plasma membrane
ANSWER: Y) Proteasome
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the only organelle present in a generic bacterium?
ANSWER: RIBOSOME
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which one of the following is always part of a virus?
W) Mitochondrion
X) DNA
Y) Genome
Z) Collar
ANSWER: Y) Genome
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which strain of influenza is apparent every year in winter?
ANSWER: INFLUENZA B
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The agent that causes tuberculosis belongs to what kingdom?
ANSWER: MONERA (do not accept: BACTERIA)
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the scientific name of the bacteria that causes tuberculosis?
ANSWER: MYCOBACTERIUM TUBERCULOSIS
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Monocytes develop into what kind of cell that contributes to the body's defense mechanisms?
ANSWER: MACROPHAGE
22. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which one of the following cells is not a phagocyte?
W) Neutrophil
X) Basophil
Y) Monocyte
Z) Macrophage
ANSWER: X) Basophil
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which disease is characterized by the malignancy of lymphocytes?
ANSWER: LYMPHOMA
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer How many stages of lymphoma are there?
ANSWER: 4
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Strokes caused by thrombi occur most commonly in which arteries?
ANSWER: CEREBRAL ARTERIES
24. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice If an internal brain hemorrhage occurs and affects Broca's area, which of the following will most likely occur?
W) Difficulty writing
X) Difficulty interpreting written language
Y) Difficulty seeing
Z) Difficulty speaking
ANSWER: Z) Difficulty speaking
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What changes do the baroreceptors in the carotid and aortic sinuses detect?
ANSWER: CHANGES IN PRESSURE (also accept: PRESSURE)
25. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Impulses generated by baroreceptors travel by what nerve to the medulla oblongata of the brain?
W) Cardiac
X) Glossopharyngeal
Y) Vagus
Z) Carotid
ANSWER: X) Glossopharyngeal
