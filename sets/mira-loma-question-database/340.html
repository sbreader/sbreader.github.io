Packet 340 (Science Bowl Chem Questions - Satyaprit Das - Oct 24th) of Mira Loma Question Database:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The conversion of liquid to gas is called?
ANSWER: vaporization (also accept: evaporation)
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Of the total volume of a gaseous mass, what % of space the gas particles actually occupy?
ANSWER: 0.1% (in contrast % of volume of liquid particles occupy is 70%)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a chemical change?
W) Rusting of iron
X) Boiling of water
Y) Burning of wood
Z) Boiling an egg
ANSWER: X) Boiling of water
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Many elements in the periodic table, do not occur in nature. And there are some unstable elements that occur in nature only for a limited time and in this process turn into other elements. What is this process called?
ANSWER: radioactive decay
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Malleable, Brittle, blue color are examples of what properties of matter?
ANSWER: Physical
3. BONUS Energy Short Answer An element discovered in 1982 has been named meitnerium to honor Lise Meitner (1878-1968), the Austrian-Swedish physicist and mathematician. What is her most notable contribution to science.
ANSWER: Nuclear fission (also accept: Discovered protactinium)
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A nitrogen atom with 7 protons and 10 electrons is a cation or anion?
ANSWER: ANION
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer Compared to the typical atom's diameter, (about 10−10 meters), the diameter of a typical nucleus is about what?
ANSWER: 10−15
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Isotopes have the same atomic number but different what?
ANSWER: mass numbers
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Group of elements in the periodic table with some of the properties of metals but not all of them are called?
ANSWER: Metalloids (also accept: semimetals)
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When a nonmetallic atom bonds to another nonmetallic atom, the bond is of what type of bond?
ANSWER: covalent
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the collective name of the elements in groups 1, 2, and 13 through 18 on the periodic table?
ANSWER: Representative elements (also accept: Main-Group elements)
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When a metallic atom bonds to a nonmetallic atom, the bond is usually what type of bond?
ANSWER: ionic
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the collective group name of the elements in groups 3 through 12 (the "B" groups)?
ANSWER: Transition metals
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Molecules, which are uncharged collections of atoms held together by all covalent bonds are called what type of compounds?
ANSWER: Molecular compounds
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the collective name for the 28 elements at the bottom of the periodic table?
ANSWER: Inner transition elements
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The law of conversation of mass states that mass is neither created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. Who in the modern times first established this law
ANSWER: ANTOINE LAVOISIER
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Quantity of heat lost or gained to change the temperature of 1 g of that substance by 1 degree C is called?
ANSWER: specific heat
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The molecular shape that keeps the negative charge of four electron groups as far apart as possible with angles of 109.5° between the atoms, is called what?
ANSWER: Tetrahedral
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Outermost electron of an atom, which are important in determining how the atom reacts chemically with other atoms are called?
ANSWER: Valance electron
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Compounds that contain only carbon and hydrogen are called?
ANSWER: hydrocarbons
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A nuclear reaction in which a massive nucleus splits into smaller nuclei with the simultaneous release of energy is called?
ANSWER: nuclear fission
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A substance that contains two or more elements, the atoms of these elements always combining in the same whole-number ratio is called?
ANSWER: Compound
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Shorthand way of writing the names of the elements is called?
ANSWER: chemical symbols
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What principle states, electrons enter orbitals of lowest energy first?
ANSWER: Aufbau principle
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The change of state of a solid to a gas is called?
ANSWER: Sublimation
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer The distance between two bonded atoms at their minimum potential energy, that is, the average distance between two bonded atoms is called?
ANSWER: bond length
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A pair of electrons that is not involved in bonding and that belongs exclusively to one atom is called?
ANSWER: a lone pair
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The energy released when 1 mole of an ionic crystalline compound is formed from gaseous ions is called?
ANSWER: lattice energy
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The fact that a chemical compound contains the same elements in exactly the same proportions by mass regardless of the size of the sample or source of the compound is called?
ANSWER: law of definite proportions
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The ability of a substance to be hammered or beaten into thin sheets
ANSWER: malleability
