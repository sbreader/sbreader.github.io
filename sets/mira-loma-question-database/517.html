ANSWER: ALBERT EINSTEIN
22. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is defined to be equal to dr/dt?
ANSWER: VELOCITY

ANSWER: SODIUM
25. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A car starts at the speed of 50 mph and ends with a speed of 80 mph, assuming the speed changed only once in the two hours, what is the acceleration of this car?
ANSWER: 15 mph per hour (also accept: 15 MPH; 15)

ANSWER: WATER
