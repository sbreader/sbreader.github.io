1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a carbonate?
W) Dolomite
X) Aragonite
Y) Malachite
Z) Labradorite
ANSWER: Z) Labradorite
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following moons has the highest surface albedo?
W) Phobos
X) Europa
Y) Io
Z) Enceladus
ANSWER: Z) Enceladus
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If the voltage across a 3-ohm resister is 6 volts, what is the current, in amperes, flowing through the circuit?
ANSWER: 2
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is an intensive property?
W) Mass
X) Volume
Y) Length
Z) Melting point
ANSWER: Z) Melting point
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a type of fine-grained igneous rock?
W) Rhyolite
X) Peridotite
Y) Komatite
Z) Basalt
ANSWER: X) Peridotite
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of geomorphic feature forms on the outside of a river bend?
ANSWER: CUT BANK (also accept: RIVER CLIFF)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice During which of the following periods were coals generally lignite deposits still containing significant volatile material?
W) Carboniferous
X) Permian
Y) Secondary
Z) Tertiary
ANSWER: Z) Tertiary
4. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true about solar dish-engine systems?
W) Direct-beam energy is concentrated at a focal point on a dish made of mirrors
X) The concentrated solar beam is converted to heat that is transferred to an engine
Y) Mercury or copper are commonly used as heat transfer media
Z) The engine uses heat to create mechanical power that drives a generator
ANSWER: Y) Mercury or copper are commonly used as heat transfer media
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which bivalve has complex eyes, including a lens and retina?
ANSWER: SCALLOP
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer According to biological classification, what is the order of rabbits?
ANSWER: LAGOMORPHA
6. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is perpendicular to the line y = -2x + 6?
W) 2y = x + 4
X) x + 2y = 4
Y) y = 2x – 6
Z) y = -(1/2)x – 6
ANSWER: W) 2y = x + 4
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the genus of the bacteria that causes typhoid fever?
ANSWER: SALMONELLA
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms is NOT a thallophyte?
W) Entamoeba
X) Cladophora
Y) Chara
Z) Ulothrix
ANSWER: W) Entamoeba
8. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer In a building, what is usually the leakiest site when it comes to energy, both in terms of losing heat in the winter and also gaining heat in the summer?
ANSWER: WINDOW
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true about honeybee flight?
W) They flap their wings very quickly
X) Their wings beat over a very short arc
Y) When they want to generate more power, they increase the arc of their wing strokes
Z) Their flight muscles are physiologically very similar to those of fruit flies
ANSWER: Z) Their flight muscles are physiologically very similar to those of fruit flies
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements is NOT diatomic in its natural state?
W) Sulfur
X) Nitrogen
Y) Hydrogen
Z) Chlorine
ANSWER: W) Sulfur
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the maximum number of electrons that an n = 4 shell can accommodate?
ANSWER: 32
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true about most carp?
W) They possess Weberian ossicles
X) They have teeth on their inferior pharyngeal bones
Y) They are highly tolerant of salt water
Z) They have a wide geographical range
ANSWER: Y) They are highly tolerant of salt water
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of a method of fertilization in which the ovum is fertilized within a fallopian tube of a fertile female donor for transplantation into an infertile recipient?
ANSWER: IN VIVO FERTILIZATION
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Thirty percent of the days of each month have been designated as vacation days. To the nearest day, how many days of vacation should be in April?
ANSWER: 9
11. BONUS Math Short Answer The sum of 3 consecutive integers is 21. What is their product?
ANSWER: 336
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer When measuring the volume of a liquid in a graduated cylinder, what is the name of the curve at the surface of the liquid?
ANSWER: MENISCUS
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By number, identify all of the following three statements that are NOT true about tornado safety 1) you should open windows if a tornado is approaching 2) highway overpasses provide safe shelters from tornadoes 3) rivers form a barrier to tornadoes.
ANSWER: 1, 2, AND 3
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following objects is likely to have the lowest albedo?
W) Weathered granite
X) Sea ice
Y) Fresh basalt
Z) Rainforest canopy
ANSWER: Y) Fresh basalt
13. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following describes when flooding is most likely to occur as the result of a slow moving, relatively long term, moderate rain event?
W) After the rain has ended
X) After it has been raining for about an hour
Y) After it has been raining for a few hours
Z) At the beginning of the rainstorm
ANSWER: W) After the rain has ended
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following four organs directly involved in excretory system processes 1) lungs 2) heart 3) skin 4) kidneys.
ANSWER: LUNGS, SKIN, AND KIDNEYS (also accept: 1, 3, AND 4)
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the air mass that forms over North Africa during the summer, carrying dust from Africa long distances?
ANSWER: SAHARAN AIR LAYER (also accept: S.A.L.)
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following characteristics is NOT true of the herbicide Paraquat?
W) It is quick acting
X) It is non-selective, killing a wide range of annual grasses and broad-leaved weeds
Y) It is linked to the development of Parkinson's disease
Z) It remains biologically active in the soil for approximately 60 days
ANSWER: Z) It remains biologically active in the soil for approximately 60 days
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following four choices that are examples of birth defects 1) spina bifida 2) hypospadias 3) Tay-Sachs 4) phenylketonuria.
ANSWER: SPINA BIFIDA, HYPOSPADIAS, TAY-SACHS, PHENYLKETONURIA (also accept: 1, 2, 3, AND 4; ALL)
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, range the following four biomes from highest to lowest average temperature 1) taiga 2) tropical rainforest 3) tundra 4) temperate deciduous forest.
ANSWER: TROPICAL RAIN FOREST, TEMPERATE DECIDUOUS FOREST, TAIGA, TUNDRA (also accept: 2, 4, 1, 3)
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following four animals that are NOT filter feeders 1) mysid 2) krill 3) whale shark 4) crab spider.
ANSWER: CRAB SPIDER (also accept: 4)
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Given the graph of a quadratic polynomial function, what is the minimum number of ordered pairs on the graph needed in order to determine the coefficients of the polynomial?
ANSWER: 3
17. BONUS Math Short Answer Fred is twice as old as Marco and Marco is 4 years older than Ray. If Larz is 2 years older than Fred, and Ray is 6 years old, how old is Larz?
ANSWER: 22 YEARS OLD

W) Nitrogen
X) Fluorine
Y) Sulfur
Z) Neon
ANSWER: Y) Sulfur

ANSWER: STRONTIUM HYDROXIDE

W) Oxygen in its atmosphere
X) A moon
Y) A magnetic field
Z) High altitude clouds
ANSWER: Y) A magnetic field

ANSWER: COMPRESSION
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the second most abundant mineral in the Earth's continental crust, composed only of silicon and oxygen?
ANSWER: QUARTZ
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name for the scientific order of frogs?
ANSWER: ANURA
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the empirical formula of C6H12O6?
ANSWER: CH2O
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer By name or number, rank the speed of sound through the following three mediums from fastest to slowest at 25° Celsius 1) aluminum 2) air 3) water.
ANSWER: ALUMINUM, WATER, AIR (also accept: 1, 3, 2)
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Mars is observed to have a very thin atmosphere compared to Earth. Which of the following statements does NOT help explain this difference?
W) Mars is further from the Sun than Earth
X) Mars is smaller than Earth
Y) Mars has less geothermal activity than Earth
Z) Mars is cooler than Earth
ANSWER: W) Mars is further from the Sun than Earth

ANSWER: A DEPRESSION

W) Color change
X) Change in size
Y) Production of heat
Z) Formation of gas
ANSWER: X) Change in size
