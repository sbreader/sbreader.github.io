Packet 2 (2018 Regional Round 2) of National Ocean Sciences Bowl:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What features on the seafloor emit hydrocarbon-rich water that is approximately the same temperature as the surrounding seawater?
W) Cold seeps
X) Black smokers
Y) White smokers
Z) Neutral plumes
ANSWER: W) Cold seeps
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term to describe the distance over which the wind blows uninterrupted over a water surface?
ANSWER: Fetch
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following species does NOT perform ecological services that slow erosion?
W) Seagrass
X) Cordgrass
Y) Oysters
Z) Clams
ANSWER: Z) Clams
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What primarily drives equatorial surface currents?
W) Trade winds
X) Earth's rotation
Y) Sea level differences
Z) Thermohaline conditions
ANSWER: W) Trade winds
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What do we call the physical, chemical or biological breakdown of rocks?
ANSWER: Weathering
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Winkler titrations are used to determine the concentration of which substance in seawater?
W) Nitrate
X) Iodine
Y) Carbon dioxide gas
Z) Oxygen gas
ANSWER: Z) Oxygen gas
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In what direction does the Coriolis Effect divert winds and currents in the Southern Hemisphere?
ANSWER: To the left
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true about nudibranchs?
W) They are both male and female
X) They can incorporate defenses from their prey into their bodies
Y) They can eat members of their own species
Z) None can swim
ANSWER: Z) None can swim
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What are the structures used to maintain the spread of a fish trawl as it is pulled through the water?
W) Birds
X) Lobs
Y) Doors
Z) Risers
ANSWER: Y) Doors
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the most abundant dissolved inorganic carbon species in the sea?
ANSWER: HCO3- (also accept: H-C-O-3 minus; Bicarbonate; Hydrogen carbonate)
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the largest burrowing clam in the world?
ANSWER: Geoduck (also accept: Pacific geoduck; Panopea generosa)
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Over what time-scale, in years, does the great ocean conveyor mix the world’s oceans?
W) 1,000
X) 14,000
Y) 7,000
Z) 17,000
ANSWER: W) 1,000
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of these provided early evidence for seafloor spreading and the Vine-Matthews- Morley hypothesis?
W) GPS data
X) The fossil record
Y) Continental drift
Z) Seafloor magnetic anomalies
ANSWER: Z) Seafloor magnetic anomalies
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the global array of approximately 3800 floats that measure the temperature and salinity of the ocean?
ANSWER: Argo
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Of the following habitat types, where would a hypoxia-tolerant species most likely be found?
W) Open ocean
X) Kelp forest
Y) Seagrass bed
Z) Marsh mudflat
ANSWER: Z) Marsh mudflat
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What term is used for a submerged ocean mountain crowned with a ring of actively growing coral reefs?
ANSWER: Atoll
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Runoff from farm fields, feed lots, yards, and city streets contribute most directly to which of the following environmental issues?
W) High levels of oxygen in the ocean
X) Subsidence
Y) Eutrophication
Z) Global Warming
ANSWER: Y) Eutrophication
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer All members of which phylum possess a mantle?
ANSWER: Mollusca (also accept: molluscs)
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Ocean transport is measured in which units?
W) Newtons
X) Meters per second
Y) Sverdrups
Z) Fathoms
ANSWER: Y) Sverdrups
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Conservative elements in the ocean have concentrations that tend to vary with what property of seawater?
ANSWER: Salinity
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the three aspects of wind that influence wave size?
ANSWER: 1. Wind speed OR wind velocity 2. Duration OR length of time the wind blows over the water 3. Fetch OR the upwind distance along open water over which the wind blows
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which type of boundary is the Mid-Atlantic Ridge?
W) Oceanic-oceanic convergent
X) Continental-oceanic convergent
Y) Transform
Z) Divergent
ANSWER: Z) Divergent
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the definition of eurythermal?
ANSWER: High tolerance for a wide range of temperatures
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the largest underwater tectonic plate?
ANSWER: Pacific plate
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the mutualistic fish that suctions onto sharks?
W) Lamprey
X) Mola mola
Y) Remora
Z) Hagfish
ANSWER: Y) Remora
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What fluid did the ancient Romans use to give their concrete strength?
ANSWER: Salt water
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which region of the environment would be sampled by a dredge?
W) Pelagic Zone
X) Pycnocline
Y) Benthos
Z) Troposphere
ANSWER: Y) Benthos
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice How far do exclusive economic zones extend beyond a country’s territorial sea baseline?
W) No more than 200 statute miles
X) No more than 200 kilometers
Y) No more than 200 leagues
Z) No more than 200 nautical miles
ANSWER: Z) No more than 200 nautical miles
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer According to the Marine Mammal Commission, what is the number one direct, anthropogenic cause of death and injury for marine mammals?
ANSWER: Fishing gear (also accept: fishing by-catch)
