1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Poiseuille's Law states that the pressure differential between two ends of a pipe is proportional to what power of the radius of the pipe, all else equal?
W) Radius to the Fourth Power
X) Radius to the First Power
Y) Radius to the Negative First Power
Z) Radius to the Negative Fourth Power

1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Structures undergo rotational motion in a differential fashion under what density wave theory?
ANSWER: LIN-SHU DENSITY
2. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Scientists at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory are studying how uranium reacts after being leaked into the environment. After being leaked, uranium is reduced to Uranium (IV), which can be found as either a biomass-associated species, or nanoparticles of uraninite. What functional group is the Uranium (IV) typically bound to allowing it to exist as a biomass-associated species?
ANSWER: PHOSPHATE GROUP

ANSWER: 6+ (also accept: URANIUM 6+)

X) Tyrosine

6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, list the following 4 events relating to the formation of a type 1A supernova in chronological order 1) A red giant and a main-sequence star exist in a binary system 2) A red giant and a white dwarf exist in a binary system 3) The main-sequence star accretes mass from the red giant 4) The white dwarf accretes mass from the red giant 5) The mass of the white dwarf reaches the Chandrasekhar limit.

TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory are studying how a symbiotic relationship between an archaea and a bacteria affects the production of methane, a greenhouse gas, from hydrogen. 14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Scientists in France were the first to sequence the genes of a polar alga. Their results revealed that the alga have more enzymes involved in lipid metabolism, especially unsaturation of fatty acids. Which of the following best explain how this alga stays warm in the polar temperatures?
W) The higher rate of metabolism in the alga releases more waste heat, keeping the alga warm.
X) The alga stay together in large colonies and use the thermal energy generated from each other to stay warm.
Y) The alga are able to produce fats, keeping them warm.
Z) Temperature has no observable effect on the alga, so there is no reason for them to stay warm.
ANSWER: Y) The alga are able to produce fats, keeping them warm.
