1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In a DC RC circuit, how does the magnitude of the current progress in respect to time?
W) Decreases linearly to zero
X) Decreases exponentially to zero
Y) Increases to a saturation point
Z) Remains constant
ANSWER: X) Decreases exponentially to zero
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In a common galvanic electrochemical cell, reduction MOST spontaneously occurs in what region of the cell?
ANSWER: cathode
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following cranial nerves has both sensory and motor components?
W) spinal accessory
X) vagus
Y) abducens
Z) trochlear
ANSWER: X) vagus
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Acronymized as "EGG"s, what dense pockets of interstellar gas are commonly found in large nebulas such as the Eagle nebula, and contain newly forming stars?
ANSWER: evaporating gaseous globules
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following rock would be able to easily scratch orthoclase feldspar?
W) Quartz
X) Apatite
Y) Fluorite
Z) Talc
ANSWER: W) Quartz
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the area bound by the functions: y=x, y=0, and x=2?
ANSWER: 2
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer This genus is usually considered the second most successful mammal after humans. It is found in all land regions of the world other than extreme polar climates and Alberta, where it was eradicated through an extensive government program. Entering many foreign places through ships or other human means, they are generally seen as pests and carry diseases such as the fleas for bubonic plague. For four points, give the common or scientific name for this extant rodent.
ANSWER: Rattus (also accept: Rat)
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Assuming the acceleration due to gravity is 10m/s2, what is the maximum range of a trajectory with an initial speed of 5m/s, assuming a level ground and negligible air resistance?
W) 0.5 meters
X) 2 meters
Y) 2.5 meters
Z) 20 meters
ANSWER: Y) 2.5 meters
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What prominent gas law states: At constant pressure, the volume of a given mass of an ideal gas increases or decreases by the same factor as its temperature on the absolute temperature scale.
ANSWER: Charles's Law
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The adult hermaphrodite of this animal is renowned for having exactly 959 somatic cells. What free-living, transparent nematode is widely used as a model organism for a variety of research purposes, which won Nobel Prizes in Physiology/Medicine in 2002 and 2006?
ANSWER: C. elegans
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following constellations is found fully in the southern hemisphere of the celestial sphere?
W) Leo
X) Orion
Y) Sagittarius
Z) Cygnus
ANSWER: Y) Sagittarius
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In glacial morphology, what are circular, steep-sided hills that are composed largely of stratified drift, which originate when glacial meltwater washes sediments into openings and depressions in the retreating terminus of the glacier?
W) esker
X) kame
Y) drumlins
Z) roche moutonnee
ANSWER: X) kame
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the determinant of the following matrix: first row [2 3], second row [-1 5]?
ANSWER: 13
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What law of hydrostatics states that "a change in the pressure of an enclosed incompressible fluid is conveyed undiminished to every part of the fluid and to the surfaces of its container", and is useful in conveying the Pressure change in a fluid to a height change?
ANSWER: Pascal's Law
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements is an alkaline earth metal?
W) Rubidium
X) Yttrium
Y) Radium
Z) Protactinium
ANSWER: Y) Radium
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What enzyme in the citric acid cycle catalyzes the only reaction where substrate level phosphorylation occurs, most often converting a GDP to a GTP?
ANSWER: Succinyl-CoA synthetase
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following objects is NOT considered a dwarf planet by IAU's 2009 standards?
W) Sedna
X) Ceres
Y) Pluto
Z) Eris
ANSWER: W) Sedna
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for volcanic rock that exhibits vesicular texture, caused by gas bubbles trapped inside?
ANSWER: scoria
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the specific term for the first element in any ordered pair?
ANSWER: abscissa
22. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What theorem for linear DC electrical networks states that any combination of voltage sources, current sources and resistors with two terminals is electrically equivalent to a single voltage source V and a single series resistor R, as if in a black box?
ANSWER: Thevenin's theorem
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice As pure water is heated, what occurs to its pH if all other factors remain constant?
W) increases
X) remains the same
Y) decreases
Z) pure water does not have a pH
ANSWER: Y) decreases

ANSWER: Theodor Schwann
