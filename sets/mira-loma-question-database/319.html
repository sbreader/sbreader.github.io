1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly describes the process of vasodilation?
W) The flow of adjacent fluids in opposing directions that maximizes heat transfer rates
X) Water diffuses through arterial membranes, absorbing heat
Y) Decreasing in the diameter of superficial vessels, reducing blood flow
Z) Increasing in the diameter of superficial vessels, increasing blood flow
ANSWER: Z) Increasing in the diameter of superficial vessels, increasing blood flow
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer This element's low density makes it useful for the construction of missiles and satellites. It is also used as windows for x-ray tubes. When alloyed with copper, it can be useful for nonsparking tools for use in oil refineries and grain elevators. For four points, name the group 2 element with the highest ionization energies.
ANSWER: BERYLLIUM
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Name this type of thermometer which detects the amount of infrared radiation emitted by an object, that is often used to give accurate values of body temperature in medical situations.
W) Resistance thermometer
X) Bimetallic strip thermometer
Y) Temporal artery thermometer
Z) Mercury thermometer
ANSWER: Y) Temporal artery thermometer
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the distance in space between the points with coordinates (-3, 6, 7) and (2, -1, 4)?
W) 7.91
X) 9.11
Y) 10.54
Z) 13.19
ANSWER: X) 9.11
5. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Rank the following four countries in order from fewest to most barrels of oil produced per day. 1: China 2: Saudi Arabia 3: United States 4: Brazil
ANSWER: 4, 1, 3, 2 (also accept: Brazil, China, United States, Saudi Arabia)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Examples of these objects include Hygiea, Euphrosyne, and Juno. The first of these objects to be discovered was discovered in 1801 by Giuseppe Piazzi. In 1891, Max Wolf pioneered the use of astrophotography to detect them. These objects are often classified into C-type, S-type, or M-type. Also sometimes named planetoids, for four points, name this group of small Solar System bodies which lie mainly between the orbits of Mars and Jupiter.
ANSWER: ASTEROIDS (also accept: PLANETOIDS UNTIL MENTIONED)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the type of polyploidy which can occur after various mechanisms can change a sterile hybrid into a fertile one, thereby creating a new species which can only reproduce with itself.
ANSWER: ALLOPOLYPLOIDY
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is true regarding liquid characteristics?
W) Liquids have short-range order and long-range order.
X) Liquids have only short-range order, not long-range order.
Y) As temperature increases, viscosity increases.
Z) Surface tension is most prominent in liquids with weak intramolecular forces.
ANSWER: X) Liquids have only short-range order, not long-range order.
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Name this law that states that the line integral of a magnetic field B around any closed path is directly proportional to the net current through the area enclosed by this path. It can be simply stated as the first right-hand rule.
ANSWER: AMPERE'S LAW
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is false regarding the heat death of the universe theory?
W) The theory holds only for an "open" or "flat" universe, not a "closed one"
X) According to the theory, the degenerate era directly follows the stelliferous era
Y) According to the theory, reionization is the process that ended the "dark ages" of the universe
Z) According to the theory, big bang nucleosynthesis occurred approximately 3 minutes after the big bang
ANSWER: W) The theory holds only for an "open" or "flat" universe, not a "closed one"
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the name for a irregularly shaped hill or mound that accumulates in a depression on a retreating glacier?
W) Eskers
X) Kettles
Y) Outwash fans
Z) Kames
ANSWER: Z) Kames
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is described as the upper layer of cells derived from the blastodisc in reptiles or from the inner cell mass in mammals, which later differentiates to form the trilaminar germ disc through gastrulation?
W) Ectoderm
X) Hypoblast
Y) Epiblast
Z) Mesoderm
ANSWER: Y) Epiblast
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which of the following four statements is or are TRUE regarding trends among the d-block? 1: All d-block elements are metals. 2: Electrons in different d-orbitals repel one another only weakly. 3: The lanthanide contraction explains that d-block atomic radii decrease across series. 4: d-block elements tend to form a wide range of alloys.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3, and 4

19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In this form of apoptosis, cellular agents chop up the DNA and fragment the organelles in a cell. It was found to be closely associated with nonmuscle myosin IIA due to the actin-myosin interactions that ultimately cause cell membranes to bulge outwards in this process. For four points, name this process in which the cell shrinks and becomes lobed, also known as zeiosis.
ANSWER: BLEBBING

W) Adams' catalyst
X) Lazier catalyst
Y) Haber catalyst
Z) Ziegler-Natta catalyst

23. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Initially pioneered by James Blyth, this form of electrical power is currently produced more in the United States than any other country. This form of energy can be stored, using induction, into a grid, and is often found near agricultural sites. Though not solar power, for four points, name the form of energy that is plentiful, renewable, widely distributed, clean, and produced in "farms."
ANSWER: WIND POWER
24.
