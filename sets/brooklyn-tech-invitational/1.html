Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for an organism's observable physical or behavioral traits, such as brown eyes or albino skin?
ANSWER: Phenotype
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following 4 taxonomic levels from broadest to most specific. 1. Class 2. Phylum 3. Genus 4. Order
ANSWER: 2, 1, 4, 3 (also accept: Phylum, Class, Order, Genus)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements is classified as a metalloid?
W) Oxygen
X) Phosphorus
Y) Gallium
Z) Antimony
ANSWER: Z) Antimony
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the coefficient of Oxygen gas in the balanced equation for the combustion of Propane?
ANSWER: 5
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Wally West completes one full lap around an 800-meter circular track in exactly 10 seconds. Which of the following best describes Wally’s average velocity over this interval?
W) 80 meters per second
X) 800 meters per second
Y) 0 meters per second
Z) 5 meters per second
ANSWER: Y) 0 meters per second
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice On another day, Barry Allen runs around a circular track of radius 18 meters at a constant speed of 12 meters per second. What is the magnitude of Barry’s centripetal acceleration?
W) 6 meters per second squared
X) 8 meters per second squared
Y) 12 meters per second squared
Z) 18 meters per second squared
ANSWER: X) 8 meters per second squared
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Compute the sum of the squares of the roots of x2-2x + 3
ANSWER: -2
4. BONUS Math Short Answer A ball is hit from a corner of a 2-meter by 1-meter pool table. After banking off two different rails, it comes to rest in the corner directly opposite from where it started. How far did the ball travel in meters?
ANSWER: √13
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of seismic wave can penetrate the outer core?
ANSWER: P-wave (also accept: PRIMARY WAVE)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A seismometer on the opposite side of the Earth of an earthquake will not detect any p-waves. What process causes this to occur?
ANSWER: Refraction
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Consider a trait that is expressed when an individual has two recessive alleles. If both the mother and father are heterozygous for this trait, what is the probability that an offspring expresses this trait?
W) 0%
X) 100%
Y) 25%
Z) 50%
ANSWER: Y) 25%
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Two organisms both have alleles AaBbCcDd for traits A, B, C, and D. Assuming each organism will pass on exactly one allele for each trait, what is the probability that their first offspring will have alleles AaBBccDD?
ANSWER: 1/128
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The solubility of gases in liquids is highest at
W) High pressure and low temperature
X) Low pressure and high temperature
Y) Low temperature and low pressure
Z) High pressure and high temperature
ANSWER: X) Low pressure and high temperature
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 molecules that are INSOLUBLE in water. 1. Sodium Chloride 2. Ammonium Hydroxide 3. Barium Sulfate 4. Iron (II) Nitrate
ANSWER: 3 only
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Emma puts a chocolate bar in the microwave and notices that when she retrieves it, there are two separate spots that have melted while the rest remains solid. What pattern do the waves inside the microwave exhibit that causes these spots to melt?
ANSWER: Interference (also accept: Interference pattern; constructive interference; standing wave)
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A person exerts a 15-newton force, pushing a crate 3 meters in the direction of the force. How much work is done?
ANSWER: 45 Joules
9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a Pythagorean triple?
W) 9, 40, 41
X) 8, 15, 17
Y) 12, 15, 19
Z) 28, 45, 53
ANSWER: Y) 12, 15, 19
9. BONUS Math Short Answer Compute the number of positive integer factors of 1800.
ANSWER: 36
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What constellation contains the brightest star in the night sky as observed from Earth?
ANSWER: Canis Major
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In which constellation can Polaris be found?.
ANSWER: Ursa Minor
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer 30% of a DNA molecule’s nucleotide bases are adenine. What percentage are cytosine?
ANSWER: 20%
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Anthony is looking at a sample of a nitrogenous base. He sees that this sample has two carbon rings. By name or number, identify which of the following nitrogenous bases Anthony could be looking at. 1. Cytosine 2. Adenine 3. Thymine 4. Guanine 5. Uracil
ANSWER: 2 and 4 (also accept: adenine and guanine)
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The reaction Mg2O3 (s) → Mg (s) + O2 (g) is an example of which type of chemical reaction?
W) Synthesis
X) Decomposition
Y) Single Replacement
Z) Double Replacement
ANSWER: X) Decomposition
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 compounds that are classified as Network Solids. 1. Graphite 2. Carbon Dioxide 3. Silicon Carbide 4. Ferric Oxide
ANSWER: 1 and 3 (Graphite and Silicon Carbide)
13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Two disks of equal mass and radii, one solid and one a hollow ring, are released from rest at the same height on one side of a smooth, symmetrical U-shaped track. They roll without slipping down one side and up the other. Which disk will reach a greater height on the opposite side?
W) The solid disk
X) The hollow ring
Y) Not enough information
Z) Both will reach the same height
ANSWER: Z) Both will reach the same height
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 40-kilogram dog stands on a scale in an elevator accelerating upward at 5 meters per second squared. Approximating g = 10 meters per second squared, what does the scale read in Newtons?
ANSWER: 600 Newtons
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Convert 135 degrees to radians.
ANSWER: 3π/4
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Complex number W makes an angle of 75 degrees with the positive real axis, and a complex number Z makes an angle of 45 degrees with the positive real axis. If W' is the reflection of W across the imaginary axis, what positive angle does the product W'Z make with the positive real axis?
ANSWER: 150 degrees (also accept: 150+360n for any positive integer n)
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Where does most volcanism occur?
W) Along converging plate boundaries
X) Plateaus with reservoirs of molten magma
Y) Around the equator
Z) Underwater hotspots
ANSWER: W) Along converging plate boundaries
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What geologic structure results from the movement of rocks along a fracture plane?
W) Pluton
X) Syncline
Y) Rift Valley
Z) Fault
ANSWER: Z) Fault
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What biological principle states that genetic information flows in only one direction, from DNA to RNA?
ANSWER: Central Dogma (also accept: Central Dogma of Molecular Biology)
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following types of RNA that are involved in translation. 1. mRNA 2. tRNA 3. rRNA
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3 (also accept: ALL)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which TWO elements are liquid at STP?
ANSWER: Bromine and Mercury (do not accept: Bromine; Mercury)
17. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following Molecular Geometries is INCORRECTLY matched with its corresponding bond angle?
W) Trigonal Pyramidal, 107*
X) Linear, 180*
Y) Tetrahedral, 90*
Z) Trigonal Planar, 120*
ANSWER: Y) Tetrahedral, 90*
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which late 1880s physicist was the first to experimentally generate and detect radio waves, providing direct proof of the existence of electromagnetic waves postulated by Maxwell?
ANSWER: Heinrich Hertz
18. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice The characteristic blue glow observed in nuclear reactors is produced when charged particles move through water faster than the speed of light in that medium. What name is attributed to this phenomenon?
W) Bremsstrahlung
X) Cherenkov radiation
Y) Bioluminescence
Z) Synchrotron radiation
ANSWER: X) Cherenkov radiation
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What are the solutions to the equation x2 + 6x-7 = 0?
ANSWER: -7, 1
19. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the x-intercepts of the cubic function x3 + 2x2-4x-8.
ANSWER: (-2,0) and (2,0) (do not accept: -2 and 2; +/- 2)
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of rock is formed from the solidification of magma?
ANSWER: Igneous
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What principle states that the oldest rocks are found on the bottom of an undisturbed rock strata?
ANSWER: Law of Superposition
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Organisms in the phylum Cnidaria are found during, and can sometimes alternate between, two main life stages, or body forms. What are the names of these forms?
ANSWER: Polyp and Medusa
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer Hemophilia is an X-linked recessive disorder. If Milo’s parents both have 1 X chromosome with the hemophilia gene, what is the probability that he will be born with hemophilia?
ANSWER: 50%
22. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which principle in quantum mechanics states that it is impossible to simultaneously know both the exact position and exact momentum of a particle?
ANSWER: Heisenberg’s Uncertainty Principle (also accept: Uncertainty Principle)
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer In 1905, Albert Einstein published a paper that explained the random motion of particles suspended in a fluid. What is the name of this phenomenon?
ANSWER: Brownian motion
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the determinant of the 2x2 matrix with first row [5, 3] and second row [2, 8]?
ANSWER: 34
23. BONUS Math Short Answer Compute the limit as theta approaches 0 of theta times cotangent of theta.
ANSWER: 1
