1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Steroids in the cells such as those in the adrenal gland are produced by which cell organelle?
W) Golgi Apparatus
X) Ribosomes
Y) Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum
Z) Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum
ANSWER: Z) Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer When conducting detoxification, which chemical group does the smooth endoplasmic reticulum usually add to drug molecules?
ANSWER: HYDROXYL
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The Calvin cycle starts with 15 carbons' worth of carbohydrate in the form of three molecules. What is the end product?
W) 15 carbons' worth of carbohydrate in the form of 6 molecules
X) 15 carbons' worth of carbohydrate in the form of 4 molecules
Y) 18 carbons' worth of carbohydrate in the form of 6 molecules
Z) 18 carbons' worth of carbohydrate in the form of 4 molecules
ANSWER: Y) 18 carbons' worth of carbohydrate in the form of 6 molecules
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the carbohydrate that is produced directly by the Calvin cycle?
ANSWER: GLYCERALDEHYDE-3-PHOSPHATE
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the period of time from 535 million to 525 million years ago in which animal diversification accelerated dramatically?
W) Edicaran Acceleration
X) Dominant Acceleration
Y) Ediacaran Explosion
Z) Cambrian Explosion
ANSWER: Z) Cambrian Explosion
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer In which era did the Cambrian Explosion occur?
ANSWER: PALEOZOIC ERA
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the single-stranded end of double-stranded restriction fragments of DNA called?
W) Single End
X) Sticky End
Y) DNA End
