1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Kirchhoff's loop rule is a consequence of what conservation principle?
ANSWER: CONSERVATION OF ENERGY (also accept: ENERGY)
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer A spacecraft approaches Jupiter head-on for a gravitational slingshot maneuver. Jupiter has an orbital velocity of 13 kilometers per second. If the spacecraft was originally moving at 20 kilometers per second, what is the maximal final speed, in kilometers per second, it can attain?
ANSWER: 46
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the distance from the point (1, 3, 5) to the origin?
ANSWER: √35
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Consider an infinite geometric series with sum equal to 8/3 and first term 7/5. What is the value of the common ratio r for this series?
ANSWER: 19/40
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms possesses cnidocytes?
W) Tapeworm
X) Jellyfish
Y) Starfish
Z) Sea cucumber
ANSWER: X) Jellyfish
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Rank the following four proteins in order of increasing oxygen-binding affinity 1) Adult hemoglobin at pH 7.4 2) Fetal hemoglobin 3) Adult hemoglobin at pH 7.2 4) Myoglobin.
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2, 4

X) Coal
Y) Natural gas
Z) Petroleum

6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is not true about early transition metals in comparison to late transition metals?
W) Easier to oxidize
X) More electropositive
Y) Higher oxidation states
Z) Larger radii
ANSWER: W) Easier to oxidize

ANSWER: CARCINOMA

X) Roche limit

ANSWER: 1 AND 3
15. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about a valid wave function is true?
W) A wave function must be single-valued
X) A wave function must approach infinity as r approaches zero
Y) The spatial integral of wave functions for different orbitals in an atom must be one
Z) The integral of the wave function times its own complex conjugate must equal zero
ANSWER: W) A wave function must be single-valued
