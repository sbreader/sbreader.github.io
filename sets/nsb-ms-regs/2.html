1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Living organisms are often divided into five Kingdoms. In which of the 5 Kingdoms would E. coli be classified?
ANSWER: MONERA
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the binomial scientific genus and species name for humans?
ANSWER: HOMO SAPIENS
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Triassic, Jurassic, and Cretaceous are all periods in what era?
ANSWER: MESOZOIC
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the correct term for a large crack in ice:
W) crevice
X) chasm
Y) canyon
Z) crevasse
ANSWER: Z) crevasse
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the density given as specific gravity, of a one centimeter cube of a metal that weighs 5 grams:
W) .5
X) 5
Y) 2
Z) .2
ANSWER: X) 5
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following has the greatest density:
W) 10 kilogram of pure water
X) 10 kilogram of aluminum
Y) 2 kilogram of silver
Z) 1 kilogram of gold
ANSWER: Z) 1 kilogram of gold
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Peter bought a soda from a vending machine with a dollar bill. The soda cost 35 cents. What is the smallest number of coins he could receive in change if pennies are not included?
ANSWER: FOUR
4. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the integer value of 105 divided by 103?
ANSWER: 100 (do not accept: 102)
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Rickets, which was a common disease in the early twentieth century, is caused by a deficiency in what vitamin:
W) riboflavin
X) vitamin B12
Y) vitamin K
Z) vitamin D
ANSWER: Z) vitamin D
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Exposure to sunlight converts cholesterol found in human skin into what vitamin?
ANSWER: VITAMIN D
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is present only where benthic organisms reside:
W) phytoplankton
X) zooplankton
Y) diatoms
Z) deep sea vents
ANSWER: Z) deep sea vents
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the rapid rise of coastal water that accompanies the arrival of a cyclone?
ANSWER: TIDAL SURGE (also accept: STORM; BASAL SURGE)
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following metals is attracted to a magnet?
W) gold
X) silver
Y) iron
Z) aluminum
ANSWER: Y) iron
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The reaction of hydrocarbons found in such things as wood and paper with oxygen to produce carbon dioxide, water and heat is called:
W) fission
X) fusion
Y) endothermic
Z) combustion
ANSWER: Z) combustion
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Metamorphism transforms limestone into a metamorphic rock called:
W) quartzite
X) marble
Y) hornfel
Z) granite
ANSWER: X) marble
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the icicle-shaped deposits of limestone hanging from the roof of a cave called:
ANSWER: STALACTITES
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the region of the body where the stapes bone is located:
W) ankle
X) ear
Y) wrist
Z) knee
ANSWER: X) ear
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Sharks belong the class Chondricthyes because they have a skeleton made of what substance?
ANSWER: CARTILAGE
10. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Engineers in the USA often report pressure in PSI's. What does the abbreviation PSI stand for?
ANSWER: POUNDS per SQUARE INCH
10. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which planet does not have an element named after it?
W) Uranus
X) Mars
Y) Mercury
Z) Pluto
ANSWER: X) Mars
11. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A parallelogram whose adjacent sides are unequal is a:
W) deltoid
X) rectangle
Y) rhomboid
Z) monogram
ANSWER: Y) rhomboid
11. BONUS Math Short Answer Solve the following equation: 9x – 13 = 14
ANSWER: 3
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer On the Centigrade temperature scale give the whole number value that is the closest to absolute zero.
ANSWER: MINUS 273
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT seen by reflection of sun light:
W) the moon
X) Mercury
Y) Mars
Z) the north star
ANSWER: Z) the north star
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Metals that can be hammered into thin sheets are called:
W) malleable
X) ductile
Y) molten
Z) pure
ANSWER: W) malleable
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer A white-colored object illuminated by a blue light will appear as what color to the human eye?
ANSWER: BLUE
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is an intracellular parasite:
W) malaria
X) sleeping sickness
Y) tapeworm
Z) shigella
ANSWER: W) malaria
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In mammals, what teeth are evolved primarily to function in gripping and tearing
W) incissors
X) canines
Y) molars
Z) wisdom
ANSWER: X) canines
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Each day, most coastal beaches encounter:
W) one high tide and one low tide
X) two high tides and one low tide
Y) two high tides and two low tides
Z) three high tides and three low tides
ANSWER: Y) two high tides and two low tides
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a metamorphic rock:
W) sandstone
X) granite
Y) quartz
Z) marble
ANSWER: Z) marble
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the cube root of 8?
ANSWER: 2
16. BONUS Math Short Answer Solve the following equation: 3x + 15 = -12
ANSWER: -9
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer At its greatest possible brightness, which is the brightest planet in the night sky?
ANSWER: VENUS
17. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The largest of the suns planets are:
W) Venus and Mars
X) Jupiter and Saturn
Y) Neptune and Pluto
Z) Mercury and Uranus
ANSWER: X) Jupiter and Saturn

W) 8.4
X) 6.2
Y) 4.6
Z) 2.4
ANSWER: Y) 4.6
18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Earth's mantle is approximately how many miles thick:
W) 1000
X) 1200
Y) 1500
Z) 1800
ANSWER: Z) 1800
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of these organisms in not a kind of worm?
W) planerian
X) fluke
Y) leech
Z) centipede
ANSWER: Z) centipede
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the common specific name for the bond that joins two amino acids to form a growing protein:
ANSWER: PEPTIDE BOND
20. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Four divided by zero is:
W) 4
X) undefined
Y) 0
Z) infinity
ANSWER: X) undefined
20. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the value of x2 – y2 when x = -5 and y = -2:
ANSWER: 21
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the common scientific term for the ratio of the density of a material to that of water at 4°c?
ANSWER: SPECIFIC GRAVITY
21. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Starch is made of what simple sugar?
W) fructose
X) mannose
Y) glucose
Z) ribose
ANSWER: Y) glucose
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A large mass of ice, formed on land by the compaction and recrystallization of snow, that is moving downhill or outward under the force of gravity is called:
ANSWER: A GLACIER

W) hillock
X) butte
Y) drumlin
Z) karst
ANSWER: X) butte
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many microgams are in a single milligram?
ANSWER: 1000
23. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The moon moves around the earth traveling primarily:
W) east to west
X) north to south
Y) west to east
Z) south to north
ANSWER: Y) west to east
24. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the square root of 169?
ANSWER: 13

W) 23 ½° North
X) 23 ½° South
Y) 26 ½° North
Z) 26 ½° South
ANSWER: W) 23 ½° North

ANSWER: LAVA
