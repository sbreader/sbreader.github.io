1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What hormone, released by the kidneys to acclimatize to high altitude, stimulates the production of red blood cells?
ANSWER: ERYTHROPOIETIN
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about asexual reproduction is FALSE?
W) It is generally favored by high environmental stress.
X) It may involve the union of sperm and egg.
Y) It is relatively inefficient in generating genetic diversity.
Z) It is more common among sessile animals.
ANSWER: W) It is generally favored by high environmental stress.

3. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A Markov chain has two states, A and B. From A, there is a 2/3 chance of going to B and a 1/3 chance of remaining at A. From B, there is a 1/2 chance of going to A and a 1/2 chance of remaining at B. After 10000 steps have run, which of the following choices is closest to the probability that a particle starting at A is now at B?
W) 1/3
X) 1/2
Y) 4/7
Z) 7/12
ANSWER: Y) 4/7
