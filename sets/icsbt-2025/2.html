1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A top on a table wobbles uniformly about an axis. What is the angle in degrees between the torque due to gravity about its base on the top and the angular momentum due to the spin of the top?
ANSWER: 90
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Eli, who weighs 50 kg is sprinting circular laps with radius 2 meters around the Belmont convention center at 10 m/s. He loses mass to gas at a rate of 2 kg/s. Assume the momentum of his gas is negligible. If the radius of the circle eli runs in and his velocity are constant, what is the magnitude of the torque exerted on Eli in Newton-Meters?
ANSWER: 40 Newton-meters
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Inside a container with a fixed pressure, hydrogen gas reacts with oxygen gas to make water vapor. Which change will result in more oxygen gas being present at equilibrium?
W) Throwing in a chunk of sodium
X) Injecting argon
Y) Lowering the temperature
Z) Turning on a dehumidifier
ANSWER: X) Injecting argon
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer An ideal gas, initially at STP, has its temperature increased to 136° C and its pressure increased to 3.0 atm. To two significant figures and in liters, what is the gasses’ new molar volume?
ANSWER: 11 L
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following telescope arrangements collects light at one end, and brings it to focus at the other end using a mirror angled at 45 degrees?
W) Galilean
X) Newtonian
Y) Cassegrain
Z) Keplerian
ANSWER: X) Newtonian
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What phenomenon present in refracting telescopes occurs as a lens is not able to bring all wavelengths of light to a single point, leading to some colors getting distorted?
ANSWER: Chromatic aberration
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the surface area of an octahedron with edge length 2?
ANSWER: 8√3
4. BONUS Math Short Answer The diagonal of a regular pentagon with side length 1 is what constant?
ANSWER: Phi (also accept: Golden ratio; Golden Mean; Golden Number; Divine Section; Extreme and Mean Ratio; any common name for the golden ratio)

W) Thymus
X) Spleen

Z) Lymph nodes
ANSWER: W) Thymus

ANSWER: 1,4,2,3
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A process in which the probability of future states only depends on the current state is known as what?
ANSWER: Markovian (also accept: Markov Chain)

7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Akhil, a Science Olympiad student, turns on the water from the 0.1 cm radius faucet in his dorm at MSU. If the radius of the straw is 0.02 cm, what is the new velocity of the water in meters per second assuming steady incompressible laminar flow?

23. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the principle of competitive exclusion?
