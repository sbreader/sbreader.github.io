1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of Newton's laws tells us that when a force acts upon an object, an equal and opposite force acts on another body?
W) First
X) Second
Y) Third
Z) Fourth
ANSWER: Y) Third
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two stones are dropped in a pond and the ripples interfere with each other to create a bigger ripple than each of the original ripples. What is this type of interference called?
ANSWER: CONSTRUCTIVE
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Within a given period, what happens to the size of atoms as you move across a period of the periodic table, from left to right?
W) They increase
X) They decrease
Y) They stay the same
Z) There is no pattern
ANSWER: X) They decrease
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many electrons does a chlorine atom have?
ANSWER: 17
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Homo sapiens is the scientific name for which of the following?
W) Neanderthals
X) Dolphins
Y) Monkeys
Z) Humans
ANSWER: Z) Humans
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Toads and salamanders live in water and on land. They are both examples of:
W) Amphibians
X) Reptiles
Y) Invertebrates
Z) Mollusks
ANSWER: W) Amphibians
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Is the radius of Mars 1/4, 1/2, 2, or 4 times the size of Earth's radius?
ANSWER: 1/2
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name the two moons which orbit Mars.
ANSWER: PHOBOS AND DEIMOS
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice While recognizing that evolution is a continual process for all organisms, historically speaking, which of the following kinds of organisms evolved most recently on Earth?
W) Reptiles
X) Insects
Y) Mammals
Z) Fish
ANSWER: Y) Mammals
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a characteristic of endothermic animals?
W) They have a higher metabolism in warm weather
X) They are more aggressive overall as compared to cold-blooded animals
Y) They are found only in warm climates
Z) They maintain a constant internal temperature at all air temperatures
ANSWER: Z) They maintain a constant internal temperature at all air temperatures
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What wattage of light bulb is closest to the energy used by the human brain?
W) 0.1
X) 1
Y) 10
Z) 100
ANSWER: Y) 10
6. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice About how many watts does the average human generate over an 8 hour period, if not doing additional exercise or other activities?
W) 1
X) 10
Y) 100
Z) 1000
ANSWER: Y) 100
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The elements chlorine, fluorine, iodine, and bromine are all members of what group of elements?
ANSWER: HALOGENS (also accept: GROUP 17)
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements would be expected to possess very different chemical properties from the others based on its placement in the periodic table?
W) Chlorine
X) Argon
Y) Iodine
Z) Fluorine
ANSWER: X) Argon
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer At room temperature, what is the state of matter of most elements?
ANSWER: SOLID
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer Most submarines can only store enough fresh water to last for about 10 days. In order to obtain fresh water, most submarines are equipped with an apparatus that converts sea water into fresh water. What is the name of the process used to convert the sea water?
ANSWER: REVERSE OSMOSIS (also accept: DESALINATION)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Extrusions from a volcano produce what types of rock?
ANSWER: IGNEOUS
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following is not a form of metamorphic rock: slate, marble, gypsum, or gneiss?
ANSWER: GYPSUM

X) 0

Z) 9

ANSWER: Z) 10,000
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What pH is considered neutral?
ANSWER: 7
11. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Work is done on the box in all of the following scenarios except:
W) A boy raising the box above his head
X) A boy holding the box still above his head
Y) A boy pushing the box up a ramp
Z) A boy kicking the box from rest across a frictionless floor
ANSWER: X) A boy holding the box still above his head
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What system in a mammalian body is responsible for removing liquids and solid wastes?
W) Circulatory
X) Respiratory
Y) Endocrine
Z) Excretory
ANSWER: Z) Excretory
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer When an organism becomes too large for muscular contractions and diffusion to effectively move nutrients throughout the body, what system is needed to transport the nutrients?
ANSWER: CIRCULATORY (do not accept: DIGESTIVE)
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Simplify 8 × 23 + 8 × 7 + 8 × 5. Express the answer as a natural number.
ANSWER: 280

X) Kinetic

X) Chemical

Z) Light
ANSWER: Z) Light

ANSWER: SIMPLE MACHINE

ANSWER: ANY 4 OF: LEVER, WEDGE, PULLEY, WHEEL & AXLE, INCLINED PLANE, SCREW

ANSWER: 1/1075

ANSWER: 96
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In most organisms that reproduce sexually, what proportion of genetic material comes from the mother?
ANSWER: 50%
21. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements it true about pollen?
W) Pollen must be transferred between plants by insects
X) Pollen travels through the style and directly fertilizes the ovule
Y) Either before or after arriving on the stigma, each pollen grain undergoes a mitotic cell division producing two sperm
Z) Pollen is a gamete
ANSWER: Y) Either before or after arriving on the stigma, each pollen grain undergoes a mitotic cell division producing two sperm
