Packet 375 (Science Bowl Questions 2010-10-11) of Mira Loma Question Database:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which layer of the Earth is heated by the radioactive decay of heavy metals?
ANSWER: The Mantle
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the state of magma in the mantle?
ANSWER: Convection
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the process in which crust is pushed under the lithosphere?
ANSWER: Subduction
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What percentage of the Earth's atmosphere is nitrogen?
W) 64%
X) 71%
Y) 78%
Z) 83%
ANSWER: Y) 78%
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the magnetic field created by the mantle's internal motion that protects the Earth's atmosphere from solar winds called?
ANSWER: Magnetosphere
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which principle states that the deposition of sediments occurs as essentially horizontal beds?
ANSWER: The principle original horizontality
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the three major types of faults?
ANSWER: Strike-slip, normal, thrust
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the most common type of absolute dating?
W) argon-argon dating
X) uranium-lead dating
Y) potassium-argon dating
Z) carbon dating
ANSWER: Z) carbon dating
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is another name for the solid, upper mantle?
ANSWER: Asthenosphere
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the Earth's inner core primarily composed of?
ANSWER: Nickel and iron (also accept: a nickel-iron alloy)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of rock is limestone?
W) Crystalline
X) Colored
Y) Amorphous
Z) Antiform
ANSWER: W) Crystalline
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What principle pertains to the formation of faults and the age of the sequences through which they cut?
ANSWER: The principle of cross-cutting relationships
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which geological term refers to any penetrative planar fabric present in rocks?
ANSWER: Foliation
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the two primary forms of horizontal compression?
ANSWER: Faulting and folding
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is a long, planar igneous intrusion known as?
ANSWER: A dike
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the oldest dated rock in the world?
ANSWER: Acasta Gneiss
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of lava flow does the Hawaiian Islands almost entirely consist of?
ANSWER: Layered basaltic lava flows
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What ice forms do scientists study to investigate the subsurface?
W) frozen lakes
X) rivers
Y) glaciers
Z) biogeochemical pathways
ANSWER: Y) glaciers
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer How do scientists use arrival times of seismic waves to image the Earth's interior?
ANSWER: Reversing them
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the correct term for a magnet that is created by a current that flows around a soft-iron core?
ANSWER: Electromagnet
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which theory proposes a mechanism by which a celestial body such as the Earth or a star generates a magnetic field?
ANSWER: Dynamo theory
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of plate boundary occurs when two plates slide towards each other commonly forming either a subduction zone or a continental collision?
ANSWER: Convergent boundaries
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What two forces are thought to influence plate motion?
ANSWER: Friction and Gravity
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the study of life from past geological times as known from fossil remains?
ANSWER: Paleontology
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What scale do scientists use to measure the magnitude of earthquakes?
ANSWER: Richter scale
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the correct term for a water-bearing stratum of permeable rock, sand, or gravel?
ANSWER: Aquifer
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the process of supplying water by artificial means?
ANSWER: Irrigation
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In which layer of the atmosphere is 75% of the atmosphere's gases?
ANSWER: Troposphere
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer List the five layers of the atmosphere in order form bottom to top.
ANSWER: Troposphere, stratosphere, mesosphere, thermosphere, exosphere
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What contemporary in earth science states that ancient geologic features are interpreted by understanding active processes that are readily observed?
ANSWER: Uniformitarianism
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name the four spheres generally recognized by earth science.
ANSWER: Lithosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere, biosphere
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What sphere shields the Earth's surface from cosmic rays?
ANSWER: Atmosphere
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What distinct portion of the hydrosphere corresponds to ice?
ANSWER: Cryosphere
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the phenomenon in which water vapors and CO2 allow the Earth's atmosphere to catch and hold the sun's energy?
ANSWER: Greenhouse effect
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is a liquid rock that forms under the surface of the earth called?
ANSWER: Magma
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for ductilely stretched rocks
ANSWER: Boudins
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In the word "geology" what does geo refer to?
ANSWER: Earth
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for an optical and meteorological phenomenon that causes a nearly continuous spectrum of light to appear in the sky when the Sun shines onto droplets of moisture in the Earth's atmosphere?
ANSWER: Rainbow
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of a quasi-periodic climate pattern that occurs across the tropical Pacific Ocean on average every five years?
ANSWER: El Niño/La Niña-Southern Oscillation
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What principle states that a sedimentary rock layer in a tectonically undisturbed sequence is younger than the one beneath it and older than the one above it?
ANSWER: The principle of superposition
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of a type of igneous intrusion where a rock forms from cooled magma deep in the earth's crust?
ANSWER: Batholith
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the decomposition of a ray of light into two rays when it passes through certain types of material depending on the polarization of the light called?
ANSWER: Birefringence
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the optical phenomenon in which mineral grains within a rock appear to be different colors when observed at different angles under a polarizing petrographic microscope?
ANSWER: Pleochroism
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What occurs when two separate crystals share some of the same crystal lattice points in a symmetrical manner?
ANSWER: Crystal twinning
24. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What do petrologists use to understand the temperatures and pressures at which different mineral phases appear, and how they change through igneous and metamorphic processes?
W) Thermometer
X) Chromatography
Y) Fluid inclusion data
Z) Hydrogen
ANSWER: Y) Fluid inclusion data
