1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following genuses is not a part of the family enterobacteriaceae?
W) Escherichia
X) Salmonella
Y) Neisseria
Z) Klebsiella
ANSWER: Y) Neisseria
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a characteristic of the family enterobacteriaceae?
W) Catalase negative
X) Oxidase negative
Y) Gram-negative
Z) Facultative anaerobes
ANSWER: W) Catalase negative
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What gaseous electronegative halogen can be found in the mineral cryolite?
ANSWER: Fluorine
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What oxygen-chlorine ion readily disproportionates in acidic conditions to form chloride and chlorate?
ANSWER: HYPOCHLORITE
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the name for the currents produced in a conducting sheet of metal when it is pulled through a magnetic field?
ANSWER: EDDY CURRENTS
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer The magnitude of the electromotive force induced in a solenoid is equal to the rate of change in what property?
ANSWER: MAGNETIC FLUX
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Consider the geometric sequence with third term 12 and first term 4/3. What is the fifth term?
ANSWER: 108
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Consider a typical, well-shuffled deck of cards. You are given 3 random cards. What is the probability they are all spades, as a fraction reduced to simplest terms?
ANSWER: 11/850
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Upon heating to 1500 C, what mineral does silica form?
ANSWER: CRISTOBALITE
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What silver-gray transition metal is often used as a substitute for diamond in jewelry? It forms an orthosilicate mineral and its 89 isotope is used in positron emission tomography cameras.
ANSWER: ZIRCONIUM
6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Biodiesel is considered cleaner than fossil fuels because it does not emit as much carbon and sulfur-based pollutants like CO2 and SO2. However, biodiesel does emit more of what pollutant that is capable of destroying ozone?
ANSWER: Nitrous Oxide
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer The state of California has devised a new way to burn biodiesel that will cause less Nitrous Oxide emissions. What is the name of this new method of burning biodiesel?
ANSWER: Low Temperature Combustion (also accept: LTC)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name for the enlargement of an artery due to the weakening of surrounding arterial walls? This enlargement can cause hemorrhaging if it bursts.
ANSWER: Aneurysm
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer A hemorrhagic stroke occurs when there is bleeding into the brain as a result of a blood vessel rupturing. Provide the name for the other type of stroke, in which blood supply to the brain is reduced due to the blocking of a blood vessel.
ANSWER: Ischemic stroke
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Vesuvian Fire is the common name for this red inorganic compound that is the combination of two polyatomic ions and is often found in demonstrations of “volcanoes”. This compound is used in pyrotechnics and was used in photography. Name this compound.
ANSWER: AMMONIUM DICHROMATE
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When dichromate is reacted with sulfuric acid, a chromium-oxygen compound is formed. This compound is a carcinogenic substance often used for the oxidation of alcohols. Give the formula for this compound.
ANSWER: CrO3
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Find the torque of a 5N force applied at a 90 degree angle 3 meters from the pivot point.
ANSWER: 15
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the moment of inertia of a rod of mass M and length L about a perpendicular axis a distance of L/4 from its end towards its center? Assume the axis is perpendicular to the rod’s axis of symmetry.
ANSWER: 7ML2/48
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following terms refers to a function where each element of one set is paired with exactly one element of the other set, and each element of the other set is paired with exactly one element of the first set?
W) Injective
X) Surjective
Y) Bijective
Z) Onto
ANSWER: Y) Bijective
10. BONUS Math Short Answer List all of the following functions that are bijective 1) f(x) = x3 + 2x 2) f(x) = x3-2x 3) f(x) = √x
ANSWER: 1
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What class of silicates is a repeating chain based on the metasilicate ion and includes gemstones such as jade?
ANSWER: PYROXENES
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What class of minerals, including tremolite, are made of chains of silicate units linked together in ladderlike structures? These are fibrous minerals that can withstand extreme heat.
ANSWER: ASBESTOS
12. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes what an anti-knock is?
W) A substance added to gasoline that prevents pre ignition
X) A substance that lowers the temperature required to combust gasoline
Y) A substance that allows for the homogenization of gasoline and ethanol
Z) A substance that mixes with gasoline, allowing it to be frozen
ANSWER: W) A substance added to gasoline that prevents pre ignition
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What compound, which was banned in the United States in 1996, was traditionally added to gasoline as an anti-knocking agent?
ANSWER: Tetraethyl Lead (also accept: TEL)
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What word is used to describe stem cells that have the ability to divide into any of the cells needed to enable the growth and development of an organism?
ANSWER: Totipotent
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the only totipotent cells in the human body?
ANSWER: Zygote
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Below 2K, liquid He-4 flows without viscosity. What is the name of this phenomenon?
ANSWER: SUPERFLUIDITY
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer Cooling a mass of bosons to extremely low temperatures until a large fraction of bosons reach the lowest quantum state causes a substance to enter this state of matter. Name this state of matter first produced in a mass of Rb atoms in 1995.
ANSWER: BOSE EINSTEIN CONDENSATE
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Find the coordinates of the center of mass of a system with a particle of mass 5kg at (1,0), mass 3kg at (-1,0), mass 2kg at (0,1) and mass 2kg at (0,-1).
ANSWER: (1/6, 0)
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer Find the rotational kinetic energy of a rod with uniform mass density with mass 12 kg and radius 2 m spinning with an angular velocity of 5 Hz.
ANSWER: 50
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the definite integral of sin x with respect to x from 0 to pi?
ANSWER: 2
16. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the integral of x7 + 3x2 from 0 to 2 with respect to x?
ANSWER: 40
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Mica and talc differ in their chemical composition because of the presence of the +3 ion of what common group 3A element.
ANSWER: Aluminum
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer KAlSi3O8 is the typical formula for what group of aluminosilicate minerals that is a major component of granite?
ANSWER: FELDSPARS
18. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following countries has led the world in carbon neutrality and was able to run 100% on renewable energy sources for almost a year?
W) Denmark
X) Siberia
Y) Ethiopia
Z) Costa Rica
ANSWER: Z) Costa Rica
18. BONUS Energy Short Answer Carbon neutrality is when the carbon emitted is equal to the carbon removed from the atmosphere, thus cancelling out the negative effects. What is the name of the process in which atmospheric Carbon is captured and stored to better achieve carbon neutrality?
ANSWER: Carbon sequestration
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the most abundant antibody in the human body?
ANSWER: Immunoglobulin G (also accept: IgG)
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer Provide the names for the 5 primary classes of immunoglobulins in the human body.
ANSWER: Immunoglobulin A, Immunoglobulin D, Immunoglobulin E, Immunoglobulin G, Immunoglobulin M (also accept: IgA, IgD, IgE, IgG, IgM)
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the geometric shape of dimethylmercury(0)?
ANSWER: LINEAR
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What are the two possible geometric shapes of coordination complexes with 4 monodentate ligands bound to a metal center?
ANSWER: TETRAHEDRAL AND SQUARE PLANAR
21. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is the best explanation of rolling friction? Assume we are discussing rolling without slipping.
W) The object rubs against the atoms of the surface it rolls upon.
X) The point on the bottom of a rolling object is instantaneously at rest.
Y) Heat is generated from rolling against a surface.
Z) Rolling friction is not experienced in objects that roll without slipping.
ANSWER: X) The point on the bottom of a rolling object is instantaneously at rest.
21. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice When the following objects are released from rest at the top of a ramp, which of the following will reach the bottom of the ramp first? Assume they all have the same mass.
W) Circular Hoop
X) Solid Sphere
Y) Solid Rod
Z) Solid Cylinder
ANSWER: Y) Solid Rod
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What 3-dimensional geometric polyhedron has four corners and six edges?
ANSWER: TETRAHEDRON
22. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the height of a regular tetrahedron with sides of length 9. Give your answer in simplest radical form.
ANSWER: 3√6
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The weathering of feldspar with CO2 and H2O produced what water soluble carbonate?
ANSWER: POTASSIUM CARBONATE
23. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a mineral in the mica family of phyllosilicates? It is known as magnesium mica.
W) Lizardite
X) Phlogopite
Y) Talc
Z) Chlorite
ANSWER: X) Phlogopite
