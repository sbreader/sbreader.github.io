1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following crystal habits is most likely to develop in an environment with very few nucleation sites?
W) Botryoidal
X) Drusy
Y) Massive
Z) Tabular
ANSWER: Z) Tabular
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three objects that are indicaters of star formation 1) Bok globules 2) OB associations 3) T Tauri associations.
ANSWER: All
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Protein phosphatases dephorsphorylate kinases to return them to their inactive state. Similarly for cyclic AMP, what enzyme degrades the secondary messenger to turn off a cell signaling pathway?
ANSWER: cAMP phosphodiesterase (also accept: Phosphodiesterase)
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three processes that would involve the active transport of ions 1) Movement of H+ from a parietal cell into the stomach lumen 2) Movement of Ca2+ from the cytosol into the sarcoplasmic reticulum 3) Movement of Na+ from saliva into taste receptor cells.
ANSWER: 1 and 2
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The tallest building in San Francisco is 300 meters tall and can be approximated as a thin rod with mass M. If an earthquake displaces the tower’s foundation such that it leans at an angle of five degrees relative to the vertical, which of the following best approximates the torque in newton meters exerted on the tower about its foundation?
W) 150MG cos(5°)
X) 300MG cos(5°)
Y) 150MG sin(5°)
Z) 300MG sin(5°)
ANSWER: Y) 150MG sin(5°)
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes how air resistance scales with an object’s velocity v?
W) Generally proportional to v
X) Proportional to v at low velocity and v2 at high velocity
Y) Proportional to v2 at low velocity and v at high velocity
Z) Generally proportional to v2
ANSWER: X) Proportional to v at low velocity and v2 at high velocity
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following transition elements crystallizes in a lattice with a 0.74 packing efficiency at room temperature?
W) Iron
X) Gold
Y) Molybdenum
Z) Chromium
ANSWER: X) Gold
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following one-to-one mixtures of liquids would be expected to show the greatest deviation from Raoult’s law?
W) Water and ethanol
X) Water and cyclohexanol
Y) Hexane and butane
Z) Acetone and chloroform
ANSWER: Z) Acetone and chloroform
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the area of a regular heptagon with side length 8 and apothem 13?
ANSWER: 364
5. BONUS Math Short Answer How many subsets of the set 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 have an odd number of elements?
ANSWER: 128
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Stanford are contributing to the Cryogenic Dark Matter Search, an experiment searching for WIMPs as candidates for dark matter. Identify all of the following three fundamental forces that are thought to affect WIMPs 1) Gravity 2) Electromagnetism 3) Weak force.
ANSWER: 1 and 3
6. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Scientists at Stanford’s Kim Lab are comparing the mean body mass of two small independent samples, with population standard deviations unknown. Which statistical test is most appropriate to determine whether the population means differ?
W) ANOVA
X) T-test
Y) Chi-square test
Z) Linear regression
ANSWER: X) T-test
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three eruptive types in terms of increasing strength 1) Plinian 2) Strombolian 3) Vulcanian.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Compared to a comet orbiting the Sun at the same relative distance, how would a comet orbiting an O-type main-sequence star most likely differ?
W) Larger dust tail, larger ion tail
X) Larger dust tail, smaller ion tail
Y) Smaller dust tail, larger ion tail
Z) Smaller dust tail, smaller ion tail
ANSWER: W) Larger dust tail, larger ion tail
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The atomic radius of hafnium is nearly the same as that of zirconium, despite hafnium being located below zirconium on the periodic table. What phenomenon contributes to this unexpectedly small increase in radius between zirconium and hafnium?
ANSWER: Lanthanide contraction
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer An isotope decays in a first order reaction with a rate constant of 0.5 inverse seconds. To two significant figures, what is the ratio of the initial concentration of the isotope to its concentration after 2.1 seconds?
ANSWER: 2.8
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What effect occurs when an external magnetic field causes atomic sublevels with different magnetic quantum numbers to shift in energy by different amounts, resulting in a splitting of spectral lines?
ANSWER: Zeeman effect (also accept: Normal Zeeman effect; anomalous Zeeman effect)

W) 1 to 4
X) 1 to 2
Y) 2 to 1
Z) 4 to 1
ANSWER: Z) 4 to 1

11. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Scientists in the Zong Group at Stanford are studying Mott insulators, which are insulators that band theory predicts to be metals. Which of the following best describes the band structure of most materials predicted to be metals?
W) Conduction and valence bands entirely overlap
X) Conduction and valence bands partially overlap
Y) Conduction and valence bands are adjacent but not overlapping
Z) Conduction and valence bands are far from each other

12. BONUS Math Short Answer Mark constructs a unit circle and inscribes a square inside the circle. He then inscribes a circle inside the square, and then a square inside that circle, and repeats this process indefinitely. The total area of all the circles approaches what value?
ANSWER: 2π
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of nebula appears blue because its interstellar dust scatters light from a nearby star rather than emitting its own radiation?
ANSWER: Reflection nebula
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Anurag has discovered a star with a peak wavelength in the UV. If the Sun has a temperature of 6000 K and a peak wavelength of 500 nm, then in kelvins, what is the minimum temperature of Anurag’s star if the star emits most of its visible light at 400 nm?
ANSWER: 7500
14. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A string is attached to a wall at one end. Eddy pulls on the other end, creating a tension T. He notices that a wave traveling along the string has speed v. If Eddy pulls harder to increase the tension to 3T, at what speed would a new wave travel along the string?
W) v
X) √3v
Y) 3v
Z) 9v
ANSWER: X) √3v
14. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the charge produced on the surface of a spherical conductor from a charge Q in a cavity near the surface of the conductor?
W) Charge less than Q evenly distributed over the surface
X) Charge less than Q unevenly distributed over the surface
Y) Charge equal to Q evenly distributed over the surface
Z) Charge equal to Q unevenly distributed over the surface
ANSWER: Y) Charge equal to Q evenly distributed over the surface
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In photoelectric devices, electrons are ejected from a metal surface only if incoming light has enough energy to overcome a material-specific threshold. What is the name of this threshold energy required to remove an electron from the surface of a metal in a vacuum?
ANSWER: Work function
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements which are true about water’s HOMO and LUMO 1) Water’s molecular plane is the nodal plane of its HOMO 2) Water’s HOMO is non-bonding 3) Water’s LUMO donates electrons when it acts as a nucleophile.
