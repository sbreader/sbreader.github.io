1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What receptors are present at the postganglionic ends of parasympathetic nerves?
W) Nicotinic Acetylcholine Receptor
X) Muscarinic Acetylcholine Receptor
Y) Alpha Adrenergic Receptor
Z) Beta Adrenergic Receptor
ANSWER: X) Muscarinic Acetylcholine Receptor
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer As end-diastolic volume increases, the stroke volume increases due to an increased contraction strength of the heart. What is the name for the law that describes this principle?
ANSWER: Frank-Starling Law (also accept: Starling’s Law of the Heart)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice When the following components are mixed in equivalent amounts, which solution would have the highest buffer capacity?
W) 1 M H2SO4 (aq) and 1M NaOH (aq)
X) 0.3 M HCl (aq) and 0.5M NH3 (aq)
Y) 2 M HC2H3O2 (aq) and 2.7 M KOH (aq)
Z) 1.2 M HF (aq) and 1.1 M NaF (aq)
ANSWER: Z) 1.2 M HF (aq) and 1.1 M NaF (aq)
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The equilibrium constant for the autoionization of water at a certain temperature is 1.0 × 10-12. If the pKa of a certain acid is 3.4 then what is the pKb of its conjugate base in the solution to 2 significant figures.
ANSWER: 8.6
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Sand drops vertically at a very small velocity at a rate of 6 kg/s into a moving cart. You apply a force to the cart in order to keep it moving at a constant speed 5 m/s. How much energy is lost to friction per unit time?
ANSWER: 75 J/S

W) Lorentz force law
X) Biot-Savart law
Y) Ampere’s law

4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many distinct ways can four people be seated around a table if 2 of the people cannot be seated next to each other?
ANSWER: 2
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Sanjay and Ne are playing a game. In the first round of the game, they each have a 1/2 chance of winning. The winner of each round has his chances of winning halved for the next round. Expressing your answer as a fraction in lowest terms, what is the chance that Ne wins at least 1 of the first 4 rounds?
ANSWER: 1023/1024
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Farallon Plate was one of three large tectonic plates that made up the great “all sea” Panthalassa before the breakup of Pangaea. Which of the following plates is not a remnant of the Farallon Plate?

X) Explorer Plate
Y) Nazca Plate
Z) Gorda Plate

5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer When mica-containing rocks undergo metamorphism and become foliated, they may turn into one of three different types of rock: phyllite, slate, or schist, BASIS Peoria Late Regionals DE-Mid Nationals DE depending on how large the resultant mica flakes are. Order these three rock types from largest flake average flake size to smallest average flake size.

11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Hot Jupiters are gas giant exoplanets that orbit extremely close to their stars. Which of the following processes is most likely responsible for the existence of Hot Jupiters?

X) Drag on a pre-existing gas giant due to the protoplanetary disk
