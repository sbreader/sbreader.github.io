7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A ground-dwelling mammal can warn nearby relatives of a predator by giving a loud alarm call. Calling reduces the caller’s own survival probability by 0.20. If the caller has two full siblings and one half-sibling nearby, should the alarm call be favored according to Hamilton’s rule?
