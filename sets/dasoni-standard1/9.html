1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the hypersensitive response in plant immunity?
W) Local production of antimicrobial chemicals and hardening of cell walls
X) Localized cell death around the area of infection
Y) Upregulation of defensive hormones such as jasmonic acid
Z) Production of salicylic acid triggering global antimicrobial defenses
ANSWER: X) Localized cell death around the area of infection
