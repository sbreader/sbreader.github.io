1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why chlorine has a higher electron affinity than fluorine?
W) Fluorine has a larger atomic radius, so the added electron is less strongly attracted.
X) Chlorine experiences less electron-electron repulsion in its valence shell compared to fluorine.
Y) Fluorine has a lower effective nuclear charge than chlorine.
Z) Chlorine’s valence electrons are at a lower energy level than fluorine’s.
ANSWER: X) Chlorine experiences less electron-electron repulsion in its valence shell compared to fluorine.
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, order the following 3 orbitals by which they will be filled up according to the Aufbau Principle 1) 5f 2) 7s 3) 6d
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements is not an exception to the Aufbau principle?
W) Chromium
X) Copper
Y) Palladium
Z) Zinc
ANSWER: Z) Zinc
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In the Rydberg Equation, to what power is the atomic number and principal quantum number raised respectively?
W) -2,2
X) 2,-2
Y) 2,2
Z) -2,-2
ANSWER: X) 2,-2
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Toluene is treated with Potassium Permanganate. What is the major product of this reaction?
ANSWER: Benzoic Acid
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What principle states that no two electrons can share the same four quantum numbers?
ANSWER: Pauli Exclusion Principle
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer An acid, HA, has a pKa of 7.9. To two sig figs, what will be the pH of a solution made from 100 mL 0.05 M NaOH and 100 mL 0.1 M HA?
ANSWER: 7.9
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer To the nearest integer percent, what is the packing efficiency of a face-centered cubic cell?
ANSWER: 74
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following molecules, by name or number, by increasing bond angle 1) NH3 2) PH3 3) H2S
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following 3 solvents by increasing ability to speed up E1 reactions 1) CCl4 2) Water 3) DMSO
ANSWER: 1,3,2
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three isoelectronic species by increasing atomic radius 1. N3- 2. Na+ 3. Ne
ANSWER: 2,3,1
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following discoveries were made thanks to the Millikan oil-drop experiment?
W) The electric charge of a single electron
X) The existence of the nucleus
Y) The true structure of benzene
Z) Millikan's Theory of Density
ANSWER: W) The electric charge of a single electron
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The equilibrium constant for the reaction A + B → C is 80 and the equilibrium constant for the reaction A+ C → 3B is 20. What is the equilibrium constant of A→B?
ANSWER: 40
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the pressure in atmospheres, to one significant figure, of 1 mole of an ideal gas in a 5 L container at 300 K?
ANSWER: 5 atm
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many intermediates are there in a pericyclic reaction?
ANSWER: 0
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Calcium carbide reacts with water to form calcium hydroxide and what gas?
ANSWER: Ethyne (also accept: acetylene)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Sulfamic Acid has formula H3NSO3 and typically exists with a charged amine group and a negatively charged oxygen. In other words, it is a neutral molecule with both a positive and a negative charge. What is the name for this type of molecule?
ANSWER: Zwitterion
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Uranium-238 and Uranium 235 is most efficiently separated through which of the following processes?
W) Effusion
X) Distillation
Y) Paper Chromatagraphy
Z) Precipitation
ANSWER: W) Effusion
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice H2S has a bond angle of about 92.1 degrees and H2O has a bond angle of about 104.5 degrees. Which of the following best explains the reason for the significantly lower bond angle of H2S?
W) Oxygen is more electronegative than Sulfur
X) Sulfur has a higher electron affinity than Oxygen
Y) Sulfur is unable to hybridize effectively
Z) Sulfur has a higher molar mass than oxygen
ANSWER: Y) Sulfur is unable to hybridize effectively
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The alpha elimination of chloroform, will lead to the formation of what highly reactive intermediate?
ANSWER: Carbene
