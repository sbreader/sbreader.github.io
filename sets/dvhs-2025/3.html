Source packet

1. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the average of the 5 numbers: 2,4,6,8 and 10?
ANSWER: 6
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer How far does a car travel if it goes 30 mph for 2 hours?
ANSWER: 60 miles
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which orbital is fully filled in a ground state helium atom?
ANSWER: 1s orbital
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By what process do human somatic cells divide to form two daughter cells?
ANSWER: Mitosis
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Ames National lab are studying high heat resistant materials made of two or more metals. What is the term for a mix of two or more metals?
ANSWER: Alloy
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer How many astronomic units are between the Earth and Sun?
ANSWER: 1
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following planets in increasing order by their distance from the sun in AU 1) Jupiter 2) Uranus 3) Neptune 4) mars
ANSWER: 4,1,2,3
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What number is 150% of 20?
ANSWER: 30
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What fundamental force is responsible for the formation of planets?
ANSWER: Gravity
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer How many up and down quarks respectively make up a proton?
ANSWER: two up and one down
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for a chemical process in which Oxygen is used to burn organic material, resulting in carbon dioxide and water?
ANSWER: Combustion
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for an organism that has two different alleles for one gene?
ANSWER: Heterozygous
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer When two heterozygous individuals are crossed, what percent of their offspring will exhibit the recessive phenotype?
ANSWER: 25%
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Albert Einstein won the Nobel Prize in physics for the discovery of what phenomenon which describes the energy of photons?
ANSWER: Photoelectric Effect
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The San Andreas Fault is an example of what kind of fault?
ANSWER: Strike Slip (do not accept: Transform)
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What specific type of plate boundary creates continental volcanic arcs such as the Cascade mountain range?
ANSWER: Ocean-Continent Convergent (do not accept: CONVERGENT)
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If Ashwin buys 1 1/4 pounds of fruit and 1/4 pound of vegetables, how much food did he buy in total?
ANSWER: 1.5 lbs (also accept: 1 1/2)
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Dougherty Valley High School is 78.9% Asian. If there are 3000 students at Dougherty Valley High School, how many students are at Dougherty Valley High School are Asian?
ANSWER: 2,367
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which scientist published a book on the laws of motion based on his interactions with an apple?
ANSWER: Newton
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer A person is standing stationary on the floor of a classroom. What force balances gravity to keep his body at rest?
ANSWER: Normal Force
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for a substance that has a pH less than 7?
ANSWER: Acid
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If the pH of a solution is 2, what is the pOH?
ANSWER: 12
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following would describe what happens when an animal cell is placed in a hypotonic solution?
W) it will shrivel
X) it will plasmolyze
Y) it will become turgid
Z) it will burst
ANSWER: Z) it will burst
17. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Most ethanol in the US is produced from which crop?
ANSWER: Corn
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What gas is the most abundant in the Earth’s atmosphere?
ANSWER: Nitrogen
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following layers of the atmosphere from bottom to top 1) mesosphere 2) stratosphere 3) troposphere 4) thermosphere
ANSWER: 3,2,1,4
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the frequency of a wave with a period of 10 seconds?
ANSWER: 0.1 Hertz (also accept: 1/10)
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify the following 3 properties as chemical or physical, respectively. 1) flammability 2) density 3) hardness
ANSWER: 1) chemical 2) physical 3) physical
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What terrestrial biome has the highest net primary productivity?
ANSWER: Tropical Rainforest
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer In the tundra biome, what is the term for the layer of the ground that is frozen year round?
ANSWER: Permafrost
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for the energy required to remove an electron from an atom?
ANSWER: Ionization Energy
23. BONUS Physics Short Answer Fermi Lab is the host to DUNE, an underground facility which will paint a picture of the universe and how it works. DUNE is designed to detect which ghostly particles?
ANSWER: Neutrinos
