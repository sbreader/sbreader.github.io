1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Carbohydrates form the structural components of cells and assist in
W) cellular recognition
X) encoding cellular functions
Y) provide insulation
Z) acts as a universal solvent
ANSWER: W) cellular recognition
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice One difference between prokaryotes and eukaryotes is that
W) an outer cell membrane is missing in prokaryotes
X) Prokaryotes lack Golgi bodies
Y) Eukaryotes are the 'living' cells
Z) The Endosymbiont theory is not applicable to prokaryotes
ANSWER: X) Prokaryotes lack Golgi bodies
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Give the full name for ATP
ANSWER: ADENOSINE TRIPHOSPHATE
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which organelle is responsible in removing of toxic elements from the cytoplasm
W) Mitochondria
X) Golgi apparatus
Y) lysosomes
Z) Centrioles
ANSWER: Y) lysosomes
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the process called when special transport molecules in plant cells carry poisons into the large central vacuole, where they are stored or chemically deactivated?
ANSWER: INTRACELLULAR DETOXIFICATION
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which plastids control colors of flowers and fruits?
W) Chloroplasts
X) Leucoplasts
Y) chromoplasts
Z) Bilbokplasts
ANSWER: Y) chromoplasts
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which type of plastids is colorless and helps stores nutrients, like starch in a potato?
ANSWER: LEUCOPLASTS
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What was so special about stromatolites?
W) they initiated spontaneous combustion
X) helped create Earth's atmosphere
Y) combined with amino acids, sugar and fat molecules
Z) defined the formation of the Earth's crust
ANSWER: X) helped create Earth's atmosphere
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the pair who sort of helped discover the DNA double helix
ANSWER: WATSON AND CRICK
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice How many hydrogen bonds are necessary to pair up guanine and cytosine?
W) 1
X) 3
Y) no hydrogen bonds exist
Z) 2
ANSWER: X) 3
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Who was responsible for founding Celera Genomics to map the genome through automated sequencing with the aid of private funds, and helping in completing the Human Genome Project?
ANSWER: CRAIG VENTER
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What enzyme synthesizes a new DNA strand by binding new complementary nucleotides to the single bases?
W) synthetitase
X) genetase
Y) polymerase
Z) ductase
ANSWER: Y) polymerase
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Give the term used to refer to animals whose eggs hatch outside the mother's body
ANSWER: OVIPAROUS
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is another name for gonosomes?
W) nerve cells
X) damaged peptide sequences
Y) sex chromosomes
Z) purgatories
ANSWER: Y) sex chromosomes
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many chromosomes are there in the cells of lampreys?
ANSWER: 174
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A diploid set of chromosomes can be best described as a
W) two copies of every chromatid
X) two copies of every chromosome
Y) a chromosome tetrad
Z) an abnormal array of chromosomes
ANSWER: X) two copies of every chromosome
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name for the failure of individual chromosome pairs to not separate for distribution into daughter cells?
ANSWER: NONDISJUNCTION
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A change in entire sets of chromosomes is a type of
W) infection
X) euploidy
Y) karyogram
Z) systemic disruption
ANSWER: X) euploidy
10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice At what phase of mitosis is it possible to view dense chromosomes under a light microscope?
W) Interphase
X) Prophase
Y) Metaphase
Z) Anaphase
ANSWER: Y) Metaphase
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When microtubules form a spindle, the chromosomes align themselves along what?
ANSWER: EQUATORIAL PLATE
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name for chromatids replicating?
ANSWER: IDENTICAL REPLICATION
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer On average, about how many cells are there in the human body?
ANSWER: TEN TRILLION (also accept: 1013)
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following occurs in Meiosis I?
W) chromatids double through DNA synthesis, resulting in a new diploid set
X) sister chromatids separate into two different gametes
Y) gametes contain only one chromatid each
Z) homologous chromosomes segregate to opposite poles
ANSWER: Z) homologous chromosomes segregate to opposite poles
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The process of entire chromosomes recombining during anaphase I is known as
ANSWER: INTERCHROMOSOMAL RECOMBINATION
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Organisms that feed on other living things are generally known as
W) biotrophs
X) carnatrophs
Y) heterotrophs
Z) salvatrophs
ANSWER: Y) heterotrophs
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Who was the scientist credited for his discovery of the nature and mode of action of the respiratory enzyme (cytochrome oxidase)?
ANSWER: OTTO HEINRICH WARBURG
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The breakdown of pyruvate into three molecules of CO2 is known as
ANSWER: OXIDATIVE DECARBOXYLATION
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The process of oxidizing the hydrogen of the NADH+ + H+ without the aid of oxygen is
W) glycolysis
X) fermentation
Y) ETC
Z) Krebs Cycle
ANSWER: X) fermentation
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The thick waxy layer on exposed outer surfaces of cells that protects them against water loss and injury is known as a(n)
W) ecological pyramid
X) cyclin
Y) cuticle
Z) homozygous mixture
ANSWER: Y) cuticle
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice One of the simplest types of tissues is known as
W) epimer
X) epithelium
Y) Fas antigen
Z) fat cell
ANSWER: X) epithelium
19. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The regulatory mechanisms that maintain an organism's internal environment within tolerable limits is known as
W) order
X) prokaryotic cell(s)
Y) nucleotide
Z) homeostasis
ANSWER: Z) homeostasis
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The fluid-filled part of the inner ear which sends nerve impulses to the brain through the cochlear nerve is known as
W) corpus luteum
X) cortex
Y) internode
Z) cochlea
ANSWER: Z) cochlea
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the large blood vessel that carries blood from the heart to the tissues of the body?
ANSWER: ARTERY (also accept: ARTERIES)
21. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Blood vessels that pass through the periosteum
W) Dissolves bacteria and filters contaminants
X) help regulate body temperature
Y) Promotes ossification
Z) carries oxygen and nutrients to the bone
ANSWER: Z) carries oxygen and nutrients to the bone
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the general name of the less dense tissue that is found inside the outer layer of the compact bone?
ANSWER: SPONGY BONE
23. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the difference between osteoblasts and osteoclasts respectively?
W) break down bones; create bones
X) support body; destroys bone
Y) create bones; breaks down bones
Z) formulates red blood cells; formulates white blood cells
ANSWER: Y) create bones; breaks down bones
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name for replica body parts which are implanted into the body or attached externally as replacements?
ANSWER: PROSTHESES

W) fission
X) flagellum
Y) filtration
Z) gel electrophoresis
ANSWER: Y) filtration
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term used to distinguish a plant that grows directly on the body of another plant?
ANSWER: EPIPHYTE
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the primary roots found in some plants commonly referred to when they grow longer and thicker than other roots?
ANSWER: TAPROOTS
