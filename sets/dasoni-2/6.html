1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Casey is doing some field work, and is currently investigating a corn plantation. Corn seeds have what type of modified cotyledon that transports nutrients to the embryo during germination?
ANSWER: Scutellum
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Neutrinos are hypothesized to be what type of fermion which is its own antiparticle?
ANSWER: Majorana fermion
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of topography would likely form through glacial processes?
W) Karst
X) Knob and Kettle
Y) Basin and range
Z) Floodplain
ANSWER: X) Knob and Kettle
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following functions is injective?
W) sine of x
X) x squared
Y) natural log of x
Z) cotangent of x
ANSWER: Y) natural log of x
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Male peacocks have long, ornamental tails whereas female peahens do not. This is because peafowl exhibit what phenomenon that increases reproductive fitness?
ANSWER: Sexual dimorphism
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Alex shoots an unpolarized beam of light of intensity I at two polarizers which are at an angle of 30 degrees with respect to each other. What is the final intensity of the light?
ANSWER: 3/8 I
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In the reaction of lead 2 nitrate and potassium iodide, what are the spectator ions?
ANSWER: potassium, nitrate (also accept: K+, NO3-)
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer While observing the surface of Mercury, Allen notices the largest region of jumbled terrain on the planet, which formed from the path of seismic energy through Mercury's interior. Which feature of Mercury is located at the geographical antipode of this feature?
ANSWER: CALORIS BASIN
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A sequence of numbers has first two terms 1 and 2, and each consecutive term is the product of the previous two terms. What is the 6th term in this sequence?
ANSWER: 32
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following locations would most likely contain transitional epithelium?
W) Small intestines
X) Bladder
Y) Bronchiole
Z) Spleen
ANSWER: X) Bladder
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Gavin has a wooden block on a spring. Which of the following changes would decrease the period of oscillation of this block?
W) Replacing the wooden block with a steel block of the same size
X) Placing the wooden block on an inclined plane
Y) Adding an identical spring in series with the first spring
Z) Adding an identical spring in parallel with the first spring
ANSWER: Z) Adding an identical spring in parallel with the first spring
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following substances that could act as a cathode in a galvanic cell with hydrogen gas at the anode 1) Chlorine gas 2) Copper 3) Sodium
ANSWER: 1 and 2
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the phenomenon observed in the Southern Hemisphere when the solar wind disturbs Earth’s magnetic field, exciting atoms and causing them to emit radiation?
ANSWER: AURORA AUSTRALIS
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many petals are in the graph of the polar rose with equation r = 4 sin(4 theta)?
ANSWER: 8
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three hormones that suppress appetite 1) Insulin 2) Leptin 3) PYY
ANSWER: All
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following changes that would increase the impedance of an AC circuit with a resistor and a capacitor in series 1) Increasing resistance 2) Increasing capacitance 3) Increasing frequency
ANSWER: 1 only
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following conditions ensures that a reaction is spontaneous?
W) Delta H greater than zero, Delta S greater than zero
X) Delta H greater than zero, Delta S less than zero
Y) Delta H less than zero, Delta S greater than zero
Z) Delta H less than zero, Delta S less than zero
ANSWER: Y) Delta H less than zero, Delta S greater than zero
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice According to the cosmological principle, the universe is which of the following?
W) Heterogenous and anisotropic
X) Heterogenous and isotropic
Y) Homogenous and anisotropic
Z) Homogenous and isotropic
ANSWER: Z) Homogenous and isotropic
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Identify all of the following points that must be on the nine-point circle of a triangle 1) orthocenter 2) circumcenter 3) centroid
ANSWER: None
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which specialized cells found at the micropylar end of the embryo sac help guide the pollen tube and facilitate the release of sperm cells into the egg?
ANSWER: Synergids
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer Filip has a circuit with two 6 ohm resistors connected in parallel, both of which are connected in series to a 5 ohm resistor. If a battery with an EMF of 12 Volts connected to this circuit, what is the voltage in Volts across one of the 6 Ohm resistors?
ANSWER: 4.5 Volts
22. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Given that the reaction N2 + 3H2 goes to 2NH3 is exothermic, which of the following conditions will push the reaction farthest to the right?
W) high temperature, high pressure
X) low temperature, high pressure
Y) high temperature, low pressure
Z) low temperature, low pressure
ANSWER: X) low temperature, high pressure
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following four effects that are typical of a standard La Nina 1. Stronger Walker Circulation 2. Low pressure in the Eastern Pacific Ocean 3. Colder Sea Surface temperatures in the Eastern Pacific 4. Weaker coastal winds along Peru.
ANSWER: 1, 3
24. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the sum of the digits of the number (102025+1)(102025-1)?
ANSWER: 36450
25. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following plant structures lacks tissues?
W) Rhizome
X) Rhizoid
Y) Trichome
Z) Peristome
ANSWER: X) Rhizoid
26. BONUS Physics Short Answer Ethan is investigating his new thermodynamics textbook. He realizes that the book stably rotates about two axes, but that it cannot stably rotate about the third axis. What is this effect known as?
ANSWER: Tennis racket theorem (also accept: Dzhanibekov theorem; intermediate axis theorem)
27. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The half-life of the second-order reaction 2A goes to B is 0.02 seconds when the concentration of A is 1 mole per liter. What is the half-life of this reaction when the concentration of A is 0.4 moles per liter?
ANSWER: 0.05 seconds
28. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is least likely to have contributed to the formation of lobate scarps and faults found on Mercury?
W) Shrinking in the size of Mercury
X) Cooling following the Late Heavy Bombardment
Y) Tidal stresses from the Sun
Z) Rapid rotation around the Sun
ANSWER: Z) Rapid rotation around the Sun
29. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the Euler totient of 15 plus the Euler totient of 30?
ANSWER: 16

ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
31. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is true about circular light polarization?
W) In circularly polarized light, the direction of the electric field is constant
X) Circularly polarized light is unaffected by polarizers
Y) Circularly polarized light can be made from a combination of linearly polarized light in different directions
Z) The sun emits circularly polarized light
ANSWER: Y) Circularly polarized light can be made from a combination of linearly polarized light in different directions
32. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In organic chemistry, what term is given to the functional group consisting of a carbonyl group attached to a nitrogen atom?

ANSWER: 20/39
35. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A short day plant and a long day plant are each exposed to a normal photoperiod, but during their critical dark period, they receive four brief light pulses in the following sequence: red, far red, red, far red.
