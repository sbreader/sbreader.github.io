Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Two groups of the same species in the same area interbreed, but eventually evolve into two different species due to reproductive isolation. What type of speciation is this an example of?
ANSWER: SYMPATRIC
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an example of a vestigial structure?
W) Pelvic bones in snakes
X) Ostrich wings
Y) Human wisdom teeth
Z) Human nails
ANSWER: Z) Human nails
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Trade winds near the equator are the main contributor to what process that creates high biological productivity on the west coasts of continents?
ANSWER: UPWELLING
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The continental rise, a collection of loose sediment located at the bottom of the continental slope, is primarily created by what type of underwater current?
ANSWER: TURBIDITY CURRENT
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab have recently developed a way to turn algae into biocrude, a potential replacement for petroleum. To do this, they gather algae from harmful algal blooms, which are likely to occur in which of the following lake types?
W) Eutrophic
X) Oligotrophic
Y) Endorheic
Z) Pluvial
ANSWER: W) Eutrophic
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying the repopulation of ecosystems after wildfires. Wildfires can often be good for ecosystems because the ash from biological debris contains what nutrient that is commonly found in fertilizers?
ANSWER: POTASH (also accept: POTASSIUM)
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A car tire has a circumference of 2 meters. Once the car travels 100 meters, how many radians will the tire have turned?
ANSWER: 100π
4. BONUS Math Short Answer 3 points A, B, and C are chosen on a circle with center O and radius 6. If angle AOB equals angle BOC and angle AOB equals 30 degrees, what is the length of arc ACB?
ANSWER: 11π
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What does the blastopore become in a marsupial?
ANSWER: THE ANUS (also accept: ANUS)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the great circle that divides the moon into a day side and a night side?
ANSWER: TERMINATOR
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The “thermite” reaction, in which aluminum metal is added to ferric oxide to produce iron metal and aluminum oxide, is which of the following types of reactions?
W) Synthesis
X) Double Displacement
Y) Reduction-Oxidation
Z) Decomposition
ANSWER: Y) Reduction-Oxidation
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the three specific three-letter sequences of nucleotides that end open reading frames?
ANSWER: UAG (also accept: UGA; UAA)
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab recently used the Large Hadron Collider to collide two photons. Using the energy of their strong magnetic fields, the collision produced massive W-bosons. This is possible because energy can be converted to mass according to what theory?
ANSWER: SPECIAL RELATIVITY (also accept: MASS ENERGY EQUIVALENCE)
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer Theoretical physicists at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory are testing potential theories for the explanation of dark matter. One such theory is attempting to confirm dark matter as being what type of particle that interacts with atomic nuclei through the Z-Boson?
ANSWER: WIMPS (also accept: WEAKLY INTERACTING MASSIVE PARTICLES)
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The sum of all the values of row n in Pascal's triangle is 16. What is the third value in row n?
ANSWER: 6
8. BONUS Math Short Answer Nancy has three children, Andy, Adam, and Aaron. In how many ways can she split her 10 oranges between them if each can have any nonnegative number of oranges?
ANSWER: 66
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following microscopes would most likely be used in a high school science lab?
W) Scanning probe microscope
X) Ion microscope
Y) Optical microscope
Z) Electron microscope
ANSWER: Y) Optical microscope
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What technique is used to study cell structure and function by centrifuging cells and studying the observed pellets?
ANSWER: CELL FRACTIONATION
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What metal comprises a Grignard reagent?
ANSWER: MAGNESIUM (also accept: Mg)
10. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following types of compounds results when carbon dioxide is reacted with a Grignard reagent?
W) Primary alcohol
X) Secondary alcohol
Y) Aldehyde
Z) Carboxylate
ANSWER: Z) Carboxylate
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following features of a glacial landscape is created by erosion instead of deposition?
W) Hanging valley
X) Esker
Y) Terminal moraine
Z) Outwash plain
ANSWER: W) Hanging valley
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What common glacial feature is formed when two cirques grow near each other, creating a sharp ridge between them?
ANSWER: ARETE
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at the Argonne National Laboratory are using the Advanced Photon Source to help study drugs for the treatment of COVID-19. COVID-19 is a single stranded RNA virus, which is about how long in diameter?
W) 500 Angstroms
X) 200 nanometers
Y) 1 micrometer
Z) 30 micrometers
ANSWER: X) 200 nanometers
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three constellations that are circumpolar when viewed from mid-southern latitudes 1) Ursa Minor 2) Octans 3) Leo
ANSWER: 2 only
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A car is in motion with a velocity of 10m/s in the positive x direction. The driver then hits the brakes and the car comes to a complete stop. In SI base units, what was the acceleration of the car from the initial brake to the stopping point if the time taken to stop was 1 second?
ANSWER: -10 (do not accept: 10)
13. BONUS Math Short Answer In the town of Kletsville, 3 klons are worth 2 klangs and 1 klarg is worth 4 klicks. When Karmen went to the store and purchased a kaleidoscope, she paid 10 klangs and was given back 8 klicks and 6 klons in change. How many klangs was the kaleidoscope worth?
ANSWER: 4
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at LIGO are using sensitive interferometers to detect gravitational waves. Whereas the primary medium for sound wave propagation is air, the medium for gravitational wave propagation is what?
ANSWER: SPACE-TIME
14. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the gravitational acceleration at the bottom of a hole that stretches halfway to the center of the Earth? Assume the density of the Earth is uniform.
W) g/4
X) g/2
Y) g
Z) 2g
ANSWER: X) g/2
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following phyla contains organisms that are commonly called "flatworms"?
W) Annelida
X) Platyhelminthes
Y) Mollusca
Z) Arthropoda
ANSWER: X) Platyhelminthes
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In 2019, LIGO detected gravitational waves from a merger that resulted in a black hole with a mass of 142 solar masses. This was the first concrete detection of what type of uncommon black hole?
ANSWER: INTERMEDIATE-MASS BLACK HOLE
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of coral reef usually forms thin circular reefs and islands around a lagoon?
ANSWER: ATOLI
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following phyla that are segmented 1) Annelida 2) Cnidaria 3) Arthropoda 4) Porifera
ANSWER: 1 and 3
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice According to Torricelli's Law, the velocity at which a liquid will exit the bottom of a vertically positioned cylinder container is directly proportional to what?
W) Height of Container
X) Atmospheric Pressure
Y) Area of the Hole
Z) Fluid Density
ANSWER: W) Height of Container
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer On a topographic map, concentric circles with hachured lines pointing inward usually indicate what feature?
ANSWER: DEPRESSION
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer If two species are members of the same order, identify all of the following 3 taxonomic categories they must also share 1) Class 2) Genus 3) Family
ANSWER: 1 only
18. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A solid uniform sphere with radius R is spinning with a velocity V that is tangential to the surface of the sphere. Which of the following best describes the value of the slope of the graph of angular momentum vs. mass of the sphere in terms of V and R?
W) Exactly equal to V times R
X) Between 75 and 100 percent of V times R
Y) Between 50 and 75 percent of V times R
Z) Between 25 and 50 percent of V times R
ANSWER: Z) Between 25 and 50 percent of V times R
19. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following animals is not correctly matched to its respective class?
W) Snake-Reptilia
X) Moose-Mammalia
Y) Millipedes-Diplopoda
Z) Clam-Gastropoda
ANSWER: Z) Clam-Gastropoda
38. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In the hyposulfite ion, what is the charge on the sulfur atom?
ANSWER: 2
39. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What spectral class of stars characteristically has the strongest Balmer lines in its spectral graphs?
W) A
X) M
Y) K
Z) B
ANSWER: W) A

W) 1/2
X) √3/2
Y) √2
Z) 1
ANSWER: W) 1/2
41. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the brightest star in the constellation Bootes?
ANSWER: ARCTURUS
42. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Give the balanced chemical equation for the “thermite” reaction, in which aluminum metal is added to ferric oxide to produce iron metal and aluminum oxide. Answer in the form w + x -> y + z
ANSWER: Fe2O3 + 2Al -> Al2O3 + 2Fe (also accept: Fe2O3 + 2Al yields Al2O3 + 2Fe)
43. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the minimum value of 10/(sin(θ)cos(θ))?
ANSWER: 20
44. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at the Argonne National Laboratory are using the Advanced Photon Source to help study drugs for the treatment of COVID-19. The COVID-19 disease is caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus, which contains the PLpro enzyme, which is used to cleave proteins into smaller components. What type of enzyme is the PLpro enzyme?
ANSWER: PROTEASE
