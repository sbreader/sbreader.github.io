Packet 9 (2018 Regional Round 9) of National Ocean Sciences Bowl:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What percentage of the ocean is deeper than 2000 meters?
W) 65
X) 75
Y) 85
Z) 95
ANSWER: Y) 85
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes a primary consumer?
W) Insectivorous
X) Omnivorous
Y) Piscavorous
Z) Herbivorous
ANSWER: Z) Herbivorous
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What rock type is formed by the lithification of calcareous ooze?
ANSWER: Chalk (also accept: limestone)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Approximately what percentage of incoming shortwave solar radiation is absorbed by ocean and land?
W) 32
X) 47
Y) 58
Z) 65
ANSWER: X) 47
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following currents is one of five major coastal currents affiliated with upwelling zones?
W) East Australia current
X) California current
Y) Gulf Stream
Z) Kuroshio current
ANSWER: X) California current
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice How is grain size in turbidites organized?
W) Uniformly
X) Finer grains higher
Y) Coarser grains higher
Z) Randomly
ANSWER: X) Finer grains higher
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What instrument measures velocity flow past a fixed point in the ocean that utilizes Eulerian frame of reference?
ANSWER: Moorings (also accept: moored profiler; Acoustic Doppler Current Profiler (ADCP); fixed current meters.)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which abiotic factor does NOT have a significant effect on the health of the feather boa kelp?
W) Temperature
X) Nutrient load
Y) Dissolved oxygen
Z) Low tide
ANSWER: Y) Dissolved oxygen
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What method of coral reef seeding is proving to be more cost and time effective, enabling larger scale reef restoration?
W) Collecting coral fragments to replant
X) Moving coral boulders to restoration sites
Y) Distributing coral gametes over a restoration site
Z) Collecting coral gametes to culture in the lab
ANSWER: Z) Collecting coral gametes to culture in the lab
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following produces a seasonal reversal of wind direction and changes in rainfall patterns?
W) Monsoon
X) Typhoon
Y) Tsunami
Z) Nor'easter
ANSWER: W) Monsoon
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice From the seafloor to the top of the mantle, what are the layers of typical oceanic crust?
W) Pillow basalts, granite, sheeted dikes
X) Sheeted dikes, gabbros, peridotite
Y) Pillow basalts, sheeted dikes, gabbros
Z) Pillow basalts, gabbros, sheeted dikes
ANSWER: Y) Pillow basalts, sheeted dikes, gabbros
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of these seas is most likely to be the one of the present day remnants of the Tethys Sea?
W) Mediterranean
X) Sargasso
Y) Chukchi
Z) Red
ANSWER: W) Mediterranean
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer To which phylum do shipworms belong?
ANSWER: Mollusca
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about the coastal ocean is NOT true?
W) Salinity and temperature of coastal waters are more variable than waters in the open ocean
X) Coastal currents are generally independent of the open-ocean gyres
Y) Benthos of coastal oceans are less diverse and abundant than open ocean benthos
Z) The mixed layer has more sources of nutrients in coastal waters than in the open ocean
ANSWER: Y) Benthos of coastal oceans are less diverse and abundant than open ocean benthos
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What name is given to the event resulting from a runaway positive feedback between formation of sea ice and land glaciers and increase in the Earth’s albedo, leading to global glaciation?
ANSWER: Snowball earth
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice When considering ocean acidification mitigation, why is it important to conserve seagrass habitats?
W) They attenuate water flow to make a barrier
X) They harbor most species with high tolerance of ocean acidification
Y) The photosynthetic grasses provide a pH buffer
Z) There are not enough seagrass beds in the world
ANSWER: Y) The photosynthetic grasses provide a pH buffer
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In 2017, researchers in Germany published an article in Nature showing a global decline in which chemical oceanographic parameter?
W) Carbon dioxide
X) Dissolved oxygen
Y) Nitrogen
Z) Hydrogen
ANSWER: X) Dissolved oxygen
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the four major eastern boundary current systems?
ANSWER: California, Peru/Humboldt, Canary, and Benguela currents
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Approximately what percent of the Earth's surface is covered by the Atlantic Ocean?
W) 37
X) 27
Y) 17
Z) 7
ANSWER: Y) 17
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the reproductive arm structure used by the male octopus?
ANSWER: Hectocotylus
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The closure of which body of water prevented mixing between the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans; thus, allowing for differences in salinity, and the formation of the global ocean conveyor belt?
W) Drake Passage
X) Panama Passage
Y) Central American Seaway
Z) Paleo-Tethys Ocean
ANSWER: Y) Central American Seaway
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the portion of the ocean, important to marine mammal communication, where low frequency sounds may travel for thousands of miles?
ANSWER: SOFAR channel (also accept: Sound Fixing and Ranging channel; DSC; Deep Sound Channel)
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What are the main nitrogenous components of teleost fish excretion?
W) Ammonia and nitrogen gas
X) Ammonia and urea
Y) Ammonia and uric acid
Z) Ammonia and trimethylamine oxide
ANSWER: X) Ammonia and urea
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A frame carrying 10 nets, as well as sensors for chlorophyll and light, is towed behind a slow moving research vessel. What is it called?
ANSWER: MOCNESS (also accept: Multiple Opening and Closing Net and Environmental Sensing System)
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which key predator in the Chesapeake Bay, also the state fish of Virginia and Maryland, is an important commercial and recreational fishery species?
ANSWER: Striped bass (also accept: rockfish; Morone saxatilis)
