1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following 4 statements that are NOT true of Uranus: 1: it rotates on an axis that is 97.9° from the perpendicular to its orbit 2: the belt-zone circulation of Uranus is dominated by the direction of sunlight 3: the cloud layers of ammonia, ammonia hydrosulfide, and water are seen because they reside above the layers of hydrogen 4: the dynamo effect of Uranus operates nearer to the surface in a layer of liquid water with dissolved ammonia and methane
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is INCORRECT about volcanoes?
W) most composite cones are located in the Ring of Fire
X) composite cones are composed of both lava and pyroclastic material
Y) composite cones are the product of gas-rich magma with basaltic composition
Z) as composite cones mature, lava tends to flow from fissures that develop on the lower flanks of the cone
ANSWER: Y) composite cones are the product of gas-rich magma with basaltic composition
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the variation in temperature that occurs when air is compressed or allowed to expand? It occurs even though heat is neither added nor subtracted.
ANSWER: ADIABATIC TEMPERATURE CHANGE
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the MOST accurate regarding dark matter in the universe?
W) cold dark matter is composed of neutrinos and other particles that travel at or near the speed of light
X) most successful models of galaxy formation require that dark matter be predominantly cold dark matter
Y) nonbaryonic matter interacts significantly with all types of radiation, especially photons with visible light
Z) after the universe was formed, since dark matter was not immune to radiation, they could not contract to form clouds while the universe was still very young
ANSWER: X) most successful models of galaxy formation require that dark matter be predominantly cold dark matter
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term is used by astronomers to refer to the energy that emanates outward in an empty vacuum, which is a primary cause for the acceleration of universe? This energy does not necessarily remain constant over time.
ANSWER: QUINTESSENCE
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following 4 statements that are TRUE about precipitation from cold clouds: 1: supercooled water droplets will readily freeze upon instantaneous contact with solid particles that have crystal forms that resemble ice 2: freezing nuclei do not become active unless the temperature reaches -10 °C or less 3: as the temperature decreases, the relative humidity of air with respect to ice decreases 4: as ice crystals consume excess water droplets, the relative humidity near the surrounding droplets is lowered
ANSWER: 1, 2, AND 4
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is LEAST accurate about the atmosphere?
W) the temperature decrease as altitude increases is called the environmental lapse rate
X) the environmental lapse rate is relatively constant, with minimal variation
Y) below the tropopause, atmospheric properties like temperature and humidity are readily transferred by large-scale turbulence and some mixing
Z) temperatures increase in the thermosphere due to the absorption of short-wave, high-energy solar radiation by atoms of oxygen and nitrogen
ANSWER: X) the environmental lapse rate is relatively constant, with minimal variation
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of front is formed when an active cold front overtakes a warm front, and the advancing cold air wedges the warm air upward?
ANSWER: OCCLUDED FRONT
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the process in which the carbon in a white dwarf is completely consumed by nuclear fusion, producing a type Ia supernova explosion? As the collapse begins, the temperature increases, but the gas cannot stop the collapse because it is degenerate.
ANSWER: CARBON DEFLAGRATION
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following 4 statements that is/are TRUE about hypernovae, or collapsars: 1: in hypernovae, the supernova explosion bypasses the outer layer of the massive star 2: hypernovae can emit jets of gamma rays because of the way it collapses 3: the rotation of the massive star speeds up the collapse of the star's equatorial regions, so it collapses first along the equatorial axis 4: since regions along the equatorial axis collapse first, they produce powerful bursts of gamma rays
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is MOST accurate about paleomagnetism and magnetic reversals?
W) when they are heated above the Curie point, magnetic minerals lose their magnetism
X) solidified minerals have magnetic properties that are variable depending on their temperature
Y) stripes of high-intensity magnetism where the crust exhibits normal polarity weaken the existing magnetic field of the Earth
Z) as magma solidifies along the narrow rifts at the crest of an oceanic ridge, it is magnetized with the polarity of the preexisting magnetic field
ANSWER: W) when they are heated above the Curie point, magnetic minerals lose their magnetism
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is a secondary vent on a volcano in which magma and lava are not present, but instead gases are emitted into the air?
ANSWER: FUMAROLE
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the BEST explanation of why the proton-proton chain only occurs near the core of a star?
W) the temperature throughout the star is hotter than the temperature of the core
X) most of the mass in the star is farthest from the core of the star
Y) a small number of reactions is necessary because protons can fuse with other protons
Z) the gas density of the core is greater than the gas density anywhere else in the star
ANSWER: Z) the gas density of the core is greater than the gas density anywhere else in the star
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the conflict between observation and theory first described by a Viennese physician and astronomer, which discusses why the night sky is black and shouldn't glow as brightly as a star's surface?
ANSWER: OLBER'S PARADOX
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following 4 statements that is/are TRUE of relative humidity: 1: it is a ratio of the air's actual water-vapor content to the amount of water vapor required for saturation at that particular temperature 2: it indicates the actual quantity of water vapor in the air 3: relative humidity varies with temperature because the amount of moisture required for saturation is a function of air temperature 4: at higher temperatures it takes more water vapor to saturate the air than at lower temperatures
ANSWER: 1, 3, AND 4
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is INCORRECT about lahars?
W) they are debris flows composed mostly of volcanic materials on the flanks of volcanoes
X) they can occur only when there is a volcanic eruption
Y) they occur when highly unsaturated layers of ash and debris become saturated with water and flow down steep volcanic slopes
Z) they generally flow preexisting stream channels
ANSWER: X) they can occur only when there is a volcanic eruption
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Giving your answer in correct SI units, calculate the discharge of a stream with a cross-sectional length of 10 m, width of 5 m, and depth of 20 m, through a time period of 10 seconds.
ANSWER: 100 m3/s
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is LEAST accurate about Jupiter's ring?
W) the particles in Jupiter's ring gradually spiral inward due to Jupiter's powerful magnetic field and the pressure of sunlight
X) dust is lost from the ring as electromagnetic effects force it out of the plane of the ring to form a low-density halo above and below the ring
Y) the intense radiation around Jupiter grinds the dust specks down to nothing, proving that the ring particles are not old
Z) Jupiter's main ring is densest at its inner edge, where the small moon Adrastea orbits
ANSWER: Z) Jupiter's main ring is densest at its inner edge, where the small moon Adrastea orbits
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the icy Kuiper belt objects that are caught in a 3:2 orbital resonance with Neptune, like that of Pluto?
ANSWER: PLUTINOS
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following 4 statements that is/are TRUE of comets? 1: comets have only one type of tail that is produced by both solar wind and solar radiation 2: type I comets are produced by the ionized gas that is carried away from the nucleus by the solar wind 3: as the ices in a comet nucleus vaporize, they release dust particles that not only form the dust tail, but also spread throughout the solar system 4: the nuclei of comets are extremely strong, and it takes a substantial amount of energy to break them up
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an example of how CO2 leaves the atmosphere?
W) deposition of carbonate sediments
X) weathering of granite
Y) decay of biomass
Z) photosynthesis by vegetation
ANSWER: Y) decay of biomass
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of fog is generally formed when warm, moist air moves over a cool surface, producing a blanket-like fog? An example setting of where this type of fog may prevail is in the San Francisco Bay.
ANSWER: ADVECTION FOG
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is INCORRECT regarding meteorites?
W) the chemical composition of chondrites is the same as that of a sample of matter from the sun with the most volatile gases removed
X) chondrules, the round bits of glassy rock that are found in chondrites, cooled extremely slowly
Y) carbonaceous chondrites contain volatiles including water and carbon compounds that would have been driven off if the meteoroid had been heated much above the room temperature
Z) carbonaceous chondrites contain CAIs, or calcium-aluminum rich inclusions, which are highly refractory
ANSWER: X) chondrules, the round bits of glassy rock that are found in chondrites, cooled extremely slowly
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In astronomy, what term refers to the passage of a planet in front of a star? This process was important in the discovery of Uranus, when a star was observed to dim a number of times as Uranus crossed in front of the star.
ANSWER: OCCULTATION
