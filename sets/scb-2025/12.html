Y) Blue-Yellow Colorblindness
Z) Red-Green Colorblindness
ANSWER: X) Green-Yellow Colorblindness
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The Delta-Notch pathway is a cell signalling pathway crucial in development. Specifically, it is involved in the process of lateral inhibition. If an embryo has a mutation in the gene encoding for the notch protein, which of the following would be a result of this?

X) Abnormal melanation patterns
Y) The development of neurons without the accompanying development of glia and progenitor cells
Z) Unusually evenly distributed goblet cell distribution
ANSWER: Y) The development of neurons without the accompanying development of glia and progenitor cells

8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In conventional superconductors, which of the following best describes how Cooper pairs form?
W) Electrons form pairs by aligning their wavefunctions in the same quantum state without interaction with the lattice
X) Two electrons directly overcome their Coulomb repulsion and bind through magnetic dipole alignment
Y) Electrons lower their energy by exchanging virtual phonons, effectively attracting each other
