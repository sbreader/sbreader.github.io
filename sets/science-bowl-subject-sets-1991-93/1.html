Packet 1 (Astronomy 1991) of Science Bowl Subject Sets 1991–93:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The largest circular storm in our solar system is on the surface of which of the following planets?
W) Jupiter
X) Venus
Y) Uranus
Z) Earth
ANSWER: W) Jupiter
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The rapidly moving stream of charged particles that is being driven away from the sun is known as what?
ANSWER: SOLAR WIND
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The biggest asteroid known is:
W) Vesta
X) Icarus
Y) Ceres
Z) Eros
ANSWER: Y) Ceres
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Rounded to the nearest day, the Mercurian year is equal to:
W) 111 days
X) 88 days
Y) 50 days
Z) 25 days
ANSWER: X) 88 days
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice One of the largest volcanos in our solar system-if not the largest-is named Olympus Mons. This volcano is located on:
W) Jupiter's moon Callisto
X) Venus
Y) Saturn's moon Titan
Z) Mars
ANSWER: Z) Mars
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer One of the largest volcanos in our solar system, if not the largest, is named Olympus Mons. Name the planet which has the volcano.
ANSWER: MARS
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice One Jupiter day is equal to which of the following?
W) 30 hrs 40 min
X) 9 hrs 50 min
Y) 3 hrs 20 min
Z) 52 hrs 10 min
ANSWER: X) 9 hrs 50 min
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The time interval between two successive occurrences of a specific type of alignment of a planet (or the moon) with the sun and the earth is referred to as:
W) a conjunction
X) an opposition
Y) a sidereal period
Z) a synodic period
ANSWER: Z) a synodic period
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer During the period between 1979 and 1998, what is the farthest planet from the sun?
ANSWER: NEPTUNE
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Of the following four times, which one best represents the time it takes energy generated in the core of the sun to reach the surface of the sun and be radiated?
W) Three minutes
X) Thirty days
Y) One thousand years
Z) One million years
ANSWER: Z) One million years
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The sunspot cycle is:
W) 3 years
X) 11 years
Y) 26 years
Z) 49 years
ANSWER: X) 11 years
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Hertzsprung-Russell Diagram of stars DIRECTLY compares what TWO of the following properties of stars: size, temperature, luminosity, density?
ANSWER: TEMPERATURE AND LUMINOSITY
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The andromeda Galaxy is which of the following types of galaxies?
W) elliptical
X) spiral
Y) barred-spiral
Z) irregular
ANSWER: X) spiral
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice About how many light years across is the Milky Way?
W) 1,000
X) 10,000
Y) 100,000
Z) 1,000,000
ANSWER: Y) 100,000
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which unlucky Apollo lunar landing was canceled after an oxygen tank exploded?
ANSWER: APOLLO 13
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What device first proved that Earth rotates on its axis?
ANSWER: FOUCAULT PENDULUM
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which is the smallest planet in our solar system?
ANSWER: MERCURY
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What distinction is Sally K. Ride known for?
ANSWER: FIRST AMERICAN WOMAN IN SPACE
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Only one planet in the solar system has a day which lasts longer than its year. Name this planet.
ANSWER: VENUS
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the heavenly body Charon, that's spelled C-h-a-r-o-n?
ANSWER: PLUTO'S MOON
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Where in space is Cassini's division?
ANSWER: BETWEEN TWO RINGS OF SATURN
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the Vostok 1?
ANSWER: THE FIRST MANNED SPACECRAFT
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Heliocentric means around:
W) Jupiter
X) the Moon
Y) the Sun
Z) Neptune
ANSWER: Y) the Sun
26. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Triton, Neptune's moon, has an ocean made of a liquid. What is this liquid?
ANSWER: NITROGEN
27. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Who was the first man to classify stars according to their brightness?
W) Aristarchus
X) Pythagorus
Y) Copernicus
Z) Hipparchus
ANSWER: Z) Hipparchus
28. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice For what reason was the Schmidt telescope specially built?
W) a sky camera
X) a radio telescope
Y) an optical telescope
Z) a solar telescope
ANSWER: W) a sky camera
29. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the star nearest to the sun?
ANSWER: ALPHA CENTAURI (also accept: PROXIMA CENTAURI)
30. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The greatest distance of a planet from the sun is called what?
W) aphelion
X) perihelion
Y) helix
Z) eccentricity
ANSWER: W) aphelion
31. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to very bright meteors or bolides?
ANSWER: FIREBALLS
32. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice How is the atmospheric pressure of Mars as compared to the atmospheric pressure of the earth?
W) about the same as the earth's
X) about 100 times as great as the earth's
Y) about 1/200th that of the earth's
Z) half as much as that of the earth's
ANSWER: Y) about 1/200th that of the earth's
33. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What gas is the main component of the atmosphere of Mars?
ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE
34. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The planet Jupiter has a mass that is:
W) equal to the combined masses of the earth and Mars
X) equal to the combined masses of Saturn and Pluto
Y) equal to the combined masses of Saturn, Neptune and Uranus
Z) greater than the combined masses of all of the planets
ANSWER: Z) greater than the combined masses of all of the planets
35. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which one of the following moon features is named Copernicus?
W) sea
X) crater
Y) mountain range
Z) rill
ANSWER: X) crater
36. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer On which day of the year does the summer solstice usually occur?
ANSWER: JUNE 21
37. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When the earth is farthest from the sun, what season is it in the Northern Hemisphere?
ANSWER: SUMMER
38. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Of the eight planets, six have one or more natural satellites. Name the only two moonless planets.
ANSWER: VENUS AND MERCURY
39. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The English nickname for this CONSTELLATION is "The Chained Maiden". Give me the astronomer's name for this constellation.
ANSWER: ANDROMEDA
40. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Hercules is a constellation which is commonly viewed in the summer in North America. In which of the seasons is the constellation "Pegasus" normally viewed?
ANSWER: AUTUMN
41. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The brightest star in this constellation is Vega. In which constellation can Vega be found?
ANSWER: LYRA
42. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The brightest star in this constellation is Aldebaran. In which constellation can this star be found?
ANSWER: TAURUS
43. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A binary star system lends itself very well to a determination of the total mass of the binary star system. What TWO observables must be measured to make the total mass determination.
ANSWER: 1) SEPARATION OF THE TWO STARS 2) THE STARS' PERIOD OF REVOLUTION
44. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Orion Nebula is a good example of what kind of nebula?
ANSWER: EMISSION NEBULA
45. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A major component of the interstellar media consists of charged particles which have speeds close to that of light. What are these charged particles called?
ANSWER: COSMIC RAYS
46. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A typical galaxy, such as our Milky Way galaxy, contains how many billion stars?
W) 10 billion
X) 40 billion
Y) 200 billion
Z) 800 billion
ANSWER: Y) 200 billion
47. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What do we call the path in the sky that the sun appears to traverse over the course of a year?
ANSWER: ECLIPTIC
48. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Refracting telescopes always contain which one of the following?
W) mirrors
X) lenses
Y) television systems
Z) film
ANSWER: X) lenses
49. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A comet's tail points in which direction?
W) toward the sun
X) toward the earth
Y) behind the comet in its orbit
Z) away from the sun
ANSWER: Z) away from the sun
50. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Spectral line splitting due to the influence of magnetic fields is called:
W) Boltzmann Effect
X) Zeeman Effect
Y) Planck Effect
Z) Zanstra's Effect
ANSWER: X) Zeeman Effect
51. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term applied to the dark, relatively smooth LUNAR plains whose name is derived from the Latin for "sea"?
ANSWER: MARE (also accept: MARIA)
52. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the collection of small rocky bodies, otherwise known as minor planets, 99.8 percent of whose orbits lie between the orbits of Mars and Jupiter?
ANSWER: ASTEROIDS
53. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Elliptical, Spiral and Irregular are classifications of what type of astronomical systems?
ANSWER: GALAXIES
54. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer I am a type of star with a relatively low effective temperature (3,000 to 4,000 Kelvins), and have a large radius-about 100 times that of the Sun. What am I?
ANSWER: RED GIANT
55. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A 5th magnitude star is how many times brighter than an 8th magnitude star?
ANSWER: 15.85 (also accept: 15.8; 15.9; 16)
56. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is true for BOTH Saturn and Jupiter?
W) both rotate faster than the Earth
X) both rotate slower than the Earth
Y) only one rotates rapidly while the other rotates very slowly
Z) their periods of rotation are linked to their period of revolution
ANSWER: W) both rotate faster than the Earth
57. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is true for ORION? Orion is:
W) the brightest star in the sky
X) a constellation
Y) the name given to a NASA spacecraft
Z) an asteroid
ANSWER: X) a constellation
58. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following men wrote the book "On the Revolutions of the Heavenly Spheres"?
W) Kepler
X) Euclid
Y) Copernicus
Z) Newton
ANSWER: Y) Copernicus
59. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to the configuration when three celestial bodies are in a line?
ANSWER: SYZYGY
60. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Of the planets that circle the Sun, which planet has the most eccentric orbit?
ANSWER: MERCURY
61. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is TRUE for Retrograde motion? Retrograde motion is:
W) caused by epicycles
X) undergone only by superior planets
Y) undergone only by inferior planets
Z) an effect due to the projection of planet orbits onto the sky
ANSWER: Z) an effect due to the projection of planet orbits onto the sky
62. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What name was given to the series of two-man U.S. space missions undertaken in the mid 1960's?
ANSWER: GEMINI
63. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is formed when a massive star, after reaching supergiant status, explodes?
ANSWER: A SUPERNOVA (also accept: TYPE II SUPERNOVA; NEUTRON STAR; BLACK HOLE)
64. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Beads of light visible around the rim of the moon at the beginning and end of a total solar eclipse are called:
W) Lunar Beads
X) Solar Beads
Y) Baily's Beads
Z) Rim Beads
ANSWER: Y) Baily's Beads
65. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Whereas latitude and longitude are the coordinates of places on earth, the coordinates used for star locations are which TWO of the following: ascension, right ascension, altitude, declination, polar angle?
ANSWER: RIGHT ASCENSION AND DECLINATION
66. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which planet is usually the brightest in the sky?
ANSWER: VENUS
67. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the last planet Voyager II visited?
ANSWER: NEPTUNE
68. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The 2.7 Kelvin cosmic background radiation is concentrated in the:
W) radio wavelengths
X) infrared
Y) visible
Z) ultraviolet
ANSWER: W) radio wavelengths
69. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which planets can never be in opposite direction from the Sun in earth's sky?
ANSWER: VENUS AND MERCURY
70. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice If you were watching a star collapsing to form a black hole, the light would disappear because it:
W) is strongly redshifted
X) is strongly blueshifted
Y) its color suddenly becomes black
Z) none of the above
ANSWER: W) is strongly redshifted
71. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which spectral region is it possible for astronomers to observe through clouds?
W) visual
X) radio
Y) ultraviolet
Z) x-ray
ANSWER: X) radio
73. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Magellanic Clouds are
W) irregular galaxies
X) spiral galaxies
Y) elliptical galaxies
Z) large clouds of gas and dust
ANSWER: W) irregular galaxies
74. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The extraterrestrial impact hypothesis of dinosaur extinction is based on finding anomalously high concentrations of a certain element at the geological boundary between the Cretaceous and Tertiary periods. What is the name of this element which was found?
ANSWER: IRIDIUM
75. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Technically, the reflecting power of an object is defined as the ratio of the radiation reflected from an object to the total amount incident upon the object. What term is used in astronomy to indicate the reflecting power of an object?
ANSWER: ALBEDO
76. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The VISUAL aurora consists of luminous arcs, rays or bands in the night sky, usually confined to high latitudes and located in the:
W) troposphere
X) stratosphere
Y) ozonosphere
Z) ionosphere
ANSWER: Z) ionosphere
77. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice This yellow-white, spectral F-type star is located in the constellation Carina south of the ecliptic. It ranks second in apparent brightness among the stars. Is this star:
W) Canopus
X) Alpha Centauri
Y) Castor
Z) Rigel
ANSWER: W) Canopus
78. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the northern latitude constellation that is easily recognized by 5 bright stars forming an irregular "W", some observers seeing not only a W but also a chair. One of the brightest novae on record appeared in this constellation in 1572.
ANSWER: CASSIOPEIA
79. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice When two heavenly bodies occupy the same longitude, the bodies are said to be in:
W) sympathy
X) conjunction
Y) parallel
Z) series
ANSWER: X) conjunction
80. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The study of the origin and evolution of the universe is known as:
W) tomography
X) cystoscopy
Y) cryology
Z) cosmology
ANSWER: Z) cosmology
81. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Penzias and Wilson made what famous discovery which supported the idea that the universe was created by a "big Bang".
ANSWER: BACKGROUND RADIATION RESULTING FROM THE BIG BANG (also accept: PRIMORDIAL FIREBALL RADIATION; 3 K RADIATION)
82. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the English astronomer and professor at Cambridge University who determined the orbit of the then unknown planet Neptune.
ANSWER: (JOHN C.) ADAMS
83. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the two constellations that make up the famous "Great Square of Pegasus".
ANSWER: PEGASUS, ANDROMEDA
84. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer This allotrope of oxygen is formed in the upper atmosphere by the action of ultraviolet radiation from the sun. Name it.
ANSWER: OZONE
85. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice According to Kepler's Laws, all orbits of the planets are:
W) ellipses
X) parabolas
Y) hyperbolas
Z) square
ANSWER: W) ellipses
86. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice According to Kepler's Laws, the cube of the mean distance of a planet from the sun is proportional to the:
W) area that is swept out
X) cube of the period
Y) square of the period
Z) fourth power of the mean distance
ANSWER: Y) square of the period
87. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of visible star is the coolest?
W) O
X) A
Y) G
Z) M
ANSWER: Z) M
88. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which type of star is maintained by the pressure of an electron gas?
W) Main Sequence Star
X) White Dwarf
Y) Neutron Star
Z) Black Hole
ANSWER: X) White Dwarf
89. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto are satellites of what planet?
W) Jupiter
X) Saturn
Y) Neptune
Z) Uranus
ANSWER: W) Jupiter
90. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The universe is estimated to be between ten and twenty billion years old. This estimate is based on the value of which constant?
W) The mass of the Earth
X) The speed of light
Y) The Hubble Constant
Z) The mass of the electron
ANSWER: Y) The Hubble Constant
91. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following first hypothesized that the Earth orbited the sun?
W) Alexander the Great
X) Copernicus
Y) Socrates
Z) Tycho Brahe
ANSWER: X) Copernicus
93. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The cosmic background radiation, a remnant of the Big Bang, is at what temperature?
W) 100K
X) 0K
Y) 5.3K
Z) 2.7K
ANSWER: Z) 2.7K
94. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the outer boundary of the sun's magnetic field?
ANSWER: HELIOPAUSE
95. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Superbubbles and chimneys are both galactic structures theorized to result from what explosive phenomena?
ANSWER: SUPERNOVAS (also accept: NOVAS)
96. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Mercury's year lasts how many Mercury sidereal days?
ANSWER: ONE AND ONE-HALF
