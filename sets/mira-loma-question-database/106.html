Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Place the following terms in the correct order from least specificity to most specificity: Kingdom, Domain, Class, Genus, Order
ANSWER: Domain, Kingdom, Class, Order, Genus
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which two domains of life consist entirely of prokaryotes?
ANSWER: Domain Bacteria and Domain Archaea (also accept: Bacteria and Archaea)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which element has the highest melting point?
ANSWER: Tungsten
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What process describes the movement of water down a potential gradient?
ANSWER: Osmosis
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What law of motion states that an object at rest will remain at rest unless compelled to do so by an outside force?
ANSWER: Newton's First Law of Motion
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer What type of collision occurs when there is no net conversion of kinetic energy into other forms?
ANSWER: Elastic collision
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Find the second derivative of the following function with respect to x: F(x) = 3x3 –27x + 9
ANSWER: F''(x) = 18x
4. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following side lengths of a triangle is impossible?
W) 3, 4, 5
X) 4, 5, 6
Y) 1, 5, 10
Z) 18, 20, 21
ANSWER: Y) 1, 5, 10
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following values is closest to the estimated age of the Earth?
W) 2.5 billion years
X) 3.5 billion years
Y) 4.5 billion years
Z) 5.5 billion years
ANSWER: Y) 4.5 billion years
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Place the following atmospheric gases in order from the highest percentage composition of the Earth's dry atmosphere to the lowest: Argon, Carbon Dioxide, Oxygen, Nitrogen, Ozone
ANSWER: Nitrogen, Oxygen, Argon, Carbon Dioxide, Ozone
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What is the most widely used form of renewable energy today?
W) Hydroelectric
X) Geothermal
Y) Wind
Z) Solar
ANSWER: W) Hydroelectric
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the mechanism that slows the process that creates a product when an excess of that product is present?
ANSWER: Negative feedback
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What term describes the structural change in a macromolecule caused by extreme conditions?
ANSWER: Denaturation
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Four atoms covalently bonded to a central atom have what type of structure?
ANSWER: tetrahedral
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What element is produced in alpha decay?
ANSWER: helium
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What term describes the tendency of a force to rotate an object about an axis, fulcrum, or pivot?
ANSWER: torque
9. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following does NOT affect the magnitude of torque?
W) Applied force
X) Length of lever arm
Y) Angle between force vector and lever arm
Z) Velocity
ANSWER: Z) Velocity
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice All numbers divisible by 17 are also divisible by which of the following values?
W) 31
X) 34
Y) 37
Z) 39
ANSWER: X) 34
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the value of the eccentricity of a circle?
ANSWER: Zero
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In astronomy, the metallicity of an object describes the proportion of its matter composed of chemical elements other than what two elements?
ANSWER: Hydrogen and Helium
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following stellar populations from greatest metallicity to lowest: Population III, Population I, Population II
ANSWER: Population I, Population II, Population III
12. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Methane produced by the anaerobic digestion of organic matter by anaerobes that is used as fuel is known as what?
ANSWER: biogas
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the process by which a signal on a cell's surface is converted to a specific cellular response in a series of steps?
ANSWER: signal transduction pathway
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is a physical or chemical agent that induces mutations in a living organism?
ANSWER: mutagen
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is a term that describes the weakly charged regions of positive and negative charge that can occur when atoms and molecules are in close relation to each other?
ANSWER: Van der Waals interactions
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What term describes the phenomenon where hydrogen bonds hold water molecules together?
ANSWER: cohesion
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following nuclear weapon designs involves the firing of one piece of fissile uranium towards another piece of fissile uranium to achieve critical mass?
W) Gun Assembly
X) Tellar-Uram
Y) Implosion
Z) Fusion-boosted fission
ANSWER: W) Gun Assembly
15. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Pure fusion nuclear weapons, should they ever be developed, would likely have which of the following effects?
W) Elimination of radioactive fallout and elimination of neutron emission
X) Greater radioactive fallout and elimination of neutron emission
Y) Elimination of nuclear fallout and greater neutron emission
Z) Greater radioactive fallout and greater neutron emission
ANSWER: Y) Elimination of nuclear fallout and greater neutron emission
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Surface acoustic waves that travel on solids and are produced on the Earth's surface during Earthquakes are called what?
ANSWER: Rayleigh Waves
16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following seismic waves is likely to be the most destructive?
W) P Waves
X) S Waves
Y) Rayleigh Waves
Z) Surface Waves
ANSWER: Z) Surface Waves
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What condition is marked by more than two sets of homologous chromosomes?
ANSWER: polyploidy
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer What genetic disease is marked by trisomy of chromosome 21?
ANSWER: Down Syndrome
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What law states that each pair of alleles segregates into gametes independently of the pair of alleles for any other gene/
ANSWER: Law of Independent Assortment
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer In what type of dominance are both phenotypes expressed for a specific gene?
ANSWER: codominance
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What principle states that the frequencies of alleles and genotypes in a population will remain constant from generation to generation, provided that only Mendelian segregation and recombination of alleles are at work?
ANSWER: Hardy-Weinberg principle
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which form of light microscopy involves tagging the specimen with fluorescent dyes or antibodies?
ANSWER: Fluorescent microscopy
