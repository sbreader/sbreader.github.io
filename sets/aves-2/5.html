1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In hemoglobin, the binding of oxygen gas to one of four binding sites increases the affinity of oxygen gas in the remaining sites. What type of allosteric activation does hemoglobin exhibit?
ANSWER: Cooperativity (also accept: Cooperative binding)
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer After the secretion of antidiuretic hormone into the bloodstream, order the following three veins that this hormone will encounter from first to last 1) Renal vein in the kidney 2) Jugular vein in the neck 3) Pulmonary vein in the chest
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many ways are there to travel from the top-left box to the bottom-right box of a grid with 4 rows and 5 columns of boxes, if you may only move 1 box to the right or 1 box down at each step?
ANSWER: 35
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Gaurav is analyzing the vector-valued function r(t) = ti + t2 j + t3 k. What is the value of r'(t) · r''(t) at t = 1?
ANSWER: 22
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Two moles of an ideal gas are held in a rigid container at 300 kilopascals of pressure. If the pressure is increased to 500 kilopascals, in joules, how much work does the gas do on its surroundings?
ANSWER: 0
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice During a typical iodometric titration, I3– and S2O32– ions react, yielding I– and S4O62– ions. Which of the following statements about this reaction is NOT true?
W) I3– is reduced to form I–
X) The reaction is spontaneous under standard conditions
Y) The oxidation state of sulfur decreases
Z) The reaction reduction potential is positive
ANSWER: Y) The oxidation state of sulfur decreases

ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The predominantly organic-matter composition of Histosols traps minerals and clay in the O horizon. Identify all of the following three soil horizons that would be underdeveloped in Histosols 1) E horizon 2) B horizon 3) C horizon
ANSWER: 1 and 2
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Ziang tosses two balls off of a cliff, each at 4 meters per second, with one ball thrown at 45 degrees above the horizontal and the other at 30 degrees below the horizontal. W) Greater than the second ball
X) Less than the second ball
Y) Equal to the second ball
Z) Depends on the height from which they were thrown
ANSWER: Y) Equal to the second ball

9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Reflecting and refracting telescopes both experience what optical phenomenon in which light rays passing through the edges of a lens or reflecting off the edges of a mirror focus at different points than those near the center, producing a blurred image?
ANSWER: Spherical aberration (do not accept: Chromatic aberration; aberration)
