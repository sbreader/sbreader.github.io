1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Materials that exhibit different physical properties on different crystal planes are described as having what property?
W) Piezoelectricity
X) Polymorphosity
Y) Heterogeneity
Z) Anisotropy
ANSWER: Z) Anisotropy
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Although the carbon-carbon bond in ethane rotates freely at room temperature, the bond in ethylene does not. Which of the following best explains this phenomenon?
W) Hydrogen atoms on adjacent carbon atoms repel each other
X) The two regions of the carbon-carbon pi bond repel each other
Y) Rotating the carbon-carbon sigma bond creates a significant energy barrier
Z) Rotation of the carbon-carbon pi bond creates a significant energy barrier
ANSWER: Z) Rotation of the carbon-carbon pi bond creates a significant energy barrier
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An engineer wants to calculate the strength of the magnets required on a maglev train, which passes over metallic loops on the track to create a changing magnetic flux. What law should the engineer use for their calculation?
ANSWER: Faraday’s law of induction (also accept: Faraday; Lenz’s law) (do not accept: Faraday effect)
