1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In the process of meiosis, which of the following is the end product?
W) One parent cell becomes two daughter cells
X) One parent cell becomes four daughter cells
Y) Two parent cells become one daughter cell
Z) Two parent cells become two daughter cells
ANSWER: X) One parent cell becomes four daughter cells
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following terms is used to describe a change in cell structure and function from generalized to specialized?
W) Differentiation
X) Development
Y) Morphogenesis
Z) Growth
ANSWER: W) Differentiation
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Simplify the following expression: (2x2) squared.
ANSWER: 4x4
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements regarding quadratic equations with real coefficients that are universally true 1) When the discriminant is zero, the roots are real 2) When the discriminant is positive, the roots are equal 3) When the discriminant is positive, the roots are imaginary.
ANSWER: 1
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice An electrical generator converts:
W) Solar energy to electric energy
X) Thermal energy to electric energy
Y) Chemical energy to electric energy
Z) Mechanical energy to electric energy
ANSWER: Z) Mechanical energy to electric energy
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three processes that are considered phase changes 1) Sodium reacting with water and producing hydrogen gas 2) Gold melting at high temperatures 3) Carbon dioxide depositing to dry ice at low temperatures.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The objects that Carl Seyfert observed are examples of what?
W) Variable stars
X) Galaxies
Y) Filaments
Z) Clusters
ANSWER: X) Galaxies
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Ablation by sunlight would primarily affect which of the following objects?
W) Pluton
X) Volcano
Y) Glacier
Z) Thunderstorm
ANSWER: Y) Glacier
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term used to describe the dark, smooth areas of the moon, previously thought to be bodies of water?
ANSWER: MARIA (also accept: MARE)
5. BONUS Energy Short Answer Magnetrons are generally found in which home device?
ANSWER: MICROWAVE OVENS
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Identify all of the following three numbers that are rational 1) 6/9 2) (√8) divided by (√2) 3) (√81) divided by 5
ANSWER: ALL OF THEM
6. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Anna buys used books for x dollars per dozen and sells them for x/9 dollars per book. If she buys the books for $72 per dozen, what is her profit per book?
W) 1 dollar
X) 2 dollars
Y) 3 dollars
Z) 4 dollars
ANSWER: X) 2 dollars
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A high-speed electron ejected from a nucleus during radioactive decay is referred to as what?
ANSWER: BETA PARTICLE (also accept: BETA MINUS PARTICLE)
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A coin weighs 3.5 grams and when placed in water, displaces a volume of 0.50 milliliters. To two significant figures, what is the density of the coin in grams per milliliter?
ANSWER: 7.0
8. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What state produces the most coal in the U.S.?
W) California
X) Wyoming
Y) Texas
Z) Iowa
ANSWER: X) Wyoming
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer What type of energy generation involves the usage of radioactive actinides?
ANSWER: NUCLEAR
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the term for igneous activity that occurs below the Earth's surface?
W) Plutonic
X) Extrusive
Y) Unconformed
Z) Conformed
ANSWER: W) Plutonic
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a catastrophic explosion of a massive star?
ANSWER: SUPERNOVA
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a vascular plant?
W) Spanish moss
X) Oak tree
Y) Green algae
Z) Fern lichen
ANSWER: X) Oak tree
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer What structural polysaccharide is found in fungal cell walls and the exoskeletons of insects?
ANSWER: CHITIN
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice While there are over 300 species of sharks in the ocean, approximately what percentage are "vicious" or are known to attack unprovoked?
W) 2
X) 5
Y) 10
Z) 25
ANSWER: W) 2
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer To the nearest whole foot, how many feet does light travel in one nanosecond?
ANSWER: ONE
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What two planets in the Solar System do not have any moons?
ANSWER: MERCURY AND VENUS
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of rocks hosts the best-preserved fossils?
W) Conglomerate
X) Sandstone
Y) Breccia
Z) Black shale
ANSWER: Z) Black shale
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Growth of plants in the ocean is generally limited by which of the following?
W) Salt and sunlight
X) Oxygen and carbon
Y) Nitrogen and phosphorus
Z) Hydrogen and sulfur
ANSWER: Y) Nitrogen and phosphorus
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer Rectification is the conversion of alternating current to direct current. What process converts direct current to alternating current?
ANSWER: INVERSION
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following organisms that are considered autotrophs 1) plants 2) cyanobacteria 3) humans.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2 (also accept: PLANTS AND CYANOBACTERIA)
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of molecules are secreted by the endocrine system?
ANSWER: HORMONES
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the molar volume, to the nearest tenth of a liter, of an ideal gas at standard pressure?
ANSWER: 22.4
15. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The chemical behavior of an atom is determined by its:
W) Atomic number
X) Nuclear binding energy
Y) Mass number
Z) Number of isotopes
ANSWER: W) Atomic number
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A circle has a circumference of 6π. What is its radius?
ANSWER: 3
16. BONUS Math Short Answer Given that f = -1, g = 7, and h = -10, what is h2 + fgh?
ANSWER: 170
17. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Coal can be burned to produce energy. It can also be chemically combined with oxygen or air in a more controlled way that yields lower harmful gas emissions. What is this alternative method of processing coal for energy called?
ANSWER: COAL GASIFICATION (also accept: GASIFICATION)
17. BONUS Energy Short Answer In geologic sequestration, carbon dioxide is often pumped back into the ground to replace other substances that are being removed. Identify all of the following replacements that are currently taking place 1) CO2 replaces methane gas 2) CO2 replaces crude oil 3) CO2 replaces potable water.
ANSWER: 1 and 2
18. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Laura wants to find the distance between two cities. The distance on a map is 18 inches. The scale on the map is 3 inches to 5 miles. How many miles is it between the two cities?
W) 5 miles
X) 15 miles
Y) 30 miles
Z) 40 miles
ANSWER: Y) 30 miles
18. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area of a trapezoid with the following properties: its altitude is 12, and it has parallel sides of length 25 and 50, with nonparallel opposite sides of length 15 and 20?

W) Changes state
X) Gains or loses electrons
Y) Reaches its boiling point

ANSWER: PLANCK'S CONSTANT

ANSWER: INTERSTITIAL FLUID (also accept: EXTRACELLULAR FLUID)
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer What parts of plants, located at the tips of the roots and shoots, contain cells that are actively growing in length?
ANSWER: MERISTEMS
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What graduated piece of glassware is used for delivering known volume of a liquid, especially during titrations?
ANSWER: BURETTE
