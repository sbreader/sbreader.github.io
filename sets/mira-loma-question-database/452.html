Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name of the atmosphere phenomenon in which temperatures in the Eastern Pacific Ocean lessen and the amount of rainfall rises?
W) Southern Pacific Climate Oscillation
X) Eastern Pacific Climate Oscillation
Y) El Niño
Z) La Niña
ANSWER: Z) La Niña
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which two of the following four is typical of a standard El Niño? 1: Rise in pressure over the Indian Ocean 2: Rise in pressure over the eastern Pacific Ocean 3: Droughts in the western Pacific 4: Extensive rainfall in the Indian Ocean
ANSWER: 1 and 3
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the most geologically active object in the Solar System?
ANSWER: IO
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following Solar System objects from largest to smallest: Mercury, Mars, the Moon, Ganymede.
ANSWER: MARS, GANYMEDE, MERCURY, THE MOON
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the country with the most wetland area in the world and contains over one quarter of the world's wetlands?
W) Russia
X) Canada
Y) Brazil
Z) Sweden
ANSWER: X) Canada
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of a type of wetland that consists of acidic, organic, dead plant material including mosses and lichens?
ANSWER: BOG (also accept: QUAGMIRE; MIRE)
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the longest canyon in the solar system that is approximately 2500 miles in length?
ANSWER: VALLES MARINERIS (also accept: MARINER VALLEY)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a basin or depression that occurs in a glaciated valley due to ice being buried in the ground and then melting?
W) Drumlin
X) Kettle
Y) Esker
Z) Kame
ANSWER: X) Kettle
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the ridge formed by two valley glaciers when they coalesce?
ANSWER: MEDIAL MORAINE
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a type of solar oscillation?
W) P-mode, or acoustic
X) G-mode, or gravity
Y) S-mode, or standing
Z) F-mode, or surface gravity
ANSWER: Y) S-mode, or standing
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name of the area between the sun's core and the sun's convective zone?
W) Radiative zone
X) Chromosphere
Y) Photosphere
Z) Electromagnetic zone
ANSWER: W) Radiative zone
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In gemology, which of the following is NOT something that you can use to identify a gem and its components?
W) Refractive Index
X) Luster
Y) Specific Gravity
Z) Spectroscopy
ANSWER: X) Luster
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following four minerals is harder than Quartz? 1: Topaz 2: Apatite 3: Orthoclase 4: Corundum
ANSWER: 1 and 4
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Big Dipper is an example of what type of astronomical pattern of stars?
ANSWER: ASTERISM (do not accept: CONSTELLATION)
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following four constellations is visible in the Northern Hemisphere during September? 1: Taurus 2: Capricornus 3: Gemini 4: Aquarius
ANSWER: 2 and 4
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following stages of the hydrologic cycle is the movement of water through the atmosphere and is responsible for the movement of evaporated ocean water to land?
W) Transpiration
X) Condensation
Y) Advection
Z) Precipitation
ANSWER: Y) Advection
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice On average, how long would it take for any given water molecule in the Earth's atmosphere to condense and precipitate to the earth?
W) About an hour
X) About a day
Y) About ten days
Z) About a month
ANSWER: Y) About ten days
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following elements is NOT formed in a star after all of its hydrogen is depleted due to fusion?
W) Lithium-6
X) Beryllium-8
Y) Carbon-12
Z) Oxygen-16
ANSWER: W) Lithium-6
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of star could have a spectral type of G5 and a luminosity equal to 100 times the sun's? Give both its name and its luminosity class (II, III, IV, etc.).
ANSWER: CLASS III GIANT STAR
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the visible phenomenon that occurs in the Antarctic regions due to solar flares and geomagnetic storms?
ANSWER: AURORA
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The OUTER Van Allen radiation belt contains most of which of the following subatomic particles?
W) neutrinos
X) photons
Y) protons
Z) electrons
ANSWER: Z) electrons
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name of the type of crescent-shaped dune where its tips point downwind?
W) Parabolic
X) Moon-shaped
Y) Transverse
Z) Barchan
ANSWER: Z) Barchan
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the type of dune created when wind directions are highly variable?
ANSWER: STAR DUNES
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of large accumulations of windblown silt, most commonly found in China?
ANSWER: LOESS
