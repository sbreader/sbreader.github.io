Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the point group symmetry of the water molecule?
W) Td
X) C2v
Y) D3h
Z) C2h
ANSWER: X) C2v
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What scientist won the Nobel Prize in Chemistry in 1936 for his study of dipole moments?
W) Willem Keesom
X) Fritz London
Y) Peter Debye
Z) Johannes van der Waals
ANSWER: Y) Peter Debye
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify which of the following statements are true. I. Bonds involving a highly charged cation will be primarily covalent. II. Bonds involving an extremely small cation will be primarily ionic. III. Bonds involving an extremely large anion will be primarily covalent.
ANSWER: I AND III
4. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What is the scientific method of dating using tree rings?
W) branchronology
X) dendrochronology
Y) arborchronology
Z) botanochronology
ANSWER: X) dendrochronology
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Who won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921 for his work with the photoelectric effect?
ANSWER: ALBERT EINSTEIN
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What unit measures the electric dipole moment and is equivalent to 1 × 10-18 statcoulombcentimeters?
ANSWER: DEBYE
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What effect describes the phenomenon of electrons being emitted from matter as a consequence of their absorption of energy from electromagnetic radiation?
ANSWER: PHOTOELECTRIC EFFECT
8. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What order is the chimaera fish part of?
ANSWER: CHIMAERIFORMES
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What substance is used as a hexadentate ligand, and is also sometimes used to dissolve limescale?
W) EDTA
X) CFC
Y) DIBAL
Z) DMSO
ANSWER: W) EDTA
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What set of rules, developed in 1923, are used to predict the ionic and covalent character of a bond based on the relative charges and sizes of the cation and anion?
ANSWER: FAJANS' RULES
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term given to an ion or molecule that binds to a central metal atom to form a coordination complex?
ANSWER: LIGAND
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many planes of reflection does the water molecule have?
ANSWER: 2
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify which of the following statements are true. I. Bonds involving a highly charged cation will have a lot of covalent character. II. Bonds involving an extremely small cation will have little to no covalent character. III. Bonds involving an extremely large anion will have a lot of covalent character.
ANSWER: I AND III
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the cartilaginous fish known informally as the ghost shark, ratfish, spookfish or rabbitfish?
ANSWER: CHIMAERA
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What features of a tree, seen in a horizontal cross section of a cut through the trunk of the tree, are the result of new growth in the vascular cambium, a lateral meristem, and are synonymous with secondary growth?
ANSWER: GROWTH RINGS (also accept: TREE RINGS; ANNUAL RINGS)
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Name four noble metals.
ANSWER: ANY 4 OF: RUTHENIUM, RHODIUM, PALLADIUM, SILVER, OSMIUM, IRIDIUM, PLATINUM, GOLD
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the most expensive?
W) gold
X) silver
Y) platinum
Z) rhodium
ANSWER: Z) rhodium
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the term used to reference the emission of short bursts of light from imploding bubbles in a liquid when excited by sound?
ANSWER: SONOLUMINESCENCE
22. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What element, gifted to him by Marie Curie, did Ernest Rutherford use as an emitter of alpha particles in his famous Gold Foil Experiment?
W) polonium
X) radium
Y) bismuth
Z) uranium
ANSWER: X) radium
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the method of separating mixtures based on their differences in boiling points?
ANSWER: DISTILLATION
24. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which scientist, author of Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica, is known as one of the fathers of differential calculus?
W) Gottfried Leibniz
X) Albert Einstein
Y) Isaac Newton
Z) Rene Descartes
ANSWER: Y) Isaac Newton
25. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What German chemist is often referred to as the "Father of Biochemistry" for coining the term "biochemistry" in 1903?
ANSWER: CARL NEUBERG
