1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following species thrives in a mangrove?
W) Blue crabs
X) Sea turtles
Y) Sea horses
Z) Alligators
ANSWER: Z) Alligators
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Flow cytometry is emerging as a valuable tool for quantifying photosynthetic cells in the environment. What property of photosynthetic cells is exploited in flow cytometry?
ANSWER: FLUORESCENCE
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name of the collection of galaxies that contains the Milky Way, M31, M33 and the Magellanic Clouds?
W) The Local Group
X) The Horsehead Nebula
Y) The extended milky way
Z) The Virgo cluster
ANSWER: W) The Local Group
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A neutrally charged atom has an atomic number of 11 and a mass number of 23. Identify the number of protons, neutrons, and electrons.
ANSWER: PROTONS: 11; NEUTRONS: 12; ELECTRONS: 11
3. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Windows can be rated on the rate at which heat flows through them through which of the following measures?
W) E-factor
X) W- factor
Y) U-factor
Z) H-factor
ANSWER: Y) U-factor
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the major end product of glycolysis?
ANSWER: PYRUVATE (also accept: PYRUVIC ACID)

W) Axillae

Y) Groin

ANSWER: DUODENUM, JEJUNUM, ILEUM (also accept: in any order)
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Mid-ocean ridge valleys are lined with what kind of faults?
ANSWER: NORMAL (also accept: DIP-SLIP)

X) Greatest elongation
Y) Inferior conjunction
