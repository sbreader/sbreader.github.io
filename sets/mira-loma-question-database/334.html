Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number indicate which of the following 3 will most often increase gene transcription 1) Methylation 2) Acetylation 3) Adding phosphate groups
ANSWER: 2, 3
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which enzyme in the epinephrine signal transduction pathway cleaves glycogen into glucose-1-phosphate?
W) G protein
X) Protein kinase A
Y) Phosphorylase Kinase
Z) Glycogen phosphorylase
ANSWER: Z) Glycogen phosphorylase
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which law states that for a fixed amount of gas at constant temperature, volume is inversely proportional to pressure?
ANSWER: Boyle's
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the approximate molar mass of glucose?
ANSWER: 180 grams
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Two charges are separated by distance d. If the distance d between the charges is doubled, the electric potential is
W) quadrupled
X) doubled
Y) unchanged
Z) halved
ANSWER: Z) halved
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer A man runs thirty meters east in 5 seconds and then forty meters north in five seconds. What is his average velocity?
ANSWER: 5 meters/ second
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which comet, dubbed the great comet of 1997, holds the record of 18 months for being visible to the unaided eye?
ANSWER: Hale-Bopp
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the approximate deepest level of earthquakes?
W) 300 km
X) 500 km
Y) 700 km
Z) 900 km
ANSWER: Y) 700 km
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which law states that if two systems are in thermal equilibrium with a third system, they must be in thermal equilibrium with each other?
ANSWER: Zeroth law of thermodynamics
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer How much heat in calories is needed to raise 10 grams of water from 75 to 85 degrees Celsius?
ANSWER: 100
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the haploid number of humans?
ANSWER: 23
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer A gene pool is undergoing Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium for a train governed by Mendelian genetics. If the frequency of the dominant allele is 0.8, give the percent of the popular expected to be homozygous dominant, heterozygous, and homozygous recessive respectively.
ANSWER: 64, 32, 4
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer An arc is 60 degrees in a circle with a diameter of 18 inches. What is the area? Give your answer as a fraction with pi as the constant.
ANSWER: 81/6 pi
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Using name or number denote all the following which affect the reaction rate. 1) Concentration of reactants 2) Temperature 3) Presence of a catalyst 4) Physical state of reactants
ANSWER: all
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The equilibrium constant for H2 (g) + I2 (g) double arrows 2HI (g) is 2.5. What is the equilibrium constant, given as a fraction or decimal to the tenth, for 4HI (g) double arrows 2H2 (g) + 2I2 (g)?
ANSWER: 0.16
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A man pushes a ten kg box a constant velocity over 4 meters. The coefficient of kinetic friction between the box and the floor is 0.3. How much work does the person do?
W) 12J
X) 40 J
Y) 75 J
Z) 120 J
ANSWER: Z) 120 J
10. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A forklift lifts a 100 kg crate at a constant velocity to a height of 8 m over 4 seconds. The forklift holds the crate in place for twenty seconds. How much power in watts does the forklift exert?
W) 0
X) 2.0 × 103
Y) 3.2 × 103
Z) 2.0 × 104
ANSWER: X) 2.0 × 103
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The sun is most likely a what generation star?
W) first
X) 2nd
Y) 3 rd
Z) 4th
ANSWER: Y) 3 rd
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which mineral tends to form first in the Bowen series?
W) olivine
X) muscovite
Y) plagioclase
Z) biotite
ANSWER: W) olivine
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is false?
W) ATP in a cell yields -7.3 kcal per mole of free energy.
X) A cell is about 40% efficient.
Y) Enzymes can lower activation energy.
Z) Enzymes can speed up the rate of reaction without being consumed.
ANSWER: W) ATP in a cell yields -7.3 kcal per mole of free energy.
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Using name/ number, denote all of the following which enzymes can do to speed up a reaction 1) act as a template for substrate orientation 2) stabilize transition state 3) provide favorable microenvironment 4) participate directly in the reaction.
ANSWER: all
