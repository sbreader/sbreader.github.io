1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice On the Mohs scale, which mineral has a hardness of 6?
W) calcite
X) potassium feldspar
Y) topaz
Z) corundum
ANSWER: X) potassium feldspar
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals can calcite scratch?
W) apatite
X) quartz
Y) gypsum
Z) topaz
ANSWER: Y) gypsum
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In what type of fracture do minerals break in a glasslike manner, resulting in smooth, curved surfaces?
ANSWER: CONCHOIDAL
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Of the following minerals, which possess the property of cleavage: Quartz, fluorite, halite, calcite.
ANSWER: FLUORITE, HALITE, CALCITE
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When quartz contains impurities that give it a purple color, what is it called?
ANSWER: AMETHYST
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The appearance or quality of light reflected from the surface of a mineral, luster, is divided into what two types?
ANSWER: METALLIC AND NONMETALLIC
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a non-silicate mineral?
W) feldspar
X) muscovite
Y) hornblende
Z) halite
ANSWER: Z) halite
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals is a sulfide?
W) chalcopyrite
X) sylvite
Y) dolomite
Z) hematite
ANSWER: W) chalcopyrite
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which minerals make up the most common mineral group, the silicates?
ANSWER: SILICON AND OXYGEN
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the science that deals with the dynamics of the ocean?
ANSWER: OCEANOGRAPHY
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The oceans account for what percentage of the Earth's total water supply?
W) 75%
X) 89%
Y) 94%
Z) 97%
ANSWER: Z) 97%
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is a common boundary where different parts of a system come in contact and interact?
W) system boundary
X) interface
Y) conjunction
Z) juxtaposition
ANSWER: X) interface
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The earth consists of which four major spheres?
ANSWER: ATMOSPHERE, BIOSPHERE, HYDROSPHERE, SOLID EARTH
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the three principal divisions of the solid earth?
ANSWER: CRUST, MANTLE, CORE
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the thickest part of the mantle?
W) asthenosphere
X) lithosphere
Y) mesosphere
Z) upper mantle
ANSWER: Y) mesosphere
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the two divisions of body waves?
ANSWER: P WAVES AND S WAVES
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of body wave compresses and pulls rocks in the direction the wave is traveling?
ANSWER: P WAVE
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which body wave cannot travel through fluids?
ANSWER: S WAVES
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the place within the earth where earthquake waves originate?
ANSWER: FOCUS
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the location on the surface directly above the origin of an earthquake?
ANSWER: EPICENTER
13. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What are solitary dunes shaped like crescents and with their tips pointing downwards called?
W) star dunes
X) barchan dunes
Y) longitudinal dunes
Z) transverse dunes
ANSWER: X) barchan dunes
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the leeward slope of a dune called?
ANSWER: SLIP FACE
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are deposits of windblown silt known as?
ANSWER: LOESS
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of weathering physically breaks up rock into smaller pieces?
ANSWER: MECHANICAL WEATHERING
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of weathering alters a rock's chemistry, changing it into a different substance?
ANSWER: CHEMICAL WEATHERING
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Talus slopes are due to what mechanical weathering process?
ANSWER: FROST WEDGING
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the controlling force of mass wasting?
ANSWER: GRAVITY
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the layer of rock and mineral fragments produced by weathering called?
W) loam
X) clay
Y) regolith
Z) humus
ANSWER: Y) regolith
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following soil horizons from bottom to top: O, E, A, C, B
ANSWER: C, B, E, A, O
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which generic soil type is characterized by an accumulation of calcium carbonate?
W) pedalfer
X) laterite
Y) pedocal
Z) regolith
ANSWER: Y) pedocal
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Pedalfers are characterized by an accumulation of which two elements?
ANSWER: ALUMINUM AND IRON
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which soil horizon is largely made of mineral matter and generally has humus present?
W) O
X) A
Y) E
Z) B
ANSWER: X) A
19. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which two soil horizons make up topsoil?
W) E, B
X) B, C
Y) A, E
Z) O, A
ANSWER: Z) O, A
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the small earthquakes that precede the major ones called?
ANSWER: FORESHOCKS
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following waves in order of earliest to latest detection: surface waves, P waves, S waves.
ANSWER: P WAVES, S WAVES, SURFACE WAVES
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which part of the mantle is broken into plates?
ANSWER: LITHOSPHERE
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which part of the mantle does the lithosphere move around on?
ANSWER: ASTHENOSPHERE
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following magmas by increasing silica content: granitic, andesitic, basaltic.
ANSWER: BASALTIC, ANDESITIC, GRANITIC
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following magmas by decreasing viscosity: basaltic, granitic, andesitic.
ANSWER: GRANITIC, ANDESITIC, BASALTIC
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to lava flows that form a relatively smooth skin that wrinkles as the still-molten subsurface lava continues to advance?
ANSWER: PAHOEHOE FLOWS
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to the basaltic lava flow that has a surface of sharp edges and spiny projections?
ANSWER: AA
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Lapilli are usually of a size closest to which of the following items?
W) baseball
X) walnut
Y) peanut
Z) basketball
ANSWER: X) walnut
24. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Volcanoes that have unusually large summit depressions that exceed 1 km in diameter are called
W) craters
X) vents
Y) calderas
Z) fumaroles
ANSWER: Y) calderas
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the fraction of the total radiation that is reflected by a surface called?
ANSWER: ALBEDO
25. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of cloud is an amorphous layer of dark gray clouds and is one of the chief precipitation-producing clouds?
W) stratus
X) cumulonimbus
Y) stratocumulus
Z) nimbostratus
ANSWER: Z) nimbostratus
