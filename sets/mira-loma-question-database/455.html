1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is commonly referred to as the oldest living lineage of vertebrates?
W) Conodonts
X) Gnathostomes
Y) Petromyzontida
Z) Chondrichthyes
ANSWER: Y) Petromyzontida
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, which of the following four statements is or are true of all conodonts? 1: The entire class of Conodonts are postulated to have been wiped out by the Permian-Triassic extinction event. 2: The feeding apparati found in conodonts are radically different from the jaws of modern animals. 3: Conodonts are widely agreed to have had large eyes, fins with fin rays, and a notochord. 4: Conodonts are considered to be closest to the modern day chondrichthyes.
ANSWER: 2 and 3
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In this phase of liquid crystal, the calamitic or rod-shaped organic molecules have no positional order but are staggered along their long axes.
W) Choleristic phase
X) Smectic phase
Y) Chiral phase
Z) Nematic phase
ANSWER: Z) Nematic phase
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, which of the following four statements is or are true of liquid crystals? 1: Thermotropic liquid crystals are created by melting the solid phase. 2: Lyotropic liquid crystals are composed of both organic and inorganic molecules. 3: Anisotropic materials have properties that do not depend on the direction of measurement. 4: Thermotropic liquid crystals are almost always found in the smectic phase.
ANSWER: 1 only
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Calculate the wavelength of a sound wave moving through air in 20 degrees Celsius if its frequency is .25 kHz and if the speed of sound at this temperature is 350 meters per second.
ANSWER: 1.4 METERS
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Three fair four-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that the sum of these dice will be exactly equal to 7?
ANSWER: 3/16 (also accept: 6/64)
8. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Three fair seven-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that the sum of these dice will be exactly equal to 15?
W) 4/49
X) 9/49
Y) 27/343
Z) 64/343
ANSWER: W) 4/49
9. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the Gibbs free energy of a system with an enthalpy of -302 Joules, an entropy of -17 Joules per kelvin, and a temperature of 115 Kelvin?
ANSWER: 1653 JOULES
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which of the following four statements about thermodynamics is or are false? 1: A reaction is said to be exergonic if its Gibbs free energy is negative. 2: A reaction is said to be nonspontaneous if it is endergonic. 3: A reaction is said to be exothermic and adiabatic if its internal temperature rises and if its enthalpy is positive. 4: A reaction is said to be endothermic and adiabatic if its internal temperature lessens and if its enthalpy is positive.
ANSWER: 3 only
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the most likely path of stellar evolution for a star of one-half solar mass on the H-R diagram, according to the red giant branch?
W) Upper-left to upper-right
X) Upper-right to lower-right
Y) Lower-right to lower-left
Z) Greatly varies
ANSWER: X) Upper-right to lower-right
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, which of the following four statements is or are FALSE regarding stellar evolution? 1: Hypernovae are explosions with energies of over 1000 supernovae. 2: Wolf-Rayet stars are extremely bright, blue stars that are incredibly rare. 3: Red supergiants will start at the upper-right of the H-R diagram, then move to the left as they evolve. 4: Pollux, Polaris, and Vega are in the main sequence of stars.
ANSWER: 1, 2, and 4 (also accept: All but 3)
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which of the following is the thin-walled, expanded portion of the alimentary tract used for the storage of food prior to digestion that is found in most gastropods, birds, and insects?
ANSWER: CROP
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, which of the following four statements is or are TRUE about the anatomy of an earthworm? 1: The pharynx in an earthworm serves to pulverize food before digestion. 2: The gizzard is the first organ in the alimentary canal to begin digestion. 3: The typhlosole in an earthworm serves to increase the surface area for nutrient absorption. 4: The crop is not found in the earthworm, but instead in insects and birds.
ANSWER: 2 and 3
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following elements in order of INCREASING electronegativities: technetium, aluminum, fluorine, potassium, osmium.
ANSWER: 4, 2, 1, 5, 3
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, which of the following four statements is or are correct about periodic trends? 1: Polarizabilities decrease from left to right along a period and increase down a group. 2: Electronegativities increase from left to right along a period and decrease down a group. 3: Ionization energies increase from left to right along a period and decrease down a group. 4: Out of carbon, aluminum, and germanium, germanium will have the highest polarizability.
ANSWER: all of them
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the approximate index of refraction of a material if its K is approximately equal to 16, assuming its relative permeability is equal to unity?
W) 2
X) 4
Y) 16
Z) 64
ANSWER: X) 4
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer By name or number, which of the following three statements is or are FALSE regarding optics? 1: The index of refraction, n, of a material, is equal to the square root of the material's K 2: The wave speed, v, of a material is equal to the inverse of the square root of its relative permeability times its relative dielectric constant. 3: In a vacuum, the relative permeability and relative dielectric constant are equal to one.
ANSWER: 1 and 2
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the fourth term of the eighth row of Pascal's triangle?
ANSWER: 35 (do not accept: 56)
20. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the fifth term of (2x+3y)5.
ANSWER: 810xy4
21. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Rank the following countries by INCREASING nuclear energy production: Japan, South Korea, the UK, the USA, and France.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1, 5, 4
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is known as the boundary between the Earth's crust and the mantle that separates the oceanic and continental crust from the underlying mantle?
ANSWER: MOHO DISCONTINUITY
24. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, which of the following four statements is or are TRUE about the Moho Discontinuity? 1: The Moho discontinuity lies between 20 to 50 kilometers below the ocean floor and 35 to 130 kilometers beneath typical continents. 2: It is suspected that the Moho is different in composition from the crust, as P-waves act differently when passing through them. 3: The Moho discontinuity is suspected to be composed of peridotite or dunite, rather than basalt. 4: Humankind has reached the Moho discontinuity.
ANSWER: 2 and 3
