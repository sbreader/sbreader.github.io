2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding magnets is FALSE?
W) Ferrofluids must contain Fe3O4 and some detergent
X) The iron oxide particles form inverted micelles around the detergent particles in ferrofluids
Y) Single molecule magnets form only in torus-shapes
Z) Ceramic magnets are made of barium ferrite or strontium ferrite
ANSWER: Y) Single molecule magnets form only in torus-shapes
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer By name or number, indicate which of the following three statements regarding electromagnetic waves is or are TRUE. 1: A linearly polarized sinusoidal plane electromagnetic wave moving in the x-direction is represented by the cross product of the y-component of the electric field and the z-component of the magnetic field. 2: The amplitude of the magnetic field in a sinusoidal plane EM wave must be equal to c times the amplitude of the electric field. 3: A wave obeys Ampere's law only if B = epsilon nought * mu nought * c * E.
ANSWER: 1 and 3
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the name for the type of thermodynamic process that happens infinitely slowly? A process of this type will go through a sequence of states that are infinitesimally close to equilibrium.
ANSWER: QUASISTATIC PROCESS
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A sequence is recursively defined by a sub n = a sub n-1 + 2 a sub n-2, for n>2. If a sub 1 = 0 and a sub 2 = 1, what is the value of a sub 7?
ANSWER: 21
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following five layers of the secondary plant body from innermost layer to outermost layer; sapwood, secondary phloem, periderm, heartwood, vascular cambrium.
ANSWER: 4, 1, 5, 2, 3 (also accept: Heartwood, Sapwood, Vascular cambium, Secondary phloem, Periderm)
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the attractive interaction that results from the rapid fluctuations in the electron distribution in two neighboring molecules, acting between all molecules? This force is the only interaction between nonpolar molecules, and as such, causes octadecane to be a waxy solid whereas pentadecane is a viscous liquid.
ANSWER: LONDON INTERACTION
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is TRUE regarding fluid flow?
W) A stream line is a curve tangent at each point to the velocity vector at that point
X) An ideal fluid can be compressed only if it is a gas
Y) A flow line is calculated by finding the second derivative of volume with respect to time
Z) Bernoulli's equation relates the pressure p, flow speed v, and temperature t
ANSWER: W) A stream line is a curve tangent at each point to the velocity vector at that point
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, indicate which of the following five rocks is higher than diorite on Bowen's reaction series. 1: Gabbro 2: Olivine 3: Granite 4: Pyroxene 5: Rhyolite
ANSWER: 1, 2, 4
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Examples of this type of property include vapor quality and viscosity. This type of property is defined as a physical property of a system that does not depend on the system size or the amount of material in the system, and is thus scale invariant.
ANSWER: INTENSIVE PROPERTY
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The radius and height of a right circular cylinder are equal. If the volume is 1/pi2, what is the diameter of the cylinder?
ANSWER: 2/pi
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Indicate which of the following three statements regarding Mendel's law of independent assortment is or are TRUE.1: Mendel's second law states that each pair of alleles segregates independently of each other pair of alleles during gamete formation. 2: By the hypothesis of dependent assortment, dihybrid crosses will result in a 9:3:3:1 phenotypic ratio. 3: The law of independent assortment applies only to genes located on the same chromosomes.
ANSWER: 1 only
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If the semimajor axis of an elliptical orbit is quadrupled, by how much will its period increase, according to Kepler's third law for elliptical orbits?
ANSWER: 8 TIMES
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is FALSE regarding the Moon?
W) The Moon is one half as bright at quarter phase than at full moon
X) The Moon is a differentiated body
Y) The Moon is the second densest satellite in the Solar System after Io
Z) The Moon is the largest moon in the solar system relative to its planet's size
ANSWER: W) The Moon is one half as bright at quarter phase than at full moon
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which of the following three statements is or are TRUE regarding the Otto Cycle? 1: It is constructed from a pair of adiabatic processes and a pair of isobaric processes. 2: Process 3-4 in the Otto Cycle is the power stroke. 3: In process 1-2, the piston moves from the crank end to the cover end.
ANSWER: 2 and 3 (also accept: All but 1)
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If sin(theta) is .057, then what is sin(pi-theta)?
ANSWER: .057
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is FALSE regarding animal signals and communication?
W) Fruit fly courtship begins with orienting, a form of identification
X) Honeybees communicate using the "round dance" and "waggle dance"
Y) In minnows, an alarm pheromone is released from an injured minnow's skin to scare away its predator
Z) Habituation is present in birds and mammals when members of their species produce repeated calls with no real outcome, termed the "cry-wolf" effect
ANSWER: Y) In minnows, an alarm pheromone is released from an injured minnow's skin to scare away its predator
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By number, which of the following three statements regarding the direction of reactions is or are TRUE? 1: Small equilibrium constants mean the equilibrium favors the reactants. 2: In isothermal titration calorimetry, ΔG = -RTlnK. 3: The reaction quotient Q has the same form as for the expression for K, but the activities refer to any stage of the reaction.
ANSWER: 1, 2, and 3 (also accept: All of them)
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name this group of living primates that includes gibbons, orangutans, chimpanzees, humans, and apes that is found worldwide, known to have existed up to 45 million years ago.
ANSWER: ANTHROPOIDS
