1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Bacteria that can only grow in the presence of oxygen are called:
W) chemoautotrophs
X) obligate anaerobes
Y) facultative anaerobes
Z) obligate aerobes
ANSWER: Z) obligate aerobes
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Alpha keratins are relatively rich in which of the following amino acids:
W) alanine
X) tyrosine
Y) tryptophan
Z) cystine
ANSWER: Z) cystine
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The number of moles of solute per kilogram of solvent best describes which of the following:
W) normality
X) molarity
Y) molality
Z) mole fraction
ANSWER: Y) molality
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If an aqueous solution at 25°C has a hydroxide ion concentration of 1 × 10-2 moles per liter, calculate both the hydrogen ion concentration in moles per liter and the pH:
ANSWER: HYDROGEN ION CONCENTRATION = 1 × 10-12 AND pH = 12
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following units is a measure of resistance:
W) erg
X) ohm
Y) joule
Z) volt
ANSWER: X) ohm
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer A rock with weight 650 newtons is on a ledge five meters above the ground. Relative to the ground, what is the gravitational potential energy, in joules, of the rock?
ANSWER: 3250
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the astronomical point in the sky that a plumb line would vertically point to?
ANSWER: ZENITH
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What distant point of the Earth’s orbit occurs on July 3rd?
ANSWER: APHELION
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Simplify the following radical into a reduced fraction: √(18/50)
ANSWER: 3/5
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the slope of a line which passes through the points (-1, 1) and (5, 3)?
ANSWER: 1/3
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Landscape features such as swamps, marshes, bogs, prairie potholes, and vernal pools, are collectively known as what:

W) esker
X) delta kame
Y) varve
Z) striations
ANSWER: Z) striations

W) temperate woodland
X) temperate evergreen forest
Y) temperate rain forest
Z) temperate deciduous forest
ANSWER: Y) temperate rain forest
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In meteorology, what does the symbol of an open circle typically represent?
ANSWER: CLEAR SKIES (also accept: CLEAR)
8. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer A type of constantly-powered nonvolatile memory that can be erased and reprogrammed in blocks instead of single bytes, is often used to hold control code in a personal computer, and is used commonly in cell phones, digital cameras and other portable devices, is called what:
ANSWER: FLASH MEMORY (also accept: FLASH)

ANSWER: FLEA
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following passive body functions consumes the most energy:
W) breathing
X) the sodium-potassium pump in all cells of the body
Y) the beating of the heart
Z) kidney filtration of plasma
ANSWER: X) the sodium-potassium pump in all cells of the body
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In a flame test for lithium, sodium, potassium, and zinc, which gives off a persistent yellow color:
ANSWER: SODIUM
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To the second decimal place, what is the weight percent of a solution prepared by dissolving 10.0 grams NaCl in 100.0 grams water?
ANSWER: 9.09
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A manual laborer moves a 40 kilogram bag of flour one meter above the ground in 5 seconds. If she does the same task in twice the time, it involves a different amount of:
W) power
X) work
Y) displacement
Z) potential energy
ANSWER: W) power

ANSWER: TROPIC OF CANCER (also accept: 23° NORTH)

ANSWER: PRECESSION
