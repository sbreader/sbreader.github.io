1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following tactile mechanoreceptors function to detect deep pressure and high-frequency vibration in the skin?
W) Merkel cells
X) Meissner’s corpuscles
Y) Ruffini endings
Z) Pacinian corpuscles
ANSWER: Z) Pacinian corpuscles
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following mechanoreceptors that are encapsulated (1) Merkel cells (2) Meissner’s corpuscles (3) Ruffini endings (4) Pacinian corpuscles.
ANSWER: 2,3,4
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer EDTA can form multiple bonds with ions such as Ca2+ or Fe2+, preventing them from continuing reactivity in solution. What is the term for a compound such as EDTA that exhibits this property?
ANSWER: CHELATING AGENT
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the denticity of EDTA?
ANSWER: 6
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is true of fictitious forces?
W) Fictitious forces are present in all references frames.
X) Fictitious forces are only present in non-inertial reference frames.
Y) Fictitious forces are only present in inertial reference frames.
Z) Fictitious forces are not real.
ANSWER: X) Fictitious forces are only present in non-inertial reference frames.
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Find the critical speed for an object to remain in circular motion in a vertical loop with radius 10m, assuming g=10.
ANSWER: 10
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What famous theorem of probability gives the chance of an event occurring if another event has occurred?
ANSWER: BAYES THEOREM
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Use Bayes Theorem to answer the following scenario. The Art Competition has entries from three painters: Pam, Pia and Pablo. Pam put in 15 paintings, 4% of her works have won First Prize. Pia put in 5 paintings, 6% of her works have won First Prize. Pablo put in 10 paintings, 3% of his works have won First Prize. What is the chance that Pam wins first place?
ANSWER: 50%
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the chemical formula of the tetrahedral silicate anions in olivine?
ANSWER: SiO44-
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What class of silicates that includes Benitoite arranges silicate tetrahedra in a ring structure?
ANSWER: CYCLOSILICATES
6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the capacitor that is used to deliver charge from an electrolytic capacitor to a battery because it is significantly faster in short-term energy storage?
ANSWER: Supercapacitor
6. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a disadvantage of using supercapacitors over batteries?
W) Supercapacitors have significantly lower energy density
X) Supercapacitors have a lower breakdown voltage
Y) Supercapacitors have a higher self-discharge rate
Z) Supercapacitors have a shorter lifespan
ANSWER: Z) Supercapacitors have a shorter lifespan
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer There are two types of nephrons in the renal cortex. The first type is the juxtamedullary nephron. Provide the name for the second type of nephron, which is much more common in the kidney.
ANSWER: Cortical nephron
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer The primary difference between juxtamedullary and cortical nephrons is the length of what structure, which functions to reabsorb certain substances and create a concentration gradient?
ANSWER: Loop of Henle
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Hexaaquia complexes contain six of what ligands bound to a metal center?
ANSWER: WATER
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the simplest pnictogen hydride?
ANSWER: AMMONIA
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Find the fluidic pressure at a depth of 10m, in a fluid of density 1000 kg/m3, assuming g=9.8.
ANSWER: 98
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer Name all of the following three statements which describe ideal fluids? 1) Fluids are inviscid 2) Fluids are compressible 3) Fluids experience laminar flow
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What paradox in geometry states that given a finite 3d solid ball, there exists a decomposition of the ball into disjoint sets from which two identical balls can be constructed.
ANSWER: BANACH-TARSKI PARADOX
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What famous theorem in math, whose proof was published in 1995, states that no distinct positive integers a, b, c can satisfy an + bn = cn for n>2.
ANSWER: FERMAT’S LAST THEOREM
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the two main types of local metamorphism caused by an igneous intrusion?
ANSWER: CONTACT AND THERMAL
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals is the highest grade index minerals?
W) Orthoclase
X) Garnet
Y) Chlorite
Z) Kyanite
ANSWER: W) Orthoclase
12. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following methods for nuclear fusion is the most well-developed and will most likely be used in the future?
W) Magnetic confinement
X) Electric pinch
Y) Inertial electrostatic confinement
Z) Inertial confinement
ANSWER: W) Magnetic confinement
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the name of the device that uses magnetic fields to confine hot plasma into a toroidal shape in the magnetic confinement nuclear fusion method?
ANSWER: Tokamak
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Protein kinases catalyze the transfer of phosphate from ATP to a protein. What enzyme functions to oppose the action of kinases by catalysing the removal of phosphate from a protein to a water molecule?
ANSWER: Protein Phosphatase (also accept: Phosphatase)
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Phosphofructokinase is a major regulatory enzyme in glycolysis. This enzyme functions by transferring a phosphate group from molecule A to molecule B. What are molecules A and B?
ANSWER: Molecule A: ATP, Molecule B: Fructose-6-phosphate
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many different shapes of d orbitals exist?
ANSWER: 5
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What are the quantum numbers n and l of a 4f electron, respectively?
ANSWER: 4 and 3
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Calculate the buoyant force on an object that displaces 3 cubic meters in a fluid of density 1200 kg/m3 assuming g=10.
ANSWER: 36
15. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is not an example of turbulent flow?
W) Flow of honey from a bottle
X) Terrestrial atmospheric circulation
Y) Air flow over a stationary golf ball
Z) Smoke rising from cigarettes
ANSWER: W) Flow of honey from a bottle
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the supremum of the following set over the real numbers {2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 11}?
ANSWER: 11
16. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following sets obeys the axiom of completeness?
W) The Natural Numbers
X) The Real Numbers
Y) The Rational Numbers
Z) The Integers
ANSWER: X) The Real Numbers
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the type of sand, found in Venezuela and Alberta, containing high concentrations of bitumen?
ANSWER: TAR SAND
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What two compounds make up gas hydrate?
ANSWER: METHANE AND WATER (also accept: CH4 AND H2O)
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the chemical by-product of biodiesel production that is commonly used to make antifreeze?
ANSWER: Glycerol (also accept: Glycerin)
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Glycerol can be reacted with concentrated nitric acid to produce nitroglycerin. What acid is used to catalyze this reaction?
ANSWER: Sulfuric
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The human skull consists of mostly irregular bones. Which of the following pairs of irregular bones forms the hard palate of the mouth?
W) Ethmoid and Vomer
X) Maxillary and Palatine
Y) Zygomatic and Sphenoid
Z) Mandible and Nasal
ANSWER: X) Maxillary and Palatine
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer What 3 paired bones fuse during adolescence to form the pelvis?
ANSWER: Ilium, Ischium, and Pubis
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT one of the five shapes of d orbitals?
W) dx2
X) dxy
Y) dyz
Z) dz2
ANSWER: W) dx2
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What unique third type of covalent bond (after sigma and pi) is found by the overlap of four lobes of d orbitals?
ANSWER: DELTA BOND
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The wave speed on a string with linear density 12 kg/m is 10m/s. What is the wave speed in a similar string with equal tension and a linear density of 6 kg/m?
ANSWER: 40
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer Find the sound intensity level in decibels of a sound of intensity 10 W/m2.
ANSWER: 130 dB
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What fundamental theorem states that for any continuous function f over the closed interval from a to b, f takes on every value between f(a) and f(b) over the interval?
ANSWER: INTERMEDIATE VALUE THEOREM
22. BONUS Math Short Answer What theorem states that if g(x) ≤ f(x) ≤ h(x) and the limit as x goes to c of g(x) is equal to the limit as x goes to c of h(x) then f(x) must have the same limit as x goes to c.
ANSWER: SQUEEZE THEOREM
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The equations of motion cannot be solved analytically for a system that has more than how many bodies?
ANSWER: TWO
23. BONUS Physics Short Answer What theorem provides a general equation that relates the average over time of the total kinetic energy of a stable system of discrete bodies, bound by potential forces, with that of the total potential energy of the system?
ANSWER: VIRIAL THEOREM
