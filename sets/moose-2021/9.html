1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which artery supplies the majority of the left atrium with blood?
ANSWER: CIRCUMFLEX ARTERY
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Jimmy is experiencing severe leg pain. Upon closer inspection, a forest of very thin, multicolored lines can be seen on Jimmy's leg. What condition is Jimmy most likely suffering from?
ANSWER: SPIDER VEINS
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Chemical weathering of silicate minerals such as feldspars typically produces members what group of hydrous minerals?
ANSWER: CLAY MINERALS (also accept: CLAY)
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Gypsum is a common mineral that precipitates from seawater. If anhydrite is instead found where gypsum is expected to be, what process must have occurred after the gypsum was deposited?
W) Differentiation
X) Diagenesis
Y) Dehydration
Z) Distillation
ANSWER: Y) Dehydration
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at the Environmental Molecular Sciences Laboratory are studying the enzymes and bacteria that aid digestion in ruminants like moose. To digest food more efficiently, ruminants have specialized stomachs with how many chambers?
ANSWER: 4
3. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab are developing methods to detect and separate radioactive elements from nearly pure substances. Their research is mostly focused on removing uranium and what other actinide that has an atomic number of 90?
ANSWER: THORIUM

7. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of an RLC circuit?
W) At frequencies lower than the resonant frequency the voltage leads the current
X) At frequencies higher than the resonant frequency the current leads the voltage
