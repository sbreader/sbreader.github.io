1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of structures would indicate that divergent evolution is taking place?
W) Analogous Structures
X) Homologous Structures
Y) Vestigial Structures
Z) Convergent Structures
ANSWER: X) Homologous Structures
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer If the endosperm of a plant with monosporic development has 45 chromosomes, how many chromosomes will the egg cell have?
ANSWER: 15
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A Grignard Reagent could be produced by reacting magnesium with an organic group and an element from what group of the periodic table?
ANSWER: HALOGENS (also accept: GROUP 7; GROUP 17)
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following four molecules that possess a resonance structure I. Ozone II. Ammonia III. Carbonate Ion IV. Sulfur Trioxide
ANSWER: I, III, IV
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In ohms, what is the absolute difference between the equivalent resistance of a 10 and 20 ohm resistor in series and in parallel?
ANSWER: 70/3 OHMS (also accept: 23 ⅓ OHMS)
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Rank the following four quarks in order of increasing mass I. Top II. Charm III. Strange IV. Up
ANSWER: IV, III, II, I
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice At which of the following latitudes on the Sun would you expect to see the most sunspots at the end of the 11 year sunspot cycle?
W) 0
X) 30
Y) 45
Z) 60
ANSWER: W) 0
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In the triple alpha process, two alpha particles fuse to form an isotope, which then fuses with another alpha particle to form a carbon-12 nucleus. What is this isotope?
ANSWER: BERYLLIUM-8
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The base of an isosceles triangle SCI is length 24 and the triangle’s area is 60. What is the length of one of its congruent sides?
ANSWER: 13
5. BONUS Math Short Answer For a ten-digit telephone number, any combination of digits is possible except those that start with zero and one. What proportion of telephone numbers start with 8 and end with 1?
ANSWER: 1/80 (SOLUTION: 108/(8 × 109))
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Brookhaven National Lab are using the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider to study the makeup of protons and neutrons, as well as their place in the early universe. To do so, they collide ions together, breaking up protons and neutrons into elementary particles. What two elementary particles are produced?
ANSWER: QUARKS AND GLUONS (also accept: UP QUARK AND DOWN QUARK)
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are using advanced microscopy to study nano-scale transistors. What law are they studying that states that the number of transistors in a dense integrated circuit doubles approximately every two years, that is in reality an empirical relationship?
ANSWER: MOORE’S LAW
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of selection defines a population that is likely to be living at a density near the limit of resources within their habitat?
ANSWER: K-SELECTED
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three neurotransmitters that are excitatory I. Glutamate II. GABA III. Norepinephrine
ANSWER: I AND III
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Hess’s Law is an application of which of the following Laws of Thermodynamics?
W) Zeroth Law
X) First Law
Y) Second Law
Z) Third Law
ANSWER: X) First Law
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following gasses will have the largest b value according to the Van der Waals equation?
W) He
X) CH4
Y) SO2
Z) CCl4
ANSWER: Z) CCl4
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A transformer has a primary voltage of 2000 volts, and a primary coil with 100 turns. If its secondary voltage is 700 volts, how many turns are in the secondary coil?
ANSWER: 35
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer A given Carnot engine has an efficiency of 60%. Brian wants to raise this efficiency to 90%. If the engine has a hot temperature of 400 K and a cold temperature of 160 K, by how much must the cold temperature be reduced to achieve the desired efficiency?
ANSWER: 120 K
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following metamorphic rocks is NOT correctly paired to its parent rock?
W) Gneiss; Granite
X) Schist; Dolostone
Y) Marble; Limestone
Z) Phyllite; Shale
ANSWER: X) Schist; Dolostone
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are TRUE regarding seismic activity I. The Modified Mercalli Scale describes the damage caused by an earthquake on a scale from I–V II. S waves will always reach a seismogram before P waves III. The 1964 Alaska earthquake was the most powerful ever recorded on the moment magnitude scale, measuring 9.2
ANSWER: NONE
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Two sets, A and B, have the same number of elements. If the union of sets A and B consists of 2022 elements, and their intersection consists of 178 elements, how many elements are in Set A?
ANSWER: 1100
11. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the derivative of f(x) = ln(x2 + 2x) at x = 2?
ANSWER: 3/4 (also accept: 0.75)
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab have been studying the structure of MPro, the main protease of SARS-CoV-2. Which of the following statements is incorrect regarding proteases?
W) They catalyze proteolysis
X) They do not help to form new protein products
Y) They play a part in cell signaling
Z) They can be inhibited by lipophilic ligands
ANSWER: X) They do not help to form new protein products
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying the properties of the neutron and the existence of the theorized ‘mirror neutron’. What is the isospin of a neutron?
ANSWER: -½
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following cellular components will have the lowest pH?
W) Endoplasmic reticulum
X) Golgi Apparatus
Y) Peroxisome
Z) Lysosome
ANSWER: Z) Lysosome
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify ALL of the following four types of bacteria that are gram positive I. Staphylococcus aureus II. Escherichia coli III. Bacillus Anthracis IV. Streptococcus Pneumoniae
ANSWER: I, III, AND IV
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following graphs represent a second-order reaction?
W) A graph of concentration vs time, which is linear
X) A graph of concentration of products vs concentration of reactants, which is linear
Y) A graph of the natural log of concentration vs time, which is linear
Z) A graph of the reciprocal of concentration vs time, which is linear
ANSWER: Z) A graph of the reciprocal of concentration vs time, which is linear
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Consider the following three sets of reactions: X(NO3) + Y → No reaction X(NO3) + Z → Z(NO3) + X Y(NO3) + Z → Z(NO3) + Y Rank the following three elements in terms of increasing activity I. X II. Y III. Z
ANSWER: II, I, III
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A 100-kilogram mass is sliding at an initial speed of 4.35 meters per second on ice, coming to rest uniformly in 3 seconds due to friction. In newtons, what is the magnitude of the force of friction on the mass due to the ice?
ANSWER: 145
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify ALL of the following scenarios where increasing the radius will decrease the electric field I. An infinite sphere of charge II. An infinite plane of charge III. An infinite line of charge IV. An dipole of charge
ANSWER: I, III, IV
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What two limits represent the upper bounds for the masses of neutron stars and white dwarfs, respectively?
ANSWER: TOLMAN-OPPENHEIMER-VOLKOFF AND CHANDRASEKHAR (also accept: TOV AND CHANDRASEKHAR) (do not accept: IN REVERSE ORDER)
