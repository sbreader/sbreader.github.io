1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer According to Noether’s theorem, translational symmetry corresponds to a conservation of what physical quantity?
ANSWER: LINEAR MOMENTUM
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Given an axis of rotation about the point (0,0), which of the following mass distributions would yield an equivalent moment of inertia compared to a mass distribution about a uniform disk with radius 3 and mass 4 about (0,0)?
W) A mass of 1 at the point (2,3) and a mass of 3 at the point (−1,0)
X) A mass of 2 at the point (3,0) and a mass of 2 at the point (0,3)
Y) A mass of 4 at the point (3,0)
Z) A mass of 1 at the point (2, √2) and a mass of 3 at the point (0, −2)
ANSWER: Z) A mass of 1 at the point (2, √2) and a mass of 3 at the point (0, −2)

3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an experimental observation of the photoelectric effect?
W) Electrons are not ejected from the metal if the frequency of the photon is not higher than the threshold energy required.
X) The intensity of the light does not impact whether electrons are ejected.
Y) Kinetic energy of the ejected electrons is linearly increased with the frequency of the incident radiation.
Z) An ejected electrons velocity can be determined solely by the work function of the metal.
ANSWER: Z) An ejected electrons velocity can be determined solely by the work function of the metal.
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice It is observed that the value for the nuclear magnetic moment is non-zero for certain nuclei. Which of the following is concluded from a nucleus that has a non-zero nuclear magnetic spin?

Y) There must be an odd amount of either protons or neutrons.
Z) There must be an even amount of protons or neutrons.
ANSWER: Y) There must be an odd amount of either protons or neutrons.
