1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Order the following 3 spectral lines for the hydrogen atom in terms of increasing wavelength of photons emitted 1) Balmer – alpha 2) Lyman-alpha 3) Paschen – alpha.
ANSWER: 2,1,3
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four Galilean moons in terms of increasing average density 1) Io 2) Europa 3) Ganymede 4) Callisto.
ANSWER: 4,3,2,1
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Livermore National Lab have been studying oxime compounds that can pass the blood-brain barrier and counteract the effects of nerve agents. These nerve agents prevent the breakdown of what neurotransmitter?
ANSWER: ACETYLCHOLINE
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the circumradius of a right triangle with legs of 3 and 4?
ANSWER: 2.5
7. BONUS Math Short Answer How many nonnegative integers n less than 243 have no digit equal to 2 in their base 3 representation?
ANSWER: 32
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Intersystem crossing occurs when a system in a singlet state undergoes a radiationless transition to a triplet state. After a molecule is excited and then undergoes intersystem crossing, it must use what process in order to relax to its ground state?
ANSWER: PHOSPHORESCENCE (do not accept: FLUORESCENCE)
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the determinant of the 2 by 2 matrix with first row 5 and –4 and second row –3 and –2?
ANSWER: –22
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A binary star system consists of a 2 solar mass and a 3 solar mass star mutually orbiting each other at a constant distance of 1 AU. In AU, how far is the center of mass of the system from the 2 solar mass star?
ANSWER: 0.6 (also accept: 3/5)
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer A uniform electric field of −2i + 4j + 5k volts per meter is present throughout a region of space. If the electric potential at the origin is given to be 0, calculate the electric potential in volts at the point (2, −3, 4).
ANSWER: –4
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer When hydrochloric acid is added to 1-butene, the major product formed is 2-chlorobutane. What rule predicts that 1-chlorobutane will not be formed in significant amounts?
ANSWER: MARKOVNIKOV’S RULE
22. BONUS Math Short Answer A positive integer has a base 16 representation of 143. In base 10, what is the largest prime factor of this integer?
ANSWER: 19
