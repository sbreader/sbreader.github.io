1. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following numbers has a multiplicative inverse modulo 39?
W) 12
X) 13
Y) 14
Z) 15
ANSWER: Y) 14
1. BONUS Math Short Answer Alice rolls a die repeatedly until she rolls a 1. What is the expected value of the sum of her rolls?
ANSWER: 21
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding the Intertropical Convergence Zone, or ITCZ 1) Its average position is usually south of the equator 2) It is pushed away from Asia in boreal winter months 3) The vertical extent of the ITCZ is generally bounded by the tropopause.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The Cartwheel galaxy is an example of what general type of galaxy, formed when one smaller companion plunges through a larger one perpendicular to its disk and is characterized by the abundance of black holes and neutron stars?
ANSWER: RING GALAXY
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What physical constant is referred to as the fundamental quantum of magnetic moment?
ANSWER: BOHR MAGNETON
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two polarizing sheets are placed in series, and an unpolarized light source passes through a polarizing sheet, and then another polarizing sheet with its polarizing direction at 30 degrees relative to the first sheet. If the initial intensity of light is given by a, what is the transmitted intensity in terms of a?
ANSWER: 3a/8
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The cornea is continuous with which outer white layer of the eye?
ANSWER: SCLERA
4. VISUAL BONUS Biology Short Answer Shown in the image is a specific protein that adopts a triple helix as a super secondary structure and requires certain cofactors for the crucial hydroxylation of amino acid residues within the protein. Answer the following two questions concerning this protein 1) What protein is depicted? 2) A and B are modified forms of what amino acid?
ANSWER: 1) COLLAGEN; 2) PROLINE
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The lowest unoccupied molecular orbital of acetylene in its ground state is what specific type of orbital?
ANSWER: SIGMA STAR (also accept: SIGMA ANTIBONDING)
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three ions in terms of increasing pKa 1) Amide anion 2) Carboxylate anion 3) Ketone enolate anion.
ANSWER: 2,1,3
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers at Argonne National Lab are working to develop catalysts for the production of liquid fuels from methane. Which of the following is likely the greatest challenge they have encountered?
W) Methane is expensive
X) Methane transportation is inefficient
Y) Methane is relatively unreactive
Z) Methane is difficult to isolate
ANSWER: Y) Methane is relatively unreactive
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab have been studying two-dimensional materials. When these one-atom thick layers are stacked on top of each other at an angle, what characteristic pattern is formed?
ANSWER: MOIRÉ PATTERN
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What term is given to a sustained muscle contraction that occurs when skeletal muscles are innervated by their motor neurons at a very high rate?
ANSWER: TETANY
7. VISUAL BONUS Biology Short Answer Shown in the image is a pathway regarding the synthesis of neurotransmitters within the brain. 1) Give the general name for these compounds derived from tyrosine, containing a benzene ring and 2 hydroxyl groups 2) Which of the following compounds is found at the post synaptic clefts of sympathetic nerves?
ANSWER: 1) CATECHOLAMINES; 2) NOREPINEPHRINE
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the specific solution to the Einstein vacuum field equations that describes a charged, nonrotating black hole?
ANSWER: REISSNER-NORDSTROM
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three mechanisms or phenomena that contribute to ocean mixing 1) Cyclones 2) Internal tidal waves 3) Vertical eddy diffusion.
ANSWER: ALL
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Reacting alkenes with peroxy acids forms an epoxide. Identify all of the following three reagents that may be used to open this ring 1) Sodium methoxide 2) Aqueous hydrochloric acid 3) Sodium cyanide.
ANSWER: ALL
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What equation in chemistry calculates the lattice energy of a crystalline ionic solid that includes a repulsion term which includes the compressibility constant of the crystal?
ANSWER: BORN-MAYER EQUATION
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many ways are there to divide 8 students into 2 equal teams?
ANSWER: 35
10. BONUS Math Short Answer A function f(x) is continuous and triply differentiable such that f triple prime of x is always positive. If f(0) = 1, f(1) = 2, and f(2) = 4, what is the minimum integer value of f(4)?
ANSWER: 12
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at NIH are studying secretion. Which of the following cell types is not correctly paired with its secretion?

Y) Alpha cells and somatostatin

11. ANSWER: INVERSE SQUARE

ANSWER: MAGNETIC CONFINEMENT (do not accept: TOKAMAK)
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the reason ketones are often treated with acid during nucleophilic substitution?
W) To make the carbonyl group less sterically hindered
X) To make the carbonyl group more sterically hindered
Y) To make the carbonyl group less electrophilic

ANSWER: Z) To make the carbonyl group more electrophilic

W) Parallel to the cellulose microfibrils
X) Parallel to the expansion enzymes
Y) Perpendicular to the cellulose microfibrils
Z) Perpendicular to expansion enzymes

15. BONUS Math Short Answer A triangular region on the coordinate plane has vertices at the points (0, 0), (0, 3), and (4, 0). If a random point is chosen on the perimeter of the region, what is the expected value of its x-coordinate?
ANSWER: 3/2
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements best explains the presence of more than three convection circulation cells in Jupiter’s atmosphere?
W) Jupiter’s strong magnetic field causes distortions within meridional heat transport
X) Jupiter’s rapid rotation causes strong Coriolis deflection
Y) Jupiter’s large size limits the size of atmospheric convection cells due to very strong gravity
Z) Helium rain within Jupiter’s atmosphere causes significant vertical wind shear
ANSWER: X) Jupiter’s rapid rotation causes strong Coriolis deflection
