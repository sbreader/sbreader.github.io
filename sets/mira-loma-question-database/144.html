1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In a molecule with dsp3 hybridization, what is the angle between two of the equatorial bonds?
ANSWER: 120 DEGREES
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice If a molecule with d2sp3 hybridization has two lone pairs, what shape will the molecule adopt?
W) trigonal planar
X) square pyramidal
Y) square planar
Z) t-shaped
ANSWER: Y) square planar
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer Cavitation occurs when the pressure of a flowing liquid drops below what point?
ANSWER: VAPOUR PRESSURE
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Trypsinogen, pepsinogen, and angiotensinogen are all examples of what type of enzymes?
ANSWER: ZYMOGENS (also accept: PROENZYMES)
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer What 27 carbon lipid is a precursor to testosterone and is also important to maintaining membrane fluidity in cells?
ANSWER: CHOLESTEROL
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Assuming small angle approximations, what is the distance, to two significant figures, to a star that has a parallax of .75?
W) 1.33 parsecs
X) 3.00 parsecs
Y) .75 parsecs
Z) 1.73 parsecs
ANSWER: W) 1.33 parsecs
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Though the moon is tidally locked to always face the Earth, it is possible to see more than 50% of the moon's surface over time. This is because of what phenomenon?
ANSWER: LIBRATION
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In a normal distribution, approximately what percent of the population will be 1 standard deviation above the mean?
ANSWER: 16 PERCENT (also accept: 15.8 PERCENT)
5. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following shapes describes the graph of the hyperbolic cosine function?
W) cycloid
X) ellipse
Y) caternary curve
Z) parabola
ANSWER: Y) caternary curve
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice North America contains an aborted rift valley that has originated in which of the following ways?
W) two continental margins began to split apart 500 million years ago
X) the North American craton began to split apart approximately 1 billion years ago
Y) the Pacific plate began to crumble under stress approximately 2 billion years ago
Z) the Juan de Fuca plate exerted pressure on the North American plate, causing it to fracture 250 million years ago
ANSWER: X) the North American craton began to split apart approximately 1 billion years ago
7. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice The synthesis of which of the following compounds is regarded as the origin of organic chemistry?
W) pepsin
X) DDT
Y) Perkin's mauve
Z) urea
ANSWER: Z) urea
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is characterized by refractive index, dispersion, specific gravity, hardness, cleavage, fracture, and luster?
ANSWER: GEMSTONES
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which optical phenomena, displayed by some rubies and sapphires, involves an enhanced reflective area in the shape of a star?
ANSWER: ASTERISM
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A carbon-carbon triple bond consists of how many pi bonds and how many sigma bonds?
ANSWER: 2 PI BONDS, 1 SIGMA BONDS
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Using molecular orbital theory, describe the second lowest energy pi bonding orbital as bonding, antibonding, or nonbonding.
ANSWER: NONBONDING
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What free subatomic particle has the longest mean lifetime of any other subatomic particle?
ANSWER: NEUTRON
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer Piezoelectricity is the ability of materials to generate an electric potential in response to what?
ANSWER: MECHANICAL STRESS
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which immunoglobulin class causes degranulation of basophils and mast cells?
ANSWER: IMMUNOGLOBULIN CLASS E
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Most proteins found in serum are synthesized in what gland of the human body?
ANSWER: LIVER
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Wien's law relates wavelength of the peak emission of a black body inversely with what quantity?
ANSWER: TEMPERATURE
