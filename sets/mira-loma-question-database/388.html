1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the cyclic ethers that are capable of encasing ions, therefore increasing their solubility in water?
ANSWER: crown ether
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the weathering type that occurs when water seeps through rock, freezes (and consequently expands), dislodging the rock and being a main cause of the Talus slope?
ANSWER: frost wedging
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 options that is or are true about glacier movement. The points towards the middle of the glacier move faster than points from outside Its movement is promoted by the zone of accumulation where the added snow helps increase mass of the glacier The glacier erodes its surroundings as it moves and make notable landmarks such as fjords
ANSWER: all of them
3. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Identify all of the following three options that is or are methods of cleaning coal and purifying it of sulfur. Coal gasification Fluidized bed combustor scrubbers
ANSWER: All of them
3. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the name of the facility that enriched Uranium is taken to in preparation for the nuclear reactor?
ANSWER: fabrication plant
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name given to the exchange of genes between different domains?
ANSWER: horizontal gene transfer
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following types of cloning that occur through the use of stem cells? I) reproductive cloning II) embryonic stem cloning III) therapeutic cloning
ANSWER: III only
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Name the two hadrons and the types of the amount of quarks that each contain (include antiquarks).
ANSWER: baryon 3 quarks and mesons 1 quark and 1 antiquark
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Given that 9 A of current pass through a conductor in 4 seconds, what was the total charge that passed through the conductor?
ANSWER: 36 C
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The relative permitivity of a substance is the ratio between the stored electricity with an applied voltage to what constant?
W) Planck's constant
X) Static permittivity
Y) Vacuum permittivity
Z) The speed of light
ANSWER: Y) Vacuum permittivity
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer An alcohol and a carboxylic acid combine to form an ester. If the alcohol is substituted with an amine, what is functional group results?
ANSWER: Amide
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the circulation cell between 30 deg and 60 deg latitude where the polar easterlies and the westerlies converge?
ANSWER: Ferrel Cell
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Given that an upright man that is 10 feet tall and 5 feet wide is traveling horizontally at 0.6c, What is his height of the man as perceived by a stationary observer?
ANSWER: 10 feet
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following four phenomena that one would experience as they near a black hole head first. Length expansion Length contraction Width expansion A speeding up of time
ANSWER: I and IV
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 arenes that are denoted with the prefix meta- . 1-ethyl -3 methybenzene 1,2 dimethlbenzene 1,3 dimethylbenzene 1,4 -dimethylbenzene
ANSWER: I and III
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Consider the reaction for the decomposition of cyclopropane into propene which is a first order reaction. Given that the rate constant is 6.93 × 10-4, at 773 K, and you start with 50 mol at time 0 s, how much is left at t = 2000s. Give your answer in mols and to 3 sig figs.
ANSWER: 12.5 mols
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Why are Buffalo and Rochester considered the snowiest cities in America?
W) They are located near a major ocean
X) They are heavily influenced by continental polar air mass from Canada
Y) They are heavily influenced by the lake-snow effect
Z) It is the way God meant it to be
ANSWER: Y) They are heavily influenced by the lake-snow effect
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the factor that is considered to be the most important and most uncertain of all the components of the Drake equation?
ANSWER: Longevity factor
15. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Identify all of the following options that is or are uses for propane gas in life? heating homes fueling appliances such as the grill and the stove To fuel indoor machinery and fleet vehicles To burn and make electricity
ANSWER: I II III
15. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer According to scientists, in what rock structure deep underground is the safest way to store nuclear waste?

16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the protein that makes the specialized hair on the legs of geckos called setae that allow them to stick onto surfaces such as walls?
ANSWER: keratin

W) Diode
X) Capacitor
Y) Transformer

20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In the binary star system, name the following features: (a) volume enclosed by each object's magnetic field and (b) the point at which the volumes of gravitation of each object meet.
