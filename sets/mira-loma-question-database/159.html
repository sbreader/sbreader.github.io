Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which type of coal has the highest carbon content?
W) Subbituminous
X) Lignite
Y) Anthracite
Z) Bituminous
ANSWER: Y) Anthracite
1. BONUS Energy Short Answer How much of the coal for the United States does surface mining account for?
ANSWER: Two-thirds/ 33%
2. BONUS Energy Short Answer About what percentage of coal is used to generate electricity?
ANSWER: 94%
3. BONUS Energy Short Answer Who pioneered the generation, transmission, and use of alternating current (AC) electricity, which reduced the cost of transmitting electricity over long distances?
ANSWER: Nikola Tesla
4. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which of William Stanley's machines allowed electricity to efficiently be transported over long distances?
ANSWER: Transformer
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer About how many watts is in one horsepower?
ANSWER: 750 (watts)
5. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer In the U.S., which machine produces the most electricity?
ANSWER: (Steam) turbine
6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer How many Btu equal one kilowatt-hour?
ANSWER: 3412 (Btu)
6. BONUS Math Short Answer How many Btu, to five significant digits, are in five food calories?
ANSWER: 19.842 (Btu)
7. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What does Btu stand for?
ANSWER: British thermal unit
7. BONUS Energy Short Answer Also known as landfill gas and biogas, what is it?
ANSWER: Methane (gas)
8. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer When corn or sugarcane ferments, what is produced?
ANSWER: Ethanol
9. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What bacteria in landfills decompose organic waste to produce methanol?
ANSWER: Anaerobic
9. BONUS Energy Short Answer Which of the following can't produce ethanol? 1) Bark 2) Sorghum 3) Yard clippings
ANSWER: None
10. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What percent of the fuel E85, mainly sold in the Midwest, is gasoline?
W) 10
X) 15
Y) 20
Z) 25
ANSWER: X) 15
10. BONUS Energy Short Answer What plant does Brazil use to create most of its ethanol?
ANSWER: Sugarcane
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Geothermal energy is generated in earth's what?
W) mantle
X) crust
Y) core
Z) asthenosphere
ANSWER: Y) core
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name three ways geothermal energy shows at the surface of the earth.
ANSWER: volcanoes, fumaroles, hot springs (also accept: geysers)
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When magma comes close to the surface, it heats ground water found trapped in porous rock or water running along fractured rock surfaces and faults, what is the feature called?
ANSWER: Hydrothermal
13. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Electricity generation power plants require water or steam at what temperatures in Fahrenheit?
W) 200- 300
X) 700-1100
Y) 500-600
Z) 300-700
ANSWER: Z) 300-700
13. BONUS Energy Short Answer After bathing, what is the most common direct use of geothermal energy?
ANSWER: Direct heating systems (also accept: heating; heating buildings)
14. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the most common industrial use of geothermal energy?
ANSWER: Dehydration
14. BONUS Energy Short Answer What are the three basic types of geothermal power plants?
ANSWER: Dry steam (plants), flash steam (plants), and binary cycle power (plants)
15. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Geothermal plants use scrubber systems to clean the air of what naturally found in steam and hot water?
ANSWER: Hydrogen sulfide
15. BONUS Energy Short Answer What In hydropower turbines, accumulated in reservoirs created by dams in what system?
ANSWER: Storage (system)
16. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer A simple generation system for tidal plants involves a what known as a barrage, across an inlet?
ANSWER: Dam
16. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which two ways can convert solar energy into electricity?
ANSWER: Photovoltaic (PV devices) or "solar cells" and Concentrating Solar Power Plants
16. BONUS Energy Short Answer Which two countries are the two commercially operating barrages located?
ANSWER: Canada and France
16. BONUS Energy Short Answer A photovoltaic cell is a nonmechanical device usually made from what alloys?
ANSWER: Silicon
17. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Photovoltaic cells, like batteries, generate what which is generally used for small loads?
ANSWER: Direct current (also accept: DC)
18. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What are the three main solar thermal power systems?
ANSWER: Parabolic trough, solar dish, and solar power tower
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Solar space heating systems can be classified as what when a collector absorbs solar radiation?
ANSWER: Active
19. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice A parabolic trough can achieve what temperatures in Fahrenheit due to its parabolic shape?
W) under 300
X) 300-500
Y) 500-700
Z) over 750
ANSWER: Z) over 750
20. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the most common type of nonconcentrating collector for solar power?
ANSWER: Flat plate (collector)
20. BONUS Energy Short Answer Flat plate collectors generally consist of three things: flat-plate absorber, heat-transporter fluid, and transparent what?
ANSWER: Cover
21. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is a tank in which water is heated to produce either hot water or steam that is circulated for the purpose of heating and power?
ANSWER: Boiler
21. BONUS Energy Short Answer You have a natural gas furnace in your home that used 81,300 cubic feet of natural gas for heating last winter. Your neighbor has an oil furnace that used 584 gallons of heating oil last winter. Which used more heat, your house or the oil furnace? (Hint: Convert to Btu)
ANSWER: Your house
22. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the fossil fuel formed by the breakdown of vegetable material trapped underground without access to air, often used in industries to power factories?
ANSWER: Coal
23. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the Chemical oxidation accompanied by the generation of light and heat?
ANSWER: Combustion
23. BONUS Energy Short Answer How many Btu are in 8 kilowatt hours (1 significant figure)?
ANSWER: 30,000
24. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the ability to do work or the ability to move an object?
ANSWER: Energy
24. BONUS Energy Short Answer Convert 70 Btu into megajoules with one significant figure.
ANSWER: 0.07
25. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is a unit for measuring the rate of work (or power) equivalent to 33,000 foot-pounds per minute or 746 watts?
ANSWER: Horsepower
25. BONUS Energy Short Answer Convert 15 food calories into megajoules with two significant figures.
ANSWER: 0.063
