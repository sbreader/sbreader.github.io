1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Breanna is running a chemical reaction and sees that the concentration of the reactant decreases linearly with time. What is the order of her reaction?
ANSWER: Zero
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Bella is melting a diorite boulder. Order the following mineral from first to last to melt 1) Pyroxene 2) Calcium plagioclase 3) Hornblende
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What sticky, fibrous polysaccharide layer is found on bacterial cells that assists in adhesion, nutrient acquisition, and hydration?
ANSWER: Glycocalyx
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What electrical circuit element is primarily responsible for preventing abrupt changes in current?
ANSWER: Inductor
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following chemical substances would have the strongest London dispersion forces?
W) chlorine
X) argon
Y) bromine
Z) krypton
ANSWER: Y) bromine
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 water masses by increasing average density. 1) Antarctic Bottom Water 2) Antarctic Intermediate Water 3) North Atlantic Deep Water
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Arrange the following three animals in terms of increasing length of the loop of Henle 1) Brown bear 2) North American beaver 3) Dromedary camel
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the value of two to the two to the two to the two?
ANSWER: 65536
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the power in Watts dissipated through a 5 Ohm resistor if it is held at a constant voltage of 20 Volts?
ANSWER: 80
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes henry's law?
W) The pH of a solution is directly proportional to the concentration of hydrogen ions
X) The solubility of a gas is linearly proportional to its partial pressure above the liquid
Y) The cell potential of a battery is inversely proportional to the concentration of hydrogen ions
Z) The pressure of a gas is directly proportional to its temperature
ANSWER: X) The solubility of a gas is linearly proportional to its partial pressure above the liquid
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the role of carbon in the CNO cycle reactions?
W) Reactant
X) Product
Y) Intermediate
Z) Catalyst
ANSWER: Z) Catalyst
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following three structures of the angiosperm reproductive system in terms of increasing ploidy 1) Endosperm 2) Microspore 3) Megasporangium
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice How many quarks are in a baryon?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 3
Z) 4
ANSWER: Y) 3
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What equation describes the relationship between pressure and temperature along a phase transition boundary?
ANSWER: Clausius-Clapeyron equation
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Aura loss follows an autosomally recessive inheritance pattern. If Ethan observes in a large population that the frequency of phenotypes with no aura is 1%, what percentage of the population are heterozygotes?
ANSWER: 18%
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What algorithm in linear algebra is used to solve systems of linear equations by adding rows and multiplying rows by constants?
ANSWER: Gaussian elimination (also accept: Row reduction)
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Filip is skiing down a frictionless hill. In which of the following directions does the net force acting on him point?
W) Out of the ramp
X) Into the ramp
Y) Up the ramp
Z) Down the ramp
ANSWER: Z) Down the ramp
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Julian wants to buy chicken wings. If his chicken wings come in packs of 8 and 9, what is the largest integer number of chicken wings that Julian cannot buy?
ANSWER: 55
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Lucas is growing a crystal of alum, which is also known as potassium aluminum sulfate. What is the chemical formula of alum?
ANSWER: KAl(SO4)2
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a small block of crust that has been sutured to a continent?
ANSWER: Terrane GEO (also accept: accretted terrane)
21. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Casey is interested in amoeboid movement, and wonders how pseudopodia extend. Which of the following proteins is most likely to be involved in generating the force required for pseudopodia extension?
W) Actin
X) Myosin
Y) Tubulin
Z) Dynein
ANSWER: W) Actin
22. BONUS Math Short Answer For some positive integer n, 2n divides 70 factorial. What is the largest possible value of n?
ANSWER: 67
23. BONUS Physics Short Answer Planck's law is a refinement of what previous law, which was physically impossible because it suffered from the ultraviolet catastrophe?
ANSWER: Rayleigh-Jeans Law
24. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice If the effusion rate of chlorine gas through a small hole is about 0.01 moles per second, which of the following is closest to the effusion rate of hydrogen gas through the same hole at the same temperature?
W) 0.01
X) 0.03
Y) 0.06
Z) 0.09
ANSWER: Y) 0.06
25. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of instabilities occurs when the environmental lapse rate equals the dry adiabatic lapse rate?
W) Absolute instability
X) Absolute stability
Y) Conditional instability
Z) Neutral stability
ANSWER: Z) Neutral stability
26. BONUS Biology Short Answer What specialized plant tissue, located between the endodermis and vascular tissues, contributes to the formation of the vascular and cork cambia and initiates lateral root formation?
ANSWER: Pericycle
27. BONUS Math Short Answer What are the coordinates of the inflection point of the function f(x) = 5x3-3x2+3x+1?
ANSWER: (1/5, 38/25)
28. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following systems that are simple harmonic oscillators 1) A mass hanging from an ideal spring 2) A large-angle pendulum 3) An LC circuit
ANSWER: 1 and 3
29. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Francium-211 undergoes nuclear decay to form lead-207. Given that Francium is element 87 and lead is element 82, how many alpha and beta plus decays happen in this decay chain, assuming these are the only two types of decay?
ANSWER: 1 alpha, 3 beta plus
30. BONUS Biology Short Answer After helicase separates a DNA strand during replication, what proteins prevent the separated strands from reannealing or being degraded by nucleases?
ANSWER: Single-stranded binding proteins
31. BONUS Math Short Answer The range of the function f(x) = 3 sin x − 4 cos x is a closed interval. What is the length of this interval?
ANSWER: 10
32. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Mr. Guisti hits a tennis ball such that its angular velocity points upwards. Because of its spin, in which of the following directions will the ball be deflected?
W) Left
X) Right
Y) Up
Z) It will not be deflected
ANSWER: W) Left
33. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If a reaction has an order of four, what are the units for the rate constant of this reaction?
ANSWER: L3/(mol3 * s)
34. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the structure and formation of spiral arms in galaxies?
W) Spiral arms are fixed structures composed of Population II stars orbiting together
X) Spiral arms are temporary features formed solely by differential rotation of the galaxy
Y) Spiral arms are regions of high density gas composed of young star-forming regions
Z) Spiral arms are created by tidal interactions that strip material from nearby galaxies
ANSWER: Y) Spiral arms are regions of high density gas composed of young star-forming regions
35. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Alex is investigating the MYC proto-oncogene family, and finds that the C-MYC, N-MYC, and L-MYC genes are paralogs. Which of the following events most likely led to the formation of these genes?
W) Speciation separated populations carrying slightly different versions of the MYC gene
X) Horizontal gene transfer introduced additional MYC-like genes from other species
Y) An ancestral proto-oncogene duplicated within the genome
Z) Independent point mutations in the MYC gene led to multiple functionally distinct variants in the same genome
ANSWER: Y) An ancestral proto-oncogene duplicated within the genome
36. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the coefficient of the x to the fourth term in the Taylor series expansion of tangent of x about x=0?
ANSWER: 0
37. BONUS Physics Short Answer Filip is 3 meters tall in his own reference frame. If Yajur moves at 0.8 times the speed of light directly upwards, in meters, how tall will Filip appear to Yajur?
ANSWER: 9/5 (also accept: 1.8)
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The orbital velocities of stars in the Milky Way are measured. As distance from the galactic center increases, velocity
W) Increases, then decreases
X) Decreases, then increases
Y) Increases initially, then remains constant
Z) Decreases initially, then remains constant
ANSWER: Y) Increases initially, then remains constant
39. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer With a value between 0 and 1, which variable in the formula for a planet's equilibrium temperature is used as a "fudge factor" for a greenhouse effect, and represents how effectively the planet radiates its energy?
ANSWER: EMISSIVITY
