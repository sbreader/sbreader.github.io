1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Felix is a dice-rolling genius. He rolls a 5 on a dice with probability 3/7. What is the expected number of rolls it takes to roll 6 5s?
ANSWER: 14
1. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the probability that there is a 1 in one of the digits of a two-digit number?
ANSWER: 0.8
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the location where an earthquake begins known as?
ANSWER: FOCUS (also accept: HYPOCENTER)
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of igneous rock texture has a fine grained texture around a coarse-grained texture due to crystallization in the magma chamber and eruption into the atmosphere mid-crystallization?
ANSWER: PORPHYRY (also accept: PORPHYRITIC)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following species is the conjugate acid of ammonia, NH3?
W) N2
X) H2
Y) NH2-
Z) NH4+
ANSWER: Z) NH4+
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify by name or number, all of the following three compounds that have "boat" and "chair" configurations? 1) Glucose 2) Water 3) Cyclohexane 4) Toluene
ANSWER: 1 AND 3 (also accept: GLUCOSE AND CYCLOHEXANE)
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The distribution of the charge density on the surface of a conducting solid depends upon which of the following properties of the conductor?
W) the density
X) the shape
Y) the size
Z) the age
ANSWER: X) the shape
4. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A roll of gold toilet paper is suspended from a wall by a frictionless axle. Someone pulls on the roll and lets go. Which of the following will happen next?
W) the paper will unroll at a constant speed
X) the paper will unroll at a decreasing speed
Y) the paper will unroll at an increasing speed
Z) the paper will immediately stop unrolling
ANSWER: Y) the paper will unroll at an increasing speed
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What mining method uses water to excavate, transport, concentrate, and recover heavy minerals from alluvial deposits?
ANSWER: PLACER (also accept: MINING)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Milankovitch cycles are caused by variations in which of the following? 1) Precession 2) Obliquity 3) Seasons 4) Eccentricity 5) Sunspots
ANSWER: 1,2,4
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Select all of the following four hormones that are secreted by the duodenum when chyme arrives from the stomach 1) Gastrin 2) Secretin 3) Leptin 4) Cholecystokinin.
ANSWER: 2 and 4
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What branch of the peripheral nervous system exerts control on the digestive system independently of the central nervous system?
ANSWER: ENTERIC NERVOUS SYSTEM
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following occurs when an electron in a hydrogen atom changes from an excited energy state to a more stable energy state?
W) electromagnetic radiation is emitted by the atom
X) electromagnetic radiation is absorbed by the atom
Y) the atom becomes a positively charged ion
Z) the atom becomes a negatively charged ion
ANSWER: W) electromagnetic radiation is emitted by the atom
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What are the coefficients of each compound in the combustion of 1 mole of hexane. Name each compound, not its chemical formula, and its coefficient and use fractions, if needed.
ANSWER: HEXANE-1, OXYGEN-19/2, CARBON DIOXIDE-6, WATER-7
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Berkeley’s Water Energy Resilience Research Institute have been researching how to increase the recharge rate of aquifers. What aquifer, the largest in the US, may be of special concern to the researchers?
ANSWER: OGALLALA AQUIFER
8. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Berkeley’s Water Energy Resilience Research Institute have created a solar umbrella to increase the wavelength of sunlight before it interacts with evaporation ponds. Which of the following is most likely an application of this technology?
W) Prevent dangerous secondary waste products from being formed by UV radiation
X) Increase the rate of evaporation in the ponds to reduce waste to a solid state
Y) Promote growth of pond organism in the pond that will process waste products
Z) Inhibits evaporation in the ponds that might introduce waste products into the atmosphere
ANSWER: X) Increase the rate of evaporation in the ponds to reduce waste to a solid state
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer You observe a winter twig and notice that it has several scars that are spaced along its length. You correctly identify these as bud scars, which consists of whorls of scales that enclose the apical bud for part of the year. If the twig has 6 such bud scars, how many years did it take for the twig to grow to its current size?
ANSWER: 7

W) Cyanobacteria
X) Alphaproteobacteria
Y) Neisseria
Z) Bartonella

W) A Horizon
X) E Horizon

Z) O Horizon
ANSWER: Z) O Horizon
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What class of neutron stars have extremely powerful magnetic fields?
ANSWER: MAGNETAR
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer This device was also known as an “electron gun.” It was used by J.J. Thompson in its namesake experiment. Name this high-vacuum tube which produces a luminous image on a fluorescent screen, and also used in television.
ANSWER: CATHODE RAY TUBE
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The transformation of shiny metallic beta tin to its less dense allotropic form known as grey tin or alpha tin, can be known as which of the following?
W) Tin transmutation
X) Tin pest
Y) Gray's disease
Z) Tinnification
ANSWER: X) Tin pest
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Two long current carrying conductors are placed in parallel 2 inches apart. If a DC current flows in the same direction through each conductor, which of the following is true?
W) The conductors attract each other
X) The conductors repel each other
Y) No force exerts on both conductors
Z) None of the above
ANSWER: W) The conductors attract each other
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer A team of skydivers jumps from a plane and holds hands to form a flower-like design. As the skydivers begin their free fall which of the following, if any, will happen next? 1) Acceleration decreases 2) Velocity increases 3) Acceleration remains constant
ANSWER: 1 AND 2

X) Convert sunlight into shorter wavelengths of light with higher energy

ANSWER: 0 AND 2

X) Pointing straight up from the surface of the disk
Y) Pointing parallel with the surface of the table
Z) No direction
ANSWER: X) Pointing straight up from the surface of the disk

W) moving with the field.

Z) moving at a 90° angle to the field.
ANSWER: Z) moving at a 90° angle to the field.

21. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is not true regarding waves?
W) Destructive interference creates nodes.
X) Diffraction occurs when a wave bends around an obstacle.
