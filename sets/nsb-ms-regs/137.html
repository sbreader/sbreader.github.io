1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Why is hair a good candidate for conducting sequencing of ancient DNA?
W) Hair contains high quantity DNA
X) Keratin in hair protects DNA from degradation
Y) The presence of melanin yields better sequencing data
Z) DNA in hair is free from mutations
ANSWER: X) Keratin in hair protects DNA from degradation
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What metabolic pathway that produces ATP is common to both aerobic cellular respiration and alcoholic fermentation?
ANSWER: GLYCOLYSIS

X) Grasses
Y) Pine trees
Z) Chrysanthemums and zinnias
ANSWER: W) Peas and beans

ANSWER: 2π

4. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice When calculating the efficiency of a solar collector, it is very important to know the incident angle. The angle depends directly on which of the following?

4. BONUS Energy Short Answer A 100-watt incandescent light bulb runs for 2000 hours. A compact fluorescent light (CFL) bulb of the same light output also runs for 2000 hours. ANSWER: $16.00
