1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice During V(D)J recombination, which of the following proteins, together with RAG2, catalyzes cleavage at recombination signal sequences to initiate double-strand breaks?

1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What specialized type of macrophages, found in the fenestrated capillaries of the liver, make up around 80 to 90% of all fixed macrophages throughout the body?
ANSWER: Kupffer cells
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following 4 structural classifications of electron-deficient structural compounds by increasing count of skeletal electron pairs 1) Nido 2) Closo 3) Hypho 4) Arachno
ANSWER: 2, 1, 4, 3
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following four types of stereochemical assignment that could correctly designate the absolute conformation of an atropisomer 1) Ra and Sa 2) Ea and Za 3) P and M 4) Δ and Λ
ANSWER: 1, 3, 4
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three changes that favor widespread flooding of continental interiors by epeiric seas 1) Faster seafloor spreading 2) Decreased continental ice volume 3) Broad uplift of continental interiors
ANSWER: 1, 2
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In the spectra of sufficiently high-redshift quasars, neutral intergalactic hydrogen removes nearly all flux immediately blueward of Lyman-alpha. What absorption feature results from this effect?
ANSWER: Gunn-Peterson trough
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is 245 squared?
ANSWER: 60025

ANSWER: 100
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The first step in the production of a LASER beam involves a process in which a single photon of a target wavelength is emitted and passes by excited atoms, causing them to release photons of the same wavelength, which begins a chain reaction to amplify the light. What is the term for this process?
ANSWER: Stimulated emission
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following five discoveries that were made at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory 1) The charm quark 2) The discovery of the quark composition of protons and neutrons 3) The tau lepton 4) The muon lepton 5) The pion meson
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3

Y) Facial
Z) Vestibulocochlear
ANSWER: Z) Vestibulocochlear
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer During eukaryotic translation elongation, what GTP-binding factor delivers aminoacyl-tRNA to the ribosomal A site?
ANSWER: eEF1A (also accept: Eukaryotic elongation factor 1-alpha)
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term describes a polymerization reaction between two immiscible phases at the point of contact?
ANSWER: Interfacial polymerization
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A coordination chemist wants to compare the electronic term energies of a transition-metal ion as ligand-field strength changes. What diagram plots these energies against ligand-field strength, with the axes commonly normalized by a Racah parameter?
ANSWER: Tanabe-Sugano diagram
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A volcanic load bends the lithosphere elastically. If effective elastic thickness increases while the load remains unchanged, which of the following changes generally occurs to the wavelength of the flexural response?
W) It decreases
X) It increases
Y) It remains constant while only amplitude changes
Z) It approaches zero
ANSWER: X) It increases
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Repeated satellite passes can measure ground deformation by comparing the phase of synthetic-aperture radar signals acquired at different times. What remote-sensing technique uses this method?
ANSWER: Interferometric synthetic aperture radar (also accept: InSAR)
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the 2026th digit after the decimal point of the base-4 expansion of 29/63?

9. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the integral from 0 to π/4 of the quantity sin(x) times tan(x) times sec(x)?
ANSWER: 1-π/4

ANSWER: 1, 2, 3, 4
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer An electron is moving in a circular path within a cyclotron and is generating a total emissive power P. If its path radius is halved and its velocity is doubled, but remains far below c, what is its new power in terms of P?
ANSWER: 64P

ANSWER: 1, 2
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Commonly seen in passionflower plants and Heliconius butterflies, passionflower plants have overtime evolved small yellow outgrowths known as stipules to mimic Heliconius eggs, causing butterflies to avoid laying their eggs on their leaves. This relationship is commonly known as what protective type of mimicry?
ANSWER: Gilbertian mimicry
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Skyler couples an aryl bromide with a phenylboronic acid using a palladium catalyst and base. What named carbon-carbon cross-coupling reaction is he performing?
ANSWER: Suzuki coupling (also accept: Suzuki-Miyaura coupling)
12. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice A transition-metal complex undergoes oxidative addition. Which of the following changes generally occur simultaneously?
W) Oxidation state decreases and coordination number decreases
X) Oxidation state increases and coordination number increases
Y) Oxidation state increases and coordination number decreases
Z) Oxidation state is unchanged while coordination number increases
ANSWER: X) Oxidation state increases and coordination number increases

W) Increasing carbon-to-oxygen ratio
X) Increasing iron-to-hydrogen ratio

Z) Conversion of the envelope primarily into helium
