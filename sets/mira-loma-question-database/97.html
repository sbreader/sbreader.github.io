1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the name for the valve in the human heart that allows blood to flow from the left atrium to the left ventricle
W) Aortic Valve
X) Tricuspid Valve
Y) Mitral Valve
Z) Pulmonary Valve
ANSWER: Y) Mitral Valve
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the smallest subdivisions of veins called
ANSWER: VENULES
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Seals, sea lions, and walruses are incorporated into what superfamily of mammals
ANSWER: PINNIPEDS
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What organelle commonly found in animal cells is a regulator of the progression of the cell-cycle
W) Centrosome
X) Lysosome
Y) Ribosome
Z) Vesicle
ANSWER: W) Centrosome
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the name for the muscle that covers the area where the shoulder blade is connected to the arm
W) Triceps
X) Deltoid
Y) Biceps
Z) Rhomboid
ANSWER: X) Deltoid
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Of the three types of muscles, which one is under voluntary control from the brain
ANSWER: SKELETAL MUSCLE
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Give the common name for the following bones of the human skeleton; coccyx, mandible, clavicle
ANSWER: TAIL BONE, JAW BONE, COLLARBONE
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the name for the region of the vertebrae that comes in contact with the skull
W) Lumbar
X) Pelvic
Y) Cervical
Z) Thoracic
ANSWER: Y) Cervical
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the outcome of a white haired short tailed dog being produced in the F2 generation, if one of the original parents displayed the dominant gene of brown hair and the recessive gene of a short tail, while the other displayed the recessive gene of white hair and the dominant gene of a long tail
W) 1/2
X) 1/4
Y) 1/8
Z) 1/16
ANSWER: Z) 1/16
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name for the change in relative frequency of a gene variant in a population due to random sampling and choice
ANSWER: GENETIC DRIFT
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The body's natural defense system, antibodies are triggered by what substances that are indications of an invasion of foreign material such as bacteria and viruses
ANSWER: ANTIGENS
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What common blood type is lacking in all antibodies
W) A
X) B
Y) O
Z) AB
ANSWER: Z) AB
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the name for the type of muscle contraction in which muscles shorten while generating force
W) Isotonic
X) Isometric
Y) Eccentric
Z) Concentric
ANSWER: Z) Concentric
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Muscle contractions result in the movement of actin over what filament
ANSWER: MYOSIN
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Using taxonomical classification, rank humans from their kingdom to their species
ANSWER: ANIMALIA, CHORDATA, MAMMALIA, PRIMATES, HOMINIDAE, HOMO, H. SAPIENS or HOMO SAPIENS
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What structure is responsible for the regulation of water concentration in the kidneys
W) Inferior Renal Capsule
X) Superior Renal Capsule
Y) Nephron
Z) Minor Calyx
ANSWER: Y) Nephron
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Occurring in the cytosol, glucose is converted to what compound in a process that releases energy crucial to the development of ATP and NADH
W) Sucrose
X) Pyruvate
Y) Fructose
Z) Lactate
ANSWER: X) Pyruvate
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Unlike animal cells, plant vacuoles are quite large and are surrounded by what membrane
ANSWER: TONOPLAST
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name for the cycle that encompasses the conversion of lactate to glucose through anaerobic glycolysis
ANSWER: CORI CYCLE
10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Movement of the neck is most often the result of the contraction and extension of which muscle
W) Rotator Cuff
X) Trapezius
Y) Levator Scapulae
Z) Deltoid
ANSWER: Y) Levator Scapulae
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What type of structure covers the ends of bones to form the smooth surface of joints
W) Cartilage
X) Elastic Cartilage
Y) Hyaline Cartilage
Z) Fibrocartilage
ANSWER: Y) Hyaline Cartilage
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the common name for the top most vertebrae of the human spinal cord located in the cervical region
ANSWER: ATLAS
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the topmost section of the small intestine from which foot exits the stomach
ANSWER: DUODENUM
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What type of immune system cell can recognize pathogens without the need for antigen processing
W) B Cells
X) T Cells
Y) Macrophages
Z) White Blood Cells
ANSWER: W) B Cells
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What cardiovascular structure brings deoxygenated blood from the lower half of the body to the heart
W) Superior Vena Cava
X) Inferior Vena Cava
Y) Femoral Vein
Z) Pulmonary Vein
ANSWER: X) Inferior Vena Cava
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer What nervous system cell is responsible for holding neurons in place, supplying oxygen and nutrients to them, insulating one neuron from another, and removing pathogens and dead neurons
ANSWER: GLIAL CELLS
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name for the structure that is responsible for the oxygenation of blood
ANSWER: ALVEOLI
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the name for the series of structures that cover the axon and are in fact an outgrowth of glial cells
W) Schwann Cells
X) Node of Ranvier
Y) Myelin Sheath
Z) Dendrites
ANSWER: Y) Myelin Sheath
23. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the taxonomical order of the only type of mammals that lay eggs
W) Monotremes
X) Marsupials
Y) Eutherians
Z) None of the Above
ANSWER: W) Monotremes
