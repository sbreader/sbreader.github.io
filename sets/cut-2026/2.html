1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the amount of ATP invested during the energy investment phase and produced during the payoff phase of glycolysis, respectively?
W) 2 ATP, 2 ATP
X) 2 ATP, 4 ATP
Y) 4 ATP, 2 ATP
Z) 4 ATP, 4 ATP
ANSWER: X) 2 ATP, 4 ATP
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following three steps in corresponding order of the central dogma 1) Translation 2) DNA replication 3) Transcription
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Combustion reactions always involve which of the following reactants?
W) hydrogen
X) carbon
Y) oxygen
Z) water
ANSWER: Y) oxygen
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 3 diatomic bonds in decreasing bond length 1) nitrogen-nitrogen bond 2) oxygen-oxygen bond 3) fluorine-fluorine bond
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer System A is in thermal equilibrium with system B, and system B is in thermal equilibrium with system C. What law of thermodynamics allows one to conclude that systems A and C are also in thermal equilibrium?
ANSWER: zeroth law of thermodynamics
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer What phenomenon occurs when two waves overlap such that their displacements are opposite in phase, causing the resulting amplitude to be reduced or canceled at certain points?
ANSWER: destructive interference
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What stream of charged particles released from the Sun causes auroras in the night sky?
ANSWER: solar wind
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Sun’s proton-proton chain is a fusion process that converts what element into what other element?
W) hydrogen to helium
X) hydrogen to lithium
Y) helium to carbon
Z) helium to oxygen
ANSWER: W) hydrogen to helium
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A geometric sequence has third term 6 and 6th term 48. What is the common ratio?
ANSWER: 2
5. BONUS Math Multiple Choice The ratio of the radii of two circles is 2:3. Which of the following is the ratio of their areas?
W) 2:3
X) 3:2
Y) 4:9
Z) 9:4
ANSWER: Y) 4:9
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at SLAC are researching leptons. What class of near-massless particles, which undergo flavor oscillation, are they most likely studying?
ANSWER: neutrinos
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at the National Institute of Health in D.C. are examining how high-fat diets alter lipid metabolism by tracking the building blocks used to assemble triglycerides. Which of the following are monomers of triglycerides?
W) Amino acids
X) Fatty acids
Y) Nucleotides
Z) Monosaccharides
ANSWER: X) Fatty acids
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following protein structures refers to the unique, linear sequence of amino acids linked by peptide bonds?
W) primary
X) secondary
Y) tertiary
Z) quaternary
ANSWER: W) primary
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following sensory receptors are responsible for the sensation of pain?
W) baroreceptors
X) thermoreceptors
Y) chemoreceptors
Z) nociceptors
ANSWER: Z) nociceptors
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice J.J Thomson developed the plum-pudding model after his discovery of which of the following subatomic particles?
W) proton
X) neutron
Y) electron
Z) neutrino
ANSWER: Y) electron
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What principle states that when two waves overlap in space, the resulting displacement at any point is the algebraic sum of the displacements produced by each wave individually?
ANSWER: superposition
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 3 elements in increasing atomic radius 1) barium 2) fluorine 3) silicon
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer The electric force between two point charges is F. If the magnitude of each charge is doubled and the distance between them is tripled, what is the new electric force in terms of F?
ANSWER: 4F/9
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the minimum number of seismic stations required to accurately locate an earthquake's epicenter?
ANSWER: 3
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Located below the lithosphere, what weak layer of the Earth’s mantle displays high ductility?
ANSWER: asthenosphere
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The variable y varies inversely with x. If y = 4, when x = 3, find y when x = 6.
ANSWER: 2
11. BONUS Math Short Answer In a group of students, 30 take math, 26 take science, and 18 take both. If every student takes at least one subject, in total, how many students are there?
ANSWER: 38
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Lab are studying condensation polymers. What process involves the splitting of water to break the bonds in these polymers?
ANSWER: hydrolysis
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are studying materials with extremely high tensile strength that consist of a single layer of carbon atoms arranged in a hexagonal lattice. What material are these researchers studying?
ANSWER: graphene (do not accept: graphite)
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 nitrogenous bases in DNA that are considered purines 1) Adenine 2) Thymine 3) Cytosine 4) Guanine
ANSWER: 1 and 4
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following percentages is the chance that the child of a mother that is a carrier and father that is not a carrier does NOT develop hemophilia, an X-linked recessive trait?
W) 0%
X) 25%
Y) 50%
Z) 75%
ANSWER: Z) 75%
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In an electrochemical cell, what is the term for the region where oxidation occurs?
ANSWER: anode
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 measurements that are units of pressure 1) pascal 2) atm 3) torr
ANSWER: all
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What component in an RLC circuit stores electric charge by separating positive and negative charges on two conductive plates?
ANSWER: Capacitor
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer What fluid mechanics principle states that a pressure applied to an enclosed, incompressible fluid is transmitted undiminished throughout the fluid and to the walls of its container?
ANSWER: pascals principle
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term is given to the upper layer of the ocean where enough sunlight penetrates for certain organisms to perform photosynthesis?
ANSWER: photic zone (also accept: euphotic zone; sunlit zone)
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 4 rocks in increasing metamorphic grade 1) schist 2) migmatite 3) phyllite 4) slate
ANSWER: 4, 3, 1, 2
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In Rehan’s factory, Machine A can complete a car in 6 hours. Machine B can complete the same car in 3 hours. If both machines work together, in hours, how long will it take to complete 1 car?
ANSWER: 2
17. BONUS Math Short Answer A fair six-sided die is rolled twice. What is the probability that both rolls are even?
ANSWER: 1/4
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists in the Doms Lab at the University of Pennsylvania are studying how cells rapidly change protein production in response to stress by analyzing molecules that carry genetic instructions from DNA to ribosomes. What molecule are they studying?
ANSWER: mRNA
18. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Researchers in the Marine Biological Laboratory at UChicago are studying how squid neurons transmit signals extremely quickly due to their unusually large axons. These signals travel as rapid changes in which of the following properties?
W) Membrane potential
X) pH gradient
Y) Osmotic pressure
Z) Light absorption
ANSWER: W) Membrane potential
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Snails with shells coiling clockwise cannot mate with those with shells coiling counterclockwise, demonstrating which of the following types of isolation?
W) gametic
X) behavioral
Y) mechanical
Z) temporal
ANSWER: Y) mechanical
19. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following accurately identifies a cancerous tumor on muscle cells?
W) Carcinoma
X) Sarcoma
Y) Adenoma
Z) Lymphoma
ANSWER: X) Sarcoma
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Glass is an example of what type of solid, which lacks the long-range, ordered atomic structure of crystalline solids?
ANSWER: amorphous
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 quantities that dilution changes 1) molarity 2) amount of solute 3) amount of solvent
ANSWER: 1 and 3
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What law states that the product of the refractive index of a medium and the sine of the angle the light makes with the normal is constant across the boundary?
ANSWER: Snell’s law
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 2 kilogram cart moving at 6 meters per second collides with and sticks to a 4 kilogram cart that is initially at rest on a frictionless track. In meters per second, what is the final speed of the carts after the collision?
ANSWER: 2
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of faults lacks significant vertical movement of rocks?
W) normal
X) reverse
Y) strike-slip
Z) oblique-slip
ANSWER: Y) strike-slip

X) low permeability, high porosity
Y) high permeability, low porosity
Z) low permeability, low porosity
ANSWER: W) high permeability, high porosity

W) 1
X) 2
Y) 3
Z) 4
ANSWER: X) 2

ANSWER: 6

In contrast to binary bits in classical computers, what fundamental unit of quantum computers allows for massive parallel processing?

24. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab are using statistical analyses to collect data on the energy output of nuclear reactions. Which of the following statements is true in a distribution that is skewed right?
W) the mean is greater than the median
X) the mean is smaller than the median
Y) the mean is equal to the median
Z) the relation cannot be determined
ANSWER: W) the mean is greater than the median
