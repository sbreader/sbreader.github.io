1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following methods of dissemination of bacterial genetic material is mismatched to its description?
W) Transduction: viral transfer of one cell’s DNA to another
X) Binary fission: cellular division

Z) Transformation: one bacteria engulfs another to integrate its DNA
