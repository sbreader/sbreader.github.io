1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which physicist won the Nobel Prize in Physics for his work on the photoelectric effect in 1923 and is credited for measuring Planck's constant by using photoelectric emission graphs?
W) Albert Einstein
X) Max Planck
Y) Robert Millikan
Z) Johannes Stark
ANSWER: Y) Robert Millikan
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Who worked with Robert Millikan on the famous oil drop experiment and is also credited with inventing the hearing aid?
ANSWER: HARVEY FLETCHER
2. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which American physicist invented the cyclotron and also worked with the Manhattan Project?
W) Robert Oppenheimer
X) Ernest Lawrence
Y) John Lawrence
Z) Edward Teller
ANSWER: X) Ernest Lawrence
2. BONUS Energy Short Answer NSCL is the largest nuclear science facility on a university campus and is a leading rare isotope research facility in the United States. What does NSCL, which is located on the campus of Michigan State University, stand for?
ANSWER: NATIONAL SUPERCONDUCTING CYCLOTRON LABORATORY
