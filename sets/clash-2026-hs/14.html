1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Louisiana State University are studying the self-propagation of surface plasmon polaritons across plasmonic metacrystals. Identify all of the following laws that act in the direct self-propagation of surface plasmon polaritons 1) Ampere-Maxwell law 2) Lorentz force law 3) Gauss's law of magnetism 4) Faraday's law of induction
ANSWER: 1 and 4
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer In superfluid helium-4, three distinct types of quasiparticles can be formed from elementary quantum excitations. What are these three quasiparticles?
ANSWER: Phonons, rotons, and maxons
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Order the following 4 values for the 3-dimensional vector 6i + 2j-3k 1) L0 norm 2) L1 norm 3) L2 norm 4) L-infinity norm
ANSWER: 1, 4, 3, 2
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Ziang is throwing darts at a circular dartboard with radius R. Assuming that the darts are randomly distributed, what is the expected value of the distance between the center of the dartboard and any given dart?
ANSWER: 2R/3
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In the synthesis pathway of thromboxanes, what class of bicyclic molecules serves as the intermediate stage in the conversion of arachidonic acid to thromboxanes started by cyclooxygenase?
ANSWER: Cyclic endoperoxides (also accept: Endoperoxides; Prostaglandin endoperoxides)
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 locations in which the enzyme chymotrypsin would likely be able to cleave 1) N-terminus of lysine 2) N-terminus of tryptophan 3) C-terminus of arginine
ANSWER: None
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In the Debye-Hückel limiting law, the mean ionic activity coefficient decreases as the square root of what solution property?
ANSWER: Ionic strength
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In NMR spectroscopy, while pure acetone is observed as a singlet, rather paradoxically, deuterated acetone can appear as a multiplet due to a residual proton signal arising from proton exchange with a non-deuterated species. What is the name of this multiplet?
ANSWER: Quintet
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term best describes needle-like ice in glacial lakes that forms in supercooled water?
ANSWER: Frazil ice

9. VISUAL BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Shown in the image are two organic molecules. The two molecules shown can both spontaneously cyclize, although at varying rates. 10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Consider a hypothetical planet, MaryLand, which is identical to Earth. If all the ice on MaryLand were to melt, which of the following best describes how the changed thermohaline circulation would affect the Redfield ratio?
