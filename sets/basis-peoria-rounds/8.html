1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of inhibitor directly competes with an enzyme by binding to the active site?
ANSWER: Competitive inhibitor

ANSWER: Saturated
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Find the center of mass of a cone with a circular base of radius 3, centered at the origin, and an apex at (0, 12, 0). Give your answer in the form (x, y, z).
ANSWER: (0, 3, 0)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Provide the molar mass of water (include units).

ANSWER: Z) Magnetic quadrupole

Y) Laplace’s equation
