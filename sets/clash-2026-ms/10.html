1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of dietary fats have a double bond in their hydrocarbon chain, causing them to have a kinked structure and are thus found in liquid form at room temperature?
W) Saturated
X) Unsaturated
Y) Trans
Z) Omega-3
ANSWER: X) Unsaturated
1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A flame test was performed to confirm the identity of a metal ion in solution. The result was a green flame. Which of the following metal ions is indicated?
W) copper
X) Sodium
Y) Strontium
Z) Zinc
ANSWER: W) copper
1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for a large ice platform floating on the ocean and extending out from a glacier?
ANSWER: Ice shelf
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer While cells typically contain a large mass of DNA in either the nucleus or nucleoid, what small circular DNA molecules freely float in the cytoplasm?
ANSWER: Plasmids
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The Rutherford Gold Foil experiment disproved J.J. Thomson’s plum pudding model of the atom, as some alpha particles deflected off of the gold foil. Which model of the atom was then proposed based on this observation?
ANSWER: Nuclear model
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What fossils are formed when volcanic ash rapidly buries trees, which then come into contact with mineral-rich groundwater, allowing for the precipitation of chalcedony?
