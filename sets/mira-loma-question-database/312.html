Packet 312 (Science Biology Campbell Questions Chapters 8-13) of Mira Loma Question Database:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the two main staples in the cellular membranes?
ANSWER: LIPIDS AND PROTEINS
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What term is used to describe molecules that possess a hydrophilic and hydrophobic region?
ANSWER: AMPHIPATIC
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In 1917, Irving Langmuir created artificial membranes by adding phospholipids dissolved in what substance to water?
ANSWER: BENZENE
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In 1925, what two Dutch Scientists hypothesized that the cell membrane is composed of a phospholipid bilayer?
ANSWER: E. GORTER AND F. GRENDEL
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The most widely accepted model of the cell membrane today, the "fluid mosaic model" was designed by what two scientists?
ANSWER: S. J. SINGER AND G. NICOLSON
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When comparing two solutions with unequal concentrations, the solution with the lower solute concentration is said to be
ANSWER: HYPOTONIC
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Solutions with the same solute concentration are referred to as
ANSWER: ISOTONIC
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Plant cells become turgid when in what type of environment?
ANSWER: HYPOTONIC
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term used for a protein that generates voltage across a membrane?
ANSWER: ELECTROGENIC
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the main electrogenic pump in plants?
ANSWER: PROTON PUMP
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the free energy change of the electron transfer from NADH to oxygen?
ANSWER: -53 KCAL/MOL (-222KJ/MOL)
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Glycolysis occurs in what part of the cell?
ANSWER: CYTOSOL
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What mode of ATP synthesis accounts for almost 90% of the ATP generated by respiration?
ANSWER: OXIDATIVE PHOSPHORYLATION
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer About how many molecules of ATP are produced for every molecule of glucose that undergoes respiration?
ANSWER: 38
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What enzyme first bonds to glucose in glycolysis to produce Glucose-6-phosphate?
ANSWER: HEXOKINASE
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In the final reaction of glycolysis, what molecule is converted into pyruvate through the introduction of pyruvate kinase?
ANSWER: PHOSPHOENOLPYRUVATE
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Glycolysis, literally meaning the "splitting of sugar", is named after the reaction in which what enzyme splits the six carbon sugar into two three carbon sugars?
ANSWER: ALDOLASE
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Although the splitting of Fructose-1, 6-biphosphate produces two different sugars, only one of the sugars is actually used to continue glycolysis. However, a reversible conversion exists between the two sugars, allowing to all be converted to the needed sugar. What is the name of the enzyme that catalyzes this reaction?
ANSWER: ISOMERASE
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In the Krebs Cycle, the addition of Acetyl CoA to what molecule creates Citrate?
ANSWER: OXALOACETATE
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The cells of what organ function to convert the lactate produced by lactic acid fermentation to pyruvate?
ANSWER: LIVER
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In Glycolysis, when Phosphoglyceromutase is introduced to 3-Phosphoglycerate, what molecule is produced?
ANSWER: 2-PHOSPHOGLYCERATE
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The features of what process in cellular respiration imply that cells evolved to perform it very early in the history of life?
ANSWER: GLYCOLYSIS
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name for the process in which an amino group is removed from amino acids?
ANSWER: DEAMINATION
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Between how many chloroplasts does a typical mesophyll cell contain?
ANSWER: 30-40
26. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What term is given to light reactions that provide the energy necessary to convert ADP to ATP?
ANSWER: PHOTOPHOSPHORYLATION
27. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In what part of the chloroplast do the light reactions of photosynthesis occur?
ANSWER: THYLAKOIDS
28. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The Calvin cycle occurs in what part of the chloroplast?
ANSWER: STROMA
29. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In nanometers, between what hundreds is the most energy absorbed by the chloroplast pigments?
ANSWER: 400 TO 500 NANOMETERS
30. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What scientist was able to demonstrate the trend of the absorption spectra by measuring the growth of aerobic bacteria placed on a filamentous alga exposed to light separated by passing it through a prism?
ANSWER: ENGELMANN
31. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Of the three types of chloroplast pigments, which type is able to absorb the greatest amount of green light?
ANSWER: CAROTENOIDS
32. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Of the three types of chloroplast pigment, what is the only pigment that can contribute directly to the light reactions necessary to drive photosynthesis?
ANSWER: CHLOROPHYLL A
33. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In the Calvin cycle, the carbon dioxide is attached to what five-carbon sugar?
ANSWER: RIBULOSE BIPHOSPHATE (RuBP)
34. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What sugar is produced as the output of the Calvin Cycle?
ANSWER: GLYCERALDEHYDE-3-PHOSPHATE (G3P)
35. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer An important part of the Calvin cycle, what protein is the most abundant in chloroplasts, and also likely to be the most abundant on Earth?
ANSWER: RUBISCO (also accept: RuBP CARBOXYLASE; RIBULOSE BIPHOSPHATE CARBOXYLASE)
36. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer If 3 molecules of CO2 are added into the Calvin cycle, how many molecules of the G3P that acts as the end product of the process must remain in the cycle in order to regenerate the RuBP needed to continue the cycle?
ANSWER: 5 MOLECULES
37. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What term is used to describe plants in which the initial carbon fixation occurs via Rubisco?
ANSWER: C3 PLANTS
38. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Believed to be left over from early ancestors of plants, what is the name of the process where Rubisco accepts O2 in an attempt to produce CO2, despite the fact that it actually decreases photosynthetic output?
ANSWER: PHOTORESPIRATION
