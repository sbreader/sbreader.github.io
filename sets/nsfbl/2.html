W) IgA
X) IgE
Y) IgG
Z) IgM
ANSWER: Y) IgG

ANSWER: LEWIS (also accept: LEWIS ACID)

3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Given two point charges a distance d apart, if you double the charge of one of those charges, what factor must the distance be changed by to increase the force by a factor of 32?
ANSWER: 1/4
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Arin is applying a force on a stick of length 4 meters pivoted at the 2 meter mark. If he applies a 10 Newton force at the 3 meter mark of the stick, with an angle of 30 degrees between the force and lever arm, the rod accelerates from rest to 5 radians per second in 4 seconds. In kilogram meters squared, what is the moment of inertia of the rod?
ANSWER: 4
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following scenarios is LEAST likely to lead to the formation of a metamorphic rock?
W) Sandstone comes into contact with a hydrothermal fluid
X) Shale undergoes differential stress
Y) Limestone comes into contact with a still-hot granitic intrusion
Z) Limestone comes into contact with acidic rainwater in a cave
ANSWER: Z) Limestone comes into contact with acidic rainwater in a cave

ANSWER: ARTESIAN SPRING (also accept: FLOWING ARTESIAN)

14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Dan is observing the reaction between nitrogen monoxide gas, and hydrogen gas to form nitrogen gas and water vapor. He observed that when he TRIPLED the concentration of nitrogen monoxide, the rate of the reaction TRIPLED. Furthermore, he observed that when he DOUBLED the concentration of hydrogen gas, the rate of the reaction INCREASED BY A FACTOR OF 8. Based on this experimental data, what is the rate law for this reaction in terms of the reaction rate constant k and the concentrations of the substances?
ANSWER: Rate = k * [NO] * [H2]3
