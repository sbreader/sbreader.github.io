1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The phenotype of the typical form of a species as it occurs in nature, such as red eyes in Drosophila, is called the...
W) Mutant type
X) Wild type
Y) Dominant type
Z) Common type
ANSWER: X) Wild type
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In a given population of a species, the fur color gene is sex-linked. When a black furred male is crossed with a brown furred female, 80 percent of the offspring have black fur and 20 percent have brown fur. Then, the black fur allele must be...
W) Dominant
X) Recessive
Y) Wild type
Z) It cannot be determined
ANSWER: Z) It cannot be determined
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Given two hypothetical nonmetals X and Y, which of the following would not necessarily be true of the molecule CX3Y?
W) The carbon atom is sp2 hybridized
X) There is a sigma and pi bond between carbon and Y
Y) X has no lone electron pairs
Z) Y is in family 6A
ANSWER: Y) X has no lone electron pairs
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Given two hypothetical nonmetals X and Y, answer the following two questions about the hypothetical polyatomic ion SX2Y-2, where sulfur is the central atom and is single-bonded to one atom of X and one atom of Y, and double bonded to the other atom of X. I. What is the hybridization of sulfur? II. How many lone electron pairs do each atom of X and Y have?
ANSWER: I: SP3D; II: SINGLE-BONDED X HAS 3, DOUBLE-BONDED X HAS 2, AND Y HAS 3
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In quantum mechanics, which law states that the total energy radiated by a blackbody per unit surface area per unit time is directly proportional to the fourth power of the body's absolute temperature?
W) Stefan-Boltzmann Law
X) Wien's Displacement Law
Y) Planck's Law of Blackbody Radiation
Z) Kirchhoff's Law
ANSWER: W) Stefan-Boltzmann Law
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice The discrepancy between the experimental plot of the blackbody radiation spectrum and the theoretical shape of the curve is that...
W) Theory predicts that higher wavelengths have higher intensity
X) Theory predicts that lower wavelengths have higher intensity
Y) Theoretical predictions fail at very large temperatures
Z) Theoretical predictions fail at very low temperatures
ANSWER: X) Theory predicts that lower wavelengths have higher intensity

7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which procedure, developed by British biochemist Frederick Sanger in 1977, allows the determination of a particular DNA sequence by placing the denatured DNA fragments into a test tube and adding both deoxyribonucleotides and dideoxyribonucleotides, and then ordering the fragments by length by a laser to determine the terminating base?
ANSWER: DIDEOXYRIBONUCLEOTIDE (DIDEOXY) CHAIN TERMINATION METHOD
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A gas chamber contains sulfur dioxide and oxygen with a partial pressure of 0.20 atmospheres each and sulfur trioxide with a partial pressure of 0.10 atmospheres. Find the equilibrium quotient for the reaction of sulfur dioxide with oxygen to form sulfur trioxide.
ANSWER: 1.25 ATM-1

W) Einstein
X) Hertz
Y) Maxwell
Z) Planck
ANSWER: X) Hertz
9. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following features of the photoelectric effect can be explained with classical physics or the wave theory of light?
W) No electrons are emitted if the light frequency falls below a cutoff frequency
X) The maximum kinetic energy of the electrons is independent of the light intensity
Y) Electrons do not have a maximum frequency at which they are emitted
Z) Electrons are emitted from the surface almost instantaneously
ANSWER: Y) Electrons do not have a maximum frequency at which they are emitted
