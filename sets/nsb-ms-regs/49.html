1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name of the point directly overhead for an observer viewing the night sky?
W) Zenith
X) Nadir
Y) Horizon
Z) Ecliptic
ANSWER: W) Zenith
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three planets that have rings 1) Jupiter 2) Neptune 3) Uranus
ANSWER: 1 and 2 and 3
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory are studying particles called tetraquarks. How many constituent quarks are in a tetraquark?
ANSWER: 4
2. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Scientists at Fermi National Accelerator Lab working on the Dark Energy Survey have measured a cosmological quantity to be 0.776, with a standard error of 0.017. Assuming normality, which of the following best describes their claim?
W) The value of the quantity definitely lies between 0.759 and 0.793
X) Throughout the universe, the value of the quantity fluctuates between 0.759 and 0.793
Y) With a probability of 68%, the value of the quantity lies between 0.759 and 0.793
Z) Their instruments recorded a value of 0.776, but they know the instrument can make errors of up to 0.017
ANSWER: Y) With a probability of 68%, the value of the quantity lies between 0.759 and 0.793
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the leading coefficient of the polynomial 6x3 – 2x2 + 7x – 9?
ANSWER: 6
3. BONUS Math Short Answer A restaurant offers a combo consisting of the diner’s choice of a soda, an appetizer, an entrée, and a dessert, to be selected from 6 kinds of soda, 4 appetizers, 7 entrees, and 3 desserts. How many combos are possible?
ANSWER: 504
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following types of quantities appears in a free-body diagram?
W) Energy
X) Force
Y) Momentum
Z) Acceleration
ANSWER: X) Force
4. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice The specific gravity of a substance is most directly related to which of the following properties?
W) Density
X) Volume
Y) Mass
Z) Moment of inertia
ANSWER: W) Density
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Looking at a picture of all of the chromosomes in a cell can help in the identification of mutations and abnormalities. What is this type of picture called?
W) Pedigree
X) DNA fingerprint
Y) Genotype
Z) Karyotype
ANSWER: Z) Karyotype
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the middle portion of the arthropod body to which the wings are usually attached?
ANSWER: THORAX
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Mendeleev organized the elements according to their reactivity. Which of the following elements would be grouped with lithium?
W) Magnesium
X) Chlorine
Y) Sodium
Z) Calcium
ANSWER: Y) Sodium
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the empirical formula of the compound formed by aluminum and chlorine?
ANSWER: AlCl3
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Gas giant planets are primarily composed of what two elements?
W) Hydrogen and nitrogen
X) Hydrogen and helium
Y) Helium and nitrogen
Z) Hydrogen and oxygen
ANSWER: X) Hydrogen and helium
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A lunar eclipse will occur when which of the following happens?
W) The moon blocks light from the sun
X) The side of the moon facing Earth is completely dark
Y) The full moon moves through Earth’s shadow
Z) The moon is fully lit by the sun
ANSWER: Y) The full moon moves through Earth’s shadow
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab are studying B fields in plasma. What is the more common term for a B field?
W) Electric field
X) Magnetic field
Y) Strong field
Z) Weak field
ANSWER: X) Magnetic field
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab are studying the element that is most abundant by mass in Earth’s core. What is this element?
W) Oxygen
X) Silicon
Y) Magnesium
Z) Iron
ANSWER: Z) Iron
