3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Curie’s law states that the magnetization of a paramagnetic sample is equal to the magnetic susceptibility times what quantity?
ANSWER: EXTERNAL MAGNETIC FIELD (also accept: APPLIED MAGNETIC FIELD; MAGNETIC FIELD)
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the name for devices with a single p-n junction which convert light into electrical current?
ANSWER: PHOTODIODE
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three materials in terms of decreasing conductivity 1) Silicon 2) Graphene 3) Glass.
ANSWER: 2,1,3
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the term for a Taylor series of a function centered at x = 0?
ANSWER: MACLAURIN SERIES
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab have been developing methods for performing artificial photosynthesis. In photosynthesis, the photolysis of water occurs in what first protein complex of the light-dependent reactions?
ANSWER: PHOTOSYSTEM 2
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three steps that are part of a Carnot cycle 1) Adiabatic expansion 2) Isobaric expansion 3) Isothermal expansion.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Aluminum oxide is able to react with both acids and bases, producing salts in both reactions. What is the term for this kind of compound?
ANSWER: AMPHOTERIC
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A group under addition consists of the integers mod 10. This group is considered abelian because addition has what property?
ANSWER: COMMUTATIVITY (also accept: COMMUTATIVE)
15. BONUS Math Short Answer A right trapezoid ABCD has bases AB and CD, and angles ABC and BCD are each 90 degrees. Side BC has length 16, side DA has length 20, and the longer base has length 31. What is the length of the shorter base?
ANSWER: 19
17. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Researchers at Argonne National Lab have been looking for cost effective ways to enrich molybdenum isotopes. They have developed a method that used parts such as acrylic contractors, which were produced via what technique?
ANSWER: 3D PRINTING
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer According to the Stefan-Boltzmann law, the total energy emitted from a blackbody is equal to the Stefan-Boltzmann constant times the fourth power of what quantity?
ANSWER: TEMPERATURE
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer What effect occurs when spectral lines are split in the presence of a magnetic field?
ANSWER: ZEEMAN EFFECT
19. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following numbers is abundant?
W) 6
X) 10
Y) 12
Z) 16
ANSWER: Y) 12
19. BONUS Math Short Answer Compute the integral from 0 to 2π of sine squared of x d x.
ANSWER: π
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What class of sweet smelling organic compound is formed from the condensation reaction of a carboxylic acid and an alcohol?
ANSWER: ESTER
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In gram staining, what is the most common dye used to counterstain crystal violet?
ANSWER: SAFRANIN
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer PYY is a hormone that suppresses the action of what hormone released by the walls of the stomach to stimulate hunger?
ANSWER: GHRELIN
