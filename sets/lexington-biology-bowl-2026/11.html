1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following dopamine pathways involves the release of dopamine from the VTA to the nucleus accumbens?

1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Anti-POMCase is a synthetic protein that inhibits the proteolytic cleavage of POMC. Which of the following would not be expected in a person who receives a treatment of Anti-POMCase?

X) Hypopigmentation of the skin
Y) Hyperkalemia in the blood
Z) Obesity

W) Branching enzyme
X) Debranching enzyme
Y) Glycogen phosphorylase
Z) Glycogen synthase

3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Katherine is studying a bunch of interconnected glial cells in-vitro and notices particularly low concentrations of K+ in the local CSF. W) Ependymal cells
X) Astrocytes
Y) Oligodendrocytes
Z) Schwann cells
ANSWER: X) Astrocytes

4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Giant viruses such as Mimiviruses often contain many unusual genes dervived from ancient hosts, such as those involved in glycolysis. Selena observes a mimiviruses containing a gene that produces a heme-containing enzyme responsible for processing drugs, steroids, and other hydrophobic compounds to make them more hydrophillic. What is the likely identity of the family of genes she is observing?
ANSWER: CYTOCHROME P450 (also accept: CYPS)
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer William shrinks himself and travels through one of Allen's nephrons. Allen has had some problems recently and decides to solve them by consuming diuretics. ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer While the optic nerve projects to the lateral geniculate nucleus, the vestibulocochlear nerve projects to which thalamic relay nucleus before reaching the auditory cortex?
ANSWER: MEDIAL GENICULATE NUCLEUS (also accept: SUPERIOR OLIVE; INFERIOR COLLICULI)
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Bartholomew received the sequence ACGCGG. Identify all of the following three statements that are true about this sequence 1) Taken as a DNA sequence, this would be recognized palindromically by Type IIP Restriction Enzymes 2) Taken as an amino acid sequence, this sequence would likely be found in the transmembrane alpha helices of GPCRs 3) Taken as a DNA sequence, this region would likely be detected by TLR9
ANSWER: 3 only
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In contrast to the brain, which utilizes the malate-aspartate shuttle, skeletal muscles instead couple NADH and FADH2 in a less efficient manner, producing 1.5 ATP produced per NADH. This coupling is done by what shuttle?
ANSWER: GLYCEROL 3 PHOSPHATE SHUTTLE
