1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The pressure on an object at 4 meters below surface level in a pool on a strange planet is 130 × 104 pascals. Assuming that the pressure at the surface of the pool is 10 × 104 pascals, and that the density of water is 100 kilograms per meter cubed, and giving your answer in newtons, what is the magnitude of the force of gravity on this planet?
ANSWER: 3000 (also accept: 3000 N/kg)
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer As a cat falls out of a plane, the drag force on it increases by 1 centinewton per second. Assuming that the force of gravity on the cat is 9.8 meters per second, how many seconds will it take for the cat to be falling at terminal velocity?
ANSWER: 980 SECONDS
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following features in the Proton NMR spectrum provides information about the number of types of different protons in a compound?
W) Integral
X) Number of signals
Y) Splitting
Z) Chemical shift
ANSWER: X) Number of signals
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many equivalent sets of protons are there in 1-methylhexane?
ANSWER: 7
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab are conducting large eddy simulations of offshore boundary layers to study the properties of eddies. Which of the following factors do NOT affect their frictional influence?
W) Surface heating
X) Strong wind speeds
Y) Contact area between the bodies
Z) Roughness of the landscape
ANSWER: Y) Contact area between the bodies
3. BONUS Energy Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Lab are studying the role of clays in mercury emission from soils. Mercury accumulation in plants causes phytotoxicity and impairs metabolic processes. Identify all of the following 3 statements that are true about mercury in plants 1) Signal transduction pathways are mobilized to neutralize the source of toxicity 2) Hg2+ is the most common form of mercury in agricultural soils 3) Plants lack the ability to escape from contaminated sites.
ANSWER: ALL OF THEM
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In the crystalloblastic series, porphyroblasts filled with inclusions of other minerals that were formed during metamorphosis are said to have what kind of texture?
ANSWER: POIKILOBLASTIC TEXTURE
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 statements regarding the TOV limit in stars that are true 1) The limit describes the maximum mass of a cold, nonrotating white dwarf 2) In stars less massive than the limit, the weight of the star is balanced by neutron-neutron interactions 3) In rigidly spinning stars, the mass limit can sometimes increase by up to 50%.
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice For which of the following functions would the Intermediate Value function not be applicable over the interval [0.5, 5]?
W) The quantity (x minus 2) to the 2 thirds power
X) The quantity (x plus 2) to the 2 thirds power
Y) The quantity (x minus 2) to the negative 2 thirds power
Z) The quantity (x plus 2) to the negative 2 thirds power
ANSWER: Y) The quantity (x minus 2) to the negative 2 thirds power
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Given that f of negative 7 is negative 3, and f prime of x is less than or equal to 2, what is the largest possible value for f of x when x equals 0?
ANSWER: 11
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the name given to the pH at which a molecule carries no net electrical charge?
W) Equivalence point
X) Isoelectric point
Y) Half-equivalence point
Z) Critical point
ANSWER: X) Isoelectric point
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If a 1.288 kilogram mass of technetium decays into an 80.5 gram mass of technetium after 140 seconds, and the natural log of 2 can be approximated as 0.7, what is the decay constant of technetium?
ANSWER: 0.02
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the frequency, in hertz, of the 3rd harmonic in a half-open tube with a fundamental frequency of 110 Hz?
ANSWER: 550
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the name of the term that describes a unit of vibrational energy that arises from oscillating atoms within a crystal, causing it to be treated as a singular particle in Quantum mechanics?
ANSWER: PHONON
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The middle lamella is a layer of sticky substances that is primarily made up of what kind of polysaccharide which is commonly used as a thickening agent?
ANSWER: PECTIN
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 cytoskeletal structures that have a 9+0 arrangement of microtubules 1) Centrioles 2) Basal bodies 3) Cilia 4) Flagella.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer During the early-AGB phase for red giant stars, the main source of energy is helium fusion in a shell surrounding the core. What two elements mainly make up the core?
ANSWER: CARBON AND OXYGEN (also accept: C AND O)
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In stellar physics, what condition occurs when internal gas pressure is not strong enough to prevent gravitational collapse, causing the collapse of interstellar gas clouds?
ANSWER: JEANS CRITERION (also accept: JEANS INSTABILITY)

X) -2

10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the indefinite integral of x times the sine of x?
ANSWER: SINE X MINUS X COSINE X PLUS C
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Light emitted in a double-slit interference experiment has an initial intensity of 13 watts per square meter before any diffractions. Giving your answer in watts per square meter, what is the intensity of the light at an interference maxima?
ANSWER: 52
11. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A simple pendulum has an angular amplitude of 60 degrees. Assuming that a gravitational torque with a magnitude of 37 newton meters acts on it, and that it has a rotational inertia of 23 kilogram meters squared, which of the following is the closest to the period of the pendulum?
W) 0.5 seconds
X) 5.4 seconds
Y) 1.2 seconds
Z) 4.9 seconds
ANSWER: X) 5.4 seconds
12. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Researchers at Argonne National Lab are looking into nuclear microreactors as a solution for powering electronic trucks. Which of the following is always a disadvantage of microreactors compared to full-scale nuclear reactors?
W) There will be more severe neutron leakage in microreactors
X) There will be more non-fission neutron capture in the reactor
Y) The moderators will need to be added to slow neutrons down in the reactor
Z) The microreactor will generate less energy per kilogram of uranium-235 than coal
ANSWER: W) There will be more severe neutron leakage in microreactors

17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National lab have been conducting research into building an unhackable quantum network. This entails that any information sent over the quantum network is unable to be intercepted and read by any parties outside of the ones for which the information was originally intended for. What is the specific name of the phenomena in quantum mechanics that lends to this quality of quantum networks?
ANSWER: WAVE FUNCTION COLLAPSE

21. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following factors does NOT directly affect the mean free path of a gas?
