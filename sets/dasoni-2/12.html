1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What theorem states that a partially ordered set that has an upper bound for every chain must also have a maximal element?
ANSWER: Zorn's Lemma
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three processes that utilize ubiquitinylation 1) Endocytosis 2) Proteasomal degradation 3) DNA repair
ANSWER: All
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What special types of functions which are used in electromagnetism satisfy the property that their Laplacian is a three-dimensional dirac delta function?
ANSWER: Green function
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What thermodynamic quantity is defined as the amount of Gibbs free energy per particle, and is proportional to the natural logarithm of pressure for an ideal gas?
ANSWER: Chemical potential
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Indicate all of the following 3 options that would occur if the average rate of seafloor spreading on Earth were to suddenly become 10 times faster 1) The Earth would immediately shift towards an icehouse climate 2) The metamorphism of serpentinite in the oceans would slow down 3) Aragonite would be expected to precipitate more frequently than Calcite.
ANSWER: None
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The Chebyshev distance, or the L-infinity metric, is defined as the minimum number of moves needed by a king on a chessboard to go from one square to another, where kings can move to any diagonally, vertically, or horizontally adjacent square in one move. Order the following three distance definitions in increasing numerical value between any two distinct points. 1) Euclidean 2) Manhattan 3) Chebyshev
ANSWER: 3 1 2
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Julian is observing the development of mosses that are growing in his backyard. He sees that at the earliest stage of development, the gametophyte starts out as a small, thread-like chain of cells, referring to what phase?
ANSWER: Protonema
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What rule in statistical mechanics relates the different degrees of freedom of a thermodynamic system, such as the number of components, number of phases, and number of ways of performing work?
ANSWER: Gibbs phase rule (also accept: phase rule)
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What model of adsorption assumes that all adsorption sites are independent and energetically equivalent, and that there are no interactions between adsorbate molecules on adjacent sites?
ANSWER: Langmuir model
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following meteorological variables does not compensate for deviation from the surface?
W) Liquid water potential temperature
X) Geopotential height
Y) Total water mixing ratio
Z) Equivalent potential temperature
ANSWER: Y) Total water mixing ratio
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer To one significant figure, what is the millionth root of one million factorial?
ANSWER: 400,000 (also accept: 4 × 105)
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What expanded version of the Nernst equation calculates a neuron's membrane potential while accounting for ions of different permeabilities?
ANSWER: Goldman-Hodgkin-Katz equation (also accept: GHK equation; Goldman equation)
13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why the neutral pion is much less stable than its charged counterparts?
W) It contains strange quarks
X) Its decay violates the OBZ rule
Y) It has a larger mass
Z) It decays via the electromagnetic force
ANSWER: Z) It decays via the electromagnetic force
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the degree of unsaturation of dodecahedrane?
ANSWER: 11
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 variable stars by increasing average age. 1) Herbig Ae/Be 2) W Virginis 3) Classical Cepheid
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What type of set in higher dimensional euclidean space is analogous to a closed, bounded interval on the real line?
ANSWER: Compact set
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Filip is currently researching sieve-tube elements, and is wondering how they stay intact throughout a plant's lifetime. Sieve-tube elements are lined with what polysaccharide, which protects the phloem and is typically found as spirally-wound chains of glucose residues?
ANSWER: Callose
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three central potentials for which there is a radius of stable circular orbit. 1) V(r) = -1/r 2) V(r) = -1/r2 3) V(r) = -1/r3
ANSWER: 1 only
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What type of pericyclic reaction breaks or forms a ring through the destruction or formation of a single sigma bond?
ANSWER: Electrocyclic reaction
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer With an exact value equal to the fifth root of 100, which constant is used to find the relationship between stellar magnitude and brightness?
ANSWER: POGSON RATIO
21. BONUS Math Short Answer Identify all of the following functions that are uniformly continuous on the open interval from zero to infinity 1) f(x) = ln(x) 2) f(x) = 1/x 3) f(x) = 1/x2.
ANSWER: None
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer The total lung capacity of a patient is 5 liters and the functional residual capacity is 3 liters. If the alveolar pressure is 10 cm of H2O and the pleural pressure is 5 cm of H2O, what is the value of the patient's lung capacitance in liters per centimeter of H2O?
ANSWER: 0.4
23. BONUS Physics Short Answer Casey is escaping from Earth on a spaceship at 0.6c while his brother, Kaison, stays at home. From Kaison's perspective, he receives a light signal from Casey 1 hour after Casey leaves. In hours, how much time has passed for Casey when he sends the light signal to his brother?
ANSWER: 0.5

BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer High undercooling, low nucleation, and low crystal growth during the cooling of an igneous rock create a unique texture, contrasted with aphanitic and phaneritic textures. What is the term for the glassy texture with a few small crystals formed under these conditions?
ANSWER: HOLOHYALINE

Y) Right, Left
