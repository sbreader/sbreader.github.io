1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following does auxin NOT stimulate?
W) Primary growth
X) Meristematic growth
Y) Apical dominance
Z) Secondary growth
ANSWER: Z) Secondary growth
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Under the Koppen climate classification system, hot deserts are designated as what?
ANSWER: BWh
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer One vibration has 10 times the energy of a second vibration. What is the amplitude of the first vibration in terms of the amplitude of the second?
ANSWER: √10 A2
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A compound that yields a hydrophobic group, a long fatty chain, and a sphingosine is known as a what?
ANSWER: SPHINGOLIPID
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer At a point of inflection of the function f(x), f'(x) will have a slope of what?
ANSWER: 0
7. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice A traditional oath to ethically practice medicine that physicians must take is widely believed to have been written by whom?
W) Hipparcos
X) Hippocrates
Y) Galen
Z) Democritus
ANSWER: X) Hippocrates
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the three-letter abbreviation for auxin?
ANSWER: IAA (do not accept: INDOLE ACETIC ACID)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the correct description of a BWk desert?
W) temperate
X) arid
Y) semiarid
Z) megathermal
ANSWER: W) temperate
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A geologist is standing at a particular location on earth's surface. He has a pendulum 1 meter in length that swings with a frequency of 10 hertz. In terms of π, what is the magnitude of the acceleration due to gravity at this location?
ANSWER: 400π2 meters per second squared
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Glyceryl esters of fatty acids, phosphoric acid, and ethanolamine are known as what?
ANSWER: PHOSPHATIDYL ETHANOLAMINES (also accept: CEPHALINS)
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the five moons of Uranus that have been discovered by observation from Earth.
ANSWER: ARIEL, MIRANDA, OBERON, TITANIA, UMBRIEL
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If at a certain point of f(x), f'(x) = 0 and f''(x) is positive, that point is what type of extrema?
ANSWER: MINIMUM (also accept: RELATIVE MINIMUM)
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Toll-like receptor 5 recognizes what?
W) CpG DNA
X) dsRNA
Y) Flagellin
Z) Lipopolysaccharide
ANSWER: Y) Flagellin
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Drumlins can be found in groups known as what?
ANSWER: DRUMLIN FIELDS (also accept: DRUMLIN CAMPS)
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A synonym for birefringence is
W) Single refraction
X) Double refraction
Y) Anisotropy
Z) Polarization
ANSWER: X) Double refraction
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Organic substances that are readily decomposed by microorganisms are said to be what?
ANSWER: BIODEGRADEABLE
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Binary stars that appear to be a single star when viewed through a telescope but are found to be binary when their spectra are examined are known as what?
ANSWER: SPECTROSCOPIC BINARIES
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the integral of e2x+ 7?
ANSWER: (1/2)e2x + 7x + C (also accept: 0.5e2x + 7x + C)
22. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A patient displays the following symptoms: a butterfly-like rash over the cheeks and bridge of the nose, inflammation, periodic remissions of illness, and connective tissue damage caused by autoimmunity. What disease does she have?
W) Chronic granuloma
X) Systemic lupus erythematosus
Y) Sarcoidosis
Z) Kaposi's sarcoma
ANSWER: X) Systemic lupus erythematosus
24. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In the equation for the magnitude of birefringence, Δn= ne- no, what do e and o stand for?
ANSWER: EXTRAORDINARY AND ORDINARY
25. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Enantiomers can be L or D. What does D stand for?
ANSWER: DEXTROROTATORY
