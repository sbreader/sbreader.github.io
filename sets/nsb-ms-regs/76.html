1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer List the following four taxonomic levels in the correct order, from broadest to narrowest 1) Species 2) Kingdom 3) Class 4) Genus.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 4, 1 (also accept: KINGDOM, CLASS, GENUS, SPECIES)
1. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice When a measurement is made repeatedly and the results are constant, the measurement is which of the following?
W) Accurate
X) Precise
Y) Significantly correct
Z) Insignificantly different
ANSWER: X) Precise
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer To the nearest gram, what is the mass of one-half liter of water at standard temperature?
ANSWER: 500
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer A cube has a density of 6 grams per centimeter cubed and a volume of 300 millimeters cubed. Rounded to the nearest tenth and in grams, what is the cube's mass?
ANSWER: 1.8
3. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice The power generated by a hydroelectric dam is proportional to all except which of the following?
W) Acceleration due to gravity
X) Water density
Y) Water flow rate
Z) Water temperature
ANSWER: Z) Water temperature
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What group of pollutants is formed during combustion when nitrogen and oxygen react?
ANSWER: NOx (also accept: NITROGEN OXIDES)
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following cellular organelles is responsible for energy production in the eukaryotic cell?
W) Golgi apparatus
X) Nucleus
Y) Mitochondrion
Z) Vacuole
ANSWER: Y) Mitochondrion
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In humans, which of the following is the name for the tube that makes up the complete digestive system?
W) Alimentary canal
X) Gastrovascular cavity
Y) Gut
Z) Gastric bypass
ANSWER: W) Alimentary canal
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the most abundant element in Earth's atmosphere?
ANSWER: NITROGEN
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a type of chemical weathering?
W) Oxidation
X) Abrasion
Y) Erosion
Z) Ice wedging
ANSWER: W) Oxidation
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The radius of a sphere is tripled. By what factor is its surface area multiplied?
ANSWER: 9
6. BONUS Math Short Answer Your scores on your first 3 math tests are 83, 88 and 91. What do you need to score on the fourth test in order to average 90 on the 4 tests?
ANSWER: 98
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Electrodes are NOT found in which of the following?
W) Electrophorus
X) Battery
Y) Permanent magnet
Z) Capacitor
ANSWER: Y) Permanent magnet
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer A railroad car with a mass of 250,000 kilograms travels 10 meters in 2 seconds before it crashes into an identical, stationary car. The first car comes to a stop and the second car begins to move. What is the speed, in meters per second, of the second car after the collision?
ANSWER: 5
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If a line is perpendicular to the line with equation y = 3x + 5 at its y intercept, what is the slope of the perpendicular line?
ANSWER: -1/3
8. BONUS Math Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 numbers that are divisible by 3 1) 123 2) 469 3) 631 4) 702.
ANSWER: 1, 4 (also accept: 123 AND 702)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following instruments measures air pressure?
W) Hygrometer
X) Thermometer
Y) Barometer
Z) Anemometer
ANSWER: Y) Barometer
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the study of vital statistics of populations and how they change over time?
ANSWER: DEMOGRAPHY (do not accept: DEMOGRAPHICS)
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In a bacterial culture, there are 2000 Escherichia coli per milliliter of tryptic soy broth. What is the term used to describe this number?
W) Dispersion
X) Density
Y) Turbidity
Z) Population
ANSWER: X) Density
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the most common inhibitory neurotransmitter in the human nervous system?
ANSWER: GABA (also accept: GAMMA AMINO BUTYRIC ACID)
11. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is a terminal for a battery?
W) Anode
X) Diode
Y) Electrolyte
Z) Transistor
ANSWER: W) Anode
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What pair of natural disasters led to the Fukushima Daiichi nuclear disaster?
W) Earthquake and tsunami
X) Tsunami and hurricane
Y) Earthquake and tornado
Z) Volcanic eruption and earthquake
ANSWER: W) Earthquake and tsunami
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following cell organelles is responsible for the function of respiration?
W) Endoplasmic reticulum
X) Lysosome
Y) Mitochondrion
Z) Golgi apparatus
ANSWER: Y) Mitochondrion
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What category of molecular transport across membranes requires the input of energy in order to move a molecule against its concentration gradient?
ANSWER: ACTIVE TRANSPORT (do not accept: BULK TRANSPORT, EXOCYTOSIS, ENDOCYTOSIS)
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A light-year is a unit for measuring what?
W) Time
X) Speed
Y) Distance
Z) Momentum
ANSWER: Y) Distance
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What landform is created when a stream meets a larger body of water, causing it to lose velocity and drop sediment?
ANSWER: DELTA
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Express the following number in scientific notation: 3,250,000.
ANSWER: 3.25 times 106
14. BONUS Math Short Answer One angle of an isosceles triangle measures 30 degrees. In degrees, what are the possible measures of the largest angle of the triangle?
ANSWER: 75 and 120
15. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Order the following three energy sources from least to greatest in terms of net electricity production in the US for the last three years 1) Coal 2) Natural gas 3) Uranium.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1 (also accept: URANIUM, NATURAL GAS, COAL)
15. BONUS Energy Short Answer What was the top natural gas producing state in the US in 2014?
ANSWER: TEXAS
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer An element's atomic number represents the quantity per atom of what subatomic particle?
ANSWER: PROTON (do not accept: ELECTRON)
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer Wind blowing across the top of a chimney creates lower pressure in the chimney, drawing smoke up through the fireplace rather than allowing it to spread into a room. What principle does this illustrate?
ANSWER: BERNOULLI'S PRINCIPLE (also accept: BERNOULLI)
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Plate tectonics is studied most frequently under which of the following branches of Earth science?
W) Geography
X) Hydrology
Y) Geology
Z) Meteorology
ANSWER: Y) Geology
17. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Students release a ball from the top of a ramp and measure the distance that the ball rolls. This is repeated with different heights for the ramp and with balls of different masses, but with the same external characteristics. The results will show that the distance a ball rolls depends on which of the following?
W) The mass of the ball only
X) The ramp height only
Y) The mass of the ball and the ramp height
Z) Neither the mass nor the height
ANSWER: X) The ramp height only
