Packet 87 (BIOLOGY) of Mira Loma Question Database:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is it called when a plant has the ability to alter its form in response to local environmental conditions?
ANSWER: DEVELOPMENTAL PLASTICITY
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Some underwater plant leaves are feathery. What does this adaptation do for the plant?
ANSWER: LESSENING THEIR RESISTANCE TO WATER
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which site on a ribosome holds the tRNA carrying the next amino acid to be added to the chain?
W) P site
X) A site
Y) E site
Z) L site
ANSWER: X) A site
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Strings of ribosomes all attached to a single mRNA strand are called what?
ANSWER: POLYRIBOSOMES (also accept: POLYSOMES)
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What use do surface leaves that are shaped like pads serve, such those that can be seen on lotus leaves?
ANSWER: AID IN FLOATATION
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the three basic organs of a plant.
ANSWER: ROOTS, LEAVES, STEMS
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What 2 components is the signal recognition particle made from?
ANSWER: PROTEIN AND RNA
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The sugars and other carbohydrates produced by photosynthesis are generally called what?
ANSWER: PHOTOSYNTHATES
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Fern sporophylls produce clusters of sporangia. What are these clusters known as?
W) Sori
X) Strobili
Y) Pinnae
Z) Megaspores
ANSWER: W) Sori
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the branched roots off of the taproot called?
ANSWER: LATERAL ROOTS
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What term described the many small roots that stem from the stem in angiosperms?
ANSWER: ADVENTITIOUS
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Indicate by words or numbers which of the following statements correctly describes similarities between charophytes and land plants? Both have rosette shaped cellulose-synthesizing complexes Both have similar peroxisome enzymes Both have similar structure of flagellated sperm
ANSWER: ALL OF THEM
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is a group of microtubules that forms between the daughter nuclei of a dividing cell called?
ANSWER: PHRAGMOPLAST
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What adaption that grow near the tips of the roots by the thousands increase the surface area of the root enormously?
ANSWER: ROOT HAIRS
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of roots support tall heavy plants?
ANSWER: PROP ROOTS
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Also known as air roots, these roots project above the water's surface, enabling the plant to obtain oxygen?
ANSWER: PNEUMATOPHORES
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer The part of a stem where the leaves are attached are called?
ANSWER: NODE
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the segment between leaves called?
ANSWER: INTERNODE
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What forms in the axil of a stem and each leaf?
ANSWER: AXILLARY BUD
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is another name for a terminal bud?
ANSWER: APICAL BUD
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Apical buds inhibit axillary buds. This inhibition is caused by
ANSWER: APICAL DOMINANCE
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name for a horizontal stem that grows just below the surface?
ANSWER: RHIZOME
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of modified stem grows horizontal to the ground and allows certain plants to reproduce asexually?
ANSWER: STOLON
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What part of the plant joins leaves to stems?
ANSWER: PETIOLE
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the three basic types of tissues in the plant tissue system?
ANSWER: DERMAL, VASCULAR, GROUND
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The bark on woody trees is an example of what type of dermal system?
ANSWER: PERIDERM
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the hairlike outgrowths of the shoot epidermis that reduce water loss and reflect excess light?
ANSWER: TRICHOME
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which vascular tissue carries transports sugars from the leaves to where they are needed?
ANSWER: PHLOEM
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is ground tissue that is internal to the vascular tissue called and what is ground tissue that is external to the vascular tissue called. Give your answer in that order?
ANSWER: PITH and CORTEX
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of plant cells have a large central vacuole and perform most of the metabolic functions in a plant?
ANSWER: PARENCHYMA CELLS
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of plant cells help support younger parts of a plant shoot and lack a secondary wall?
ANSWER: COLLENCHYMA CELLS
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Plants are able to achieve indeterminate growth because they have perpetually embryonic tissues called what?
ANSWER: MERISTEMS
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the specific type of meristematic tissue that is found at the bases of grass stems and allows for the rapid growth and regrowth of many monocots.
ANSWER: INTERCALARY MERISTEM
