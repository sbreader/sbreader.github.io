1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Strychnine is a rat toxin that is also poisonous to humans because it acts as an antagonist for what neurotransmitter used in the spinal cord?
ANSWER: GLYCINE
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of marine fishes' nephrons 1) Their primary function is to excrete divalent ions 2) They lack a proximal tubule 3) They lack a distal tubule
ANSWER: 1 and 3

ANSWER: ZINC
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A vesicle is carrying newly synthesized polypeptides from the rough endoplasmic reticulum to the Golgi. Which of the following coat proteins will most likely be found on the membrane of the vesicle?
