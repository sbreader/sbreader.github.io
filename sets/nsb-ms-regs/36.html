1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The temperature outside is 85° F and the air is holding a maximum amount of moisture. If the temperature drops rapidly to 65°F, which of the following will MOST likely happen:
W) it will rain
X) it will become sunny
Y) humidity is relatively unchanged
Z) the relative humidity is lowered by about 25%
ANSWER: W) it will rain

6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true about ocean water:
