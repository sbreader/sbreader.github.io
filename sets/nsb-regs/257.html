1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following stars has a positive apparent magnitude?
W) Polaris
X) Sirius
Y) Canopus
Z) The Sun
ANSWER: W) Polaris
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following assumptions is typically made when performing potassium-argon dating?
W) Potassium-40 only decays into argon-40
X) Some percentage of argon-40 produced by decay leaks out of the rock
Y) Potassium-40 has a half-life of 1.3 million years
Z) All argon-40 in a rock was produced by potassium-40 decay
ANSWER: Z) All argon-40 in a rock was produced by potassium-40 decay
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A set of test scores is normally distributed with mean 62 and standard deviation 8. What score corresponds to a Z-score of –1.25?
ANSWER: 52
2. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the sum of the squares of the first 19 positive integers?
ANSWER: 2470
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three atoms in terms of increasing atomic radius 1) Argon 2) Fluorine 3) Lithium.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Grignard reagents are generally made in ether-based solvents due to the instability of the metal-organic species. Identify all of the following three solvents that are considered ethereal 1) Dioxane 2) Tetrahydrofuran 3) Dichloromethane.
ANSWER: 1, 2
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice An electron is moving along the x-axis in the positive direction, within a uniform magnetic field that is oriented toward the positive z-axis. In which of the following directions does the Lorentz force act?
W) Positive y-axis
X) Negative y-axis
Y) Positive z-axis
Z) Negative z-axis
ANSWER: W) Positive y-axis
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer A hollow pipe closed at both ends has a fundamental frequency of 100 hertz. In meters, what is the length of the pipe?
ANSWER: 1.7
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What iron-containing proteins are the main enzymes associated with the metabolism of drugs and toxins in the liver?
ANSWER: CYTOCHROME P450 (also accept: CYP)
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following amino acids is typically phosphorylated by protein kinase A?
W) Tyrosine
X) Serine
Y) Tryptophan
Z) Isoleucine
ANSWER: X) Serine
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Oak Ridge National Lab is contributing to the production of cancer radiation treatments by producing actinium-227, which undergoes two decay transitions to become the medically-useful isotope radium-223. What two types of decay does actinium-227 undergo to become radium-223?
ANSWER: BETA AND ALPHA (also accept: ALPHA AND BETA)
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab have been studying crystallized biomolecules with electron microscopy. In one type of experiment, the samples are immersed in liquid nitrogen to form vitreous ice before imaging. What type of electron microscopy is this?
ANSWER: CRYOELECTRON MICROSCOPY (also accept: CRYO; CRYO-EM)
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following geologic phenomena led to the formation of submarine canyons?
W) Bioturbation
X) Tsunamis
Y) Turbidity currents
Z) Volcanic eruptions
ANSWER: Y) Turbidity currents
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of Earth’s Milankovitch cycles has a period of approximately one hundred thousand years?
W) Axial tilt
X) Precession
Y) Eccentricity
Z) Obliquity
ANSWER: Y) Eccentricity
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following hormones most directly affects the metabolic rate of the body?
W) Thyroid-stimulating hormone
X) Thyrotropin-releasing hormone
Y) Triiodothyronine
Z) Testosterone
ANSWER: Y) Triiodothyronine
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following medications would likely be most effective in treating a human patient with influenza?
W) Penicillin
X) Ampicillin
Y) Zanamivir
Z) Chloroquine
ANSWER: Y) Zanamivir
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are developing thermoelectric materials that can convert thermal energy to electrical energy via what effect?
ANSWER: SEEBECK EFFECT (also accept: PELTIER-SEEBECK EFFECT) (do not accept: PELTIER EFFECT)
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are part of the CUORE collaboration that is attempting to observe what ghostly leptons?
ANSWER: NEUTRINOS
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the absolute value of the complex conjugate of –6 + i?
ANSWER: √37
10. BONUS Math Short Answer f of x is a twice-differentiable function such that f prime of x and f double prime of x are both less than 0 for all real numbers. If f of 3 equals 22 and f of 5 equals 17, what is the largest possible integer value for f of 9?
ANSWER: 6
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A reading of zero on what two temperature scales refers to absolute zero?
ANSWER: KELVIN AND RANKINE (also accept: in any order)
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of imaging that depend on ionizing radiation 1) Magnetic resonance imaging 2) Computerized tomography 3) Positron emission tomography.
ANSWER: 2, 3
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer An aqueous-phase redox reaction has all of its stoichiometric coefficients doubled. Identify all of the following three quantities that are exactly doubled 1) Equilibrium constant 2) Delta-G 3) Cell potential.
ANSWER: 2
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of bonding 1) Xenon dichloride has a bent molecular geometry 2) Sulfur dioxide and carbon disulfide have the same molecular geometry 3) Krypton tetrafluoride has no net dipole moment.
ANSWER: 3
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In seeds, what is the term for the layer of sporophyte tissue that envelops and protects the megasporangium?
ANSWER: INTEGUMENT
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer If blood from an incompatible ABO group is mistakenly transfused, it can agglutinate. What class of antibodies mediates this process?
ANSWER: IgM (also accept: M; IMMUNOGLOBULIN M)
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If the temperature of an ideal gas is quadrupled, by what factor is the speed of sound in that gas multiplied?
ANSWER: 2
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer The position of a particle in meters is described by the function x(t) = t3 + 4t2 + 3t, where t is expressed in seconds. What is the acceleration, in meters per second squared, at time t equals 5?
ANSWER: 38
15. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab have designed a fuel rod cladding alloy made of iron, chromium, and aluminum to replace current zirconium alloys. Which of the following must be a property of this cladding?
W) Converts fast neutrons into thermal neutrons
X) Converts thermal neutrons into fast neutrons
Y) Has a high neutron cross-section
Z) Has a low neutron cross-section
ANSWER: Z) Has a low neutron cross-section
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab have been examining how glaucoma drugs interact with a human enzyme that converts carbon dioxide and water to bicarbonate. What is the common name of this enzyme?
ANSWER: CARBONIC ANHYDRASE (also accept: HUMAN CARBONIC ANHYDRASE)
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Adding ethyl-magnesium bromide to which of the following would produce a tertiary alcohol?
W) A ketone
X) An aldehyde
Y) An alkene
Z) An ether
ANSWER: W) A ketone
16. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements best describes how chlorine adds to an alkene?
W) Both chlorine atoms add to the same carbon atom
X) Chlorine atoms add with syn-stereoselectivity to adjacent carbon atoms
Y) Chlorine atoms add with anti-stereoselectivity to adjacent carbon atoms
Z) Only one chlorine atom adds to the carbon atoms
ANSWER: Y) Chlorine atoms add with anti-stereoselectivity to adjacent carbon atoms
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If a pyramid has 8 vertices, how many edges does it have?
ANSWER: 14

ANSWER: 2π/15

ANSWER: IO
18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A galaxy is receding from an observer such that the hydrogen alpha line appears to be at 657 nanometers instead of 656.3 nanometers. Which of the following best describes the changes in the beta and gamma lines for this galaxy?
W) They will both be shifted longer by 0.7 nanometers
X) They will both be shifted by slightly less than 0.7 nanometers
Y) They will both be shifted by slightly more than 0.7 nanometers
Z) They will both be shifted shorter by 0.7 nanometers

ANSWER: GLUON

To two significant figures and in joules, what is the potential energy of a configuration where four such point charges are placed in a square with side length 1 meter?
ANSWER: 5.4

ANSWER: 5

ANSWER: –6

ANSWER: ALL OF THEM

ANSWER: pH AND POTENTIAL (also accept: pH AND VOLTAGE)

ANSWER: ALL OF THEM
