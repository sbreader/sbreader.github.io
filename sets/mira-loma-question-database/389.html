4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are a physical or chemical agent that induces mutations in a living organism?
ANSWER: mutagen
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In a velocity versus time graph, at what point does the object reach its highest point?
ANSWER: the x-intercept (when the graph crosses the x-axis)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In a displacement versus time graph, at what point does the object reach its highest point?
ANSWER: the maximum y-value
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If no other forces are acting upon an object, the normal force is equal to what other force?
ANSWER: weight (gravity)
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In uniform circular motion, this fictitious force that is based on the frame of reference is called what?
ANSWER: centrifugal force
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Name the two types of collisions.
ANSWER: elastic and inelastic
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What quantity is conserved during elastic collisions but not during inelastic collisions?
ANSWER: kinetic energy
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What quantity is conserved in both elastic and inelastic collisions?
ANSWER: momentum
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The fixed pivot point on a lever is called what?
ANSWER: fulcrum
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Torque on an object gives rise to what other quantity?
ANSWER: angular acceleration
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In reference to seesaws, what direction, relative to the ground, is "positive?"
ANSWER: counterclockwise
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Seesaw equilibrium depends on two quantities. Name them.
ANSWER: weight and distance from fulcrum
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The product of force and a perpendicular moment arm distance yields what quantity?
ANSWER: torque
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Name the term for an object's maximum velocity in free fall.
ANSWER: terminal velocity
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Name the simple machine that is used to slide objects up to a platform or other elevated area.
ANSWER: inclined plane
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The force that is exerted on a simple machine is called what?
ANSWER: effort force
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Name the simple machine that among its members includes bottle openers, oars, and crowbars.
ANSWER: lever
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What class is a lever that has the fulcrum between the effort force and the resistance force?
ANSWER: 1st class
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What class is a lever that has the resistance force between the effort force and the fulcrum?
ANSWER: 2nd class
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What class is a lever that has the effort force between the resistance force and the fulcrum?
ANSWER: 3rd class
22. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A machine puts out 350J of useful work for every 500J inputted. What is the efficiency of this machine?
ANSWER: 70%
23. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Under ideal conditions, the length of an inclined plane divided by the height of the plane equals what value?
ANSWER: ideal mechanical advantage
24. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the mechanical advantage of a fixed pulley that hangs the weight on its left side, loops the rope over the top of the pulley, and has the effort force pulling down on the right?
ANSWER: 1
25. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If the speed of an object is tripled, its kinetic energy is how many times its original?
ANSWER: 9
26. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A person having a mass of 60kg exerts a horizontal force of 200N in pushing a 90kg object a distance of 6 meters along a horizontal floor. He does this at constant velocity in 6 seconds. Using g = 10 m/s2, what is the weight of this person?
ANSWER: 600N
29. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The 1,000N weight of a pile-driver falls freely from rest and drops 25 meters. It strikes a steel beam, driving it 3cm into the ground. The kinetic energy of the weight of the pile-driver right before it hits the beam is what?
ANSWER: 25000J
31. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer An icosahedron has faces of what shape?
ANSWER: Triangles
32. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Which Greek philosopher are the Platonic solids named after?
ANSWER: Plato
33. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The dodecahedron is covered in faces of what shape?
ANSWER: Pentagons
34. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer As a polygon approaches an infinite number of sides, what geometric figure does it become?
ANSWER: Circle
35. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many possible 7-digit telephone numbers can be made from the following criteria: The first number is not even. The third number is prime. The fourth and fifth numbers, when added, yield an odd number. The seventh number is 1.
ANSWER: 100,000
36. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the derivative of 2cos5x?
ANSWER: -10sin5x
37. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The indefinite integral of x is what?
ANSWER: (x2)/2 + C
38. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many sides of a cube must be painted red so that when picking two adjacent faces, one will always be red?
ANSWER: Four
39. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many prime numbers are there between 1 and 20?
ANSWER: 8
40. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer 45% of what number is 90?
ANSWER: 200
