1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Wild-type mice have a brown coat color called agouti, while a dominant mutation in this gene produces a yellow coat color. 8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why carboxylic acids tend to be more acidic than simple alcohols?
W) In carboxylic acids, electron density is withdrawn by the oxygen atom in the carbonyl group
X) Once deprotonated, carboxylic acids can spread the negative charge over both oxygen atoms through resonance
Y) Because they are bulkier, carboxylic acids have less area exposed for the solvent to stabilize the conjugate base
