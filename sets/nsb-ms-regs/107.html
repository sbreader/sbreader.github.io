1. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A pair of headphones originally priced at 80 dollars is on sale at 25% off. If the sales tax is 10%, what is the total price, in dollars, of these headphones?
W) 45
X) 52
Y) 66
Z) 68
ANSWER: Y) 66
1. BONUS Math Short Answer Simplify 3x2 times (2x – 8 + 5x3) minus (4x5 + 7x).
ANSWER: 11x5 + 6x3 – 24x2 – 7x
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Plant cells differ from animal cells in that only plant cells possess:
W) Nuclei
X) Mitochondria
Y) Cell walls
Z) Lysosomes
ANSWER: Y) Cell walls
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What fatty layer covers some neurons at regular intervals and is responsible for the coloring of white matter in the brain?
ANSWER: MYELIN
3. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the name of the heat energy trapped in the Earth's interior?
ANSWER: GEOTHERMAL
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Colony collapse disorder is a phenomenon associated with what animals?
ANSWER: HONEY BEES
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which factor does not influence the speed needed to put a satellite into orbit?
W) Mass of the satellite
X) Radius of the orbit
Y) Shape of the orbit
Z) Mass of the planet
ANSWER: W) Mass of the satellite
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer Ice is placed into a beaker partially filled with water at room temperature and standard atmospheric pressure. If all of the ice melts, what is the lowest possible temperature of the new volume of water at the end of the melting process in degrees Celsius?
ANSWER: 0
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The climate of northwestern Europe is most directly affected by the:
W) Gulf stream
X) Abyssal zone
Y) Trade winds
Z) Labrador Current
ANSWER: W) Gulf stream
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Ninety-nine percent of the atmosphere is composed of what two gases?
ANSWER: NITROGEN AND OXYGEN (also accept: N2 AND O2; N AND O)
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice By weight, what element is MOST common in petroleum?
W) Carbon
X) Hydrogen
Y) Oxygen
Z) Nitrogen
ANSWER: W) Carbon
6. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following activities best describes the practice of science as inquiry?
W) Placing different objects in a tank of water to determine which ones sink
X) Following directions in constructing a bridge made of popsicle sticks
Y) Determining what materials are needed to build a terrarium
Z) Assessing the conditions of a road to determine how long a trip will take
ANSWER: W) Placing different objects in a tank of water to determine which ones sink
7. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice In the United States, where does most of the hydropower production originate?
W) Oceanic coastal regions
X) Banks of the Mississippi river
Y) Bases of mountain streams
Z) Coasts of the Great Lakes
ANSWER: W) Oceanic coastal regions
7. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following requires the greatest percentage of energy in a household?
W) Lighting
X) Refrigeration
Y) Cooking
Z) Heating
ANSWER: Z) Heating
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following are abiotic factors?
W) Plants
X) Animals
Y) Sunlight
Z) Microorganisms
ANSWER: Y) Sunlight
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What specific major group of flowering plants, is traditionally recognized as possessing seeds with one primordial leaf and includes plants such as maize, rice, and wheat?
ANSWER: MONOCOT (also accept: MONOCOTYLEDON)
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Find the slope of the line with equation 4x + 5y = -10
ANSWER: -4/5
9. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the product of the mean, median, and mode of the following list of seven numbers: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13?
ANSWER: 99/7 (also accept: 14 1/7)
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A 300 Newton stone sits atop a hill making an angle of 30 degrees with the horizontal. What is the force of gravity acting on the stone?
ANSWER: 300 NEWTONS
10. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about sound is not true?
W) Sound usually travels faster in solids than liquids
X) The speed of sound is not dependent on the density of the medium
Y) Sound is a pressure wave
Z) The speed of sound depends on the temperature of the medium
ANSWER: X) The speed of sound is not dependent on the density of the medium
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The International Astronomical Union divides the night sky into regions based on star patterns, such as Aquila, Cygnus, and Orion. What are these regions called?
ANSWER: CONSTELLATIONS
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Cellular respiration is used by the cell to produce what energetically relevant molecule?
ANSWER: ATP (also accept: ADENOSINE TRIPHOSPHATE)
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Carbon dioxide enters plant leaves through what pores?
ANSWER: STOMATA
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A characteristic feature of the stratosphere is the relative abundance of:
W) Clouds
X) Ozone
Y) Ions
Z) Dust
ANSWER: X) Ozone
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What extrusive igneous rock is formed when felsic lava cools rapidly and forms a glassy texture?
ANSWER: OBSIDIAN
14. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What commission in the US is responsible for licensing nuclear power plants and overseeing their construction and operation?
ANSWER: NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION (also accept: NRC)
14. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Barrages with built in sluices are used to harvest energy from what source?
W) Wind
X) Tides
Y) Coal
Z) Natural gas
ANSWER: X) Tides
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Joseph Priestly discovered that a certain gas could be dissolved in water to form seltzer. What gas is found dissolved in seltzer?
ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE
15. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice What fundamental force is primarily responsible for the formation of black holes?
W) Strong nuclear
X) Weak nuclear
Y) Electromagnetic
Z) Gravity
ANSWER: Z) Gravity
16. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer The prefix "nano-" refers to 10 raised to what power?
ANSWER: -9

W) Wavelength
X) Frequency
Y) Amplitude
Z) Harmonics
ANSWER: Y) Amplitude

ANSWER: -4

ANSWER: AMETHYST

ANSWER: PRIME MERIDIAN (also accept: GREENWICH MERIDIAN)

W) Gamma rays
X) Beta particles

ANSWER: W) Gamma rays

W) Gelatin
X) Honey

Z) Vinegar

ANSWER: INCOMPLETE DOMINANCE

ANSWER: 2, 3, 1

ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
