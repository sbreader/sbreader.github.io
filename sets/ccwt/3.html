1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Name all of the following 3 compounds which contain Arsenic 1) Paris Green 2) Lewisite 3) Roxarsone.
ANSWER: All
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The Haber process has a Δ H value of approximately how many kJ/M:
W) -55 to -65
X) -65 to -75
Y) 85 to 95
Z) -85 to -95
ANSWER: Z) -85 to -95
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Clays of Iridium are most commonly found near which landforms:
W) Impact craters
X) River deltas
Y) Shield volcanoes
Z) Pyroclastic flows
ANSWER: W) Impact craters
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number list all of the following 4 periods that are a part of the Paleozoic Era 1) Devonian 2) Siderian 3) Neogene 4) Carboniferous.
ANSWER: 1 AND 4
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What law of electromagnetism describes the magnetic field generated by a constant electric current, named after two French Scientists?
ANSWER: Biot-Savart Law
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following gases would have the largest root mean square velocity:
W) Helium gas at 200 Kelvin
X) Carbon tetrachloride at 400 Kelvin
Y) Hydrogen gas at 200 Kelvin
Z) Helium gas at 20 Kelvin
ANSWER: Y) Hydrogen gas at 200 Kelvin
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is the approximate degree measure of theta when changing the point (-28,21) into polar coordinates:
W) 127
X) 143
Y) 233
Z) 323
ANSWER: X) 143
4. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Given that log10 2 ≈ 0.30 and log10 e ≈ 0.43, which of the following powers of ten is closest in value to 20!?
W) 1015
X) 1018
Y) 1021
Z) 1024
ANSWER: X) 1018
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What fat-soluble secosteroid, also known as calciferol, helps bones absorb Phosphorus and Calcium?
ANSWER: Vitamin D (also accept: Vitamin D2)
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, name all of the following that good sources of Vitamin D 1) Sunlight 2) Fish Oils 3) Egg Yolk 4) Common Nuts
ANSWER: 1) Sunlight 2) Fish Oils 3) Egg Yolk (also accept: All but 4)
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The tau particle, along with three types of neutrinos are part of the lepton family. What are the other two particles that are classified as leptons?
W) Meson and Proton
X) Electron Neutrino and Muon
Y) Electron and Muon
Z) Electron and Tau Neutrino
ANSWER: Y) Electron and Muon
6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice What vector represents the energy of an electromagnetic wave?
W) Jefimenko Vector
X) Poynting Vector
Y) London Vector
Z) Lorenz Vector
ANSWER: X) Poynting Vector
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following reactions adds an alkyl group to a benzene ring?
W) Friedel-Crafts Acylation
X) Suzuki Coupling
Y) Friedel-Crafts Alkylation
Z) Birch Reduction
ANSWER: Y) Friedel-Crafts Alkylation
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Within 10 grams, what is the mass of oxygen that reacts with 9.61 grams of propane?
ANSWER: 349 grams (also accept: any value within 10 grams)
8. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is equivalent to sin(43°)cos(47°) + sin(47°)cos(43°):
W) cos(40°)
X) sin(50°)
Y) sin(90°)
Z) cos(90°)
ANSWER: Y) sin(90°)
8. BONUS Math Short Answer What is 53 choose 3?
ANSWER: 23426
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Uranus and what other planet exhibit retrograde rotation?
ANSWER: Venus
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT associated with La Niña:
W) Southeasterly Trade Winds weaken
X) Upwelling off South America nearly ceases
Y) Australia experiences increased precipitation
Z) The Eastern Pacific warms
ANSWER: Y) Australia experiences increased precipitation
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following bonds in order of increasing bond length 1) Iodine-Iodine 2) Fluorine-Bromine 3) Fluorine-Fluorine 4) Bromine-Iodine
ANSWER: 3) Fluorine-Fluorine, 2) Fluorine-Bromine, 4) Bromine-Iodine, 1) Iodine-Iodine (also accept: 3,2,4,1)
10. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is true of the Diels-Alder reaction, in which a conjugated diene and a substituted alkene react to form a cyclohexene derivative?
W) Its standard enthalpy and entropy of reaction are both positive
X) It occurs via a concerted mechanism
Y) It exhibits poor regioselectivity
Z) It proceeds through antarafacial interactions
ANSWER: X) It occurs via a concerted mechanism
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for a malignant tumor of connective or other non epithelial tissue?

X) Scallop

ANSWER: X) Scallop
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Compute the determinant of the two by two matrix, from left to right, with first row 2, -3 and second row 4,15.
ANSWER: 42
12. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the equation of the plane that has a normal line with equation: x=4+5t, y=5+6t, z=6-7t and that passes through the point (-1,1,2)?
ANSWER: 5(x+1) + 6(y-1)-7(z-2) = 0 or 5x + 6y-7z = -13
