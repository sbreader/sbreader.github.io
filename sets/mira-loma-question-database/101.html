1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Leaves develop from which of the following?
W) leaf primordia
X) leaf shoots
Y) leaf roots
Z) tap root
ANSWER: W) leaf primordia
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer E. Histolytica is of what genus?
ANSWER: ENTAMOEBA
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Dictyostelium discoideum is what kind of organism?
W) gastropod
X) crustacean
Y) cellular slime mold
Z) plasmodial slime mold
ANSWER: Y) cellular slime mold
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What two viral surface proteins are in H1N1?
ANSWER: Hemagglutinin and neuraminidase
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Within viroids, what shape are the RNA molecules in?
ANSWER: CIRCULAR
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A virus that is carcinorgenic is called what?
ANSWER: (AN) ONCAVIRUS
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer Substitutions that change one amino acid to another one are called what?
ANSWER: MISSENSE MUTATIONS
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A graded change in a character along a geographic axis is called what?
ANSWER: (A) CLINE
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the phenomena in which there are marked differences between two sexes in secondary sexual characteristics which are not directly associated with reproduction or survival.
ANSWER: SEXUAL DIMORPHISM
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Water molds, white rusts and downy mildews are called which of the following?
W) Oogoniums
X) Oomycetes
Y) Oocytes
Z) Oospores
ANSWER: X) Oomycetes
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer The most common lethal genetic disease in the United States is what?
ANSWER: CYSTIC FIBROSIS
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Nondisjunction of homologous chromosomes in meiosis one will lead to
W) two anueploidic gametes and two normal gametes
X) three anueploidic gametes and one normal gamete
Y) four anueploidic gametes and no normal gametes
Z) four normal gametes and no anueploidic gametes
ANSWER: Y) four anueploidic gametes and no normal gametes
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The most abundant organic compound on Earth is what?
ANSWER: CELLULOSE
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Sucrose has what glycosidic linkage?
W) alpha 1-4
X) beta 1-4
Y) alpha 1-2
Z) beta 1-2
ANSWER: Y) alpha 1-2
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What secondary structure does spider silk contain?
ANSWER: BETA-PLEATED SHEETS
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What structure do polysaccharides composed solely of alpha glucose have?
ANSWER: HELICAL
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the process in which bacteria reproduce called?
W) Cytokinesis
X) Bacterial Fission
Y) Binary Fission
Z) Cytocellular Fission
ANSWER: Y) Binary Fission
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are maternal substances in eggs that influence the course of early development?
ANSWER: CYTOPLASMIC DETERMINANTS
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer An individual's unique set of genetic markers is called what?
ANSWER: GENETIC PROFILE
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice An individual that has more than 2 chromosome sets that are derived from a single species is called what?
W) polploid
X) autopolyploid
Y) aneuploid
Z) tetraploid
ANSWER: X) autopolyploid
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the ultimate source for new alleles?
ANSWER: MUTATION
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Within mammals, which of the following is hair?
W) shared ancestral character
X) shared derived character
Y) non-ancestral character
Z) non-derived character
ANSWER: X) shared derived character
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What two main periods exist in the Cenozoic Era?
ANSWER: Neogene and Paleogene
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of these was a proposed "tree of life"
W) Ring of life
X) Equilibrium tree
Y) Gradualist model
Z) Hardy-Wienburg tree
ANSWER: W) Ring of life
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Gram negative bacteria counterstains what color?
ANSWER: RED (also accept: PINK)
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a common shape of a prokaryote?
W) Spiral
X) Double Helix
Y) Oval
Z) Rectangular
ANSWER: W) Spiral
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Transduction is which of the following?
W) Carrying bacterial genes from one host cell to another
X) Carrying viral genes from one host cell to another
Y) A cell's uptake of foreign DNA from its surroundings
Z) None of the above
ANSWER: W) Carrying bacterial genes from one host cell to another
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Homologous genes that are found in different species because of speciation are called what?
ANSWER: ORTHOLOGOUS GENES
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the 3 domains of life?
ANSWER: ARCHAEA, EUKARYA, BACTERIA
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Most bacterial cell walls contain what sugar?
ANSWER: PEPTIDOGLYCAN
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a structural adaptation in which an original cell produces a copy of its chromosome and surrounds it with a tough wall?
W) Spore
X) Exospore
Y) Endospore
Z) Autospore
ANSWER: Y) Endospore
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Transduction is a form of
W) Horizontal Gene transfer
X) Vertical Gene transfer
Y) Exo-Horizontal Gene transfer
Z) Exo-Vertical Gene transfer
ANSWER: W) Horizontal Gene transfer
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the phenomena in which the genotype and possibly phenotype of a prokaryotic cell are altered by the uptake of foreign DNA from its surroundings?
ANSWER: TRANSFORMATION
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Plasmids that carry resistance genes are called what?
W) A-plasmids
X) B-plasmids
Y) R-plasmids
Z) F-plasmids
ANSWER: Y) R-plasmids
18. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Surface-coating colonies of prokaryotes are called what?
W) Surface colonies
X) Chemotrophs
Y) Biofilms
Z) Biotrophs
ANSWER: Y) Biofilms
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Gram-positive bacteria usually stains what color?
ANSWER: DARK BLUE (also accept: VIOLET)
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which group of bacteria consists of photoautotropic prokaryotes with plant-like, oxygen generating photosynthesis?
ANSWER: CYANOBACTERIA
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Extreme halophiles live in what environments?
W) Highly Saline Environments
X) Highly Thermal Environments
Y) Highly Acidic Environments
Z) Highly Pressurized Environments
ANSWER: W) Highly Saline Environments
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer Apicomplexans are usually what type of organism?
ANSWER: PARASITES
21. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Proteins secreted by certain bacteria and other organisms are called what?
W) Exoproteins
X) Cytocidal proteins
Y) Exotoxins
Z) Proteophages
ANSWER: Y) Exotoxins
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer Parasites that cause disease are known as what?
ANSWER: PATHOGENS
22. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the name of the group of unicellular algae that have a unique glass-like wall made of hydrated silica embedded in a organic matrix called?
W) Stramenophiles
X) Ciliates
Y) Diatoms
Z) Algae
ANSWER: Y) Diatoms
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the symbiotic relationship in which one organism is not harmed, and the other is harmed?
ANSWER: AMENSALISM
23. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Forams are named for their what?
W) Tests
X) Flagella
Y) Capsules
Z) Glycopeptidan Endomembrane
ANSWER: W) Tests
23. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of these groups of protists have a delicate, intricately symmetrical skeleton generally made of silica?
W) Ciliates
X) Rhizarians
Y) Psuedoskeletists
Z) Radiolarians
ANSWER: Z) Radiolarians
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In charophytes, a layer of which durable polymer prevents exposed zygotes from drying out?
ANSWER: SPOROPOLLENIN
24. BONUS Biology Short Answer In many lycophytes and in most gymnosperms, groups of sporophylls form cone-like structures called what?
ANSWER: STROBILI
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the symbiotic relationship in which both organisms are harmed?
ANSWER: COMPETITION
25. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Within Bryophytes, what is the gamete-producing structure called?
W) Gametophyte
X) Gametophore
Y) Angiosperm
Z) Gymnosperm
ANSWER: X) Gametophore
