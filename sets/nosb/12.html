Source packet

2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What name is given to the area where the northeast and southeast trade winds converge?
ANSWER: Intertropical Convergence Zone
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are two stressors that affect rocky coast, intertidal organisms?
ANSWER: ANY 2 OF: dessication, temperature variation, salinity extremes, high wave energy, sun exposure, predation, log battering, or ice scour
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer How do snapping shrimp make their characteristic noise?
ANSWER: Collapse of the bubble created by the shrimp's claw
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What submarine was used in the discovery of the Galapagos hydrothermal vents in the late 1970's?
ANSWER: Alvin
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the longest coral reef system in the world?
ANSWER: Great Barrier Reef
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are two human ailments caused by phytoplanktonic toxins which become concentrated in the tissues of edible shellfish?
ANSWER: ANY 2 OF: Paralytic shellfish poisoning, amnesic shellfish poisoning, neurotoxic shellfish poisoning, diarrhetic shellfish poisoning
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of thermometer is used to measure temperature in deep water?
ANSWER: A reversing thermometer
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The knot, a unit of speed, is equal to how many nautical miles?
ANSWER: One
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the second largest whale in the world?
ANSWER: Fin Whale (also accept: Fin)
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the most accurate navigational system in the south Indian Ocean?
ANSWER: Satellite navigation
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer How many appendages does a decapod have?
ANSWER: 10
25. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the major difference between a jetty and a groin?
ANSWER: Groins protect beaches, jetties protect entrances to bays, rivers, and harbors
26. BONUS Biology Short Answer The salt marsh grass Spartina alterniflora is an example of which type of plant that grows in highly saline waters?
ANSWER: Halophyte
28. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of tissue, filled with air spaces for buoyancy in the water, is found in hydrophytes?
ANSWER: Aerenchyma
29. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What would you measure with a CTD?
ANSWER: conductivity, temperature, pressure (also accept: salinity, temperature, pressure)
30. BONUS Biology Short Answer What three factors regulate primary production?
ANSWER: Sunlight, grazing (consumption by herbivores), Nutrient /trace element availability
31. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of vessel, used in the 1960s and 1970s, evolved from the design of a catamaran and was used as oceanographic research vessels or submarine rescue ships?
ANSWER: SWATH (also accept: Small Waterplane Area Twin Hull)
32. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the traditional unit of ocean water salinity?
ANSWER: parts per thousand by weight (also accept: grams/kilogram)
34. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer About 41 percent of U.S. marine waters are protected in some way, but what percent is considered highly protected in no-take MPAs to protect sensitive species and habitats?
ANSWER: Three
36. BONUS Biology Short Answer After a centric diatom has divided several times, a daughter cell reaches a critical size that is too small for further division. How then do they reproduce?
ANSWER: They reproduce sexually
37. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the names for the two glacial periods in an ice age?
ANSWER: Stadials AND Interstadials
38. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of relationship exists when both organisms benefit, such as the cleaner shrimp and large reef fish?
ANSWER: Mutualism
39. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is one potential anthropogenic source of nitrogen to a coastal marine environment?
ANSWER: ANY 1 OF: Power plant exhaust, Automobile emissions, Sewage treatment plant, Stormwater Runoff, CAFO (concentrated animal feeding operations) OR Flooding
40. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which science predicts and tabulates positions of heavenly bodies so as to be useful in celestial navigation?
ANSWER: Nautical astronomy
41. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is one function of marine bacteria in a food web?
ANSWER: ANY 1 OF: they recycle nutrients; they are decomposers; they are a food source for other organisms
42. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The telemetry and power to the Hawaii-2 Observatory were made available via a retired telephone cable from what company?
ANSWER: AT & T
44. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Why does water boil and freeze at temperatures very different from other substances having similar molecular weights?
ANSWER: Hydrogen bonding between water molecules
45. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What does a transmissometer measure?
ANSWER: Water clarity (also accept: transparency)
46. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer How do capillary waves aid in the formation of larger waves?
ANSWER: Capillary waves increase the roughness (hence increase the surface area for wind contact) of the ocean surface
48. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is surimi?
ANSWER: It is a processed fish product
50. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What process often occurs on the seaward side of seawalls?
ANSWER: Erosion
51. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In which ocean would you find the Gulf Stream?
ANSWER: The North Atlantic
53. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer New sea floor is created by what kind of major tectonic process?
ANSWER: Sea-floor spreading
55. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What process distributes heat in water when said water is heated from below?
ANSWER: Convection
56. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are two types of divergent plate boundaries?
ANSWER: -Oceanic (Mid-ocean ridge) -Continental (rift valley)
57. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Place the following FIVE navigational advances in correct chronological order of discovery: magnetic compass, chronometer, Global Positioning System, astrolabe, gyrocompass
ANSWER: 1- magnetic compass, 2- astrolabe, 3- chronometer, 4- gyrocompass, 5- Global Positioning System (GPS)
58. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How does the pH change as one goes from fresh water to the marine environment?
ANSWER: The pH increases
59. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the deepest zone of the benthic province?
ANSWER: Hadal zone
60. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What are two sources of NEW nutrients to the ocean?
ANSWER: ANY 2 OF: River inputs, Atmospheric inputs (dust, fixed nitrogen from lightning), In-situ nitrogen fixation, Underwater volcanoes, Nitrification
62. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What process caused worldwide sea levels to rise starting 18,000 years ago?
ANSWER: Melting of large ice sheets at the end of the last ice age
64. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What geologic period do we currently live in?
ANSWER: Quaternary
67. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is one of the three government agencies responsible for researching, drafting, and publishing nautical charts?
ANSWER: ANY 1 OF: National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA); National Ocean Service (NOS); OR Department of Commerce
68. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What effect is the increase in atmospheric carbon dioxide concentration likely to have on pH?
ANSWER: pH will decrease
71. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the two taxonomic superfamilies of nudibranchs?
ANSWER: Dorids (or Doridoidea) AND aeolids (or Aeolidioidea)
72. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What caused the east-west distortion on early maps?
ANSWER: Difficulty in accurately measuring time
73. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are isolated volcanic peaks in the ocean called?
ANSWER: Seamounts
77. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the two major types of deep-sea oozes?
ANSWER: Calcareous AND siliceous
78. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Greenwich Mean Time was formerly used as the international civil time standard. What has officially superseded GMT in that function?
ANSWER: Coordinated Universal Time (also accept: UTC)
80. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the vessel that was first used to dive to the bottom of the Marianas Trench?
ANSWER: The Trieste
81. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What two chemical species are principally responsible for buffering seawater?
ANSWER: Carbonate and Bicarbonate
82. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What two forces result in the theoretical surface current pattern known as an Ekman spiral?
ANSWER: friction (drag) and the Coriolis "force
83. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Who is known as the "Father of Oceanography"?
ANSWER: Matthew Fontaine Maury
84. BONUS Biology Short Answer Zooxanthellae, which have a symbiotic relationship with coral polyps, provide energy for the algae through which process?
ANSWER: Photosynthesis
