1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Patrick stretches a rubber band and notices that beyond a certain length, the rubber band becomes weaker and does not return to the original length. What is the term for this type of permanent deformation of materials?
ANSWER: Plastic (also accept: Plastic deformation; plasticity)
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the image created by a concave lens of an object located between the lens and its focal point?
W) Real, upright, and smaller than its real size
X) Real, upright, and larger than its real size
Y) Virtual, upright, and smaller than its real size
Z) Virtual, upright, and larger than its real size
ANSWER: Y) Virtual, upright, and smaller than its real size
2. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following characterizes the function f(x) = |x| at the point x = 0?
W) Both continuous and differentiable
X) Continuous but not differentiable
Y) Differentiable but not continuous
Z) Neither continuous nor differentiable
ANSWER: X) Continuous but not differentiable
2. BONUS Math Short Answer How many 5 digit palindromes have neither 2 nor 6 as a digit?
ANSWER: 448
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following metamorphic rocks are NOT correctly matched with their respective protoliths?
W) Marble, limestone
X) Slate, shale;
Y) Quartzite, basalt
Z) Gniess, Slate;
ANSWER: Y) Quartzite, basalt
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of coal has the lowest carbon content?
W) Anthracite
X) Bituminous
Y) Sub-bituminous
Z) Lignite
ANSWER: Z) Lignite
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Leaf senescence, fruit ripening, and the triple response are all processes stimulated by what gaseous plant hormone?
ANSWER: Ethylene
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer During the secretory phase, the combined effects of estradiol and what other hormone mantains and develops the uterine lining?
ANSWER: Progesterone
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the molecular geometry of iodine trifluoride?
W) Trigonal planar
X) Trigonal pyramidal
Y) T-shaped
Z) Tetrahedral
ANSWER: Y) T-shaped
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of a highly volatile compound 1) It has a low value of delta H of vaporization 2) It has a low vapor pressure 3) It has relatively weak intermolecular forces.
ANSWER: 1 and 3
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at the Johns Hopkins Salzberg Lab are using computational tools to discover novel transcripts produced from genes. What process during gene expression allows a single gene to generate multiple different mRNA transcripts?
ANSWER: Alternative splicing (do not accept: RNA splicing)
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at Stanford’s Diffenbaugh Lab are studying how pressure systems contribute to extreme weather. Which of the following is most commonly associated with high-pressure systems?
W) Rising air and widespread precipitation
X) Strong convection and thunderstorms
Y) Sinking air and clear skies
Z) Rapid cyclogenesis
ANSWER: Y) Sinking air and clear skies
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Selena has two rooms. One room has three good bunnies and two bad bunnies, and the other room has one good bunny and four bad bunnies. If she randomly and uniformly picks a room and a bunny from that room, what is the probability that she picks a bad bunny?
ANSWER: 3/5
7. BONUS Math Multiple Choice The derivative of sin(x) cos(x) is equivalent to which of the following trigonometric functions?
W) sin(x)
X) cos(x)
Y) sin(2x)
Z) cos(2x)
ANSWER: Z) cos(2x)
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Nodes of Ranvier are regions of the axon not surrounded by what lipid-rich insulating layer deposited by glial cells?
ANSWER: Myelin (also accept: Myelin sheath)
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of horizontal gene transfer that involve the exchange of DNA between two living organisms 1) Transformation 2) Transduction 3) Conjugation.
ANSWER: 3 only
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three minerals by increasing number of planes of cleavage 1) Olivine 2) Biotite 3) Fluorite.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What roughly spherical region of icy objects surrounds the Solar System far beyond the Kuiper Belt and is thought to be the primary source of long-period comets?
ANSWER: Oort cloud
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The difference in electric potential between two points is equal to the negative line integral of what quantity along path connecting those two points?
ANSWER: Electric field
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer Avery pushes on a syringe with a force of 1 newton. If the body of her syringe is circular with a radius of 1 centimeter, to one significant figure, how much pressure in pascals is applied to the fluid exiting the syringe?
ANSWER: 3000
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Scientists at UCLA’s VAST Lab are studying big data compression. In least-squares optimization, what term describes the relationship between the residual vector and the column space of the matrix when the residual has zero dot product with every vector in that column space?
ANSWER: Orthogonal (also accept: Perpendicular)
11. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at UCLA’s Belongie Lab are studying how machines recognize visual patterns. What process occurs when a computer correctly identifies an unseen handwritten digit by using information learned from similar examples in the same dataset?
W) Extrapolation
X) Interpolation
Y) Random sampling
Z) Quantization
ANSWER: X) Interpolation
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Akshath adds silver nitrate to his tea and notices that it changes from green to blue. If he analyses his tea with spectroscopy, which of the following statements would likely be true?
W) The peak absorption wavelength increases
X) The peak absorption wavelength decreases
Y) The peak emission wavelength increases
Z) The peak emission wavelength decreases
ANSWER: W) The peak absorption wavelength increases
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three materials by increasing band gap size 1) Germanium 2) Silicon 3) Diamond.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the reciprocal of the sum of the reciprocals of 3, 4, and 6?
ANSWER: 4/3
13. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the sum of the digits of the base 9 representation of the number 1348?
ANSWER: 4
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Two blocks of mass 2m and m are traveling directly towards each other at speed v. If the blocks collide perfectly inelastically, then in terms of v, what will be the final speed of the blocks?
ANSWER: 3v
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer Baby Saja flies by Earth on a spaceship at a speed of 3/5 times the speed of light relative to the Earth. What is the ratio of the width of Earth he measures from the spaceship to the width of Earth measured by an observer stationary relative to Earth?
ANSWER: 4/5 (also accept: 0.8; 4:5)
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What component of an electrochemical cell typically allows for ion migration between the half-cells to maintain electrical neutrality and complete the circuit?
ANSWER: Salt bridge
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three compounds that are interstitial alloys 1) Steel 2) Brass 3) Bronze.
ANSWER: 1 only
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Aditya observes a cell in which the rate of exocytosis is much greater than the rate of endocytosis. Which of the following organelles in this cell would experience the smallest reduction in the size of its membrane?
W) Mitochondria
X) Endoplasmic reticulum
Y) Golgi apparatus
Z) Endosome
ANSWER: W) Mitochondria
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three features that are shared between all chordates 1) Presence of a vertebrae 2) Development of the blastopore into the anus 3) Bilateral body symmetry.
ANSWER: 2 and 3
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at the Johns Hopkins Fridman Lab are studying how electric fields in neurons affect cytoskeletal structure. Which of the following statements about electric fields is NOT true?
W) Electric field lines point in the direction of decreasing electric potential
X) Electric field lines cannot cross each other
Y) No work is done when a charge moves along an equipotential surface
Z) Equipotential surfaces are parallel to electric field lines
ANSWER: Z) Equipotential surfaces are parallel to electric field lines
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists at the Johns Hopkins Rabb Lab are studying kidney injury. Identify all of the following three functions that are primarily performed by the kidneys 1) Regulation of water and electrolyte balance 2) Filtration of blood to remove metabolic waste products 3) Metabolism of drugs and toxins through enzymatic breakdown.
ANSWER: 1 and 2
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The galactic merger of the Milky Way and Andromeda galaxies will likely result in a short burst of star formation before evolving into what type of galaxy?
W) Spiral
X) Tidal
Y) Elliptical
Z) Irregular
ANSWER: Y) Elliptical
18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Freshly cleaved surfaces of many metallic or sulfide minerals appear brighter than older exposed surfaces. Which of the following best explains this observation?
W) Slip planes exibit higher reflectivity
X) Cleaving aligns crystal lattices
Y) Weathering produces an oxide coating on exposed surfaces
Z) Internal cleavage planes scatter less light
ANSWER: Y) Weathering produces an oxide coating on exposed surfaces
19. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A thin spherical shell of radius a contains a charge q. The electric field at a distance r outside the shell is proportional to which of the following expressions?
W) 1/r3
X) 1/r2
Y) 1/r
Z) r
ANSWER: X) 1/r2

ANSWER: 3

W) NaCl
X) KBr

21. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In a room of 25 people, 20 people like cats and 12 people like dogs. Given that nobody in the room dislikes both cats and dogs, how many people like both cats and dogs?
ANSWER: 7

ANSWER: (3/2)π + √3
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A major food source for benthic species is the dead organic matter that sinks down from the productive surface waters of the photic zone. What is the name of this material?
ANSWER: Detritus (also accept: Marine snow)
22. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The receptor to which of the following hormones would act as a transcription factor?
W) Testosterone
X) Oxytocin

Z) Prolactin
ANSWER: W) Testosterone
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Due to ocean acidification, calcium carbonate dissolves more readily in deep water. The upward shift of what oceanic boundary reflects the shallower limit of carbonate sediment accumulation?
ANSWER: Carbonate compensation depth (also accept: CCD; lysocline)
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The First and Second Great Oxygenation Events, driven by the rise of oxygen-producing microorganisms, occurred during what geologic eon?
ANSWER: Proterozoic
