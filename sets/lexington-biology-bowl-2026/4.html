Packet 4 (Round 4) of Lexington Biology Bowl 2026:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Ced 3 and Ced 4 are examples of what class of proteases that cause apoptosis?
ANSWER: CASPASES
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following three steps of lipid digestion in order 1) formation of chylomicrons 2) digestion by gastric lipase 3) emulsification by bile salts
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Bacteria are able to attach to their host cells via short hair like pili possessing adesins. What is the term for these appendages?
W) Flagella
X) Endospore
Y) Glycocalyx
Z) Fimbria
ANSWER: Z) Fimbria
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following three structures in the circulatory system by increasing partial pressure of oxygen 1) Pulmonary vein 2) Pulmonary artery 3) Aorta
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Rank the following three nitrogenous bases by increasing molecular weight 1) Guanine 2) Uracil 3) Thymine
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The Pre-Botzinger complex is responsible for generating the inspiratory breathing rhythm in mammals. It is found within the ventral respiratory group located in which of the following regions of the brain?
W) Pons
X) Medulla
Y) Midbrain
Z) Cerebellum
ANSWER: X) Medulla
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following carbohydrates is not an aldose?
W) Glyceraldehyde
X) Erythrose
Y) Ribulose
Z) Glucose
ANSWER: Y) Ribulose
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Agrobacterium tumefaciens is frequently used to generate tumors in which of the following organisms?
W) Mammals
X) Plants
Y) Insects
Z) Humans
ANSWER: X) Plants
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Pufferfishes and blue-ringed octopuses are highly venomous due to their symbiotic associations with bacteria that produce what sodium channel-inhibiting neurotoxin?
ANSWER: TETRODOTOXIN
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What specialized organelle found primarily in germinating seeds is responsible for the conversion of fat energy into the formation of sugars?
ANSWER: GLYOXYSOME
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly pairs a cis-acting element with the trans-acting factor that binds to it?
W) Promoter and RNA Polymerase
X) Operator and Repressor protein
Y) Enhancer and Transcription Factor
Z) Insulator and Inducer
ANSWER: X) Operator and Repressor protein
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer An estuary is an example of what transitional areas between communities of organisms?
ANSWER: ECOTONE
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Unevenly thick, nonlignified cell walls are characteristic of what type of plant ground tissue?
W) Parenchyma
X) Collenchyma
Y) Sclerenchyma
Z) Aerenchyma
ANSWER: X) Collenchyma
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following plant phyla is vascular?
W) Bryophyta
X) Hepatophyta
Y) Lycophyta
Z) Anthocerophyta
ANSWER: Y) Lycophyta
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Fumarase is used to synthesize malate through the combination fumarate and what other molecule?
ANSWER: WATER (also accept: H2O)
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Simon notices that in humans, zygotes that have monosomy syndromes are almost always inviable. However, a few are able to develop to full adulthood. These few that develop to adulthood have the monosomy of which chromosome?
ANSWER: X CHROMOSOME
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Sean goes into his farm and marks 50 pigs. When he samples 10 pigs a week later, 1 of them are marked. How many pigs does Sean estimate he has on his farm?
ANSWER: 500
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What waxy polymer found in the casparian strip blocks the apoplastic route, forcing nutrient transport through the symplastic route instead?
ANSWER: SUBERIN
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What term describes the specialized nitrogen-fixing cells in a filamentous chain of Anabaena Cyanobacterium?
ANSWER: HETEROCYSTS
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Damon the Doctor is examining Evin's health after reports of hypoxia. After a not so careful examination, Evin is diagnosed with pernicious anemia. Evin's condition is caused by the autoimmune death of parietal cells that secrete what protein required for the absorption of Vitamin B12?
ANSWER: INTRINSIC FACTOR (also accept: IF)
