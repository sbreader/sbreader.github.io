1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The shell theorem applies to forces which have magnitudes proportional to what power of distance?
ANSWER: 2 (also accept: INVERSE-SQUARE LAW)
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice The Rayleigh form of what phenomenon explains why the daytime sky is blue?
W) Photoelectric effect
X) Scattering
Y) Dispersion
Z) Diffraction
ANSWER: X) Scattering
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Research at Jefferson National Laboratory has determined the weak charge of a proton, a measure of the strength of the weak force on an object. Which of the following are the three bosons that carry the weak force?
W) W, Z+, Z-
X) W, Z, Z-
Y) W+, W-, Z
Z) W+, W, Z
ANSWER: Y) W+, W-, Z
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer To measure the weak charge of a proton, the scientists at Jefferson National Lab scientists bombarded liquid hydrogen with an intense beam of highly polarized electrons. The high polarization of the beam influences the quantum spin of the electrons. In a highly polarized beam, the spin of most particles in the beam will be in what direction or directions relative to the beam direction?
ANSWER: PARALLEL TO THE BEAM DIRECTION AND ANTIPARALLEL TO THE BEAM DIRECTION (also accept: PARALLEL AND ANTIPARALLEL)

4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following BEST explains the purpose of performing the indole test on a bacterium?

ANSWER: SYNODIC MONTH
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is true regarding mean solar days and sidereal days?
W) a sidereal day is defined as the time interval from 12:00 AM one day to 12:00 AM the next
X) a mean solar day is approximately 2 minutes shorter than a sidereal day
Y) a mean solar day is approximately 4 minutes longer than a sidereal day
