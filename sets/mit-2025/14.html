TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at MIT’s Picower Institute have been studying the glymphatic system, a system similar to the function of the lymphatic system for circulating cerebrospinal fluid. ANSWER: EPENDYMAL CELLS
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at the Broad Institute are characterizing the functions of noncoding sequences in the genome. ANSWER: 1 AND 3
