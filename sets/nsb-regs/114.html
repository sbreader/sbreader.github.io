1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following are two optical factors that reduce atmospheric transparency?
W) Reflectivity and absorption
X) Reflectivity and conduction
Y) Absorption and convection
Z) Advection and precipitation
ANSWER: W) Reflectivity and absorption
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following four layers of the Earth in order from the Earth’s surface to its center 1) liquid core 2) lithosphere 3) asthenosphere 4) solid core.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1, 4 (also accept: LITHOSPHERE, ASTHENOSPHERE, LIQUID CORE, SOLID CORE)

5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice On a per gram basis, why do carbohydrates contain less energy than hydrocarbons?
