Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following 4 compounds by increasing dipole moment 1) CH3F 2) CH2F2 3) CHF3 4) CF4
ANSWER: 4, 3, 1, 2
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 3 statements that are true about ionization energy 1) Ionization energy of neutral elements is always endothermic 2) the second ionization energy of an atom is always greater than the first ionization energy 3) the ionization energy of a gas phase atom is greater than that of the gas phase diatomic molecule of the same element
ANSWER: 1, 2
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following 3 compounds by increasing electrophilicity 1) aldehyde 2) carboxylic acid 3) ketone
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Abhinav is trying to separate an alcohol and a carboxylic acid using a separatory funnel. Adding which of the following substances would be most helpful in accomplishing this task?
W) Phosphoric Acid
X) Hydrochloric Acid
Y) Sodium Bicarbonate
Z) Sodium Hydroxide
ANSWER: Y) Sodium Bicarbonate
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What theory describes the bonding, electronic structure, and orbital arrangements in coordination complexes by applying molecular orbital theory and crystal field theory?
ANSWER: Ligand Field Theory
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Fictitious gases A and B are introduced to the left and right ends, respectively, of a 5m hollow tube and allowed to meet. At their meeting spot, they react and form a visible, solid ring in the tube. If the molar mass of A is 80 g/mol and the molar mass of B is 5 g/mol, how many meters from the middle of the tube will the two gases meet?
ANSWER: 1.5
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following gases has the highest van der waals A factor?
W) Boron Trifluoride
X) Argon
Y) Hydrazine
Z) Oxygen
ANSWER: Y) Hydrazine
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three reactions which will be endothermic 1) First Electron Affinity of Beryllium 2) First Electron Affinity of Phosphorus 3) Second Electron Affinity of Oxygen
ANSWER: 1, 3
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following allotropes of phosphorus has the highest electrical conductivity?
W) White Phosphorus
X) Red Phosphorus
Y) Black Phosphorus
Z) Violet Phosphorus
ANSWER: Y) Black Phosphorus
5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What are the HOMO and LUMO of nitric oxide, respectively?
W) Pi bonding, pi antibonding
X) Pi antibonding, pi antibonding
Y) Sigma bonding, pi antibonding
Z) Pi antibonding, sigma antibonding
ANSWER: X) Pi antibonding, pi antibonding
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Glucose is an example of which of the following types of compounds?
W) hydrate
X) hemiacetal
Y) acetal
Z) Ester
ANSWER: X) hemiacetal
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the steric repulsions between the two inward-facing hydrogen atoms at either end of a cyclohexane boat conformation?
ANSWER: Flagpole Interactions
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice XeF6 adopts which of the following geometries in the gas phase?
W) Octahedral
X) Capped octahedral
Y) Pentagonal bipyramidal
Z) Square Planar
ANSWER: X) Capped octahedral
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The species [Mn(CO)5] with a charge z obeys the 18 electron rule. What is the sign and magnitude of the charge z?
ANSWER: -1
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following functional groups would have the most downfield carbon in an H-NMR spectrum?
W) Amide
X) Alcohol
Y) Amine
Z) Carboxylic Acid
ANSWER: Z) Carboxylic Acid
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the first step of an aromatic nitration reaction?
W) The aromatic ring attacks the nitrate ion
X) the nitrate ion undergoes an elimination reaction
Y) the aromatic ring is protonated
Z) the aromatic ring is deprotonated by the bisulfate ion
ANSWER: X) the nitrate ion undergoes an elimination reaction
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the chemical formula of fuming sulfuric acid which is created by bubbling sulfur trioxide into sulfuric acid?
ANSWER: H2S2O7
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three radicals by decreasing stability 1) Vinyl Radical 2) Aryl Radical 3) Methyl Radical
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following four carboxylic acid derivatives by increasing reactivity 1) Amide 2) Anhydride 3) Acyl Chloride 4) Ester
ANSWER: 1, 4, 2, 3
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Helium-4, Oxygen-16, Calcium-40 are all examples of what nuclei, which have exceptionally high binding energy and enhanced stability compared to neighboring isotopes due to having numbers of protons and neutrons that are both a certain type of number?
ANSWER: Doubly Magic Nuclei
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following could be the electron configuration of a low spin octahedral complex?
W) t2g4 eg0
X) t2g4 eg1
Y) t2g4 eg2
Z) t2g4 eg3
ANSWER: W) t2g4 eg0
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In the disproportionation of phosphorous acid to form phosphoric acid and phosphine, what is the ratio between the amount of phosphoric acid and phosphine produced?
W) 1:2
X) 2:1
Y) 1:3
Z) 3:1
ANSWER: Z) 3:1
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following types of ions will be solvated by water the strongest?
W) Low ionic radius, Low Charge
X) Low ionic radius, High Charge
Y) High ionic radius, Low Charge
Z) High ionic radius, High Charge
ANSWER: X) Low ionic radius, High Charge
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav heats Calcium Oxalate and notices two drops in mass of the sample. What is the identity of the compounds after the first and second drop in mass?
ANSWER: Calcium Carbonate and Calcium Oxide (also accept: CaCO3 and CaO)
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer While hydroboration oxidation of terminal alkenes yields alcohols, the hydroboration oxidation of terminal alkynes yields what functional group?
ANSWER: Aldehyde
14. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following reactions could not lead to a racemic mixture?
W) Reaction of a Ketone with Lithium Aluminum Hydride
X) Free Radical Halogenation
Y) Reaction of Acetone with Hydrogen Cyanide
Z) Reaction of Ethanal with Sodium Methanethiolate
ANSWER: Y) Reaction of Acetone with Hydrogen Cyanide
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the compact diagrams that show the standard reduction potentials for different oxidation states of a chemical element using arrows with the reduction potential listed above the arrow?
ANSWER: Latimer Diagrams
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three complexes by decreasing wavelength of max absorbance 1) hexafluorocobaltate(III) ion 2) hexacyanocobaltate(III) ion 3) hexaaquacobalt(III) ion
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, order the following three types of stereochemical conformations by increasing likelihood of leading to an elimination reaction 1) Coplanar 2) Antiperiplanar 3) Periplanar
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The Swern Oxidation oxidizes primary alcohols to aldehydes through the use of trimethylamine, DMSO, and what other compound?
ANSWER: oxalyl chloride (also accept: (COCl)2; C2O2Cl2)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following processes is used to industrially produce titanium?
W) Dow Process
X) Hall Heroult Process
Y) Kroll Process
Z) Claus Process
ANSWER: Y) Kroll Process
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The Monsanto Process is commercially used to produce acetic acid. What transition metal is present in the catalyst used for this process?
ANSWER: Rhodium
