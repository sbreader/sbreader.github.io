1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the molecular geometry of the trihydrogen cation, H3+?
ANSWER: Equilateral Triangle
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Both AlCl3 and ICl3 dimerize to form Al2Cl6 and I2Cl6, respectively. Identify by name or number all of the following three ways in which these latter structures differ 1) Hybridization of central atoms 2) Geometry of molecule 3) Connectivity of atoms.
ANSWER: 1,2
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Allene is a hydrocarbon in which a central carbon is double-bonded to two different methylene (CH2) groups. Identify all of the 3 following statements that are correct about allene 1) The molecule is planar 2) The central carbon is sp-hybridized 3) Allene has 2 distinct H-NMR signals
ANSWER: 2 only
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three ketones by increasing reactivity 1) 3-fluorobutan-2-one 2) 3-methoxybutan-2-one 3) 3-chlorobutan-2-one
ANSWER: 2,3,1
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following ligand types is considered a strong-field ligand that often results in low-spin complexes?
W) Halides
X) Water
Y) Ammonia
Z) Cyanide
ANSWER: Z) Cyanide
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the primary reason as to why BF3 is a weaker Lewis Acid than expected?
W) The greater electronegativity of fluorine causes a stronger inductive effect.
X) Effective pi-backbonding from the fluorine 2p orbital to the boron 2p orbital reduces the deficiency of the boron atom.
Y) The larger size of the Iodine Atom in BI3 creates less steric hindrance for an incoming Lewis base compared to BF3.
Z) Boron is a non-metal, while the heavier halogens have some metallic character, affecting bond polarity.
ANSWER: X) Effective pi-backbonding from the fluorine 2p orbital to the boron 2p orbital reduces the deficiency of the boron atom.
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What equation calculates reaction rate based on temperature by analyzing the activated complex rather than collision energy, and relates the rate constant of a reaction to the Gibbs free energy of activation?
ANSWER: Eyring Equation
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Given that the Bohr radius for hydrogen is 52.9 picometers, calculate, in picometers, the most probable distance of the electron from the nucleus for a hydrogen atom in the 2p orbital.
ANSWER: 211.6
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following describes the solubility of Ag in AgCl as NH3 is slowly added?
W) Increases
X) Decreases
Y) Increases, then decreases
Z) Decreases, then increases
ANSWER: Z) Decreases, then increases
5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Sudeep is performing a titration to determine the molar mass of a monoprotic acid by titrating it with 0.10M sodium hydroxide. He adds 2g of the unknown acid and 50 mL of water to the titration flask and begins his titration. Which of the following errors will lead to Sudeep getting a lower molar mass than expected?
W) He adds 1.5 grams of the acid instead of 2 grams
X) He spills some of the solution in the titration flask before beginning his titration
Y) The volumetric pipette had distilled water in it before he added the 0.10 M sodium hydroxide
Z) He titrates to a dark red color instead of a faint pink
ANSWER: Z) He titrates to a dark red color instead of a faint pink
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In Aldol Condensations, the elimination of water does not occur by a typical E1 or E2 reaction. What is the name of the mechanism for the elimination in Aldol Condensations?
ANSWER: E1cb

ANSWER: 1,2,3

13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Carl is trying to protect his iron statue with a silver coating! 15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the average oxidation number of molybdenum in PMo12O407-?
ANSWER: 5 ⅔ (also accept: 17/3, 5.67)
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Akhil has solutions of two different fluorescent compounds, with one solution fluorescing green and the other solution fluorescing red. When the solutions are mixed, the intensity of green fluorescence decreases while the intensity of red fluorescence increases. What mechanism of energy transfer is likely occurring?
ANSWER: Förster resonance energy transfer (also accept: FRET; fluorescence resonance energy transfer; resonance energy transfer; RET; electronic energy transfer; EET)
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In contrast to Förster resonance energy transfer, an excited electron can be transferred from an excited molecule to another molecule. What is the name of this mechanism?
ANSWER: Dexter electron transfer (also accept: Dexter energy transfer; Dexter electron exchange)
