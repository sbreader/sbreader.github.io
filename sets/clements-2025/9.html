1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Individuals with obsessive-compulsive personality disorder have a high rate of anxiety, attention to detail, and perfectionism. A psychologist suspects that a person may have OCPD and refers them to obtain an MRI of their brain. Which of the following regions of the brain will be most illuminated?
W) Orbitofrontal cortex
X) Hypothalamus
Y) Hippocampus
Z) Ventral tegmental area
ANSWER: W) Orbitofrontal cortex
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the hormone responsible for Venus flytrap digestive enzyme secretion.
ANSWER: JASMONIC ACID
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The use of concentrated nitric acid as an oxidizing agent results in the creation of which nitrogen-containing gas?
ANSWER: NO2
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following four oxides of nitrogen in order of increasing average oxidation state of nitrogen 1) NO+ 2) N2O 3) NO 4) NO3-.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1, 4
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A 500 nm laser shines from air through a thin layer of water with index of refraction 1.33 sitting on a plate of glass with index of refraction 1.5. To one significant figure and in meters, what is the minimum thickness of the water layer so that the light reflects back to the laser?
ANSWER: 2 × 10-7 METERS
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice The Paschen-delta spectral line involves the transition between which two energy levels?
W) 4 to 3
X) 5 to 3
Y) 6 to 3
Z) 7 to 3
ANSWER: Z) 7 to 3
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Al, Lex, and Zander are sharing a large block of cheese. They take turns cutting off half of what remains and eating it: first Al eats half of the cheese, then Lex eats half of the remaining half, then Zander eats half of what remains, then back to Al, and so on. They stop when the cheese is too small to see. About what fraction of the original block of cheese does Al eat in total?
W) 4/7
X) 2/3
Y) 3/5
Z) 3/4
ANSWER: W) 4/7
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Faye colors each square in a 3-by-3 grid of squares either red, white, blue, or green so that every 2-by-2 square contains one square of each color. How many different designs can Faye make?
ANSWER: 72
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The main sequence turnoff point is used to determine which of the following properties of a star cluster?
W) Age
X) Composition
Y) Density
Z) Size
ANSWER: W) Age
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the changes occurring to Earth’s orbit when its eccentricity is increasing according to Milankovitch cycles?
W) Semimajor axis remains the same, semiminor axis increases
X) Semimajor axis remains the same, semiminor axis decreases
Y) Semiminor axis remains the same, semimajor axis increases
Z) Semiminor axis remains the same, semimajor axis decreases
ANSWER: X) Semimajor axis remains the same, semiminor axis decreases
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at the Sandia National Laboratories are investigating different types of batteries. Which of the following battery types is most prone to the memory effect, where the battery capacity is lowered if not fully discharged before recharging?
W) Lithium-Ion
X) Nickel-Metal Hydride
Y) Nickel-Cadmium
Z) Alkaline
ANSWER: Y) Nickel-Cadmium
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are synthesizing Plutonium 238 for NASA mission power sources. What type of generator is Pu-238 used in?
ANSWER: RADIOISOTOPE THERMOELECTRIC GENERATOR (do not accept: NUCLEAR GENERATOR)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What cell mechanism repairs bulky lesions in DNA?
ANSWER: NUCLEOTIDE EXCISION REPAIR
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer In homologous end joining, what is the junction between two strands of DNA called?
ANSWER: HOLLIDAY JUNCTION
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The d-orbital splitting energy of a tetrahedral metal ion complex is what fraction of that of an octahedral complex?
ANSWER: 4/9
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A square planar metal ion complex splits the 5 d-orbitals into how many distinct energy levels based on their shape?
ANSWER: 4
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following materials which exhibit paramagnetism at room temperature 1) Steel 2) Copper 3) Iron 4) Argon.
ANSWER: NONE
9. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Paramagnetism occurs because a large number of small magnetic dipoles orient themselves in similar directions when exposed to an external magnetic field. As the temperature of the system increases, what happens to its magnetic moment?
W) Decreases because lower energy states are more likely in higher temperatures
X) Decreases because higher energy states are more likely in higher temperatures
Y) Increases because lower energy states are more likely in higher temperatures
Z) Increases because higher energy states are more likely in higher temperatures
ANSWER: X) Decreases because higher energy states are more likely in higher temperatures
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Determine the limit as x approaches 0 of ln(x + 1)/(2 sin x).
ANSWER: 1/2 (also accept: .5; 0.5)
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Using the MacLaurin series for sine of x to obtain an approximation for the sine of 1 radian, determine the sine of 1 radian to 3 significant figures.
ANSWER: 0.841
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer An increase of solar radiation will result in an increase of the temperature of the earth, and as a result, an increase of radiation from the earth as well. By which law can the earth’s new radiative balance be calculated?
ANSWER: STEFAN-BOLTZMANN LAW
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Assume an inaccurate calculation for the earth’s equilibrium temperature is performed with the assumption that the earth is a perfect blackbody. Which of the following would be true of the earth’s actual temperature?

Y) Higher due to higher emission
