1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What peptide hormone, secreted by the S cells of the duodenum and jejunum in response to acidic chyme in the stomach, stimulates the pancreas to release bicarbonate in order to neutralize the acid?

1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 choices which are true regarding the HPT axis, also known as the hypothalamic-pituitary-thyroid axis 1) TRH is released by the anterior pituitary in response to low levels of thyroid hormones in the blood 2) The anterior pituitary receives TRH and produces TSH in response 3) TSH stimulates the thyroid gland to produce T3 and T4 4) Excess amounts of T3 and T4 help stimulate increased secretion of TSH
