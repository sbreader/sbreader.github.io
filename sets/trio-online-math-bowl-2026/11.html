2. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Let f(n) denote the smallest positive integer that has at least n positive factors. If f(n) = nα for a variable alpha, then which of the following best describes the behavior of alpha as n tends towards infinity?

X) alpha approaches a finite constant greater than 1
Y) alpha grows sub-logarithmically in n
Z) alpha grows logarithmically in n
ANSWER: Y) alpha grows sub-logarithmically in n

6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Ryan is looking at the 2012 Kyrgyzstan Olympiad, and sees a functional equation. He plugs in f(x) = x and f(x) = -x, and finds that they both work. He then sets off on proving that these are the only solutions. He eventually proves that f(t)2 = t2, and concludes that he is done. However, he has fallen for what trap, which states that f(t) = t or -t for any t, which means there are still an infinite amount of functions in contention?
ANSWER: POINTWISE

7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer While x2-y2 = n can be simply factored and solved over the integers using difference of squares, equations such as x2-my2 = n, for some integer m, cannot be always factored.
