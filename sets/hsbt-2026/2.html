1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice During a heavy rainstorm, Ethan notices streams forming in the steep gulches of the Ko'olau mountains. However, the next day, he notices that the streams have disappeared. Which of the following terms would best describe these streams?
W) Intermittent
X) Ephemeral
Y) Antecedent
Z) Perennial
ANSWER: X) Ephemeral
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three atmospheric boundaries in increasing average temperature 1) Tropopause 2) Stratopause 3) Mesopause.
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the granules that store sugar in muscles for short term, rapid access during exercise?
ANSWER: Glycosomes
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the primary respiratory pigment that transports oxygen in muscles?
ANSWER: Myoglobin
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer When adding electrons to the 2p subshell of a nitrogen atom, electrons occupy individual orbitals to minimize their mutual repulsion. This is a consequence of what rule, which states that electrons fill degenerate orbitals singly before pairing up?
ANSWER: Hund's rule
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In a galvanic cell with a sodium nitrate salt bridge, which of the following best describes the motion of the nitrate ions as the cell discharges?
W) They move toward the cathode to balance the charge of the depositing cations
X) They move toward the anode to balance the charge of the dissolved cations
Y) They remain stationary to prevent mixing of the half-cells
Z) They are consumed in the redox reaction at the electrode surface
ANSWER: X) They move toward the anode to balance the charge of the dissolved cations
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Haru is analyzing a geometric sequence with a first term of 6 and a third term of 24. What is the positive difference between the maximum and minimum possible values for the fourth term in this geometric sequence?
ANSWER: 96
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Let ABC be a triangle, and let I be the intersection of the angle bisectors. If angle A equals 68 degrees, what is angle BIC equal to?
ANSWER: 124
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Chris releases a solid spherical ball down a hill, and it rolls down without slipping. At the bottom, the surface suddenly becomes frictionless, and the ball begins rolling up another hill. Which of the following best explains what happens?
W) The ball returns to its original height, energy is conserved
X) The ball returns to its original height, energy is not conserved
Y) The ball ends at a lower height, energy is conserved
Z) The ball ends at a lower height, energy is not conserved
ANSWER: Y) The ball ends at a lower height, energy is conserved
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Which of the following four objects can produce a real image? 1) Convex lens 2) Concave lens 3) Convex mirror 4) Concave mirror.
ANSWER: 1 AND 4
6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer In an effort to comply with state energy laws, Hawaii closed its last coal-fired power plant in 2022. Identify all of the following three varieties of coal that have a higher rank than sub-bituminous coal 1) Bituminous coal 2) Lignite 3) Anthracite.
ANSWER: 1 and 3
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer In order to meet the baseload energy demand of consumers, power grids must incorporate "firm" power sources that ensure grid stability. Identify all of the following four renewable energy sources that could be considered "firm" 1) Solar 2) Wind 3) Geothermal 4) Biomass.
