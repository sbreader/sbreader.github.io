1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice This type of stain is used to identify bacteria with waxy material in their cell walls. It is primarily used to stain organisms in pathogenic genera of bacteria, the most famous of which are the Mycobacteria. Which of the following is the name of this stain?
W) Gram stain
X) Simple stain
Y) Acid-fast stain
Z) Endospore stain
ANSWER: Y) Acid-fast stain
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer The acid-fast stain is most commonly used on organisms of the genus Mycobacterium. Name the 2 most important pathogens from this genus.
ANSWER: Mycobacterium Tuberculosis, Mycobacterium Leprae
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following conditions will cause gases to deviate from ideal behavior?
W) High temperature
X) Low volume
Y) High pressure
Z) High molar mass
ANSWER: Y) High pressure
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following gases is most ideal?
W) CO2
X) XeF2
Y) Cl2
Z) H2O
ANSWER: W) CO2
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A ball of charge -50e lies at the center of a hollow spherical metal shell that has a net charge of -100e. What is the charge on the shell’s inner surface?
ANSWER: +50E
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer A spherical nonconducting balloon has a uniform positive charge on its surface. If the balloon is expanded, does the magnitude of the electric field due to the charge increase, decrease, or remain the same at points that 1) are inside the balloon 2) are on the balloon’s surface 3) were and still are outside?
ANSWER: 1) SAME; 2) DECREASE; 3) SAME
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice If a Pascal’s triangle is left-justified, the diagonal bands sum to which of the following sequences of numbers?
W) Squares of the natural numbers
X) Fibonacci numbers
Y) Triangle numbers
Z) Prime numbers
ANSWER: X) Fibonacci numbers
4. BONUS Math Short Answer A bean machine with four rows of pegs has five boxes at the bottom. A bean is dropped into the top. Assuming the bean has an equal chance to bounce either direction at each peg, what is the chance it will land in the middlemost box as a fraction reduced to simplest terms?
ANSWER: 3/8

W) 100
X) 20,000
Y) 50,000

7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What system of neurotransmitters are made up of decarboxylated amino acids? Two of the most famous and important neurotransmitters from this group are serotonin and dopamine.
W) Cholinergic
X) Biogenic
Y) Neuropeptide
Z) Amino acid
ANSWER: X) Biogenic
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer During the resting state of cell membranes, all ion channels are closed except for one specific type, which opens and closes at random intervals. Name this type of ion channel.
ANSWER: Leakage channel
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Only what 2 stable elements are liquid at room temperature?
ANSWER: Mercury and Bromine
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Mercury is an extremely poisonous metal to humans. What form of mercury is formed when microorganisms convert the element from an inorganic form to an organic form through the addition of carbon? Additionally, this substance is the most common organic mercury compound in the environment.
ANSWER: Methylmercury
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What scientist received the 1907 Nobel Prize in physics for his creation of the first interferometer?
ANSWER: A. A. MICHELSON
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer Sunlight illuminates a thin film of oil that floats on water, which has a greater index of refraction than the oil. The edge of the film has thickness L < 0.1 λ. Is the edge dark or bright?
ANSWER: BRIGHT
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the term for a generalization of the notion of a triangle or tetrahedron to arbitrary dimensions.?
ANSWER: SIMPLEX
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the surface volume of a tesseract with side length 2.
ANSWER: 64
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for hard, densely compacted sand that has built up over time?
ANSWER: LOESS
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A fluorescence microscope is a microscope that absorbs the light energy from photons and re-emits that light as new photons to create an image. If the re-emitted photons have half the momentum of the original photons, how many times longer is the wavelength of the new photons than that of the old photons?
ANSWER: 2

13. BONUS Biology Short Answer The thalamus is an important structure in the brain that is responsible for relaying information. All sensory information, except for one sense, passes through the thalamus. Which sense does not pass through the thalamus and directly travels to the cerebral cortex, which explains why it can be a potent trigger of memories and emotion.
ANSWER: Olfaction (also accept: Sense of smell / smell)

15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A horizontal force F pushes a block of mass m against a vertical wall. The coefficient of friction between the block and the wall is μ. What value of F is necessary to keep the block from slipping down the wall?

X) μ mg
Y) mg/μ
Z) mg(1-μ)
ANSWER: Y) mg/μ

19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice PTH is an important hormone in maintaining calcium levels in the bloodstream. What hormone counteracts the effects of PTH and what does this hormone do to calcium levels?

X) Calmodulin, which decreases calcium levels in the bloodstream.
Y) Calcitonin, which increases calcium levels in the bloodstream.
Z) Calmodulin, which increases calcium levels in the bloodstream.
