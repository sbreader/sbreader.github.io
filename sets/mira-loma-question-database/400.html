Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What two branches of igneous rock are formed when the magma inside of the substance begins to cool?
ANSWER: PLUTONIC AND VOLCANIC ROCK
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The formation of different types of igneous rock when magma is cooled within the substance is known as which of the following?
W) intrusion
X) foliation
Y) erosion
Z) submersion
ANSWER: W) intrusion
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When chemical precipitates are deposited and then followed by the compaction of particulate matter and cementation, what type of rock is created?
ANSWER: SEDIMENTARY ROCK
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Any chemical, physical, or biological change undergone in sedimentary rock after an initial deposition is known as what process?
ANSWER: DIAGENESIS
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The science and study of solid liquid matter that usually constitutes the Earth is known as what?
ANSWER: GEOLOGY
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a time period in which most geological formations took place from the Pre-Cambrian scale?
W) Jurassic
X) Paleozoic
Y) Mesozoic
Z) Archean
ANSWER: Z) Archean
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Diamonds that are created usually from technological processes other than being naturally made in the environment are usually known as which of the following?
W) synthetic
X) spectroscopy
Y) covalent
Z) premature
ANSWER: W) synthetic
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the deposition process in which most synthetic diamonds are made?
ANSWER: CHEMICAL VAPOR DEPOSITION
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Aplite, a formation of rock that is very fine, is part of what volcanic rock type?
ANSWER: IGNEOUS
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following describes a type of igneous rock that is mainly of mafic composition and essentially a silica or under saturated base?
W) Akadite
X) Dacite
Y) Basanite
Z) Picrite
ANSWER: Y) Basanite
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The most common type of name that is usually given to rock types that were formed near the Earth's crust is known as what?
ANSWER: SEDIMENTARY ROCKS
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a component of most sedimentary rocks near the Earth's crust?
W) quartz
X) tephra
Y) dikes
Z) ruby
ANSWER: W) quartz
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Throughout mantle convection near the Earth's crust, material near the crust is cooled into what types of natural boundaries?
W) volcanic rock
X) oceans and the atmosphere
Y) lakes and seas
Z) rivers and rock
ANSWER: X) oceans and the atmosphere
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The theory that usually explains the large scale motion of the Earth's lithosphere, or known as an "earthquake," is known as the what?
ANSWER: PLATE TECTONICS THEORY
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What layer of the Earth contains the crust, uppermost mantle and the major parts of the sedimentary appearances?
ANSWER: THE LITHOSPHERE
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The outermost layer of the Earth in which is composed merely of soil and subject to soil formation is known as what?
ANSWER: THE PEDOSPHERE
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Any type of substance that usually creates a current flow of electricity that shifts around an soft-iron core of the Earth's crust is known as what?
ANSWER: ELECTROMAGNET
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following was the scientist to coin the term, "biosphere," to explain the "place on earth's surface in where life dwells?"
W) Lynn Margulis
X) Arthur Tansley
Y) Vladimir Vernandsky
Z) Edward Suess
ANSWER: Z) Edward Suess
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A global sum of the Earth's atmosphere in which most of the ecosystems present on Earth are together is known as what?
ANSWER: BIOSPHERE
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What layer of the Earth's atmosphere usually protects it from solar wind?
ANSWER: MAGNETOSPHERE
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When water vapor and carbon dioxide allow the Earth's atmosphere to catch and hold the sun's energy, a phenomenon occurs which is known as what?
ANSWER: GREENHOUSE EFFECT
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The second most abundant mineral in the Earth's continental crust after feldspar is known as what?
ANSWER: QUARTZ
13. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a gemstone?
W) sapphire
X) quartz
Y) feldspar
Z) pearl
ANSWER: W) sapphire
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When a sudden release of energy in the Earth's crust creates seismic waves, the result is a commonly known term used to describe what?
ANSWER: AN EARTHQUAKE
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following describe the point on the Earth's surface in that is directly above the hypocenter?
W) epicenter
X) calticenter
Y) danocenter
Z) faltocenter
ANSWER: W) epicenter
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice During most of the movement of tectonic plates, what resisting force usually causes the surfaces to grind once in contact?
W) momentum
X) inertia
Y) friction
Z) placement
ANSWER: Y) friction
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A field that usually exerts a force on particles with the property of an electric charge that is reciprocally affected by the presence of those particles is known as what?
ANSWER: ELECTROMAGNETISM
16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a fundamental force that takes place near the Earth's crust?
W) gravitational force
X) intermolecular force
Y) radioactive force
Z) decay force
ANSWER: W) gravitational force
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The composition of Earth's atmosphere re composed of what two elements?
ANSWER: NITROGEN AND OXYGEN
17. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the lowest portion of the Earth's atmosphere?
W) mesosphere
X) thermosphere
Y) exosphere
Z) troposphere
ANSWER: Z) troposphere
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What layer of the Earth's atmosphere is primarily assigned to contain relatively high concentrations of ultraviolet light?
ANSWER: THE OZONE LAYER
18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which portion of Earth's atmosphere contains the ozone layer?
W) exosphere
X) troposphere
Y) magnetosphere
Z) stratosphere
ANSWER: Z) stratosphere
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to the strip of boundary that describes the split between the Earth's atmosphere and outer space?
ANSWER: THE KARMAN LINE
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the largest layer of the Earth's atmosphere, which is directly above the mesosphere and below the exosphere?
ANSWER: THERMOSPHERE
20. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following layers of the Earth's atmosphere plays an important part in atmospheric electricity?
W) thermosphere
X) magnetosphere
Y) ionosphere
Z) stratosphere
ANSWER: Y) ionosphere
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The oxygen in the earth's atmosphere was probably produced is by which of the following?
W) volcanos
X) photosynthetic organisms
Y) radioactive minerals
Z) meteors
ANSWER: X) photosynthetic organisms
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which region of the electro-magnetic spectrum will cause sunburns?
W) ultraviolet
X) infrared
Y) visible
Z) invisible
ANSWER: W) ultraviolet
23. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The available incoming solar radiation is known as what?
W) reflection
X) insolation
Y) reradiation
Z) absorption
ANSWER: X) insolation
25. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Of the following minerals, the one most resistant to both chemical and mechanical weathering is what?
W) calcite
X) muscovite
Y) hornblende
Z) quartz
ANSWER: Z) quartz
