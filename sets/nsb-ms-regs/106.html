1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which species of tuna is in immediate danger of extinction due to overfishing?
W) Big eye tuna
X) Albacore
Y) Yellowfin tuna
Z) Southern bluefin tuna
ANSWER: Z) Southern bluefin tuna
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What four categories of biological macromolecules make up cells?
ANSWER: PROTEINS, NUCLEIC ACIDS, CARBOHYDRATES, AND LIPIDS (do not accept: FATS)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes how a chemical change occurs in a substance?
W) It changes state
X) It gains, loses, or shares electrons
Y) It reaches its boiling point
Z) It is ground into a powder
ANSWER: X) It gains, loses, or shares electrons
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Every ocean wave is generated by an energy source. What is the most likely cause of waves that reach over 100 feet in amplitude?
ANSWER: EARTHQUAKES
3. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice In which of the following states would you be more likely to find a methane digester on a farm or ranch?
W) Missouri
X) Wisconsin
Y) Utah
Z) Maryland
ANSWER: X) Wisconsin
3. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice The amount of CO2 produced from burning natural gas will be what percent of the CO2 produced from burning coal, for the same amount of energy output?
W) 30%
X) 50%
Y) 65%
Z) 80%
ANSWER: X) 50%
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice If you have a parent flower that has red petals and the other parent has white petals and they have offspring that are pink, which of the following genetic terms best applies?
W) Co-dominance
X) Expressivity
Y) Pleiotropy
Z) Incomplete dominance
ANSWER: Z) Incomplete dominance
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What organ system, unique to aquatic vertebrae, is used to detect movements and pressure changes in water?
ANSWER: LATERAL LINE
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the spectral class of the Sun?
ANSWER: G (also accept: G2, G2V)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following accurately describes the structure of the Sun?
W) Convective core, convective envelope
X) Radiative core, radiative envelope
Y) Convective core, radiative envelope
Z) Radiative core, convective envelope
ANSWER: Z) Radiative core, convective envelope
6. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A couple bought a house and calculated that they would pay 30% of their combined monthly income of $5,700 toward the monthly mortgage payment on the house. How much will the couple pay for their monthly mortgage payment?
W) $1,650
X) $1,710
Y) $1,900
Z) $2,400
ANSWER: X) $1,710
6. BONUS Math Short Answer The perimeter of your garden is 10 4/5 yards. You purchased wooden planks to edge the garden, but you accidentally bought 12 1/3 yards of wood. How many yards of wood will be unused?
ANSWER: 1 8/15 (also accept: 23/15)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In the spring of 2013, scientists from the University of Michigan and Louisiana State University predicted that a particular coastal region of the US would experience a record breaking dead-zone. For what region was this prediction made?
W) Gulf of Mexico
X) Puget Sound
Y) Gulf of California
Z) George's Bank
ANSWER: W) Gulf of Mexico
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the MOST common term for the general type of nourishment characteristic of mushrooms, in which the organism derives its nutrients by absorbing the biological products of a dead or dying organism?
ANSWER: SAPROPHYTIC (also accept: SAPROBE, SAPROPHAGE, SAPROPHYTE, SAPROZOIC,; SAPROTROPH)
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT correct about the gravitational force between two objects?
W) It behaves independently of the law of conservation of energy
X) It is a form of potential energy
Y) It is directly proportional to the mass of the two objects
Z) It is related to the distance between the objects
ANSWER: W) It behaves independently of the law of conservation of energy
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What substances, by definition, produce ions when dissolved in water?
W) Electrolytes
X) Elements

ANSWER: W) Electrolytes
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What term refers to the tip of a plant shoot or root that produces auxin, causing the shoot or root to increase in length?
ANSWER: APICAL MERISTEM (do not accept: APICAL OR MERISTEM ALONE)

14. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The largest voltage drop would be measured across which of the following circuit components?

X) Individual circuit component with 2 amp current and 8.5 ohm resistance

14. BONUS Physics Short Answer A diffraction grating for light acts somewhat like a prism: when light is viewed through it, the constituent colors that blend to create the original light are separated. If a white LED bulb is viewed through a diffraction grating, give the constituent colors that will be viewed by the observer looking through the grating, in the order that they appear.
ANSWER: RED, ORANGE, YELLOW, GREEN, BLUE, INDIGO, AND VIOLET (also accept: VIOLET, INDIGO, BLUE, GREEN, YELLOW, ORANGE, AND RED; VISIBLE SPECTRUM)

23. BONUS Math Short Answer In the arithmetic operation long division, answers are often expressed as a whole number quotient with a whole number remainder. ANSWER: MIXED FRACTION AND DECIMAL (also accept: 2 AND 3)
