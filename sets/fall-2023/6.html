1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In the light reaction of photosynthesis, what role does plastocyanin play in chloroplasts?
W) Photooxidation of water
X) Splitting of water
Y) Transporting electrons to P680
Z) Transporting electrons to P700
ANSWER: Z) Transporting electrons to P700
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Unlike mosses and most ferns, spermatophytes have morphically distinctive spores. What phenomenon describes this evolution to dimorphic spores?
ANSWER: HETEROSPORY
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer For incompressible, laminar flow in a circular pipe of constant radius, the dynamic pressure drop through a section of the pipe is inversely proportional to the radius to what power?
ANSWER: 4
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer If the moment of inertia of a uniform sphere rotating on its axis is I, then what is the moment of inertia of a spherical shell of the same mass in terms of I?
ANSWER: 5I/3
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following solvents is most suitable for an SN1 reaction?
W) Acetone
X) Ethanol
Y) THF
Z) DMSO
ANSWER: X) Ethanol
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true about elements in the d-block 1) Elements in the first row reach higher oxidation states than rows two and three 2) Ionization energy increases from left to right 3) Atomic radii generally differ greatly across the d-block.
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The graph of the equation y = sine x + x lies in what two quadrants of the Cartesian plane?
ANSWER: FIRST AND THIRD (also accept: in any order)
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Two six-sided dice are randomly stacked on top of each other. What is the probability that the sum of numbers on the ten remaining faces is greater than 32?
ANSWER: 5/6
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers working with Professor Hitoshi Murayama have been studying high-energy events in particle accelerators. They found that electrons and positrons annihilate to form what fundamental particles?
ANSWER: PHOTONS
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at the Joint Genome Institute at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are studying shiitake mushrooms, which are white rot fungi that can decompose many of wood’s components. What component of wood is a polymer made of phenol groups?
ANSWER: LIGNIN
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What X-ray telescope has an extremely eccentric orbit around the Earth, with an apogee about a third of the distance from the Earth to the Moon?
ANSWER: CHANDRA OBSERVATORY
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three attributes that lead to a smaller annual temperature range 1) Being farther inland 2) Having less cloud cover 3) Being on a leeward coast instead of a windward coast.
ANSWER: NONE
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three intermolecular forces that can be found in pure methylamine 1) Ion-dipole 2) London dispersion forces 3) Hydrogen bonding.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding atomic orbitals 1) 3p orbitals possess zero angular momentum 2) The 2s and 2p orbitals of hydrogen are different in energy 3) 3d orbitals have two radial nodes.
ANSWER: NONE
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is 165 squared?
ANSWER: 27225
8. BONUS Math Short Answer The top of an hourglass filled with sand has the shape of an upside-down cone with a height of 3 centimeters and a radius of 1 centimeter. If sand is draining at a rate of 2 cubic centimeters per minute, how long, to the nearest minute, will it take for the top of the hourglass to be completely empty?
ANSWER: 2
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The position of a particle traveling in one dimension is given by x(t) = 3t3 + t2 – 2t + 2. What is the acceleration of the particle at t = 2?
ANSWER: 38
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 500-gram block slides horizontally at 4 meters per second into a spring fixed to a wall. The block compresses the spring by 20 centimeters before momentarily coming to rest. Assuming there is no friction, what is the spring constant of the spring, in newtons per meter?
ANSWER: 200
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A scientist is trying to determine whether a segment of DNA is an enhancer. They conduct a reporter assay using GFP as a reporter gene. Which of the following types of visualization methods should they use to see the expression of GFP?
W) X-ray crystallography
X) Light microscope
Y) Fluorescence microscope
Z) Electron microscope
ANSWER: Y) Fluorescence microscope
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer CRISPR originally evolved as a defense mechanism against what type of pathogen?
ANSWER: BACTERIOPHAGE (also accept: PHAGE)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a divergent plate boundary between two continental plates?
W) Chile Ridge
X) East African Rift Valley
Y) San Andreas Fault
Z) Himalayas Mountains
ANSWER: X) East African Rift Valley
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true regarding the planet Uranus?
W) Greater than 80% of Uranus’ mass is accounted for by water, methane, and ammonia
X) Uranus has the second lowest density of the solar system’s planets
Y) Uranus’ magnetic axis is offset from its axis of rotation by about a third of the planet’s radius
Z) Uranus is warmer than Neptune
ANSWER: Z) Uranus is warmer than Neptune
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists in the Yaghi Lab at UC Berkeley study a particular class of semi-organic materials that are great at adsorbing gases such as carbon dioxide and methane. What is the name for the general class of materials they are studying?
ANSWER: METAL–ORGANIC FRAMEWORKS (also accept: MOFs)
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers in the Accelerator Technology and Applied Physics division of Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are working on various solutions to fusion energy. One approach involves tokamaks, machines which use a powerful magnetic field to confine plasma in what shape?
ANSWER: TORUS
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three processes that are examples of chemical weathering 1) Frost wedging 2) Root wedging 3) Salt wedging.
ANSWER: NONE
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Daniel digs a deep hole in his backyard and observes five different layers in the soil. Order the following three soil horizons from top to bottom 1) O 2) C 3) B.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements primarily explains why the rate of effusion in gases is much slower than the velocity of individual molecules?
W) Random collisions constantly shift the paths of gaseous molecules during diffusion
X) Small container volumes create a bottleneck for particles
Y) Gravity causes particles to move downwards rather than laterally
Z) Intermolecular forces slow the rate of effusion
ANSWER: W) Random collisions constantly shift the paths of gaseous molecules during diffusion

ANSWER: 1 AND 3
16. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Eric is conducting an experiment in which he shoots particles one-by-one through a double slit. After firing 1000 particles through his apparatus, which of the following particles should he expect to produce the most obvious wave-like diffraction pattern?

X) Water molecules
Y) Alpha particles
Z) Protons
ANSWER: W) Electrons

ANSWER: 20π
17. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Juan is analyzing a certain function f consisting of a numerator and denominator. When he plugs in zero for x, he finds that the numerator goes to zero. In order to use L’Hopital’s rule to evaluate the overall limit as x approaches zero, the denominator must approach which of the following?
W) 0
X) 1
Y) Positive infinity
Z) Negative infinity
ANSWER: W) 0
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at the Liquid Sunlight Alliance of Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are designing new semiconductor materials for more efficient solar panels. What property of semiconductors is most closely related to the wavelengths of light that will be absorbed by the photovoltaic cell?
ANSWER: BAND GAP
18. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at the Nuclear Science Division of Lawrence Berkeley National Lab make heavy elements using the 88-inch cyclotron. Which of the following explains why the high velocities generated by cyclotrons are essential to the discovery of superheavy elements?
W) High speeds generate strong interactions between nuclei
X) High speeds increase temperature, allowing for fusion
Y) High speeds are required to overcome repulsive forces between nuclei
Z) High speeds allow for studies to be performed at reasonable timescales
ANSWER: Y) High speeds are required to overcome repulsive forces between nuclei
