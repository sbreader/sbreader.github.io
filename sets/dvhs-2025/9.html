20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Lead 206 undergoes Alpha decay. Given that lead has an atomic number of 82, what are the mass number and atomic number, respectively, of the heavier element formed in the decay?
ANSWER: Mass Number: 202, Atomic Number: 80
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which unit is known as the amount of energy needed to raise 1 g of water by 1 degree celsius?
ANSWER: Calorie
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When sodium chloride reacts with fluorine gas to produce sodium fluoride and chlorine gas, what is the sum of the coefficients in the balanced equation?
ANSWER: 6
20. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Jaevin is drinking his favorite soda, Dr. Pepper. When he pops the can, the carbon dioxide fizzes out of the top. Which of the following laws explains why the carbon dioxide gas leaves the can rapidly?
W) Boyle’s Law
X) Charles’s Law
Y) Avogadro’s Law
Z) Henry’s Law
ANSWER: Z) Henry’s Law
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which of the following are considered colligative properties? 1) boiling point elevation 2) freezing point depression 3) osmotic pressure
ANSWER: ALL
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many lone pairs are on the oxygen atom in a water molecule?
ANSWER: 2
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the molecular geometry of Xenon Tetrafluoride?
ANSWER: Square Planar
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which principle in chemistry states that when a system experiences a disturbance, it will respond to restore a new equilibrium state?
ANSWER: Le Châtelier’s Principle
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Alan has a habit of tasting chemicals in Chemistry class. During a lab, he tastes an unknown substance and finds it to be bitter. The substance nearly slips out of his hand on the way to his mouth. Which of the following substances could Alan have tasted?
W) hydrochloric acid
X) bleach
Y) vinegar
Z) pure water
ANSWER: X) bleach
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following substances contains an ionic bond?
W) NO3-
X) N2O4
Y) Pb(NO3)2
Z) H2O
ANSWER: Y) Pb(NO3)2
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is a possible set of quantum numbers for a 3d orbital?
W) n=3, l=0
X) n=3, l=1
Y) n=3, l=2
Z) n=3, l=3
ANSWER: Y) n=3, l=2
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the hybridization of the Sulfur atom in SF6?
W) sp3
X) sp2
Y) d2sp3
Z) dsp3
ANSWER: Y) d2sp3
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following that are considered intermolecular bonds. 1) hydrogen bonding 2) covalent bonding 3) dipole-dipole interactions
ANSWER: 1 and 3
20. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Armaan is rubbing his hair with a balloon and notices that his hair starts to stick up. Which of the following is true:
W) protons are transferred from his hair to the balloon
X) protons are transferred from the balloon to his hair
Y) electrons are transferred from his hair to the balloon
Z) electrons are transferred from the balloon to his hair
ANSWER: Y) electrons are transferred from his hair to the balloon
20. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is true regarding phase changes?
W) the temperature does not change during a phase change
X) the heat added will cause the temperature to increase
Y) the heat added will cause the temperature to decrease
Z) you must know the entropy to determine whether the temperature will change
ANSWER: W) the temperature does not change during a phase change
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To 3 significant figures, what is the volume of 1 mole of CO2 at 1 atm and 0 degrees Celsius?
ANSWER: 22.4 L
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the molecular geometry of SF6?
ANSWER: Octahedral
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many degrees of unsaturation are in Benzene, C6H6?
ANSWER: 4
