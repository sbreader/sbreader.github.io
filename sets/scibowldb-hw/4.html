Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Lake Baikal, the world’s second most voluminous lake, contains this much of the world’s surface fresh water.
W) 10%
X) 20%
Y) 30%
Z) 50%
ANSWER: X) 20%
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer During what Epoch of the Cenozoic Era was the most recent ice age on Earth?
ANSWER: pleistocene (also accept: pleistocene ice age)
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a speleothem?
W) Stalactite
X) Stalagmite
Y) Helictite
Z) Precipitate
ANSWER: Z) Precipitate
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which region of the electro-magnetic spectrum will cause sunburns?
W) ultraviolet
X) infrared
Y) visible
Z) blue
ANSWER: W) ultraviolet
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Iceland is least likely to have
W) a fissure eruption
X) a lava plateau
Y) an explosive eruption
Z) a shield volcano
ANSWER: Y) an explosive eruption
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following noble gases in order of highest to lowest abundance in Earth's atmosphere: helium, argon, neon, xenon.
ANSWER: ARGON, NEON, HELIUM, XENON (also accept: Ar, Ne, He, Xe)
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of mass wasting is most common in regions of permafrost?
ANSWER: Solifluction
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of rock contains visible phenocrysts?
W) Scoria
X) Ultrafelsic
Y) Komatiite
Z) Porphyry
ANSWER: Z) Porphyry
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scleractinian, rugose, and tabulate are three types of corals. Which one still exists today?
ANSWER: Scleractinian
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a well known hanging trough waterfall?
W) Yosemite Falls
X) Niagara Falls
Y) Victoria Falls
Z) Yellowstone Falls
ANSWER: W) Yosemite Falls
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What clay mineral is produced by the alteration of potash feldspar also used in ceramics?
ANSWER: Kaolinite
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following describes the appearance of a mineral in transmitted light?
W) Porosity
X) Translucence
Y) Opaqueness
Z) Diaphaneity
ANSWER: Z) Diaphaneity
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following minerals by increasing relative hardness: apatite, calcite, fluorite.
ANSWER: Calcite, fluorite, apatite
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The density of continental crust is about
W) 1.7 g/cm3
X) 2.7 g/cm3
Y) 3.7 g/cm3
Z) 4.7 g/cm3
ANSWER: X) 2.7 g/cm3
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The lithosphere has a depth of about
W) 100 km
X) 150 km
Y) 200 km
Z) 250 km
ANSWER: W) 100 km
16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which of the following does hot rock from the core-mantle boundary nucleate and travel through the mantle to become a diapir in the Earth's crust?
W) Cinder Cone
X) Mantle Plume
Y) Fissure Vent
Z) Supervolcano
ANSWER: X) Mantle Plume
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A circular cavity ground out of bedrock by running water is known as what?
ANSWER: Pothole
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the net direction of Ekman transport?
W) In Direction of Wind Flow
X) 20 degrees to direction of wind flow
Y) 45 degrees to direction of wind flow
Z) 90 degrees to direction of wind flow
ANSWER: Z) 90 degrees to direction of wind flow
19. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A stratovolcano with a collapsed crater is known as which of the following?
W) cinder cone
X) fissure vent
Y) caldera
Z) shield volcano
ANSWER: Y) caldera
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The last reversal of Earth's magnetic field occurred approximately how long ago?
W) 90,000 years ago
X) 220,000 years ago
Y) 450,000 years ago
Z) 780,000 years ago
ANSWER: Z) 780,000 years ago
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not an example of a concordant rock mass?
W) Lopolith
X) Sill
Y) Laccolith
Z) Dike
ANSWER: Z) Dike
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is defined as the rate of decrease of an atmospheric variable such as temperature with height?
ANSWER: Lapse Rate
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice All of the following are examples of chemically precipitated sedimentary rock except:
W) limestone
X) sandstone
Y) dolomite
Z) marble
ANSWER: X) sandstone
24. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following soil types is most commonly found in the tropics?
W) Oxisols
X) Alfisols
Y) Ultisols
Z) Histosols
ANSWER: W) Oxisols
