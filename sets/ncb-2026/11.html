1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What complexes are formed, respectively when ammonia is added to tetrachloroplatinate(II) and when chloride is added to tetraammineplatinum(II)?
W) cis-PtCl2(NH3)2 and cis-PtCl2(NH3)2
X) cis-PtCl2(NH3)2 and trans-PtCl2(NH3)2
Y) trans-PtCl2(NH3)2 and cis-PtCl2(NH3)2
Z) trans-PtCl2(NH3)2 and trans-PtCl2(NH3)2
ANSWER: X) cis-PtCl2(NH3)2 and trans-PtCl2(NH3)2
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Consider two solids with formula AB2 and AB, such that they have the same Ksp. For what Ksp will the two solids have the same molar solubility?
W) ½
X) ¼
Y) ⅛
Z) 1/16
ANSWER: Z) 1/16
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following reactions will never require an acidic workup to create a protonated product?
W) Lithium aluminum hydride reduction
X) Grignard reaction
Y) Michael addition
Z) Clemmensen reduction
ANSWER: Z) Clemmensen reduction
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following 3 d-orbitals by how much they are affected by Jahn-Teller distortions 1) dz2 2) dxz 3) dxy
ANSWER: 2,3,1
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What theory in physical chemistry is typically used to predict the rates of electron transfer reactions?
ANSWER: Marcus Theory
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following laws does NOT account for the quantization of energy levels?
W) Bragg’s Law
X) Planck’s Law
Y) Rydberg Equation
Z) Schrödinger Equation
ANSWER: W) Bragg’s Law
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds does not commonly exhibit dimerism?
W) Acetic Acid
X) Borane
Y) Cyclopentadiene
Z) Tri-tert-butoxyaluminum
ANSWER: Z) Tri-tert-butoxyaluminum
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A solution of sodium sulfate has a boiling point of 106°C. An equal volume of equimolar barium nitrate is mixed with the aforementioned solution. To the nearest whole number, what is the new boiling point of the solution in degrees celsius?
ANSWER: 104°C
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer To avoid negbait, this a disclaimer that the listed hydrocarbons in the choices will not ascend up by one carbon. Order the following four cycloalkanes by increasing ring strain 1) Cyclopropane 2) Cyclopentane 3) Cyclononane 4) Cyclotetradecane
ANSWER: 4, 2, 3, 1
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Norbornene is a hydrocarbon formed from the Diels-Alder reaction of cyclopentadiene and ethene. What is the IUPAC name of norbornane?
ANSWER: bicyclo[2.2.1]heptane
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the specific type of electronic transition responsible for the intense color in the oxoanions permanganate (MnO4-) and chromate (CrO42-)
W) d-to-d transition
X) Metal-to-Ligand Charge Transfer
Y) Ligand-to-Metal Charge Transfer
Z) π → π* transition
ANSWER: Y) Ligand-to-Metal Charge Transfer
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following accurately describes the splitting of a d-orbital in an octahedral complex?
W) two orbitals are increased in energy by ⅖ the octahedral splitting energy and three orbitals are lowered by ⅗ the octahedral splitting energy
X) three orbitals are lowered in energy by ⅖ the octahedral splitting energy and two orbitals are increased in energy by ⅗ the octahedral splitting energy
Y) two orbitals are increased in energy by ⅗ the octahedral splitting energy and three orbitals are lowered by ⅖ the octahedral splitting energy
Z) two orbitals are lowered in energy by ⅗ the octahedral splitting energy and three orbitals are increased by ⅖ the octahedral splitting energy
ANSWER: Y) two orbitals are increased in energy by ⅗ the octahedral splitting energy and three orbitals are lowered by ⅖ the octahedral splitting energy
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What quantity can be thought of as the "escaping tendency" of a substance, and representing the effective pressure of gases and liquid mixtures?
ANSWER: Fugacity
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What scale is used to measure the acidity of superacids?
ANSWER: Hammett Scale
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Superacids are defined as being stronger than which of the following acids?
W) Hydrochloric Acid
X) Hydroiodic Acid
Y) Nitric Acid
Z) Sulfuric Acid
ANSWER: Z) Sulfuric Acid
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In certain sterically hindered biaryl compounds such as BINAP, restricted rotation about the aryl–aryl single bond leads to stable, isolable stereoisomers. What term describes these stereoisomers?
ANSWER: Atropisomers
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three reactions by increasing enantiomeric excess of the major organic product 1) Solvolysis of 2-bromo-2-methylpentane with methanol 2) Nucleophilic attack of 2-bromobutane by cyanide 3) Reduction of 2-butanone by lithium aluminum hydride
ANSWER: 3,1,2
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What type of isomerism is exhibited by nitro and thiocyanate ligands, in which an ambidentate ligand can bind to a central metal ion through different donor atoms?
ANSWER: Linkage Isomerism
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Though very rare, quadruple bonds can occur in compounds containing transition metals, with the first crystallographic study of a compound with a quadruple bond was provided by Frank Albert Cotton for salts of Re2Cl82-. Which of the following options correctly identifies the bonds which compose a quadruple bond?
W) 1 σ bond, 3 π bonds
X) 2 σ bonds, 2 π bonds
Y) 1 σ bond, 2 π bonds, 1 δ bond
Z) 1 σ bond, 1 π bond, 2 δ bonds
ANSWER: Y) 1 σ bond, 2 π bonds, 1 δ bond
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three ions by increasing entropy of hydration 1) Li+ 2) K+ 3) Al3+
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What quantity represents the number of available quantum states per unit volume per unit energy, and is the proportionality constant, rho, between the change in energy density and the change in wavelength in Rayleigh Jean's Law?
ANSWER: Density Of States
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The transition state for which of the following steps in the radical halogenation of an alkane would have the greatest radical character?
W) Hydrogen abstraction in chlorination
X) Hydrogen abstraction in bromination
Y) Halogen abstraction in chlorination
Z) Halogen abstraction in bromination
ANSWER: X) Hydrogen abstraction in bromination
13. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following reactants would have the highest energy intermediate when undergoing cyanohydrin formation?
W) Acetaldehyde
X) Acetone
Y) 2-butanone
Z) Benzophenone
ANSWER: Z) Benzophenone
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Cyclohexene is treated with OsO4, followed by NaHSO3 and water. How many stereoisomers does the major organic product possess?
ANSWER: 1
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three reactions that can be catalyzed by both bases and acids 1. Fischer esterification 2. Amide hydrolysis 3. Hemiacetal formation
ANSWER: 2 and 3
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In computational chemistry, approximations are used in order to speed up quantum chemical calculations. However, approximations do not always hold. Near conical intersections of potential energy surfaces, what approximation based on the difference in mass between nuclei and electrons cannot be used?
ANSWER: Born-Oppenheimer approximation
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Instead of fluorescing, excited states can also relax without releasing radiation. What dimensionless quantity describes the probability of radiative decay compared to nonradiative decay?
ANSWER: Oscillator strength
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A certain molecule A is a special form of an oxide of lead. It is formed by the reaction of PbO and O2 in a 6 to 1 molar ratio at 450 degrees Celsius. No other products are formed in this reaction. What is the empirical formula of A?
ANSWER: Pb3O4. (do not accept: Pb6O8)
