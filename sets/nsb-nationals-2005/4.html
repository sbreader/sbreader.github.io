Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Growth and development are synonymous, respectively, with which of the following:
W) division and multiplication
X) multiplication and division
Y) multiplication and differentiation
Z) zygote and embryo
ANSWER: Y) multiplication and differentiation
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the main products of plasma cells called?
ANSWER: ANTIBODIES
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Fish in an aquarium require oxygen to live, which is usually done by pumping air into the fish tank using a mechanical pump. If all other things remain constant, the most effective transfer of oxygen to water comes from:
W) small bubbles of air
X) large bubbles of air
Y) bubbling of cold air
Z) slow bubbling of air
ANSWER: W) small bubbles of air
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Cellulose is a polymer of repeating units of what molecule?
ANSWER: GLUCOSE
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A color made from the addition of two primary colors is called:
ANSWER: SECONDARY COLOR
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer What law yields an equation that is used in determining crystal structure from interference patterns produced by monochromatic X-rays?
ANSWER: BRAGG’S LAW
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true about meteorites:
W) most of the meteorites that have been found have a high iron content
X) iron meteorites often contain 10-20% nickel
Y) most of the iron meteorites are octahedrites
Z) on rare occasions, acid-etched iron meteorites display a pattern called the Widmanstatten pattern
ANSWER: Z) on rare occasions, acid-etched iron meteorites display a pattern called the Widmanstatten pattern
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Give the common name for the phenomenon which is the result of sunlight reflecting off the Earth and faintly illuminating the darkened portion of the moon:
ANSWER: EARTHSHINE
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In statistics, what is the common term for the square root of the variance?
ANSWER: STANDARD DEVIATION
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Rounded off to the nearest whole year, how many years would it take for your money to double in an investment that was increasing with an annual compound rate of 3.5%?
ANSWER: 20 YEARS
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT likely to have been formed by glaciers:
W) arete
X) hanging valley
Y) fjord
Z) dike
ANSWER: Z) dike
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Give the common name of the historic mineral that was the original source of radium and the first substance in which helium was discovered on earth:
ANSWER: PITCHBLENDE (also accept: URANINITE; UO2)
7. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which federal agency is primarily in charge of our National Laboratories and is charged with ensuring a stable supply of energy for the nation?
ANSWER: DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY (also accept: DOE)
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the error of a compass as a consequence of the difference between magnetic North and true North?
ANSWER: DECLINATION (also accept: VARIATION)
8. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Ultimately, the character of the pixel which is primarily responsible for the resolution of a CRT is:
W) size
X) number
Y) shape
Z) color
ANSWER: W) size
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer What does the D in DRAM stand for?
ANSWER: DYNAMIC
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice DNA is produced from RNA:
W) by reverse transcriptase
X) by an oligoside
Y) by DNA polymerase
Z) in yeast as a protective measure
ANSWER: W) by reverse transcriptase
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following does NOT belong to the Class Arachnida:
W) spider
X) tick
Y) mite
Z) shrimp
ANSWER: Z) shrimp
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following are NOT carbonate minerals:
W) calcite
X) magnesite
Y) dolomite
Z) graphite
ANSWER: Z) graphite
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Give the symbol for the element that is derived from the Latin word Plumbum:
ANSWER: Pb
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer When a green colored object is illuminated by a green light it will appear as what color to the human eye?
ANSWER: GREEN
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer How many more times louder is 70 decibels than 10 decibels?
ANSWER: ONE MILLION
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice According to Kepler’s Third Law, plotting the squares of the periods of the planet against the cubes of the semi-major axes of their orbits will result in what sort of graph:
W) a parabolic line
X) a straight line
Y) an irregular jagged line
Z) a hyperbolic line
ANSWER: X) a straight line
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Who’s book, titled “Concerning the Revolutions of Celestial Spheres”, is considered to mark the birth of modern astronomy?
ANSWER: COPERNICUS
13. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following mathematicians is responsible for the Cartesian plane that is also known as the rectangular coordinate system:
W) Pythagoroas
X) Rene Descartes
Y) Kirk Godel
Z) Fredric Gauss
ANSWER: X) Rene Descartes
13. BONUS Math Short Answer The imaginary unit i is numerically defined as what?
ANSWER: √(-1) (also accept: i2 = -1)
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A glacier that has neither advanced nor retreated for many years deposits which of the following:
W) terminal moraine
X) end moraine
Y) ground moraine
Z) medial moraine
ANSWER: X) end moraine
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The name of this mineral is derived from the Latin word which means “to flow”. One of its major uses is as a flux to help melt iron ore. What is its name?
ANSWER: FLUORITE (also accept: CALCIUM FLUORIDE; CaF2)
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Photobiology is the branch of science which is basically concerned with:
W) how organisms see
X) how to record images of living systems with various optical instruments
Y) the effects of visible and ultraviolet radiation on living systems
Z) how living systems produce visible light
ANSWER: Y) the effects of visible and ultraviolet radiation on living systems
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer At what temperature, rounded off to whole degrees Centigrade, does distilled water reach its maximum density at sea level?
ANSWER: 4 DEGREES
16. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following would be a measurement of a computer screen’s dot pitch:
W) 0.25 millimeters
X) 70 hertz
Y) 1200 dpi
Z) 650 megabytes
ANSWER: W) 0.25 millimeters
16. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is a common type of video card that has an onboard processor that can substantially increase speed and boost graphical and video performance:
ANSWER: GRAPHICS ACCELERATOR CARD
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Transfer RNA with an amino acid attached to it is called:
W) a charged tRNA
X) an amino acyl tRNA
Y) a tRNA with an amino acid attached
Z) t-primed RNA
ANSWER: X) an amino acyl tRNA
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer This anatomical term means “nearer the midsagittal plane”:
ANSWER: MEDIAL
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Under which of the following conditions will iron rust:
W) in water
X) in water and oxygen
Y) in oil
Z) in oil and water
ANSWER: X) in water and oxygen
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In order to compare two different gas samples, scientists use what is called the STP. What does STP stand for?
ANSWER: STANDARD TEMPERATURE AND PRESSURE (also accept: 273 K AND 1 ATM; 0° C AND 760 mm Hg)
19. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Cathode rays are actually:
W) photons
X) electrons
Y) protons
Z) positrons
ANSWER: X) electrons
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer In radio transmission, what does the acronym AM stand for?
ANSWER: AMPLITUDE MODULATION
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The time between two successive meridian transits of the Sun as observed from a stationary spot on the Earth’s surface is called:
W) the stellar day
X) an apparent solar day
Y) lunar day
Z) an apparent solar month
ANSWER: X) an apparent solar day
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The celestial coordinate analogous to latitude is called:
ANSWER: DECLINATION
21. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Where is the y intercept for y = sinx?
ANSWER: ZERO
21. BONUS Math Short Answer The product of 6 factorial is how many times larger than the product of 5 factorial?
ANSWER: 6
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A high pressure center is associated with what kind of weather:
W) warm and dry
X) warm and wet
Y) cool and dry
Z) cool and wet
ANSWER: Y) cool and dry
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to the region of the Earth’s mantle located from about 75 to 300 kilometers below the surface, where rocks have little strength?
ANSWER: ASTHENOSPHERE
23. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about a brain cell from a ‘normal’ individual human:
W) some DNA sequences are present in multiple copies
X) most of the DNA codes for protein
Y) most genes are transcribed
Z) most genes are arranged in operon-like clusters
ANSWER: W) some DNA sequences are present in multiple copies
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer The gustatory system has to do mostly with what human sense?
ANSWER: TASTE
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true about the Periodic Table:
W) metals tend to gain electrons while nonmetals tend to lose electrons
X) metals tend to lose electrons while nonmetals tend to gain electrons
Y) elements with metallic properties are found on the left side of the table
Z) alkali metals are found in the vertical column that contains lithium
ANSWER: W) metals tend to gain electrons while nonmetals tend to lose electrons
24. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Until the early 1960’s, what group of elements were called the inert gases:
ANSWER: NOBLE GASES
25. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A crane lifts a 4,000 ton concrete slab 10 feet above the ground in 5 seconds. If it does the same task in 10 seconds, this involves a different amount of what:
W) energy
X) work
Y) power
Z) potential energy
ANSWER: Y) power
25. BONUS Physics Short Answer A coil of wire that produces a magnetic field is typically called a:
ANSWER: SOLENOID
