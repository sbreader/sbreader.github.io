1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In 1890, this German physician published four criteria by which a casual relationship between a causative microbe and disease could be established. In addition to his work in formulating the postulates that now bear his name, he isolated the bacteria that cause antharax, cholera, and tuberculosis. Name this founder of bacteriology.
ANSWER: ROBERT KOCH
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Match the following bacterial morphologies to their correct described shapes 1. spherical shape 2. helically-twisted cylinder 3. rod shape A. spirochete B. coccus C. bacillus
ANSWER: 1. B 2. A 3. C
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Through X-ray crystallography, it is determined that the atoms of a metal form body-centered cubic unit cells. If the radius of the atom is r, what is the length of one of the sides of the unit cell, expressed in terms of r?
ANSWER: 4r/( √3)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The Standard Model of particle physics is a theory concerning the interactions of the fundamental forces. Which of the following statements is NOT TRUE of the standard model?
W) fermions are categorized into three generations, with particle mass increasing as generation number increases
X) the Z boson mediates the weak interaction and is electrically neutral as well as its own antiparticle
Y) although quarks in generations two and three rapidly decay, neutrinos in generations higher than one are stable
Z) bosons are particles governed by Bose-Einstein statistics and include composite particles such as stable nuclei of even mass number, e.g. helium-4
ANSWER: Y) although quarks in generations two and three rapidly decay, neutrinos in generations higher than one are stable
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Order the following 4 leptons from heaviest to lightest: top quark, up quark, muon neutrino, strange quark
ANSWER: top quark, strange quark, up quark, muon neutrino

7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A reaction occurs such that delta S system is positive and delta H system is negative. Which of the following is TRUE regarding the reaction?

8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Viscosity is internal friction in a fluid that oppose relative motion. Which of the following statements is NOT TRUE about viscosity?

X) Bernoulli's equation cannot be applied to the motion of a viscous fluid through a pipe
Y) the velocity of a viscous fluid moving through a pipe at the pipe wall is zero
Z) as the viscosity of a fluid increases, its tendency for laminar flow also increases

10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer As the universe expanded and cooled in the time following the big bang, the glow from the warm gas faded into infrared wavelengths. What term do cosmologists give to this period lasting for about 400 million years?

18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Magnet quenching is an abnormal termination of magnet operation when part of the superconducting coil enters a normal state. By name or number, identify all of the following four situations that could result in a quench 1. the magnetic field inside the magnet is too large 2. the rate of change of the field inside the magnet is too large 3. defects inside the magnet itself 4. the magnetic field is repelled under the physical surface of the magnet
