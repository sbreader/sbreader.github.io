1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not considered a metabolic stage of cellular respiration?
W) Glycolysis
X) Citric acid cycle
Y) Electron transport
Z) Oxiosmosis
ANSWER: Z) Oxiosmosis
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The process of glycolysis results in the synthesis of what molecule?
ANSWER: Pyruvate
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following enzymes are not involved in glycolysis?
W) Hexokinase
X) Phosphoglucoisomerase
Y) Amylase
Z) Enolase
ANSWER: Y) Amylase
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Where does the Krebs cycle take place in the cell?
W) The cytoplasm
X) The mitochondrial outer compartment
Y) The mitochondrial crista
Z) The mitochondrial matrix
ANSWER: Z) The mitochondrial matrix
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name given to the protein complex in the mitochondrial membrane that synthesizes adenosine triphosphate?
ANSWER: ATP synthase
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice If a ray of light travels from a medium with a low index of refraction to a medium of high index of refraction, the ray of light will bend—
W) Away from the normal
X) Towards the normal
Y) The light will not bend
Z) Not enough information is given
ANSWER: X) Towards the normal
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The human eye is most sensitive to what color of light?
ANSWER: Yellow
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice If an object is place in front of a concave mirror, beyond the center of curvature, the resulting image of the object will be—
W) Inverted, reduced in size, and between the center of curvature and focal point.
X) Inverted, reduced in size, and beyond the center of curvature.
Y) Right side up, larger in size, and between the center of curvature and focal point.
Z) Right side up, larger in size, and beyond the center of curvature.
ANSWER: W) Inverted, reduced in size, and between the center of curvature and focal point.
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If a ray of light reflects off of a convex mirror parallel to the principal axis, the reflected ray of light will reflect as if it is coming from what point?
ANSWER: The focal point
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice You are standing on a sidewalk, and a fire truck races by with its siren blaring. While the truck is passing you, the siren's pitch will sound—
W) Higher than it actually is the whole time.
X) Lower than it actually is the whole time.
Y) Higher than it actually is, and then lower than it actually is.
Z) Lower than it actually is, and then higher than it actually is.
ANSWER: Y) Higher than it actually is, and then lower than it actually is.
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a metalloid?
W) Polonium
X) Silicon
Y) Boron
Z) Germanium
ANSWER: W) Polonium
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Water spontaneously rising from a sequoia's roots to all its parts is an example of what phenomenon?
ANSWER: Capillary action
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer If a substance is 100 times more acidic than another substance with a pH level of 4, what is the first substance's pH level?
ANSWER: 2
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice 1s22s22p63s1 is the electron configuration of what element?
W) Sodium
X) Magnesium
Y) Aluminum
Z) Silicon
ANSWER: W) Sodium
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Aluminum reacting with copper (II) chloride dehydrate to produce copper and aluminum chloride is an example of a—
W) Acid base reaction
X) Double displacement reaction
Y) Single displacement reaction
Z) Decomposition reaction
ANSWER: Y) Single displacement reaction
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The equation (x-4)2-(y+9)2=16, when graphed, will form what conic structure?
ANSWER: Hyperbola
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the sum of the infinite series 3+1.5+0.75+…?
ANSWER: 6
18. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following cannot be the length of the third side of triangle with two sides measuring 17 and 6 units in length?
W) 22 units
X) 12 units
Y) 17 units
Z) 10 units
ANSWER: Z) 10 units
19. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is equal to (5x-25)(5x+25)?
W) 25x2+250x+625
X) x2-25
Y) 25x2-625
Z) x2+10x+25
ANSWER: Y) 25x2-625
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the volume of a sphere with a radius of 3 pi in terms of pi?
ANSWER: 36pi4
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals does not have a black color?
W) Anthracite coal
X) Biotite
Y) Bituminous coal
Z) Azurite
ANSWER: Z) Azurite
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Calcium-rich minerals are part of which branch of the Bowen's reaction series?
ANSWER: Continuous series
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The volcano, Olympus Mons, can be found on which planet?
W) Mercury
X) Venus
Y) Mars
Z) Jupiter
ANSWER: Y) Mars
