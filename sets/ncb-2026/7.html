1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the phenomenon by which an electron vacancy in an inner electron shell releases energy by ejecting another electron from an outer shell instead of emitting a photon?
ANSWER: Auger Effect
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the reason for the lower pKa of perchloric acid when compared to hypochlorous acid?
W) The chlorine atom in HClO4 is more electronegative, pulling electron density away from the O–H bond more strongly than in HClO
X) The conjugate base of HClO4 is stabilized by extensive resonance over multiple oxygen atoms, whereas the conjugate base of HClO has little to no resonance stabilization
Y) HClO4 forms stronger hydrogen bonds with water than HClO, making it easier to donate a proton
Z) The O–H bond in HClO4 is longer simply due to increased molecular size, making proton loss easier
ANSWER: X) The conjugate base of HClO4 is stabilized by extensive resonance over multiple oxygen atoms, whereas the conjugate base of HClO has little to no resonance stabilization
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Sarah is performing IR spectroscopy on a certain compound and notices a strong peak at 1680 inverse centimeters. Identify all the functional groups that could be present in this molecule 1. Alkene 2. Ketone 3. Amide
ANSWER: All
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice When making lithium enolates using LDA, a certain temperature is most commonly used to ensure the reactive species will survive. Which of the following is the closest to this temperature, in degrees Celsius?
W) -80
X) -40
Y) 40
Z) 80
ANSWER: W) -80
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Tetraethylenepentamine, abbreviated TEPA, is a pentadentate ligand. By name of number, name all of the following ligands that can coexist with t in an octahedral complex. 1. Chloride 2. Ethylene diamine 3. EDTA 4. Fulminate
ANSWER: 1 and 4
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why cyanide is so high on the spectrochemical series?
W) Cyanide is a strong sigma donor
X) Cyanide is a strong sigma acceptor
Y) Cyanide is a strong pi donor
Z) Cyanide is a strong pi acceptor
ANSWER: Z) Cyanide is a strong pi acceptor
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What quantity is equal to Gibbs free energy minus work?
ANSWER: Helmholtz Free Energy
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, order the following four stages of a Carnot cycle from first to last 1) adiabatic compression 2) adiabatic expansion 3) isothermal compression 4) isothermal expansion
ANSWER: 4,2,3,1
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term is used to explain the bonding in H3+ and B2H6 in which 2 electrons are shared between 3 atoms?
ANSWER: 3-center-2-electron Bond (also accept: Banana bond)
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three substituents by increasing activating strength for a benzene ring undergoing electrophilic aromatic substitution 1) methoxy 2) nitro 3) chloro.
ANSWER: 2,3,1
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Annulenes are compounds consisting of a single ring containing a fully conjugated π system. [X] Annulene denotes an Annulene with X Carbon atoms. Which of the following Annulenes does not experience aromatic stabilization?
W) [6]-Annulene
X) [10]-Annulene
Y) [14]-Annulene
Z) [18]-Annulene
ANSWER: X) [10]-Annulene
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many stereoisomers does the Co(NH3)2(Cl)2(CO)2 have?
ANSWER: 6
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three statements which are true 1) The oxidation state of molybdenum in molybdate is +6. 2) Ni(CN)42- is diamagnetic. 3) Oxidative addition increases the oxidation state of a metal center by 2.
ANSWER: 1,2,3
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term is given to the lowest possible energy that a quantum mechanical system may have?
ANSWER: Zero Point Energy
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A certain reaction is performed at several temperatures and its equilibrium constant K is measured. Kyle graphs ln(K) against 1/T and notes that the line of best fit has a positive slope and a negative y-intercept. What are the signs of the enthalpy change and entropy change, respectively, of this reaction?
ANSWER: Negative, negative
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three properties that are likely to increase as one descends down a group on the periodic table 1) band gap 2) electronegativity 3) metallic character
ANSWER: 3 only
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In electrophilic aromatic substitution reactions, what is the name for the carbocation intermediate that is stabilized by resonance?
ANSWER: Sigma Complex
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify the three compounds as nonaromatic, aromatic, and antiaromatic 1) cyclopetadiene 2) cyclopentadienyl cation 3) cyclopentadienyl anion
ANSWER: nonaromatic, antiaromatic, aromatic
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three types of cubic structures by increasing number of nearest neighbors 1) Face Centered Cubic 2) Simple Cubic 3) Diamond Cubic
ANSWER: 3,2,1
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice When copper is dissolved in excess ammonia, what color is the solution?
W) Light blue
X) Dark Blue
Y) Light Green
Z) Dark green
ANSWER: X) Dark Blue
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for the temperature change of a real gas or liquid as it is forced from high to low pressure through a valve rapidly?
ANSWER: Joule-Thomson Effect
12. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Raaghav is evaluating the lattice energy of cesium chloride using the Born-Haber cycle. Which of the following values is not included in the Born-Haber Cycle of cesium chloride?
W) The ionization energy of cesium
X) The electron affinity of chlorine
Y) The atomization of cesium
Z) The sublimation of cesium
ANSWER: Z) The sublimation of cesium
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What adjective is used to describe catalysts that have lost their potential or stopped working due to impurities?
ANSWER: Poisoned
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What name is given to chemical substances consisting of a lattice-like, solid structure that completely traps guest molecules?
ANSWER: Clathrates
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Robert is performing mass spectroscopy and finds that there are two peaks of M and M+2 of nearly equal intensity. What element is likely present in this compound?
ANSWER: Bromine
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A molecule has 2 chlorine atoms. What is the ratio of the intensities of the three peaks at M, M+2, and M+4 in the molecular ion region?.
ANSWER: 9:6:1
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In the gas phase, aluminum chloride exists as the dimer Al2Cl6. The Al-Cl-Al bridging interaction is best described as a three-center, four-electron bond. Which of the following best describes the nature of the bonding of one of these Al-Cl-Al units?
W) The bond has one bonding MO, one antibonding MO, and one nonbonding MO
X) The bond has two bonding MO’s and two antibonding MO’s
Y) The bond has one bonding MO and one antibonding MO
Z) The bond has two bonding MO’s, two antibonding MO’s, and two nonbonding MO’s
ANSWER: W) The bond has one bonding MO, one antibonding MO, and one nonbonding MO
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many isomers would a trigonal prismatic complex with general formula MA4B2 have?
ANSWER: 3
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Maxwell’s Demon was a famous thought experiment proposed by James Clerk Maxwell, which describes a tiny being controlling a door between two gas chambers, sorting fast and slow molecules without doing work. What fundamental theorem was Maxwell’s Demon proposed to contradict?
ANSWER: 2nd Law of Thermodynamics
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The Lindemann-Hinshelwood mechanism is the most common type of mechanism for unimolecular reactions. Take the reaction A yields B. It begins with two molecules of A colliding, transforming one of the molecules to an energized form called A*. A* will then turn into B. What is the rate of the reaction with respect to A at low concentrations of A and high concentrations of A, respectively?
ANSWER: 2 and 1
