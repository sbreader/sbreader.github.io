1. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Identify all the following three categories of fuel cells that can be considered types of proton exchange membrane fuel cells? 1) Direct-methanol 2) Molten carbonate 3) Phosphoric acid.

1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules is used as a standard for determining the octane rating of gasoline?
W) 2,2,4-trimethylpentane
X) N-octane
Y) 3-methylheptane
Z) 2,2,3,3-tetramethylbutane
ANSWER: W) 2,2,4-trimethylpentane
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What gas accounts for approximately 78% of the dry volume of Earth’s atmosphere?
W) Oxygen
X) Nitrogen
Y) Argon
Z) Carbon dioxide
ANSWER: X) Nitrogen
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer COROT was a mission run by the European Space Agency to find what type of celestial objects?
ANSWER: EXOPLANETS (also accept: EXTRASOLAR PLANETS; PLANETS)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What principle states that the net response at a given point caused by two waves is the sum of the responses that those waves would have caused individually?
ANSWER: SUPERPOSITION
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the term for the useful work that can be obtainable from a closed system at constant temperature?
ANSWER: HELMHOLTZ FREE ENERGY
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The citric acid cycle is a component of what kind of metabolic process?
W) Aerobic respiration
X) Anaerobic respiration
Y) Fermentation
Z) Photosynthesis
ANSWER: W) Aerobic respiration
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes a lysogenic cell?
W) It contains a prophage
X) It is immune to viral infection
Y) It is actively producing viral particles
Z) It is about to lyse
ANSWER: W) It contains a prophage

W) Strong acid
X) Weak acid
Y) Strong base
Z) Weak base

W) Positive, positive
X) Positive, negative
Y) Negative, positive

ANSWER: Y) Negative, positive

ANSWER: 36

ANSWER: 2
7. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the primary gaseous element of wood gas, a product of gasification?
ANSWER: NITROGEN

X) Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider
Y) High Flux Isotope Reactor

X) Lactic acid fermentation

9. BONUS Physics Short Answer To the nearest microfarad, what is the capacitance of a standard parallel plate capacitor with plate separation of 1 micron and plate area of 1 meter squared?
ANSWER: 9
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice If n is an integer, and xn is positive for all nonzero real numbers x, which of the following must be true about n?
W) n is greater than zero
X) n does not equal zero
Y) n is even

ANSWER: Y) n is even

Y) (8, –2)

W) Alcohol

13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Membrane depolarization of an action potential is able to pass from one cardiac cell to another through what type of connection between cells?
W) Desmosomes
X) Tight junction
Y) Occluding junction
Z) Gap junction
ANSWER: Z) Gap junction
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements best describes the difference in responses of plasma cells and cytotoxic T-cells?
W) Plasma cells respond the first time the invader is present, cytotoxic T-cells respond subsequent times
X) Plasma cells execute the cell-mediated response, cytotoxic T-cells execute the humoral response
Y) Plasma cells secrete antibodies against a pathogen, cytotoxic T-cells kill virus-infected cells

ANSWER: MULTIMETER

ANSWER: W) Rotational kinetic energy

ANSWER: GRAPHITE

19. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In the distant future, the Andromeda Galaxy will collide with the Milky Way. Which of the following would a hypothetical alien presently living in Andromeda observe about Balmer lines from our galaxy?
W) Their wavelengths are shorter than expected
X) Their wavelengths are longer than expected
Y) Their frequencies are lower than expected
Z) They are not subject to Zeeman splitting
ANSWER: W) Their wavelengths are shorter than expected

ANSWER: SINE = –15/17, TANGENT = –15/8 (also accept: –1 7/8)

ANSWER: 50% (also accept: 0.5; 1/2)

ANSWER: 267

ANSWER: PHANEROZOIC
23. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Global warming leads to positive feedbacks that accelerate the greenhouse effect. Which of the following is an example of this?
W) Plants photosynthesize more efficiently in a CO2-rich atmosphere
X) Reduced snow cover allows more solar radiation to warm the ground
Y) More CO2 is sequestered by rock weathering in a warmer atmosphere
Z) A warmer Earth loses more radiation to space
ANSWER: X) Reduced snow cover allows more solar radiation to warm the ground
