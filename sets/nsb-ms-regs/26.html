1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Specific gravity is a measure of what?
W) Relative mass
X) Relative density
Y) Relative elasticity
Z) Relative bouyancy
ANSWER: X) Relative density
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the term for a contour line connecting points of equal temperature on a weather map?
W) Isobar
X) Isochore
Y) Isotherm
Z) Isostasy
ANSWER: Y) Isotherm
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Solve the following equation for s: -143 = -11s
ANSWER: 13
2. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the least common denominator of the three fractions 1/6, 3/10, and 1/28?
ANSWER: 420
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice An atom that loses its outer electron or electrons readily is which of the following?
W) Active nonmetal
X) Active metal
Y) Inactive nonmetal
Z) Inactive metal
ANSWER: X) Active metal
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Two objects have the same size and shape but one is twice as heavy as the other. If air resistance is negligible, what happens when they are dropped simultaneously from a building?
W) The heavy one lands on the ground before the light one
X) They land on the ground at the same time and have the same speed
Y) They land on the ground at the same time but the heavy one has more speed
Z) They land on the ground at the same time but the heavy object has a lower acceleration
ANSWER: X) They land on the ground at the same time and have the same speed
4. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer How many kilowatt-hours, rounded to the nearest tenth, does it require to light a 100-watt bulb for 1 hour?
ANSWER: 0.1
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer The energy stored in a uranium atom can be released via what type of nuclear reaction?
ANSWER: FISSION
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Doppler radar is best used in which of the following applications?
W) Forecasting weather
X) Searching for extraterrestrial life
Y) Mapping the ocean floor
Z) Tracking marine life
ANSWER: W) Forecasting weather
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Plates on the Earth's surface interact at three different types of tectonic boundaries. Which of the following describes the three interactions?
W) Hot spots, fringing reefs, and earthquake zones
X) Transform faults, subform faults, and reform faults
Y) Seafloor spreading, transform faults, and subduction
Z) Continental drift, seafloor spreading, and hot spots
ANSWER: Y) Seafloor spreading, transform faults, and subduction
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the triplet of bases in the mRNA that codes for a specific amino acid?
ANSWER: CODON
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is a large community of plants and animals that live in the same place?
W) Biome
X) Biosphere
Y) Quadridome
Z) Abiotic environment

7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is the SI unit of mass?

X) Kilogram

Z) Pound
ANSWER: X) Kilogram
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for the change in enthalpy that must occur for ice to become water at zero degrees Celsius?
ANSWER: HEAT OF FUSION (also accept: ENTHALPY OF FUSION)
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What element makes up about 24% of the Sun’s mass?
ANSWER: HELIUM
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer A researcher designs an experiment in which she compares the predation on a group of white mice to the predation on a control group of grey mice. What type of variable is fur color in this experiment?
ANSWER: INDEPENDENT
9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following integers is a perfect square?

X) 125

ANSWER: Y) 225
9. BONUS Math Multiple Choice If k, m, and n are 3 different non-zero numbers and 1/k + 1/m = 1/n, what is k in terms of m and n?
W) 1 over (m – n)
X) 1 over (n – m)
Y) mn over (m – n)
Z) mn over (n – m)
ANSWER: Y) mn over (m – n)
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Forest fires are important in some ecosystems for all of the following reasons except:
W) They create a warmer climate, increasing precipitation
X) They thin understory, making way for increased growth
Y) They help clear dead and dying organisms
Z) They can remove non-native, non-fire-resistant species
ANSWER: W) They create a warmer climate, increasing precipitation

ANSWER: W) Mass and velocity
