Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the fracture of a mineral?
W) Tendency of some minerals to break unevenly along curved or irregular surfaces
X) Tendency of some minerals to break along flat surfaces
Y) Tendency of minerals to dissolve in water
Z) An optical property of a mineral
ANSWER: W) Tendency of some minerals to break unevenly along curved or irregular surfaces
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes a fractured surface of a mineral with sharp jagged irregularities?
ANSWER: HACKLY
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of breaking wave rolls into steep beaches and are known for their destructive nature?
ANSWER: SURGING BREAKERS
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of breaking wave curls over forming a tunnel until the waves break? This type is best for surfing.
ANSWER: PLUNGING BREAKERS
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which type of currents develops when the waves are not hitting the beach perpendicularly?
W) Upwelling
X) Deep Water Currents
Y) Longshore Current
Z) Gyres
ANSWER: Y) Longshore Current
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are potentially dangerous effects of the longshore currents which occur when the longshore currents are parallel to the beach and bounce seaward?
ANSWER: RIP CURRENTS (also accept: RIP TIDES)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which type of wind drives large surface currents year round?
W) Trade winds
X) Global winds
Y) Zephyr
Z) Chinook
ANSWER: W) Trade winds
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which trade winds are located in the highest latitudes?
ANSWER: POLAR EASTERLIES
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which types of currents flow in large rotating loops?
ANSWER: GYRES
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In the Southern Hemisphere, which way do the gyres rotate?
ANSWER: COUNTERCLOCKWISE
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is a narrow strip of land connecting two bigger portions of land?
W) Strait
X) Cape
Y) Sound
Z) Isthmus
ANSWER: Z) Isthmus
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is a narrow inlet that extends from a larger body of water into land?
ANSWER: ARM
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the highest point on a wave called?
ANSWER: CREST
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is wave a forward motion of?
ANSWER: ENERGY
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which type of reef is separated from land by lagoons?
W) Fringing Reef
X) Veiling Reef
Y) Atolls
Z) Barrier Reef
ANSWER: Z) Barrier Reef
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which land feature do atolls surround?
ANSWER: LAGOONS
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What are flat-topped extinct underwater volcanoes, located mostly in Pacific, called?
W) Guyots
X) Mid-Ocean Ridges
Y) Seamounts
Z) Continental Slope
ANSWER: W) Guyots
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is a gently sloping underwater pile of crushed submarine fans called?
ANSWER: CONTINENTAL RISE
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the flat part of the seafloor called, normally extending from the continental rise to mid-ocean ridges?
W) Continental margin
X) Passive margin
Y) Abyssal plain
Z) Continental shelf
ANSWER: Y) Abyssal plain
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are depressions in the abyssal plains, formed when oceanic tectonic plates slip under continental plates?
ANSWER: TRENCHES
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the point in oceans where the water temperature drops the fastest?
ANSWER: THERMOCLINE
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the point in oceans where the water density rapidly changes?
ANSWER: PYCNOCLINE
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the five mechanical layers of the Earth.
ANSWER: LITHOSPHERE, ASTHENOSPHERE, MESOSPHERE, OUTER CORE, INNER CORE (also accept: in any order)
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which compositional layer of the Earth is located under the crust?
ANSWER: MANTLE
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which type of volcano is gently sloping and not explosive due to low viscosity and low silicon content?
W) Stratovolcano
X) Shield volcano
Y) Lava Dome
Z) Composite volcano
ANSWER: X) Shield volcano
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of volcano forms when gas accumulates in the magma chamber?
ANSWER: LAVA DOME
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which pyroclastic particles range in size from 2 mm to 64 mm in diameter?
W) Lapilli
X) Volcanic ash
Y) Ignimbite
Z) Tephra
ANSWER: W) Lapilli
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What volcanic hazard takes the form of a fast moving gaseous cloud of hot ashes and other materials thrown out from an erupting volcano?
ANSWER: NUÉE ARDENTE
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the full name for the boundary between the crust and the mantle?
ANSWER: MOHOROVICIC DISCONTINUITY
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In which mechanical layer of the Earth are the plate tectonics located?
ANSWER: LITHOSPHERE
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which seismic wave does not travel through liquid?
W) P Wave
X) L Wave
Y) T Wave
Z) S Wave
ANSWER: Z) S Wave
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which seismic wave travels the slowest in rolling motions?
ANSWER: L WAVES (also accept: LONG WAVES)
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which scale measures the magnitude of an earthquake from the amplitude of the largest seismic wave recorded?
ANSWER: RICHTER SCALE
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which scale measures the intensity of an earthquake at any one location?
ANSWER: MERCALLI SCALE
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of Hawaii's islands is the youngest?
W) Hawaii
X) Kauai
Y) Maui
Z) Oahu
ANSWER: W) Hawaii
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which of Hawaii's islands is the oldest?
ANSWER: KAUAI
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which type of magma is capable of rising to the surface and solidifying within the crust to form plutons?
W) Basaltic Magma
X) Felsic magma
Y) Mafic Magma
Z) Andesitic Magma
ANSWER: X) Felsic magma
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which method do scientists often use to determine the location of an earthquake's epicenter, based on the relative locations of cities and landmarks close to the earthquake?
ANSWER: TRIANGULATION
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which two seismic waves do scientists consider when applying triangulation?
ANSWER: P WAVE AND S WAVE
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of fold in the rock layer makes the rock sink down due to compression?
ANSWER: SYNCLINE
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which fold is equivalent to anticlines, but is comprised of layers shaped like an inverted bowl?
ANSWER: DOME
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In normal faults, which way does the hanging wall move in relative to the footwall?
W) Right
X) Left
Y) Up
Z) Down
ANSWER: Z) Down
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In which type of fault does the hanging wall move up in relative to the footwall?
ANSWER: REVERSE FAULT
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following species is NOT a fossil evidence for continental drift?
W) Mesosaurus
X) Laganosuchus
Y) Lystrosaurus
Z) Cynognathus
ANSWER: X) Laganosuchus
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In which period of the Mesozoic Era did the Cynognathus live?
ANSWER: TRIASSIC PERIOD
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In which geological time period did the first flowering plants evolve and the first birds appear?
ANSWER: JURASSIC PERIOD
24. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In which geological time period did the first amphibians appear and the fish become abundant?
ANSWER: DEVONIAN PERIOD
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which famous astronomical theory is a set of hypotheses regarding climate change?
ANSWER: MILANKOVITCH THEORY
25. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which term is used by Milankovitch and other scientists to describe the shape of the Earth's orbit around the Sun?
ANSWER: ECCENTRICITY
