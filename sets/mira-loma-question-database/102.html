1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In what shape is the DNA of an E. Coli bacterium in?
W) Spherical
X) Circular

ANSWER: X) Circular

ANSWER: PLASMID
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Muscle tears involve the rupture of which of these intercellular junctions?
