1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In green plants, energy is stored mainly in which of the following forms?
W) Cellulose
X) Starch
Y) Glucose
Z) ATP
ANSWER: X) Starch
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What sheet of muscle separates the thoracic and abdominopelvic cavities?
ANSWER: DIAPHRAGM
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The earliest known single-celled life forms on Earth belong to which of the following taxonomic groups?
W) Fungi
X) Protoctista
Y) Plantae
Z) Monera
ANSWER: Z) Monera
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the chief commercial ore of aluminum?
ANSWER: BAUXITE
3. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A differentiable function f has domain the closed interval from –3 to 4 and range the closed interval from 5 to 12. If f prime of x is greater than 0 for all x in the domain, what is f of –3?
W) –3
X) 4
Y) 5
Z) 12
ANSWER: Y) 5
3. BONUS Math Short Answer How many two-digit positive integers do NOT have 5 or 6 as a digit?
ANSWER: 56
4. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Methyl-tertiary-butyl-ether, abbreviated MTBE, replaced lead in gasoline due to several environmental benefits. In order to raise octane rating in current gasoline, what has subsequently replaced MTBE?
ANSWER: ETHANOL
4. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is the best estimate of the electricity consumption of a petascale supercomputer today?
W) 8 kilowatts
X) 80 kilowatts
Y) 8 megawatts
Z) 80 megawatts
ANSWER: Y) 8 megawatts
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of an ionic compound?
W) Methane
X) Chlorine dioxide
Y) Dinitrogen trioxide
Z) Calcium sulfide
ANSWER: Z) Calcium sulfide
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the geometry of sulfur tetrafluoride?
ANSWER: SEESAW
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A proton is traveling counterclockwise in a circle in the x = 0 plane. In what direction is its magnetic field vector?
ANSWER: POSITIVE X
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the adjective for the type of materials that will create an induced magnetic field opposite to an externally applied magnetic field?
ANSWER: DIAMAGNETIC
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Most long-period comets are thought to originate from what region in the solar system?
ANSWER: OORT CLOUD
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In 1943, a volcano suddenly appeared in a corn field in the village of Paricutin and began erupting lava bombs and ash. What type of volcano was this?
ANSWER: CINDER CONE (also accept: SCORIA CONE)
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What quantity, in milliliters, of a 0.5 molar solution of sulfuric acid would be required to neutralize 30 mL of a 0.1 molar solution of sodium hydroxide?
ANSWER: 3
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are TRUE of ideal concentration cells 1) They obey the Nernst equation 2) At equilibrium, they have a positive redox potential 3) They have a positive standard redox potential.
ANSWER: 1
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of tissue do monocot roots possess at their center that dicot roots do not?
W) Xylem
X) Pith
Y) Collenchyma
Z) Trichomes
ANSWER: X) Pith
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three processes that are considered active transport 1) Uptake of glucose via channels 2) Sodium-calcium exchange 3) Mucus secretion.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What type of circuits, which include types such as high-pass and low-pass filters, are composed of a resistor and a capacitor?
ANSWER: RC CIRCUIT
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer An object’s position x as a function of time t is x(t) = t3-t2 + 3t + 5. What is the object’s acceleration at time 2?
ANSWER: 10
11. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following weather conditions and times of year is best for achieving efficient energy conversion in a solar thermal plant?
W) Partly cloudy day in fall
X) Rainy day in spring
Y) Partly sunny day in winter
Z) Overcast day in summer
ANSWER: W) Partly cloudy day in fall
11. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following accounts for the largest proportion of total US domestic energy production?
W) Natural gas
X) Petroleum
Y) Coal
Z) Nuclear
ANSWER: W) Natural gas
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the period of the function with equation y = 4 sine (7x + 3) + 2?
ANSWER: 2π/7 (also accept: (2/7)π)
12. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area in the first quadrant between the graphs of y = 4x and y = x3?
ANSWER: 4
13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A mass on a spring oscillates at a frequency of 200 hertz, creating a low-pitch sound. Which of the following can be done to increase the frequency of the sound produced?
W) Increase the mass
X) Use a stiffer spring
Y) Approach the mass-spring system at a high speed
Z) Remove the air in the room
ANSWER: X) Use a stiffer spring
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three particles that are leptons 1) Neutrino 2) Gluon 3) Positron.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In collies, what neurotransmitter is released by somatic motor neurons to induce muscle contraction?
ANSWER: ACETYLCHOLINE
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice With which of the following is protein binding to a TATA box activity associated?
W) DNA replication
X) Transcription
Y) Translation
Z) Elongation
ANSWER: X) Transcription
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In what quadrants does the graph of the equation y = 4x – 3 lie?
ANSWER: 1, 3, AND 4 (also accept: FIRST, THIRD, FOURTH; ALL BUT SECOND)
15. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the least common multiple of 14, 16, and 18?
ANSWER: 1008
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer DOE scientists recently published a paper in which nanoscale circuitry was developed by printing a carbon-based semiconductor on a germanium surface. What allotrope of carbon are the semiconductors based on?
ANSWER: GRAPHENE
16. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the only baseload electrical power source in the US that is virtually carbon neutral, and supplies 20% of baseload electrical power?
ANSWER: NUCLEAR POWER (also accept: FISSION; URANIUM)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Consider the reaction of two moles of hydrogen gas and one mole of oxygen gas to generate two moles of steam. Which of the following statements is TRUE?
W) Delta H is greater than delta U
X) Delta H is less than delta U
Y) Delta H is equal to delta U
Z) Delta H is greater than delta U at high temperatures, but less at low temperatures
ANSWER: X) Delta H is less than delta U
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three diatomic molecules that MO theory predicts to be stable 1) Li2 2) Be2 3) B2.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice We see the same side of the moon here on Earth all the time due to what effect?
W) Lagrange point
X) Gravitational lensing
Y) Tidal locking
Z) Asynchronous spin
ANSWER: Y) Tidal locking
18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What layer of the Earth's atmosphere varies in depth with the time of day and reflects radio waves long distances?
W) Photosphere
X) Stratosphere
Y) Troposphere
Z) Ionosphere
ANSWER: Z) Ionosphere
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The hormone estrogen contains five stereocenters. How many stereoisomers of estrogen are possible?
ANSWER: 32

ANSWER: 1 AND 3 (also accept: CHIRAL CHROMATOGRAPHY AND CRYSTALLIZATION WITH PLUS TARTARIC ACID)

ANSWER: CRATER LAKE

ANSWER: –28

ANSWER: VITAMIN D
