1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A steel ball of mass m is dropped from a height h and falls onto the table. It rebounds to almost exactly the same height. Which of the following best describes the force exerted on the ball?
W) Less than mg
X) Exactly mg
Y) between mg and 2 mg
Z) Greater than 2 mg
ANSWER: Z) Greater than 2 mg
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer A special gas has a heat capacity c(T) = alpha * T. The change in entropy of the gas when heating it from 100 K to 200 K is delta S. In terms of delta S, what is the change in entropy of the gas when heating it from 200 K to 600 K?
ANSWER: 4*DELTA S
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer For a reaction split into its elementary steps, the rate of change of the intermediates can often be treated as 0. What is the term for this method, used in chemical kinetics, to derive the rate law for a reaction?
ANSWER: Steady state approximation
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If the time needed for the concentration of species A to decrease from 1.0 M to 0.50 M is 10 minutes, and the time needed for the concentration to decrease from 0.50 M to 0.25 M is 5 minutes, what is the overall reaction order for the process?
ANSWER: Zeroth
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What water soluble plant pigments conferring a purple or red color to a plant are crucial for photoprotective and antioxidant processes?
ANSWER: Anthocyanins
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Two birds are sitting on a tree branch. Unfortunately, a predator is approaching the second bird. The first bird can save the second bird, but this carries a 50% chance of getting eaten itself. If the second bird is not saved, its chance of death is 100%. The first bird would have 4 more offspring if it lives, while the second would have 6. Identify all of the following three relations between the two birds that would make an altruistic act by the first bird evolutionarily advantageous 1) Cousins 2) Siblings 3) Twins
ANSWER: 2 and 3 (also accept: Siblings and Twins)

10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly describes a difference between dyneins and kinesins?
W) Dyneins move along microtubules, while kinesins move along microfilaments
X) Dyneins function in retrograde transport, while kinesins function in anterograde transport
Y) Dyneins prevent microtubule catastrophe, while kinesins do not

18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements best explains why the dissolution of solid Calcium Sulfate has a negative change in entropy?
W) Calcium Sulfate dissolves exothermically, thus the loss in heat causes a decrease in microstates of the system
X) Solid Calcium Sulfate is an ionic compound, thus the atoms can be far more spread out over a larger surface area
Y) Aqueous Ca2+ and SO42- ions are tightly hydrated, thus decreasing the number of ways the ions can be arranged in water
Z) Ca2+ and SO42- are held together by restrictive strong electrostatic forces in solid Calcium Sulfate, thus there are more ways of arranging the Ca2+ and SO42- ions in aqueous solution
