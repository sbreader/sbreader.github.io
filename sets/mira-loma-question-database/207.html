1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following organic compounds has the highest boiling point?
W) CH4
X) CH3OH
Y) CH3OCH2CH3
Z) CH3CH2CH3
ANSWER: X) CH3OH
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Oxygen can be prepared in the lab by heating potassium chlorate in the presence of a catalyst. How many moles of diatomic oxygen gas could be obtained from one mole of KClO3?
ANSWER: 1.5 MOLES
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Indicate, by name or number, all of the following 3 statements that is/are TRUE concerning thiols: 1: thiols are more easily oxidized than alcohols 2: disulfides are easily oxidized to thiols 3: mercaptan is another name for a thiol
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer 2500 liters of oxygen gas are produced at 1.00 atm of pressure. It is to be compressed and stored in a 2.00 liter cylinder. Assuming that temperature is constant, what is the pressure of the oxygen in the cylinder?
ANSWER: 1250 ATMOSPHERES
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is produced in the hydrogenation reaction of an aldehyde?
W) ketone
X) carboxylic acid
Y) secondary alcohol
Z) primary alcohol
ANSWER: Z) primary alcohol
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Consider the following reaction at equilibrium: 2 CO2(g) 2 CO (g) + O2(g), where ΔH = -514 kJ. According to Le Châtelier's Principle, an increase in temperature will do which of the following?
W) increase the partial pressure of O2(g)
X) increase the partial pressure of CO2(g)
Y) decrease the value of the equilibrium constant
Z) increase the value of the equilibrium constant
ANSWER: Y) decrease the value of the equilibrium constant
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Name all of the following 3 statements that is/are TRUE regarding reactions and reaction rates: 1: reaction rates can be positive or negative 2: units of the rate constant are independent of the overall reaction order 3: the instantaneous rate of a reaction can be determined from the graph of molarity versus time at any point on the graph
ANSWER: 3 ONLY
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What law governs the relationship between absorbed light to the concentration of the substance absorbing the light?
ANSWER: BEER'S LAW
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The rate law of the overall reaction A + B C is rate = k[A]2. Which of the following will NOT increase the rate of the reaction?
W) increasing the concentration of reactant A
X) increasing the concentration of reactant B
Y) increasing the temperature of the reaction
Z) adding a catalyst to the reaction
ANSWER: X) increasing the concentration of reactant B
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the molarity of a solution made by diluting 0.050 L of 0.10 M HCl solution to a volume of 1.0 L?
ANSWER: 0.0050 M (also accept: 5.0 × 10-3 M HCl)
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following types of conformations is most energetically favorable for a six-member ring, and involves the staggering of hydrogen atoms so that the bond angle between the carbon atoms is exactly 109.5°?
W) eclipsed conformation
X) staggered conformation
Y) chair conformation
Z) boat conformation
ANSWER: Y) chair conformation
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Classify each of the following 3 compounds as either weak or strong bases, respectively: ammonia, fluoride ion, sodium hydroxide
ANSWER: WEAK, WEAK, STRONG
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What type of molecule is formed when the proton of the COOH group in an amino acid is transferred to the NH2 group of that same amino acid?
ANSWER: ZWITTERION
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Name all of the following four compounds that could be added to sodium acetate to produce a buffer: 1: acetic acid 2: hydrochloric acid 3: potassium acetate 4: sodium chloride
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following processes produces a decrease in the entropy of the system?
W) freezing water to form ice
X) mixing of two gases into one container
Y) boiling water to form steam
Z) dissolution of solid KCl in water
ANSWER: W) freezing water to form ice
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many sigma bonds are in one molecule of ethane?
ANSWER: 7
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following cycloalkanes has only one conformation?
W) cyclohexane
X) cyclopentane
Y) cyclobutane
Z) cyclopropane
ANSWER: Z) cyclopropane
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds has a central atom that is sp3d2 hybridized?
W) XeF4
X) PCl5
Y) PH3
Z) BeF2
ANSWER: W) XeF4
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer According to MO theory, what is the bond order of the N-N bond in the N22+ ion?
ANSWER: 2
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Consider the following reaction: A + B → P. Given that the reaction rate did not change when the concentration of B was tripled, what is the reaction order in B?
ANSWER: 0
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is correct about a substance which has unpaired electrons?
W) it is a permanent magnet
X) it is slightly attracted to a magnet
Y) it is slightly repelled by a magnet
Z) it is not affected by a magnetic field
ANSWER: X) it is slightly attracted to a magnet
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In which of the following types of isomers are the bonds the same, but the spatial arrangements around the atom different?
W) structural isomers
X) linkage isomers
Y) coordination-sphere isomers
Z) stereoisomers
ANSWER: Z) stereoisomers
