1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following ligands induces the largest electronic d orbital splitting in a transition metal complex?
W) Chloro
X) Aqua
Y) Cyano
Z) Ethylenediamine
ANSWER: Y) Cyano
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Consider an organic compound with no rings in its structure, and with the chemical formula C12H16O2. What is the number of double bonds in its structure?
ANSWER: 5
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If the radial dependence of the electric field of a monopole is 1/(r2), what is the long range radial dependence of an electric dipole?
ANSWER: 1/(r3)
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer A weight is sinking in a fluid. The drag force on the weight is equal to a constant times its velocity squared times the density of the fluid. In SI base units, give the dimensions of the constant.
ANSWER: METERS SQUARED
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following cell types is associated with haversian canals?
W) Osteocytes
X) Chondrocytes
Y) Fibroblasts
Z) Leukocytes
ANSWER: W) Osteocytes
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following four plants that have evolved mechanisms to reduce bio-energetically wasteful photorespiration 1) Cactus 2) Oak trees 3) Potatoes 4) Rice.
ANSWER: 1
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The greenhouse effect on Venus is primarily attributed to what gas?
ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE (also accept: CO2)
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer To the nearest power of ten, how many times as intense is the light we receive from a star with apparent magnitude negative 1 as that of another star with apparent magnitude 4?
ANSWER: 100
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If 6 dice are rolled and the numbers on the top faces are added together, how many different sums are possible?
ANSWER: 31
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the largest prime factor of the quantity 42014 + 42015 + 42016 + 42017?
ANSWER: 17
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice The Advanced Light Source can be used to identify elements in batteries and fuel cells. Which of the following tools is most likely used for these purposes at the ALS?
W) Fluorescence microscopy
X) X-ray absorption spectroscopy
Y) NMR spectroscopy
Z) Confocal microscopy
ANSWER: X) X-ray absorption spectroscopy
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at the Joint Genome Institute have been using new types of fungi to produce biofuels from plant material. Where are these fungi naturally found?
W) Forest soils
X) Coral reefs
Y) Animal guts
Z) Swamps
ANSWER: Y) Animal guts
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Compton scattering is the scattering of X-ray photons from what particle?
ANSWER: ELECTRON
7. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the half-life of a free neutron?
W) 1 second
X) 10 minutes
Y) 100 hours
Z) 1000 years
ANSWER: X) 10 minutes
8. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer In fluidized bed combustors used for clean coal, what is added to the coal to keep pollutants in check?
ANSWER: LIMESTONE (also accept: CALCIUM CARBONATE)
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer The great majority of the transistors in integrated circuits are of the CMOS type. What do the letters of the acronym represent?
ANSWER: COMPLEMENTARY METAL OXIDE SEMICONDUCTOR
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Consider the hypothetical reaction 2A, aqueous, plus 2B, gaseous, yields 3C, aqueous, plus D, gaseous, where the change in enthalpy of the reaction is -22 kilojoules. Which of the following will drive the reaction toward the products?
W) Increasing the temperature
X) Increasing the volume of the reaction vessel at constant temperature
Y) Increasing the pressure
Z) Increasing the amount of D
ANSWER: Y) Increasing the pressure
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following solvents would most accelerate an SN2 reaction?
W) Hexane
X) Dichloromethane
Y) DMSO
Z) Ethyl acetate
ANSWER: Y) DMSO
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In the integers mod 12, what is 5 times 8?
ANSWER: 4
10. BONUS Math Short Answer f of x is a twice differentiable function such that f prime of x and f double prime of x are both less than 0 for all real numbers. If f of 3 equals 12 and f of 5 equals 8, what is the largest possible integer value for f of 7?
ANSWER: 3
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the genus of the harmless gut bacterium that can be transformed by a bacteriophage into a virulent form associated with cholera?
ANSWER: VIBRIO
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A disease-linked, spontaneous, congenital germ line mutation creates a restriction site for HaeIII. A diagnostic test for this mutation involves restriction digestion with endonuclease HaeIII. If the DNA of a patient with this mutation is tested and imaged using an agarose gel, which of the following is expected?
W) No diagnostic point mutation exists on either maternal or paternal chromosomes
X) Both maternal and paternal chromosomes have the diagnostic point mutation
Y) Either the maternal or the paternal chromosome has the point mutation, but not both
Z) The test was inconclusive
ANSWER: Y) Either the maternal or the paternal chromosome has the point mutation, but not both
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer According to the Stefan-Boltzmann law, a star with surface temperature in kelvins that is three times that of the Sun would emit how many times as much energy as the Sun?
ANSWER: 81
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The closed universe is a solution of Einstein's equations of general relativity in which the mass density of the Universe exceeds what parameter?
ANSWER: CRITICAL DENSITY
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What viral reproductive cycle kills the infected host cell while the virus is dividing?
ANSWER: LYTIC CYCLE
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the number of cells present in a mature male angiosperm gametophyte?
ANSWER: 3
14. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Brookhaven National Lab scientists recently made strides on understanding the nature of Cooper pairs. To which of the following disciplines is this discovery most relevant?
W) Particle physics
X) Superconductor physics
Y) Nuclear physics
Z) Computational chemistry
ANSWER: X) Superconductor physics
14. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Argonne National Lab are using x-rays to probe porosity in 3D-printed titanium alloys. Which of the following best explains why porosity is undesirable?
W) Porosity decreases resistance to fatigue
X) Porosity decreases flexibility
Y) A large range of pore sizes reduces printing resolution
Z) Porosity limits the size of the structures that can be printed
ANSWER: W) Porosity decreases resistance to fatigue

ANSWER: PHOTOELECTRIC EFFECT
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer CPT symmetry is a fundamental concept of nuclear science and theoretical physics. What do the C, P, and T stand for?
ANSWER: Charge conjugation, Charge; Parity inversion, Parity; Time reversal, Time (also accept: TIME)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three elements in terms of increasing electron affinities 1) Carbon 2) Nitrogen 3) Oxygen.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What effect, which applies to the chemistry of real gases and is exploited in the liquefaction of air, causes a gas's temperature to decrease in an adiabatic isenthalpic expansion?
ANSWER: JOULE-THOMSON EFFECT (also accept: JOULE-THOMSON EXPANSION; JOULE-KELVIN EFFECT)
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the volume of a circular cylinder formed by rotating a 5 by 6 rectangular region about one of its shorter sides?
ANSWER: 180π
18. BONUS Math Short Answer The sums of three whole numbers taken in pairs are 11, 13, and 14. What is the product of these three numbers?
ANSWER: 240
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of extrusive igneous rock is considered intermediate between basalt and dacite and possesses approximately 60% silica content?
ANSWER: ANDESITE

ANSWER: Y) North Pacific Deep Water

ANSWER: AGROBACTERIUM

ANSWER: 1 AND 2

ANSWER: 1.5 (also accept: 3/2; 1 ½; 3 TO 2)
