1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following rocks is an extrusive ultramafic rock which almost exclusively formed in the Archean eon and does not have a modern counterpart?

1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Peter is trapped inside a comet! Order the following layers of a comet from innermost to outermost 1) Halo 2) Coma 3) Nucleus.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Dr. Borick recently got back the results of a DNA sequencing test and found out that his genome is 23% adenine. According to Chargaff's Rule, what percent of his genome is guanine?
ANSWER: 27%
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, select all of the following species for which Chargaff's Rule does not apply 1) HIV 2) Hepatitis B 3) Streptococcus pyogenes.
ANSWER: 1, 2
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Ashray is working on creating a new chemical at Lexington High School. He has 46 grams of nitrite (molar mass 46g/mol) in a container and wants to add mercury to create mercurous nitrite. Given your answer to three significant figures, how many grams of mercury (molar mass 200 g/mol) should he add to create 1 mol of true dimeric mercurous nitrite?
ANSWER: 400 grams

ANSWER: 255

5. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A mass on a rough surface with a constant coefficient of friction, is given an initial velocity v, and travels a distance d. W) d

Y) 3d/2
Z) 2d
ANSWER: X) 5d/4
