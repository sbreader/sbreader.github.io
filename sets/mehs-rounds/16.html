1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A sound wave travels in a medium that is four times as dense as the medium that another sound wave is traveling in. However, the other sound wave has double the amplitude of the first sound wave, and has the same angular frequency and velocity. By what factor is the intensity of the first sound wave larger than the intensity of the second sound wave?
ANSWER: 1
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer In a magical parallel universe ice cream gets colder as its surroundings get warmer without any work done on the ice cream by an external force. For this to happen, what quantity must be negative?
ANSWER: ENTROPY
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A compound with the formula C6H12 does not decolorize bromine in CHCl3 solution. Which compound could this be?
W) 1-hexene
X) Trans 3-hexene
Y) Cyclohexane
Z) 3-methyl 1-pentene
ANSWER: Y) Cyclohexane
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The hydride of which two elements can be used in the reduction of aldehydes or ketones to alcohols?
ANSWER: LITHIUM, ALUMINUM (also accept: Li, Al)
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab have been using neutron-neutron scattering to reveal the behavior of cholesterol on membranes. In the lipid bilayer, cholesterol is found in abundance in lipid rafts. Which of the following statements regarding lipid rafts is NOT true?
W) Lipid rafts are more loosely packed than the surrounding bilayer
X) Lipid rafts are enriched in sphingolipids
Y) Lipid rafts in the cell membrane influence membrane fluidity and protein trafficking
Z) Lipid rafts are involved in signal transduction processes
ANSWER: W) Lipid rafts are more loosely packed than the surrounding bilayer
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are conducting research on light dark matter. Identify all of the following 3 statements regarding light dark matter that is true 1) Light dark matter is lighter than warm, hot, and cold dark matter 2) Light dark matter WIMPs have masses of less that 1 GeV 3) Light dark matter particles are not bounded by the Lee-Weinberg limit because of their extremely high temperature.
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In many stars, energy is radiated in the form of photons and neutrinos. What similar process, which occurs within neutron stars and white dwarfs, emits neutrinos and causes a rapid disappearance of thermal energy from the interior of a star?
ANSWER: URCA PROCESS
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 statements regarding chinook winds that are true 1) Temperature drastically falls in areas that chinook winds have recently moved through 2) Compressional heating provides the internal warmth for a chinook 3) The appearance of a foehn wall is a telltale sign of an impending chinook.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3

6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the relationship between (E)-2-butene and (Z)-2-butene?
ANSWER: DIASTEREOMERS
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A current of 33 megaamperes is sent through a straight wire 3 meters under a laptop. Assuming that vacuum permeability is 4π × 10-7, and giving your answer in teslas rounded to the nearest tenth, what is the magnitude of the magnetic field that the laptop is exposed to from the wire?
ANSWER: 2.2
7. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Jebediah is on a spacecraft that has just entered the sphere of influence of the moon from an elliptical orbit around Earth. The spacecraft is currently traveling in front of the moon. Which of the following is true about the spacecraft’s orbit assuming that no external forces alter the current orbit?
W) The spacecraft will re-enter an elliptical orbit around Earth
X) The spacecraft will continue orbiting around the moon
Y) The spacecraft will fly out of the sphere of influence of Earth
Z) The spacecraft will fly out of the solar system
ANSWER: W) The spacecraft will re-enter an elliptical orbit around Earth
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In orchids, which of the following specific types of parasitic symbiosis always occurs at some point of their life stage and is characterized by orchids getting all or part of its food from fungi rather than from photosynthesis?
W) Hemiparasitism
X) Holoparisitism
Y) Adelphoparasitism
Z) Myco-heterotrophism
ANSWER: Z) Myco-heterotrophism
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer In yeasts and mammalian cells, the SNARE proteins mediate the fusion of vesicles with the target membrane. During membrane fusion, what 2 SNARE proteins located on separate membranes combine to form a trans-SNARE complex?
ANSWER: V-SNARE AND T-SNARE

X) Amphiboles

ANSWER: 9.4

11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If a deuterium nucleus resonates at 450 megahertz in a field strength of 4.8 teslas, at what frequency, in megahertz, will the same deuterium nucleus resonate at a field strength of 16.8 teslas?
ANSWER: 1575
11. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A 5-meter-long solenoid has 1500 coils of wire and carries a current of 4 amps. Given that the permittivity of free space is 1.25 times 10 to the negative 6, which of the following is the strength of the magnetic field at the center of the solenoid, in teslas?
W) 0.15
X) 0.015
Y) 0.0015
Z) 0.00015
ANSWER: Y) 0.0015
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at Brookhaven National Lab are researching new semiconductor designs which scale down better than traditional semiconductors. Traditional semiconductors only work under which of the following conditions?
W) When the entropy of the semiconductor is above 5
X) When the heat of the semiconductor is below 40 celsius
Y) The correspondence limit
Z) When the force exerted on the semiconductor is below 500 newtons
ANSWER: Y) The correspondence limit
