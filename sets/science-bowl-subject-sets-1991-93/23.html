1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice For the hydrogen atom, which series describes electron transitions to the N = 2 orbit?

6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A pendulum which is suspended from the ceiling of a railroad car is observed to hang at an angle of 10 degrees to the left of vertical. Which of the following answers could explain this phenomena?

A boy throws a ping pong ball into this hole. The ping pong ball would:
W) fall to the center of the Earth and remain forever suspended there.
X) fall to the opposite side of the Earth's surface and remain forever suspended there.
