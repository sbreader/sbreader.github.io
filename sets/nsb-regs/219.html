1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What was the most abundant atmospheric gas during the Proterozoic Eon?
ANSWER: NITROGEN (also accept: DINITROGEN; N2)
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A transgressive stratigraphic sequence can be related to which of the following events?
W) Sea-level rise
X) Orogeny
Y) Oxbow lake formation
Z) Significant rainfall events
ANSWER: W) Sea-level rise
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Marie places 4.4 grams of dry ice in a vacuum-sealed 5-liter bag at standard temperature. After the dry ice reaches standard temperature, then, to the nearest tenth of a liter, how much volume will it fill?
ANSWER: 2.2
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three molecules that typically undergo an Sn1 reaction in the presence of a nucleophile 1) Methyl-bromide 2) 2-bromo-propane 3) Tert-butyl-amine.
ANSWER: 2
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Los Alamos National Lab are measuring the positron flux in Earth’s upper atmosphere. Which of the following is NOT TRUE concerning positrons?
W) They decay into muons
X) They have a positive charge
Y) They are first-generation leptons
Z) They can interact with electrons to generate two gamma rays
ANSWER: W) They decay into muons
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Lawrence Berkeley National Lab scientists are studying the teeth of parrotfish due to their unique structure. Parrotfish can be classified best as which of the following?
W) Lobe-finned fish
X) Hagfish
Y) Ray-finned fish
Z) Sharks
ANSWER: Y) Ray-finned fish
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Consider a circuit in which two light bulbs with equal resistance values are connected in parallel. If one of the light bulbs burns out, by what factor is the brightness of the other bulb multiplied?
ANSWER: ONE
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer Consider a 200-newton-meter torque that acts on a solid disk of radius one meter and mass 100 kilograms. What is the angular velocity of the disk after 5 seconds, in radians per second?
ANSWER: 20
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice For a conic section, which of the following values of eccentricity would indicate that the conic section is an ellipse?
W) 0
X) 0.5
Y) 1
Z) 1.5
ANSWER: X) 0.5
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What are the coordinates of the image of the point (–1, 5) when it is reflected through the line y = x – 4?
ANSWER: (9, –5) (also accept: x = 9 AND y = –5)
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of connective tissue links muscles to bones?
ANSWER: TENDON
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of SDS-PAGE 1) It requires addition of antioxidant to the inner gel chamber 2) It requires protein denaturation using urea 3) It relies on SDS binding proportional to protein mass.
ANSWER: 1, 3
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of sub-stellar object can fuse deuterium but not hydrogen?
ANSWER: BROWN DWARF
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer An asteroid revolves around the Sun every 64 Earth-years. To the nearest whole astronomical unit, what is the semimajor axis of the asteroid?
ANSWER: 16
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Similar to his famous gold foil experiment, Rutherford bombarded nitrogen-14 atoms with alpha particles, and observed that each event released a proton. Into what isotope was the nitrogen converted?
ANSWER: OXYGEN-17
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A certain steel guitar has strings of length 1 meter and cross sectional area 2 × 10-6 square meter. If the Young’s modulus of steel is 2 × 1011 newtons per square meter, then in newtons, what is the tension in the string when it is stretched 0.02 meter for tuning?
ANSWER: 8000
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The organisms that form arbuscular mycorrhizae are part of what group of fungi?
W) Chytrids
X) Glomeromycetes
Y) Basidiomycetes
Z) Ascomycetes
ANSWER: X) Glomeromycetes
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Starting with the fixation of atmospheric nitrogen and ending with the assimilation of nitrogen compounds by plants, order the following three ions of the nitrogen cycle chronologically 1) Ammonium 2) Nitrate 3) Nitrite.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The graph of a continuous function f contains the points (3, –6) and (8, –4). The intermediate value theorem guarantees an x-value such that f of x equals which of the following?
W) –5
X) –2
Y) 2
Z) 5
ANSWER: W) –5
10. BONUS Math Short Answer How many zeroes occur at the right of the number of permutations of 60 objects taken 20 at a time?
ANSWER: 5
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying O-linked glycosylation of the tau protein. This post-translational process plays a role in the pathogenesis of what human disease?
ANSWER: ALZHEIMER’S DISEASE
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying cytokine production during disease. Identify all of the following three statements that are true of cytokines 1) They are peptides 2) They are transcription factors and signal via diffusing through the cell membrane 3) They can be released by T cells.
ANSWER: 1, 3
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following could be the products of anaerobic fermentation of glucose?
W) Ethanol and water
X) Acetone and water
Y) Ethanol and carbon dioxide
Z) Acetone and carbon dioxide
ANSWER: Y) Ethanol and carbon dioxide
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three ligands that are considered bidentate 1) Oxalate 2) Ethylene-diamine 3) Cyanide.
ANSWER: 1, 2
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms is the primary infection target of the virus T2?
W) E. coli
X) S. cerevisiae
Y) D. melanogaster
Z) M. musculus
ANSWER: W) E. coli
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer What microsatellite genetic markers do forensic scientists typically use for crime scene DNA analysis?
ANSWER: STRs (also accept: SHORT TANDEM REPEATS)
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Find the value of a for which the following vectors are perpendicular: 9i – 3j and ai + 36j.
ANSWER: 12
14. BONUS Math Short Answer The graph of f(x) = (x2-2x-24)/(x2-16) has a removable discontinuity at x = a for some real number a. What is the limit as x approaches a of f of x?
ANSWER: 5/4 (also accept: 1¼; 1.25)
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab use the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider to study a state of matter thought to have also existed in the early universe. This matter is composed of quarks and what other strongly-interacting fundamental particles?
ANSWER: GLUONS
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab are studying the Alpha Centauri star system. This system contains how many stars?
ANSWER: THREE
16. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In a rectifier, which of the following pairs of current types describes the input and output of the device, respectively?
W) DC-AC
X) DC-DC
Y) AC-DC
Z) AC-AC
ANSWER: Y) AC-DC
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer One mole of an ideal monatomic gas has a net energy change of 1245 joules. To one significant figure and in degrees Celsius, how much does its temperature change?
ANSWER: 100
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of drainage pattern will develop on bedrock that is highly jointed?
W) Radial
X) Dendritic
Y) Rectangular
Z) Deltaic

W) Desert
X) Peat bog
Y) Boreal forest
Z) Subhumid climate
ANSWER: X) Peat bog

W) 145
X) 155
Y) 290
Z) 589
ANSWER: Y) 290

ANSWER: 10

ANSWER: 8970

ANSWER: ONE

ANSWER: 1, 3

ANSWER: AV NODE (also accept: ATRIOVENTRICULAR NODE)

ANSWER: 2
