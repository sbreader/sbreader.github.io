2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly describes a chemical equilibrium?
W) the concentration of reactants equals the concentration of products
X) the forward reaction rate equals the reverse reaction rate
Y) the reaction has stopped and no further molecular collisions occur
