1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following periodic trends best explains why silicon dioxide adopts a network covalent structure of silicon oxygen single bonds in quartz, while carbon dioxide adopts a molecular form with two double bonds?
W) Silicon is larger than carbon
X) Silicon has a lower first ionization energy than carbon
Y) Silicon has a lower fourth ionization energy than carbon
Z) Silicon has a greater electron affinity than carbon
ANSWER: W) Silicon is larger than carbon
