Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly identifies the change in pH and pOH as the temperature of a sample of pure water is increased?
W) Increase, Increase
X) Increase, Decrease
Y) Decrease, Increase
Z) Decrease, Decrease
ANSWER: Z) Decrease, Decrease
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecular geometries will exhibit facial-meridional isomerism?
W) Square Pyramidal
X) Octahedral
Y) Tetrahedral
Z) Square Planar
ANSWER: X) Octahedral
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 3 nuclei that are NMR-active 1) Oxygen-18 2) Fluorine-19 3) Phosphorus-31
ANSWER: 2, 3
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Though sulfur typically exists as a Cyclo-octasulfur, it also exists as Cyclo-hexasulfur with formula S6. In this allotrope, the molecule exhibits what shape, which is also the most stable conformation of cyclohexane?
ANSWER: Chair Conformation
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three compounds which are aromatic 1) Styrene 2) Anisole 3) Acetone
ANSWER: 1, 2
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Carl has a terrarium that has become infested with weeds. To kill the weeds, he decides to mix hydrogen gas and bromine gas, in the presence of light. By name or number, identify all of the following three terms which could be used to describe this reaction 1) Radical Reaction 2) Reduction Oxidation Reaction 3) Combustion Reaction
ANSWER: 1, 2
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In the derivation for the Michaelis-Menten Equation, the concentration of enzyme-substrate complex is assumed to be relatively constant. This is an example of what assumption, which states that the concentration of an intermediate remains relatively constant through time?
ANSWER: Steady-state Approximation
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the hybridization of the central atom in phosphorous pentachloride?
ANSWER: sp3d (also accept: dsp3)
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three elements by increasing number of atoms in their most stable allotrope at room conditions 1) Sulfur 2) Oxygen 3) Phosphorous
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The reaction between 3-bromobutan-2-ol and Sodium Hydroxide creates a product containing what functional group, which consists of a three membered ring including an oxygen?
ANSWER: Epoxide
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The pyramidal inversion of ammonia is much faster than the pyramidal trimethylamine by around a factor of 105. This is due to what phenomenon, where a subatomic particle passes through an energy barrier it classically lacks the energy to overcome?
ANSWER: Quantum Tunnelling
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three ionic compounds by decreasing lattice energy 1) Magnesium oxide 2) Calcium oxide 3) Potassium chloride
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice White tin and Grey tin act like which of the following types of materials respectively?
W) Metal, Metal
X) Metal, Metalloid
Y) Metalloid, Metal
Z) Metalloid, Metalloid
ANSWER: X) Metal, Metalloid
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Actinide Contraction is caused by improper shielding by which of the following orbitals?
W) 4d
X) 4f
Y) 5d
Z) 5f
ANSWER: Z) 5f
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the class of organometallic lithium-copper compounds used to form carbon-carbon bonds in organic synthesis?
ANSWER: Gilman Reagent
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name given to the class of materials, which are commonly used in solar cells, that share a specific crystalline structure and have chemical formulas of the form ABX3, and are named after a calcium titanium oxide mineral?
ANSWER: Perovskites
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Born-Haber Cycles rely on what law, which states that the enthalpy changes of the steps of a reaction sum up to the overall reaction enthalpy?
ANSWER: Hess's Law
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Metallic Character generally increases when moving in which of the following directions across the periodic table?
W) Up and to the left
X) Up and to the Right
Y) Down and to the left
Z) Down and to the Right
ANSWER: Y) Down and to the left
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three values which could be changed by the addition of a catalyst 1) Equilibrium Constant 2) Enthalpy 3) Activation Energy
ANSWER: 3
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following alkyl halides is most likely to undergo an SN2 reaction?
W) methyl chloride
X) methyl bromide
Y) ethyl chloride
Z) ethyl bromide
ANSWER: X) methyl bromide
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What quantity is sum of enthalpy of fusion and enthalpy of vaporization?
ANSWER: enthalpy of sublimation
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following methods would be best for separating methanol and water?
W) Filtration
X) Decantation
Y) Chromatography
Z) Distillation
ANSWER: Z) Distillation
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Given that A yields 2B has an equilibrium constant of 32 and B yields C + D has an equilibrium constant of 1/4, what is the equilibrium constant for 2C+2D yields A?
ANSWER: 1/2 (also accept: 0.5)
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The energy of a photon is directly proportional to which of the following?
W) Intensity
X) Frequency
Y) Wavelength
Z) Amplitude
ANSWER: X) Frequency
25. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, order the following three conformation of butane by increasing stability 1) Gauche 2) Staggered 3) Eclipsed
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
