1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A wooden block with a coefficient of static friction greater than the coefficient of kinetic friction is placed on a rough surface. When the surface is at an angle of 25° to the horizontal, the block does not move, and when the angle to the horizontal is 35° the block slides down the surface. Which of the following could be the coefficient of static friction?
W) 0.4
X) 0.6
Y) 0.8
Z) 1.0
ANSWER: X) 0.6

BONUS Physics Short Answer Another wooden block with a mass of 6 kg and coefficient of static friction greater than the coefficient of kinetic friction is placed on a ramp at an inclination of 45°. The coefficient of kinetic friction between the block and the ramp is found to be 0.5. A block of unknown mass is connected to the block on the ramp with a massless frictionless pulley, hanging over the edge of the ramp. Assuming g = 10, what is the mass of the block that hangs over the ramp in simplest radical form?
ANSWER: 1.5√2 (also accept: 3√2/2)
2. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Researchers at the Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory are exploring the use of ionic liquids as a biomass pretreatment. These researchers sought to decrease the cost of ionic liquids by synthesizing them directly from hydrophobic monomers that can be found within the cell wall. Which of the following is this monomer that assists in the transport of water in plants by strengthening the vascular tissue, allowing it to withstand pressure changes?
W) Cholesterol
X) Cellulose
Y) Galactose
Z) Lignin
ANSWER: Z) Lignin

W) 0.1%
X) 10%
Y) 30%
Z) 70%
ANSWER: Y) 30%

3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer An adsorption isotherm apparatus consists of a dosing chamber with a volume of 40 milliliters as well as a sample chamber. The two airtight chambers are initially separated by a valve and at a pressure of 0 torr. Helium gas is let into the dosing chamber until the pressure reaches 750 torr. The valve is then opened, and the helium expands into the sample chamber. Once the pressure has equilibrated between both chambers, it is measured to be 300 torr. In milliliters, determine the volume of the sample chamber.
ANSWER: 60

14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory are studying the formation of short-lived cumulus clouds during fair weather. Which of the following best explains why these clouds have a significant effect at cooling the surface?
W) The large amounts of precipitation from cumulus clouds cools the surface.
X) The strong updrafts created by cumulus clouds bring cool air over the surface.
Y) The cumulus clouds have a high albedo, so there is less incoming solar radiation hitting the surface.
Z) The cumulus clouds have a low albedo, so there is less incoming solar radiation hitting the surface.
ANSWER: Y) The cumulus clouds have a high albedo, so there is less incoming solar radiation hitting the surface.
