1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Kirchhoff's loop rule is based on the conservation of what quantity?
ANSWER: ENERGY
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer What elementary particle mediates the electromagnetic force?
ANSWER: PHOTON
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What cells, found in the seminiferous tubules, provide nutrients for cells undergoing spermatogenesis?
ANSWER: SERTOLI CELLS
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is FALSE regarding the menstrual cycle in humans?
W) A peak in estradiol causes the LH surge
X) The follicle develops into the corpus luteum after ovulation
Y) Low levels of estradiol stimulate the hypothalamus to secrete gonadotropin-releasing hormone
Z) The luteal phase of the ovarian cycle is coordinated with the secretory phase of the uterine cycle
ANSWER: Y) Low levels of estradiol stimulate the hypothalamus to secrete gonadotropin-releasing hormone
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which scientist first proposed the heliocentric model of the solar system?
ANSWER: NICOLAS COPERNICUS (also accept: COPERNICUS)
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What sequence, which resembles a tuning fork, is used as a morphological classification scheme for galaxies?
ANSWER: HUBBLE SEQUENCE (also accept: HUBBLE TUNING FORK DIAGRAM)
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If a fair coin is flipped 7 times, what is the probability that all 7 flips are heads?
ANSWER: 1/128
4. BONUS Math Short Answer How many sides does a regular polygon with 20 diagonals have?
ANSWER: 8 (also accept: OCTAGON)
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Indicating sign, what is the oxidation state of oxygen in hydrogen peroxide?
ANSWER: -1
5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is true?
W) Both CCl4 and CCl2F2 are polar
X) Both CCl4 and CCl2F2 are nonpolar
Y) CCl4 is polar, but CCl2F2 is nonpolar
Z) CCl4 is nonpolar, but CCl2F2 is polar
ANSWER: Z) CCl4 is nonpolar, but CCl2F2 is polar
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at SLAC are testing a new form of cancer radiation therapy that reduces bombardment duration from minutes to under a second. Cancer is often caused by mutations to tumor suppressor genes and what other class of genes?
ANSWER: PROTO-ONCOGENES (do not accept: ONCOGENES)
6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at SLAC are testing a new form of cancer radiation therapy that greatly reduces bombardment duration. Which of the following types of radiation is the most penetrating?
W) Alpha
X) Beta
Y) Gamma
Z) Delta
ANSWER: Y) Gamma
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A truck moving at a constant velocity suddenly breaks with a constant 5000-newton force. If the brakes do 25 kilojoules of work, then how far does the truck travel while braking before it comes to a complete stop?
ANSWER: 5 METERS
7. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A 12-volt battery is connected to three parallel resistors, with resistances of 3, 24, and x ohms. The current through the battery is 6 amps. What is the resistance, x, of the third resistor?
W) 4
X) 8
Y) 12
Z) 16
ANSWER: X) 8
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In eukaryotic messenger RNA, which of the following is always the first codon to be translated?
W) AAA
X) AUG

ANSWER: X) AUG
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of horizontal gene transfer involves the transfer of DNA through bacteriophages?
ANSWER: TRANSDUCTION
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes the small, asteroid sized bodies that are involved in the creation of planets?
ANSWER: PLANETESIMALS
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three epochs of the Cenozoic era from oldest to youngest 1) Oligocene 2) Eocene 3) Pliocene.
ANSWER: 2,1,3
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following numbers has the most positive integer factors?
W) 28
X) 44
Y) 49
Z) 72
ANSWER: Z) 72
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the length of the major axis of the ellipse with equation x2-10 x + 4 y2-8 y = 7?

W) H2

ANSWER: FERMIONS

ANSWER: VIRTUAL PARTICLES

X) Switch
