1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What biochemical cycle, commonly used in plants, utilizes acetate as a source of phosphoenolpyruvate for carbohydrate synthesis?

1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A patient presents to your emergency department with diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA). They have fruity smelling breath with every exhale. In order, what causes the fruit smelling breath, and what causes the acidosis?
W) acetoacetate, β-hydroxybutyrate
X) Acetoacetate, acetone
Y) Acetone, β-hydroxybutyrate
Z) β-hydroxybutyrate, acetoacetate
ANSWER: Y) Acetone, β-hydroxybutyrate

9. BONUS Physics Short Answer A cylindrical capacitor has inner radius a, outer radius b, and length L ≫ b. A cylindrical dielectric shell with constant κ fills the region from radius a to radius c, where a < c < b. Express the capacitance in terms of ε0, κ, a, b, c, and L.
ANSWER: 2πε0L/((1/κ) ln(c/a) + ln(b/c))
