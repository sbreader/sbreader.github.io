1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following chemical compounds in order of increasing covalent character 1. MgO 2. Al2O3 3. SiO2

2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is false regarding strike and dip?
W) A Brunton transit can measure both
X) They are 90 degrees apart from each other
Y) On a geologic map, the number on the symbol is the dip
Z) Strike and dip is typically used for linear features
ANSWER: Z) Strike and dip is typically used for linear features
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Lucas, Filip, and Julian are trying to find a sleeping arrangement at MIT Science Bowl. How many different sleeping arrangements are possible?
ANSWER: 4
