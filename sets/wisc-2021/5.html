TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true about a swinging frictionless pendulum 1) The period is inversely proportional to acceleration due to gravity 2) The pendulum experiences maximum horizontal acceleration in the middle of its swing 3) The length of the rope is directly proportional to the period.
ANSWER: NONE OF THEM
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following properties of a liquid is NOT needed to determine the surface tension of the liquid by using the Eötvös rule?
W) Critical temperature
X) Density
Y) Vapor pressure
Z) Molar mass
ANSWER: Y) Vapor pressure
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What embryonic hormone, secreted in the first trimester, maintains the secretion of progesterone and estrogen by the corpus luteum and is commonly used as the target of the monoclonal antibodies used in pregnancy tests?
ANSWER: HUMAN CHORIONIC GONADOTROPIN (also accept: HCG)
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What specific genetic component has the ability to integrate copies of itself in other parts of the genome with the aid of reverse transcriptase?
ANSWER: RETROTRANSPOSON (do not accept: TRANSPOSON)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the mass limit for neutron stars, past which gravity overcomes the degeneracy pressure and the star collapses into a black hole?
ANSWER: TOLMAN-OPPENHEIMER-VOLKOFF LIMIT (also accept: OPPENHEIMER-VOLKOFF LIMIT; TOV LIMIT)

Y) Enceladus’ south pole has warmer areas corresponding to the geysers

ANSWER: W) Enceladus has a small amount of liquid water near it south pole

6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at UC Davis have developed a tool that can predict whether trees in an area will regrow on their own after a wildfire. What term in ecology refers to the recolonization of a previously occupied land after a major disturbance?
