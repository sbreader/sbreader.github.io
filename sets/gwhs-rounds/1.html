1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A circuit consists of a 10V battery and two resistors in parallel. Which of the following will increase the current in the circuit?
W) Making the two resistors in series.
X) Taking out one of the resistors.
Y) Adding another resistor in parallel with the two resistors.
Z) Adding another resistor in series with one of the resistors.
ANSWER: Y) Adding another resistor in parallel with the two resistors.
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer A circuit consists of a 10 V battery, two resistors in series, and another resistor in parallel with the other two. If one of the resistors in series has a resistance of 2 Ω, the one in parallel has a resistance of 6 Ω, and the current in the circuit is 3 A, find the resistance of the remaining resistor.
ANSWER: 11/2 (also accept: 5.5)
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Laboratory are studying neutron matter, a dense form of matter composed mainly of neutrons with very few protons or electrons. This type of matter can be found in what stellar objects that can result from the death of a massive star?
ANSWER: NEUTRON STAR
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The data generated from the research on neutron matter can help scientists understand neutron star mergers. Which of the following can best be used to detect such a merger?
W) Infrared radiation
X) Gravitational Waves
Y) Microwaves
Z) Ultraviolet radiation
ANSWER: X) Gravitational Waves
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following equations/laws is used to describe the cell voltage of nonstandard cells?
W) Nernst Equation
X) Boyle's Law
Y) Arhennius Equation
Z) Standard Cell Potential Equation
ANSWER: W) Nernst Equation
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The Nernst Equation applies to electrochemical cells. What kind of reaction occurs in these cells?
ANSWER: REDOX
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the viral reproduction cycle that results in the destruction of the infected cell and its membrane?
W) Lysogenic
X) Lytic
Y) Prophase
Z) Translation
ANSWER: X) Lytic
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What glycoprotein does a HIV virus bind with?
ANSWER: CD4 (do not accept: T-CELLS)
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the period of the equation y equals tangent of x?
ANSWER: π
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Order the following 3 functions of x from least to greatest at π/3 1) x 2) tangent of x 3) sine of x
ANSWER: 3,1,2
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a characteristic of O spectral type stars?
W) Ionized helium
X) Strongest hydrogen lines
Y) Blue color
Z) Highest temperature class
ANSWER: X) Strongest hydrogen lines
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer If you were to discover a white star with a surface temperature of 8,000 K, what spectral class would that star be in?
ANSWER: A
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory are using powerful lasers to simulate conditions in supernovae that create shock waves. These shock waves release streams of high-energy particles, which are known as what?
ANSWER: COSMIC RAYS
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a way that supernovae form?
W) A white dwarf reaches a mass lower than the Chandrasaker limit
X) A white dwarf reaches a mass greater than the Chandrasekhar limit
Y) A massive star undergoes a helium flash
Z) A protostar begins fusion
ANSWER: X) A white dwarf reaches a mass greater than the Chandrasekhar limit
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following make up the backbone of fats and oils?
W) Glycerol
X) Glucose
Y) Carboxylic Acid
Z) Phosphate Group
ANSWER: W) Glycerol
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which two amino acids are normally not found in alpha helices?
ANSWER: PROLINE AND GLYCINE
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the term for a conductor that loses its electrical resistance under certain conditions, usually at lower temperatures?
ANSWER: SUPERCONDUCTOR
9. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Room temperature superconductors and their discovery are relevant to the progression of science in which of the following ways?
W) They would allow scientists to use superconductors is environments cooled to near absolute zero
X) They would make the superconductor have less resistance, increasing efficiency
Y) They would allow superconductors to be used in a much wider variety of environments, increasing versatility
Z) They would make the temperature of the room go down
ANSWER: Y) They would allow superconductors to be used in a much wider variety of environments, increasing versatility
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the chemical formula of hexanol?
W) C4H10
X) C6H14
Y) C6H14O
Z) C3H8O
ANSWER: Y) C6H14O

ANSWER: ISOMERS

ANSWER: FRONT

W) Warm Fronts
X) Cold Fronts
Y) Stationary Fronts

13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a condition necessary for Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium?
W) Natural selection does not act on the population
X) The population size is finite
Y) An absence of gene flow
Z) Individuals within the population mate randomly
ANSWER: X) The population size is finite
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer A population is in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium. If 36% of the members are homozygous dominant for a certain allele, what percentage of the population is heterozygous for that allele?
ANSWER: 48%
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Argonne National Laboratory are studying the structure of an enzyme that uses carbon dioxide, water, and sunlight to create a liquid fuel, whose chemical formula is CH3OH. What is the common name for this chemical?
ANSWER: METHANOL
14. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the name for the technique that the researchers used to identify the structure of the enzyme, which is used to determine atomic and molecular structure of a crystallized protein?
ANSWER: X-RAY CRYSTALLOGRAPHY

ANSWER: VOLTAGE DECREASES, ELECTRIC FIELD DECREASES, CHARGE REMAINS CONSTANT
