Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The boundary between a continent and a deep-ocean basin is known as what?
W) Continental margin
X) Continental slope
Y) Continental shelf
Z) Continental boundary
ANSWER: X) Continental slope
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the tendency of a mineral to break along planes of weak bonding?
ANSWER: CLEAVAGE
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not true of minerals?
W) They possess orderly crystalline structures
X) They can be organic
Y) They are solid
Z) They have a definite chemical composition
ANSWER: X) They can be organic
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer To the nearest whole number, what is the Mohs hardness for quartz?
ANSWER: 7
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the fundamental building block of all silicates?
ANSWER: SILICON-OXYGEN TETRAHEDRON
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is mineral name of the compound with the chemical name lead sulfide?
ANSWER: GALENA
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a mineral?
W) Ice
X) Gold
Y) Graphite
Z) Glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate
ANSWER: Z) Glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals displays cleavage in two orthogonal planes?
W) Plagioclase
X) Olivine
Y) Muscovite
Z) Hornblende
ANSWER: W) Plagioclase
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the process whereby lava or magma cools to form igneous rock?
ANSWER: CRYSTALLIZATION
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A rock that has large crystals embedded in a matrix of smaller crystals is said to have what type of texture?
ANSWER: PORPHYRITIC TEXTURE
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What layer of soil is known as the subsoil?
W) O horizon
X) A horizon
Y) E horizon
Z) B horizon
ANSWER: Z) B horizon
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Swelling clay soils are also known as
W) Mollisols
X) Vertisols
Y) Ultisols
Z) Alfisols
ANSWER: X) Vertisols
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the third most abundant element in Earth's crust?
ANSWER: ALUMINUM
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the principal ore of aluminum?
W) Corundum
X) Feldspar
Y) Plagioclase
Z) Bauxite
ANSWER: Z) Bauxite
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In a delta, a river divides into several smaller channels known as
W) Trellises
X) Distributaries
Y) Yazoo tributaries
Z) Dendritic patterns
ANSWER: X) Distributaries
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of dam is used to store floodwater and then let it out slowly?
ANSWER: FLOOD-CONTROL DAM
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer To the nearest whole number, what percent of fresh water is groundwater?
ANSWER: 14%
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The water table is the upper limit of what zone?
ANSWER: ZONE OF SATURATION
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The area of the water table is known as what?
W) Zone of impermeability
X) Zone of differentiation
Y) Zone of aeration
Z) Zone of anaeration
ANSWER: Y) Zone of aeration
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Impermeable layers, such as clay, that hinder or prevent the movement of water are known as what?
ANSWER: AQUITARDS
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the term for the percentage of the total volume of rock or sediment that consists of pore spaces?
W) Permeability
X) Porosity
Y) Infiltration
Z) Saturation
ANSWER: X) Porosity
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The source of an earthquake is known as the
W) Center
X) Epicenter
Y) Locus
Z) Focus
ANSWER: Z) Focus
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Small earthquakes that precede a major earthquake are known as what?
ANSWER: FORESHOCKS
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Who proposed the theory of continental drift in his book The Origin of Continents and Oceans?
ANSWER: ALFRED WEGENER (also accept: WEGENER)
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The supercontinent that contained all the earth's landmass is known as Pangea. What is the global ocean that existed at the same time Pangea did?
W) Panthea
X) Panmaria
Y) Marialassa
Z) Panthalassa
ANSWER: Z) Panthalassa
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the general term for dissolved gases held under high pressure in magma?
ANSWER: VOLATILES
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Continued eruption from a flank eruption in a volcano can produce a
W) Parasitic cone
X) Fumarole
Y) Caldera
Z) Crater
ANSWER: W) Parasitic cone
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the opposite of an anticline?
ANSWER: A SYNCLINE
16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Faults in which the movement is primarily parallel to the inclination of the fault surface are known as
W) Strike-slip faults
X) Dip-slip faults
Y) Transform faults
Z) Divergent faults
ANSWER: X) Dip-slip faults
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The concept of a floating crust in gravitational balance is called
W) Adjustment
X) Continental buoyancy
Y) Isostasy
Z) Gravitational equilibrium
ANSWER: Y) Isostasy
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the scientific term for mountain-building?
ANSWER: OROGENESIS
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Who proposed the theory of uniformitarianism?
W) Lyell
X) Hutton
Y) Lamarck
Z) Ussher
ANSWER: X) Hutton
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the theory that states that Earth's landscapes have been primarily developed by great catastrophies?
ANSWER: CATASTROPHISM
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Discomformities and noncomformities are two types of
W) Unconformities
X) Inclusions
Y) Exclusions
Z) Angularities
ANSWER: W) Unconformities
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The matching up rocks of similar age in different regions is known as what?
ANSWER: CORRELATION
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Name the bowl-shaped depression at the head of a glacier.
W) Arete
X) Cirque
Y) Glacial trough
Z) Horn
ANSWER: X) Cirque
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Ridges of material along the side of a valley after a glacier leaves are known as what?
ANSWER: LATERAL MORAINES
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What era of geologic time is also known as the Age of Reptiles?
ANSWER: MESOZOIC ERA
21. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not true of the Cenozoic Era?
W) Foraminifera became important
X) Angiosperms experienced rapid development
Y) Mammals became dominant
Z) Archaeopteryx lived during this era
ANSWER: Z) Archaeopteryx lived during this era
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What often forms on the leeward side of a mountain and is characterized by a dry and arid climate?
ANSWER: RAIN SHADOW DESERT
22. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In the latitude zone poleward of the wet tropics and equatorward of the subtropical deserts lies a transitional climatic region called
W) Tropical wet and dry
X) Arid
Y) Semiarid
Z) Steppe
ANSWER: W) Tropical wet and dry
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the study of measuring the depth of ocean and mapping its topography?
ANSWER: BATHYMETRY
23. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of sonar was developed after World War II by the US Navy and can be used to find mines?
W) Ping
X) Echo Sounder
Y) Sidescan
Z) High-resolution multibeam
ANSWER: Y) Sidescan
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What scale places hurricanes in 5 different number categories?
W) Fujita Scale
X) Saffir-Simpson Scale
Y) Beedle-Tatum Scale
Z) Avery Scale
ANSWER: X) Saffir-Simpson Scale
24. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A tornado with a wind speed of 500 kilometers per hour has a Fujita scale reading of what?
ANSWER: F5
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A dome of water 65 to 80 kilometers wide that sweeps across the coast where a hurricane's eye makes landfall is known as what?
ANSWER: STORM SURGE
25. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A cyclone with wind speeds between 61 and 119 kilometers is known as a
W) Tropical depression
X) Tropical storm
Y) Level 1 hurricane
Z) Level 2 hurricane
ANSWER: X) Tropical storm
