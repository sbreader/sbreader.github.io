1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Some caterpillars develop large eye spots and an appendage that mimics a snake's head and smell, deterring bird predators. This is an example of what type of mimicry, where harmless organisms evolve to resemble dangerous or toxic species to avoid predation?
ANSWER: batesian mimicry
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice During which of the following phases of Meiosis do the homologous chromosomes separate?
W) Metaphase I
X) Anaphase I
Y) Metaphase II
Z) Anaphase II
ANSWER: X) Anaphase I
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Consider the reaction between methane and oxygen gas to form carbon dioxide and water. Which of the following is the reducing agent in this reaction?
W) methane
X) oxygen gas
Y) water
Z) carbon dioxide
ANSWER: W) methane
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 quantities that are path functions 1) internal energy 2) heat 3) work
ANSWER: 2 and 3
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following angles in degrees maximizes the velocity of a ball when it is thrown?
W) 30
X) 45
Y) 60
Z) the velocity is independent of the angle
ANSWER: Z) the velocity is independent of the angle
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Order the following 3 positively charged quarks in increasing mass 1) up 2) top 3) charm
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly describes Hubble’s law?
W) galaxies at a greater distance are moving away faster than galaxies closer to Earth
X) galaxies at a greater distance are moving away slower than galaxies closer to Earth
Y) stars at a greater distance are moving away faster than stars closer to Earth
Z) stars at a greater distance are moving away slower than stars closer to Earth
ANSWER: W) galaxies at a greater distance are moving away faster than galaxies closer to Earth

ANSWER: 1, 3, 2

12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying high-energy particles. The muons that hit the Earth result from the decay of pions produced when what high-energy particles collide with atoms in the upper atmosphere?
ANSWER: cosmic rays
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice During the industrial revolution, moths with peppered colors began to survive more than pure white moths. Over time, peppered moths became more prevalent than pure white moths. Which of the following types of selection does this display?
W) Disruptive
X) Stabilizing
Y) Directional
Z) Balancing
ANSWER: Y) Directional
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer In the teacher’s lounge, Rehan notices that when he places an overripe banana next to an underripe banana, the underripe banana will become ripe after a day. This phenomenon is due to the release of what gaseous plant hormone?
ANSWER: Ethylene
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In addition to using standard enthalpies of formation and bond enthalpies, the enthalpy change for a given reaction can be calculated by applying what law, which states that the total enthalpy change for the overall reaction is the sum of the enthalpy change values for each step?
ANSWER: Hess’s law
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer There is a galvanic cell in which the Ag+ to Ag half-reaction has a standard reduction potential of +0.80 volts, and the Cu2+ ion to Cu half-reaction has a standard reduction potential of +0.34 volts. In volts to 2 decimal places, what is the standard cell potential of this galvanic cell?
ANSWER: +0.46
