Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In most insects, what excretory system carries out regulation of hemolymph and eliminates nitrogenous wastes?
ANSWER: MALPIGHIAN TUBULES (also accept: MALPIGHIAN TUBULE SYSTEM)
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the common promoter sequence found in most eukaryotes, responsible for the formation of the transcription initiation complex.
ANSWER: TATA BOX (also accept: GOLDBERG-HOGNESS BOX)
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Big Bob, a sumo wrestler with mass 100 kg is moving to the left at 5 m/s and collides with tiny Tim, who is initially at rest. If the collision can be modelled as perfectly inelastic, and their final velocity is 4 m/s to the left, what is the mass of tiny Tim?
ANSWER: 25 kg
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer A ball of mass 10 kg is stationary inside a box of mass 40 kg (without friction) and side length 1 m, which is also initially at rest. At time t equals 0, a force is applied to the ball, giving it a velocity of 10 m/s. Every time the ball bounces off of the box, one sixteenth of the total mechanical energy of the system is lost in the form of heat. Assume friction is negligible throughout the problem. To the nearest ten meters, what is the displacement of the small ball at t = 5 minutes?
ANSWER: 600 m
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What type of reagent can be added to a ketone to form a tertiary alcohol, and is characterized by containing Magnesium and a halogen?
ANSWER: GRIGNARD REAGENT
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds DOES NOT form ionic bonds?
W) MgBr2
X) AlN
Y) AlCl3
Z) NaI
ANSWER: Y) AlCl3
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What important theorem of calculus relates definite integrals to antiderivatives?
ANSWER: FUNDAMENTAL THEOREM OF CALCULUS (also accept: FTC)
4. BONUS Math Short Answer The point P at (3,2) is reflected across the line y-2x=1 to point P’. What are the coordinates of P’?
ANSWER: (-1,4)
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, arrange the following 3 cloud types in increasing order of the altitude at which they form: Altocumulus, cirrus, stratus
ANSWER: 3,1,2
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 3 processes that lift air 1. Orographic lifting 2. Frontal wedging 3. Convergence
ANSWER: All (also accept: 1,2,3)
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is the closest to the percentage of energy produced in the United States that is derived from nuclear energy?
W) 5%
X) 10%
Y) 15%
Z) 20%
ANSWER: Z) 20%
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the mass number of most common isotope that is used in nuclear power plants?
ANSWER: 235
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What pathway of DNA repair incorporates enzymes known as DNA glycosylases, which utilize a method of “flipping-out” to remove errors in strands?
W) Mismatch Repair
X) Non-homologous End Joining
Y) Base Excision Repair
Z) Nucleotide Excision Repair
ANSWER: Y) Base Excision Repair
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which follicular organ located in the basilar membrane contains mechanoreceptors responsible for sound transduction?
ANSWER: ORGAN OF CORTI
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What theorem states that the average kinetic energy of an orbital system equals the negative of half the average potential energy?
ANSWER: Virial theorem
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the term for the velocity an object must attain for it to leave the gravitational field of the solar system?
ANSWER: Third cosmic velocity
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What equation relates the pH of a solution to the pKa plus the logarithm of the ratio of the concentration of the conjugate base to the concentration of the acid?
ANSWER: HENDERSON HASSELBALCH EQUATION
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What law states that a cyclic, planar molecule is aromatic if it has 4n+2 delocalized π electrons?
ANSWER: HUCKEL’S RULE
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Evaluate the 15th derivative of cos(x) dx.
ANSWER: sin(x)
10. BONUS Math Short Answer When does the function x1/x reach its maximum value, assuming x is greater than 0?
ANSWER: e (also accept: EULER’S CONSTANT)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following rocks would be classified as felsic?
W) Gabbro
X) Rhyolite
Y) Andesite
Z) Diorite
ANSWER: X) Rhyolite
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not an iron ore mineral?
W) Hematite
X) Magnetite
Y) Bauxite
Z) Limonite
ANSWER: Y) Bauxite
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Ecosystems tend to have large transfers of energy, when organisms consume each other and are consumed in turn. Approximately what percent of energy is transferred from one trophic level to the next?
W) 5%
X) 10%
Y) 25%
Z) 50%
ANSWER: X) 10%
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer What hydrocarbon is defined to have both a Research Octane Number and Motor Octane Number of 0?
ANSWER: Heptane (also accept: n-heptane)
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not involved in the formation of a blood clot?
W) Christmas factor
X) Fibrin
Y) Heparin
Z) Thrombin
ANSWER: Y) Heparin
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 receptors that are NOT pattern recognition receptors 1) Toll like receptors 2) C3A receptors 3) C-type lectin receptors 4) NOD-like receptors
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Heisenberg’s uncertainty principle states that the product of the uncertainties of which two physical quantities is equal to half the reduced Planck constant?
ANSWER: Momentum and position
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify Name all of the following that are state functions: Pressure, heat, work, enthalpy.
ANSWER: Pressure and enthalpy
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What experiment used silver atoms to determine that angular momentum is quantized?
W) Geiger-Marsden experiment
X) Stern-Gerlach experiment
Y) Millikan Oil Drop experiment
Z) Rutherford gold foil experiment
ANSWER: X) Stern-Gerlach experiment
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the only optically active isomer of C4H9Cl?
ANSWER: 2-Chlorobutane
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Titu’s lemma is a direct consequence of what famous mathematical inequality, named after a man who helped pioneer the field of complex analysis?
ANSWER: Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality
16. BONUS Math Short Answer Expressed as a common fraction, what is the circumradius of a triangle with sides 13, 14, and 15?
ANSWER: 65/8
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the long grooves left at the bottom of glaciers by large moving rock fragments?
ANSWER: Glacial striations
17. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a feature left behind by a retreating glacier?
W) Guyot
X) Kettles
Y) Drumlin fields
Z) Eskers
ANSWER: W) Guyot
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, order the following coal types in increasing carbon content: Bituminous, lignite, peat, anthracite
ANSWER: Peat, lignite, bituminous, anthracite
18. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the name of the grey, hard, and porous fuel, derived from coal or petroleum, that has high carbon content and is often used to smelt iron ore?
ANSWER: Coke
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What vitamin is a part of acetyl coenzyme A?
ANSWER: VITAMIN B5 (also accept: PANTOTHENIC ACID)
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer In the RAAS pathway, what protein promotes the secretion of aldosterone, playing a key role in blood pressure regulation?
ANSWER: ANGIOTENSIN (also accept: ANGIOTENSIN II)
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What theorem or law gives the relationship between the rate of change in magnetic flux and the induced current?
ANSWER: Faraday’s Law
20. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 10 volt battery is hooked up to a circuit with a resistor. The current through this wire is 5 Amperes. If the width of the resistor is doubled and the length is also doubled, what is the new current through the resistor?
ANSWER: 10 A
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the carbonyl condensation reaction that occurs when two esters are treated with a base such as sodium ethoxide?
ANSWER: Claisen Condensation
21. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following acids in terms of increasing strength: HMnO4, H2SO4, H2SO3
ANSWER: H2SO3, H2SO4, HMnO4 (also accept: 3, 2, 1)
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many numbers less than 100 are relatively prime to 100?
ANSWER: 40
22. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the remainder when 1222 is divided by 10?
ANSWER: 4
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What crystal structure has 3 axes of unequal length and unequal intersection angles?
ANSWER: TRICLINIC
23. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a reason to not use the mineral streak test?
W) The mineral color is very light
X) The mineral color is very dark
Y) The mineral is very soft or brittle
Z) The mineral is harder than the streak plate
ANSWER: Z) The mineral is harder than the streak plate
