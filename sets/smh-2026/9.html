X) Always decreasing

4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Consider a molecule of oxygen gas. The bond dissociation energy of O2 is 498 kJ/mol. 5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following gives the best explanation of the reason why the east coast of the United States sees much greater barrier island prevalence compared to the west coast?
