1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, indicate which 2 of the following 4 phylums are deuterostomes: 1: Arthropoda 2: Chordata 3: Mollusca 4: Echinodermata

2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In which of the following ideal expansion processes is dQ=0, meaning that no heat is transferred to or from the fluid?
W) Isothermal
X) Isobaric
Y) Adiabatic
Z) Constant-Volume
ANSWER: Y) Adiabatic

ANSWER: All of them

W) circle
X) ellipse
Y) parabola
Z) hyperbola

X) Cray-1
Y) EDSAC

ANSWER: Y) EDSAC

17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements best explains the principle of magnetic braking in some rotors, roller coasters, and Telma retarders?
W) The target and the brake are oppositely charged ferromagnets
X) The brake is an electromagnet that activates behind the target
Y) Eddy currents generated within the target induce an opposing magnetic field
Z) The brake electromagnetically draws the target towards it, causing frictional forces
ANSWER: Y) Eddy currents generated within the target induce an opposing magnetic field
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 4 galactic locations that contain a majority of Population I stars: 1: core of a large elliptical galaxy 2: core of a spiral galaxy 3: arms of a spiral galaxy 4: globular cluster of a galaxy halo

W) if Q, then P
X) if not P, then not Q
Y) if not Q, then P

X) polyphyletic
