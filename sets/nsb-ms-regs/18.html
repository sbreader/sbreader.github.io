1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the most common term for the energy an object has because of its position or configuration?
ANSWER: POTENTIAL
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Which one of the four basic forces of the universe is most directly involved with maintaining the planets in their orbits?
ANSWER: GRAVITY
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Based on size and location, which of the 2 basic types of glaciers dominates Greenland today?
ANSWER: CONTINENTAL (also accept: ICE SHEET)
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following fossils is the oldest:
W) trilobite
X) snake
Y) petrified wood
Z) megalodon tooth
ANSWER: W) trilobite
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the measure, in degrees, of each angle in an equilateral triangle?
ANSWER: 60 (also accept: 60, 60, 60)
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following can sometimes result after a moderate to severe blow to the head:
W) Down syndrome
X) meningitis
Y) impetigo
Z) concussion
ANSWER: Z) concussion
4. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the year of a person's death who was born in the year 580 BC and lived 63 years?
ANSWER: 517 BC (also accept: 517)
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many limbs are tetrapods typically considered to have?
ANSWER: 4
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the most common source of energy for brain cells in humans:
W) nucleic acids
X) proteins
Y) glucose
Z) pectins
ANSWER: Y) glucose
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the most common term for the ratio of the output force to the input force of a simple machine?
ANSWER: MECHANICAL ADVANTAGE (also accept: ACTUAL MECHANICAL ADVANTAGE)
6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following most directly contributes to the actual mechanical advantage always being less than the ideal mechanical advantage:
W) the basic design of any machine
X) friction
Y) how simple machines are combined
Z) the input distance always being less than the output distance
ANSWER: X) friction
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a mineral:
W) quartz
X) topaz
Y) basalt
Z) diamond
ANSWER: Y) basalt
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the hottest region of planet Earth:
W) the mantle
X) the inner core
Y) the outer core
Z) the crust
ANSWER: X) the inner core
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Find the volume, in inches cubed, of a rectangular solid whose dimensions are 3-inches by 5-inches by 8-inches:
ANSWER: 120
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the kelvin temperature that represents a condition of no molecular motion?
ANSWER: 0 (also accept: ABSOLUTE ZERO)
9. BONUS Energy Short Answer How many grams are in 1 metric ton?
ANSWER: 1 MILLION (also accept: 1 × 106)
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the ultimate energy source for nearly all life on Earth?
ANSWER: THE SUN
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the environmental factor most directly involved in phototropism?
ANSWER: LIGHT (also accept: SUNLIGHT; SUN)
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice If a chimpanzee were to throw darts at a Periodic Table, he would most likely hit an element with which of the following characteristics:
W) a naturally radioactive element
X) a solid at room temperature
Y) a gas at room temperature
Z) a liquid at room temperature
ANSWER: X) a solid at room temperature
11. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following has the most inertia:
W) 1 kilogram of iron
X) 2 kilograms of flour
Y) 5 newtons of iron
Z) 4 kilograms of iron
ANSWER: Z) 4 kilograms of iron
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the BEST example of the chemical weathering of rock:
W) cracking of rock by plant roots
X) rain percolating through limestone
Y) frost action
Z) abrasion
ANSWER: X) rain percolating through limestone
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the "river" of high altitude wind over North America that marks the southern boundary between polar air to the north and warmer air to the south?
ANSWER: POLAR JET STREAM (also accept: JET STREAM)
13. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Minuends and subtrahends are used in which of the following mathematical operations:
W) addition
X) subtraction
Y) multiplication
Z) division
ANSWER: X) subtraction
13. BONUS Math Short Answer If the area of a square field is 1,600 square feet, how many feet long is one of its sides?
ANSWER: 40
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the general name for a plastic material composed of a polymer of styrene?
ANSWER: POLYSTYRENE (also accept: STYROFOAM)
14. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is a product found in most toothpastes for the purpose of making the teeth more resistant to decay by promoting remineralization:
W) fluoride
X) melamine
Y) titanium dioxide
Z) diethylene glycol
ANSWER: W) fluoride
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the scientific name of the human windpipe?
ANSWER: TRACHEA
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer The organ of Corti is directly involved in what human sense?
ANSWER: HEARING
16. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is TRUE regarding mixing pigments:
W) violet is typically considered a primary color for pigments
X) only primary pigment colors can be mixed to give other colors
Y) as more pigments are mixed, the darker the resulting mixture becomes
Z) magenta and cyan are complementary pigment colors
ANSWER: Y) as more pigments are mixed, the darker the resulting mixture becomes
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer What color will a red colored object appear to a human if it is illuminated by a red light?
ANSWER: RED

X) specific gravity

ANSWER: WATER (also accept: H2O)

ANSWER: MERCURY; VENUS

ANSWER: 80

ANSWER: CELLULOSE
