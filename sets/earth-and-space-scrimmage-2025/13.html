1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer After a massive star enters the asymptotic giant branch post helium flash, fusion products are often dredged up to the surface and can be clearly detected in the stellar spectrum, creating what type of star?
ANSWER: Carbon star
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Pluto and its largest moon, Charon, are tidally locked with each other. Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding the Pluto-Charon system 1) Pluto is always closer to their barycenter 2) Pluto and Charon have the same synodic period relative to each other 3) Pluto and Charon have the same sidereal period relative to each other
ANSWER: All
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following 3 minerals from innermost to outermost if all three minerals are euhedral in a metamorphic porphyroblast 1) Garnet 2) Rutile 3) Chlorite
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What optical phenomenon describes the faint emission of light due to atmospheric processes, causing the night sky to never be completely dark?
ANSWER: Airglow (also accept: nightglow)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A Sersic profile plots the intensity of a galaxy against the distance from its center, with a high Sersic index corresponding to a steeper decay profile near the center of the galaxy. Which of the following types of galaxies generally exhibits the highest Sersic index?
W) Massive elliptical galaxy
X) Low mass elliptical galaxy
Y) Massive spiral galaxy
Z) Low mass spiral galaxy
ANSWER: W) Massive elliptical galaxy
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following components from innermost to outermost of a rotating black hole 1) Corotating photon sphere 2) Counterrotating photon sphere 3) Ergosphere
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The D’’ layer is located closest to which of the following seismic discontinuities?
W) Repetti
X) Moho
Y) Lehmann
Z) Gutenberg
ANSWER: Z) Gutenberg
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, rank the following characteristics of seawater in a typical ocean basin in order of increasing depth at which their maximum value occurs 1) chlorophyll a concentration 2) nitrate concentration 3) temperature
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Tonalite can be distinguished from granite by an abundance in which of the following minerals instead of alkali feldspar?
W) Augite
X) Plagioclase
Y) Quartz
Z) Zircon
ANSWER: X) Plagioclase
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Arcus clouds are commonly identified along the leading edge of storms with deep convection systems. Identify all of the following that are types of arcus clouds 1) Scarf 2) Shelf 3) Roll 4) Wall
ANSWER: 2 and 3
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 which are expected to occur during a Martian dust storm 1) Increased albedo 2) Reduced surface temperatures 3) Reduced upper atmospheric temperatures
ANSWER: 1 and 2
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three effects by increasing strength on a non-rotating pebble-sized space rock orbiting the Sun 1) Poynting-Robertson drag 2) Radiation pressure 3) Yarkovsky effect
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice On average, which of the following types of volcanoes would one expect to score the lowest on the VEI?
W) Cinder cone
X) Tuff ring
Y) Spatter cone
Z) Stratoconoe
ANSWER: X) Tuff ring
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What mineral is most commonly formed as a product of the contact metamorphism involving dolomite and silica-rich fluids?
ANSWER: Wollastonite
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In fluvial environments, what riverbed feature is formed from supercritical flow where sediments are deposited upstream and eroded downstream of a dune?
ANSWER: Antidune
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Modal layering in certain layered mafic intrusions causes them to resemble which of the following rock types in texture and structure?
W) Biochemical sedimentary rocks
X) Clastic sedimentary rocks
Y) Compositional metamorphic rocks
Z) Extensional metamorphic rocks
ANSWER: X) Clastic sedimentary rocks
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which normative mineral indicates silica undersaturation in a CIPW calculation?
W) Quartz
X) Nepheline
Y) Orthoclase
Z) Olivine
ANSWER: X) Nepheline
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following levels in the troposphere which are generally radar bright 1) Lifting condensation level 2) Melting level 3) Tropopause
ANSWER: 2 only
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What band-shaped feature in the sky causes distortions and data gaps in all-sky surveys like 2MASS by obscuring background objects?
ANSWER: Zone of Avoidance
10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice RR Lyrae variables often pulsate on timescales much shorter than many other radial pulsators of similar radius and composition. In terms of wave behavior in a stratified stellar envelope, which factor most directly explains these shortened periods?
W) A reduction in group velocity of the acoustic mode due to partial ionization zones
X) A decrease in the wave crossing time due to excitation of higher overtones
Y) Suppression of fundamental mode driving by radiative damping in the stellar core
Z) Coupling between acoustic and gravity waves in the He II ionization zone
ANSWER: X) A decrease in the wave crossing time due to excitation of higher overtones
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Saros cycle, used to describe solar and lunar eclipses, has a period defined by the time between which the eclipse patterns begin to repeat themselves. Half of a Saros is known as a sar. In synodic months, which of the following is closest to the length of a sar?
W) 111.5
X) 222.5
Y) 333.5
Z) 444.5
ANSWER: W) 111.5
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The heaviest natural elements are primarily created through which of the following processes?
W) Core collapse supernovae
X) Thermonuclear supernovae
Y) s processes in the core of asymptotic giant stars
Z) Neutron star mergers
ANSWER: Z) Neutron star mergers
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes how increasing lithospheric thickness and decreasing mantle viscosity affect foreland basin geometry and subsidence timing?
W) Basins become narrower and deeper, subsidence occurs more slowly
X) Basins become broader and shallower; subsidence occurs more rapidly
Y) Basins become narrower and deeper, subsidence occurs more rapidly
Z) Basins become broader and deeper; subsidence occurs more slowly
ANSWER: X) Basins become broader and shallower; subsidence occurs more rapidly
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following explains why a simple ternary solid-solution series cannot be used to describe apatite?
W) Size and charge mismatch among substituents
X) Lack of isomorphic sites
Y) Rapid diffusion during formation prevents ordering
Z) High-temperature crystallization prevents solid solution
ANSWER: W) Size and charge mismatch among substituents
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Brunt–Väisälä frequency is the frequency at which a parcel of air will oscillate at a stable boundary. Identify all of the following which are true of the Brunt–Väisälä frequency 1) Cyclogenesis is more likely at higher frequencies 2) Higher wind shear increases the frequency 3) It is imaginary in an unstable atmosphere
ANSWER: 3 only
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding phacoliths 1) They are discordant intrusions 2) They can accumulate along either synclines or anticlines 3) They tend to be more enriched in rare-earth elements than their surrounding
ANSWER: 2 and 3
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the least accounted for by the Lambda-CDM model?
W) Cosmic microwave background
X) Accelerated expansion of the universe
Y) Hot dark matter
Z) Abundance of hydrogen, helium and lithium in the universe
ANSWER: Y) Hot dark matter
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What theoretical remnant is left behind by a Type Iax supernova, when only half of the white dwarf’s mass is lost in the explosion?
ANSWER: Zombie star
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following which would result if Atlantic meridional overturning circulation were slowed 1) Increase in north Atlantic sea surface salinity 2) Decrease in north Atlantic sea surface temperatures 3) Shrinking of Arctic sea ice
ANSWER: 2 only
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Tropopause folding occurs when stratospheric air intrudes into the troposphere near jet stream troughs. Identify all of the following three effects that are likely to occur due to tropopause folding 1) Transport of ozone-rich, dry stratospheric air into the upper troposphere 2) Detectable Increase in surface ultraviolet radiation due to ozone column variations 3) Intrusion of dry parcels of air inducing convection
ANSWER: 1 and 3
