1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Studded with proteins such as cytochromes and ATP synthase, what is the term for the internal compartments of mitochondria that increase the surface area available for cellular respiration?
ANSWER: cristae
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is FALSE regarding mitochondria?
W) Because of the long evolutionary lineage of mitochondria, its proteome is relatively static between similar species
X) There is a near-absence of genetic recombination in mitochondrial DNA
Y) mitochondrial disease is very prevelant among various pathologies, such as schizophrenia or Alzheimer's disease
Z) mDNA is quite small, only encoding about 37 genes in humans
ANSWER: W) Because of the long evolutionary lineage of mitochondria, its proteome is relatively static between similar species
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A planet's escape velocity is v at its surface. At a height off the ground equal to the radius of the planet, what is the new escape velocity in terms of v?
ANSWER: v √2/2
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, indicate which 2 of the following 4 minerals exhibit perfect octahedral cleavage, designated as {1 1 1}: 1: quartz 2: diamond 3: fluorite 4: halite
ANSWER: 2 and 3
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Mineral cleavage is often designated by a sequence of 3 numbers that indicate the planes and direction of cleavage. For example, octahedral cleavage is designated {1 1 1}. What is the name of this notation system?
ANSWER: Miller index
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, indicate which 2 of the following 4 organic compound groups have significant electron delocalization: 1: n-alkane 2: carboxylate ion 3: diols 4: phenols
ANSWER: 2 and 4
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many numbers from 1 to 200, inclusively, are the equal to the cube of an integer?
ANSWER: 5
5. BONUS Math Short Answer A square is inscribed in a circle with radius r. What is the probability that a randomly selected point within the circle will NOT be within the square? Expess your answer in terms of pi.
ANSWER: (pi-2)/pi
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Modern generators all owe their mechanics to what law of electromagnetism, that states that an induced voltage will be proportional to the change in magnetic flux?
ANSWER: Faraday's Law
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 3 characteristics can usually be found in monotremes: 1: possession of a cloaca 2: three middle ear bones 3: no corpus callosum
ANSWER: all of them
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A wire has a current running through it. At a point with a perpendicular distance R away from the wire, the magnetic field is measured to be B. In terms of B, what is the magnetic field of a point at 4R of the perpendicular distance away from the wire?
ANSWER: B/4
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer What theorem in differential geometry is a statement about the integration of differential forms on manifolds, thus allowing one to express Maxwell's equations in both integral and differential form?
ANSWER: Stokes' theorem
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Jupiter possesses 4 distinct rings. The inner two are known as the halo ring and the main ring. What is the term for the outer two rings, which are very thick and faint?
ANSWER: gossamer rings
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 4 reactions that would have positive standard electrode potentials: 1: reduction of Sodium 2: reduction of Gold 3: oxidation of Chlorine 4: oxidation of Zinc
ANSWER: 2 and 4
10. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following quantities will change the LEAST in an electrochemical cell, if the quantity of a reactant is doubled
W) Gibbs free energy change
X) maximum electrical work
Y) cell current
Z) cell emf
ANSWER: Z) cell emf
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A rectangle has a perimeter of p. If the width of the rectangle is 2/3 of its length, express its length in terms of p.
ANSWER: 3p/10
12. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following isotopes is used the MOST as the fuel for fast breeder reactors?
W) U-235
X) U-238
Y) Pu-239
Z) Np-237
ANSWER: X) U-238
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer Another alternative in breeder reactors involves creating fissile U-233 from the fertile, abundant 232-isotope of which element?
ANSWER: Thorium
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 4 groups of plants that possess vascular systems for water and nutrient transport: 1: bryophytes 2: ferns 3: angiosperms 4: gymnosperms
ANSWER: 2,3,4
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In theoretical physics, what theory extends the study of Yang-Mills theory for quarks, by describing the interactions between quarks and gluons via the strong nuclear force, and postulates the two very peculiar properties known as confinement and asymptotic freedom?
ANSWER: Quantum chromodynamics
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer During a flood, the velocity of a river triples and its discharge quadruples. Compared to the original levels, by what factor does the competence of the river change?
ANSWER: 9 times
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following processes may increase the base level of a river?
W) constructing a dam
X) uplifting the adjacent ground
Y) increasing water flow via diversion
Z) dredging sediment from the riverbed
ANSWER: W) constructing a dam
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name of number, indicate which 2 of the following 4 quantities MUST remain constant if one wishes to apply Boyle's Law: 1: temperature 2: pressure 3: volume 4: number of moles of gas
ANSWER: 1 and 4
17. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A basketball team had a win-loss ratio of 3:1. After the team won six games in a row, its win-loss ratio became 5:1. How many games did the team win BEFORE winning their last six games in a row?
W) 6
X) 9
Y) 15
Z) 24
ANSWER: X) 9
17. BONUS Math Short Answer A square has its four vertices located at (0,5), (5,4), (4,-1), and (-1,0). Expressing your answer in simplest radical form, what is the area of this square?
ANSWER: 26 units2
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Living things are often classified by how they obtain their energy. What is the term for organisms that do not fix carbon, yet obtain their energy from light, such as various purple and green bacteria?
W) photoautotroph
X) chemoautotroph
Y) photoheterotroph
Z) chemoheterotroph
ANSWER: Y) photoheterotroph
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is a process of chemoheterotrophic extra-cellular digestion involved in the processing of dead or decayed organic matter which usually occurs in fungi, assisted via active transport of materials into hyphae?
ANSWER: saprotrophic nutrition
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In plants, this process begins with the formation of the cell plate, and in animals, the cleavage furrow. It is usually symmetrical, other than the notable exception of oogenesis, where the ovum takes almost all of the original cytoplasm. Found in both mitosis and meiosis, for four points, name the process that occurs at the end of cell replication, the eukaryotic equivalent to binary fission.
ANSWER: cytokinesis
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In general relativity, what is the term for a location where the quantities that are used to measure the gravitational field become infinite in a way that does not depend on the coordinate system, the most notable of which are the initial state of the universe before the Big Bang, and black holes?
ANSWER: singularity
20. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice In general relativity, which general solution or metric describes the gravitational field outside a spherical, non-rotating mass, where the cosmological constant is assumed to equal zero? Examples of applications include slowly rotating bodies like the Earth or Sun, or chargeless, non-rotating black holes.
W) Schwarzschild
X) Godel
Y) Kerr
Z) Einstein
ANSWER: W) Schwarzschild
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the astronomical term for the distance within which a celestial body, held together only by its own gravity, will disintegrate due to a second celestial body's tidal forces exceeding the first body's gravitational self-attraction?
ANSWER: Roche limit
