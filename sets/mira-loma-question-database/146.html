Source packet

1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Each month, the moon crosses the ecliptic at two points. What are these points called?
ANSWER: NODES
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What are boundaries where tectonic plates form subduction zones called?
W) Divergent
X) Convergent
Y) Transform
Z) Strike-slip
ANSWER: X) Convergent
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following plates that border the South American plate: Cocos plate, Nazca plate, Scotia plate, Caribbean plate
ANSWER: NAZCA PLATE, SCOTIA PLATE, CARIBBEAN PLATE
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about classification of stars is not true?
W) M type stars have strong titanium oxide spectral lines
X) Canopus is an F0 type star
Y) The approximate temperature of an O type star is 40,000 Kelvin
Z) A type stars have weak hydrogen Balmer lines
ANSWER: Z) A type stars have weak hydrogen Balmer lines
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer If the surface temperature of a star is 6000 Kelvin, what is the value, in nanometers, of the wavelength of maximum intensity?
ANSWER: 500 NANOMETERS
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are small hills of sand and gravel that are dumped at or near the edge of glacial ice called?
ANSWER: KAMES
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name three of the four mechanisms that are responsible for glacial ablation.
ANSWER: ANY 3 OF: MELTING, CALVING, SUBLIMATION, WIND EROSION
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Most of the energy released by a slowing neutron star is in the form of a flow of high speed particles called
ANSWER: PULSAR WIND
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Neutron stars often undergo a sudden increase in the pulse rate. What is this phenomenon called?
ANSWER: GLITCH
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which of the following environmental conditions would you most expect to find a bedrock of quartz?
W) Low acidity
X) High rainfall
Y) Long periods of weathering
Z) High dissolution of minerals
ANSWER: W) Low acidity
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Increased weathering causes a layer of heterogeneous material overlying the bedrock called
ANSWER: REGOLITH
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of sand dunes describes crescent-shaped dunes whose two crescent horns point downwind?
W) Barchan
X) Transverse
Y) Blowout
Z) Linear
ANSWER: W) Barchan
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What term is used to describe wind-faceted pebbles that have several curved or almost flat surfaces that meet at sharp ridges?
ANSWER: VENTIFACTS
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Of the following four types of meteorites, which accounts for the least percent of the meteorites that fall to the Earth?
W) Chondrites
X) Achondrites
Y) Stony-iron
Z) Iron
ANSWER: Y) Stony-iron
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The lunar surface contains long, winding channels that are often found near the edges of maria. These channels were evidently cut by flowing lava. What are these channels called?
ANSWER: SINUOUS RILLES
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the type of seafloor sediment that consists of minerals that crystallize directly from seawater through various chemical reactions.
ANSWER: HYDROGENEOUS SEDIMENT
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Manganese nodules contain manganese and iron, yet they are mined primarily because they are enriched with three other elements. Name any two of these three elements.
ANSWER: ANY 2 OF: NICKEL, COBALT, COPPER
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Of Uranus's five major moons, which is made primarily of ice?
ANSWER: MIRANDA
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Miranda's surface contains oval-shaped features which may have been caused by upwellings of warm material below the surface. Such features also exist on Venus. In planetary geology, what are these features called?
ANSWER: CORONA
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer After examining the 1906 San Francisco earthquake, H.F. Reid of Johns Hopkins University formulated a theory that explained the mechanism behind earthquakes. What was the name of this theory?
ANSWER: ELASTIC REBOUND THEORY
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The interaction of the gravity between two stars and their rotation around their center of mass create five points of stability in their orbital plane called
W) Roche lobes
X) Roche surface
Y) Lagrangian subspace
Z) Lagrangian points
ANSWER: Z) Lagrangian points
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of supernovae have spectra that contain no hydrogen lines and is seen as the violent explosion of a white dwarf star?
ANSWER: TYPE 1A SUPERNOVA
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What name is given to currents parallel to the beach shore that are produced by oblique waves?
ANSWER: LONGSHORE CURRENTS
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Give the name of the constellation that was divided into Carina, Vela, and Puppis in the mid-18th century.
ANSWER: ARGO NAVIS (also accept: ARGO)
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which prominent spring asterism is bounded by the stars Arcturus, Spica, Denebola, and Cor Caroli?
ANSWER: DIAMOND OF VIRGO
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following forms of precipitation is formed when supercooled raindrops freeze on contact with solid objects?
W) Glaze
X) Rime
Y) Hail
Z) Gaupel
ANSWER: W) Glaze
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer On some days, a halo can be seen around the Sun or the Moon. Which type of cloud causes this phenomena?
ANSWER: CIRROSTRATUS
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following objects has a gravitational redshift of about 0.01 percent?
W) Sun
X) White dwarf
Y) Neutron star
Z) Black hole event horizon
ANSWER: X) White dwarf
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In a binary system containing neutron stars, matter from a white dwarf falls to the surface of the neutron star, causing helium ash to accumulate. This helium then fuses explosively into carbon and emits this kind of electromagnetic radiation. What is this type of radiation?
ANSWER: X-RAY
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Intense deformation can cause folds to become titled beyond the vertical. What are such folds called?
ANSWER: OVERTURNED
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Upfolds, or arches, of layered rocks are called?
ANSWER: ANTICLINES
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following igneous structures is discordant in nature?
W) Sills
X) Laccoliths
Y) Joints
Z) Dikes
ANSWER: Z) Dikes
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer These igneous features are formed in the same way as sills, but from more viscous lava. What are they called?
ANSWER: LACCOLITH
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following nuclear products requires a main sequence star mass of about 8 solar masses in order to ignite fusion?
W) Helium
X) Silicon
Y) Neon
Z) Carbon
ANSWER: X) Silicon
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What two properties of Cepheid variable stars can scientists use to determine the distance to the star?
ANSWER: PERIOD AND LUMINOSITY
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Superluminal expansion was discovered using which of the following technologies?
W) Very long baseline interferometry
X) Gamma-ray astronomy
Y) low range x-ray imaging
Z) far-infrared sensing
ANSWER: W) Very long baseline interferometry
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the expanded alternate name of quasars?
ANSWER: QUASI-STELLAR OBJECTS
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following rocks is nonfoliated?
W) Slate
X) Gneiss
Y) Marble
Z) Schist
ANSWER: Y) Marble
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Sometimes, assemblages of marine rocks are found high up on land. What are these assemblages called?
ANSWER: OPHIOLITE SUITES
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which is an example of rocks near the surface that are enacted upon by brittle deformation and are affected by shearing?
W) Mylonites
X) Fault Breccia
Y) Basement Rock
Z) Scarps
ANSWER: X) Fault Breccia
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name the slowest of all unconsolidated mass movement.
ANSWER: CREEP
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the primary ore of zinc?
ANSWER: SPHALERITE
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By what process does seafloor spreading generate magma from peridotite in the mantle?
ANSWER: DECOMPRESSION MELTING
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which planet is our solar system is about 17 times the mass of Earth and was the first planet found my mathematical prediction rather than empirical observation?
W) Jupiter
X) Saturn
Y) Uranus
Z) Neptune
ANSWER: Z) Neptune
24. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to small nebulae that fluctuate in brightness, named after the two astronomers who discovered it?
ANSWER: HERBIG-HARO OBJECT
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following stars is seen predominantly during the summer?
W) Spica
X) Rigel
Y) Betelguese
Z) Sirius
ANSWER: W) Spica
25. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the process by which a new planet's interior releases gasses to form the atmosphere of the planet called?
ANSWER: OUTGASSING
