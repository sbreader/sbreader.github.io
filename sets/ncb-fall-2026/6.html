Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Let NR3 and PR3 be a nitrogen and phosphorus compound respectively with three random groups R attached to them that are the same in both compounds. How does the energy required for the inversion of the nitrogen compound compare to that of the phosphorus compound?
W) Higher
X) Lower
Y) Equal
Z) Depends on the group R
ANSWER: X) Lower
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In the solid state, PCl5 exists as an ionic solid of PCl4+ and PCl6-. What are the molecular geometries of these two compounds respectively?
ANSWER: Tetrahedral and Octahedral
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following 3 ring sizes by increasing prevalence of formation in organic chemistry 1) 4-member 2) 5-member 3) 6-member
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three chemical formulas by increasing amount of isomers 1) C4H6 2) C4H8 3) C4H10
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In a unit cell with face-centered cubic packing, what fraction of each corner atom lies within the unit cell?
ANSWER: 1/8
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice If two electrons occupy a d orbital, in which d orbital will their coulombic repulsion energy be minimized?
W) 3d
X) 4d
Y) 5d
Z) 6d
ANSWER: Z) 6d
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice One common example of a colloid is an aerosol. Which of the following describes an aerosol?
W) Liquid dispersed in Gas
X) Gas dispersed in Liquid
Y) Solid dispersed in Liquid
Z) Liquid dispersed in Liquid
ANSWER: W) Liquid dispersed in Gas
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three complexes by increasing magnitude of their charge 1) Ferrocyanide 2) Diamminesilver 3) Cobalt Tetrachloride
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three chemical species that are paramagnetic 1) F2 2) O2 3) NO
ANSWER: 2, 3
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When calcium carbide reacts with water, it forms calcium hydroxide and what other product?
ANSWER: Ethyne (also accept: Acetylene)
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In SN2 reactions, what name is given to the characteristic change in configuration of the chiral center due to backside attack?
ANSWER: Walden Inversion
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following explains why cyclooctatetraene is not antiaromatic?
W) It does not satisfy Huckel's Rule
X) It is in resonance with a noncyclic form
Y) it exhibits nonplanarity
Z) its pi electrons are not conjugated
ANSWER: Y) it exhibits nonplanarity
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What theory describes the splitting of a metal's d-orbitals in coordination complexes due to the electrostatic repulsion from surrounding ligands?
ANSWER: Crystal Field Theory
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following four ligands by increasing denticity 1) ethylenediamine 2) porphyrin 3) Chloride 4) EDTA
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2, 4
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What name is given to the region in an IR spectrum with wavenumber more than 1500 inverse centimeters and has few peaks and provides the useful info about the bonds present in a compound?
ANSWER: Diagnostic region (also accept: Functional group region)
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three molecules which may be released during a condensation reaction 1) Water 2) Carbon Dioxide 3) Ammonia
ANSWER: 1, 3
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Manganese is least stable in which of the following oxidation states?
W) +1
X) +3
Y) +4
Z) +7
ANSWER: W) +1
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To one significant figure and in kilopascals, what is the pressure inside a sealed 8 liter flask held at 250 Kelvin, containing three moles of an ideal gas?
ANSWER: 800
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice How does adding a small amount of base and a small amount of acid respectively change the rate of tautomerization?
W) increase, increase
X) increase, decrease
Y) decrease, increase
Z) decrease, decrease
ANSWER: W) increase, increase
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What type of spectroscopy is used to determine the binding energies, electronic structure, and composition of atoms and molecules?
ANSWER: Photoelectron spectroscopy
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following ligands would be expected to produce the largest crystal field splitting energy, according to the spectrochemical series?
W) Iodide
X) Water
Y) Ammonia
Z) Cyanide
ANSWER: Z) Cyanide
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The ability to add and subtract reduction potential comes from reduction potential being which of the following types of function?
W) Path
X) State
Y) Extensive
Z) Intensive
ANSWER: Z) Intensive
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer While the Pauling Scale characterizes electronegativity on a scale of 0 to 4, Mulliken Electronegativity is the average between what two quantities?
ANSWER: Ionization Energy and Electron Affinity
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, order the following 3 elements by increasing number of electrons in their highest energy d-orbital 1) Palladium 2) Scandium 3) Chromium
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three species by increasing bond order 1) Peroxide anion 2) Superoxide anion 3) Molecular Oxygen
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer As opposed to addition polymers which join monomers together with double or triple bonds, what type of polymers link monomers with reactive functional groups while releasing small molecules like water?
ANSWER: Condensation Polymers
14. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following IUPAC names is correct?
W) 1,3-dimethylpropane
X) 2,3-Dimethylpropane
Y) 3,4-dimethylpentane
Z) 2,3,4-trimethylpentane
ANSWER: Z) 2,3,4-trimethylpentane
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Analogous to the octet rule, what rule states that stable transition metal complexes tend to have their nine valence orbitals filled?
ANSWER: 18 electron rule
15. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following accurately describes FCC and HCP respectively?
W) 2 Alternating Layers, 2 Alternating Layers
X) 2 Alternating Layers, 3 Alternating Layers
Y) 3 Alternating Layers, 2 Alternating Layers
Z) 3 Alternating Layers, 3 Alternating Layers
ANSWER: Y) 3 Alternating Layers, 2 Alternating Layers
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is observing a compound with two chiral centers but notes that the compound only has 3 stereoisomers. This is an example of what general class of molecules which have two or more chiral centers but possess a plane of symmetry or an inversion center in some stereoisomers?
ANSWER: Meso Compound
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three reactions which are considered reductions 1) Amine to Nitro 2) Alkene to Alkane 3) Alcohol to Ether
ANSWER: 2
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The formation of disulfide bridges from two thiol groups is an example of which of the following types of reactions?
W) substitution
X) elimination
Y) reduction
Z) oxidation
ANSWER: Z) oxidation
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav drops a fatty acid into water. By name or number, order the following three structures by likelihood that they will form 1) Micelle 2) Liposome 3) Lipid Bilayer
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
