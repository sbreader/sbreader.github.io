3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following occurs during synapsis of meiosis:
W) non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes cross over
X) sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes cross over
Y) non-sister chromatids of non-homologous chromosomes cross over
Z) sister chromatids of non-homologous chromosomes cross over
ANSWER: W) non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes cross over

ANSWER: 1N AND 2S

6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A primary consequence of acid rain in the northeast U.S. has been:
W) salt-water intrusion in coastal wells
X) the leaching out of toxic heavy metals in lakes and streams

9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following was the big surprise in the classic gold foil experiments of Rutherford, Geiger and Marsden:
