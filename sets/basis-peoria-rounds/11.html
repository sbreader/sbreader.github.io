5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In Mexico in 1943, a volcanic vent started erupting from a small, otherwise boring depression in a small corn field. The volcano was powerful enough to throw rock fragments as high as 20,000 feet into the air. The volcano erupted for the next nine years, and the mountain soon engulfed the entirety of the nearby village of Paricutin. A lava flow also covered much of the village of San Juan Parangaricutiro. Then, however, the eruptions abruptly ended. What type of volcano was Paricutin?
ANSWER: CINDER CONE
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Ivanpah, one of the largest solar power plants in the United States converts sunlight into electricity via what method?
W) Heating up and melting salts, which, by the Thermoelectric effect, turn heat into electricity
X) Photovoltaic Effect
Y) Photoelectrolysis
Z) Heating up water to produce steam which pushes a turbine
ANSWER: Z) Heating up water to produce steam which pushes a turbine

7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice This glial cell is only present in the CNS and is responsible for producing cerebrospinal fluid. Name this type of cell, which appear similar to epithelial cells and possess cilia to distribute cerebrospinal fluid.

7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Oligodendrocytes and Schwann cells both perform very similar functions in different parts of the nervous system. Which of the following is an accurate comparison of the structures of these two cells.
W) Schwann cells possess many processes that myelinate multiple axon segments whereas Oligodendrocytes wrap around and myelinate only one axon segment.
X) Oligodendrocytes possess many processes that myelinate multiple axon segments whereas Schwann cells wrap around and myelinate only one axon segment.
Y) Both Schwann cells and Oligodendrocytes wrap around and myelinate only one axon segment.
Z) Both Schwann cells and Oligodendrocytes possess many processes that myelinate multiple axon segments.
ANSWER: X) Oligodendrocytes possess many processes that myelinate multiple axon segments whereas Schwann cells wrap around and myelinate only one axon segment.

12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Physicist Thomas Johann Seebeck discovered that there is a buildup of electric potential, which created a magnetic field, across a temperature gradient in a wire. Not noticing the electric current in the wire, he named it the “thermomagnetic effect” Later, Hans Ørsted recognized and corrected Seebeck’s mistake, renaming the effect to what?
ANSWER: Thermoelectric Effect

15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is a statement of Pascal’s Principle?
W) A change in the pressure applied to an enclosed incompressible fluid is transmitted undiminished to every portion of the fluid and to the walls of the containing vessel
X) A body fully or partially immersed in a fluid is buoyed up by a force equal to the weight of the fluid that the body displaces
Y) If the speed of a fluid particle increases as it travels along a horizontal streamline, the pressure of the fluid must decrease, and conversely
Z) In laminar flow the velocity of the moving fluid at any fixed point does not change with time, either in magnitude or in direction
ANSWER: W) A change in the pressure applied to an enclosed incompressible fluid is transmitted undiminished to every portion of the fluid and to the walls of the containing vessel
