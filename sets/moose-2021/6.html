Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of skin cell produces melanin?
ANSWER: MELANOCYTE
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Hair follicles are located in what layer of the skin?
ANSWER: DERMIS (also accept: RETICULAR LAYER)
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The existence of what entity would explain the excessively large orbital velocities of galaxies in clusters such as the Coma cluster?
ANSWER: DARK MATTER
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What must be the value of the electric charge of any hypothetical dark matter particle?
ANSWER: 0
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Ames Lab are studying methods of converting paper manufacturing waste into useful chemicals. One step in this process involves the oxidation of cyclohexanol. What compound results from this reaction?
ANSWER: CYCLOHEXANONE
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying a class of materials called perovskites. One common type of perovskite has the chemical formula CsPbBr3. What is the oxidation state of lead in this material?
ANSWER: +2 (also accept: 2)
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the chemical formula, including charge, for the chlorate anion?
ANSWER: ClO3-
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following compounds that are aromatic 1) Aniline 2) Toluene 3) Hexane
ANSWER: 1 and 2
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following particle diameters, in millimeters, would be considered silt?
W) 0.001
X) 0.01
Y) 0.1
Z) 1
ANSWER: X) 0.01
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice An unknown soil contains 30% clay, 40% silt, and 30% sand. Which of the following soil types would it be?
W) Clay
X) Sand
Y) Silt
Z) Clay Loam
ANSWER: Z) Clay Loam
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three organelles that contain nucleic acids 1) Nucleus 2) Ribosomes 3) Mitochondria
ANSWER: All
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the three regions of the Golgi Apparatus?
ANSWER: CIS, MEDIAL, AND TRANS (also accept: TRANS, MEDIAL, AND CIS; MEDIAL, CIS, AND TRANS)
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The equilibrium constant is calculated using which of the following characteristics of reactants and products?
W) Number of moles
X) Concentration
Y) Density
Z) Molar mass
ANSWER: X) Concentration
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following that are true of the equilibrium constant 1) It can provide a measure of how much a weak acid or base dissociates 2) Pure liquids cannot be included in the equilibrium constant expression 3) The equilibrium constant cannot be used for any strong acid
ANSWER: 1 and 2
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab are studying the collection of matter around massive objects like black holes. This collection of matter mostly happens through the formation of what structure around black holes and other objects?
ANSWER: ACCRETION DISK
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab are studying the effects of magnetic fields on astronomical events. Events like solar flares and geomagnetic storms are caused by what process, where magnetic field lines violently break and reform in plasmas?
ANSWER: MAGNETIC RECONNECTION
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the product of all multiples of 2 between -10 and 10?
ANSWER: 0
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What formation, located in the axil of a leaf, forms a lateral shoot between a leaf and a stem?
W) Stamen
X) Axillary bud
Y) Terminal bud
Z) Apical meristem
ANSWER: X) Axillary bud
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the force that pushes the plasma membrane of a plant cell against the cell wall, the loss of which causes leaves to wilt?
ANSWER: TURGOR PRESSURE (also accept: HYDROSTATIC PRESSURE)
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer The escape velocity of a planet is denoted as v. If the planet's mass is multiplied by the square root of 2 and the planet's radius is decreased by a factor of the square root of 32, what is the new escape velocity in terms of v?
ANSWER: 2√2 v (also accept: 2v√2; v2√2)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The main sequence turnoff on an H-R Diagram of a cluster of stars mainly helps to find what about the cluster?
ANSWER: AGE
11. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about functions is false?
W) An even function is such that f(x) = f(-x)
X) Even functions cannot have more than one x intercept
Y) Odd functions are such that f(x) = -f(-x)
Z) Odd functions must pass through more than one quadrant of the coordinate plane
ANSWER: X) Even functions cannot have more than one x intercept
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab are studying how water particles condense on aerosols to form clouds. What is the name of this process?
ANSWER: COLLISION COALESCENCE
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The reason for the high luminosities and surface temperatures of Wolf-Rayet stars is that they mostly lack what structure that absorbs most of the energy emitted by the core?
W) An envelope
X) A magnetic field
Y) A convective zone
Z) A stellar wind
ANSWER: W) An envelope
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following tectonic plates forms a mid-ocean ridge at its boundary with the Pacific plate?
W) Nazca
X) North American
Y) Australian
Z) Phillipine
ANSWER: W) Nazca
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Lab are studying biogeochemical cycles in the Tims Branch riparian wetland. Riparian zones are important interfaces between land and what bodies of water?
ANSWER: STREAMS
14. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A substance reaches a temperature and pressure above its critical point. Which of the following best describe the phase of this substance?
W) Solid
X) Liquid
Y) Gas
Z) Supercritical Fluid
ANSWER: Z) Supercritical Fluid
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following fault types is characterized by both extensional and shear stresses?
W) Normal
X) Reverse
Y) Strike-slip
Z) Oblique-slip
ANSWER: Z) Oblique-slip
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Fermilab are using a liquid argon chamber to find supernovae by detecting the lightest known particles in the universe. What are these particles called?
ANSWER: NEUTRINOS
15. BONUS Math Short Answer A bug is standing at the point (0, 0). The bug then takes eight random steps either north, south, east, or west. Each step is a length of one unit and takes 10 seconds. After one minute, what is the probability that the bug is at the point (-3, 3)?
ANSWER: 5/1024
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer To what organelle does mRNA bind to after leaving the nucleus?
ANSWER: RIBOSOME
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the name of the curve created when a string is hung from two fixed points under the influence of gravity?
ANSWER: CATENARY (also accept: COSH; HYPERBOLIC COSINE)
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer During what epoch in the history of the universe did the faint Cosmic Microwave Background radiation we observe today form?
ANSWER: RECOMBINATION EPOCH (also accept: RECOMBINATION)
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab are using cryo-electron microscopy to study solute carriers in cells. Identify all of the following 3 substances that solute carriers commonly transport 1) bicarbonate 2) urea 3) ammonia
ANSWER: All
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Force experienced in liquids due to buoyancy is described by what physical principle?
ANSWER: ARCHIMEDES' PRINCIPLE
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the organelle containing catalase in which beta-oxidation of fatty acids takes place?
ANSWER: PEROXISOME
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes an organism that cannot tolerate oxygen and functions best at a temperature of 55 degrees Celsius?
W) Mesophilic anaerobe
X) Thermophilic aerobe
Y) Cryophilic aerobe
Z) Thermophilic anaerobe
ANSWER: Z) Thermophilic anaerobe
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer Mass A of 2 kilograms is moving at a velocity of 8 m/s in the positive x-direction. At the same time, a Mass B of 4 kilograms behind Mass A is moving at a velocity of 16 m/s, directly toward Mass A. If the two masses undergo a perfectly inelastic collision, what will be the final velocity of the Mass A – Mass B system to three significant figures in meters per second?
ANSWER: 13.3
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are enzymes that catalyze the same reactions but have different chemical and physical properties?
ANSWER: ISOZYMES
40. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the coefficient of the xyz term in the expansion of (2x + 3y + 4z)3?
ANSWER: 144
41. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes an image with chromatic aberration?
W) Sharp image with only a few original colors present
X) Sharp image with all original colors present
Y) Blurred image with only a few original colors present
Z) Blurred image with all original colors present
ANSWER: Z) Blurred image with all original colors present
42. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In which quadrant of the coordinate plane would an angle with measure 982° lie?
ANSWER: III (also accept: 3)
43. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the remainder when 4183 is divided by 7?
ANSWER: 4
44. BONUS Math Short Answer If log base 10 of 2 is 0.301 and log base 10 of 3 is 0.477, how many digits long is 630?
ANSWER: 23
45. BONUS Math Short Answer Expressed in the form (x, y, z), what point satisfies the following three systems of equations: 4x + 3y = 12; 5x + 3y = 15; 8y + 4z = 16.
ANSWER: (3, 0, 4)
46. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What two planets in our solar system are in a mean-motion resonance with each other?
ANSWER: JUPITER AND SATURN (also accept: SATURN AND JUPITER)
