Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The First Law of Thermodynamics equates:
W) velocity and pressure
X) entropy and energy
Y) heat and work
Z) temperature and energy
ANSWER: Y) heat and work
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Convert an atmospheric pressure of 0.25 atmospheres into mm Hg:
ANSWER: 190
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the common name for the instrument consisting of a weighted glass tube or bulb which, when floated in a liquid, indicates the liquid’s relative density?
ANSWER: HYDROMETER
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer According to conventional nomenclature of nuclear science, what case and letter is used to symbolize mass number?
ANSWER: UPPER CASE A
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Biologists usually refer to the head of a tapeworm by what name?
ANSWER: SCOLEX
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the joint that is formed by the articulation of the tibia, fibula, and femur:
W) wrist
X) knee
Y) elbow
Z) ankle
ANSWER: X) knee
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Olympus Mons, one of the largest volcanoes in our solar system, is located on which planet:
W) Mars
X) Venus
Y) Titan
Z) Callisto
ANSWER: W) Mars
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name the Greek astronomer who ranked the naked-eye stars into six groups of relative brightness in the second century B.C.?
ANSWER: HIPPARCHUS
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The number 22.5 is 5 percent of what number?
ANSWER: 450
5. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following numbers is divisible by 9:
W) 3191
X) 3527
Y) 212,526
Z) 439,327
ANSWER: Y) 212,526
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Many large volcanoes are marked near their summit by a large, basin-shaped volcanic depression, more or less circular in form and steep-walled, which is called?
ANSWER: CALDERA
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The governing factor that controls flow within the Hydrologic System is:
W) humidity
X) capillary pressure
Y) vapor pressure
Z) gravity
ANSWER: Z) gravity
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many significant figures are in the number 3.8400 × 104?
ANSWER: 5
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer To the nearest gram, convert 20 ounces into grams:
ANSWER: 567 (also accept: 566; 568)
8. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following would offer the most protection against power-failure data loss:
W) UPS
X) surge protector
Y) data backup
Z) line conditioner
ANSWER: W) UPS
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the name for the measure of memory or storage capacity that is equal to 2 to the 40th power bytes or, in decimal, approximately a thousand gigabytes?
ANSWER: TERABYTE
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Acid rain, a major environmental problem, results primarily from the production of what aqueous acid?
ANSWER: SULFURIC ACID (also accept: H2SO4)
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the apparatus commonly found in chemistry labs that is designed to store materials in dry air that consists of a glass bowl, an air-tight lid, and a place where a drying agent, such as silica gel, is placed:
ANSWER: DESICCATOR (also accept: DESSICATOR)
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the most common term for a change in velocity divided by the time it takes to make the change?
ANSWER: AVERAGE ACCELERATION
10. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice How would one compute the change in centripetal force if an object moving with uniform circular motion triples its radius while maintaining the same kinetic energy:
W) multiplying by 3
X) dividing by 3
Y) multiplying by 9
Z) dividing by 9
ANSWER: X) dividing by 3
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Where is intercellular fluid normally found?
ANSWER: OUTSIDE OF CELLS (also accept: IN BETWEEN CELLS; IN THE INTERSTITIUM)
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Koch’s Postulates have to do mainly with which of the following:
W) culturing of viruses
X) causative agents of viral diseases
Y) proving what bacterial agents cause what diseases
Z) how to prove that a certain gene causes a certain disease
ANSWER: Y) proving what bacterial agents cause what diseases
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer How many Viking spacecraft have landed on Mars?
ANSWER: 2
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which star is the next nearest neighbor to the Sun beyond the Alpha Centauri system having the largest known proper motion of any star:
W) Wolf 359
X) Sirius
Y) Barnard’s Star
Z) 61 Cygni
ANSWER: Y) Barnard’s Star
13. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is a rational number:
W) 1 + √4
X) 2 + √5
Y) √6
Z) π
ANSWER: W) 1 + √4
13. BONUS Math Short Answer If the average of 5 numbers is 16, what is the sum of the 5 numbers?
ANSWER: 80
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the layer lying between the base of the Earth’s lithosphere and a depth of about 2900 kilometers, where rock exists in a hot, weak, plastic-like form?
ANSWER: ASTHENOSPHERE
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following geological periods is oldest?
W) Cambrian
X) Cretaceous
Y) Tertiary
Z) Devonian
ANSWER: W) Cambrian
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Pure gold is how many karats?
ANSWER: 24
15. BONUS Math Short Answer How many yards are in 1 rod?
ANSWER: 5.5
16. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What does the acronym SRAM stand for?
ANSWER: STATIC RANDOM ACCESS MEMORY
16. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice What is the hexadecimal equivalent of the binary number 1011?
W) 7
X) 8
Y) A
Z) B
ANSWER: Z) B
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which acid-base theory states that any molecule or ion that can produce a proton is an acid, and any that can take up a proton is a base:
ANSWER: BRØNSTED-LOWRY THEORY (also accept: BRØNSTED; BRONSTED-LOWRY THEORY; BRONSTED)
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What general chemical term best describes a metal that can be drawn out into thin wire or threads without breaking?
ANSWER: DUCTILE
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer When mixing equal proportions of green and red light, what color light is produced?
ANSWER: YELLOW
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer For an electric saw that draws 8 amps on a 120 volt line, what is the resistance of the saw?
ANSWER: 15 OHMS
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The stigma, style, and ovary make up what part of a plant?
ANSWER: CARPEL (also accept: PISTIL)
19. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Microtubules, intermediate filaments, and microfilaments are integral components of what basic infrastructure of a eukaryotic cell?
W) flagella
X) cilia
Y) cytoskeleton
Z) cell wall
ANSWER: Y) cytoskeleton
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The geology of the surface of Venus appears to be dominated by:
W) volcanoes
X) plate tectonics
Y) erosion by flowing water
Z) old impact craters
ANSWER: W) volcanoes
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer At the Sun’s distance of 8 kiloparsecs from the Galactic center, how many years would it take for material here to complete one rotational circuit?
ANSWER: 225 MILLION
21. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Convert 5π/3 radians into degrees:
ANSWER: 300°
21. BONUS Math Short Answer What are the median, mean and mode of the following set of numbers: 2, 3, 3, 8, 8, 8, 10, 14
ANSWER: MEDIAN = 8; MEAN = 7; MODE = 8
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If you cut a Mobius Strip in half down the center of the strip, how many pieces do you end up with?
ANSWER: ONE
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer If a woman’s mass on Earth is 60 kg, what is her mass, in kilograms, on a planet where the gravity is six times less?
ANSWER: 60 (also accept: THE SAME)
23. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In a flame test for lithium, sodium, potassium, and magnesium, which gives off a red color:
ANSWER: LITHIUM
24. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Total distance traveled divided by total elapsed time is most commonly called:
W) velocity
X) acceleration
Y) average velocity
Z) average speed
ANSWER: Z) average speed
24. BONUS Physics Short Answer If a wave has a frequency of 50 hertz, what is its period, in seconds?
ANSWER: 0.02 SECONDS
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In an autologous tissue transplant, from whom does the person receive the tissue?
ANSWER: THEMSELVES
25. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The margin of an ash tree leaf would most generally be called:
W) divided
X) entire
Y) serrate
Z) lacerate
ANSWER: Y) serrate
