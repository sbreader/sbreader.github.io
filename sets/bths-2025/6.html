1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice From among the following, choose one which is not an exothermic process.
W) Methanol synthesis
X) Catalytic cracking
Y) Ammonia synthesis
Z) Oxidation of sulfur
ANSWER: X) Catalytic cracking
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is a catalytic process?
W) Hydrogenation process
X) Coking
Y) Pyrolysis
Z) Thermal Cracking
ANSWER: W) Hydrogenation process
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Asher has 6 identical pens and three pockets: one in his hoodie and two in his pants. How many different ways can Asher distribute his pens among these 3 pockets?
ANSWER: 28
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Now, the 6 pens are each a different color. Asher wants to distribute these 6 distinct-colored pens into his left pant pocket, right pant pocket and hoodie pocket, ensuring that each of his two pant pockets gets at least 1 pen. How many different ways can he do this?
ANSWER: 602 (36-2*26+1)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A star on the Hayashi Track primarily transports energy through which process?
ANSWER: CONVECTION (also accept: CONVECTIVE HEAT TRANSFER)
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In what kind of binary system can only the orbit of the brighter component be observed?
ANSWER: ASTROMETRIC BINARIES (also accept: ASTROMETRIC BINARY STARS)
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice During the enrichment process, uranium-235 is separated from its other isotopes using a gas centrifuge. Which of the following is the name of the gas used in this process?
W) Uranium dioxide
X) Uranium hexafluoride
Y) Gummite
Z) Urania
ANSWER: X) Uranium hexafluoride
4. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the ratio of the velocity of a beta particle to that of an alpha particle, if they have the same non-relativistic kinetic energy?
W) 50
X) 100
Y) 150
Z) 200
ANSWER: X) 100
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice P. Aeruginosa has a thin peptidoglycan layer and can result in sepsis. What color would a sample of P. Aeruginosa be after having been stained with crystal violet and washed with iodine and ethanol?
W) Clear
X) Red
Y) Violet
Z) Amber
ANSWER: W) Clear
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a positively charged counterstain used to color gram-negative bacteria after they are washed with alcohol during the process of gram staining?
W) Xylene Cyanol
X) Fuchsine
Y) Safranin
Z) Cresol Red
ANSWER: Y) Safranin
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes a Bollman extractor?
W) It is a static bed leaching equipment.
X) It is used for extraction of oil from oilseed.
Y) It is a centrifugal extractor.
Z) It employs only counter-current extraction.
ANSWER: X) It is used for extraction of oil from oilseed.
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the main mechanism of separation in the Bollman extractor?
ANSWER: PERCOLATION
7. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the ratio of the area of a triangle with side lengths 13, 14, and 15 to the area of its inscribed circle?
W) 7/(3π)
X) 13/(5π)
Y) 21/(4π)
Z) 7/(π)
ANSWER: Y) 21/(4π)
7. BONUS Math Short Answer Compute the sum from k = 1 to infinity of k/3k
ANSWER: ¾ (also accept: 0.75)
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which astronomer created the first star catalog and discovered the Earth’s precession, both of which were later expanded on by Ptolemy’s Almagest?
ANSWER: HIPPARCHUS
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice On the vernal equinox, an observer in the northern hemisphere would see the sun pass through the first point of ares at what time?
W) Midnight
X) 6 am
Y) Noon
Z) 6 pm
ANSWER: Y) Noon
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the name of the boundary of a black hole where Einstein’s field equations begin to yield more than one solution for the curvature of spacetime.
W) Cauchy horizon
X) Event horizon
Y) Roche limit
Z) Bekenstein bound
ANSWER: W) Cauchy horizon
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the term for the phenomenon whereby x-rays scattered from materials have decreased energy?
ANSWER: Compton Effect (also accept: COMPTON SCATTERING)
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following would contribute to tumorigenesis?
W) Upregulation of pro-apoptotic proteins.
X) Decreased level of protein MDM2.
Y) Overexpression of gene FGF8.
Z) Lowered activity of enzyme xanthine oxidase.
ANSWER: Y) Overexpression of gene FGF8.
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer A patient exhibiting symptoms of Chagas Disease is likely infected by what protozoan parasite?
ANSWER: TRYPANOSOMA CRUZI (also accept: T. CRUZI)
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The relation, Sc = Pr = 1, is valid, when the mechanism of what transfer is the same?
W) heat and mass
X) mass and momentum
Y) heat and momentum
Z) heat, momentum and mass
ANSWER: W) heat and mass
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer List the two components of The Schmidt Number ratio.
ANSWER: MOMENTUM DIFFUSIVITY AND MASS DIFFUSIVITY (also accept: KINEMATIC VISCOSITY AND MASS DIFFUSIVITY; MOMENTUM DIFFUSIVITY AND DIFFUSION COEFFICIENT; KINEMATIC VISCOSITY AND DIFFUSION COEFFICIENT)
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If a random real number is chosen uniformly on the closed interval [0,1], what is the probability that the square root of that number is greater than ½
ANSWER: 3/4 (also accept: 0.75)
12. BONUS Math Short Answer If two random real numbers are both chosen uniformly on the closed interval [0,1], what is the expected value of the square root of their product?
ANSWER: 4/9
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a crescent-shaped dune whose tips point downwind?
W) Longitudinal
X) Barchan
Y) Parabolic
Z) Transverse
ANSWER: X) Barchan
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the chain of volcanoes that stretches from northern California into British Columbia in Canada?
ANSWER: Cascade Range (also accept: Cascade arc; Cascades; Cascade Volcanoes; Cascade Volcanic Arc)
14. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice As the square root of a particle’s mass increases, the particle’s probability of tunneling through a potential energy barrier:
W) Increases linearly
X) Decreases linearly
Y) Increases exponentially
Z) Decreases exponentially
ANSWER: Z) Decreases exponentially
14. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Two neutral hydrogen atoms are ten centimeters apart. Which of the following is NOT true of the system?
W) Gravity, though very weak, is acting between them
X) The neutral charge means the electromagnetic force between them can be ignored
Y) The range is too great for the strong nuclear force to act
Z) The electromagnetic force will repel the hydrogen atoms
ANSWER: Z) The electromagnetic force will repel the hydrogen atoms
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The Ubiquitin-Proteasome System is one of the ways a cell can remove misfolded or unneeded proteins. Which of the following proteins attaches the ubiquitin tag to the substrate?
W) E1
X) E2
Y) E3
Z) UBE4B
ANSWER: Y) E3
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer The E3 Ligase FBXW7 is responsible for attaching ubiquitin to numerous substrates involved in cancer development, including c-MYC, KLF5, and c-JUN. What family of proteins is FBXW7 part of?
ANSWER: F-BOX PROTEINS
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The McCabe Delta L law states that the:
W) molar heats of vaporization of components are nearly equal
X) linear crystal growth rate depends on the degree of supersaturation
Y) linear crystal growth rate does not depend on the crystal size
Z) linear crystal growth rate depends on the crystal size
ANSWER: Y) linear crystal growth rate does not depend on the crystal size
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The McCabe-Thiele process is based on what assumption about the pressure of the distillation column?
ANSWER: ISOBARIC (also accept: CONSTANT PRESSURE)
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following conditions is NOT caused by infection by a prion?
W) Creutzfeldt-Jakob Disease
X) Mad Cow Disease
Y) Charcot -Marie-Tooth Disease
Z) Fatal Familial Insomnia
ANSWER: Y) Charcot -Marie-Tooth Disease
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Fatal familial insomnia is a rare neurodegenerative disease. A patient exhibiting symptoms of fatal familial insomnia, or any other heritable neurodegenerative prion disease, is likely to have a mutation in what gene?
ANSWER: PRNP (also accept: PRION PROTEIN; CD230; CLUSTER OF DIFFERENTIATION 230)
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following moons in the solar system does not have a high albedo?
W) Europa
X) Phobos
Y) Enceladus
Z) Triton
ANSWER: X) Phobos
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of solar eclipse happens when the moon is close to its apogee?
ANSWER: Annular (also accept: Ring of Fire Eclipse)
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the term for sound waves with frequencies below the limit of human hearing, or about 20 hertz?
ANSWER: Infrasound (also accept: low-frequency sound)
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer A motor is powered by four batteries in series, which produce a total electromotive force of 6 volts. The motor draws 3 Amperes and develops a 4.50 volt back electromotive force at normal speed. Each battery has 0.100 ohms of internal resistance. What is the resistance of the motor, in ohms?
ANSWER: 0.1 ohms
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In sickle cell anemia, what amino acid replaces glutamic acid at the sixth position of the beta-chain?
ANSWER: VALINE
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer All of the essential amino acids are in the s-configuration except which one?
ANSWER: CYSTEINE
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the formula used for estimating the number of civilisations in the Milky Way capable of radio communication at a given instant?
ANSWER: Drake Equation
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the supernova remnant designated Messier 1 and is in the constellation Taurus?
ANSWER: Crab Nebula (also accept: M1; NGC 1952; Taurus A)
