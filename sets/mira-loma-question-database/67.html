1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of blood vessels has the lowest blood velocity?
W) Artery
X) Arteriole
Y) Capillary
Z) Vein
ANSWER: Y) Capillary
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which specific type of molecular orbital is caused by two atomic orbitals that are "out of phase" and repulsive, and destabilize the molecule as a whole?
ANSWER: ANTIBONDING PI ORBITAL
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is not true of induction in electromagnetics?
W) A magnetic field is induced by a changing electric field
X) An electric field is induced by a changing magnetic field
Y) The direction of an induced magnetic field is determined by the right-hand rule
Z) The direction of an induced magnetic field is in the same direction of the flux of the electric field
ANSWER: Z) The direction of an induced magnetic field is in the same direction of the flux of the electric field
