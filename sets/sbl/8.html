1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice According to hard-soft acid-base theory, which of the following elements forms the hardest ions?
W) Sodium
X) Phosphorus

1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer An organic chemist conducts a series of experiments to determine the identity of an unknown compound. First, mass spectrometry reveals that the compound has a molecular formula of C4H8. Then, lack of reaction with bromine water reveals that the compound contains no double bonds. How many different compounds could this be?
ANSWER: 2

TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements regarding thunderstorm development that are true 1) Severe thunderstorms may sustain themselves if there is a mild degree of vertical wind shear 2) Entrainment leads to downdraft formation in a mature thunderstorm 3) Dissipation may occur if the downdraft cuts off the updraft.

ANSWER: 1 AND 2

4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding CAM photosynthesis is false?
W) CAM photosynthesis does not utilize PEP Carboxylase
X) CAM photosynthesis is characterized by the opening of stomata in the night

Z) CAM photosynthesis is more water efficient than C4 photosynthesis

4. BONUS Biology Short Answer In a hybrid zone between two recently diverged species, the hybrid offspring is more fit than either of the parent species. ANSWER: 2 AND 3

9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements best describes the chemical weathering of iron-bearing pyroxenes?
