1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer How many ways are there to position 5 equal positive charges so that they are in a stable configuration?
ANSWER: 0
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Viraj is walking up a downward-moving escalator at a speed of 10 meters per second relative to the lab frame. However, the escalator is moving down at 4 meters per second. If Viraj weighs 60 kilograms, how much power does Viraj use to climb for 10 seconds, given that g equals 10 meters per second squared?
ANSWER: 8400 WATTS
2. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice f of x is a function that is continuous and differentiable over the reals, f of 0 equals 7, and f of 5 equals –3. f prime of x must have what value at at least one point on the interval between x equals 0 and x equals 5?
W) –4
X) –2
Y) 0
Z) 2
ANSWER: X) –2

ANSWER: 7

W) Sympatric
X) Peripatric
Y) Allopatric
Z) Parapatric
ANSWER: W) Sympatric

BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following four steps in the signal amplification of epinephrine to trigger glycogen breakdown 1) cyclic AMP activates protein kinase A 2) G proteins convert adenylyl cyclase into its active form 3) Epinephrine binds to a cell-membrane receptor 4) Phosphorylase kinase is activated.

ANSWER: PERIDOTITE

W) Magma pressure at the center of a mid-ocean ridge

15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes why two earthquakes might get the same score on the Richter scale but different scores on the Mercalli intensity scale?

X) One earthquake occurred in an area more prone to earthquakes, and the other occurred in an area less prone to earthquakes.
