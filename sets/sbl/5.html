1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true regarding d-orbitals?
W) They can overlap to form delta bonds
X) They have an azimuthal quantum number of 3
Y) They possess planar symmetry
Z) They can be involved in metal-ligand bonding
ANSWER: X) They have an azimuthal quantum number of 3

9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains the origin of the helium flash in medium mass red giant stars?
W) The reinitiation of hydrogen fusion in the core produces excess helium, which glows incandescently on a timescale of about 10 years
X) The core mass exceeds the Chandrasekhar limit and leads to a short but intense brightening
Y) After reaching a critical temperature, the inert helium core begins fusion and loses its degeneracy in a short runaway fusion event
Z) Large amounts of helium-4 are produced by the photon-induced splitting of beryllium-8, which produces a tremendous amount of energy and causes a short brightening
