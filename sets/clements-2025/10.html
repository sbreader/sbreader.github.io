ANSWER: 1, 3, 4
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Alex is constructing magnetoplasmadynamic thrusters (MPDTs) in his garage. MPTDs rely on the electric and magnetic pressures to function. Assuming the thruster generates a magnetic field of 1 Tesla, and an electric field of 1000 V/m, what is the ratio of the magnetic pressure to the electric pressure to the nearest power of 10?
ANSWER: 1011
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Every integer between 1 and 63 is converted into its binary representation, and these binary representations are all concatenated together. How many 1s are in the final binary string?
ANSWER: 192
4. BONUS Math Short Answer An unfair coin is weighted to come up heads 70% of the time. If the coin is flipped 100 times, estimate to 1 significant figure the probability that the coin lands heads up at most 69 times.
ANSWER: 0.5 (also accept: 50%)

6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Laboratory are studying radix sort. Radix sort is of interest because it can be easily parallelized by running different buckets on different processors. What is the worst-case big O time complexity of Radix sort with n items and k buckets?
ANSWER: O(n + k)

20. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is false regarding transition state theory?

X) The rate of a reaction is proportional to the concentration of the activated complex multiplied by the frequency with which it crosses the energy barrier.
