1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Kiran is a bacteria living in Liam-land, where every organism eats too much and is constantly farting. Assuming that farts in Liam-land are composed of 100% methane, and that Kiran is thriving in this environment, which of the following types of organisms could Kiran be?
W) Thermophile
X) Extremophile
Y) Halophile
Z) Methanophile
ANSWER: Z) Methanophile
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What currently accepted model of the cell membrane describes it as a dynamic structure with other components embedded or attached?
ANSWER: Fluid mosaic model
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term given to the group 17 elements, such as fluorine, chlorine, and iodine?
ANSWER: Halogens
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice A sample of Francium-223 has a mass of 64 grams and a half-life of 22 minutes. Which of the following is closest to the length of time it will take the sample to reach a mass of 0.25 grams?
W) 10 minutes
X) 1 hour
Y) 3 hours
Z) 10 hours
ANSWER: Y) 3 hours
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Earth has air. As a skydiver falls to Earth, she eventually stops accelerating, asymptotically reaching what constant velocity?
ANSWER: Terminal Velocity (also accept: Terminal Speed)
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer When cranked to the max, a pitching machine launches a baseball so fast that it never reaches the ground, leaving Earth's gravitational field entirely. What is the term for the velocity the ball was launched at?
ANSWER: Escape Velocity
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Yash owns a fertilizer company and has accidentally dumped a large amount of fertilizer in a lake. Which of the following is likely to occur in the lake?
W) Lower water temperature
X) Reduced nutrient concentration
Y) Algal blooms
Z) Increased oxygen levels
ANSWER: Y) Algal blooms
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the last stage of coral reef development around a subsiding volcanic island?
W) Barrier Reef
X) Fringing Reef
Y) Circular Reef
Z) Atoll
ANSWER: Z) Atoll
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer By name or number, identify which of the following are true regarding operations. 1. Multiplying two negatives always results in a positive 2. Dividing two positives always results in a number greater than 1 3. Squaring an integer always results in an even number
ANSWER: 1 only
5. BONUS Math Short Answer In simplest form, what is 36/5 divided by 3/10?
ANSWER: 24
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not one of the major classes of macromolecules?
W) Nucleic Acids
X) Proteins
Y) Fatty Acids
Z) Carbohydrates
ANSWER: Y) Fatty Acids
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, order the following 4 phases of mitosis in chronological order. 1. Anaphase 2. Metaphase 3. Telophase 4. Prophase
ANSWER: 4, 2, 1, 3
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, select all of the following four statements that are true of compounds formed by metallic bonds. 1. Their valence electrons flow around easily in a sea of electrons 2. They are brittle 3. They only conduct in solution 4. They are ductile
ANSWER: 1 and 4
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To the nearest integer degree, what is the angle between the bonds of carbon dioxide?
ANSWER: 180°
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Aaron picked up ice skating for fun over winter break. He starts spinning with an initial angular velocity of 14 radians per second. He then pulls his arms in, halving his rotational inertia. In radians per second, what is his new angular velocity?
ANSWER: 28
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A car accelerates from an initial velocity of 4 meters per second at a rate of 5 meters per second squared in the same direction as the velocity. After 4 seconds, in meters, how far will the car have traveled?
ANSWER: 56
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes igneous rocks that solidified below Earth's surface?
ANSWER: Plutonic (also accept: Intrusive)
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for an interstellar cloud of dust, which can be nurseries for the birth of new stars?
ANSWER: Nebula
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In degrees, what is the interior angle of a regular dodecagon?
ANSWER: 150
10. BONUS Math Short Answer By name or number, order the following 4 number sets from smallest to largest. 1. Real 2. Whole 3. Natural 4. Complex
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1, 4
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many chromosomes would an individual with Down's syndrome possess?
ANSWER: 47
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What term refers to the observable characteristics of an organism, rather than the genetic information of that organism?
ANSWER: Phenotype
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following 1-cubic centimeter samples of the following elements has the greatest mass?
W) Sodium
X) Chlorine
Y) Osmium
Z) Gold
ANSWER: Y) Osmium
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many lone pairs does each oxygen atom have in carbon dioxide?
ANSWER: 2
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following quantities that are vectors. 1. Current 2. Distance 3. Work
ANSWER: None
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer Newton is sitting under a peach tree when a peach suddenly falls from a height of 5 meters on the ground in front of him. Ignoring air resistance and assuming g = 10 meters per seconds squared, how long did it take for the peach to fall, in seconds?
ANSWER: 1
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the term for the time it takes for a star to return to the same place in the sky?
W) Circumpolar
X) Sidereal
Y) Zenithal
Z) Parallax
ANSWER: X) Sidereal
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Liam is a star, who after a long life of eating matter, gains enough mass that when he dies and explodes, the core collapses in on itself. However, he is just not heavy enough to form a black hole. What other stellar object has Liam most likely formed, characterized by the combining of protons and electrons?
ANSWER: Neutron Star
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Simplify the fraction into its lowest form: 42/91.
ANSWER: 6/13
16. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area of the right triangle with base length 3 and hypotenuse length 5?
ANSWER: 6
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The cell membrane is composed of all but which of the following four macromolecule types?
W) Protein
X) Nucleic acid
Y) Carbohydrate
Z) Lipid
ANSWER: X) Nucleic acid
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Naman wishes to dual major in history and biology. As he learns about endosymbiosis in bio class, he begins to mentally recite the theoretical process of endosymbiosis. Rank the following three steps of endosymbiosis from first to last. 1. The host cell and engulfed prokaryote undergo a mutually beneficial symbiotic relationship 2. The engulfed prokaryote's genes are integrated into the host cell's nucleus 3. The host cell engulfs the prokaryote through phagocytosis
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, order the following elements by increasing atomic radius. 1. Hydrogen 2. Lithium 3. Sodium
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following elements in order of increasing ionization energy. 1. Nitrogen 2. Oxygen 3. Fluorine
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
19. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Two balls, A and B, are moving with the same kinetic energy. Ball A has a mass of 5 kilograms. If ball A has twice the velocity of ball B, what is the mass of ball B in kilograms?
W) 10
X) 20
Y) 40
Z) There is not enough information to determine a solution
ANSWER: X) 20
19. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Two objects of mass A and mass B collide completely elastically. Assume mass A is moving towards mass B, which is stationary. Which of the following choices will cause the ratio of the final velocity of mass A to the initial velocity of mass A to be the smallest?
W) A = B
X) A = 4B
Y) A >> B
Z) A << B
ANSWER: Z) A << B
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Aurora Borealis, or the Northern Lights, is caused by charged particles interacting with Earth's magnetic field near the poles. Which of the following is the origin of these charged particles?
W) The Sun
X) Terrestrial planets in the solar system
Y) Gas giants in the solar system
Z) Stellar deaths
ANSWER: W) The Sun
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer William Herschel is famous for creating his own telescopes, which he used to discover which planet?
ANSWER: Uranus
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A student has a sample of 30 grams of carbon-14. After approximately 5,730 years, he is left with 15 grams of this substance. What percent of carbon-14 was lost to this decay process?
ANSWER: 50%
21. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the equation of the line going through the points (-1,-1) and (3, 7)?
ANSWER: y=2x+1
