1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In which rare, X-linked recessive disease does the absence of a relay protein in immune cells lead to symptoms such as eczema, thrombocytopenia, immune deficiency, and bloody diarrhea?
ANSWER: WISKOTT-ALDRICH SYNDROME (WAS)
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which mechanism leads to the greatest decline of CD4+ cells in AIDS?
W) HIV Infection
X) Apoptosis
Y) Cancer
Z) Interference in biochemical pathways
ANSWER: X) Apoptosis
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Complete the analogy: linear acceleration is to force as angular acceleration is to what?
ANSWER: TORQUE

ANSWER: 10 NEWTON-METERS
3. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The graph of the polar function r = 3 + 4cosθ can be classified as which of the following?
W) Limacon
X) Rose curve
Y) Circle
Z) Lemniscate
ANSWER: W) Limacon
3. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following functions would be a lemniscate?
W) r = 8-2sinθ
X) r = 2cos(3θ)
Y) r = 5sin(θ)
Z) r2 = 4sin(2θ)
ANSWER: Z) r2 = 4sin(2θ)

ANSWER: CORIOLIS EFFECT
5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Sigma bonds do not differ from pi bonds in that sigma bonds...
W) Have the maximum electron density along the line-of-centers
X) Are cylindrically symmetrical about the line-of-centers
Y) Are only found when two hybrid orbitals overlap
Z) Can be found between any two bonded atoms
ANSWER: Y) Are only found when two hybrid orbitals overlap
