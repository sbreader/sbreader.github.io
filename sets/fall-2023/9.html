Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the ratio of the root mean square velocity of a sulfur dioxide molecule to that of a helium molecule at the same temperature?
W) 1/4
X) 1/2
Y) 2
Z) 4
ANSWER: W) 1/4
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Rank the following four media in terms of increasing index of refraction 1) Water 2) Vacuum 3) Glass 4) Diamond.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3, 4
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for points in space at which the gravitational forces from two large bodies balance to produce an equilibrium position for objects with much smaller mass?
ANSWER: LAGRANGE POINTS (also accept: LAGRANGIAN POINTS)
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following accurately contrasts glacial valleys and river valleys?
W) Rivers tend to form U-shaped valleys, while glaciers form V-shaped valleys.
X) River valleys tend to be much larger than glacial valleys.
Y) River valleys tend to occur at higher elevations than glacial valleys.
Z) River valleys tend to accumulate smaller sediment than glacial valleys.
ANSWER: Z) River valleys tend to accumulate smaller sediment than glacial valleys.
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The Born-Mayer equation is best used to calculate the minimum potential energy of what type of solids?
ANSWER: IONIC
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that must be true of a cell with a standard reduction potential of 0 1) Its equilibrium constant is equal to one 2) It forms hydrogen as one of its half-reactions 3) Change in Gibbs free energy is negative.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Yeast spend the majority of their life cycles as which of the following types of organisms?
W) Single-celled haploids
X) Single-celled diploids
Y) Multicellular haploids
Z) Multicellular diploids
ANSWER: W) Single-celled haploids
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following amino acids is most likely to have phi and psi angles found in the forbidden regions of a Ramachandran plot?
W) Glycine
X) Lysine
Y) Proline
Z) Valine
ANSWER: W) Glycine
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at the Rittle Lab of UC Berkeley are studying chemical transformations involving Grignard reagents. Grignard reagents contain what alkaline earth metal?
ANSWER: MAGNESIUM
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at the He Lab at UC Berkeley are interested in studying the evolutionary implications of transposable elements in the human genome. Which of the following mutations can NOT be linked to transposable elements?
W) Single nucleotide substitution
X) Exon shuffling
Y) Chromosomal duplication
Z) Translocation of genome sequences
ANSWER: W) Single nucleotide substitution
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The function f(x) = x3 + 6 is reflected across the x-axis and rotated 180 degrees about the origin. What is the y-value of the new function when x is 3?
ANSWER: -21
6. BONUS Math Short Answer What are the two eigenvalues of the 2-by-2 matrix with first row 2, 1 and second row 2, 3?
ANSWER: 1 AND 4
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at the Space Sciences Laboratory at UC Berkeley are observing ultraviolet light patterns emitted from the luminous part of the Earth’s exosphere, most of which is scattered radiation from the Sun. What is the term for this part of the exosphere, which is hypothesized to extend out almost 6 million kilometers from Earth?
ANSWER: GEOCORONA
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers in the Reimer group utilize solid-state nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy to study minerals with paramagnetic properties. Minerals with such properties would likely contain which of the following metal ions?
W) Gallium 3+
X) Aluminum 3+
Y) Zinc 2+
Z) Iron 3+
ANSWER: Z) Iron 3+
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer FOP is a rare disease that leads to the ossification of muscle tissue. What ion is transported out of muscle tissues in this process?
ANSWER: CALCIUM (also accept: Ca2+)
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Appressoria are specialized structures formed during fungal pathogenesis that penetrate thick cell walls using what type of pressure?
W) Gravitational Pressure
X) Turgor Pressure
Y) Selective Pressure
Z) Osmotic Pressure
ANSWER: Z) Osmotic Pressure
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly describes the relationship between the pressures and volumes of real gases compared to ideal gases?
W) Both are higher
X) Both are lower
Y) Pressure is higher and volume is lower
Z) Pressure is lower and volume is higher
ANSWER: Z) Pressure is lower and volume is higher
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following ligands, when forming a transition metal complex with Co2+, will appear the highest wavelength color?
W) Br-
X) Cl-
Y) NH3
Z) CO
ANSWER: Z) CO
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following materials has the greatest Young’s Modulus?
W) Steel
X) Vulcanized rubber
Y) Polyethylene terephthalate
Z) Silicon carbide
ANSWER: Z) Silicon carbide
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer Suppose a very long cylindrical resistor loses heat proportional to its surface area and power dissipated. Assuming the voltage across the resistor stays constant, if the radius of the resistor is doubled, by what factor is the amount of lost heat multiplied?
ANSWER: 2
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three image defects which would be present when using a refracting telescope with a spherical lens 1) Spherical aberration 2) Chromatic aberration 3) Field curvature.
ANSWER: ALL
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the difference in amplitudes and energies for two earthquakes that differ by 2 units of magnitude on the Richter scale?
W) 10-fold difference in both amplitude and energy
X) 10-fold difference in amplitude, 32-fold difference in energy
Y) 100-fold difference in both amplitude and energy
Z) 100-fold difference in amplitude, 1000-fold difference in energy
ANSWER: Z) 100-fold difference in amplitude, 1000-fold difference in energy
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the partial derivative with respect to y of the expression xy2 + x3y?
ANSWER: 2xy + x3
12. BONUS Math Short Answer How many strings of two ones, three twos, and three threes can be made such that neither of the ones are adjacent?
ANSWER: 420
13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Researchers in the UC Berkeley Fluid Mechanics Lab are investigating the effects of different propeller configurations on the resulting wake turbulence. Which of the following vector operations would most likely be performed on a velocity field to quantify the severity of the wake turbulence?
W) Gradient
X) Divergence
Y) Advective derivative
Z) Curl
ANSWER: Z) Curl
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Berkeley Lab’s CAMERA Center use mathematical models to approximate the Schrodinger equation to model the state of electrons. What computational method models electron behavior based on electron cloud density?
ANSWER: DENSITY FUNCTIONAL THEORY
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a rock associated with meteorite impacts?
W) Tektites
X) Lechatelierite
Y) Breccias
Z) Migmatites
ANSWER: Z) Migmatites
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following three Milankovitch cycles in terms of increasing period 1) Eccentricity 2) Precession 3) Obliquity.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following are NOT considered stereoisomers?
W) Diastereomers
X) Cis-trans isomers
Y) Enantiomers
Z) Constitutional isomers
ANSWER: Z) Constitutional isomers
15. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of products would result from a disproportionation reaction involving phosphorus acid?
W) Diphosphorus and phosphite
X) Hydrogen ions and phosphate
Y) Phosphoric acid and phosphine
Z) Tetraphosphorus and phosphorus monoxide
ANSWER: Y) Phosphoric acid and phosphine
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following wavelengths of light, in nanometers, is absorbed best by photosystem II?
W) 670
X) 680
Y) 690
Z) 700
ANSWER: X) 680
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer In insect populations, many harmless pollinators mimic the coloration of bees and wasps to deter predation. What is the name of this defensive technique?
ANSWER: BATESIAN MIMICRY (do not accept: MULLERIAN MIMICRY)
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The nth roots of unity are the complex solutions to the equation xn = 1. How many of the 13th roots of unity have a positive real part?
ANSWER: 7
17. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the perimeter of a 30-60-90 triangle whose incircle has area pi?
ANSWER: 6 + 4√3 (also accept: 6 + 4 TIMES THE SQUARE ROOT OF 3)
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A hollow cylinder slides down a frictionless inclined plane and acquires linear velocity of 2 meters per second. In meters per second and to two significant figures, what is the final velocity of the same cylinder rolling without slipping down the inclined plane?
ANSWER: 1.4
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer Bob, who weighs 50 kilograms, is standing on a 100-kilogram sled on a frictionless surface. If he walks 1 meter from the center of the sled to its edge, how far does the sled travel in the opposite direction, to the nearest tenth of a meter?
ANSWER: 0.3
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which part of the male reproductive system is responsible for transporting sperm to the urethra?
W) Epididymis
X) Seminal vesicle
Y) Vas deferens
Z) Prostate
ANSWER: Y) Vas deferens
19. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In order to extract necessary nutrients from the soil, plants acidify and participate in cation exchange in the soil. Which of the following gasses is excreted by the plant to facilitate cation exchange?
W) CO2
X) N2
Y) O2
Z) CH4
ANSWER: W) CO2
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer When calculating the pH of highly diluted weak acids, the dissociation of water must be taken into account. What is the term for this reaction?
ANSWER: AUTOPROTOLYSIS (also accept: SELF-IONIZATION; AUTOIONIZATION)
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Consider the reversible, isothermal expansion of 1 mole of gas in a closed piston at 300 kelvins. To the nearest joule per kelvin, what is the change in entropy of the system as it expands from 3 to 6 liters?
ANSWER: 6
21. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Consider the function f(x) = the square root of the absolute value of the quantity x + 5. Which of the following statements is true regarding this function?
W) It is differentiable at x = –5
X) It is twice differentiable at x = –5
Y) It is continuous at x = –5
Z) It has a vertical asymptote at x = –5
ANSWER: Y) It is continuous at x = –5
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of atmospheric instability is exhibited when the lapse rate is 7 degrees Celsius per 1000 meters?
ANSWER: CONDITIONAL INSTABILITY
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of supernova does not show hydrogen, silicon, or helium spectral features?
ANSWER: IC (also accept: TYPE IC)
23. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true about leptons?
W) They have half-integer spin
X) They have nonzero charge
Y) They participate in weak force interactions
Z) They participate in strong force interactions
ANSWER: Z) They participate in strong force interactions
23. BONUS Physics Short Answer John is singing a 100-hertz note while driving towards Dylan at 50 meters per second. Dylan sings what he hears back at John. Assuming the speed of sound is 300 meters per second, what frequency note, in hertz, does John hear?
ANSWER: 140
