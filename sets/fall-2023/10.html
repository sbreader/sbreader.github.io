1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three physical properties that are shared between enantiomers 1) Boiling point 2) Density 3) Electrical conductivity.
ANSWER: ALL
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A particle on a ring is a model that can be used to more accurately describe electrons in molecules such as benzene. Aside from energy, what other quantity is quantized as a consequence of this model?
ANSWER: ANGULAR MOMENTUM
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following winds is NOT a downslope wind?
W) Monsoon
X) Foehn
Y) Chinook
Z) Santa Ana
ANSWER: W) Monsoon
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Polaris has an apparent magnitude of 2.00, while Vega has an apparent magnitude of 0.03. Which of the following is closest to the ratio of luminous flux that Earth receives from Vega to that which it receives from Polaris?
W) 0.16
X) 0.5
Y) 2
Z) 6.25
ANSWER: Z) 6.25
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A coin with radius 1 is dropped so that its center lies within a square with side length 4. What is the probability that the entire coin lies fully within the square?
ANSWER: 1/4
3. BONUS Math Short Answer How many times does the function f(x) = x/9 + sine x intersect the x axis?
ANSWER: 5
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the complex of surface proteins found in antigen presenting cells that bind to helper T cells?
ANSWER: MAJOR HISTOCOMPATIBILITY COMPLEX II (also accept: MHC II)
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Biologists couldn’t categorize Tarsiers into a specific monophyletic group until they incorporated genomic sequencing. Which concept describes the ambiguity of tarsiers in the phylogenetic tree?
W) Synapomorphy
X) Plesiomorphy
Y) Polytomy
Z) Automorphy
ANSWER: Y) Polytomy
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the ratio of the mass of a proton to the mass of an electron?
W) 180
X) 250
Y) 1800
Z) 2500
ANSWER: Y) 1800
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer A long, conducting cylinder generates an electric field of magnitude E at a certain distance R from its central axis. In terms of E, what is the magnitude of the electric field at a distance of 4R?
ANSWER: E/4
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Berkeley Lab have discovered the perovskite CsGeBr3, which is ferroelectric, meaning it generates its own electric field. Similar to ferromagnetic materials, ferroelectric materials demonstrate their unique properties only below a certain temperature. What is this temperature called?
ANSWER: CURIE TEMPERATURE
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Scientists in the Cohen research team of UC Berkeley employ a technique called thermal dissociation-laser induced fluorescence to detect the presence of reactive nitrogen oxides in the atmosphere. Which of the following is a product of the thermal decomposition of HNO3?
W) NO3-
X) NO2
Y) HNO
Z) NO
ANSWER: X) NO2
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following molecules that would increase transcription rates of the lac operon after binding upstream 1) X-gal 2) cAMP-CRP protein complex 3) Lac repressor
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following features that are present in both fern and gymnosperm life cycles 1) Free Living Gametophyte 2) Free Living Sporophyte 3) Wind dispersal of seeds
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A cube with density 0.5 grams per centimeter-cubed floats on top of water with density 1 gram per centimeter-cubed. If the acceleration due to gravity is 10 meters per second-squared, what fraction of the cube’s volume floats above the water?
ANSWER: 1/2
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the equivalent resistance, in ohms, of a tetrahedron-shaped circuit where each edge contains a 1 ohm resistor, with voltage applied to any two adjacent vertices?
ANSWER: 0.5
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals is the calcium rich end member of plagioclase feldspars?
W) Anorthite
X) Sanidine
Y) Halite
Z) Albite
ANSWER: W) Anorthite
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three events that would produce a type I supernova 1) Two white dwarfs in a binary system merge into a single star exceeding the Chandrasekhar limit 2) A star loses its hydrogen-rich atmosphere before collapsing 3) A red giant collapses into a neutron star.
ANSWER: 1
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three unit cells in order of increasing number of atoms per cell 1) Face-centered cubic 2) Simple cubic 3) Body-centered cubic.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three compounds in order of increasing viscosity at 20 degrees Celsius 1) Water 2) Heptane 3) Isopropyl alcohol.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What positive value of x minimizes the sum of the quantity 32 over x2 and the quantity 2x2?
ANSWER: 2
11. BONUS Math Short Answer A farmer performs a study on cattle weight, determining the average weight to be 500 kg, with a standard deviation of 30 kg. To the nearest whole number of kilograms, what is the weight of a cow that lands on the 90th percentile (z = 1.282), assuming cattle weight follows a normal distribution?
ANSWER: 538
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Professor Richard Bamler at UC Berkeley studies a partial differential equation called the Ricci Flow and has discovered a 4-D Ricci soliton that exhibits torus symmetry. A similar differential equation is the heat equation, which has well-behaved sine and cosine solutions. Which mathematical technique can be used to decompose functions into those solutions?
ANSWER: FOURIER ANALYSIS
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at the Berkeley Network for Neutrinos, Nuclear Astrophysics, and Symmetries are using neutrino detectors to observe various particle interactions. Rank the following three fundamental forces in order of strongest to weakest interaction with neutrinos 1) Gravitational force 2) Strong force 3) Weak force.
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following immunoglobulins possess a pentamer isotype?
W) IgM
X) IgG
Y) IgA
Z) IgE
ANSWER: W) IgM
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Cancer cells are genetically unstable because mutation rates increase at an exponential rate. Identify all of the following three cell mechanisms that can contribute to genetic instability 1) DNA repair 2) Oncogene Acetylation 3) DNA replication
ANSWER: ALL
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three processes that are involved in the Otto cycle 1) Adiabatic compression 2) Isochoric heating 3) Isothermal expansion.
ANSWER: 1
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 2 centimeter dipole comprised of 5 microcoulomb charges is oriented so its dipole moment is pointed 60 degrees from an electric field of 10 volts per meter. After a long time, what is the magnitude of total work done by the electric field in microjoules?
ANSWER: 0.5 (also accept: 1/2)
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If the second derivative of a function is 4x, f’(0) = ⅓, and f(0) = 6, calculate f(3).
ANSWER: 25
15. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area bounded by a circle with radius 1 and its inscribed angle of 30 degrees?
ANSWER: π/6 + 1/2 (also accept: (PI + 3)/6; PI/6 + 1/2)
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains the slowing of Earth’s axial rotation?
W) Reversals of Earth's magnetic field induce oppositely-oriented magnetic subfields
X) Thermally-driven atmospheric tides generate friction with Earth’s surface
Y) Tectonic movement is generally opposite to Earth’s angular momentum
Z) Earth’s angular momentum is being transferred to the Moon
ANSWER: Z) Earth’s angular momentum is being transferred to the Moon
16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about condensation and precipitation is true?
W) Cirrus clouds are a main producer of precipitation
X) Cloud droplets are the same size as raindrops
Y) The Bergeron process generates precipitation without forming ice crystals
Z) The collision-coalescence process explains precipitation in the tropics
ANSWER: Z) The collision-coalescence process explains precipitation in the tropics

X) H2O
Y) NH3
Z) CCl4
ANSWER: X) H2O

ANSWER: 0.25

ANSWER: 2

ANSWER: SEROTONIN
