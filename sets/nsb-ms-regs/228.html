1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What do you call a microorganism that lives and grows in extremely hot environments that would kill most other microorganisms?
ANSWER: THERMOPHILE (also accept: EXTREMOPHILE)
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about thermophiles in Yellowstone National Park is NOT true?
W) Many thermophiles live in hot pools of water
X) Studies of thermophiles have been helpful to the process of DNA fingerprinting
Y) Most thermophiles are in the kingdom Eubacteria
Z) Thermophiles are often brightly colored
ANSWER: Y) Most thermophiles are in the kingdom Eubacteria
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the heaviest element produced in a main sequence star?
ANSWER: IRON

X) Supernova
Y) Eclipsing binary star
Z) Brown dwarf
ANSWER: Z) Brown dwarf
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In which of the three spheres of Earth does life exist throughout much of the sphere?
ANSWER: HYDROSPHERE
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following lists the four upper spheres of Earth in the correct order of their chronological development?
W) Atmosphere, biosphere, lithosphere, hydrosphere
X) Hydrosphere, lithosphere, biosphere, atmosphere
Y) Lithosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, biosphere
Z) Biosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, lithosphere

9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice If the Earth's axis lost its tilt, which of the following would least likely occur?
W) Changes in ocean surface currents
X) Increased amount of seasonal changes across the planet

10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true about rock avalanches?
