2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 3 metals which are classified as transition elements: 1: Lead 2: Sodium 3: Iron

3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Jim pulls a lead block across a flat frictionless surface with a rope inclined 60 degrees to the horizontal. If the force he exerts is 10 Newtons and he pulls the block a total of 5 meters, express using proper SI notation the amount of work he performs during this task.
ANSWER: 25 Joules
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following facts regarding Saturn's moon Titan is false?
W) Titan is Saturn's largest moon
X) Titan has an insignificant atmosphere mostly composed of carbon dioxide
Y) The Huygens probe recently landed on Titan
Z) Titan was discovered by Dutch astronomer Christiaan Huygens
ANSWER: X) Titan has an insignificant atmosphere mostly composed of carbon dioxide
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 4 rocks that are igneous rocks: 1: mica schist 2: granite 3: peridotite 4: arkose sandstone
ANSWER: 2 and 3
6. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Triangle ABC is an isoceles triangle with the following angle measures: A –50 degrees, B –50 degrees, C –80 degrees. Which of the following side lengths is the greatest?
W) side AB
X) side BC
Y) side AC
Z) multiple sides have equal lengths
ANSWER: W) side AB
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The New Horizons space probe is planned to visit Pluto, and other objects of what surrounding area of asteroids?
ANSWER: Kuiper Belt
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, indicate which 2 of the following 4 plant tissues have the primary function of transporting substances throughout the plant? 1: xylem 2: phloem 3: ground tissue 4: dermal tissue
ANSWER: 1 and 2
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer An ideal gas is put into a chamber of constant volume, where its pressure is measured. In terms of its original density, state the new density after the Kelvin temperature is tripled.
ANSWER: the same (also accept: has not changed)
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains how a spinning gyroscope is able to stay upright?
W) enough friction is generated at the base of the gyroscope
X) the magnetic field of the gyroscope counters the Earth's magnetic field
Y) conservation of angular momentum negates the downward torque of gravity
Z) the force of gravity does not apply to spinning objects
ANSWER: Y) conservation of angular momentum negates the downward torque of gravity
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the 88 segments of the celestial sphere, as defined by the IAU, often known as?
ANSWER: constellations
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 3 methods that are suitable in lifting air parcels: 1: wind convergence 2: orographic lifting 3: frontal wedging
ANSWER: all of them
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer An isoceles trapezoid has a height of 4 meters and a diagonal length of 5 meters. If the shorter base of the trapezoid is 2 meters long, what is the area of the trapezoid, in proper SI units?
ANSWER: 12 meters squared
14. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice In radiometric dating, which of the following radioactive elements would have the longest half-life?
W) Uranium-238
X) Potassium-40
Y) Iodine-131
Z) Technetium-99
ANSWER: W) Uranium-238
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Lamarck's Theory of Evolution, which postulated that the traits that an individual gained during its lifetime could be passed down to its offspring, operates by what proposed mechanism?
ANSWER: inheritance of acquired characteristics
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements would be the most suitable in constructing the electrode of a standard hydrogen electrode?
W) hydrogen
X) gold
Y) platinum
Z) silver
ANSWER: Y) platinum
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Two oppositely charged particles are separated by a distance "d", and the magnitude of the force between them is "F". If the distance between the particles is doubled, and the charge of one of the particles is reversed, what is the nature and magnitude of the new force?
W) attractive, ½ F
X) attractive, ¼ F
Y) repulsive, ½ F
Z) repulsive, ¼ F
ANSWER: Z) repulsive, ¼ F
