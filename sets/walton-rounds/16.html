1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following forms of DNA is the most common?
W) A-DNA
X) B-DNA
Y) Y-DNA
Z) Z-DNA
ANSWER: X) B-DNA
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is secreted by cells in the juxtaglomerular apparatus in response to decreased delivery of Na+ and Cl-to the macula densa?
W) antidiuretic hormone
X) angiotensin I
Y) renin
Z) aldosterone
ANSWER: Y) renin
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the difference between neutron diffraction and X-ray diffraction?
W) X-ray diffraction needs large crystallization
X) There is no difference as both can be used to study the structure of nucleic acids
Y) Neutron diffraction is used for the precise structure
Z) Neutron diffraction deals with electron clouds
ANSWER: Y) Neutron diffraction is used for the precise structure
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer By name or number, identify each of the following 4 particles that do NOT follow Bose-Einstein statistics? 1) alpha particle 2) proton 3) photon 4) neutrino
ANSWER: 2 AND 4 (also accept: PROTON AND NEUTRINO)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the placement of the L3 Langrangian point of Earth and the Sun?
W) Between Earth and the Sun
X) On the far side of the Earth from the Sun
Y) On the far side of the Sun from the Earth
Z) On the orbit of the Earth
ANSWER: Y) On the far side of the Sun from the Earth
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice There are large concentrations of dust collected at the L4 and L5 Lagrangian points of the Earth and the moon. What are these clouds of dust known as?
W) Kordylewski Clouds
X) Van Allen Belts
Y) Gegenschein Clouds
Z) Lagrange Nebulae
ANSWER: W) Kordylewski Clouds
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer 2,3-dimethylbutan-2-ol undergoes an elimination reaction in the presence of an acid catalyst. What rule determines that the major product will be 2,3-dimethylbut-2-ene rather than 2,3-dimethylbut-1-ene?
ANSWER: Zaitsev’s Rule
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice You microwave a grape to make some plasma, but to save your microwave you place the grape in an upside down laboratory beaker. Which of the following best describes why the observed plasma is green in color?
W) Ions from salts in the grape burn green
X) Boron from the beaker’s glass burns green
Y) The frequency of the microwave induce a green color
Z) The plasma is at a temperature where it emits green light
ANSWER: X) Boron from the beaker’s glass burns green
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Given x2-6x+1=0, find x2 + 1/(x2).
W) 6
X) 25
Y) 34
Z) 48
ANSWER: Y) 34
5. BONUS Math Short Answer If for a polynomial P(x), the value P(x)-3P(x+1)+3P(x+2)-P(x+3) is equal to 12 for all values of x, what is the leading term of P?
ANSWER: 2X3
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Lab are using powerful magnets to measure the g-factor of a certain particle as a way to test the Standard Model of particle physics. What is this particle which they are researching, a heavier analog of the electron?
ANSWER: MUON
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Ames and Argonne National Labs have devised a method to transform polyethylene waste products into a liquid by cleaving the long polymer chains. The reaction occurs on the surface of a platinum and strontium titanate catalyst in a high pressure hydrogen atmosphere. What is the type of reaction that occurs in this process?
ANSWER: HYDROGENOLYSIS
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Near light speed muons are formed by cosmic rays in the upper atmosphere and then hit the Earth’s surface. However, based on their half-life and speed, these muons should not be able to reach Earth’s surface. What relativistic effect, from the muons point of view, allows them to reach the surface?
ANSWER: LENGTH CONTRACTION (do not accept: TIME DILATION)
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer A space pilot flies in a spaceship travelling at .6c for 1 minute, to the nearest tenth, how many minutes have passed for a stationary, outside observer?
ANSWER: 1.25 MINUTES
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify which of the following 4 compounds contains N-acetylglucosamine. 1) Collagen 2) Peptidoglycan 3) Keratin 4) Chitin
ANSWER: 2 AND 4 (also accept: PEPTIDOGLYCAN AND CHITIN)
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Lynn Margulis first proposed what theory, which provides an explanation for the development of mitochondria and some other organelles in eukaryotic cells.
ANSWER: SYMBIOGENESIS (also accept: ENDOSYMBIOTIC THEORY)
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following results from mixing equal volumes of 0.1 Molar calcium nitrate and silver fluoride solutions?
W) no precipitate and a colorless solution
X) a white precipitate and a colorless solution
Y) no precipitate and a colored solution
Z) a colored precipitate and a colorless solution
ANSWER: X) a white precipitate and a colorless solution
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer NO2, a brown gas, is in equilibrium with colorless N2O4 according to the exothermic reaction 2 NO2 yields N2O4. Identify each of the following four changes that would cause a mixture of these gases to darken in color 1) Heating the mixture 2) Decreasing the volume of the container 3) Injecting argon into the mixture 4) Decreasing the pressure of the container.
ANSWER: 1 AND 4
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Ames Laboratory are studying Homes scaling, which supports the BCS theory of superconductivity. This first microscopic description of superconductivity, explains that superconductivity results from the condensation of what pairs of coupled electrons?
ANSWER: COOPER PAIRS
10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab have developed the first forensic proteomics method to quickly detect ricin, a lethal toxin. Which of the following best describes the method by which ricin acts in the human body?
W) Ricin inhibits protein synthesis within cells
X) Ricin binds to acetylcholinesterase
Y) Ricin inhibits certain enzymes important to cellular respiration
Z) Ricin attacks certain amino acids important to cellular respiration
ANSWER: W) Ricin inhibits protein synthesis within cells
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of drugs is used to treat glaucoma by lowering the intraocular pressure.
W) Androgenic drug
X) Androgenic blocking drug
Y) Cholinergic drug
Z) Anticholinergic drug
ANSWER: Y) Cholinergic drug

W) Carboxyglutamate
X) Desmosine

Z) Selenocysteine
ANSWER: Y) Hydroxyproline
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Doline karst topography is distinguished by its frequency of what natural feature?
ANSWER: SINKHOLE (also accept: CENOTE)
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following levels of ground cover would lead to the highest recharge rate for an underlying aquifer, assuming other factors remain constant for each?
W) Land with no tree coverage
X) Land with sparse tree coverage
Y) Land with intermediate tree coverage
Z) Land with very dense tree coverage
ANSWER: Y) Land with intermediate tree coverage
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A mixture of two liquids has a very strongly positive deviation from the vapor pressure predicted by Raoult’s Law. Which of the following best describes the flow of heat when the two liquids are initially mixed.
W) Exothermic
X) Endothermic
Y) No heat flow
Z) Impossible to determine without the identity of the liquids
ANSWER: X) Endothermic
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Hafnium, which is directly below zirconium on the periodic table, has an almost identical radius to zirconium. What phenomenon explains why their radii are so similar despite a large change in atomic mass and number of electrons?
ANSWER: LANTHANIDE CONTRACTION
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What value describes the distance to which a magnetic field can penetrate a superconductor before it is expelled by the Meissner effect?
ANSWER: LONDON PENETRATION DEPTH
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer In the infinite square well model, what is the zero-point energy of a particle in a one-dimensional box?
ANSWER: PLANCK'S CONSTANT SQUARED DIVIDED BY 8
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If ten desks are in a row, how many ways can one choose 3 of them so that no two are adjacent?
ANSWER: 56
15. BONUS Math Short Answer 500 chickens sit in a circle. Each one pecks one of its neighbors with equal probability. What is the expected number of chickens that get pecked twice?
ANSWER: 125
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Brookhaven National Laboratory have accelerated single platinum atoms in order to break what strongest carbon bond in organic chemistry which may be found in teflon?
ANSWER: CARBON-FLUORINE BOND
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The National Energy Technology Laboratory is doing research on gas hydrates, which may be the largest source of organic carbon on earth. What kind of compounds are gas hydrates, where one molecule is contained within the crystal lattice of another?
ANSWER: CLATHRATE COMPOUNDS
