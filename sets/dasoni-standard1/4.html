1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Progression through the cell cycle is regulated by the concentrations of what family of proteins?
ANSWER: CYCLINS
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What immune cell is characterized by its CD8 receptor?
W) Helper T cell
X) Cytotoxic T cell
Y) Macrophage
Z) Neutrophil
ANSWER: X) Cytotoxic T cell
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The reaction of a Lewis acid with a Lewis base forms what type of bond?
ANSWER: COORDINATE COVALENT (also accept: DATIVE)
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If the enthalpy of formation of one mole of sulfur trioxide from sulfur and oxygen is negative 400 kilojoules and the enthalpy of formation of two moles of sulfur trioxide from oxygen and two moles of sulfur dioxide is negative 200 kilojoules, what is the enthalpy of formation, in kilojoules to one significant figure, of one mole of sulfur dioxide?
ANSWER: -300
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the oceanic layer, relatively close to the surface, where density increases quickly with depth?
ANSWER: PYCNOCLINE
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name for the darkest portion of a sunspot?
W) Umbra
X) Penumbra
Y) Plage [PLAH]]
Z) Granule
ANSWER: W) Umbra
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A Sophie-Germain prime is a prime number p such that 2p+1 is also prime. What is the smallest prime number that is not a Sophie-Germain prime?
ANSWER: 7
4. BONUS Math Short Answer How many points of intersection are there between the diagonals of a decagon?
ANSWER: 210
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice An object sits at rest on a ramp. When given a small push, it starts accelerating down the ramp. Which of the following must be true about the coefficients of static friction μs and kinetic friction μk?
W) μk is greater than 1
X) μk is less than μs
Y) μk is equal to μs
Z) It depends on the normal force
ANSWER: X) μk is less than μs
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer On the alien planet Florpum, they use a different measurement system than Earth. Their unit for mass is equivalent to 250 grams. Their unit for time is equivalent to one minute and 40 seconds. Finally, their unit for length is equivalent to 6 kilometers. Their unit of force is equivalent to how many newtons?
ANSWER: 0.15 N
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Davidson HS A Team members are currently studying cenotes, dolines, and sinkholes created by the chemical weathering of limestone. In what kind of topography would you find these features?
ANSWER: KARST
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Davidson MS B team members are currently studying the cytoskeleton. What term is given to the microfilament-mediated flow of cytoplasm within a cell?
ANSWER: CYTOPLASMIC STREAMING
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice RNA polymerase III is responsible for synthesizing transfer RNA. Which of the following types of bonds does it form?
W) Phosphodiester
X) Glycosidic
Y) Peptide
Z) Ether
ANSWER: W) Phosphodiester
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Phosphofructokinase is an enzyme involved in glycolysis. When ATP levels are high, ATP binds to a site on phosphofructokinase other than the active site, inhibiting it. What is the term for this type of enzymatic regulation?
ANSWER: ALLOSTERY (also accept: ALLOSTERIC)
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three acids in order of increasing strength of their conjugate bases 1) Water 2) Sulfuric acid 3) Ammonium
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1 (also accept: SULFURIC ACID, AMMONIUM, WATER)
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The Haber process is the gas-phase reaction between nitrogen and hydrogen to form ammonia. Using Le Chatlier’s principle, identify all of the following four changes that would shift the reaction towards the products, given that the reaction is exothermic 1) Adding catalyst 2) Increasing temperature 3) Increasing pressure 4) Adding nitrogen gas
ANSWER: 3 AND 4 (also accept: INCREASING PRESSURE, ADDING NITROGEN GAS)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Ahana draws a HR diagram but accidently flips the temperature axis. However, she plots the approximate locations of the various types of stars correctly, relative to the new axes. Where on this HR diagram would Ahana place a white dwarf?
W) Bottom left
X) Bottom right
Y) Top left
Z) Top right
ANSWER: X) Bottom right
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four events from earliest to most recent 1) Breakup of Pangea 2) End of the Devonian 3) The Younger Dryas 4) The KT Extinction
ANSWER: 2, 1, 4, 3 (also accept: END OF THE DEVONIAN, BREAKUP OF PANGEA, THE KT EXTINCTION, THE YOUNGER DRYAS)
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Nate has lost his seventy narbles; luckily, he owns several larbles which he can trade away. Three larbles may be traded for seven carbles, and four carbles may be exchanged for five narbles. How many larbles does Nate need to have to re-acquire his seventy narbles?
ANSWER: 24
10. BONUS Math Short Answer A right triangle’s shorter leg has a length of 15, and the length of its longer leg differs from the length of the hypotenuse by 1. What is the perimeter of the triangle?
ANSWER: 240
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Emmy replaces a battery in a circuit with two identical batteries in series. This new circuit has twice the resistance of the old circuit. By what factor is the power dissipated multiplied?
W) 1/2
X) 2
Y) 4
Z) 8
ANSWER: X) 2
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer Megan spins a cannonball tied to a string in a circle at constant speed. The cannonball weighs 5 kilograms, the string is 2 meters long, and it completes one revolution in 1 second. In Newtons, what is the difference in tension in the string when it is at the bottom of its arc versus the top?
ANSWER: 98 N
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Davidson MS B team members are studying fountain pens. Fountain pens transport water-based ink to the nib using feeds that have thin channels cut in it and do not depend on gravity to work. What physical effect do fountain pens likely use to transport ink?
ANSWER: CAPILLARY ACTION
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer Davidson MS B team members are studying combinations of resistors. How many ways can two resistors with integer resistance, in ohms, be placed in parallel to form a structure with a net resistance of 4 ohms? Assume that the order of the resistors does not matter.
ANSWER: 5

ANSWER: -12
16. BONUS Math Short Answer Ahana has a bag of 8 different patterned beanbags. For each beanbag, Ahana flips a fair coin, putting the beanbag in either a “heads” pile or “tails” pile accordingly. What is the probability that the two piles have the same number of beanbags?
ANSWER: 35/128
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Light takes a path between two points that always minimizes the time taken. What is this fact known as?
ANSWER: FERMAT’S PRINCIPLE (also accept: PRINCIPLE OF LEAST TIME)
17. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A battery is short circuited using a straight piece of wire. In an ideal system, infinite current would pass through the short circuit. Which of the following best explains the most probable reason why this does not happen in reality?
W) Eddy currents limit the overall current
X) The battery does not reliably provide a constant voltage to the circuit
Y) Electrons cannot be accelerated infinitely fast
Z) There is resistance in the battery and wire which reduces current
ANSWER: Z) There is resistance in the battery and wire which reduces current
