1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice John has come in contact with a butterfly. This butterfly looks like a monarch butterfly, but after further observation, he realizes that it’s actually a viceroy butterfly, which shares similar coloration patterns and behavior. Which of the following types of mimicry does this represent:
W) Batesian
X) Mullerian
Y) Vavilovian
Z) Mertensian
ANSWER: X) Mullerian

W) P
X) Cl
Y) Ge
Z) Se
ANSWER: X) Cl

W) 1-pentene
X) 2-pentene
Y) Cyclopentane
Z) 2-methyl-1-butene
ANSWER: Y) Cyclopentane
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Arin is jogging with components of velocity of 4 meters per second north and 3 meters per second west. Adrian is running towards him with velocity of 3 meters per second south and 21 meters per second east. In meters per second, what is Adrian’s velocity relative to Arin?
ANSWER: 25
