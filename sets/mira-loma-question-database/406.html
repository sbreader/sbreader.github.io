Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In a parallel resistance circuit consisting of one circuit with 10 ohms resistance and the other made up of another parallel circuit 15, calculate the total resistance.
ANSWER: 6 Ohms
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer In a circuit consisting of a 24 volt battery and parallel resistors with resistances of 8 Ohms and 4 Ohms, calculate the total current in the system.
ANSWER: 9 Amperes
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following in order of decreasing bond angles. 1: NH4 2: BF3 3: NH3
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The molecule AX5Y has how many different stereoisomers?
ANSWER: 1
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the interaction between the Pacific Plate and the North American Plate?
W) Divergent Boundary
X) Transform Boundary
Y) Convergent Boundary
Z) Convection Current
ANSWER: X) Transform Boundary
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name the discoverer of the Continental Drift Theory, and name one set of two continents that he used as proof for the theory.
ANSWER: Alfred Wegener; Europe/North America (also accept: Alfred Wegener; Africa/South America)
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When one develops sepsis, what product is formed that also decreases the pH of blood?
ANSWER: Lactic Acid
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which node delays the signal from the upper to the lower chamber of the heart?
ANSWER: AV Node (also accept: Atrio- Ventricular Node)
5. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Today, what is the most exploited source of renewable energy?
ANSWER: Hydroelectric Energy
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What cycle is needed to regenerate pyruvate from lactic acid?
ANSWER: Cori's Cycle
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Give the double derivative of 5x3+ 6x2.
ANSWER: 30x + 12
6. BONUS Math Short Answer A circle is inscribed in a triangle with side lengths 3, 4, and 5. Find the radius of the circle.
ANSWER: 1
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Dark Energy tends to do what in the universe?
W) Increase Temperature
X) Give off x-rays
Y) Increase speed of expansion
Z) Decrease temperature
ANSWER: Y) Increase speed of expansion
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name the five dwarf planets.
ANSWER: Eris, Ceres, Pluto, Haumea, Makemake
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What does VSEPR stand for?
ANSWER: Valence Shell Electron Pair Repulsion
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Using VSEPR, determine the five possible ways bonds can be positioned in relation to the central atom.
ANSWER: Linear, Trigonal Planar, Tetrahedral, Trigonal Bipyramidal, Octahedral
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The seemingly random motion of pollen on still water is a result primarily of what?
W) uneven movement of water molecules
X) small micro-currents in the water
Y) temperature differences within the water
Z) air currents moving the water
ANSWER: W) uneven movement of water molecules
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer Whose theory states that the root mean square is proportional to the square root of time?
ANSWER: Einstein
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The first differentiation of the formula for the volume of a sphere is the formula for what?
ANSWER: Surface area of a sphere
10. BONUS Math Short Answer An one mile long train enters a 10 mile long tunnel at a speed of 2 miles an hour. How long will it be until the train fully emerges from the tunnel?
ANSWER: 5.5 hours (also accept: 5 and a half hours; 5 hours 30 minutes)
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What process is used to increase the elasticity of rubber?
W) Bessemer Process
X) Vulcanization
Y) Immersification
Z) Dehydrogenation
ANSWER: X) Vulcanization
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Who first discovered the benefits of the Vulcanization process to create a durable tire brand?
ANSWER: Charles Goodyear
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which nerve slows down the SA node in the heart?
ANSWER: VAGUS NERVE
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer The ending of the vagi nerves releases what hormone when danger is not threatening?
ANSWER: Acetylcholine
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Sympathetic nerves release what hormone when danger is imminent?
ANSWER: Norepinephrine
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice When acetylcholine is released, the nodes become more permeable to what ion?
W) Magnesium
X) Chlorine
Y) Potassium
Z) Sodium
ANSWER: Y) Potassium
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A tetrahedral molecule has what bond angle?
ANSWER: 109.5 Degrees
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Name the family that contains 90 degree bonds, can be highly explosive, and also be used to inactivate an enzyme in Parkinson's disease.
ANSWER: Cubanes
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the triple integral of the equation 24x+12?
ANSWER: X4 + 2x3
15. BONUS Math Short Answer What angle theorem did the Ancient Greeks take advantage of to calculate the radius of the Earth?
ANSWER: Alternating Angles
16. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Carbon makes up what percentage of petroleum by weight?
W) 30-40%
X) 40-50%
Y) 70-80%
Z) 80-90%
ANSWER: Z) 80-90%
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Bears are able to hibernate because they store up what kind of fat?
ANSWER: Brown Fat
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When two spiral galaxies collide, what shape galaxy forms?
ANSWER: Elliptical Galaxy
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which galaxy is the Milky Way headed to collide with in about 3 billion years?
ANSWER: Andromeda (Galaxy)
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which of the following affects the frequency of a pendulum? 1: Mass 2: Initial Velocity 3: Length of string attached
ANSWER: 3 ONLY
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer Who first stated the law for determining the time of the period of a pendulum bob's swing?
ANSWER: Christiaan Huygens
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which alcohol causes permanent blindness is ingested?
ANSWER: Methanol
19. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the essential component to the smell perceived?
W) Number of carbons
X) Shape of the molecule
Y) Bond angles in the molecule
Z) Length of the molecule
ANSWER: X) Shape of the molecule
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Mustard Gas is lethal to humans because it does what?
W) Creates HCl in moist areas of the human body
X) produces cyanide gas when it mixes with air
Y) forms an alkali compound
Z) forms a free-radical
ANSWER: W) Creates HCl in moist areas of the human body
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The molecule AX3 can take the form of which three molecules?
ANSWER: Trigonal Planar, Tetrahedral, Trigonal Bipyramidal
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The existence of similar mountain ranges on the opposite sides of the Atlantic is an example of what theory?
ANSWER: Continental Drift
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the common name for the belt of hot spots and volcanic activity around the Pacific Rim?
ANSWER: (Pacific) Ring of Fire
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Nitrate has how many resonance forms?
ANSWER: 3
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many different groups must be around a carbon to form an enantiomer?
ANSWER: 4
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is different between enantiomers?
W) Density
X) Optic Properties
Y) Chemical Reactivity
Z) Polarity
ANSWER: X) Optic Properties
23. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In a chemical equation, what do you put in front of each compound to differentiate between enantiomers?
ANSWER: L, D
24. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the sum of the exterior angles of an octagon?
ANSWER: 360 Degrees
24. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the sum of the interior angles of a dodecagon?
ANSWER: 1800 Degrees
24. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice If two vectors have angle theta between them, the cross- product of these two vector is obtained by multiplying the product of the two vectors with what?
W) sin theta
X) cos theta
Y) tan theta
Z) cosec theta
ANSWER: W) sin theta
