1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In the Northern Hemisphere, during which of the following months would the mid-latitude oceans experience the strongest thermocline?
W) January
X) April
Y) July
Z) September
ANSWER: Z) September
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding precipitation 1) Virga most commonly forms in dry environments 2) Graupel grows through accretion of supercooled water droplets 3) Sleet can grow to large sizes through thunderstorm updrafts that keep it aloft
ANSWER: 1 and 2
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following events can only occur during a full moon?
W) Spring tide
X) Neap tide
Y) Solar eclipse
Z) Lunar eclipse
ANSWER: Z) Lunar eclipse
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Sun and other nearby star clusters are located inside an interstellar cavity known as the Local Bubble. Which of the following best describes how the Local Bubble formed?
W) Stellar wind from massive star formation
X) Recent supernova
Y) Gravitational disturbance of the Pleiades moving group
Z) Natural irregularities in the cosmic web
ANSWER: X) Recent supernova
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes why inferior mirages occur?
W) A hot layer of air aloft refracts light upwards
X) A hot layer of air aloft refracts light downwards
Y) A hot layer of air at the surface refracts light upwards
Z) A hot layer of air at the surface refracts light downwards
ANSWER: Y) A hot layer of air at the surface refracts light upwards
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three factors that tend to increase the magnitude of earthquakes at a subduction zone 1) Fluid lubrication 2) High rock rigidity 3) Large area of rupture
ANSWER: 2 and 3
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the mode of heat transfer of a 3 solar mass red giant?
W) Radiative core, convective envelope
X) Convective core, radiative envelope
Y) Radiative throughout
Z) Convective throughout
ANSWER: W) Radiative core, convective envelope
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding the monolithic collapse theory of the Milky Way is the least accurate?
W) A single, massive gas cloud collapsed to form the Milky Way
X) A disk was formed perpendicular to the cloud’s rotational axis
Y) All disk stars were formed at a similar time
Z) Monolithic collapse is described as a top-down theory
ANSWER: Y) All disk stars were formed at a similar time
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In the binary phase diagram for diopside and anorthite, the diopside liquidus, anorthite liquidus and the solidus intersect at what point, where the two minerals and the melt exist in equilibrium?
ANSWER: Eutectic
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding stratigraphy 1) A paraconformity is also called a pseudoconformity as it is hard to identify 2) Stratovolcanoes often violate the law of original horizontality 3) A strata located above is always older than the one beneath
ANSWER: 1 and 2
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the radius of the observable universe in billions of light years?
W) 5
X) 15
Y) 45
Z) 100
ANSWER: Y) 45
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three futures of the universe that are likely to occur given a universe with an average density less than the critical density 1) Big Crunch 2) Big Freeze 3) Big Rip
ANSWER: 2 and 3
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three environments that tend to create symmetrical ripple marks 1) Beach 2) Lake bed 3) Delta
ANSWER: 1 and 2
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What features are formed when an ice stream flows from a valley into the sea for tens of kilometers?
ANSWER: Ice tongues
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Algol Paradox is most closely associated with which of the following types of supernovae?
W) Ia
X) Ib
Y) Ic
Z) II
ANSWER: W) Ia
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following three orbital resonances from least to most stable 1) 1 to 2 2) 3 to 4 3) 7 to 12
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three properties that can be used reliably to distinguish between celestite and quartz 1) Cleavage 2) Luster 3) Hardness
ANSWER: 1 and 3
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Soils with an equal composition of sand, silt and clay are classified under what soil texture?
ANSWER: Loam
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the formation mechanism of serpentinite?
W) Hydration of ultramafic protolith
X) Slow cooling of ultramafic magma beneath the seafloor
Y) Contact metamorphism of pillow basalts
Z) Underwater suturing between tectonic plates
ANSWER: W) Hydration of ultramafic protolith
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following three gases in increasing volume produced in volcanic eruptions 1) H2S 2) CO2 3) HCl
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In contrast to the s-process, which slowly creates elements heavier than iron in giant stars, what process is thought to rapidly form heavy elements during supernovae via neutron capture?
ANSWER: r-process
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice On the main sequence, the rate of fusion is primarily a function of which of the following?
W) Core hydrogen volume
X) Core pressure
Y) Mean core temperature
Z) Age
ANSWER: Y) Mean core temperature
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements best explains why most meteors burn up in the mesosphere, even though the thermosphere has a higher temperature?
W) The mesosphere experiences stronger gravity, accelerating the meteor
X) The thermosphere is too shallow and cannot heat up the meteor before it passes through
Y) The mesosphere has higher particle density, leading to higher friction with the meteor
Z) The sodium layer in the mesosphere chemically reacts with the meteor to produce heat
ANSWER: Y) The mesosphere has higher particle density, leading to higher friction with the meteor
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three changes that would likely cause an existing meandering river to become incised 1) Rapid sedimentation at river mouth 2) Construction of dam upstream 3) Tectonic uplift
ANSWER: 3 only
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following features is least likely to be found at a site of meteorite impact?
W) Stischovite
X) Glass
Y) Breccia
Z) Peridotite
ANSWER: Z) Peridotite
13. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following greenhouse gases has the lowest global warming potential?
W) Water vapor
X) Carbon dioxide
Y) Methane
Z) Sulfur hexafluoride
ANSWER: W) Water vapor
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following elements is typically the largest by mass in igneous rocks?
W) Iron
X) Silicon
Y) Oxygen
Z) Carbon
ANSWER: Y) Oxygen
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A cold monomictic lake, such as those found in Antarctica, generally overturns during which of the following seasons?
W) Spring
X) Summer
Y) Autumn
Z) Winter
ANSWER: X) Summer
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following asteroid belt objects has the greatest vertical density gradient?
W) Ceres
X) Vesta
Y) Hygiea
Z) Pallas
ANSWER: X) Vesta
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer On the galaxy color magnitude diagram, the red sequence primarily consists of what major class of galaxy?
ANSWER: Elliptical
