8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about the Daniell cell is NOT correct?
W) Zinc is oxidized
X) Copper is reduced
Y) There is a net transfer of 1 mol of electrons per reaction
Z) Zinc and Copper II sulfate are used in the solution
ANSWER: Y) There is a net transfer of 1 mol of electrons per reaction
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 3 steps of the Williamson Ether Synthesis in the correct order 1) Sodium attaches to the alkoxide ion 2) The alcohol is deprotonated 3) Alkoxide attacks the alpha carbon of the alkyl halide.
ANSWER: 2,1,3
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What functional group contains a three-membered ring with an oxygen atom and 2 carbon atoms, all connected via single bonds?
ANSWER: EPOXIDE
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 3 elements by increasing electron affinity 1) Carbon 2) Nitrogen 3) Oxygen.
ANSWER: 2,1,3 (also accept: NITROGEN, CARBON, OXYGEN)
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements is most stable at a +1 oxidation state?
W) Indium
X) Thorium
Y) Germanium
Z) Thallium
ANSWER: Z) Thallium

X) Amorphous

Z) Heterogenous

W) Hydroboration Oxidation
X) Hydrohalogenation
Y) Ozonolysis
Z) Oxymercuration Demercuration

ANSWER: 1,2,3

ANSWER: 1

ANSWER: 30
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true regarding acid-catalyzed hydration reaction of alkenes?
W) It is a syn addition
X) An alcohol will be produced
Y) Water acts as the electrophile

8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Hydrogen peroxide goes through what type of reaction where oxygen is both oxidized and reduced?

8. BONUS Physics Short Answer What law states that the relationship between the temperature of a blackbody and the max wavelength at which it emits the most light is always equal to a constant?
ANSWER: WIEN’S LAW
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A carbonyl compound can be changed into an alkene via what reaction using a phosphorus ylide?

8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following reagents would be most effective for converting N,N-dimethylpropanamide into N,N-dimethylpropanamine?
W) NaBH4 and ether
X) NaBH4, and water
Y) LiAlH4 and ether
Z) LiAlH4, and water

8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Consider the coordination compound, K2[Cu(CN)4]. A coordinate covalent bond exists between which two species?
ANSWER: Cu2+ AND CN- (also accept: COPPER AND CYANIDE)
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The reaction of benzaldehyde in basic condition into benzyl alcohol and benzoic acid is an example of what disproportionation reaction where aldehydes are converted into an alcohol and a carboxylic acid.
ANSWER: CANNIZZARO REACTION

Y) Trimethyl silane

W) Insertion
X) Deletion

13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following most accurately describes the role of tropomyosin?
