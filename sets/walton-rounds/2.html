1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What gland secretes epinephrine?
ANSWER: ADRENAL GLAND (also accept: S)
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following that do not have chloroplasts 1) Fungi 2) Algae 3) Animals 4) Plants
ANSWER: 1 AND 3 (also accept: FUNGI AND ANIMALS)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice At STP, which element is most expected to exist as a monatomic gas?
W) Calcium
X) Hydrogen
Y) Nitrogen
Z) Neon
ANSWER: Z) Neon
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If there are 0.01 moles of hydronium ions in a 10L solution, what is the pH of the solution?
ANSWER: 3
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the term that describes a function that is the slope of the tangent line of another function?
ANSWER: DERIVATIVE
3. BONUS Math Short Answer In a 2 x 2 matrix, with the row 1 reading 3, 4; and row 2 reading 2, 5; find the determinant.
ANSWER: 7
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the names of both moons of Mars?
ANSWER: PHOBOS AND DEIMOS
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a vector quantity?
W) Work
X) Force
Y) Displacement
Z) Velocity
ANSWER: W) Work
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer When a 500 gram block is lifted up 3 meters, assuming g is 9.8 m/s2, what is the change in potential energy of the block in joules?
ANSWER: 14.7 JOULES
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Argonne National Laboratory recently researched a new way to observe diamond anvil pressure experiments. Most of the events that occur in these anvils are illuminated by x-ray and visible light. What type of light has photon energy between the energy levels of these two types of electromagnetic radiation?
W) Radio
X) Infrared
Y) Ultraviolet
Z) Gamma
ANSWER: Y) Ultraviolet
6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice What is the amount of radioactive material needed to maintain a nuclear chain reaction called?
W) Critical volume
X) Critical mass
Y) Sustainable volume
Z) Sustainable mass
ANSWER: X) Critical mass
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Fullerenes are allotropes of what element?
ANSWER: CARBON
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In a fullerene, each carbon atom is bonded to how many other carbon atoms?
ANSWER: 3
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice At which stage of meiosis do cells from a sperm cell precursor contain a haploid set of chromosomes, each consisting of two sister chromatids?
W) Anaphase I
X) Metaphase I
Y) Prophase II
Z) Anaphase II
ANSWER: Y) Prophase II
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What do you call the process where a phosphate group is added to a substance using the energy of sunlight?
ANSWER: PHOTOPHOSPHORYLATION (do not accept: PHOSPHORYLATION)
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Given that g = -10 m/s2, calculate the average velocity of a ball dropped from a building during the 5th second of its fall, assuming negligible air resistance.
W) -45 m/s
X) -50 m/s
Y) -55 m/s
Z) -60 m/s
ANSWER: X) -50 m/s
9. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A plane points its head 45 degrees east of north and flies at 141 km/hr. If the plane is flying into a wind blowing due west at 100 km/hr, what is the net direction and speed that the plane travels with respect to the ground, to the nearest whole number?
W) Due north at 141 km/hr
X) Due east at 141 km/hr
Y) Due north at 100 km/hr
Z) Due east at 100 km/hr
ANSWER: Y) Due north at 100 km/hr
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Convert r = 2019 csc(θ) from polar form to rectangular form.
ANSWER: Y = 2019
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the cosine of the angle which makes a minor arc with length 2π in a circle with a radius of 6.
ANSWER: 1/2
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Redshift of distant galaxies is evidence for which of the following?
W) Expansion of the universe
X) The Uncertainty Principle
Y) Black holes
Z) Dark matter
ANSWER: W) Expansion of the universe

ANSWER: NEPTUNE, URANUS, SATURN, JUPITER, MARS, EARTH, VENUS, MERCURY

ANSWER: FERMENTATION
