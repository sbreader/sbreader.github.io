1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Objects known as tests settle down on the ocean floor to produce the most common biogenous sediment called calcareous ooze. What is the origin of the many testa that form this?
W) Hard shells of microscopic organisms living near the surface.
X) Bones, teeth, and scales of fish from middle ocean layers.
Y) Deposits from filter feeders at the ocean bottom.
Z) Industrial waste products from human use.
ANSWER: W) Hard shells of microscopic organisms living near the surface.
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following sediment types is NOT a hydrogenous sediment, or minerals crystallized directly from seawater through various chemical reactions?
W) Inorganic Calcium Carbonate deposits
X) Manganese nodules
Y) Abyssal clay
Z) Evaporites
ANSWER: Y) Abyssal clay
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The height, length, and period of a wave depend in part on the distance that wind has traveled across open water. What is the scientific term for this?
ANSWER: fetch
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Energy in a wave is transmitted by which of the following motions of the individual water particles?
W) Wave-like
X) Circularly
Y) Horizontally
Z) Vertically
ANSWER: X) Circularly
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer This phenomenon primarily occurs when outgoing long wavelength radiation reradiated from Earth is absorbed by certain gas molecules in the atmosphere, such as water vapor or carbon dioxide. Name this effect which is responsible for much of global warming on Earth and Venus.
ANSWER: Greenhouse effect
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a commonly projected change of effect of global warming in the twenty-first century?
W) Fewer cold waves and frost days over nearly all land areas.
X) Intensifed floods associated with El Nino in many regions.
Y) Increase in precipitation events more many regions.
Z) Decreased drying over continental interiors and associated risk of drought.
ANSWER: Z) Decreased drying over continental interiors and associated risk of drought.
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice When rocks at great temperatures and pressures are subject to stresses that are unrecoverable, they are said to undergo what type of deformation?
W) Brittle deformation
X) Ductile deformation
Y) Fluid deformation
Z) Folding deformation
ANSWER: X) Ductile deformation
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Also known as downfolds or troughs, what dipping folds of structural geology contain younger areas known as hinges?
W) synclines
X) anticlines
Y) homoclines
Z) unicline
ANSWER: W) synclines
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A specific type of overturned fold, what structures lie on their sides so that a plane extending through the axis of the fold would be horizontal? They are frequently displayed as nappes, and are found in mountainous regions such as the Alps.
ANSWER: recumbent folds
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Coming from the Latin words for fire and fragment, what is the general term for ejected fragments from volcanic eruptions, most commonly caused by volatiles in rhylotic magma?
ANSWER: pyroclastic materials
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which is the term for pyroclasts that are larger than 64mm, and are ejected as incandescent lava?
W) tuff
X) lapilli
Y) blocks
Z) bombs
ANSWER: Z) bombs
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Based on the works of William Smith, what is one of the most important and basic principles in historical geology, which states "Fossil organisms succeed one another in a definite and determinable order, and therefore any time period can be recognized by its fossil content"?
ANSWER: principle of fossil succession
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In using radiometric dating to date rocks, which one of the most useful isotopes, with a beta-decay half-life of approximately 1.3 billion years, is used for time periods younger than a hundred thousand years as it is very abundant in minerals such as micas and feldspars?
W) U-235
X) Rb-87
Y) K-40
Z) F-20
ANSWER: Y) K-40

8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In which body of water in Nova Scotia can the highest diurnal tidal changes be found?
ANSWER: Bay of Fundy
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A parcel of humid air has a dry adiabatic lapse rate of 10°C/km and a wet adiabatic lapse rate of 6°C/km. If the environmental lapse rate is 5°C/km, what is the stability of the air?
W) absolute stability
X) conditional stability
Y) conditional instability
Z) absolute instability
ANSWER: W) absolute stability
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In the case of conditional instability, an air parcel must be elevated above its condensation level for instability to occur. Name the scientific terms for three of the four ways that air can be physically lifted above this level.
ANSWER: orographic lifting, frontal wedging, convergence, localized convection
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following conditions is NOT one that is required for tropical cyclogenesis?
W) Wind shear must be relatively weak
X) The cyclone must form within 500km of the equator
Y) Ocean temperatures generally must be above 26°C
Z) There must be a low-level disturbance to initiate formation
ANSWER: X) The cyclone must form within 500km of the equator

22. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following conditions is NOT one that must be met for an artesian well to flow?
