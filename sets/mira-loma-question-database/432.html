1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The "round" dance of honeybees returning to the nest indicates that
W) there is a source of food less than 50m from the hive
X) there is a source of food more than 50m from the hive, and in the same direction as the sun
Y) there is a source of food more than 50m from the hive, and in the opposite direction as the sun
Z) the queen is ready to mate with drones
ANSWER: W) there is a source of food less than 50m from the hive
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A bird which eats frogs catches a bright-red one, and quickly finds that it tastes bitter and poisonous. It is observed that this bird thereon avoids all frogs of bright color. This is an example of
W) imprinting
X) spatial learning
Y) cognitive mapping
Z) associative learning
ANSWER: Z) associative learning
