Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many different isomers are possible for the molecule C2H2Br4?
ANSWER: 2
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The relaxation of the Thoracic Diaphragm results in what action to the lungs?
W) Expansion of the lungs
X) Compression of the Lungs
Y) Brief expansion, followed by compression
Z) None of the Above
ANSWER: X) Compression of the Lungs
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A triangle is inscribed in a square which is inscribed in a pentagon and so on so forth to a 50 sided figure. What is the sum of all the sides in this shape?
ANSWER: 1272 sides
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Great Comet of 1811, as it was dubbed, was visible to the naked eye for nearly 9 months. It was surpassed in 1997 by Comet Hale- Bopp. How many times longer was Comet Hale-Bopp visible to the naked eye compared to The Great Comet?
ANSWER: 2 times
5. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Name the type of coal that produces the least amount of soot, while having one of the highest burning efficiencies.
W) Lignite
X) Coke
Y) Anthracite
Z) Bituminous Coal
ANSWER: Y) Anthracite
5. BONUS Energy Short Answer List the two countries with the most natural gas reserves known in the world.
ANSWER: Russia, Iran
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If the fundamental frequency of a wave is 440 Hertz, what is the frequency of the third overtone?
ANSWER: 1760 Hertz
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer August Kekule won the recognition for which he is honored today for his discovery of the structure of what molecule?
ANSWER: Benzene
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which nerve controls the movement of the Thoracic diaphragm, and which nerves is it composed of?
ANSWER: Phrenic Nerve; C3, C4, C5
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which diaphragm is placed higher up in the human body when it is relaxed?
ANSWER: Right Diaphragm
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The quality that allows listeners to differentiate between different instruments is called what?
ANSWER: Timbre
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What layer of the Earth is responsible for Continental Drift?
ANSWER: Asthenosphere
11. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the difference in magnitude between a magnitude 6 earthquake and a magnitude 2 earthquake?
ANSWER: 106 (also accept: 10 to the 6; 1,000,000 times)
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer An erg is the force done by a what over the distance of one centimeter? Also, how many joules is this quantity?
ANSWER: dyne, 10-7Joules
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If a circle is inscribed in an octagon, how many congruent pairs are there?
ANSWER: 8
12. BONUS Math Short Answer Match the following titles to the person they belong to. 1) Father of Geometry. 2) Founder of Infinitesimal Calculus.
ANSWER: Euclid, Leibniz
