2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following accurately describes the difference between uplift and subsidence?
W) Subsidence is the motion of a surface as it moves downward relative to a datum, while an uplift is an increase in elevation.
X) Subsidence is the downflow motion of water molecules as the water cycle occurs, while an uplift is when the water is evaporated.
Y) Subsidence is the downflow of magmatic rocks into the earth’s outer core, while an uplift is when magma flows out of the earth’s crust.
Z) Subsidence is the pumping in certain areas due to the convergence of water, while an uplift is when water is sucked due to the divergence of water.
ANSWER: W) Subsidence is the motion of a surface as it moves downward relative to a datum, while an uplift is an increase in elevation.
