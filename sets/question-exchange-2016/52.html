Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What molecule can cause the splitting of alkenes at their double bond locations, forming an intermediate with a five member ring?
W) Hydrochloric Acid
X) Mercury compounds
Y) Ozone
Z) Water
ANSWER: Y) Ozone
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What type of reaction involves the splitting of one molecule and formation of two free radicals?
ANSWER: Homolysis
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What term refers to the amount of genetic material passed on to the next generation not only by the individual, but by family members as well?
W) Relative fitness
X) Inclusive fitness
Y) Adaptation coefficient
Z) Evolution score
ANSWER: X) Inclusive fitness
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer You have to decide if it makes biological sense to save one of your relatives. If you do not save them, their chance of dying is 100%. If you save them, they are guaranteed to survive. You have a 50% chance of dying if you make an attempt to save them. You can produce one more offspring, while your relative can produce two. What is the minimum coefficient of relatedness required for it to make sense to save your relative?
ANSWER: 25% (also accept: 0.25)
6. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Approximately how many geothermal wells are located at The Geysers?
W) 150
X) 350
Y) 550
Z) 1000
ANSWER: X) 350
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term is used to describe the electromagnetic radiation with no discernible source left over from the early universe?
ANSWER: COSMIC MICROWAVE BACKGROUND (also accept: COSMIC BACKGROUND RADIATION; CMB; COSMIC MICROWAVE RADIATION)
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An object with a mass of 5 kg is dropped from a cliff. Just before touching the ground, it has a velocity of 10 m/s. Assuming gravitational acceleration as 10 m/s2 and air resistance is negligible, what is the height of the cliff?
ANSWER: 5 meters
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer A car accelerates from rest to a final velocity of 16 m/s over a distance of 32 m. What is the acceleration of the car in m/s2?
ANSWER: 4 m/s2
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the name for a hypothesis that states that there is no statistically significant relationship between two phenomena, and which is usually the first step in examining data?
ANSWER: Null hypothesis
11. BONUS Math Short Answer You determine that there is a linear relationship between two variables when the variable values are very small. You assume that this relationship holds even when the variables are very large. What is this practice called?
ANSWER: Extrapolation
