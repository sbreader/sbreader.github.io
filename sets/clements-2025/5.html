1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the mobile electron acceptor in the Electron Transport Chain that shuttles electrons from complexes I and II to complex III.
W) Hemoglobin
X) Cytochrome C
Y) Cytochrome A
Z) Ubiquinol
ANSWER: Z) Ubiquinol
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the cycle that can convert lactate to glucose, and is a form of gluconeogenesis?
ANSWER: THE CORI CYCLE
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice 1-bromopropane has a higher boiling point than 1-fluoropropane. Which of the following explains why?
W) 1-bromopropane displays hydrogen bonding that 1-fluoropropane does not.
X) 1-bromopropane has stronger dipole-dipole forces than 1-fluoropropane.
Y) The covalent bonds in 1-bromopropane are stronger than those in 1-fluoropropane.
Z) 1-bromopropane has more electrons and greater polarizability than 1-fluoropropane.
ANSWER: Z) 1-bromopropane has more electrons and greater polarizability than 1-fluoropropane.
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rounded to the nearest tenth of a degree, what is the freezing point of a solution containing 88 grams of sodium chloride dissolved in 1.00 kg of water?
ANSWER: -5.6 degrees Celsius
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Assume Alex is a blackbody. He emits a power P and a peak wavelength λ. If his peak wavelength triples, what is his new power in terms of P?
W) 3P
X) 81P
Y) P/3
Z) P/81
ANSWER: Z) P/81
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Blackbodies are considered perfect Lambertian radiators. For a Lambertian surface, the intensity of radiation is directly proportional to which of the following?
W) the sine of the angle between the observer’s line of sight and the normal
X) the cosine of the angle between the observer’s line of sight and the normal
Y) the sine squared of the angle between the observer’s line of sight and the normal
Z) the cosine squared of the angle between the observer’s line of sight and the normal
ANSWER: X) the cosine of the angle between the observer’s line of sight and the normal

ANSWER: 2520

X) 604
Y) 607
Z) 610

6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Laboratory are exploring the strength of electromagnetic interactions of charged particles. What dimensionless constant, related to the coupling constant in quantum electrodynamics and the fine splitting of the hydrogen spectral lines, is equal to approximately 1/137?
ANSWER: FINE-STRUCTURE CONSTANT
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Laboratory are investigating the intriguing properties of the helium-4 atom, which achieves superfluidity at low temperatures. The helium-4 atom contains an even number of nucleons, allowing it to be governed by what theory of quantum statistics?
ANSWER: BOSE-EINSTEIN STATISTICS (also accept: B-E)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What blue/green absorbing pigments are found in high concentration in carrots, and used in photosynthesis?
ANSWER: CAROTENOIDS

ANSWER: 1, 2
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three ligands in terms of increasing strength 1) H2O 2) I- 3) CN-
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The formation of high spin metal complexes due to coulombic repulsion energy is in apparent violation of what principle?
ANSWER: Aufbau principle
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What are the units of action?
ANSWER: KILOGRAMS * METERS2 / SECOND (also accept: JOULES * SECONDS)
9. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Action is equal to the integral over time of which of the following quantities?
W) Kinetic energy plus potential energy
X) Kinetic energy minus potential energy
Y) Potential energy minus kinetic energy
Z) Kinetic energy minus thermal energy
ANSWER: X) Kinetic energy minus potential energy

ANSWER: 420

18. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory are studying the behavior of light. They shine 2 intersecting laser beams perpendicularly to each other, and find that the laser beams appear not to interact with each other. Which of the following best explains this?
W) The electromagnetic force does not mediate any photon-only processes
X) Photons carry no mass and thus cannot interact with each other
Y) Photons do not generally interact with particles, massless or otherwise
Z) Light waves do not engage in interference
ANSWER: W) The electromagnetic force does not mediate any photon-only processes
