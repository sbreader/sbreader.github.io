BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In the reaction of HBr with propene, two products, 1-bromopropane, and 2-bromopropane are expected to be produced, yet only 2-bromopropane is present in significant amounts. 8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice If an object is moving at a velocity v relative to a stationary observer, which of the following is true?
