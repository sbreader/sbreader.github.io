1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What kingdom of organisms is primarily responsible for converting biomass into ethanol commercially?
ANSWER: FUNGI
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Scientists at SLAC are using the Linac Coherent Light Source to study protein crystal structures. Which of the following best describes a main challenge to this approach?
W) Protein crystallography is low-resolution
X) Protein crystallography cannot study ligand binding
Y) Some proteins crystallize into structures too large to study
Z) Some proteins cannot be crystallized
ANSWER: Z) Some proteins cannot be crystallized
