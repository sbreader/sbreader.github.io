1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A 1 liter solution contains 1 mole of acetic acid and 1 mole of sodium acetate. To the nearest milliliter, how much of a 1 molar solution of sodium hydroxide must be added in order to raise the pH of the solution by 1 unit?
ANSWER: 818
3. BONUS Math Short Answer A square and a regular hexagon each have a circumradius of 1. What is the ratio of the area of the hexagon to the area of the square?
ANSWER: 3√3/4 (also accept: 3 ROOT 3 OVER 4)
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following types of magnetism is characterized by a lack of unpaired electrons?
W) Ferromagnetism
X) Ferrimagnetism
Y) Paramagnetism
Z) Diamagnetism
ANSWER: Z) Diamagnetism
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer A ball is dropped from a height of 30 meters and repeatedly bounces on the ground, rebounding to half of its previous height every time. What is the limit of the total distance that the ball travels?
ANSWER: 90
6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Fermilab are constructing liquid argon chambers in order to better detect hypothetical sterile neutrinos. Sterile neutrinos are only able to interact with which of the following fundamental forces?
W) Electromagnetic
X) Gravitational
Y) Strong
Z) Weak
ANSWER: X) Gravitational
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The inheritance of the Igf-2 gene in mice is known for only expressing the phenotype of the father. This is an example of what phenomena which is mediated by DNA methylation?
ANSWER: GENOMIC IMPRINTING (also accept: IMPRINTING) (do not accept: EPIGENETICS)
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the efficiency of a Carnot refrigerator with a hot reservoir of 500 Kelvin and a cold reservoir of 100 Kelvin?
ANSWER: 80%
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer In units of kilogram meters squared, what is the moment of inertia of a uniform cylinder of radius 3 meters and mass 4 kilograms about its central axis?
ANSWER: 18
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the depth in the ocean at which carbon dioxide, water, and bicarbonate are in chemical equilibrium?
ANSWER: CARBONATE COMPENSATION DEPTH (also accept: CCD)
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Mass spectrometry can be used to analyze a chemical species by measuring the ratio of its mass to what other property?
ANSWER: CHARGE
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 statements regarding the one-dimensional particle in a box model that are true 1) The particle can have exactly 0 energy 2) The particle can never escape the box 3) The particle's energy is directly proportional to its mass.
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Phillip makes a set of the 9 smallest positive multiples of 1000. Jay takes Phillip’s set and increases the smallest number by 11 and decreases the largest number by 4. Identify all of the following 3 quantities that are bigger for Jay’s set than Phillip’s set 1) Mean 2) Median 3) Range.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
11. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the smallest positive argument, in degrees, of a complex number z such that z4 = –1?
ANSWER: 45
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Fermilab are investigating the induced wave broadening by the generalized uncertainty principle. Classically, this wave broadening may be caused by the addition of multiple waves that are in phase with each other, resulting in a final wave that is more intense than its substituents. What is the term for this phenomenon?
ANSWER: CONSTRUCTIVE INTERFERENCE (do not accept: INTERFERENCE)
14. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements best describes the proportionality between the drag force and the velocity of a relatively small object traveling through a viscous fluid?
W) Drag force is proportional to velocity
X) Drag force is proportional to velocity squared
Y) Drag force is proportional to velocity cubed
Z) Drag force is proportional to velocity to the fourth
ANSWER: W) Drag force is proportional to velocity
15. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Two complex numbers x and y have the property that, for a certain positive integer n, x to the power of n equals y to the power of n. Which of the following statements regarding x and y must be true?
W) They have the same argument
X) They have the same complex conjugate
Y) They have the same magnitude
Z) They have the same real part
ANSWER: Y) They have the same magnitude
15. BONUS Math Short Answer A computer starts printing the positive integers in ascending order. How many digits are necessary to print the first 1000 integers?
ANSWER: 2893
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The line segment connecting the origin and the point (40, 40) is drawn. Including its endpoints, how many lattice points does the segment contain?
ANSWER: 41
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer A hollow sphere with radius 2 meters and mass 3 kg uniformly distributed around its surface starts rolling without slipping down a 75 meter long slope from rest with an angle of declination of 30 degrees. What percentage of the sphere’s kinetic energy is rotational kinetic energy?
ANSWER: 40
20. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding glacial flow is not true?
W) Glacial flow is mechanistically driven by basal slip and plastic deformation
X) Polar glaciers flow primarily through basal slip
Y) The bottom of a polar glacier flows slower than the top
Z) The sides of a warm glacier flow slower than the center
ANSWER: X) Polar glaciers flow primarily through basal slip

W) Ammonia
X) Dichloromethane
Y) Phosphorus pentachloride
Z) Sulfur hexafluoride
ANSWER: W) Ammonia
