Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following ecologists wrote The Fitness of the Environment?
W) Arthur Cain
X) Rachel Carson
Y) Lawrence Henderson
Z) John Beard
ANSWER: Y) Lawrence Henderson
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which atoms of water molecules have a partial negative charge?
ANSWER: oxygens
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the maximum number of hydrogen bonds that a single water molecule is capable of forming?
ANSWER: 4
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for a mixture that is completely homogenous?
ANSWER: solution
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for number of moles of solute per liter of solution?
ANSWER: molarity
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In a sample of water at human body temperature, about how many of the molecules will be undergoing hydrogen bonds with four other water molecules?
W) 1%
X) 5%
Y) 15%
Z) 50%
ANSWER: Y) 15%
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following refers to the sphere of water molecules that surround dissolved ions in aqueous solutions?
W) ionization molecules
X) aqueous sphere
Y) hydration shell
Z) dissolving atoms
ANSWER: Y) hydration shell
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer To two significant figures, how many Joules are one calorie?
ANSWER: 4.2
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following 3 substances in order of increasing acidity 1. Blood 2. Rainwater 3. Urine
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Ice is about how much less dense than water?
W) 5% less dense
X) 10% less dense
Y) 15% less dense
Z) 20% less dense
ANSWER: X) 10% less dense
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the pH of human blood?
W) 7
X) 7.4
Y) 7.8
Z) 8.2
ANSWER: X) 7.4
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What property of water allows insects to walk on it?
ANSWER: surface tension
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is hydrophilic?
W) cell membrane exterior
X) granite
Y) cotton
Z) estrogen
ANSWER: Y) cotton
