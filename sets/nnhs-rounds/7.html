1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What general term, which encompasses damping, refers to the gradual loss of amplitude of a wave as it propagates through a medium?
ANSWER: ATTENUATION (also accept: EXTINCTION)
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following must be constant in coherent light?
W) Frequency
X) Phase difference
Y) Spatial orientation
Z) Photon energy
ANSWER: X) Phase difference
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following four cells that are considered antigen-presenting cells 1) Dendritic cell 2) B cell 3) Cytotoxic T cell 4) Macrophage.
ANSWER: 1, 2, AND 4
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What immune cells secrete histamine and circulate through the bloodstream?
ANSWER: BASOPHILS
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for seamounts that are weathered down until they become submerged and flat-topped?
ANSWER: GUYOTS
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer During the summer, the sun moves through what constellation?
ANSWER: ORION
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following equations represents a cardioid?
W) r = 2
X) r = 3 + 2cos(θ)
Y) r = 4 – 4sin(θ)
Z) r = 1 + 5sin(θ)
ANSWER: Y) r = 4 – 4sin(θ)
4. BONUS Math Short Answer A marble factory adds a red mark to every other marble, a blue mark to every third marble, and a green mark to every fifth marble. After the factory produces 600 marbles, how many marbles will have both a red and a blue mark, but no green mark?
ANSWER: 80
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In aqueous solution, chloride will form a precipitate with which of the following cations?
W) Cu2+
X) Zn2+
Y) Ni3+
Z) Pb2+
ANSWER: Z) Pb2+
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of a cyclic ether with a three membered ring that is highly reactive due to its high ring strain?
ANSWER: EPOXIDE
6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Researchers at Argonne National Lab are studying ways to increase the scalability of efficient battery technology. One way they are doing this is by replacing the catalyst in polymer electrolyte membrane fuel cells. What metal typically serves as the catalyst?
ANSWER: PLATINUM
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers at Argonne National Lab have developed the Oleo sponge, which has shown promise for cleaning oil spills from bodies of water. In particular, the Oleo sponge can capture what extremely thin layer of oil that coats the water’s surface and is traditionally tough to remove?
ANSWER: OIL SHEEN
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice At high temperatures, how many vibrational degrees of freedom does ozone have?
W) 2
X) 3
Y) 6
Z) 9
ANSWER: X) 3
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following four pairs that represent thermodynamic conjugate pairs 1) Pressure and volume 2) Enthalpy and Helmholtz free energy 3) Gibbs free energy and mole number 4) Chemical potential and particle number.
ANSWER: 1 AND 4
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What enzyme, commonly found in retroviruses, transcribes an RNA template into a DNA strand?
ANSWER: REVERSE TRANSCRIPTASE
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following four parts of the circulatory system by the order that oxygenated blood from the lungs will travel 1) Left atrium 2) Pulmonary artery 3) Pulmonary vein 4) Right ventricle.
ANSWER: 3,1,4,2
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice According to the no-hair theorem, black holes are characterized by all of the following properties EXCEPT:
W) Mass
X) Angular momentum
Y) Temperature
Z) Electric charge
ANSWER: Y) Temperature
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following three salts from lowest to highest concentration in ocean water 1) Sodium chloride 2) Sodium sulfate 3) Magnesium chloride.
ANSWER: 2,3,1
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the largest number of $3.50 sandwiches that one can buy with $20?
W) 4
X) 5
Y) 6
Z) 7
ANSWER: X) 5
10. BONUS Math Short Answer A jar contains 4 red marbles and 6 blue marbles. Marbles are randomly removed from the jar, one at a time, until no marbles remain in the jar. What is the probability that the last marble removed is red?
ANSWER: 2/5
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What scientist used a cathode ray tube to discover electrons?
ANSWER: J.J. THOMSON (also accept: THOMSON)
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Osmium is often considered to be one of the densest elements. What is the coordination number of a pure osmium crystal?
ANSWER: 12
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Lab are using low energy electron diffraction to examine the structure of graphene. They found that because the electron position normal to the layer is precisely known, their wave vector must have a spread, which is transferred to the diffracted electrons. What principle is responsible for this effect?
ANSWER: UNCERTAINTY PRINCIPLE (also accept: HEISENBERG UNCERTAINTY PRINCIPLE)
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Lab are studying the properties of materials under high pressures. These researchers are part of what field of physics that studies matter where the number of constituents is large and the interaction between them is strong?
ANSWER: CONDENSED MATTER PHYSICS
13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A positive pion and a proton interact, forming a neutrino and two other particles. Which of the following could be the other two products of this decay?
W) Proton and proton
X) Positron and neutron
Y) Photon and proton
Z) Photon and positron
ANSWER: W) Proton and proton
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer Oil with a density of 0.8 grams per cubic centimeter falls at a rate of 2 liters per second from a height of 10 meters onto a cart, which is moving at a constant speed of 5 meters per second in the absence of friction. What force must be applied to the cart to maintain its speed?
ANSWER: 8 NEWTONS
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A mother with blood type A has a child with blood type O. How many possibilities are there for the father’s genotype?
ANSWER: 3
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What glycoproteins connect integrins to the extracellular matrix?
ANSWER: FIBRONECTINS

W) Limonite
X) Hematite
Y) Magnetite
Z) Apatite
ANSWER: W) Limonite
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following four planets that can NOT be in opposition with the Sun 1) Mercury 2) Venus 3) Mars 4) Jupiter.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A data set has a mean of 30 and a standard deviation of 5. What is the z-score of the data point 37?
ANSWER: 1.4
16. BONUS Math Short Answer Dan is completing a 300 mile road trip and drives at an average speed of 50 miles per hour for the first 150 miles of the trip. What must his average speed be during the remaining 150 miles of the road trip to average a speed of 60 miles per hour over the course of the entire trip?
ANSWER: 75
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What law states that the rate of effusion is inversely proportional to the square root of the molar mass of a gas?
ANSWER: GRAHAM’S LAW
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements about acids that are true 1) Increasing the concentration of a weak acid increases percent dissociation 2) Phosphoric acid is a triprotic acid 3) The pH of pure water is independent of temperature.
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab are using the Sloan Digital Sky Survey to examine the expansion of the universe during the middle 11 billion years of its history. X) Fine structure constant
Y) Cosmological constant
Z) Hubble’s constant

ANSWER: LIGNIN
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In general relativity, what statement describes the indistinguishability of a gravitational force and a fictitious force caused by a non-inertial reference frame?
ANSWER: EQUIVALENCE PRINCIPLE
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer Light that consists of two plane waves with equal magnitudes and a phase difference of 90° exhibits what type of polarization?
ANSWER: CIRCULAR (do not accept: ELLIPTICAL)
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In which of the following environments would semelparity be most favored?
W) A relatively constant environment
X) An environment in which offspring survival rate is low
Y) An environment in which adult survival rate is high
