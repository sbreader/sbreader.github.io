1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the active form of vitamin D that raises blood calcium?
ANSWER: CALCITRIOL
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Two organisms, aa bb and a+a+ bb, produce 1000 offspring. Among them: 442 are a+a b+b, 508 are aa bb, 24 are a+a bb, and 26 are aa b+b. What is the recombination frequency between the two genes?
ANSWER: 5% (also accept: 0.05)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements correctly describes the behavior of pure water as temperature increases?
W) The pH of water increases because more H+ ions are produced at higher temperatures.
X) The pH of water remains constant at 7.00 because water is neutral at all temperatures.
Y) The pH of water decreases, but the water remains neutral.
Z) The pH of water decreases, making it acidic at higher temperatures due to increased ionization.
ANSWER: Y) The pH of water decreases, but the water remains neutral.
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The equilibrium constant of a reaction at 200 Kelvin is 7.4 times the equilibrium constant of the same reaction at 300 Kelvin. To two significant figures in kilojoules per mole, what is the standard change in enthalpy of the reaction?
ANSWER: -10
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A parallel-plate capacitor is connected to a battery and remains connected while a dielectric material with dielectric constant k is inserted between the plates. Which of the following quantities increases? 1) The surface charge 2) The capacitance 3) The electric field 4) The stored energy.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 4
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer The potential energy of a charged spherical conductor of radius R carrying total charge Q can be expressed as Q squared divided by (A times epsilon naught R), where A is a constant. What is A?
ANSWER: 8π

6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Sandia National Laboratory are using IR spectroscopy to determine the presence of functional groups on organic molecules. 7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What biological phenomenon describes how population size and individual fitness are positively correlated?
ANSWER: ALLEE EFFECT
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the principle that helps create the simplest phylogenetic tree with the least amount of evolutionary changes to explain the data?
ANSWER: MAXIMUM PARSIMONY (also accept: PARSIMONY; OCCAM’S RAZOR)
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The triple point of ammonia is 195.40 K at 0.006 bar, while its melting point is 195.42 K at 1 bar pressure. Which statement about ammonia is correct?
W) Solid ammonia is denser than liquid ammonia at 195.4 K.
X) Ammonia vapor cannot coexist with liquid ammonia at equilibrium at 195.5 K.
Y) Ammonia vapor cannot coexist with solid ammonia at equilibrium at 195.3 K.
Z) Solid ammonia and liquid ammonia cannot coexist at equilibrium at 195.5 K.
ANSWER: W) Solid ammonia is denser than liquid ammonia at 195.4 K.
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The electrolysis of water is endothermic, requiring 286 kilojoules of energy to decompose one mole of water at 25°C. Given that the enthalpy of formation of hydrogen peroxide is -136 kilojoules per mole, what is the standard enthalpy in kilojoules for the decomposition of one mole of hydrogen peroxide into water and oxygen?

14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A spontaneous endothermic chemical reaction takes place at constant pressure and volume in a well-insulated container. Which of the following statements is true as the reaction proceeds?

X) The temperature increases while the reactants’ internal energy decreases.
Y) The temperature decreases while the reactants’ internal energy increases.
