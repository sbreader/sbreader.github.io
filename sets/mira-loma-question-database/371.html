1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which protozoan parasite causes malaria in humans?
ANSWER: Plasmodium falciparum
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following epochs from oldest to youngest: Ediacaran, Paleogene, Permian, and Devonian.
ANSWER: 1, 4, 3, 2
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following are parasites which can only survive in animal cells?
W) Cyanobacteria
X) Spirochites
Y) Chlamydia
Z) Gram-positive
ANSWER: Y) Chlamydia
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Multiple: Which of the following is false?
W) Ciliates have two types of nuclei: tiny micronuclei and large macronuclei.
X) Stramenophiles are known for their flagella.
Y) Diatoms are unicellular algae that have a unique wall made of hydrated silica.
Z) The largest and most complex algae are brown algae, most marine and unicellular.
ANSWER: Z) The largest and most complex algae are brown algae, most marine and unicellular.
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Multiple: The difference between lycophytes and conifers is that
W) Lycophytes are bryophytes while conifers are vascular plants.
X) Conifers require seeds to spread while lycophytes lack seeds.
Y) Lycophytes belong to phylum Lycophyta while conifers are in Anthophyta.
Z) Conifers have twice as many species as do lycophytes.
ANSWER: X) Conifers require seeds to spread while lycophytes lack seeds.
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Using numbers or words, denote which of the following belong to phylum coniferophyta. 1) Common juniper 2) Douglas fir 3) European larch 4) Sequoia
ANSWER: All
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for small, raised areas which dot the periderm, in which living cells can exchange gases with outside air?
ANSWER: Lenticels
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer Short: in numbers or words, denote which of the following four are true 1) Tracheids and vessel elements are tubular, short cells that function while alive. 2) Collenchyma cells have thicker primary walls than parenchyma and support young parts of the plant shoot. 3) Sclerenchyma cells are less rigid than collenchyma cells and can elongate. 4) Most parenchyma cells lack secondary walls.
ANSWER: 2, 4
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Short: Leukocytes recognize microbes using receptors named what?
ANSWER: TLR (also accept: Toll-like receptor)
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Assume an adult human at rest with a heart rate of about 72 beats per minute. In numbers or words, order the following steps of the cardiac cycle from which takes more time to occur to which takes the least time to occur 1) Atrial and ventricular diastole 2) Ventricular systole and atrial diastole 3) Atrial systole and ventricular diastole
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The period of a pendulum with length 1000 m is what?
W) 20 pi
X) 30 pi
Y) 40 pi
Z) 50 pi
ANSWER: W) 20 pi
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Calculate the change in entropy if 400 Joules are absorbed during a reversible, constant temperature process between two equilibrium states if the constant temperature is 300 Kelvin.
ANSWER: 4/3 J/K
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer The reversible method of transforming electrical energy into mechanical energy is called what?
ANSWER: Piezoelectric effect
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The solutions to x3 +2x2-x-2 are what?
ANSWER: +/- 1, -2
8. BONUS Math Short Answer The distance between the foci of (x-3)2/ 10-(y-4)2/20 =1 is what? (Radical answers accepted)
ANSWER: 2 x root 30
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the dot product of <1,2,3> and <3,4,2>?
ANSWER: 17
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Given polar coordinates 3, pi, convert to Cartesian.
ANSWER: (-3, 0)
11. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the inverse of x/ root (x2 +7) .
ANSWER: Root 7 x/ (root 1-x squared)
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Give the formula for Formic acid.
ANSWER: HCHO2
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Multiple: Which is false?
W) Methanol is a primary alcohol
X) Homologous series differ from the next member by a CH group
Y) Alkanes, aka paraffins or saturated hydrocarbons, only have single covalent bonds between carbons
Z) Alkyl groups have general formula Cn H2n+1
ANSWER: X) Homologous series differ from the next member by a CH group
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Denote in numbers/ words: Which of the following ions has -2 charge? 1) carbonate 2) Dichromate 3)Silicate 4) Manganese
ANSWER: 123
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In numbers or words, denote which of the following have the same electron pair arrangement as molecular shape. 1) Hydrogen sulfide 2) Carbon tetrachloride 3) Aluminum chloride
ANSWER: 2 and 3
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Multiple: Which of the following is not a reason to use AC?
W) Analogs are constantly changing because currents and voltages in electric circuits constantly change.
X) Radio waves are high-frequency radio signals.
Y) DC cannot flow through semiconductors.
Z) Frequencies of wireless communication have advanced from kHz to GHz.
ANSWER: Y) DC cannot flow through semiconductors.
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer Find the total resistance of a parallel circuit with two resistors. The first has a value of 40 ohms and the second a value of 10 ohms.
ANSWER: 8 ohms
16. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Multiple: In a solar cell, which of the following is true?
W) N-type and P-type silicon attraction cause the electric field that powers the cell.
X) At the junction, it becomes easier for P-type silicon to cross over to the N-type.
Y) The electric field acts as a diode and pushes P-type electrons into N-type.
Z) Photons produce disruptions of neutrality, sending electrons to P-type and causing holes in N-type.
ANSWER: W) N-type and P-type silicon attraction cause the electric field that powers the cell.
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In numbers or words, denote which of the following is false about biofuels 1) Fermented corn produces ethanol, which is a smog-reducing additive in gasoline. 2) The E in E85 for fuel rating is for ethanol. 3) Soybeans are more useful than corn for biodiesel. 4) Separating glycerin from the oil's fatty acid alkyl esters using a catalyst is a step in creating biodiesel.
ANSWER: None
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Multiple: Which devices extend perpendicular to the direction of wave travel and capture or reflect the power of the wave?
W) Offshore capture
X) Oscillating wave device
Y) Terminator devices
Z) Point absorber
ANSWER: Y) Terminator devices
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer Calculate the Lagrangian of a ball at height 3 m which has a current velocity of 20 m/s and a mass of 5 kg.
ANSWER: 850 JOULES
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Metal poor stars are also known as what population type?
ANSWER: Population 2
18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Earth/ Astro Multiple: Which of the following is false about the big bang nucleosynthesis?
W) It finished when the universe was about 3 minutes old.
X) The temperature dropped below that of possible nuclear fission.
Y) Principally, lithium was not produced.
Z) Deuterium and helium-4 were among the light elements produced.
ANSWER: Y) Principally, lithium was not produced.
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of volcano is the most explosive, producing a caldera after explosions due to vent collapses?
ANSWER: Rhyolite caldera complex
19. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Earth/ Astro Multiple: Which of the following is a compact quasi-stellar object associated with a probable supermassive black hole at the center of an active, giant elliptical galaxy?
W) Quasar
X) Seyfert galaxies
Y) Clustered population
Z) Blazar
ANSWER: W) Quasar
