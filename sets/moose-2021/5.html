Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three muscle tissues which are not voluntary 1) Skeletal muscle 2) Smooth muscle 3) Cardiac muscle
ANSWER: 2 and 3
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What fibrous connective tissue is made up of collagen and attaches bone to bone?
ANSWER: LIGAMENT
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What effect is responsible for the rotation of air in large storms such as hurricanes and anticyclones?
ANSWER: CORIOLIS EFFECT
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Friction near Earth's surface usually slows down winds and makes the Coriolis effect less pronounced. What is the general name for winds high in the atmosphere that have very little friction and are affected more by the Coriolis effect?
ANSWER: GEOSTROPHIC WINDS
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are studying the bending and distortion of light around extremely massive objects such as galaxy clusters. What is the name for this effect?
ANSWER: GRAVITATIONAL LENSING
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The Kepler Space Telescope is being used to detect the presence of exoplanets and is specifically looking for ones like Earth that could host life. The vast majority of these planets are discovered by the slight dimming of a star as a planet passes between it and Earth. What is the name for this event?
ANSWER: TRANSIT
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The element palladium is found in which of the following sections of the periodic table?
W) Transition metals
X) Lanthanides
Y) Actinides
Z) Halogens
ANSWER: W) Transition metals
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The valence electrons of the transition metal elements primarily fill what subshell in their atomic structure?
ANSWER: D
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following kingdoms of organisms contains Saccharomyces cerevisiae?
W) Fungi
X) Animalia
Y) Archaebacteria
Z) Plantae
ANSWER: W) Fungi
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of reflector telescope has the eyepiece directly behind the objective mirror?
ANSWER: CASSEGRAIN
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A gas that experiences enough pressure to directly become a solid is said to have gone through what phase transition?
ANSWER: DEPOSITION
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer List all of the following characteristics of organisms that apply to the kingdom Fungi 1) Eukaryotic 2) Heterotrophic 3) Photosynthetic 4) Vascular
ANSWER: 1 and 2
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A test consists of 4 true/false questions, 8 questions with four options, and 3 questions with options true, false, and neither. If you guess on every question, what is the expected value of correct answers?
ANSWER: 5
7. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice To recycle polyethylene, the chains are chopped up to create smaller chains that are 20-30 carbons long. Which of the following can these hydrocarbon chains be used for?
W) Motor oil
X) Synthetic rubber
Y) Pen ink
Z) Nylon
ANSWER: W) Motor oil
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the only phylum whose members contain no true tissues?
ANSWER: PORIFERA (also accept: SPONGE)
8. BONUS Math Short Answer A test consists entirely of true or false questions. If the chance of you knowing the correct answer is 80% and there is a 10% probability that you accidentally put an answer you didn't intend to, what is your expected score expressed as a percent?
ANSWER: 82% (also accept: 82)
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following can be considered a strong base?
W) KOH
X) H2SO4
Y) CH3COOH
Z) H2O
ANSWER: W) KOH
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following phyla which contain organisms that have bilateral symmetry 1) Cnidaria 2) Platyhelminthes 3) Mollusca 4) Porifera
ANSWER: 2 and 3
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer An object that is 0.1 parsecs away has, to the nearest arcsecond, what parallax angle?
ANSWER: 10
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following compounds that are amphoteric 1) Water 2) Aluminum oxide 3) Sulfuric acid
ANSWER: 1 and 2
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at UW Madison are studying how to use brain cell grafts to reverse symptoms of neural diseases. These grafts utilize what group of pluripotent cells that can be differentiated into almost any type of cell in the body?
ANSWER: STEM CELLS
11. BONUS Math Short Answer Set L consists of all positive integers that are not square or cube numbers in increasing order. What is the 57th term of set L?
ANSWER: 67
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The deadliest extinction event in Earth's history, commonly known as the Great Dying, occurred approximately how many million years ago?
W) 1.8
X) 66
Y) 252
Z) 541
ANSWER: Y) 252
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following telescopes would be best used for imaging an accretion disk?
W) Spitzer
X) Hubble
Y) Arecibo
Z) Chandra
ANSWER: Z) Chandra
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If a wave with a frequency of 400 Hertz interferes with a wave with a frequency of 900 Hertz, what is the beat frequency produced in Hertz?
ANSWER: 500
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying the causes and prevention of diseases affecting the brain. What common neurodegenerative disease is thought to be caused in part by the accumulation of amyloid-beta plaques between neurons?
ANSWER: ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying how radioactive iodine damages the body. Radioactive iodine is especially dangerous to humans because it tends to accumulate in what gland?
W) Pituitary
X) Adrenal
Y) Hypothalamus
Z) Thyroid
ANSWER: Z) Thyroid
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Most well-known dinosaur species, including T. rex, Velociraptor, Stegosaurus, and Brachiosaurus, lived in what two geologic periods?
ANSWER: JURASSIC AND CRETACEOUS (also accept: CRETACEOUS AND JURASSIC)
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the 3-carbon molecule that is a product of the Calvin-Benson cycle and is essential for the production of glucose in photosynthesis?
ANSWER: G3P (also accept: GLYCERALDEHYDE-3-PHOSPHATE; PGAL)
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 5 kilogram block undergoes simple harmonic motion after being attached to a spring with a spring constant of 125 Newtons per meter. If the maximum displacement of the block from the equilibrium point is 4 meters, what is the maximum velocity attainable by the block in SI base units?
ANSWER: 20
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What clastic sedimentary rock is composed of large fragmented particles that are cemented together by diagenesis?
ANSWER: BRECCIA
16. BONUS Energy Short Answer Scientists at the Joint Genome Institute and Environmental Molecular Sciences Laboratory are studying ruminants like moose to understand how they turn substances like twigs or bark into useful energy. This research can be used to improve the production of what general class of fuels?
ANSWER: BIOFUELS
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true about prokaryotic DNA?
W) It is present in the cytoplasm
X) It is circular instead of linear
Y) It does not form chromatin
Z) It contains introns in addition to exons
ANSWER: Z) It contains introns in addition to exons
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following that are direct outputs of the Calvin Benson Cycle 1) Glucose 2) ATP 3) NADPH 4) NADP+
ANSWER: 4 only
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Ames Lab are conducting research on plastic recycling. One type of plastic they are studying is polyethylene, the most common type of plastic in the world. Polyethylene is made up of repeating monomers that have what chemical formula?
ANSWER: C2H4
18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A geologist is taking samples of rock from the island of Hawai'i. Which of the following rocks would be most prevalent in their samples?
W) Granite
X) Sandstone
Y) Basalt
Z) Hornfels
ANSWER: Y) Basalt
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer Deletion of three nucleotides from a strand of single-stranded DNA could cause which of the following to occur 1) Loss of an amino acid 2) Frameshift mutation 3) Nonsense mutation
ANSWER: 1 only
38. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If tanθ is 60/61, what is the sum of all the possible values of sinθ?
ANSWER: 0
39. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the magnification of a telescope with a focal length of 100 cm when using an eyepiece with a focal length of 5mm?
ANSWER: 200X (also accept: 200; 200 times)
40. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the focus of a parabola with equation y=x2/8?
ANSWER: (0, 2)
41. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In the Lewis dot structure of the ammonia molecule, how many lone pairs are on the central atom?
ANSWER: 1
42. BONUS Math Multiple Choice The graph of f(x) is decreasing on the intervals (-∞,-c) and (0, c) and is increasing on the intervals (-c, 0) and (c,∞). If c is a positive real number, which of the following could be equal to f(x)?
W) x2-2
X) x3-2
Y) |x2-2|
Z) |x3-2|
ANSWER: Y) |x2-2|
43. BONUS Math Short Answer An ellipse has equation ax2+by2=c2. If c is quadrupled and a is halved, by what factor is the area of the ellipse increased?
ANSWER: 32
44. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the molecular geometry of the xenon tetrafluoride molecule?
ANSWER: SQUARE PLANAR
