1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Give the molecular structure of the following three compounds, respectively 1) ammonia 2) boron trichloride 3) iodine heptafluoride.

1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What two bond angle measures are present in a pentagonal bipyramidal geometry?
ANSWER: 72° AND 90°
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice According to the Benchmark Model, which of the following components of the universe is dominant today?
W) matter
X) radiation
Y) empty space
Z) cosmological constant
ANSWER: Z) cosmological constant
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What Greek letter denotes the ratio of the current density of the universe to the critical density of the universe?
ANSWER: OMEGA
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of immunity arises from the ability of T and B cells to retain memory of each specific pathogen they encounter?
ANSWER: ACQUIRED IMMUNITY
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer After hematopoiesis occurs in the bone marrow, differentiated T cells migrate to what organ in the human body?
ANSWER: THYMUS
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following experiments first led to the proposition of the Lorentz-Fitzgerald contraction?
W) Michelson-Morley experiment
X) Hafele-Keating experiment
Y) Miller oil drop experiment
Z) Davisson-Germer experiment
ANSWER: W) Michelson-Morley experiment
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer By number, identify all of the following statements which are true about potentials 1) The minimum of any potential curve behaves as a quadratic 2) The Lagrangian is the quantity calculated by subtracting the potential energy from the kinetic energy of a system 3) The gravitational potential of a system approaches infinity as the objects travel infinitely far apart.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the only type of moraine which lies perpendicular to the path of the glacier?

W) Einstein-de Sitter universe
X) Milne universe
Y) Friedmann universe

ANSWER: a = √b
7. BONUS Math Short Answer Each side of the base of a square pyramid is reduced by 90%. By what factor must the height of the square pyramid be multiplied in order to maintain the same volume as the original square pyramid?
ANSWER: 100
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The delay in voltage response of a MOSFET is usually modeled using what circuit element?
ANSWER: CAPACITOR
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer By name or number, list all of the following statements which are true about Gauss’s law 1) The electric field due to any spherical distribution of charge is equal to that of an equivalent point charge at its geometric center 2) The flux through a closed surface by a point charge outside the surface is equal to its quantity of charge divided by the electric permittivity constant 3) The flux through a closed surface of arbitrary shape or size is the same as that through a spherical surface, as long as the enclosed charge is the same.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following convergence patterns does NOT result in subduction or obduction?
W) oceanic-oceanic convergence
X) oceanic-continental convergence
Y) continental-continental convergence
Z) none of the above
ANSWER: Y) continental-continental convergence

ANSWER: ISOSTATIC REBOUND
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of these describes an adaptation to an arid environment?
W) A short loop of Henle, which allows more filtrate to be removed from the blood
X) A short loop of Henle, which allows less water to be removed from the filtrate
Y) A long loop of Henle, which allows more filtrate to be removed from the blood
Z) A long loop of Henle, which allows more water to be removed from the filtrate
ANSWER: Z) A long loop of Henle, which allows more water to be removed from the filtrate

19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Organisms that reproduce sexually exhibit zygotic, gametic, or sporic meiosis. One way to determine the type of life cycle an organism has is by:

X) determining if gametes are multicellular or unicellular
Y) observing embryonic development
Z) determining when in the life cycle fertilization occurs
ANSWER: W) comparing the diploid and haploid forms of the organism
