1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following features used for waste excretion are found in insects?
W) Flame Bulbs
X) Metanephridia
Y) Intestines
Z) Malpighian Tubules

1. BONUS Biology Short Answer In a population of 100 red pandas at Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium, the blue-eyed allele is dominant to the black-eyed allele. If 49 red pandas are black-eyed, how many pandas are heterozygous for this trait?
ANSWER: 42
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the coefficient of performance of an ideal Carnot refrigerator with a low temperature reservoir at 400 K and a high temperature reservoir at 500 K?
ANSWER: 4
2. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice An object in the solar system is traveling in such a way that its velocity is always equal to the escape velocity from that position. Which of the following best describes the body’s trajectory?
W) Circular
X) Elliptical
Y) Parabolic
Z) Hyperbolic
ANSWER: Y) Parabolic
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the formal charge of carbon in methanol?
ANSWER: 0
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why amides are planar around the nitrogen atom?
W) Steric hindrance
X) Resonance
Y) The complete presence of a double bond
Z) The presence of a positive charge
ANSWER: X) Resonance
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In base 4, what is the value of 0.121212 … repeating?
ANSWER: 2/5

ANSWER: 4π√2/3

ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which specific type of supernova is most directly associated with a white dwarf exceeding the Chandrasekhar limit?
ANSWER: Type Ia (do not accept: Type I)

ANSWER: 1 and 3
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Place the following four events of mammalian digestion in the order that they occur 1) Food enters the mouth 2) Peristalsis pushes the bolus down 3) Amylase begins digestion of polysaccharides 4) Gastrin is released into the bloodstream and promotes gastric acid production and secretion
ANSWER: 4,1,3,2

20. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why ground-based ultraviolet telescopes are uncommon?
W) UV light is absorbed by the Earth’s atmosphere
X) UV light is too faint compared to visible light
Y) UV detectors cannot function at low altitudes
Z) UV light is completely reflected by clouds
ANSWER: W) UV light is absorbed by the Earth’s atmosphere
