Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Glauconite may be what kind of sediment?
W) Lithogenic
X) Biogenic
Y) Cosmogenic
Z) Authigenic
ANSWER: Z) Authigenic
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A Flinder's Rod and Kelvin's Balls both serve a similar purpose on a ship. What is it?
ANSWER: Correcting a ship's compass
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the general effect of wave refraction on the shape of a coastline?
W) Carve out irregular embayments
X) Smooth out headlands and fill in embayments
Y) Transport sediment uniformly along the coast
Z) Push the entire coastline back
ANSWER: X) Smooth out headlands and fill in embayments
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which large-bodied molluscan class has proliferated with recent environmental change?
ANSWER: Cephalopoda (also accept: Cephalopods)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which two cations form significant complexes with bicarbonate in seawater?
W) Sodium and Potassium
X) Magnesium and Sodium
Y) Potassium and Magnesium
Z) Sodium and Calcium
ANSWER: X) Magnesium and Sodium
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Vienna Standard Mean Ocean Water is an international reference standard for which of the following?
W) Salinity
X) Isotopic composition
Y) Temperature
Z) Ionic composition
ANSWER: X) Isotopic composition
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In the Southern Hemisphere, what type of eddy would lead to a raised sea surface height?
ANSWER: Warm-core (also accept: Anti-cyclonic; counter-clockwise)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The peak in Cesium-137 typically observed in sediment cores corresponds to which event?
W) The Chernobyl disaster in 1986
X) A peak in global nuclear testing in 1963
Y) The Fukushima meltdown in 2011
Z) Early weapons tests at Bikini Atoll in 1946
ANSWER: X) A peak in global nuclear testing in 1963
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the two islands separated by only 4km, but 21 hours in time?
ANSWER: The Diomede Islands (also accept: Diomedes)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the initial impact of zebra mussels on invaded waterways?
W) Massive reduction of phytoplankton
X) Clogged sewer drains
Y) Boat fouling
Z) No major impact
ANSWER: W) Massive reduction of phytoplankton
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of muscle produces lactate in fishes?
ANSWER: White muscle
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What volcanic feature drives advective heat loss from sediment-covered ridge-flanks by providing permeable recharge and discharge sites?
W) Black smokers
X) Methane hydrates
Y) White smokers
Z) Seamounts
ANSWER: Z) Seamounts
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer An expedition aboard what former naval vessel from 1872-1876 gave birth to the modern sciences of marine biology and oceanography?
ANSWER: HMS Challenger (also accept: Challenger)
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is meant by the constancy of composition of seawater?
W) Seawater is always salty
X) Salt is always sodium chloride
Y) The salinity of the ocean is constant
Z) The major ions that make up ocean salts are always in the same proportions
ANSWER: Z) The major ions that make up ocean salts are always in the same proportions
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How can an ephemeral river lead to greater genetic differentiation between populations?
W) Reduced water and gene flow between different areas
X) Increased water and gene flow between different areas
Y) More habitats available
Z) Fewer habitats available
ANSWER: W) Reduced water and gene flow between different areas
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In the Arctic, what are two seasonal physical processes that alter the salinity of seawater without significantly affecting ion ratios?
ANSWER: Freezing and thawing (also accept: water mass advection; turbulent mixing between water masses of different salinities)
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The North Atlantic Oscillation occurs when there is a change in the relative strengths of atmospheric pressure between which pressure regions at sea level?
ANSWER: Icelandic Low and Azores High
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is one consequence of a blue-green algae bloom in a lake?
W) Hyperoxic waters
X) Good boating conditions
Y) Osmotic waters
Z) Anoxic waters
ANSWER: Z) Anoxic waters
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the carbonate concentration in micro-equivalents per kilogram of seawater with a carbonate concentration of 255 micromoles per kilogram?
ANSWER: 510
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice How do the currents on the western coast of the US make it a good place to study ocean acidification on nearshore animals?
W) Coastal upwelling results in high pH relative to average
X) Coastal upwelling results in low pH relative to average
Y) The California Current results in high temperatures relative to average
Z) The California Current results in low temperatures relative to average
ANSWER: X) Coastal upwelling results in low pH relative to average
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for algal or diatomaceous growth on any plant surfaces in the ocean?
ANSWER: Epiphytes (also accept: epiphytic)
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What effect will a 30 millibar rise in atmospheric pressure have on the tidal range?
ANSWER: It would have no effect on the range
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Where did an earthquake-created turbidity current break at least 12 submerged telegraph cables over a 12 hour period?
ANSWER: The Grand Banks (also accept: Newfoundland) (do not accept: Atlantic; North Atlantic)
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following seawater properties is NOT usually measured spectrophotometrically?
W) pH
X) Temperature
Y) Nitrate
Z) Phosphate
ANSWER: X) Temperature
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the main morphological difference between stickleback fish populations?
ANSWER: Number of spines (also accept: body armor)
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which term is used to describe a mineral created in situ from the precipitation of dissolved chemicals?
W) Autochthonous
X) Allochthonous
Y) Lithogenous
Z) Cosmogenous
ANSWER: W) Autochthonous
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How is the distribution of zooxanthellae in giant clam tissues different than in corals?
W) They form cylindrical pillars
X) They form sheets
Y) They are less abundant
Z) They are only deep in the tissues
ANSWER: W) They form cylindrical pillars
