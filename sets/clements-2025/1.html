1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How many subunits does a ribosome have?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 3
Z) 4
ANSWER: X) 2
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which amino acid is known as the start amino acid?
ANSWER: METHIONINE
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice For an exothermic reaction, which of the following best describes the effect of increasing the temperature on the forward and reverse reactions?
W) The forward rate increases while the reverse rate decreases.
X) The reverse rate increases while the forward rate decreases.
Y) Both the forward and reverse rates increase, but the forward rate increases more than the reverse rate.
Z) Both the forward and reverse rates increase, but the reverse rate increases more than the forward rate.
ANSWER: Z) Both the forward and reverse rates increase, but the reverse rate increases more than the forward rate.
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Ms. Zuber mixes together 25 mL of 0.2 M carbonate ion and 20 mL of 0.5 M bicarbonate ion. If she titrates this solution with 1.2 M HCl, what titrant volume does she need to use to reach the final equivalence point of her titration?
W) 10 mL HCl
X) 12.5 mL HCl
Y) 16.7 mL HCl
Z) 20 mL HCl
ANSWER: Y) 16.7 mL HCl
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which of the following statements is true about the position of a ping pong ball in a bucket of water in a downward-moving elevator? 1) If the elevator slows down, the ball will sink in the water. 2) If the elevator speeds up, the ping pong ball may completely rise out of the water. 3) If the elevator speeds up, the ping pong ball may rise in the water, but it cannot completely exit the water.
ANSWER: NONE
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer An ocean world has the same radius as Earth but twice its density. However, its surface atmospheric pressure is half of Earth’s. To two significant figures, at what water depth in meters would the absolute pressure equal three times the gauge pressure?
ANSWER: 1.3
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Rix graphs the equation y = x2 + 2x + 4. If Rix shifts his graph 4 units to the left and then 2 units to the right, what is the vertex of his resulting parabola?
ANSWER: (-3, 3)
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Rix continues to graph conic sections. If he graphs one circle, one ellipse, one parabola, and one hyperbola, what is the maximum number of intersections he can have?
ANSWER: 24
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term that describes the type of melting that hot solid mantle rock undergoes as it ascends towards lower pressures?
ANSWER: DECOMPRESSION MELTING
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the largest intrusive bodies?
ANSWER: BATHOLITHS
6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are studying machine learning. What widely used type of machine learning has models learn from unlabeled data without human input?
ANSWER: UNSUPERVISED LEARNING (also accept: DEEP LEARNING)
6. BONUS Math Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are studying neural networks. Neural networks can be represented as what general type of mathematical object consisting of edges and weighted nodes?
ANSWER: GRAPHS
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for a virus that attacks bacteria?
ANSWER: BACTERIOPHAGE (also accept: PHAGE)
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the cycle in cellular respiration that produces NADH and FADH2?
ANSWER: KREBS CYCLE (also accept: CITRIC ACID CYCLE; TCA CYCLE)
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Phosphoric acid has pKa values of pKa1 = 2.15, pKa2 = 7.20, and pKa3 = 12.35. Which species is most abundant at pH = 11.5?
W) Phosphoric acid
X) Dihydrogen phosphate ion
Y) Hydrogen phosphate ion
Z) Phosphate ion
ANSWER: Y) Hydrogen phosphate ion
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A reaction achieves equilibrium at 28 degrees Celsius. If the activation energy of the forward reaction is 48 kJ per mol and the activation energy of the reverse reaction is 66 kJ per mol, then to one significant figure in joules per mole Kelvin, what is the change in entropy of the reverse reaction?
ANSWER: 60
