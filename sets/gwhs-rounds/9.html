1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is the main reason why metal catches fire if you microwave it, but a chicken nugget doesn't?
W) The glucose in the chicken nugget has a specific heat much higher than metal, causing the metal's temperature to drastically rise while the chicken nugget's temperature rises by a few degrees
X) Evaporative cooling from the water in the chicken nugget allows the chicken nugget's temperature to raise only a few degrees
Y) The microwaves released by the microwave increase in frequency as the albedo of the substance in the microwave increases
Z) The chicken nuggets do heat up significantly but the autoignition temperature of the chicken nuggets is very high because of the saturated fatty acids in the chicken nugget
ANSWER: X) Evaporative cooling from the water in the chicken nugget allows the chicken nugget's temperature to raise only a few degrees
