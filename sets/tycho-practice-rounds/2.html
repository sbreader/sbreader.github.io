4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following statements best describes the function f(x) = xe-x on the interval of the entire real line?

X) Always decreasing over the entire interval
Y) Decreasing until a minimum at x=1, then increasing
Z) Increasing until a maximum at x=1, then decreasing
ANSWER: Z) Increasing until a maximum at x=1, then decreasing
