1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following hormones is NOT synthesized by cholesterol?

1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three options that correctly matches the hormone to the cell that produces it in humans I. Intrinsic factor, Chief Cell II. Erythropoietin, Peritubular Cell III. Renin, Juxtaglomerular Cells
ANSWER: II AND III
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following four steps from first to last in mass spectrometry. I. Deflection II. Detection III. Acceleration IV. Ionization
ANSWER: IV, III, I, II
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice A hydrogen atom emits light as it changes from a state with a principal quantum number n+4 to a state with principal quantum number n. Which change causes the greatest change in the energy of the emitted photon?
W) Changing the initial state to n+5
X) Changing the initial state to n+3
Y) Changing the final state to n+1
Z) Changing the final state to n-1
ANSWER: Z) Changing the final state to n-1
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer From lowest to highest, rank the following three configurations of charge by the magnitude of electric field strength at a distance of 1 meter from the origin I. Sphere of uniform charge density centered at the origin with radius 2 meters and total charge 1 coulomb II. Spherical shell of uniform charge density centered at the origin with radius 2 meters and total charge 2 coulombs III. Point charge centered at the origin with total charge 1 coulomb
ANSWER: II, III, I
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true I. Fraunhofer diffraction is a near-field approximation of the Kirchoff diffraction formula II. The Rayleigh criterion states that angular resolution is proportional to aperture diameter divided by the wavelength of light III. Two light waves will display a strong interference pattern if the difference between their optical path lengths is larger than the coherence length of the light source
ANSWER: NONE

ANSWER: 20 YEARS

7. BONUS Biology Short Answer In the ELISA assay, which detects the presence of a ligand in a liquid sample, an enzyme is linked to what type of protein that is directed against the ligand to be measured?

8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Order the following four gases from most to least similar in character to an ideal gas I. H2O II. SO2 III. N2 IV. CO2
ANSWER: III, IV, I, II
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer You are given the first order reaction 2 A → B + C. The starting concentration of A is 0.2 mol/L, and after 10 s, the concentration of B rises to 0.05 L/mol. Giving your answer as a constant times a logarithm, what is the rate constant of this reaction?
ANSWER: (1/10) ln 2 (also accept: -(1/10) ln(0.5))
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Two point-charges are arranged so that when they are a distance d apart, the electric potential energy of the system is 1 keV. If additional charges are brought in from infinitely far away to create a regular tetrahedron of point-charges with side length d, what is the new electric potential energy in the system?
ANSWER: 6 keV
