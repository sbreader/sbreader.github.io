6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following four statements is false?
W) For light with a frequency less than the threshold frequency, no electrons are emitted
X) For light with a frequency less than the threshold frequency, light intensity does not have a direct relationship with the amount of electrons emitted
Y) For light with a frequency greater than the threshold frequency, the potential energy of the emitted electrons increase linearly with respect to the light's frequency
