1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What organic functional group is formed in the reaction of a ketone with an excess of sodium borohydride?
ANSWER: ALCOHOL (also accept: SECONDARY ALCOHOL)
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Consider the two reactions A forms B, and B forms C. The rate constant for the dissociation of A is 1 molar per second and the equilibrium constant is 0.5. The second reaction has a forward rate constant of 2 molar per second. Assuming the steady state approximation holds for B, what’s the rate of formation of C as a multiple of concentration of A?
ANSWER: 0.5 TIMES THE CONCENTRATION OF A
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding oceanic productivity is false?
W) Productivity in tropical oceans is limited by nutrient supply
X) Productivity is lowest in the open ocean
Y) Mid-latitude oceans have one phytoplankton bloom per year
Z) Productivity in the southern Atlantic is greater than in other polar oceans
ANSWER: Y) Mid-latitude oceans have one phytoplankton bloom per year

ANSWER: 4, 3, 1, 2

6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A fetus that has congenital adrenal hyperplasia suffers a mutation to an enzyme 21-hydroxylase, leading to virilization of the XX fetus. Which of the following statements is NOT true?

X) The mutation to 21-hydroxylase leads to increased cortisol production

7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Let u and v be unit length vectors in R3. Identify all of the following four choices that could describe the locus of vectors x in R3 satisfying (u dot x) = 0 and (v dot x) = 0 simultaneously 1) the empty set 2) a single point 3) a line 4) a plane.
