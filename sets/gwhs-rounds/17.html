1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What quantity, relevant to the calculation of Reynold’s Number, has units of pascal seconds and is defined as the ratio of viscosity of a fluid to the density of the fluid.

W) 5.0 × 102
X) 2.0 × 104
Y) 5.0 × 106
Z) 2.0 × 108
ANSWER: Z) 2.0 × 108
