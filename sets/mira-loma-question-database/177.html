Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following metals is a "noble metal"?
W) Copper
X) Zinc
Y) Palladium
Z) Bismuth
ANSWER: Y) Palladium
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A highly concentrated mixture of hydrochloric acid and nitric acid, what acid is one of the only ones that can dissolve gold, platinum, and palladium?
ANSWER: aqua regia
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The ozone hole has been growing primarily because of the introduction of what common class of organic halides into the atmosphere?
ANSWER: Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The ozone layer shields the surface of the Earth from mainly what type of radiation?
W) Infrared
X) Ultraviolet
Y) X-ray
Z) Gamma ray
ANSWER: X) Ultraviolet
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice About how old are the earliest fossil records present in Earth's history?
W) 500 million years
X) 1 billion years
Y) 2 billion years
Z) 3 billion years
ANSWER: Z) 3 billion years
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the accretionary structures formed in shallow water, that hold much fossil evidence, such as those of the oldest life found?
ANSWER: stromatolite
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The SI unit corresponding to the Imperial Curie, what is the derived unit for radioactivity?
W) Sievert
X) Rem
Y) Gray
Z) Becquerel
ANSWER: Z) Becquerel
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is a Becquerel expressed in SI base units?
ANSWER: s-1 (also accept: decay per second)
5. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Usually consisting of a large glass tube containing a smaller glass tube running its entire length, in which the hot fluids pass, what piece of laboratory glassware is used to cool hot vapors or liquids?
W) condenser
X) cooling flask
Y) cuvette
Z) cold finger
ANSWER: W) condenser
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Condensers are commonly used in what method of separating mixtures, based on differences in their volatilities in a boiling liquid mixture?
ANSWER: distillation
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice By weight, which element comprises the majority of normal human body?
W) oxygen
X) hydrogen
Y) carbon
Z) nitrogen
ANSWER: W) oxygen
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following seismic scales is now relatively obsolete, as it has been superceded by another scale?
W) Modified Mercalli
X) Richter
Y) Japanese "shindo"
Z) Moment Magnitude
ANSWER: X) Richter
7. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice On the Richter Scale, a difference of 2.0 on the magnitude would indicate how many times more energy being released?
W) 10
X) 100
Y) 1000
Z) 10,000
ANSWER: Y) 1000
8. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following units is NOT a unit commonly used to measure atomic masses?
W) dalton
X) universal mass unit
Y) unified atomic mass unit
Z) yoctogram
ANSWER: Z) yoctogram
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Currently, which specific atom is used to calibrate the atomic mass unit? Be specific.
ANSWER: Carbon-12 (do not accept: carbon)
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice When expressing quantities of temperature using the Kelvin Scale, what is the proper format for a value such as absolute zero?
W) zero degrees Kelvin
X) zero Kelvin degrees
Y) zero Kelvin
Z) zero
ANSWER: Y) zero Kelvin
9. BONUS Energy Short Answer Besides absolute zero, what other temperature-pressure value is a standard for the Kelvin scale?
ANSWER: the triple point of water
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Graphite, diamond, and buckminsterfullerenes are considered what forms of carbon?
W) isotopes
X) allotropes
Y) compounds
Z) macromolecules
ANSWER: X) allotropes
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Buckminsterfullerenes, allotropes of carbon, are fullerenes that contain how many carbons?
ANSWER: 60
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which type of microscope fires electrons that pass through a thin sheet of specimen, allowing observations of the internal ultrasctructure of cells?
W) Light microscope
X) Heavy microscope
Y) Scanning electron microscope
Z) Transmission electron microscope
ANSWER: Z) Transmission electron microscope
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What types of atoms are transmission electron microscope slides stained with, to enhance electron density of certain parts of the specimen, specifically cells?
ANSWER: heavy metals
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following boundaries is not considered a major mass extinction event?
W) Permian-Triassic
X) Triassic-Jurassic
Y) Jurassic-Cretaceous
Z) Cretaceous-Tertiary
ANSWER: Y) Jurassic-Cretaceous
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The Permian-Triassic extinction event is most commonly referred to informally as the…?
ANSWER: Great Dying
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What material do most crustaceans, insects, and others use for structure of their exteriors?
W) cellulose
X) chitin
Y) keratin
Z) calcium
ANSWER: X) chitin
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Fungi also use chitin. What cell organelle is it mainly present in?
ANSWER: cell wall
14. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What branch of classical mechanics describes the motion of objects without considerating why?
W) Kinematics
X) Kinetics
Y) Dynamics
Z) Statics
ANSWER: W) Kinematics
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Who was the first scientist to suggest a modern theory of heliocentrism, often regarded as the starting point of modern astronomy and the epiphany that began the Scientific Revolution?
W) Brahe
X) Galileo
Y) Kepler
Z) Copernicus
ANSWER: Z) Copernicus
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In what renowned book did Copernicus publish his theory of heliocentrism?
ANSWER: On the Revolutions of the Celestial Spheres (allow for other Latin translations)
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Though the chemical formula and even structure may be alike, "mirror images" of organic compounds play a role in medicine, in which only one form can treat an illness. What is the term for this type of isomerization?
W) diastereomers
X) stereoisomers
Y) geometric isomers
Z) mirror isomers
ANSWER: X) stereoisomers
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following common medications and supplements does not have any stereoisomers?
W) Salbutamol (albuterol)
X) Menthol
Y) Retinol (Vitamin A)
Z) Ascorbic Acid (Vitamin C)
ANSWER: Y) Retinol (Vitamin A)
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following symptoms is not a major problem caused by scurvy?
W) Damaged vision
X) Gum disease
Y) Poor wound healing
Z) Formation of "spots" on the skin
ANSWER: W) Damaged vision
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer In the Royal Navy of Great Britain, what specific fruit was predominately implemented in sailors' diets to prevent scurvy? Hint: This gave rise to a common sailors' nickname.
ANSWER: limes
19. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following scientists is NOT one who is credited with the founding of radio transmission technology?
W) Edison
X) Tesla
Y) Marconi
Z) Hertz
ANSWER: W) Edison
19. BONUS Energy Short Answer A modern application of radio, what tag-like chip is incorporated into a product, animal, or person for the purpose of identification and tracking using radio waves?
ANSWER: Radio Frequency Identification Device (RFID)
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A renowned English chemist and physicist who contributed to the fields of electromagnetism and electrochemistry, who has the SI unit of Capacitance named after him, along with the constant that is equal to a mole of electrons?
ANSWER: Michael Faraday
20. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice In which of the following problems did Faraday NOT offer a solution to?
W) The effect of magnetic fields on electricity
X) The effect of electric fields on magnetism
Y) The effect of magnetic fields on light
Z) The effect of electric fields on conductors
ANSWER: X) The effect of electric fields on magnetism
21. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Vinyl Chloride, the monomer polymerized in PVC, has what IUPAC name?
W) Chloroethane
X) 1-Chloroethylene
Y) cis-Phenylchloroethylene
Z) trans-hydroxylchloroethylene
ANSWER: X) 1-Chloroethylene
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The principal industrial method for producing the lighter alkenes, what cracking type involves diluted heavy hydrocarbons briefly heated in a furnace anerobically?
W) Catalytic cracking
X) Thermal cracking
Y) Hydrocracking
Z) Steam cracking
ANSWER: Z) Steam cracking
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is a broad term encompassing any volatile, flammable liquid hydrocarbon mixture, often used in cracking processes, and is considered a carcinogen?
ANSWER: naphtha
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice On October 4th, 1957, which Soviet satellite was the first man-made object ever put in orbit?
W) Vostok 1
X) Voskhod 1
Y) Sputnik 1
Z) Soyuz 1
ANSWER: Y) Sputnik 1
24. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What was NOT one of Issac Newton's major accomplishments?
W) The creation of a modern refractor telescope
X) The innovation of calculus
Y) A study on the rate of heat loss
Z) A compilation of the laws of motion
ANSWER: W) The creation of a modern refractor telescope
24. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Newton's work in optics produced a very functional reflector telescope. What is NOT an advantage of the Newtonian design?
W) Freedom from chromatic aberration
X) No central obstruction of the light path
Y) A shorter focal ratio, leading to wider field of view
Z) More compact and inexpensive than other models
ANSWER: X) No central obstruction of the light path
25. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is a device used to measure the distance between two symmetrically opposing sides, where its tips are adjusted to fit across the points to be measured, then read using a ruler?
ANSWER: caliper
25. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice What variation to the more traditional caliper has both inside and outside "jaws", fit on a ruler that can also measure depth?
W) oddleg caliper
X) divider caliper
Y) IOD caliper
Z) Vernier caliper
ANSWER: Z) Vernier caliper
