1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following phyla does not contain algae?
W) Bryophyta
X) Charophyta
Y) Chlorophyta
Z) Streptophyta
ANSWER: W) Bryophyta
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 diseases that have an X-linked inheritance pattern 1) Cystic fibrosis 2) Huntington’s disease 3) Duchenne muscular dystrophy.
ANSWER: 3 ONLY
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the limit as x approaches infinity of the fraction with numerator 7 x2 + 4 x and denominator 4 x2 + 10 x + 11?
ANSWER: 7/4
3. BONUS Math Short Answer Albert and Bill each pick a random integer between 1 and 10 inclusive. If the numbers are different, Albert pays Bill 5 dollars. If the numbers are the same, Bill pays Albert x dollars. Given that the expected value of playing this game is 0, what is x?
ANSWER: 45
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following instruments did Michelson and Morley use to disprove the existence of the luminiferous ether?
W) Laser
X) Torsion balance
Y) Interferometer
Z) Cyclotron
ANSWER: Y) Interferometer
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three spectral classifications from coldest to hottest 1) A 2) B 3) G.
ANSWER: 3,1,2
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which of the following petrogenic environments would hornfels-facies metamorphism most likely be expected?
W) Subduction zone
X) Continent-continent convergence
Y) Mid-ocean ridge
Z) Pluton aureole
ANSWER: Z) Pluton aureole
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following gases has a compression factor greater than 1 for all pressures at 300 kelvins?
W) Methane
X) Ammonia
Y) Dimethyl ether
Z) Hydrogen
ANSWER: Z) Hydrogen
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A passenger is standing on a scale in an elevator. As the elevator accelerates upwards, the scale reads 830 newtons. The elevator then begins to accelerate downwards at the same magnitude, with the scale now reading 650 newtons. In newtons, what is the weight of the passenger?
ANSWER: 740
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer A circuit consists of a 12 volt battery and a 3 ohm resistor. In watts, how much power is dissipated in this resistor?
ANSWER: 48
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Vernalization is a treatment to many plants used to promote which response?
ANSWER: FLOWERING
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer During apoptosis, the release of what iron coordinating protein in the mitochondria is responsible for the activation of caspase enzymes?
ANSWER: CYTOCHROME C
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Sulfuric acid in the Earth’s atmosphere is formed from the hydration of what specific secondary air pollutant?
ANSWER: SULFUR TRIOXIDE (also accept: SO3)
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer If the Schwartzchild radius of the sun is given as Rs, then in terms of Rs, what is the Schwartzchild radius of a star with a mass of 8 solar masses?
ANSWER: 8 Rs
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Points A and B lie on a circle centered at point O such that angle AOB is 90 degrees. The tangents to the circle at A and B intersect at point C. If the radius of the circle is 7, what is the length of segment AC?
ANSWER: 7
11. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the only rational root of the polynomial x3 – 3 x2 + 3 x – 2?
ANSWER: 2
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Ames Lab have been studying the novel properties of high-temperature superconductors. In low-temperature superconductors, what process is responsible for the expulsion of a magnetic field as a material transitions into a superconducting state?
ANSWER: MEISSNER EFFECT
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer There are 3 distinct marbles in a bag. Every second, Alice draws a random marble and puts it back, stopping when she has drawn all 3 marbles at least once. What is the probability that she stops after exactly 3 seconds?
ANSWER: 2/9
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Alice, Bob, Charlie, and David are each assigned a seat in a row of 4 chairs. However, they ignore their assigned seats and choose where to sit at random. What is the probability that exactly 1 person is in their assigned seat?
ANSWER: 1/3
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Reuptake inhibitors may be used to act on what neurotransmitter that binds to 5-HT channels?
ANSWER: SEROTONIN
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Lithium fusion is often useful for identifying which of the following substellar structures?
W) Black dwarf
X) White dwarf
Y) Brown dwarf
Z) Exoplanet
ANSWER: Y) Brown dwarf
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Internal waves in the ocean propagate along a distinct density boundary that separates two layers. Most internal waves are observed in what marine environment?
W) Near the bottom of the seafloor in abyssal regions
X) Near river mouths on the continental shelf
Y) On the continental slope
Z) Within polar pycnoclines
ANSWER: X) Near river mouths on the continental shelf
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer In the rocket equation, the fuel consumption rate multiplied by the fuel exhaust speed represents what quantity?
ANSWER: THRUST (also accept: THRUST FORCE)
17. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the bond angle in degrees found in hydrogen sulfide?
W) 90
X) 107
Y) 120
Z) 180
ANSWER: W) 90
19. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are using ferroelectric ceramic materials with engineered crystal defects to create a “super” version of what energy-storing circuit component?
ANSWER: CAPACITOR
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer In order to determine the grain structure of ceramic microcrystals, scientists at Idaho National Lab are generating internal sound waves and using laser pulses to measure the frequencies of the acoustic phonons that result. How many distinct phonon frequencies must they measure in order to ascertain the general grain structure?
ANSWER: 3 (also accept: THREE)
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three quantities that remain constant for a system in static equilibrium 1) Force 2) Torque 3) Linear momentum.
ANSWER: ALL
20. BONUS Physics Short Answer What technique can be used to slow and trap atoms at low temperatures by using a light source tuned to a frequency just below an electronic transition to selectively target atoms moving toward the light beam?
ANSWER: DOPPLER COOLING (also accept: LASER COOLING)
21. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the domain of the function y = the square root of the quantity 4 – x times the square root of the quantity 4 + x?
W) x is greater than –4 and less than 4
X) x is greater than –4 and less than or equal to 4
Y) x is greater than or equal to –4 and less than 4
Z) x is greater than or equal to –4 and less than or equal to 4
ANSWER: Z) x is greater than or equal to –4 and less than or equal to 4
21. BONUS Math Short Answer The Spiral of Theodorus is a spiral of right triangles made by starting with a right triangle with both legs equal to 1, and making each successive triangle have one leg equal to 1 and the previous triangle's hypotenuse as the other leg. What is the ratio of the areas of the tenth triangle and the first triangle?
ANSWER: √10 (also accept: ROOT 10)
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Alex receives a critical injury to his substantia nigra and thus experiences a lack of dopamine release to the basal nuclei. He will likely exhibit symptoms of what neurologic disease?
ANSWER: PARKINSON’S DISEASE
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer What region of the brainstem connects the medulla to the thalamus and is responsible for innervating specific stretch receptors during inspiration?
ANSWER: PONS
