1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Points A, B, and C all lie on a circle. What is the measure of angle ABC in degrees if it intercepts an arc of 122°?

1. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the volume of a circular cylinder formed by rotating a 7 by 6 rectangular region about one of its longer sides?
ANSWER: 252π
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In the year 79 AD, the people of Pompeii were asphyxiated and then buried by ash from what volcano?
W) Mount Pelee
X) Mount Vesuvius
Y) Mount Tambora
Z) Mount Mayon
ANSWER: X) Mount Vesuvius

W) Astrolabe
X) Bolometer
Y) Refractor telescope
Z) Optical interferometer
ANSWER: X) Bolometer

ANSWER: 8

ANSWER: –25
4. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Coke is produced as a byproduct of the burning of what fuel?
ANSWER: COAL
4. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Electric vehicles convert about 60% of the electrical energy from the grid to power at the wheels. In a gasoline-powered vehicle, which of the following is closest to the percentage of the energy stored in gasoline that is converted to energy at the wheels?
W) 5
X) 10
Y) 20
Z) 50
ANSWER: Y) 20
