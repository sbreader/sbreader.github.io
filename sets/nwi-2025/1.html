X) Echidna

1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a keystone species?
W) Beavers
X) Grass
Y) Mosquitos
Z) Ants
ANSWER: W) Beavers
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following ions is not isoelectronic with argon?
W) S2-
X) Cl-
Y) Na+
Z) Ca2+
ANSWER: Y) Na+
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many moles of O2 would be required to completely combust 1 mole of butene, with formula C4H8?
ANSWER: 6
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A ball of mass 8 kg is dropped from the top of a 5 meter tall building. Assuming gravitational acceleration is 10 m/s2 and neglecting air resistance, what is the velocity, in meters per second, right before the ball hits the ground?
ANSWER: 10
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer A ball is launched vertically with an initial velocity of 8 m/s. Assuming gravitational acceleration is 10 m/s2 and neglecting air resistance, what is the maximum vertical displacement in meters and how long does it take to reach that height in seconds?
ANSWER: 3.2 meters, 0.8 seconds
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Oscar is visiting a new ice cream shop. The ice cream shop has 4 ice cream flavors, 5 toppings, 4 syrup flavors, and 3 types of cones. If Oscar chooses one type in each of the four categories, how many different ice cream combinations can he have?
ANSWER: 240
4. BONUS Math Short Answer 5 friends, Alice, Bob, Charlie, Dan, and Earl are going to watch the new hit movie Moana 2. If all 5 seats they reserved are in one row and Dan and Alice must sit together, how many ways are there to organize these 5 people?
ANSWER: 48

W) altitude
X) pressure
Y) temperature
Z) local gravity

5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following 3 glacial features which are erosional in nature 1) drumlin 2) moraine 3) kettle lake
ANSWER: 3 only

ANSWER: Electrolysis
6. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice The ALTEMIS experiment at Savannah River National Lab involved the intentional contamination of groundwater with radioactive nuclides from 1955 to 1988. Which of the following sediment types did the scientists most likely install?
W) Clay
X) Gravel
Y) Sand
Z) Silt
ANSWER: W) Clay

8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Oscar wants to create a Daniell cell as a gift for Daniel helping him with his electrochemistry homework. ANSWER: 1.1
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the name of the theorem that states work done on an object is equal to its change in energy?
