1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In which phase of mitosis do kinetochore complexes form, allowing microtubules to attach to chromosomes?

1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is false?
W) DNA polymerase III covalently adds nucleotides to the 5' end of an RNA primer/ existing DNA.
X) Telomeres don't prevent DNA molecules from shortening due to replication.
