1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following statements that are true regarding salt fingering 1) It is gravity driven 2) It occurs when cold, fresh water underlies warm, salty water 3) Heat diffuses more quickly than salinity
ANSWER: All
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of desert landscapes is LEAST likely to be found where deflation rates are high?
W) Badlands
X) Erg
Y) Hamada
Z) Reg
ANSWER: X) Erg
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In order for a moon to be stripped from its host planet, it must cross which of the following boundaries?
W) Roche limit
X) L3
Y) Hill sphere
Z) Event Horizon
ANSWER: Y) Hill sphere
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Callisto, an inner moon of Jupiter, has a crater-covered surface. In contrast, outer moons such as Himalia and Amalthea receive significantly less cratering. This is mainly a result of what effect, where potential impactors are directed towards the planet and results in more impacts on the inner moons?
ANSWER: Gravitational focusing
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best resembles pseudomorphism?
W) Andalusite rearranging into kyanite as pressure increases
X) Limonite possessing a cubic shape as a result of substitution of pyrite
Y) Twinning of aragonite resulting in a hexagonal appearance
Z) Recrystallization of garnet resulting in coarser grains due to high temperature and pressure
ANSWER: X) Limonite possessing a cubic shape as a result of substitution of pyrite
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best characterizes the upper atmospheric zonal circulation at latitudes 30N and 60N, respectively?
W) Westerly, easterly
X) Westerly, Westerly
Y) Easterly, Westerly
Z) Easterly, Easterly
ANSWER: X) Westerly, Westerly
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice At which of the following locations is melange least likely to be found?
W) Accretionary wedge
X) River delta
Y) Base of continental slopes
Z) Fold thrust belts
ANSWER: X) River delta
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 events caused by orographic forcing that promote lee-side cyclone formation 1) Enhanced low-level convergence on the lee side 2) Strengthening of upper-level wind shear on the lee side 3) Warming of air masses due to compression on the windward side
ANSWER: 1 and 2
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What hypothesis states that naked singularities do not exist in the universe?
ANSWER: Cosmic censorship hypothesis
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following four moons of Jupiter whose material compose the Gossamer rings 1) Io 2) Europa 3) Callisto 4) Ganymede
ANSWER: None
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following conditions that are conducive to the formation of chevron faults 1) Compressional stress 2) Layers with contrasting stiffness 3) Being deep in the Earth’s crust
ANSWER: 1 and 2
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding internal waves in the ocean 1) They are wind driven 2) They tend to more closely follow the thermocline than the halocline 3) They can propagate vertically and horizontally
ANSWER: 2 and 3
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer White dwarfs are hypothesized to cool to become black dwarfs over time periods that are longer than the age of the universe. Similarly, red dwarfs will evolve into what type of star by increasing its surface temperature over trillions of years?
ANSWER: Blue dwarf
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The solar wind slows down below the speed of sound after it crosses which of the following boundaries?
W) Heliosheath
X) Heliopause
Y) Heliosphere
Z) Termination shock
ANSWER: Z) Termination shock
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Carbonatite, an igneous rock containing high concentrations of carbonate and rare earth elements, is most likely to form in which of the following environments?
W) Continental rifts
X) Mid-ocean ridges
Y) Supervolcano hotspots
Z) Cinder cones
ANSWER: W) Continental rifts
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding thalwegs, line connecting the lowest points in on the river bed 1) Erosion occurs at the thalweg 2) The thalweg is generally more sinuous than the river 3) Water in the channel flows the fastest at the thalweg
ANSWER: 1 and 2
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Chocolate Hills in the Philippines is noted for its abundance of cone-shaped hills, as well as mogotes, poljes and cenotes. Which of the following karst landscapes does this describe?
W) Kegelkarst
X) Paleokarst
Y) Salt karst
Z) Tower karst
ANSWER: W) Kegelkarst

15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In an alternative universe, the ionization energy of an hydrogen atom is ten times higher than the universe we know. How would the redshift of the cosmic microwave background in the alternative universe compare to our universe?

Y) It is the same
Z) It cannot be determined
ANSWER: W) It is higher
