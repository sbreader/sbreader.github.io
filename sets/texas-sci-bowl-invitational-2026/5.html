Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer On thin filaments, what protein blocks myosin-binding sites at rest?
ANSWER: Tropomyosin
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three levels of protein structure that would contains ionic bonds 1) Primary 2) Secondary 3) Teritiary
ANSWER: 3 only
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What laboratory technique, often used to monitor the progress of organic reactions, separates small amounts of compounds on a silicacoated plate and reports their movement as an Rf value?
ANSWER: Thin-Layer Chromatography (also accept: TLC)
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Sam performs paper chromatography on a mixture of dyes. One dye spot travels 4.5 centimeters, while the solvent front travels 9.0 centimeters. What is the Rf value of the dye?
W) 0.25
X) 0.50
Y) 0.75
Z) 2.00
ANSWER: X) 0.50
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In the game of Catan, a hexagontal tile is surrounded by 2 rings of tiles, for a total of 19 tiles. How many tiles would an additional ring require?
ANSWER: 18
3. BONUS Math Short Answer Park is taking a 100-question test. For each question, he has a 1% chance of getting it wrong. Let X be the probability that he gets a perfect score. Let Y be the probability that he misses exactly 2 questions. What is the ratio of X to Y?
ANSWER: 99/50
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The temperature gradient across the bottom of a pot is 40 degrees centigrade per centimeter. If the pot has a radius of 10 centimeters and thermal conductivity of 1/π, how many seconds will it take for the stove to transfer 100 Joules of energy?
ANSWER: 2.5 seconds
4. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Two stars, star A and star B, have the same radius. The temperature of Star B is twice that of Star A but Star B is twice as far from Earth as Star A. Star B has brightness I. What is the brightness of Star A?
W) I/4
X) 2I
Y) 4I
Z) 8I
ANSWER: W) I/4
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following objects would not be visible in the sky at the same time as the others?
W) Cassiopeia
X) Crux
Y) Large Magellanic Cloud
Z) Centaurus A
ANSWER: W) Cassiopeia
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following planets in terms of increasing magnetic field strength. 1) Mercury 2) Earth 3) Mars
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three parts of a flower that would produce pollen 1) Stamen 2) Carpel 3) Sepal
ANSWER: 1 only
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following processes would most likely not be a mechanism of sympatric speciation?
W) Sexual selection
X) Polyploidy
Y) Niche partitioning
Z) Geographic isolation
ANSWER: Z) Geographic isolation
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer At certain compositions of water and ethanol in solution, you may not use distillation to separate them. What is the term for this type of special mixture?
ANSWER: Azeotrope
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When 64 grams of methane are completely combusted, to the nearest integer, how many grams of carbon dioxide are produced?
ANSWER: 176
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is 715 (mod 50)?
ANSWER: 43
8. BONUS Math Short Answer A coffee shop is trying to determine the optimal price of their coffee. They know that if the price is P, the number of cups sold will be 1200 − 200P . If the average cost to produce 1 cup of coffee is $1.50, what price of coffee will produce the highest total profit?
ANSWER: $3.75
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer When plotting log voltage versus current through a resistor, Alia observes a straight line with positive slope. Which of the following statements are true? 1) The resistor is ohmic 2) The resistor is ohmic only at large currents 3) The resistor could model an incadescent light bulb
ANSWER: 3
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer A water pipe has pistons on either end. The left and right pistons have radii of 3 and 5 meters respectively. Rish pushes on the left piston with a force of 90 Newtons. What force, in Newtons, does the water exert on the right piston?
ANSWER: 250
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Corundum would be classified as which of the following mineral groups?
W) silicate
X) oxide
Y) carbonate
Z) phosphate
ANSWER: X) oxide
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following that are not sources of lift for thunderstorm formation. 1) dryline 2) cold front 3) entrainment
ANSWER: 3 only
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice p53 would most likely not stimulate which of the following processes?
W) DNA repair
X) Cell cycle
Y) Apoptosis
Z) Antioxidant response
ANSWER: X) Cell cycle
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Long-term potentiation in hippocampus is dependent on which glutamate receptor?
ANSWER: NMDA receptors
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the bond order of the S–O bond in a sulfate ion?
W) 1
X) 3/2
Y) 4/3
Z) 2
ANSWER: X) 3/2
12. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In the gas-phase ground state of copper, how many unpaired electrons are present, and which orbital contains the unpaired electron or electrons?
W) 1 unpaired electron, 3d
X) 1 unpaired electron, 4s
Y) 2 unpaired electrons, 3d and 4s
Z) 2 unpaired electrons, 4s and 4p
ANSWER: X) 1 unpaired electron, 4s
13. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A knight is a chess piece that can move in an L, moving 2 squares in one direction and then 1 square in an orthogonal direction. What is the minimum number of knight moves required for the knight to end exactly 3 squares right of its original position and in the same row?
W) 2
X) 3
Y) 4
Z) 5
ANSWER: X) 3
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Alice, Bob, and Carol each take the same test, receiving a score between 0 and 100, inclusive. If Alice scored at least a 95, and the average score of the group is 80, which of the following statements is not ALWAYS true? 1) The average of Bob and Carol was below 75 2) Bob and Carol both scored below average 3) If Bob scored better than Carol, then Carol must have scored below 70.
ANSWER: 2, 3
14. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice An electron with velocity in the negative x direction enters a magnetic field in the positive z direction. In what direction will the electron accelerate?
W) Positive x
X) Negative y
Y) Positive y
Z) Positive z
ANSWER: X) Negative y
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer From the surface of a planet, Warith falls into the center of a uniform sphere planet in 10 seconds. If the mass of the planet increases by a factor of 4 and the radius increases by a factor of 9, how many seconds would it take now for Warith to fall into the center of the planet?
ANSWER: 135 (seconds)
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for an object that orbits the Sun but maintains a one to one orbital resonance with a planet.
ANSWER: Quasi-moon (also accept: quasi-satellite)
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Jupiter’s moons Europa has a surface with relatively few craters. What best explains this phenomenon?
W) Jupiter’s gravity pulls asteroids away from Europa, preventing collisions
X) Europa’s volcanism creates new crust that covers older craters
Y) Europa’s internal ocean erupts and refreezes on the surface
Z) Europa’s orbit intersects with very few potential impactors
ANSWER: Y) Europa’s internal ocean erupts and refreezes on the surface
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes some of the resulting gametes if one pair of homologous chromosomes fail to separate in meiosis I?
W) Monosomy
X) Tetrasomy
Y) Monoploidy
Z) Triploidy
ANSWER: W) Monosomy
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following amino acids has an aromatic side chain?
W) Lysine
X) Glutamic acid
Y) Glycine
Z) Tryptophan
ANSWER: Z) Tryptophan
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The formation of water from hydrogen and oxygen gas is an exothermic reaction with ΔS < 0. If the pressure and temperature of the reaction chambers was increased, which direction would the reaction shift?
W) Leftwards
X) Rightwards
Y) No change
Z) Indeterminate
ANSWER: Z) Indeterminate
17. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The standard hydrogen electrode, which serves as the reference point of 0 Volts, typically uses platinum. Which best explains the reason for this?
W) Platinum increases the cell’s voltage due to its electronegativity
X) The product platinum hydride is crucial in galvanic cells
Y) The reduction potential of platinum electrodes is also at zero volts
Z) Platinum is chemically inert, allowing electrons to easily transfer
ANSWER: Z) Platinum is chemically inert, allowing electrons to easily transfer
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer 2 candles of the same height have different thicknesses, but each burn at a uniform rate. The thicker candle can burn for twice as long as the thinner candle. Assuming the thicker candle is lit 2 hours before the thin candle, and the thin candle stayed alight for 3 hours, how many hours after the lighting of the thick candle were the heights of the flames the same?
ANSWER: 4
18. BONUS Math Short Answer If the log base 12 of 2 is 0.279, and the log base 12 of 5 is 0.648, to 3 decimal places, what is the log base 12 of 30?
ANSWER: 1.369
19. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice An infinitely long thin-walled cylindrical shell has a uniform surface charge density. A small point charge q is inside this insulating shell. Which of the following is NOT true?
W) As the point charge moves inside of the cylinder, its electric potential energy (due to only the cylinder) remains the same
X) The object must be in static equilibrium
Y) The object may still possess kinetic energy
Z) The electric field produced by the cylindrical shell is zero at every point inside the hollow interior
ANSWER: X) The object must be in static equilibrium
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer A planet orbiting the sun has its semimajor axis suddenly increased by a factor of 64. By what factor does the time it takes for the planet to complete a revolution around the sun increase?
ANSWER: 512
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes a fold in rock strata in which the age decreases toward the fold axis?
ANSWER: Syncline
20. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following rocks would most likely be found at an area of marine transgression?
W) basalt
X) diorite
Y) shale
Z) migmatite
ANSWER: Y) shale
