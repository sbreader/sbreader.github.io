1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What disorder affecting the peripheral nervous system causes ascending paralysis of the body, is usually triggered by an acute infection, and is an autoimmune neuropathy that is a rare side effect of the influenza vaccine?
ANSWER: Guillain-Barre syndrome
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer This potentially fatal disease occurs with damage to mitochondria. The exact cause is unknown, but it has been associated with aspirin consumption by children with viral illness. Name this syndrome that causes fatty liver with minimal inflammation and severe encephalopathy, as well as causing hypoglycemia.
ANSWER: Reye's Syndrome
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A subway canal is dug in a straight line from the 4-H center in Washington D.C. to Beijing, China. If this subway is only powered by gravity and is frictionless, approximately how long will it take to complete a round trip?
W) 21 minutes
X) 42 minutes
Y) 84 minutes
Z) 168 minutes
ANSWER: Y) 84 minutes
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 4 orders of reactions for which the half-life is independent of the concentration: 1: zeroth-order 2: first-order 3: second-order 4: third-order
ANSWER: 2 only
5. BONUS Math Short Answer A circle of radius r is inscribed in a square. What is the probability that a randomly selected point within the square will NOT be within the circle?
ANSWER: (4-pi)/4
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A powered gear with 10 teeth drives an axle gear with 20 teeth. By name or number, indicate all of the following 4 statements that are true about gear ratios: 1: These gears have a gear ratio of 1:2. 2: This powered gear turns twice as the axle gear turns once. 3: Higher gear numbers have higher gear ratios. 4: Reverse gears are often expressed with negative gear ratios.
ANSWER: 2 and 4
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A population of 1000 squirrels has 500 successfully breeding females and 300 successfully breeding males. Calculate the effective population size of these squirrels, Ne.
ANSWER: 750
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Assume two completely plane-parallel layers of immiscible fluids, the heavier on top of the light, both under the influence of Earth's gravity. Once the equillibrium is disturbed, what type of instability will result, which will cause the formations of descending "fingers" and ascending "mushroom caps"?
ANSWER: Rayleigh-Taylor instability

ANSWER: moving group
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for the class of drugs with analgesic and antipyretic effects and which have, in higher doses, anti-inflammatory effects, but are not narcotics or steroids?

11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer On a horizontal line, there are four points. From left to right, they are A, B, C, and D, respectively. If AB > CD, indicate all of the following 3 statements that must be true: 1: AB > BC 2: AC > BD 3: AC > CD

12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following heat engine cycles does not use adiabatic expansion and compression steps, but rather uses isothermal steps for those actions?
W) Stirling
X) Carnot
Y) Rankine
Z) Otto
ANSWER: W) Stirling
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer What thermodynamic cycle, similar in steps to the Rankine cycle, uses adiabatic and isobaric steps to power gas turbine engines, and is the basis of the jet engine?
ANSWER: Brayton (Joule) cycle
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Drugs that interfere with the secretion or action of this hormone are often used as antihypertensives. One example is spironolactone, which lowers blood pressure by blocking the receptor for this steroid. Part of the Renin-angiotensin system, what mineralocorticoid promotes reabsorption of Sodium ions in the collecting duct?
ANSWER: aldosterone

14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What equation is a nonlinear partial differential equation that describes the capillary pressure difference sustained across the interface between two static fluids, such as water and air, due to the phenomenon of surface tension?

ANSWER: Artesian aquifer

20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In 1956, Wu et al. found a clear violation in this principle in the beta decay of cobalt-60.
