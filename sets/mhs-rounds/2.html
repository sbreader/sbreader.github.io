1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An object initially at rest has a moment of inertia of 5 kilogram meters squared. If a constant torque of 30 newton meters is applied to it for 10 seconds, what is its final angular velocity in radians per second?
ANSWER: 60
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer A capacitor of 5 farads is attached in series with a resistor and a battery. If the emf of the battery is 6 volts and the voltage drop across the resistor is 3 volts, what is the charge on the capacitor?
ANSWER: 15 COULOMBS
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Located 7000 light years from us, the Eagle Nebula, like most other nebulae, is composed primarily of what diatomic gas?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Evaporating Gaseous Globules, also known as, EGGs are protrusions on nebulae that can form which of the following:
W) Protostars
X) Protoplanetary Disks
Y) Accretion Rings
Z) Quasar Jets
ANSWER: W) Protostars
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer For the compound, Hydrogen Peroxide, what is the oxidation state respectively for hydrogen and oxygen?
ANSWER: +1, −1
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice An electron starts from an energy state and releases energy by going down quantized energy levels, which of the following will release the most energy?
W) n = 5 to n = 3
X) n = 5 to n = 2
Y) n = 4 to n = 2
Z) n = 3 to n = 2
ANSWER: X) n = 5 to n = 2
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What metal replaces iron when comparing hemoglobin to hemocyanin?
ANSWER: COPPER
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer In what specific phase of meiosis does crossing over take place?
ANSWER: PROPHASE I
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is true of the graphs of x squared and e to the power of x?
W) They intersect at one point
X) They are both even functions
Y) They are both odd functions
Z) Their derivatives are always positive
ANSWER: W) They intersect at one point
5. BONUS Math Short Answer The sum of the roots of a quadratic equation is 10. If the product of the roots is 24, find the quadratic equation with x squared coefficient of 1.
ANSWER: X2-10X + 24
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The amount of work done on a system is zero for what type of thermodynamic process?
W) Isothermal
X) Isobaric
Y) Adiabatic
Z) Isochoric
ANSWER: Z) Isochoric
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer A balloon expands from an initial volume of 1 liter to a final volume of 3 liters. If the pressure on the balloon was halved during this process, and the initial temperature of the balloon was 300 Kelvin, what is the final temperature of the balloon in Kelvin?
ANSWER: 450 Kelvin
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Pitchblende is the most common ore of what mineral?
ANSWER: URANIUM DIOXIDE (also accept: URANINITE) (do not accept: URANIUM; URANIUM OXIDE)
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 4 sedimentary rocks from least to greatest clast size 1) Quartz Sandstone 2) Conglomerate 3) Shale 4) Siltstone
ANSWER: 3,4,1,2
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Lab are studying Dynamic Nuclear Polarization Nuclear Magnetic Resonance to increase the intensity of standard nuclear magnetic resonance. Nuclear magnetic resonance only works on nuclei with a nonzero value of what quantity?
ANSWER: SPIN
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers at Argonne National Lab are studying the effects of platinum on electrocatalysts. Platinum is able to perform its catalytic roles primarily due to its electrons in what class of orbitals?
W) 5s
X) 4d
Y) 5d
Z) 6p
ANSWER: Y) 5d
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 quantities that, when increased, would increase the solute potential 1) Temperature 2) Concentration 3) Ionization Constant.
ANSWER: NONE
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What medical procedure is used to remove heavy metals in the body by using namesake agents?
ANSWER: CHELATION
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many ways are there to seat 6 people around a round table if Yuchen and Rohan must sit together?
ANSWER: 48
10. BONUS Math Short Answer A triangle has sides of 13, 14, and 15. What is its inradius?
ANSWER: 4

ANSWER: NEUTRON
