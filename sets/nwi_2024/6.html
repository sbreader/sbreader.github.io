1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Unpolarized light is passed through two polarizing filters, the first directed vertically and the second directed 45 degrees with respect to the vertical. Which of the following is closest to the fraction of the intensity of the light that emerges from the filter?
W) 0.25
X) 0.5
Y) 0.75
Z) 0.9
ANSWER: W) 0.25
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer You are in a spaceship and you throw a tennis ball. It takes 2.4 seconds from the instant you throw the ball until it hits a tennis racket. Another spaceship is traveling at a speed 4/5 of the speed of light relative to you. From the frame of reference of the other ship, to the nearest tenth of a second, how long does it take for the ball to hit the racket?
ANSWER: 4.0

5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In celebration of December, Arnold is writing the lyrics to his new Christmas song. If the amount of lyrics he writes on any given day is modeled by x3/3 − 16x2 + 192x + 25, with December 1st being x=1, on which day of December will Arnold write the most lyrics?

ANSWER: EPIDERMAL GROWTH FACTOR (also accept: GROWTH FACTOR) (do not accept: TRANSCRIPTION FACTOR)
