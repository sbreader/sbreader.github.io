1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about Bose-Einstein condensates is NOT true?
W) Bose-Einstein condensates are typically formed when a gas of bosons at low densities is cooled to temperatures very close to absolute zero
X) In a Bose-Einstein condensate, a small fraction of bosons occupy the lowest quantum state
Y) A Bose gas obeys Bose-Einstein statistics
Z) Bose-Einstein condensate may work as a mechanism for superfluidity and superconductivity
ANSWER: X) In a Bose-Einstein condensate, a small fraction of bosons occupy the lowest quantum state
