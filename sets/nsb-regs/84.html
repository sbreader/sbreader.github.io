5. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is a characteristic of alpha decay?
W) Alpha decay occurs in heavy nuclei where the electrostatic repulsion between the protons in the nucleus is large
X) The mass of a nucleus that undergoes alpha decay will be changed by only a small amount
Y) Neither the mass number nor the atomic number is changed
Z) The atomic number increases by 1
ANSWER: W) Alpha decay occurs in heavy nuclei where the electrostatic repulsion between the protons in the nucleus is large
