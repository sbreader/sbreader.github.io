1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Giving your answer in terms of the speed of light, and rounded to the nearest hundredth, what is the nuclear binding energy of a hydrogen cation?
ANSWER: 0
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the combined resistance of three resistors, connected in parallel, which have individual resistances of 10 ohms, 15 ohms, and 6 ohms?
ANSWER: 3 OHMS
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is not an example of a free radical?
W) Hydroxyl group
X) Nitric oxide
Y) Carbenium
Z) Superoxide
ANSWER: Y) Carbenium
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which gas law states that, when the volume remains constant, the pressure of a given mass of gas is directly proportional with the temperature of the gas?
ANSWER: GAY-LUSSAC’S LAW
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are studying geothermal brines in an attempt to elucidate geothermal energy. Which of the following features could NOT develop in a geothermal region?
W) Mudpots
X) Geyser
Y) Hot springs
Z) Waterspout
ANSWER: Z) Waterspout
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying groundwater flow using predictive models. Identify all of the following statements regarding groundwater that are true 1) The topography of the water table varies with precipitation 2) Primary porosity refers to the new pore spaces that formed after the first rock formation 3) Aquitards are sediments or rocks that have low permeability and porosity.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following terms would a geologist most likely use to describe how easily fluids are able to pass through an aquifer?
W) Porosity
X) Permeability
Y) Viscosity
Z) Fluidity
ANSWER: X) Permeability
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What general type of glacier exists in or adjacent to mountainous regions and flows from higher elevations to lower elevations?
ANSWER: MOUNTAIN GLACIER (also accept: ALPINE GLACIER)
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the sum of the first 4 perfect cubes?
W) 75
X) 100
Y) 125
Z) 144
ANSWER: X) 100
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area of a regular hexagon, given that the length of the apothem is 6 inches?
ANSWER: 72√3 (also accept: 72 ROOT 3)
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following chemical processes is exothermic?
W) Melting of ice
X) Breaking of bonds in a molecule
Y) Condensation of nitrogen gas
Z) Combustion of methane
ANSWER: Y) Condensation of nitrogen gas
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To 3 decimal places, what is the number of kilojoules of energy in a 240 calorie cupcake, given that the specific heat of water is 4.2 joules per gram degree celsius?
ANSWER: 1.01 KILOJOULES
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The total potential energy that a capacitor with capacitance 5 farads can store is 14.4 joules. Giving your answer in coulombs, what is the maximum charge that the capacitor can store?
ANSWER: 12
7. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Alpha decay occurs in atoms due to which of the following phenomena?
W) Pair production
X) Quantum tunneling
Y) Proton capture
Z) Electron capture
ANSWER: X) Quantum tunneling
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Marine snow is the name of the continuous shower of organic matter that supplies food for organisms living in the benthic zone. What is the general name that refers to all of the dead organic matter that comprises marine snow?
ANSWER: DETRITUS
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organs is an excretory and osmoregulatory system found in insects that are responsible for removing nitrogenous wastes?
W) Protonephridia
X) Metanephridia
Y) Malpighian tubules
Z) Kidneys
ANSWER: Y) Malpighian tubules
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following features would be classified as a trace fossil?
W) Carbon impression of a leaf
X) Insect embedded in amber
Y) A dinosaur’s footprints
Z) The preserved bones of a dinosaur
ANSWER: Y) A dinosaur’s footprints
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of following statements about volcanic eruptions that are true 1) Molten rock that has emerged through a volcano is referred to as a lava flow 2) Pyroclastic debris are clouds of pre-existing igneous rock and blobs of lava sent into the sky through a volcanic eruption 3) Effusive eruptions are characterized by lava flowing out of a vent, creating a lava flow.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3

ANSWER: PASCAL’S PRINCIPLE

X) Irreversible
Y) Adiabatic
Z) Isobaric
ANSWER: W) Reversible

Y) ħ
Z) ħ2

X) Gel electrophoresis

17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Argonne National Lab have been conducting research into superfluids by exploring computer simulations of the Bose-Einstein condensate. Identify all of the following particles can’t be in matter that is considered to be a Bose-Einstein condensate 1) Proton 2) Electron 3) Pion 4) Neutron.
ANSWER: 3 ONLY
17. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab have been streamlining the process of building a nuclear reactor. Which of the following is the main reason for nuclear reactors to have a large reactor core?
W) To reduce the chance of nuclear meltdown
X) A large reactor core reduces neutron leakage
Y) A large reactor core can hold more uranium fuel
Z) Neutrons move slower in a larger reactor core
ANSWER: X) A large reactor core reduces neutron leakage

ANSWER: 3 PRIME TO 5 PRIME

X) Liver

Z) Spleen
ANSWER: X) Liver
