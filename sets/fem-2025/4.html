1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Lateral branches in eudicots grow from discrete axillary buds nestled between the petiole and main branch. In contrast, lateral roots arise from what continuous ring of tissue surrounding the stele?
ANSWER: Pericycle
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer In molecules of nucleic acid, hydrogen bonds exist between complementary bases. However, molecules of nucleic acids are also further stabilized by what attractive forces between aromatic nucleobase systems?
ANSWER: Pi-stacking (also accept: Van Der Waals)
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer According to the BGV theorem, a universe that undergoes expansion on average over its lifetime must have originated from what infinitely dense point in space time?
ANSWER: Singularity
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer The angular frequency of an electron moving in a circle due to a uniform magnetic field is proportional to the radius of the circle raised to what power?
ANSWER: 0
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of these compounds is nonaromatic?
W) Cyclobutadiene
X) Pyridine
Y) Cyclopentadiene
Z) Benzene
ANSWER: Y) Cyclopentadiene
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A 2 by 2 by 2 lattice of carbon monoxide molecules has two equally possible configurations for each molecule. In terms of the Boltzmann constant k, what is the residual entropy of this lattice?
ANSWER: 8k ln 2
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Which of the following four transformations are affine? 1) Translations 2) Shears 3) Rotations 4) Dilations
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3, 4
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Gabriel’s horn is formed by revolving the graph of y = 1/x for x ≥ 1 around the x-axis. What is the volume of Gabriel’s horn?
ANSWER: π
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Advai is studying a specimen of andesite porphyry from the Andes mountains. He plots its grain-size distribution on a graph of frequency versus grain size. Which of the following best describes the expected grain-size distribution of this rock?
W) Uniform
X) Unimodal
Y) Bimodal
Z) Multimodal
ANSWER: Y) Bimodal
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A melt that exhibits crystal settling would be most depleted in which of the following elements?
W) Sodium
X) Silicon
Y) Magnesium
Z) Potassium
ANSWER: Y) Magnesium
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Prader-Willi syndrome only manifests if the mutant allele is inherited from the paternal genome. This is an example of what genetic phenomenon where genes are silenced in a parent-specific manner?
ANSWER: Genomic Imprinting
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Select ALL of the following three proteins that are produced by the liver 1) Angiotensinogen 2) Albumin 3) Insulin-like growth factor 2
ANSWER: ALL
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Advaith is zooming about on his new car. He wants to go navigate a vertical circular loop of radius 4 m. Assuming that the car and Advaith are point masses, at what minimum speed, in m/s, must he enter the loop in order to complete it without falling off?
ANSWER: 14
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer A car moves along a circular track of radius 25 meters. Its speed increases uniformly from 10 m/s to 40 m/s over a time period of 10 seconds. What is the magnitude of its total initial acceleration?
ANSWER: 5
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following 0.1 molar solution has lowest pH?
W) NaCl
X) NaOH
Y) HCl
Z) H2CO3
ANSWER: Y) HCl
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice According to the Hammond Postulate, in an exothermic reaction, the transition state resembles which of the following?
W) Products
X) Intermediate
Y) Reactants
Z) Catalyst
ANSWER: Y) Reactants
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the 5th derivative of ln x?
ANSWER: 24/x5
9. BONUS Math Short Answer Compute the median of the angles in a triangle with sides of length 5, 7, and 8.
ANSWER: 60°
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains how infrared satellites can determine cloud height?
W) Higher clouds contain more ice crystals that reflect infrared light
X) Lower clouds emit more infrared radiation because they are warmer
Y) Taller clouds block more ultraviolet radiation from the Sun
Z) Lower clouds scatter microwave signals more efficiently
ANSWER: X) Lower clouds emit more infrared radiation because they are warmer
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Ring rain is a process in which material from Saturn’s rings drifts inward and falls into the planet’s atmosphere. Order the following three molecules in terms of increasing abundance in Saturn’s ring rain. 1) Water 2) Carbon dioxide 3) Methane
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice An elemental analysis of an unknown pathogen reveals that it does not contain any phosphorus. Which of the following diseases is likely caused by this pathogen?
W) Infectious Mononucleosis
X) Malaria
Y) Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease
Z) Trichinosis
ANSWER: Y) Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice After conducting a bulk RNA-seq experiment, Arjun is analyzing the sequence composition of his reads. Which of the following noncoding RNA types is not encoded by the genome?
W) siRNA
X) miRNA
Y) piRNA
Z) snRNA
ANSWER: W) siRNA
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the surface charge density on a charged conductor of irregular shape?
W) Uniform throughout the volume
X) Uniform throughout the surface
Y) Higher in smoother regions, lower in sharp regions
Z) Higher in sharp regions, lower in smoother regions
ANSWER: Z) Higher in sharp regions, lower in smoother regions
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer A pipe closed on one end and open on the other end has a length of 50 cm. Assuming the speed of sound is 340 m/s, what is the fundamental frequency of the pipe in Hertz?
ANSWER: 170

W) NaClO
X) HClO3
Y) Cl2O
Z) Cl2
ANSWER: Y) Cl2O
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer At 298 K, substance A has a vapor pressure of 20 torr and substance B has a vapor pressure of 30 torr. What is the mole fraction of substance A in an ideal solution of these two substances with vapor pressure 24 torr?
ANSWER: 0.6
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Theenash can buy nuggets in 3 different sized packs; one with 3 nuggets, one with 5 nuggets, and one with 7 nuggets. Given that Theenash can buy an infinite number of any of these packs, what is the largest number of nuggets that Theenash cannot buy?
ANSWER: 4
14. BONUS Math Short Answer How many ways are there to insert the operations + and − in between the numbers 1, 2, 3, … 2027, in that order, so that the resulting sum is even?
ANSWER: 22026
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains how the very first free oxygen molecules formed in the Earth’s early atmosphere over four billion years ago?
W) Oxygen was released in large amounts by cyanobacteria during photosynthesis
X) Oxygen atoms were split from water and carbon dioxide molecules by ultraviolet sunlight
Y) Oxygen was created when the Earth’s atmosphere cooled and condensed
Z) Oxygen formed spontaneously from nitrogen gas in the early atmosphere
ANSWER: X) Oxygen atoms were split from water and carbon dioxide molecules by ultraviolet sunlight
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Expressing your answer to the nearest degree, what is the inclination of Earth’s orbital plane to the ecliptic?
ANSWER: 0

ANSWER: 20

ANSWER: −34
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why scientists have difficulty pinpointing the sources of cosmic rays?
