1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A positive charge with charge Q is placed next to a grounded hollow metal sphere. From far away, the electric field looks like the electric field of what other arrangement?

Y) Point charge with charge greater than Q

ANSWER: W) Point charge with charge less than Q
