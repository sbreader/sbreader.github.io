1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following basaltic formations is indicative of non-subaerial volcanic activity?
W) Pillows
X) Colonnades
Y) Pahoehoe flows
Z) A’a flows
ANSWER: W) Pillows
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following environments would most likely produce the most well-developed soil over time?
W) Steep slope, arid climate
X) Steep slope, humid climate
Y) Gentle slope, arid climate
Z) Gentle slope, humid climate
ANSWER: X) Steep slope, humid climate
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In boron trichloride, what is the formal charge on the central boron atom?
ANSWER: 0
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Water is completely electrolyzed to produce hydrogen and oxygen, and the resulting mixture of ideal gases is collected. If the volume of the mixture produced is 30 milliliters, how many milliliters of hydrogen were produced?
ANSWER: 20
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following three portions of the cytoskeleton in increasing radius of the fiber 1) Intermediate filaments 2) Microtubules 3) Microfilaments.
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer ABC transporters are ATP dependant pumps that export compounds from the cell against their concentration gradients. Identify all of the following three ions in a human cell that ABC transporters would possibly export 1) Na+ 2) K+ 3) Cl-.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Alice and Bob are standing ten steps apart on a number line. Each second, Bob has a 50% chance to take one step towards Alice and a 50% chance to stay still. What is the expected number of seconds until they meet?
ANSWER: 20
4. BONUS Math Multiple Choice For the definite integral from 0 to 5 of e−x, which of the following Riemann sums with a step-size of 0.2 gives the smallest approximation?
W) Left Riemann sum
X) Right Riemann sum
Y) Midpoint Riemann sum
Z) Trapezoidal sum
ANSWER: X) Right Riemann sum
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Marine biologists at the Darwin Project discovered the marine bacteria SAR11 exclusively uses purines as its source of carbon and nitrogen. Which of the following canonical nucleotide bases would promote DNA synthesis when added to a SAR11 cell culture?
W) Adenine
X) Cytosine
Y) Thymine
Z) Uracil
ANSWER: W) Adenine
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The Voigt Lab used gene-swapping to create microbes that can fix nitrogen into ammonia. These microbes are performing the biotic analogue of what industrial process used to produce most ammonia fertilizer consumed worldwide?
ANSWER: HABER-BOSCH PROCESS (also accept: HABER)
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following circuits has an equivalent resistance to a circuit with three 2-ohm resistors in parallel?
W) Two 3-ohm resistors in parallel
X) Four 1.5-ohm resistors in parallel
Y) One 1-ohm and one 2-ohm resistor in parallel
Z) One 2-ohm and one 3-ohm resistor in parallel
ANSWER: Y) One 1-ohm and one 2-ohm resistor in parallel
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer A cube of side length 5 centimeters is floating in a tank containing water covered by a 1 centimeter thick layer of oil of density 0.6 grams per cubic centimeter. The cube displaces 100 cubic centimeters of fluid. To the nearest gram, what is the cube’s mass?
ANSWER: 90
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What layer in a body of water is characterized by a density gradient that rapidly changes with depth?
ANSWER: PYCNOCLINE
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the Wilson Cycle?
W) The formation of transform faults along mid-ocean ridges
X) The cyclical formation and breakup of supercontinents
Y) The alternation of magnetic field polarity over geologic time
Z) The sequence of volcanic island formation at hot spots
ANSWER: X) The cyclical formation and breakup of supercontinents
8. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A test in MIT’s 6.191 class had a mean score of 58 and a standard deviation of 15. Assuming scores are normally distributed, which of the following is closest to the percentage of scores below 73?
W) 50
X) 68
Y) 84
Z) 95
ANSWER: Y) 84
8. BONUS Math Short Answer Aryan has invented a new version of solitaire chess. He places a pawn in the bottom left corner of the board, and every turn, he can choose to move the pawn one space up, one space right, or do nothing and leave the pawn where it is. After four moves, how many possible squares could the pawn be?
ANSWER: 15
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Aryan is studying a certain plant. He is trying to figure out what must be true about the plant if it stores malic acid at night in it’s central vacuole. Which of the following statements MUST also be true of this plant?
W) It utilizes PEP carboxylase
X) It is a halophyte
Y) It undergoes significant photorespiration
Z) It opens it’s stomata during the day
ANSWER: W) It utilizes PEP carboxylase

W) Shading by taller plants
X) Grazing by herbivores
Y) Extreme temperature variations
Z) Strong winds
ANSWER: X) Grazing by herbivores
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The Fu Lab is studying the properties of layered graphene materials. What is the hybridization of each carbon atom in graphene?
ANSWER: sp2

W) −6
X) −2
Y) +2
Z) +6
ANSWER: Z) +6
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term describes the minimum amount of energy an incoming photon must have to cause the ejection of an electron from a metal surface?
ANSWER: WORK FUNCTION
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Wrapping aluminum foil around silver oxide in a salt solution results in visible brightening of the silver. Which of the following metals can also be used instead of aluminum foil?
W) Gold
X) Sodium
Y) Tin
Z) Platinum
ANSWER: Y) Tin
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice How does the work done by the drag force on an object moving downwards change as the object approaches terminal velocity?
W) Remains constant
X) Approaches zero
Y) Decreases
Z) Increases
ANSWER: Z) Increases
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements about electric field lines that are true 1) Electric field lines always intersect the surface of conductors at right angles 2) Electric field lines can have sharp corners 3) Electric field lines can intersect each other.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2 (also accept: ALL EXCEPT 3)
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers from the MIT LIGO Group are studying gravitational waves emitted by a neutron star collapsing into what compact stellar object?
ANSWER: BLACK HOLE
13. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers in the Berger lab are studying Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks, or KANs. As opposed to traditional neural networks that learn weights and biases, KANs learn what functions that are used to introduce nonlinearities in standard neural networks?
ANSWER: ACTIVATION FUNCTIONS
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What physical constant in thermodynamics and statistical physics is equal to the ratio between the ideal gas constant and Avogadro’s number?
ANSWER: BOLTZMANN’S CONSTANT
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer In fluid mechanics, the compressibility is the negative partial derivative of volume with respect to pressure, divided by volume. In terms of SI base units, what are the units of compressibility?
ANSWER: METER SECOND SQUARED PER KILOGRAM (also accept: SECOND SQUARED METER PER KILOGRAM; METERS SECOND SQUARED INVERSE KILOGRAM) (do not accept: INVERSE PASCAL)
