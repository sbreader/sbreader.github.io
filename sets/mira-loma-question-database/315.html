Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the name for the parameter in microscopy which accentuates differences in parts of a sample, and has been improved greatly in the past hundred years due to staining technology?
W) Magnification
X) Resolution
Y) Intensity
Z) Contrast
ANSWER: Z) Contrast
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following techniques of light microscopy is correctly matched with its corresponding effect?
W) Brightfield; enhances differences in density
X) Differential-interference-contrast; highlights specific locations in a cell
Y) Confocal; focusing of light
Z) Fluorescence; enhances contrast
ANSWER: Y) Confocal; focusing of light
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the name for the type of microscope most commonly used for studying the internal ultrastructure of cells by profiling a thin section of a specimen?
W) Light microscope
X) Confocal microscope
Y) Scanning electron microscope
Z) Transmission electron microscope
ANSWER: Z) Transmission electron microscope
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer For each of the following three cell locations, give the most likely choice of microscopy that would be used to view them: beating of cilia on a cell, cell internal ultrastructure, integrins within a membrane.
ANSWER: SEM, TEM, SEM
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, which of the following five structures or features can be found in prokaryotic cells as well as eukaryotic cells? 1: Ribosomes 2: Fimbriae 3: Chromosomes 4: Cell wall 5: Lysosomes
ANSWER: 1, 3, and 4
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Rank the following types of pellets from first-formed to last-formed, when a normal animal cell is put through cell fractionation: microsomes, nuclei, mitochondria, ribosomes.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1, 4 (also accept: Nuclei, Mitochondria, Microsomes, Ribosomes)
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following structures or features can be found in both animal cells and plant cells?
W) Peroxisomes
X) Lysosomes
Y) Plasmodesmata
Z) Centrosomes
ANSWER: W) Peroxisomes
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the name of the cell feature or structure that provides an environment for material to be sorted before it is degraded by the lysosome?
W) Ribosome
X) Peroxisome
Y) Endosome
Z) Centrosome
ANSWER: Y) Endosome
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer By number, indicate which of the following statements is or are TRUE regarding lysosomes. 1: Lysosomes digest food or other resources using hydrolytic enzymes. 2: Phagocytosis occurs only in amoebae and protists. 3: Lysosomes can be made by budding from the trans face of the Golgi body. 4: Lysosomal membrane and enzymes are created by the smooth ER.
ANSWER: 1 and 3
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice One cell has a plethora of ER- and membrane-bound ribosomes, but has a below-average amount of free ribosomes. Which of the following would NOT be a likely function of this cell?
W) Creation of carrier proteins
X) Enzymatic breakdown of sugars
Y) Production of lysosomal proteins
Z) Secretion of digestive enzymes
ANSWER: X) Enzymatic breakdown of sugars
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, indicate which of the following three statements regarding the rough ER is or are TRUE. 1: Most secretory proteins created by the rough ER are glycoproteins. 2: Proteins are folded into their natural shape on the outer surface of the ER. 3: Transitional ER is a site from which vesicles emerge.
ANSWER: 1 and 3
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the structure located on the nuclear side of the surface of the nucleus that mechanically supports the shape of the nucleus?
ANSWER: NUCLEAR LAMINA (also accept: NUCLEAR MATRIX)
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of nuclear structures and their functions is NOT correct?
W) Nucleolus; transcription site of rRNA
X) Nuclear pore; site of protein secretion
Y) Nuclear lamina; translation site of rRNA
Z) Chromatin; contains DNA
ANSWER: Y) Nuclear lamina; translation site of rRNA
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Where, within the cell, would be the most likely location for the synthesis of lipids, including oils, phospholipids, and steroids?
ANSWER: SMOOTH ENDOPLASMIC RETICULUM
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, which of the following processes is or are NOT characteristic of smooth endoplasmic reticulum? 1: Synthesis of lipids and steroids 2: Drug detoxification 3: Metabolism of carbohydrates 4: Creation of membrane proteins
ANSWER: 4 only
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the process by which cells break down their own damaged organelles through usage of lysosomes.
ANSWER: AUTOPHAGY
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, indicate which of the following five pairs of a process and its explanation are incorrectly matched. 1: Pinocytosis; cellular drinking 2: Autophagy; cellular suicide 3: Apoptosis; digestion of food 4: Phagocytosis; cellular repair 5: Endocytosis; engulfing of molecules
ANSWER: 2, 3, and 4
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer By number, which of the following statements is or are TRUE about the Golgi body? 1: Vesicles coalesce on the trans face to form new Golgi cisternae. 2: According to the cisternal maturation theory, Golgi cisternae move in a cis-to-trans direction. 3: Vesicles can transport some proteins backward to less mature Golgi cisternae and to the ER. 4: Glycoproteins are synthesized at the cis face of the Golgi body.
ANSWER: 2 and 3
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Found mainly in freshwater protists, which of the following is the type of vacuole often used to pump excess water out of the cell?
W) Food vacuole
X) Central vacuole
Y) Contractile vacuole
Z) Lytic vacuole
ANSWER: Y) Contractile vacuole
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding the contractile vacuole is FALSE?
W) The CV is a protective mechanism that prevents lysis.
X) The CV is found mainly in hypotonic or hypoosmotic environments.
Y) The stage in which water flows into the CV is the systole, and the expulsion stage is the diastole.
Z) The spongiome and the CV collectively are known as the contractile vacuole complex.
ANSWER: Y) The stage in which water flows into the CV is the systole, and the expulsion stage is the diastole.
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By number, which of the following statements is or are TRUE about mitochondria? 1: The space within a mitochondrion contains infoldings named cristae. 2: The mitochondrial matrix contains both DNA and free ribosomes. 3: Only one membrane is found in mitochondria. 4: Mitochondria are usually about 1-10 micrometers long.
ANSWER: 1, 2, and 4
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Match the following four functions to the mitochondrion, using the word bank of cristae, intermembrane space, inner mitochondrial membrane, and the outer membrane. You may repeat or not use words. The functions are: 1: Storage of cytochrome c 2: ATP synthase 3: Oxidative phosphorylation 4: F1 particle storage
ANSWER: 1: intermembrane space, 2: inner mitochondrial membrane, 3: inner mitochondrial membrane, 4: cristae
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A chloroplast contains flattened, interconnected sacs that, when stacked, form structures named what?
W) Thylakoids
X) Stroma
Y) Grana
Z) Stomata
ANSWER: Y) Grana
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the portion of the chloroplast which serves to connect nearby grana.
ANSWER: LAMELLAE (also accept: STROMA THYLAKOIDS; INTERGRANA THYLAKOIDS)
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the type of specialized peroxisome which are found in the fat-storing tissues of plant seeds that contain enzymes that initiate the conversion of fatty acids to sugar.
ANSWER: GLYOXYSOMES
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true regarding glyoxysomes in cells?
W) They are found only in haploid cells.
X) They are found only in plant cells.
Y) They contain enzymes which facilitate gluconeogenesis.
Z) They convert fatty acids to sugars.
ANSWER: W) They are found only in haploid cells.
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the three main components of the cytoskeleton of a cell.
ANSWER: MICROFILAMENTS, MICROTUBULES, INTERMEDIATE FILAMENTS
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, indicate which of the following five is or are functions of microfilaments. 1: Cell motility 2: Changes in cell shape 3: Cytoplasmic streaming 4: Muscle contraction 5: Anchorage of nucleus
ANSWER: 2, 3, and 4
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following cytoskeletal elements is matched properly with its associated protein?
W) Microfilaments; actin
X) Intermediate filaments; tubulin
Y) Microtubules; keratin
Z) Intermediate filaments; actin
ANSWER: W) Microfilaments; actin
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer By number, indicate which of the following statements regarding the cytoskeletal system is or are TRUE. 1: Motor proteins "walk" along microfilaments when powered by ATP. 2: Intermediate filaments are "permanent," while microfilaments allow for change in shape. 3: Microfilaments are, by far, the thickest cytoskeletal element. 4: Microtubules are used to move chromosomes from their kinetochores during cell division.
ANSWER: 1, 2, and 4
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the general name for protrusions of microtubules on the outside of a membrane which can both function in cell movement and in cellular signalling?
W) Fimbriae
X) Flagella
Y) Cilia
Z) Archaellum
ANSWER: Y) Cilia
17. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is FALSE regarding cell movement and centrioles?
W) Centrioles are each composed of nine triplets of microtubules.
X) Flagella move by using a power stroke and a recovery stroke, thereby moving its cell.
Y) The ultrastructure of both flagella and cilia are based upon the "9+2" pattern.
Z) Dynein cross-linkages are responsible for the bending movements of a motile organelle.
ANSWER: X) Flagella move by using a power stroke and a recovery stroke, thereby moving its cell.
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer These types of temporary projections of eukaryotic cells are found in several varieties, including Lobopodia, Filopodia, Reticulopodia, and Axopodia. They are formed by microtubule and filament structures, and are found primarily in ameboids. Their functions include movement and capturing prey. For four points, name this type of movement also known as ameboid movement that can also be used in reproduction.
ANSWER: PSEUDOPODIA
18. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following properties of a cell is LEAST likely to contribute to cytoplasmic streaming in a plant or animal cell?
W) Dynein interactions
X) Sol-gel interactions
Y) Actin-myosin interactions
Z) Parallel actin filament interactions
ANSWER: W) Dynein interactions
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In which of the following compartments within a plant cell would pectin most likely be found?
W) Central vacuole
X) Chloroplast
Y) Plasma membrane
Z) Middle lamella
ANSWER: Z) Middle lamella
19. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In which flexible and thin wall of a plant cell would cellulose fibrils most likely be found to direct growth?
W) Extracellular matrix
X) Primary cell wall
Y) Middle lamella
Z) Secondary cell wall
ANSWER: X) Primary cell wall
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the name of the component of a cell's extracellular matrix which consists of a small core protein with many carbohydrate chains covalently attached?
W) Fibronectin
X) Integrins
Y) Collagen
Z) Proteoglycans
ANSWER: Z) Proteoglycans
20. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT characteristic of the extracellular matrix?
W) Collagen fibers embedded within proteoglycan complexes
X) Membrane proteins named integrins with one subunit
Y) Fibronectins attached to integrins
Z) Polysaccharide molecules with hundreds of proteoglycan molecules attached noncovalently
ANSWER: X) Membrane proteins named integrins with one subunit
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the type of intercellular junction occurring only in plant cells, characterized by the flow of cytosol, which unifies the plant into one living continuum.
ANSWER: PLASMODESMATA
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer By number, name the two out of the following four statements which are TRUE regarding intercellular junctions in animal tissues. 1: Tight junctions are always bound together by specific proteins. 2: Desmosomes are cellular "rivets" which fasten cells together using intermediate filaments. 3: Gap junctions are characterized by the fact that neighboring cells showing this junction are often more than 100 micrometers apart. 4: Communicating junctions are known as plasmodesmata in animal cells.
ANSWER: 1 and 2
22. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of scientists reasoned first that cell membranes must be composed of phospholipid bilayers?
W) Davson and Danielli
X) Singer and Nicholson
Y) Gorter and Grendel
Z) Frye and Eddin
ANSWER: Y) Gorter and Grendel
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer Rank the following membrane models and discoveries from earliest to most current. 1: Discovery of the phospholipid bilayer 2: Fluid mosaic model 3: "Sandwich" model 4: Development of the freeze-fracture method
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2, 4
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the organic molecule found wedged between phospholipid molecules in the plasma membranes of animal cells, often thought of as a "temperature buffer" for a membrane.
ANSWER: CHOLESTEROL
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which of the following statements regarding the fluidity of membranes is or are TRUE? 1: Phospholipids move laterally about 107 times per second, but flip-flop vertically approximately once per month. 2: Unsaturated phospholipids give rise to more viscous membranes. 3: Cholesterol can both reduce fluidity and reduce solidification in a membrane. 4: The freeze-fracture method showed that the fluid mosaic model was correct.
ANSWER: 1, 3, and 4
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following events regarding the membrane system within a cell chronologically. 1: Proteins and glycolipids transported via vesicles to the plasma membrane. 2: Modification of glycoproteins and addition of carbohydrates to lipids 3: Fusion of vesicles to membrane, resulting in the secretion of proteins 4: Synthesis of membrane proteins and lipids in the ER
ANSWER: 4, 2, 1, 3
25. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is FALSE regarding membrane permeability?
W) Aquaporins can allow entry of up to 3 billion water molecules per second
X) Water molecules can only fit ten at a time in an aquaporin's central channel
Y) Water molecules, even without aquaporins, can diffuse very quickly across a membrane
Z) Transport proteins are specific for the substance it translocates
ANSWER: Y) Water molecules, even without aquaporins, can diffuse very quickly across a membrane
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, indicate which of the following 3 statements is or are TRUE regarding selective permeability. 1: Hydrocarbons, carbon dioxide, and oxygen can cross through a membrane easily. 2: Glucose transporters within a membrane will also accept similar molecules, like fructose. 3: There are two types of transport proteins: carrier and channel.
ANSWER: 1 and 3
