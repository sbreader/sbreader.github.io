1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What very rare genetic disorder affecting one in every 100,000 to 250,000 male births is characterized by abnormal immune system function, eczema, and decreased blood clots throughout the body?
ANSWER: Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three choices that are true regarding Nanopore sequencing 1) NovaSeq 6000 is a common Nanopore sequencing platform 2) Nanopore sequencing involves passing a DNA/RNA molecule through a small pore 3) Nanopore is dependent on PCR copying
ANSWER: 2
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Akhil records a proton NMR spectrum of a conformationally restricted molecule. Two vicinal protons have a dihedral angle of approximately 180 degrees. According to the relationship connecting vicinal proton coupling constants to dihedral angle, what named relationship predicts that these protons should exhibit a relatively large coupling constant?
ANSWER: Karplus relationship
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A transition-metal complex shows a smaller Racah B parameter than the corresponding free metal ion because ligand bonding has reduced interelectronic repulsion among the metal d electrons. What effect describes this decrease?
ANSWER: Nephelauxetic effect
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Extensive removal of plagioclase during fractional crystallization can produce a negative europium anomaly in the residual melt. Which of the following best explains this effect?
W) Divalent europium can substitute for calcium in plagioclase
X) Trivalent europium can substitute for aluminum in plagioclase
Y) Tetravalent europium can substitute for silicon in plagioclase
Z) Europium becomes highly volatile before feldspar crystallizes
ANSWER: W) Divalent europium can substitute for calcium in plagioclase
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The accompanying QAPF diagram shows the composition of a plutonic rock containing 25 percent quartz, 20 percent alkali feldspar, and 55 percent plagioclase. Which of the following rock names corresponds to its position on the diagram?
W) Granite
X) Granodiorite
Y) Tonalite
Z) Quartz syenite
ANSWER: X) Granodiorite
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Let f(z) = 1/2z2-z + 2. Which of the following best characterizes the fixed point at z=2?
W) Superattracting
X) Attracting
Y) Indifferent
Z) Repelling
ANSWER: Y) Indifferent
4. BONUS Math Short Answer How many ways can a cube be fully colored with two colors, assuming that two colorings are the same if one can be obtained by rotating the cube to yield the other?
ANSWER: 10
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Many-body gravitational systems generally lack closed-form solutions but can still be evolved accurately on a computer by repeatedly advancing the equations of motion in small time steps. What general computational approach is this?
ANSWER: Numerical integration (also accept: Numerical time integration)
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer In the Kerr geometry of a rotating black hole, what region outside the event horizon is bounded by the static limit and contains no observers that can remain stationary relative to infinity?
ANSWER: Ergosphere
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A large mountain range divides two species of horses into two separate populations. After a long period of time, scientists gather horse samples from both of the new groups and combine them together. However, after analyzing the data set of the combined group, the scientists notice that there is a decrease in the number of heterozygotes and an increase in the number of homozygotes. This result can be explained by what genetic effect?
ANSWER: Wahlund effect
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer The Bombay phenotype is a rare blood type in which the blood is falsely characterized to appear as the Standard O. This is due to the absence of the H antigen caused by the mutation of what specific gene?
ANSWER: FUT1 gene (also accept: FUT gene)
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In ferrocene, each cyclopentadienyl ligand binds to iron through five contiguous carbon atoms. Give the hapticity descriptor for each cyclopentadienyl ligand.
ANSWER: η5 (also accept: Eta-five)
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer For a binary mixture held at constant temperature and pressure, complete the Gibbs-Duhem relation by giving the expression equal to zero involving the amounts n1, n2 and infinitesimal chemical-potential changes dμ1, dμ2.
ANSWER: n1 dμ1 + n2 dμ2 = 0
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Remineralization of sinking organic matter adds dissolved carbon dioxide to deep seawater. Which of the following correctly describes the resulting changes to pH and carbonate-ion concentration?
W) Both increase
X) pH increases while carbonate-ion concentration decreases
Y) pH decreases while carbonate-ion concentration increases
Z) Both decrease
ANSWER: Z) Both decrease
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In the polluted troposphere, nitrogen dioxide photolysis produces atomic oxygen, which reacts with molecular oxygen to form ozone. However, the simple photostationary cycle does not produce sustained net ozone accumulation. Which of the following mechanisms permits VOCs to contribute to net ozone production?
W) Organic peroxy radicals convert NO to NO2 without consuming ozone
X) Volatile organic compounds directly convert molecular nitrogen into ozone
Y) Nitric oxide irreversibly removes all reactive nitrogen from the atmosphere
Z) Organic peroxy radicals inhibit the photolysis of nitrogen dioxide
ANSWER: W) Organic peroxy radicals convert NO to NO2 without consuming ozone

ANSWER: 3/5

ANSWER: Pores of Kohn
