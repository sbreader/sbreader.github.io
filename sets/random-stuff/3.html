1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following factors has the greatest effect on soil formation?
W) Time
X) Climate
Y) Biological activity
Z) Slope
ANSWER: X) Climate
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of soil would be found if you dug up a sample of soil from California?
W) Pedalfers
X) Pedocals
Y) Laterites
Z) Histosols
ANSWER: X) Pedocals
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Coby is in his submarine exploring the mid-Atlantic ridge. Which types of smokers would he most commonly see?
W) Black smokers
X) White smokers
Y) Gray smokers
Z) He would fail to pilot the submarine and he would accidentally fall inside
ANSWER: W) Black smokers
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four moons from smallest to largest based off of their diameters: 1 – Titan; 2 – Ganymede; 3 – Triton; 4 – Oberon
ANSWER: 4,3,1,2 (also accept: Oberon, Triton, Titan, Ganymede)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What makes up the largest percentage of the universe’s composition?
W) Dark energy
X) Dark matter
Y) Matter
Z) Radiation
ANSWER: W) Dark energy
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 4 elements that compose the universe from most abundant to least abundant: 1 – Carbon, 2 – Hydrogen, 3-Helium, 4 – Iron
ANSWER: 2,3,1,4 (also accept: Hydrogen, Helium, Carbon, Iron)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following technologies is used to measure the depth of ocean floors?
W) LASER
X) VLBI
Y) SONAR
Z) CHARA
ANSWER: Y) SONAR
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following 4 layers in the Ocean from shallowest to deepest: 1-Continental shelf, 2-Abyssal Plain, 3 – Oceanic trench, 4-Continental slope
ANSWER: 1,4,2,3 (also accept: Continental shelf, Continental slope, Abyssal Plain, Oceanic trench)
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What year will Halley’s Comet next appear in the night sky?
W) 2026
X) 2061
Y) 2098
Z) 2125
ANSWER: X) 2061
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following 4 Big Bang eras from earliest to latest: 1 – GUT Era, 2 – Planck Era, 3 – Particle Era, 4 – Electroweak Era
ANSWER: 2,1,4,3 (also accept: Planck Era, GUT Era, Electroweak Era, Particle Era)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which of the following layers of the ionosphere do auroras generally occur in?
W) D-Layer
X) E-Layer
Y) F1-Layer
Z) F2-Layer
ANSWER: X) E-Layer
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following cloud types from lowest altitude to highest altitude: 1 – Cirrocumulus, 2 – Nimbostratus, 3 – Stratocumulus
ANSWER: 2,3,1 (also accept: Nimbostratus, Stratocumulus, Cirrocumulus)
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following mostly makes up the chemical composition of Venus’ clouds?
W) Carbon dioxide
X) Water vapor
Y) Sulfuric acid
Z) Carbonyl sulfide
ANSWER: Y) Sulfuric acid
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The clouds on Venus rain sulfuric acid, but this rain never reaches the ground due to the high temperatures which evaporates the rain before it reaches the ground, causing them to rise up into the clouds again. What rare phenomenon describes this type of precipitation shown by Venus’ clouds?
ANSWER: Virga (also accept: Virga rain)
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In a flowing stream, which of the following particles would get deposited first?
W) Clay
X) Sand
Y) Silt
Z) Pebbles
ANSWER: Z) Pebbles
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 rocks that have a crystalline texture: 1-Conglomerate, 2 – Rock salt, 3-Dolostone, 4-Siltstone
ANSWER: 2 and 3 (also accept: Rock salt and Dolostone)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Sanjay, David, and Stanford all hop on a boat that is traveling down a meandering river. At which parts of the river would they be traveling at the fastest speed?
W) At the cut bank
X) At the point bar
Y) At the floodplain
Z) They wouldn’t travel because they’re too heavy for the boat to hold up
ANSWER: W) At the cut bank
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes the imaginary line that connects the lowest points in a river?
ANSWER: Thalweg
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following continents from least to greatest based on the percentage of glaciers they hold: 1 – North America, 2 – Antarctica, 3 – Asia, 4 – Africa
ANSWER: 4,3,1,2 (also accept: Africa, Asia, North America, Antarctica)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Sanjay, David, and Stanford get hired by NASA to go on the next Apollo mission to the moon. They aim to make a landing in the Mare Tranquillitatis. What rock would they most likely find there?
W) Anorthosites
X) Granites
Y) Basalts
Z) They wouldn't find anything because they would fail the landing
ANSWER: Y) Basalts
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What track do infant stars of less than 3 solar masses that are in the PMS stage of stellar evolution follow?
ANSWER: Hayashi Track
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of volcanoes mostly make up Mars’ volcanoes?
W) Shield Volcanoes
X) Stratovolcanoes
Y) Composite Volcanoes
Z) Cinder cones
ANSWER: W) Shield Volcanoes
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The no-hair theorem states that all black hole solutions of the Einstein-Maxwell equations of gravitation and electromagnetism in general relativity can be completely characterized by only three externally observable factors. Which of the following is not one of the factors?
W) Linear Momentum
X) Angular Momentum
Y) Electric charge
Z) Mass
ANSWER: W) Linear Momentum
13. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Middleton High School science bowl team is going on a field trip. They visit a black hole and Sanjay accidentally falls in. David, being the good teammate he is, does absolutely nothing. From David’s perspective, how would Sanjay’s fall appear?
W) His fall would appear to take longer than it would appear to Sanjay
X) His fall would appear to be shorter than it would appear to Sanjay
Y) His fall would take the same amount of time from both people’s perspectives
Z) Sanjay wouldn’t fall in because his mass is too large for the black hole to suck in
ANSWER: W) His fall would appear to take longer than it would appear to Sanjay
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Aqua decides to build his own submarine to go explore the ocean. Which of the following vertical ocean zones would he mostly likely find Anglerfish in?
W) Photic Zone
X) Twilight Zone
Y) Aphotic Zone
Z) The Abyss
ANSWER: Y) Aphotic Zone
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What term is used to describe how the constantly moving system of deep-ocean circulation that is driven by temperature and salinity?
ANSWER: Ocean conveyor belt

W) Wind speed
X) Duration of wind
Y) Fetch
Z) Water temperature
ANSWER: Z) Water temperature
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a protective structure that was built to protect coastal erosion?
W) Jetties
X) Groins
Y) Seawalls
Z) Harbors
ANSWER: Z) Harbors
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following comets famously collided with Jupiter, providing the first direct observation of an extraterrestrial collision in the Solar System?
W) Hale-Bopp
X) ISON
Y) Shoemaker-Levy 9
Z) Hyakutake
ANSWER: Y) Shoemaker-Levy 9
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Based off of the planets visible in the night sky to the naked eye, answer these questions: 1 – Which planet moves the slowest across the night sky on average, 2 – Which planet moves the fastest across the night sky on average?
ANSWER: 1) Saturn; 2) Mercury
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes organisms living on or in the bottom of the ocean that mainly survive by feeding on marine snow?
ANSWER: Benthos (also accept: Benthic organisms)
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Classification of Marine organisms often fall under the 3 categories of Plankton, Nekton, and Benthos. Match the following 3 organisms with their respective classification: 1 – Squid, 2 – Diatoms, 3 – Sea star
ANSWER: 1 is Nekton, 2 is Plankton, and 3 is Benthos OR Squid are Nektons, Diatoms are Planktons, and Sea stars are Benthos
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice As a comet moves closer to the Sun, the comet nucleus starts sublimating and will leave behind a tail. Which way will the comet’s tail point as it orbits the Sun?
W) Away from the Sun
X) Towards the Sun
Y) Pointed towards Polaris
Z) In a random direction
ANSWER: W) Away from the Sun

ANSWER: Hydrogen and Helium

X) Ordovician
Y) Silurian
Z) Devonian

19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What eon, era, period, and epoch are we currently in?
