1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the boundary that the northeast trade winds and the southeast trade winds meet along called?
ANSWER: INTERTROPICAL CONVERGENCE ZONE
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name the cells as described by the three-cell model of atmospheric circulation from northern most cell to cell closest to the equator.
ANSWER: POLAR, FERREL, HADLEY
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following three choices about tornadoes that are true: A hook-shaped echo seen on a Doppler Radar indicates the possible presence of a tornado. Within multi-vortex tornadoes, the smaller whirls are called micro vortices A rising, spinning column of air within a thunderstorm is called a mesocyclone.
ANSWER: 1, 2, AND 3
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The discovery of what phenomenon led to the abandonment of the steady-state theory in 1965?
ANSWER: COSMIC BACKGROUND RADIATION
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A yellow box contains 8 items, 3 of which are defective. A green box contains five items, two of which are defective. If an item is drawn at random from each box, what is the probability both items will be nondefective?
W) 3/20
X) 3/8
Y) 5/13
Z) 8/13
ANSWER: X) 3/8
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Which test for statistical significance would be used to test the difference between two sample means?
ANSWER: STUDENT'S T-TEST
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Name one of the two mathematicians who initiated the modern study of set theory in the 1870s.
ANSWER: GEORG CANTOR (also accept: RICHARD DEDEKIND)
4. BONUS Math Short Answer What shape does a probability density function of a student's t-test assume?
ANSWER: BELL
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Give the equation of the vertical asymptote of the quantity 2x –1 times the quantity x + 3 all divided by the quantity x+3 squared?
ANSWER: x = -3
6. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice In terms of pi, what is the volume of the region between the two concentric spheres with radii of 2 and 5.
W) 24pi
X) 86pi
Y) 125pi
Z) 156pi
ANSWER: Z) 156pi
6. BONUS Math Short Answer A right circular cone with a base radius of 4 is inscribed in a sphere with a radius of 5. Give the ratio of the volume of the cone to the volume of the sphere. Give your ratio in integer form.
ANSWER: 32:125
7. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice In the geometric sequence, 1, 2, 4…, what is the 13th term?
W) 24
X) 1024
Y) 4096
Z) 8192
ANSWER: Y) 4096
7. BONUS Math Short Answer Given the geometric sequence 1, 2, 4…, find the sum of the first 13 terms.
ANSWER: 8191
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following definitions of species does not account for organisms that reproduce asexually?
W) Biological
X) Morphological
Y) Ecological
Z) Phylogenetic
ANSWER: W) Biological
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In embryo development of a plant, what term is given to the thread of cells that anchors the embryo to the parent plant?
ANSWER: SUSPENSOR
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which part of the large intestine is especially large in herbivores due to the need to ferment ingested material?
ANSWER: CECUM
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, give all of the following that are true about regulation of gene expression? The operator, the promoter, and the genes they control make up the operon. The repressor binds to the operator and blocks the attachment of DNA polymerase to the promoter. Tryptophan can function as a corepressor by cooperating with a repressor protein. An inducer works by inactivating a repressor.
ANSWER: 1, 3, and 4
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name for the cluster of proteins that assemble on the promoter sequence on the upstream end of the gene?
ANSWER: TRANSCRIPTION INITIATION COMPLEX
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following crystals is incorrectly paired?
W) Crystalline cesium chloride is a ionic crystal
X) Graphite is a covalent crystal
Y) Sodium chloride is a molecular crystal
Z) Crystalline gallium is a metallic crystal
ANSWER: Y) Sodium chloride is a molecular crystal
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which quantum number accounts for the average distance of the electron from the nucleus in a particular orbital?
W) Principal Quantum number
X) Angular Momentum Quantum Number
Y) Magnetic quantum number
Z) Electron Spin Quantum Number
ANSWER: W) Principal Quantum number
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, give all of the following that are true about stereochemistry. 1. The cis and trans isomers of coordination compounds generally have similar colors, melting points, and dipole moments. 2. Isomers that are not superimposable with their mirror images are called chiral. 3. The optical isomers of a compound have identical physical and chemical properties.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for a mixture that has equal amounts of left- and right-handed enantiomers of a chiral molecule?
ANSWER: RACEMIC MIXTURE
16. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Lenz's law is an electrical restatement of which conservation law?
W) momentum
X) angular momentum
Y) mass
Z) energy
ANSWER: Z) energy
18. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice An electron moves at a constant speed at right angles to a uniform flux. If the flux density were doubled, the force on the electron would be?
W) unaffected
X) halved
Y) doubled
Z) quadrupled
ANSWER: Y) doubled
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer An emf of 0.05 volts is induced in a wire when it moves at right angles to a uniform magnetic field with a speed of 5 meters per second. If the length of the wire is 10 centimeters, what is the flux density, in teslas?
ANSWER: 0.1
