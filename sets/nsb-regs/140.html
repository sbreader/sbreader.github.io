1. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true?
W) 37 is congruent to 6 mod 5
X) 71 is congruent to 5 mod 3
Y) 43 is congruent to 8 mod 7

ANSWER: W) 37 is congruent to 6 mod 5
1. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the y-intercept of the line passing through the point (4, –3) that is perpendicular to the line 3x – 7y = 12.
ANSWER: 19/3 (also accept: (0, 19/3))
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice At the National Renewable Energy Lab, scientists are using sunlight to split what chemical compound during photoelectrochemical processes that employ stacks of light-absorbing semiconductors immersed in an acid-water solution?
W) Methane
X) Ammonia
Y) Hydrogen Peroxide
Z) Water
ANSWER: Z) Water
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of secondary batteries 1) They are not rechargeable 2) They require a constant source of energy 3) The electricity-producing reaction in them is chemically reversible.
ANSWER: 3

W) Sodium carbonate
X) Phosphoric acid
Y) Sodium hydroxide
Z) Calcium chloride
ANSWER: X) Phosphoric acid

7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice You are observing a distant star with an orbiting planet whose orbital plane is perfectly oriented so you can observe the exoplanet’s transit in front of the star. In which of the following orientations will the star-planet system appear brightest?

X) When the planet is directly behind the star
