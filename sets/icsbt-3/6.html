Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following changes would not result in the increase of conduction speed of a neuron:
W) Decreasing the diameter of the axon
X) Decreasing capacitance of the axon
Y) Increasing myelination of the axon
Z) Increasing internodal distance within the normal physiological range
ANSWER: W) Decreasing the diameter of the axon
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Commonly found in the root nodules in legumes, what oxygen binding protein maintains a low oxygen concentration and results in a reddish appearance?
ANSWER: Leghemoglobin
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Cyclohexane can take multiple possible conformations. Which of its conformations is LEAST stable?
ANSWER: Half-chair
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Compounds like glucose contain a hydroxyl group in the axial position instead of the equatorial position. This is due to what stereoelectronic effect which maximizes p orbital overlap?
ANSWER: Anomeric Effect
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Along the west coast of North America, a persistent wind blowing toward the south produces which direction of net Ekman transport in the Northern Hemisphere?
W) Toward the coast
X) Away from the coast
Y) Toward the north
Z) Toward the south
ANSWER: X) Away from the coast
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following 3 aluminum silicate polymorphs by the conditions they most strongly favor, from lowest pressure to highest pressure. 1) Kyanite 2) Andalusite 3) Sillimanite
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many positive integers less than 1166 have an odd number of positive divisors?
ANSWER: 34
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Three distinct circles and four straight lines are drawn in a plane in general position. What is the maximum possible number of intersection points?
ANSWER: 36
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In a conventional cyclotron with a fixed magnetic field, why does a charged particle eventually fall out of phase with the accelerating electric field as it reaches relativistic speeds?
W) The particle's charge decreases as its speed rises
X) Relativistic momentum increases, lowering the cyclotron frequency and causing phase slippage
Y) The magnetic force approaches zero at high speed
Z) The accelerating electric field loses amplitude as the particle gains energy
ANSWER: X) Relativistic momentum increases, lowering the cyclotron frequency and causing phase slippage
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer An electron moves perpendicular to a 200-tesla magnetic field at 0.1c. Ignoring any electric field, what is the magnitude of the magnetic force on the electron to two significant figures?
ANSWER: 9.6 × 10-10 N
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What peptide hormone, released primarily by atrial cardiomyocytes in response to atrial stretch and increased blood volume, promotes natriuresis and lowers blood pressure?
ANSWER: Atrial natriuretic peptide (also accept: ANP)
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer L-type calcium channels help coordinate cardiac muscle contraction by permitting the influx of what ion during the action potential plateau?
ANSWER: Ca2+
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following organic reactions would be most suitable for the alkylation of a benzene ring:
W) Suzuki
X) Fischer
Y) Friedel-Crafts
Z) Diels-Alder
ANSWER: Y) Friedel-Crafts
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following benzene ring interaction configurations from least to most favorable. 1) Sandwich 2) Parallel-Displaced 3) T-Shaped
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Akhil and Advik are studying binary star systems. What apparent contradiction describes binary star systems in which the less massive star is more evolved than its more massive companion because of substantial mass transfer?
ANSWER: Algol paradox
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice For otherwise identical planets, which change would produce the largest radial-velocity signal in the host star?
W) Decreasing planet mass
X) Increasing orbital distance
Y) Increasing planet mass
Z) Decreasing orbital inclination toward face-on
ANSWER: Y) Increasing planet mass
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The Sierpiński triangle consists of an equilateral triangle recursively subdivided into smaller self-similar triangles. What general geometric pattern does it exemplify?
ANSWER: Fractal
9. BONUS Math Short Answer For how many positive values of x is 20/(x+1) + 20/(3(x+1)) a positive integer?
ANSWER: 26
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following choices is closest to the amount of Joules of heat needed to raise the temperature of 3 moles of molecular hydrogen gas held at 1 atm constant pressure from 280 K to 300 K?
W) 1900
X) 1800
Y) 1700
Z) 1600
ANSWER: Y) 1700
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer A gas initially held at 400 K at 3,000 Pa in a 4 L vessel. If the gas isobarically compresses until its temperature reaches 300 K, how much work do the gas's surroundings do on it to the nearest Joule?
ANSWER: 3 J
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Hydrogenosomes, reduced mitochondria that produce hydrogen gas, are commonly found in what subclass of the supergroup Excavata?
ANSWER: Parabasalids
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Rank the following three coefficients of relatedness between two individuals in increasing order. 1) Skyler and his first cousin Alex 2) Akhil and his brother Arush 3) Michael and his mom's father
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Raoult's Law is an example of a limiting law because it becomes more accurate as what quantity approaches 0:
W) Concentration of Solvent
X) Vapor Pressure of Solvent
Y) Concentration of Solute
Z) Vapor Pressure of Solute
ANSWER: Y) Concentration of Solute
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Assume a solution of A and B is ideal according to Raoult's law. The vapor pressures at STP of A and B are 15 and 35 respectively, and the mole fraction of A is 0.6. Assuming the solution is also at STP, what is the solution's vapor pressure?
ANSWER: 23
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following metamorphic textures is most indicative of partial melting of the original rock?
W) Slaty
X) Hornfelsic
Y) Phyllitic
Z) Migmatitic
ANSWER: Z) Migmatitic
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 factors that would generally increase the likelihood of deep convection in the atmosphere. 1) Increasing low-level moisture 2) Increasing surface temperature 3) Decreasing temperature aloft 4) Increasing convective inhibition
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Advik walks from one corner of a 124 by 18 rectangular grid to the opposite corner, moving only along grid lines in the positive horizontal or vertical direction. What is the length of any shortest path, in unit segments?
ANSWER: 142
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Let s be the sum of all primes less than 110. What is the unit digit of s3?
ANSWER: 0
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A 3 kg disk of radius 1.5 meters is rolling without slipping at a speed of 3 m/s. What are the sums of the velocities of the points at the top and bottom of the disk to the nearest tenth of a m/s?
ANSWER: 6.0 m/s
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer A photon has a wavelength 620 nanometers. If its wavelength is halved, by what factor does its energy change?
ANSWER: 2
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Plants such as biennials and spring bulbs often undergo what process, in which they are exposed to cold temperatures for long periods of time to induce flowering?
ANSWER: Vernalization
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following peptides involved in pain signaling and regulation does not have a primary function to decrease pain perception?
W) Enkephalin
X) Dynorphin
Y) Substance P
Z) Endorphin
ANSWER: Y) Substance P
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following has a positive enthalpy of solution:
W) NaCl
X) HCl
Y) MgSO4
Z) LiCl
ANSWER: W) NaCl
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Osamason wants to use his laser eyes to vaporize Nettspend's sapphire jewelry and wants to know how much energy it will take. What thermodynamic cycle, which finds the lattice enthalpy of an ionic solid, will help him find this energy?
ANSWER: Born-Haber Cycle
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A planet and its star have the same average density as a second planet-star system, but every linear dimension in the first system is larger. Which planetary property increases most directly with planetary radius if density remains constant?
W) Surface gravity
X) Mean density
Y) Orbital eccentricity
Z) Albedo
ANSWER: W) Surface gravity
18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The magnetic inclination recorded in an ancient lava flow is primarily useful for estimating which paleogeographic quantity?
W) Longitude
X) Latitude
Y) Elevation
Z) Distance from the mid-ocean ridge
ANSWER: X) Latitude
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Three fair dice are rolled, conditioned on all three results being greater than 3. What is the probability that exactly two of the three results are equal?
ANSWER: 2/3
19. BONUS Math Multiple Choice What is the smallest integer value of x greater than 1, such that the following inequality holds true: sin x + 2cos x + tan x > 0?
W) 3
X) 4
Y) 5
Z) 6
ANSWER: Z) 6
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What distribution describes the probability of a molecule in a system to have a specific translational velocity at a given temperature?
ANSWER: Maxwell-Boltzmann Distribution (do not accept: Boltzmann Distribution)
20. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists compare protons at 10,000 kelvin with neutral hydrogen atoms at 4,000 kelvin. Neglecting electron mass, what is the ratio of the protons' root-mean-squared speed to that of the neutral hydrogen atoms?
ANSWER: √(5/2)
