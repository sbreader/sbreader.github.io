1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What phenomenon is generated when an object propagates in a medium faster than the speed of sound in that medium?
ANSWER: SHOCK WAVE (also accept: SHOCK; SONIC BOOM)
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer To two significant figures and in meters, what is the wavelength of a sound wave with a frequency of 260 hertz?
ANSWER: 1.3
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of lifting mechanisms would most likely occur near a mountain range?
W) Frontal
X) Orographic
Y) Convective
Z) Convergence
ANSWER: X) Orographic
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three characterisitcs that describe hot spots 1) They are zones of crustal uplift 2) They have an elevated geotherm 3) They can be over 100 kilometers across.
ANSWER: ALL OF THEM
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A triangle has two sides of lengths 39 and 57. What is the largest possible integer length of the third side?
ANSWER: 95
3. BONUS Math Short Answer The line with equation y = 3x + 2 is reflected across the line y = x. What are the slope and y-intercept of its image, respectively?
ANSWER: SLOPE = 1/3, y-INTERCEPT = –2/3
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The fact that a solution of hydrofluoric acid will become less acidic if you add sodium fluoride to it is an example of what effect?
ANSWER: COMMON ION EFFECT (also accept: LE CHATELIER'S PRINCIPLE)
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If a rate constant is expressed in hertz, what order must the reaction be?
ANSWER: FIRST (also accept: ONE)
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Ames Lab are developing new techniques to study substances that lower the activation energy of chemical reactions. What is the general term for these substances?
ANSWER: CATALYST
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab are developing techniques to prevent the leakage of information in future quantum computers. This information leakage occurs because of what quantum mechanical phenomenon in which a particle crosses classicallyforbidden energy barriers?
ANSWER: QUANTUM TUNNELING (also accept: TUNNELING)
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Phages are viruses that infect what group of organisms?
ANSWER: BACTERIA
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the genetic material carried by HIV?
W) dsDNA
X) ssDNA
Y) dsRNA
Z) ssRNA
ANSWER: Z) ssRNA
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Consider the first-order decomposition of N2O5 into nitrogen dioxide and oxygen. In order to get a linear fit, which of the following parameters should be graphed against time?
W) Natural log of the concentration of N2O5
X) Concentration of N2O5
Y) Concentration of N2O5 squared
Z) One over the concentration of N2O5
ANSWER: W) Natural log of the concentration of N2O5
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three elements in order of increasing melting point 1) Tin 2) Carbon 3) Germanium.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2

W) 3
X) 6
Y) 8
Z) 12
ANSWER: Z) 12
8. BONUS Math Short Answer Evaluate the integral from 0 to π/2 of sine 3x dx.
ANSWER: 1/3
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following three stages of development chronologically 1) Fetus 2) Embryo 3) Zygote.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Professional antigen-presenting cells such as macrophages and dendritic cells typically present foreign antigens to which of the following types of cells?
W) Helper T
X) Cytotoxic T
Y) Memory B
Z) Plasma

10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab have been studying a bacterium that makes a new derivative of what cobalt-containing vitamin?
ANSWER: B12 (also accept: COBALAMIN)
10. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab are studying medical imaging scintillators that absorb high-energy light and release lower-wavelength light that can be detected with a camera. In what type of imaging are these materials most likely used?
W) X-ray imaging
X) MRI
Y) Photoacoustic imaging
Z) Ultrasound
ANSWER: W) X-ray imaging
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the adjective for a constellation that never appears to set in the night sky throughout the year?
ANSWER: CIRCUMPOLAR
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Apollo 11 astronauts brought back primarily what type of rock from the Mare Tranquillitatis?
ANSWER: BASALT
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three physical quantities that are considered scalar quantities 1) Speed 2) Work 3) Acceleration.
ANSWER: 1, 2
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three vectors that must be constant for an object in an unperturbed orbit 1) Angular momentum 2) Linear momentum 3) Acceleration.
ANSWER: 1
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following classes of macromolecules can be digested by salivary amylase?
W) Proteins
X) Carbohydrates
Y) Lipids
Z) Nucleic acids
ANSWER: X) Carbohydrates
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Holdfasts, stipes, and blades are characteristic of what group of multicellular algae?
ANSWER: BROWN ALGAE (also accept: PHAEOPHYCAE; PHAEOPHYTA)
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Gabriela is studying a map showing rivers flowing through a mountainous region. She notices that the V-shaped river valleys are often much wider than the rivers themselves. Which of the following statements best explains her observation?
W) The rivers carried more water in the past
X) The valleys were formed by glaciers and were later replaced by rivers
Y) A series of cut-banks and point-bars formed as the rivers developed, widening the valley
Z) Mass wasting and stream erosion have widened the valley
ANSWER: Z) Mass wasting and stream erosion have widened the valley
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following three sources in increasing order of share of the world's total freshwater 1) Lakes and reservoirs 2) Atmospheric water vapor 3) Groundwater.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
15. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Research by the Pacific Northwest National Laboratory and the University of New Mexico has produced a new material for catalytic converters in vehicles. Identify all of the following three factors that drove this research 1) The cost of platinum 2) The lower temperatures produced by higher-efficiency engines 3) The inefficiency of platinum as a catalyst at high temperatures.
ANSWER: 1, 2
