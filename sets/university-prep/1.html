Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What proteins have a cysteine residue at their active site and are responsible for apoptosis in animal cells?
W) Kinesins
X) Proteases
Y) Caspases
Z) Kinases
ANSWER: Y) Caspases
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer IAP is a family of proteins thought to inhibit apoptosis. What does the acronym IAP stand for?
ANSWER: INHIBITOR OF APOPTOSIS
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What type of intermolecular interactions exists between noble gas solids?
W) Delocalized covalent bonds
X) London dispersion forces
Y) Directional covalent bonds
Z) Ionic bonds
ANSWER: X) London dispersion forces
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the only element in the periodic table that does NOT freeze at 1 atm?
ANSWER: HELIUM
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What type of friction results from the deformation of an object moving across a line?
ANSWER: ROLLING FRICTION
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Assuming gravity is 10 m/s2, what is the minimum coefficient of friction necessary between an object and a surface if the object is traveling at 5 m/s in a uniform circular path with a radius of 5 meters?
W) 0.25
X) 0.5
Y) 0.6
Z) 0.75
ANSWER: X) 0.5
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following Galilean moons is the only one known to have its own magnetic field?
W) Callisto
X) Io
Y) Ganymede
Z) Europa
ANSWER: Y) Ganymede
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the largest of Jupiter’s inner satellites?
W) Amalthea
X) Thebe
Y) Adrastea
Z) Metis
ANSWER: W) Amalthea
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What type of prime number is formed when one takes a power of 2 and subtracts 1?
W) Goldbach prime
X) Euclidean prime
Y) Mersenne prime
Z) Euler’s prime
ANSWER: Y) Mersenne prime
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the sum of the first five prime numbers?
ANSWER: 28
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What is the world’s largest tidal power station as of January 2014?
W) Rance Tidal Power Station
X) Sihwa Tidal Power Station
Y) Race Rocks Tidal Power Station
Z) Annapolis Royal Generating Station
ANSWER: X) Sihwa Tidal Power Station
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer In ebb generation, what structure fills a basin at high tide and releases seawater to generate energy?
ANSWER: SLUICE GATE
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following hormones is produced and released by the posterior pituitary gland?
W) Oxytocin
X) Prolactin
Y) Growth hormone
Z) Luteinizing hormone
ANSWER: W) Oxytocin
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What structure of the brain is responsible for regulating hormone secretion by the anterior pituitary gland?
ANSWER: HYPOTHALAMUS
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What scientist devised an electronegativity scale and won Nobel Prizes in both chemistry and peace?
W) Amedeo Avogadro
X) Archibald Scott Couper
Y) John Dalton
Z) Linus Pauling
ANSWER: Z) Linus Pauling
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Ions that contain the same number of electrons are referred to by what term?
ANSWER: ISOELECTRONIC IONS
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In special relativity, the lowercase gamma is used to symbolize what quantity?
W) Speed of light
X) Inertial reference frame
Y) Lorentz factor
Z) Euclidean factor
ANSWER: Y) Lorentz factor
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What happens to an individual’s apparent weight, when viewed by an outside observer, as the individual travels near the speed of light?
ANSWER: INCREASES
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Matterhorn exemplifies what type of glacial formation that results in an angular mountaintop?
W) Glacial striations
X) Arete
Y) Glacial horn
Z) Cirque
ANSWER: Y) Glacial horn
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of landform results from the glacial deposition of unstratified and unsorted material?
ANSWER: MORAINES
11. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the coefficient of the 4th term in the following expression: (3x + 2y)5?
W) 320
X) 720
Y) 1440
Z) 1680
ANSWER: X) 720
11. BONUS Math Short Answer List the terms that exist in the 5th row of Pascal’s Triangle.
ANSWER: 1, 5, 10, 10, 5, 1
12. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Humphry Davy created the first incandescent light bulb using what metal?
W) Tungsten
X) Iridium
Y) Platinum
Z) Molybdenum
ANSWER: Y) Platinum
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer What does the acronym ITER, a nuclear fusion research facility, stand for?
ANSWER: INTERNATIONAL THERMONUCLEAR EXPERIMENTAL REACTOR
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What enzyme is responsible for splicing out errors in DNA replication?
W) Ligase
X) Primase
Y) Helicase
Z) Nuclease
ANSWER: Z) Nuclease
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer What enzyme compensates for the shortening of chromosomal endings during eukaryotic cell replication and plays an important role in aging and cancer?
ANSWER: TELOMERASE
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice According to Le Chatelier’s principle, what effect would a catalyst have on the rate of a chemical reaction?
W) Increase forward reaction
X) Increase backward reaction
Y) Increase forward and backward reactions equally
Z) Have no influence on reaction rates
ANSWER: Y) Increase forward and backward reactions equally
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If heat is added to an endothermic reaction, in which direction does the equilibrium shift?
ANSWER: RIGHT (also accept: TOWARD THE PRODUCTS)
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice If the charge on one plate of a capacitor is +15 Coulombs and the capacitance is 25000 microfarads, what is the potential energy stored in the capacitor?
W) 9000 Joules
X) 8000 Joules
Y) 4500 Joules
Z) 18000 Joules
ANSWER: Y) 4500 Joules
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the potential energy stored in a spring that is compressed 4 meters from its rest position given that k=50 N/m?
ANSWER: 400 JOULES
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A massive star fuses with which element before undergoing core collapse and becoming a supernova?
W) Silicon
X) Oxygen
Y) Platinum
Z) Iron
ANSWER: Z) Iron
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer White dwarfs typically transform into what type of supernova because they lack hydrogen?
ANSWER: TYPE I
17. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the fourth prime term in the Fibonacci sequence?
W) 3
X) 7
Y) 13
Z) 89
ANSWER: Y) 13
17. BONUS Math Short Answer Leonardo Fibonacci considered the idealized population of what animal as a model for his sequence?
ANSWER: RABBITS
18. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice As of January 2014, what is the largest capacity that a hydroelectric power station can generate in megawatts?
W) 2,000
X) 4,000
Y) 10,000
Z) 20,000
ANSWER: Z) 20,000
18. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the name of the facility in Livermore, California, that uses laser based inertial confinement to attempt nuclear fusion?
ANSWER: NATIONAL IGNITION FACILITY (also accept: NIF)
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What gaseous hormone promotes fruit ripening and flowering in pineapples?
W) Ethylene
X) Methylene
Y) Cytokinins
Z) Abscisic acid
ANSWER: W) Ethylene
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the predominant natural auxin in plants called?
ANSWER: INDOLEACETIC ACID (also accept: IAA)
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What indicator is blue above a pH of 7.6, and yellow below a pH of 6.0?
W) Bromophenol Blue
X) Litmus
Y) Bromothymol Blue
Z) Phenolphthalein
ANSWER: Y) Bromothymol Blue
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What type of titration is used when the endpoint of a normal titration is difficult to identify?
ANSWER: BACK TITRATION (also accept: REVERSE TITRATION)
21. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A single fission reaction with uranium-235 produces approximately how much energy?
W) 100 MeV
X) 200 MeV
Y) 300 MeV
Z) 400 MeV
ANSWER: X) 200 MeV
21. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What chemical element has an atomic number 90, is named after a Norse god, and is used as a substitute nuclear fuel?
ANSWER: THORIUM
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What planet has the highest albedo in the solar system?
W) Earth
X) Mars
Y) Mercury
Z) Venus
ANSWER: Z) Venus
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer An albedo value of 0 indicates that a surface has what property?
ANSWER: PERFECT ABSORPTION (also accept: IDEAL ABSORPTION)
23. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice If set A contains all factors of 12 and set B contains all prime numbers, what numbers are in the intersection of set A and set B?
W) 1,2,4
X) 2,3,6
Y) 2,3
Z) 2,3,6,7
ANSWER: Y) 2,3
23. BONUS Math Short Answer What principle states that a proper subset of S is any subset of S other than S itself?
ANSWER: PRINCIPLE OF EXTENSIONALITY
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What type of batteries depend on the reaction between zinc and manganese dioxide?
W) Lithium-ion batteries
X) Daniell batteries
Y) Alkaline batteries
Z) Bunsen batteries
ANSWER: Y) Alkaline batteries
24. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the atomic mass of the plutonium isotope most commonly used in nuclear fission?
ANSWER: 239
25. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What hormone stimulates the release of digestive enzymes from the pancreas?
W) Secretin
X) Cholecystokinin
Y) Cytokinins
Z) Gastrin
ANSWER: X) Cholecystokinin
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer What hormone promotes the breakdown of glycogen in the liver and the release of glucose into the blood?
ANSWER: GLUCAGON
