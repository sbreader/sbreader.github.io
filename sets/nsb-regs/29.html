1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice If a barometer were constructed using water instead of mercury, by what factor would the height of the mercury barometer be multiplied to give the height of the water barometer?
W) 1 over 13.6
X) 1
Y) 13.6
Z) 33.2
ANSWER: Y) 13.6
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To the nearest degree Celsius, what is the boiling point at standard pressure of a 2-molal solution of urea in water?
ANSWER: 101
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following four types of supernovae whose remnants generally create neutron stars 1) Type Ia 2) Type Ib 3) Type Ic 4) Type II
ANSWER: 2 and 3 and 4
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If a sound wave traveling in air has a frequency of 5,000 hertz, how far, in centimeters, does the wave travel in a single period?
ANSWER: 6.8
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer Consider a solid spherical conductor that has a small spherical cavity in its center. You place an excess charge on the conductor and wait for it to reach equilibrium. Identify all of the following three places that some of the excess charge can be found 1) Surface of the conductor 2) Surface of the cavity 3) Between the surfaces of the conductor and cavity.
ANSWER: 1
3. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Fermi National Accelerator Lab scientists, as a part of the SENSEI collaboration, are trying to detect dark matter particles with specialized "skipper" CCDs. CCDs are composed of what semiconductor element?
ANSWER: SILICON
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Ames Lab are studying the formation of alumina under various conditions. What is the chemical formula of alumina?
ANSWER: Al2O3
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the general term for metabolic reactions that release energy by breaking down complex molecules?
ANSWER: CATABOLIC (also accept: CATABOLISM)
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What plant ground tissue type performs a majority of the metabolic functions of a plant and plays a relatively small role in structural support?
ANSWER: PARENCHYMA
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following cannot be the rightmost two digits, in order, of the square of an integer?
W) 24
X) 27
Y) 56
Z) 89
ANSWER: X) 27
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the average of the first 10 prime numbers?
ANSWER: 12.9 (also accept: 129/10)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following tectonic plates is largest?
W) African
X) Arabian
Y) Eurasian
Z) Pacific
ANSWER: Z) Pacific
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer An atom of uranium-238 undergoes two beta-minus decays and four alpha decays. What is the mass number of the product?
ANSWER: 222
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three compounds that contain both covalent and ionic bonds 1) Phosphorus oxychloride 2) Ammonium sulfate 3) Potassium chloride.
ANSWER: 2
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A weak acid has a Ka of 1.6 × 10-5. Assuming that dissociation is small, what is the percent dissociation of a one-molar solution of this acid in water?
ANSWER: 0.4 (also accept: 0.4 PERCENT)
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If the half-life of a radioisotope is 25,000 years, what fraction of an original sample will remain after 100,000 years?
ANSWER: ONE SIXTEENTH (also accept: 6.25%; 0.0625)
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer At a bottling factory, a robotic arm automatically raises a 4000-newton palette up 6 meters to load it into a canning machine every 5 minutes. In watts, how much average power is required to operate the robotic arm?
ANSWER: 80
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab have been studying a silencer protein known as PRC2. PRC2 silences genes by chemically modifying what proteins around which DNA is wound?
ANSWER: HISTONES
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying alloys composed of rare-earth elements. Identify all of the following three metals that may be examined in these studies 1) Cerium 2) Cesium 3) Tellurium.
ANSWER: 1
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The function y = f of x has zeroes at 4 and 12. If its graph is vertically stretched using a factor of 2, what are the zeroes of the result?
W) 2 and 6
X) 4 and 12
Y) 6 and 14
Z) 8 and 24
ANSWER: X) 4 and 12
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the ratio of the volume of a sphere of diameter π to the volume of a cube with edge length π?
ANSWER: π/6 (also accept: 1/6 π)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the most abundant gas in Mars’ atmosphere?
ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE (also accept: CO2)
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Neutron stars are generally created as remnants of what type of supernovae?
ANSWER: TYPE TWO (also accept: TWO; ONE B; ONE C; II; Ib; Ic; Type 1b; Type 1c)
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What phylum of animals contains organisms that do not possess tissues?
W) Cnidaria
X) Porifera
Y) Nematoda
Z) Echinodermata
ANSWER: X) Porifera
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Pterosaurs were the first tetrapods to evolve what key characteristic of their clade?
ANSWER: FLIGHT (also accept: POWERED FLIGHT; WINGED FLIGHT)
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Two cars leave from the same spot, driving directly away from each other on a straight road, at 20 and 30 miles per hour, respectively. How many minutes will it take for the two cars to be 10 miles apart?
ANSWER: 12
13. BONUS Math Short Answer If f of x equals the natural log of cosine x, what is f prime of π/3?
ANSWER: –√3
14. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following conditions is sufficient for describing a system in unstable equilibrium?
W) Net force is zero
X) Net force is at a minimum
Y) Potential energy is at a strict minimum
Z) Potential energy is at a strict maximum
ANSWER: Z) Potential energy is at a strict maximum
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer The acceleration function for a particle’s motion is differentiable and integrable everywhere. Identify all of the following three quantities that can be determined with no additional information 1) The position of the particle at a particular time 2) The change in position of the particle over a given period of time 3) The change in velocity of the particle over a given period of time.
ANSWER: 3
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at SLAC National Lab recently began construction on the Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment, or DUNE, in South Dakota. What property of neutrinos are scientists at DUNE trying to measure?
W) Spin
X) Temperature
Y) Charge
Z) Mass
ANSWER: Z) Mass
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab are researching ways to increase oil production in the leaves of plants. In what plant structure do oils, such as soybean oil or cocoa butter, normally accumulate?
ANSWER: SEEDS

W) The enzyme stabilizes the products
X) The enzyme stabilizes the reactants

Z) The enzyme provides a mechanism with lower-energy intermediates

Y) Basidiomycetes

ANSWER: 17

ANSWER: 5/9
20. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Kepler’s second law states that each planet moves so that a line from the sun to the planet sweeps out equal areas in equal periods of time. It is a direct result of the conservation of which of the following physical quantities?
W) Linear momentum
X) Linear kinetic energy
Y) Angular momentum
Z) Angular kinetic energy
ANSWER: Y) Angular momentum

ANSWER: 72

ANSWER: EARTH, SUN, AND MOON (also accept: in any order)

ANSWER: TOTIPOTENT (also accept: TOTIPOTENCY) (do not accept: PLURIPOTENT)
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the adjective for organisms that lay eggs that hatch outside the mother's body?
ANSWER: OVIPAROUS
