1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What part of the mammalian female reproductive tract is responsible for making gametes?
W) Ovary
X) Oviduct
Y) Uterine horn
Z) Uterine body
ANSWER: W) Ovary
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What enzyme is responsible for transcribing DNA to RNA in bacteria?
ANSWER: RNA POLYMERASE (also accept: RNAP) (do not accept: RNA polymerase II)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Consider a graph with the mass of a sample on the y-axis and the sample volume on the x-axis. Which of the following substances would have the steepest slope on this graph?
W) Snow
X) Ice
Y) Rain
Z) Steam
ANSWER: Y) Rain
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Barium will form an ionic compound in a one-to-one ratio with which of the following elements?
W) Titanium
X) Arsenic
Y) Sulfur
Z) Iodine
ANSWER: Y) Sulfur
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following stars is located closest to the north celestial pole?
W) Polaris
X) Vega
Y) Regulus
Z) Rigel
ANSWER: W) Polaris
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice White dwarfs are found in which of the following regions of the H-R diagram?
W) Top right
X) Top left
Y) Bottom right
Z) Bottom left
ANSWER: Z) Bottom left
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab are searching for enzymes that can break down lignin. Lignin is found in the highest quantities in what plant tissue?
W) Wood
X) Leaf
Y) Fruit
Z) Seed
ANSWER: W) Wood
4. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Scientists at Fermi National Accelerator Lab have measured the magnetic moment of the muon to high precision. Their value exceeded the prediction of theorists by 3.3 standard deviations. Which of the following percentages is closest to the probability of obtaining a value at least this large by chance, assuming that the theoretical value is in fact correct and the measurement error is normally distributed?
W) 0.05
X) 0.5
Y) 2
Z) 10
ANSWER: W) 0.05
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the square of the binomial (x + 12)?
ANSWER: x2 + 24x + 144
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Simplify the fraction with numerator 27 minus 45 and denominator 9 minus 12.
ANSWER: 6
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes why objects dropped in a vacuum on Earth will all fall with the same acceleration?
W) The balance of air resistance with gravity imposes a terminal velocity
X) Gravitational force does not depend on mass
Y) Buoyancy balances the force of gravity
Z) Gravitational force is proportional to mass while acceleration is inversely proportional to mass
ANSWER: Z) Gravitational force is proportional to mass while acceleration is inversely proportional to mass
6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following quantities would have the same units as pressure?
W) Energy times time
X) Force per area
Y) Volume times temperature
Z) Mass per volume
ANSWER: X) Force per area
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the protective caps at the end of eukaryotic chromosomes that tend to shorten as the cell divides?
ANSWER: TELOMERES
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Botanists successfully created viable offspring between two different bamboos that live in the same forest, but noticed that these two species flower during different seasons. Which of the following types of isolation is occurring?
W) Behavioral
X) Mechanical
Y) Geographic
Z) Temporal
ANSWER: Z) Temporal
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What type of reaction combines an acid and a base to form a salt and water?
W) Decomposition
X) Neutralization
Y) Single replacement
Z) Redox
ANSWER: X) Neutralization
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To the nearest gram, what is the mass of one-half mole of sodium chloride?
ANSWER: 29
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What moon is known for its thick nitrogen atmosphere and methane lakes?
W) Ganymede
X) Titan
Y) Io
Z) Charon
ANSWER: X) Titan
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What greenhouse gas is primarily responsible for the runaway greenhouse effect on Venus?
ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE (also accept: CO2)
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Livermore National Lab have validated the possibility that planets composed primarily of hydrogen and helium may experience helium rain. One of the planets in our solar system thought to experience this phenomenon is what gas giant that is home to the great red spot superstorm?
ANSWER: JUPITER
10. BONUS Energy Short Answer Pacific Northwest National Lab scientists are utilizing magnetic nanoparticles to extract a metal from brine water. What is this valuable, lightweight metal, known for its use in modern battery technology?
ANSWER: LITHIUM

ANSWER: 9

W) Work function
X) Semiconductivity
Y) Photoelectric effect

ANSWER: Y) Photoelectric effect

ANSWER: 0

ANSWER: PHOTOSYNTHESIS

ANSWER: 2 only

W) Potassium
X) Carbon
Y) Hydrogen
Z) Fluorine

W) Tectonic plates
X) Ocean circulation
Y) Magnetic field
Z) Ozone layer
ANSWER: Y) Magnetic field
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes how geysers form?
W) Spontaneous eruption from a pressurized, confined aquifer
X) Separation of water-rich magma from molten rock as it cools and crystallizes
Y) Superheating of groundwater by a nearby magma chamber
Z) Squeezing of groundwater out of fractured rock by tectonic forces
ANSWER: Y) Superheating of groundwater by a nearby magma chamber
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Brookhaven National Lab scientists are developing mathematical models of COVID-19 transmission in society. The goal of mass vaccination is to achieve what threshold, at which the immunized population is sufficiently large to shield the susceptible population?
ANSWER: HERD IMMUNITY

ANSWER: 1 and 3
