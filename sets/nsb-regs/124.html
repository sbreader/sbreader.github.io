Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Log base 100 of 50 is equal to log base 10 of 50 divided by log base 10 of what number?
ANSWER: 100
1. BONUS Math Short Answer A bag contains 22 socks: 6 are red, 8 are white, and 8 are blue. How many socks must you take out to be certain that you will have at least one pair of blue socks?
ANSWER: 16
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the molecular geometry of methane?
ANSWER: TETRAHEDRAL
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A student mixes together aqueous equimolar solutions of calcium chloride and ammonium phosphate. Which two of the component ions stay in solution?
ANSWER: AMMONIUM AND CHLORIDE
3. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is a renewable, primary energy source?
W) Nuclear
X) Fossil
Y) Geothermal
Z) Electricity
ANSWER: Y) Geothermal
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Fermilab physicists are conducting an experiment to look for the neutrinoless decay of muons. What is the only particle that is formed in this hypothetical decay?
ANSWER: ELECTRON
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If one wants to double the period of a simple pendulum, what ratio of new-to-old length is required?
ANSWER: 4 (also accept: 4 TO 1)
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer A light ray hits a mirror on the ground at 25 degrees measured from the horizontal. What is the angle, in degrees, between the incident and reflected ray?
ANSWER: 130
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What part of the nitrogen cycle can be performed by lightning?
ANSWER: NITROGEN FIXATION
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Intense California storms, poor Peruvian anchovy fishing, and high South American sea surface temperatures in Western South America are characteristic of what episodic event?
ANSWER: EL NINO (also accept: ENSO)
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In the Griffith experiment, nonpathogenic bacteria were found to be able to cause disease after being exposed to heat-killed virulent bacteria. Which of the following substances caused this transformation?
W) Toxins
X) Proteins
Y) RNA
Z) DNA
ANSWER: Z) DNA
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What region of the human eye retina has the highest density of photoreceptors?
ANSWER: FOVEA (also accept: FOVEA CENTRALIS)
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If an electron is placed half a meter away from the surface of a thin metallic shell of diameter 1 meter, what is the electric field, in volts per meter, at the center of the shell?
ANSWER: 0
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the eqivalent capacitance, in microfarads, of a 3-capacitor circuit consisting of 2-microfarad, 3-microfarad, and 4-microfarad capacitors connected in series?
ANSWER: 12/13
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What planet in the solar system is the most dense?
ANSWER: EARTH
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer An extremely high-mass star will not undergo a supernova until the mass of its iron core exceeds what limit?
ANSWER: CHANDRASEKHAR LIMIT (also accept: 1.4 SOLAR MASSES)
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The Peter Auger cosmic ray observatory is operated by DOE scientists from Fermilab. Which of the following statements is TRUE of how cosmic rays are detected by this observatory?
W) The observatory detects cosmic rays by measuring the local electric field
X) The observatory directly observes cosmic rays interacting with water
Y) The observatory detects the shower of energetic particles produced by a cosmic ray
Z) The observatory uses interferometry to detect cosmic rays
ANSWER: Y) The observatory detects the shower of energetic particles produced by a cosmic ray
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists at SLAC have been studying the iron sulfur cluster in a group of liver-detoxifying enzymes to determine the source of their high activity towards a wide variety of substrates. What is the general name for this group of enzymes?
ANSWER: CYTOCHROME P450
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The total amount of useful work extractable from a chemical reaction at constant pressure and temperature is equal to the negative change in what state function?
ANSWER: GIBBS FREE ENERGY (also accept: G)
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three molecules that are matched correctly to their hybridization 1) Xenon difluoride and dsp3 2) Nitrogen tri-iodide and sp2 3) Sulfur tetrafluoride and d2sp3.
ANSWER: 1
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the result of the first step of glycolysis in a fibroblast?
W) Glucose-6-phosphate is fully committed to the glycolysis pathway
X) Fructose-1,6-bisphosphate is split into two three-carbon sugars
Y) ATP is extracted from glucose-6-phosphate
Z) Glucose-6-phosphate can no longer exit the cell
ANSWER: Z) Glucose-6-phosphate can no longer exit the cell
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer In the Krebs cycle, oxidation steps of cycle intermediates are coupled with what type of reaction that reduces the number of carbons in the intermediates?
ANSWER: DECARBOXYLATION (also accept: OXIDATIVE DECARBOXYLATION)
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the greatest common factor of 36 and 92?
ANSWER: 4
12. BONUS Math Short Answer Expressing your answer in terms of m, evaluate the function f(x) = 3x2-12x + 15 when x = m-4.
ANSWER: 3m2-36m + 111
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the ratio of the absolute temperature at the Sun’s core to that of its surface?
W) 25
X) 250
Y) 2,500
Z) 25,000
ANSWER: Y) 2,500
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three galaxies that are thought to have a supermassive black hole at or near their center 1) Milky way 2) Andromeda Galaxy 3) Whirlpool Galaxy.
ANSWER: ALL
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What specific quantity on a reaction diagram does the addition of an enzyme change?
ANSWER: ACTIVATION ENERGY (also accept: TRANSITION STATE ENERGY)
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice One purpose of fermentation is to regenerate which of the following reagents?
W) NADH
X) NAD+
Y) FADH2
Z) FAD
ANSWER: X) NAD+
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three quantities that are vector quantities 1) Impulse 2) Momentum 3) Energy.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 3-kilogram block slides along a horizontal surface, and the coefficient of kinetic friction between them is 0.5. The block’s initial velocity is 20 meters per second. To the nearest second, how long does it take for the block to come to a stop?
ANSWER: 4
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Mendeleev famously predicted the existence of eka-silicon in 1871 as well as a list of properties this theoretical element would have, many of which turned out to be correct when the element was discovered in 1886. What is the IUPAC name of this element?
ANSWER: GERMANIUM
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three quantities that are positive for the gas-phase decomposition reaction of carbon dioxide to form carbon monoxide and molecular oxygen at 300 kelvins 1) Delta-H 2) Delta-S 3) Delta-G.
ANSWER: ALL
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many exterior angles does an octagon have?
ANSWER: 16 (do not accept: 8)
17. BONUS Math Short Answer What are the coordinates of the vertex of the graph of y = -3x2-12x-7?
ANSWER: (–2, 5) (also accept: x = –2 AND y = 5)
18. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Researchers at SLAC are currently working on mining methods to expand proven resources of uranium. Which of the following contains the largest amount of uranium?
W) Ocean water
X) Salt mines
Y) Gold deposits
Z) Coal mines
ANSWER: W) Ocean water
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab discovered a protein important in DNA repair called XPG, whose function was often lost in breast, ovarian, and other cancer types. XPG is in what class of cancer-preventing genes, examples of which also include APC and p53?
ANSWER: TUMOR SUPPRESSOR
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In a stream, what is the term for the sediment load that slides and rolls along the stream bed?
ANSWER: BED LOAD
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following three materials in increasing order of speed of P-waves when traveling through them 1) Ultramafic 2) Felsic 3) Mafic.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
20. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following adjacent pairs of thermodynamic processes are a part of a Carnot cycle?
W) Isothermal expansion and isentropic expansion
X) Isentropic compression and isochoric heating
Y) Isentropic compression and isobaric heating
Z) Isothermal compression and isochoric cooling
ANSWER: W) Isothermal expansion and isentropic expansion
20. BONUS Physics Short Answer The force on an object was kept constant for 1 second and then changed to a new value for each successive second, for a total of five force values over a five-second time interval. All five forces were in the same direction and had magnitudes of 0 newtons, 5 newtons, 15 newtons, 5 newtons, and 0 newtons. What was the magnitude of the total impulse, in newton seconds, exerted on the object during this time?
ANSWER: 25
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a Lewis acid?
W) Aluminum chloride
X) Ammonia
Y) Carbon dioxide
Z) Magnesium ion
ANSWER: X) Ammonia
21. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three molecules in increasing order of their acidity in water 1) Ethanol 2) Ethyl mercaptan 3) Acetic acid.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If the probability of it raining on a given day is 0.21, what is the probability of it NOT raining?
ANSWER: 0.79 (also accept: 79/100)
22. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area between the curves y = x3 and y = x2 + 3 between x = 0 and x = 1?
ANSWER: 37/12 (also accept: 3 1/12)
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What plant hormones regulate cell division in the shoots and roots and are generally synthesized in the roots?
ANSWER: CYTOKININS
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following four mutations that could result from a single-nucleotide substitution 1) Silent 2) Missense 3) Nonsense 4) Frameshift.
ANSWER: 1, 2, AND 3
