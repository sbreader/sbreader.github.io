1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Indicate all of the following three statements that are TRUE concerning Southern blots 1. They are used for the detection of specific DNA sequences 2. 2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following orbitals is the LUMO of the carbonyl functional group?
W) C-O sigma
X) C-O sigma-star
Y) C-O pi
Z) C-O pi-star
ANSWER: Z) C-O pi-star
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer An inhibitor added to a enzyme results in an increase in its Michaelis constant K-sub-M, but V-max is discovered to remain constant. The inhibitor is likely noncompetitive

2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three compounds in terms of increase rate of E1 elimination: t-Butyl triflate Isopropyl chloride Isopropyl triflate
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Consider an object placed in front of a concave mirror, at a distance less than the focal length of the mirror. Which of the following statements is TRUE of the image?
W) It is real and upright
X) It is real and inverted
Y) It is virtual and upright
Z) It is virtual and inverted
ANSWER: Y) It is virtual and upright
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Consider a region of electric charge which has potential in volts V = x2 + y2 + z2 for x,y,z in position units. Which of the following charge configurations would produce this electric potential?
W) Constant negative charge density
X) Negative charge density that decreases with increasing distance from the origin
Y) Constant positive charge density
Z) Positive charge density that decreases with increasing distance from the origin
ANSWER: W) Constant negative charge density
