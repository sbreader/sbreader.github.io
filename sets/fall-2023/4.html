1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following changes is most likely to increase the solubility of a saturated zinc hydroxide solution?
W) Adding more zinc hydroxide
X) Adding potassium hydroxide
Y) Adding zinc chloride
Z) Adding hydrochloric acid
ANSWER: Z) Adding hydrochloric acid
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three compounds from first to last to condense in a distillation experiment 1) Water 2) Ethanol 3) Acetone.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
2. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following functions of x is monotonic?
W) Absolute value of x
X) Sine x
Y) Arctangent x
Z) x2
ANSWER: Y) Arctangent x
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Given that October 20th on a given year is a Saturday, on what day of the week is December 11th?
ANSWER: TUESDAY
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a protist?
W) Elodea
X) Sand dollars
Y) Sea grass
Z) Kelp
ANSWER: Z) Kelp
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT formed in the breakdown of lactose by beta galactosidase?
W) ATP
X) Water
Y) Galactose
Z) Glucose
ANSWER: W) ATP
4. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Researchers in the Prashi Group at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are developing photonic heaters to drive solar-powered desalination, which would be able to treat water with high levels of dissolved solids. What is a common method of water purification involving a semi-permeable membrane?
ANSWER: REVERSE OSMOSIS
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers in the Cohen Lab at UC Berkeley are working to develop instrumentation to detect the concentration of sulfates in the biosphere by quantitative precipitation. This is most similar to what common chemistry laboratory technique?
ANSWER: GRAVIMETRIC ANALYSIS
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Rank the following three subatomic particles in terms of increasing Compton wavelength 1) Electron 2) Electron neutrino 3) Proton.
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two wheels have the same mass and radius. Wheel A is a ring and has all its mass concentrated on the edge, while wheel B is a solid disk. If wheel A rotates half as fast as wheel B, what is the ratio of the rotational kinetic energy of wheel A to wheel B?
ANSWER: 1/2 (also accept: 0.5)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following properties of limestone BEST explains why it is suited to produce karst topography?
W) Limestone easily dissolves in water
X) Limestone is produced by viscous silicate lava
Y) Limestone can be formed by carbonate-enriched hot spring waters
Z) Limestone is naturally deposited in the ocean as calcareous algae die
ANSWER: W) Limestone easily dissolves in water
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Black holes can be completely characterized by their mass, spin, and what other property?
ANSWER: CHARGE
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Let f of x be a monic, cubic polynomial. If the three roots of f are 1, 3, and 5, what is f of 4?
ANSWER: –3
7. BONUS Math Short Answer After rolling two fair six-sided dice, what is the expected value of the product of their top faces?
ANSWER: 49/4 (also accept: 12.25)
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Order the following four fundamental forces from weakest to strongest 1) Electromagnetic 2) Strong 3) Gravitational 4) Weak.
ANSWER: 3, 4, 1, 2
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A large tank of unknown liquid has a top which is open to the atmosphere. The liquid leaks out of the tank from a small hole in the side at a horizontal velocity of 2.5 meters per second. In centimeters, at what depth is the hole from the surface of the liquid?
ANSWER: 62.5 (also accept: 125/2)
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A scientist is interested in modeling real gases which cannot be approximated as ideal due to their significant intermolecular attractions. What famous equation describes real gas behavior by directly modifying the ideal gas law?
ANSWER: VAN DER WAALS EQUATION
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is true regarding the titration of sulfuric acid with sodium hydroxide?
W) pH equals pKa at the endpoint
X) pH equals pKa at the equivalence point
Y) The Ka of H2SO4 is lower than the Ka of HSO4-
Z) The pKa of H2SO4 is lower than the pKa of HSO4-
ANSWER: Z) The pKa of H2SO4 is lower than the pKa of HSO4-
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What structures in the lung are responsible for oxygen exchange with pulmonary capillaries?
ANSWER: ALVEOLI
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer An HIV hijacks a host epithelial cell and integrates its own genetic code into the cell. What is the name of the host cell DNA after integration?
ANSWER: PROVIRUS
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists in the Reimer Group work jointly with local startups to study silicate-based minerals for carbon capture. What is the name for the group of characteristically green-colored minerals mostly made up of iron magnesium silicates?
ANSWER: OLIVINE
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Livermore National Lab have designed detectors to study the behavior of antineutrinos. According to the Standard Model, what is the magnitude of the spin of antineutrinos?
ANSWER: 1/2
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In 1980, UC Berkeley earth science Professor Walter Alvarez discovered that the Cretaceous–Paleogene boundary contains abnormal levels of which of the following elements?
W) Iron
X) Radium
Y) Iridium
Z) Silicon
ANSWER: Y) Iridium
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term given to large crystals embedded within the groundmass of igneous rocks?
ANSWER: PHENOCRYSTS
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the molecular geometry of phosphorus trichloride?
ANSWER: TRIGONAL PYRAMIDAL
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true about the exothermic decomposition of dinitrogen tetroxide into nitrogen dioxide 1) Increasing temperature shifts the reaction right 2) Increasing pressure shifts the reaction right 3) The reaction is spontaneous at room temperature.
ANSWER: 3 ONLY
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The 1985 eruption of Nevado del Ruiz killed around 25,000 people, mainly due to melting ice and snow causing a mudflow which buried the town of Armero. What is the term for this type of volcanic mudflow?
ANSWER: LAHAR
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which of the following locations would you MOST likely find a protostar?
W) H II region
X) H I region
Y) Planetary nebula
Z) Supernova remnant
ANSWER: W) H II region
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In eukaryotic transcription, what part of the DNA sequence does RNA polymerase bind to?
ANSWER: PROMOTER (also accept: TATA BOX)
15. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following describes why nitrogen is often a limiting nutrient despite being common in the atmosphere?
W) Decomposers use up the majority of bioavailable nitrogen
X) Assimilation of atmospheric nitrogen only occurs in anaerobic conditions
Y) The strong triple bond in nitrogen gas renders it inert to biological reactions
Z) Animals must rely on plants for nitrogen
ANSWER: Y) The strong triple bond in nitrogen gas renders it inert to biological reactions
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the thermal efficiency, to the nearest tenth of a percent, of a thermodynamic engine that has a net power output of 350 kilowatts and rejects heat at a rate of 50 kilowatts?
ANSWER: 87.5
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer If a 2-ampere current flows through a 5-millihenry inductor, what is the energy, in joules, stored in the inductor?
ANSWER: 0.01
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are studying communication between plants via chemical signals such as plant hormones. Which of the following plant hormones causes elongation of cells in shoots and helps in regulating plant growth?
W) Jasmonates
X) Cytokinins
Y) Auxins
Z) Strigolactones
ANSWER: Y) Auxins
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are studying various means of increasing energy efficiency. What thermodynamic cycle gives the hypothetical maximum efficiency that can be achieved by a heat engine operating between two temperatures?
ANSWER: CARNOT CYCLE
18. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice According to the rational root test, which of the following is NOT a possible root of the following polynomial: 2x3 + 7x2 – 3x + 6?
W) –2
X) 1/2
Y) 3
Z) 4
ANSWER: Z) 4
18. BONUS Math Short Answer A random variable X has a mean of 7 and a standard deviation of 6. Another random variable Y has a mean of 10 and a standard deviation of 8. What are the mean and standard deviation, respectively, of X + Y?
ANSWER: MEAN = 17, STANDARD DEVIATION = 10 (also accept: 17 AND 10) (do not accept: 10 AND 17)
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three organelles that are theorized to have originated through the process of endosymbiosis 1) Mitochondria 2) Chloroplast 3) Lysosome.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following metabolic processes that are undertaken by mature erythrocytes 1) Glycolysis 2) Citric acid cycle 3) Oxidative phosphorylation.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer According to the Grand Unified Theory, in the moments just after the Big Bang, three of the four fundamental forces of the universe were unified as one. Of these three, which was the first to separate from the others?
ANSWER: STRONG NUCLEAR FORCE (also accept: STRONG FORCE)
20. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT considered evidence for the fact that the Mediterranean Sea dried to form a salt desert 5 to 6 million years ago?
W) Stromatolites are found in many estuaries around the Mediterranean Sea
X) A sharp decrease in the marine fossil record approximately 5 to 6 million years ago
Y) A seismically reflective layer of salt on the bottom of the Mediterranean sea
Z) The Nile River cuts its bed 2500 meters below sea level at the Nile Delta
ANSWER: W) Stromatolites are found in many estuaries around the Mediterranean Sea

ANSWER: v√3 (also accept: v TIMES THE SQUARE ROOT OF 3)
