1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Aldric shoots water from a water gun at a speed of 6.5 m/s holding the hose close to the ground. At what angle should the nozzle of the water gun be pointed to in order so that the water lands 2.5 meters away? Assume that the acceleration due to gravity is 10 m/s2.
ANSWER: 18 AND 72 DEGREES

3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Thomas Jefferson National Lab are conducting research on the precise lifespan of neutral pions to help elucidate the theory of quantum chromodynamics. Which of the following statements regarding pions is true?
W) In pions, both the strong and electromagnetic force have infinite ranges due to their masslessness
X) Pions are generally stable, causing the decay to photons, neutrinos, or electrons over long periods of time
Y) Pions can be emitted by neutrons and absorbed by electrons, and vice versa

3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying biofuel perennial plants through land surface models. 11. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Based on the following descriptions, which of the following devices obey Ohm’s law?
