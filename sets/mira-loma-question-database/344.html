Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer About how old do scientists believe the earth is
ANSWER: 4.6 BILLION YEARS (also accept: 4.5 BILLION YEARS)
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists believe the Earth came about with this theory
ANSWER: NEBULAR HYPOTHESIS
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the number of main oceans and the number of continents on the Earth's surface
ANSWER: 3 MAJOR OCEANS; 7 CONTINENTS
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following was NOT one of the three body masses on Earth about 200 million years ago
W) Laurasia
X) Tethys
Y) Gondawana
Z) Panthalassa
ANSWER: Z) Panthalassa
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the four major parts of the Earth's inner layers
ANSWER: CRUST, MANTLE, OUTER CORE, INNER CORE
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What element makes up the majority of the Earth's core
ANSWER: IRON
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer How many parts are there for the outer structure of the Earth
ANSWER: 3
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In which of the three "spheres" of the Earth do the continents reside
ANSWER: LITHOSPHERE
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer This theory is based off of tectonic plates and states that these plates keep the Earth's land masses and water bodies in constant motion
ANSWER: PLATE TECTONICS
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A change in the state of matter is a _______ change
ANSWER: PHYSICAL
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is person who studies minerals called
ANSWER: MINERALOGIST
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name three valuable minerals
ANSWER: GOLD, SILVER, DIAMOND ETC
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Because a mineral is not formed from living things or anything of the sort, it is classified as...
ANSWER: INORGANIC
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In what state of matter is a mineral always in
ANSWER: SOLID
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer How many different arrangements of crystals exist
ANSWER: 6
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Minerals that contain metals and nonmetals that are able to be extracted are known as...
ANSWER: ORES
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By which process are metals removed from ores
ANSWER: SMELTING
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are rare minerals referred to as
ANSWER: GEMS
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a precious stone
W) Aquamarine
X) Sapphire
Y) Gold
Z) Emerald
ANSWER: Y) Gold
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for a characteristic of a mineral on how that mineral reflects light
ANSWER: LUSTER
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What kind of luster does gold have
ANSWER: METALLIC
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is a porcelain plate better known as
ANSWER: STREAK PLATE
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is a streak plate used to classify
ANSWER: DIFFERENT MINERALS; COMPARISON OF MINERALS
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the three different types of rocks
ANSWER: IGNEOUS, METAMORPHIC AND SEDIMENTARY
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the two distinct types of rocks that are both classified as igneous
ANSWER: PLUTONIC AND VOLCANIC ROCKS
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A rock, classified as igneous and formed from large crystals would be known as
ANSWER: COARSE GRAINED
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer An igneous rock in which crystals do not form at all are known as
ANSWER: OBSIDIAN (also accept: VOLCANIC GLASS)
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the entire process of the possibilities in which a rock can change known as
ANSWER: ROCK CYCLE
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A given rock is exposed to extremely high temperatures and melts. It then cools to a solid and is exposed to weathering and erosion from nature. Because of these forces, it is buried and compacted. What kind of rock can it now be classified as
ANSWER: SEDIMENTARY ROCK
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an element that is present in the chemical makeup of the Earth's crust
W) Oxygen
X) Calcium
Y) Carbon
Z) Silicon
ANSWER: Y) Carbon
17. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the percentage the mass of the crust of the Earth takes up in direct ratio to the mass of the entire Earth
W) less than 1%
X) 1% -5%
Y) 6% -10%
Z) more than 10%
ANSWER: W) less than 1%
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which cycle shows the movement of water
ANSWER: HYDROLOGIC CYCLE
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer According to the Hydrologic Cycle, after water returns to the ocean, what process occurs to it
ANSWER: EVAPORATION
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In the Hydrologic Cycle, what happens to the water after it precipitates from the cloud
ANSWER: RUNS OFF; RUNOFF
19. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following instances CANNOT occur in the Hydrologic Cycle
W) Water is evaporated after it reaches the ocean
X) Immediately after water evaporates, it precipitates
Y) Water comes to the plants and soil after the runoff
Z) Immediately after reaching the lakes and streams, water is evaporated
ANSWER: X) Immediately after water evaporates, it precipitates
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A material through which water can move at a relatively quick pace is known as
ANSWER: PERMEABLE
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the SI unit of permeability, which was named after the scientist who also developed a law regarding permeability
ANSWER: DARCY (also accept: MILLIDARCY)
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Thick sheets of ice in polar regions can be classified as
ANSWER: CONTINENTAL GLACIERS
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are two major continental glaciers
ANSWER: GREENLAND AND Antarctica
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term used to describe scientists who study the seas
ANSWER: OCEANOGRAPHERS
22. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following boundaries best describes the salinity of ocean water
W) 13-17 parts per thousand
X) 23-27 parts per thousand
Y) 33-37 parts per thousand
Z) 43-47 parts per thousand
ANSWER: Y) 33-37 parts per thousand
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In which part of the atmosphere is ozone, a form of oxygen, present
ANSWER: STRATOSPHERE
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the upper part of the thermosphere known as
ANSWER: EXOSPHERE
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The part of the area around Earth that extends beyond the atmosphere is known as
ANSWER: MAGNETOSPHERE
24. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The magnetosphere consists of protons and electrons which both contain high radiation. These two separate belts of high radiation are known as
ANSWER: VAN ALLEN RADIATION BELTS
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What instrument is used to measure air pressure
ANSWER: BAROMETER
25. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer You do not notice the air pressure that is present everywhere on Earth because the pressure of the air outside is equal to
ANSWER: THE PRESSURE INSIDE YOUR BODY (also accept: variations)
