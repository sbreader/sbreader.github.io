1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Melting which of the following substances involves the breaking of covalent bonds?
W) Dry ice
X) Sand
Y) Ice
Z) Sodium chloride
ANSWER: X) Sand
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about the anions fulminate and cyanate, which have formulas CNO– and NCO– respectively:
W) Fulminate is linear while cyanate is bent
X) Nitrogen has a neutral formal charge in both fulminate and cyanate
Y) Cyanate is more stable than Fulminate
Z) Nitrogen has one lone pair in both molecules
ANSWER: Y) Cyanate is more stable than Fulminate
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Pyridine is an aromatic compound with formula C5H5N that will undergo Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution reactions. When reacted with Br2 at 300 degrees celsius, what will be the major product?
ANSWER: 3-bromopyridine
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Reacting a carboxylic acid with diazomethane results in which of the following functional groups?
W) Ester
X) Ether
Y) Amine
Z) Alcohol
ANSWER: W) Ester
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three ligands by increasing denticity 1) Ammonia 2) EDTA 3) Oxalate
ANSWER: 1,3,2
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer An aqueous solution of copper(II) chloride is electrolyzed with a constant current for 100,000 seconds. At the end of the electrolysis, 2.00 moles of copper metal are deposited at the cathode. To the nearest whole number, what is the magnitude of the current, in amperes?
ANSWER: 4
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the thermodynamic equation defining the relationship between the number of phases, components, and degrees of freedom in a system at equilibrium?
ANSWER: Gibbs’s Phase Rule
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many rotational modes of motion are there for hydrogen sulfide?
ANSWER: 3
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In Potions class, Harry Potter mixes together solutions of polyjuice potion and veritaserum and notices the mixture positively deviates from Raoult's Law. What are the signs, respectively, of the enthalpy of mixing and the entropy of mixing?
W) positive, positive
X) positive, negative
Y) Negative, positive
Z) negative, negative
ANSWER: W) positive, positive
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the smallest F-Cl-F bond angle in ClF3?
W) 62.5
X) 87.5
Y) 92.5
Z) 117.5
ANSWER: X) 87.5
5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is true regarding the spontaneity of the combustion of pentane?
W) The reaction is spontaneous at all temperatures
X) The reaction is non-spontaneous at all temperatures
Y) The reaction is only spontaneous at sufficiently high temperatures
Z) The reaction is only spontaneous at sufficiently low temperatures
ANSWER: W) The reaction is spontaneous at all temperatures
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three types of carbon radicals by increasing stability 1) benzylic radical 2) vinyl radical 3) tertiary radical.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To fluorinate nitrobenzene, order the following three reagents from first to last to be applied 1) H2, Pd/C 2) HBF4 3) NaNO2 and HCl
ANSWER: 1,3,2
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What constant is used to determine the total electrostatic lattice energy of an ionic crystal, accounting for the attraction and repulsion between all ions?
ANSWER: Madelung constant
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 4 orbitals in order of increasing electronegativity 1) s 2) p 3) d 4) f
ANSWER: f,d,p,s (4, 3, 2, 1)
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What theory explains non-ideal behavior in dilute electrolyte solutions by calculating mean ionic activity coefficients based on electrostatic interactions?
ANSWER: Debye–Hückel theory
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What equation is an empirical extension of the Debye-Hückel theory used to estimate individual ion activity coefficients?
ANSWER: Davies equation
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Compound A radioactively decays to compound B with rate constant 5 s-1. Compound A also radioactively decays to compound C with rate constant 17 s-1. What is the overall rate constant of compound A's decay, in s-1?
ANSWER: 22
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the sum of the whole number coefficients of the balanced equation between dichromate and iron (II)?
ANSWER: 36
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Compounds containing conjugated π systems are known to absorb UV-Vis Light. What is the name of the part of the compound that absorbs the light?
ANSWER: chromophore
10. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following types of reaction is classified as a [3,3]-sigmatropic rearrangement?
W) Diels-Alder
X) Claisen rearrangement
Y) Julia olefination
Z) Claisen condensation
ANSWER: X) Claisen rearrangement
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three compounds in terms of increasing melting point 1) Hydrogen 2) Deuterium 3) Tritium
ANSWER: 1,2,3
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following ligands that are pi acceptors 1) carbon monoxide 2) ammonia 3) cyanide ion
ANSWER: 1,3
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In enzyme kinetics, what equation is used to measure the rate of enzymatic catalysis as a function of the concentration of the substrate?
ANSWER: Michaelis-Menten equation
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the late-19th-century failure of classical physics, where the Rayleigh-Jeans law incorrectly predicted that an ideal black body would emit infinite energy at short wavelengths?
ANSWER: Ultraviolet Catastrophe
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Let the ideal gas constant R be 8 J/mol*K. The following reaction has Kc = 0.04 at 500 Kelvin: PCl5 (g) in equilibrium with PCl3 (g) + Cl2(g). What is the value for Kp for the system at the same temperature?
ANSWER: 160
13. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a correct statement about batteries?
W) Lithium ion batteries are considered to be a safety risk because of explosion or fire
X) Sulfuric acid is the acid most commonly found in lead acid storage batteries
Y) Calcium oxide is the base most commonly found in alkaline batteries
Z) Nickel cadmium batteries are becoming decreasingly popular because of the memory effect affecting their lifetime
ANSWER: Y) Calcium oxide is the base most commonly found in alkaline batteries
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three five-membered aromatic heterocycles in order of increasing tendency to undergo electrophilic aromatic substitution 1. Furan 2. Thiophene 3. Pyrrole
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
14. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Cyclopentane adopts which of the following configurations in its most stable state?
W) Planar
X) Boat
Y) Envelope
Z) Chair
ANSWER: Y) Envelope
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In rare cases, polydentate ligands may also act as a monodentate ligand when only one of the donor atoms binds to a metal center. What is the name for this type of coordination?
ANSWER: Hypodentate
15. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Hansong is trying to examine the C-O bond length of a carbon monoxide ligand in certain metal complexes. Complex A has chromium as the center, while complex B has tungsten as the center. Besides these differences, the complexes are identical. Which of the following best describes the most likely difference in C-O bond length between the complexes and why?
W) Complex A has the longer bond length because chromium is more electronegative than tungsten.
X) Complex A has the longer bond length because chromium engages in stronger pi backbonding.
Y) Complex B has a longer bond length because chromium is more electronegative than tungsten.
Z) Complex B has the longer bond length because tungsten engages in stronger pi backbonding.
ANSWER: Z) Complex B has the longer bond length because tungsten engages in stronger pi backbonding.
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The partial derivative of Gibbs Free Energy with respect to the number of moles at a constant temperature, pressure, and composition is what quantity?
ANSWER: Chemical potential
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To two significant figures, and in kilojoules, what is the change in Gibbs Free Energy accompanying mixing 0.5 mole of each of two non-volatile liquids at 298 K, assuming they do not react?
ANSWER: -1.7 kJ
