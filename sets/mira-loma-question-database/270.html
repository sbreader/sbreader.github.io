1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the chemical that is added to natural gas to make it smell like rotten eggs?
W) Methane
X) Mercaptan
Y) Pure sulfur
Z) Methanol
ANSWER: X) Mercaptan
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What two components would generally make up the layer above the natural oil and gas deposits?
ANSWER: SAND AND SILT
3. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What would be a legitimate, possible description of sulfur-rich crude oil?
W) Sour
X) Sweet
Y) Light
Z) Heavy
ANSWER: W) Sour
4. BONUS Energy Short Answer How many liters are there in a single barrel of crude oil?
ANSWER: 159 LITERS
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer By numbers or words, which two of the following four would be considered secondary energy sources? 1: Hydrogen 2: Electricity 3: Coal 4: Petroleum
ANSWER: 1 and 2 (also accept: Hydrogen and Electricity)

W) China
X) Japan
Y) Persia
Z) Russia
ANSWER: Y) Persia
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer By words or numbers, which two of the following four are in the top five hydropower producing states? 1: Alabama 2: Florida 3: Louisiana 4: Oregon
ANSWER: 1 and 4 (also accept: Alabama and Oregon)

W) 75%
X) 50%
Y) 25%
Z) 10%
ANSWER: Y) 25%

ANSWER: (TIDAL) BARRAGES, FENCES, AND/OR TURBINES
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice To one significant figure, what is the relative speed of light inside of a vacuum in meters per second?
W) 3 × 107 meters per second
X) 3 × 108 meters per second
Y) 2 × 107 meters per second
Z) 2 × 108 meters per second
ANSWER: X) 3 × 108 meters per second
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the relativistic energy of a 3.5 gram weight? Use two significant figures for the constant c and express your answer in joules.
ANSWER: 320,000,000,000,000 (also accept: 320 TRILLION; 3.2 × 1014 JOULES)
13. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice How many ergs and gigawatt hours, respectively, are equal to 2 hundred thousand joules? Use significant figure rules.
W) 2 × 1014 ergs and 6 × 10-10 gigawatt hours
X) 2 × 1013 ergs and 6 × 10-9 gigawatt hours
Y) 2 × 1012 ergs and 6 × 10-8 gigawatt hours
Z) 2 × 1011 ergs and 6 × 10-7 gigawatt hours
ANSWER: Y) 2 × 1012 ergs and 6 × 10-8 gigawatt hours
14. BONUS Math Short Answer By the assigned numbers, which of the following four are equal to 6.0 × 109 ergs? All answers will have two significant figures. 1: 6000 joules 2: .17 watt hours 3: .6 kilojoules 4: 3.7 × 1021 electronvolts
ANSWER: 2, 3, and 4 (also accept: All but 1)
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What would be an acceptable definition of an 'energy carrier'?
W) A company that regulates energy and "carries" it to homes
X) Something that is not produced from anything else
Y) A form of energy that carries usable energy from one place to another
Z) Something that is always a byproduct of other chemical processes
ANSWER: Y) A form of energy that carries usable energy from one place to another
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By numbers or words, which of the following four are properties of hydrogen? 1: Flammable 2: Toxic 3: Odorless 4: Has faint yellow color
ANSWER: 1 and 3 (also accept: Flammable and Odorless)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following chemical formulas correctly describes ATP, or adenosine triphosphate?

Y) C12H5O13P3
Z) C10H16O13P2

ANSWER: 320 WATTS

ANSWER: 1.3 × 1018 JOULES (also accept: 1.3 × 1025 ERGS; 8.4 × 1036 ELECTRONVOLTS)
