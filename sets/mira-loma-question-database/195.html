4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice When iron meteorites are sliced open, polished, and etched with nitric acid, they reveal regular bands that are caused by alloys of iron and nickel that formed billions of years ago as the molten iron cooled and solidified. What is the name given to these bands?
W) chondrules
X) calcium-aluminum-rich inclusions
Y) Kirkwood's gaps
Z) Widmanstätten patterns
ANSWER: Z) Widmanstätten patterns
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Name all of the following that are true about perfect numbers 1) All even perfect numbers are generated by the formula 2p−1(2p −1) 2) Perfect numbers can be expressed in binary as a string of ones. 3) Each Mersenne prime generates one even perfect number.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the work done on an object when a force of 6 Newtons is applied at an angle of 60° above the horizontal, moving the object 2 meters?
ANSWER: 6 JOULES
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about static friction?
W) It is constant for a sliding object
X) Calculating the maximum force of static friction between an object and a surface requires only the coefficient of static friction
Y) It will resist the motion of a sliding object until the force on the object is greater than the force of friction
Z) In general, the coefficient of kinetic friction is greater than the coefficient of static friction
ANSWER: Y) It will resist the motion of a sliding object until the force on the object is greater than the force of friction

W) octahedron
X) dodecahedron
Y) icosahedron
Z) truncated icosahedron
ANSWER: Z) truncated icosahedron
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Eclipses follow a pattern which repeats itself in a cycle of 18 years and 11 2/3 days because, after one cycle, the moon and the nodes of its orbit return to the same place with respect to the sun. What is the name given to this cycle?
ANSWER: SAROS CYCLE
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which of the following is true of specific heat? 1. If the temperature of the system increases, the energy, Q is negative 2. If the temperature of the system decreases, Q is negative, corresponding to energy flowing out of the system 3. When ΔT is positive, energy is flowing into the system 4. When ΔT is negative, Q is also negative
ANSWER: 2, 3, AND 4

15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about genes, chromosomes, and proteins?
W) Genes control the production of proteins in an organism's cells
X) Chromosomes are made up of RNA
Y) A single gene on a chromosome contains only one pair of nitrogen bases

18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which law states that the rate at which an object radiates energy is proportional to the fourth power of its absolute temperature, taking into account the surface area and emissivity of the object, among other factors?
ANSWER: STEFAN'S LAW
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer When atoms are cooled to ultracold temperatures, they begin to behave as bosons, all inhabiting the same quantum state. In this state of matter, all the clustered atoms have the same velocity, blurred only by the Heisenberg uncertainty principle, and the atoms behave as superfluids. What is the name of this state of matter?
ANSWER: BOSE-EINSTEIN CONDENSATE
