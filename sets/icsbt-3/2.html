1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Corals, sea anemones, and jellyfish are all a part of what Phylum?
ANSWER: Cnidaria
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer In order for the stomach to convert pepsinogen to pepsin, it requires the use of hydrochloric acid, which is produced by what specific type of cells?
ANSWER: Parietal Cells
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Students in Dr. Li's AP Chemistry class are messing around as usual. Skyler has found himself a solution of BaCl2, and he decides to slowly add aqueous NaCl into the solution. He then notices that as he adds more and more NaCl to the solution, a white precipitate begins to form, which exemplifies what effect?
ANSWER: Common Ion Effect
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules has a trigonal pyramidal molecular geometry?
W) BF3
X) CO2
Y) NH3
Z) CH4
ANSWER: Y) NH3
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following cloud types is most directly associated with strong vertical development?
W) Altocumulus
X) Stratocumulus
Y) Cumulonimbus
Z) Noctilucent
ANSWER: Y) Cumulonimbus
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following 4 rocks by decreasing degree of foliation 1) Slate 2) Gneiss 3) Schist 4) Phyllite
ANSWER: 2, 3, 4, 1
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The decimal 0.24242424... can be simplified as a fraction A/B, where A and B are positive integers relatively prime to each other. What is A x B?
ANSWER: 264
4. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Given that a fair coin is flipped 8 times, what is the probability that at least 5 heads are flipped?
W) 37/128
X) 93/256
Y) 1/2
Z) 163/256
ANSWER: X) 93/256
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer First invented in 1947, this circuit component replaced vacuum tubes for switching circuits, amplifying signals, and performing computations. It is the fundamental component used to make all modern computers. It consists of junctions of silicon either doped to be P or N type. What is the name for this component?
ANSWER: Transistor
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 9 V battery is connected to a 4 Ω resistor and a 6 Ω resistor wired in parallel. What is the sum of the currents through either of the resistors to the nearest hundredth of an Ampere?
ANSWER: 3.75 A
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Skyler is able to comprehend language quite well throughout his day to day life, but struggles with verbal communication with his peers. This is due to the impediment of what specific area in the brain?
ANSWER: Broca's Area
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following three sections of the small intestine by increasing typical length 1) Duodenum 2) Jejunum 3) Ileum
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer While preparing qualitative-analysis reagents, a student compares three silver halides. Order the following three salts by increasing solubility in water 1) AgF 2) AgCl 3) AgBr
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A chemist prepares an acetate buffer in which the concentration of acetate is 100 times the concentration of acetic acid. If the pKa of acetic acid is 4.76, what is the pH of the buffer?
ANSWER: 6.76
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the motion of air around a high pressure system in the Southern Hemisphere?
W) Clockwise, spiralling outward
X) Clockwise, spiralling inward
Y) Counterclockwise, spiralling outward
Z) Counterclockwise, spiralling inward
ANSWER: Y) Counterclockwise, spiralling outward
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes normally graded bedding?
W) A sedimentary bed in which grain size decreases upward from coarse material at the bottom to finer material at the top
X) A sedimentary bed in which grain size increases upward from fine material at the bottom to coarser material at the top
Y) A set of inclined sedimentary layers formed by migration of ripples or dunes
Z) Alternating thin layers of silt and clay deposited during seasonal changes in a lake
ANSWER: W) A sedimentary bed in which grain size decreases upward from coarse material at the bottom to finer material at the top
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In integer modulo 7, what is 112-92?
ANSWER: 5
9. BONUS Math Short Answer Calculate the determinant of the 3x3 matrix with first row 2, 3, 4, second row 4, 3, 6, and third row 2, 6, 1.
ANSWER: 30
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What gas law states that, at a constant volume, pressure and temperature are directly proportional?
W) Boyle's Law
X) Charles's Law
Y) Gay-Lussac's Law
Z) Avogadro's Law
ANSWER: Y) Gay-Lussac's Law
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer A helium balloon is held at sea level with a pressure of 101,600 Pa, a temperature of 25 Celsius, and a volume of 7 L. The balloon is then released and floats up to the tropopause. There it has a temperature of -60 Celsius and a pressure of 30,000 Pa. What is the new volume of the balloon to the nearest tenth of a liter?
ANSWER: 16.9 L

ANSWER: Chiasmata (also accept: Chiasma)

X) 9; 6
Y) 12; 18
Z) 18; 12
ANSWER: Z) 18; 12

W) C2
X) N2
Y) O2
Z) F2
ANSWER: Y) O2

ANSWER: 1, 3, 4, 2
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Alex has a large wardrobe with four shirts, four socks, and four shorts. He has to go out for a date later tonight and needs to pick one shirt, one sock, and one shorts. ANSWER: 3/8
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The small end of a hydraulic piston is a circle with a radius of 1 meter. The large end of the piston is a circle with a radius of 3 meters. If 10 Newtons of force is applied to the small end, what is the output force on the large end, in Newtons?
ANSWER: 90 N
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer City engineers are constructing a water pipe to supply water to a power plant on a hill. In the section at the bottom of the hill, the water flows at an average speed of 3 meters per second. The hill is 30 meters tall, and once the water reaches the top section, it must be at a pressure of 300,000 Pa and flowing at a speed of 1 meter per second. At what pressure should the water be pumped at the base of the hill to the nearest whole number, in Pascals?
ANSWER: 590,000 Pa
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 choices that are core histones 1) H2A 2) H5 3) H4

16. BONUS Biology Short Answer A large, randomly mating population of beetles is in Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium for a gene with two alleles, Big A and Little A. Individuals with Little A, Little A display a yellow-shell phenotype, while individuals with Big A, Big A and Big A, Little A display a green-shell phenotype. In a sample of 10,000 beetles, 900 display the yellow-shell phenotype. How many beetles in this population are expected to be heterozygous?
ANSWER: 4200
