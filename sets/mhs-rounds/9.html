1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What quantity measures the third derivative of displacement?
ANSWER: JERK
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the minimum angle of incidence required for a light ray to undergo total internal reflection given that the material’s index of refraction is 2?
ANSWER: 30 DEGREES
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What part of Earth’s interior is noted for causing seismic waves to slow down due to the melting of peridotite?
ANSWER: LOW-VELOCITY ZONE (also accept: LVZ)
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three features that can be found on normal faults 1) Fault scarps 2) Forearc basin 3) Horsts
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following most accurately describes the coordination number?
W) It is the number of atoms assigned in a unit cell.
X) It is the number of atoms bonded to another atom in a unit cell.
Y) It is the sum of the sphere volumes in a unit cell.
Z) It is the density of atoms in a unit cell.
ANSWER: X) It is the number of atoms bonded to another atom in a unit cell.
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The dissolving metal reduction converts alkynes into E alkenes, this is done in part because group 1A elements dissolve in a liquid form of what molecule most commonly?
