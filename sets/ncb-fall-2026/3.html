Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many distinct bonds angles are present in the compound sulfur tetrafluoride, which has chemical formula SF4?
ANSWER: 3
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following acids is the weakest?
W) HClO
X) HClO2
Y) HClO3
Z) HClO4
ANSWER: W) HClO
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the compounds has the highest bond dissociation energy?
W) F2
X) Cl2
Y) Br2
Z) I2
ANSWER: X) Cl2
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three types of magnetism by increasing strength 1) paramagnetism 2) ferromagnetism 3) diamagnetism
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three bases by increasing yield of the Hofmann product formed when used in an elimination reaction 1) Ethoxide 2) Tert-butoxide 3) Hydroxide
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the formal charge of sulfur in sulfur hexafluoride?
ANSWER: 0
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The square root of the probability density function is equal to what function which is denoted by the greek letter psi?
ANSWER: Wave Function
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following types of interactions is the strongest?
W) Hydrogen Bonding
X) Ion-Dipole
Y) Dipole-Dipole
Z) London Dispersion Forces
ANSWER: X) Ion-Dipole
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What name is given the amount of energy necessary to remove an electron from a gas phase atom?
ANSWER: Ionization Energy
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Aspirin, also known as 2-(acetyloxy)benzoic acid, is treating with sodium bicarbonate. Which of the following may be the reason why?
W) increase the electrophilicity of the carboxyl group
X) Increase the solubility of the aspirin in water
Y) Reduce the rate of hydrolysis of the ester functional group
Z) Make the carboxyl group a better leaving group
ANSWER: X) Increase the solubility of the aspirin in water
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the points at which the wave function of an orbital is equal to 0?
ANSWER: Nodes
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Boron-12 will undergo which of the following types of radioactive decay?
W) alpha
X) beta minus
Y) beta plus
Z) gamma
ANSWER: X) beta minus
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What constant in the Arrhenius Equation, represents the frequency of molecular collisions and the probability that a collision will successfully lead to a chemical reaction?
ANSWER: Pre-exponential factor
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is washing his hands with soap. Soap molecules are effective because they are examples of what type of molecules which contain both a hydrophobic region and hydrophilic region?
ANSWER: Amphipathic (also accept: Amphiphilic)
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A compound with a multiple of which of the following numbers in their π system cannot be aromatic?
W) 2
X) 3
Y) 4
Z) 5
ANSWER: Y) 4
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What name is given to molecular orbitals that neither increases nor decreases the bond order between atoms, and contain electrons that either do not participate in bonding or are localized on a single atom?
ANSWER: Non-bonding orbital
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following accurately compares the strength of carbon-carbon and silicon-silicon single and double bonds?
W) Carbon forms stronger single bonds and double bonds
X) Carbon forms stronger single bonds but weaker double bonds
Y) Silicon forms stronger single bonds and double bonds
Z) Silicon forms stronger single bonds but weaker double bonds
ANSWER: W) Carbon forms stronger single bonds and double bonds
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements would NOT create a p-type semiconductor when doped into silicon?
W) Boron
X) Indium
Y) Antimony
Z) Gallium
ANSWER: Y) Antimony
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following values is not needed for the Born-Haber Cycle of MgF2?
W) First ionization energy of magnesium
X) Second ionization energy of magnesium
Y) First electron affinity of Fluorine
Z) Second Electron Affinity of Fluorine
ANSWER: Z) Second Electron Affinity of Fluorine
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three conformations of cyclohexane by increasing stability 1) Twist-Boat 2) Boat 3) Half-Chair
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three allotropes of Phosphorus by increasing reactivity 1) White Phosphorus 2) Black Phosphorus 3) Red Phosphorus
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice For which of the following signs of ΔH and ΔS respectively will a reaction always be spontaneous?
W) negative, negative
X) negative, positive
Y) positive, negative
Z) positive, positive
ANSWER: X) negative, positive
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following plots for concentration and absorbance will the graph be linear?
W) concentration against absorbance
X) concentration against absorbance2
Y) concentration2 against absorbance
Z) concentration against 1/absorbance
ANSWER: W) concentration against absorbance
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements has an atomic radius closest to that of magnesium?
W) Sodium
X) Calcium
Y) Scandium
Z) Beryllium
ANSWER: Y) Scandium
25. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is studying gauche interactions in butane. To do this, he uses what diagram which is similar in shape to a six-spoked wheel?
ANSWER: NEWMAN PROJECTION
