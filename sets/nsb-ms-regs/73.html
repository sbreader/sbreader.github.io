1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following lists contains only metals?
W) Magnesium, phosphorus, lead
X) Phosphorus, argon, lead
Y) Magnesium, manganese, lead
Z) Magnesium, helium, manganese
ANSWER: Y) Magnesium, manganese, lead
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer The speed of sound in air on a given day is 340 meters per second. You are standing 85 meters from a barn when you give a short, loud, yelp. How long in seconds does it take you to hear your own echo?
ANSWER: 0.5 (also accept: ONE-HALF)
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Express the following product in scientific notation: (4 × 108)(8 × 10-3).
ANSWER: 3.2 × 106
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Identify all of the following four statements that are always true 1) if three points are coplanar, they are collinear 2) if two points are collinear, they are coplanar 3) three points determine a plane 4) if two lines are cut by a transversal, the alternate interior angles are equal.
ANSWER: 2 (also accept: IF TWO POINTS ARE COLLINEAR, THEY ARE COPLANAR)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Why do hurricanes NOT occur at the equator?
W) The water is too warm
X) There is not enough land mass
Y) The Coriolis effect is too weak
Z) The days are too long
ANSWER: Y) The Coriolis effect is too weak
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What kind of rock is found in the dark craters of the Moon?
ANSWER: BASALT
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the primary source of all weather on Earth?
W) Clouds
X) The Sun
Y) Geographic features
Z) Human actions
ANSWER: X) The Sun
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Earth has many types of mountains. Which of the following is the best example of a folded mountain?
W) Mountains formed when a part of the Earth's crust is broken into large blocks, which are shifted by crust movement
X) Mountains formed when tectonic movements squeeze rock layers together like an accordion
Y) Mountains formed when molten rock rises through the crust and pushes up the rock layers above it
Z) Mountains formed when molten rock erupted onto the Earth's surface
ANSWER: X) Mountains formed when tectonic movements squeeze rock layers together like an accordion
5. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice E-85, also known as "flex-fuel," gets its name from which of the following?
W) It is 85% more efficient than gasoline
X) It is a mixture of 85% gasoline and 15% ethanol
Y) It is a mixture of 85% ethanol and 15% gasoline
Z) It has an equivalent octane rating of 85
ANSWER: Y) It is a mixture of 85% ethanol and 15% gasoline
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The most common type of fuel cell reacts what two gases to produce electricity?

X) Genetic drift
Y) Founder effect

11. BONUS Biology Short Answer In pea plants, tall is dominant over short. Suppose you cross a tall pea plant with a short pea plant. The first generations of offspring or the F1 generation are all tall. You then cross two of these F1 generation pea plants to create a new generation of offspring, the F2 generation. What fraction of the F2 generation pea plants will be tall?
ANSWER: 3/4

17. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice If Huntington's disease is determined by a dominant allele and is always fatal, then which of the following explains its continued inheritance?
W) Huntington's disease only shows up in a fraction of the individuals who carry the allele
X) There is heterozygote advantage for the alleles that cause the disease in tropical regions
Y) Huntington's disease shows up later in life, typically after the individual has reproduced
Z) Huntington's disease has a cure that allows individuals who have it to not show any symptoms and reproduce
ANSWER: Y) Huntington's disease shows up later in life, typically after the individual has reproduced
