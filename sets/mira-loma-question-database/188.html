Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following 4 minerals in which titanium is abundant. Ilmenite Sphene Rutile Carnotite
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Vega is the second brightest star in the northern celestial hemisphere after this star with a visual magnitude of -0.05.
ANSWER: ARCTURUS (also accept: Alpha Bootis)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which of the following elements is densest?{Plutonium, tungsten, Tantalum, Uranium, gold}
ANSWER: Plutonium
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the catabolic process in which a partial degradation of sugars occurs without the help of oxygen.
ANSWER: Fermentation
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer According to ancient mayan numerals the number 7 is represented by 2 resting on 1 bar, knowing this, how many dots and bars would be used to represent the number 10?
ANSWER: No dots, 2 lines
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Choose the correct electron configuration for aluminum.
W) 1s2,2s2,2p6,3s2,3p3
X) 1s2,2s2,2p1
Y) 1s2,2s2,2p6,3s2,3p1
Z) 1s2,2s2,2p6,3s2,3p6
ANSWER: Y) 1s2,2s2,2p6,3s2,3p1
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In Lewis structure how many lone electron pairs are shown in one molecule of H2S?
ANSWER: 2
8. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the daily food intake of a normal adult human in megajoules?
W) 5-7 megajoules
X) 1-2 megajoules
Y) 6-8 megajoules
Z) 8-10 megajoules
ANSWER: Y) 6-8 megajoules
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When pili connect a bacterium to another of its kind they form a bridge which allows what to transfer between the bacteria?
ANSWER: Plasmid
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following numbers is has the factor, 9?
W) 1304
X) 45032
Y) 1503
Z) 8965
ANSWER: Y) 1503
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A triangle with the three vertices, (4,0) (0,0) and (0,4) is which of the following? Scalene Isosceles Right Equilateral
ANSWER: 2 and 3
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Whether a sugar is an aldose or a ketose is dependent on the location of this functional group
ANSWER: Carbonyl Group
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In, Er = mc2, what does Er represent?
ANSWER: rest mass energy
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The southern segment of the San Andreas fault is known as
W) Mojave segment
X) Navajo segment
Y) Hollister segment
Z) Gualala segment
ANSWER: W) Mojave segment
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Mount St. Helens was an example of what phenomenon when viscous lava forces its way up and causes a bulge in the mountain.
ANSWER: Cryptodome
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which 20th century scientist proved that any differentiable symmetry of the action of a physical system has a corresponding conservation law?
ANSWER: Emmy Noether
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What phase of helium-4 occurs when viscosity of a fluid vanishes and heat capacity becomes infinite?
ANSWER: Superfluidity
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The Meissner Effect describes an event in which a magnet does this.
ANSWER: Levitates
23. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In each segment of a worm there is a pair of excretory tubes called…
W) Setae
X) Nephrostome
Y) Metanephridium
Z) Coelom
ANSWER: Y) Metanephridium
