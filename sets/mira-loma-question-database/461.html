1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many different types of histones exist?
ANSWER: 5
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of histones are not found within the neucleosome core?
W) H1
X) H2A
Y) H3
Z) H4
ANSWER: W) H1
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Centripetal force depends on velocity, radius, and mass. It may be observed in our solar system or a washer bound to a string rotating about a stick. If a rotating object increases its speed by a factor of three, by what factor will the centripetal force increase?
ANSWER: NINE
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer If the mass of a rotating object is ten kilograms, its velocity is ten meters per second, and the distance from the center is ten meters, what is its centripetal force?
ANSWER: 100 NEWTONS
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which era is also called "The Age of Mammals"?
W) Permian
X) Devonian
Y) Cenozoic
Z) Holocene
ANSWER: Y) Cenozoic
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Also called "The Age of Man", the Holocene Period is divided into two epochs. Human civilization developed in one, and mammoths, mastodons, and the first humans developed in the other. In which epoch did early humans evolve in?
ANSWER: PLEISTOCENE
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Two cubes are composed of congruent, cubic blocks. No matter what the dimensions of the two cubes are, you cannot combine them and rearrange the blocks to produce another cube. This is an application of what famous theorem?
W) Pythagorean Theorem
X) Fermat's Last Theorem
Y) Binomial Theorem
Z) Prime Number Theorem
ANSWER: X) Fermat's Last Theorem
