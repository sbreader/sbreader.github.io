1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the four types of vertebrae in the human spine from superior to inferior 1) Lumbar 2) Sacral 3) Cervical 4) Thoracic
ANSWER: 3, 4, 1, 2 (also accept: CERVICAL, THORACIC, LUMBAR, SACRAL)
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which region in the brain controls breathing and heart rate?
ANSWER: MEDULLA (also accept: MEDULLA OBLONGATA)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following metals had the lowest reduction potential?
W) Nickel
X) Manganese
Y) Gold
Z) Silver
ANSWER: X) Manganese
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds contains both an sp2-hybridized nitrogen and exhibits tautomerism under physiological conditions?
W) Aniline
X) Imidazole
Y) Pyridine
Z) Pyrrole
ANSWER: X) Imidazole
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Elias did not pay attention to Mr. Shah in modern physics, and has somehow managed to create the isotope Hydrogen-201. What is the most likely decay process of Hydrogen-201?
W) Beta minus decay
X) Beta plus decay
Y) Spontaneous fission
Z) Hydrogen-201 is not likely to decay within 1030 years
ANSWER: W) Beta minus decay
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer An atom of Vanadium-49 undergoes electron capture. What are the products of this reaction?
ANSWER: Ti-49 AND AN ELECTRON NEUTRINO
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Which of the following statements about polyhedra are NOT true? 1) There are five Platonic solids 2) There are nine regular polyhedra 3) Polyhedra do not have curved surfaces.
ANSWER: NONE

ANSWER: 36
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer After undergoing a helium flash, red giant stars generally move onto what branch of the HR diagram?
ANSWER: HORIZONTAL BRANCH
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best characterizes the spectrum of light emitted by a star?
W) Continuous spectrum with bright emission lines
X) Continuous spectrum with dark absorption lines
Y) Discrete spectrum with bright emission lines
Z) Discrete spectrum with dark absorption lines
ANSWER: X) Continuous spectrum with dark absorption lines
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory are using a logic gate approach to tackle quantum computing. In classical computing, logic gates are functions that accept binary inputs and generate binary outputs. In quantum computing, logic gates must meet the additional requirement that they are reversible, or bijective. Which of the following classical gates is reversible?
W) AND
X) COPY
Y) OR
Z) NOT
ANSWER: Z) NOT

Which of the following activation functions are linear? 1) Rectified linear unit 2) Gaussian-error linear unit.
ANSWER: NONE
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of ecological relationship do epiphytes have to trees?

7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of mimicry, often seen with ants, is used to gain access into the ant nest?
ANSWER: WASMANNIAN
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following solutes will produce the lowest boiling point elevation when dissolved in 1.00 kg of water?
W) Five moles of glucose
X) Three moles of sodium chloride
Y) One mole of aluminum chloride
Z) Four moles of potassium nitrate
ANSWER: Y) One mole of aluminum chloride
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Consider a reaction with reactants of oxygen gas and uranyl chloride gas and products of triuranium octoxide and chlorine gas, where the molecular formula of uranyl chloride is UO2Cl2. If the reaction is run at temperature T, and the ideal gas constant is R, what is the ratio Kc over Kp of the reaction in terms of R and T?
ANSWER: R TIMES T (also accept: RT)

If the police car’s siren is blaring at a constant pitch of 440 Hz, what is the pitch that Mr. Shillingburg hears to 3 significant figures? Assume that the speed of sound is 340 m/s.

14. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Two atomic orbitals have the same shape and orientation but have a different number of nodes. Which statement about the quantum numbers of these orbitals is correct?

Y) They have the same value of ml but different values of l.

18. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory are measuring the length of a 1.50 meter rod. 20. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Ms. White is performing a titration between a 0.100 M solution of a weak monoprotic acid and a 0.100 M solution of a weak base. Which of the following statements is true regarding the pH at the equivalence point and the choice of indicator for this titration?
W) The pH at the equivalence point will be exactly 7.0, so bromothymol blue is ideal.
X) The pH at the equivalence point depends solely on the concentration of the titrant, not the acid-base strengths.
Y) The solution will have poor buffering near the equivalence point, and no single indicator will show a sharp color change.
Z) Congo red is ideal because the pH at the equivalence point will be strongly acidic due to hydrolysis of the weak acid.
ANSWER: Y) The solution will have poor buffering near the equivalence point, and no single indicator will show a sharp color change.
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A uniform solid disk can rotate about different axes. ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
21. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A disk of moment of inertia I, mass M, and radius R lies flat on a frictionless horizontal surface. A cord is wrapped tightly around the disk, and a constant force T is applied to the end of the cord. After the disk has accelerated some distance, which of the following is the ratio of the rotational kinetic energy to total kinetic energy of the disk?
W) I/(MR2)
X) (MR2)/I
Y) I/(MR2 + I)
Z) (MR2)/(MR2+I)
ANSWER: Z) (MR2)/(MR2+I)
