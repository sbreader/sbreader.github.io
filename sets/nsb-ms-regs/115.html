Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT generally characteristic of oceanic-continental plate boundaries:
W) high mountain systems
X) wide continental shelves
Y) ocean trenches
Z) earthquake prone areas
ANSWER: X) wide continental shelves
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of an island arc collision zone:
W) Japan
X) Oregon
Y) Maine
Z) Chile and Peru
ANSWER: W) Japan
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In what quadrant of the coordinate system are both x and y negative?
ANSWER: 3 (also accept: III; THIRD)
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Bottled water is selling for 60-cents per bottle. If you buy a dozen bottles and hand the cashier a ten-dollar bill, how much change are your owed, in dollars and cents?
ANSWER: $2.80
3. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is CLOSEST to the volume of 4 liters in quarts:
W) 3.8
X) 4.0
Y) 4.2
Z) 4.4
ANSWER: Y) 4.2
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following 3 choices in order of INCREASING mass: largest moon of Saturn; Brown dwarf star; Earth
ANSWER: LARGEST MOON OF SATURN; EARTH; BROWN DWARF STAR
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following terms do biologists most often use to describe animals that do not move by their own design or effort:
W) saprophytic
X) instar
Y) motile
Z) sessile
ANSWER: Z) sessile
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the base nucleotide to which adenine binds on its opposing strand in DNA?
ANSWER: THYMINE
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Light typically travels in waves that are oriented in all planes traveling in a given direction. What is the term for light waves that are traveling in one plane?
ANSWER: POLARIZED (also accept: LINEAR POLARIZED)
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Name all of the following 4 choices that are electromagnetic radiation: radio waves; x-rays; cathode rays; visible light
ANSWER: ALL
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name all the planets that appear to have phases like our Moon to Earthbound observers?
ANSWER: MERCURY; VENUS
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The greatest annual variation in the intensity of solar radiation on Earth throughout the year is in which of the following locations:
W) tropics
X) north of the Arctic Circle
Y) south of the Antarctic Circle
Z) temperate, middle latitude zone
ANSWER: Z) temperate, middle latitude zone
7. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Pick any arbitrary point in the interior of a circle 12-inches in diameter and cut the circle into 8 slices by cutting at 45° angles through that arbitrary point. If you color alternate pieces blue and yellow, then the total area of the blue slices will:
W) equal the area of the yellow slices
X) be twice the area of the yellow slices
Y) be one-half the area of the yellow slices
Z) be one-third the area of the yellow slices
ANSWER: W) equal the area of the yellow slices
7. BONUS Math Short Answer Consider the four angles formed by two intersecting lines. If one angle measures 50°, find the measure of the remaining three angles, in degrees:
ANSWER: 50; 130; 130
8. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a property of diamonds:
W) extremely hard
X) transparent
Y) poor thermal conductor
Z) good electrical insulator
ANSWER: Y) poor thermal conductor
8. BONUS Math Short Answer Give the Roman numeral equivalent of 2009:
ANSWER: MMIX (do not accept: MMVIIII)
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What anatomical structure in boney fish allows them to adjust their buoyancy and stay afloat without swimming?
ANSWER: SWIMBLADDER (also accept: AIR BLADDER; GAS BLADDER)
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the most common name for the fluid in a spider that serves a similar function to blood in vertebrates?
ANSWER: HAEMOLYMPH (also accept: HEMOLYMPH)
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In which of the following units would capacitance most likely be given:
W) webers
X) farads
Y) teslas
Z) ohms
ANSWER: X) farads
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer Name all of the following 3 choices that are DC voltage sources: 9-volt battery; lightning; static electricity
ANSWER: ALL
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes how volcanic glass or obsidian is formed:
W) magma cools rapidly and crystals do not form
X) magma cools slowly and 1 large crystal is formed
Y) magma cools in direct contact with water
Z) magma is heated, cooled, then reheated
ANSWER: W) magma cools rapidly and crystals do not form
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The dominant component of most magma is:
W) carbon
X) sulfur dioxide
Y) silicon dioxide
Z) nitrogen
ANSWER: Y) silicon dioxide
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Any number, except zero, to the zero power equals what?
ANSWER: 1
12. BONUS Math Short Answer Give the numerical value of 20 factorial divided by 18 factorial:
ANSWER: 380
13. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer From what element are most semiconductors primarily composed?
ANSWER: SILICON
13. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is CLOSEST to 56 miles per hour in kilometers per hour:
W) 20
X) 50
Y) 90
Z) 120
ANSWER: Y) 90
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What organelle with attached ribosomes is most directly responsible for the translation of proteins destined to be transported out of the cell?
ANSWER: ENDOPLASMIC RETICULUM (also accept: ER; RER; ROUGH ER; ROUGH ENDOPLASMIC RETICULUM) (do not accept: SER or SMOOTH ER or SMOOTH ENDOPLASMIC RETICULUM)
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name all of the following 4 functional groups that are found in all amino acids: carboxyl; ketone; amino; phosphate
ANSWER: CARBOXYL; AMINO
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the resultant force when a force of 50 newtons is opposed in an opposite direction by a 10-newton force?
ANSWER: 40
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer A dog-sled team runs at 15 kilometers per hour for 6 hours and gets stuck in a partially melted lake for 3 hours, where it make no progress. It then runs at 10 kilometers per hour for another 5 hours and crosses the finish line. What is the dog sled team's average speed for the entire race, in kilometers per hour?
ANSWER: 10
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Banded iron formations that are present in mountain ranges around the world formed during the Achaean Era as a direct result of the presence of:
W) nitrogen
X) oxygen
Y) hydrogen
Z) methane
ANSWER: X) oxygen
16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a carbonate mineral:
W) halite
X) fluorite
Y) feldspar
Z) dolomite
ANSWER: Z) dolomite
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many terms are in the following variable expression and what is the coefficient of the second term: 5x2 – x – 12yz2 + 9
ANSWER: TERMS = 4; COEFFICIENT = –1
17. BONUS Math Short Answer Sue and Sierra are making necklaces. If Sue makes an average of 3 every 30 minutes and Sierra averages 6 every hour, how many necklaces do they average every hour if they work together?
ANSWER: 12
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What specific cell in your body becomes sickle-shaped if you are afflicted with sickle cell anemia?
ANSWER: RED BLOOD CELL (also accept: RBC; ERYTHROCYTE; RED CORPUSCLE)
18. BONUS Energy Short Answer Name all of the following 4 laboratory tools that would require a user to read a meniscus: graduated pipet; pH paper; watch glass; Buchner funnel
ANSWER: GRADUATED PIPET
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the most common name of the plant pigments that give fruits and many plant parts their orange color?
ANSWER: CAROTENOIDS
19. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms has an archegonium:
W) moss
X) bird
Y) frog
Z) oak tree
ANSWER: W) moss
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the most commonly used scientific name for the compound with the chemical formula C6H12O6, which is a product of photosynthesis?
ANSWER: GLUCOSE (also accept: D-GLUCOSE; DEXTROSE)
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Arrange the following 4 substances in order of DECREASING pH: normal human saliva; black coffee; bleach; stomach acid
ANSWER: BLEACH; NORMAL HUMAN SALIVA; BLACK COFFEE; STOMACH ACID
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Stratovolcanoes are typically found in which of the following locations:
W) continents and volcanic arcs
X) the abyssal plain
Y) along basaltic plateaus
Z) above hot spots in Earth's crust
ANSWER: W) continents and volcanic arcs
21. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of the troposphere:
W) the air is much dryer above the troposphere
X) the troposphere is 78% nitrogen gas and 21% oxygen gas
Y) the troposphere extends from the Earth's surface up to an average of 27 miles
Z) the troposphere ends where temperature no longer varies with height
ANSWER: Y) the troposphere extends from the Earth's surface up to an average of 27 miles
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer For the equation y = mx + b, b represents what value?
ANSWER: Y-INTERCEPT (also accept: WHERE THE LINE CROSSES THE Y; ORDINATE AXIS)
22. BONUS Math Short Answer Find a number such that 10-times the number reduced by 3 equals 4-times the number increased by 15:
ANSWER: 3
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer From what constellation do the Geminids meteor shower appear to originate?
ANSWER: GEMINI
23. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following astronomers devised a system of the orbits for the planets that is considered heliocentric and published in his book "On the Revolutions of the Heavenly Spheres":
W) Galileo
X) Copernicus
Y) Newton
Z) Kepler
ANSWER: X) Copernicus
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many pairs of wings do beetles typically have?
ANSWER: 2
24. BONUS Biology Short Answer How many cervical vertebrae are found in most mammals?
ANSWER: 7
25. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer How many wavelengths per second are equal to 5 kilohertz?
ANSWER: 5,000
25. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the percent efficiency for a lever that has a work input of 2,000 joules and a work output of 1,600 joules?
ANSWER: 80
