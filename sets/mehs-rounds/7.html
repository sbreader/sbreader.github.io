1. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 60 kg person is standing in an elevator which is moving upwards at a rate of 3.2 meters per second. During this upward movement, what is the gravitational force experienced by the person in the elevator, in newtons?
ANSWER: 588
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains what occurs to the melting and boiling points of a solvent when solid potassium phosphate is dissolved?
W) Melting point increases, boiling point increases
X) Melting point increases, boiling point decreases
Y) Melting point decreases, boiling point increases
Z) Melting point decreases, boiling point decreases
ANSWER: Y) Melting point decreases, boiling point increases
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The existence of which of the following properties explains why adding energy to water at the boiling point does not increase its temperature?
W) Enthalpy of vaporization
X) Melting point decreases, boiling point increases
Y) Melting point increases, boiling point decreases
Z) Melting point increases, boiling point increases
ANSWER: W) Enthalpy of vaporization
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are studying the origin of iron meteorites. Which of the following statements regarding meteorites is NOT true?
W) Iron meteorites often have widmanstätten patterns
X) Stony-iron meteorites are the most common meteorites in falls
Y) Most types of chondrites contain chondrules
Z) Stony meteorites are made up of minerals that contain silicates
ANSWER: X) Stony-iron meteorites are the most common meteorites in falls
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying how the hadley cell is affected by sea temperature changes. Identify all of the following 3 statements regarding the hadley cell that are true 1) Subtropical highs may form where the hadley and ferrel cells converge 2) A high pressure zone is formed at the equator where the hadley cells converge 3) Trade winds located within the hadley cell flow from the equator to the horse latitudes.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY

11. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice The angular velocity of a real pendulum is inversely proportional to which of the following quantities?

W) A torque of equal magnitude but opposite direction of the original torque is applied to the same end of the box on the Z axis
X) A torque of equal magnitude but that same direction as the original torque is applied to the opposite end of the box on the X axis
