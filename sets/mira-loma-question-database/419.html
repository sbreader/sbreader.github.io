1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The van't Hoff factor, represented by I, is a measure of the effect of a solute upon colligative properties of a solution. Which of the following explanations best explains why carboxylic acids such as acetic acid have a van't Hoff factor less than one when dissolved in benzene?
W) carboxylic acids readily disassociate in benzene
X) carboxylic acids form dimers through hydrogen bonding when placed in benzene
Y) carboxylic acids do not disassociate nor associate in benzene due to weak intermolecular forces between the solute and solvent
Z) carboxylic acids have a negative free energy of solvation in benzene
ANSWER: X) carboxylic acids form dimers through hydrogen bonding when placed in benzene
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Bob the bricklayer pulls a 10 kg stack of bricks straight up a 1 m high wall. He then decides to build a ramp that is elevated at 30 degrees above the vertical and push the bag of bricks up the ramp to the top of the wall. If the ramp is 1 meter high and Bob pushed the bag for 2 meters, neglecting the force of friction, what is the difference in work done on the bag between pushing the bricks up the ramp and lifting them straight up to the nearest Joule?
ANSWER: 0 JOULES
