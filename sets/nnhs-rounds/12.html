Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What device first demonstrated Earth’s rotation?
ANSWER: FOUCAULT PENDULUM
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding the Standard Model 1) The Higgs boson is the only elementary particle with a spin of 0 2) Neutrinos have a lepton number of 1 3) Quarks have a baryon number of 1.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following chemicals is NOT needed for Sanger sequencing?
W) dNTPs
X) ddNTPs with fluorescent tags
Y) DNA Ligase
Z) Primer
ANSWER: Y) DNA Ligase
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What specialized lymph capillaries take up chylomicrons from fat absorption?
ANSWER: LACTEALS
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a Milankovitch cycle?
W) Eccentricity
X) Obliquity
Y) Rotation
Z) Precession
ANSWER: Y) Rotation
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes circular, nearly-vertical shafts found in glaciers that are formed by meltwater melting a specific region in the ice?
ANSWER: MOULIN
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Let complex number z = 3cos(2π/3) + 3sin(2π/3)i. Which of the following is a real number?
W) z
X) z3
Y) z5
Z) z7
ANSWER: X) z3
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Let A equal the sum of the reciprocals of the perfect fourth powers, and let B equal the sum of the reciprocals of the odd perfect fourth powers. What is the ratio of B to A?
ANSWER: 15/16
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following atoms can NOT become hypervalent?
W) Calcium
X) Sulfur
Y) Fluorine
Z) Phosphorous
ANSWER: Y) Fluorine
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following four bonds that are found in all esters 1) Carbon-carbon double bond 2) Carbon-oxygen double bond 3) Carbon-oxygen single bond 4) Oxygen-oxygen single bond.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab recently discovered a new microbial pathway that produces ethylene and methane. The pathway produces these carbon-containing molecules as byproducts to make what sulfur-containing amino acid?
W) Methionine
X) Alanine
Y) Leucine
Z) Tryptophan
ANSWER: W) Methionine
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are breaking up biomass using a combination of water and tetrahydrofuran. Identify all of the following four properties that correctly characterize tetrahydrofuran 1) Heterocyclic 2) Low boiling point 3) Polar 4) Saturated.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3, AND 4 (also accept: ALL)
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about the Reynolds number is FALSE?
W) A high reynolds number is characteristic of turbulent flow
X) Viscous forces dominate at high Reynolds numbers
Y) It is dimensionless
Z) It is proportional to fluid velocity
ANSWER: X) Viscous forces dominate at high Reynolds numbers
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer What type of particle is its own antiparticle?
ANSWER: MAJORANA PARTICLE (also accept: MAJORANA FERMION)
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What hormone, which is a derivative of tryptophan, is produced by the pineal gland?
ANSWER: MELATONIN
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Geoffrey has found a new inhibitor to the hexokinase enzyme. To study its effects, Geoffrey compares the Lineweaver-Burke plot of the original enzyme to the Lineweaver-Burke plot of the enzyme with inhibitor added. He notices that the y-intercept stayed the same, but the slope increased. What type of inhibitor did Geoffrey find?
W) Competitive
X) Noncompetitive
Y) Uncompetitive
Z) Mixed
ANSWER: W) Competitive
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following drainage patterns would be formed on a surface that is relatively uniform?
W) Dendritic
X) Radial
Y) Rectangular
Z) Trellis
ANSWER: W) Dendritic
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the spectral class of the red supergiant Betelgeuse, located in the Orion constellation?
ANSWER: M
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following could be the length of the hypotenuse of a triangle with positive integer side lengths?
W) 18
X) 19
Y) 20
Z) 21
ANSWER: Y) 20
10. BONUS Math Short Answer A certain ribbons store offers a 2-ribbon package for $3 and a 5-ribbon for $7. What is the maximum number of ribbons that can be bought at this store with $16?
ANSWER: 11
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the degree of unsaturation of phenol, which has the molecular formula C6H6O?
ANSWER: 4
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many signals would be expected in the 1H-NMR spectrum of cyclopentadiene?
ANSWER: 3
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Researchers at Lawrence Livermore National Lab are studying the structure of neutron stars by looking at the location of high-energy neutrons. Which of the following is closest to the maximum possible solar mass of a neutron star?
W) 3.0
X) 2.2
Y) 1.4
Z) 0.6
ANSWER: X) 2.2
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Livermore National Lab are analyzing hadrons composed of what second generation quark with +2/3 charge?
ANSWER: CHARM
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What phenomenon occurs when two particles have quantum properties that are dependent on each other, which Albert Einstein termed “spooky action at a distance”?
ANSWER: QUANTUM ENTANGLEMENT
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the period of an ideal pendulum that consists of a uniform, rigid rod of mass 10 kilograms and length 0.3 meters?
ANSWER: 2π/7 SECONDS
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Two toxic species of animals that both have similar bright displays of coloration exhibit which of the following phenomena?
W) Mullerian mimicry
X) Batesian mimicry
Y) Mertensian mimicry
Z) Cryptic coloration
ANSWER: W) Mullerian mimicry
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer Rank the following three cell types by increasing mitochondria density 1) Cardiac myocyte 2) Hepatocyte 3) Erythrocyte.
ANSWER: 3,2,1
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What force supports white dwarfs and prevents them from collapsing due to gravity?
ANSWER: ELECTRON DEGENERACY PRESSURE
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the difference in time between a sidereal year and a tropical year?
W) 2 minutes
X) 20 minutes
Y) 3 hours
Z) 1 day
ANSWER: X) 20 minutes
16. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Consider the following statement: if n is an integer, then the square root of n is also an integer. Which of the following values of n would disprove this statement?
W) -4
X) 20.25
Y) 25
Z) 0
ANSWER: W) -4
16. BONUS Math Short Answer Three circles, each with radius 4, are externally tangent to each other. A fourth circle is placed in the center of the three circles and is externally tangent to the other three circles. What is the radius of the fourth circle?
ANSWER: 8√3/3-4
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements are true about mass spectroscopy 1) Heavier ions are deflected less than lighter ions 2) All atoms are ionized into cations 3) The mass spectrum of chlorine gas has two peaks.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Expressing your answer to two significant figures, what is the osmotic pressure of a solution when 3 moles of sodium chloride are added to 4 kilograms of water, at 23° celsius?
ANSWER: 3.7 × 106 PASCALS (also accept: 3,700 KILOPASCALS)
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Researchers at Argonne National Lab are studying conditions on water-rich exoplanets. Which of the following is closest to the albedo of Earth’s oceans?
W) 0.1
X) 0.3
Y) 0.7
Z) 0.9
ANSWER: W) 0.1
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Argonne National Lab are studying the catalytic conversion of carbon dioxide and water into ethanol. Suppose that a researcher has 132 grams of carbon dioxide and 36 grams of water. How many moles of ethanol can the research produce?
ANSWER: 2/3
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What quantity equals the time integral of the Lagrangian?
ANSWER: ACTION
19. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Unpolarized light with an intensity of 200 watts per meter squared travels through two polarizers. W) 200
X) 100
Y) 50
Z) 25
ANSWER: Z) 25

ANSWER: MICROGLIA
