Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Soil composed of which of the following grain sizes will drain water the fastest?
W) Clay
X) Silt
Y) Sand
Z) Gravel
ANSWER: Z) Gravel
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following forces is the strongest driver of plate movement?
W) Ridge push
X) Slab pull
Y) Slab suction
Z) Convection Currents
ANSWER: X) Slab pull
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following features would most strongly indicate that a galaxy has undergone a recent merger event with another galaxy?
W) Low star formation rate
X) Well-defined symmetric spiral arms
Y) Low brightness in the infrared
Z) A long tidal tail extending tens of kiloparsecs
ANSWER: Z) A long tidal tail extending tens of kiloparsecs
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The flatness problem in cosmology was resolved by the proposal of what period in the early universe, which also explains the uniformity of the cosmic microwave background?
ANSWER: Inflation
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the coloration of an extremely silica rich obsidian with no magnesium or iron impurities?
W) Black
X) Clear
Y) Red
Z) Brown
ANSWER: X) Clear
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes how the salinity of groundwater generally changes with increasing depth?
W) Increases
X) Decreases
Y) Increases then decreases
Z) Decreases then increases
ANSWER: W) Increases
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 types of solar eclipses during which the Sun’s photosphere is generally not visible at maximum eclipse 1) Partial 2) Annular 3) Total
ANSWER: 3 only
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three sedimentary rocks that would likely dissolve more quickly as a result of ocean acidification 1) Chalk 2) Coquina 3) Diatomite
ANSWER: 1 and 2
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Oh no, the mass of the Sun suddenly multiplied by a factor of 4. Assuming all other factors unchanged and in terms of Earth’s orbital period T, which of the following would be the new orbital period of Earth around the Sun?
W) ¼ T
X) ½ T
Y) T
Z) 2T
ANSWER: X) ½ T
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 stream measurements that are generally expected to be greater downstream 1) Capacity 2) Competence 3) Sinuosity
ANSWER: 1 and 3
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Vishnu wakes up and observes a rise in temperature and a steady drop in air pressure. Which of the following types of weather can he most anticipate to occur later in the day?
W) Clear skies
X) Light precipitation
Y) Thunderstorm
Z) Hailing
ANSWER: X) Light precipitation
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Anhydrite is the product of the dehydration of what mineral?
ANSWER: Gypsum
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following masses of stars on the main sequence that will pass through the instability strip at some point in their life 1) 1 solar mass 2) 3 solar masses 3) 10 solar masses
ANSWER: All
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The surface of Io is rapidly replenished by intense volcanic activity. Which of the following would you least expect to find on Io’s surface?
W) Active Lava Flows
X) Eruptive Craters
Y) Impact Craters
Z) Mountains
ANSWER: Y) Impact Craters
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true regarding placer deposits?
W) They are chemically stable
X) They have specific gravities between 1 and 1.5
Y) They can be both fluvial and aeolian in origin
Z) Igneous intrusion can weather to produce Placer deposits
ANSWER: X) They have specific gravities between 1 and 1.5
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of supernova that form via the core collapse mechanism 1) Type Ia 2) Type Ib 3) Type II
ANSWER: 2 and 3
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of precipitation is most likely to form when a warm layer of air overlies a thin, sub-freezing layer of air near the surface?
W) Sleet
X) Hail
Y) Graupel
Z) Freezing rain
ANSWER: Z) Freezing rain
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Under ideal conditions, the Coriolis force is proportional to what powers of both the speed of the air current and the planet’s rotation rate, respectively?
W) 1, 1
X) 1, -1
Y) -1, 1
Z) -1, -1
ANSWER: W) 1, 1
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In addition to forming kilonovae, neutron star mergers can form relativistic jets that create what extremely luminous event?
ANSWER: Gamma ray bursts
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Great Oxygenation Event signified a great increase in atmospheric and oceanic oxygen levels. Which of the following correctly describes a primary evidence for the Great Oxygenation Event in the geologic record?
W) Initial appearance of pyrite in the rock record
X) Disappearance of pyrite from the rock record
Y) Widespread and repeated deposition of banded iron formations
Z) A single isolated occurrence of banded iron formation
ANSWER: Y) Widespread and repeated deposition of banded iron formations
