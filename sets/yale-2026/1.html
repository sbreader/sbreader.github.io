Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the shortest phase of mitosis, in which sister chromatids move toward opposite ends of the cell?
ANSWER: ANAPHASE
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Using a quadrant-based method to measure population size would be least effective for a population with which of the following distributions?
W) Clustered
X) Competitive
Y) Random
Z) Uniform
ANSWER: W) Clustered
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Triangle ABC has angle A equal to 40 degrees and angle B equal to 60 degrees. In degrees, what is the measure of angle C?
ANSWER: 80
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What principle in chemistry govern how electrons are sequentially placed in atomic subshells?
ANSWER: Aufbau Principle
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Tectonic plates are able to move on top of what ductile layer in Earth's upper mantle?
ANSWER: Asthenosphere
3. BONUS Math Short Answer A point P has the peculiar property that the sum of its x and y coordinates is equal to 15. Point P is reflected about the line y=x and dilated by a factor of 2 about the origin. What is the new sum of the x and y coordinates of P?
ANSWER: 30
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A person on the surface of Earth weighs 720 newtons. If the moon's gravitational acceleration is 1/6th of Earth's, how much, in newtons, would the person weigh on the moon?
ANSWER: 120
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which location could a geologist likely find metamorphic rocks?
W) River Delta
X) Pyroclasts
Y) Meandering Streams
Z) Accretionary Wedge
ANSWER: Z) Accretionary Wedge
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at the Department of Molecular Biophysics and Biochemistry are researching tryptophan, one of 9 amino acids humans cannot produce, but rather obtain from their diet. What term describes these 9 amino acids?
ANSWER: Essential
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer A ball is dropped from the same height in three different situations. Order the following three situations by increasing impulse exerted on the ball when it strikes the ground 1) The ball rebounds off the ground to half of the original height 2) The ball rebounds off the ground to one fourth the original height 3) The ball does not bounce at all when it hits the ground
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Because both extremely low and extremely high human birth weights reduce survival, natural selection on birth weight is best classified as which of the following?
W) Artificial
X) Directional
Y) Disruptive
Z) Stabilizing
ANSWER: Z) Stabilizing
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers at Yale's Osuji Lab are studying the structure and dynamics of complex fluids. In a mixture of two liquids, both liquids are soluble in the same solvent and have similar boiling points, but one liquid is significantly more polar than the other. Which of the following methods is most appropriate to separate the two liquids?
W) Filtration
X) Simple distillation
Y) Liquid-liquid extraction
Z) Centrifugation
ANSWER: Y) Liquid-liquid extraction
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The temperature of a star is doubled and its radius is halved. By what factor is its luminosity multiplied by?
ANSWER: 4
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the set of lymphoid organs at the back of the throat, that act as most people's first line of defense against inhaled pathogens?
ANSWER: TONSILS
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Particle accelerators typically accelerate particles closest to which of the following speeds in kilometers per second?
W) 300
X) 3000
Y) 30000
Z) 300000
ANSWER: Z) 300000
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is true about the standard entropy of a substance?
W) It is zero for elements in their standard states
X) It is always positive for pure substances above 0 Kelvin
Y) It is lower for a gaseous state than a liquid at a given temperature
Z) It is always independent of pressure
ANSWER: X) It is always positive for pure substances above 0 Kelvin
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at Yale's School of Public Health are investigating cystic fibrosis. Two parents who do not have cystic fibrosis have a child who does. What type of inheritance pattern does cystic fibrosis follow?
ANSWER: Recessive (also accept: Recessive autosomal)
9. BONUS Math Short Answer On the equator, the Earth has a radius of about 6,000 kilometers. In kilometers and in terms of pi, what is the arc length between longitude equals 0 degrees and 90 degrees?
ANSWER: 3000π
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What dimensionless quantity measures the relative amounts of products and reactants in a reaction mixture?
ANSWER: Reaction quotient (also accept: Q)
10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following accurately describes why the Arctic is a desert?
W) Under the snow and ice are large amounts of sand from glacial abrasion
X) The Arctic has low precipitation due to low humidity
Y) There is little rooted vegetation
Z) Precipitation patterns vary minimally between seasons
ANSWER: X) The Arctic has low precipitation due to low humidity
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer For Tony, an outfit consists of a pair of pants and a shirt. If Tony has 3 pairs of pants and 4 shirts but refuses to wear his two Yale shirts with cargo shorts, how many distinct outfits can he wear?
ANSWER: 10
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two springs with spring constants 10 and 15, respectively, are attached in series. What is the spring constant of this combination?
ANSWER: 6
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the general term for unsorted glacial sediments?
ANSWER: Till (also accept: Glacial till)
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Yale's School of Engineering and Applied Science are researching self-amputation in organisms. Unlike humans, animals like salamanders and flatworms are able to utilize what type of cells capable of differentiating into any specialized cell during the regeneration process?
ANSWER: Stem
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A rock sits on a table. What force exerted by the table on the rock opposites the gravitational force the rock feels from the Earth?
ANSWER: Normal
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three choices that would likely be characteristic of plants living in non-polar deserts 1) Developed root systems 2) High stomatal density 3) Waxy coating on leaves
ANSWER: 1 and 3
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at Yale are researching caloric restriction as a means of combating stem cell aging. If a person were aiming to minimize calorie consumption, which of the following organic compounds should they avoid?
W) Proteins
X) Carbohydrates
Y) Lipids
Z) Alcohols
ANSWER: Y) Lipids
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In the synthesis of ammonia from nitrogen and hydrogen gas, what is the expected yield of ammonia, in moles, from a reaction of 3 moles nitrogen gas with 6 moles hydrogen gas?
ANSWER: 4
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following traditional biological kingdoms is polyphyletic?
W) Plantae
X) Protista
Y) Fungi
Z) Animalia
ANSWER: X) Protista
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Vega is an A0V star. Which of the following colors is Vega?
W) Blue-White
X) Yellow
Y) Yellow-Orange
Z) Orange-Red
ANSWER: W) Blue-White
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three elements in order of lowest to highest magnitude of electron affinity 1) Fluorine 2) Chlorine 3) Bromine
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer Ball A is thrown directly right with a speed of 30 m/s from a height of 8 meters and lands at a distance 40 meters away. Ball B is thrown similarly, except with a speed of 15 meters per second. In meters, at what distance away does Ball B land?
ANSWER: 20
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The test grades in David's math class follow a normal distribution with a mean of 60 and a standard deviation of 10. David's teacher applies a flat curve to the class, boosting everyone's final grade by 20 points. After the boost, what are the mean and standard deviation of the test grades?
ANSWER: 80 and 10
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at the Ocean, Atmosphere and Climate Modeling group are investigating current patterns in the Pliocene epoch. What epoch are we currently in?
ANSWER: Holocene
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three clouds which form an anvil top at the tropopause 1) Cumulus 2) Cumulonimbus 3) Cirrus
ANSWER: 2 only
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer A man with colorblindness has a child with a woman that is a carrier for colorblindness. What is the probability that their child is colorblind?
ANSWER: 1/2 (also accept: 50%; 0.5)
19. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice System A has 256 microstates, while system B has 64 microstates. What is the ratio of the entropy of system B to the entropy of system A?
W) 1/4
X) 3/4
Y) 4/3
Z) 4
ANSWER: X) 3/4
19. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following units are used to represent molar Gibbs free energy?
W) Joules
X) Joules per kelvin
Y) Joules per mole
Z) Joules per kelvin per mole
ANSWER: Y) Joules per mole
20. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Two unknown point charges are located inside a sphere with radius 7 meters. Currently, the next electric flux is F. If the sphere's radius is tripled. What is the new net electric flux in terms of F?
W) 0.5F
X) 1F
Y) 2F
Z) Cannot be determined
ANSWER: X) 1F
