1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What layer of the Sun’s atmosphere is immediately interior to the corona?
ANSWER: THE CHROMOSPHERE
1. BONUS Energy Short Answer According to the internationally accepted system of scientific units called the SI system, what are the basic units for mass and time?
ANSWER: KILOGRAM AND SECOND
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The source of energy for the movements of crustal plates is currently thought to be the result of:
W) tidal motion that produces a net westward movement of landmasses
X) the pulling apart of the poles resulting from the Earth's rotation
Y) convection currents produced within the mantle
Z) remnant magnetism
ANSWER: Y) convection currents produced within the mantle
