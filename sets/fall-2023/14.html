1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer For a solid composed of five diatomic molecules with two possible orientations for each molecule, how many distinct microstates of the system are possible?

1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following ethers has the highest ring strain?
W) Propylene oxide
X) Dimethyl ether
Y) THF
Z) Dioxane
ANSWER: W) Propylene oxide
2. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is TRUE about the function f(x) = the fraction with numerator x + 4 and denominator x + 6?
W) The limit does not exist at x = 0
X) The limit as x approaches –6 from the right is positive infinity
Y) The limit as x approaches negative infinity is 1
Z) The limit as x approaches –4 from the left is negative infinity
ANSWER: Y) The limit as x approaches negative infinity is 1

ANSWER: 74
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Pollen grains contain microspores which are needed to fertilize a megaspore in the ovule of a heterosporous seed plant. Which of the following describes where the microspore is produced in a pine gymnosperm?
W) Thylakoid
X) Archegonium
Y) Microsporangium
Z) Pollen tube
ANSWER: Y) Microsporangium

4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists in the Sarpong Group at UC Berkeley are focused on developing strategies for synthesizing complex biomolecules found in nature, or “natural products.” To do this, they often work backwards from their target molecule, decomposing it into simpler precursors. ANSWER: (1, –2)
