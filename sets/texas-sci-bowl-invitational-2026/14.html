TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Jerry’s local pest control company is advertising that they have developed a new pesticide called Arizonotoxin that works on insects by degrading the excitatory neurotransmitter released at insect neuromuscular junctions. What neurotransmitter is most likely to be effected by Arizonotoxin?

1. BONUS Biology Short Answer In Saccharomyces Cerevisiae, two genes A and B each have two alleles, capital A, lowercase a, capital B, and lowercase b. A diploid is constructed by mating haploids dominant in each gene to haploids recessive in each gene, and then the diploid is induced to undergo meiosis. You dissect 200 unordered tetrads and score them. 120 of the tetrads are parental ditypes, 70 are tetratypes, and 10 are nonparental ditypes. What is the map distance between genes A and B in centimorgans to the nearest tenth?
ANSWER: 32.5

4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A particle of mass m moves in an attractive inverse-square potential V(r) = −k/r and is in a bound orbit with total energy E < 0. In terms of E and k, what is the semi-major axis a of the orbit?
W) a = k/(2E)

ANSWER: X) a = −k/(2E)
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two identical pendulums of length L and mass m are coupled by a spring of constant k attached at the bobs. For small oscillations, what is respectively the symmetric normal mode frequency and then antisymmetric normal mode frequency?
ANSWER: ω1 = √(g/L) (symmetric mode) and ω2 = √(g/L + 2k/m) (antisymmetric mode)
