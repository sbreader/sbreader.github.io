Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is investigating solids and notices that many of them have a similar heat capacity. What law states that the molar heat capacity of many solid elements is constant and equal to approximately 3 times the ideal gas constant?
ANSWER: Dulong-Petit
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav has a solution of Silver, Barium, and Magnesium cations. Abhinav adds three anions to selectively precipitate out the cations one by one. By name or number, order the following three anions from first to last to be applied 1) Chloride 2) Hydroxide 3) Sulfate
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice How many different energy levels are present in benzene?
W) 2
X) 3
Y) 4
Z) 5
ANSWER: Y) 4
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three heterocycles by increasing aromaticity 1) Furan 2) Thiophene 3) Pyrrole
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What constant is used to determine the electrostatic potential of an ion in a crystal by approximating the ions as point charges?
ANSWER: Madelung Constant
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice At an external pressure of 1 atm and a temperature of 0.000001 K, what is the most stable state of matter of helium?
W) Solid
X) Liquid
Y) Gas
Z) Plasma
ANSWER: X) Liquid
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three classes of compounds by increasing number of lone pairs on the sulfur 1) Sulfide 2) Sulfone 3) Sulfoxide
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Giving your answer to two significant figures and in scientific notation, if an acid has a pKa of 6, how many moles of the acid would need to be added to 1 liter of water for the solution to have a pH of 5.00?
ANSWER: 1.1 × 10-4
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three gases by increasing compressibility factors at STP 1) Helium 2) Chlorine 3) Hydrogen
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three molecular geometries that may exhibit chirality 1) Octahedral 2) Square Pyramidal 3) Seesaw
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term refers to the ability of a specific group or atom to move to an adjacent electron-deficient or electron-rich center during a molecular rearrangement?
ANSWER: Migratory Aptitude
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Cumulenes are compounds that contain a chain of three or more consecutive, adjacent double bonds, with [X]-Cumulene being a cumulene with X carbons. By name or number, identify all of the following cumulenes which are planar 1) [4]-Cumulene 2) [5]-Cumulene 3) [6]-Cumulene
ANSWER: 1, 3
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the π-bonding interaction between a filled or half filled orbital of a transition metal atom and a vacant orbital on an adjacent ion or molecule?
ANSWER: π-backbonding
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following point groups describes a molecule with octahedral geometry and six identical ligands, such as sulfur hexafluoride?
W) Td
X) D4h
Y) Oh
Z) C4v
ANSWER: Y) Oh
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is observing a sample of cyclopentadiene and notices he is unable to isolate pure cyclopentadiene. This is because cyclopentadiene performs what reaction with itself to dimerize?
ANSWER: Diels Alder Reaction
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, order the following three reagents by first to last to be applied in the conversion of benzene to benzoic acid 1) Zinc Amalgam and HCl 2) Aluminum Chloride and Acetyl Chloride 3) Chromic Acid
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the predicted group of superheavy elements with specific "magic numbers" of protons and neutrons?
ANSWER: Island of stability
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Given that the reduction of A to A- is 0.5 V and that the reduction of A- to A3- is -1.3 V, what is the reduction potential of A to A3-?
ANSWER: -0.7 V
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Amy reacts a certain compound X with iodine and sodium hydroxide and then performs an acid work-up on the product and observes a yellow color. Which of the following functional groups was most likely present in compound X?
W) Primary Alcohol
X) Secondary Alcohol
Y) Ketone
Z) Aldehyde
ANSWER: Y) Ketone
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the product formed from the reaction of propene with benzene in phosphoric acid?
ANSWER: Isopropyl Benzene (also accept: Cumene)
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three molecules that possess a center of inversion 1) trans-dichloroethylene 2) cis-dichloroethylene 3) Benzene
ANSWER: 1, 3
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the bonding mode of carbon monoxide when acting as a ligand in metal carbonyl complexes?
W) Sigma donation from carbon to metal, with pi backbonding from metal d-orbitals into the CO pi star orbital
X) Sigma donation from oxygen to metal only
Y) Pure pi donation from the C-O pi bond
Z) Ionic bonding between C4- and the metal center
ANSWER: W) Sigma donation from carbon to metal, with pi backbonding from metal d-orbitals into the CO pi star orbital
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Given that compound M has pKa1 of 4.1 and pKa2 of 6.7, what is the isoelectric point of compound M?
ANSWER: 5.4
12. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following species does NOT have mixed oxidation states?
W) S2O32-
X) S2O62-
Y) S2F4
Z) N2O3
ANSWER: X) S2O62-
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer For a molecule that can only move in 2D space, what is the kinetic energy of the particle in terms of boltzmann's constant, k, and Temperature, T?
ANSWER: kT
13. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Abhinav is running an electrochemical cell in which silver is the cathode and copper is the anode. How would adding ammonia to the cathode and anode affect the cell potential respectively?
W) increase, increase
X) increase, decrease
Y) decrease, increase
Z) decrease, decrease
ANSWER: Y) decrease, increase
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three functional groups which will lead to anionic polymer addition 1) Nitro 2) Methoxy 3) Methyl
ANSWER: 1
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A straight chain hydrocarbon with no branching, rings, or triple bonds has formula C15H20. When treated with Ozone followed by DMS, what is the maximum number of aldehyde groups that could be formed?
ANSWER: 12
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The reaction between Iron (III) ions and Ferrocyanide ions will create what iconic blue dye?
ANSWER: Prussian Blue
15. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecular geometries has the highest steric number?
W) Square antiprismatic
X) Trigonal prismatic
Y) Capped square antiprismatic
Z) Pentagonal bipyramidal
ANSWER: Y) Capped square antiprismatic
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Used in the synthesis of strained cyclic compounds, what reaction consists of reacting α-halo ketones with a nucleophilic base to form carboxylic acid derivatives?
ANSWER: Favorskii Rearrangement
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 3 statements which are true about silicon based compounds when compared to carbon based compounds 1) SN2 is more favorable for silicon based compounds 2) SN1 is more favorable for silicon based compounds 3) E2 is more favorable for silicon based compounds
ANSWER: 1
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the process by which α glucose and β glucose are able to equilibrate through the conversion of one isomer to the other through the open chain form?
ANSWER: Mutarotation
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the process by which amino acids are created from aldehydes through the addition and hydrolysis of a nitrile?
ANSWER: Strecker Synthesis
