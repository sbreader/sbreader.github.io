1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The bottleneck effect and founder effect are both examples of what general evolutionary phenomenon where allele frequencies change based on random chance?
ANSWER: Genetic drift
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer When a cell is in metaphase, order the following three structures by increasing distance from the metaphase plate 1) Kinetochore 2) Centromere 3) Centrosome
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A rhombus has perimeter 40. What is the maximum possible area of the rhombus?
ANSWER: 100
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Assuming an Instagram reel takes 9 seconds to watch, what is the maximum number of entire reels Rohan can scroll in a 20 minute period?
ANSWER: 133
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the changes in the oxidation numbers of manganese and carbon, respectively, when potassium permanganate is reacted with oxalic acid?
W) Increases, increases
X) Increases, decreases
Y) Decreases, increases
Z) Decreases, decreases
ANSWER: Y) Decreases, increases
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following processes requires the largest input of energy for a 100-gram sample of water?
W) Heating from 40 °C to 50 °C
X) Melting
Y) Vaporization
Z) Sublimation
ANSWER: Z) Sublimation
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A helium flash in low-mass stars marks their transition into which of the following branches of the HR diagram?
W) Red giant branch
X) Horizontal branch
Y) Asymptotic giant branch
Z) Subgiant branch
ANSWER: X) Horizontal branch

ANSWER: 2

ANSWER: 0

X) 4

Z) 6

7. BONUS Math Short Answer Variables A, B, C, and D are related such that A is proportional to B to the sixth power, B is inversely proportional to C to the third power, and C is proportional to D to the one-half power. To what power of D is A proportional to?
ANSWER: -9
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The rate constant for a certain reaction's rate law has units of liters squared per mol squared seconds. What is the overall reaction order of this reaction?
ANSWER: Third

W) Less than 78 °C
X) 78 °C
Y) Between 78 °C and 100 °C
Z) 100 °C
ANSWER: Y) Between 78 °C and 100 °C
