W) Ketone
X) Alcohol
Y) Ether
Z) Alkyne

ANSWER: CHELATION
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at SLAC National Accelerator Lab are studying the properties of dark matter by observing dwarf galaxies around the Milky Way. Which of the following types of theoretical dark matter forms consists of particles that travel with ultrarelativistic velocities?
W) Hot
X) Warm
Y) Cold
Z) Mixed
ANSWER: W) Hot
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Idaho National Lab are seeking ways to design a nuclear power system for the moon. Identify all of the following 3 statements regarding the moon that are true 1) The sinuous rilles on the moon were formed by the cutting by flowing lava 2) Lunar crater counts temporarily surged during the late heavy bombardment 3) Lunar anorthosites mainly make up the maria while basalts mainly make up the highlands.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice As ocean acidification decreases, which of the following happens to the carbonate compensation depth?
W) It will rise closer to the sea surface
X) It will fall towards the sea floor
Y) It will rise to the aragonite compensation depth
Z) It will fall to the lysocline
ANSWER: W) It will rise closer to the sea surface

ANSWER: MARINE TRANSGRESSION

7. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Ike and Jungleman set up a double-slit experiment, where the light filtering through the slits is projected onto a large photon detector. Ike turns on the light, and checks to see where the first 10 photons emitted landed. Which of the following statements about the first photons is true?
W) The photons will all land on an interference maxima
X) The photons will all land on an interference minima
Y) The photons will all land on random spots
Z) The photons will all land on the same spot
ANSWER: Y) The photons will all land on random spots

8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding genetic drift is NOT true?
W) Genetic drift can cause allele frequencies to change randomly
X) The bottleneck effect is usually observed when a large group separates from a population
Y) Genetic drift can cause rare alleles to become fixed
Z) The founder effect causes some groups in populations to diverge and become distinct
ANSWER: X) The bottleneck effect is usually observed when a large group separates from a population

14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding proper motion is NOT true?
