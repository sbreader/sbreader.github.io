1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer This type of terrestrial biome is found near the equator in South America, Central Africa, and Southern Asia. It receives the highest rainfall, and it is among the most productive and diverse biomes on Earth. Name this biome.
ANSWER: TROPICAL RAINFOREST
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following patterns of dispersion would be exhibited by fungi whose spores have been dispersed by the wind?
W) Clumped
X) Uniform
Y) Random
Z) Loose
ANSWER: Y) Random
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is an Arrhenius base?
W) Ammonia
X) Sodium hydroxide
Y) Ethanol
Z) Butoxide
ANSWER: X) Sodium hydroxide
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice According to the VSEPR Model, which of the following hybridizations produces a molecule that is planar?
W) d2sp3
X) dsp3
Y) sp3
Z) sp2
ANSWER: Z) sp2
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A ball of mass 4 kilograms is swung on a rope with tension 9 Newtons. If the radius at which the ball swings is 4 meters, how fast is the ball traveling?
ANSWER: 3 METERS/SECOND
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which effect, caused by the rotation of the Earth, causes a deflection in the path of moving objects so that they appear to move along a curve rather than in a straight line?
ANSWER: CORIOLIS EFFECT
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A vase has been bought for $21. It was on sale for 30% off. What was the original cost of the vase?
ANSWER: $30
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many ways can you pay for a 30-cent pencil using only quarters, and dimes, and nickels?
ANSWER: 5
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which two gas giants are referred to as the "ice giants" because their atmosphere is composed largely of ices such as ammonia and methane?
ANSWER: URANUS AND NEPTUNE
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer This gas giant was the first to be predicted through mathematical calculation rather than emperical evidence. It has an atmosphere with wind speeds as high as 2100 km/hr. It has active and visible weather patterns, such as the Great Dark Spot. Name this planet.
ANSWER: NEPTUNE

Z) Mutualism

ANSWER: KEYSTONE SPECIES
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which law is an expression of the law of conservation of energy and states that the enthalpy change for a reaction involving a series of steps is equal to the sum of the enthalpy changes of the individual steps?
ANSWER: HESS'S LAW
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The Friedel-Crafts reactions are which type of reactions?
W) SN1 nucleophilic substitution
X) SN2 nucleophilic substitution
Y) Electrophilic aromatic substitution
Z) Electrophilic Aliphatic substitution
ANSWER: Y) Electrophilic aromatic substitution
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is a nonconservative force?
W) Gravity
X) Friction
Y) Electromagnetic force
Z) Spring force
ANSWER: X) Friction
18. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a result of the fact that gravity is a conservative force?
W) The line integral through any closed loop is zero
X) The change in potential energy is independent of the path taken
Y) The curl of the vector field is zero
Z) The direction of the force of gravity is always opposite the direction of motion
ANSWER: Z) The direction of the force of gravity is always opposite the direction of motion
