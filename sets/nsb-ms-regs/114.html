Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for a 50/50 mixture of enantiomers?
ANSWER: RACEMIC MIXTURE (also accept: RACEMATE)
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three factors that can affect the rate of a chemical reaction 1) Changing the temperature 2) Changing the concentration of the reactants 3) Adding a catalyst
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Proteus and Nereid are moons of what planet?
ANSWER: NEPTUNE
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Early refractor telescopes were hindered by distortion because light rays of different colors are refracted to different focal points. This effect is known as what?
ANSWER: CHROMATIC ABERRATION
3. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Argonne National Lab are developing solar cells made from perovskite materials. Currently, most commercial solar cells are made from what material?
W) Gallium arsenide
X) Silicon
Y) Cadmium telluride
Z) Calcium titanate
ANSWER: X) Silicon
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at the Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility are studying pions. Pions are formed from what two flavors of quarks?
ANSWER: UP AND DOWN (also accept: UP, DOWN; DOWN, UP)
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In a geometric sequence with first two terms 1 and 3, what is the sum of the first 4 terms?
ANSWER: 40
4. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the ratio of the volume of a sphere to that of a right circular cone whose base has the same radius as the sphere and whose height equals the diameter of the sphere?
ANSWER: 2 (also accept: 2 TO 1; 2:1)
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following physical quantities is equal to the rate of change of momentum?
W) Energy
X) Acceleration
Y) Force
Z) Torque
ANSWER: Y) Force
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer A fixed amount of an ideal gas originally has a temperature of 300 kelvins, a volume of 20 liters, and a pressure of 1 atmosphere. If its pressure is increased to 3 atmospheres and its volume is decreased to 5 liters, then in kelvins, what will be the new temperature of the gas?
ANSWER: 225
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Thermoregulation in mammals is primarily carried out by what part of the brain?
ANSWER: HYPOTHALAMUS
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer From what primary germ layer does the lens of the mammalian eye develop?
ANSWER: ECTODERM
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Based on their bond-dissociation energies, which of the following bonds is strongest?
W) C triple bond C
X) H single bond F
Y) N triple bond N
Z) C triple bond N
ANSWER: Y) N triple bond N
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Born-Haber cycles are an application of what law that states that a series of reactions can be combined to determine the enthalpy change of a reaction in question?
ANSWER: HESS’S LAW (also accept: HESS)
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Short-period comets originate beyond the orbit of Neptune in what region of the solar system?
ANSWER: KUIPER BELT
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A star has a temperature of 2500 kelvins. Which of the following best categorizes the peak emission wavelength of the star?
W) Yellow light
X) Orange light
Y) Red light
Z) Infrared light
ANSWER: Z) Infrared light
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at the Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility are studying a particle decay process that involves neutrino oscillations. Which of the following could physicists infer when they discovered neutrino oscillations?
W) Neutrinos are sterile
X) Neutrinos have mass
Y) Neutrinos are baryons
Z) Neutrinos are tachyons
ANSWER: X) Neutrinos have mass
9. BONUS Energy Short Answer Argonne National Lab scientists are using machine learning to understand the behavior of heat-resistant materials at very high temperatures. Place the following three materials in increasing order of melting point 1) Stainless steel 2) Alumina 3) Cast iron
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following values is NOT in the range of f(x) = ex – 2?
W) –2
X) –1
Y) 0
Z) 1
ANSWER: W) –2
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Two complex numbers have absolute values of 5√3 and 2√3, and angles of 263 degrees and 158 degrees, respectively. What are the absolute value and the angle degree measure of their product?
ANSWER: 30; 61
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following laws is most useful for calculating the electrical field generated by a stationary point charge at a particular distance?
W) Coulomb's Law
X) Biot-Savart Law
Y) Ampere's Law
Z) Faraday's Law
ANSWER: W) Coulomb's Law
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer Rank the following three substances in order of increasing speed of sound in that substance at STP 1) Iron 2) Sea water 3) Air
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms is most likely to be a primary consumer in a lake ecosystem?
W) Zooplankton
X) Cyanobacteria
Y) Predatory fish
Z) Detritus-eating crayfish
ANSWER: W) Zooplankton
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Instead of lungs, many bony fish possess what homologous gas-filled organ that allows for adjustment of buoyancy?
ANSWER: SWIM BLADDER (also accept: GAS BLADDER; AIR BLADDER; FISH MAW)
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What rule states that electrons will half-fill orbitals before pairing up?
ANSWER: HUND’S RULE (also accept: HUND'S)
13. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules contains carbon-carbon bonds with the most s-character?
W) Ethane
X) Ethene
Y) Ethyne
Z) Propane
ANSWER: Y) Ethyne
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A brine pool containing sulfate and calcium is undergoing evaporation. What mineral will most likely result from this process?
ANSWER: GYPSUM
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Denitrification by bacteria in very low oxygen environments can lead to the production of what potent greenhouse gas?
ANSWER: NITROUS OXIDE (also accept: N2O; DINITROGEN MONOXIDE)
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab are fabricating silicon chips doped with vanadium dioxide. What is the formal oxidation state of vanadium in vanadium dioxide?
ANSWER: +4 (also accept: 4)
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists supported by the Department of Energy are developing materials to serve as artificial connections between muscle and bone. What is the term for the natural version of these structures?
ANSWER: TENDONS
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the amplitude of a sinusoidal graph whose maximum value is 15 and minimum value is –7?
ANSWER: 11
16. BONUS Math Short Answer Let f(x) be the fraction with numerator 2x + 8 and denominator x2 + x – 12. How many horizontal asymptotes and how many vertical asymptotes does the graph of f have?
ANSWER: ONE HORIZONTAL; ONE VERTICAL
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If the distance between two gravitationally-bound bodies is tripled, by what factor is the gravitational force between them multiplied?
ANSWER: 1/9
17. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Two electrons within the same orbital of an atom must have opposite spins. Which of the following principles most directly explains this phenomenon?
W) Aufbau principle
X) Uncertainty principle
Y) Hund's rule
Z) Pauli exclusion principle
ANSWER: Z) Pauli exclusion principle
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of transmembrane protein is typically involved in attaching epithelial cells to the extracellular matrix?
ANSWER: INTEGRIN
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three organisms that are chondrichthyans 1) Sharks 2) Rays 3) Hagfish
ANSWER: 1, 2
