1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why H2O absorbs infrared light but O2 does not?
W) H2O has no net dipole moment while O2 does
X) H2O exhibits hydrogen bonding while O2 does not
Y) H2O can vibrate asymmetrically while O2 cannot
Z) H2O has single bonds while O2 has double bonds
ANSWER: Y) H2O can vibrate asymmetrically while O2 cannot
