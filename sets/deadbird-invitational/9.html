Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Jessica the geologist is studying rocks at Yosemite National Park and observes a large rock formation with many thin layers peeling from it, like an onion. This feature was most likely formed by what type of weathering that causes layers of rock to gradually separate from a massive rock?
ANSWER: EXFOLIATION (also accept: SHEETING)
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes shear stress in rocks?
W) Differential stress that squeezes a rock mass like a vise
X) Differential stress that elongates rock bodies
Y) Differential stress that causes one part of a rock body to slide past another
Z) Differential stress that is applied gradually, causing rocks to deform elastically
ANSWER: Y) Differential stress that causes one part of a rock body to slide past another
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following satellites has the highest albedo in the solar system?
W) Europa
X) Earth’s moon
Y) Titan
Z) Enceladus
ANSWER: Z) Enceladus
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the reduced mass of a binary star system with stars of masses 4 and 6 solar masses?
ANSWER: 2.4 (also accept: 12/5)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of unconformity is demonstrated when a sedimentary rock like shale, lies over an older intrusive igneous rock like granite?
ANSWER: NONCONFORMITY
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of air lifting mechanism occurs along air mass boundaries when warm air is pushed up to form large clouds?
ANSWER: FRONTAL LIFTING (also accept: FRONTAL WEDGING)
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What natural cycle describes a person’s daily sleep and wake cycle?
ANSWER: CIRCADIAN RHYTHM
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer In biology, what type of behavior practices helping other individuals at a cost to oneself?
ANSWER: ALTRUISM
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following vitamins is NOT water-soluble?
W) A
X) B6
Y) B12
Z) C
ANSWER: W) A
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following 4 steps of glucose breakdown from first to last 1) Oxidative phosphorylation 2) Glycolysis 3) Oxidation of pyruvate 4) TCA cycle.
ANSWER: 2,3,4,1
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of algae contain seaweeds and is the most complex?
W) Brown
X) Green
Y) Red
Z) Golden
ANSWER: W) Brown
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the protective bony structure that covers the breathing gills of a fish?
ANSWER: OPERCULUM
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the state when the forward rate of a reaction is equal to the reverse rate?
ANSWER: EQUILIBRIUM
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the molecular geometry of phosphorus pentachloride?
ANSWER: TRIGONAL BIPYRAMIDAL
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What unitless quantity gives the ratio of the speed of light in a vacuum to the speed of light in a certain material?
ANSWER: INDEX OF REFRACTION
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following units measures the ratio between the moles of solute to the kilogram mass of the solvent?
W) Mole fraction
X) Molarity
Y) Molality
Z) Van’t hoff factor
ANSWER: Y) Molality
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following substances would have the lowest vapor pressure at room temperature?
W) 1-butanol
X) n-pentane
Y) 2-propanone
Z) Ethene
ANSWER: W) 1-butanol
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the molecular geometry of the sulfur atom in IF5?
W) Trigonal bipyramidal
X) See-saw
Y) Square pyramidal
Z) Octahedral
ANSWER: Y) Square pyramidal
10. BONUS Math Short Answer An ellipse is constructed on the coordinate plane with equation x2 / 16 + y2 / 81 = 1. A circle is then constructed with equation x2 + y2 = 9. What is the value of the area inside the ellipse but outside the circle?
ANSWER: 27π
11. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following constants is equal to the sum of the reciprocal of the factorials of the whole numbers?
W) π
X) e
Y) φ
Z) √3
ANSWER: X) e
11. BONUS Math Short Answer If January 1st in a given year fell on a Sunday, assuming that there is no leap day, on what day of the week would December 15th fall?
ANSWER: FRIDAY
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If the recursive sequence tn is defined by tn = 2tn-1 + 4 and t1 = 3, what is the value of t4?
ANSWER: 52
12. BONUS Math Short Answer Eddy has a beyblade collection. He tells you that when he divides his beyblades evenly into sets of 7 he has 2 left over, and when he divides them into sets of 13 he has 1 left over. From this information, you cannot determine his exact number of beyblades, but you can determine the infinite list of solutions for how many stamps he could have. What is the difference between each solution in this list?
ANSWER: 91
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Scientists at Idaho National Lab are studying isotope stability. Which of the following is the most stable isotope of carbon?
W) Carbon-11
X) Carbon-12
Y) Carbon-13
Z) Carbon-14
ANSWER: X) Carbon-12
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are studying the mechanisms of asexual reproduction in bees. What type of asexual reproduction utilized by some bee species involves the development of the egg without being fertilized?
ANSWER: PARTHENOGENESIS
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab are looking into the history of the moon by studying the craters that formed. These craters were formed during what period where there was an increased surge in debris impact rate?
ANSWER: LATE HEAVY BOMBARDMENT
14. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Scientists at Los Alamos National Lab are studying types of magnetism. Which of the following is indicated by the presence of paramagnetism in a molecule?
W) The molecule possesses unpaired electrons
X) The molecule possesses no unpaired electrons
Y) The molecule is positively charged
Z) The molecule is negatively charged
ANSWER: W) The molecule possesses unpaired electrons
