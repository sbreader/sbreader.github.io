1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the sedimentary rock formed by compaction and cementation of rounded, pebble-size particles?
ANSWER: CONGLOMERATE
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of a good index fossil?
W) Easy to identify
X) Geographically widespread
Y) Slow evolution
Z) Easy to preserve
ANSWER: Y) Slow evolution
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an evolutionary adaptation of plants to arid climates?
W) Leaves have a waxy coating
X) Carbon incorporation and the Calvin cycle are separated
Y) Stomata are closed at night
Z) Leaves are absent
ANSWER: Y) Stomata are closed at night
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following mammal species is usually considered diurnal?
W) Eastern fox squirrel
X) Black-footed ferret
Y) Virginia opossum
Z) Raccoon
ANSWER: W) Eastern fox squirrel
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the specific chemical term for the minimum pressure required to produce liquefaction of a substance at the critical temperature?
ANSWER: CRITICAL PRESSURE
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Name the molecule with the formula CH3COOCH2CH2CH3.
ANSWER: PROPYL ETHANOATE (also accept: PROPYL ACETATE)
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The variables x and y vary directly. Which of the following equations could represent the relationship between x and y?
W) (1/5)x = 1/y
X) x/y = 1/5
Y) xy = 1/5
Z) xy = 5
ANSWER: X) x/y = 1/5
4. BONUS Math Short Answer An open box is to be made from a rectangular piece of material 8 inches by 6 inches by cutting equal squares of side x from the four corners and turning up the sides. In terms of x, give an expression for the volume of the box in cubic inches.
ANSWER: x(8-2x)(6-2x) (also accept: 4x3 – 28x2 + 48x)
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A rock is thrown vertically upwards with speed v from the edge of a cliff. At the same moment, a second rock is thrown vertically downwards with the same initial speed v. Neglecting air resistance, which of the following statements is true?
W) The rock that was thrown upwards reaches the bottom of the cliff with a higher velocity
X) The rock that was thrown downwards reaches the bottom of the cliff with a higher velocity
Y) Both rocks reach the bottom of the cliff with the same velocity at the same time
Z) Both rocks reach the bottom of the cliff with the same velocity but at different times
ANSWER: Z) Both rocks reach the bottom of the cliff with the same velocity but at different times
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer A mass is spun horizontally at the end of a string. If its speed is changed so that the centripetal force is doubled, in simplest decimal form to the nearest tenth, what is the ratio of final speed to initial speed?
ANSWER: 1.4
