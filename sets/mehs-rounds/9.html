1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A semi-compressible cube with sides of length 2 meters is filled with an incompressible fluid. If a force of 5 newtons is applied to the front of the cube, giving your answer in pascals, what is the change in pressure of the face at the bottom of the cube?
ANSWER: 1.25
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer An RLC circuit has a resistor of resistance 47 ohms, an inductance of 9 henries, a capacitor of 37 farads, and a driving frequency of 103 radians. What is the impedance of the circuit at resonance?
ANSWER: 47
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules has the shortest nitrogen-oxygen bond length?
W) Nitrite ion
X) Nitrous oxide
Y) Dinitrogen trioxide
Z) Nitric oxide
ANSWER: Z) Nitric oxide
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the doubly-eponymous phenomenon that describes the cooling of a gas as it expands as a result of weakening intermolecular forces?
ANSWER: JOULE-THOMSON EFFECT (also accept: JOULE-KELVIN EFFECT)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Livermore National Lab are doing research on the stellar evolution of brown dwarfs. What element is used to distinguish brown dwarf stars from main sequence stars due to its presence in the atomic spectra of brown dwarfs?
ANSWER: LITHIUM
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Livermore National Lab are studying how clouds affect solar radiation and the process of precipitation within them. Identify all of the following 3 statements concerning clouds that are true 1) Cumulus clouds form from thermals 2) Convection is the main form of heat transport in thermal bubbles and causes the rising and sinking of air in cumulus clouds 3) Stratus clouds can not form when a thick layer of fog rises.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the process in which a mid-latitude cyclone develops and increases in strength?
ANSWER: CYCLOGENESIS
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding streams is NOT true?
W) The line connecting the lowest points of a stream is called the thalweg
X) Erosion in a stream generally occurs at the cut bank
Y) Water flows faster in the hyporheic zone than it does in the open channel
Z) The competence of a stream is the maximum particle size it is able to carry
ANSWER: Y) Water flows faster in the hyporheic zone than it does in the open channel

ANSWER: 23
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains how viscosity and surface tension of a liquid are affected with an decrease in temperature?
W) Viscosity increases, surface tension increases
X) Viscosity increases, surface tension decreases
Y) Viscosity decreases, surface tension increases
Z) Viscosity decreases, surface tension decreases
ANSWER: W) Viscosity increases, surface tension increases
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If the partial pressures of oxygen and nitrogen gas are 4.0 atm and 3.0 atm, respectively, and they combine to form dinitrogen pentoxide with a k value of 3 times 10 to the negative 4, what is the equilibrium partial pressure of oxygen?
ANSWER: 2.85
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What quantity must be zero for an ideal carnot engine?
ANSWER: ENTROPY
7. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following could be done to slow the discharge of a fully-charged capacitor in an RC circuit without increasing the maximum charge that the capacitor could hold?
W) Increase the voltage of the circuit
X) Increase the resistance of the circuit
Y) Increase the capacitance of the capacitor
Z) Decrease the resistance of the circuit
ANSWER: Z) Decrease the resistance of the circuit

X) Secretion

Z) Metastasis is the process by which cancer cells spread to locations away from their original site
