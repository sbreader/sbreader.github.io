1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following factors does not affect the flow rate of a fluid through a horizontal pipe?
W) Diameter of the pipe
X) Viscosity of the fluid
Y) Pressure difference across the pipe
Z) Length of the pipe
ANSWER: Z) Length of the pipe
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer What principle explains the relationship between the velocity and pressure of a fluid in steady, incompressible flow?
ANSWER: BERNOULLI’S PRINCIPLE
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following quantities is always not unitless?
W) Absorbance
X) Turbidity
Y) Specific Gravity
Z) Equilibrium Constant
ANSWER: X) Turbidity
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the positive difference between the number of rotational and vibrational degrees of freedom of ammonia at 320 Kelvin?
ANSWER: 3
3. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Mr. Edgar is using an AI detector tool to determine if students are using ChatGPT to write essays. His AI detector tool detects all students who are using ChatGPT, but also tends to label human-written essays as AI written. What terms best describe Mr. Edgar’s AI detector tool?
W) High sensitivity, high specificity
X) High sensitivity, low specificity
Y) Low sensitivity, high specificity
Z) Low sensitivity, low specificity
ANSWER: X) High sensitivity, low specificity
3. BONUS Math Short Answer In Mr. Edgar's class of 30 students, 5 students used ChatGPT. Of those 5 students, all 5 were detected by Mr. Edgar’s AI detector tool. 3 students who didn’t use ChatGPT were also flagged by Mr. Edgar’s AI detector tool. What is the negative predictive value, to the nearest percent, of Mr. Edgar’s AI detector tool?
ANSWER: 100
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Olivine is best categorized as which of the following types of silicates?
W) Nesosilicates
X) Inosilicates
Y) Tectosilicates
Z) Phyllosilicates
ANSWER: W) Nesosilicates
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the region on the HR diagram where a combination of size and temperature causes stars to become unstable and pulsate?
ANSWER: INSTABILITY STRIP
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is ∫01 (8x3-6x5) dx?
W) 0
X) 1
Y) 2
Z) 3
ANSWER: X) 1
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Identify the carrying capacity of a population of deer, P, given the differential equation dP/dt = 4P(1-P/24)
ANSWER: 24
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds burns purple in a flame test?
W) Potassium
X) Calcium
Y) Sodium
Z) Lithium
ANSWER: W) Potassium
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In a given reaction, if delta H is negative, and delta S is positive, what does this indicate about the sign of delta G and the spontaneity of the reaction respectively?
W) Negative, nonspontaneous
X) Negative, spontaneous
Y) Positive, nonspontaneous
Z) Positive, spontaneous
ANSWER: X) Negative, spontaneous
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the relationship between two different coordinate frames that move at a constant velocity relative to each other in spacetime?
ANSWER: LORENTZ TRANSFORMATION
7. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Approximately how much more intense is 50 decibels than 10 decibels?
W) 4 times
X) 8 times
Y) 16 times
Z) 32 times
ANSWER: Y) 16 times
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which of the following 3 cellular structures are involved in cyclic photosynthetic electron flow? 1) Photosystem 1 2) Photosystem 2 3) Plastocyanin
ANSWER: 1 and 3
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which statement is true regarding the molecular structures of the active reaction centers of pigments P680 and P700?
W) P680’s is larger
X) P700’s is larger
Y) They are identical molecules
Z) P680’s is nonpolar while P700’s is polar
ANSWER: Y) They are identical molecules
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The upper limit for the mass of neutron stars is known as which of the following?
W) Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff limit
X) Roche limit
Y) Chandrasekhar limit
Z) Eddington limit
ANSWER: W) Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff limit
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What does the term “glitch” imply when used with neutron stars?
W) Increase in size
X) Increase in luminosity
Y) Decrease in period
Z) Decrease in mass
ANSWER: Y) Decrease in period

W) 5
X) 10
Y) 20
Z) 25
ANSWER: W) 5

ANSWER: 198761.5
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A current of 7 amperes flows through a 10 ohm resistor for 60 seconds. How many coulombs have passed through the resistor?
ANSWER: 420

ANSWER: 20.4

W) Boron
X) Gold
Y) Cadmium
Z) Hafnium
ANSWER: X) Gold
