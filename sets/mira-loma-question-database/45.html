1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following hormones are NOT produced by the thyroid or parathyroid?
W) thyroxine
X) parathormone
Y) oxytocin
Z) calcitonin
ANSWER: Y) oxytocin
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What instrument measures the rate of radioactive decay based on the ions and electrons produced as a radioactive particle passes through a gas-filled chamber?
ANSWER: GEIGER COUNTER (also accept: GEIGER-MULLER COUNTER)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Name all of the following subatomic particles that have a stable lifetime: electron, electron neutrino, muon, muon neutrino, tau
ANSWER: ELECTRON, ELECTRON NEUTRINO, MUON NEUTRINO (also accept: 1, 2, AND 4)
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer State the type of function, the vertex, and the y-intercept respectively of the following function: y –3 = 3(x –2)2
ANSWER: FUNCTION: PARABOLA; VERTEX: (2, 3); Y-INTERCEPT: (0, 15)
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true regarding glaciers?
W) common particles that are transported as stratified drift include sand and gravel
X) drumlins, which occur in clusters, are asymmetrical hills composed of stratified drift
Y) in areas that were previously centers of ice accumulation, land has slowly been rising in the last few thousand years
Z) ground moraines have leveling effects, filling in low spots while clogging stream channels
ANSWER: X) drumlins, which occur in clusters, are asymmetrical hills composed of stratified drift
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is TRUE regarding semiconductors?
W) an n-type semiconductor has more holes than electrons
X) a p-type semiconductor has impurity atoms that release free electrons, enhancing the conductivity of the semiconductor
Y) in a p-n junction, an electrical barrier is formed which conducts current more readily in one direction than the other
Z) the conductivity of nonmetals decreases with rising temperature
ANSWER: Y) in a p-n junction, an electrical barrier is formed which conducts current more readily in one direction than the other
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the hypothesis that mitochondria and plastids were formerly components of prokaryotic cells that begin living within larger cells, leading to the formation of eukaryotic cells?
ANSWER: ENDOSYMBIOSIS
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following in INCORRECT regarding batteries?
W) in a lead storage battery, lead serves as the anode while silver dioxide serves as the cathode
X) the alkaline dry cell lasts longer than the common dry cell battery because zinc corrodes less rapidly under basic conditions than acidic conditions
Y) in a fuel cell, reactants are continuously supplied to the galvanic cell
Z) physical damage to batteries can cause them to fail
ANSWER: W) in a lead storage battery, lead serves as the anode while silver dioxide serves as the cathode
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is INCORRECT about cloud chambers?
W) it consists of a cylindrical chamber closed by a glass window at the upper end and a movable piston at the lower end
X) tiny droplets of moisture condense and form vapor trails if the saturated air is suddenly cooled by the piston
Y) regardless of their charges, particles will travel in the same direction
Z) strong electric or magnetic fields will bend the paths of the fog tracks
ANSWER: Y) regardless of their charges, particles will travel in the same direction
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following numbers is evenly divisible by 9?
W) 72891
X) 63884
Y) 46133
Z) 55414
ANSWER: W) 72891
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is MOST accurate regarding Lagrangian points?
W) gas at the L2 and L3 points can be trapped
X) gas at the L4 and L5 points is critically stable
Y) mass transfer that is driven by stellar wind tends to occur rapidly
Z) strong stellar wind from one star that passes L1 is not captured by the other star
ANSWER: Z) strong stellar wind from one star that passes L1 is not captured by the other star
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why the earth's outer core is believed to be liquid?
W) P-waves traveling from the Earth's core disappear at the outer core because liquids have no resistance to shear
X) S-waves traveling from the Earth's core disappear at the outer core because liquids have no resistance to shear and cannot sustain a shear wave
Y) the outer core is liquid due to the high pressure on the iron-nickel alloy of which the core is composed
Z) the Mohorovicic discontinuity cannot conduct heat
ANSWER: X) S-waves traveling from the Earth's core disappear at the outer core because liquids have no resistance to shear and cannot sustain a shear wave
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What term is used to describe molecules consisting of two parts, having an ionized region at one end and a nonpolar region at the other?
ANSWER: AMPHIPATHIC
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is TRUE about buffered solutions?
W) when H+ is added to a buffered solution, it reacts essentially to completion with the weak acid that is present
X) when OH- is added to a buffered solution, it reacts essentially to completion with the weak base that is present
Y) the pH in the buffered solution is determined by the ratio of the concentrations of the weak acid and weak base
Z) buffered solutions contain small concentrations of a weak acid and its corresponding weak base
ANSWER: Y) the pH in the buffered solution is determined by the ratio of the concentrations of the weak acid and weak base
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the equivalent resistance of 3 resistors connected in parallel with R1 providing 8 ohms, R2 providing 4 ohms, and R3 providing 8 ohms?
ANSWER: 2 OHMS
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the area of a trapezoid if its two bases have lengths 5 and 10 cm, and it has a height of 4 cm?
ANSWER: 30 SQUARE CENTIMETERS
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following statements that are TRUE about the proton-proton chain. 1: In order to occur, it requires a minimum temperature of 10,000,000 K 2: In the first step, deuterium is formed while emitting a positron and a neutrino 3: The net result of the reaction is the transformation of 2 hydrogen nuclei into one helium nucleus and energy 4: The positrons that are produced combine with free electrons and convert their mass into gamma rays
ANSWER: 2 AND 4
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is LEAST accurate regarding the differential heating of land and water?
W) evaporation from water bodies is greater than that from land surfaces
X) land surfaces are opaque, so heat is absorbed at the land surface
Y) the water in water bodies does not mix with the water below, leaving the water undistributed
Z) the specific heat of water is far greater than that of land
ANSWER: Y) the water in water bodies does not mix with the water below, leaving the water undistributed
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following in INCORRECT about genetic diseases?
W) Familial Hypercholesterolemia is an autosomal recessive disease affecting 1 in every 500 people
X) Cystic Fibrosis is the most lethal genetic disease in Caucasians
Y) an individual needs to receive mutant alleles from both his parents in order to get cystic fibrosis
Z) in Down's syndrome, the fertilized cell has a translocation of chromosome 21
ANSWER: W) Familial Hypercholesterolemia is an autosomal recessive disease affecting 1 in every 500 people
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is most accurately descriptive of positron production?
W) the effect of positron production is to change a neutron into a proton
X) positron production occurs below the zone of stability for nuclides
Y) after positron production, the nuclide has a lower neutron/proton ratio than the original nuclide
Z) positron production occurs in nuclides whose neutron/proton ratios are too big
ANSWER: X) positron production occurs below the zone of stability for nuclides
21. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is INCORRECT regarding subatomic particles?
W) quarks combine in groups of two to produce mesons
X) baryons have half-integral spins
Y) leptons are influenced by the strong and weak forces
Z) the positron is an example of antimatter
ANSWER: Y) leptons are influenced by the strong and weak forces
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following forms of mass wasting that are rapid-moving as opposed to slow-moving: slump, creep, solifluction, debris flow, rockslide
ANSWER: SLUMP, DEBRIS FLOW, AND ROCKSLIDE (also accept: 1, 4, AND 5)
23. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a cellular response induced by cAMP?
W) protein synthesis and Ca2+ transport in the endoplasmic reticulum
X) active transport by the cell membrane
Y) secretion from microtubules
Z) lipid synthesis by enzymes
ANSWER: Z) lipid synthesis by enzymes

ANSWER: 524 AND 786
