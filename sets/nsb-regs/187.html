1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In orbital mechanics, what are the points called where a small object can maintain a constant position relative to two larger massive bodies?
ANSWER: LAGRANGIAN POINTS (also accept: L POINTS)
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Order the following four materials from most negative to most positive in terms of their charge affinity when they undergo triboelectric contact charging 1) Paper 2) Silver 3) Rabbit fur 4) PVC plastic.
ANSWER: 4, 2, 1, 3 (also accept: PVC PLASTIC, SILVER, PAPER, RABBIT FUR)
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the average speed of the Earth’s tectonic plates in centimeters of relative motion per year?
W) 0.1
X) 3
Y) 50
Z) 200
ANSWER: X) 3
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The ocean floor can be mapped by altimeters on satellites. Which of the following best describes how this method functions?
W) The altimeter maps the ocean surface with radar, which is affected by underlying topography
X) The altimeter maps the floor using sonar beams attached to the body of the altimeter
Y) The altimeter communicates with an underwater remotely operated vehicle, which maps the ocean floor beneath it
Z) The altimeter communicates with a manned submersible, which maps the ocean floor beneath it
ANSWER: W) The altimeter maps the ocean surface with radar, which is affected by underlying topography
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Los Alamos National Lab scientists recently observed a special type of Bose-Einstein condensate in a two-dimensional lattice. This room-temperature condensate was composed of what quasiparticles that carry vibrational energy in the lattice?
ANSWER: PHONON

What is the name of these eukaryotic DNA elements that are outside gene promoters and contain binding sites for transcription factors?
ANSWER: ENHANCERS
