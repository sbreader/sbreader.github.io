1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What consensus sequence surrounding the AUG start codon in eukaryotic mRNA promotes efficient recognition by the initiating ribosome?
ANSWER: Kozak sequence
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer UV radiation commonly has negative effects on DNA, causing thymine dimers to form. What type of repair system, which is known to repair bulky lesions, can be used to remove thymine dimers?
ANSWER: Nucleotide Excision Repair
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In the Contact Process, sulfur dioxide and oxygen establish an exothermic equilibrium over a vanadium(V) oxide catalyst. Identify all of the following three changes that increase the equilibrium amount of SO3. 1) Decreasing the temperature 2) Decreasing the volume 3) Adding SO3
ANSWER: 1, 2
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What equation relates the temperature dependence of an equilibrium constant to the standard reaction enthalpy?
ANSWER: Van't Hoff equation
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following changes generally occurs in plagioclase feldspar crystallizing from a cooling magma?
W) It becomes progressively more calcium-rich
X) It becomes progressively more sodium-rich
Y) It becomes progressively more iron-rich
Z) Its composition remains constant
ANSWER: X) It becomes progressively more sodium-rich
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 factors that generally increase a stream's competence. 1) Increasing flow velocity 2) Increasing turbulence 3) Decreasing discharge
ANSWER: 1, 2
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the derivative of 2x3 sin x?
ANSWER: 6x2 sin x + 2x3 cos x
4. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Skyler reads a 2010-page textbook. How many total times does the digit 1 appear among all page numbers from 1 through 2010?
W) 1599
X) 1600
Y) 1601
Z) 1602
ANSWER: Z) 1602
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer For a ball-surface interface, the coefficient of kinetic friction is 0.1 and the coefficient of static friction is 0.2. If a ball rolls without slipping and masses 2.5 kg, what is the magnitude of the maximum frictional force to the nearest tenth of a Newton?
ANSWER: 4.9 N
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 3-ohm resistor and a 6-ohm resistor are connected in parallel. What is their equivalent resistance, in ohms?
ANSWER: 2 ohms
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of glial cell, which possesses a star shaped formation, works to regulate blood flow and form the blood brain barrier?
ANSWER: Astrocytes
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Rank the following 4 enzymes used in glycolysis in chronological order. 1) Enolase 2) Phosphoglycerate kinase 3) Hexokinase 4) Phosphofructokinase
ANSWER: 3, 4, 2, 1
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The Ideal Gas Constant divided by the Boltzmann Constant results in what other physical constant?
ANSWER: Avogadro's Number
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The Equipartition Theorem states that every energetical mode contributes what factor of the Ideal Gas Constant R to Total Internal Energy?
ANSWER: 1/2
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice At the same temperature and pressure, which of the following best describes the density of humid air compared to dry air?
W) Greater, because water vapor increases average molecular mass
X) Greater, because humid air contains more molecules
Y) Lower, because water has a lower molecular mass than nitrogen and oxygen
Z) Lower, because water vapor contributes no pressure
ANSWER: Y) Lower, because water has a lower molecular mass than nitrogen and oxygen
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 sedimentary structures that can be used to determine paleocurrent direction. 1) Cross-bedding 2) Flute casts 3) Symmetrical ripple marks
ANSWER: 1, 2
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In triangle ABC, cevian AD meets side BC at D. Given AB, AC, AD, and BD, what theorem relates these lengths to CD and is especially useful for solving for the missing segment?
ANSWER: Stewart's theorem
9. BONUS Math Short Answer Using only quarters, nickels, and pennies, how many ways are there to make 74 cents?
ANSWER: 30
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A CD is angularly accelerating at a rate of 5 rad/s squared from rest. How much time must elapse for the disk to be rotating at 70 rad/s to the nearest second?
ANSWER: 14 s
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer As a ball with radius 0.2 m rolls downhill, it accelerates at a rate of 11 m/s squared from rest. After 12 s, what is its angular velocity to the nearest rad/s?
ANSWER: 660 rad/s
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which type of epithelial tissue would most likely be found on the lining of the esophagus?
W) Simple Squamous
X) Stratified Squamous
Y) Stratified Columnar
Z) Simple Cuboidal
ANSWER: X) Stratified Squamous
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What condition caused by deficient secretion of antidiuretic hormone leads to excessive production of dilute urine and increased thirst?
ANSWER: Central diabetes insipidus (also accept: Diabetes insipidus)

W) fluoro
X) aqua

X) 1,4 Polymerization

X) B

ANSWER: 12
