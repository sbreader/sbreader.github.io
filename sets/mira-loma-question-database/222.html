1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following four techniques that are used to determine the 3-dimensional tertiary structure of proteins I. X-ray crystallography II. Edmann degradation III. Nuclear magnetic resonance IV. 2-dimensional gel electrophoresis
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice An enveloped virus derives its envelope primarily from...
W) The proteins translated from the viral genome
X) The lipids synthesized by enzymes translated from the viral genome
Y) Lipids from the plasma membrane of the host cell
Z) Lipids from the viral envelope of the mother virus
ANSWER: Y) Lipids from the plasma membrane of the host cell
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the monomers that polymerize to form the viral capsid?
ANSWER: CAPSOMERES
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following sugars is a ketone?
W) Glyceraldehyde
X) Threose
Y) Ribose
Z) Dihydroxyacetone
ANSWER: Z) Dihydroxyacetone
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following sugars that have a β −1, 4 glycosidic linkage I. Cellulose II. Glycogen III. Lactose
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In which of the following countries is the greatest percentage of the population lactose-intolerant?
W) Finland
X) China
Y) India
Z) Australia
ANSWER: X) China
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Many drugs that lower blood cholesterol levels, known as statins, act by inhibiting this enzyme, which is crucial to the mevalonate pathway that synthesizes cholesterol and other isoprenoids. Name this enzyme.
ANSWER: HMG-COA REDUCTASE
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following functional groups would lower the pKa of an organic compound?
W) Carboxyl
X) Carbonyl
Y) Amine
Z) Ester
ANSWER: W) Carboxyl
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which type of fermentation occurs primarily in muscle cells in humans, where the acidic organic acid produced must be converted back to pyruvate in the liver as part of the Cori cycle?
ANSWER: LACTIC ACID FERMENTATION
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which key regulatory enzyme in glycolysis converts fructose-6-phosphate to fructose1,6-bisphosphate?
W) Hexokinase
X) Glucokinase
Y) Phosphoglucoisomerase
Z) Phosphofructokinase
ANSWER: Z) Phosphofructokinase
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name all of the following organic molecules that are reducing agents: NAD+, NADH, FAD, FADH2, NADPH
ANSWER: 2, 4, 5
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not true of photosystems in land plant chloroplasts?
W) They are tetramers
X) They consist predominantly of chlorophyll a molecules
Y) They have a high concentration of magnesium and calcium
Z) Photosystem II is used in cyclic electron flow
ANSWER: Z) Photosystem II is used in cyclic electron flow
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a primarily anabolic pathway?
W) Gluconeogenesis
X) Calvin cycle
Y) Glyoxylate cycle
Z) Beta-oxidation
ANSWER: Z) Beta-oxidation
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the innermost membrane system of a chloroplast?
ANSWER: THYLAKOID
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a difference between eukaryotic and prokaryotic genomes?
W) Prokaryotes generally have a smaller number of larger genes
X) Eukaryotic chromosomes have more intensive packing and a higher protein concentration
Y) Prokaryotic genomes often contain more polycistronic mRNA
Z) A larger percentage of the eukaryotic genome codes for transcription factors
ANSWER: W) Prokaryotes generally have a smaller number of larger genes
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which protein molecule is involved in regulation of gene expression through positive gene regulation, and binds to the promoter to increase the affinity for RNA polymerase to the promoter region?
ANSWER: CAP (CATABOLITE ACTIVATOR PROTEIN)
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following groups is not a lophotrochozoan?
W) Nematodes
X) Flatworms
Y) Molluscs
Z) Rotifers
ANSWER: W) Nematodes
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Arrange the following four groups in order of their proximity to angiosperms I. Charophytes II. Anthocerophytes III. Pterophytes IV. Lycophytes
ANSWER: 1, 2, 4, 3
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following hormones does not increase plant primary growth?
W) Auxin
X) Abcissic acid
Y) Giberellin
Z) Cytokinin
ANSWER: X) Abcissic acid
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer According to recent research, which amino acid in plants that is secreted by the synergids helps guide the pollen tube towards the micropyle?
ANSWER: GABA (GAMMA-AMINO BUTYRIC ACID)
22. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The movement of the phloem sap is best characterized as...
W) Active transport
X) Osmotic flow
Y) Bulk flow
Z) Solute flow
ANSWER: Y) Bulk flow
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following animals which would have a very long loop of Henle I. Jackrabbit II. Human III. Whale
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which group of eicosenoid hormones is derived from arachadonic acid and participates in delivery, inflammation, blood pressure, and other activities?
ANSWER: PROSTAGLANDINS
25. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice An infant skull contains many fontanelles, or gaps between the cranial bones, which predispose the infant to fatal head injuries. Which of the following best explains the presence of these potential time bombs in the infant skull?
W) They permit increased blood flow to the cranial bones
X) They permit expansion of the cranium in response to the developing brain
Y) They serve as early anchors for the cranial meninges
Z) They are vestigial structures that actually pose a disadvantage to the infant
ANSWER: X) They permit expansion of the cranium in response to the developing brain
