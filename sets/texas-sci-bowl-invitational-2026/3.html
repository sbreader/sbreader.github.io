Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following three parts of the respiratory system from earliest to latest to encounter outside air during inhalation 1) Alveoli 2) Trachea 3) Bronchi
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What molecule is required for myosin to detach from actin?
ANSWER: ATP
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A solution of a metal chloride produces a bright green flame in a flame test. Which metal is responsible for this color?
W) Lithium
X) Barium
Y) Calcium
Z) Strontium
ANSWER: X) Barium
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the phenomena that explains why adding HCl to aqueous lead chloride decreases the concentration of lead ions?
ANSWER: Common Ion Effect
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If four fair six-sided dice are rolled, what is the most likely value of the sum of values showing on top of the dice?
ANSWER: 14
3. BONUS Math Short Answer Warith is selecting cards from a standard 52-card deck of cards. What is the chance that, if he selects 2 cards without replacement, they share either the same suit or value?
ANSWER: 5/17
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The 1945 Nobel Prize was awarded to which quantum phsyicist for discovery of his namesake principle?
W) Werner Heisenberg
X) Wolfgang Pauli
Y) Paul Dirac
Z) Erwin Schrodinger
ANSWER: X) Wolfgang Pauli
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two long, parallel wires carrying currents of 2 amperes are placed a distance d apart. The force per unit length on the wires due to each other is 0.1 Newtons per centimeter. In centimeters, what is d?
ANSWER: 8 × 10-6
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In a marine environment, under what line does calcareous ooze no longer form?
ANSWER: Carbon Compensation Depth (also accept: CCD)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following processes that lead to the occurance of epeirogeny. 1) Glacial rebound 2) Continental collision 3) Mantle upwelling
ANSWER: 1, 3
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Loss of negative feedback by thyroid hormone on the pituitary and hypothalamus would most likely cause which of the following conditions?
W) Low TSH, low TRH
X) High TSH, low TRH
Y) High TSH, high TRH
Z) Low TSH, high TRH
ANSWER: Y) High TSH, high TRH
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer A testcross yields 18% recombinants between two loci. In centimorgans, what is their approximate map distance?
ANSWER: 18
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Chromium is a transition metal that infamously violates which chemical guideline in its ground state?
W) Aufbau Principle
X) Hund’s rule
Y) Pauli’s Exclusion Principle
Z) Proust’s Law
ANSWER: W) Aufbau Principle
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In another flame test, a 3.3 eV photon is emitted. In nanometers, which of the following would be closest to the wavelength of this photon?
W) 400
X) 500
Y) 600
Z) 700
ANSWER: W) 400
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Michael is playing Zetamac, where he can solve 68 questions in a 120-second round. If he maintains from this rate, how many questions could he solve in a 5 minute round?
ANSWER: 170
8. BONUS Math Short Answer Expressed as a fraction, what is the probability of drawing 2 queens without replacement from a 54-card deck of cards?
ANSWER: 2/477
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The following equations are plotted on a pressure versus volume graph. Which of the following equations could represent an isothermal process, where k is some constant?
W) PV = k
X) PT = kV
Y) P = kV
Z) PV/T = k
ANSWER: W) PV = k
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer An object is placed 24 centimeters away from a concave lens. An object is placed in front of the lens, and the image forms 8 centimeters from the lens on the same side as the object. What is the magnitude of the focal length of the lens in centimeters?
ANSWER: 12
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which of the following locations would there be the lowest density of population II stars?
W) Galactic bulge
X) Galactic halo
Y) Globular clusters
Z) Spiral arms
ANSWER: Z) Spiral arms
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer RR Lyrae stars pulsate due to their radiative opacity varying with their temperature. What is the name for this interaction?
ANSWER: Kappa-mechanism (also accept: Eddington Valve)
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three parts of cellular respiration that occur in the cytosol 1) Glycolysis 2) Krebs Cycle 3) Oxidative phosphorylation
ANSWER: 1 only
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What enzyme is responsible for relieving strain in the double helix ahead of the replication fork?
ANSWER: Topoisomerase
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Dichromate and chromate ions can coexist in aqueous equilibrium. What is the coefficent of the hydrogen ion in reaction of the dichromate anion and water?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 3
Z) 4
ANSWER: X) 2
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Let the Ka of acetic acid be 1.8 × 10-5, and the base-10 log of 1.8 be 0.255. A buffer is created with 0.1 M acetic acid and 0.01 M sodium acetate. To two significant digits, what is the pH of the buffer?
ANSWER: 3.7
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the product of 7 + 3i and its conjugate?
ANSWER: 58
13. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Two points are chosen at random from the circumference of a unit circle. What is the probability the chord connecting them has length exceeding √3?
W) 0
X) 1/6
Y) 1/3
Z) 1/2
ANSWER: Y) 1/3
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The current through a wire is doubled. Which of the following stay constant? 1) Magnetic field strength around the wire 2) Drift speed of electrons through the wire 3) Electric flux through a cross section of wire
ANSWER: 3 only
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 1 kilogram block slides on horizontal surface with coefficient of kinetic friction 0.2. If the block has an initial speed of 5 meters per second, and the acceleration due to gravity is 10 meters per second squared, how far in meters does the block travel before stopping?
ANSWER: 6.25 meters
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name of the process in which water-saturated soils behave as a liquid as seismic waves pass through them?
W) Subsidence
X) Isostasy
Y) Compaction
Z) Liquefaction
ANSWER: Z) Liquefaction
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which of these processes are causes of eustatic sea level rise? 1) Sediment loading of continental margins 2) Isostatic rebound of continental crust 3) Thermal expansion of seawater
ANSWER: 3 only
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What enzyme extends chromosome ends using an RNA template?
ANSWER: Telomerase
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which hormone is water-soluble and typically uses cell-surface receptors?
W) Cortisol
X) Aldosterone
Y) Thyroxine
Z) Epinephrine
ANSWER: Z) Epinephrine
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Thai has a saturated salt solution at a very high temperature. He begins cooling it down very slowly, preventing any disturbances and allowing the excess solute to remain. What term describes the solution that remains?
ANSWER: supersaturated
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Calculate the Ksp of a salt AX2 if 6 moles of the salt dissolved into 3 liters of water creates a perfectly saturated solution.
ANSWER: 32
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the product of the least common multiple and greatest common divisor of 14 and 22?
ANSWER: 308
18. BONUS Math Short Answer If 2 fair 6-sided dice are rolled, what is the probability of the sum being above 7, but NOT 12?
ANSWER: 7/18
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An ideal gas sample at 300 Kelvin and pressure 1200 pascals is compressed isothermally to half of its original volume. What is its new pressure in pascals?
ANSWER: 2400 (pascals)
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer Sunlight exerts 9 micro pascals of radiation pressure on a 20 square-meter, 9 kilgoram light sail. To the nearest half of a minute, how long will it take for the light sail to travel 2 meters?
ANSWER: 7.5
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following stages of intermediate-mass stellar evolution in increasing chronological order. 1) Red giant branch 2) Asymptotic giant branch 3) Blue loop
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Two objects, A and B, orbit the Sun with periods of 0.75 years and 3 years, respectively. How many years are there between conjunctions of A and B?
ANSWER: 1
