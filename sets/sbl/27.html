5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab are studying halide perovskites and their use in photovoltaic applications. Which of the following compounds is a halide perovskite commonly used in PV systems?
W) Magnesium bromide
X) Methylammonium lead trichloride
Y) Calcium titanite
Z) Lead (IV) fluoride
ANSWER: X) Methylammonium lead trichloride
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Idaho National Lab are studying the in situ imaging of boehmite suspended in liquid using a scanning electron microscope to understand how the particles aggregate after forming a colloid. The backscattering of electrons produced by the microscope was found to produce results in accordance with what effect that describes the scattering of light in a colloid?
ANSWER: TYNDALL EFFECT

ANSWER: PSEUDOVECTOR

ANSWER: NUÉE ARDENTE

16. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A boy is standing on the outer edge of a frictionless merry-go-round spinning clockwise at constant angular velocity omega. As viewed from above, the boy is on the eastern side of the merry-go-round. If he throws a ball radially inward, in what direction will it be deflected by the Coriolis force?
W) North
X) East
Y) South
Z) West
ANSWER: Y) South
