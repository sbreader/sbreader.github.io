1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A plant exposed to sunlight on its left side will elongate via a process called positive phototropism towards the source of light. Which plant hormone is responsible for such tropisms?
W) Cytokinin
X) Abscisic Acid
Y) Auxin
Z) Gibberellin
ANSWER: Y) Auxin
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Dicot plants are defined as having two cotyledons among a variety of other characteristics. Which of the following, if any, is classified as a Dicot plant? 1) Hibiscus 2) Orchid 3) Rose
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Assuming R = 8, what is the molar mass, in grams per mole, of a gas with a density of 1 g/cm3, a temperature of 27 C, and a pressure of 16 atm?
ANSWER: 150
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the pH of a 1.0 M solution of HCl?
ANSWER: 0
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a strong acid?
W) HNO3
X) H2SO4
Y) HNO2
Z) HI
ANSWER: Y) HNO2
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is not true about momentum?
W) Momentum is measured in Newtons per second
X) Momentum is a vector
Y) Momentum is conserved in both elastic and inelastic collisions
Z) Momentum is equal to mass times velocity
ANSWER: W) Momentum is measured in Newtons per second
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following has the greatest momentum?
W) A 1000kg elephant at rest
X) A 40kg car traveling at 10 m/s
Y) A 15kg scooter traveling at 15 m/s
Z) A 1kg cat traveling at 50m/s
ANSWER: X) A 40kg car traveling at 10 m/s
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer To the nearest whole number, what is the length of the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs of length 24 and 8?
ANSWER: 25
4. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is a pythagorean triple?
W) 5, 10, 11
X) 6, 9, 12
Y) 5, 12, 14
Z) 8, 15, 17
ANSWER: Z) 8, 15, 17
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which period in the Paleozoic Era is often referred to as the Age of Fish?
ANSWER: DEVONIAN
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The last great Snowball Earth occurred mostly in which period, which is named after this event?
ANSWER: CRYOGENIAN
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a path function?
W) Work
X) Heat Capacity
Y) Enthalpy
Z) Heat
ANSWER: Y) Enthalpy
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What two quantities are constant when calculating Gibbs’ free energy?
ANSWER: Pressure and temperature
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which of the five main lobes of the brain contains the auditory cortex and is thus responsible for gathering and compiling data from the auditory nerve?
ANSWER: TEMPORAL (also accept: TEMPORAL LOBE)
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The Sodium Potassium pump regulates the cell potential of neurons and is located in the cell membrane. The pump transports three Sodium molecules out of the cell for every two potassium molecules transported in. What type of transport is the Sodium Potassium Pump engaged in?
W) Active Transport
X) Diffusion
Y) Facilitated Diffusion
Z) Osmosis
ANSWER: W) Active Transport
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the longest bond?
W) Carbon Carbon Single Bond
X) Carbon Carbon Double Bond
Y) Carbon Carbon Triple Bond
Z) Carbon Nitrogen Triple Bond
ANSWER: W) Carbon Carbon Single Bond
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What law of optics states that the incident index multiplied by the sine of the incident angle is equal to the refracted index multiplied by the sine of the refracted angle?
ANSWER: SNELL’S LAW
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the term for materials that have the same optic properties in all directions?
ANSWER: ISOTROPIC
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Factor the following expression. A2-6AB + 9B2
ANSWER: (A-3B)2
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What feature is formed when a glacier flows over an already existing moraine?
ANSWER: DRUMLIN
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer When a tributary glacier meets the main glacier, and the two glaciers eventually recede, what feature is formed, in which a waterfall may also occur?
ANSWER: HANGING VALLEY
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What law states that the entropy of an isolated system always increases?
ANSWER: Second Law of Thermodynamics
12. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the zeroth law of thermodynamics?
W) Energy cannot be created or destroyed; it can only be changed from one form to another.
X) If two thermodynamic systems are each in thermal equilibrium with a third system, then they are all in equilibrium with each other.
Y) When the temperature of a perfect crystal is 0, then its entropy will also be 0.
Z) In a closed system, the change in energy of the system is always 0.
ANSWER: X) If two thermodynamic systems are each in thermal equilibrium with a third system, then they are all in equilibrium with each other.

19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Fish living in both saltwater and freshwater experience major osmotic issues with maintaining water balance from being either hypertonic or hypotonic to their environment.
