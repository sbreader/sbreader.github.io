1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which type of ground tissue has a primary cell wall that is unevenly thickened and provides flexible mechanical support?
W) Parenchyma
X) Collenchyma
Y) Sclerenchyma
Z) Procambium
ANSWER: X) Collenchyma
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What type of cell junction functions as a rivet, linking epithelial cells into sheets?
W) Gap junction
X) Tight junction
Y) Desmosome
Z) Adherens junction
ANSWER: Y) Desmosome
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many sigma bonds are contained in the nitrate ion?
ANSWER: 3
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What intermolecular force explains why octane has a much higher boiling point than methane?
ANSWER: LONDON DISPERSION (also accept: VAN DER WAALS)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the large depression formed by a volcano’s magma chamber collapsing post-eruption?
ANSWER: CALDERA
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer How many atmospheric cells are located in each hemisphere?
ANSWER: 3
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the area of the region bounded by the lines y equals 0, x equals 0, x equals 4, and y equals x plus 2?
ANSWER: 16
4. BONUS Math Multiple Choice What is the sum of the distinct prime factors of 2022?
W) 1013
X) 4056
Y) 3036
Z) 342
ANSWER: Z) 342
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the temperature of the cosmic microwave background, in Kelvin?
W) 0.03
X) 3
Y) 300
Z) 3,000,000
ANSWER: X) 3
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer The period of a perfect pendulum is 2 seconds. If the mass of the pendulum is doubled, its length halved, and it is moved to a planet with gravitational acceleration twice that of Earth, what is the new period, in seconds?
ANSWER: 1
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Davidson HS B Team members are studying the forces acting on moving and static objects. A box is sliding down a ramp at an increasing speed. By name or number, order the following three forces on the box by increasing magnitude 1) Gravity 2) Friction 3) Normal force
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1 (also accept: FRICTION, NORMAL FORCE, GRAVITY)
6. BONUS Math Short Answer Davidson MS B Team members are studying non-Newtonian fluids like silly putty and flubber. A mysterious bouncy ball has the strange property of bouncing 50% higher on each successive bounce. If the ball is dropped from a height of 10 meters, how much total distance, in meters, will it have covered just before its 4th bounce?
ANSWER: 152.5
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In what type of mimicry do two unpalatable species resemble each other?
ANSWER: MULLERIAN
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer In a population of twenty-five phoenixes, one individual is blue. Assuming that the population is in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium and that the allele for red feather color is dominant over the allele for blue feather color, how many individuals are heterozygous?
ANSWER: 8
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A neutron-rich isotope such as tritium would most likely decay by which of the following mechanisms?
W) Beta minus decay
X) Beta plus decay
Y) Alpha decay
Z) Electron capture
ANSWER: W) Beta minus decay
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What law states that the net enthalpy change of a reaction is independent of the number of steps the reaction occurs in?
ANSWER: HESS’S LAW
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three minerals from hardest to softest on the Mohs scale 1) Apatite 2) Gypsum 3) Orthoclase
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2 (also accept: ORTHOCLASE, APATITE, GYPSUM)
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following space telescopes observes in the visible part of the electromagnetic spectrum and was launched to help discover earth-sized exoplanets?
W) Spitzer
X) COBE
Y) Compton
Z) Kepler
ANSWER: Z) Kepler
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Sam has 3 red balls and 5 green balls. If he chooses two balls at random without replacement, what is the probability they are the same color?
ANSWER: 13/28
10. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Given that a perfect number is any number n whose factors sum to 2 times n, which of the following numbers is perfect?
W) 18
X) 24
Y) 28
Z) 36
ANSWER: Y) 28
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the probability that a particle in an infinite square well will be found outside of the well?
ANSWER: 0
11. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice How will the temperature of the cosmic microwave background radiation change in the future?
W) It will be hotter
X) It will be colder
Y) It will stay constant
Z) Some parts will get hotter while others will get colder
ANSWER: X) It will be colder
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Davidson HS B Team members are studying the Harvard Spectral Classification. Order the following four spectral classes from coldest to hottest 1) A 2) K 3) B 4) F
ANSWER: 2, 4, 1, 3 (also accept: K, F, A, B)
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Davidson MS A team members are studying weak acids. If the Ka of acetic acid is 2 × 10-5, what is its Kb in scientific notation?
ANSWER: 5 × 10-10
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which chamber of the heart is the pulmonary vein connected to?
ANSWER: LEFT ATRIUM
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which protein blocks the myosin binding sites on actin?
W) Troponin
X) Alpha-actinin
Y) Tropomyosin
Z) Desmin
ANSWER: Y) Tropomyosin
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What reaction order has a half-life independent of concentration?
ANSWER: FIRST (also accept: ONE)
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Consider the decomposition of ozone into oxygen gas. If oxygen gas is evolved at a rate of 6.0 × 10-5 molar per second, to two significant figures and in molar per second, at what rate does ozone disappear?
ANSWER: 4.0 × 10-5 (also accept: -4.0 × 10-5)
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In stars, what is the last element produced before nuclear fusion requires more energy than it produces?
ANSWER: IRON
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three layers of the sun from innermost to outermost 1) Convective zone 2) Photosphere 3) Radiative zone
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2 (also accept: RADIATIVE ZONE, CONVECTIVE ZONE, PHOTOSPHERE)
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer On each of the three days from Monday to Wednesday, the probability of rain is 40%. Given that the occurrence of rain on any two days is independent, what is the probability that there is no rain on any of the days? Express your answer as a fraction.
ANSWER: 27/125
16. BONUS Math Short Answer Two circles of radius 3 are externally tangent to one another and internally tangent to another, larger, circle. Giving your answer in terms of pi, find the smallest possible area of the region inside the larger circle and outside the two smaller circles.
ANSWER: 18π (also accept: 18 PI)
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following necessarily implies that angular momentum is conserved?
W) There is no angular momentum in the system
X) Linear momentum is conserved
Y) The net torque is zero
Z) All forces acting on the system are conservative
ANSWER: Y) The net torque is zero
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer Schrodinger’s equation describes the evolution over time of what mathematical description of a quantum state?
ANSWER: WAVEFUNCTION
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Davidson HS A Team members are currently studying freezing fog. Freezing fog is fog formed at temperatures below freezing and can lead to icy roads as the supercooled droplets in the fog freeze on the road surface. What type of precipitation behaves most similarly to freezing fog?
ANSWER: FREEZING RAIN
18. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Davidson MS A team members are studying the immune system. Which of the following best describes humoral immunity?
W) Cytokines recruiting neutrophils to the site of infection
X) Antibody release by plasma cells
Y) Killing of infected cells by cytotoxic T cells
Z) Release of interferon by virus-infected cells
ANSWER: X) Antibody release by plasma cells
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the net FADH2 yield per molecule of glucose in the Krebs cycle?
ANSWER: 2
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer One method of karyotyping involves adding colchicine to cells, a drug that causes microtubules to dissociate into individual tubulin units. What phase of mitosis would this arrest cells in?
ANSWER: METAPHASE
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the titration between chloric acid and ammonia?

X) The equivalence point is at pH 7
Y) The equivalence point is at a pH greater than 7
Z) The equivalence point depends on the indicator used

ANSWER: 6
