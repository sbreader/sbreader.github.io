1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the phase of meiosis II during which the chromosomes are positioned on the metaphase plate and are attached to the microtubules extending from the centrioles.
ANSWER: METAPHASE II
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which of the following four statements is or are TRUE regarding meiosis and mitosis? 1: In mitosis, synapsis of homologous chromosomes does not occur. 2: The number of daughter cells in mitosis equals the number of daughter cells of meiosis I. 3: Meiosis is responsible for asexual and sexual reproduction. 4: The daughter cells of mitosis have 2n chromosomes each, and the daughter cells of meiosis II have n chromosomes each.
ANSWER: 1, 2, and 4 (also accept: All but 3)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about ammonia is FALSE?
W) Liquid ammonia undergoes much less autoprotolysis than water.
X) Ammonia is very soluble in water due to NH3 forming hydrogen bonds with H2O.
Y) Ammonium salts decompose when heated.
Z) Ammonia is a strong Bronsted base in water.
ANSWER: Z) Ammonia is a strong Bronsted base in water.
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which of the following three statements regarding the Ostwald process is or are TRUE? 1: The Ostwald process results in the production of HNO3. 2: In the first step, ammonia is oxidized and nitrogen's oxidation number increases. 3: In the second step, nitrogen's oxidation number increases.
ANSWER: 1 and 2 (also accept: All but 3)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding circuits is TRUE?
W) Kirchoff's voltage law states that for every loop in a circuit, the sum of the voltages applied is 0.
X) Kirchoff's current law states that for every loop in a circuit, the sum of the currents flowing is 0.
Y) Resistances are additive when connected in a combination circuit.
Z) The energy stored in a capacitor is defined as ½ (Q2 / V).
ANSWER: W) Kirchoff's voltage law states that for every loop in a circuit, the sum of the voltages applied is 0.
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Roger has a 5 microfarad capacitor. Find the energy stored in this capacitor if the voltage applied to it is 5 Volts.
ANSWER: 62.5 μJ
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the domain and range of the function defined by g(x) = 1/(x+1) + 2?
ANSWER: Domain: all reals but -1; range: all reals but 2
4. BONUS Math Short Answer The graph f(x) = 3x2 on the closed interval [0,2] is split at the line x=k such that the areas on both sides of the line are equal. Find k.
ANSWER: ∛4 (also accept: CUBE ROOT OF 4)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Denote by number which of the following four statements regarding stratovolcanoes is or are TRUE. 1: Stratovolcanoes are conical volcanoes made of hardened lava, tephra, and pumice. 2: Stratovolcanoes are characterized by highly explosive eruptions. 3: Stratovolcanoes produce highly mafic magma. 4: Stratovolcanoes are among the most common types of volcanoes.
ANSWER: 1, 2, and 4
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding muscle tissues is TRUE?
W) Cardiac muscle is striated and is responsible for voluntary movements.
X) Smooth muscle is not striated and is responsible for involuntary movements.
Y) Skeletal muscle is striated and is responsible for involuntary movements.
Z) Skeletal muscle is found in the esophagus, stomach, intestines, and bronchi.
ANSWER: X) Smooth muscle is not striated and is responsible for involuntary movements.
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer State whether each given animal is a temperature conformer or regulator: river otter, largemouth bass, rotifer, sea turtle, termite.
ANSWER: REGULATOR, CONFORMER, CONFORMER, CONFORMER, CONFORMER
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Find the pH of a solution made from .02 M HC2H3O2 and .20 M C2H3O2- that has an acid dissociation constant for HC2H3O2 of 1 × 10-5.
W) 4.0
X) 5.0
Y) 6.0
Z) 7.0
ANSWER: Y) 6.0
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Denote by number which of the following four statements is or are TRUE regarding Bronsted-Lowry theory. 1: A weak acid will be completely dissociated, whereas a strong acid will be partially dissociated. 2: All Lewis acids are Bronsted-Lowry acids, but not all Bronsted-Lowry acids are Lewis acids. 3: The Bronsted-Lowry concept applies to aqueous solutions only. 4: In the reaction H2O + NH3 ↔ OH- + NH4+, the ammonium ion is the conjugate acid of ammonia.
ANSWER: 4 only
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Indicate by number which of the following three statements is or are true regarding the quadratic regression model C = -.93F + 85.65F2. 1: C is surjective. 2: C is injective. 3: d2C/(dF)2 = -.93
ANSWER: 2 only
8. BONUS Math Short Answer Which of the following is or are TRUE regarding functions? 1: The derivative of an even function is always even. 2: In Boolean algebra, 0 AND 1 is 0. 3: All trigonometric functions are transcendental functions. 4: li(x) is defined as the integral from 0 to x of dt/ln(t).
ANSWER: 2, 3, and 4 (also accept: All but 1)
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Preethi is swinging her brother on a 5 meter long rope. The magnitude of the force being exerted on Karthik is 20 newtons. Find Karthik's torque when the angle between the force vector and Preethi's lever arm vector is 90 degrees.
ANSWER: 100 NEWTON METERS
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice On the Hertzsprung-Russell Diagram, which of the following is the most accurate location of the instability strip?
W) Lower-left
X) Lower-right
Y) Upper-middle
Z) Lower-middle
ANSWER: Y) Upper-middle
