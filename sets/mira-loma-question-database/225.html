Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Complete the analogy: two neurons:synapse::one neuron and one muscle cell
ANSWER: MOTOR END PLATE (also accept: NEUROMUSCULAR JUNCTION)
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Research on what enzyme's chromosome-lengthening and anti-aging properties won the Nobel Prize in Physiology and Medicine in 2009?
ANSWER: TELOMERASE
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the dew point when the dry-bulb temperature is 24 degrees C and the wet-bulb temperature is 15 degrees C?
W) 4 degrees C
X) 8 degrees C
Y) 18 degrees C
Z) 36 degrees C
ANSWER: X) 8 degrees C
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Also known as 2,4-dichlorophenoxacetic acid, what toxic substance was used in the Vietnam War to clear foliage from the dense forests of Vietnam and Cambodia?
ANSWER: AGENT ORANGE
6. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A class of data in a histogram ranges from 0 to 10. In this case, the number 5 is known as the
W) class width
X) class mark
Y) class center
Z) class divider
ANSWER: X) class mark
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What immunoglobulin class is most prevalent in the secondary immune response?
ANSWER: IgG
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What chemist, who worked at Caltech, sequenced hemoglobin, established the theory of hybridization, and developed modern organic chemistry?
ANSWER: LINUS PAULING
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer During what era did the initial opening of the Atlantic Ocean most likely occur?
ANSWER: MESOZOIC
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which of the following amino acids is hydrophobic: methionine, glutamine, cysteine, isoleucine, and alanine?
ANSWER: METHIONINE, ISOLEUCINE, ALANINE
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Who proposed the plum-pudding model of the atom?
W) J. J. Thomson
X) Rutherford
Y) Yanagisawa
Z) Bohr
ANSWER: W) J. J. Thomson
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the derivative of f(x) = x4+ 2x2+ 8x+ 9?
ANSWER: 4x3+ 4x+8
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Where must an observer stand on Earth in order to see the entire sky over the course of a year?
W) the north pole
X) the south pole
Y) the equator
Z) anywhere
ANSWER: Y) the equator
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Blood can only clot in the presence of what ion?
ANSWER: Ca2+
16. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What American psychologist and education philosopher, working at Harvard at the turn of the 20th century, developed the theory of cognitivism?
ANSWER: WILLIAM JAMES
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice California's San Andreas fault is an example of a?
W) tear fault
X) upthrust fault
Y) dip-slip fault
Z) Strike-slip fault
ANSWER: Z) Strike-slip fault
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What type of dihydroxlyation was developed in 1976 and converts an alkene to a cis vicinal diol?
ANSWER: UPJOHN DIHYDROXYLATION
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Discovered in 1845, what effect is the first experimental evidence that light and magnetism are interrelated?
ANSWER: FARADAY EFFECT
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Escape velocity depends on what two characteristics of the celestial body?
ANSWER: MASS AND RADIUS
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Babies cannot walk because their axons have not been ______, resulting a weak transmission of electrical impulses that precludes bipedal locomotion?
ANSWER: MYELINATED
23. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What American psychologist applied his theory of behaviorism to teach birds to play piano and published a book known as Walden II in the Sixties?
ANSWER: B. F. SKINNER
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In what period did Archaeopteryx live in?
ANSWER: JURASSIC PERIOD
25. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the most common enzyme on Earth?
ANSWER: RUBISCO
