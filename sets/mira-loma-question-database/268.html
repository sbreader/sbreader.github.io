1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Pahoehoe can best be described as what type of lava?
W) rocky, hard, and hot
X) pillowy and viscous
Y) smooth, hummocky, and ropy
Z) pahoehoe is magma
ANSWER: Y) smooth, hummocky, and ropy
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the type of lava that is known to be loose, broken, rocky, and sharp?
ANSWER: 'A'A
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the primary mineral that is found in obsidian?
W) Quartz
X) Feldspar
Y) Basalt
Z) Pumice
ANSWER: W) Quartz
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Prasiolite is a form of quartz that is commonly known for being what color?
ANSWER: GREEN
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer This geologic period began with a major extinction event, and is the second period of the Paleozoic Era. It ended with another mass extinction that wiped out 60% of all marine life.
ANSWER: ORDOVICIAN (PERIOD)
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The highest volcano in Asia, located in Iran, is known as
W) Elbrus
X) Damavand
Y) Kilimanjaro
Z) Mauna Kea
ANSWER: X) Damavand
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the type of conical volcano that is made up of many layers and is known for its occasional explosive eruptions?
ANSWER: STRATOVOLCANO
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice When measuring atmospheric composition, one would most commonly use what unit?
W) Parts per trillion
X) Parts per trillion by volume
Y) Parts per million
Z) Parts per million by volume
ANSWER: Z) Parts per million by volume
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name the top three types of gases that occur in the Earth's atmosphere.
ANSWER: NITROGEN (N2), OXYGEN (O2), AND ARGON (AR)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the term for the property of chemical elements to have two or more different forms?
W) Polyelementism
X) Fullerism
Y) Allotropy
Z) Parelementism
ANSWER: Y) Allotropy
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following are NOT properties of oxygen? 1: It has a density of 1.429 grams per liter 2: It has four stable isotopes 3: Appears light blue when in large quantities
ANSWER: 2 and 3
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which one of the following months is NOT in the hurricane season in the Northwest Pacific?
W) March
X) April
Y) May
Z) June
ANSWER: W) March
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A tropical storm is classified as having a lower limit of how many knots?
ANSWER: 34 KNOTS (also accept: 35 KNOTS)
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What percentage of the Earth's atmosphere's mass does the Troposphere take up?
W) Approximately 90%
X) Approximately 75%
Y) Approximately 50%
Z) Approximately 35%
ANSWER: X) Approximately 75%
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which two of the following five do not occur in the top five most common elements in the Earth's mantle? 1: Calcium 2: Oxygen 3: Aluminum 4: Sodium 5: Magnesium
ANSWER: 3 and 4
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The largest earthquake ever recorded in the 20th century was in Chile. What was its approximate magnitude on the Richter scale?
W) 10.4
X) 9.9
Y) 9.5
Z) 8.8
ANSWER: Y) 9.5
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The intensity and apparent effects of an earthquake is quantified on what scale?
ANSWER: (MODIFIED) MERCALLI (INTENSITY) SCALE
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following would always be immiscible with water?
W) Salt
X) Acetic Acid
Y) Ethyl Alcohol
Z) Gasoline
ANSWER: Z) Gasoline
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Immiscible metals are completely unable to form what type of mixture?
ANSWER: ALLOYS
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice When talking about faults, what is slip defined as?
W) Measurement of the distance between two formerly adjacent points on different plates
X) Measurement of the length of the footwall
Y) Measurement of the length of the hanging wall
Z) Measurement of the force between two plates
ANSWER: W) Measurement of the distance between two formerly adjacent points on different plates
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name two of the four tectonic plates that make up the African plate.
ANSWER: MADAGASCAR PLATE, NUBIAN PLATE, SEYCHELLES PLATE, OR SOMALI PLATE
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A crevasse often occurs in _____, whereas a crevice primarily occurs in ______.
W) ice, a volcano
X) snow, rock
Y) ice sheets, mountains
Z) glaciers, rock
ANSWER: Z) glaciers, rock
24. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the main force shown when a cirque is created?
ANSWER: EROSION
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is a common commercial use for sulfites?
W) Yeast prevention
X) Dried fruit preservatives
Y) Cleansing agents
Z) Polishing agents
ANSWER: X) Dried fruit preservatives
26. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The suffix -ite is most commonly used in chemistry and geology to talk about what kind of compounds?
ANSWER: SALTS
27. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Infiltration is best described as what process of the water cycle?
W) The sum of evaporation and transpiration
X) Evaporates from the underground
Y) Running down mountains into valleys
Z) Going into the ground
ANSWER: Z) Going into the ground
28. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following would help or change the process and amount of infiltration? 1: Difference in precipitations 2: Speed of condensation 3: Slope of the land 4: Different soil characteristics
ANSWER: 1, 3, and 4
29. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which one of the following pHs of different rain samples would be considered acid rain?
W) 5.7
X) 9.9
Y) 5.6
Z) 5.8
ANSWER: Y) 5.6
30. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which two of the following four would not be a direct result of a strong acid rain? 1: Railway tracks weakened or disintegrating 2: Coal stops burning 3: Minerals brought to the surface of the ground 4: Buildings and structures weakened
ANSWER: 2 and 3
31. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice On a topographical map, how would you tell how a stream or a river is flowing?
W) By the surrounding deltas or lakes
X) The surrounding contour lines' directions; the "V" shape points upstream
Y) The surrounding contour lines' directions; the "V" shape points downstream
Z) The geological declination
ANSWER: X) The surrounding contour lines' directions; the "V" shape points upstream
32. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the force that drives streamflow and allows it to continue moving?
ANSWER: GRAVITY
33. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following would best describe the two bodies of water that surround an estuary?
W) a river and a lake
X) a river delta and an ocean
Y) a river and a sea
Z) a strait and a river
ANSWER: Y) a river and a sea
34. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the official name for an underwater mountain that does not reach the surface of the water?
ANSWER: A SEAMOUNT
35. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name of the protist that causes a phenomenon called "red tide"?
W) Phytoplankton
X) Ciliates
Y) Halvaria
Z) Slime mold
ANSWER: W) Phytoplankton
37. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Of the following assorted terms, which would be at ground level (and only at ground level)?
W) Epicenter of an earthquake
X) Hypocenter of an earthquake
Y) Mid-ocean ridges
Z) Buttes
ANSWER: W) Epicenter of an earthquake
39. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice About what percentage of tsunamis occur in the Pacific Ocean?
W) 20%
X) 45%
Y) 60%
Z) 80%
ANSWER: Z) 80%
40. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which three of the following are possible causes of a tsunami? 1: Large amounts of ocean sediment 2: Hurricanes 3: Large Earthquakes 4: Meteor Impacts
ANSWER: 1, 3, and 4 (also accept: All but 2)
41. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not one of the four classifications of thunderstorms?
W) Supercell
X) Squall Line
Y) Cyclone
Z) Single cell
ANSWER: Y) Cyclone
42. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of a widespread, long-lived straight-line windstorm oftentimes associated with squall lines?
ANSWER: DERECHO
43. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a real type of landslide?
W) Submarine landslide
X) Topple
Y) Lateral spread
Z) Transition landslide
ANSWER: Z) Transition landslide
44. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The term, "lahar," is most closely associated with which kind of landslide?
ANSWER: DEBRIS FLOW (also accept: DEBRIS (LAND)SLIDE)
45. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice How many major ocean gyres are there?
W) Too many to count
X) Number changes too often to estimate
Y) 5
Z) 20
ANSWER: Y) 5
46. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following are possible influences of an ocean current's direction and strength? 1: Salinity differences 2: Breaking waves 3: Coriolis force 4: Shoreline configurations
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3, and 4
47. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not another name for an underwater sinkhole?
W) Submarine Cave
X) Blue hole
Y) Vertical cave
Z) Sea Cavern
ANSWER: Z) Sea Cavern
48. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for a cavernous rock that hangs off the ceiling?
ANSWER: STALACTITE

W) 0 or 180 degrees
X) 45 or 225 degrees
