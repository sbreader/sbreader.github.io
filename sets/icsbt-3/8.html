1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What specific plastid found in plants has no color and synthesizes and stores lipids and fats?
ANSWER: Elaioplast (do not accept: Leucoplast)
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true about small regulatory RNAs. 1) MicroRNA precursors are processed by Drosha in the nucleus 2) siRNAs can arise from double-stranded RNA and guide RISC to complementary targets 3) shRNAs can be expressed from introduced DNA vectors
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3 (also accept: All)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer At the Boyle temperature of a real gas, attractive and repulsive nonideal effects cancel to first order in density. What virial coefficient is therefore zero?
ANSWER: Second virial coefficient
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Reagents that have the ability to add carbon-carbon bonds are some of the most important in organic chemistry due to their usefulness in synthesizing larger compounds. What is the name for the propane ring forming reaction, which uses a carbenoid radical to attack a double bond?
ANSWER: Simmons-Smith reaction

W) Northerly
X) Southerly
Y) Easterly
Z) Westerly
ANSWER: Z) Westerly

8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Two planets orbit the same star with identical semimajor axes but different eccentricities. Which of the following correctly compares their orbital periods and specific orbital energies?

Y) Their periods differ, but their specific orbital energies are equal
Z) Both are different

10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Swedish researchers at the Chalmers University of Technology used a superconducting circuit with a SQUID modulated at gigahertz frequencies to simulate a perfect mirror moving at 5% the speed of light. 17. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In the calibration of a pH meter, a Sodium phosphate buffer is used. Using simple calculations for the pH returns a value of around 0.5 higher than the actual recorded value. This is because:
