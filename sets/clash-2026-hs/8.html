1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A hydraulic press is formed such that the radii of the 2 ends of a tube are 4 and 8 meters, respectively. If a force of 100 newtons is applied to the side with radius 4 meters, in newtons, what is the force that will come out of the side with radius 8 meters?
