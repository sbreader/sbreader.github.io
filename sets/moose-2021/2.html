1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the names for the 3 domains in the system of biological classification?
ANSWER: ARCHAEA, EUKARYA, AND BACTERIA (also accept: ARCHAEA, EUKARYOTA, AND BACTERIA)
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice If two animals look similar but produce infertile offspring, they are definitely not the same
W) Genus
X) Species
Y) Class
Z) Phylum
ANSWER: X) Species
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What name is usually given to the temperature at which the particles in a substance have zero kinetic energy?
ANSWER: ABSOLUTE ZERO
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The first black hole merger detected was between black holes of 29 and 36 solar masses. These two collided to form a black hole of 52 solar masses. The remaining 3 solar masses were radiated away as what?
ANSWER: GRAVITATIONAL WAVES (also accept: GRAVITATIONAL RADIATION; GRAVITATIONAL ENERGY)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the aromatic hydrocarbon with a ring structure and 3 double bonds, with a formula of C6H6?
ANSWER: BENZENE
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab are studying how molecules interact in extreme conditions. At temperatures close to absolute zero, gases interact differently and create what exotic state of matter?
ANSWER: BOSE-EINSTEIN CONDENSATE
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the product of 278 and 464?
W) 109654
X) 113778
Y) 118776
Z) 128992
ANSWER: Z) 128992
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following forces is the reason for why hydrocarbons with longer carbon chains have stronger intermolecular forces?
W) Hydrogen Bonding
X) London Dispersion Forces
Y) Dipole-Dipole forces
Z) Dipole-Induced Dipole Forces
ANSWER: X) London Dispersion Forces
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Unsorted, unstratified sediment that does not match the surrounding bedrock is a defining characteristic of what depositional environment?
ANSWER: GLACIAL (also accept: GLACIER)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A geologist finds an outcrop of rock near a coastline. The lowest layer she sees is composed of limestone and coquina, which is overlain by shale and cross-bedded sandstone. Which of the following events does this sequence likely show?
W) Marine transgression
X) Marine regression
Y) Aquifer drainage
Z) Trade wind reversal
ANSWER: X) Marine regression
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for noncoding regions of pre-mRNA that are spliced out before translation?
ANSWER: INTRONS
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following four particles responsible for mediating the weak force 1) W+ Boson 2) W- Boson 3) Z Boson 4) Higgs Boson
ANSWER: 1 and 2 and 3
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A car is traveling at a constant speed. Which of the following must be true?
W) The forces on the car must be balanced, since the car is not accelerating or decelerating
X) The forces on the car must be unbalanced, since the car is moving, so a force has to acting on the car
Y) The forces on the car could be balanced or unbalanced, since the velocity is not known
Z) There is no force on the car, since the car's speed is constant
ANSWER: Y) The forces on the car could be balanced or unbalanced, since the velocity is not known
7. BONUS Energy Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory are studying ways to create more efficient batteries. One way the researchers are doing this is by changing a normally irreversible reaction that uses lithium at the anode, replacing what substance usually used as an anode in lithium-ion batteries?
ANSWER: GRAPHITE (also accept: CARBON)
8. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Department of Energy scientists are researching improvements to batteries in consumer electronics and in the nation's electricity grid. While the electricity grid rarely uses batteries, consumer products such as electric cars, computers, and phones all use what type of rechargeable battery?
ANSWER: LITHIUM-ION
8. BONUS Math Short Answer You insert a joker into a standard deck of 52 cards. It is possible that you put the joker at the top, the bottom, or anywhere in between. What is the probability that the joker is in the bottom half of the deck?
ANSWER: 26/53
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In a standard deck of 52 cards, what is the probability that the top card is either black or a jack?
ANSWER: 7/13
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer A substrate binds to an enzyme at what site?
ANSWER: ACTIVE SITE
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What type of catalytic molecule is pepsin?
W) Starch
X) Enzyme
Y) Nucleotide
Z) Steroid
ANSWER: X) Enzyme
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer In Joules, what is the gravitational potential energy of a box of mass 50 kilograms that is 10 meters off the ground, assuming g = 9.8 m/s2?
ANSWER: 4900
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice When Sir Goat pushes a block up a ramp, he finds that more work was needed than calculated due to the friction between the block and the ramp. This is because friction is considered to be
W) A non-conservative force
X) A conservative force
Y) An extraneous force
Z) A fictitious force
ANSWER: W) A non-conservative force
11. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the equation of a parabola in vertex form that passes through the points (1, 2) and (9, 2), with a vertex at (5, 3)?
ANSWER: -1/16(x-5)2+3

W) 5
X) 5.5

ANSWER: Y) 6
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers are studying how vitamin E acetate, a chemical found in vaping oils, affects the lungs when directly inhaled. Specifically, this chemical damages the pulmonary surfactant, a membrane surrounding what small air sacs that allow gaseous exchange in the lungs?
ANSWER: ALVEOLI
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying the effects of e-cigarettes on humans. Vapes and e-cigarettes are addictive because they contain nicotine, a chemical that releases what neurotransmitter often associated with reward?
ANSWER: DOPAMINE

ANSWER: 168

ANSWER: 22
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Give the final numbers of protons and neutrons of an atom of Bismuth-209, which is element number 83, after the atom undergoes alpha decay.
ANSWER: 81 and 124 (also accept: 124 and 81)
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In a chemical reaction, what is the term for what happens when a substance gains electrons, and what happens to the oxidation state, respectively?
W) Oxidation, decrease in oxidation state
X) Oxidation, increase in oxidation state
Y) Reduction, decrease in oxidation state
Z) Reduction, increase in oxidation state
ANSWER: Y) Reduction, decrease in oxidation state
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying coastal erosion and vulnerability. By name or number, identify all of the following three coastal features that are associated with erosion 1) Tombolo 2) Sea arch 3) Barrier island
ANSWER: 2 only
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying the conditions that affect the formation of wildfires. In dry conditions, most wildfires are started by what common weather phenomenon?
ANSWER: LIGHTNING
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Huntington's disease is an autosomal dominant trait. If Joe's dad is heterozygous for Huntington's disease and Joe's mom does not have Huntington's disease, what is the probability that Joe's sister has Huntington's disease?
ANSWER: 1/2 (also accept: 50%; 0.5)
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The AB blood type is an example of which of the following types of genetic inheritance?
W) Incomplete dominance
X) Epistasis
Y) Codominance
Z) Complete dominance
ANSWER: Y) Codominance
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What specific type of stars are the hottest of all stars, reaching temperatures as high as 400,000 Kelvin and producing highly pronounced carbon or nitrogen spectral lines?
ANSWER: WOLF-RAYET STARS
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The gut lining comes from which embryonic germ layer?
ANSWER: ENDODERM
19. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of projectile motion on a level plane with no air resistance?
W) A projectile's initial and final velocity will have a similar angle with respect to the vertical
X) The velocity of a projectile at the top of its path is zero
Y) The horizontal displacement of a projectile is proportional to its initial velocity squared
Z) The horizontal displacement of a projectile is proportional to the inverse of the acceleration due to gravity
ANSWER: X) The velocity of a projectile at the top of its path is zero
