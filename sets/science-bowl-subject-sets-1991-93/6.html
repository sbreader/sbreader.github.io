Packet 6 (Biology 1993) of Science Bowl Subject Sets 1991–93:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In plant studies, many species of angiosperms lack secondary growth and therefore do not become woody. They are generally called by which of the following?
W) trees
X) bines
Y) herbs
Z) none of the above
ANSWER: Y) herbs
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What TWO primary chemical compounds are used in photosynthesis?
ANSWER: WATER AND CARBON DIOXIDE (also accept: H2O AND CO2)
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The ovary of a flowering plant can develop into a:
W) spore
X) fruit
Y) cone
Z) seed
ANSWER: X) fruit
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The part of the flower that is adapted to catch pollen is the:
W) stigma
X) style
Y) anther
Z) ovule
ANSWER: W) stigma
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Fungi depend on other organisms for:
W) water
X) food
Y) carbon dioxide
Z) hydrogen
ANSWER: X) food
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the generic name for a biocide effective against certain plants?
ANSWER: HERBICIDE
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Neuron is the name given to which special type of biological cell?
ANSWER: NERVE CELL
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the highly insoluble (polysaccharide) carbohydrate which is a major supporting material in plants?
ANSWER: CELLULOSE
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The letters DNA are used to abbreviate what substance found in chromosomes?
ANSWER: DEOXYRIBONUCLEIC ACID
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In a cell, aerobic respiration occurs mainly in the:
W) ribosomes
X) mitochondria
Y) Golgi apparatus
Z) chloroplasts
ANSWER: X) mitochondria
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice As mitosis begins, the structure in the nucleus that disappears is the:
W) spindle
X) nucleolus
Y) chromatid
Z) centromere
ANSWER: X) nucleolus
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The process of osmosis is best illustrated by the movement of:
W) salts in blood stream.
X) oxygen into red blood cells.
Y) carbon dioxide through stomata.
Z) water into root hair cells.
ANSWER: Z) water into root hair cells.
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Cilia are distinguished from flagella by their:
W) arrangement of microtubules.
X) length and number.
Y) function.
Z) color.
ANSWER: X) length and number.
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Molecular oxygen formed during plant photosynthesis is derived exclusively from:
W) CO2
X) Water
Y) carbohydrates
Z) none of the above
ANSWER: X) Water
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In the digestive system, proteins are broken down into:
W) simple sugars.
X) fatty acids.
Y) amino acids.
Z) glycerol.
ANSWER: Y) amino acids.
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Respiration that does NOT require oxygen is called:
W) photosynthesis
X) oxidation
Y) anaerobic respiration
Z) aerobic respiration
ANSWER: Y) anaerobic respiration
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The process by which glucose is broken down and carbon dioxide and alcohol are released is:
W) glycolysis
X) photosynthesis
Y) respiration
Z) fermentation
ANSWER: Z) fermentation
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What controls the steps in the respiration of glucose?
W) enzymes
X) rate of photosynthesis
Y) absence of NAD
Z) amount of water present
ANSWER: W) enzymes
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What has the most energy in its chemical bonds?
W) AMP
X) ADP
Y) ATP
Z) All are equal
ANSWER: Y) ATP
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice During an early step of respiration, glucose is converted into two identical molecules of:
W) CO2
X) ATP
Y) pyruvic acid
Z) chlorophyll
ANSWER: Y) pyruvic acid
21. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice When you do strenuous exercise, oxygen is used up rapidly. What substance is formed.
W) pyruvic acid
X) lactic acid
Y) glucose
Z) alcohol
ANSWER: X) lactic acid
22. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following macromolecules is primarily responsible for the insolubility of cell membranes in water?
W) starch
X) cellulose
Y) protein
Z) phospholipid
ANSWER: Z) phospholipid
23. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In biological studies, which of the following terms could be used to replace the word dehydration?
W) encystation
X) desiccation
Y) hydrolysis
Z) none of these
ANSWER: X) desiccation
24. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following terms refers to the study of tissues?
W) physiology
X) pathology
Y) histology
Z) none of these
ANSWER: Y) histology
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What term refers to the development of viable individuals from unfertilized eggs?
ANSWER: PARTHENOGENESIS
26. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The common energy-storage polysaccharide in animals is:
W) glycogen
X) fructose
Y) starch
Z) collagen
ANSWER: W) glycogen
27. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Locomotion and heat production are functions of which system?
W) lymphatic
X) respiratory
Y) skeletal
Z) muscular
ANSWER: Z) muscular
28. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The pituitary and thyroid glands are components of the:
W) endocrine system
X) cardiovascular system
Y) lymphatic system
Z) respiratory system
ANSWER: W) endocrine system
29. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The primary function of carbohydrates is to:
W) break down molecules.
X) aid in digestion.
Y) supply the body with energy.
Z) regulate the flow of chyme.
ANSWER: Y) supply the body with energy.
30. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In a comparison of the brain found in lower classes of vertebrates to the brain in higher classes of vertebrates the area showing most increase in relative size is:
W) cerebellum
X) optic lobe
Y) medulla
Z) cerebrum
ANSWER: Z) cerebrum
31. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The outer most layer of skin is called the:
W) endodermis
X) mesoderm
Y) epidermis
Z) dermis
ANSWER: Y) epidermis
32. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The major organ in mammals for detoxifying chemicals is:
W) kidney
X) liver
Y) pancreas
Z) stomach
ANSWER: X) liver
33. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a nitrogenous waste compound excreted by animals?
W) urea
X) ammonia
Y) uric acid
Z) pyridine
ANSWER: Z) pyridine
34. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The clavicles are the bones commonly referred to as:
W) the shoulder blades
X) the shin bones
Y) the collar bones
Z) none of the above
ANSWER: Y) the collar bones
35. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following nourishes a developing seed?
W) pollen tube
X) nectar
Y) endosperm
Z) zygote
ANSWER: Y) endosperm
36. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which organs do the pulmonary arteries service?
ANSWER: LUNGS
37. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer White blood cells are called what?
ANSWER: LEUCOCYTES (also accept: LEUKOCYTES)
38. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The small intestine is composed of three segments. Two of these segments are the jejunum and the ileum. Name the third segment.
ANSWER: DUODENUM
