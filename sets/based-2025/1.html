1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which organelle, responsible for producing energy in eukaryotes, is commonly referred to as "The Powerhouse Of The Cell?"
ANSWER: MITOCHONDRIA
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following four organelles that contain DNA 1. Chloroplast 2. Nucleus 3. Mitochondria 4. Lysosome
ANSWER: 1, 2, AND 3
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Max is driving his car. His initial speed is 20 meters per second, and he accelerates by 2 meters per second squared for 5 seconds. Over those 5 seconds, how far does he travel in meters?
W) 75
X) 100
Y) 125
Z) 150
ANSWER: Y) 125
2. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about the coefficients of static friction and kinetic friction between two surfaces is false?
W) The coefficients are always unitless
X) The coefficient of kinetic friction must be less than or equal to the coefficient of static friction
Y) The coefficients do not depend on any external forces
Z) The product of normal force and the coefficient of static friction always equals the static frictional force
ANSWER: Z) The product of normal force and the coefficient of static friction always equals the static frictional force
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Hydrogen-4 decays to tritium through what process?
W) Beta plus decay
X) Beta minus decay
Y) Alpha decay
Z) Neutron emission
ANSWER: Z) Neutron emission
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, order the following three isotopes of hydrogen by increasing natural abundance 1. Protium 2. Deuterium 3. Tritium
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In the arithmetic sequence with first term 6 and fifth term 18, what is the seventh term?
ANSWER: 24

ANSWER: 1024/15

8. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Warren is a director of green energy at Saratoga High School. He is looking to make the school greener by eliminating the use of non-renewable energy resources. Which of the following energy sources is non-renewable?
W) Wind
X) Solar
Y) Geothermal
Z) Nuclear
ANSWER: Z) Nuclear
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer Biofuels are another type of renewable resource. By name or number, identify all of the following three plants which would likely be useful for biofuel production 1. Sugar beet 2. Pine 3. Corn
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer After a severe rainstorm, Alex observes two species of trout inhabiting the same section of river. A few years later, he comes back to see that only one species is still living in the same river. What principle, which states that two species cannot coexist in the same niche, is demonstrated by these trout?
ANSWER: COMPETITIVE EXCLUSION PRINCIPLE (also accept: GAUSS'S PRINCIPLE)
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer The competitive exclusion principle states that two species occupying the same niche cannot coexist for long periods of time. However, species with different niches may sometimes adapt to fit each others’ needs. What is the name of this phenomenon, driven by natural selection for complementary traits?
ANSWER: COEVOLUTION
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice If the volume of a sample of gas was halved while keeping temperature constant, which of the following properties would be doubled?
W) Internal energy
X) Pressure
Y) Moles of gas
Z) Entropy

10. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Alex is conducting an experiment where he places a cotton ball dipped in ammonia at one end of a glass tube and a cotton ball dipped in hydrochloric acid at the other. X) Closer to the hydrochloric acid

12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice While making s'mores on a campfire, Max feels warmth from the fire, yet cannot directly see the infrared photons being emitted. What property of an infrared photon is too large for the human eye to observe?
W) Frequency
X) Wavelength
Y) Energy
Z) Velocity
ANSWER: X) Wavelength
12. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A positive electric charge is moved at a constant speed between two locations in an electric field, with no work done by or against the field at any time during the entire process. Under which of the following conditions can this occur?
W) The charge is moved in the direction of the electric field
X) The charge is moved opposite to direction of the electric field
Y) The charge is moved along an equipotential line
Z) The charge is moved perpendicularly to equipotential lines
ANSWER: Y) The charge is moved along an equipotential line
