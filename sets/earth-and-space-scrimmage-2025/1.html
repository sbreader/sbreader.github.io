Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Lightning associated with storms most commonly occurs within which of the following layers of the atmosphere?
W) Troposphere
X) Stratosphere
Y) Mesosphere
Z) Thermosphere
ANSWER: W) Troposphere
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of stellar objects that are fully convective 1) Red dwarf 2) White dwarf 3) Neutron star
ANSWER: 1 Only
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals is generally NOT found in granite?
W) Quartz
X) Feldspar
Y) Mica
Z) Olivine
ANSWER: Z) Olivine
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following regions of the seafloor in increasing thickness of sediment accumulation 1) Continental shelf 2) Continental slope 3) Abyssal plain
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice As viewed from Earth, at which of the following points in Mercury's orbit does it exhibit the maximum angular separation from the Sun?
W) Inferior conjunction
X) Superior conjunction
Y) Opposition
Z) Greatest elongation
ANSWER: Z) Greatest elongation
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three igneous rocks by increasing viscosity of the magma they solidified from 1) Peridotite 2) Gabbro 3) Diorite
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of galaxies is generally considered to be the oldest?
W) Lenticular
X) Elliptical
Y) Spiral
Z) Barred-spiral
ANSWER: X) Elliptical
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The formation of coesite is most closely associated with what type of metamorphism, which occurs when a shockwave rapidly propagates through the parent rock?
ANSWER: Impact (also accept: Shock)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The most massive stars, such as Wolf-Rayet stars, will most likely end their life with which of the following types of supernovae?
W) Ia
X) Ib
Y) Ic
Z) II
ANSWER: Y) Ic
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following compositional layers of Earth is believed to have the highest concentration of magnesium?
W) Continental crust
X) Oceanic crust
Y) Mantle
Z) Core
ANSWER: Y) Mantle
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, arrange the following three hydrogen line series in order of increasing frequency 1) Balmer 2) Lyman 3) Paschen.
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly describes the effect of a La Nina event on the west coast of the United States compared to normal conditions?
W) Higher productivity, higher temperature
X) Higher productivity, lower temperature
Y) Lower productivity, higher temperature
Z) Lower productivity, lower temperature
ANSWER: X) Higher productivity, lower temperature
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of erosion that can occur in tundra environments due to freeze-thaw cycles 1) Creep 2) Frost wedging 3) Solifluction.
ANSWER: ALL
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Bob is considering a star with an apparent magnitude of negative 2.5 and an absolute magnitude of positive 2.5. To the nearest parsec, how far away is Bob's star?
ANSWER: 1 Parsec
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The polar jet stream is an example of what type of current, where the pressure gradient force is balanced with the Coriolis force?
ANSWER: Geostrophic
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Galena is tasty. Which of the following environments do galenas most commonly form in?
W) Volcanic
X) Shallow marine
Y) Hydrothermal
Z) Igneous intrusion
ANSWER: Y) Hydrothermal
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The rotation of the metallic hydrogen layer in Jupiter is believed to generate the planet's magnetic field through what effect?
ANSWER: Dynamo effect
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When observing distant galaxies, their light is sometimes bent to form an Einstein ring as seen by observers on Earth. This due to what physical effect, described by Einstein's theory of general relativity?
ANSWER: Gravitational lensing
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three glacial landforms that can be used to identify flow direction 1) Drumlin 2) Kame 3) Cirque
ANSWER: 1 and 3
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the pressure at the surface and upper-level of a hurricane, respectively?
W) High, high
X) High, low
Y) Low, high
Z) Low, low
ANSWER: Y) Low, high
