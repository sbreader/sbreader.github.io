2. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What form of ethanol fuel blend is commonly used by flex-fuel vehicles in the US and Europe?
W) E50
X) E75
Y) E85
Z) E90
ANSWER: Y) E85
3. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer State by name or number which of the following types of fuels is or are created by algae based carbohydrate fermentation? 1: Biobutanol 2: Bioethanol 3: Biomethanol 4: Biopropanol
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
6. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Thorium-232, after neutron capture and beta decay, produces what isotope, which would be the product of a theoretical thermal breeder reactor?
ANSWER: URANIUM-233
7. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Denote by name or number which of the following are current types of geothermal power plants. 1: Dry Steam2: Flash Steam3: Binary Cycle
ANSWER: ALL
8. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What collective term for the new system of electricity transmission in the US, as stated by the DOE, seeks to increase energy efficiency by 2%?
ANSWER: SMARTGRID
9. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What technology, a form of biopower, is used to convert organic waste to electricity or heat, through anaerobic digestion?
ANSWER: METHANE RECOVERY
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the power of a 100 kg vehicle accelerating at a constant 5 m/s2, which travels 100 m in 5 seconds?
ANSWER: 10,000 W
11. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Denote by name or number all of the following products which are made from petroleum. 1: Tires 2: Heart Valves 3: Batteries 4: Jet Fuel
ANSWER: 1, 2 and 4
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the major rock type which is the medium for fracking?
ANSWER: SHALE

W) 10
X) 15
Y) 20
Z) 25
ANSWER: Y) 20
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the name of the device which transfers electrical energy from one circuit to another through inductively coupled conductors?
ANSWER: TRANSFORMER
17. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Most current wind machines in use are of what type, with blades shaped like airplane propellers, increasing energy output?
ANSWER: HORIZONTAL AXIS

ANSWER: (NEUTRON) MODERATOR
19. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Enriched uranium is a kind of uranium in which the amount of which isotope has been increased through the process of isotope separation?
W) U-233
X) U-235
Y) U-237
Z) U-238
ANSWER: X) U-235
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the form of electromagnetic radiation emitted when a charged particle (such as an electron) passes through a dielectric medium at a speed greater than the phase velocity of light in that medium?
ANSWER: CHERENKOV RADIATION
22. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What element is the primary constituent of crystal lattices in photovoltaic cells?
ANSWER: SILICON
