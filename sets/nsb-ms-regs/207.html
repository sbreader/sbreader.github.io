1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A car rounds a curve on a level road. What most directly provides the centripetal force on the car?
W) Inertia
X) Gravity
Y) Friction between the tires and the road
Z) The force applied to the steering wheel
ANSWER: Y) Friction between the tires and the road
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Two resistors of equal resistance R are in parallel. What is the equivalent resistance of this circuit?
W) 0 R
X) ½ R
Y) R
Z) 2 R
ANSWER: X) ½ R
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Basin and Range region of Nevada is characterized by an abundance of what kind of faults?
ANSWER: NORMAL (also accept: DIP-SLIP)
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice You are looking west, at a fault in a region of shear. Across the fault, on the western side, the stream bed has been offset to the south, though no vertical movement has been detected. How do you describe this fault?
W) Low angle thrust fault
X) Left-lateral fault
Y) Right normal fault
Z) Oblique-slip fault
ANSWER: X) Left-lateral fault
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the smallest integer radius of a circle centered at the point (3, 4) such that the point (9, 9) is within the interior of the circle?
ANSWER: 8
3. BONUS Math Multiple Choice In triangle ABC, angle C measures 90 degrees and angle A measures 41 degrees. The length of AC is 5 feet. Which expression is equivalent to the length of BC?
W) 5 tan 41
X) 5 tan 49
Y) 5 sin 49
Z) 5 sin 41
ANSWER: W) 5 tan 41

ANSWER: CHLOROFLUOROCARBONS (also accept: CFCS)

W) Earth's ionosphere
X) Earth's magnetic field
Y) Nothing – it all reaches the Earth's surface
Z) Earth's stratosphere
ANSWER: X) Earth's magnetic field

9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice At the most basic level, wind is caused by differences in atmospheric pressure. When there is a difference, air moves in which of the following directions?
