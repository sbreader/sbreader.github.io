1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A 50-kilogram mass is sliding at an initial speed of 5.25 meters per second on ice, coming to rest uniformly in 3.5 seconds due to friction. In newtons, what is the magnitude of the force of friction on the mass due to the ice?
ANSWER: 75
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Gloria is preparing to image a biological sample, so she places it underneath a 50-millimeter glass slide. Given that the index of refraction of the glass is 1.2 and that she is viewing the sample from directly overhead, then, to the nearest whole millimeter, what is the apparent depth of the sample?
ANSWER: 42
2. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Berkeley Lab researchers used broad-spectrum infrared light to determine the reactivity of a heterogeneous catalyst at different points on the surface of the catalyst. What user facility at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab did they use to generate the infrared light?
ANSWER: ALS (also accept: ADVANCED LIGHT SOURCE)
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab have discovered a new method to filter out heavy isotopes of hydrogen using a functionalized form of what class of polymeric compounds, which consist of artificially-constructed porous carbon-based scaffolds ligating metal ions?
ANSWER: METAL-ORGANIC FRAMEWORK (also accept: MOF)
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When fermentation occurs in humans, pyruvate is reduced by NADH to form what molecule?
ANSWER: LACTATE (also accept: LACTIC ACID)
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer What flexible amino acid is present on nearly every third residue of collagen, due to its ability to fit on the inside of the triple helix?
ANSWER: GLYCINE

ANSWER: sp3d (also accept: dsp3; DSP3)
