1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the balanced internal environment in living organisms called?
ANSWER: Homeostasis (also accept: Dynamic Equilibrium)
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Structures in cells are called what?
ANSWER: Organelles

X) sodium carbonate decahydrate
Y) magnesium sulfate heptahydrate
Z) calcium sulfate hemihydrate
ANSWER: X) sodium carbonate decahydrate

3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice When a ray of light traveling through a transparent medium encounters a boundary leading to another transparent medium, this is called
W) refraction
X) reflection
Y) Huygen's principle
Z) Snell's law of refraction
ANSWER: W) refraction

12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The absolute age of an igneous rock can be found by doing what?
