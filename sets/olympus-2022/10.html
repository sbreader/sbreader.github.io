1. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Scientists are studying new methods to bring nuclear fusion onto the grid as a reliable energy source. What is the term for the method of fusion which involves heating and compressing a fuel target, typically a pellet containing deuterium and tritium?

X) Inertial confinement fusion
Y) Inertial electrostatic confinement
Z) Muon-catalyzed fusion
ANSWER: X) Inertial confinement fusion
1. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following failures in reasoning would NOT cause a multi-stage probabilistic reasoning process to underestimate a probability?
