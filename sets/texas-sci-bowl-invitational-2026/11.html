1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Dr. Elizabeth Frankenstein has Triple X syndrome and two out her three X chromosomes have the same mutation for hemophilia. Her father has hemophilia and her mother is neither affected nor a carrier. Which of the following accurately describes the origin of the nondisjunction event?
W) Meiosis I nondisjunction from her mother
X) Meiosis I nondisjunction from her father
Y) Meiosis II nondisjunction from her mother
Z) Meiosis II nondisjunction from her father
ANSWER: Z) Meiosis II nondisjunction from her father
