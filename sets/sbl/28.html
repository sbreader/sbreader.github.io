1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three ions in terms of increasing size 1) Na+ 2) K+ 3) Mg2+.
ANSWER: 3,1,2
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding 3p orbitals 1) They possess a nodal plane 2) They possess a radial node 3) They are generally involved in more efficient pi bonding than 2p orbitals.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What term is given to the ability of the brain to change when given different stimuli?
ANSWER: NEUROPLASTICITY (also accept: PLASTICITY)
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following four plant hormones that promote growth 1) Abscisic acid 2) Gibberellins 3) Auxins 4) Cytokinins.
ANSWER: 2,3,4
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of galaxy is commonly thought of as an intermediate between spiral galaxies and elliptical galaxies and are denoted as S0 in the Hubble classification scheme?
ANSWER: LENTICULAR
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements best describes the dominant formation mechanism of precipitation in cold clouds?
W) Supercooled water droplets merge due to electrostatic forces and eventually fall after reaching a critical mass
X) Ice crystals merge due to electrostatic forces and eventually fall after reaching a critical mass
Y) Water vapor diffuses from above supercooled water droplets to ice crystals, which fall after reaching a critical mass
Z) Water vapor diffuses from above ice crystals to supercooled water droplets, which fall after reaching a critical mass
ANSWER: Y) Water vapor diffuses from above supercooled water droplets to ice crystals, which fall after reaching a critical mass
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the slope of the directrix of the parabola y = 2 x2 + 3?
ANSWER: 0
4. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the largest 3-digit integer n such that n choose 2 is divisible by 32?
ANSWER: 961
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are investigating the use of metal organic frameworks, or MOFs, for the removal of nitrogen dioxide from the atmosphere. Aluminum-based MOFs are able to catalyze the reaction between water and nitrogen dioxide dimers to produce what acid?
ANSWER: NITRIC ACID
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The Wu experiment showed that parity symmetry is violated by what fundamental interaction?
ANSWER: WEAK FORCE (also accept: OBVIOUS EQUIVALENTS)
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 9000 kg block is traveling through empty space at 15 m/s when it collides perfectly elastically with a 1 kg block initially at rest. If the collision is head-on, to the nearest m/s, what is the final speed of the 1 kg block?
ANSWER: 30
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Olivine exists as a continuous solid solution between forsterite and fayalite. These two endmembers are silicates of which two metals?
ANSWER: IRON AND MAGNESIUM (also accept: Fe AND Mg)
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What specific type of pyroclastic flow is characteristically incandescent and flows as a dense, hot cloud of volcanic gases?
ANSWER: NUÉE ARDENTE
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 vitamins that are water soluble 1) Pyridoxine 2) Tocopherol 3) Cholecalciferol.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Los Alamos National Lab are studying the decay modes of various nuclei. Which of the following decay modes would a nucleus of plutonium-242 most likely undergo?
W) Alpha decay
X) Beta plus decay
Y) Beta minus decay
Z) Gamma decay
ANSWER: W) Alpha decay
9. BONUS Math Short Answer Researchers at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab are modeling the range of quantum entanglement of fermions by using asymmetric square matrices. These matrices are asymmetric because they do not remain constant after what function has been applied to them?
ANSWER: TRANSPOSE (also accept: TRANSPOSITION)
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the sum of the roots of the cubic polynomial f(x) = x3 + 10 x2 – 7 x + 10?
ANSWER: –10
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 functions that are concave down on the interval from x = 0 to x = 1 1) sin(x) + cos(x) 2) sin(x) * cos(x) 3) sin(x) / cos(x).
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer When the solvation of copper cations is studied, it is found that the electrostatic potential energy of the interaction is dependent on the distance between the copper ions and water ligands to what power?
ANSWER: –2
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following quantities does a galvanometer measure?
W) Voltage
X) Current
Y) Resistance
Z) Magnetic field
ANSWER: X) Current

ANSWER: RIGOR MORTIS

ANSWER: LIGNIN

BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Novel molecular dynamics studies of protein structure have revealed that the contribution of hydrogen bonding to alpha helix stability has historically been vastly overstated. The study showed that the true greatest proportion of free energy relief is the interaction between strand-adjacent carbonyl groups. Which of the following molecular orbital interactions accounts for this phenomenon?
W) Nonbonding to σ*
X) Nonbonding to π*

23. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A man is standing near a loudspeaker and observes a loudness of 100 decibels. The loudspeaker suddenly increases in intensity so that the man now observes a loudness of 105 decibels. To the nearest tenth, how many times as intense is the loudspeaker now compared to before?
ANSWER: 3.2

ANSWER: 6.4
