1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Pions and gluons are examples of particles that mediate forces, and fall under what category of elementary particles that have exclusively integer spins?
ANSWER: BOSONS
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following would lower the temperature at which water boils:
W) Increasing pressure
X) Decreasing pressure
Y) Increasing kinetic energy
Z) Decreasing kinetic energy
ANSWER: X) Decreasing pressure
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Simon, Aarin, Ayaan and Adam are playing golf. If they each have a 1/3 chance of getting a hole-in-one, what is the probability none of them get a hole-in-one?
ANSWER: 16/81
2. BONUS Math Short Answer For a birthday party, a balloon is blown up to a radius of 10 inches. A segment of length 16 inches is then drawn on its surface. After a few days, the balloon shrinks to a radius of 5 inches. What is the length of the segment, in inches, assuming that the balloon is always a perfect sphere?
ANSWER: 8
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Earthworms use what type of excretory system?
W) Protonephridia
X) Malpighian tubules
Y) Kidneys
Z) Metanephridia
ANSWER: Z) Metanephridia

W) Neutrophil
X) Macrophage
Y) Dendritic cell
Z) Natural killer cell
ANSWER: W) Neutrophil
