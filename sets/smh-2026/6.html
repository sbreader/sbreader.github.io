ANSWER: 1/4
3. BONUS Math Short Answer A disease called senioritis is spreading across South Middle Hollylake such that 25% of the population has it! Fortunately, there is a test for this. For a person with senioritis, this test will always return positive; however, for a person without senioritis, there is a 4% chance that the test returns positive, also known as its false positive rate. If Ayden takes the test and it returns positive, what is the probability that Ayden has senioritis?
ANSWER: 25/28
