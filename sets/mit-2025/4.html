1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Daniel is driving his 2 metric ton car along Memorial Drive at 20 meters per second when he collides with Fiona’s stationary car. If Daniel’s car slows to a stop within 0.5 seconds, what was the time-averaged impact force experienced by Fiona’s car in Newtons?
ANSWER: 80,000
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer An asteroid of radius 100 meters and surface gravitational acceleration 0.1 meters per second squared spins with angular velocity 0.02 radians per second. Garfield, of mass 100 kilograms, sits on the equator of this asteroid. To the nearest Newton, what is the normal force of the asteroid on Garfield?
ANSWER: 6
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Titus is camping in Plumas National Forest when he observes the following series of clouds approaching him from a distance: Cirrus, Altostratus, Nimbostratus. Which of the following weather fronts is likely approaching him?
W) Warm Front
X) Cold Front
Y) Occluded Front
Z) Dryline
ANSWER: W) Warm Front
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of fog most commonly develops alongside a thermal inversion?
W) Radiation fog
X) Upslope fog
Y) Steam fog
Z) Advection fog
ANSWER: W) Radiation fog
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is most likely true about the first enzymes on earth?
W) They were composed of amino acid subunits
X) They contained phosphodiester bonds
Y) They were similar in structure to most current enzymes
Z) They required other macromolecules for replication
ANSWER: X) They contained phosphodiester bonds
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following three blood vessels from first to last in terms of distance needed to reach them from the right ventricle 1) Pulmonary vein 2) Pulmonary artery 3) Hepatic portal vein
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the molecular geometry of sulfur atom in the sulfate ion?
ANSWER: TETRAHEDRAL
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The atomic weights of lithium, oxygen, and sulfur are 7, 16, and 32 atomic mass units, respectively. To the nearest hundredth, how many moles of lithium sulfate are in a 13.2 gram sample?
ANSWER: 0.12
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Two numbers have a sum of 6 and a difference of 8. What is the sum of the squares of the numbers?
ANSWER: 50
5. BONUS Math Short Answer How many four digit positive integers satisfy the property that the product of their digits is 2025?
ANSWER: 6
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The Walker Lab developed a peptide therapy to sequester free hemes in the bloodstream. What metal at the center of hemes binds to their peptide?
ANSWER: IRON
6. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice MIT engineers used aluminum from soda cans and seawater to sustainably produce hydrogen. What type of electrochemical cell could be more sustainable as a result of their work?
W) Leclanché cell
X) Downs cell
Y) Bunsen cell
Z) Fuel cell
ANSWER: Z) Fuel cell
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Dideoxynucleotide triphosphates, used for Sanger Sequencing, lack what functional group at the 3’ carbon necessary for DNA elongation?
ANSWER: HYDROXYL (also accept: OH)

W) Chytridiomycota
X) Glomeromycota
Y) Ascomycota
Z) Basidiomycota
ANSWER: X) Glomeromycota
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the smallest three digit integer with no odd prime factors?
ANSWER: 128
8. BONUS Math Short Answer Two of the digits of a three digit number are 2 and 9. Given that the number is divisible by 11, what are all possible values for the remaining digit?
ANSWER: 0,4,7
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three features that definitively indicate a retreating glacier 1) Terminal Moraine 2) Kettle Lakes 3) Esker
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of storm system is characterized by a mesocyclone within a thunderstorm and spatially separated updraft and downdraft regions?
ANSWER: SUPERCELL
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers in the Radosevich group at MIT are studying homogenous catalysis based on p-block elements. Which of the following is not a p-block element?
W) Helium
X) Oxygen
Y) Phosphorus
Z) Bromine
ANSWER: W) Helium
10. BONUS Energy Short Answer Economists at MIT applied regression to school assignment models. To handle multicollinearity in their dataset, the researchers added a penalty term to the objective that is only dependent on the regression model’s derived weights. This approach is an example of what general technique in machine learning to prevent overfitting?
ANSWER: REGULARIZATION (do not accept: VARIANTS OF REGULARIZATION)
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer When a sound wave is emanated isotropically from a point source, the intensity of the wave is linearly proportional to what power of the distance from the point source?
ANSWER: −2 (also accept: -2; NEGATIVE 2)

X) Beryllium

21. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A physics textbook is at rest on a flat table. Ethan attempts to push the textbook but fails to overcome friction. Which of the following statements cannot be true regarding this situation?
W) The magnitude of friction force is greater than the magnitude of normal force
X) The coefficient of kinetic friction is zero
Y) Ethan does positive work on the textbook
Z) The friction force is less than the coefficient of static friction times the normal force
ANSWER: Y) Ethan does positive work on the textbook
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer A copper sphere of radius 3 centered at the origin has a spherical cavity of radius 0.25 centered at x equals 0.5. After thermal expansion, the volume of the copper sphere increases by a factor of three. What is the new x position of the center of the cavity, assuming that the center of the entire sphere doesn’t move?
ANSWER: ∛3/2 (also accept: one-half times the cube root of 3)

Z) The ozone layer above Antarctica is not recovering
