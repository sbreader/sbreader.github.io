Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Fermi National Lab are studying the decay modes of the lightest group of mesons. What is the name of this group of 3 particles?
ANSWER: PIONS (also accept: PI MESONS)
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at Los Alamos National Lab are studying the effects of Super El Nino events in the past, including the one that occurred from 2015 to 2016. Identify all of the following three changes that are consequences of an El Nino event 1) Increases in soil moisture in India 2) High air pressure in the Western Pacific 3) High sea surface temperatures off the coast of Peru.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
2. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice In the prime factorization of a perfect square, the exponents of the prime factors must be which of the following types of numbers?
W) Even
X) Odd
Y) Prime
Z) Perfect squares
ANSWER: W) Even
2. BONUS Math Short Answer A right circular cylinder has height 15 and diameter 18. What is its surface area?
ANSWER: 432π
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the volume of a 10-gram sample of water as a function of temperature?
W) Linearly increasing from 0 to 100 degrees Celsius
X) Linearly decreasing from 0 to 100 degrees Celsius
Y) Non-linear with a local maximum at 4 degrees Celsius
Z) Non-linear with a local minimum at 4 degrees Celsius
ANSWER: Z) Non-linear with a local minimum at 4 degrees Celsius
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Lightning strikes a lightning rod on a building that is 3 kilometers away from you. To the nearest whole second, how long will it take the sound of the thunder to reach you?
ANSWER: 9
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What vitamin is essential in humans for the synthesis of several blood clotting factors?
ANSWER: K (also accept: PHYLLOQUINONE; MENAQUINONE; PHYTOMENADIONE; K1; K2)
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Coronaviruses are positive-sense single-stranded RNA viruses. Which of the following describes how their genetic material is processed after entering a cell?
W) Transcribed to mRNA with RNA-dependent RNA polymerase
X) Directly translated by host ribosomes
Y) Reverse transcribed to DNA with reverse transcriptase
Z) Translated after assembly of viral ribosomes
ANSWER: X) Directly translated by host ribosomes
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the value of the reaction quotient when the change in Gibbs free energy is zero?
ANSWER: K (also accept: EQUILIBRIUM CONSTANT)
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Match the flame test colors blue, green, and purple to the following three metals or compounds 1) Boric acid 2) Potassium 3) Cesium.
ANSWER: 1) GREEN; 2) PURPLE; 3) BLUE (also accept: BORIC ACID IS GREEN; POTASSIUM IS PURPLE; CESIUM IS BLUE)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Jet streams are located at what boundary in Earth's atmosphere?
ANSWER: TROPOPAUSE
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Speleothem cave deposits are most typically composed of what type of rock?
ANSWER: LIMESTONE
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists supported by the Department of Energy are developing new ways to synthesize carbon fiber. In carbon fiber, what is the hybridization state of the carbon atoms?
ANSWER: sp2
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists supported by the Department of Energy are producing catalysts for methanol production. They are synthesizing methanol via the oxidation of what organic starting material?
ANSWER: METHANE
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If f is a continuous function such that ∫48 f(x) dx = 24, what is the average value of f on the closed interval from 4 to 8?
ANSWER: 6
8. BONUS Math Short Answer If the product of cosine x and sine x is -1/6, what is the value of (cos x-sin x)2?
ANSWER: 4/3 (also accept: 1 1/3)
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer J.J. Thomson won the Nobel Prize in Physics for calculating the charge-to-mass ratio of what particles in cathode rays?
ANSWER: ELECTRONS
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer The x-component and the y-component of an object’s velocity are both 5 meters per second. In meters per second, what is the speed of this object?
ANSWER: 5√2
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Ducks have webbed feet while chickens do not. This morphogenetic difference is mediated by BMP4 and Gremlin, which are proteins involved in what cellular process?
ANSWER: APOPTOSIS
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three compounds that are derivatives of cholesterol 1) Testosterone 2) Cortisol 3) Retinol.
ANSWER: 1, 2
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Kate is performing an experiment where she has a chamber containing an inert gas. The chamber is at a fixed temperature and she varies the pressure of the chamber. She generates a curve by plotting pressure on the x-axis and PV over RT on the y-axis. At which of the following fixed chamber temperatures, in kelvins, would this curve be the MOST linear?
W) 250
X) 500
Y) 1000
Z) 1500
ANSWER: Z) 1500
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following four acids that are considered weak acids 1) HF 2) CH3COOH 3) HCN 4) H3PO4.
ANSWER: ALL
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the gap between Saturn’s A and B rings?
ANSWER: CASSINI DIVISION (also accept: CASSINI)
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In kelvins, which of the following is closest to the radiative equilibrium temperature of Earth?
W) 100
X) 250
Y) 300
Z) 350
ANSWER: X) 250
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Argonne National Lab researchers are studying photocatalysts made of Cu2O to reduce carbon dioxide into methanol. What is the IUPAC name for Cu2O?
ANSWER: COPPER (I) OXIDE (do not accept: CUPROUS OXIDE)
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists at SLAC National Accelerator Facility are studying photo-iso-mer-i-zation in opsins. Identify all of the following three statements that are true of opsins 1) They are G-protein-coupled receptors 2) They are found in the retina 3) They are covalently bound to retinal when active.
ANSWER: ALL
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the discriminant of the quadratic polynomial 8x2 + 7x-2?
ANSWER: 113
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Lloyd is asked by his teacher to subtract 4 from a number and multiply the result by 2. Instead, he subtracts 2 and then multiplies the result by 4, giving him 124. What is the correct answer to the teacher’s question?
ANSWER: 58
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following are the units of entropy?
W) Joules
X) Newtons per kelvin
Y) Watts
Z) Joules per kelvin
ANSWER: Z) Joules per kelvin
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer A car starting at rest undergoes a constant acceleration of 16 meters per second squared. In meters per second, what is the speed of the car after it has traveled half a meter?
ANSWER: 4
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What chromosome are Barr bodies derived from?
ANSWER: X
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In eukaryotic cells, the electron transport chain directly performs which of the following actions?
W) Generating ATP
X) Generating GTP
Y) Pumping protons into the mitochondrial matrix
Z) Pumping protons into the intermembrane space
ANSWER: Z) Pumping protons into the intermembrane space
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What principle states that two electrons cannot have the same quantum numbers?
ANSWER: PAULI EXCLUSION PRINCIPLE
17. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is true regarding the energetics of electrochemical cells?
W) For a voltaic cell, E-cell is positive and delta G is positive
X) For a voltaic cell, E-cell is positive and delta G is negative
Y) For an electrolytic cell, E-cell is negative and delta G is negative
Z) For an electrolytic cell, E-cell is positive and delta G is negative
ANSWER: X) For a voltaic cell, E-cell is positive and delta G is negative
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In what phase is the Moon when moonrise occurs at noon?
ANSWER: FIRST QUARTER
18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of stars has a luminosity most similar to that of red dwarfs?
W) Red giants
X) Yellow dwarfs
Y) White dwarfs
Z) Brown dwarfs
ANSWER: Y) White dwarfs
