1. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The graph of which of the following equations has a negative slope:
W) y = 2x – 4
X) y = –2x + 4
Y) y = –4
Z) x = –2
ANSWER: X) y = –2x + 4
2. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true:
W) all fires burn with a yellow flame
X) snowflakes and raindrops often contain bacteria
Y) a general rule for solubility is that ‘like dissolves like’
Z) a super-cooled liquid is a liquid at a temperature below its normal freezing point
ANSWER: W) all fires burn with a yellow flame
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following BEST describes the range of sizes of most animal cells:
W) 0.2 to 2.0 micrometers
X) 2.0 to 4.0 micrometers
Y) 5.0 to 20.0 micrometers
Z) 12.0 to 40.0 micrometers
ANSWER: Y) 5.0 to 20.0 micrometers
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is most advantageous to plants that depend on insects for pollination:
W) absence of rhizomes
X) sticky pollen
Y) small green flowers
Z) numerous stomata
ANSWER: X) sticky pollen
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name all of the following biological molecules that are considered polymers: glucose; cellulose; DNA; RNA
ANSWER: CELLULOSE; DNA; RNA (also accept: ALL BUT GLUCOSE)
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following materials in pure form would register the LOWEST amount of ohms for a given electric current at 20°C if all other variables are constant:
W) silver
X) gold
Y) tin
Z) lead
ANSWER: W) silver
4. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following optical devices produces a real image:
W) plane mirror
X) convex mirror
Y) concave lens
Z) convex lens
ANSWER: Z) convex lens
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A semidiurnal tidal pattern is more typical of which of the following locations:
W) the East Coast of the U.S.
X) the West Coast of the U.S.
Y) the Gulf of Mexico
Z) the North Pole
ANSWER: W) the East Coast of the U.S.
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the most notable product of the hydrolysis of feldspar:
W) chalk
X) sandstone
Y) clay minerals
Z) halite
ANSWER: Y) clay minerals
6. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the next 3 terms of the arithmetic sequence whose first 3 terms are: 23, 15, 7
ANSWER: –1, –9, –17
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is one of the most likely causes of algal blooms in waterways along the Chesapeake Bay:
W) fertilizers
X) natural pesticides
Y) nitrogen-fixing bacteria
Z) plastics pollution
ANSWER: W) fertilizers
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Giving your answer as winter, spring, summer, or fall, in what season will "dead zones" be most prevalent in the Chesapeake Bay?
ANSWER: SUMMER
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Pampas, steppes, and savannas are all different types of:
W) deserts
X) tropical forests
Y) tropical shrublands
Z) grasslands
ANSWER: Z) grasslands
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following biomes takes up more square area of the world than any other biome:
W) rainforest
X) savanna
Y) taiga
Z) tundra
ANSWER: Y) taiga
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Hydrogen bonding is NOT important in the physical and chemical properties of:
W) liquid water
X) water ice
Y) DNA
Z) methane gas
ANSWER: Z) methane gas
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Arrange the following 3 diatomic molecules in order of INCREASING number of bonds between atoms: oxygen; fluorine; nitrogen
ANSWER: FLUORINE; OXYGEN; NITROGEN
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a key step in the rock cycle:
W) cementation
X) melting
Y) cooling
Z) evaporation
ANSWER: Z) evaporation
10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Most of Earth's deserts in the northern hemisphere are located near which of the following latitudes:
W) 0°
X) 30°
Y) 70°
Z) 90°
ANSWER: X) 30°
12. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is typically used to sterilize laboratory glassware:
W) hot plate
X) freeze dryer
Y) autoclave
Z) fume hood
ANSWER: Y) autoclave
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of the Big Dipper:
W) it is a well known constellation that can be seen from mid-northern latitudes
X) it appears on the opposite side of the North Star from Cassiopeia
Y) it is sometimes called the Plough
Z) assuming clear skies, it can be seen throughout the night every night of the year from mid-northern latitudes
ANSWER: W) it is a well known constellation that can be seen from mid-northern latitudes
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the most likely highest power for an objective lens on a typical student compound light microscope:
W) 25-times
X) 100-times
Y) 500-times
Z) 1500-times
ANSWER: X) 100-times
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following 4 parts of the human intestine from the FIRST to receive food from the stomach to the LAST: colon; duodenum; ileum; jejunum
ANSWER: DUODENUM; JEJUNUM; ILEUM; COLON
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of pH:
W) the terms basic or alkaline describe a solution with pH greater than 7
X) the highest value on the pH scale is 14
Y) strong acids dissociate almost completely in water
Z) a pH meter operates by measuring small differences in the density of different fluids
ANSWER: Z) a pH meter operates by measuring small differences in the density of different fluids
14. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice A decrease of 1 unit on the pH scale represents which of the following:
W) a 10-fold increase in the hydrogen ion concentration
X) a 10-fold decrease in the hydrogen ion concentration
Y) a 100-fold increase in the hydrogen ion concentration
Z) a 100-fold decrease in the hydrogen ion concentration
ANSWER: W) a 10-fold increase in the hydrogen ion concentration
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Earth's atmosphere contains several different layers that are most often defined according to:
W) pressure
X) moisture
Y) wind velocity
Z) temperature

ANSWER: ALL

17. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following terms is used most often by astronomers for the total energy emitted per unit time from a star:
W) apparent magnitude
X) watts
Y) luminosity
Z) stellar unit
ANSWER: Y) luminosity
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The presence of which of the following is the most important characteristic that allows xylem to function in plants:
W) plastids and chloroplasts
X) strong thick walls
Y) large fluid-filled vacuoles
Z) nuclei and endoplasmic reticulum
ANSWER: X) strong thick walls
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name all of the following 4 structures that are composed mostly of keratin: bird's beak; snake teeth; human fingernail; mammalian fur
ANSWER: BIRD'S BEAK; HUMAN FINGERNAIL; MAMMALIAN FUR (also accept: ALL BUT SNAKE TEETH)
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Francium has the lowest electronegativity of the elements. This would indicate that francium is in what corner of the periodic chart:
W) upper left
X) lower left
Y) upper right
Z) lower right
ANSWER: X) lower left

W) sodium chloride
X) potassium chloride
Y) barium sulfate
Z) sodium hydroxide
ANSWER: Y) barium sulfate
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer At any one time, there are always how many major tidal bulges on Earth?
ANSWER: 2
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which 2 of the following 4 choices are primarily responsible for the creation of two major tidal bulges on the Earth: gravity; wind; inertia; sunlight
ANSWER: GRAVITY; INERTIA

ANSWER: 72
22. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What uranium isotope is most common on Earth?
ANSWER: URANIUM-238 (also accept: 238)

ANSWER: CROCUS
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What natural plant hormone is most directly involved in controlling phototropism?
ANSWER: AUXIN (also accept: INDOLE ACETIC ACID; IAA)

ANSWER: SIMPLE SUGARS; mRNA
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Knowing that carbon is in group 4 and period 2 on the Periodic Table, give the electron configuration of the carbon atom:
ANSWER: 1s2 2s2 2p2 (also accept: [He]2s2 2p2)
24. BONUS Physics Short Answer Arrange the following 4 materials in DECREASING order of thermal conductivity: granite; rubber; gold; lead
ANSWER: GOLD; LEAD; GRANITE; RUBBER
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the MOST common name for the last stellar stage in the life of our Sun?
ANSWER: WHITE DWARF (also accept: BLACK DWARF)
25. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The decay rate of the radioactive isotope potassium-40 into stable argon-40 can be used to radiometrically date rocks. If it takes 1.28 billion years for half the potassium-40 to decay into argon-40, a rock that contains a 50/50 ratio of the two is how many billion years old, to the second decimal place?
ANSWER: 1.28
