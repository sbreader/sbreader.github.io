Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the quantum mechanical interaction between molecular orbitals of the same symmetry derived from s and p atomic orbitals that have similar energy levels?
ANSWER: s-p mixing (also accept: orbital mixing)
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the type of magnetism found in iron nanoparticles, where thermal energy randomly flips the material's magnetic direction, resulting in strong magnetic behavior under an external field but zero net magnetization once that field is removed?
ANSWER: Superparamagnetism
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the optimal path along which a nucleophile can attack a carbonyl group to maximize orbital overlap while minimizing electron repulsion?
ANSWER: Burgi Dunitz Trajectory
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What set of rules is used to predict the favorability of intramolecular reactions?
ANSWER: Baldwin's rules
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following ions does not receive its color from Ligand to Metal Charge Transfer?
W) Cerium (IV)
X) Chromate
Y) Permanganate
Z) Cobalt (III)
ANSWER: Z) Cobalt (III)
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following ions is the worst reducing agent?
W) Vanadium (II)
X) Chromium (II)
Y) Manganese (II)
Z) Iron (II)
ANSWER: Y) Manganese (II)
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the diagram used in molecular spectroscopy to illustrate the electronic and vibrational energy states of a molecule?
ANSWER: Jablonski diagram
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 3 quantities which the flux of a gas is proportional to 1) concentration gradient with respect to position 2) concentration gradient with respect to time 3) 2nd concentration gradient with respect to position
ANSWER: 1
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is studying a peptide with a mass of 900 daltons using mass spectrometry, and he decides to use electrospray ionization. By name or number, identify all of the following four mass-to-charge ratios at which the presence of a peak would be surprising 1) 901 2) 451 3) 301 4) 43
ANSWER: 4
5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Abhinav is studying an unknown organic molecule containing only carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen. He accidentally spills all of his compound on his only copy of the mass spectrum, but he can still make out peaks at 77 m/z, 120 m/z, and 148 m/z. Which of the following is most likely Abhinav's molecule, given that they all have the same molar mass?
W) 4-phenylbutyraldehyde
X) propyl phenyl ketone
Y) p-isopropylbenzaldehyde
Z) 1-allyl-4-methoxybenzene
ANSWER: X) propyl phenyl ketone
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three carbons by increasing chemical shift in Carbon-13 NMR 1) Carbon in Carboxylic Acid 2) Carbon in Ketone 3) Carbon in Amine
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following accurately describes how the envelope conformation in cyclopentane affects torsional strain and angle strain respectively?
W) Reduces Torsional Strain, Reduces Angle Strain
X) Reduces Torsional Strain, Increases Angle Strain
Y) Increases Torsional Strain, Reduces Angle Strain
Z) Increases Torsional Strain, Increases Angle Strain
ANSWER: X) Reduces Torsional Strain, Increases Angle Strain
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer As opposed to the Ray–Dutt twist, octahedral complexes containing three bidentate chelate rings can undergo what process in which racemization occurs via the formation of a trigonal prismatic intermediate?
ANSWER: Bailar Twist
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Chalcophiles are elements that are likely to form sulfides, selenides, and tellurides. Which of the following metals is NOT a chalcophile?
W) Zinc
X) Lead
Y) Sodium
Z) Copper
ANSWER: Y) Sodium
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Abhinav is studying a phosphorescent compound by dissolving it in water and illuminating it with light, but he does not observe any emission. He realizes that the phosphorescence is being quenched. This is most likely caused by the presence of which of the following species?
W) Oxygen
X) Nitrogen
Y) Water
Z) other molecules of the phosphorescent compound
ANSWER: W) Oxygen
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is studying another compound that fluoresces at 500 nm and phosphoresces at 600 nm. The compound fluoresces with a rate constant of 100 s-1, undergoes internal conversion to the ground state at 50 s-1, and undergoes intersystem crossing to the triplet state at 50 s-1. The triplet state phosphoresces with a rate constant of 1 s-1 and is quenched by a species of concentration 0.1 M with a quenching rate constant of 90 M-1 s-1. What are the quantum yields, respectively, of fluorescence and phosphorescence?
ANSWER: 0.5 and 0.025 (also accept: 1/2 and 1/40)
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term is used to describe a special type of constant-boiling mixture formed by three or more components whose boiling point falls between the highest and lowest boiling points of its individual constituents?
ANSWER: Saddle Azeotrope
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following combinations of compounds forms a minimum boiling point azeotrope?
W) Ethanol and Water
X) Water and Hydrazine
Y) Chloroform and Acetone
Z) Water and Nitric acid
ANSWER: W) Ethanol and Water
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What name is given to the functional group consisting of a carbon that is both double bonded to another carbon atom as well as an oxygen atom?
ANSWER: Ketene
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following four peaks by increasing intensity in the mass spectrum of a compound with 2 chlorine atoms and 1 bromine atom 1) M 2) M+2 3) M+4 4) M+6
ANSWER: 4, 3, 1, 2
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term refers to the number of catalytic cycles or substrate molecules converted per active site per unit of time?
ANSWER: Turnover Frequency
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the point defect in a crystal lattice where an equal number of cations and anions are missing from their typical positions, creating vacancies while keeping the crystal electrically neutral?
ANSWER: Schottky Defect
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer As opposed to coagulation, which is the irreversible aggregation of colloidal particles, what is the name for the reversible aggregation of colloidal particles?
ANSWER: Flocculation
12. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice How does adding boron and phosphorus, respectively, change the energy of the Fermi level of a silicon semiconductor?
W) decreases, decreases
X) decreases, increases
Y) decreases, stays the same
Z) stays the same, decreases
ANSWER: X) decreases, increases
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer While Nile Red fluoresces a strong yellow-gold in nonpolar environments such as neutral lipid droplets, its emission shifts toward deep red as the polarity of its surroundings increases. This is an example of what phenomenon in which a substance changes its color depending on the polarity or hydrogen-bonding ability of the surrounding medium?
ANSWER: Solvatochromism
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three compounds by increasing Nitrogen-Hydrogen Bond Angle 1) Ammonia 2) Hydrazine 3) Hydroxylamine
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What group of compounds, which contain an N-methoxy-N-methyl group, form stable tetrahedral intermediates during the addition of organolithium or organomagnesium reagents, through the chelation of the metal through the methoxy group?
ANSWER: Weinreb Amides
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three protecting groups which are removed via acidic conditions 1) Fmoc 2) THP 3) Trityl
ANSWER: 2, 3
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Pure metals are most likely to be found in which of the following parts of a Pourbaix diagram?
W) Top Right
X) Top Left
Y) Bottom Right
Z) Bottom Left
ANSWER: Z) Bottom Left
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What term, coined by Douglas Stephan, describes combinations of Lewis acids and bases that cannot form a traditional adduct due to steric hindrance?
ANSWER: Frustrated Lewis Pairs
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Tungsten is often not used in hydrogenation reactions since it binds too strongly to hydrogen, making it an ineffective catalyst. This is an example of what principle, which states that a good catalyst must interact with reactants and products with an intermediate strength?
ANSWER: Sabatier Principle
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Drops of ethanol, methanol, or acetone can spontaneously move, deform, and split into smaller drops when placed on a liquid with a different composition. This is an example of what phenomenon which occurs due to differences in surface tension driving fluid flow along the liquid interface?
ANSWER: Marangoni Effect
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The Catalytic Triad is a set of three amino acid residues found in the active site of certain enzymes which consists of an acidic amino acid, a basic amino acid, and a nucleophilic amino acid. By name or number, rank the following three amino acids by increasing likelihood of being the nucleophilic amino acid 1) Cysteine 2) Serine 3) Valine
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the method by which a peptide chain is built step-by-step while anchored to an insoluble compound?
ANSWER: Merrifield Synthesis
