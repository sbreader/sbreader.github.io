Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In a prokaryotic cell, where is the DNA concentrated?
W) Nucleoid
X) Nucleus
Y) Nuclear membrane
Z) Nucleolus
ANSWER: W) Nucleoid
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which two domains consist of prokaryotes?
ANSWER: BACTERIA AND ARCHAEA
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What organelles carry out protein synthesis?
ANSWER: Ribosomes
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is a stack of thylakoids called?
W) Cristae
X) Stroma
Y) Granum
Z) Centrosome
ANSWER: Y) Granum
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Where are free ribosomes located?
ANSWER: CYTOSOL
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer The chloroplast is a member of a family of closely related plant organelles called what?
ANSWER: PLASTIDS
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Phagocytosis forms what type of vacuole?
W) Food vacuole
X) Central vacuole
Y) Contractile vacuole
Z) Lytic vacuole
ANSWER: W) Food vacuole
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What do many freshwater protists have that pump excess water out of the cell?
ANSWER: CONTRACTILE VACUOLES
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What are the sites of cellular respiration?
W) Chloroplasts
X) Peroxisome
Y) Mitochondria
Z) Lysosome
ANSWER: Y) Mitochondria
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the free-energy change in kcal of the breakdown of one mole of glucose in cellular respiration?
ANSWER: -686 kcal/mol
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the degradative processes that break down complex molecules called?
ANSWER: Catabolic pathways
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What process consumes energy to build complicated molecules from simpler ones?
ANSWER: ANABOLIC PATHWAYS
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the formula for Gibbs Free Energy?
ANSWER: ΔG = ΔH-TΔS
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many chromosomes do somatic cells have?
ANSWER: 46
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which phase is the shortest part of the cell cycle?
ANSWER: MITOTIC PHASE (also accept: M PHASE)
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the three subphases of interphase?
ANSWER: G1, S, G2
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the ability of a cell to gain or lose water?
ANSWER: TONICITY
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the control of water balance in cells called?
ANSWER: OSMOREGULATION
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which field of biology studies the classification of living beings?
ANSWER: Taxonomy
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the named taxonomic unit at any level?
ANSWER: TAXON
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What phenomenon causes polar molecules and ions to move across the lipid bilayer with the help of transport proteins?
W) Osmosis
X) Osmoregulation
Y) Active Transport
Z) Facilitated Diffusion
ANSWER: Z) Facilitated Diffusion
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the two types of transport proteins?
ANSWER: CHANNEL PROTEINS AND CARRIER PROTEINS
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What measures the portion of a system's energy that can perform work when temperature and pressure are uniform throughout a system?
W) Enthalpy
X) Entropy
Y) Temperature
Z) Free Energy
ANSWER: Z) Free Energy
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What group of living being is out of the five-kingdom classification?
ANSWER: VIRUSES
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is a phage reproductive cycle that culminates in death of the host cell?
ANSWER: LYTIC CYCLE
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Where does hematopoiesis occur?
W) Plasma of blood
X) Muscle tissue
Y) Bone Marrow
Z) Erythrocytes
ANSWER: Y) Bone Marrow
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the four main types of anemia?
ANSWER: Nutrient-deficiency anemia, anemia caused by blood loss, hemolytic anemia, and aplastic anemia
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How long do red blood cells live?
W) 60 days
X) 80 days
Y) 100 days
Z) 120 days
ANSWER: Z) 120 days
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the three main blood group systems?
ANSWER: ABO system, Rh system, MN system
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What two smaller molecules is a fat constructed from?
ANSWER: Glycerol and fatty acids
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of fat is formed by three fatty acid molecules joining to glycerol by an ester linkage?
ANSWER: TRIACYLGLYCEROL
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What transfers amino acids from the cytoplasmic pool of amino acids to a ribosome?
ANSWER: tRNA (also accept: TRANSFER RNA)
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the nucleotide triplet at the end of a tRNA called?
ANSWER: ANTICODON
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are circular RNA molecules that infect plants called?
ANSWER: VIROIDS
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are infectious proteins called?
ANSWER: PRIONS
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which scientist suggested that species can change?
W) Hutton
X) Malthus
Y) Cuvier
Z) Lamarck
ANSWER: Z) Lamarck
19. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice When did Lamarck publish his theory for how life evolves?
W) 1795
X) 1809
Y) 1813
Z) 1825
ANSWER: X) 1809
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is an organism's ability to alter or "mold" itself in response to local environmental conditions called?
W) Plasticity
X) Transduction
Y) Heterochrony
Z) Catastrophism
ANSWER: W) Plasticity
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are accumulated charactistics of external form that vary little among plants within the species called?
ANSWER: MORPHOLOGY
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many chambers does a fish heart have?
ANSWER: 2
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer What divides the single ventricle of a reptile heart?
ANSWER: SEPTUM
22. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the synthesis of RNA under the direction of DNA called?
W) Translation
X) RNA processing
Y) Conjugation
Z) Transcription
ANSWER: Z) Transcription
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer Where does translation occur in a cell?
ANSWER: RIBOSOMES
23. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Adult stem cells are able to give rise to multiple but not all cell types. What is this called?
W) Totipotent
X) Pluripotent
Y) Ruminant
Z) Polyploidy
ANSWER: X) Pluripotent
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the process in which the nucleus of an unfertilized egg cell is replaced with the nucleus of a differentiated cell?
ANSWER: NUCLEAR TRANSPLANTATION
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the two major populations of membrane proteins?
ANSWER: Integral Proteins and Peripheral Proteins
24. BONUS Biology Short Answer What kind of Integral protein completely spans the membrane?
ANSWER: TRANSMEMBRANE PROTEIN
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the five phases of mitosis, starting with Prophase
ANSWER: Prophase, Prometaphase, Metaphase, Anaphase, Telophase
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the shortest stage of mitosis?
ANSWER: ANAPHASE
