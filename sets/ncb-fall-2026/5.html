Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following acids has a different proticity than the rest?
W) Pyrophosphoric Acid
X) Citric Acid
Y) Phosphoric Acid
Z) Boric Acid
ANSWER: W) Pyrophosphoric Acid
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Assuming both solutions have the same solvent, at what molarity will a Sodium Nitrate solution and a 3 molar Magnesium Nitrate solution have the same boiling point?
ANSWER: 4.5
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer While TLC uses the retardation factor to measure polarity, in gas chromatography, what term refers to the ratio of the time an analyte spends in the stationary phase versus the mobile phase?
ANSWER: Retention Factor (do not accept: Retention Time)
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Ylides are a subclass of what type of compounds, which are overall neutral but contain both positively and negatively charged parts?
ANSWER: Zwitterion
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the pKa of an alkane?
W) 30
X) 40
Y) 50
Z) 60
ANSWER: Y) 50
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The Rydberg Equation cannot be used on which of the following?
W) Hydrogen Atom
X) He+ ion
Y) Boron4+ ion
Z) Li3+ ion
ANSWER: Z) Li3+ ion
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three acids by increasing number of acidic protons 1) Oxalic Acid 2) Formic Acid 3) Arsenic Acid
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following reactions will NOT yield a precipitate?
W) Hydrobromic Acid and Barium Nitrate
X) Copper and Dilute Ammonia
Y) Barium Hydroxide and Magnesium Sulfate
Z) Silver Nitrate and Hydrochloric Acid
ANSWER: W) Hydrobromic Acid and Barium Nitrate
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three carboxylic acid derivatives by increasing number of oxygen atoms 1) Anhydride 2) Amide 3) Ester
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of adjectives can be used to describe phosphorous acid?
W) diprotic, weak
X) diprotic, strong
Y) triprotic, weak
Z) triprotic, strong
ANSWER: W) diprotic, weak
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three classes of phosphorus compounds by increasing oxidation state of phosphorus 1) Phosphites 2) Phosphines 3) Phosphates
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Eriochrome Black T is a common indicator used in what types of titrations which are commonly used to determine the concentration of metal ions by forming a stable, soluble coordination complex with a chelating agent?
ANSWER: Complexometric (also accept: Complexometric Titration)
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Boron Trifluoride and Nitrogen Dioxide both undergo what general process, in which two similar molecules combine into a single larger complex?
ANSWER: Dimerization
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Consider a leaving group X-. Which of the following sets of terms would describe the most favorable X-?
W) low pKb, low ionic radius
X) high pKb, low ionic radius
Y) low pKb, high ionic radius
Z) high pKb, high ionic radius
ANSWER: Z) high pKb, high ionic radius
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is trying to calculate the freezing point depression of MgCl2 but finds in his calculations that it is off by a factor of 3. This is likely because he forgot to account for what value, the ratio of the actual number of particles in a solution to the number of formula units initially dissolved?
ANSWER: Van't Hoff Factor
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many oxygen atoms are present in Mercury (I) Phosphate?
ANSWER: 8
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A ground state multi-electron atom is irradiated with photons such that a single electron is now in an excited state. How do the energies of the HOMO and LUMO change respectively?
W) increase, increase
X) increase, decrease
Y) decrease, increase
Z) decrease, decrease
ANSWER: X) increase, decrease
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of compounds are the closest in bond angle?
W) H2O and NH3
X) H2S and PH3
Y) H2Se and AsH3
Z) H2Te and SbH3
ANSWER: Z) H2Te and SbH3
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What name is given to the process in which a triglyceride is converted into a glycerol and salts?
ANSWER: Saponification
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the acid anhydride of phosphoric acid?
ANSWER: Phosphorus (V) Oxide (also accept: P4O10)
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three values that are the highest for fluorine 1) Electronegativity 2) Electron Affinity 3) Reduction Potential
ANSWER: 1, 3
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice For an exothermic reaction, how do the forward and backward rates change respectively with an increase in temperature?
W) increase, increase
X) increase, decrease
Y) decrease, increase
Z) decrease, decrease
ANSWER: W) increase, increase
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three elements by increasing number of stable allotropes 1) Carbon 2) Phosphorus 3) Chlorine
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
25. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Ben reacts 2-butyne with nickel boride and hydrogen gas. What is the final product of this reaction?
W) Butane
X) cis-2-butene
Y) trans-2-butene
Z) Ethanal
ANSWER: X) cis-2-butene
