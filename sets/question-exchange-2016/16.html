1. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What term describes deposits of natural gas that contain a high sulfide content?
ANSWER: SOUR GAS
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is a DNA Molecule used to artificially carry foreign genetic material into a cell called?
W) Promoter
X) Operator
Y) Vector
Z) Transgene
ANSWER: Y) Vector
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which of the following are major types of vectors? I) Viral II) Plasmids III) Cosmids IV) Artificial Chromosomes
ANSWER: ALL (also accept: I, II, III, IV; ALL OF THEM)
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the angle which divides the spacelike and timelike zones on a Minkowski space-time diagram?
W) 15 degrees
X) 30 degrees
Y) 45 degrees
Z) 60 degrees
ANSWER: Y) 45 degrees
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Light travels at three hundred million meters per second. In hertz, what is the frequency of light with a wavelength of 600 nanometers?
ANSWER: 5 × 1014 (also accept: 500 TERAHERTZ)
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of star is characterized by its high rate of mass loss due to extreme winds?
W) Magnetar
X) Wolf-Rayet star
Y) Lambda Boötis star
Z) T Tauri star
ANSWER: X) Wolf-Rayet star
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What general mineral does the continuous series of Bowen’s reaction series concern?
ANSWER: PLAGIOCLASE (also accept: PLAGIOCLASE FELDSPAR)
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Name the scientist who came up with the concept of orbital hybridization?
ANSWER: LINUS PAULING
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By number, list which ones of the following are strong acids 1) HF 2) HCl 3) H2O 4) HCN 5) HClO3
ANSWER: 2 AND 5 (also accept: HCl AND HClO3)
11. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following numbers is a semiprime?
W) 3
X) 5
Y) 8
Z) 10
ANSWER: Z) 10
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer A jerk of 6 meters per second cubed is placed on an object. Starting from rest what is the distance the object covers in 4 seconds in kilometers?
ANSWER: 0.064 (also accept: 64 METERS)
