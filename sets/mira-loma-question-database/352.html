1. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following would not be changed if all of the members of a data set were doubled in size?
W) The standard deviation
X) The mean
Y) The outliers
Z) The skew
ANSWER: Z) The skew
1. BONUS Math Short Answer A population of birds has a mean lifetime of 20 years and a standard deviation of 3 years. Humans then infect the birds with a disease which removes 5 years from the birds' lives with a standard deviation of 4 years removed. After all birds, including new offspring, are infected with the disease, what will the probability be, to the nearest tenth of a percent, that a bird lives for at least 20 years?
ANSWER: 2.5%
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a characteristic of Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium?

X) No migration
Y) Random mating
Z) No natural selection
ANSWER: W) A small population size

7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A paleontologist recovers a small amount of tissue from a 400 year old preserved skin of a dodo.
