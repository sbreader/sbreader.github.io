2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding dynamic tide theory is false?
W) Amphidromic points represent points in the ocean that experience zero tidal range
X) Cotidal lines rotate around amphidromic points

ANSWER: Y) The number of amphidromic points present in the oceans may vary drastically throughout the year

ANSWER: Z) Lack of spiral arms

6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at Ames National Laboratory are studying the effects of the molecule 6-aminonicotinamide, a common inhibitor for the pentose phosphate pathway. For a patient without a functioning oxidative phase of the pentose phosphate pathway, which of the following best describes how they can get ribose-5-phosphate?
W) They can only get it by eating specific essential meat proteins
X) The reactions of the non-oxidative phase are reversible, so intermediates from glycolysis can be used to generate ribose-5-phosphate
Y) Ribose-5-phosphate is released after the damage of DNA, which can be picked up by the cell to be reused
Z) Ribose-5-phosphate can be recycled in the cell, but at a slower rate than the human body requires it to
ANSWER: X) The reactions of the non-oxidative phase are reversible, so intermediates from glycolysis can be used to generate ribose-5-phosphate

7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a reason why a pain receptor can cause interneurons to perceive pain in locations other than where the nociceptor is, a phenomenon known as referred pain?
W) The nociceptive pathway excites nociceptors that they converge with
