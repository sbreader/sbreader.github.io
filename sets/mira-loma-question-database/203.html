1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following geologic formations is most closely associated with divergent plate boundaries?
W) deep-ocean trenches
X) rift valleys
Y) fracture zones
Z) island arcs
ANSWER: X) rift valleys
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the line where the plane of the Earth's orbit intersects the plane of the moon's orbit, and points in the direction of the sun during eclipses?
ANSWER: LINE OF NODES
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Indicate, by number, all of the following 4 shoreline features which have origins in the deposition of sediment along the shore where the wave energy is low: 1: tombolo2: sea stack3: wave-cut cliff4: spit
ANSWER: 1 AND 4
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The existence of what type of low-energy radiation, predicted theoretically in the 1940s, detected in 1951, and emitted when an electron in a neutral hydrogen atom drops from its higher, metastable energy level, allows astronomers to map the cold hydrogen that fills much of the interstellar medium?
ANSWER: 21-CM RADIATION
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the general term given to the event of giant expansion in biodiversity which marked the beginning of the Paleozoic era?
ANSWER: CAMBRIAN EXPLOSION
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What group of animals, which is capable of determining its position not only in the ocean but also during long migrations, and includes most adult fish, squid, marine mammals and reptiles, consists of all animals capable of swimming by means of propulsion other than ocean currents?
ANSWER: NEKTON
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Great Basin Desert of Nevada, an example of a rainshadow desert, best exemplifies which of the following mechanisms for the rising of air?
W) frontal wedging
X) localized convective lifting
Y) converging
Z) orographic lifting
ANSWER: Z) orographic lifting
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Indicate, by name or number, all of the following 4 locations in the Milky Way Galaxy that would be most likely to contain Population I stars:1: nuclear bulge2: disk3: spiral arms4: halo
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In the evolution of a giant star, what object is formed directly after the helium flash, and consequently leads to the formation of a white dwarf?
ANSWER: PLANETARY NEBULA
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The cosmic background radiation that is observed today, with a temperature of roughly 2.7 K, consists of photons which were predominantly active in which of the following time periods following the Big Bang?
W) singularity
X) recombination
Y) dark age
Z) reionization
ANSWER: X) recombination
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What planet of Jupiter, which is predominantly composed of rock and ice, does not have a distinguishable core, and features a layer of liquid water around 100 km below its surface, is tidally locked to Jupiter and is well-known as the outermost Galilean moon in terms of orbit?
ANSWER: CALLISTO
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer If you observe distinct layers of metamorphic rock and sedimentary rock in a stratum, implying a period of uplift and erosion of overlying rocks, which basic type of unconformity will it MOST likely be?
ANSWER: NONCONFORMITY
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What common type of hydrogenous sediment, which can be up to 20 cm in diameter and is often littered across large areas of the deep seafloor, is a rounded, hard lump of metallic elements that precipitate in concentric layers around a central object?
ANSWER: MANGANESE NODULES
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following factors in the formation of soil is most influential, exerting the strongest impact on not only the nature of the soil itself but also the conditions of the other factors?
W) parent material
X) time
Y) climate
Z) topography
ANSWER: Y) climate
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Indicate, by name or number, all of the following 3 solar features that are thought to occur primarily in the photosphere, as opposed to other layers of the sun:1: granules2: spicules3: flares
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the general term given to the interval of radio waves between 21-cm radiation of neutral hydrogen and 18-cm radiation of OH, which is thought to contain likely wavelengths in the search for extraterrestrial life?
ANSWER: WATER HOLE
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Indicate, by name or number, all of the following 4 geologic features that is/are components of Karst topography: 1: cone of depression 2: sinkhole 3: solution valley 4: spring
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true regarding the cycle of the seasons?
W) light from the winter-solstice sun spreads out over a larger area of northern latitudes
X) at the vernal equinox, the sun crosses the celestial equator going northward
Y) due to certain circulation patterns in the atmosphere, northern and southern hemispheres exchange little heat
Z) the variation of Earth's distance from the sun is a significant factor in the timing of the seasons
ANSWER: Z) the variation of Earth's distance from the sun is a significant factor in the timing of the seasons
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the point on the main sequence of the H-R diagram where stars evolve to the right to become giants, and which is also used by astronomers to estimate the age of the cluster?
ANSWER: TURNOFF POINT
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What common type of fold is formed by the upfolding or arching of rock layers, and are often displayed where highways are built to cut through their deformed strata?
ANSWER: ANTICLINE
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What scale, developed and put into use in the 1970s, is a commonly used guide to tornado intensities, and produces a rating that is determined by assessing the worst damage produced by a storm?
ANSWER: ENHANCED FUJITA INTENSITY SCALE (also accept: EF INTENSITY SCALE)
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the streamlined asymmetrical hills composed of till, which vary in height and occur in clusters, and usually are clear indicators of the direction of movement of the glacier?
ANSWER: DRUMLINS
