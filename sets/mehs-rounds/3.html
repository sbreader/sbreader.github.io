1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer At what point could a force act upon a body to cause only linear acceleration of that body?
ANSWER: CENTER OF MASS (do not accept: CENTER OF GRAVITY)
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following quantities for which the internal energy of an ideal gas would be directly proportional 1) Temperature 2) Degrees of freedom 3) Entropy.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the concentration of chloride ions when 600 milliliters of a 0.40 molar solution of hydrochloric acid is mixed with 400 milliliters of a 0.20 molar solution of beryllium chloride?
W) 0.30 moles per liter
X) 0.40 moles per liter
Y) 0.50 moles per liter
Z) 0.60 moles per liter
ANSWER: X) 0.40 moles per liter
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Arrange the following three ions from smallest to largest atomic radius 1) Na+ 2) F- 3) Ca2+.
ANSWER: 1,3,2
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Researchers at Argonne National Lab are doing research on the value of the Hubble constant, which describes the rate at which the universe is expanding. Which of the following types of energy has the greatest effect on the universe’s expansion?
W) Kinetic
X) Gravitational
Y) Radiant
Z) Dark
ANSWER: Z) Dark
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Ames National Lab are doing research on stellar evolution. Identify all of the following three statements regarding stellar evolution that are true 1) Stars with less than 3 solar masses in the PMS phase of evolution follow the Hayashi track on the HR diagram 2) A star with 30 solar masses may undergo a type II supernova at the end of its life, collapsing into a black hole 3) Stars with masses less than or equal to the sun convert hydrogen to helium through the CNO cycle.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of a subsurface body of permeable rock that can contain or transmit groundwater?
ANSWER: AQUIFER
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following features does tidal locking explain?
W) Why the Earth’s ocean generates waves
X) Why there are 2 high tides and 2 low tides in a day
Y) Why the same side of the moon always faces the Earth
Z) Why tsunamis form from displacement
ANSWER: Y) Why the same side of the moon always faces the Earth
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice In degrees, what is the measure of an interior angle in a regular decagon?
W) 120
X) 135
Y) 144
Z) 150
ANSWER: Y) 144
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Calculate the sum of the zeroes in the quadratic equation: 3x squared minus 24x minus 60
ANSWER: 8
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following bonds has the greatest ionic character?
W) Li – F
X) Mg – O
Y) Rb – Cl
Z) Cs – F
ANSWER: Z) Cs – F
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name given to a compound that is formed when an atom with a small enough radius occupies a gap within a metal lattice?
ANSWER: INTERSTITIAL
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What angle do the electric and magnetic fields have to be relative to each other to be considered a crossed field?
ANSWER: 90° (also accept: π/2 rad)
7. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following describes the spontaneous escape of an electron from a well of negative potential difference?
W) Heisenberg’s uncertainty principle
X) Quantum tunneling
Y) Rydberg constant
Z) Paschen series
ANSWER: X) Quantum tunneling
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the organic molecules that can form different kinds of nucleic acids depending on their arrangement?
ANSWER: NUCLEOTIDES
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is responsible for assisting enzymes in biochemical transformation?
W) Coenzyme
X) Cofactor
Y) Apoenzyme
Z) Helper T cells
ANSWER: X) Cofactor
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Icebergs are produced when large chunks of ice break from the front of a glacier during what process?
ANSWER: CALVING
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of mineral groups contain quartzes, feldspars, and pyroxenes, and make up about 95% of our crust?
W) Silicates
X) Oxides
Y) Sulfates
Z) Carbonates
ANSWER: W) Silicates
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Given that there are 12 people in a party, how many distinct handshakes can occur between 2 people?
W) 24
X) 42
Y) 60
Z) 66
ANSWER: Z) 66
10. BONUS Math Short Answer If it takes Alex 15 minutes to clean a room and Barbara 25 minutes to clean the same room, how much time, in minutes, will it take for the room to be cleaned if they work together?
ANSWER: 75/8 (also accept: 9 3/8; 9.375)
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In an ideal step up transformer with 120 coils, 90 volts, and a resistance of 15 ohms on the primary side, calculate the secondary voltage, in volts, if there are 900 coils on the other side.
ANSWER: 675
11. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A truck is driving at a speed of 14 meters per second when the driver sees a squirrel running across the road, causing the driver to apply the brakes, causing the truck to decelerate at a rate of -3.5 meters per second squared until the truck stops moving. What is the displacement of the truck, in meters, during its deceleration?
W) 21
X) 28
Y) 35
Z) 56
ANSWER: X) 28
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at Ames National Lab have been studying nuclear reactors. Why is it preferable to slow down the neutrons produced by fission in a reactor?
W) To minimize neutron leakage
X) To minimize risk of nuclear meltdown
Y) To better induce fission in the reactor
Z) To better induce resonance capture of neutrons
ANSWER: Y) To better induce fission in the reactor
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Thomas Jefferson National Lab have been studying an elementary particle to determine its role in keeping nucleons together. What is the name of this elementary particle, known as the messenger of the strong nuclear force?
ANSWER: GLUON
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following terms is used to describe an individual’s collection of genes?
W) Phenotype
X) Genotype
Y) Gene expression
Z) Genome
ANSWER: X) Genotype
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the motor protein that is responsible for binding to actin to pull the actin inwards, generating the force in muscle contraction?
ANSWER: MYOSIN
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following rocks is most likely to form from volcanic lava?
W) Pumice
X) Conglomerate
Y) Marble
Z) Granite
ANSWER: W) Pumice
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What orbit shape does a comet have when its orbital velocity is greater than the escape velocity of the body it is orbiting?
ANSWER: HYPERBOLIC
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A month with 30 days has the same number of Mondays and Wednesdays. How many of the seven days of the week could possibly be the first day of this month?
ANSWER: 3

ANSWER: 150√3 (also accept: 150 ROOT 3)
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the electrochemical potential of a galvanic cell with a sodium anode (E nought = -2.71 V) and a chlorine cathode (E nought = 1.36 V)?
W) -4.07 V
X) -1.35 V
Y) 1.35 V

ANSWER: Z) 4.07 V
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the sum of the coefficients in the smallest balanced equation for the reaction: cobalt(II) acetate reacts with sodium hydroxide to form sodium acetate and cobalt hydroxide?
ANSWER: 6
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Lab have been studying the hall Effect in superconductors. Aside from a running current and electrostatic force, what other force must be acting on a current running through the conductor for the hall effect to occur?
ANSWER: MAGNETIC FORCE
17. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at Fermi National Lab are researching the properties of neutrinos. The oscillation of neutrinos proves that they have what property?
W) Kinetic energy
X) Charge
Y) Angular momentum
Z) Mass
ANSWER: Z) Mass
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the generic term to describe archaebacteria that live near hydrothermal vents?
ANSWER: EXTREMOPHILES
18. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not an example of a negative feedback loop?
W) Production of insulin in response to high glucose levels
X) Childbirth
Y) Thermoregulation
Z) ADH secretion to retain water when dehydrated
ANSWER: X) Childbirth
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the transition layer in the ocean characterized by a steep temperature gradient, located between warmer water at the surface and the cooler deep water?
ANSWER: THERMOCLINE
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The stratosphere is the second major layer in the Earth’s atmosphere. Identify all of the following three statements regarding the stratosphere that are true 1) The increase in temperature with increasing altitude is caused by the presence of ozone 2) The stratosphere contains most of Earth’s weather 3) The boundary separating the troposphere and the stratosphere is the stratopause.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
20. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Set A has 24 elements, and set B has 16 elements. What is the greatest possible number of elements that can exist in the intersection of sets A and B?
W) 0
X) 8
Y) 16
Z) 24
ANSWER: Y) 16
20. BONUS Math Short Answer If two mutually exclusive events A and B have probabilities 0.43 and 0.29, respectively, what is the probability of A or B occurring?
ANSWER: 0.72
21. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following set of conditions must be fulfilled for a body to be in static equilibrium?
W) The net force on the body is greater than zero, while the net torque on the body is zero
X) The linear momentum of the body is greater than zero, while the angular momentum of the body is zero
Y) The net force and the net torque on the body are zero
Z) Both the net force and the net torque on the body are greater than zero
ANSWER: Y) The net force and the net torque on the body are zero
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer In an RL circuit, the energy stored by the inductor decreases over time due to the reduction of the rate of change of what quantity?
ANSWER: CURRENT
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the empirical formula for cyclopropane?
W) CH2
X) C2H3
Y) CH
Z) CH3
ANSWER: W) CH2

BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 statements that are true regarding resonance structures 1) Electrons are delocalized in resonance structures 2) Sulfur trioxide has 2 resonance structures 3) Resonance structures where the most electronegative element has a positive charge are the most common.

23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the property that cell membranes exhibit by allowing some substances to pass through to the cell while restricting the flow of others?
ANSWER: SELECTIVE PERMEABILITY (also accept: SEMIPERMEABILITY)

ANSWER: TCAGCAGT
