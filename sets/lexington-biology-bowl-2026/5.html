Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Signal amplification can be made more efficient through what type of proteins responsible for packing relay proteins and signalling molecules together?
ANSWER: SCAFFOLDING PROTEINS
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following values is closest to the equilibrium potential of the Na+ ion in a resting neuron, in millivolts?
W) -90
X) -60
Y) +60
Z) +90
ANSWER: Y) +60
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of microscopy would be used to enhance the contrast in unstained, transparent samples using a light microscope?
W) Normarski
X) Bright field
Y) Dark field
Z) Phase contrast
ANSWER: W) Normarski
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of pregnancy refers to the attachment and growth of the embryo outside of the uterus?
ANSWER: ECTOPIC PREGNANCY
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Some people are naturally immune to HIV because of a 32-base pair deletion mutation on chromosome 3. This deletion results in the lack of what co-receptor for CD4, required for HIV to enter lymphocytes?
ANSWER: CCR5
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following four components of the cardiac conduction system in order 1) Bundle of His 2) Purkinje fibers 3) SA node 4) AV node
ANSWER: 3, 4, 1, 2
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Importins are proteins that recognize the nuclear localization signal and transport proteins into the nucleus. Given that the binding site of importins is primarily composed of acidic binding residues, which of the following residues is LEAST likely to be found in a nuclear localization signal?
W) Arginine
X) Histidine
Y) Lysine
Z) Glutamate
ANSWER: Z) Glutamate
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of outcomes in hybrid zones that would lead to increased species separation 1) Reinforcment 2) Fusion 3) Stability
ANSWER: 1 only
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Unlike other senses, olfactory cells synapse with the orbitofrontal cortex instead of what major region of the diencephalon?
ANSWER: THALAMUS
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Chemotherapy drugs, such as 5-fluorouracil, are often "base analogs" that mimic the structure of nitrogenous bases. At which specific point in the cell cycle would a cell treated with 5-fluorouracil be most likely to undergo apoptosis?
W) G1
X) S
Y) G2
Z) M
ANSWER: X) S
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Crossing over in meiosis is mediated by the formation of what protein structure between homologous chromosomes?
ANSWER: SYNAPTONEMAL COMPLEX
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In a population with species that have partially overlapping niches, the realized niche of any given species is best described as which of the following compared to it's fundamental niche?
W) Larger
X) Smaller
Y) Equal
Z) Unrelated
ANSWER: X) Smaller
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Yinghan is growing vegetables in his garden and observes purple streaks on the leaves of the mango plants. Which of the following nutrients is most likely deficient in his mango plants?
W) Boron
X) Nitrogen
Y) Chlorine
Z) Phosphorus
ANSWER: Z) Phosphorus
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Sharks, manta rays and skates belong to what clade of fish whose skeletons are not ossified and made of cartilage?
ANSWER: CHONDRICHTHYES
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The autonomic nervous system is composed of the sympathetic, parasympathetic, and what other division of the nervous system that regulates the digestive organs?
ANSWER: ENTERIC NERVOUS SYSTEM
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What family of proteins, commonly classified into v and t types, facilitate membrane fusion by overcoming the repulsive forces between lipid bilayers?
ANSWER: SNARE
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What process, occuring during the maturation of T and B cells, is responsible for the generation of new antibodies through random shuffling of gene segments?
ANSWER: VDJ RECOMBINATION (also accept: VJ RECOMBINATION)
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Organisms that drastically modify habitats, such as beavers, are known as what?
ANSWER: ECOSYSTEM ENGINEERS
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In which of the following organisms do spindle microtubules pass through cytoplasmic tunnels of an intact nuclear envelope during mitosis?
W) Diatoms
X) Dinoflagellates
Y) Yeasts
Z) Humans
ANSWER: X) Dinoflagellates
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Crypts and trichromes are found in what group of plants adapted for dry climates?
ANSWER: XEROPHYTES
