1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What family of viruses is known to derive their nuclear envelope from the host cell’s nuclear envelope, and replicate within the host cell nucleus, leaving small copies of chromosomes that remain latent?
ANSWER: Herpesviridae
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What technique for DNA amplification is a promising alternative to PCR-based diagnostics, because it is carried out in a single tube at a constant temperature?
W) Multiplex PCR
X) LAMP
Y) qPCR
Z) RT-PCR
ANSWER: X) LAMP
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Find the quadratic with integer coefficients and smallest positive leading coefficient that has the same sum and product of the roots as 4 x9 − 6 x6 − 3 x2 + 1?

3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice An ionic salt is dissolved in water and forms a red precipitate upon the addition of sodium carbonate to the solution. Which of the following could be the identity of the original ionic salt?

Y) Iron (III) sulfate
Z) Copper (II) acetate
ANSWER: Y) Iron (III) sulfate
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is a possible method of synthesizing 1,3,5-tribromobenzene?
W) Treating benzene with sulfur trioxide, followed by excess bromine, followed by sulfuric acid
