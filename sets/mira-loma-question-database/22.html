Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The cell organelle mainly responsible for photosynthesis is what?
ANSWER: CHLOROPLAST
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer The chloroplast is the site of which of the two main processes in photosynthesis?
ANSWER: LIGHT REACTIONS
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The released oxygen at the conclusion of photosynthesis comes from which reactant?
ANSWER: WATER
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer At which photosystem is water split?
ANSWER: PHOTOSYSTEM II
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What molecule is the final electron acceptor in the electron transport chain of cellular respiration?
ANSWER: OXYGEN
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer In oxidative respiration, ATP is actually generated by what process, using ATP synthase?
ANSWER: CHEMIOSMOSIS
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A transport protein that generates voltage across a membrane is known specifically by what term?
ANSWER: ELECTROGENIC PUMP
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer This voltage across the membrane is more commonly known as what?
ANSWER: MEMBRANE POTENTIAL
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The star-shaped array of short microtubules extending from each centrosome during mitosis is called what?
ANSWER: ASTER
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer The aster begins formation in which phase of mitosis?
ANSWER: PROPHASE
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name all of the following that are criteria of the Hardy-Weinberg theory: random mating, large population size, crossing over, independent assortment of chromosomes.
ANSWER: RANDOM MATING, LARGE POPULATION SIZE
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the other three criteria for the Hardy-Weinberg theory.
ANSWER: NO NATURAL SELECTION, NO NET MUTATIONS, NO GENE FLOW
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What enzyme allows some viruses to synthesize a full DNA strand from a single strand of RNA?
ANSWER: REVERSE TRANSCRIPTASE
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Viruses that utilize reverse transcriptase are called what?
ANSWER: RETROVIRUSES
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer If the mother and father have ABO blood types AB and B, respectively, what is the only ABO blood type the child could NOT have?
ANSWER: O
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Given that a child's blood type is O and his mother's blood type is AB, what blood type could the father have?
ANSWER: NOT POSSIBLE
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A failure of sister chromatids to separate during meiosis II is called what?
ANSWER: NONDISJUNCTION
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer Nondisjunction can also occur in meiosis I when what two entities do not separate?
ANSWER: HOMOLOGOUS CHROMOSOMES
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many total hydrogen bonds are found between a molecule of adenine and an molecule of thymine in DNA?
ANSWER: 2
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer Whose rules state that in a cell's DNA, the quantity of cytosine must equal the quantity of guanine, and the quantity of adenine must equal the quantity of thymine?
ANSWER: CHARGAFF
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Meselson and Stahl came up with which theory of DNA replication, which is now commonly accepted as correct?
ANSWER: SEMICONSERVATIVE MODEL
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer According to the semiconservative model, how many strands of daughter DNA will be identical to the parent after two replications?
ANSWER: NONE
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The two characteristics specific to mammals are what?
ANSWER: MAMMARY GLANDS, HAIR
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Marsupials lack which part of the reproductive complex that feeds the embryo during gestation?
ANSWER: PLACENTA
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The only marsupial that does not live in Australia is what?
ANSWER: POSSUM
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer The echidna and the platypus are types of what branch of mammals?
ANSWER: MONOTREMES
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Okazaki fragments can best be described as
W) bone chips caused by a fracture
X) segments of the lagging strand of DNA during elongation
Y) enamel shavings lost during a dental appointment
Z) pieces of leaves shed during the summer months
ANSWER: X) segments of the lagging strand of DNA during elongation
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Elongation is a part a larger process in which RNA is synthesized under the direction of DNA. Name this process.
ANSWER: TRANSCRIPTION
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Amino acids contain an amino and what other functional group?
ANSWER: CARBOXYL
15. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice How many structural isomers exist for the molecule butane?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 3
Z) 4
ANSWER: X) 2
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Organic chemistry is the study of compounds containing what element?
ANSWER: CARBON
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the primary enzyme found in saliva?
ANSWER: AMYLASE
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The senses of smell and taste are related to which receptors located above the sinuses?
ANSWER: OLFACTORY
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the four basic tastes.
ANSWER: SWEET, SOUR, SALTY, BITTER
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Amylose is the most basic type of what organic compound?
ANSWER: STARCH
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer What kind of linkage forms between the branches of amylopectin?
ANSWER: ALPHA 1-6 LINKAGE
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The linkage between a glycerol and a fatty acid in a lipid is called what?
ANSWER: ESTER LINKAGE
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer The quality of being both hydrophobic and hydrophilic simultaneously is termed what?
ANSWER: AMPHIPATHIC
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Enzymes are classified as what type of macromolecule?
ANSWER: PROTEIN
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the enzyme that allows humans to digest lactose.
ANSWER: LACTASE
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The first chamber of a cow's stomach is called what?
ANSWER: RUMEN
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In total, how many chambers does a cow stomach have?
ANSWER: 4
22. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following food items contains the most insoluble fiber?
W) apple skin
X) coconut shell
Y) orange peel
Z) plum skin
ANSWER: Z) plum skin
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer A food label claims 15g of total carbohydrates and 10g of sugars per serving. What is the maximum amount of insoluble fiber this food contains per serving?
ANSWER: 5 GRAMS
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The condition related to the consumption of raw eggs is called what?
ANSWER: SALMONELLA
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A nucleotide is composed of a phosphate group, a pentose, and what other compound?
ANSWER: A NITROGENOUS BASE
24. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the purine bases.
ANSWER: ADENINE AND GUANINE
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Vertebrates are distinguished from Chordates in what key difference?
ANSWER: NERVE CORD ATTACHED TO BACKBONE
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer How many vertebrae are in the human spine?
ANSWER: 34
