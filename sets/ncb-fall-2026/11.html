Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What effect describes the change in temperature of a real gas or liquid as it expands from a high-pressure zone to a low-pressure zone through a valve?
ANSWER: Joule Thomson Effect
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Consider two compounds AB and AB2 such that they have the same Ksp and molar solubility. What is the molar solubility of the two compounds given that they are both slightly soluble?
ANSWER: 0.25
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Typical epoxidation reactions, such as one using meta-chloroperoxybenzoic acid, produce an approximately equal mixture of both epoxide enantiomers. However, diethyl tartrate can be used in what reaction to selectively control the epoxide enantiomer obtained?
ANSWER: Sharpless Asymmetric Epoxidation (also accept: Sharpless Epoxidation)
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three isomers of xylene by increasing number of C-13 NMR Signals 1) ortho-xylene 2) meta-xylene 3) para-xylene
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What name is given to organometallic complexes, such as Ferrocene, featuring a metal atom between two parallel, planar organic aromatic rings?
ANSWER: Metallocenes (also accept: Sandwich Compounds; Sandwich Complexes)
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Hydrofluoric acid is unable to be kept in silicon dioxide since it creates what silicon containing acid?
ANSWER: Hexafluorosilicic Acid (also accept: H2SiF6)
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The Kohn–Sham equations are a way to state what equation in terms of density, which predicts how the state of a particle evolves over time, and determines the specific probability of finding it in any given location?
ANSWER: Schrodinger's Equation
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the mass spectrometry technique which measures the weight and identity of large molecules like proteins, DNA, and bacteria through the use of lasers, flight tubes, and measuring time?
ANSWER: MALDI-TOF Mass Spectrometry
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The acidity of HBF4 and HSbF6 is commonly measured using what scale which is used for very concentrated solutions of strong acids and superacids?
ANSWER: Hammett Acidity Function
5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds has the shortest C=O bond length?
W) Carbon dioxide
X) Formaldehyde
Y) Formamide
Z) Formyl Chloride
ANSWER: W) Carbon dioxide
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three reactions which will be favored at high temperatures 1) 1,2 addition of bromine to 1,3-butadiene 2) Retro Diels-Alder 3) Wittig Reaction
ANSWER: 2
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav wants to convert 2,2-propanediol to propane. By name or number, order the following three reagents from first to last to be applied 1) Sulfuric Acid 2) Potassium Hydroxide and Water 3) Hydrazine
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer EDTA typically has a denticity of 6, however under certain conditions it can have a denticity of 4 or 5. This makes EDTA an example of what type of ligand, which is polydentate and can change its denticity depending on the coordination environment, steric hindrance, or reaction conditions?
ANSWER: Flexidentate ligand
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three d orbitals by increasing energy in a square pyramidal complex 1) dxy 2) dx2-y2 3) dz2
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Kirchhoff's Law states that the enthalpy at some temperature T2 is equal to the enthalpy at some T1 plus the integral from T1 to T2 with respect to temperature of what quantity?
ANSWER: constant pressure heat capacity
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules is most likely to experience the nuclear overhauser effect?
W) Sulfur Trioxide
X) Silica
Y) Hemoglobin
Z) Argon
ANSWER: Y) Hemoglobin
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following reactants is most likely to exhibit a non-integer reaction order in the rate law?
W) Helium
X) Carbon
Y) Sulfur
Z) Chlorine
ANSWER: Z) Chlorine
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In which of the following solutions will AgCl be most soluble?
W) 0.1 M HCl
X) 0.1 M NaOH
Y) 0.1 M NaNO3
Z) Distilled water
ANSWER: Y) 0.1 M NaNO3
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the ruthenium-based catalysts with carbene complex ligands which are used to catalyze olefin metathesis?
ANSWER: Grubbs' Catalysts
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three aromatic compounds which could undergo azo coupling with benzenediazonium 1) Phenol 2) Aniline 3) Nitrobenzene
ANSWER: 1, 2
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three ligands which exhibit linkage isomerism 1) Carbonate 2) Thiosulfate 3) Nitrite
ANSWER: 2, 3
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The complex Co(NH3)5SO4Br exists in two different forms where one is blue and one is purple. This is due to what type of isomerism, where complexes exchange a ligand and a counterion?
ANSWER: Ionization Isomerism
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What equation of state is used to represent real gases as an infinite taylor series?
ANSWER: Virial equation of state (also accept: Virial Expansion)
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the temperature at which an antiferromagnetic material transitions into a paramagnetic state?
ANSWER: Neel Temperature
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In which of the following solvents would hydrochloric acid be most acidic?
W) tetrahydrofuran
X) Water
Y) Hexane
Z) Glacial Acetic Acid
ANSWER: Z) Glacial Acetic Acid
13. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following substitutions is most likely to lead to an inverse kinetic isotope effect?
W) Hydrogen-1 with Hydrogen-2
X) Boron-10 with Boron-11
Y) Carbon-12 with Carbon-13
Z) Nitrogen-14 with Nitrogen-15
ANSWER: W) Hydrogen-1 with Hydrogen-2
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer While the Schiemann Reaction is used to fluorinate aryl diazonium, performing the chlorination, bromination, and iodination of aryl diazoniums is done through what reaction which uses Copper (I) Halides?
ANSWER: Sandmeyer Reaction
14. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Bryan is trying to fluorinate methylcyclohexane with KF. Which of the following reagents might Bryan find useful?
W) 12-Crown-4
X) 15-Crown-5
Y) 18-Crown-6
Z) 21-Crown-7
ANSWER: Y) 18-Crown-6
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What name is given to the phenomenon in which certain ligands in square planar complexes speed up the substitution of the ligand directly opposite to them?
ANSWER: Trans Effect
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What term is used to describe substances which absorb enough moisture from the air to completely dissolve into a liquid solution?
ANSWER: Deliquescent
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The shapes of the wavefunctions for the particle in the one-dimensional box can be written as a function of which of the following types of functions?
W) Sine
X) Cosine
Y) Sine squared
Z) Cosine Squared
ANSWER: W) Sine
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is studying the decay pathways of a compound A, in which A yields B and A yields C via parallel pathways. In this pathway, the half life of the decomposition of A to B is 200 s and the half life of the decomposition of A to C is 300 s. What is the overall half life of A?
ANSWER: 120
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the molecular cation which is believed to have been the first molecule formed in the universe after the Big Bang and is thought to be the strongest acid in existence?
ANSWER: Helium Hydride (also accept: HeH+; Helium hydride ion)
17. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Protonated molecular hydrogen, which has formula H3+, is found in the interstellar medium. Which of the following best explains why this ion can survive for long periods of time in outer space?
W) Outer space has a low temperature and density
X) Planets can successfully shield the molecule from cosmic rays
Y) The vacuum in outer space caused the decomposition of H3+ to occur via a pathway with a higher activation energy
Z) H3+ is the most stable form of hydrogen in the interstellar medium
ANSWER: W) Outer space has a low temperature and density
