1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The Suess group at MIT is exploring iron sulfur clusters in cells. If an iron sulfur cluster has four iron atoms and four sulfur atoms with a net charge of +2, what is the expected spin state of the lower oxidation state iron species in the cluster?
ANSWER: 2
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The Suess group at MIT is exploring a redox active protein with four iron atoms and four sulfur atoms in a cubane structure. One of the irons is redox active and changes between iron III and iron II. By name or number, identify all of the statements which are true about this complex. 1) Increasing the number of hydrogen bonds to the sulfur atoms will increase the standard reduction potential of the cluster 2) All of the iron centers are high spin 3) Each iron is formally bonded to four sulfur atoms
ANSWER: 1, 2, and 3 (also accept: all)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Xavier is trying to convert a carboxylic acid to an acid chloride using oxalyl chloride with dichloromethane as a solvent. To accelerate the reaction, Xavier should add a drop of which of the following substances to his reaction flask?
W) N,N-dimethylformamide
X) water
Y) 4-dimethylaminopyridine
Z) tosylic acid
ANSWER: W) N,N-dimethylformamide
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Zoe is trying to perform a Heck coupling between an aryl bromide and an olefin using 1 mole percent of palladium acetate and 2 mole percent of triphenylphosphine along with a slight excess of base. By name or number, identify all of the following three changes which would dramatically improve Zoe’s yield 1) Using 2 mole percent palladium acetate 2) Using 3 mole percent triphenylphosphine 3) Changing the palladium source to Pd(dba)2
ANSWER: 2 and 3
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Antiferromagnets have 0 net magnetization, regardless of the external field applied. What is the name of the temperature above which antiferromagnetic materials become paramagnetic due to thermal disorder?
ANSWER: Neel temperature (also accept: Neel)
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following five elements which are ferromagnetic 1) Cobalt 2) Copper 3) Ruthenium 4) Dysprosium 5) Holmium
ANSWER: 1 and 4
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Two of the side lengths of a triangle are 50 units and 51 units. If the tangent of the angle measure between the two sides is 8 / 15, what is the area of the triangle?
ANSWER: 600
4. BONUS Math Short Answer To the nearest integer, what is the value of 27182719 / 27192718?
ANSWER: 1000
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Actin molecules polymerize and depolymerize at both plus and minus ends. Which of the following statements correctly describes the rate of actin polymerization in the absence of a capping protein?
W) Polymerization occurs quickest at the plus end
X) Polymerization occurs quickest at the minus end
Y) Polymerization occurs equally quickly at the plus and minus ends
Z) Polymerization does not occur without capping proteins
ANSWER: W) Polymerization occurs quickest at the plus end
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In muscle cells, what molecule caps the minus end of actin filaments?
W) CapZ
X) Tropomodulin
Y) Tropomyosin
Z) CapA
ANSWER: X) Tropomodulin
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The root-mean-square radial velocity of stars in one galaxy is 20 kilometers per second. In kilometers per second, which of the following is closest to the velocity dispersion of the stars in the galaxy?
W) 25
X) 35
Y) 45
Z) 60
ANSWER: X) 35
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The concept of cosmological redshift is described by what metric which is based on the universe being isotropic and homogenous?
ANSWER: FLRW metric
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What model of solids predicts that the heat capacity of a solid is proportional to the temperature cubed at low temperature by using phonons to describe atomic lattice vibrations?
ANSWER: Debye model
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer The dependence of electrical resistance with respect to temperature has multiple different contributions. One term with logarithmic dependence arises from what effect, which describes the interaction of magnetic impurities and conduction electron magnetic moments?
ANSWER: Kondo effect
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The mutations D10A and H840A affect which two domains in Streptococcus pyogenes Cas9, respectively?
ANSWER: RuvC; HNH
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three statements about KRAB domains that are true 1) Transcription factors with a KRAB domain generally repress RNA polymerase activity 2) Transcription factors with a KRAB domain generally promote RNA polymerase activity 3) KRAB is often used alongside CRISPR technology to further modulate transcription
ANSWER: 1 and 3
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the dual polyhedron of a regular icosahedron?
ANSWER: Dodecahedron (also accept: regular dodecahedron)
9. BONUS Math Short Answer Evaluate the integral from negative infinity to infinity of e−(x2 + 8x + 9) dx.
ANSWER: e7 √π (also accept: √π e7; e7 · √π)
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Lin-Shu density wave theory was proposed as a solution to what problem, which suggested that differential rotation of a galaxy would lead to dissolution of the spiral arms?
ANSWER: Winding problem
10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of volcanic eruptions occurs when seawater enters the magma chamber and causes a steam explosion?
W) Plinian
X) Phreatic
Y) Surtseyan
Z) Strombolian
ANSWER: Y) Surtseyan
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Because of Jahn-Teller distortion, hexaaquacopper (II) exhibits tetragonal elongation. In such a complex, what is the highest energy d orbital?
ANSWER: dx2 − y2
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is true of the lowest-energy d -orbitals in the complex [Cu(CN)4]2-?
W) It is a singly degenerate dxy orbital
X) It is a singly degenerate dz2 orbital
Y) It is a set of doubly degenerate dxz and dyz orbitals
Z) It is a set of triply degenerate dxy, dxz, and dyz orbitals
ANSWER: W) It is a singly degenerate dxy orbital
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers in the Kavli Institute at MIT have discovered 4 new exoplanets using the Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite. Although TESS relies on the transit method, the first exoplanets were discovered using what other method which involves measuring the shift in the wavelength of spectral lines emitted by a parent star?
ANSWER: Doppler radial velocity method
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer The Liu group at MIT is researching string theory and its relationship with quantum gravity. String theory relies on how many spatial and temporal dimensions, respectively?
ANSWER: 9 spatial and 1 temporal
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What specific distribution describes the probability of k successes in n draws from a finite population without replacement?
ANSWER: Hypergeometric (do not accept: geometric)
13. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the value of the approximation of arctan(1) using the first three nonzero terms of the power series of arctan(x) centered at x = 0?
ANSWER: 13/15
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true about g-anisotropy in EPR spectroscopy?
W) Any deviation from the g-factor of the free electron is due to spin-orbit coupling
X) For one single transition, the peak measured at the lower magnetic field corresponds to a lower g-tensor component in the corresponding dimension
Y) In the absence of hyperfine splitting, g-anisotropy accounts for different splitting patters in EPR spectroscopy
Z) Different g values for the three different components of a g-tensor resuts in a rhombic splitting pattern
ANSWER: X) For one single transition, the peak measured at the lower magnetic field corresponds to a lower g-tensor component in the corresponding dimension
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 3 species which would result in one isotropic peak in EPR spectroscopy 1) [Mn(H2O)6]2+ 2) [Cr(H2O)6]3+ 3) [Fe(H2O)6]2+
ANSWER: 1 and 2
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at MIT’s Mediated Matter group are currently studying ways to ameliorate the decrease in the global bee population. Bees are quite unique in that bees employ a haplo-diploid sex-determination system. Which of the following fractions gives the coefficient of relatedness between a female bee and her maternal aunt?
W) 1/8
X) 1/4
Y) 3/8
Z) 3/4
ANSWER: Y) 3/8
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at MIT’s Bartel group are currently studying RNA regulation. What’s the name of the complex in bacterial cells that’s responsible for the degradation of RNA, typically mRNA?
ANSWER: Degradosome
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true about plant nutrients?
W) Symptoms of iron deficiency usually appear first in older leaves.
X) Aluminum toxicity is a major problem for crops grown in acidic soil environments.
Y) Manganese deficiency directly affects the oxygen-evolving complex.
Z) Chlorosis is a general condition that can be caused by several types of nutrient deficiencies.
ANSWER: W) Symptoms of iron deficiency usually appear first in older leaves.
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer In some cells infected with Rhizhobia, what pink pigment protein buffers oxygen concentration to allow both cellular respiration and nitrogenase activity to occur?
ANSWER: Leghemoglobin

W) 250 nanometers
X) 600 nanometers
Y) 1560 nanometers

ANSWER: Z) 1690 nanometers
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer An ideal parallel plate capacitor with circular plates of radius 1 millimeter is charged rapidly, so that the electric field increases from 0 to 9 volts per meter in 10-16 seconds. 20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the name of the statement that there is no set with cardinality strictly between that of the integers and the real numbers?
ANSWER: Continuum hypothesis
20. BONUS Math Short Answer Suppose that more than half of all n-digit integers contain the digit 7. What is the smallest possible value of the positive integer n?
ANSWER: 7
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements best explains why the 2p sigma bonding orbitals in dinitrogen are higher in energy than the 2p pi bonding orbitals?
W) Nitrogen’s p orbitals are relatively diffuse which allows for effective pi bonding interactions
X) The small size of nitrogen causes the sigma bonding set to rise in energy due to electrostatics
Y) The p orbital sigma bonding set interacts with the s orbital sigma bonding set which causes further mixing
Z) The shape of p orbitals makes them favor pi bonding
ANSWER: Y) The p orbital sigma bonding set interacts with the s orbital sigma bonding set which causes further mixing
