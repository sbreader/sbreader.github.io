1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the type of karst formation in which the landscape consists of a maze of isolated steep-sided hills which rise abruptly from the ground, interconnected with caves and passageways?
ANSWER: TOWER KARST
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following 4 statements that is/are TRUE: 1: tower karst forms in wet tropical and subtropical regions having thin beds of loosely jointed limestone 2: sinkholes can form gradually when limestone immediately below soil is dissolved by rainwater that is freshly charged with limestone 3: karst regions lack surface drainage systems 4: where streams do exist in karst regions, their paths are very long
