Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An electron is traveling with its velocity directed in the positive x direction through a uniform magnetic field directed in the positive y direction. In what direction does the magnetic field act on the electron?
ANSWER: NEGATIVE Z
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice According to the Born interpretation of quantum mechanics, the square of an orbital wave function describes which of the following?
W) The probability that a electron can be found in a certain area
X) The frequency of the electron
Y) The speed of the electron
Z) The exact position of the electron
ANSWER: W) The probability that a electron can be found in a certain area
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the term used to describe molecules with a nonzero dipole moment?
W) Ionic
X) Nonpolar
Y) Polar
Z) Secondary
ANSWER: Y) Polar
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the process in which a substance in the gaseous state transitions directly into a solid?
ANSWER: DEPOSITION
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab have studied how secondary organic aerosols have affected the Amazon rainforest’s atmosphere. In what atmospheric layer does the majority of the atmosphere’s mass reside in?
ANSWER: TROPOSPHERE
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab are using the MRI experiment to elucidate the creation of celestial bodies around black holes. Identify all of the following three statements regarding black holes that are true 1) The event horizon of a black hole is the point where the escape velocity surpasses the speed of light 2) Black holes gain mass through accretion 3) Black holes are inside their own Schwarzschild radii.
ANSWER: ALL (also accept: 1, 2, AND 3)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the astronomical term that describes the gas, dust, and radiation found between stars in a galaxy?
ANSWER: INTERSTELLAR MEDIUM (do not accept: NEBULA)
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What astronomical term describes the process of separating out different constituents of a planetary body as a result of its physical or chemical behavior?
ANSWER: PLANETARY DIFFERENTIATION (also accept: DIFFERENTIATION)
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is the expected value for the sum of three consecutive dice rolls?
W) 9
X) 10.5
Y) 12.25
Z) 15
ANSWER: X) 10.5
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the probability of getting at least 3 heads when flipping a total of 5 coins?
ANSWER: 1/2 (also accept: 0.5; 50%)
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a physical change?
W) Burning of a candle
X) Heating sugar to form caramel
Y) Mixing hydrochloric acid with a weak base
Z) Vaporization of gasoline
ANSWER: Z) Vaporization of gasoline
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 molecules that are polar 1) Sulfur trioxide 2) Benzene 3) Ammonia 4) Carbon monoxide.
ANSWER: 3 AND 4
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Jebediah Kerman throws a baseball with an initial speed of 8 meters per second at a 45 degree angle from the ground. To two significant figures and in meters, how far away does the baseball land from Jebediah?
ANSWER: 6.5
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer How many degrees of freedom does an ideal monatomic gas have?
ANSWER: 3
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of structures, which are examples of convergent evolution, are similar structures which have evolved independently in two organisms but serve the same purpose?
ANSWER: ANALOGOUS STRUCTURES
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding model organisms 1) They are easy to grow in labs 2) Lessons learned from a model organism are often applicable to a much wider group 3) They are species that are extensively studied to understand biological phenomena.
ANSWER: ALL (also accept: 1, 2, 3)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of soils is dark and fertile and forms in forested regions in the eastern part of the United States?
W) Pedalfer
X) Pedocal
Y) Laterite
Z) Humus
ANSWER: W) Pedalfer
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In the interstellar medium, dust scatters photons with shorter wavelengths of light and causes stars to appear redder than they should be. What astronomical effect explains why this happens in stars?
ANSWER: INTERSTELLAR REDDENING
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Rounded to the nearest whole number, which of the following is the square root of 114?
W) 10
X) 11
Y) 12
Z) 13
ANSWER: X) 11
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the sum of the integers from 1 to 99?
ANSWER: 4950
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Given that the momentum of a photon is 0.2 kilogram meters per second, and assuming that planck’s constant is 6.6 × 10-34, what is the photon’s Broglie wavelength to two significant figures and in scientific notation?
ANSWER: 3.3 × 10-33 METERS
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the potential difference, in volts, across a parallel plate capacitor containing a uniform electric field of 4 newtons per coulomb with a plate separation of 8 centimeters?
ANSWER: 0.32
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab are conducting research on nearly massless, electrically neutral particles that normally pass through matter unhindered. What particles are they most likely studying?
ANSWER: NEUTRINOS
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying how tiny atmospheric particles called aerosols influence clouds. Identify all of the following three statements regarding aerosols that are true 1) Aerosols are atmospheric particles that can be solid 2) Aerosols are atmospheric particles that can be liquid 3) Aerosols are atmospheric particles that can only be gaseous.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In which of the following cell organelles does the Krebs cycle occur?
W) Mitochondria
X) Chloroplasts
Y) Peroxisomes
Z) Nuclei
ANSWER: W) Mitochondria
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Rank the following steps in cellular respiration in chronological order 1) Krebs cycle 2) Glycolysis 3) Electron transport chain.
ANSWER: 2,1,3
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Greenhouse gases are gases in the atmosphere that trap the Sun’s heat. Which of the following gases exhibits the greatest greenhouse effect in Earth’s atmosphere?
W) Carbon dioxide
X) Water vapor
Y) Methane
Z) Ozone
ANSWER: X) Water vapor
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What soil horizon is rich in minerals that have moved down from the A and E horizons?
ANSWER: B HORIZON
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What parent trigonometric function has a period of 2π and passes through the origin?
ANSWER: SINE FUNCTION (also accept: SINE)
15. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the smallest value of x in radians within the interval from 0 to 2π where the sine of x is equal to 1/2?
ANSWER: π/6 (do not accept: 30 o)
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following substances is not a compound?
W) Diamond
X) Limestone
Y) Water
Z) Lye
ANSWER: W) Diamond
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the magnetic quantum number of a chlorine atom’s final electron in its ground state?
ANSWER: 0
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab are conducting research on magnetic reconnection events. Which of the following features is most likely to be produced on the sun during reconnection events?
W) Starquakes
X) Auroras
Y) Solar flares
Z) Supernovas
ANSWER: Y) Solar flares
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab have conducted research on neutrinos using deep underground detectors. Unfortunately, when scientists were trying to detect neutrinos being produced by the sun, they only observed 1/3 of the expected amount. Out of the three flavors of neutrinos, which kind was the sun emitting as a result of nuclear fusion?
ANSWER: ELECTRON NEUTRINOS
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organelles is present in animal cells but not in plant cells?
W) Chloroplast
X) Golgi apparatus
Y) Cell wall
Z) Lysosome
ANSWER: Z) Lysosome
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer To what site does mRNA first bind in a ribosome?
ANSWER: A-SITE
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Stars with masses less than or equal to that of the Sun convert hydrogen to helium using what type of nuclear fusion?
ANSWER: PROTON-PROTON CHAIN
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of equilibrium is reached when the rate of solar radiation absorption equals the rate of emission in Earth’s atmosphere?
ANSWER: RADIATIVE EQUILIBRIUM
20. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is a prime number?
W) 169
X) 171
Y) 173
Z) 175
ANSWER: Y) 173
20. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the largest prime factor of 114?
ANSWER: 19
21. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following thermodynamic processes, when performed, would always result in no work being done on a closed system?
W) Adiabatic
X) Cyclic
Y) Isochoric
Z) Isobaric
ANSWER: Y) Isochoric
21. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following quantities is not conserved in a closed system?
W) Momentum
X) Angular momentum
Y) Velocity
Z) Charge
ANSWER: Y) Velocity
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following nucleons is the most common isotope of chlorine?
W) Chlorine-33
X) Chlorine-35
Y) Chlorine-37
Z) Chlorine-39
ANSWER: X) Chlorine-35
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following four elements that are liquid at room temperature and atmospheric pressure 1) Silicon 2) Bromine 3) Lithium 4) Mercury.
ANSWER: 2 AND 4
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When polar substances such as water are placed in a graduated cylinder, a meniscus is formed. This is an example of what phenomenon?
ANSWER: ADHESION (do not accept: HYDROGEN BONDING; COHESION)
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding surface tension 1) Surface tension is the primary reason that insects denser than water are capable of floating and sliding on a body of water 2) Liquid water has the greatest surface tension of all liquids 3) Surface tension is the measure of energy required to increase the surface area of a liquid due to intermolecular forces.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
