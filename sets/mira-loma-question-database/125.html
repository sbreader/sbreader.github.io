1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the first element in the aluminum group?
ANSWER: Boron
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer After which element does the Lanthanide series start?
ANSWER: Lanthanum
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the abbreviation of kinetic energy?
ANSWER: KE
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What does a dot in a chemical equation usually refer to?
ANSWER: loosely bonded
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of change is occurring in the digestion of food?
ANSWER: Chemical
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Using joules, what is the most common unit for specific heat?
ANSWER: joules per grams degree Celsius (J/g°C)
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the chemical formula for the compound octane?
ANSWER: C8H18
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the relative electrical charge of a proton?
ANSWER: +1
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the chemical formula for the substance Galena?
ANSWER: PbS
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the anion name of bromine?
ANSWER: Bromide
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the classical name of titanium (IV)?
W) Titanous
X) Titan
Y) Titanic
Z) Titanium
ANSWER: Y) Titanic
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the electrical charge of acetate?
ANSWER: -1
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the anion name for the anion formula ClO-
ANSWER: Hypochlorite
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many objects is equivalent to one gross?
ANSWER: 144 objects
8. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the value of one hundred moles of ions?
ANSWER: 602.2 × 1023 ions
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the Greek letter delta written above or below an arrow usually interpreted as, in a chemical equation?
ANSWER: Heat (also accept: Heat is added)
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What are compounds containing only hydrogen and carbon called?
ANSWER: Hydrocarbons
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What are the discrete packets through which energy is emitted called?
ANSWER: Quanta
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the electron configuration of neon?
ANSWER: 1s22s22p6
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In organic chemistry, what is a reaction in which two substances join to produce one compound called?
ANSWER: Addition Reaction
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What are moth balls made of today?
ANSWER: Para-dichlorobenzene
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the degree equivalent of tertiary alcohol?
ANSWER: 3 degrees
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the boiling point in °C of methyl ethyl ether?
ANSWER: 8°C
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the general formula for ethers?
ANSWER: ROR'
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the small repeating unit of a polymer?
ANSWER: Monomer
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What enzyme catalyzes the digestion of maltose to glucose?
ANSWER: Maltase
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the bond connecting amino acids in a protein called?
ANSWER: Peptide linkage (also accept: Peptide bond)
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the most common name of the structure of DNA?
ANSWER: Double helix
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the process by which DNA directs the synthesis of RNA called?
ANSWER: Transcription
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer According to what theorem is the conservation of energy a consequence of fact that the laws of physics do not change over time?
ANSWER: Noether's theorem
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer What type of photon is the lowest quantum mechanical energy state of photons?
ANSWER: Virtual photons
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What quasiparticle represents the quantization of the modes of lattice vibrations of periodic, elastic crystal structures of solids?
ANSWER: Phonon
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer The energy of a harmonic oscillator is alternatively what two types of energy?
ANSWER: Kinetic and potential
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In an ideal gas, the interaction potential between particles is essentially what generalized function?
ANSWER: Delta function
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the name for the thermodynamic property that is a measure of the energy not available for work in a thermodynamic process?
ANSWER: Entropy
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the SI unit for energy?
ANSWER: Joules
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When oil reacts with oxygen, what type of energy is released?
ANSWER: Potential energy
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the process in which chemical potential energy is retrieved from sunlight by plants?
ANSWER: Photosynthesis
20. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the name of a physical system that does not interact with its surroundings?
ANSWER: Isolated system
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What type of energy includes thermal energy and radiant energy?
ANSWER: Kinetic energy
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are three major forms of fossil fuels?
ANSWER: Coal, oil, and natural gas
22. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What energy does hydroelectric power use from moving water to make electricity?
ANSWER: Kinetic energy
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is a kind of renewable energy that is found in landfills?
ANSWER: Biomass
23. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What kind of energy is associated with the motion or position of an object?
ANSWER: Mechanical energy
23. BONUS Energy Short Answer What kind of energy were main reasons for Chernobyl and the Three Mile Island disaster?
ANSWER: Nuclear energy
24. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What kind of energy is the part the total, internal energy of a sample of matter that results in the material's temperature?
ANSWER: Thermal energy
24. BONUS Physics Short Answer The thermal energy is partitioned equally between what all available quadratic degrees of particles?
ANSWER: Degrees of freedom
25. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What kind of energy is of electromagnetic waves?
ANSWER: Radiant energy
25. BONUS Physics Short Answer What measure is needed to calculate the quantity of radiant energy with respect to time?
ANSWER: Integrating radiant flux
