8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Nucleotide analogs have provided great insight into the molecular structure and function of ATP-binding proteins and nucleotide-binding proteins in general. Which of the following statements is NOT true regarding these nucleotide analogs?
W) MgF3- can be used to mimic the transition state for a phosphoryl transfer reaction
X) MgADP·VO4 can be used to trap the metaphosphate transition state for phosphoryl transfer

9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at Brookhaven National Lab have been measuring the rates of diffusion through cell membranes to aid in the development of drug delivery. Which of the following scenarios best increases the fluidity of a cellular membrane?
