Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Boyle's law relates what two variables of a gas?
ANSWER: pressure and volume
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What quantity in classical mechanics is defined as the integral of force with respect to distance?
ANSWER: Work
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If Lucas and 19 other people at the US Physics Camp and 5 of them are chosen at random for the IPhO team, then to the nearest percent, what is the chance that Lucas is on the IPhO team?
ANSWER: 25%
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 cloud types from lowest to highest altitude 1) Altocumulus 2) Cirrus 3) Stratus
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What experiment, in an attempt to simulate primordial conditions on Earth, demonstrated that amino acids could form from inorganic precursors?
ANSWER: Miller-Urey experiment
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best classifies ammonium chloride?
W) Weak acid
X) Weak base
Y) Strong acid
Z) Strong base
ANSWER: W) Weak acid
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice When a blue light is shined on a metal, Shayan observes electrons emitted from its surface. Which of the following colors of light must also cause the metal to emit electrons?
W) Red
X) Yellow
Y) Green
Z) Violet
ANSWER: Z) Violet
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What are the roots of the quadratic f(x)=x2-13x+42?
ANSWER: 6 and 7
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 4 metamorphic rocks by decreasing metamorphic grade 1) Gneiss 2) Phyllite 3) Slate 4) Schist
ANSWER: 1, 4, 2, 3
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Filip's patient has been diagnosed with pernicious anemia. Which of the following vitamins is his patient most likely to be deficient in?
W) B1
X) B3
Y) B9
Z) B12
ANSWER: Z) B12
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following substances has the highest entropy?
W) water at 0 degrees celsius
X) ice at 0 degrees celsius
Y) water at 100 degrees celsius
Z) steam at 100 degrees celsius
ANSWER: Z) steam at 100 degrees celsius
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What ideal thermodynamic engine has the maximum possible efficiency?
ANSWER: Carnot engine
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Identify all of the following functions that are even 1) cosine of x 2) cotangent of x 3) cosecant of x
ANSWER: 1 only
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes how the Sun is currently moving on the H-R diagram?
W) Up and to the left
X) Up and to the right
Y) Down and to the left
Z) Down and to the right
ANSWER: X) Up and to the right
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer During his Campbell doomscroll, Filip realizes he wants to review the structure of antibodies. What molecular interactions are involved in connecting the light and heavy domains of an antibody?
ANSWER: Disulfide bridges
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Galvanized steel consists of normal steel coated with what other metal to prevent oxidation of the iron?
ANSWER: Zinc
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A parallel-plate capacitor with a charge of 3 Coulombs is disconnected from a circuit. If a slab of insulating wood is inserted between the two plates, what is the magnitude of the charge, in Coulombs, on each plate?
ANSWER: 3
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the y-intercept of the line connecting the points (3,5) and (-4, -2)?
ANSWER: (0,2) (also accept: 2)
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following mechanisms primarily resists gravity during the protostar stage?
W) Nuclear fusion
X) Magnetic pressure
Y) Thermal pressure
Z) Degeneracy pressure
ANSWER: Y) Thermal pressure
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What region of undifferentiated, actively dividing cells is located at the tips of roots and shoots, enabling plants to extend?
ANSWER: Apical meristem
21. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements has the highest first electron affinity?
W) Nitrogen
X) Oxygen
Y) Fluorine
Z) Neon
ANSWER: Y) Fluorine
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer Ethan is spinning a steel weight tied to a massless rope. If he wants to spin it at double the angular velocity, by what factor should he increase his tugging force on the rope?
ANSWER: 4
23. BONUS Math Short Answer Five people line up for a group photo. If two of them do not want to stand next to each other, how many distinct lineups can they form?
ANSWER: 72
24. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three organisms that have four-chambered hearts 1) Yellow songbird 2) Marsh crocodile 3) Bluefin tuna
ANSWER: 1 and 2
25. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many total electrons would be present in a filled 5f subshell?
ANSWER: 14
26. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the name of the effect where light bends around obstacles and forms an interference pattern?
ANSWER: Diffraction
27. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the average value of the function f(x) = x+5 from x = 0 to x = 3?
ANSWER: 13/2 (also accept: 6.5)
28. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 types of crystal faces by increasing inhibition experienced during crystal growth 1) Euhedral 2) Subhedral 3) Anhedral
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
29. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following scenarios would best represent epistasis?
W) Homozygous recessive alleles at one locus mask the phenotype of another locus
X) A single gene influences unrelated phenotypes
Y) Two genes independently assort and additively contribute to a phenotype
Z) One allele is expressed over another allele at a locus
ANSWER: W) Homozygous recessive alleles at one locus mask the phenotype of another locus
30. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Shayan is titrating his acetic acid, which has a pKa of 4.76, with 0.1 molar sodium hydroxide. To three significant figures, what is the pH of the solution at the half-equivalence point?
ANSWER: 4.76
31. BONUS Physics Short Answer A ball starts at rest at the top of a hill and rolls without slipping until it reaches the bottom. If the height of the hill is quadrupled, how many times longer will it take the ball to reach the bottom of the hill?
ANSWER: 2
32. BONUS Math Short Answer Filip walks at 2 meters per second and runs at 5 meters per second. If Filip's biology class is 480 meters away and he takes 2 minutes to get to his class, how many meters did Filip have to run?
ANSWER: 400
33. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which empirical law relates the luminosity and velocity dispersion of an elliptical galaxy?
ANSWER: FABER-JACKSON RELATION
34. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Eegan hears the vibrations of a tuning fork, and is wondering how his brain interprets the incoming sound. Which of the following lobes of the brain would process this information?
W) Frontal
X) Parietal
Y) Temporal
Z) Occipital
ANSWER: Y) Temporal
35. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many moles of oxygen gas are required for the complete combustion of one mole of methane?
ANSWER: 2
36. BONUS Physics Short Answer Shayan throws a soccer ball at a speed of 50 meters per second at a 60 degree angle to the horizontal. Ignoring air resistance, what is the ratio between the range of the soccer ball and its maximum height?
ANSWER: 4√3/3
37. BONUS Math Short Answer Identify all of the following statements that are true about Pascal's triangle 1) the sum of the elements in a row of Pascal's triangle is always a power of 2 2) coloring all of the odd numbers in Pascal's triangle results in a Sierpinski triangle 3) the triangular numbers are found in the third diagonal of Pascal's triangle.
ANSWER: All
38. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What class of chemically peculiar variable stars found near Delta Scuti stars on the H-R diagram have strong magnetic fields and exhibit high frequency, low amplitude pulsations likely due to primary pulsations in higher p-modes?
ANSWER: Rapidly Oscillating Ap Stars
39. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three structures that are ectoderm derivatives 1) Myocardium 2) Tooth enamel 3) Adrenal cortex
ANSWER: 2 only
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Both Ethans are hiking up a mountain at dawn and see a layer of clouds over the valley below them. Which type of inversion is most likely responsible for this layer?
W) Subsidence inversion
X) Radiation inversion
Y) Frontal inversion
Z) Marine inversion
ANSWER: X) Radiation inversion
