2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What law relates the diffusive flux to the concentration field, by postulating that the flux goes from regions of high concentration to regions of low concentration, with a magnitude that is proportional to the concentration gradient?
ANSWER: Fick's first law
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice According to the Bohr effect, when blood in a more acidic environment
W) hemoglobin binds to oxygen with less affinity
X) hemoglobin binds to carbon dioxide with less affinity
Y) hemoglobin will dissociate from its erythrocyte
Z) pH is quickly reverted to normal by buffering capacity
ANSWER: W) hemoglobin binds to oxygen with less affinity
