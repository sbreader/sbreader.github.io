1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Mizuki is dropping an egg out of the window of a five-floor building. The egg has a probability of k/5 of cracking, where k is the number of the floor the egg is dropped from. Mizuki randomly picks a floor where the probability a given floor is chosen is proportional to the number of the floor. If Mizuki drops her egg out of the window on a randomly chosen floor, what is the probability her egg cracks?
ANSWER: 11/15
1. BONUS Math Short Answer Madeline is distributing 15 strawberries between 3 friends and herself. If she wants each person to get at least two strawberries, how many ways are there for her to distribute her strawberries?
ANSWER: 120
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In order to minimize losses due to resistance, induction cookers often use Litz wires, which consist of multiple thin wires twisted into one large wire. The higher surface area acts to reduce what effect, where alternating current tends to travel through the surface and not the body of a conductor?
ANSWER: Skin effect
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three points that are on or within the future light cone of the origin on a Minkowski diagram 1) (2, 1) 2) (−3, 4) 3) (−5, −6).
ANSWER: 2 only
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What meteorological process occurs when an increase in horizontal wind shear increases the temperature gradient between two air masses, eventually leading to the formation or intensification of a distinct weather boundary?
ANSWER: Frontogenisis

5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at the Stanford Institute for Materials and Energy Sciences recently discovered a new kind of magnetic order in materials in which magnetic coils are spaced irregularly but align in a single preferred direction. What term describes this kind of order, also seen in liquid crystals, bacterial suspensions, and superconductors?
ANSWER: Nematic
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at SLAC are working on upgrades to the Large Hadron Collider that will improve its study of high-mass particles. In particular, they intend to characterize the Higgs potential by measuring what effect only observable when multiple Higgs bosons are present?

7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The fundamental theorem of calculus is a specific case of what general result in calculus that states that the integral of a differential form over a region’s boundary is equal to the integral of its exterior derivative over the region itself?
ANSWER: Stokes’ theorem (also accept: Generalized Stokes’ theorem)
7. BONUS Math Short Answer Alex draws triangle ABC such that the angle bisector of angle BAC intersects BC at D. Given that ABC has perimeter 19, AB = 4, and AC = 7, what is the length of BD?
ANSWER: 32/11
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Kat wants to create a ramp for which a ball released at the top reaches the bottom in the shortest possible time. What shape should be used to produce such a ramp?
ANSWER: Brachistochrone (also accept: Cycloid; tautochrone)
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A halfpipe is constructed such that one side has strong friction and the other side is frictionless. A uniform solid sphere begins rolling from height H on the side with friction. In terms of H, what is the maximum height the sphere reaches on the frictionless side?
ANSWER: (5/7)H
9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Scientists at UCLA’s Tao Lab are studying the implications of random matrices in quantum physics. Which of the following facts is NOT implied by a nonsingular square matrix?
W) Its columns are linearly independent vectors
X) The null space is the trivial subspace
Y) It is invertible
Z) Zero is an eigenvalue
