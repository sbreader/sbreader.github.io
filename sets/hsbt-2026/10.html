1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Ogives are alternating light and dark bands of ice that appear on certain glaciers. Which of the following best describes the mechanism of formation of ogives?
W) Seasonal accumulation of windblown volcanic ash during the winter and clean snowfall during the summer
X) Differential melting and dirt accumulation as ice moves through an icefall
Y) Periodic surges at the glacier’s headwall that cause the buckling and folding of ice
Z) Deposition of dark silt through vertical crevasses during seasonal melt cycles
ANSWER: X) Differential melting and dirt accumulation as ice moves through an icefall
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three events from earliest to latest to occur in the early universe 1) Cosmic inflation 2) Big Bang nucleosynthesis 3) Quark-hadron transition.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Neurilemma are the outermost layer of a myelin sheath formation, and are technically present in incompletely myelinated nerve axons. In which of the following patterns of myelination can neurilemma be observed, and what type of glial cell exhibits this characteristic?
W) Centrifugal, Oligodendrocyte
X) Centripital, Oligodendrocyte
Y) Centrifugal, Schwann Cell
Z) Centripital, Schwann Cell
ANSWER: Z) Centripital, Schwann Cell
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer On an EKG, you observe multiple P spikes before the QRS complex. What specific cardiac irregularity is this indicative of?
ANSWER: Atrial block
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In a standard Pourbaix diagram, the standard reduction potential of a phase transition is plotted against which of the following quantities?
W) Delta G
X) Delta G over F
Y) pH
Z) pH over n
ANSWER: Y) pH

ANSWER: 2, 4, 1, 3
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What theorem states that for all integers n greater than 3, there exists a prime between n and 2n?
ANSWER: Bertrand's postulate
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Min Shin is analyzing the properties of a matrix using its eigenvalues. What is the sum of the squares of the eigenvalues of the 2x2 matrix with first row 1, 5, and second row 3, 7?
ANSWER: 80
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The Considere criterion states that this phenomenon occurs when the strain hardening equals the true stress. As a material under tensile stress stretches, one particular spot rapidly thins out. What is this phenomenon, where the final breaking of the material happens?
ANSWER: Necking
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer The dispersion relation of a phonon is given by omega equals 2 sine of the quantity pi times k over 2 quantity. What is the group velocity of such phonon wavepackets at k equals 2?
ANSWER: Negative pi
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice The C-rate of a battery refers to the measure of the rate at which it is charged or discharged relative to its maximum capacity. Which of the following best describes the power output and overall efficiency associated with a higher C-rate?

X) Higher, lower
Y) Lower, higher
Z) Lower, lower

8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the biosynthetic process in plants that converts MTA into methionine, and is necessary for the sustained synthesis of ethylene?

10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Which of the following are true about Fibonacci numbers? 1) Consecutive Fibonacci numbers are relatively prime 2) The only Fibonacci numbers that are perfect squares are 0 and 1 3) The sum of the first n Fibonacci numbers is itself 1 less than a Fibonacci number.

12. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Transmission lines and substations are typically designed to operate at only 50% to 70% capacity during normal conditions, providing a buffer if one route drops offline. 14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer One of the greatest benefits to sexual reproduction is the increased biodiversity through recombination processes. What process occurs in continuously non-recombinant genetic elements like the mitochondrial genome or Y chromosome, that leads to error accumulation due to continuous drift?
ANSWER: Muller’s ratchet
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice When an asexually reproducing population is sufficiently small, the decline in fitness due to Muller’s ratchet is enough to reduce population size, leading to a self-propagating positive feedback loop of mutational accumulation. What is this event known as?

X) Mutational Breakdown
Y) Mutational Meltdown
Z) Mutational Shift
ANSWER: Y) Mutational Meltdown

18. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Despite their widespread implementation across Hawaii, commercial solar cells have one of the lowest conversion efficiencies for renewable energy, consistently ranking below 30%.
