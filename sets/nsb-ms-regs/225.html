1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Scientists at Sandia National Lab are testing to see if electrical stimulation can improve working memory and reasoning skills. What cortex of the brain are they stimulating?
W) Parietal
X) Occipital
Y) Prefrontal
Z) Temporal
ANSWER: Y) Prefrontal
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Chemists at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory have developed a copper catalyst that converts carbon dioxide to ethanol in an acidic environment. How many electrons are needed to reduce two molecules of carbon dioxide to ethanol in an acidic solution?
ANSWER: 12
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the line that separates night from day on the Moon?
ANSWER: TERMINATOR
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What component of the Milky Way galaxy is comprised of a roughly spherical collection of stars and star clusters with almost no gas or dust?
W) Disk
X) Bulge
Y) Halo
Z) Globular cluster
ANSWER: Y) Halo
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for a severe allergic reaction that can cause hypotension and difficulty breathing, and can be triggered by events such as bee stings or peanut exposure?
ANSWER: ANAPHYLAXIS
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Within which of the following tissues would one find the largest number of reticulocytes, which are immature red blood cells?
W) Bone
X) Heart
Y) Spleen
Z) Liver
ANSWER: W) Bone
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A certain gas-filled container has a volume of 15 milliliters and a pressure of 40 pounds per square inch. In pounds per square inch, what would the pressure inside the container be if the volume were increased to 60 milliliters while keeping the temperature constant?
ANSWER: 10
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three observations that indicate a chemical change occurred in a reaction 1) A phase change 2) A color change 3) Gas is evolved.
ANSWER: 2, 3
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Himalayas are an example of which of the following kinds of mountain systems?
W) Fault-block
X) Folded
Y) Upwarped
Z) Volcanic
ANSWER: X) Folded
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is characteristic of a cold front?
W) Boundary between air masses has a gentle slope
X) Typically preceded by cirrus clouds
Y) Associated with moderate amounts of precipitation
Z) It moves faster than a warm front
ANSWER: Z) It moves faster than a warm front
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If the nth term of a sequence is given by 5n + 7, then what is the 100th term minus the 99th?
ANSWER: 5
6. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the y-coordinates of all points of intersection of the parabola y = x2 – 4x – 7 and the line y = –5x – 1.
ANSWER: –11 AND 14
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying blood samples from patients infected with what virus that causes a deadly hemorrhagic fever and is believed to naturally reside in fruit bats?
ANSWER: EBOLA VIRUS (also accept: EBOLA)
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer The ICARUS detector is moving to Fermilab from its previous home in CERN. The detector is filled with 760 tonnes of liquid argon to detect what class of particles?
ANSWER: NEUTRINOS (also accept: STERILE NEUTRINOS)
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Meteor showers occur when small meteoroids enter Earth's atmosphere. What type of celestial object is the source material for most major meteor showers on Earth?
ANSWER: COMET
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following four regions where hydrogen is fused in the Sun 1) Corona 2) Chromosphere 3) Photosphere 4) Core.
ANSWER: 4
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer To the nearest gram, what is the mass of one mole of helium atoms?
ANSWER: 4
9. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Andrew has two conducting spheres, A and B, that are in contact with each other. He brings a negatively-charged rod close to sphere A and immediately separates the two spheres. Which of the following best describes the resulting charges on the spheres once the rod has been removed?
W) Both spheres are negative
X) Sphere A is positive, while sphere B is negative
Y) Sphere A is negative, while sphere B is positive
Z) Both spheres are positive
ANSWER: X) Sphere A is positive, while sphere B is negative
10. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice The effect of physiological ions binding to G protein-coupled receptors that transmit information is being studied by researchers at Los Alamos National Laboratory using nuclear magnetic resonance. Which of the following could NOT be an ion they are studying?
W) Calcium
X) Magnesium
Y) Zinc
Z) Sulfur
ANSWER: Z) Sulfur
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Los Alamos National Lab are trying to use muons to study fracture patterns in materials. Muons are most related to what subatomic particle that can be found in hydrogen atoms?
ANSWER: ELECTRON
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Simplify the fraction with numerator 8 and denominator 4 times the cube root of 4.
ANSWER: ∛2 (also accept: CUBE ROOT OF 2)

ANSWER: COENZYME A (also accept: CoA)
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of tissue is the main component of the skeletons of sharks and rays?
ANSWER: CARTILAGE
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice When plotting the energy of the components of a slow reaction, such as the conversion of diamond into graphite, versus the reaction progress, which of the following best represents the shape of the curve?
W) Linearly upward
X) Linearly downward
Y) A trough in the middle
Z) A peak in the middle
ANSWER: Z) A peak in the middle
