Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What term describes the maximum population size that an environment can sustain?
ANSWER: Carrying capacity
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a product of aerobic respiration?
W) Oxygen gas
X) Carbon dioxide gas
Y) Water
Z) ATP
ANSWER: W) Oxygen gas
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What gas law states that the pressure of a gas is inversely proportional to its volume at constant temperature?
ANSWER: Boyle’s Law
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Rutherford’s gold-foil experiment included firing alpha particles at gold foil and measuring their deflection. What isotope’s nucleus closely resembles an alpha particle?
W) Protium
X) Deuterium
Y) Helium-3
Z) Helium-4
ANSWER: Z) Helium-4
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If a fibonacci sequence starts with 2 and 5, what is its 5th number?
ANSWER: 19
3. BONUS Math Short Answer In Austin, Texas, the sales tax rate is 8.25%. What is the minimum integer subtotal, in cents, such that the total price, including tax and in cents, is also an integer?
ANSWER: 400
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A Coulomb is equal to which of the following?
W) ampere per second
X) kilogram per second squared
Y) ampere-second
Z) meter-second per kilogram-ampere
ANSWER: Y) ampere-second
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer A ball with a kinetic energy of 40 Joules moves in a circle with radius 2 meters. What is the magnitude of the centripetal force in Newtons?
ANSWER: 40
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following stars is a white dwarf?
W) Betelgeuse
X) Sirius B
Y) Rigel
Z) Canopus
ANSWER: X) Sirius B
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following parts of the Sun in terms of increasing temperature. 1) Core 2) Photosphere 3) Corona
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A mutation in the spliceosome components would most directly affect which of the following processes?
W) DNA replication
X) rRNA processing
Y) Removal of introns from pre-mRNA
Z) Protein folding in the ER
ANSWER: Y) Removal of introns from pre-mRNA
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer If the membrane potential is equal to the equilibrium potential for K+, what is the net movement of K+ across the membrane
ANSWER: 0
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Ben has 36 liters of 2M HCl solution. How many liters of deionized water should he add to result in a 1M solution?
W) 36
X) 54
Y) 72
Z) 144
ANSWER: W) 36
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Ben has a mystery solution. He first records the molarity. Then, he records the molality. These two measurements are almost exactly the same. Assuming perfectly accurate and precise instruments and the solution at STP, what is the most likely solvent?
W) Ethanol
X) Glycerol
Y) Water
Z) Hexane
ANSWER: Y) Water
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Jonathan eats chicken nuggets at a rate of 15 boxes per hour. Michael eats them at a rate of 25 boxes per hour. In minutes, how long would it take them to eat 60 boxes of chicken nuggets combined?
ANSWER: 90
8. BONUS Math Short Answer How many 3 digit multiples of 7 are there?
ANSWER: 128
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A ball with momentum 5 kilogram meters per second to the right collides with a second ball. The second ball now has a momentum of 3 kilogram meters per second to the right. What is the new momentum of the first ball in kilogram meters per second to the right?
W) 2
X) 3
Y) 5
Z) 8
ANSWER: W) 2
9. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A gas is in a closed container at pressure P . The gas doubles in volume and halves in temperature. What is the new pressure?
W) P/4
X) P/2
Y) P
Z) 2P
ANSWER: W) P/4
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What property of a mineral can not be observable with the naked eye?
W) luster
X) diaphaneity
Y) color
Z) birefringence
ANSWER: Z) birefringence
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What common group of silicates exhibits perfect 90 degree cleavage in two directions, a key distinguishing feature of minerals such as orthoclase and plagioclase?
ANSWER: Feldspar
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What class of disease is casued by the failure to eliminate self-reactive lymphocytes?
ANSWER: Autoimmune diseases
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following biogeochemical cycles does not have a gaseous phase?
W) Carbon
X) Nitrogen
Y) Sulfur
Z) Phosphorus
ANSWER: Z) Phosphorus
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Who discovered the neutron?
ANSWER: James Chadwick
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rohan heats an ideal gas to 27 degrees Celsius in a rigid container. A barometer reads 1 atmospheric gauge pressure. Then, he pressurizes the ideal gas until the gauge reads 3 atmospheric gauge pressure. If this process is isochoric, what is the temperature, in Celsius, of the gas?
ANSWER: 327
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Given that a car travels exactly 4 times as far as a bus on the same amount of gas, how many people would have to be on a bus for the bus to be more fuel-efficient per person than a car with 3 people?
ANSWER: 13

W) 1/2
X) 1/3
Y) 1/4
Z) 1/5
ANSWER: Y) 1/4

W) 60
X) 120
Y) 180
Z) 300
ANSWER: W) 60
14. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Two carts start 40 meters apart and start moving towards each other. The cart on the left moves at a speed of 3 meters per second and the cart on the right moves at a speed of 5 meters per second. How many seconds does it take for both carts to collide?
W) 4
X) 5

X) dike

ANSWER: 2,3
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which checkpoint primarily ensures that DNA damage is repaired before replication?
W) G0 checkpoint
X) G1/S checkpoint
Y) G2/M checkpoint
Z) Metaphase checkpoint
ANSWER: X) G1/S checkpoint

ANSWER: Oxygen
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Fluorene (with two "e"s) and Fluorenone are similar organic compounds, with the exception that fluorenone has a ketone functional group. Which statement identifies which compound dissolves more readily in water and why?
W) Fluorene, it has a lower molecular weight
X) Fluorene, it is less polar
Y) Fluorenone, it has a higher molecular weight
Z) Fluorenone, it is more polar
ANSWER: Z) Fluorenone, it is more polar

If all hydrogen atoms were instead substituted with fluorine atoms, to the nearest gram per mole, what would be the molecular weight of this new compound?
ANSWER: 346 gram/mol
