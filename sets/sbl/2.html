1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer 10 people are standing in a line. How many ways are there to choose 5 people such that no 2 of them are standing next to each other?
ANSWER: 6
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Alice estimates the value of the natural log of a large integer n by plotting the graph of y = ln(x) and performing the Euler method with a step size of 1 starting from the point (1, 0). She notices that, as n grows to infinity, the difference between her estimate and the true value of ln(n) approaches what constant?
ANSWER: EULER-MASCHERONI CONSTANT
19. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following numbers has the highest 2-adic valuation?
W) 62
X) 64
Y) 66
Z) 68
ANSWER: X) 64

ANSWER: 19
1. BONUS Math Short Answer Right triangle ABC is inscribed in a circle centered at point O. Another circle is drawn internally tangent to the previous circle such that it is tangent to the hypotenuse of ABC. Given that the legs of triangle ABC have length 5 and 12, what is the radius of the smaller circle?
ANSWER: 13/4
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding the speed of sound in seawater is not true?
W) There exists a depth at which the speed of sound in seawater reaches a minimum
X) The speed of sound in seawater increases as temperature increases
Y) For a given temperature, the speed of sound in seawater is always greater than that of freshwater
Z) The speed of sound in seawater can be expressed as a bivariate function of temperature and salinity
ANSWER: Z) The speed of sound in seawater can be expressed as a bivariate function of temperature and salinity
2. VISUAL BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Shown in the image is a color-magnitude diagram of Messier object 5, with individual stars plotted as dots. Answer the following three questions regarding this image 1) What type of variable star is represented by the population at letter E? 2) What stellar evolutionary stage is represented by stars near letter D? 3) Is M5 an open cluster, a globular cluster, or a galaxy?
ANSWER: 1) RR LYRAE; 2) HORIZONTAL BRANCH; 3) GLOBULAR CLUSTER
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Real objects do not radiate as much thermal energy as predicted by the Stefan-Boltzmann law. What quantity is equal to the ratio of an object’s measured thermal radiation to its ideal thermal radiation?
ANSWER: EMISSIVITY
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Electrons can possess magnetic moments due to their spin and orbital angular momentum. What relativistic phenomenon is a result of the interaction between these two?
ANSWER: SPIN-ORBIT COUPLING (also accept: SPIN-ORBIT INTERACTION)
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Rank the following three ions in terms of decreasing concentration in the intracellular fluid of a resting neuron 1) Na+ 2) Cl– 3) K+.
ANSWER: 3,1,2
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of plant ground tissue that possess secondary cell walls 1) Parenchyma 2) Collenchyma 3) Sclerenchyma.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many normal vibrational modes does one molecule of acetylene have?
ANSWER: 7
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three carbon dioxide vibrational modes that are considered to be Raman active 1) Asymmetric C – O stretch 2) Symmetric C – O stretch 3) Symmetrical bend in plane.
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Lab are working on new solvent extraction methods that employ magnetic fields to act on paramagnetic metal ions. Identify all of the following three metal ions that are paramagnetic 1) Na+ 2) Cr3+ 3) Zn2+.
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Fermilab are conducting particle physics experiments in order to ascertain information about the early universe. One of their experiments focuses on the conversion of what particles to electrons without the emission of neutrinos?
ANSWER: MUONS
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Insects secrete what form of nitrogenous waste?
ANSWER: URIC ACID
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer During inflammation, which class of leukocytes may migrate into the blood to become mast cells and release histamine?
ANSWER: BASOPHILS
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Galactic fountains are features that spread relatively hot, metal-enriched gas high above the disk of a spiral galaxy. Which of the following best describes how most galactic fountains form?
W) Intense stellar winds and supernovae
X) Tidal forces from a supermassive black hole
Y) Relativistic jets from a neutron star
Z) Tidal forces from orbiting dwarf galaxies
ANSWER: W) Intense stellar winds and supernovae
8. VISUAL BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Shown in the image is a seismic cross section of the rock under the Santos Basin, located in the South Atlantic. Answer the following two questions regarding the image 1) Identify the primary mineral of the purple unit 2) What type of normal fault can be seen near the bottom of the profile?
ANSWER: 1) HALITE; 2) LISTRIC FAULT (also accept: SALT)
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds is the least suitable nucleophile for an SN2 reaction?
W) Ethanol
X) Methylamine
Y) Sodium iodide
Z) Sodium methoxide
ANSWER: W) Ethanol
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three liquids in terms of increasing viscosity 1) Mercury 2) Water 3) Diethyl ether.
ANSWER: 3,2,1
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the coefficient of the xy3 term in the binomial expansion of (3 x + 2 y) to the 4th?
ANSWER: 96
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab have been developing methods for performing artificial photosynthesis. In these methods, titanium dioxide is used for the water-splitting reaction that is traditionally catalyzed by what enzyme within photosystem 2 in plants?
ANSWER: OXYGEN-EVOLVING COMPLEX (also accept: WATER-SPLITTING COMPLEX)
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Livermore National Lab are using short laser pulses to remove atoms from the surface of metals. Lasers are generated by shooting photons at excited electrons, causing them to relax to their ground state. What is the term for this process?
ANSWER: STIMULATED EMISSION

W) Carnot cycle
X) Stirling cycle
Y) Otto cycle
Z) Brayton cycle
ANSWER: Z) Brayton cycle

BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding electric field lines 1) Induced electric field lines form closed loops 2) Equipotential lines are always parallel to electric field lines 3) More closely-spaced electric field lines correspond to stronger electric fields.

13. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice When H2 forms a side-on complex with a tungsten center, which of the following is most likely to occur?
W) The H – H bond strength decreases because tungsten donates electrons into the H – H sigma star orbital
X) The H – H bond strength increases because tungsten donates electrons into the H – H sigma orbital
Y) The H – H bond strength decreases because tungsten donates electrons into the H – H pi star orbital
