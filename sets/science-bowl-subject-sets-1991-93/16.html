Packet 16 (General Science 1991) of Science Bowl Subject Sets 1991–93:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What name is given to wood whose normal cells have been replaced with mineral deposits?
ANSWER: PETRIFIED WOOD
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Pollination by birds is called:
W) autogamy
X) ornithophily
Y) entomophily
Z) anemophily
ANSWER: X) ornithophily
3. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What percent of fire-related deaths are due to smoke inhalation rather than burns?
W) 10%
X) 50%
Y) 80%
Z) 99%
ANSWER: Y) 80%
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer To what familiar fruit is the plantain similar?
ANSWER: BANANA
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What U.S. President was recognized as a world authority on American game animals?
ANSWER: THEODORE ROOSEVELT
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The fastest-running terrestrial animal is:
W) cheetah
X) lion
Y) man
Z) jaguar
ANSWER: W) cheetah
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What wonder drug was used for three years by the Army and Navy before becoming available to the general public in 1944?
ANSWER: PENICILLIN
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The scarab, worshipped by the Egyptians, is what type of beetle?
ANSWER: DUNG BEETLE
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A cloud at ground level is called what?
ANSWER: FOG
13. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What invention in about 1450 A.D. revolutionized communication and the world?
ANSWER: THE PRINTING PRESS
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the name for the new technology whereby a glass fiber carries as much information as hundreds of copper wires?
ANSWER: FIBER OPTICS
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What mammal lays eggs?
ANSWER: PLATYPUS (also accept: ECHIDNA; MONOTREMES)
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Linseed oil comes from what plant?
ANSWER: FLAX
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the largest North American terrestrial rodent, distinguished by its spiny covering?
ANSWER: PORCUPINE
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Albacore is a type of:
W) shell-fish
X) tuna
Y) marble
Z) meteoroid
ANSWER: X) tuna
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The only species of cat that lives and hunts in groups is:
W) lion
X) leopard
Y) jaguar
Z) cougar
ANSWER: W) lion
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Linen is made from what plant?
ANSWER: FLAX
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is arachnology?
ANSWER: THE STUDY OF ARACHNIDS (also accept: STUDY OF SPIDERS; STUDY OF SCORPIONS)
22. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is another name for the Paleolithic Age?
ANSWER: OLD STONE AGE (also accept: THE STONE AGE; STONE AGE)
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What nocturnal animal group makes up one-fourth of all mammals species?
ANSWER: BATS (also accept: CHIROPTERA)
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the whale that has a long protruding tusk?
ANSWER: NARWHAL
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What land mammal holds the record for the greatest age?
ANSWER: MAN (also accept: HUMANS; HUMAN)
26. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the better known name for the deadly poison prussic acid?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN CYANIDE (also accept: CYANIDE; HCN)
27. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What colorless, pungent gas is often dissolved in water to yield a solution that is used as a biological preservative?
ANSWER: FORMALDEHYDE
28. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the rest mass of a photon?
ANSWER: ZERO
29. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What yellow metal is an alloy of copper and zinc?
ANSWER: BRASS
30. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name given to the process, discovered by Goodyear, of adding sulfur to heated rubber?
ANSWER: VULCANIZATION
31. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Used primarily in fertilizers, which element is never found free in nature even though it makes up about 2.5% of the earth's crust?
ANSWER: POTASSIUM
32. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What element, whose symbol derived from the Latin for "liquid silver", has been found in Egyptian tombs from 1500 B.C.?
ANSWER: MERCURY
33. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What poisonous alkaloid is extracted from tobacco leaves and widely used as an insecticide?
ANSWER: NICOTINE
34. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for steel alloyed with chromium?
ANSWER: STAINLESS STEEL
35. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Table sugar, from sugar cane or beet, is what type of sugar?
ANSWER: SUCROSE (also accept: DISACCHARIDE; SACCHAROSE)
36. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which prefix is often used with scientific terms to indicate that something is the same, equal or constant?
W) iso
X) mega
Y) meta
Z) quasi
ANSWER: W) iso
37. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The study of phenomena at very low temperatures is called:
W) heat transfer
X) morphology
Y) crystallography
Z) cryogenics
ANSWER: Z) cryogenics
38. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The branch of medical science which is concerned with the study of disease as it affects a community of people is called:
W) epidemiology
X) oncology
Y) paleontogy
Z) pathology
ANSWER: W) epidemiology
39. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice The study of how people use tools to perform work and how people physically relate to their working environment is called:
W) engineering
X) ergonomics
Y) agronomy
Z) physiology
ANSWER: X) ergonomics
41. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What famous scientist and inventor participated in the invention of the Aqua-Lung?
W) Thomas Edison
X) Isaac Newton
Y) Leonardo da Vinci
Z) Jacques Cousteau
ANSWER: Z) Jacques Cousteau
42. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Superconductivity is a material property associated with:
W) cooling a substance without a phase change
X) frictionless liquid flow
Y) a loss of thermal resistance
Z) a loss of electrical resistance
ANSWER: Z) a loss of electrical resistance
43. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A type of plastic that is biodegradable has been in the news lately. The ingredient that makes it biodegradable is:
W) vegetable oil
X) petroleum
Y) cornstarch
Z) leather
ANSWER: Y) cornstarch
44. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Who was the marine biologist and author of Silent Spring who was one of the first people to warn of the dangers of pesticides like DDT?
W) Jacques Cousteau
X) Rachel Carson
Y) Charles Darwin
Z) Marlin Perkins
ANSWER: X) Rachel Carson
45. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice If a metal can be drawn into wires relatively easily it is called:
W) malleable
X) ductile
Y) extractive
Z) tactile
ANSWER: X) ductile
46. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to the invasion of warm surface waters off the Peruvian coast that has been identified with strange climatic effects in recent years?
ANSWER: EL NIÑO (also accept: EL NINO)
47. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Occasionally, a bad cold will cause a decrease in a persons hearing ability. What is the name of the tube that becomes blocked to cause this problem?
ANSWER: EUSTACHIAN TUBE (also accept: AUDITORY TUBE)
48. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the general type of mammal that gives birth to undeveloped young that are kept in pouches.
ANSWER: MARSUPIAL
49. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In a musical instrument that produces a fundamental note, what would the wavelength of the second harmonic be relative to the fundamental?
ANSWER: ONE-HALF THE FUNDAMENTAL (also accept: HALF; 1/2)
50. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Cystitis is the infection of which of the following?
W) liver
X) urinary bladder
Y) pancreas
Z) lung
ANSWER: X) urinary bladder
51. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the subdivision in the Plant Kingdom comprising over 20,000 species of Mosses and Liverworts?
ANSWER: BRYOPHYTES (also accept: BRYOPHYTA)
52. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the medical term given to the inflammation or infection of the mucous membranes of the nose.
ANSWER: RHINITIS
53. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Automobile catalytic converters cause the carbon monoxide and hydrocarbons which result from incomplete combustion to be converted into what two substances?
ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE AND WATER (also accept: CO2 AND H2O)
54. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice At room temperature, most elements are in which phase of matter?
W) solid
X) liquid
Y) gas
Z) plasma
ANSWER: W) solid
55. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The per capita birth rate of a population is known as its:
W) mortality
X) natality
Y) population density
Z) carrying capacity
ANSWER: X) natality
56. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Who is called the Father of the Nuclear Navy?
W) Edward Teller
X) Robert Oppenheimer
Y) Hyman Rickover
Z) Chester Nimitz
ANSWER: Y) Hyman Rickover
57. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The science of weights and measures is called:
W) metrology
X) meteorology
Y) mineralogy
Z) morphology
ANSWER: W) metrology
58. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer The following are the names of man-made structures in the United States. Identify the type of structure. Hungry Horse, Yellowtail, Dworshak, Don Pedro, Cougar, Glen Canyon, Grand Coulee and Hoover. Name the type of structure.
ANSWER: DAMS
59. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is primarily composed of calcium carbonate?
W) Fish scales
X) Shark teeth
Y) Oyster Shells
Z) Whale bones
ANSWER: Y) Oyster Shells
60. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What compound is a common component of air pollution, but is essential in the upper atmosphere to protect life on earth?
ANSWER: OZONE
61. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Water flows through a horizontal pipe at a constant volumetric rate. At a location where the cross sectional area decreases, does the velocity of the fluid increase, decrease, or stay the same?
ANSWER: INCREASES (also accept: INCREASE)
62. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What radioactive element is routinely used in treating hyperthyroidism, and in reducing thyroid activity?
W) Iron-59
X) Gold-198
Y) Cobalt-60
Z) Iodine-131
ANSWER: Z) Iodine-131
63. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is osteoporosis?
ANSWER: LOSS OF CALCIUM FROM BONES (also accept: LOSS OF BONE DENSITY; BONE LOSS)
64. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Yeast, used in making bread is a:
W) plant
X) fungus
Y) bacteria
Z) seed
ANSWER: X) fungus
65. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The Statue of Liberty is green because of:
W) green stone
X) oxidized brass
Y) steel painted green
Z) oxidized copper
ANSWER: Z) oxidized copper
66. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the phase of quartz consistently found in meteorite impact craters, but rarely found in non-meteorite-related geological formations.
ANSWER: STISHOVITE
67. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer At what point are the Celsius and Fahrenheit scales equal?
ANSWER: -40 DEGREES (also accept: -40)
68. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following vacuum pumps operates by using a very cold surface to trap the gases on the cold surface?
W) sublimation pump
X) turbo pump
Y) cryopump
Z) diffusion pump
ANSWER: Y) cryopump
69. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Using the fewest names of the members of this family, identify the tree family which includes among its members the: Fink, myrtle, turkey, jacks, laurel, white, live and pin. Name the tree family.
ANSWER: OAK
70. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Using the fewest names of the members of this family, identify the tree family which includes among its members the: Pignut, scarit, mockernut, bitternut, black and shagbark. Name the tree family.
ANSWER: HICKORY
71. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Using the fewest names of the members of this family, identify the tree family which includes among its members the: Chinese, cut-leaved, manchurian, persian, english, black and american. Name the tree family.
ANSWER: WALNUT
72. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice The word atom is from a Greek word meaning:
W) small
X) indivisible
Y) unseen
Z) visible
ANSWER: X) indivisible
73. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Name the famous mathematician/physicist who was born in the year Galileo died.
ANSWER: ISAAC NEWTON (also accept: NEWTON)
