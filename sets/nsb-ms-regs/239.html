1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In which direction does the vector of centripetal acceleration point in uniform circular motion?
ANSWER: TOWARD THE CENTER OF THE CIRCLE
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer If an object in uniform circular motion has a centripetal acceleration of 2 meters per second squared and is moving in a circle with a radius of 2 meters, what is its velocity in meters per second?
ANSWER: 2
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many carbon atoms are in a molecule of propanol?
ANSWER: 3
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following substances would be least likely to dissolve in water?
W) Polar compound
X) Nonpolar compound
Y) Ionic compound
Z) Salt
ANSWER: X) Nonpolar compound
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice During which stage of mitosis do the chromosomes line up in the center of the nucleus?
W) Anaphase
X) Prophase
Y) Interphase
Z) Metaphase
ANSWER: Z) Metaphase
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer During what stage of meiosis does crossing over take place?
ANSWER: PROPASE ONE
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following choices is NOT a condition required for the formation of a thunderstorm?
W) Humid air in low- to mid-troposphere
X) Atmospheric instability
Y) Relatively low temperatures
Z) A source of uplift
ANSWER: Y) Relatively low temperatures
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for an elongated cluster of thunderstorm cells that is accompanied by a continuous gust front at the line's leading edge?
ANSWER: SQUALL LINE
5. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer According to its nutrition facts, a candy bar contains 100 calories. Giving your answer to the nearest hundred, what would the equivalent be in joules?
ANSWER: 418,400 (also accept: 418,000)
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Giving your answer to the nearest thousand, how many joules of energy are required to raise the temperature of 2 kilograms of water by 3 degrees Celsius?
ANSWER: 25,000
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What type of device converts heat energy into mechanical work?
ANSWER: HEAT ENGINE
6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following devices exemplifies a reverse heat engine?
W) Diesel engine
X) Electrical generator
Y) Refrigerator
Z) Steam engine
ANSWER: Y) Refrigerator
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not an example of a cartilaginous fish?
W) Longnose skate
X) Pink salmon
Y) Tiger shark
Z) Sting ray
ANSWER: X) Pink salmon
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the adjective for a fish that is born in fresh water, swims to salt water, and returns to fresh water to spawn?
ANSWER: ANADROMOUS
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following could be used to neutralize an acid spill?
W) Hydrogen sulfide
X) Calcium oxide
Y) Water
Z) Bromine
ANSWER: X) Calcium oxide
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer According to the Lewis definition of an acid, what can a Lewis acid accept from a Lewis base?
ANSWER: AN ELECTRON PAIR (also accept: ELECTRONS)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice About what percentage of worldwide rainfall occurs in the tropics?
W) 14%
X) 45%
Y) 66%
Z) 85%
ANSWER: Y) 66%
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Tropical Rainfall Measuring Mission (TRMM) satellite was launched in 1997 with the primary mission of measuring rainfall from space using passive microwave and active radar sensors. Which of the following is NOT a benefit of TRMM?
W) Increased understanding of the water cycle
X) Increased understanding of the heat release associated with rainfall
Y) Increased understanding of contrails
Z) Better likelihood of accurate climate models
ANSWER: Y) Increased understanding of contrails
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the integer value that is equal to log 5 + log 2?
ANSWER: 1
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the volume of a rectangular pyramid in cubic meters if the length of the base is 3.5 meters, the width of the base is 4 meters and the height of the pyramid is 6 meters?
ANSWER: 28
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following quantities is always conserved in an inelastic collision?
W) Kinetic energy
X) Mechanical energy
Y) Momentum
Z) Impulse
ANSWER: Y) Momentum
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer The linear momentum of a particle is directly proportional to which two quantities?
ANSWER: MASS AND VELOCITY
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Give the name currently used for the heterogeneous group of fungi in which scientists have never observed sexual reproduction.
ANSWER: IMPERFECT FUNGI (also accept: DEUTEROMYCOTA)
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the best known product made from organisms classified as imperfect fungi.
ANSWER: PENICILLIN
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the sum of all positive integer factors of 6?
ANSWER: 12
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Assuming all functions are defined, and expressing your answer in simplest form, evaluate (n2 + 7n – 8)/(n – 1) × (n2 – 4)/(n2 + 6n – 16).
ANSWER: n + 2
14. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer In a four stroke internal combustion engine, during which stroke is the energy transformed from chemical potential energy to kinetic energy?
ANSWER: POWER
14. BONUS Energy Short Answer A car's radiator is an example of a component that transfers heat generated within a solid material to a fluid medium. What is such a component called?
ANSWER: A HEAT SINK

W) Water vapor
X) Oxygen

16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many positive factors does the number 1051 have?
ANSWER: 2
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Since the 1970's, what have scientists observed about the size of global amphibian population?
ANSWER: THEY HAVE DECLINED
17. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly matches an order of amphibians and its characteristics?
W) Squamata: legs, clawed toes and external ears
X) Anura: long bodies and tails
Y) Apoda: tentacles and fishlike scales
Z) Urodela: no tails, can jump
ANSWER: Y) Apoda: tentacles and fishlike scales

X) Circular

ANSWER: 1/25

21. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The similarities between the lungs of a reptile and the air bladder of a fish can be described, in evolutionary terms, as:
W) Homologous structures
X) Erroneous structures
Y) Vestigial structures
Z) Inert structures
ANSWER: W) Homologous structures
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer The similarities between the wings of a bat and the wings of a dragonfly can be described, in evolutionary terms, as what type of structures?
