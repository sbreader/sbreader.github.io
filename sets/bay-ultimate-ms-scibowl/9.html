Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In England, two types of trees are commonly found: white trees and black trees. Because of this, the local moth population has evolved to become split between white moths and black moths, while intermediate colored moths are a very small portion of the population. This is an example of which of the following types of selection?
W) Stabilizing
X) Directional
Y) Disruptive
Z) Artificial
ANSWER: Y) Disruptive
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer In an episode of House, M.D., Dr. House states that he is hunting for "wabbits". He is able to do this because wabbits reproduce extremely quickly and give their offspring little parental care. This is an example of what type of selection, strongly associated with Type 3 survivorship curves?
ANSWER: R-selected
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Naman is trying to learn about the winemaking industry, for which he needs to obtain ethanol. He has obtained a mixture of ethanol and water, and knows that the boiling point of ethanol is 79°C, and of water is 100°C. What process could he use to separate the ethanol?
ANSWER: Distillation (also accept: Fractional distillation)
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three acids given their concentrations by increasing pH. 1. 0.1M hydrochloric acid 2. 1M sulfuric acid 3. 0.1M acetic acid
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The temperature of a gas is directly proportional to what characteristic of the gas particles?
W) Speed
X) Momentum
Y) Average kinetic energy
Z) Density
ANSWER: Y) Average kinetic energy
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A ball is thrown fast enough into a pool from above so that it touches the bottom. Which of the following best describes the buoyant force the ball experiences from the moment it touches the water to the moment it hits the bottom?
W) Always increases
X) Remains constant
Y) Increases, then remains constant
Z) Increases, then decreases
ANSWER: Y) Increases, then remains constant
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the property exhibited by planets such as Mercury, where its orbit wobbles across a long period of time, and is noticeable in other spinning objects like gyroscopes and spinning tops?
ANSWER: Precession
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the process by which new oceanic crust is formed, occurring at mid-ocean ridges?
ANSWER: Seafloor spreading
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Given a function f(x) = x2 + 5, what is f inverse of 69?
ANSWER: Undefined
5. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which is true regarding a distribution that is heavily skewed to the right?
W) The mean is greater than the median
X) The median is greater than the mean
Y) The median and mean are roughly equal
Z) The mean and the median have no fixed relationship
ANSWER: W) The mean is greater than the median
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Select all of the following three taxa that are not phyla. 1. Chordata 2. Brachiopoda 3. Fungi
ANSWER: 3 only
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice When Liam the orangutan is ready to mate, he presents an elaborate eating routine for potential mates. However, when Lucas the tree is ready to mate, he presents an elaborate dance routine for potential mates. These two are unable to mate together due most directly to what concept?
W) Behavioral isolation
X) Habitat isolation
Y) Mechanical isolation
Z) Temporal isolation
ANSWER: W) Behavioral isolation
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Select all of the following four acids that are polyprotic. 1. Sulfuric acid 2. Nitric acid 3. Hydrochloric acid 4. Acetic acid
ANSWER: 1 only
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which thermodynamic law states that the enthalpy change of a reaction is constant, regardless of the pathway taken?
ANSWER: Hess’ Law
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What mathematical operation is used to find torque when force and distance from where the force is applied and the axis of rotation are given?
ANSWER: Cross product
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A simple pendulum has a period of 4 seconds. The string is then cut in half and tied to the top of a box, which is then accelerated upward at 10 meters per second squared. In seconds, what is the new period of the pendulum?
ANSWER: 2
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of properties best describes mafic rocks?
W) High silica, High melting point
X) Low silica, High melting point
Y) High silica, low melting point
Z) Low silica, Low melting point
ANSWER: X) Low silica, High melting point
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three options that are atmospheric circulation cells. 1. Ferrel 2. Beaufort 3. Hadley
ANSWER: 1 and 3
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Given that quantity x has a standard deviation of 1 and quantity y has a standard deviation of 2, if z = x + y, what is the standard deviation of z?
W) √3
X) 2
Y) √5
Z) 3
ANSWER: Y) √5
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Ayden and Austin are both mowing lawns. Ayden can mow a lawn in 25 minutes and Austin can mow a law in 15 minutes. If they work together, how many minutes will they take to mow 4 lawns?
ANSWER: 37.5
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following processes is most used by yeast in anaerobic conditions?
W) Citric acid cycle
X) Pyruvate oxidation
Y) Oxidative phosphorylation
Z) Alcohol fermentation
ANSWER: Z) Alcohol fermentation
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The flow of which ion along its concentration gradient is most involved in RE-establishing resting potential?
W) Ca2+
X) Cl-
Y) K+
Z) Na+
ANSWER: Y) K+
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the point in a titration at which the indicator changes color?
ANSWER: Endpoint
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the formal charge of Xenon in Xenon Tetrafluoride?
ANSWER: 0
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Eric has a function of velocity with respect to time but really wants a function of acceleration with respect to time. What mathematical operation should he apply to his function to get what he wants?
ANSWER: Derivative
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer Water flows in a pipe of radius 2 centimeters at a velocity of 5 meters per second. If the water then flows through a pipe of radius 1 centimeter, what is its new velocity in meters per second?
ANSWER: 20
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which famous comet was used to study orbital mechanics, the history of the solar system, and has a roughly 76 year orbital period?
ANSWER: Halley's Comet
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, order the following 3 fusion processes by the increasing mass a star would need in order to use that process. 1. CNO cycle 2. Proton-proton Chain 3. Triple Alpha Process
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
15. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer By name or number, identify which of the following are true statements in boolean algebra. 1. 1 and 0 = true 2. 1 or 0 = true 3. 1 or 1 = true
ANSWER: 2, 3
15. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes a difference between an array and a list in languages such as Java or C++?
W) Arrays can only act as stacks while lists can act as other data structures
X) Lists can only act as stacks while arrays can act as other data structures
Y) Arrays can change length while lists have a fixed length
Z) Lists can change length while arrays have a fixed length
ANSWER: Z) Lists can change length while arrays have a fixed length
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the most accepted model for muscle movement?
ANSWER: Sliding filament model
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name for the method of transport through plants that involves movement through the non-living parts of the plant?
ANSWER: Apoplastic transport (also accept: Apoplastic route)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, order the following three compounds by order of increasing volatility. 1. Methane 2. Water 3. Ethanol
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What are the coefficients of octane, oxygen, carbon dioxide, and water, respectively, in the balanced reaction of the combustion of octane?
ANSWER: 2, 25, 16, 18
18. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A bubble of volume V is floating in the air when it connects to a bubble of 2V, allowing gas exchange to occur between two. Which of the following best describes what happens?
W) The bubbles both become the same size
X) The smaller bubble gets consumed by the bigger bubble
Y) The bigger bubble gets consumed by the smaller bubble
Z) Neither of the bubbles change size
ANSWER: X) The smaller bubble gets consumed by the bigger bubble
18. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A block rests on an inclined plane at an angle of 30 degrees. What is the minimum coefficient of static friction to make this possible?
W) ⅓
X) ½
Y) 1/√3
Z) 1/√2
ANSWER: Y) 1/√3

TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer While doing physics research in Antarctica, Liam notices that his radiation detectors are measuring slightly more radiation than when he was doing the same research in San Jose. ANSWER: Cosmic Microwave Background (also accept: Cosmic Microwave Background Radiation; CMB; CMBR)
