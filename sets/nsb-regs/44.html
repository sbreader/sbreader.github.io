Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Two years after carbon dioxide was sequestered by the Pacific Northwest National Lab near Wallula, Washington, the material was converted into mineral substances resembling what type of sedimentary rock?
W) Rock gypsum
X) Limestone
Y) Coal
Z) Rock salt
ANSWER: X) Limestone
1. BONUS Energy Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding nuclear energy in the United States 1) Most common reactors are thermal reactors 2) Most common reactors use U-235 as a fuel 3) Water can be used as a moderator.
ANSWER: ALL
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What star has the lowest apparent magnitude as observed from Earth?
ANSWER: THE SUN (also accept: SOL)
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of star must be present prior to a Type One-A supernova?
ANSWER: WHITE DWARF
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Before amino acids can be used in translation, the free amino acid must be bonded to what class of molecules?
ANSWER: tRNA
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Certain plants have leaves in which bundle-sheath cells are tightly packed around veins, which are then surrounded by mesophyll cells. This anatomy is associated with plants that perform what type of photosynthesis?
ANSWER: C4 (also accept: HATCH-SLACK PATHWAY; HATCH-SLACK CYCLE)
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following terms best describes the relationship between 1-butanol and 4-butanol?
W) Structural isomers
X) Same compound
Y) Diastereomers
Z) Enantiomers
ANSWER: X) Same compound
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of xenon tetrafluoride 1) It is tetrahedral 2) It has no net dipole moment 3) It contains two unpaired electrons.
ANSWER: 2
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A 1,000-kilogram block of density 800 kilograms per cubic meter is placed in a fluid of density 1,000 kilograms per cubic meter. What percentage of the block’s total volume floats above the surface of the fluid?
ANSWER: 20
5. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A lightning bolt strikes a power station, transmitting a current of 30,000 amps for 100 microseconds into it, which is currently charging a 2,000-milliampere-hour battery. Assuming that the charge from the lightning bolt is perfectly transmitted into the battery, which of the following percentages is closest to the percentage of the battery that it would charge?
W) Less than 1
X) 25
Y) 50
Z) 75
ANSWER: W) Less than 1
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What value of x minimizes the function f(x) = x2-6x + 2?
ANSWER: 3
6. BONUS Math Multiple Choice If f(x) = √(x+4) and g(x) = √(x-4), what is the domain of the function (f(x))/(g(x))?
W) All real numbers greater than 4
X) All real numbers greater than or equal to 4
Y) All real numbers greater than or equal to –4
Z) All real numbers between –4 and 4
ANSWER: W) All real numbers greater than 4
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Under limited nutrient conditions Bacillus subtilis initiates a morphological change, notably documented by Robert Koch, that results in the formation of what structure?
ANSWER: ENDOSPORE (also accept: SPORE)
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for phages that can reproduce using both the lytic and lysogenic cycle?
ANSWER: TEMPERATE
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following acids is triprotic?
W) Sulfuric acid
X) Hydrocyanic acid
Y) Pyruvic acid
Z) Arsenic acid
ANSWER: Z) Arsenic acid
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three liquids in increasing order of solubility in water at STP 1) Hexane 2) Ethanol 3) Ethyl acetate.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The grand unified theory joins together all of the fundamental forces except which one?
ANSWER: GRAVITY
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer Bill pulls a sled with 10 newtons of force using a rope that is angled 60 degrees above the horizontal. How much work in joules does he perform on the sled if he pulls the sled for 3 meters over the course of 4 seconds?
ANSWER: 15
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer To what geologic era would you travel if you wanted to observe living dinosaurs?
ANSWER: MESOZOIC
10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice In a cooling magma chamber, which of the following minerals will crystallize first?
W) Quartz
X) Feldspar
Y) Amphibole
Z) Olivine
ANSWER: Z) Olivine
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is 27 raised to the 4/3 power?
ANSWER: 81
11. BONUS Math Short Answer One endpoint of a diameter of a sphere centered at (3, 4, –7) is (5, –6, 2). What are the coordinates of the other endpoint of that diameter?
ANSWER: (1, 14, –16) (also accept: x = 1, y = 14, AND z = –16)
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer SLAC researchers recently published a report on the development of a new, cheap catalyst for the oxygen-evolving reaction relevant to fuel production. On what molecule do these catalysts act to evolve oxygen?
ANSWER: WATER (also accept: H2O)
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of waves that can be modeled and predicted using the current iteration of the Community Atmosphere Model, currently being used by Lawrence Berkeley National Lab researchers 1) Tsunamis 2) Tidal bores 3) Wind wave.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice When performing bomb calorimetry, which of the following physical quantities is being held constant?
W) Pressure
X) Volume
Y) Temperature
Z) Entropy
ANSWER: X) Volume
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three molecules in terms of increasing dipole moment 1) O-dihydroxybenzene 2) M-dihydroxybeneze 3) P-dihydroxybenzene.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
14. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about the function f of x = cosine of x?
W) It is neither even nor odd
X) It has the horizontal asymptote y = 1
Y) It has period 2π
Z) It is not differentiable at x = 0
ANSWER: Y) It has period 2π
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Simplify the following expression involving the complex number i: 4i + 3i2 + 2i3 + i4
ANSWER: –2 + 2i (do not accept: 2i – 2)
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer SLAC scientists recently developed a new technique for probing the electronic structure of solid materials via high harmonic generation, or HHG. In this technique, light is produced via the oscillations of what subatomic particle?
ANSWER: ELECTRON
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Chemists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab developed ReACT protein-labeling technology to address the fact that, while previous bioconjugation techniques were very effective at labeling sulfur atoms, they often disrupted the function of the protein. This is because previous techniques labeled what amino acid?
ANSWER: CYSTEINE
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What form of magnetism is demonstrated when a material's magnetic moment opposes the direction of an externally-applied magnetic field?
ANSWER: DIAMAGNETISM
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer If a radioactive element’s half-life is 60 days, then, to the nearest whole number, how many grams of a 1-kilogram sample remain after 300 days?
ANSWER: 31
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the term for the set of planets that will exhibit a full range of phases as observed from Earth?
W) Terrestrial
X) Jovian
Y) Inferior
Z) Superior
ANSWER: Y) Inferior
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Main-sequence stars larger than our sun can fuse hydrogen into helium using what cycle, which involves the usage of carbon as a catalyst?
ANSWER: CNO CYCLE (also accept: CARBON-NITROGEN-OXYGEN CYCLE)
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three Linnaean classifications that apply to the leopard 1) Carnivora 2) Chordata 3) Canidae.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three organisms that are part of the phylum Coniferophyta 1) Sequoia 2) Orchid 3) Douglas fir.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A pair of ten-sided dice, each with the numbers one through 10 printed on the faces, is rolled. What is the most probable sum of the two numbers rolled?
ANSWER: 11
19. BONUS Math Short Answer What is 8 to the power of log base 2 of 5?
ANSWER: 125
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In the citric acid cycle, how many molecules of NADH are generated from the conversion of citrate to isocitrate?
ANSWER: ZERO
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer What plant hormone, which can be synthesized by almost all known plants, is responsible for stomatal closure during dry stress?
ANSWER: ABSCISIC ACID (also accept: ABA)
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The function describing an object’s position is transformed into a function describing velocity by what operation from calculus?
ANSWER: DERIVATIVE (also accept: DIFFERENTIATION; DERIVATIVE OPERATION)
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two 6-kilogram masses are connected by a spring with a force constant of 300 newtons per meter. If the spring is compressed by five centimeters, what is the initial acceleration, in meters per second squared, of one of the masses?
ANSWER: 2.5
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three chemicals whose addition will increase the percent ionization of acetic acid in a solution 1) Water 2) Concentrated acetic acid 3) Concentrated hydrochloric acid.
ANSWER: 1
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three reactions that would favor reactants over products 1) A reaction with K eq of 3 2) A reaction with positive delta G 3) An endothermic reaction with negative delta S.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of well has water coming out of the ground freely because the pressure surface is above ground level?
ANSWER: FLOWING ARTESIAN (do not accept: ARTESIAN)
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of unconformity occurs when layers of rock are deposited on an erosional surface of horizontal, undeformed rock?
ANSWER: DISCONFORMITY
