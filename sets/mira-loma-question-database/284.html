6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is false regarding nuclear energy?
W) Breeder reactors use Uranium-238 to produce energy.
X) Some thermal breeder reactors use fluoride based molten salt to achieve high operating temperatures.
Y) Uranium-234, Uranium-238, and Plutonium-237 are the primary isotopes used as nuclear fuel in reactors.
Z) Fusion reactors would use deuterium and tritium as fuel.
ANSWER: Y) Uranium-234, Uranium-238, and Plutonium-237 are the primary isotopes used as nuclear fuel in reactors.
