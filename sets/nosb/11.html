Packet 11 (2018 Regional Round 11) of National Ocean Sciences Bowl:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the deep, active seismic area that delineates a subduction zone?
ANSWER: Benioff zone (also accept: Wadati-Benioff zone)
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In low and middle latitudes, a permanent thermocline is always present at approximately which depth, in meters?
W) 20-100
X) 100-500
Y) 200-1000
Z) 1000-2000
ANSWER: Y) 200-1000
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What would allow scientists to assume that net global ocean freshwater transport (averaged over several years) is nearly zero?
ANSWER: (Measurements indicating) mean salinity is nearly constant
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The oldest ocean crust known to remain today is approximately how many million years old?
W) 340
X) 210
Y) 170
Z) 130
ANSWER: W) 340
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice If you wanted to take in situ images of plankton and particulates with an underwater microscope, what instrument would you use?
W) CTD
X) ADCP
Y) DPPT
Z) VPR
ANSWER: Z) VPR
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Averaged over the global oceans, evaporation exceeds precipitation by approximately how many grams of water per year?
W) 5 × 1018
X) 126 × 1018
Y) 40 × 1018
Z) 17 × 1018
ANSWER: Y) 40 × 1018
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the nontechnical name given to the atmospheric river that transports moisture from Hawaii to the US West coast?
ANSWER: The Pineapple Express
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following structures does an adult swordfish lack?
W) Pectoral fin
X) Swim bladder
Y) Lateral line
Z) Teeth
ANSWER: Z) Teeth
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the process called during ice formation when salt is pushed from the forming ice and expelled into the surrounding water?
ANSWER: Brine rejection
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What genus of bacteria was recently discovered to consume plastic in the ocean?
ANSWER: Ideonella
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to water trapped in open spaces that exist between particles in sediments?
ANSWER: Pore water (also accept: interstitial water)
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the lowest temperature that Antarctic fishes can withstand, in degrees Celsius?
W) -2.5
X) 0
Y) 5
Z) 10
ANSWER: W) -2.5
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice How would the solubility of carbon dioxide in a warming climate affect the concentration of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere?
W) The concentration will increase, causing a positive feedback loop
X) The concentration will increase, causing a negative feedback loop
Y) The concentration will decrease, causing a positive feedback loop
Z) The concentration will decrease, causing a negative feedback loop
ANSWER: W) The concentration will increase, causing a positive feedback loop
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What species is used instead of pesticides in salmon farming to cure sea lice?
ANSWER: Lumpsuckers (also accept: lumpfish; Cyclopterus lumpus)
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the name of the physical state that ensues when waves reach their maximum size for a given set of storm conditions?
W) Full Fetch Conditions
X) Storm Surge State
Y) Fully Developed Sea
Z) High Spring Tide
ANSWER: Y) Fully Developed Sea
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following fishes undergoes a period of suspended development or diapause?
W) Salmon
X) Killifish
Y) Butterflyfish
Z) Sturgeon
ANSWER: X) Killifish
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In which geological time period do scientists theorize that all marine phyla first appeared collectively in the ocean?
ANSWER: Cambrian
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The adage – "Red sky at night, sailors' delight. Red sky in morning, sailors take warning" – has scientific basis at what latitudes in both the Northern and Southern hemispheres?
ANSWER: At 30 to 60 degrees latitude
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Annually, what percentage of carbon dioxide emitted to the atmosphere is absorbed by the ocean?
W) 49%
X) 37%
Y) 74%
Z) 26%
ANSWER: Z) 26%
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the two ways that a benthic invertebrate like a limpet can travel long distances?
ANSWER: Rafting AND larval dispersal
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true regarding non-dispersive, shallow-water waves?
W) Phase velocity depends on wavelength
X) Group velocity depends on depth
Y) Phase and group velocities are the same
Z) Energy moves at the same velocity for all wavelengths
ANSWER: W) Phase velocity depends on wavelength
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the measurement and description of the physical features of oceans, seas, coastal areas, lakes and rivers?
ANSWER: Hydrography
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following analytical instruments would be used to quantify trace metal constituents of seawater?
W) Inductively coupled plasma mass spectometry
X) Gas chromatography mass spectometry
Y) Scintillation counting
Z) X-ray diffraction
ANSWER: W) Inductively coupled plasma mass spectometry
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer For filter feeders, what are two ways that food particles can reach the animal?
ANSWER: ANY 2 OF: external currents, currents created by flagella or cilia, currents created by muscular activity
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What does the name of the IODP drill ship Chikyu mean when translated to English?
W) Core
X) Ocean
Y) Earth
Z) Drill
ANSWER: Y) Earth
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What evidence best supports the claim that New Zealand sits on its own continent, Zealandia?
W) The ocean over Zealandia is shallow
X) It is 6 times larger than Madagascar
Y) The crust of Zealandia is mostly thicker than oceanic crust
Z) Zealandia is made primarily of siliceous, continental rocks
ANSWER: Z) Zealandia is made primarily of siliceous, continental rocks
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To two significant digits, what is the activity coefficient for ionic calcium in seawater if the actual concentration is 10.3 moles per kilogram and the effective concentration is 9.3 moles per kilogram?
ANSWER: 0.90
