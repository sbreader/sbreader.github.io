1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Sanjay Suresh, currently experiencing rhabdomyolysis, can only exert a maximum force of 10 newtons on any object. An object with a mass of 2 kilograms is currently 5 meters above the ground. What is the minimum amount of work, in joules, Sanjay can perform on the block as it falls?
ANSWER: 50
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Given a planet with mass M and radius R, under which of the following changes would the surface gravitational force be the greatest?
W) The planet’s radius doubles and its density remains the same
X) The planet’s radius is halved and its density is halved
Y) The planet’s density is doubled and its radius is halved
Z) The planet’s radius is halved and its density remains the same
ANSWER: W) The planet’s radius doubles and its density remains the same
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The statement that angles between two corresponding faces of a crystal are constant is attributed to which scientist?
ANSWER: STENO
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the type of hydrothermal vent that has water of intermediate temperature, emitting lighter colored minerals containing elements such as barium and calcium?
ANSWER: WHITE SMOKER
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The Van’t Hoff equation is used to calculate the equilibrium constant when what other parameter is changed?
ANSWER: TEMPERATURE
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following expressions does not equal to the gas constant R?
W) PV/nT
X) M(urms)2/3T (big M times RMS squared divided by 3T)
Y) PVm/MT (P V little m divided by the quantity big M times T)
Z) PM/rhoT
ANSWER: Y) PVm/MT (P V little m divided by the quantity big M times T)
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The medulla oblongata checks the pH of what fluid as an indicator for blood CO2 concentration?
ANSWER: CEREBROSPINAL FLUID (also accept: CSF)
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What amino acid has the highest hydrophobicity?

8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers at Ames national lab are investigating the microstructure of polycyclosilanes. In these cyclosilanes, what is the molecular geometry present around each silicon atom?

Y) Trigonal Pyramidal

9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Two true-breeding pea plants for pea color are crossed. Given that 100% of the offspring are yellow, which of the following must be true?
W) One of the parents had the recessive gene
X) One of the parents had both the dominant and recessive gene
Y) Neither parents were homozygous
Z) Gene linkage must have occurred
ANSWER: Y) Neither parents were homozygous
