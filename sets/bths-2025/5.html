1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Alzheimer’s Disease is a neurodegenerative disorder that can lead to memory loss, decrease in balance and coordination, and even death. Which of the following is a drug for Alzheimer’s Disease?
W) Remoraid
X) Sentret
Y) Galantamine
Z) Reuniclus
ANSWER: Y) Galantamine
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The bite of a deer tick is correlated to which of the following parasitic diseases?
W) Leishmaniasis
X) Babesiosis
Y) Malaria
Z) Toxoplasmosis
ANSWER: X) Babesiosis
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the name for the energy required to decompose a nucleus into its components?
W) Nucleon’s energy
X) Ionization energy
Y) Binding energy
Z) Radioactive energy
ANSWER: Y) Binding energy
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following radiotracers can be used to assess the damage to the heart muscle after a heart attack?
W) iodine-131
X) carbon-14
Y) thallium-201
Z) None of the above
ANSWER: Y) thallium-201
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A circle in 2D space has a radius of 3. It undergoes a linear transformation defined by the matrix [2,2 | 3,4]. What is its new area?
ANSWER: 18π
3. BONUS Math Short Answer Given the set {1,2,3}, how many pairs of subsets A and B can be formed such that A and B are disjoint?
ANSWER: 27
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, name all the places where mass wasting can NOT occur? 1) gentle slopes 2) steep slopes 3) flat areas
ANSWER: None of them
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of active galaxy accounts for 10% of all galaxies, and is characterized by active nuclei with luminosities comparable to the brightness of all its stars?
ANSWER: Seyfert Galaxies
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Object A and Object B are both thrown upwards, If object B is rotating and has twice the kinetic energy of object A, what is the ratio of the maximum height of object A to the maximum height of object B?
ANSWER: 1/2
5. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A one kilogram block of ice at 0 degrees celsius is placed into a perfectly insulated, sealed container that has 2 kilograms of water also at 0 degrees celsius. If the container is flexible and the water and ice completely fill the container, which statement best describes what will eventually happen to the system?
W) The water will freeze leaving only ice
X) The ice will melt leaving only water
Y) The water will freeze, then melt
Z) The amount of water and ice will remain constant
ANSWER: Z) The amount of water and ice will remain constant
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Regions of a gene that contain a high frequency of guanine and cytosine connected by a phosphodiester bond are known as what?
ANSWER: CPG ISLANDS (also accept: CGIs)
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice CpG islands play an important role in gene expression and are usually present near or in promoter regions of many genes. How do these CpG islands affect gene expression in humans?
W) DNA Methylation of cytosine residues leads to gene silencing
X) DNA Methylation of guanine residues leads to gene silencing
Y) DNA Acetylation of cytosine residues leads to gene silencing
Z) DNA Acetylation of guanine residues leads to gene silencing
ANSWER: W) DNA Methylation of cytosine residues leads to gene silencing
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What do ligands behave as?
W) Arrhenius bases
X) Arrhenius acids
Y) Lewis bases
Z) Lewis acids
ANSWER: Y) Lewis bases
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When chromite is reduced by carbon, what does it produce?
ANSWER: FERROCHROME (also accept: Fe(s) + 2Cr(s) + 4CO(g))
8. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following functions exhibit constant curvature?
W) x3
X) √(3-x2)
Y) 3x
Z) log3(x)
ANSWER: X) √(3-x2)
8. BONUS Math Multiple Choice An ant stands on a vertex of a cube with a side length of 3. What is the shortest distance it must travel along the surface of the cube to reach the opposite vertex of the cube?
W) 3√3
X) 6
Y) 3√5
Z) 3√6
ANSWER: Y) 3√5
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In stellar structure, what is the balance between the outward pressure of gas and the inward force of gravity called?
ANSWER: Hydrostatic equilibrium (also accept: hydrostatic balance; hydrostasy)
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of optical telescopes?
W) They are able to be used in interferometers
X) They are affected by light pollution
Y) They are unaffected by chromatic aberration
Z) They can study very faint sources
ANSWER: Y) They are unaffected by chromatic aberration
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A simple harmonic oscillator consists of a block attached to a spring which is set up on a table that exerts a frictional force. Which of the following systems has friction as an internal force?
W) The system consisting of only the table
X) The system consisting of only the block
Y) The system consisting of the block and spring
Z) The system consisting of the block, table, and spring
ANSWER: Z) The system consisting of the block, table, and spring
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer Which principle states that every point on a wavefront is a source of wavelets that spread out in the forward direction at the same speed as the wave itself.
ANSWER: Huygens Principle (also accept: Huygens-Fresnel Principle)
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice One of the key traits of plants is that they alternate between two types of generations throughout multiple life cycles. The names for these types of generations are:
W) Charophyte and sporophyte
X) Sporophyte and gametophyte
Y) Gametophyte and charophyte
Z) Hydrophyte and charophyte
ANSWER: X) Sporophyte and gametophyte
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term that refers to the symbiotic relationship between fungi and plants?
ANSWER: MYCORRHIZA (also accept: MYCORRHIZAE)
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of an object that absorbs all electromagnetic radiation it comes in contact with?
ANSWER: BLACKBODY
12. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following scientists developed the quantum model for the hydrogen atom?
W) Schrodinger
X) Planck
Y) de Broglie
Z) Einstein
ANSWER: W) Schrodinger
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the only number that satisfies the equation x2 = x4 but not the equation x = x2?
ANSWER: -1
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Compute two times the cosine of the imaginary unit, denoted as ‘i’.
ANSWER: 1/e + e (also accept: e-1 + e)
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of metamorphism is characterized by long term low temperatures and high differential pressure?
ANSWER: Dynamic metamorphism (also accept: Cataclasis)
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The richest uranium ores in the United States are found in rocks from which era?
ANSWER: Mesozoic
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following must be true when an object moving in the x-y plane is getting closer to the origin?
W) The Velocity in both the x-direction and y-direction is less than zero
X) The product of the x-position and velocity in the x-direction plus the product of the y-position and velocity in the y-direction is less than zero
Y) The Velocity in both the x-direction and y-direction is greater than zero
Z) The product of the x-position and velocity in the x-direction plus the product of the y-position and velocity in the y-direction is greater than zero
ANSWER: X) The product of the x-position and velocity in the x-direction plus the product of the y-position and velocity in the y-direction is less than zero
15. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A particle with total energy E moves in one dimension in a region where the potential energy is U. The acceleration of the particle is zero when:
W) The potential energy equals the total energy
X) The rate of change of potential energy equals zero
Y) The potential energy equals zero
Z) The kinetic energy equals zero
ANSWER: X) The rate of change of potential energy equals zero
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Many plants release toxins in order to prevent herbivory. Which of the following is NOT a toxin?
W) Microcystin
X) Forskolin
Y) Botox
Z) Ricin
ANSWER: X) Forskolin

Z) Blue-green algae

Assuming both ships maintain constant speeds and travel in straight lines, after how many seconds will the ships be closest to each other?
W) 108
X) 120

ANSWER: 3,2,1 (also accept: Stellar Associations, Open Clusters, Globular Clusters)
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Most nuclei more massive than which element are formed by neutron capture?
ANSWER: Iron
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What atom undergoes a spin-flip transition, where the direction of the electron's spin is reversed relative to the spin of the proton, which releases a photon with a wavelength of twenty one centimeters?

20. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the name of the phenomenon where a lens cannot focus all wavelengths of light at a single point?
ANSWER: Chromatic aberration (also accept: chromatic distortion; color fringing; spherochromatism; purple fringing)

W) Algae
X) Ferns
