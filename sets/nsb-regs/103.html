2. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Line losses occur when electricity is transported long distances over transmission lines. Which of the following units is an appropriate measure for line losses?

ANSWER: X) Megawatts per mile
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Dr. Ecniecs, who loves riddles, gave the following riddle about the periodic table: Element A is left-right adjacent to element B. What are the identities of elements A and B?
