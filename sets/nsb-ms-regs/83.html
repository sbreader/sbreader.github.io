Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following anatomical structures is missing in the hagfish:
W) heart
X) brain
Y) notocord
Z) jaw
ANSWER: Z) jaw
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name all of the following 4 organisms that are echinoderms: squid; snails; starfish; lobsters
ANSWER: STARFISH
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is the most common electronic device used for storing electric energy in a process commonly called charging:
W) capacitor
X) transformer
Y) rectifier
Z) semiconductor
ANSWER: W) capacitor
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer What modern solid-state electronic device is used as an amplifier in modern radios and, through its wide use as an electronic switch, is a basic component in modern logic circuits?
ANSWER: TRANSISTOR
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Giant kelp found in oceans do NOT possess which of the following structures:
W) holdfasts
X) gas bladders
Y) stipes
Z) roots
ANSWER: Z) roots
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What numerical albedo value is given to a surface that reflects 100% of incident light?
ANSWER: 1
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice People who are lactose intolerant are unable to digest the sugar present in which of the following:
W) milk
X) wheat
Y) peanuts
Z) eggs
ANSWER: W) milk
5. BONUS Energy Short Answer Arrange the following 3 choices in INCREASING order of distance: 1 mile; 2 kilometers; 1,600 yards
ANSWER: 1,600 YARDS; 1 MILE; 2 KILOMETERS
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the most common term used for the biological process of converting atmospheric nitrogen into a form readily available for use in organisms?
ANSWER: NITROGEN FIXATION (also accept: FIXATION)
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following groups of organisms is most commonly associated with the ability to fix nitrogen:
W) plants
X) animals
Y) plants and animals
Z) bacteria
ANSWER: Z) bacteria
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is the inverse of electrical resistance:
W) voltage
X) conductivity
Y) amperage
Z) ohms
ANSWER: X) conductivity
7. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is the current carrier in a typical electrical conductor:
W) free protons
X) mobile electrons
Y) stable atoms
Z) hydrogen ions
ANSWER: X) mobile electrons
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which 2 of the following 4 choices represent the suspended load of a river that gives a slow-moving river a cloudy appearance: gravel; clay; silt; sand
ANSWER: CLAY; SILT
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The average depth of the ocean basin floor is:
W) 1,000 to 2,000 meters
X) 2,000 to 4,000 meters
Y) 4,000 to 6,000 meters
Z) 6,000 to 10,000 meters
ANSWER: Y) 4,000 to 6,000 meters
10. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Name all of the following 4 choices that are NOT SI units: meter; kilogram; degree Celsius; mole
ANSWER: DEGREE CELSIUS
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Convert 5 meters per second to kilometers per hour:
ANSWER: 18
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a lipid:
W) lipase
X) myoglobin
Y) trypsin
Z) corn oil
ANSWER: Z) corn oil
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms contains the most chitin by percent of body weight:
W) leopard
X) ant
Y) squid
Z) whale
ANSWER: X) ant
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Nitrogen, sulfur, and selenium are all:
W) metals
X) nonmetals
Y) metalloids
Z) noble gases
ANSWER: X) nonmetals
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Name all of the following 3 substances that are allotropes or different chemical forms of carbon: diamond; graphite; topaz
ANSWER: DIAMOND; GRAPHITE
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a volcanic rock that is so lightweight that it can sometimes float on water:
W) obsidian
X) pitchstone
Y) pumice
Z) basalt
ANSWER: Y) pumice
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What 4 planets of our solar system have the highest mean density?
ANSWER: MERCURY; VENUS; EARTH; MARS (also accept: in any order)
14. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes two angles that share a common side and a common vertex and do not overlap:
W) adjacent
X) parallel
Y) alternate
Z) vertical
ANSWER: W) adjacent
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Subtract the following 2 polynomials and simplify: (6x2 – 7x + 13) – (–x2 + 4x – 3)
ANSWER: 7x2 – 11x + 16
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the common name for the organic solvent which is the main ingredient in nail polish remover?
ANSWER: ACETONE
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What element makes up about 24% of the Sun's mass?
ANSWER: HELIUM
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What parts of a flower make up the corolla?
ANSWER: PETALS
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name all of the following 4 plants that have male and female flowers on the same plant: apple tree; tomato; maple tree; pumpkin
ANSWER: ALL
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many carbons are there in an ethanol molecule?
ANSWER: 2
17. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is LEAST accurate regarding most solids:
W) particles in a solid vibrate around fixed positions
X) particles in a solid have no kinetic energy
Y) most solids are crystalline
Z) solids have definite melting points in their pure state
ANSWER: X) particles in a solid have no kinetic energy
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following volcanic landforms is characterized by relatively quiet eruptions with little or no pyroclastic flows:
W) stratovolcano
X) cinder cone
Y) shield
Z) caldera
ANSWER: Y) shield
18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Mudstone, or mudrock, is which of the following:
W) fine-grained sedimentary rock
X) coarse-grained sedimentary rock
Y) fine-grained metamorphic rock
Z) coarse-grained metamorphic rock
ANSWER: W) fine-grained sedimentary rock
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If an ordered pair has an x-coordinate of zero, its graph lies on what part of the coordinate plane?
ANSWER: Y-AXIS (also accept: VERTICAL AXIS)
20. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer In the United States, what crop is primarily used for ethanol fuel production?
ANSWER: CORN (also accept: MAIZE)
20. BONUS Energy Short Answer Name whether the following 3 substances are primarily derived from plants or animals, respectively: coal; cotton; lard
ANSWER: COAL = PLANT; COTTON = PLANT; LARD = ANIMAL
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many chambers does the heart of a shark have?
ANSWER: 2
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name all of the following 4 groups of organisms in which mitosis occurs: birds; reptiles; mollusks; bacteria
ANSWER: BIRDS; REPTILES; MOLLUSKS (also accept: ALL BUT BACTERIA)
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the density, in grams per cubic centimeter, of a sample of plastic that floats with 45% of its volume submerged in pure water?
ANSWER: 0.45
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer Ignoring friction, what is the acceleration, in meters per second squared, when 1,000 newtons of force are applied to move a 200-kilogram crate across a level floor?
ANSWER: 5
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Marble is a metamorphic rock primarily composed of:
W) quartz
X) granite
Y) dolomite
Z) calcite
ANSWER: Z) calcite
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What unstable substance does the stratosphere contain that functions to protect life on Earth?
ANSWER: OZONE (also accept: OZONE LAYER; O3)
24. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If 2 coplanar parallel lines are intersected by a transversal line, what is the sum of all angles formed?
ANSWER: 720
24. BONUS Math Short Answer Malcolm drove his car a distance of 2 miles in a time of 2 minutes 30 seconds. Find his average speed, in miles per hour:
ANSWER: 48
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name for the plastic-ware consisting of two overlapping round plastic or glass dishes used by microbiologists in which to grow bacteria?
ANSWER: PETRI DISH (also accept: PETRI)
25. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is typically used by a microbiologist for growing bacterial cultures in petri dishes:
W) autoclave
X) incubator
Y) laminar flow hood
Z) fume hood
ANSWER: X) incubator
