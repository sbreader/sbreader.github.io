1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer While the set of all invertible square matrices forms a general linear group, the set of all square matrices forms what weaker algebraic structure, defined as a group that lacks guaranteed inverses?
ANSWER: MONOID
1. BONUS Math Short Answer A number n is squarish if the distance from n to the nearest perfect square is itself a perfect square. The quantity of squarish numbers less than N approaches α Nβ as N goes to infinity. What is the value of beta?
ANSWER: 3/4
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer One of the important results in p-adic analysis is the statement that if a polynomial equation has a simple root modulo a prime number p, then this root corresponds to a unique root of the same equation modulo any higher power of p. What is the name for this lemma?
ANSWER: HENSEL’S LEMMA
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Consider all degree-five polynomials with integer coefficients and leading coefficient 7. For each polynomial, take the greatest common divisor of all the values it produces at integer inputs. What is the largest possible value of this greatest common divisor?
ANSWER: 840
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The combinatorial game of nim involves removing objects from distinct heaps. A game with multiple heaps can always be reduced to a single heap by the nim-sum operation. This is due to what theorem in game theory, which states that any impartial game is always equivalent to a single nim heap?
ANSWER: SPRAGUE GRUNDY THEOREM (also accept: SPRAGUE THEOREM; GRUNDY THEOREM)
