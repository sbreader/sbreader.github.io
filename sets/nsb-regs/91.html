1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Uniform circular motion is achieved by applying force in what direction?
W) Toward the center of rotation
X) Away from the center of rotation
Y) In the direction of motion
Z) Against the direction of motion
ANSWER: W) Toward the center of rotation
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer What radioactive isotope of carbon is prominently used in radiometric dating?
ANSWER: CARBON-14 (also accept: 14)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is considered a strong acid?
W) Hydrofluoric acid
X) Nitric acid
Y) Formic acid
Z) Nitrous acid
ANSWER: X) Nitric acid
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following polyprotic acids has only two pKa values?
W) Ascorbic acid
X) Phosphoric acid
Y) Arsenic acid
Z) Citric acid
ANSWER: W) Ascorbic acid
3. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A and B are statements, and B is false. Which of the following is true regarding the statement "B implies A?"
W) It is true regardless of the truth value of A
X) It is false regardless of the truth value of A
Y) It is true only when A is true and false otherwise
Z) It is true only when A is false and false otherwise
ANSWER: W) It is true regardless of the truth value of A
3. BONUS Math Short Answer The line with equation y = 5x + 4 is reflected across the line y = x. What are the slope and y-intercept of its image, respectively?
ANSWER: SLOPE = 1/5, y-INTERCEPT = –4/5 (also accept: 0.2 and –0.8; respectively)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of fault created the Loma Prieta earthquake in 1989?
W) Normal
X) Reverse
Y) Strike-slip
Z) Thrust
ANSWER: Y) Strike-slip
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the parent rock of slate?
ANSWER: SHALE
5. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Wind, moving water, sunlight, and heat from Earth’s interior are sources of what type of energy?
W) Renewable
X) Fossil
Y) Alternative
Z) Reusable
ANSWER: W) Renewable
5. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following was not one of the top three carbon-dioxide-emitting countries in 2014?
W) India
X) China
Y) United States
Z) Russia
ANSWER: Z) Russia
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which chamber in the heart has the highest systolic pressure?
W) Right atrium
X) Right ventricle
Y) Left atrium
Z) Left ventricle
ANSWER: Z) Left ventricle
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer List the correct order of a secretory protein pathway from synthesis to secretion 1) Golgi apparatus 2) Endoplasmic reticulum 3) Plasma membrane.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3 (also accept: ENDOPLASMIC RETICULUM, GOLGI APPARATUS, PLASMA MEMBRANE)
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of rock is found in most cave systems?
W) Sandstone
X) Granite
Y) Basalt
Z) Limestone
ANSWER: Z) Limestone
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer According to the Richter scale, to the nearest whole number, how many times as much energy does a 5.7 earthquake release as a 4.7 earthquake?
ANSWER: 32
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice At the DOE's Advanced Photon Source, researchers are studying industrial catalysts used in petrochemical processing. Which of the following correctly describes most of these catalysts?
W) Organic molecules
X) Enzymes
Y) Homogeneous inorganic
Z) Heterogeneous inorganic
ANSWER: Z) Heterogeneous inorganic
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Lawrence Berkeley National Lab scientists have been studying XPG, a protein found to play a role in protecting the body from cancer. Which of the following proteins likely plays a similar role to XPG?
W) Phosphofructokinase
X) p53
Y) Aldolase
Z) Carbonic anhydrase
ANSWER: X) p53
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A one-to-one function has its graph in the second quadrant. In which quadrant will the graph of its inverse lie?
ANSWER: FOURTH (also accept: 4)
9. BONUS Math Short Answer If the line tangent to the graph of the differentiable function f of x at the point (–2, 5) passes through the point (3.5, –3), then what is value of the derivative of f of x when x = –2?
ANSWER: –16/11 (also accept: –1 5/11)
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What gas, whose methylated derivative is used in rocket fuels, is produced by bubbling ammonia through a sodium hypochlorite solution?
ANSWER: HYDRAZINE (also accept: N2H4)
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The isomerization reaction of a particular cis-alkene to its trans-isomer is first order. In terms of liters, moles, and seconds, what are the units for the rate constant of this reaction?
ANSWER: INVERSE SECONDS (also accept: SECONDS TO THE NEGATIVE ONE; 1 OVER SECONDS)
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Grasses provide an example of a root system that is best described as which of the following?
W) Monocot tap
X) Dicot tap
Y) Monocot fibrous
Z) Dicot fibrous
ANSWER: Y) Monocot fibrous
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer If a Gram stain were performed on E. coli with saffranin as the counterstain, what color would the bacteria appear to be?
ANSWER: RED (also accept: PINK)
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer On a standing wave, what term refers to the points at which the amplitude is maximized?
ANSWER: ANTI-NODE (do not accept: PEAK)
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Suppose the Earth’s radius were decreased by half but its mass remained the same. To one decimal place, by what factor will the surface escape velocity be multiplied?
ANSWER: 1.4 (do not accept: √2)
13. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer While fluorescent lamps have a much higher efficiency than incandescent bulbs, they require special disposal concerns due to their construction involving what heavy metal vapor?

13. BONUS Energy Short Answer What are the coolant and the moderator, respectively, in pressurized heavy water reactors?
ANSWER: HEAVY WATER AND HEAVY WATER (also accept: DEUTERIUM OXIDE AND DEUTERIUM OXIDE; D2O AND D2O)
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the repeating unit found in skeletal muscles?
