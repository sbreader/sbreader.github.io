1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Responsible for the hum of transformers, what effect occurs when ferromagnetic materials change their dimensions in response to an applied magnetic field?
ANSWER: MAGNETOSTRICTION
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer A circuit consists of a 9 volt battery connected in series to a 2 ohm resistor, an variable resistor, and a 3 ohm resistor. What is the maximum power that the variable resistor can dissipate, to the nearest watt?
ANSWER: 4
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following techniques would be most suitable for analyzing mRNA?
W) Northern Blot
X) Southern Blot
Y) Eastern Blot
Z) Western Blot
ANSWER: W) Northern Blot
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What vitamin is an essential part of Coenzyme A, which is involved in the oxidation of pyruvate in cellular respiration?
ANSWER: VITAMIN B5 (also accept: PANTOTHENIC ACID)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The highest redshift quasar to have ever been observed has a redshift between which of the following?
W) 2 to 4
X) 4 to 6
Y) 6 to 8
Z) 8 to 10
ANSWER: Y) 6 to 8
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three supercontinents from oldest to youngest 1) Pangaea 2) Rodinia 3) Gondwana.
ANSWER: 2,3,1
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The first and second terms of an arithmetic and a geometric sequence are both 8 and 12. What is the positive difference between the 4th term of the arithmetic sequence and the 4th term of the geometric sequence?
ANSWER: 7
4. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Three points in the plane, A, B, and C, form a nondegenerate triangle. Each of the points move in a single, arbitrary direction at different constant speeds. At most how many distinct instances can the three points be collinear?
W) 0
X) 1
Y) 2
Z) 3
ANSWER: Y) 2
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What law states that the molar specific heat capacity of any solid is approximately 3 times the ideal gas constant?
ANSWER: DULONG-PETIT LAW
5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following macrocyclic compounds would be the most useful for the preparation of alkyl fluorides?
W) Uroporphyrinogen III
X) Benzene
Y) 18-crown-6
Z) 2,3-epoxy-2-methylpentane
ANSWER: Y) 18-crown-6
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at the Pacific Northwest National Laboratory are studying GDF-15, which can delay the onset of type 1 diabetes. What pancreatic cells does GDF-15 act on?
ANSWER: BETA CELLS
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are looking into the use of terpenes as a potential energy source and biofuel. All terpenes are derived from what specific hydrocarbon with chemical formula C5H8?
ANSWER: ISOPRENE (also accept: 2-METHYL-1,3-BUTADIENE)
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists first discovered CP symmetry violation by studying the decay of which of the following particles?
W) Kaons
X) Pions
Y) Hyperions
Z) Neutrons
ANSWER: W) Kaons
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer An object is placed 2 meters in front of a diverging lens, producing an image that is 50 centimeters in front of the lens. What is the power of the lens?
ANSWER: -3/2 DIOPTERS
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer According to the neutral theory of evolution, what phenomena is the ultimate cause of all variation between species?
ANSWER: GENETIC DRIFT
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What abnormal condition is caused by the hypersecretion of growth hormone after the epiphyseal plates close?
ANSWER: ACROMEGALY
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice According to the Faber-Jackson relation, doubling the velocity dispersion of a galaxy will multiply the luminosity by approximately which of the following?
W) 2√2
X) 4
Y) 8
Z) 16
ANSWER: Z) 16
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes alternating light and dark bands found on some glaciers?
ANSWER: OGIVES
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice According to the prime number theorem, the number of primes less than n asymptomatically approaches which of the following functions of n?
W) n
X) n over the natural log of n
Y) n over the natural log of the natural log of n
Z) the natural log of n over n
ANSWER: X) n over the natural log of n
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the curl of the function f(x, y, z) = (xy, yz, z2)?
ANSWER: (-y, 0, -x)
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice How many stereoisomers does 2, 3-pentanediol have?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 3
Z) 4
ANSWER: Z) 4
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In X-ray spectroscopy, what element is commonly used as a window material due to its low atomic mass, making it nearly transparent to radiation?
ANSWER: BERYLLIUM
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Lab are studying high-entropy alloys. What type of alloy is formed when small atoms fill in the “holes” in metal lattices?
ANSWER: INTERSTITIAL ALLOY (also accept: INTERSTITIAL)
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Lab are studying multiple principal element alloys. What rule can be used to determine the mole fraction of each metal in an alloy from its phase diagram?
ANSWER: LEVER RULE
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What type of scattering occurs when light interacts with phonons or other acoustic modes?
ANSWER: BRILLOUIN SCATTERING (also accept: BRILLOUIN)
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer In degrees, what is the vertex angle of a shock cone produced by an object traveling at 2 times the speed of sound?
ANSWER: 60
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In mammals, which of the following molecules does NOT bind to a Toll-like receptor?
W) Flagellin
X) Lipopolysaccharide
Y) CpG DNA
Z) Spectrin
ANSWER: Z) Spectrin
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is FALSE regarding CRISPR-Cas9 technology?
W) CRISPR was initially discovered in bacteria as a defense against viruses
X) The guide RNA must exactly match the sequence of DNA for the Cas9 protein to bind
Y) The guide RNA only needs to be about 20 base pairs long
Z) CRISPR-Cas9 systems from different bacterial species can have different protospacer-adjacent motifs
ANSWER: X) The guide RNA must exactly match the sequence of DNA for the Cas9 protein to bind
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of sand dunes is most likely to migrate slowly with the wind?
W) Barchan
X) Transverse
Y) Longitudinal
Z) Parabolic
ANSWER: W) Barchan
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer To one significant figure, what is the Schwarzchild radius of a 100-solar-mass black hole, given that the mass of the sun is approximately 2 × 1030 kg?
ANSWER: 3 × 105 METERS
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the trace of a six by six matrix, where every element in the matrix is -2?
ANSWER: 12
16. BONUS Math Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following four numbers can be written as the sum of two perfect squares 1) 27 2) 129 3) 221 4) 418
ANSWER: 3 ONLY (also accept: 221)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is FALSE regarding SN1 and SN2 mechanisms?
W) SN1 reactions are first order while SN2 reactions are second order
X) SN2 reactions are characterized by an inversion of stereochemistry
Y) SN1 reactions work best in polar aprotic solution
Z) Primary carbons are more likely to react with a SN2 mechanism than a SN1 mechanism
ANSWER: Y) SN1 reactions work best in polar aprotic solution
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Toluene is reacted with nitric acid in sulfuric acid solution. By name or number, order the following three products in order of increasing prevalence in the products 1) Ortho – nitrotoluene 2) Meta – nitrotoluene 3) Para – nitrotoluene.
ANSWER: 2,3,1 (also accept: META – NITROTOLUENE, PARA – NITROTOLUENE, ORTHO – NITROTOLUENE)
18. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Laboratory are working to reduce toxic leaching from land-filled electronics waste. They have done this by developing a solder that does not contain what metal?
ANSWER: LEAD
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Laboratory are studying organometallic halide perovskites, which are often deployed in solar cells. They experimentally confirmed the presence of what effect in these materials, which is characterized by momentum-dependent splitting of spin bands in bulk crystals?
ANSWER: RASHBA EFFECT
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following four vector quantities that are pseudovectors 1) Area vector 2) Linear momentum 3) Electric field 4) Torque.
ANSWER: 1 AND 4 (also accept: AREA VECTOR AND TORQUE)
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists have discovered exotic mesons composed of 4 quarks, known as tetraquarks. What is the baryon number of a tetraquark?
ANSWER: 0
