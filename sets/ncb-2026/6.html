1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Compound X undergoes both α decay ⅓ of the time and β decay ⅔ of the time with an overall half-life of 80 seconds. What is the half-life of the alpha decay process of compound X?
ANSWER: 240s
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly identifies the stronger bond and reasoning when comparing the Fluorine-Fluorine bond in F2 and the Chlorine-Chlorine bond in Cl2?
W) The F–F bond is stronger due to better orbital overlap and a shorter bond length
X) The F-F bond is stronger due to Fluorine's higher electronegativity
Y) The Cl-Cl bond is stronger due to decreased lone pair repulsion
Z) The Cl–Cl bond is stronger because chlorine atoms are larger, allowing more effective overlap of their orbitals than fluorine
ANSWER: Y) The Cl-Cl bond is stronger due to decreased lone pair repulsion
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the 3 following types of amines that can be produced through reductive amination 1) Primary Amine 2) Secondary Amine 3) Tertiary Amine
ANSWER: 1,2,3
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Anthracene is a compound that consists of 3 fused benzene rings. What is the chemical formula for Anthracene?
ANSWER: C14H10
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What name is given to the specific composition and temperature, at which a mixture of substances melts and solidifies at the lowest possible temperature, lower than any of its individual components?
ANSWER: Eutectic point
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which element was used in a catalyst that was discovered by Fritz Haber to speed up the Haber process enough that it occurred significantly under industrially feasible conditions?
ANSWER: Iron
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many more radial nodes does a 5p orbital have than a 4f orbital?
ANSWER: 3
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer One mole of an ideal gas doubles its volume at a constant temperature of 298 K. To two significant figures, what is the work done by the gas in joules?
ANSWER: 1700 J
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following metal ions does not typically form a complex ion with hydroxide?
W) Aluminum
X) Zinc
Y) Cadmium (II)
Z) Sodium
ANSWER: Z) Sodium
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When 50 mL of an aqueous solution with an unknown concentration of KIO3 mixed with an excess of KI is titrated with 0.5 M sodium thiosulfate, it takes 25 mL of sodium thiosulfate solution to reach the endpoint. To one significant figure, what is the concentration of iodine?
ANSWER: 0.04
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following 4 carboxylic derivatives by increasing reactivity 1) Amide 2) Acid Chloride 3) Ester 4) Anhydride
ANSWER: 1,3,4,2
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify which of the following four compounds can be used to convert an acid chloride into an aldehyde 1) Lithium aluminum Hydride 2) Lithium tri aluminum hydride 3) Sodium Borohydride
ANSWER: 2 only
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements, if doped into silicon, creates an n-type semiconductor?
W) Aluminum
X) Silicon
Y) Phosphorus
Z) Sulfur
ANSWER: Y) Phosphorus
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What is most hydrogen gas produced industrially used for?
W) Hydrogen fuel cells
X) Production of ammonia
Y) Hydrogenation in organic processes
Z) production other hydrogen species
ANSWER: X) Production of ammonia
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Entropy is directly proportional to which of the following?
W) the number of microstates
X) the natural log of the number of microstates
Y) the number of microstates squared
Z) the inverse of the number of microstates
ANSWER: X) the natural log of the number of microstates
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds is most likely to be classified as a Bronsted-Lowry Acid in water?
W) NH3
X) H2O
Y) OH-
Z) Fe3+
ANSWER: X) H2O
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the oxidation state of P in the resulting compound formed when P4O10 is dissolved in water?
ANSWER: +5
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Amy is performing an experiment with the half reaction of the reduction of the permanganate ion in which she plots the pH on the x-axis and cell potential on the y-axis. If Permangante and Mn(II) ions are kept at a constant concentration of 1M, what is the slope of the line which she plots in terms of RT/F?
W) -8RT/5F
X) 8RT/5F
Y) -8ln(10)RT/5F
Z) 8ln(10)RT/5F

10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following techniques would be MOST useful for determining the difference between two enantiomers?
W) Comparison of Boiling Point
X) Combustion Analysis
Y) Mass Spectrometry
Z) Measuring optical rotation using a polarimeter
ANSWER: Z) Measuring optical rotation using a polarimeter
10. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Why does pyrrole readily undergo electrophilic aromatic substitution, but pyridine does not?
W) The nitrogen atom in pyrrole is sp3 hybridized, whereas the nitrogen atom in pyridine is sp2 hybridized.
X) Pyrrole has two double bonds, whereas pyridine has 3.
Y) The nitrogen atom in pyrrole is bonded to a hydrogen atom, whereas the nitrogen atom in pyridine is not.
Z) The lone pair on the nitrogen atom of pyrrole is delocalized around the ring, whereas the lone pair on the nitrogen atom of pyridine is not.
ANSWER: Z) The lone pair on the nitrogen atom of pyrrole is delocalized around the ring, whereas the lone pair on the nitrogen atom of pyridine is not.
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following cubic structures has the highest packing efficiency?
W) Simple cubic
X) Body-centered cubic
Y) Face-centered cubic
Z) Diamond cubic
ANSWER: Y) Face-centered cubic
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A certain cobalt(II) complex is paramagnetic and has three unpaired electrons. Is this complex more likely to be in a tetrahedral or octahedral geometry, assuming a weak-field ligand?
ANSWER: Tetrahedral
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The Nernst Heat Theorem states the entropy change accompanying any physical or chemical transformation approaches zero as the temperature approaches zero provided all the substances involved are perfectly crystalline. This is a derivation of what law of thermodynamics?
W) 0th Law of Thermodynamics
X) 1st Law of Thermodynamics
Y) 2nd Law of Thermodynamics
Z) 3rd Law of Thermodynamics
ANSWER: Z) 3rd Law of Thermodynamics
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer For an ideal gas, what quantity is given by the enthalpy – the moles times the ideal gas constant times the temperature?
ANSWER: Internal Energy
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What law states that the absorbance of light passing through a solution is directly proportional to its concentration and the path length of the sample?
ANSWER: Beer-Lambert Law
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is a type of photoluminescence where materials emit light slowly after absorbing radiation, creating a "glow-in-the-dark" effect that persists after the source is removed?
ANSWER: Phosphorescence
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term refers to the complex, unique vibration patterns specific to each molecule in IR Spectroscopy that is located below 1500 cm-1?
ANSWER: Fingerprint region
14. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Cyclopentadiene reacts with an aldehyde-substituted alkene in a Diels-Alder reaction. Which of the following explains why boron trifluoride accelerates the reaction?

X) It coordinates with the dienophile, raising its HOMO
