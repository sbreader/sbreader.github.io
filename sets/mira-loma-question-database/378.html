1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the correct name for the postzygotic barrier that causes hybrids to produce offspring that is sterile or feeble?
W) Reduced Hybrid Fertility
X) Hybrid Breakdown
Y) Offspring Degeneration
Z) Reduced Hybrid Viability
ANSWER: X) Hybrid Breakdown
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Based off the Heidy-Weinburg Equilibrium, if 60 out of a 100 alleles are A and the rest are B, then in the population, how many people contain at least one copy of the B allele?
ANSWER: 64
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the area under of the curve of the function f(x) = cos x from the interval 0 to 3π?
ANSWER: 0
2. BONUS Math Short Answer An isosceles trapezoid with an area of 128, side lengths of 10, 10, 10, and x have a height of 8. What is x?
ANSWER: 22
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Ben is pushing a grocery filled cart with an average power of P. Ben then has to push the cart up a ramp, thereby exerting twice as much force and taking three times longer. In terms of P, what was the average power that Ben had?
ANSWER: 2/3 P
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Denote all of the following four statements that are differences between Type 1 supernovas and Type 2 supernovas. 1: Type 1 supernovas involve stars that have hydrogen and Type 2 supernovas involve stars that lack hydrogen 2: Type 1 supernovas die off more gradually than Type 2 supernovas 3: Type 1 supernovas occur with Population 2 stars while Type 2 stars occur in Population 1 stars 4: Type 1 emit about 10 billion solar luminosities whereas Type 2 supernovas only emit about 1 billion solar luminosities
ANSWER: 2 AND 4
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the luminous material that makes up the Cygnus loop?
ANSWER: SUPERNOVA REMNANT
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the molecule that penicillin interferes with during the synthesis of the bacterial cell wall? The molecule takes on a bag shape that prevents bacteria from bursting from their internal osmotic pressure?
ANSWER: PEPTIDOGLYCAN
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Denote all of the following three statements that are true about penicillin 1: Penicillin is made up of a thiazolidine rine fused with a α-lactam ring 2: The stability of penicillin can be explained by the fact that its structure cannot undergo rearrangements 3: The β-lactam ring is very labile
ANSWER: 3
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In a plant, magnesium is relatively mobile and iron does not move freely. Which of the following is correct?
W) In a deficiency of iron, the older leaves will yellow before the younger because the younger has more drawing power for nutrients in short supply
X) In a deficiency of magnesium, the younger leaves will yellow before the older leaves because the older leaves has adequate supplies in store
Y) In a deficiency of iron, the younger leaves will yellow before the older leaves because the older leaves has adequate supplies in store
Z) In a deficiency of magnesium, the older leaves will yellow before the younger because the younger has more drawing power for nutrients in short supply
ANSWER: Z) In a deficiency of magnesium, the older leaves will yellow before the younger because the younger has more drawing power for nutrients in short supply
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Denote all of the following three statements that is or are not true about micronutrients. 1: Boron has a role in cell wall function and is in the form H2BO3- 2: Nickel, in the form Ni2+ and is involved in nitrogen metabolism 3: Molybdenum is active in the formation of chlorophyll
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A softball team has four boys and three girls. If the girls are required to play consecutively, as in one after another with no boys in between, how many ways are there to order the teams?
ANSWER: 720
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What are the names of the bright and dark parallel bands produced when Thomas Young demonstrated interference in light waves?
ANSWER: FRINGES (also accept: FRINGE)
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the areas of low rainfall on the leeward slopes of mountain ranges?
ANSWER: RAIN SHADOWS (also accept: RAIN SHADOW)
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer According to the Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle, the product of the uncertainties of the location and momentum of a particle cannot be smaller than what constant, in terms of h bar?
ANSWER: ½ h bar
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The left side of the Schrodinger equation is commonly written as Hψ. What is the proper name for H?
ANSWER: HAMILTONIAN
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is incorrect?
W) When PIP2 is cleaved, inositol triphosphate is left embedded in the plasma membrane
X) Scaffolding proteins permanently hold together signaling-pathway proteins at synapses
Y) Two percent of our genes code for protein kinases
Z) Phosphorylation cascades allow for a small amount of signal molecules to produce a large cellular response
ANSWER: W) When PIP2 is cleaved, inositol triphosphate is left embedded in the plasma membrane
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer List the following three enzymes in the order of their role in apoptosis and label each one as either active or inactive. CED 4, CED 9, CED 3
ANSWER: CED 9 INACTIVE, CED 4 ACTIVE, CED 3 ACTIVE
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Two circles of radius 2 and 10 are externally tangent. Line PQ is tangent to the first circle at point P and the second circle at point Q. What is the length of segment PQ?
ANSWER: 4√5
12. BONUS Math Short Answer A cube's volume increases by 180 units cubed per second. When the side length is two, how fast is the surface area increasing?
ANSWER: 360
