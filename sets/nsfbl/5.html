1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In contrast to the Central Nervous System, where oligodendrocytes and astrocytes serve as glial cells, what type of cell myelinates the axon in the PERIPHERAL nervous system?
ANSWER: SCHWANN CELLS
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify ALL of the following four statements that are true about mycorrhizae I. They cannot transfer nutrients from one plant to another II. Their growth depends on nutrients obtained from the plant III. They permit plants to survive in phosphorus-poor soil IV. They increase the absorptive surface of plants
ANSWER: II, III, AND IV
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Acids are normally stored in glass containers. However, some are stored in plastic. Which of the following concentrated acids is usually stored in a plastic container?
W) HF
X) HNO2
Y) H2SO4
Z) H3PO4
ANSWER: W) HF
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer On a cubic unit cell, there are atoms on 4 of the corners, 3 of the faces, and 1 in the center. How many atoms are contained within this unit cell?
ANSWER: 3
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Amy is playing a pipe-organ! When she keeps both ends of the organ open, she observes that the wavelength of her fundamental harmonic is 0.9 m. What are the NEXT two harmonic wavelengths?
ANSWER: 0.45 AND 0.3 m
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Arin has just discovered a new element, Sciencium-211. Unfortunately, this isotope of Sciencium is very unstable, so it decays into three particles. If momentum is conserved, to the nearest 10 kilometers per second, what is the speed of the third particle?
W) 100
X) 120
Y) 140
Z) 160
ANSWER: Z) 160

W) Paleocene-Eocene
X) Eocene-Oligocene
Y) Oligocene-Miocene
Z) Miocene-Pliocene

5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Given the Polynomial P(x) = ax3 + bx2 + cx + d, Identify all of the following statements that are always true regarding this polynomial I. 5. BONUS Math Short Answer A fair coin is flipped 8 times. What is the probability that the number of heads is a multiple of 4?
ANSWER: 9/32
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at Argonne National Lab are using x-ray light sources to help develop treatments for infectious diseases. To do so, they use particle accelerators. Which of the following is a magnetic induction accelerator?
W) Cyclotron
X) Synchrotron
Y) Storage ring
Z) Betatron
ANSWER: Z) Betatron
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Lab are studying the properties of rare earth materials and their role in making permanent magnets. ANSWER: MAGNETIC ANISOTROPY
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What irregularly shaped areas of the pancreas contain the cells that secrete glucagon and insulin, the two hormones that control glycogenolysis and glycogenesis?
ANSWER: ISLETS OF LANGERHANS
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Two parents are a couple, who are both blood type A and who both have normal vision, have a son who is blood type O and colorblind. What is the probability that their next child will be a son who also has blood type O, but has normal vision?
ANSWER: 1/16

10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding Venus is TRUE?

X) The reaction shifts to the right, turning darker brown
