1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The total length of DNA in a human nucleus is approximately how much?
W) 2.2 m
X) 2.2 cm
Y) 2.7 mm
Z) 4.2 km
ANSWER: W) 2.2 m
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice If an organism is found to excrete oxygen. It is most likely which of the following organisms?
W) vertebrate
X) bacterium
Y) fungus
Z) plant
ANSWER: Z) plant
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Bird droppings (guano), is a useful fertilizer because it contains the element
W) oxygen
X) hydrogen
Y) nitrogen
Z) iron
ANSWER: Y) nitrogen
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Waste products are removed from the kidney by the
W) ureter
X) urethra
Y) inferior vena caba
Z) aorta
ANSWER: W) ureter
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the functional unit in the kidney?
W) aorta
X) ureter
Y) cortex
Z) nephron
ANSWER: Z) nephron
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In the descending limb of Henle's loop water leaves the nephron by what?
W) diffusion
X) osmosis
Y) evaporation
Z) condensation
ANSWER: X) osmosis
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Kidney dialysis machines remove waste products from what?
W) the urine using a partially permeable membrane
X) the blood using a partially permeable membrane
Y) the blood by replacing it with fresh blood
Z) the bladder directly
ANSWER: X) the blood using a partially permeable membrane
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name 2 nitrogenous excretory products.
ANSWER: ammonia, urea, trimethylamine oxide, uric acid
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice If you wanted to observe the true color of a coral polyp of diameter 0.02 mm which of the following would be the best to use?
W) unaided eye
X) light microscope with staining
Y) light microscope without staining
Z) electron microscope
ANSWER: Y) light microscope without staining
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer The membrane-bound structures within eukaryotic a cell are known as what?
ANSWER: organelles

W) 10-20 μm
X) 50-70 μm
Y) 100-200 μm
Z) 10-20 mm

X) mesosome
Y) tail
Z) flagellum
ANSWER: Z) flagellum

X) linear and contained in the nucleus
Y) circular but not contained in the nucleus
Z) circular and contained in the nucleus
ANSWER: X) linear and contained in the nucleus

W) Golgi apparatus
X) rough endoplasmic reticulum
Y) nucleus
Z) ribosome
ANSWER: Y) nucleus
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The Krebs cycle occurs in the what?
W) rough endoplasmic reticulum
X) Golgi apparatus
Y) chloroplast
Z) mitochondrion
ANSWER: Z) mitochondrion
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the main reserve food for animal cells?
ANSWER: glycogen
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The Singer and Nicolson model for membrane structure says that a membrane consists of what?
W) phospholipid layer
X) phospholipid bilayer
Y) protein bilayer
Z) protein layer
ANSWER: X) phospholipid bilayer
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the movement of a substance from an area of high concentration to an area of low concentration?
ANSWER: diffusion
10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the interphase in the cell cycle?
W) passive phase between 2 active phases
X) active phase during DNA replication and many other processes occur
Y) period when mitosis occurs
Z) period when cytokinesis occurs
ANSWER: W) passive phase between 2 active phases
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How are replicated DNA molecules (chromosomes) moved to opposite ends of the cell?

11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is 1 of the functions of the Golgi apparatus?
W) prepare substances for exocytosis
X) excrete waste
Y) synthesize proteins
Z) respire
ANSWER: W) prepare substances for exocytosis
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the passive movement of water molecules, across a partially-permeable membrane from a region of lower solute concentration to a region of higher solute concentration know as?
ANSWER: osmosis
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the process by which the cell takes up a substance by surrounding it with a membrane known as?
