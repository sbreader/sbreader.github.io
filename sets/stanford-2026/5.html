1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Once threshold is reached in the axon hillock, which of the following ion channels would open to generate an action potential?
W) Ligand-gated sodium channels
X) Ligand-gated potassium channels
Y) Voltage-gated sodium channels
Z) Voltage-gated potassium channels
ANSWER: Y) Voltage-gated sodium channels
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following processes is least likely to directly produce a gene duplication on a chromosome?
W) Chromosomal segment inversion
X) Movement of transposable elements
Y) Uneven crossing over
Z) DNA polymerase slippage during replication
ANSWER: W) Chromosomal segment inversion
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following processes result in the emission of a positron?
W) Alpha decay
X) Beta plus decay
Y) Beta minus decay
Z) Gamma decay
ANSWER: X) Beta plus decay

X) 20

3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What platonic solid has the largest interior angles on its faces?
ANSWER: Dodecahedron
3. BONUS Math Short Answer Richard has an ice cream cone with a height that is twice its radius. He fills the cone completely with ice cream, and also adds a hemisphere above the cone with radius equal to that of the cone. What fraction of the total ice cream is contained within the cone?
ANSWER: 1/2
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers in the Johns Hopkins University Clancy Lab are studying the crystal structures of p-type organic semiconductors. Which of the following elements acts as a p-type dopant when introduced into silicon?
W) Phosphorus
X) Arsenic
Y) Antimony
Z) Boron
ANSWER: Z) Boron

ANSWER: 3, 2, 1

X) Linearly decreasing
Y) Exponentially increasing

ANSWER: Y) Exponentially increasing
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Although methanol can be made by partially oxidizing methane, this is not widely used industrially because methane readily undergoes complete oxidation into what carbon compound?
ANSWER: Carbon dioxide (also accept: CO2)
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true about the Daniell cell, an electrochemical cell with a zinc anode and copper cathode 1) Zinc ions are oxidised at the anode 2) Copper ions travel from the cathode to the anode 3) Electrons flow from the anode to the cathode.
ANSWER: 1 and 3
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When a few individuals become isolated from a larger population, the smaller group may establish a new population whose gene pool differs from the source population. What term best describes this phenomenon?
ANSWER: Founder effect

ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The magnetic field within an ideal solenoid is proportional to distance from its central axis raised to what power?
ANSWER: 0
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A ladder with a weight of 50 newtons rests on a floor with friction and leans at a 45-degree angle against a frictionless wall. In newtons, how much force does the wall apply to the ladder to keep it stationary?
ANSWER: 25

W) 3
X) 5
Y) 7
Z) Impossible to determine

9. BONUS Math Short Answer Identify all of the following three transformations that are always linear 1) Translation 2) Shear 3) Dilation.
ANSWER: 2 and 3
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at UCLA’s Wang Lab are studying superconductors. What effect describes the expulsion of magnetic flux from a material as it enters the superconducting state?
ANSWER: Meissner Effect
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at UCLA are classifying the extinct genus Prototaxites, a heterotroph with cell walls lacking chitin. Identify all of the following three conclusions that are reasonable 1) It cannot be an animal, since all animals lack cell walls 2) It cannot be a fungus, since all fungi have chitinous cell walls 3) It cannot be a plant, since plants are autotrophic.
ANSWER: 1 and 2
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Aurora typically occur within what electromagnetic layer of the atmosphere, which consists of free electrons and can reflect radio waves?
ANSWER: Ionosphere (also accept: Thermosphere)
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Despite the lunar near side and far side having experienced similar rates of large meteor impacts, extensive basaltic floods are observed almost exclusively on the near side. Which of the following could explain this asymmetry?
W) The near side contains a higher abundance of impact-generated melt
X) The far side has thicker crust, suppressing magma ascent to the surface
Y) The near side has experienced more recent tidal heating
Z) The far side cooled more rapidly following lunar formation
ANSWER: X) The far side has thicker crust, suppressing magma ascent to the surface
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice As a balloon rises to a higher altitude, which of the following best explains why the gas behaves more ideally under these conditions?
W) The average kinetic energy of the particles decreases
X) Gas particles experience weaker intermolecular forces
Y) Collisions between particles become inelastic
Z) Gas particles occupy a larger fraction of the container volume
ANSWER: X) Gas particles experience weaker intermolecular forces
