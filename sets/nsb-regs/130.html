1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is the unit of the product of resistance and capacitance?

1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice For photons traveling through a vacuum, a photon in the ultraviolet spectrum has which of the following characteristics, compared to a photon in the infrared spectrum?

X) Same speed
Y) Lower frequency

ANSWER: X) Same speed
