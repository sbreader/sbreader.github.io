1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What law states that all actions have an equal and opposite reaction?
ANSWER: Newton's third law of motion
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer When the frequency of an external driving force equals one of the normal modes of oscillation, what phenomenon may occur where the amplitude may rapidly increase?
ANSWER: Resonance
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the ratio of the area of a regular hexagon with side length 2 to a regular hexagon with side length 1?
ANSWER: 4
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Farmer Dea has 20 animals on her farm. There are 4-legged cows and 2-legged chickens, both with 1 head. If she counts 56 legs, how many chickens does she have?
ANSWER: 12
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following DNA bases pairs with thymine?
W) Adenine
X) Cytosine
Y) Guanine
Z) Uracil
ANSWER: W) Adenine
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer What human cells are responsible for transporting vital oxygen through the body?
ANSWER: Red blood cells (also accept: Erythrocytes; RBCs)
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following atoms has the largest atomic radius?
W) Lithium
X) Beryllium
Y) Boron
Z) Carbon
ANSWER: W) Lithium

X) Decomposition
Y) Single replacement
Z) Double replacement
ANSWER: W) Synthesis

X) Decreases

ANSWER: 0

X) Roots
