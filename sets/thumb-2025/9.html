1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes how the wet and dry adiabatic lapse rates in a given environment change as the temperature is lowered?
W) They grow closer in magnitude but never intersect
X) They grow closer in magnitude and eventually intersect
Y) They grow further apart in magnitude
Z) The difference between them remains constant
ANSWER: W) They grow closer in magnitude but never intersect
2. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Heisenberg’s uncertainty principle states that certain pairs of quantities, called conjugate variables, cannot both be precisely known at the same time. Which of the following pairs are NOT conjugate variables?
W) Frequency and time
X) Energy and time
Y) Magnetic field and magnetization
Z) Angular momentum and angular position
ANSWER: Y) Magnetic field and magnetization
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a possible ring configuration for cyclohexane?
W) Chair
X) Half Chair
Y) Boat
Z) Half Boat
ANSWER: Z) Half Boat
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Aryan was stabbed with a rusty nail, which gave him tetanus. After a week, he begins to show symptoms of lockjaw, stopping him from talking, thank God. Which of the following best explains why the preferred treatment includes both an immunoglobin AND antibiotic regimen?
W) Immunoglobins bind the pathogen, antibiotics kill the pathogen
X) Immunoglobins bind the pathogen, antibiotics kill opportunistic infections
Y) Immunoglobins bind the toxin, antibiotics kill the pathogen
Z) Immunoglobins bind the toxin, antibiotics kill opportunistic infections
ANSWER: Y) Immunoglobins bind the toxin, antibiotics kill the pathogen
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Order the following three expressions from smallest to largest 1) 211 2) 37 3) 55
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What principle is often depicted as the final rung on the cosmic distance ladder, allowing astronomers to relate the distance to the most distant galaxies through their recessional velocities?
ANSWER: Hubble’s law
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following four fundamental particles that interact via the strong force 1) Electron 2) Muon 3) Tau neutrino 4) Antineutrino
ANSWER: None
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following effects best explains the covalency of the cobalt-iodine bonds in the complex CoI63+?
W) Inductive effect
X) Hyperconjugation
Y) Jahn-Teller effect
Z) Nephelauxetic effect
ANSWER: Z) Nephelauxetic effect
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, rank the following four zones associated with the growth of new bone tissue from closest to the epiphysis to closest to the diaphysis. 1. Zone of ossification 2. Zone of calcification 3. Zone of proliferation 4. Zone of hypertrophy
ANSWER: 3 4 2 1
10. BONUS Math Short Answer If you have a very large n sided die numbered from 1 through n, what is the expected number of rolls you need to do to obtain a sum of at least n?
ANSWER: e (also accept: Euler’s number) (do not accept: Euler’s constant)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice At its greatest eastern elongation, Venus appears in which of the following phases as viewed from Earth?
W) Waxing gibbous
X) Waning gibbous
Y) First quarter
Z) Last quarter
ANSWER: Y) First quarter
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer A parallel-plate air capacitor is connected to a battery and charged to store 10 J of energy. While the capacitor remains connected to the battery, a dielectric slab with dielectric constant 3 is partially inserted so that it covers exactly half of the plate area. What is the new amount of energy, in Joules, stored in the capacitor?
ANSWER: 20
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, order the following three ligands by decreasing field strength on the spectrochemical series 1. CO 2. NH3 3. H2O
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Lambda phages face the critical decision of staying lytic or turning lysogenic upon infecting a bacterium. They’ve evolved to sense the concentrations of other lambda phages in an area. Which of the following best explains a situation where a phage would stay lytic?
W) When phage concentration is low, as this implies poor bacterial fitness
X) When phage concentration is low, as this implies reduced intraspecific competition
Y) When phage concentration is high, as this implies good bacterial fitness
Z) When phage concentration is high, as this implies increased intraspecific competition
ANSWER: X) When phage concentration is low, as this implies reduced intraspecific competition
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Alex learned in calculus that all differentiable functions are continuous, but the reverse is not always true. What function exemplifies this, being continuous at every point but differentiable at none?
ANSWER: Weierstrass function
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A region in the Northern Atlantic is cooling despite being in a warming atmosphere. This “cold blob” is primarily due to the weakening of what current system in the Atlantic Ocean?
ANSWER: AMOC (also accept: Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation)
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In Feynman diagrams, internal lines connecting interaction vertices represent what class of particles that is thought to give rise to the Casimir effect?
ANSWER: Virtual particles
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Mao is trying to identify a chemical structure with formula C7H6O using H1 NMR. He notices one downfield peak with a chemical shift of 10 ppm and a tell-tale cluster of peaks around 7.5 ppm. Knowing the molecule yields a positive result in the Tollen’s test, what is the name of this molecule?
ANSWER: Benzaldehyde
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Lipinski's rule of five is a somewhat archaic law that is used as a guideline to design drugs with good oral pharmacokinetics. By name or number, identify all of the following three conditions that most drugs with good oral pharmacokinetics should have high amounts of 1. Hydrogen bonding sites 2. Molecular weight 3. Lipid solubility
ANSWER: 3 only

W) 0
X) 1

Z) 3

22. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice In Hamiltonian mechanics, the total time derivative of the Hamiltonian must be zero according to the conservation of energy. According to Hamilton’s equations, this condition is only met if the Hamiltonian does NOT explicitly depend on which of the following variables?

X) Generalized momentum
Y) External force
Z) Time

27. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The Poisson spot describes a central bright point that occurs in the shadow of a circular object. Which of the following best describes the mechanism that gives rise to the Poisson spot?
W) Near-field refraction
X) Far-field refraction
Y) Near-field diffraction
Z) Far-field diffraction
ANSWER: Y) Near-field diffraction
28. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In the Diels-Alder cycloaddition to form cyclohexene, which two molecular orbitals are interacting to form the new bonds?
