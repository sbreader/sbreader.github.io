Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What organelle functions to isolate a human cell's chromosomes from the cytoplasm?
ANSWER: NUCLEUS
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the primary oxygen-carrying protein found in red blood cells?
ANSWER: HEMOGLOBIN
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What physical state of matter is intermediate between a solid and gas?
ANSWER: LIQUID
2. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following BEST describes the term static:
W) stationary
X) low
Y) constant
Z) used
ANSWER: W) stationary
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the general name for the rising and falling of sea levels in response to the forces exerted by the Moon and Sun?
ANSWER: TIDES
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Approximately what fraction of Earth's surface is covered by oceans:
W) one-half
X) two-thirds
Y) seven-tenths
Z) four-fifths
ANSWER: Y) seven-tenths
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many degrees are in one-third of a right angle?
ANSWER: 30
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Round the number 535,279 to the nearest ten-thousand:
ANSWER: 540,000
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The number 4.56 × 1011 has how many zeroes?
ANSWER: 9
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Giving your answer in grams, subtract 200 grams from 6.2 kilograms:
ANSWER: 6,000 (also accept: 6 × 103)
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many permanent teeth does a typical adult human have?
ANSWER: 32
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer How many canine teeth does the typical human adult have?
ANSWER: 4
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Mary tried to lift her couch by exerting 500 newtons of force but it doesn't budge. If the couch weighed 2,000 newtons, how much work did she do:
W) 0
X) 50 watts
Y) 500 joules
Z) 2000 joules
ANSWER: W) 0
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer Jeff walks west in a straight path for 100 meters and then east in a straight path for 150 meters. What is the magnitude of his displacement, in meters?
ANSWER: 50
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The hydrologic cycle describes the circulation of Earth's:
W) tides
X) water
Y) sediments
Z) rocks
ANSWER: X) water
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the rock that makes up most of the ocean floor and volcanic ocean islands?
ANSWER: BASALT
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In a right triangle, what is the name for the side opposite the 90° angle?
ANSWER: HYPOTENUSE
9. BONUS Math Short Answer Rounded to the nearest meter, find the length of the hypotenuse of a right triangle if the legs measure 5 and 12 meters?
ANSWER: 13
10. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the SI base unit for length?
ANSWER: METER
10. BONUS Energy Short Answer Name all of the following 3 choices that are measurements units for speed: mach; newton; knots
ANSWER: MACH; KNOTS
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Most of the heart is composed of which of the following types of tissues:
W) epithelial
X) connective
Y) nervous
Z) muscle
ANSWER: Z) muscle
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer In the taxonomical name Homo sapiens, what taxonomical rank does sapiens represent?
ANSWER: SPECIES
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What atomic particle balances the charge of protons to produce a neutral atom?
ANSWER: ELECTRON
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Isotopes of the same element are based on their different numbers of what atomic particle?
ANSWER: NEUTRONS
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the Gas giant planets is closest to the Sun?
ANSWER: JUPITER
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the 2 main factors causing the metamorphism of rocks?
ANSWER: HEAT AND PRESSURE (also accept: in any order)
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the name for any segment with one endpoint at the center of a circle and the other endpoint on the circle?
ANSWER: RADIUS (also accept: RADII)
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If there are 640 ounces of water in 5 gallons, how many ounces are in ½ gallon?
ANSWER: 64
15. BONUS Energy Short Answer Name all of the following 4 choices that are measurement units of area: meter; hectare; acre; mile
ANSWER: HECTARE; ACRE
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is generally considered the basic unit of life?
ANSWER: THE CELL
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer In what organelle of a plant cell does photosynthesis occur?
ANSWER: CHLOROPLAST (also accept: PLASTID)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer An ionic state of an atom of hydrogen would have how many protons?
ANSWER: 1
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the biochemical process that converts grape juice into wine or soy beans into soy sauce?
ANSWER: FERMENTATION
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Giving your answer as north, south, east, or west, a southerly wind blows FROM what direction?
ANSWER: SOUTH
18. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following clouds has little vertical development:
W) altostratus
X) altocumulus
Y) cirrus
Z) nimbostratus
ANSWER: Y) cirrus
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the exact circumference, in centimeters, of a circle whose radius is 3 centimeters?
ANSWER: 6π
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the common name for the antiseptic, found in many homes, that decomposes into water and oxygen?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN PEROXIDE
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the chemical formula for hydrogen peroxide?
ANSWER: H2O2
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the scientific name for the white of a cooked egg?
ANSWER: ALBUMEN
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer What term is generally used for biological catalysts?
ANSWER: ENZYMES
22. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What term do scientists use for the force of gravity acting on anything that has mass?
ANSWER: WEIGHT
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many protons are present in a single helium nucleus?
ANSWER: 2
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A weather forecast of 100% relative humidity suggests an increased possibility of:
W) rain
X) wind
Y) rising temperature
Z) drying conditions
ANSWER: W) rain
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for all Great Circles that pass through the north and south poles?
ANSWER: LONGITUDES (also accept: MERIDIANS)
24. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Every triangle has how many vertices?
ANSWER: 3
24. BONUS Math Short Answer If the perimeter of a triangle is 45 centimeters and the lengths of two of its sides are 16 and 12 centimeters, find the length of the third side, in centimeters:
ANSWER: 17
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What general term describes the angle or degree north or south of the equator?
ANSWER: LATITUDE
25. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The geographic north pole is at what latitude?
ANSWER: 90 (also accept: 90° NORTH)
