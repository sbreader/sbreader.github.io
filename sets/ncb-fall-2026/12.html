Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the temperature at which an amorphous material shifts from a hard, brittle, glassy state to a soft, flexible, and rubbery state?
ANSWER: Glass Transition Temperature
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three reactions by increasing equilibrium constant at STP 1) Fischer Esterification 2) Dimerization of nitrogen dioxide 3) Dissolution of AgCl
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following reagents would be most suited for the removal of a Boc protecting group?
W) Raney Nickel
X) Trifluoroacetic acid
Y) Palladium on Carbon
Z) Phosphoryl Trichloride
ANSWER: X) Trifluoroacetic acid
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Polyurethane is a versatile plastic polymer which is commonly used worldwide in protective wood coatings, adhesives, and foams. Polyurethane has which of the following types of linkages?
W) Carbamate
X) Amide
Y) Glycosidic
Z) Ester
ANSWER: W) Carbamate
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following ligands will typically have the most stabilizing effect on a transition metal ion?
W) Chloride
X) Porphyrin
Y) Bipyridine
Z) Water
ANSWER: X) Porphyrin
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following describes the slope between two species A and AO on a frost diagram if the oxidation is slightly spontaneous?
W) Positive and High in Magnitude
X) Positive and Low in Magnitude
Y) Negative and High in Magnitude
Z) Negative and Low in Magnitude
ANSWER: Z) Negative and Low in Magnitude
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What constant represents the most probable distance between the nucleus and an electron in a ground state hydrogen atom?
ANSWER: Bohr Radius
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Amino acids and other optically active compounds in their pure enantiomeric form tend to racemize over time. By name or number, rank the following three values for this process by increasing value 1) Δ H 2) Δ S 3) Δ G
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds has a van't hoff factor less than one in benzene?
W) Glucose
X) Acetic Acid
Y) Silver Phosphate
Z) Boron Nitride
ANSWER: X) Acetic Acid
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the geometry found in H2O2 and S2F4 where two atoms or groups attached to adjacent bonded atoms are separated by a dihedral angle typically between 60° and 120°?
ANSWER: Gauche (also accept: Skew)
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is performing H-NMR on aromatic compounds, and notices that the protons are downshifted more than he expected. This is due to what phenomenon in which the induced magnetic field increases the circulation of π electrons creating an induced, local magnetic field?
ANSWER: Diamagnetic Anisotropy (also accept: Ring Current)
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following sets of terms could be used to describe nitrosobenzene, with chemical formula C6H5NO?
W) Activated Ring, Ortho-Para Director
X) Deactivated Ring, Ortho-Para Director
Y) Activated Ring, Meta Director
Z) Deactivated Ring, Meta Director
ANSWER: X) Deactivated Ring, Ortho-Para Director
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements would form the most stable ozonide?
W) Sodium
X) Magnesium
Y) Potassium
Z) Calcium
ANSWER: Y) Potassium
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Mossbauer Spectroscopy is most likely to be utilized for the analysis of which of the following?
W) Proteins
X) Iron Ores
Y) Hazardous Air Pollutants
Z) Triglycerides
ANSWER: X) Iron Ores
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term refers to the minimum amount of energy a quantum system can have?
ANSWER: zero-point energy
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice A 1s electron in a hydrogen atom is promoted to an orbital in the n = 4 shell. Which orbital was this electron most likely promoted to?
W) 4s
X) 4p
Y) 4d
Z) 4f
ANSWER: X) 4p
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Under basic conditions, chlorine often disproportionates into chloride and another product; however, this product differs based on temperature. What is the identity of this other product at low and high temperatures respectively?
W) Hypochlorite, Chlorate
X) Chlorite, Chlorate
Y) Hypochlorite, Perchlorate
Z) Chlorite, Perchlorate
ANSWER: W) Hypochlorite, Chlorate
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A student titrates a mixture of CO32- and HCO3- with 0.100 M HCl, using phenolphthalein and methyl orange as indicators. The first equivalence point was reached once 6 mL of total HCl had been dispensed, and the second equivalence point was reached once 18 mL of total HCl had been dispensed. What is the ratio of CO32- to HCO3- in the mixture prior to the titration?
ANSWER: 1:1
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In nucleophilic aromatic substitution, what name is given to the negatively charged intermediate that is stabilized by resonance?
ANSWER: Meisenheimer Complex (also accept: Jackson–Meisenheimer complex)
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three alkene reactions by increasing yield 1) Hydroboration Oxidation 2) Hydrogenation 3) Hydrohalogenation
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three types of isomerism which would necessarily change the color of a metal complex 1) fac-mer isomerism 2) Linkage isomerism 3) Ionization Isomerism
ANSWER: 2, 3
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three vibrational modes which may be raman active 1) Symmetric stretch 2) Asymmetric stretch 3) Bend
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer If a molecule is linear and is heteroatomic, what is the ratio of its constant pressure heat capacity to its constant volume heat capacity?
ANSWER: 7/5
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The Davies equation is the empirical extension of what theory, which is used to calculate the activity coefficients of electrolyte solutions at moderately high concentrations?
ANSWER: Debye-Hückel Theory
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What adjective is used to describe a highly reactive ion in an inert, aprotic solvent, which is completely free of solvation?
ANSWER: Naked
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three spectral line series which lie in multiple regions of the electromagnetic spectrum 1) Balmer 2) Brackett 3) Lyman
ANSWER: 1
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Jun is messing around with aromatic heterocycles and reacts pyridine with a mixture of HNO3 and H2SO4 at room conditions. Which of the following is the major product of this reaction?
W) 2-nitropyridine
X) 3-nitropyridine
Y) 4-nitropyridine
Z) Pyridinium Cation
ANSWER: Z) Pyridinium Cation
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What highly reactive functional group is formed from the photolysis of an azide, and consists of a hypovalent nitrogen analogous to a carbene?
ANSWER: Nitrene
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three liquid crystal phases by increasing disorder 1) Smectic 2) Nematic 3) Cholesteric
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three ligands by increasing rate of substitution that would occur at the trans position 1) Ammonia 2) Nitro 3) Chloride
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three values which would be fixed in the grand canonical ensemble 1) Number of Particles 2) Energy 3) Chemical Potential
ANSWER: 3
16. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements are correct regarding a spontaneous process that is both isenthalpic and isobaric?
W) The process must also be adiabatic
X) The process must also be isentropic
Y) The process must also be Isochoric
Z) The process is not isothermal
ANSWER: W) The process must also be adiabatic
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer D-Glucose and D-Mannose are an example of what type of compounds which have multiple chiral centers but differ only by the configuration of a single chiral center?
ANSWER: Epimers
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer D-Glucose primarily exists in solution as Beta-D-glucopyranose, but the axial hydroxyl isomer Alpha-D-glucopyranose is present in higher quantities than predicted purely by steric hindrance. What stereoelectronic effect stabilizes the axial configuration via hyperconjugation between the endocyclic oxygen lone pair and the C-O sigma star orbital?
ANSWER: Anomeric effect
