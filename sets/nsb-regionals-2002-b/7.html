1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT considered a state function of a system in a reaction:
W) work
X) enthalpy

Z) volume
ANSWER: W) work
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the law that states that the overall enthalpy change for a reaction is equal to the sum of the enthalpy changes for the individual steps in the reaction:
ANSWER: HESS’S LAW (also accept: HESS)

X) yellow
Y) red
Z) blue
ANSWER: Y) red
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer Consider a lever with a 4-gram mass located 12 centimeters from its fulcrum. A 6-gram mass is placed on the opposite side of the lever. How far, in centimeters, must the 6-gram mass be placed from the fulcrum to equally balance the 4-gram mass?
ANSWER: 8 CENTIMETERS
