1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a body that is so massive that light and information cannot escape its gravitational pull?
ANSWER: BLACK HOLE
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What parameter of a planet's orbit is a measure of how closely the orbit approximates a perfect circle?
W) Inclination
X) Semi-major axis
Y) Radial velocity
Z) Eccentricity
ANSWER: Z) Eccentricity
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What element composes the largest percentage of the dry weight of a human being?
ANSWER: CARBON
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What specialized type of adipose tissue is found in mammalian infants and is solely used for heat production?
ANSWER: BROWN FAT
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What vector quantity includes both the speed of an object and the direction of its motion?
ANSWER: VELOCITY
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A solid composed of an unknown material has a mass of 10 grams and a volume of 4.6 centimeters cubed. To two significant figures and in grams per centimeters cubed, what is the density of this solid?
ANSWER: 2.2
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What type of nuclear-energy-producing reaction uses hydrogen isotopes like deuterium and tritium?
ANSWER: FUSION (also accept: NUCLEAR FUSION)
4. BONUS Energy Short Answer Scientists across the nation use the Advanced Light Source to study the structures of various samples. At what national lab is this facility located?
ANSWER: LAWRENCE BERKELEY NATIONAL LAB (also accept: LBNL)
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Round 5,137.58 to the nearest ten.
ANSWER: 5,140
5. BONUS Math Short Answer A rhombus has diagonals of length 9 and 32. What is its area?
ANSWER: 144
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Materials that compose the Earth's crust have densities that are different from the bulk density of the Earth. Which of the following is closest to the average density, in grams per cubic centimeter, of Earth's continental crust?
W) 2.7
X) 3.3
Y) 4.5
Z) 5.5
ANSWER: W) 2.7
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The geologic time scale is divided into eons, eras, periods, epochs, and ages. Which of the following best describes how geologic time is divided in the Phanerozoic Eon?
W) Divisions are based on time periods of equal length
X) Divisions are based on evidence of paleoclimates
Y) Divisions are based on domination by groups of major fauna
Z) Divisions are based on domination by major rock types
ANSWER: Y) Divisions are based on domination by groups of major fauna
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What molecule is used as the energy currency of the cell due to its high-energy phosphorous-oxygen bonds?
ANSWER: ADENOSINE TRIPHOSPHATE (also accept: ATP)
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What specific energetic aspect of a reaction do enzymes reduce?
ANSWER: ACTIVATION ENERGY (also accept: TRANSITION STATE)
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying the properties of the nuclide nickel-78. If nickel has atomic number 28, how many neutrons does nickel-78 have?
ANSWER: 50
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at SLAC are developing low-cost water-purification devices that generate hydrogen peroxide using solar energy, oxygen, and what other molecule?
ANSWER: WATER (also accept: H2O)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Assuming all locations have similar weather conditions, at which of the following locations would you expect a barometer to yield the highest reading?
W) Key West, Florida
X) Denver, Colorado
Y) Chicago, Illinois
Z) Nashville, Tennessee
ANSWER: W) Key West, Florida
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Oceanic crust ranges in age from newly formed to about 200 million years old. In what area of the ocean floor would you find the oldest crust?
W) Along the rift valley
X) Along a continental coastline
Y) Within the abyssal plain
Z) Adjacent to an atoll
ANSWER: X) Along a continental coastline
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Kate sips some juice and notices that it is sour. What can she reasonably hypothesize about the juice?
W) It likely contains an acid
X) It likely contains a base
Y) It would likely neutralize with acetic acid
Z) It would likely neutralize with muriatic acid
ANSWER: W) It likely contains an acid
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A carbohydrate contains 15 oxygen atoms. How many hydrogen atoms does it contain?
ANSWER: 30
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the volume of a right rectangular prism with dimensions 3 by 4 by 7?
ANSWER: 84
11. BONUS Math Short Answer A horse on the outer edge of a circular carousel is 40 feet from the center of rotation. A horse on the inner edge is 15 feet from the center. During a ride of 8.5 rotations, to the nearest hundred feet, how much further in linear distance will the outer horse travel than the inner horse?
ANSWER: 1300
12. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory have built an observatory to study how rocks fracture, a process that influences greenhouse gas emissions, impacts groundwater, and induces seismicity. In particular, this research is most relevant to the harvesting of what energy resource?
W) Shale gas
X) Crude oil
Y) Geothermal energy
Z) Cellulosic ethanol
ANSWER: W) Shale gas
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab have been studying dendrite formation, a process that limits rechargeable battery life, in next-generation lithium manganese nickel oxide cathodes. These dendrites are primarily composed of what element?
ANSWER: LITHIUM
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a chemical change?
W) Cutting a piece of paper
X) Boiling water
Y) Dissolving table salt in water
Z) Burning toast
ANSWER: Z) Burning toast
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three substances in order of increasing viscosity 1) Honey 2) Cold water 3) Hot water.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The structural stability of bone is due to the presence of a highly-mineralized form of what protein?
ANSWER: COLLAGEN
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of mimicry is demonstrated by the nonvenomous snake eel, which looks like the highly venomous banded sea snake?
ANSWER: BATESIAN MIMICRY
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the distance, in astronomical units to the nearest whole number, from the Earth to the Sun?
ANSWER: ONE

W) 2
X) 20
Y) 50
Z) 100
ANSWER: W) 2
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If a line is perpendicular to the line with equation y = –3x + 4 at the point (1, 1), what is the slope of the perpendicular line?
ANSWER: 1/3
16. BONUS Math Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 numbers that are divisible by 9 1) 128 2) 469 3) 504 4) 738.
ANSWER: 3 AND 4 (also accept: 504 AND 738)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer For any given element, the quantity of what particle is specified by its atomic number?
ANSWER: PROTONS
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of speed and velocity 1) The average speed always equals the average velocity 2) The average speed always equals the magnitude of the average velocity 3) If a particle's position-versus-time graph is a horizontal line, then both its speed and velocity are zero.

W) Endoplasmic reticulum
X) Ribosome
Y) Mitochondrion

ANSWER: GENE (also accept: LOCATION OF A GENE, LOCUS, LOCI)

ANSWER: 72

ANSWER: 2

ANSWER: 3

ANSWER: 44
