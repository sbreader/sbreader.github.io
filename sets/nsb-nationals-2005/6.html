Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The fundamental forces of nature are most correctly given as:
W) gravitational, electromagnetic, strong interactive, weak interactive
X) chemical, mechanical, friction and atomic
Y) gravitational, electromotive, magnetic, atomic
Z) graviton, photon, lepton, hadron
ANSWER: W) gravitational, electromagnetic, strong interactive, weak interactive
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer The throbbing caused by sounds of slightly different frequencies that results because of periodic constructive and destructive interference is called:
ANSWER: BEATS (also accept: BEATING)
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following fins is NOT found on fish:
W) pectoral
X) lateral
Y) dorsal
Z) pelvic
ANSWER: X) lateral
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What reflex causes the extension of the big toe and fanning of the other toes when the bottom of the foot is stroked?
ANSWER: BABINSKI SIGN (also accept: BABINSKI REFLEX; BABINSKI RESPONSE)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the best example of a polysaccharide:
W) glucose
X) galactose
Y) sucrose
Z) cellulose
ANSWER: Z) cellulose
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the concentration, in parts per billion, of a solution that contains 1 microgram of solute per liter?
ANSWER: 1 PPB (also accept: 1 PART PER BILLION)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a superior planet:
W) Jupiter
X) Venus
Y) Mars
Z) Pluto
ANSWER: X) Venus
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Mars has a thin atmosphere composed mainly of what gas?
ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE (also accept: CO2)
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In the following data set, which of the numbers are irrational: {-8, 2/3, √3, π}
ANSWER: √3 and π
5. BONUS Math Multiple Choice A person with lung cancer is given an MRI. Four radiologists examine each MRI independently. If each specialist can detect lung cancer with 80% accuracy, what is the probability that at least one of them will detect cancer in this patient:
W) 0.998
X) 0.01
Y) 0.25
Z) 0.8
ANSWER: W) 0.998
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A famous downslope wind that occurs in North America on the East side of the Rocky Mountains is called a:
W) sirocco
X) mistral
Y) foehn
Z) chinook
ANSWER: Z) chinook
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the upper surface of the saturated zone of groundwater called:
ANSWER: THE WATER TABLE
7. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice An experiment is testing whether a certain water-based plant fertilizer helps plants grow better. The experimental group contains plants that are given 20 milliliters of the fertilizer per day. Which of the following would be the best control in this experiment:
W) an equal number of plants that received nothing in place of the fertilizer
X) an equal number of plants that received 20 milliliters of pure water in place of the fertilizer
Y) an equal number of plants that received twice as much fertilizer
Z) there is no need for a control
ANSWER: X) an equal number of plants that received 20 milliliters of pure water in place of the fertilizer
7. BONUS Energy Short Answer This term is used by scientists when referring to the belief that the simplest explanation is most likely the correct explanation?
ANSWER: OCKHAM’S RAZOR
8. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Why should you periodically defragment the hard drive:
W) to prevent damage to the read/write heads on the hard drive
X) to prevent the reoccurrence of error code 653 at startup
Y) to identify bad sectors on the hard drive
Z) to improve the quality of the sound card
ANSWER: W) to prevent damage to the read/write heads on the hard drive
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the network device used to regenerate or replicate a signal over the same medium and is used to regenerate analog or digital signals distorted by transmission loss:
ANSWER: REPEATER
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is the emission source for microwaves in a typical microwave oven:
W) small cyclotron
X) cadmium disk bombarded with a soft x-ray source
Y) magnetron
Z) laser lamp
ANSWER: Y) magnetron
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer How many neutrons and how many protons are present in a single deuterium nucleus?
ANSWER: ONE PROTON AND ONE NEUTRON
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In mammals, the embryos have a physiological connection to the mothers body during development. This is an example of:
W) viviparity
X) ovoviviparity
Y) oviparity
Z) parity
ANSWER: W) viviparity
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer In plants, this naturally occurring auxin has the abbreviation IAA:
ANSWER: INDOLE ACETIC ACID
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following dominates the gasses in the Earth’s atmosphere, making up some 78% of the air by volume:
W) nitrogen
X) oxygen
Y) helium
Z) hydrogen
ANSWER: W) nitrogen
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Never found uncombined on Earth, what element occurs abundantly in limestone, gypsum and fluorite:
ANSWER: CALCIUM (also accept: Ca)
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following planets has the highest escape velocity:
W) Saturn
X) Uranus
Y) Jupiter
Z) Mars
ANSWER: Y) Jupiter
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which moon is the only other body in our solar system, besides the Earth, that has an atmosphere of mostly nitrogen?
ANSWER: TITAN
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the formula for the volume of a sphere of radius R:
ANSWER: (4/3)πR3 (also accept: 4/3 π R3)
13. BONUS Math Multiple Choice The Babylonian number system is base 60, which makes it:
W) vigesimal
X) quinary
Y) binary
Z) sexagesimal
ANSWER: Z) sexagesimal
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What kind of rocks are formed by the lithification of sediments:
ANSWER: SEDIMENTARY ROCKS
15. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What Clark University physics professor was an American pioneer of rocket theory after whom a major NASA space center is named?
ANSWER: ROBERT GODDARD (also accept: GODDARD)
15. BONUS Energy Short Answer The most common recommended method for calibrating a 1 milliliter automatic pipette gun would be to do what?
ANSWER: WEIGH A MILLILITER OF WATER FROM THE PIPET
16. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is a standard datatype for Java, but not for standard C:
W) float
X) char
Y) byte
Z) integer
ANSWER: Y) byte
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following exhibits the least resistivity at 0°C:
W) mercury
X) tungsten
Y) gold
Z) silver
ANSWER: Z) silver
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer Which element has the simplest absorption spectrum?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The following pathway, CO2 + H2O + energy yields (CH2O)n + O2, represents which of the following:
W) cellular respiration
X) photosynthesis
Y) an irreversible reaction
Z) glycolysis
ANSWER: X) photosynthesis
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the vector for Rocky Mountain Spotted Fever?
ANSWER: THE TICK (also accept: DERMACENTOR)
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is an organic molecule:
W) CaSO4
X) CH4
Y) NH3
Z) H2O
ANSWER: X) CH4
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To the nearest whole number, convert 0.300 atmosphere into millimeters of mercury, or torr:
ANSWER: 228 MILLIMETERS OF MERCURY (also accept: 228 TORR)
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the term for the amount of energy released from each square meter of an object’s surface each second:
W) energy flux
X) Pascal’s Law
Y) Boltzman’s Equation
Z) Kelvin’s Law
ANSWER: W) energy flux
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer When a superior planet is at quadrature in reference to the Earth, what is its elongation in degrees?
ANSWER: 90°
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the fossilized resin of ancient trees which forms through the natural polymerization of sap:
ANSWER: AMBER
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the common name for the test that gives the hardness of the material by the size of an indent left in it by a standard steel ball?
ANSWER: THE BRINELL TEST
22. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is the name for a rotating machine that converts mechanical energy into electrical energy by an armature rotating in a field produced by permanent magnets:
W) electric motor
X) turbine
Y) magneto
Z) kinetocycle
ANSWER: Y) magneto
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Air on Earth consists mainly of three gases. Give their percent composition rounded off to the nearest whole number:
ANSWER: 78% NITROGEN; 21% OXYGEN; AND 1% ARGON
23. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In beta minus emission, the number of nucleons in the atom:
W) increases by one
X) decreases by one
Y) increases by four
Z) remains the same
ANSWER: Z) remains the same
23. BONUS Physics Short Answer Order the following categories of electromagnetic radiation from the LOWEST frequency to the HIGHEST: FM radio, UHF TV, AM radio, microwaves
ANSWER: AM RADIO; FM RADIO; UHF TV; MICROWAVES
24. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice George Gamow made a contribution to biology by correctly interpreting the:
W) X-ray crystallographic structure of DNA
X) existence of reverse transcriptase
Y) minimum number of letters in the code for amino acids
Z) existence of oncogenes
ANSWER: Y) minimum number of letters in the code for amino acids
24. BONUS Biology Short Answer What three main parts make up the carpel of a plant?
ANSWER: STIGMA, STYLE, AND OVARY
25. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which one of the following has the greatest tendency to lose an electron?
W) Zn
X) Cl-
Y) Br2
Z) a mixture of PbSO4 and H2O
ANSWER: W) Zn
25. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Round the following number to four significant figures and express the result in standard exponential notation: 0.006543210
ANSWER: 6.543 × 10-3
