1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Sensitive to light intensity, but unable to distinguish between different wavelengths, what are the photoreceptor cells in the retina that allow vision in dim light?
ANSWER: RODS (also accept: ROD CELLS)
1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following describes the function of a catalyst?
W) It provides the same reaction pathway with a lower activation energy
X) It provides an alternate pathway with a higher activation energy
Y) It provides an alternate pathway with a lower activation energy
Z) It provides the same reaction pathway with a higher activation energy
ANSWER: Y) It provides an alternate pathway with a lower activation energy

3. BONUS Physics Short Answer What principle states that no two identical fermions can occupy the same quantum state within a quantum system?

4. BONUS Math Short Answer Stephen has an equilateral triangle. He wants to draw a single line, horizontally, across the triangle such that both parts of the divided triangle have the same area. 6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In polymerase chain reaction, or PCR, Taq polymerase is often used during the elongation phase. What property of this specific DNA polymerase makes it ideal for PCR?
W) resistance to denaturation under heat
X) higher efficiency than other DNA polymerases
Y) resistance to denaturation under high pH
Z) lower error rate than other DNA polymerases
ANSWER: W) resistance to denaturation under heat
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice To amplify DNA, an alternative to PCR is the LAMP assay. Which of the following is NOT a difference between these two techniques?
W) PCR requires a thermocycler, unlike LAMP
X) PCR products are usually visualized through gel electrophoresis, unlike those of LAMP
Y) PCR uses two primers, while LAMP uses four to six
Z) PCR is mainly used for detection of certain genes, while LAMP is used to clone them
ANSWER: Z) PCR is mainly used for detection of certain genes, while LAMP is used to clone them
