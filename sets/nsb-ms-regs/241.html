1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice During the Deep Water Horizon oil spill, marine microbes were observed to consume some of the released hydrocarbons. Which of the following crude oil components is most likely to be degraded quickly by marine microbes?
W) Naphthalene
X) Dodecane
Y) Xylene
Z) Ethane
ANSWER: Z) Ethane
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the most detrimental type of point mutation?
W) Silent mutation
X) Nonsense mutation
Y) Missense mutation
Z) Frameshift mutation
ANSWER: Z) Frameshift mutation
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What property must be known to calculate the de Broglie wavelength of a particle?
W) Pressure
X) Electric charge
Y) Probability
Z) Momentum
ANSWER: Z) Momentum
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer Determine the speed of sound, in m/s to two significant figures, traveling through air at a temperature of 50°C.
ANSWER: 360
3. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Identify the maximum power point tracking algorithm that is easier to implement, does not require any voltage or current sensors, and tends to oscillate around the solution point once one is found.
ANSWER: PERTURB AND OBSERVE
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The energy in a thunderstorm comes from which of the following?
W) Evaporation of water drops
X) Temperature gradients in the atmosphere
Y) Condensation of water drops that form the cloud
Z) Electrical energy from charged particles in the cloud
ANSWER: Y) Condensation of water drops that form the cloud

W) Beaker
X) Pipette
Y) Graduated cylinder
Z) Burette
ANSWER: X) Pipette

X) Fume hood
Y) Fire extinguisher
Z) Goggles
ANSWER: X) Fume hood

ANSWER: MARBLE

ANSWER: TWO
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Find the solutions of the quadratic equation: x2-4x-12 = 0.
ANSWER: -2, 6
6. BONUS Math Short Answer A student leaves for school on the bus. Her bus travels 25 miles per hour, but stops for 1 minute after every 2 minutes of travel. The route is 8 miles. To the nearest whole number, how many minutes does the trip take?
ANSWER: 28
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice You are constructing a simple ecosystem model to predict microbial growth in a marine environment. Which of the following elements are you least likely to include in your model as a possible limiting nutrient?

ANSWER: FISH AND AMPHIBIANS
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A particular gem has no refraction at angles above 40.7°, there is only total internal reflection. Therefore, 40.7° corresponds to which of the following for this gem?
W) Angle of refraction
X) Critical angle
Y) Angle of incidence
Z) Maximum angle
ANSWER: X) Critical angle

ANSWER: PLANETARY NEBULA

W) Radio
X) UV

W) 1/4
X) 1/2
Y) 4/7
Z) 3/4
ANSWER: Y) 4/7
12. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice In a regenerative fuel cell system, the byproducts of a fuel cell are utilized to do which of the following?
W) Produce electricity via electrolysis
X) Provide power to a solar energy system
Y) Provide power to a wind energy system
Z) Produce reactants that are resupplied to the fuel cell via electrolysis
ANSWER: Z) Produce reactants that are resupplied to the fuel cell via electrolysis
12. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Researchers are beginning to focus on butanol instead of ethanol as a replacement for gasoline. What is the primary reason for this shift in focus?
W) Butanol is more cost-effective than ethanol
X) Butanol is easier to work with
Y) Butanol can be used in cars without any modifications
Z) Butanol contains more chemical energy than ethanol
ANSWER: Y) Butanol can be used in cars without any modifications

23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A cycloid is generated by tracing a point on the edge of a circle as the circle is rolled along a line. What fraction of the area of a cycloid generated by a circle of radius r is the area of a cycloid generated by a circle of radius r/2?
ANSWER: 1/4
