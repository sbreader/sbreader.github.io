1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following hormones is responsible for increasing the concentration of calcium in the blood?
W) TRH
X) Vasopressin
Y) Parathyroid
Z) Thyroxine
ANSWER: Y) Parathyroid
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How much energy in kilojoules will be needed to heat a 100g sample of water from -5 degrees Celsius to 90 degrees Celsius, given that the specific heat of ice, liquid water, and water vapor is 2 J/ g C, 4 J/g C, and 2 J/ g C and the enthalpy of fusion and enthalpy of vaporization is 333 J/g and 2444 J/ g respectively
ANSWER: 70.3 kJ
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Winds that are morphed to run parallel with isobars due to the effects of pressure gradient force and the Coriolis effect are called what?
W) Cyclonic Winds
X) Geostrophic Winds
Y) Jet streams
Z) Shear Winds
ANSWER: X) Geostrophic Winds
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which of the following three statements are true about the inclined plane? They reduce the amount of force needed to be applied by increasing the distance of the applied force They decrease the amount of work done by decreasing the amount of force applied They increase the distance during which the force is applied
ANSWER: I and III
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about conifers?
W) Its microstrobuli produce seeds while its megastrobuli produce pollen
X) Its microstrobuli produce pollen while its megastrobuli produce seeds
Y) Both the microstrobuli and the megastrobuli produce pollen
Z) Both the microstrobuli and the megastrobuli produce seeds
ANSWER: X) Its microstrobuli produce pollen while its megastrobuli produce seeds
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which law states that the reaction enthalpies of various simple reactions can be added together in order to find the reaction enthalpy of a more complex reaction?

25. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Robert invests 100 dollars in each of the following three companies: A, B, and C. The next year, Company A's stock goes up by 20 percent, Company B's decreases by 25 % and Company C stays the same. The next year, Company A goes down by 20 % from last year, Company B goes up by 25 % from last year, and Company C still remains the same. Which of the following is true of the stocks of Company A, B, and C?
W) B is less than A which is less than C
X) B is greater than A which is less than C
Y) B is less than A which is greater than C
Z) B is less than C which is less than A
ANSWER: W) B is less than A which is less than C
