1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If the density of a metal alloy is 5,430 kilograms per cubic meter, what is its specific gravity, rounded to the second decimal place?
ANSWER: 5.43
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer An Olympic runner braces himself against a stationary set of foot blocks and accelerates with a horizontal magnitude of 2 meters per second squared. How many newtons of horizontal force does the runner exert on the blocks to produce this acceleration if his mass is 70 kilograms?
ANSWER: 140
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains the relative lack of hydrogen in our atmosphere in spite of its abundance in the Universe:
W) most of it is tied up in the water molecule
X) most of it is consumed in photosynthesis
Y) most of it escaped into space long ago because of its low mass
Z) most of it was an unstable isotope that decayed into helium
ANSWER: Y) most of it escaped into space long ago because of its low mass
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following locations contains relatively young coasts that formed as a consequence of continental rifting:
W) southern coasts of the Aleutian Islands
X) Gulf of California
Y) Hawaii
Z) northeastern Japan
ANSWER: X) Gulf of California
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the x-intercept of a graph of the following equation: y = –7x + 7
ANSWER: 1 (also accept: 1, 0)
3. BONUS Math Short Answer Multiply the polynomial, 3x2 – 4xy + y2, by –3xy, and simplify:
ANSWER: –9x3y + 12x2y2 – 3xy3

W) paper cellulosic
X) borosilicate glass
Y) polyester
Z) ceramic
ANSWER: Z) ceramic
4. BONUS Energy Short Answer Convert 60 miles per hour into feet per second:
ANSWER: 88
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following BEST describes muscle tone:
W) maximum strength of a muscle
X) length versus width of a muscle
Y) average energy use or oxygen use of a fully contracted muscle
Z) amount of tension or contraction of a resting muscle
ANSWER: Z) amount of tension or contraction of a resting muscle
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following terms most commonly refers to nerves leaving the brain:
W) posterior
X) rostral
Y) efferent
Z) involuntary
ANSWER: Y) efferent
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Randy was precise in his lab experiment but he was not accurate. Which of the following best describes his measurements:
W) his data was close to the true value but not reproducible
X) his data was close to the true value and reproducible
Y) his data was not close to the true value but reproducible
Z) his data was not close to the true value and reproducible
ANSWER: Y) his data was not close to the true value but reproducible
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Using a gravitational acceleration of 10 meters per second and giving your answer in appropriate SI units, if dropped off a cliff, how fast will a rock be falling at the end of 2 seconds, if friction is ignored?
ANSWER: 20 METERS PER SECOND

X) granite

Z) peridotite

W) another molecule is reduced
