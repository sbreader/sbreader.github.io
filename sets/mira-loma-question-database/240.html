11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true about groundwater?
W) a cone of depression, which is formed after heavy pumping from a well, is the effect of a drawdown
X) for small domestic wells, cones of depression are relatively significant
Y) an extremely steep cone of depression can substantially lower the water table
Z) heavy pumping that lowers the water table significantly can also cause nearby shallow wells to run dry
ANSWER: X) for small domestic wells, cones of depression are relatively significant
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is INCORRECT about MHD power?
W) in an MHD generator, a plasma of electrons and positive ions expands through a nozzle and moves at supersonic speed through a magnetic field
X) brushes carry the current to the external load circuit
Y) motion of charges gives rise to a voltage and flow of current
Z) an MHD generator can operate at any temperature to which the plasma is heated
ANSWER: X) brushes carry the current to the external load circuit
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of the Krebs cycle?
W) the two-carbon group from acetyl-CoA joins with oxaloacetate to form the six-carbon molecule, citrate
X) the condensation reaction of the conversion of acetyl-CoA into citrate is reversible
Y) the condensation reaction is inhibited when the cell's ATP concentration is high
Z) when ample amounts of ATP are present, the Krebs cycle shuts down and acetyl-CoA is channeled into fat synthesis
ANSWER: X) the condensation reaction of the conversion of acetyl-CoA into citrate is reversible
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of isomerism?
W) in coordination isomerism, the composition of the complex ion varies
X) cis-trans isomerism occurs when atoms or groups of atoms assume different positions around a rigid ring or bond
Y) in optical isomerism, isomers have opposite effects of plane-polarized light
Z) in trans-isomerism, molecules are next to each other, while in cis-trans isomerism, molecules are across from each other
ANSWER: Z) in trans-isomerism, molecules are next to each other, while in cis-trans isomerism, molecules are across from each other

18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is LEAST accurate regarding transport across cell membranes?
W) osmosis is the movement of water from a region of high concentration to low concentration
X) diffusion is the spontaneous movement of molecules from a region of lower concentration to higher concentration
Y) diffusion provides the means by which small molecules pass in and out of individual cells
Z) in active transport, the molecule of ion becomes attached to the carrier protein straddling the cell membrane
