1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If infinity is taken to have a voltage of 0, what is the voltage at a point halfway between two charges of 10 coulombs that are 10 meters apart due to the two charges? Give answers in terms of the Coulomb constant if necessary.

1. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 12 V battery is connected to a capacitor with plate separation 1 cm. If the plate separation is increased to 2 cm, what is the new voltage between the plates of the charged capacitor?
ANSWER: 12 V

ANSWER: X-RAY PHOTOELECTRON SPECTROSCOPY (also accept: X-RAY)

12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about an accretionary wedge?
W) Accretionary wedges are found at divergent plate boundaries on the ocean floor and is part of the system through which seafloor spreading occurs.
X) Accretionary wedges occur at active continental margins and are sites where material is accumulating due to a piece of subducting oceanic lithosphere pushing sediment together.
