Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer ABC transporters are so named for their dependance on what nucleotide, which is mainly synthesized through chemiosmosis?
ANSWER: ATP
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following terms best describes the zygote, a cell that has the ability to differentiate into any other cell type?
W) Totipotent
X) Pluripotent
Y) Multipotent
Z) Oligopotent
ANSWER: W) Totipotent
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Sister chromatids in mitosis are held together by cohesins located at what part of the chromatids consisting of repetitive sequences?
ANSWER: CENTROMERE
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Tumors often direct the production of new blood vessels to supply them with more nutrients. What is the name for this process?
ANSWER: ANGIOGENESIS
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What organelle on the surface of the rough endoplasmic reticulum is responsible for the difference in texture between the rough endoplasmic reticulum and smooth endoplasmic reticulum?
W) Chloroplast
X) Mitochondria
Y) Ribosome
Z) Lysosome
ANSWER: Y) Ribosome
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms utilizes pulmocutaneous respiration?
W) Human
X) Bird
Y) Frog
Z) Sheep
ANSWER: Y) Frog
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Let Hyder2 be a gene that is 67 amino acids long. What is the minimum number of nucleotides required in the open reading frame to encode Hyder2?
ANSWER: 204
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In certain regions of Africa, the frequency of the sickle-cell allele is abnormally high. This is because although being homozygous recessive for sickle-cell is dangerous, being a heterozygote can provide protection against malaria. What term describes this phenomenon?
ANSWER: HETEROZYGOTE ADVANTAGE
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In a T-cell receptor, the alpha and beta chains are connected via what structure composed of oxidized cysteine residues?
ANSWER: DISULFIDE BRIDGE (also accept: DISULFIDE BOND)
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organelles do not possess a double membrane?
W) Chloroplast
X) Mitochondria
Y) Nucleus
Z) Peroxisome
ANSWER: Z) Peroxisome
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In which of the following populations of individuals would genetic drift be the strongest?
W) 10 individuals
X) 100 individuals
Y) 1000 individuals
Z) 10,000 individuals
ANSWER: W) 10 individuals
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What specific type of torpor is used by animals living in hot and dry areas in order to conserve body water?
ANSWER: ESTIVATION
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The filament and anther are parts of what male reproductive organ found in flowers?
ANSWER: STAMEN
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Inhibiting lactate dehydrogenase is likely to impact which of the following general groups of organisms the most?
W) Obligate aerobe
X) Faculative anaerobe
Y) Obligate anaerobe
Z) Green algae
ANSWER: Y) Obligate anaerobe
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Saltatory conduction describes the “jumping” of action potentials across myelinated neurons through their regeneration at what areas along the axons?
ANSWER: NODES OF RANVIER
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer As opposed to the lock and key model, what model proposes that enzymes may often change their conformation to bind to different substrates?
ANSWER: INDUCED FIT
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A fact that the long beak of a hummingbird fits so well with the flowers it drinks its nectar from is an example of what phenomena when species evolve together?
ANSWER: COEVOLUTION
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Allen is a little organism that lives on William. Allen likes to secrete poison into Wiliam's skin, hurting William although gaining nothing himself. Which of the following types of symbiotic interaction does this best exemplify?
W) Mutualism
X) Commensalism
Y) Ammensalism
Z) Parasitism
ANSWER: Y) Ammensalism
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The Yang Cycle is a metabolic pathway occuring in vascular tissue of plants. In this pathway, methionine is converted into what alkene hormone that is responsible for fruit ripening?
ANSWER: ETHYLENE (also accept: ETHENE)
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at the Metropolitan Correctional Center in New York City are investigating the Epstein-Barr virus. To which of the following broader families does the Epstein-Barr virus belong?
W) Adenoviridae
X) Herpesviridae
Y) Poxviridae
Z) Retroviridae
ANSWER: X) Herpesviridae
