Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the term for the tendency of an object to resist changes in its motion?
ANSWER: INERTIA
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three properties that correctly describe a solid 1) Definite shape 2) Definite volume 3) Ability to change shape to match its container.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the Earth's largest coral reef?
ANSWER: GREAT BARRIER REEF
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Extensional boundaries are typically associated with what type of faulting?
W) Reverse
X) Thrust
Y) Normal
Z) Recumbent
ANSWER: Y) Normal
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Solve the following equation for y: y – 16 = 16 – y.
ANSWER: 16
3. BONUS Math Short Answer A circle with radius 7 is inscribed in a square. To the nearest 10, what is the area of the region inside the square but outside the circle?
ANSWER: 40
4. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Scientists working at Ames Laboratory have devised a way to increase the catalytic activity of nanoparticles in fuel cell applications. What quantity related to the particles did scientists focus on increasing?
W) Number
X) Length
Y) Surface area
Z) Volume
ANSWER: Y) Surface area
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What reaction, in which the starting material is decomposed in the presence of high temperatures without oxygen, is used to make biochar?
ANSWER: PYROLYSIS
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer To what constellation does the star Sirius belong?
ANSWER: CANIS MAJOR
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What astronomical term comes from the Greek meaning "yoked together" and is used to describe the alignment of three celestial bodies?
ANSWER: SYZYGY
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for alternative versions of the same gene?
ANSWER: ALLELE
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name for the phase of mitosis in which chromosomes condense and the nuclear membrane breaks down?
ANSWER: PROPHASE
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab are hunting for a signature of the Big Bang in what light neutral class of leptons?
ANSWER: NEUTRINO
7. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the coolant used in most nuclear reactors in the United States?
ANSWER: WATER (also accept: LIGHT WATER, H2O)
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the defining mineral for a Mohs hardness of 1?
ANSWER: TALC
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Where does peridotite form?
W) Bottom of the ocean
X) Upper mantle
Y) Convergent plate boundaries
Z) Evaporite deposits
ANSWER: X) Upper mantle
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If the perimeter of a triangle is 42, and the lengths of two of its sides are 18 and 11, what is the length of its third side?
ANSWER: 13
9. BONUS Math Short Answer The mean number of weekly work hours for 13 part-time employees at a store is 18. The mean number of hours for 5 of the employees is 13. To the nearest whole number, what is the mean number of hours for the rest of the store's employees?
ANSWER: 21
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What term describes an observable trait or traits of an organism?
ANSWER: PHENOTYPE
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer Fermentation occurring in humans produces what molecule?
ANSWER: LACTIC ACID (also accept: LACTATE)
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the general term for the substances that are used up in a chemical reaction?
ANSWER: REACTANTS
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of energy 1) Energy from the Sun reaches the Earth via conduction 2) Car engines convert chemical energy to both thermal and mechanical energy 3) Absolute zero is the value zero on the Celsius scale.
ANSWER: 2
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Most megacities are surrounded by a brown haze. Researchers working with the Pacific Northwest National Laboratory investigated chemicals found in pollution that could be causing the brown haze. What chemicals found in most pollution caused by cars is responsible for the brown haze?
ANSWER: NITROGEN OXIDES (also accept: NOX, NO and NO2)
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers at Brookhaven National Lab are studying quark-gluon plasma. What user facility at BNL are they using to produce this type of matter?
ANSWER: RELATIVISTIC HEAVY-ION COLLIDER (also accept: RHIC)
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the general term for a polymer composed of sugars?
ANSWER: POLYSACCHARIDE (also accept: STARCH)
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer During what phase of interphase is DNA actively being synthesized?
ANSWER: S PHASE
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is 60% of 30 plus 60% of 40?
ANSWER: 42
14. BONUS Math Short Answer A cyclist travels 0.48 kilometers in one minute. What is the average speed of this cyclist in meters per second?
ANSWER: 8
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following types of energy transformation occurs in a lightbulb?
W) Kinetic to electrical
X) Electrical to kinetic
Y) Electrical to radiant
Z) Electrical to chemical
ANSWER: Y) Electrical to radiant
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer A vessel is pressurized to 500 Pascals and is applying force over an area of 20 meters squared. What is the force, in newtons, applied by this vessel?
ANSWER: 10,000
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What group of lipids that are composed of two fatty acid tails and a phosphate group can be found in the lipid bilayer of the cell membrane?
ANSWER: PHOSPHOLIPID
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Hexokinase is an enzyme that catalyzes the first step of glycolysis. What functional group does it add to the glucose molecule?
ANSWER: PHOSPHATE
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Solve the following equation for x: (x – 4) cubed = 27.
ANSWER: 7
17. BONUS Math Short Answer A hula hoop with a radius of 15 inches is cut into 5 congruent pieces. To the nearest whole number of inches, what is the length of each piece?
ANSWER: 19
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What property of protostars or protoplanets leads to accretion?
W) Composition
X) Mass
Y) Temperature
Z) Velocity
ANSWER: X) Mass
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What spectral class is the star Sirius, which has a temperature of approximately 10,000 kelvins?
ANSWER: A (also accept: A1)
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the molecular formula of carbon tetrachloride?
ANSWER: CCl4
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true for heating or cooling a substance in a closed system 1) The freezing point of a liquid is the same temperature as the melting point of the corresponding solid 2) At the boiling point, a heated liquid will continue to rise in temperature until completely converted into a gas 3) Condensation only occurs at the boiling point temperature.
ANSWER: 1
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In humans, chemical digestion of sugars begins in what organ?
ANSWER: MOUTH
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer Besides carbon and oxygen, what other two elements compose the largest portion of the mass of human beings?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN AND NITROGEN (also accept: H AND N)
21. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer For what real numbers is the following rational function not defined: the fraction with numerator x2 times (x2 + 1) and denominator (x2 – 1) times (x + 8)?
ANSWER: –8, –1, AND 1 (also accept: –8 AND ±1)
21. BONUS Math Short Answer Rounding your answer to the nearest 10,000, what is 449 times 382?
ANSWER: 170,000
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Democritus was an early Greek philosopher whose theories about matter gave what particle its name?
ANSWER: ATOM
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer Jonathan starts at one corner of a square field and walks along the four edges forming the perimeter until he reaches his starting point. Identify all of the following three statements that are true of his journey 1) After walking along one edge, his displacement and distance traveled are equal in magnitude 2) After walking along two edges, his displacement and distance traveled are equal in magnitude 3) After walking along four edges, his displacement and distance traveled are equal in magnitude.
ANSWER: 1
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer One consequence of Arctic warming is permafrost melting. What is the primary greenhouse gas being released by melting permafrost?
ANSWER: METHANE
23. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Crater Lake in Oregon is an example of a caldera. Which of the following statements best describes how this caldera formed?
W) A lava dam blocked a river and later broke free, forming the caldera
X) A mega-eruption blew off the side of Mount Mazama
Y) A lava dome formed in the crater and later eroded leaving the caldera behind
Z) The summit of Mount Mazama collapsed into the empty magma chamber below
ANSWER: Z) The summit of Mount Mazama collapsed into the empty magma chamber below
