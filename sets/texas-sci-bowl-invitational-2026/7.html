1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In fast-twitch glycolytic fibers, which adaptation is most likely?
W) High mitochondrial density
X) High myoglobin content
Y) High glycogen content
Z) High capillary density
ANSWER: Y) High glycogen content
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What enzyme involved in the production of microRNA converts primary microRNA into pre microRNA in the nucleus?
ANSWER: Drosha
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Also known as the 12-6 potential, what is the name of the function that relates the potential energy of interacting atoms?
ANSWER: Lennard-Jones Potential

ANSWER: 2 only

ANSWER: 5

ANSWER: 146097
