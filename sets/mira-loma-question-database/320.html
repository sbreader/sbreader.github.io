1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name for an elongated ridge of sand that projects from the land into the mouth of an adjacent bay that often hooks inward due to the dominant direction of the longshore current?
W) Baymouth bar
X) Spit
Y) Tombolo
Z) Shoal
ANSWER: X) Spit
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name this high-velocity cloud of gas that connects the Large and Small Magellanic clouds due to a near-collision of both galaxies nearly 2.5 billion years ago?
ANSWER: MAGELLANIC STREAM
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Name this vortex of air that rises and rotates around a vertical axis that can tighten and intensify, producing a tornado. This type of meteorological event is often discovered using Doppler radar.
W) Waterspout
X) Derecho
Y) Mesocyclone
Z) Extratropical cyclone
ANSWER: Y) Mesocyclone
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer This type of astronomical structure, often exceeding 50 megaparsecs in length, form the boundaries between large voids in the universe. They consist of gravitationally bound galaxies and superclusters. If multiple of these objects join together, they can form enormous blobs of gas known as Lyman-alpha blobs. For four points, name the largest known cosmic structures in the universe, also referred to as great walls.
ANSWER: FILAMENT
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the type of subtropical climate associated with lands in North Africa, California, Chile, and South Australia, characterized by having three times as much precipitation in a winter month as in the driest summer month?
ANSWER: Mediterranean CLIMATE (DRY-SUMMER SUBTROPICAL)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name for the type of main sequence star, occurring only in clusters, that are more luminous, more massive, and bluer than stars in the rest of the cluster?
W) Blue stragglers
X) Hypergiants
Y) Quark stars
Z) Exotic stars
ANSWER: W) Blue stragglers
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following four statements is or are TRUE regarding glaciers? 1: Lateral moraines are formed when two valley glaciers coalesce to form a single ice stream. 2: Medial moraines are formed when a glacier wastes away, forming ridges. 3: A ground moraine is a gently rolling layer of till deposited under a retreating glacier. 4: End moraines can be distinguished as terminal end moraines or recessional end moraines.
ANSWER: 3 and 4
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name this distant region of the Solar System that is sparsely populated by icy minor planets and is known to be a subset of trans-Neptunian space. The innermost portion of this region overlaps with the Kuiper belt, but its outer limits reach much farther from the Sun. Objects from the Oort cloud and Hill sphere have been known to originate from this region. For four points, name this region of space that includes objects such as Sedna and Eris.
ANSWER: SCATTERED DISC
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name for the clastic formed of predominantly silt-sized sediment formed by wind-blown dust?
W) Loam
X) Regolith
Y) Desert pavement
Z) Loess
ANSWER: Z) Loess
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following three statements is or are TRUE regarding stellar nucleosynthesis? 1: The triple alpha process is a set of nuclear fusion reactions by which 3 alpha particles are transformed into carbon. 2: A helium flash is the runaway fusion of helium in the core of high temperature stars. 3: When radioactive species will capture a proton more quickly than it can beta decay, the hot CNO cycles occur.
ANSWER: 1 and 3 (also accept: All but 2)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following four statements about soil order is or are TRUE? 1: The C horizon is part of the solum, or "true soil." 2: The E horizon is light colored mineral particles and is the site of leaching. 3: Andisols are deposited by recent volcanic activity. 4: Vertisols are moderately weathered soils that form under boreal forests.
ANSWER: 2 and 3
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding supernovae is FALSE?
W) Type Ia supernovae exhibit a strong singly ionized silicon line
X) Type Ib supernovae show a non-ionized helium line
Y) Type Ic supernovae exhibit a weak or non-existant helium line
Z) Type IIb supernovae characteristically don't show hydrogen
ANSWER: Z) Type IIb supernovae characteristically don't show hydrogen
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the rapid change of density with depth of seawater, often presenting a high gravitational stability and a significant barrier to mixing between the water above and below it?
ANSWER: PYCNOCLINE
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name this astronomical process that occurs when the surface of a star or a planet cools, causing the pressure to drop and thereby causing the body to shrink. Then, this shrinkage finally causes the heating of the core of the planet.
ANSWER: KELVIN-HELMHOLTZ MECHANISM
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of stability would most likely result when air's environmental lapse rate is less than the wet adiabatic rate?
W) Absolute instability
X) Absolute stability
Y) Conditional instability
Z) Conditional stability
ANSWER: X) Absolute stability
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding star formation is INCORRECT?
W) A Bok globule is a dark cloud of dense cosmic dust and gas in which star formation happens
X) Herbig-Haro objects are small patches of nebulosity associated with newly born stars
Y) A stellar nursery is defined as a dark nebula within which star formation is occuring
Z) The ratio between CO luminosity and H2 mass in molecular clouds is thought to be constant
ANSWER: Y) A stellar nursery is defined as a dark nebula within which star formation is occuring
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Name this grey to dark grey intermediate intrusive igneous rock composed principally of plagioclase feldspar, biotite, hornblende, and pyroxene.
W) Granite
X) Rhyolite
Y) Diorite
Z) Andesite
ANSWER: Y) Diorite
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name this tight open cluster of stars in the heart of the Orion Nebula, in the constellation of Orion, discovered by Galileo. This cluster is used as a common example of a system which ejects stars at high velocities, and has a class of multiple stars named after it.
ANSWER: TRAPEZIUM
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Name this large sheetlike body of rock that has been moved more than 2 km from its original position that, when folded and broken, forms a large-scale recumbent fold.
W) Nappe
X) Klippe
Y) Window
Z) Autochton
ANSWER: W) Nappe
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following four statements regarding Pluto is or are TRUE? 1: As of November 2012, Pluto is known to have five moons. 2: Clyde W. Tombaugh discovered Pluto in 1930. 3: Pluto is known as a trans-Neptunian object as well as a dwarf planet. 4: Pluto's orbital period is approximately 246 earth-years long.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3, and 4 (also accept: All of them)
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly names the type of drainage pattern associated with alternating bands of resistant and less-resistant rock?
W) Radial
X) Trellis
Y) Dendritic
Z) Rectangular
ANSWER: X) Trellis
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding volcanoes is TRUE?
W) Continued activity from a flank eruption may produce a small parasitic cone.
X) Located at the summit of many volcanoes is a steep-walled depression called a vent.
Y) Many secondary vents emit only gases and are called pipes.
Z) Bombs are ejected as hardened lava and blocks are ejected as incandescent lava.
ANSWER: W) Continued activity from a flank eruption may produce a small parasitic cone.
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following four statements regarding white dwarves is or are TRUE? 1: White dwarves are thought to be the final evolutionary state of 97% of stars in our galaxy 2: The maximum mass for a non-rotating white dwarf is approximately .7 solar masses. 3: A white dwarf that approaches the Chandrasekhar limit might explode as a Type 1b supernova via a helium flash. 4: Black dwarves are thought to result from white dwarves, but none are thought to exist yet.
ANSWER: 1 and 4
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following rocks is a fine-grained foliated rock that breaks along wavey surfaces and has a glossy sheen?
W) Slate
X) Schist
Y) Phyllite
Z) Gneiss
ANSWER: Y) Phyllite
