Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Dynein, a motor protein in the cell, travels on which of the following cytoskeletal elements?
W) Microtubules
X) Intermediate filaments
Y) Microfilaments
Z) Actin filaments
ANSWER: W) Microtubules
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not likely to occur during apoptosis?
W) Membrane blebbing
X) Cell shrinkage
Y) Mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization
Z) Replication of the centrosome
ANSWER: Z) Replication of the centrosome
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following ions has the smallest atomic radius?
W) S2−
X) Cl−
Y) K+
Z) Ca2+
ANSWER: Z) Ca2+
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly describes the signs of delta G and delta H respectively in the boiling of liquid water at 25 degrees Celsius and 1 atmosphere?
W) positive, positive
X) positive, negative
Y) negative, positive
Z) negative, negative
ANSWER: W) positive, positive
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is cardinality of the set of positive factors of 36?
ANSWER: 9
3. BONUS Math Short Answer How many vertices does a shape with 30 edges and 14 faces have?
ANSWER: 18
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The time taken for a muon to decay was used to prove what theory?
W) DeBroglie Wavelength
X) General Relativity
Y) Special Relativity
Z) Alpha Decay
ANSWER: Y) Special Relativity
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer A proton is accelerated through a 1 volt potential difference. The proton then moves directly toward another proton. To two significant figures, what is the distance of closest approach of the protons in nanometers?
ANSWER: 1.4
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the typical orbital eccentricity of interstellar comets that fly by the Sun, such as the recent 3I/Atlas?
W) 0
X) Between 0 and 1
Y) 1
Z) Greater than 1
ANSWER: Z) Greater than 1
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A star has a photosphere temperature of 6000 Kelvin. In what part of the electromagnetic spectrum does it emit most strongly?
W) Radio
X) Infrared
Y) Visible
Z) X-ray
ANSWER: Y) Visible
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the neurotransmitter at vertebrate skeletal neuromuscular junctions?
ANSWER: Acetylcholine (also accept: Ach)
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following terms describes a gene in one species that evolved from a common ancestral gene in a different species due to a speciation event?
W) Xenolog
X) Homolog
Y) Paralog
Z) Ortholog
ANSWER: Z) Ortholog
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Hess’s Law of Constant Heat Summation relies on the fact that enthalpy is what special type of function?
ANSWER: State Function
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What would the units of the rate constant in a reaction be if this reaction hypothetically followed fifth order kinetics?
ANSWER: s-1 · M-4 (also accept: liters to the fourth whole over second times moles to the fourth)
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Rounded to the nearest percent, if an event is optimally encoded with 5 bits, what is its probability?
ANSWER: 3%
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In Kelvin, the absolute temperature of an engine’s heat source is three times as much as its heat sink. What is the carnot efficiency of the engine?
ANSWER: 2/3
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer The speed of light inside an object is slower by a factor of square root two compared to air. If the light is incident on the object at an angle of 45 degrees, by what angle in degrees will it refract inside the object?
ANSWER: 30
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What primary spectral feature is present in type II supernova that is not present in type I?
ANSWER: Hydrogen lines
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Spectral splitting around sunspots is due to what magnetic effect?
ANSWER: Zeeman effect
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What structure of a nephron creates the medullary osmotic gradient?
ANSWER: Loop of Henle (also accept: nephron loop)
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which vessels are the main site of resistance and control of blood flow distribution?
ANSWER: Arterioles
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds is least soluble in water?
W) Ba(NO3)2
X) KBr
Y) CaCO3
Z) NH4Cl
ANSWER: Y) CaCO3
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the identity of the unstable intermediate which quickly decomposes in the reaction of baking soda and vinegar?
ANSWER: Carbonic Acid
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A bacteria population doubles every hour. Assuming the human population to be exactly 8 billion, to the nearest whole number, how many hours will it take for a bacteria population with a single bacteria to exceed the human population?
ANSWER: 33
13. BONUS Math Short Answer A frog starts at the center of a 3 by 3 grid. Every second it randomly chooses a direction to hop exactly one cell in, such that it remains in the grid. After 100 seconds, what is the probability it is back to the center?
ANSWER: 1/3
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A 3 farad capacitor is charged to 2 volts. In Joules, what is the energy stored in the capacitor?
ANSWER: 6
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer A person in an elevator accelerating upwards at 10 meters per second squared steps on a scale which reads 1000 Newtons. If gravity is 10 meters per second squared, how many kilograms does this person actually weigh?
ANSWER: 50 (kilograms)
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the type of tide that occurs when the Sun, Moon, and Earth are all aligned?
ANSWER: Spring Tide
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What was the main significance behind the evolution of grazing invertebrates prior to the Cambrian Explosion?
W) They increased nutrient content of soil through metabolic waste.
X) They decreased the oxygen content of oceans by increasing decomposition.
Y) They triggered global cooling by sequestering atmospheric carbon.
Z) They created space on the seafloor by destroying microbial mats.
ANSWER: Z) They created space on the seafloor by destroying microbial mats.
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Temperate bacteriophages can replicate either via the lytic cycle or what other cycle involving the integration of bacteriophage DNA into the host cell?
ANSWER: Lysogenic
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Marina the pet store employee notices that her female guppy fish ignore brighter, larger, male mates in favor of male mates that have already been chosen by a female mate. What is the term for this phenomenon?
ANSWER: Mate Choice Copying (also accept: mate copying; non-independent mate choice)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the equation that calculates the redox potential of a cell in nonstandard conditions?
ANSWER: Nernst Equation
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all the possible intermolecular forces that may be present in a water-acetone solution 1) Hydrogen Bonds 2) Dipole-dipole moment interactions 3) London Dispersion Forces
ANSWER: All
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the smallest palindromic cubic number with an even number of digits?
ANSWER: 1331
18. BONUS Math Short Answer What is 426 base 7 divided by 1000 base 2 in base 10?
ANSWER: 27
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer When two waves interact with each other, their amplitudes may line up and cancel each other out. What is this phenomenon called?
ANSWER: Destructive Interference
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer A stationary ambulance emits a siren at a frequency of 700 hertz. A car drives directly toward the ambulance at 50 meters per second. Take the speed of sound in air to be 350 meters per second. What frequency in hertz does the driver in the car hear?
ANSWER: 800 Hertz
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following rocks has the highest level of metamorphism?
W) slate
X) limestone
Y) gneiss
Z) schist
ANSWER: Y) gneiss
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What scale is used to measure the energy released from an earthquake, which can be calculated from the rupture area, average displacement, and shear modulus?
ANSWER: Moment Magnitude Scale (do not accept: Richter)
