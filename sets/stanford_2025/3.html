1. BONUS Math Short Answer Let a and b be real numbers. Identify all of the following three shapes that could possibly describe the region of the xy -plane satisfying the two inequalities x + y ≤ a and x2 + y2 ≤ b2 1) Circle 2) Semicircle 3) Line.

Z) The stamen of one flower to the stamen of another flower

8. BONUS Physics Short Answer Suppose there are two thermal reservoirs with temperatures 300 and 400 kelvin. Identify all of the following three situations that would violate the second law of thermodynamics 1) A 100% efficient heat engine operating between the reservoirs 2) An engine transferring heat from the hot reservoir to the cold reservoir with zero work done 3) An engine transferring heat from the cold reservoir to the hot reservoir with zero work done.
ANSWER: 1 and 3

9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Two identical containers are filled with equal moles of two different gases, A and B, at the same temperature. Which of the following statements is true about the properties of the gases?
W) The average molecular speed of A and B are the same
X) The average kinetic energy of the molecules in A and B are the same
Y) The number of molecular collisions per second with the container’s walls for A and B are the same
Z) The root-mean-square speed of A and B are the same
ANSWER: X) The average kinetic energy of the molecules in A and B are the same
