1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which 6th century pandemic killed approximately 6 million people and contributed to a loss of territory by empires?
ANSWER: The Plague of Justinian
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The plague of Justinian was caused by Yersinia pestis, which, despite its rare status in the modern day, still has not been eradicated. Why is it not considered a candidate for eradication?

Y) It can remain on surfaces for long periods of time
