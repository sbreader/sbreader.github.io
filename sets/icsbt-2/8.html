1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Auxins are derived from tryptophan. Which of the following neurotransmitters in humans would you expect to be the most structurally similar to auxin?
W) Acetylcholine
X) Dopamine
Y) Serotonin
Z) Epinephrine
ANSWER: Y) Serotonin
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the coefficient of relatedness between a worker bee and her maternal aunt?
ANSWER: 3/8
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A triangle in a 3d space has vertices at the points (1, 2, 3), (2, 4, 6), and (3, 6, 9). What are the coordinates for the centroid of this triangle?
ANSWER: (2, 4, 6)
2. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the equation of the monic cubic with real coefficients with two roots being 1 and i?
ANSWER: x3-x2 + x-1
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A solid medium initially has young's modulus Y, density rho, and initial speed of sound v. If its young's modulus is increased to 8Y and density to 2rho, in terms of v, what is the new speed of sound in the medium?
ANSWER: 2v
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer On another planet, thrown horizontally from an initial height of 6 meters at 8 meters per second, a ball lands 8√3 meters away. With the same height and initial speed, it is tossed at an angle 30 degrees from the horizontal. In meters, how far away does it land?
ANSWER: 12√3
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the specific pressure level where an air parcel becomes saturated and condensation begins?
ANSWER: Lifting condensation level (also accept: LCL)
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following processes primarily drives the secular cooling of Earth’s outer core?
W) Thermal convection
X) Radioactive decay
Y) Crystallization in the inner core
Z) Mantle overturn
ANSWER: Y) Crystallization in the inner core
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Consider the reaction of chlorocyclopentane with NaOH. Which of the following plots would yield a straight line?
W) Concentration of chlorocyclopentane vs. time
X) Natural logarithm of concentration of chlorocyclopentane vs. time
Y) 1 over concentration of chlorocyclopentane vs. time
Z) 1 over concentration of chlorocyclopentane2 vs. time
ANSWER: Y) 1 over concentration of chlorocyclopentane vs. time
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What industrial process involves the smelting of cryolite and using electricity to create aluminum?
ANSWER: Hall-Heroult process
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Among the 20 canonical amino acids, which one would occupy the least area on a Ramachandran plot?
ANSWER: Proline
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Rumi is looking to study actively dividing cells. Identify all of the following three microscopy techniques which she could use to do so 1) Nomarski microscopy 2) Confocal microscopy 3) Stereomicroscopy
ANSWER: 1 and 2
7. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Order the following AIs by increasing typical energy consumption during training 1) Large Language Model 2) K-nearest neighbors 3) Convolutional Neural Network
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
7. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Generative AI training has become extremely energy intensive. Which of the following is NOT true of the impacts of generative AI?
W) Water consumption rates of data centers is higher in water-abundant areas
X) Generative AI training has indirectly depleted freshwater resources
Y) A single ChatGPT query costs over 5 times more electricity than a web search
Z) Generative AI training has indirectly increased air pollution
ANSWER: W) Water consumption rates of data centers is higher in water-abundant areas
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A geometric series has first term ½ and third term ⅛. What is the sum of the fifth and seventh term?

ANSWER: 208/25

ANSWER: 2, 1, 3, 4
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer A space ship with a mass of 5000 kilograms moves at 4/5 c. What is its relativistic momentum in kilogram meters per second expressed in scientific notation to one significant figure?
ANSWER: 2 × 1012
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What hypothetical process extracts energy from a rotating black hole by taking advantage of the frame-dragging effect in its ergosphere?
ANSWER: Penrose process

13. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an example of the curse of dimensionality?
