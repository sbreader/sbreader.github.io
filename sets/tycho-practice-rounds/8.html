1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Indicate all of the following three statements that are TRUE of glycogen 1. It is more branched than starch 2. Branches begin with alpha,1-6 glycosidic linkages 3. Glycogenesis requires the hydrolysis of high-energy phosphate bonds.
ANSWER: ALL OF THEM
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is TRUE of a gas-phase reaction where the change in enthalpy is -1000 kJ and the change in internal energy is -1020 kJ?
W) The equilibrium constant increases with temperature
X) The reaction is spontaneous at all temperatures
Y) The reaction consumes more gas molecules than it produces
Z) The rate constant of the forward reaction decreases with temperature
ANSWER: Y) The reaction consumes more gas molecules than it produces
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules is the starting point for the synthesis of glycogen, and is the substrate of UDP-glucose pyrophosphorylase?
W) Glucose-1-phosphate
X) Glucose-4-phosphate
Y) Glucose-6-phosphate
Z) Fructose-1,6-bisphosphate
ANSWER: W) Glucose-1-phosphate
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three solvents in terms of increasing dielectric constant: Hexane DMSO Ethyl acetate
ANSWER: 1. HEXANE, 3. ETHYL ACETATE, 2. DMSO (also accept: 1, 3, 2)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If the ionization energy of a particle in a 2s orbital in hydrogen is E, what is the energy of a photon in terms of E required to promote a hydrogen electron from 1s to 2p?
ANSWER: 3E
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer A capacitor of capacitance 1 microfarad is charged with a constant current of 1 milliamp for a period of 1 second, after which the capacitor is unhooked from the battery and the distance between plates is doubled. What is now the voltage difference between the plates in volts?
ANSWER: 2000
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Consider a triangle with integer sides, two of which have length 5 and 6. What is the number of possible lengths of the third side?
ANSWER: 9
4. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following functions is a solution to the differential equation y'' + 4y = 1?

Z) y = sin(4x) + 1/4

11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT TRUE of faults and tectonics?
W) Horsts are downward-faulted blocks in basin-and-range provinces
