1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the x-intercept of the graph with equation y = 7?
ANSWER: THERE IS NONE
1. BONUS Math Multiple Choice If a line passes through point (1, 6) and has a slope of 4, which of the following is the equation of the line?
W) x = 2y + 4
X) x = 4y + 2
Y) y = 4x + 2
Z) y = 2x + 4
ANSWER: Y) y = 4x + 2

X) It has a magnitude

25. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of chemosynthesis?
W) A Venus flytrap breaking down the phosphates in a fly
X) A plant obtaining fixated nitrogen from bacteria rich soil
Y) Prokaryotes obtaining energy from sulfur compounds from the sea vents on the ocean floor
Z) A human being breaking down vitamins and minerals for metabolic digestion
ANSWER: Y) Prokaryotes obtaining energy from sulfur compounds from the sea vents on the ocean floor
