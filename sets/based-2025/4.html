Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Plants and fungi often inhabit the same areas, as they require each other to survive. What symbiotic relationship between a plant and a fungus involves the exchange of nutrients between fungal hyphae and plant roots?
ANSWER: MYCORRHIZAE
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What class of plant hormones, named after a genus of parasitic plants, is responsible for the attraction of mycorrhizae as well as regulation of root and shoot branching?
W) Gibberellins
X) Auxins
Y) Jasmonates
Z) Strigolactones
ANSWER: Z) Strigolactones
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A Wheatstone bridge is used to measure what electrical quantity?
W) Voltage
X) Current
Y) Resistance
Z) EMF
ANSWER: Y) Resistance
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer Max is working with a Wheatstone bridge and balances it, such that the current between the midpoints of the legs is 0. The known leg contains 150 ohm and 210 ohm resistors, and the unknown leg contains a 400 ohm and unknown resistor. The 150 and 400 ohm resistors are connected to the positive terminal of the battery. What is the unknown resistance, in ohms?
ANSWER: 560
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer While out on a midnight walk, Max looks up and sees a twinkling, twinkling little star. This twinkling is due to what distortion effect, which occurs because of turbulence in Earth’s atmosphere?
ANSWER: SEEING (also accept: ASTRONOMICAL SEEING)
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, indicate which of the following three statements are true of elliptical galaxies 1. They are generally younger than spiral galaxies 2. They consist mostly of red stars 3. They have abundant dust and gas spread out within them
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Max is still a real gas. Under which of the following conditions would he act most like an ideal gas?
W) High temperature, high pressure
X) High temperature, low pressure
Y) Low temperature, high pressure
Z) Low temperature, low pressure
ANSWER: X) High temperature, low pressure
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following acid and conjugate base pairs could be used to construct a buffer?
W) HF and F-
X) HCl and Cl-
Y) H2SO4 and HSO4-
Z) HClO4 and ClO4-
ANSWER: W) HF and F-
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer By name of number, name all of the following four units which are NOT SI base units 1. Candela 2. Coulomb 3. Joule 4. Mole
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Max, tired of Quinn whipping out the waterfall analogy for electric potential every chance he gets, gently pushes Quinn off a waterfall 5 meters tall with a horizontal velocity of 24 meters per second. To two significant figures and in meters per second, what is Quinn's velocity when he hits the water?
ANSWER: 26
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Students at Saratoga High School are studying bacteria. What common, spherical gram-positive bacterial species, which sometimes acts as a pathogen, is known for its golden color and strains with notorious antibiotic resistance?
ANSWER: STAPHYLOCOCCUS AUREUS
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer The Gram stain is used to visualize many different species of bacteria, including S. aureus. This staining procedure takes advantage of the binding of crystal violet dye to what molecule, found in large quantities on the cell walls of gram-positive bacteria?
ANSWER: PEPTIDOGLYCAN
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Even with the best optical telescope, background space between stars and galaxies will seem completely dark. However, observing in what range of the electromagnetic spectrum will reveal a nearly uniform faint glow left by radiation left over from the Big Bang?
W) X-Ray
X) Ultraviolet
Y) Infrared
Z) Microwave
ANSWER: Z) Microwave
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What feature, situated near the equator, consists of a semi-permanent low pressure region caused by the collision of trade winds, which results in clouds and frequent precipitation?
ANSWER: INTERTROPICAL CONVERGENCE ZONE (also accept: ITCZ)
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Saratoga High School students continue their tour of Europe by learning about the Paris Climate Agreement, which aims to limit the extent of human CO2 emission. In addition to causing global warming, CO2 contributes to what environmental issue that arises when it dissolves in ocean water?
ANSWER: OCEAN ACIDIFICATION
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Warren is observing a theoretical post-actinide element. What is its lowest principal energy level that can include a theoretical g orbital?
ANSWER: 5 (also accept: n = 5)
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following four particles in order of increasing mass 1. Electron 2. Proton 3. Neutron 4. Neutrino
ANSWER: 4, 1, 2, 3 (also accept: NEUTRINO, ELECTRON, PROTON, NEUTRON)
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In the equilibrium for the dissolution of KNO3 in water, to what power is exponent raised of the concentration of solid KNO3?
W) -1
X) 1
Y) 2
Z) KNO3 does not appear in the equilibrium expression
ANSWER: Z) KNO3 does not appear in the equilibrium expression
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the determinant of the 3x3 identity matrix?
ANSWER: 1
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Calculate the determinant of the following three by three nonidentity matrix: First row: 1, 7, 3 Second row: 0, 4, -2 Third row: 0, 0, 5
ANSWER: 20
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Arjun is an employee of Ethan’s wastewater treatment plant and needs to remove toxic heavy metals from the water. He knows that the hydroxides of these metals are highly insoluble, and that by Le Chatelier's principle, adding a strong electrolyte hydroxide will promote the precipitation of heavy metal hydroxide. What is the name of this effect?
ANSWER: COMMON ION EFFECT
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A reaction is at equilibrium at 550 Kelvin. If the measured change in enthalpy is 20 kilojoules, what is the change in entropy, to the nearest joule per kelvin?
ANSWER: 36
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Every year, Ethan plans a two-week trip to the Hawaiian islands. However, his intentions to visit Big Island have been foiled by yet another eruption of Kilauea, which has covered a once-scenic area of brushland with barren, black lava. Should Ethan want to restore the ecosystem so he can visit the island again, what natural process should he try to accelerate, where gradual weathering allows vegetation to colonize previously devoid and barren land?
ANSWER: PRIMARY SUCCESSION
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer After half a year of promoting succession, Ethan observes small shrubs growing on the inconveniently placed lava rock. What term describes these organisms, which are the first to settle in the new environment?
ANSWER: PIONEER SPECIES
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Arjun is fascinated by rockets, so he decides to spectate a SpaceX Starship liftoff. If the rocket tilts slightly during liftoff and the airflow moves across its body at an angle, what aerodynamic force will act perpendicular to the airflow?
ANSWER: LIFT
13. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Ethan is enjoying a nice evening walk when he looks up and sees glowing SpaceX Starship debris. What is the primary source of the glow produced by spacecraft during atmospheric reentry?
W) Latent heat released due to the lack of cooling from the vacuum of space
X) Frictional heating of debris with colliding air molecules
Y) Extreme heating from adiabatic compression of air in front of the debris
Z) Combustion of debris material with abundant atmospheric oxygen
ANSWER: Y) Extreme heating from adiabatic compression of air in front of the debris
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Warren is looking through his drawer. He has 4 red socks, 6 blue socks, and 7 black socks. How many socks must he pull in order to guarantee he has a pair of each color?
ANSWER: 15
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Now that Warren has his socks, he is ready to choose the rest of his outfit. He can customize 3 features: socks, shirt, jacket. He has red, blue, and black socks, green, blue, and black shirts, and blue and black jackets. How many unique outfits can he make, given that at least two features of his outfit must have matching colors?
ANSWER: 12
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Students at Saratoga High School are studying the effects of the SIRT6 gene, a histone H3 and H4 deacetylase. Should SIRT6 activity be upregulated, how would the rate of gene transcription change in result?
W) Transcription would increase.
X) Transcription would decrease.
Y) Transcription would remain constant.
Z) Transcription would increase, and then decrease.
ANSWER: X) Transcription would decrease.
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Further research has shown that SIRT6 expression is required to maintain proper telomeric structure. Decreased SIRT6 expression results in increased genomic instability due to a defect in what specific DNA repair pathway, which repairs double-strand breaks using a homologous sequence as a template?
ANSWER: HOMOLOGOUS RECOMBINATION
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer During 7th period, Max desperately wants to fall asleep, but can't unless the decibel level is 40 or less. If his teacher is currently lecturing 1.5 meters away and Max registers this as a 60 decibel sound, how far away, in meters, should he sit so he can peacefully fall asleep?
ANSWER: 15
16. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A 10 centimeter tall object is placed 20 centimeters in front of a concave mirror with a focal length of 15 centimeters. Which of the following describes the size, in centimeters, and orientation of the image?
W) 60, upright
X) 60, inverted
Y) 30, upright
Z) 30, inverted
ANSWER: Z) 30, inverted
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the value of the integral from −π/2 to π/2 of x3 cos(x) dx?
ANSWER: 0
17. BONUS Math Short Answer At what point do the lines 4x + 2y = 4 and -3x + 5y = 23 intersect?
ANSWER: (-1, 4) (also accept: x = -1 AND y = 4)
18. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Alex has a chair for his laundry. Because Alex is lazy, clean clothes pile up on the chair as the week progresses. Every weekend, he clears the chair starting from the top to the bottom. What data structure operates most similarly to Alex’s laundry chair?
ANSWER: STACK
18. BONUS Math Short Answer Alex further observes his laundry chair, which has a capacity of 120 pieces of laundry. If laundry arrives at a rate of 20 pieces a day, and the chair starts with 40 pieces of laundry, how many times will Alex's laundry chair be cleared by the 2025th day?
ANSWER: 337
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When Lord Kelvin estimated the age of the Earth, he accounted solely for radiative cooling. His estimate was incorrect due to what other major source of heat within Earth?
ANSWER: RADIOISOTOPE DECAY
19. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Arjun calls himself "hydrophobic," meaning that he is afraid of the liquid state. Which of the following effusive features would be safe for Arjun to visit, due to the expulsion of only gaseous matter?
W) Fumaroles
X) Cinder cones
Y) Geysers
Z) Cold seeps
ANSWER: W) Fumaroles
20. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Warren is studying attendance for his AP Statistics class. If he notices that each student has a 90% chance to show up to class each day, and there are 30 people in his class, what distribution will result from tallying the total number of people that show up each day over many days?
W) Normal distribution
X) Binomial distribution
Y) Poisson distribution
Z) Chi-squared distribution
ANSWER: X) Binomial distribution
20. BONUS Math Short Answer Warren continues to analyze the amount of students that show up for class. If now, the probability of someone showing up for class dropped to 75% and 32 students are enrolled in the class, what is the expected number of students that show up to class?
ANSWER: 24
21. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following particles has the same charge as an electron?
W) Positron
X) Photon
Y) Tau
Z) Down quark
ANSWER: Y) Tau
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following three particles that are bosons 1. Gluon 2. Photon 3. Neutrino
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following metals would give off a purple flame in a flame test?
W) Lithium
X) Sodium
Y) Potassium
Z) Rubidium
ANSWER: Y) Potassium
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When arsine gas, AsH3, reacts with solid silver nitrate, AgNO3, a double replacement reaction occurs and Ag4AsNO3, along with nitric acid, is formed. What is the oxidation number of As in Ag4AsNO3?
ANSWER: NEGATIVE THREE (do not accept: THREE; POSITIVE THREE)
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for when the Earth is closest to the Sun, the apsis which occurs roughly two weeks after the winter solstice?
ANSWER: PERIHELION
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What line, calculated by finding the depth at which seismic wave velocities dramatically shift as a result of rock density, is defined as the boundary between the crust and the mantle?
ANSWER: MOHOROVICIC DISCONTINUITY (also accept: MOHO DISCONTINUITY; MOHO)

ANSWER: -8
