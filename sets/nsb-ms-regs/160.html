Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In the RGB additive color system, what color is complementary to green?
ANSWER: MAGENTA
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To the nearest gram, what is the mass of two moles of nitrogen atoms?
ANSWER: 28
2. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice If m is an integer and 15m is divisible by 12, which of the following could be m?
W) 2
X) 3
Y) 4
Z) 5
ANSWER: Y) 4
2. BONUS Math Short Answer If x varies inversely as the square root of y and x equals 20 when y equals 49, what is the value of x when y = 64?
ANSWER: 35/2 (also accept: 17 1/2; 17.5)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Most long-period comets are thought to originate in what part of the solar system?
ANSWER: OORT CLOUD (do not accept: KUIPER BELT)
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Algol variables are binary star systems where the brightness of the system dips twice each orbital cycle. What phenomenon causes these dips?
ANSWER: ECLIPSES (also accept: SYZYGY)
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Brookhaven National Lab scientists are exploiting the base pairing of DNA to attach nanoparticles to one another. Under normal base pairing rules, what DNA base is complementary to cytosine?
ANSWER: GUANINE (also accept: G)
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying the chemistry of aluminum production, including the dissolution of aluminum hydroxide. What is the chemical formula for neutral aluminum hydroxide?
ANSWER: Al(OH)3
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following criteria is most important in determining if a lens is converging or diverging?
W) Its composition
X) The thickness of its center compared to its edges
Y) Its index of refraction
Z) The angle of incident light
ANSWER: X) The thickness of its center compared to its edges
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Suppose that a 10-kilogram object is moving initially with a velocity of 10 meters per second across a surface, and experiences a frictional force that stops it in 2 seconds. What is the coefficient of kinetic friction, to one decimal place?
ANSWER: 0.5
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the specialized membrane-bound compartments in eukaryotic cells that carry out specific functions for the cell?
ANSWER: ORGANELLES
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following geologic periods was ended by a major mass extinction?
W) Pennsylvanian
X) Cretaceous
Y) Jurassic
Z) Silurian
ANSWER: X) Cretaceous
7. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following translations changes the graph of y = x2 into that of y = (x + 4)2 + 6?
W) 6 units down and 4 units to the left
X) 6 units down and 4 units to the right
Y) 6 units up and 4 units to the left
Z) 6 units up and 4 units to the right
ANSWER: Y) 6 units up and 4 units to the left
7. BONUS Math Short Answer One angle of an isosceles triangle measures 68 degrees. In degrees, what are the possible measures of the smallest angle of the triangle?
ANSWER: 44; 56
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following transformations is NOT a chemical change?
W) Dissolving a salt in water
X) Vaporizing a drop of ethanol
Y) Mixing solutions of magnesium sulfate and barium nitrate
Z) Heating calcium carbonate until it expels a gas
ANSWER: X) Vaporizing a drop of ethanol
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The atomic number of chlorine is 17. What does this necessarily imply about chlorine atoms?
W) They have a mass number of 34
X) They contain 17 protons
Y) They contain 17 neutrons
Z) They contain 17 electrons
ANSWER: X) They contain 17 protons
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Archaeopteryx was a dinosaur that is thought to be one of the first members of what modern-day class of animals?
ANSWER: AVES (also accept: BIRDS)
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What muscle in the human body causes extension at the elbow joint?
ANSWER: TRICEPS (also accept: TRICEPS BRACHII)
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the degree of the product of the three binomials x2 + 5, x6 + x2, and 7x-6?
ANSWER: 9
10. BONUS Math Short Answer A bag contains 200 identically-sized marbles. Exactly 10% are purple, 20% are blue, 30% are orange, and the rest are red. What is the probability of randomly selecting first an orange marble, replacing it, and then selecting a blue one?
ANSWER: 6% (also accept: 0.06; 3/50)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Hadley cell is associated with what set of prevailing winds found in tropical latitudes?
ANSWER: TRADE WINDS
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three rock types that contain a significant proportion of calcium carbonate 1) Limestone 2) Travertine 3) Coquina.
ANSWER: 1 and 2 and 3
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Brookhaven National Lab have developed a new catalyst for the combustion of ethanol in fuel cells. Other than high-energy electrons that can be used for power, what are the two primary chemical products of this reaction?
ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE; WATER (also accept: CO2; H2O)
12. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are developing methods to prevent damage to tokamaks during operation. For what application are tokamaks designed?
W) Containment of antimatter
X) Generation of high-pressure environments
Y) Creation of high-intensity ion beams
Z) Sustained nuclear fusion
ANSWER: Z) Sustained nuclear fusion
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following terms is used to describe the extra copy of a chromosome synthesized during S phase prior to mitosis?
W) Homologous chromosome
X) Sister chromatid
Y) Centromere
Z) Mitotic spindle
ANSWER: X) Sister chromatid
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer What type of mimicry involves two distasteful species resembling each other?
ANSWER: MÜLLERIAN
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If a right triangle has an exterior angle of measure 160 degrees, what is the degree measure of the larger of its acute interior angles?
ANSWER: 70
14. BONUS Math Short Answer The graph of the line with equation y = 3x – 4 is reflected through the y-axis. What is the slope-intercept equation of the reflected line?
ANSWER: y = –3x – 4
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A star's apparent magnitude is most directly related to which of its properties?
W) Brightness
X) Temperature
Y) Size
Z) Age
ANSWER: W) Brightness
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What process is primarily responsible for the ongoing heating and volcanic activity that occurs on some of Jupiter’s moons?
ANSWER: TIDAL HEATING (also accept: TIDAL FRICTION)
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the SI unit for luminous intensity?
ANSWER: CANDELA
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of water solubility 1) Increasing the temperature increases solubility of sugar 2) Increasing the temperature increases the rate of dissolving of sugar 3) Increasing the temperature increases the solubility of calcium carbonate.
ANSWER: 1 and 2
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at the National Renewable Energy Lab collaborated with counterparts at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab to map electronic relationships between ternary metal nitrides, such as the compound zinc antimony nitride. In most of these compounds, what is the oxidation state of the nitrogen atom?
ANSWER: –3
17. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers at Lawrence Livermore National Lab recently studied a new phase of water ice in the lab. What type of bond is responsible for holding together adjacent water molecules in normal water ice?
W) Hydrogen bond
X) Covalent bond
Y) Ionic bond
Z) Metallic bond
ANSWER: W) Hydrogen bond
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Eusociality and intraspecies communication are most apparent in which of the following groups of insects?
W) Flies
X) Grasshoppers
Y) Ants
Z) Beetles
ANSWER: Y) Ants
18. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following parts of the brain is responsible for controlling heart rate and breathing?
W) Cerebrum
X) Cerebellum
Y) Thalamus
Z) Medulla
ANSWER: Z) Medulla
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What name is given to the study of ocean depth and seafloor topography?
ANSWER: BATHYMETRY
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three features of a geologic section that could be used to determine the relative age of a series of rocks 1) Strike and dip 2) Intrusion of dikes 3) Cross-cutting relationships.
ANSWER: 1 and 2 and 3
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The pH value of a solution is defined as the negative log of the concentration of what ion?
ANSWER: HYDRONIUM (also accept: HYDROGEN; H+; H3O+)
20. BONUS Physics Short Answer All objects that have a temperature above absolute zero release some energy by emitting photons. What is the term for this type of energy?
ANSWER: RADIANT (also accept: RADIATION)
21. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following genetic disorders results from a chromosomal trisomy?
W) Red-green colorblindness
X) Down syndrome
Y) Polydactyly
Z) Tay-Sachs disease
ANSWER: X) Down syndrome
21. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following scenarios is the LEAST likely to result in allopatric speciation?
W) Two species of wolves living on either side of a now-underwater isthmus
X) Two bird species living on nearby islands formed by rising sea levels
Y) Two species of ground squirrels separated by a large canyon formed by an earthquake
Z) Two species of fish moving back and forth across a tropical reef system
ANSWER: Z) Two species of fish moving back and forth across a tropical reef system
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Pulsars were first discovered from observations in what region of the electromagnetic spectrum?
ANSWER: RADIO (also accept: RADIO WAVES)
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of solar activity that are associated with the photosphere 1) Sunspots 2) Flares 3) CMEs.
ANSWER: 1 only
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A car travels 350 miles in 9 hours. To the nearest mile per hour, over the nine-hour period, what is the average speed of the car?
ANSWER: 39
23. BONUS Math Short Answer A flower bed is formed in the shape of a 120-degree sector of a circle of radius 10 feet. To the nearest foot, what is the perimeter of the flower bed?
ANSWER: 41
