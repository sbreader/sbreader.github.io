1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following processes is not used in the engine that produces the maximum efficiency?
W) Isothermal compression
X) Adiabatic expansion
Y) Isothermal expansion
Z) Isobaric compression
ANSWER: Z) Isobaric compression
1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which of the following 3 choices would one expect in the synthesis reaction of nitrogen and hydrogen producing ammonia if an inert gas was added to the reaction vessel and no other change was made? A shift to the left A shift to the right No shift at all
ANSWER: III
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a possible consequence for high speed meanders?
W) cut-banks
X) point bars
Y) oxbow lakes
Z) a high gradient
ANSWER: Z) a high gradient
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the 4 following choices that are plausible characteristics of the terrain left behind by a receding glacier. I) pluvial lakes II) Kames III) proglacial lakes IV) Ground Moraine
ANSWER: II, III, and IV
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Mosses, Liverworts, Algae, and Ferns can be classified under which of the following in the plant kingdom?
W) Angiosperms
X) Seed bearing plants
Y) Spore bearing plants
Z) Gymnosperms
ANSWER: Y) Spore bearing plants
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Before being classified as prokaryotes, in what class of plants were bacteria first classified as?
ANSWER: Schizomycetes
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the four following options that are true about a pendulum's period. The period is directly proportional to mass The period is inversely proportional to the mass The period Is directly proportional to the square root of the length of the string The period Is inversely proportional to the square root of the length of the string
ANSWER: III only
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The arm span of a monkey is 4m. If the monkey is flying past a stationary observer at a speed of 0.8 c with its arms outstretched, what is the armspan of the monkey that is perceived by the observer. Give your answer to the tenths place.
ANSWER: 2.4 m
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer John is walking east at a speed of 3 miles per hour, while Bob is also walking east, but at a speed of 5 miles per hour. If Bob is now 1 mile west of John, how many minutes will it take for Bob to catch up to John?
ANSWER: 30
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Mr. and Mrs. Lopez have two children. When they get into their car (with five seats) two people sit in the front, and the other two sit in the back. Either Mr. Lopez or Mrs. Lopez must sit in the driver's seat. How many seating arrangements are possible?
ANSWER: 12
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 choices that are not characteristic of biodiesel. It is a based from vegetable oil or animal fat oils It can be used with petrol diesel in a standard diesel engine It has been officially defined as a dual- alkyl ether by the National Biodiesel Board
ANSWER: III ONLY
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Bob places a reaction in a bomb calorimeter. Bob checks the screen for the internal energy of the system at the beginning and ending of the reaction. Given that Bob controls the temperature of the entire reaction with a heat sink at 45 degrees Celsius, what must be the difference in the readings that Bob received during the beginning and end of the reaction?
ANSWER: 0 J
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many antibonding orbitals does a molecule of nitrogen gas have?
ANSWER: 1
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following tells us the probability of finding an electron at a particular distance from the nucleus?
W) Wavefunction squared
X) Radial Distribution function
Y) radial wavefunction
Z) angular wavefunction
ANSWER: X) Radial Distribution function
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the sedimentary rock formed when glacial till becomes lithified and strata becomes striated rock fragments and are most commonly found atop grooved and polished rock surfaces?
ANSWER: Tillite
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following 4 options is true of comets? I) A type I tail is characterized by a straight and wispy trail created by neutral and ionized gases II) Short period comets come from the Oort cloud III) active regions in comets spew out gas that bend backward to form a tail IV) The type II tail is immune to magnetic fields.
ANSWER: I, III, and IV
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Bob feels happy, hungry, and sleepy throughout the day. Which of the following hormones is most responsible for these responses in Bob?
W) Thyroxine
X) Dopamine
Y) Epinephrine
Z) Serotonin
ANSWER: Z) Serotonin
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which of the following is not a function of the Norepinephrine hormone? I) A boosted supply of oxygen and glucose to brain and muscles II) triggered inflammatory response III) Break down of lipids in fat cells IV) Increased Blood Pressure
ANSWER: II only
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the displacement of an object that travels 5 km North, 4 km East, and 2km South?
ANSWER: 5km
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Through what field theory do the four fundamental forces come from?
ANSWER: Gauge Theory
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Find the definite integral from 1 to 4 of the function x3+6x2 +6x+ 8. Leave your answer as a fraction.
ANSWER: 1035/4
12. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What energy source is most common in the United States as a renewable energy source?
W) Hydroelectric
X) Geothermal
Y) Biofuels
Z) Biomass
ANSWER: W) Hydroelectric
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 choices that are ways that the United States acquires geothermal energy 1) Dry steam method 2) Flash steam method 3) Hydraulic fracturing 4) Binary cycle method
ANSWER: All of them
