1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is FALSE regarding stratified drift?
W) some deposits of stratified drift are issued by streams that come directly from the glacier
X) sediment that is deposited as till, picked up again, and deposited through glacial streams are classified as stratified drift
Y) stratified drift is the main constituent of moraines that mark the edges of glaciation
Z) generally, larger sediments will be deposited closest to the boundary of a glacier and finer particles will be transported farther away
ANSWER: Y) stratified drift is the main constituent of moraines that mark the edges of glaciation

TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following 4 statements that is/are TRUE of mass transfer: 1: if an evolving star expands so far that it fills its Roche surface, it will be forced to take on the teardrop shape of the Roche surface 2: mass transfer that is driven by stellar wind through the inner Lagrangian point is generally rapid 3: the Algol paradox describes how the less massive star in a binary system can become a giant, while the more massive star is still on the main sequence 4: if two binary stars have both expanded to fill their Roche surfaces, they can both merge into a single, rapidly rotating star
