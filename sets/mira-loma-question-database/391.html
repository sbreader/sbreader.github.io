1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the phylum of plants whose phylum is derived from the word Hepaticus?

2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the part of the bryophyte that conducts the material from the foot to the capsule? Anthocerophytes lack these.
ANSWER: SETA
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The period of a simple pendulum is T. In terms of T, if the length of the pendulum is increased by 7 and the pendulum is moved to a planet where the acceleration due to gravity is 9 times that of the original, then what is the new period?
ANSWER: √7T/3
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the chemical formula of potassium tetrachloroaluminate?
ANSWER: ALCL4K
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The period of a conical pendulum is T, its length L, and the acceleration due to gravity G. If the conical pendulum is moved to a new planet and the length is multiplied by 4 and the period decreased by 3, then in terms of G, what would have to be the planet's acceleration due to gravity?
ANSWER: 36G

9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice There is an area enclosed by the functions f(x) = √x, y = 0, and x = 4. Which of the following has the largest volume?

X) Rotating the function around the y-axis
Y) Rotating the function around the line x = 4
Z) Rotating the function around the line y = x
