Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term describing chromosomes after crossing over has occurred?
ANSWER: RECOMBINANT (do not accept: HOMOLOGOUS)
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer On which locations of the chromosome does crossing over occur?
ANSWER: CHIASMA (also accept: CHIASMATA)
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Livermore National Lab are using computers to predict the folding of proteins into three-dimensional structures. Protein folding begins with chains of amino acids, which are considered to be what level of protein structure?
ANSWER: PRIMARY
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The upper stratosphere is much hotter than the lower stratosphere due to the absorption of sunlight by what gas?
ANSWER: OZONE (also accept: O3)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the chemical formula of Vanadium (II) oxide?
W) V2O
X) VO2
Y) VO
Z) V2O2
ANSWER: Y) VO
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Scientists at UW Madison are working to reverse the effects of genetic mutations on eyesight. To do this, they are modifying nucleic acids to carry the correct amino acids instead of the ones instructed by the mutated genes. What group of nucleic acids are they modifying?
W) DNA
X) mRNA
Y) tRNA
Z) rRNA
ANSWER: Y) tRNA
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the largest 5 digit palindrome such that the sum of its digits is 16 and its units digit is 4?
ANSWER: 44044
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Meanders are often present deep in bedrock in places such as the Grand Canyon. For this to exist, which of the following processes must have occurred to the surrounding rock?
W) Metamorphism
X) Uplift
Y) Liquefaction
Z) Faulting
ANSWER: X) Uplift
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What feature of a floodplain forms when a meander erodes until it is cut off from the rest of the stream?
ANSWER: OXBOW LAKE (also accept: BILLABONGS; RESACAS)
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the genus and species taxonomic classification of the wolf?
ANSWER: CANIS LUPUS
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following ranks of taxonomic classification from broadest to most specific. 1) Phylum 2) Genus 3) Family 4) Domain
ANSWER: 4, 1, 3, 2
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the term for any process in which pressure is conserved?
ANSWER: ISOBARIC
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice An ideal gas undergoes isothermal expansion from a volume of 10 Liters to a volume of 30 Liters. If the final pressure was 0.6 atmospheres, what was the initial pressure of the gas?
W) 0.2 atmospheres
X) 0.3 atmospheres
Y) 1.2 atmospheres
Z) 1.8 atmospheres
ANSWER: Z) 1.8 atmospheres
7. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Los Alamos National Lab are using radioactive actinium-225 to treat cancer. Which of the following is a reason why using this atom is not a good long-term solution for cancer treatment?
W) It is rarely found in nature
X) It has a half-life of only 10 days
Y) It emits high-energy alpha particles
Z) It can be produced from thorium-229
ANSWER: W) It is rarely found in nature
8. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer While not directly creating it, fossil fuels are used commercially to produce what light gas that is rarely found in the atmosphere?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN (also accept: H2)
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following nitrogenous bases is present ONLY in DNA, and not in RNA?
W) Thymine
X) Adenine
Y) Uracil
Z) Guanine
ANSWER: W) Thymine
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following that are part of a DNA molecule. 1) Nitrogenous base 2) Phosphate group 3) Sugar group
ANSWER: All
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the name for the temperature at which the ferromagnetic properties of a material change drastically?
ANSWER: CURIE POINT
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Galileo's Law of Odd Numbers holds in any situation where what kinematic quantity, defined as the rate of change of velocity, is constant?
ANSWER: ACCELERATION
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the volume of a prism with a height of 3 and a regular hexagonal base of side length 4?
ANSWER: 72√3
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A cube has a side length of 2. If the width is halved, the height is tripled, and the length is quadrupled, what will be the resulting volume of the cube?
ANSWER: 48
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What stellar remnant forms after the red giant phase of a medium-mass star that surrounds the core white dwarf and is only visible for a few thousand years?
ANSWER: PLANETARY NEBULA
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What class of degenerate stars are the bright remnants of stars with masses under 8 solar masses?
ANSWER: WHITE DWARFS
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer Scientists at Ames Lab are developing new technologies to turn metals into powders. Powdered metals are useful because they maximize what property of the metal?
ANSWER: SURFACE AREA
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer DOE researchers are studying the deformation of graphene in response to external forces. Graphene is a two-dimensional allotrope of what element?
ANSWER: CARBON
13. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following phenomena creates seismic shadow zones where neither P-waves nor S-waves are detected?
W) Reflection
X) Diffraction
Y) Refraction
Z) Interference
ANSWER: Y) Refraction
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What layer in the Earth blocks S-waves from an earthquake but allows P-waves to travel through?
ANSWER: OUTER CORE (do not accept: CORE)
14. BONUS Math Short Answer In a class of 9 people, 8 people score 78 points on a test. What is the lowest integer score that Max can receive such that the average score of the class is greater than 80?
ANSWER: 97
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer To the nearest hundred, what is 842 + 231-982?
ANSWER: 100
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When a strong acid is added to a strong base of equal concentration and volume and the reaction proceeds to completion, what two products are created?
ANSWER: WATER AND SALT (also accept: H2O AND A SALT; SALT AND WATER) (do not accept: NaCl)
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The proton-proton chain is an example of what general process that stars use to make energy?
ANSWER: NUCLEAR FUSION (also accept: FUSION)
16. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Princeton Plasma Physics Lab is currently conducting research on nuclear fusion as a possible future fuel source. Their research is mostly focused on maintaining what necessary condition for fusion?
W) High temperature
X) Substance purity
Y) Absence of gravitational pull
Z) Absence of magnetic field
ANSWER: W) High temperature
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A red flower is crossed with a white flower, and all the flowers in the next generation are pink. What does the gene for flower color appear to exhibit?
W) Mosaicism
X) Incomplete dominance
Y) Blending
Z) Linkage
ANSWER: X) Incomplete dominance
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Most inherited genetic diseases which occur more often in men than in women are found on which chromosome?
ANSWER: X CHROMOSOME
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What spectral class of stars do the majority of stars belong to?
W) M
X) G
Y) A
Z) O
ANSWER: W) M
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What quantity directly determines the radiant flux of light emitted by a star assuming blackbody-type radiation?
ANSWER: SURFACE TEMPERATURE (also accept: TEMPERATURE)
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the formula and name for the straight chain alkane with 10 carbons?
ANSWER: DECANE (also accept: C10H22; N-DECANE)
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following hydrocarbons in order of increasing boiling point. 1) Propane 2) Ethane 3) Butane
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is found in eukaryotes but not in prokaryotes?
W) Cell membrane
X) Cytoskeleton
Y) Ribosomes
Z) Nucleolus
ANSWER: Z) Nucleolus
20. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a type of muscle?
W) Cardiac
X) Smooth
Y) Epithelial
Z) Skeletal
ANSWER: Y) Epithelial
41. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Many research and scientific satellites are located in a low Earth orbit, between 100 and 400 miles above Earth's surface. In what layer of Earth's atmosphere are these satellites in?
ANSWER: THERMOSPHERE
42. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Solve the following equation for a: a + 4 = 7a-8
ANSWER: 2
43. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the chemical formula of methanol?
ANSWER: CH4O (also accept: CH3OH)
44. BONUS Math Short Answer How many different ordered pairs of positive integers x and y exist such that 7x + 6y = 126?
ANSWER: 2
45. BONUS Math Multiple Choice What is the remainder when x3 + 4x2-8x + 16 is divided by x-4?
W) 100
X) 104
Y) 108
Z) 112
ANSWER: Z) 112
