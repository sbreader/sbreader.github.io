1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Consider a simple parallel RL circuit connected to an AC power source. The peak current through the inductor is 10 milliamps. What will the peak current through the inductor become, in milliamps, if the frequency is halved and the peak voltage is doubled?
ANSWER: 40
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice An object is thrown vertically upward at time t = 0, at a speed of 40 meters per second, from the top of a building that has a height of 100 meters. Assuming acceleration due to gravity equals 10 meters per second squared, at what time t in seconds will the object hit the ground?
W) 4
X) 8
Y) 10
Z) 15
ANSWER: Y) 10

Y) Haiti is underlain by thrust faults
