1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which one of the following are condensation polymers?
W) Polystyrene
X) Polyvinyl Chloride
Y) Polytetrafluoroethylene
Z) Polyester
ANSWER: Z) Polyester
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What value is the difference between the estimated heat capacity of an ideal gas under constant pressure and the estimated heat capacity of an ideal gas at constant volume?
ANSWER: Ideal Gas Constant
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the scientific name for the plant widely regarded as the “plant model organism”, also known as thale cress?
ANSWER: Arabidopsis thaliana
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Mesophyll cells, which are located within plant leaves and have a high chloroplast population, come in two varieties. What are the names of these two varieties?
W) Spongy and Rigid
X) Smooth and Rough
Y) Spongy and Palisade
Z) Palisade and Porous
ANSWER: Y) Spongy and Palisade
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Find the roots of: x3 − 4 x2 + 5 x − 2
ANSWER: 1 and 2
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer John is picking his clothes for the day. He has four pairs of socks, three pairs of pants, two shirts, and five hats. How many possible outfits can he make?
ANSWER: 120
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the process in a tropical cyclone when the outer eyewall contracts on the inner eyewall, forming a larger, more consolidated eyewall?
W) Eyewall choking cycle
X) Double eyewall cycle
Y) Eyewall replacement cycle
Z) Multi-eyewall cycle
ANSWER: Y) Eyewall replacement cycle
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of Io, a moon of Jupiter. 1) It is the highest density moon in the Solar System. 2) It contains glaciers at the poles. 3) It has an orbital period of less than three days.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The insides of an organism are lined with water filtering choanocytes, which are linked with amoebocytes, which help deliver nutrients throughout the organism’s body. Which phylum is this organism a member of?
W) Platyhelminthes
X) Cnidaria
Y) Echinodermata
Z) Porifera
ANSWER: Z) Porifera
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of members of the phylum Cnidaria?
W) They display polymorphism.
X) They are free floating and have no nervous system.
Y) They have a gastrovascular cavity in their body that is used to digest food.
Z) Their embryos show indirect development.
ANSWER: X) They are free floating and have no nervous system.
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the fifth derivative of the position vector?
W) Jerk
X) Snap
Y) Jounce
Z) Crackle
ANSWER: Z) Crackle

ANSWER: 1080 N
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which one of these is NOT a primary diagnostic property of a mineral?

X) Streak

7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A moon orbits on the border of a ring, helping to define the ring’s limit. Another moon orbits within a ring, clearing a small gap within said ring. What type of moon would these moons be designated as?
ANSWER: SHEPHERD MOON

9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of volcano is the steepest towards the crater?
ANSWER: STRATOVOLCANO (also accept: COMPOSITE VOLCANO)
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the process when the minimum pressure in a low pressure system drops 24 millibars in 24 hours?
W) Tropical cyclogenesis
X) Extratropical transition
Y) Bombogenesis
Z) Rapid deepening
ANSWER: Y) Bombogenesis

Y) Neopaleozoic
Z) Cambrian
ANSWER: X) Neoproterozoic

ANSWER: TRANSFORM FAULT BOUNDARY (also accept: TRANSFORM FAULT)
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the transition zone between the mantle and the core?
ANSWER: D DOUBLE PRIME LAYER

ANSWER: 100 SPACE DIAGONALS
13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A ball with mass 5 kg moves at 6 m/s towards a second stationary ball. The perfectly elastic collision sends the first ball back at 2 m/s, while the first ball shoots forward at 20 m/s. What is the mass in kilograms of the second ball?
W) 0.5
X) 1
Y) 2
Z) 4
ANSWER: Y) 2

X) F2 = 2F1, U2 = 4U1
Y) F2 = F1, U2 = U1
Z) F2 = F1, U2 = 4U1

17. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Aqua regia is used to dissolve gold. It contains hydrochloric acid and nitric acid. Why is nitric acid needed to dissolve gold?
W) Nitric acid is needed to form complex ions with gold so that hydrochloric acid can dissolve them.
X) Nitric acid converts various metals into their nitrate salts, which are highly insoluble in water, but dissolves readily in an acidic solution.
Y) Nitric acid prevents the chlorine in hydrochloric acid from forming complex ions with the gold by bonding with the chlorine, which leads to gold’s dissolution.
Z) Nitric acid converts various metals into their nitrate salts, which are highly soluble in the aqua regia solution.
ANSWER: Z) Nitric acid converts various metals into their nitrate salts, which are highly soluble in the aqua regia solution.
