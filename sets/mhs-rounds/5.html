14. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Consider a reaction for which, at high temperatures, where Gibbs Free Energy is negative. If the reaction system is at equilibrium under standard conditions, and then the temperature of the system is raised by 50 K, which of the following statements will be true?

X) The activation energy will be increased at this temperature.
Y) The ΔH° and ΔS° for this reaction are negative.
