Source packet

1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Einstein’s theory of relativity includes what famous formula which equates the energy of a particle with its rest mass?
ANSWER: E=mc2
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which group of elements are considered stable because they contain a full outer shell?
ANSWER: Nobel Gases
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the basic unit of life known as?
ANSWER: Cells
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer In what organelle in prokaryotic cells does protein synthesis take place?
ANSWER: Ribosomes
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Most semiconductors are made from which metalloid?
ANSWER: Silicon
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the most abundant gas in the universe?
ANSWER: Hydrogen
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Stars that die may produce brilliant explosions. What are these explosions known as?
ANSWER: Supernova
6. BONUS Math Short Answer How many prime numbers are less than 10?
ANSWER: 4
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the term for a change in velocity with respect to time?
ANSWER: Acceleration
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for a transition from gas to liquid?
ANSWER: Condensation
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Carbon dioxide transitions directly into gas from solid at STP. What is the term for this phase change?
ANSWER: Sublimation
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What organ system is primarily responsible for defending the body from infections?
ANSWER: Immune system
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What astronomic body does most of the energy that reaches Earth come from?
ANSWER: The SUN
10. BONUS Energy Short Answer What kind of light rays from the sun are known to cause sunburn?
ANSWER: Ultraviolet
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the names of Mars’s two moons?
ANSWER: Phoebos and Deimos
12. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the value of 5!?
ANSWER: 120
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer How much momentum does a 7kg object have when moving at 8 m/s?
ANSWER: 56
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer How much kinetic energy does a 7kg object have moving at 8 m/s?
ANSWER: 224 J
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the value of absolute zero on the Kelvin scale?
ANSWER: 0
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What temperature scale is commonly used in the United States to measure air temperature as well as body temperature?
ANSWER: Fahrenheit
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the genetic material inside the nucleus of eukaryotic cells made from?
ANSWER: DNA (also accept: Deoxyribonucleic Acid)
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer RNA is a nucleic acid like DNA except that RNA contains what base not found in DNA?
ANSWER: Uracil
16. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Most solar panels are made from what element, which can be doped to make P type and N type?
ANSWER: Silicon
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which scientist proposed the theory of continental drift, which was ignored until long after he died?
ANSWER: Alfred Wegener
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The Knicks won game 5 of the Eastern Conference finals by a score of 111 to 94. By how many points did they win?
ANSWER: 17
18. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the slope of a line passing through the points (5,6) and (3,12)?
ANSWER: 3
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer How much power is dissipated by a circuit with a voltage of 12 volts and total current 11 amperes?
ANSWER: 132
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What subatomic particle is responsible for the chemical properties of an element?
ANSWER: Electrons
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Some atoms have the same number of protons but different number of neutrons. What are such atoms called?
ANSWER: isotopes
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for a random often harmful change in the genetic material of an organism?
ANSWER: mutation
22. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the term for the tools that help step down the voltage between transmission lines and homes?
ANSWER: Transformers
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer On what chart would you find a plot of a star's temperature versus luminosity?
ANSWER: HR diagram
