4. BONUS Math Multiple Choice The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality is a powerful tool in linear algebra and analysis. Which of the following is true about the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality?

X) It provides a relationship between the expectation of a convex function and the function of the expectation.
Y) It establishes an upper bound on the inner product between two vectors in terms of their norms.

ANSWER: Y) It establishes an upper bound on the inner product between two vectors in terms of their norms.

8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice An enzyme catalyzes the hydrolysis of an ester with a certain activity, but this activity is lost in a 3 M urea solution. What is the most likely explanation for the loss of activity?
