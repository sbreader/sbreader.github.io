1. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following would not necessarily imply a discontinuity of the function f(x) when x=1
W) A vertical asymptote exists at x=1
X) The limit as x approaches 1 from the right is 2, while the limit as x approaches 1 from the left is 1
Y) The derivative of f(x) at x=1 does not exist
Z) The limit as x approaches 1 is negative infinity
ANSWER: Y) The derivative of f(x) at x=1 does not exist
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The protein pepsin would be likely denature in a
W) Solution with a pH of 7
X) Solution with a pH of 2
Y) Solution with a temperature of 310 Kelvin
Z) Solution that exceeds the saturation point of its substrates
ANSWER: W) Solution with a pH of 7
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Of the following amino acids, which ones are polar: Glycine, Leucine, Serine, Cysteine, Glutamine, Proline, and Tryptophan.
ANSWER: SERINE, CYSTEINE, GLUTAMINE
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer What SI unit, defined as a mole per second is used to measure catalytic activity?
ANSWER: KATAL
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What scientist, known for winning two Nobel prizes, one in Chemistry and one in Peace, is credited with pioneering the fields of hybridization and electronegativity?
ANSWER: LINUS PAULING
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A ball is thrown directly upwards at a speed of 10 m/s, assuming that g=10 m/s2, calculate the maximum height the ball the reach in meters
ANSWER: 5 METERS
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 4 kg ball is thrown directly upwards at 10 m/s. Assuming g = 10 m/s2, what is its kinetic energy 1 second after being thrown?
ANSWER: 0 JOULES
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following theories is currently favored by scientists to explain the formation of Earth's moon
W) The fission theory
X) The capture theory
Y) The co-formation theory
Z) The giant impact theory
ANSWER: Z) The giant impact theory
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following does not describe a depositional landform created by a glacier?
W) Moraine
X) Cirque
Y) Kame
Z) Esker
ANSWER: X) Cirque
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What glacial landform is described as long, winding ridges formed from streams flowing within or under a glacier?
ANSWER: ESKER
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the cube-root of 81 in simplest radical form?
ANSWER: 3∛3 (also accept: 3 TIMES THE CUBE ROOT OF 3)
8. BONUS Math Short Answer Given a triangle ABC, with angle A having 60 degrees, and sides b and c being 4 meters and 7 meters in length respectively, what is the length of side a in meters and in simplest radical form?
ANSWER: √37 meters (also accept: SQUARE ROOT OF 37 meters)
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What proteins serve in folding polypeptides into their conformations by providing a controlled environment separate from any influences that may change the outcome?
ANSWER: CHAPERONINS (also accept: CHAPERONE PROTEINS)
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What term is given to a display of condensed chromosomes arranged by pairs, often ordered according to the size and shape of the pairs?
ANSWER: KARYOTYPES
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Although technically not capable of forming a single bond, what type of interactions are described as a lattice of positively charged ions sharing conduction electrons arranged as a "sea of electrons"
W) Hydrogen bonding
X) van der Waals force
Y) Metallic bonding
Z) Ionic bonding
ANSWER: Y) Metallic bonding
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements exhibits the highest melting point
W) Carbon
X) Gold
Y) Lead
Z) Tungsten
ANSWER: W) Carbon
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What phenomena is defined as the ejection of any magnetic field in a substance undergoing a change into the superconducting state?
ANSWER: MEISSNER EFFECT (also accept: MEISSNER-OCHSENFIELD EFFECT)
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer What well-known English scientist was revealed to have discovered and failed to publish what would later become Ohm's Law, Charles' Law, and Dalton's Law of Partial Pressures, receiving credit for an experiment where he accurately estimated the density of the Earth, which would later aid in calculating the gravitational constant?
ANSWER: HENRY CAVENDISH
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What constellation, meaning the "water serpent", is the largest of the 88 constellations in the sky?

15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer At a car park there are 100 vehicles, 60 of which are cars, 30 are vans and the remainder of which are trucks. Assuming every vehicle is equally likely to leave, what is the probability of a car leaving second given a van or a truck had left first? Give the answer as a fraction
ANSWER: 20/33

19. BONUS Physics Short Answer According to Coulomb's law, if two forces repel each other with a force, F, by what factor would F increase if the charge of both objects were doubled and the distance between them were quadrupled?
