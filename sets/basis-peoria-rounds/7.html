1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is not true?
W) Most of the muscles that move the hand are located in the forearm.
X) The clavicle is the most frequently fractured bone in the body.
Y) The elbow joint is the weakest joint in the body.
Z) The masseter is the most powerful muscle in the body by weight.
ANSWER: Y) The elbow joint is the weakest joint in the body.
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is incorrectly matched?
W) Ball and socket joint – Glenohumeral joint
X) Plane joint – Between adjacent vertebrae
Y) Pivot joint – Between atlas and axis
Z) Condylar joint – Between tarsal bones
ANSWER: Z) Condylar joint – Between tarsal bones
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What reagent is used to test for the presence of aldehydes and is famous for producing a silver mirror when positive?
ANSWER: TOLLEN'S REAGENT
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What famous reaction do aldehydes undergo in reaction with themselves under basic conditions that forms a difunctionalized compound?
ANSWER: ALDOL REACTION
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The π+ meson decays by the process π+ → μ+ + v. To what lepton family does this neutrino belong?
ANSWER: MUON
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following are always conserved in particle physics 1) intrinsic spin 2) lepton number 3) baryon number 4) charge 5) strangeness?
W) 2, 3, 4, 5
X) 1, 3, 5
Y) 1, 2, 3, 4
Z) All
ANSWER: Z) All
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following methods for numerical integration is most accurate?
W) Simpson’s rule
X) Double step rule
Y) Rectangular rule
Z) Trapezoidal rule
ANSWER: W) Simpson’s rule
4. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the highest degree polynomial for which a 15-point Gaussian quadrature rule is exact?
ANSWER: 29

W) 1050
X) 740
Y) 385
Z) 145
ANSWER: Z) 145
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice At around wavelength, in nanometers, is the Lyman Beta spectral line for hydrogen?
W) 10,000
X) 1000
Y) 100
Z) 10
ANSWER: Y) 100
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory are using the machine known as DUNE to learn more about how supernovae occur by studying and observing which of the following particles?
W) Barions
X) Fermions
Y) Mesons
Z) Neutrinos
ANSWER: Z) Neutrinos
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists from Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory were stationed in Antarctica to conduct the IceCube experiment at the IceCube Observatory. While in Antarctica, these scientists discovered ultra high-energy neutrinos. Where are these high-energy neutrinos believed to have come from?
ANSWER: Outside our solar system
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How do the ascending and descending tracts of the nervous system differ?
W) Ascending tracts carry sensory information to the brain, and descending tracts carry motor commands from the brain.
X) Both ascending and descending tracts carry sensory information to the brain.
Y) Ascending tracts carry sensory information from the brain, and descending tracts carry sensory information to the brain.
Z) Both ascending and descending tracts carry motor information from the brain.
ANSWER: W) Ascending tracts carry sensory information to the brain, and descending tracts carry motor commands from the brain.
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the region that the spinal cord spans?
W) Brainstem to lower thoracic region
X) Brainstem to upper lumbar region
Y) Brainstem to lower lumbar region
Z) Brainstem to sacral region
ANSWER: X) Brainstem to upper lumbar region
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A carboxylic acid can be formed from the oxidation of an aldehyde in what reaction, first discovered in 1899 that often uses mCPBA as a reagent?
ANSWER: Baeyer-Villiger Oxidation
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Answer the following questions about organic chemistry. 1) What is the term for a cyclic ester formed by the Baeyer-Villiger oxidation of a cyclic ketone? 2) What order of carbocation has the highest migratory aptitude? 3) The Baeyer-Villiger oxidation of ketones forms what functional group?
ANSWER: 1) Lactone; 2) Tertiary; 3) Ester

W) Galvani potential
X) Fermi level
Y) Volta potential
Z) Kelvin force
ANSWER: Y) Volta potential

10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the term for a Taylor series where zero is the point where the derivatives are considered?
ANSWER: MACLAURIN SERIES
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Pangaea split up along the equator into which two landmasses?
ANSWER: LAURASIA AND GONDWANA
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The oxygenation of the atmosphere led oxygen to react with iron in the ocean, which came out of solution and built up on the ocean floor, creating what features?
ANSWER: BANDED IRON FORMATIONS
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Brookhaven National Lab are searching for a hypothetical type of neutrino that can only interact with other particles through gravity. What is this neutrino called?
ANSWER: Sterile Neutrino (also accept: Inert Neutrino)
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Brookhaven National Laboratory are looking for a particle that mixes with the three known neutrino types to prove the existence of the sterile neutrino. What are these three known neutrino types?
ANSWER: Muon, Tau, and Electron
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Provide the name for a fibrous sheet of connective tissue that fills the gap between two bones.
ANSWER: Interosseous membrane
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Interosseous membranes fill the wide gap between two bones. For example, there is an interosseous membrane that connects the radius and ulna in the forearm. Interosseous membranes are an example of what broader type of joint?
W) Gomphosis
X) Synchondrosis
Y) Syndesmosis
Z) Symphysis
ANSWER: Y) Syndesmosis
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What are the three basic types of pericyclic reactions?
ANSWER: CYCLOADDITION REACTIONS, ELECTROCYCLIC REACTIONS, AND SIGMATROPIC REARRANGEMENTS (also accept: CYCLOADDITION, ELECTROCYCLIC, AND SIGMATROPIC)
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A) What type of cycloaddition is a Diels-Alder reaction? B) How many pi bonds are formed in a sigmatropic rearrangement? C) What is the name of the [3,3] sigmatropic rearrangement often found in aryl, allylic ethers?
ANSWER: A) [4+2]; B) 0; C) CLAISEN REARRANGEMENT
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The K α x-rays arising from a cobalt target have a wavelength of about 179 pm. Is the wavelength of the K α x-rays arising from a nickel target greater or less than 179 pm?
ANSWER: LESS
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer An electron is in a quantum state for which the magnitude of the electron’s orbital angular momentum L is (2√3) * ħ. How many projections of the electron’s orbital magnetic dipole moment on a z axis are allowed?
ANSWER: 7
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What theorem in vector calculus theorem relates the integral of the curl of the vector field over some surface, to the line integral of the vector field around the boundary of the surface?
ANSWER: KELVIN-STOKES THEOREM (also accept: CURL THEOREM)
