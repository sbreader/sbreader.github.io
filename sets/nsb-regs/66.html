12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Different species of hummingbirds have bills that vary in length and shape, matching the structure of the flowers on which they feed. Which of the following evolutionary scenarios best explains this diversity of hummingbird bill morphology?
W) Coevolution of flowers and hummingbirds, leading to specialization of hummingbirds on particular flowers
X) Random mutation of plants to develop varying flower shapes, and subsequent evolution of hummingbird bills to match these shapes
