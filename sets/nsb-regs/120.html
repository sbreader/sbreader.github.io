1. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the system of linear equations: y – 2x = -2, y + x = 4?

X) Consistent, Independent
Y) Inconsistent, Dependent
Z) Inconsistent, Independent
ANSWER: X) Consistent, Independent
1. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the largest possible sum of the digits on a 12-hour digital clock that displays the hours and minutes?
ANSWER: 23

X) Autoclave

2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In dragons, the alleles for yellow flame color and blue flame color are incompletely dominant. If a homozygous yellow-flame dragon mates with a homozygous blue-flame dragon, all of the brood will have green flame. Which of the following choices represents an expected probability for a brood produced by two green-flame dragons?
W) 75% green flame
X) 75% blue flame
Y) 25% yellow flame
Z) 25% green flame
ANSWER: Y) 25% yellow flame

4. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true regarding a system undergoing rotation?
W) Torque is the physical quantity describing a force causing rotation about an axis
X) Moment of inertia describes the tendency of an object to resist changes in angular momentum

Z) Conservation of angular momentum explains why pulsars will never stop spinning

16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A reaction is dependent upon the concentrations of L, M, and N. Provide the reaction orders for L, M, and N, as well as the overall order for the reaction.
ANSWER: L: 2; M: 0; N: 3 Overall Order: 5
