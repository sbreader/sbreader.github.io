1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which of the following four structures are parts of the female reproductive organ in flowers? 1) Anther 2) Stigma 3) Filament 4) Style
ANSWER: 2, 4
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer The ratio of which of the following four plant hormones affects seed germination? 1) Abscisic acid 2) Auxins 3) Ethylene 4) Gibberellins
ANSWER: 1, 4
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following acids is not considered strong?
W) Hydroiodic acid
X) Hydrobromic acid
Y) Hydrochloric acid
Z) Hydrofluoric acid
ANSWER: Z) Hydrofluoric acid
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Kiran is late for his chemistry conference in which he must show off his model of an atom. Being in a rush, he just shoves all 6 of his valence electrons in the s-orbital and the 2 lowest p-orbitals. This violates which law, which says that electrons will always fill each orbital first before doubling up?
W) Aufbau principle
X) Pauli exclusion principle
Y) Hund’s rule
Z) Heisenberg uncertainty principle
ANSWER: Y) Hund’s rule
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice When exposed to a magnetic field, a material is weakly repelled. Which of the following best describes this material?
W) Ferromagnetic
X) Diamagnetic
Y) Paramagnetic
Z) Not magnetic
ANSWER: X) Diamagnetic
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following are used to transfer power across vast distances efficiently, allowing electricity to go from a power plant to homes?
W) Generators
X) Motors
Y) Transformers
Z) Switches
ANSWER: Y) Transformers
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the process by which sediment is converted into sedimentary rock?
ANSWER: Lithification (do not accept: compaction; cementation)
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following atmosphere layers do comets burn up in?
W) Troposphere
X) Stratosphere
Y) Mesosphere
Z) Thermosphere
ANSWER: Y) Mesosphere
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A point (2, 3) is first reflected across the line y = x, then reflected across the y-axis, then moved two units to the left. What is the final position of this point?
ANSWER: (-5, 2)
5. BONUS Math Short Answer By name or number, identify which of the following 4 lines lies have at least some part of it inside a circle? 1. Diameter 2. Chord 3. Tangent 4. Secant
ANSWER: 1, 2, 4
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of viruses?
W) Viruses can live on their own
X) Many viruses have circular chromosomes
Y) Viruses are enclosed by a protein coat
Z) Viruses possess the ability to evolve quickly
ANSWER: W) Viruses can live on their own
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the TRP operon?
W) Always activated
X) Always repressed
Y) Inducible
Z) Repressible
ANSWER: Z) Repressible
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the endpoint on the Pressure- Temperature curve that marks the conditions for liquids and gases to coexist?
ANSWER: Critical point
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice On the NFPA 704 Safety diamond, which describes various hazards associated with working with particular chemicals, what does the yellow square correspond to?
W) Safety
X) Reactivity
Y) Flammability
Z) Specific Hazards
ANSWER: X) Reactivity
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which effect says that the observed frequency of a sound wave changes based on the wave source's relative velocity to the speaker?
ANSWER: Doppler Effect
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer Which trigonometric function is present in Snell's Law?
ANSWER: Sine
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which rocky planet exhibits retrograde rotation?
ANSWER: Venus
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a unit of distance?
W) Light-year
X) Astronomical Unit
Y) Parsecs
Z) Arcminute
ANSWER: Z) Arcminute
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In a group of 10 people, 8 play Clash Royale and 6 play Brawl Stars. Given that these two are independent, if a random person chosen plays Clash Royale, what is the probability that they don't play Brawl Stars?
ANSWER: 0.4 (also accept: ⅖, 4/10)
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Ann has 720 credits that she could use to spend on League of Legends skins. She wants 10 skins, with a legendary skin costing 100 credits while an epic skin costs 60 credits. If she spends all her credits, how many epic skins will she have?
ANSWER: 7
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following hormones is secreted by the pituitary gland and aids in the development of the reproductive system?
W) Adrenaline
X) Follicule stimulating hormone
Y) Serotonin
Z) Insulin
ANSWER: X) Follicule stimulating hormone
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Lucas is attending a Teen Talk session, where the teacher introduces the concept of empathy. According to the teacher, engaging with empathy will help make Lucas feel good. This could NOT be due to the release of which of the following hormones?
W) Endorphins
X) Norepinephrine
Y) Oxytocin
Z) Dopamine
ANSWER: X) Norepinephrine

ANSWER: 4, 2, 3, 1

W) 0.0003 moles
X) 0.003 moles
Y) 0.03 moles
Z) 0.3 moles
ANSWER: X) 0.003 moles
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the term for a thermodynamic process in which no heat flows in or out of the system?
ANSWER: Adiabatic

ANSWER: 4, 1, 3, 2

However, Naman has run into a plant that he does not know the genotype of. In order to determine the genotype of his unknown plant, he decides to breed it with a homozygous recessive plant. What is the term for this type of genetic breeding experiment?
ANSWER: Test cross

ANSWER: 2 only
