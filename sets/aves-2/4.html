1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following levels of protein structure would least likely be affected by extreme pH or temperature conditions?
W) Primary
X) Secondary
Y) Tertiary
Z) Quaternary
ANSWER: W) Primary
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Starting with the puncturing of skin by a splinter, order the following three responses from first to last to occur 1) Secretion of histamine by mast cells 2) Migration of neutrophils 3) Dilation of blood vessels
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the ratio of the surface area of a hemisphere of radius R to the surface area of a hemisphere with radius 2R?
ANSWER: 1/4
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Calculate the area of a triangle with side lengths 4, 5, and 7.
ANSWER: 4√6
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In order to produce nitric acid in the Ostwald process, a large volume of ammonia is required as a precursor. What industrial process is commonly used to produce this ammonia, using nitrogen and hydrogen gas?
ANSWER: Haber-Bosch process (also accept: Haber process)
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three elements by increasing wavelength of the color from their flame test 1) Potassium 2) Copper 3) Calcium
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Advai is observing stars in the sky while standing on Earth's surface. What star has the lowest apparent magnitude?
ANSWER: Sun
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Iris Nebula is characterized by bright blue clouds. Which of the following types of nebula is it?
W) Emission
X) Absorption
Y) Reflection
Z) Planetary
ANSWER: Y) Reflection
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A magnetic field in a region is perpendicular to the electric field in the same region. If a proton and electron are both placed at rest inside the region, what is the relation between the directions of their respective electromagnetic force vectors?
W) Same directions
X) Opposite directions
Y) The proton and electron do not move
Z) Not enough information
ANSWER: X) Opposite directions
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three circuit components that store energy 1) Resistor 2) Capacitor 3) Inductor
ANSWER: 2 and 3
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following processes would not require ATP hydrolysis?
W) Dynein motor protein movement along microtubules
X) Transport of H+ ions by complex III in the mitochondria
Y) Phosphorylation of glucose into glucose 6-phosphate
Z) Sodium-potassium pump conformational change
ANSWER: X) Transport of H+ ions by complex III in the mitochondria
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following three organisms by increasing density of genes per megabase pair in their genomes 1) E. coli 2) Homo sapiens 3) S. cerevisiae
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Points A and B satisfy AB = 6. How many points C in the plane exist such that AC = 4 and BC = 2?
ANSWER: 1
7. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the ratio of the sum of the first 10 even positive integers to the sum of the first 10 odd positive integers?
ANSWER: 11/10
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice When comparing rubidium chloride with barium fluoride, which of the following characteristics is higher for barium fluoride than rubidium chloride?
W) Lattice energy
X) Ionic character
Y) Solubility in water
Z) Melting point
ANSWER: W) Lattice energy
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The absorption of light and excitation of electrons in long conjugated pi bond systems is well approximated by what quantum mechanical model, which considers electrons inside a finite well bounded by infinite potential energy?
ANSWER: Particle in a box (also accept: Infinite square/potential well)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three stages of thunderstorm development in which downdrafts are the dominant force 1) Developing 2) Mature 3) Dissipating
ANSWER: 3 only
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following exoplanet detection techniques would be the least useful in detecting hot Jupiters?
W) Radial velocity
X) Transit method
Y) Infrared direct imaging
Z) Gravitational microlensing
ANSWER: Z) Gravitational microlensing
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer To allow the proton-proton chain to occur, stars would need to reach billions of kelvins for protons to overcome the energy barrier. Yet, our sun only reaches 15 million kelvin. What phenomenon allows protons to collide despite not having enough kinetic energy to overcome Coulombic repulsion?
ANSWER: Quantum tunneling
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer Yunyi has a spring of length L and an attached block of mass M. It oscillates with a period of 10 seconds. If he cuts the spring into two identical pieces and attaches them in parallel to the mass, what is the new period of the system, to the nearest second?
ANSWER: 5
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What repetitive DNA sequences protect chromosome ends from degradation and from being mistaken as damaged DNA by repair enzymes?
ANSWER: Telomeres
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following muscle types is not directly innervated by the sympathetic nervous system?
W) Smooth muscle
X) Skeletal muscle
Y) Cardiac muscle
Z) Enteric muscle
ANSWER: X) Skeletal muscle
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Identify all of the following three sets of numbers which have no bijection with the natural numbers 1) Integers 2) Rational numbers 3) Irrational numbers
ANSWER: 3 only
12. BONUS Math Short Answer The point (-2, 4) is rotated 90 degrees clockwise about the point (2, 1) and then reflected across the x-axis. What are the coordinates of the final position of the point?
ANSWER: (5, -5)
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following organic compounds is not a hydrocarbon?
W) Butane
X) Butene
Y) Butyne
Z) Butanal
ANSWER: Z) Butanal
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three molecules by increasing number of major resonance structures 1) NO3- 2) CO2 3) SO42-
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the border between the zone of accumulation and zone of ablation, where accretion and wasting of glacial ice are equal?
ANSWER: Equilibrium line (also accept: Snow line)
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three boundaries in the solar system by increasing distance from the Sun 1) Heliopause 2) Sun's Roche limit 3) Frost line
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Forrest weighs 1000 Newtons. If he jumps onto a bridge that can sustain 1100 Newtons of weight, the bridge may break because of which of the following quantities that is most directly related to the force exerted onto the bridge?
W) Impulse
X) Energy
Y) Velocity
Z) Displacement
ANSWER: W) Impulse
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 10 meter bar is traveling at 0.6c in the direction of the bar relative to Ethan. To one significant figure, how long is the bar in his reference frame?
ANSWER: 8
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When plants adapted to terrestrial life, their leaves evolved to have what waxy layer, coating the epidermis, which prevented excess water loss?
ANSWER: Cuticle
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three cell types that can experience plasmolysis 1) Fungal cell 2) Plant cell 3) Animal cell
ANSWER: 1 and 2
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A polygon drawn such that each of its vertices lies on a lattice point in the plane has 14 lattice points on its boundary and 10 lattice points in its interior. What is the area of this polygon?
ANSWER: 16
17. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the value of x that satisfies the Mean Value Theorem of Derivatives of y = 3x2 + 1 from 0 to 6?
ANSWER: 3
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements is the most suitable dopant for producing p-type silicon semiconductors?
W) Phosphorus
X) Boron
Y) Antimony
Z) Arsenic
ANSWER: X) Boron
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Given that the acid dissociation constants of H3PO4 are pKa1 = 2.15, pKa2 = 7.20, and pKa3 = 12.32, then to two significant figures, what is the pH of a 0.5 M KH2PO4 solution?
ANSWER: 4.7
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In the tropics, warm clouds below the freezing point temperature produce precipitation through what process, involving the development of large liquid droplets that break apart into smaller ones?
ANSWER: Collision-coalescence (do not accept: Bergeron)
19. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice As the temperature of an ideal blackbody increases, how does its radiance and wavelength change, respectively?
W) Increases, increases
X) Increases, decreases
Y) Decreases, increases
Z) Decreases, decreases
ANSWER: X) Increases, decreases
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Dea places an electric field source within a sphere. What law states that the total enclosed charge within the surface is proportional to the total flux that passes through that same surface?
ANSWER: Gauss's Law
20. BONUS Physics Short Answer A stream of 1 kilogram beads moving at 10 meters per second collides elastically with a stationary force sensor such that a bead collides with the sensor every 7 seconds. In newtons, what is the time-averaged force experienced by the force sensor?
ANSWER: 20/7
