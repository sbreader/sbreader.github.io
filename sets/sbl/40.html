1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the divergence of the vector field f(x, y, z) = xy i + yz j + xz k?
ANSWER: x + y + z
1. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the largest positive integer x such that x2 + 7 x + 41 is a perfect square?
ANSWER: 25
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following igneous rocks is not correctly paired with its composition?
W) Granite, felsic
X) Andesite, intermediate
Y) Rhyolite, mafic
Z) Komatiite, ultramafic
ANSWER: Y) Rhyolite, mafic
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What mechanism, characterized by the anomalous relation between temperature and opacity in some stars, drives brightness variation in pulsating variables?
ANSWER: KAPPA MECHANISM (also accept: EDDINGTON VALVE)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer To two significant figures, and in scientific notation, what is the De Broglie wavelength of an object with a mass of 3 kg moving at a velocity of 4 m/s.
ANSWER: 5.5 × 10-35 m
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A particle with its spin in the same direction as its motion is said to be which of the following?
W) spin-up
X) spin-down
Y) left-handed
Z) right-handed
ANSWER: Z) right-handed

ANSWER: F IBRONECTIN
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What term is given to animals which develop embryo within the body of a parent?
ANSWER: VIVIPARITY
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the value of the radial distribution function of a 1s orbital at r = 0?
ANSWER: 0
5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Paints exhibit what property, where a fluid’s viscosity decreases when a stress is applied, then returns to a more viscous state after some time?
W) Thixotropy
X) Anisotropy
Y) Rheopexy
Z) Isotropy
ANSWER: W) Thixotropy
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Brookhaven National Lab have begun building a quantum-enhanced x-ray microscope. The microscope will split an x-ray beam into two different streams, with one stream hitting a molecular sample and the other hitting a detector. What quantum mechanical phenomenon will the researchers use to figure out the structure of the sample?
ANSWER: QUANTUM ENTANGLEMENT
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest national lab are studying speciation. What type of speciation occurs when two geographically adjacent populations of a species develop reproductive isolation from each other?
ANSWER: PARAPATRIC
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Elevated levels of glucagon normally stimulate which of the following biological pathways?
W) Glycolysis
X) Glycogenesis
Y) Pentose Phosphate Pathway
Z) Glycogenolysis
ANSWER: Z) Glycogenolysis

ANSWER: 2 ONLY
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the most massive galaxy in the Local Group?
ANSWER: ANDROMEDA GALAXY
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Degeneracy pressure, unlike regular gas pressure, does not have a temperature-dependent term. Instead, degeneracy pressure is proportional to the five-thirds power of what physical parameter of the gas?
ANSWER: DENSITY

ANSWER: SILVER

ANSWER: 79

ANSWER: 1/8
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab have been studying the methyl transferases for the post translational modification of other proteins. Which of the following metabolites is a major donor of methyl groups?
W) Porphyrin
X) Glutathione
Y) SAM
Z) Melanin
ANSWER: Y) SAM
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Brookhaven National Lab are studying the crystal structure of the CFTR inhibitory factor. Identify all of the following that are true about the CFTR protein and gene 1) In patients with cystic fibrosis, the CFTR protein does not allow the movement of chloride ions 2) In patients with cholera, the CFTR protein transports excessive amount of chloride ions 3) The CFTR gene is encoded in mitochondrial DNA.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2

W) Capacitor
X) Inductor
Y) Resistor
Z) Rectifier

12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two identical blocks, A and B, each have an edge length of 8 cm and a density of 100 kg/m3. Block A is placed in a container with water of density 1000 kg/m3, while Block B is placed in an identical container with ethanol of density 800 kg/m3. In cubic cm, how much more of Block B is submerged in the liquid than Block A?
ANSWER: 12.8 (also accept: 64/5)

14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What term describes the amount of air that moves in and out of the lungs during a normal breath?
ANSWER: TIDAL VOLUME

ANSWER: 5
15. BONUS Math Short Answer Alice is standing on the x-axis at x = 1. Every second, she flips a coin; if it’s heads, she moves one unit to the right. Otherwise, she moves one unit to the left. What is the probability that she first reaches x = 3 before reaching x = 0?
ANSWER: 1/3
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The catastrophic collision hypothesis suggests that a large celestial body may have collided with Earth to create the Moon. Which of the following is not evidence in support of this hypothesis?
W) Lunar rocks have an average density similar to that of the Earth’s mantle and crust
X) Lunar rocks have composition similar to that of Earth’s mantle
Y) The Moon has a higher density than Earth
Z) Lunar rocks have revealed that the lunar surface was once molten
ANSWER: Y) The Moon has a higher density than Earth
16. VISUAL BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Shown in the image is a color-magnitude diagram of Messier object 5, with individual stars plotted as dots. Answer the following three questions regarding this image 1) What type of variable star is represented by the population at letter E? 2) What stellar evolutionary stage is represented by stars near letter D? 3) Is M5 an open cluster, a globular cluster, or a galaxy?
ANSWER: 1) RR LYRAE; 2) HORIZONTAL BRANCH; 3) GLOBULAR CLUSTER
