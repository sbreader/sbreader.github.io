6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding the combination of 2 springs with equal positive spring constants is correct.
W) The equivalent spring constant for springs in series is always greater than that of springs in parallel.
X) The equivalent spring constant for springs in parallel is always greater than that of springs in series.
Y) The equivalent spring constant for springs in parallel is always equal to the spring constant for springs in series squared.
