Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the class of steroid hormones responsible for increased lipolysis and beta-oxidation in response to stress?
ANSWER: glucocorticoids
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer EVIL King Henry really wants to have a boy. He will have five children, each with an equal chance of being a boy or a girl. What are the chances he will have exactly one boy?
ANSWER: 5/32
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many lone pairs of electrons are on the Carbon atom in CO2?
ANSWER: 0
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following reactions is Endothermic?
W) CH4 (g) + 2O2 (g) → CO2 (g) + 2H2O (l)
X) 4Al (s) + 3O2 (g) → 2Al2O3 (s)
Y) N2 (g) + 3H2 (g) → 2NH3 (g)
Z) N2 (g) + O2 (g) → 2NO (g)
ANSWER: Z) N2 (g) + O2 (g) → 2NO (g)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A simple pendulum has a length of 2 meters. Approximating g=10 m/s2, what is its period in seconds?
ANSWER: 2.8s (also accept: 2.81s)
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Light travels from water to air and strikes the boundary at an angle larger than a certain critical angle, causing the light to reflect entirely back into the water. What is the term used to describe this phenomenon?
ANSWER: Total internal reflection (do not accept: Reflection)
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Given a closed region with perimeter 12, compute the maximum area this region can have.
ANSWER: 36/π
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Name all of the following three values of r that would cause rn to diverge as n approaches infinity. 1. r = -⅔ 2. r = ⅘ + (⅕ * i) 3. r = 6/7-(4/7 * i)
ANSWER: 3 ONLY
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What process is responsible for the color of the sky?.
ANSWER: Rayleigh scattering
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What kind of scattering is responsible for the white appearance of clouds?
ANSWER: Mie scattering (also accept: non-molecular scattering; aerosol particle scattering)
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following nervous system networks is most closely connected to the functions of the brainstem?
W) limbic system
X) somatic system
Y) autonomic system
Z) somatosensory system
ANSWER: Y) autonomic system
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The somatosensory cortex is involved in proprioception and the processing of sensory input. In which of the following lobes of the brain is the somatosensory cortex found?
W) frontal
X) parietal
Y) temporal
Z) occipital
ANSWER: X) parietal
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the hybridization of the Xenon atom in Xenon Tetrafluoride?
ANSWER: sp3d2
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 5 organic molecules that are considered isomers of 1-butanol. 1) 2-butanol 2) 3-butanol 3) 2 -methyl 1-propanol 4) 2-methyl 2-propanol 5) Pentane
ANSWER: 1, 3, and 4 (2-butanol, 2-methyl 1-propanol, and 2-methyl 2-propanol)
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the units of electrical potential?
W) Joules per coulomb
X) Newton-meters
Y) Newtons per coulomb
Z) Joules per second
ANSWER: W) Joules per coulomb
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer By name or number, order the following four types of electromagnetic radiation in order of INCREASING frequency 1. Visible light 2. Microwaves 3. X-rays 4. Ultraviolet radiation
ANSWER: 2, 1, 4, 3 (also accept: Microwaves, Visible light, Ultraviolet radiation, X-rays)
9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Right triangle ABC has a right angle at C, c = 3, and a + b = √17. Compute the area of the triangle.
W) √2
X) √3
Y) 2
Z) √5
ANSWER: Y) 2
9. BONUS Math Short Answer Stephen has four integers. When he adds them three at a time, the sums are 46, 50, 54 and 57. Compute the largest of the original four integers.
ANSWER: 23
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Two stars are in a circular orbit 2 astronomical units apart from each other. Star A has a mass of 0.3 solar masses, while star B has a mass of 1.7 solar masses. Giving your answer as a whole number, what is the period of the stars in terms of Earth years?
ANSWER: 2 years
10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice NASA’s Spitzer, Chandra, and James Webb telescopes each take a photo of the same object in their own optimized wavelengths. The object is most visible in Chandra’s photograph. Of the following, the object is most likely to be a:
W) Exoplanet
X) Type II supernova
Y) Planetary nebula
Z) Asteroid
ANSWER: X) Type II supernova
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The aorta pumps blood from what compartment of the heart?
ANSWER: LEFT VENTRICLE
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following parts of the heart’s conduction pathway is found within the interventricular septum?
W) Sinoatrial node
X) Bundle of His
Y) Atrioventricular node
Z) Purkinje fibers
ANSWER: X) Bundle of His
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules is INCORRECTLY matched with its molecular geometry?
W) CO2, linear
X) CH4, tetrahedral
Y) H2S, linear
Z) NH3, trigonal pyramidal
ANSWER: Y) H2S, linear
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following four properties that are considered physical properties. 1. Color 2. Taste 3. Reactivity 4. Flammability
ANSWER: 1 and 2 (Color and Taste)
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer By name or number, identify ALL of the following three statements that are true 1. A concave mirror can form both real and virtual images 2. Terminal velocity depends on mass 3. The speed of sound in air increases with temperature
ANSWER: 1, 2, and 3 (also accept: ALL)
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer A paper bag hangs from a string attached to the ceiling. A constant horizontal wind pushes the bag so that the string makes a 45° angle with the vertical. If the weight of the bag is 10 newtons, what is the magnitude of the horizontal force from the wind on the bag in Newtons?
ANSWER: 10 Newtons
14. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice There are 4 Identical Graph Theory, 3 different Number Theory, and 2 different Geometry books on a shelf. If all the books on the same subject have to be next to each other, which of the following is the number of different ways the books can be arranged on the shelf?
W) 6
X) 36
Y) 72
Z) 1728
ANSWER: Y) 72
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Emma the playground bandit is planning on stealing candy from 10 children on a bench. To avoid getting caught, she plans on stealing from 4 children, none of whom are next to each other. In how many ways can she do this?
ANSWER: 35
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following graphs would best model a Cepheid Variable star's luminosity through the course of a year?
W) Constant
X) Sine
Y) Linear
Z) Exponential
ANSWER: X) Sine
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the process in which ionized helium increases in opacity as it heats up, driving cyclic changes in the luminosity of Cepheid Variables?
ANSWER: Kappa Mechanism (also accept: KAPPA OPACITY MECHANISM; EDDINGTON VALVE)
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Sebastian has the plague. The bacterium that causes the plague is taken up by a macrophage, which presents the bacterium’s epitope to a certain type of cell. To which of the following types of lymphocytes could the macrophage have presented the epitope?
W) CD8 T cell
X) CD4 T cell
Y) plasma cell
Z) memory B cell
ANSWER: X) CD4 T cell
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer After the CD4 T cell is activated, it activates a B cell with the same epitope. The B cell proliferates into plasma cells, which create antibodies. What is the term for the process these antibodies will undergo, during which they will bind to antigens on the surface of the bacterium and promote phagocytosis?
ANSWER: opsonization (also accept: antibody opsonization; antibody-mediated opsonization)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules contains only one nonbonding pair of valence electrons?
W) HCN
X) CH4
Y) C6H12O6
Z) O2
ANSWER: W) HCN
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer State the full, balanced chemical reaction with reactants Magnesium Chloride and Iron (III) Phosphate.
ANSWER: 3MgCl2 + 2FePO4 → Mg3(PO4)2 + 2FeCl3
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What point, named after its 19th century discoverer, indicates the temperature at which ferromagnets lose their magnetic properties?
ANSWER: CURIE POINT (also accept: CURIE TEMPERATURE; TC)
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer Sous vide is a cooking technique where food is sealed in a bag and cooked in temperature-controlled water. A 100-gram aluminum steak pouch is placed in 70°C water after being at 20°C room temperature. The aluminum’s specific heat capacity is 900J/kg°C. Giving your answer in Joules, calculate how much heat energy is absorbed by the pouch.
ANSWER: 4500 Joules
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Order the following numbers from least to most factors. 1.111 2.112 3.113 4.114
ANSWER: 3, 1, 4, 2 (113, 111, 114, 112)
19. BONUS Math Short Answer Mary has a little lamb and a large rectangular barn. The barn is 20 meters long and 10 meters wide. She ties the lamb to one of the barn's corners with a rope that is 12 meters long. What is the total area, in square meters, that Mary’s lamb can graze?
ANSWER: 109π

21. BONUS Biology Short Answer Asher goes to the doctor and is found to display unusual expression of human leukocyte antigen II – the human version of major histocompatibility complex II – on his thyrocytes. These complexes, not normally found on these cells and induced by cytokines, present autoantigens to helper T-cells and cause Asher to have hypothyroidism. What is the term used to describe the disease Asher has?
ANSWER: HASHIMOTO’S DISEASE (also accept: Hashimoto’s thyroiditis; chronic lymphocytic thyroiditis; autoimmune thyroiditis; struma lymphomatosa)
22. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly describes the motion of a mass on a spring undergoing simple harmonic oscillation?
W) The acceleration is maximum when the velocity is maximum
X) The velocity is zero when the acceleration is maximum
Y) The acceleration is zero at the maximum displacement
Z) The velocity is proportional to displacement and opposite in direction
ANSWER: X) The velocity is zero when the acceleration is maximum
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer Asher drops an apple from the top of a mast on a boat moving at constant velocity. To Asher, the apple falls straight down. To an observer on the shore, what path does the apple follow?
ANSWER: Parabolic path (also accept: Parabolic)
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer At the bank, Al exchanged a $10 bill for an equal number of nickels, dimes, and quarters. How many of each type of coin did Al receive?
ANSWER: 25
23. BONUS Math Short Answer Emma the playground bandit wants to steal candy from the 10 children sitting on a bench. This time, she can steal from any number of children she wants, but she still can't steal from any two children sitting next to each other. In how many different ways can she do this?
ANSWER: 144
