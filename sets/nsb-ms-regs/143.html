1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A kilocalorie equals 4,184 of what SI derived unit for energy?
ANSWER: JOULE
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three reactions that are exothermic 1) Burning gasoline 2) Water vapor condensing 3) Melting ice.
ANSWER: 1, 2
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the log base 5 of 1/25?
ANSWER: –2
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Solve the following equation for x over the negative integers: x4 – 28x2 + 75 = 0
ANSWER: –5
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying how plants respond to extreme weather events, like heat waves. In times of high ambient temperature, plants will close stomata to conserve what?
ANSWER: WATER (also accept: H2O)
3. BONUS Energy Short Answer Scientists at Idaho National Lab are developing new methods to quickly charge electric vehicles. What type of battery, the most common used in modern electric vehicles, is being charged?
ANSWER: LITHIUM-ION (also accept: LI-ION, LIB)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following will be the next stage in the evolution of the Sun?
W) Supernova
X) Red giant
Y) Stellar nebula
Z) White dwarf
ANSWER: X) Red giant
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice An astronomer is observing a gas cloud and notes several infrared spectral lines. These spectral lines could allow the astronomer to infer what?
W) The elemental composition of the cloud
X) The chemical bonds present in the cloud
Y) The rate of collision of the molecules in the cloud
Z) The ionization state of the atoms in the cloud
ANSWER: X) The chemical bonds present in the cloud
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the adjective for the type of interference that occurs when two waves cancel each other out?
ANSWER: DESTRUCTIVE
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of the astronauts aboard the International Space Station 1) They are massless 2) They are in free-fall 3) They experience Earth's gravity.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following animals has been observed to communicate via a dance language, including maneuvers such as the waggle dance?
W) Chimpanzee
X) Peacock
Y) Honeybee
Z) Hyena
ANSWER: Y) Honeybee
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a major function of the large intestine?
W) Mechanically breaking down food
X) Chemically breaking down food
Y) Absorbing nutrients
Z) Absorbing water
ANSWER: Z) Absorbing water
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many integers are there from –8 to 5, inclusive?
ANSWER: 14
7. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area of a rhombus with diagonals of length 24 and 28?
ANSWER: 336
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A stroke is most commonly caused when blood flow is cut off in what organ in the body?
ANSWER: BRAIN
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice To what phylum does baker's yeast belong?
W) Ascomycota
X) Basidiomycota
Y) Zygomycota
Z) Glomeromycota
ANSWER: W) Ascomycota
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Eurasian plate and the Indian plate meet at what type of plate boundary?
W) Divergent
X) Ocean-ocean convergent
Y) Ocean-continent convergent
Z) Continent-continent convergent
ANSWER: Z) Continent-continent convergent
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three quantities that typically decrease in a stream from headwaters to mouth 1) Water velocity 2) Water discharge 3) Stream channel width.
ANSWER: NONE OF THEM
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is 40% of 50 plus 40% of 40?
ANSWER: 36
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the surface area of a regular square pyramid with slant height 4 whose base has sides of length 7?
ANSWER: 105
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the distance, in meters, between the electron and proton in the neutral hydrogen atom?
W) 10-16
X) 10-13
Y) 10-10
Z) 10-7
ANSWER: Y) 10-10
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer You take an object and reduce its absolute temperature in half. To the nearest degree Celsius, what temperature will the object approach if you repeatedly perform this procedure?
ANSWER: –273
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at the DOE Ames Research Laboratory have been using advanced TEM to map nanocrystal magnetic fields formed in bacteria in liquids. What mineral is present in these bacteria?
W) Gypsum
X) Magnetite
Y) Calcite
Z) Mica
ANSWER: X) Magnetite
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are using new methods to study proteins that are soluble in water but cannot be crystallized. All such flexible proteins fall into what larger class of proteins that often have spherical shapes and usually have functional roles within the cell?
ANSWER: GLOBULAR PROTEINS (also accept: SPHEROPROTEINS)
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If the height of a square pyramid is multiplied by 9, by what factor must the area of the base be multiplied so that the volume remains constant?
ANSWER: 1/9
13. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the units digit of 8 to the 2019th power?
ANSWER: 2
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Colonization of plant roots by a fungus can allow increased capture of nutrients from the soil. What is the name for this symbiotic structure?
ANSWER: MYCORRHIZA (MYCORRHIZAE)
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer In a dicot leaf, what is the layer of tissue responsible for most photosynthesis and carbon dioxide absorption?
ANSWER: PALISADE MESOPHYLL
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following pieces of glassware would be LEAST useful for measuring a given volume of liquid?
W) Test tube
X) Graduated cylinder
Y) Erlenmeyer flask
Z) Burette
ANSWER: W) Test tube
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer An ice cube has a density of 0.92 grams per milliliter and a volume of 400 cubic centimeters. If the ice cube is floating in water, then, in cubic centimeters, what volume of the cube will be sticking out of the water?
ANSWER: 32
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following locations would you study if you wanted to collect a sample of atmospheric carbon dioxide from one thousand years ago?
W) Sea floor sediment
X) Glacier ice
Y) Sand dunes
Z) Ocean water
ANSWER: X) Glacier ice
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following three sediment grain sizes in order of increasing porosity 1) Clay 2) Sand 3) Gravel.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
17. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory are using new imaging modalities to study DNA damage. In particular, they are studying damage to DNA caused by what region of the EM spectrum that is divided into A, B, and C ranges?
W) Infrared
X) Visible
Y) Ultraviolet
Z) X-ray
ANSWER: Y) Ultraviolet
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab used new proteomics methods to study blood markers in patients with cirrhosis. Cirrhosis is a disease in which scar tissue is laid down in what human organ?
ANSWER: LIVER
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer During muscular contraction, myosin is cross-linked to what other protein in order to shorten the muscle filament?
ANSWER: ACTIN

W) Fishes
X) Amphibians
Y) Reptiles
Z) Birds

19. BONUS Math Short Answer If an arithmetic sequence has third term 16 and seventh term 44, what is its 19th term?
ANSWER: 128
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Cassini division is associated with what planet?
ANSWER: SATURN

W) 10,000
X) 100,000
Y) 1 million
Z) 10 million
ANSWER: Z) 10 million

ANSWER: BLOOD

ANSWER: 1, 3
