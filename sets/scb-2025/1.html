1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The selective pressure on birth weight in humans acts against extreme phenotypes (low and high birthweights) because intermediate birth weights have a higher reproductive fitness. Which of the following types of selection does human birth weight exemplify?
W) Disruptive
X) Stabilizing
Y) Balancing
Z) Directional
ANSWER: X) Stabilizing
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three conditions are assumptions made for Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium 1) Negligible genetic drift 2) Random mating 3) Equal sex ratio
ANSWER: 1,2
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A characteristic purple color for a flame test indicates which of the following elements?
W) Barium
X) Potassium
Y) Strontium
Z) Sodium
ANSWER: X) Potassium
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer For the lewis structure of the carbonate ion, CO32- how many lone pairs of electrons are present on the central atom?
ANSWER: 0
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes gabbro?
W) Extrusive, felsic
X) Extrusive, mafic
Y) Intrusive, felsic
Z) Intrusive, mafic
ANSWER: Z) Intrusive, mafic
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the subsurface boundary between the unsaturated zone and saturated zone where the water pressure in the pore spaces equals atmospheric pressure?
ANSWER: Water table
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following integers are prime?
W) 81
X) 91
Y) 101
Z) 111
ANSWER: Y) 101
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Polynomial f(x) has a degree of 2 and polynomial g(x) has a degree of 3. What is the degree of polynomial f(g(x))?
ANSWER: 6
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three quantities that are not vectors 1) Velocity 2) Speed 3) Distance
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer The potential energy of a 2 kg object is 40J and has 0 initial velocity. At a later instant, the object is moving at 4 m/s. What is the object’s potential energy?
ANSWER: 24 J
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Humans experience a life history where mortality rate starts low in offspring and increases rapidly in the elderly. What type of survivorship curve characterizes this type of behavior?
ANSWER: Type I
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Pharyngeal gill slits, a post anal tail and notochord are all examples of traits possessed by which phylum, to which humans belong?
ANSWER: Chordata (also accept: Chordates; Phylum Chordata)
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Naphthol Violet, Bromocresol Green, and Methyl Red are all examples of what type of compound, which marks the color change at the endpoint of a titration?
ANSWER: Indicator
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three phase transitions which are exothermic 1) Deposition 2) Sublimation 3) Condensation
ANSWER: 1,3
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term refers to the layer of the ocean in which temperature rapidly decreases with increasing depth?
ANSWER: Thermocline
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following atmospheric circulation cells is primarily responsible for driving the low-latitude, easterly trade winds?
W) Polar
X) Ferrel
Y) Hadley
Z) Supercell
ANSWER: Y) Hadley
9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice If the least common multiple of two integers a and b is 72, which of the following cannot be a value of the greatest common factor of a and b?
W) 1
X) 3
Y) 9
Z) 27
ANSWER: Z) 27
9. BONUS Math Short Answer In a certain triangle SCB, angle S is equal to 101 degrees and angle C is equal to 21 degrees. What is the measure of angle B?
ANSWER: 58
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What principle states that the weight of a displaced fluid equals the buoyant force?
ANSWER: Archimedes Principle
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer An object moves at a speed of 2 m/s and collides with a 4 kg object initially at rest. If the final velocity of the object is 3 m/s, what is the original velocity of the 2 kg object?
ANSWER: 9 m/s
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Bacteria often need to turn multiple sets of genes on and off at a time. To this end, their genome is organized into groups of genes organized under a single promoter. What are these genomic structures known as?
ANSWER: Operons
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer The chloroplast contains stacks of thylakoids that perform the light dependent reactions. What is the term for the stacks of these within the chloroplast?
ANSWER: Granum
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice An isotope of lead is obtained after Uranium-238 undergoes eight alpha decays and six beta decays. What is the mass number of this isotope?
W) 204
X) 206
Y) 208
Z) 210
ANSWER: X) 206

W) Red Dwarf
X) Yellow Dwarf
Y) Blue Giant
Z) Red Supergiant
ANSWER: W) Red Dwarf
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What two planets in the solar system rotate clockwise?
ANSWER: Uranus and Venus
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In how many ways can Alex, Bob, Connor, Dylan, and Erick sit it in a line of 5 chairs such that Alex and Erick must sit together?
ANSWER: 48
14. BONUS Math Short Answer At what points do the functions y = x2 and y = 2x intersect?
ANSWER: (0, 0) and (2, 4) (also accept: 0,0; 2,4) (do not accept: 0 and 2)
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is true of work?
W) When an object is lifted in a gravitational field, gravity does positive work
X) Work is always path independent
Y) The work done by friction is path dependent
Z) According to the work energy theorem, work is a vector
ANSWER: Y) The work done by friction is path dependent
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer A particle with charge 1 C and mass 10 kg moves in with a speed of 5 m/s in a magnetic field of 0.5 T, what is the radius of the orbit?
ANSWER: 100 meters
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The globular form of what protein forms polymer filaments to create microfilaments in the cytoskeleton?
W) Actin
X) Keratin
Y) Myosin
Z) Tubulin
ANSWER: W) Actin

ANSWER: Proline
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A given reaction has a rate constant with units of hertz. What is the overall reaction order for this reaction?
ANSWER: First
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 compounds that are soluble in water 1) Barium Hydroxide 2) Silver Chloride 3) Magnesium Hydroxide
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer During nuclear fusion in stars, the atoms have to overcome what electromagnetic barrier in order to collide?
ANSWER: Coulomb Barrier
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer During what era in the early universe did electrons combine with atomic nuclei to form atoms?
ANSWER: Recombination Era

ANSWER: 6√6
19. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Sanjay has a bag of marbles. He gives 20% of the marbles to his friend Monish. He then gives 10% of the remaining to his classmate, Arjun. Finally he gives 25% of the remaining marbles to his brother. What percentage of the original marbles does he still have?
W) 27
X) 36
Y) 45
Z) 54

20. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly describes a capacitor?
W) A capacitor generates a magnetic field perpendicular to the current
X) A capacitor generates a magnetic field parallel to the current

Z) A capacitor generates an electric field parallel to the current
ANSWER: Z) A capacitor generates an electric field parallel to the current

ANSWER: 27 W
