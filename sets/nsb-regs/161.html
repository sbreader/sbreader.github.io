1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true:
W) any foreign substance added to water will cause a freezing point drop

5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Earth’s greenhouse effect is primarily caused by which of the following:
