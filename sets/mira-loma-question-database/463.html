1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In the Bohr Model, what is the name for the path of the electron around the nucleus?
W) Orbit
X) Orbital
Y) Suborbital
Z) Cloud
ANSWER: W) Orbit
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In which orbital are the valence electrons in sodium?
W) 1d
X) 3s
Y) 3p
Z) 3d
ANSWER: X) 3s
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which subatomic particle contributes an almost negligible amount to the atomic mass?
ANSWER: ELECTRON
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Alpha particles have how many protons and neutrons?
ANSWER: 2 PROTONS, 2 NEUTRONS
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for the energy required to move an electron from the outermost principal energy level?
ANSWER: IONIZATION ENERGY
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Alpha particles resemble the nucleus of which element?
ANSWER: HELIUM
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the pH of a 0.001 M solution of hydrochloric acid?
ANSWER: 3
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which one of the following has a pH closest to that of distilled water?
W) Ammonia
X) Iodine
Y) Milk
Z) Bleach
ANSWER: Y) Milk
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is not true of enzymes?
W) They are made of protein
X) They lower the activation energy of a compound
Y) They chemically bond to the substrate
Z) They contribute to mechanical digestion
ANSWER: Z) They contribute to mechanical digestion
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What enzyme is responsible for the digestion of starch?
ANSWER: AMYLASE
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which carbohydrate is also known as "fruit sugar"?
ANSWER: FRUCTOSE
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which carbohydrate is present in RNA?
ANSWER: RIBOSE
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What volume will 1 mol of an ideal gas occupy?
W) 22.4 L
X) 8.21 L
Y) 6.022 L
Z) Depends on the gas
ANSWER: W) 22.4 L
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What mass will 1 mol of an ideal gas have?
W) 22.4 g
X) 8.21 g
Y) 6.022 g
Z) Depends on the gas
ANSWER: Z) Depends on the gas
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In a hemihydrate, what is the ratio of the compound to water?
ANSWER: 2:1
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Where do gas hydrates most commonly occur in nature?
W) In the lungs of some animals
X) At the bottom of the ocean
Y) In the Earth's core
Z) In the thermosphere
ANSWER: X) At the bottom of the ocean
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for two compounds that have the same molecular formula but different structures?
ANSWER: ISOMERS (also accept: ISOMERISM)
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many isomers are there of pentane?
ANSWER: 3
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In which process do fructose and glucose chemically combine and release water?
ANSWER: DEHYDRATION SYNTHESIS
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What kind of carbohydrate is formed when fructose and glucose combine?
W) Monosaccharide
X) Disaccharide
Y) Oligosaccharide
Z) Polysaccharide
ANSWER: X) Disaccharide
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which one of the following is not part of a nucleotide?
W) Amine
X) Deoxyribose
Y) Phosphate
Z) Base
ANSWER: W) Amine
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In a DNA molecule, what is the molar ratio of cytosine to guanine?
ANSWER: 1:1
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the three-letter abbreviation for isoleucine?
ANSWER: ILE
13. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice How many different amino acids are found in nature?
W) 10
X) 20
Y) 200
Z) 1200
ANSWER: Y) 200
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer To four significant digits, how many atomic mass units are in one gram?
ANSWER: 6.022 × 1023 (also accept: 60, 220, 000, 000, 000, 000, 000, 000)
14. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the mass of one electron, in atomic mass units?
W) 1/200 amu
X) 1/2000 amu
Y) 1/20,000 amu
Z) 1/200,000 amu
ANSWER: X) 1/2000 amu
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the term for the distance between the nuclei to two bonded atoms?
W) Radius
X) Bond length
Y) Bond radius
Z) Molecular length
ANSWER: X) Bond length
15. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is incorrect?
W) In a covalent bond, electrons are shared
X) A hydrogen bond is between polar molecules
Y) A disulfide bridge is a strong covalent bond
Z) A metallic bond is a weak ionic bond
ANSWER: Z) A metallic bond is a weak ionic bond
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which one of the following is not a particle?
W) Alpha particle
X) Positron
Y) Deuteron
Z) Gamma particle
ANSWER: Z) Gamma particle
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What will the mass number of an atom with a mass number of 194 after emitting a beta particle be?
ANSWER: 194
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the SI unit of absorbed dose?
ANSWER: GRAY
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What does the unit of absorbed dose "rad" stand for?
ANSWER: RADIATION ABSORBED DOSE
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which element has the lowest density?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the atomic weight of hydrogen?
ANSWER: 1.008 AMU (also accept: 1.01 AMU)
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In which kind of reaction does the energy released exceed the activation energy?
ANSWER: EXOTHERMIC
20. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Why do smaller particles dissolve more easily than larger ones?
W) They have a smaller mass
X) They have a larger total surface area
Y) They have a smaller density
Z) Its atoms dissociate more easily
ANSWER: X) They have a larger total surface area
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many moles of oxygen are in one mole of ozone?
ANSWER: 3
21. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which one of the following is not contributing significantly to global warming?
W) Carbon dioxide
X) Water vapor
Y) Hydrogen chloride
Z) Sulfur hexafluoride
ANSWER: Y) Hydrogen chloride
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In an amino acid, which group is opposite to the amino group?
ANSWER: CARBOXYL GROUP
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many amino acids are used by all forms of life in the synthesis of protein?
ANSWER: 20
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the formula for alcohol groups?
ANSWER: OH (also accept: ROH)
23. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When two monosaccharides are chemically combined, one releases an alcohol and the other releases hydrogen. What is the byproduct of this reaction?
ANSWER: WATER
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which one of the following does not react with hydrochloric acid?
W) Sodium acetate
X) Calcium carbonate
Y) Sodium bicarbonate
Z) Sodium carbonate
ANSWER: W) Sodium acetate
24. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In general, which of the following react with hydrochloric acid?
W) Carbonyls
X) Carbonates
Y) Amines
Z) Benzenes
ANSWER: X) Carbonates
25. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What are the components of lipids?
W) Glycerol and glycogen
X) Glycogen and fatty acids
Y) Glycerol and fatty acids
Z) Glycerol and phenols
ANSWER: Y) Glycerol and fatty acids
25. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What group do phospholipids contain that other lipids don't?
ANSWER: PHOSPHATE
