1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which gene is often activated by the p53 gene and halts the cell cycle by creating products that bind to cyclin-dependent kinases?
W) p17
X) p19
Y) p21
Z) p23
ANSWER: Y) p21
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name for the gene, named for rat sarcoma, that is a G protein?
ANSWER: RAS GENE
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the rarest of the following elementary steps?
W) Unimolecular Reaction
X) Dimolecular Reaction
Y) Bimolecular Reaction
Z) Termolecular Reaction
ANSWER: Z) Termolecular Reaction
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the slowest step in the sequence of steps leading to product formation called?
ANSWER: RATE-DETERMINING STEP
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following quarks has the highest rest energy?
W) Top
X) Bottom
Y) Charmed
Z) Strange
ANSWER: W) Top

ANSWER: FERMI NATIONAL ACCELERATOR LABORATORY (also accept: FERMILAB)
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What was the first quasar to be identified?
W) 3C 279
X) 3C 273
Y) Cloverleaf
Z) Einstein Cross
ANSWER: X) 3C 273
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which period in geologic history did the first land plants appear on Earth?
W) Devonian
X) Silurian
Y) Ordovician
Z) Cambrian
ANSWER: Y) Ordovician
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which two periods in Earth's history are known as the "Age of Amphibians"?
ANSWER: PERMIAN AND CARBONIFEROUS
11. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice When n is multiplied by four, the result is the same as when n is added to four. What is the value of 3n?
W) 1
X) 3/4
Y) 4/3
Z) 4
ANSWER: Z) 4

ANSWER: $6

W) Field's Metal
X) Rose's Metal
Y) Galinstan
Z) Pewter
ANSWER: W) Field's Metal

ANSWER: ELECTRUM
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which category would the common white mushroom often found in grocery stores fall under?
W) Zygomycetes
X) Glomeromycetes
Y) Basidiomycetes
Z) Ascomycetes
ANSWER: Y) Basidiomycetes

17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In a flame test, which of the following elements produces a brick red color?
W) Barium
X) Calcium

18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which one of the following groups of cations has no precipitating reagent: Group 1, Group 2, Group 3, Group 4, Group 5?
ANSWER: GROUP 5
19. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements concerning harmonics is false?
W) If one end of a pipe is closed, a node must exist at that end
X) If one end of a pipe is closed, there are no even multiples of the fundamental harmonic
Y) If a pipe is open on both sides, an antinode exists
Z) If a pipe is open at both ends, there are no odd multiples of the fundamental harmonic
ANSWER: Z) If a pipe is open at both ends, there are no odd multiples of the fundamental harmonic
