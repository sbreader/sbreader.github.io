1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not characteristic of a parasympathetic reaction
W) Constriction of bronchi
X) Stimulation of gall bladder
Y) Slowed heart rate
Z) Stimulated release of glucose
ANSWER: Z) Stimulated release of glucose
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What word is used to designate neurons in the peripheral nervous system responsible for sensory reception, literally meaning in Latin "to bring toward"?
ANSWER: AFFERENT
2. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer When providing first-aid to minor sports injuries that cause swelling such as a twisted ankle, it is recommended that the injured person follow the acronym RICE. What does RICE stand for?
ANSWER: REST, ICE, COMPRESSION, ELEVATION
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Arrange the following substances from lowest to highest vapor pressure: distilled water, ethanol, mercury, propane
ANSWER: MERCURY, DISTILLED WATER, ETHANOL, PROPANE
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Using implicit differentiation, what is dy/dx of the equation y2+x=2 at the point (1,1)?
ANSWER: NEGATIVE ONE-HALF
3. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following answers is equivalent to the statement cos2x-sin2x?
W) 2cos2x-1
X) 2cosxsinx
Y) 2-sin2x
Z) 1
ANSWER: W) 2cos2x-1
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Although the color of some minerals very widely, scientists can use this property, also called powder color, to more easily identify a mineral as it is relatively consistent despite the apparent difference in color.
ANSWER: STREAK
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Coulomb's Constant, 8.988 × 109, is defined using what units?
ANSWER: N·m2/C2 (also accept: NEWTON METERS SQUARED PER COULOMB SQUARED)
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer A ball is thrown off a 20 meter tall cliff at an upwards velocity of 2 m/s. Neglecting air friction and assuming that g=10 m/s2, what is the velocity of the ball when it returns to the 20 meter height from whence it was thrown
ANSWER: NEGATIVE 2 METERS PER SECOND (do not accept: 2 METERS PER SECOND)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Fred Hoyle, known for coining the term "Big bang" and his work on stellar nucleosynthesis, supported what popular theory in 1950's that went against the Big Bang Theory and stated that the universe always will maintain a uniform appearance?
ANSWER: STEADY STATE
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Stellar nucleosynthesis in larger stars is capable of producing many heavy elements. What element acts the definite, largest element to be created through stellar nucleosynthesis?
W) Hydrogen
X) Helium
Y) Oxygen
Z) Iron
ANSWER: Z) Iron
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is not defined as a strong base?
W) Lithium Hydroxide
X) Sodium Hydroxide
Y) Ammonia
Z) Rubidium Hydroxide
ANSWER: Y) Ammonia
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What generally non-corrosive acid from a family containing carbon and boron atoms is regarded as the strongest acid known to man, estimated at 1,000 times stronger than sulfuric acid?
ANSWER: CARBORANE SUPERACID (also accept: H(CHB11Cl11))
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Although the lock and key model was the primarily accepted model for decades, what model, proposed in 1958 by Daniel Koshland, is the generally accepted explanation for the function of enzymes?
ANSWER: INDUCED FIT
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A linearly increasing graph with a saturation point in which the graph levels out best describes a graph that relates enzyme activity to
W) pH
X) Substrate concentration
Y) Temperature
Z) Time
ANSWER: X) Substrate concentration
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In 1939, what American scientist was awarded the Nobel Prize in physics for his invention and studies of the cyclotron?
ANSWER: ERNEST LAWRENCE
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the name of the theorized particle believed to carry the fundamental force of gravity?
ANSWER: GRAVITON
10. BONUS Math Short Answer Given that the definite integral of f(x) on the closed integral [1,3] is 1 and the definite integral of f(x) on the closed integral [1,5] is 6, what is the definite integral of f(x) from 5 to 3?
ANSWER: NEGATIVE FIVE
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The presence of volcanoes in the Hawaiian chains despite being located near any plate boundary is explained by what geological phenomena?
ANSWER: HOT SPOTS
11. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Santa Anna winds in Southern California, known for exacerbating the many wildfires common in the summer season, is described as a
W) Sea breeze
X) Katabatic wind
Y) Rain shadow wind
Z) Chinook wind
ANSWER: X) Katabatic wind
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Tripling the mass of two bodies and doubling their distance will increase the gravitational attraction between them by a factor of what?
ANSWER: 9/4
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Of the four fundamental forces, which force is generally described as having the smallest range?
ANSWER: WEAK NUCLEAR FORCE
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What extremely energetic astronomical phenomena is responsible for most, if not all of the Universe's heavier elements?
ANSWER: SUPERNOVA
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A star with a surface temperature of at least 10,000 Kelvin will exhibit what color?
ANSWER: BLUE
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A bond between an element with an electronegativity of 1 and an element with an electronegativity of 3.16 will most likely be
W) Polar covalent
X) Ionic
Y) Non-polar covalent
Z) Metallic
ANSWER: X) Ionic
14. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules is described as relatively non-polar?
W) Phosphatidycholine
X) Oleic Acid
Y) Water
Z) Glucose
ANSWER: X) Oleic Acid
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following substances would typically require protein channels to diffuse through the plasma membrane
W) Water
X) Oxygen
Y) Benzene
Z) Carbon Dioxide
ANSWER: W) Water
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What term is given to channel proteins designed to facilitate the diffusion of water molecules through the plasma membrane?
ANSWER: AQUAPORINS
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The bovine spongiform encephalopathy, commonly known as Mad Cow Disease, is caused by an infection of
W) Bacteria
X) Protazoa
Y) Viruses
Z) Prions
ANSWER: Z) Prions
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following diseases is currently not classified as a genetic disorder?
W) Cystic Fibrosis
X) Hemophilia
Y) Adrenoleukodystrophy
Z) Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis
ANSWER: Z) Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the definite integral of f(x) = x2 -2cosx + sinx + 1 on the closed interval [5,5]?
ANSWER: 0
17. BONUS Math Short Answer Evaluate 1 – cos2(π/6) in simplest rational form.
ANSWER: 1/4
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following minerals from hardest to softest: Orthoclase, Calcite, Corundum, Talc, Gypsum, Topaz
ANSWER: CORUNDUM, TOPAZ, ORTHOCLASE, CALCITE, GYPSUM, TALC
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Albite is a plagioclase feldspar containing what two metals in its structure?
ANSWER: SODIUM AND ALUMINUM
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What fundamental force is manifested in the change of one quark into another quark, or one lepton into another lepton?
ANSWER: WEAK NUCLEAR FORCE
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer In 1979, three scientists received a shared Nobel Prize in Physics for their contributions in discovering the unification of what two fundamental forces in extreme temperature?
ANSWER: WEAK NUCLEAR AND ELECTROMAGNETIC FORCES
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which celestial object has the greatest apparent magnitude?
ANSWER: THE SUN
20. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Canopus, the second brightest star in the night sky, is located in what constellation?
W) Carina
X) Vela
Y) Puppis
Z) Pyxis
ANSWER: W) Carina
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A gas container contains 0.75 atm of methane, 760 torr of oxygen, roughly 25.3 kPa of nitrogen, and 380 mm Hg of helium. What is the total pressure of the container in atm rounded to the nearest tenth?
ANSWER: 2.5 atm
21. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Assuming that a gas is ideal, tripling the temperature of the gas and the number of moles and halving the pressure of the gas will result in an increase of the volume of its container by what factor?
ANSWER: EIGHTEEN
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What motor proteins are responsible for the bending motion of cilia and flagellum in cells?
ANSWER: DYNEINS
22. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The genetic disorder known as Tay-Sachs disease manifests itself in the malformation of what cellular organelle?
W) Lysosomes
X) Endoplasmic Reticulum
Y) Golgi Apparatus
Z) Mitochondria
ANSWER: W) Lysosomes
