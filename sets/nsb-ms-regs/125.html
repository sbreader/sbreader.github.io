1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice From what source does the energy required for photsynthesis come?

1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What compartment of the heart pumps blood to the lungs?
ANSWER: RIGHT VENTRICLE
2. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Most ethanol in the U.S. is produced from what crop?
ANSWER: CORN

W) Anthracite
X) Bituminous
Y) Lignite
Z) Subbituminous
ANSWER: W) Anthracite

ANSWER: ESTUARY
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the production of usable energy directly from inorganic molecules?
ANSWER: CHEMOSYNTHESIS

4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the structure capable of transporting water only upward in plants?
ANSWER: XYLEM

X) Density

ANSWER: HOOKE'S LAW

10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the multiplicative inverse of –7/3?
ANSWER: –3/7
10. BONUS Math Multiple Choice A student makes the following conjecture: "If a quadrilateral is a rhombus, then it must be a parallelogram." If someone doesn't believe this conjecture is true, which of the following would be necessary to have a counterexample to it?
W) A rhombus that is a parallelogram
X) A parallelogram that is a rhombus
Y) A rhombus that is not a parallelogram
Z) A parallelogram that is not a rhombus
ANSWER: Y) A rhombus that is not a parallelogram

ANSWER: SMALL INTESTINES

ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE (also accept: CO2)
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the greatest integer less than or equal to the log base 3 of 579?
ANSWER: 5
12. BONUS Math Short Answer Given f of x = 4x2 – 3 and g of x = 4 over (x – 5), calculate g of f of 3.
ANSWER: 1/7
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly explains why glucose is converted to glucose-6-phosphate in the first step of glycolysis?
W) The cell membrane is impermeable to large, negatively-charged molecules
X) It enables the splitting of glucose in half during the next step
Y) The isomerization of glucose is less favorable than that of glucose-6-phosphate
Z) Glucose-6-phosphate can also be incorporated into glycogen
ANSWER: W) The cell membrane is impermeable to large, negatively-charged molecules
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Many antibiotics inhibit bacterial protein synthesis, but not eukaryotic protein synthesis. Which of the following best explains why?
W) The antibiotics cut up bacterial mRNA, but not eukaryotic mRNA
X) Eukaryotes have enzymes to destroy the antibiotics, but bacteria do not
Y) Eukaryotes can export the antibiotics, preventing them from damaging the cell
Z) The antibiotics affect bacterial but not eukaryotic ribosomes
ANSWER: Z) The antibiotics affect bacterial but not eukaryotic ribosomes
