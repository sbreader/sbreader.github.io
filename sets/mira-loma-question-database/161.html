1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following correctly describes xenoliths?
W) A foreign rock fragment that becomes enveloped in a larger rock when the larger rock is cooling and hardening
X) A offshoot of a batholith that encounters foreign rock
Y) An igneous body that accumulates at the bottom of a shield volcano as a result of lava flows
Z) An igneous body that cuts across parallel laccoliths
ANSWER: W) A foreign rock fragment that becomes enveloped in a larger rock when the larger rock is cooling and hardening
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What forms when the rock fragment that is becoming enveloped in a larger rock is of the same composition as the surrounding rock?
ANSWER: AN AUTOLITH (also accept: COGNATE INCLUSION)
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the structure of mica?
W) Triclinic
X) Monoclinic
Y) Orthorhombic
Z) Cubic
ANSWER: X) Monoclinic
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name the lenticular single mica crystals that are often used as shear sense indicators.
ANSWER: MICA FISH
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of sandstone would specifically be found at and around alluvial fans?
W) Graywacke
X) Quartz Arentite
Y) Lithic Sandstone
Z) Arkose
ANSWER: Z) Arkose
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which material makes up the fine-grained matrix found in greywacke?
ANSWER: CLAY
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following phenomena is not experienced before a cold front passes?
W) Warm temperature
X) South-southwest winds
Y) Steadily rising dew point
Z) Steadily falling pressure
ANSWER: Y) Steadily rising dew point
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In which type of occluded front is the air behind the front colder than the air ahead of it?
ANSWER: COLD-TYPE OCCLUDED FRONT
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which type of drainage network forms in valley and ridge terrain, where rocks of varying resistance to erosion are folded into anticlines and synclines?
W) Dendritic
X) Rectangular
Y) Trellis
Z) Radial
ANSWER: Y) Trellis
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which type of stream erodes a ridge formed by structural deformation to form a steep-walled gorge?
ANSWER: ANTECEDENT STREAM
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which term can be used to describe small round pools of water on top of a glacier that have a layer of dirt on the bottom?
W) Cryoconites
X) Sun Cups
Y) Diagenic Ice Mounds
Z) Rock Pedestals
ANSWER: W) Cryoconites
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the exposed part of a mountain peak that has not been covered by a surrounding glacier called?
ANSWER: NUNATAKS
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the specific name for an unconformity where upper beds overlie metamorphic or igneous rock?
W) Singular Unconformity
X) Nonconformity
Y) Disconformity
Z) Angular unconformity
ANSWER: X) Nonconformity
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name the famous unconformity in Scotland that was named after an 18th-century Scottish geologist. This geological site provided evidence for uniformitarianism and the age of the earth. Name this unconformity.
ANSWER: HUTTON'S UNCONFORMITY
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following mountain ranges is an example of fault-block mountain ranges?
W) Appalachians
X) Andes
Y) Cantilever
Z) Basin and Range
ANSWER: Z) Basin and Range
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which type of sand dunes feature a slip face oriented so that it is convex downwind?
W) Barchan
X) Blowout
Y) Transverse
Z) Lateral
ANSWER: X) Blowout
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Nd-dune belts behind beaches are usually composed of which type of sand-dune?
ANSWER: TRANSVERSE
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which moraine is found along the edge of a glacier where it scrapes against the side walls of the valley?
W) End moraine
X) Medial moraine
Y) Ground moraine
Z) Lateral moraine
ANSWER: Z) Lateral moraine
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Within 5 percent, how much of Earth's total land area is covered by permafrost?
ANSWER: 25 PERCENT (also accept: ANY ANSWER FROM 20 PERCENT TO 30 PERCENT)
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following would be most useful when utilizing tephrochronology?
W) Volcanic ash
X) Wood
Y) Quartz
Z) Carbonate
ANSWER: W) Volcanic ash
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What was the name of the Harvard geologist who, in 1909, proposed the "cycle of erosion" theory?
ANSWER: WILLIAM MORRIS DAVIS
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which type of air mass is generally categorized by cold, dry, and stable air?
W) cP
X) mP
Y) cT
Z) mT
ANSWER: W) cP
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term used to describe heavy snow showers that occur when a cold, dry air mass moces over a relatively warm lake?

13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What does ENSO stand for?
ANSWER: EL NINO/SOUTHERN OSCILLATION
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the turning of water with increasing depth called?
ANSWER: EKMAN SPIRAL
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a way in which deeper waters differ from shallower waters?
W) Deeper waters are colder
X) Deeper waters contain less carbon dioxide
Y) Shallower waters are under lower pressure
Z) Deeper waters lack carbonate oozes
ANSWER: X) Deeper waters contain less carbon dioxide
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer CCD is the depth at which shells dissolve in seawater. What does CCD stand for?
ANSWER: CARBONATE COMPENSATION DEPTH

X) 1 percent
Y) 0.1 percent
Z) 0.01 percent
ANSWER: Z) 0.01 percent
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The process by which water enters a rock or soil through joints or small pore spaces between particles is called what?
ANSWER: INFILTRATION
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes a large mineral crystal in a metamorphic rock which has grown within the finer grained groundmass.
ANSWER: PORPHYROBLASTS

Y) Uranium-238

17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the daughter isotope of the parent isotope Potassium-40?

Z) Cuestas

X) Diodorus Siculus
