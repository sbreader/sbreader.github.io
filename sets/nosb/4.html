Packet 4 (2018 Regional Round 4) of National Ocean Sciences Bowl:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice On the Atlantic Flyway, where do birds stopping in the Beaufort Barrier Islands originate?
W) Arctic
X) Antarctic
Y) Northern United States
Z) Mexico
ANSWER: W) Arctic
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the specific heat capacity of water in joules per kilogram per degree Celsius?
ANSWER: 4000 joules per kilogram per degree Celsius
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following describes neap tides?
W) Strong tides that occur when Mercury is in retrograde
X) Low tides in shellfishing season
Y) Weak tides that occur when the sun and moon form a right angle with the Earth
Z) Freak low tides that strand fish on the beach
ANSWER: Y) Weak tides that occur when the sun and moon form a right angle with the Earth
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the system that drives tube feet movement in sea stars?
ANSWER: Water vascular system (also accept: hydrovascular system; vascular system)
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What serves as the geographical line of demarcation between two consecutive calendar dates?
W) Prime Meridian
X) Tropic of Cancer
Y) International Date Line
Z) Tropic of Capricorn
ANSWER: Y) International Date Line
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the depth at which the dissolution of carbonate begins to rapidly increase?
W) Carbonate compensation depth
X) Dissolution initiation depth
Y) Lysocline
Z) Carbonate saturation horizon
ANSWER: Y) Lysocline
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In which hemisphere are the "Roaring Forties," "Furious Fifties," and "Screaming Sixties" located?
ANSWER: The Southern Hemisphere
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which group of organisms has the largest diel migration on the planet?
W) Zooplankton
X) Whales
Y) Sardines
Z) Sea stars
ANSWER: W) Zooplankton
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice As of 2017, which of the following is the only currently operating undersea research laboratory in the world?
W) Poseidon
X) Nautilus
Y) Neptune
Z) Aquarius
ANSWER: Z) Aquarius
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer If a warming climate causes a decrease in downwelling of deep waters in the polar regions, what effect must this have on the upwelling of nutrient rich waters?
ANSWER: Upwelling must also decrease
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What environmental driver is predicted to shift species ranges poleward?
ANSWER: Climate change (also accept: increasing average temperature; temperature)
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Approximately, what is the average depth of the ocean?
W) 4 km
X) 5 km
Y) 6 km
Z) 7 km
ANSWER: W) 4 km
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the process by which dissolved metal particles come out of a solution, creating a mineral deposit?
ANSWER: Precipitation
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice By about how much has sea level risen over the past one hundred years?
W) 15 millimeters
X) 50 millimeters
Y) 15 centimeters
Z) 50 centimeters
ANSWER: Y) 15 centimeters
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Why is sea level falling, not rising, in places like Finland and Sweden?
ANSWER: Post-glacial uplift (also accept: glacial rebound; crustal rebound; isostatic rebound)
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following sharks does not have typical large sharp teeth?
W) Nurse shark
X) Basking shark
Y) Black-tipped reef shark
Z) Hammerhead shark
ANSWER: X) Basking shark
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In what gyre – located near Chile and Easter Island-was an accumulation of plastic recently discovered?
ANSWER: South Pacific Gyre
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the hard structural components found in sponges?
ANSWER: Spicules
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In the 1950s, what were ocean bottom seismometers developed to do?
W) Give early warnings for tsunamis
X) Study the structure of the oceanic crust
Y) Monitor clandestine nuclear tests
Z) Track whale pod movements
ANSWER: Y) Monitor clandestine nuclear tests
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following marine invertebrate lifecycles does NOT contain a trochophore larval stage?
W) Gastropods
X) Bivalves
Y) Annelids
Z) Nematodes
ANSWER: Z) Nematodes
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What would be a visible sign of a shallowly submerged obstacle like a sandbar or a coral reef?
ANSWER: Wave breaking (also accept: a change in water color; a decrease in wavelength; a decrease in wave velocity)
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements is NOT macronutrient in seawater?
W) Nitrogen
X) Hydrogen
Y) Phosphorous
Z) Silicon
ANSWER: X) Hydrogen
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the function of the dorsal fin in dolphins?
ANSWER: Stability
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The sea floor is divided into what two major regions?
ANSWER: Continental margins (OR submerged edges of continent) AND Deep Sea Plain (OR Deep Sea Floor OR Deep Ocean Basin OR Abyssal Plain)
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a product of chemosynthesis?
W) Formaldehyde
X) Sulfur
Y) Water
Z) Oxygen
ANSWER: Z) Oxygen
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the large-scale, low-latitude overturning circulations where warm air rises at the equator and sinks at 30 degrees north or south latitude?
ANSWER: Hadley cells
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Gradual settling or sudden sinking of the Earth’s surface caused by underground material movement describes what process?
W) Faulting
X) Subduction
Y) Slipping
Z) Subsidence
ANSWER: Z) Subsidence
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following changes is an indirect effect of climate change on a snail?
W) Heart rate
X) Growth rate
Y) Behavior
Z) Body temperature
ANSWER: Y) Behavior
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What mineral compound is responsible for the black-colored seawater that is released from some hydrothermal vents?
ANSWER: Iron sulfides
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Gravity is NOT the restoring force for which of the following waves?
W) Internal waves
X) Tsunamis
Y) Tides
Z) Capillary waves
ANSWER: Z) Capillary waves
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the distinguishing feature of chondrichthyes skeletal structures?
ANSWER: Cartilage instead of true bones
