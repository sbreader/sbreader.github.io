TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice During an electrochemistry lab, you carefully construct a voltaic cell with two electrodes: a lead cathode and magnesium anode based on the standard reduction potentials. When you connect the two electrodes using wires and a voltmeter, you notice that no voltage flows through the voltmeter. Which of the following is the most likely cause of the lack of voltage?
W) There are no anions in the solution that are able to be reduced by the cell
