1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer One study found that people were willing to pay the same amount to save 2,000 birds as 20,000 birds. This is an example of what cognitive bias, in which people tend to ignore the size of problems and instead judge problems based on more superficial characteristics?
ANSWER: Scope insensitivity (also accept: Scope neglect)
