1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why it is possible to find nuggets of pure gold and silver in nature, but sodium appears only in compounds?
W) Gold and silver are denser than sodium
X) Gold and silver are less reactive than sodium
Y) Gold and silver are less common than sodium
Z) Gold and silver are solids at room temperature
ANSWER: X) Gold and silver are less reactive than sodium
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer An object has a mass of 3 kilograms and a force accelerates the object from rest to a speed of 4 meters per second over a distance of 2 meters. What is the work done in joules by this force?
ANSWER: 24
2. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is the least volume?
W) 1.9 pints
X) 3.5 cups
Y) 1 quart
Z) 1/3 of a gallon
ANSWER: X) 3.5 cups
2. BONUS Math Short Answer What are two consecutive positive odd integers whose product is 783?
ANSWER: 27 AND 29
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What early scientific instrument was used to determine if an object was carrying an electric charge?
ANSWER: ELECTROSCOPE
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What volume, in milliliters, of a 10 molar hydrochloric acid stock solution is needed to make 2 liters of a 3 molar hydrochloric acid solution?
ANSWER: 600
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a monotreme?
W) Hedge-hog
X) Platypus
Y) Anteater
Z) Wombat
ANSWER: X) Platypus
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the two types of bladders in the human body?
ANSWER: GALL AND URINARY
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the most common type of cloud formed when warm air rises and reaches a level of cool air, where the moisture in the air cools and condenses?
ANSWER: CUMULUS
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer A chemical is added to a cell that prevents endocytosis but does not prevent exocytosis. As a result, what would be expected to happen to the volume of the cell over time?
ANSWER: IT WILL DECREASE
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of elements are liquid at room temperature?
W) Mercury and cesium
X) Bromine and cesium
Y) Mercury and gallium
Z) Mercury and bromine
ANSWER: Z) Mercury and bromine
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The half-life of francium-212 is 19 minutes. How many minutes will it take for 1 gram of this isotope to decay to 0.125 grams?
ANSWER: 57
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The presence of clays on the surface of Mars strongly suggests the past presence of what substance?
ANSWER: LIQUID WATER (also accept: WATER)
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer You look at a thin section of a sedimentary rock composed of quartz, plagioclase, and amphibole grains surrounded by calcite cement. Which of those minerals formed last?
ANSWER: CALCITE CEMENT (also accept: CALCITE)
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What two monosaccharides make the disaccharide sucrose?
ANSWER: FRUCTOSE AND GLUCOSE
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By number, identify all of the following three statements that are TRUE about catalysts 1) they change the rate of the reaction 2) they change the chemical equilibrium of the reaction 3) they decrease the activation energy.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
9. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Relative to conventional sources, such as liquid oil and natural gas, combustion of biomass for heat and electricity does NOT create more of which of the following?
W) Greenhouse gases
X) Particulates
Y) Polyaromatic hydrocarbons
Z) Dioxins
ANSWER: W) Greenhouse gases
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What are the names of three processes through which thermal energy can be transferred?
ANSWER: ANY 3 OF: CONDUCTION, CONVECTION, MASS TRANSFER, ADVECTION, AND RADIATION
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If the area of rhombus ABCD is 78 square centimeters and the altitude to the base CD is 7.5 centimeters, to the nearest integer, what is the length in centimeters of a side of the rhombus?
ANSWER: 10
