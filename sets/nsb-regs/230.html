1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following metals exhibits a strong inert-pair effect?

X) Scandium

1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following diatomic ions is isoelectronic with the dioxygen molecule?
W) N2+
X) OF+
Y) OF-
Z) NO+
ANSWER: X) OF+
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Coral polyps extract what compound out of seawater to form hard external skeletons?
ANSWER: CALCIUM CARBONATE
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Trees growing on the edge of a river’s meander are most likely to fall into the river due to:
W) Deposition on the inside of the meander
X) Deposition on the outside of the meander
Y) Erosion on the inside of the meander
Z) Erosion on the outside of the meander
ANSWER: Z) Erosion on the outside of the meander

ANSWER: PHOTOELECTRIC EFFECT (do not accept: PHOTOVOLTAIC EFFECT)

14. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Suppose you compress a gas in a cylinder with a piston by pushing down on the piston. What is the best explanation for why the gas gets warm?
