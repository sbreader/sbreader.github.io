7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Ecdysteroids often act in accordance with the levels of what other hormone to promote either molting or metamorphosis?
ANSWER: JUVENILE HORMONE
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A square coil has side length 10 meters and lies on the xy plane. It starts traveling at a speed of 5 m/s in the positive x direction. At time t = 0, the rightmost edge of the square enters a 5 tesla magnetic field directed in the positive z direction. At time t = 3, what is the magnitude of the induced emf?
ANSWER: 250
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Greg flips 4 coins. What is the probability that he flips at least 2 heads?
ANSWER: 11/16
11. BONUS Math Short Answer A square ABCD has side length 1. Derik starts at point A and wants to go to side BC, then to side AD, then to point C. What is the minimum possible length of his path?
ANSWER: √10 (also accept: ROOT 10)
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A beam of light travels through air and strikes water with a refractive index of 1.3 at an angle such that the reflected beam is completely polarized. What is the term for this angle?
ANSWER: BREWSTER’S ANGLE
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many integers from 1 to 60 inclusive are not divisible by neither 2 nor 3?
ANSWER: 20
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A unit circle is inscribed in a square, and that square is inscribed in another circle. What is the radius of the other circle?
ANSWER: √2 (also accept: ROOT 2)
18. BONUS Math Short Answer A complex number z is called a primitive nth root of unity if n is the smallest positive integer such that z to the power of n equals 1. How many distinct primitive 8th roots of unity exist?
ANSWER: 4
22. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at the Joint Genome Institute are identifying fungal enzymes that attach carbohydrates to hydroxyl groups within plant cell walls. Which of the following molecules is not a monosaccharide that forms carbohydrates?
W) Fructose
X) Galactose
Y) Glucose
Z) Sucrose
ANSWER: Z) Sucrose
22. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at Los Alamos National Lab are developing new models for vertical mixing in Langmuir circulation. Which of the following would not be characteristic of Langmuir circulation?
W) Aggregation of long strips of plankton
X) Adjacent cells of opposite rotational circulation
Y) Zones of convergence separated by zones of divergence
Z) Driven primarily by Ekman spirals
ANSWER: Z) Driven primarily by Ekman spirals
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following acids is not diprotic?
W) Carbonic acid
X) Citric acid
Y) Phosphorous acid
Z) Sulfuric acid
ANSWER: X) Citric acid
