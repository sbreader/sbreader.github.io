X) oxygen
Y) fluorine
Z) sulfur

ANSWER: tyndall effect

ANSWER: 1 and 2
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A rigid object with mass of 3 kilograms has a moment of inertia of 2 kilogram-meter squared about an axis through its center of mass. In kilogram-meter squared, what is the moment of inertia of the object about a parallel axis that is 2 meters away from the center of mass-axis?
ANSWER: 14
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following describes the hamiltonian operator in quantum mechanics?
W) kinetic energy of a system
X) potential energy of a system
Y) sum of kinetic and potential energies of a system
Z) difference between kinetic and potential energies of a system
ANSWER: Y) sum of kinetic and potential energies of a system

W) Sun at perihelion, Moon at perigee

Y) Sun at aphelion, Moon at perigee
Z) Sun at aphelion, Moon at apogee

5. BONUS Math Short Answer A function f is continuous on the closed interval [1,5] and differentiable on open interval (1,5). If f(1) = 2, and f(5) = 14, what value must f’(c) equal for some c on the open interval (1,5)?

6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer At the Smithsonian Tropical Research Institute, ecologists are studying how species interactions shape rainforest diversity. When two species compete for the same resource, what ecological principle states that they cannot coexist indefinitely?
ANSWER: competitive exclusion principle (also accept: Gause’s law)
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers at the University of Sydney are studying thermodynamic cycles. For a process at constant volume, heat flow equals the change in which of the following thermodynamic quantities?
W) Enthalpy
X) Gibbs free energy
Y) Entropy
Z) Internal energy
ANSWER: Z) Internal energy

13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When an elk moves up into the mountains from sea level, the lower oxygen concentration in the high mountain air stimulates the elk to breathe more rapidly and deeply.
