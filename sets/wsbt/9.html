1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 choices that are true regarding phyllotaxy. 1) Opposite is the most common phyllotaxy 2) Sunflowers exemplify a distichous phyllotaxy 3) Grasses exemplifies a whorled phyllotaxy
ANSWER: NONE
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 statements that are true of polymetallic nodules 1) They are most abundant in abyssal plains 2) They grow extremely slowly 3) They are mostly composed of manganese oxide by mass.
ANSWER: ALL

ANSWER: 3 2 1
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Evaluate the sum from x = 1 to infinity of (−1)x/x!.
ANSWER: 1/e − 1
3. BONUS Math Multiple Choice x and y are chosen independently and uniformly at random from the closed interval [0,2]. What is the probability that x · y < 1?
W) (2 · ln(2) + 1)/4
X) (2 · ln(2) − 1)/4
Y) (ln(2) + 1)/2
Z) (ln(2) − 1)/2
ANSWER: W) (2 · ln(2) + 1)/4
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What fictitious force is a consequence of a reference frame with a changing angular velocity?
ANSWER: Azimuthal force (also accept: Euler force)

W) sine
X) cosine
Y) hyperbolic tangent
Z) tangent
ANSWER: X) cosine

5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify the following statements which are true regarding nucleation 1) Nucleation is usually unpredictable even in identical systems 2) Heterogeneous nucleation is much more common than homogeneous nucleation 3) Nucleation can produce solids, liquids, and gasses

6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What organic compound, which recycles ADP to ATP in vertebrates, is often used as a booster of athletic performance and is known to increase retention of water in myocytes?

ANSWER: 1 and 2

ANSWER: 320 BILLION SOLAR MASSES
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Define a sequence with the following recurrence relation: xn+1 = (x1)xn for n ≥ 1. For example, x2 = x1x1 and x3 = x1x1x1. If x1 = √2, then evaluate limn→∞ xn
