1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following phases is NOT part of mitosis?
W) interphase
X) prophase
Y) telophase
Z) metaphase
ANSWER: W) interphase
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer During the latter stages of mitosis, what change that involves the splitting of the cell occurs?
ANSWER: cytokinesis
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What part of the digestive tract mainly serves to absorb water and ions from waste material, and process it for excretion?
ANSWER: Large Intestine
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The appendix is an extension of which section of the large intestine?
W) Colon
X) Cecum
Y) Rectum
Z) Sigmoid
ANSWER: X) Cecum
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which B complex vitamin is a component of the coenzymes NAD+ and NADP+?
W) Thiamine
X) Niacin
Y) Biotin
Z) Folacin
ANSWER: X) Niacin
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer A deficiency in Vitamin B3, Niacin, can lead to what disease?
ANSWER: Pellagra
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following worms is NOT part of phylum Platyhelminthes?
W) Planarians
X) Flukes
Y) Tapeworms
Z) Ribbonworms
ANSWER: Z) Ribbonworms
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer Most flatworms have a simple excretory apparatus that consists of what networks of ciliated tubules called "flame bulbs"?
ANSWER: protonephridia
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A Ribosome contains each of the following sites for tRNA except
W) M site
X) P site
Y) A site
Z) E site
ANSWER: W) M site
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer The enzyme that bonds an amino acid group to the tRNA in translation is known as what?
ANSWER: aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following animals did not develop from the lobe-finned lineage of fish?
W) coelacanths
X) lungfishes
Y) seahorses
Z) tetrapods
ANSWER: Y) seahorses
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the general term for vertebrates that have jaws, which branched off from lampreys out of the animals with backbones?
ANSWER: gnathostomes
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In what type of biological signaling are secreted molecules diffused locally and trigger a response in neighboring cells?
W) endocrine
X) paracrine
Y) autocrine
Z) synaptic
ANSWER: X) paracrine
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following actions is NOT a consequence of a paracrine response?
W) Blood clotting
X) Tissue repair
Y) Allergies
Z) "fight or flight"
ANSWER: Z) "fight or flight"
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Using the typical trophic efficiency between trophic levels, if 5000 Joules of energy enter the primary consumers, how much energy will be transferred to the tertiary consumers?
W) 5000 Joules
X) 1250 Joules
Y) 50 Joules
Z) 5 Joules
ANSWER: Y) 50 Joules
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the process where ATP donates an inorganic phosphate group to a substrate, in order to destabalize it and "couple" the reaction?
ANSWER: phosphorylation
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Huntington's Disease a debillitating genetic disorder which is autosomal dominant. If both your parents are heterozygously dominant for the trait, what is the chance that you will be afflicted?
W) 100%
X) 75%
Y) 50%
Z) 25%
ANSWER: X) 75%
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer Let's say, by chance, that all 4 of your parent's (same ones from the tossup) children become homozygously dominant for this horrible chorea. About how many will die from this disease before reaching the age of 20?
ANSWER: 0
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice About what percent of DNA do exons (actual coding regions) comprise?
W) 1%
X) 10%
Y) 50%
Z) 100%
ANSWER: W) 1%
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Transposable elements make up a major portion of DNA. Which American geneticist first gave evidence for these "wandering DNA segments", with her study of maize?
ANSWER: Barbara McClintock
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In grafting, what is the term for the twig grafted onto the main root system?
W) stock
X) plug
Y) node
Z) scion
ANSWER: Z) scion
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer When a shoot is cut, the cut end has a mass of dividing, undifferentiated cells called what?
ANSWER: callus
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which is NOT a method that animals excrete nitrogenous waste in?
W) nitrous acid
X) uric acid
Y) ammonia
Z) urea
ANSWER: W) nitrous acid
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer List the following three excretion methods from the least energy-intensive to the most energy-intensive: Uric acid, ammonia, urea.
ANSWER: ammonia, urea, uric acid
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which phase of an action potential involves blocked sodium gates and opened potassium gates?
W) Resting state
X) Rising phase
Y) Falling phase
Z) Depolarization
ANSWER: Y) Falling phase
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the "downtime" following an action potential when a second one cannot be initiated by ANY means possible called?
ANSWER: absolute refractory period

W) FSH
X) estradiol
Y) progesterone
Z) estrogen
ANSWER: X) estradiol
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Starting from menstruation, list the following phases of the uterine cycle: ovulation, proliferative phase, menstrual flow phase, secretory phase.
ANSWER: menstrual flow phase, proliferative phase, ovulation, secretory phase

W) plasmodial slime molds
X) cellular slime molds
Y) gymnamoebas
Z) entamoebas

X) ribosomes

Z) chromosomes

20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What cell organelle is responsible for synthesizing ribosomes, and are heavily pronounced in cells that produce many proteins, such as pancreatic cells?
