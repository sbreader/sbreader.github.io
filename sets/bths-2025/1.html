Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What two types of ions do salts break into during hydration?
ANSWER: CATIONS AND ANIONS
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which two ions are produced during the hydration of NH4NO3?
W) None
X) NH3 and HNO3
Y) NH4+ and NO3-
Z) NH3 and NO3-
ANSWER: Y) NH4+ and NO3-
2. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A perfect square can NOT have which of the following units digits?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 6
Z) 9
ANSWER: X) 2
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Out of the following three options, name all the solution(s) that satisfy cos(2x) + sin(x) = 0 I. π/2 II. 4π/3 III. 11π/6
ANSWER: I and III
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Rocks in a metamorphic facies would share which of the following?
W) Porosity
X) Mineral composition
Y) Metamorphic Grade
Z) Protolith
ANSWER: Y) Metamorphic Grade
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Ishtar terra and Aphrodite terra, both named after female deities, are among the most massive highlands of which planet in our solar system?
ANSWER: VENUS
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What happens to the redshift of galaxies as their distance from Earth increases?
ANSWER: INCREASES
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer If an object rotates at a rate of 6 revolutions per minute, How many revolutions does it make in 90 seconds?
ANSWER: 9
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A female patient has experienced delayed puberty, infertility, and heart defects. Through genetic testing, it is discovered that she is missing one X chromosome. Which of the following terms describes the genetic disorder she has?
W) Klinefelter Syndrome
X) Down Syndrome
Y) Turner Syndrome
Z) Kearns-Sayre Syndrome
ANSWER: Y) Turner Syndrome
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer The occurrence of an abnormal number of chromosomes in a cell, excluding occurrences in which complete sets are duplicated or missing, is known as what?
ANSWER: ANEUPLOIDY (do not accept: TRIPLOIDY; HAPLOIDY)
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of these is a weak electrolyte?
W) Potassium hydroxide
X) Sodium hydroxide
Y) Nitric acid
Z) Acetic acid
ANSWER: Z) Acetic acid
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of acids that dissociate in multiple steps?
ANSWER: POLYPROTIC ACIDS (also accept: ABLE DIPROTIC)
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many integers divisible by 3 lie between 37 and 73?
ANSWER: 12
7. BONUS Math Short Answer How many 4 digit palindromes are divisible by 3?
ANSWER: 30
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which principle states that present-day natural laws and processes have operated throughout geologic time?
ANSWER: UNIFORMITARIANISM
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following boundaries does subduction occur?
W) Convergent
X) Divergent
Y) Transform
Z) Continental
ANSWER: W) Convergent
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is true in order for an object to be in equilibrium?
W) A constant net force and a net torque of 0
X) A constant net force and a constant net torque
Y) A net force of 0 and a net torque of 0
Z) A net force of 0 and a constant net torque
ANSWER: Y) A net force of 0 and a net torque of 0
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer Which of the following statements are true? 1) Change in momentum is net force multiplied by time 2) kinetic energy is conserved in an inelastic collision 3) Pressure is defined as Force over Area
ANSWER: 1,3 (also accept: ALL BUT 2)
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By number or name, identify all the following nitrogenous bases that are considered pyrimidines 1) guanine 2) adenine 3) uracil 4) cytosine.
ANSWER: URACIL AND CYTOSINE (also accept: 3 AND 4)
10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following RNA codons signals a ribosome to stop protein translation?
W) UUC
X) UGU
Y) AGU
Z) UAA
ANSWER: Z) UAA
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following atoms has the least attraction for electrons?
W) Nitrogen
X) Chlorine
Y) Oxygen
Z) Fluorine
ANSWER: W) Nitrogen
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What color does the oxidation of copper metal with nitric acid produce?
ANSWER: GREEN
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The square root of 154 lies between the consecutive integers A and B. What is the sum of A and B?
ANSWER: 25
12. BONUS Math Short Answer Daniel drives for 80 miles at an average speed of 40 miles per hour, then continues for 120 miles at an average speed of 60 miles per hour. What is Daniel’s average speed, in miles per hour, for the entire trip?
ANSWER: 50
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Minerals composed of only one element are known as:
W) Single Elements
X) Silicates
Y) Carbonates
Z) Native Elements
ANSWER: Z) Native Elements
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What type of soils develop in humid regions such as the eastern United States and are rich in aluminum and iron oxides?
ANSWER: PEDALFER
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Wavelength is most commonly denoted by which greek letter?
ANSWER: LAMBDA
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer A unicycle accelerates to 30 meters per second starting from rest. It has a wheel with a radius of 4 meters. If it took 2 seconds to reach this speed, what is the angular acceleration of its wheel in radians per second squared?
ANSWER: 15/4 (also accept: 3.75)
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, name all of the following 4 factors that are considered biotic factors 1) water 2) soil 3) predators 4) parasites
ANSWER: PREDATORS AND PARASITES (also accept: 3 AND 4)
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Reproductive isolation refers to ecological barriers that prevent two species from producing viable and fertile offspring. What is the term for the type of reproductive isolation that results when the sperm of one species cannot be detected by the egg of the other?
ANSWER: GAMETIC ISOLATION (also accept: GAMETIC)
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer At 1 atm, in a 4 liter container, at 22 degrees Celsius, how many moles of nitrogen are present?
ANSWER: 0.33 MOLES (also accept: ⅓ mole)
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer To 5 significant figures, what is the molar volume of oxygen at 0 degrees Celsius and 1 atm?
ANSWER: 22.397
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the y-intercept of the function xcos(x)
ANSWER: 0
17. BONUS Math Short Answer How many positive integer divisors does 2025 have?
ANSWER: 15
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which soil horizon is also known as the zone of accumulation?
W) O
X) A
Y) B
Z) C
ANSWER: Y) B
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the granular ice formed when freshly fallen snow compacts and partly melts and refreezes?
ANSWER: FIRN
19. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In a simple harmonic oscillator, the force that brings an object back to the equilibrium position is known as a:
W) Returning Force
X) Restoring Force
Y) Equilibrium Force
Z) Frictional Force
ANSWER: X) Restoring Force
19. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following elementary particles has the LEAST mass?
W) Top quark
X) Bottom quark
Y) Up quark
Z) Strange quark
ANSWER: Y) Up quark
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, name all of the 4 following components of a DNA nucleotide 1) phosphate group 2) deoxyribose sugar 3) nitrogenous base 4) ribose sugar
ANSWER: PHOSPHATE GROUP, DEOXYRIBOSE SUGAR, NITROGENOUS BASE (also accept: 1, 2, 3; ALL EXCEPT 4)
20. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following enzymes allow for retroviruses to transcribe their RNA into DNA?
W) reverse transcriptase
X) reverse transcription protein
Y) transcription reverser
Z) helicase
ANSWER: W) reverse transcriptase
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a postulate of the Kinetic Molecular Theory? Kelvin temperature of the gas
W) The volume of individual particles is negligible
X) The particles are in constant random motion
Y) The average kinetic energy of a collection of gas particles is directly proportional to the
Z) Particles exert a small amount of force on each other
ANSWER: Z) Particles exert a small amount of force on each other
21. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the law that describes the distribution of velocities of particles in an ideal gas?
ANSWER: MAXWELL-BOLTZMAN DISTRIBUTION LAW
