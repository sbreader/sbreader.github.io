Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the scientific term for the ratio between the density of a given substance to that of water when both are at the same temperature?
ANSWER: SPECIFIC GRAVITY (also accept: RELATIVE DENSITY)
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following pure substances has the highest melting point at 1 atmosphere of pressure:
W) magnesium oxide
X) diamond
Y) sodium chloride
Z) cesium chloride
ANSWER: X) diamond
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the MOST common anatomical synonym for ventral, when locating a part of the human body in relation to another part?
ANSWER: ANTERIOR
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the names for the pyrimidine bases found in DNA?
ANSWER: CYTOSINE; THYMINE
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer According to one of the most common color triangles, if blue and green are primary colors, what is the third primary color?
ANSWER: RED
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the most common name for the pattern of light and dark lines produced when coherent light is passed through two horizontal slits in a thin opaque sheet, causing superposition of waves projected on a wall?
ANSWER: INTERFERENCE PATTERN (also accept: INTERFERENCE)
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the smaller of two integers whose sum is 19 and whose product is 48?
ANSWER: 3
4. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following numbers is evenly divisible by both 11 and 3:
W) 7791
X) 7553
Y) 5181
Z) 8769
ANSWER: Y) 5181
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is best classified as a plutonic intrusive rock:
W) obsidian
X) granite
Y) basalt
Z) pumice
ANSWER: X) granite
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The principle constituent of most granites is:
W) feldspar
X) muscovite
Y) calcite
Z) dolomite
ANSWER: W) feldspar
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice The related quantities charted on a line graph are most often called:
W) results
X) lines
Y) sets
Z) variables
ANSWER: Z) variables
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many grams of sodium chloride is in 2,000 milliliters of 0.9% NaCl solution?
ANSWER: 18
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What basic force of the universe is most responsible for holding the planets of our solar system in their orbits?
ANSWER: GRAVITY
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the proper names of the 5 celestial bodies called wanderers by Greek astronomers because they had unusual paths in the sky throughout the year?
ANSWER: MERCURY; VENUS; MARS; JUPITER; SATURN (also accept: in any order)
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Group 1 cations and group 7 anions combine to form:
W) metal alloys
X) alkali halides
Y) alkaloids
Z) organometallic compounds
ANSWER: X) alkali halides
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Give the chemical name for the compound formed when pure iron and pure sulfur are mixed together and heated:
ANSWER: IRON SULFIDE (also accept: IRON(II) SULFIDE; FERROUS SULFIDE)
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer From what chamber of the human heart does blood carried by the left pulmonary artery leave?
ANSWER: RIGHT VENTRICLE
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What human gland or organ produces tri-iodothyronine?
ANSWER: THYROID
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A white-colored object illuminated by a green light will appear as what color to the human eye?
ANSWER: GREEN
10. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A child learns that mixing approximately equal amounts of the paint colors orange and green produces brown. This is an example of:
W) additive color mixing
X) summative color generation
Y) subtractive color synthesis
Z) neutral coloration
ANSWER: Y) subtractive color synthesis
11. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice One-fifth of 0.04% is equal to:
W) 8 × 10-2
X) 8 × 10-3
Y) 8 × 10-4
Z) 8 × 10-5
ANSWER: Z) 8 × 10-5
11. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the prime factorization of 240:
ANSWER: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 5 (also accept: 24 × 3 × 5)
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice When spreading centers reach above the ocean’s surface, they typically form which of the following types of volcanoes:
W) shield
X) composite
Y) stratovolcano
Z) cinder cone
ANSWER: W) shield
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Mount Vesuvius in Italy is a typical:
W) mud volcano
X) caldera
Y) cinder cone
Z) composite volcano
ANSWER: Z) composite volcano
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The fertilizer potash provides plants with what element necessary for healthy growth and which is also the seventh most abundant element on Earth?
ANSWER: POTASSIUM (also accept: K)
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer From what protein is gelatin, which is used as a food additive, most commonly derived?
ANSWER: COLLAGEN
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer First proposed by Georges Lemaitre, what is the name, most likely coined by Fred Hoyle, for the theory that the universe originated at a finite time many eons ago from an extremely compressed hot state?
ANSWER: BIG BANG
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In what constellation is the star Polaris found?
ANSWER: URSA MINOR (do not accept: LITTLE DIPPER)
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the pH of a solution with a pOH of 6.8 at 25°C?
ANSWER: 7.2
15. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following substances is water soluble:
W) ethanol
X) dichloro-methane
Y) chloroform
Z) benzene
ANSWER: W) ethanol
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Yersinia, Drosophila and Homo are all what taxonomical category?
ANSWER: GENUS (also accept: GENERA)
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer From what monosaccharide is cellulose primarily composed?
ANSWER: GLUCOSE (also accept: β-D-GLUCOPYRANOSYL; D-GLUCOSE)
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Although in many respects difficult to define, what term is MOST often described as a push or a pull that can cause an object to accelerate?
ANSWER: FORCE
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the MOST common term for the inwardly directed force exerted on an object to keep the object moving in a circle?
ANSWER: CENTRIPETAL (also accept: CENTRIPETAL FORCE)
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Giving your answer in centimeters, what is the length of a side of a square whose diagonal measures 12√2 centimeters?
ANSWER: 12
18. BONUS Math Short Answer Multiply the following 4 numbers and give your answer in scientific notation: 30,000 × 3,000 × 30 × 0.1
ANSWER: 2.7 × 108
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the oldest Eon on the Geological Time Scale within which the first organisms appeared between 550 million and 3.4 billion years ago?
ANSWER: PRECAMBRIAN
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Homo sapiens evolved during which geological era of the Geological Time Scale?
ANSWER: CENOZOIC
20. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Anthropogenic means:
W) generated by humans
X) created many years ago
Y) carried in primate genes
Z) harmful to elderly people
ANSWER: W) generated by humans
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The primary anthropogenic cause of acid rain is the emission of what 2 gases?
ANSWER: SULFUR OXIDES, NITROGEN OXIDES (also accept: SULFUR DIOXIDE AND NITROGEN DIOXIDE; NITROGEN DIOXIDE AND SULFUR DIOXIDE; NITROGEN OXIDES, SULFUR OXIDES; SO2 AND NO2) (do not accept: SULFURIC ACID; NITRIC ACID; SULFURIC ACID or NITRIC ACID)
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the primary elemental product of stellar hydrogen fusion?
ANSWER: HELIUM
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In the equatorial celestial coordinate system, what terms are synonymous with longitude and latitude, respectively?
ANSWER: LONGITUDE = RIGHT ASCENSION; LATITUDE = DECLINATION
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the atomic number for the ground state element with the following electron configuration: 1s2 2s2 2p5
ANSWER: 9
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many grams of KCl, with a molar mass of 75, are needed to make 1 liter of a 3 molar solution?
ANSWER: 225
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Giving your answer in standard form, what is the last term in the binomial expansion of the following: (x + y)6
ANSWER: y6
23. BONUS Math Short Answer Simplify the following expression by combining like terms: 12.03A – 4.03B – 0.03(A – 40)
ANSWER: 12A – 4.03B + 1.2
24. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What specific law of motion most directly implies that every action has an equal and opposite reaction?
ANSWER: NEWTON’S THIRD LAW (also accept: THIRD LAW)
24. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Jay walks to his car and drives to the store. The trip takes 20 minutes. Jay figures how far he traveled on foot and by car and divides the distance by how long the trip took. Which of the following BEST describes what Jay has computed:
W) total velocity
X) average acceleration
Y) average speed
Z) instantaneous velocity
ANSWER: Y) average speed
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The sinoatrial node is located in what human organ?
ANSWER: HEART
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer In what chamber of the human heart is the sinoatrial node located?
ANSWER: RIGHT ATRIUM
