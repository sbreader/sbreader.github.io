1. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Currently, almost all used nuclear fuel from power plants in the U.S. is stored at which of the following?
W) The power plant site
X) A high-level waste repository
Y) A low-level waste storage facility
Z) A monitored retrieval storage facility
ANSWER: W) The power plant site
1. BONUS Energy Short Answer Rank the following heat sources in order of increasing efficiency 1) Geothermal heat pump 2) Electric space heater 3) Combustion furnace.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many oxygen atoms are there in a peroxide ion?
ANSWER: 2
2. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A ball is thrown into the air with a velocity of 3 meters per second to the east and 4 meters per second away from the Earth. At the top of its arc, what is the velocity of the ball in meters per second?
W) 5, up and east
X) 5, east
Y) 3, up and east
Z) 3, east
ANSWER: Z) 3, east
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Bacteria or fungi are commonly grown in a lab on what semi-solid substance?
ANSWER: AGAR (also accept: AGAR PLATES, NUTRIENT AGAR)
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What unit is most frequently used to measure the thickness of the ozone layer?
ANSWER: DOBSON UNITS
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer To the nearest whole number, how many days will it take to travel 4800 miles traveling at a constant speed of 60 miles per hour?
ANSWER: 3
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Given the arithmetic sequence with first three terms –12, –10, –8, what is the 60th term?
ANSWER: 106
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Fulgurites are tubes of fused silica produced during what type of natural event?
ANSWER: LIGHTNING (also accept: A LARGE ELECTRICAL DISCHARGE)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following sectors of American society uses the greatest amount of freshwater?
W) Agriculture
X) Mining
Y) Industry
Z) Public use
ANSWER: W) Agriculture
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In fishes, what structures, located in fluid-filled sacs in the inner ear, stimulate sensory cells lining the sacs that convert mechanical to electrical energy?
ANSWER: OTOLITHS (also accept: EARSTONES)
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Although not scientifically verified, which of the following plant communities has been hypothesized to utilize allelopathy?
W) Chaparral
X) Grassland
Y) Kelp forest
Z) Tundra
ANSWER: W) Chaparral
7. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following processes is NOT used to produce hydrogen gas fuel?
W) Gasification
X) Electrolysis
Y) Dry milling
Z) Fermentation
ANSWER: Y) Dry milling
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following chemical processes combines methane with water to produce hydrogen gas and carbon monoxide?
W) Gas synthesis
X) Incomplete combustion
Y) Decomposition
Z) Steam reforming
ANSWER: Z) Steam reforming
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An engine does 10 joules of work while exhausting 40 joules of waste heat. What is the percent efficiency of the engine?
ANSWER: 20
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two identical metallic spheres, the first with 5 coulombs of charge and the second with -3 coulombs of charge, are touched together for an instant. What is the charge on the first sphere, in coulombs, after the two spheres touch?
ANSWER: 1
9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following fractions is largest?
W) 8/3
X) 9/4
Y) 12/5
Z) 16/7
ANSWER: W) 8/3
9. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the remainder when the polynomial x6 – x4 + x + 3 is divided by x + 2?
ANSWER: 49
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice If you were looking at isobars on a weather map showing high latitudes and high altitudes, which of the following would indicate the fastest wind speed?
W) 4-millibar lines over a 100-kilometer spacing
X) 4-millibar lines over a 50-kilometer spacing
Y) 2-millibar lines over a 100-kilometer spacing
Z) 2-millibar lines over a 50-kilometer spacing

10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Categorize the following three plate pairs by whether they meet at divergent, convergent, or transform boundaries 1) North American and Eurasian plates 2) Indian and Eurasian plates 3) Australian and Pacific plates.
ANSWER: 1-DIVERGENT; 2-CONVERGENT; 3-CONVERGENT
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What portion of the human digestive system is responsible for nearly all nutrient absorption?
W) Cecum
X) Stomach
Y) Small intestine
Z) Large intestine

X) Heartwood rings
Y) Phloem rings
Z) Xylem rings
ANSWER: Z) Xylem rings
12. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Randomized block design differs from complete randomization by:
W) Blocking or eliminating ineligible experimental subjects prior to treatment
X) Blocking or eliminating results from a randomized treatment that are too far outside the acceptable results
Y) Sorting experimental subjects into homogenous groups prior to treatment
Z) Sorting experimental subjects into homogenous groups based on results
ANSWER: Y) Sorting experimental subjects into homogenous groups prior to treatment
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is a crescograph used to measure?
ANSWER: PLANT GROWTH RATE

W) Anthropogenic pollution
X) Higher evaporation than precipitation
Y) High surface run-off
Z) No freshwater inputs
ANSWER: X) Higher evaporation than precipitation
13. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of the Kuiper belt?
