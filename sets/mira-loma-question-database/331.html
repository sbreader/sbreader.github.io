1. BONUS Math Short Answer What two derivative rules can be used to prove that the derivative of sec x = tan x sec x?
ANSWER: QUOTIENT RULE AND CHAIN RULE
2. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes where the absolute maximum would be of a parabola that opens down?
W) axis of symmetry
X) wherever the x value is greatest
Y) the vertex
Z) above the parabola
ANSWER: Y) the vertex
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Suppose the vertex of a parabola is given by the point (4,6). What is the equation of the axis of symmetry?
ANSWER: X = 4
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the arc length of an arc with an angle of 60 degrees and a radius of 8 centimeters?
ANSWER: 8 π / 3 centimeters
3. BONUS Math Short Answer The distance formula is sometimes referred to as a rewritten version of which theorem?
ANSWER: PYTHAGOREAN THEOREM
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the derivative of y = x with respect to x?
ANSWER: dy/dx = 1 (also accept: y' = 1)
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Suppose x is an angle with a measure of 35 degrees. What is the value of sin2 x + cos2 x?
ANSWER: 1
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the area of an equilateral triangle with a side length of 6 centimeters?
ANSWER: 9√3 square centimeters
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area of a regular hexagon with a side length of 6 centimeters?
ANSWER: 54√3 square centimeters
6. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Consider the rational function (x2 + 5x + 6)/(x + 2). Which of the following statements is true?
W) There is a vertical asymptote at x = -2
X) There is a horizontal asymptote at y = -2
Y) There is a point of discontinuity at (-2, 1)
Z) The graph of the function is a parabola
ANSWER: Y) There is a point of discontinuity at (-2, 1)
6. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the equation of the horizontal asymptote of the function (2x6 + 5x3 + 91x)/(3x6)?
ANSWER: y = 2/3
7. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of these is the name of the 3rd derivative of the position function?
W) velocity function
X) snap function
Y) jerk function
Z) acceleration function
ANSWER: Y) jerk function
7. BONUS Math Short Answer An object moves in a straight line according the position function f(t) = 3t2 + 2t3 − 4t where the displacement is measured in meters. What is the acceleration of the object when t = 2 seconds?
ANSWER: 30 m/s2
8. BONUS Math Short Answer In the closed interval [0, 2π ], measured in radians, for what two values of A makes it such that sin A is equal to cos A?
ANSWER: π/4 and 5π/4
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A triangle has two angles with measures 30 degrees and 60 degrees with corresponding sides with lengths 3 cm and 3√3 cm respectively. What is the length of the third side?
ANSWER: 6 cm
9. BONUS Math Multiple Choice A t-shirt was originally priced at $90. It was sold for $109.80 to a customer. How much of a price increase did the shirt undergo? Give your answer as a percent.
W) 23%
X) 22%
Y) 20%
Z) 30%
ANSWER: X) 22%
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Solve for x if logx 256 = 4.
ANSWER: x = 4
10. BONUS Math Short Answer An exponential function is an inverse of which function?
ANSWER: LOGARITHMIC FUNCTION
11. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the equation of the line with a slope of 2 which passes through the origin in slope-intercept form?
ANSWER: y = 2x
12. BONUS Math Short Answer The imaginary number was first defined by who?
ANSWER: RAFAEL BOMBELLI
13. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the fifth letter of the Greek alphabet?
W) zeta
X) delta
Y) epsilon
Z) phi
ANSWER: Y) epsilon
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Augustin-Louis Cauchy's definition of the limit involves which two Greek letters?
ANSWER: EPSILON AND DELTA
14. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The value sin (2x) is equal to which of the following?
W) 1-tan x
X) 2 sin x tan x
Y) 1-cos x
Z) 2 sin x cos x
ANSWER: Z) 2 sin x cos x
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Consider f(x) = sin x + 1. What is the only root in the closed interval [0, 2π ]?
ANSWER: 3π/2
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Which theorem states that no three positive integers can satisfy the equation a n + b n = c n for any value of n greater than 2?
ANSWER: FERMAT'S LAST THEOREM
15. BONUS Math Short Answer Fermat proposed this theorem in a book called what?
ANSWER: ARITHMETICA
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Which conjecture states that every even integer greater than 2 can be expressed as a sum of two prime numbers?
ANSWER: GOLDBACH'S CONJECTURE
16. BONUS Math Multiple Choice According to the Collatz Conjecture, if you start with any arbitrary positive integer and if you multiply an odd number by 3 then add 1, and if you divide an even number by 2, you will always end up at what number?
W) 0
X) 2
Y) 1
Z) 3
ANSWER: Y) 1
17. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The equation y = √(25 − x2) is an equation of which of the following?
W) ellipse
X) hyperbola
Y) semicircle
Z) circle
ANSWER: Y) semicircle
17. BONUS Math Short Answer The equation y = √(25 − x2) has a radius of what?
ANSWER: 5
18. BONUS Math Short Answer The slope of the line tangent to a function is known more commonly as what?
ANSWER: DERIVATIVE OF A FUNCTION
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What are the two primary topics in calculus?
ANSWER: DIFFERENTIATION AND INTEGRATION
19. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area under a curve more commonly referred to as?
ANSWER: INTEGRAL OF THE FUNCTION
20. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following accurately describes the relationship between the continuity in a function and the derivative of the function?
W) If a function is continuous in a given interval, the function is also differentiable in that interval
X) If a function is differentiable in a given interval, the function is also continuous in that interval
Y) There is nothing in common between differentiability and continuity
Z) Continuity and differentiability don't exist for a function
ANSWER: X) If a function is differentiable in a given interval, the function is also continuous in that interval
20. BONUS Math Short Answer Describe the continuity of the function y = x2 + x + 2.
ANSWER: CONTINUOUS EVERYWHERE
21. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A regular hexagon consists of which of the following?
W) 2 regular trapezoids
X) 6 isosceles triangles
Y) 6 right triangles
Z) 6 equilateral triangles
ANSWER: Z) 6 equilateral triangles
21. BONUS Math Short Answer Suppose there is a bag with 6 marbles. 2 are red, 2 are blue and 2 are green. What is the probability that two reds will be picked consecutively without replacement?
ANSWER: 1/15
22. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following sets is the largest?
W) natural numbers
X) decimals
Y) integers
Z) rational numbers
ANSWER: Z) rational numbers
22. BONUS Math Short Answer Who is known as the father of trigonometry?
ANSWER: HIPPARCHUS
23. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice If set A is defined as {1, 2, 3} and set B is defined as {9,10,11}, then what is A ∪ B?
W) {1,2,3}
X) {9,10,11}
Y) the set does not exist
Z) {1,2,3,9,10,11}
ANSWER: Z) {1,2,3,9,10,11}
23. BONUS Math Short Answer Which math subject is literally translated to Ancient Greek as "Earth-measuring"?
ANSWER: GEOMETRY
24. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Euler's formula states that eix is equal to what
ANSWER: cos x + i sin x
25. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Differential calculus and integral calculus are related by which theorem?
ANSWER: FUNDAMENTAL THEOREM OF CALCULUS
25. BONUS Math Short Answer Who first introduced modular arithmetic?
ANSWER: Carl Friedrich Gauss
