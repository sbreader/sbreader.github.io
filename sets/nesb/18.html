1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Low, layered gray clouds that produce light precipitation are called
W) cumulus.
X) stratus.
Y) cirrus.
Z) nimbus.
ANSWER: X) stratus.
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The tropical rain forest habitat covers only about seven percent of the Earth’s surface but contains what percent of Earth’s species?
W) 50 to 80
X) 25 to 40
Y) 10 to 25

ANSWER: W) 50 to 80

Z) extra large tunicates.
ANSWER: X) crown-of-thorns starfish.
