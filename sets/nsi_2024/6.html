1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Oxygenation of the blood in the lungs increases hemoglobin’s affinity for oxygen over carbon dioxide. What is the name given to this effect?
ANSWER: HALDANE EFFECT
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In the glycolytic pathway, Fructose 1,6 bisphosphate is broken down into glyceraldehyde 3 phosphate and what other compound which can be isomerized into another glyceraldehyde 3 phosphate?

X) Phosphoenol Pyruvate
Y) Phosphoglyceraldehyde
Z) 1, 3 Bisphosphoglycerate

6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab are studying the lac operon. In the lac operon of E. coli, lactose serves as an inducer that enables the transcription of genes involved in lactose metabolism. However, the presence of glucose inhibits the lac operon even if lactose is available. What is the name of the regulatory mechanism that allows glucose to inhibit the lac operon?
ANSWER: catabolite repression
