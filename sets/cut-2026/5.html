Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer During embryonic development, traits such as webbed fingers undergo what process referred to as programmed cell death?
ANSWER: apoptosis
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following 3 cytoskeletal elements in increasing size 1) Intermediate filaments 2) Microtubules 3) Microfilaments
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the correct noble gas electron configuration of sulfur?
W) [Ne] 3s2 3p1
X) [Ne] 3s2 3p2
Y) [Ne] 3s2 3p3
Z) [Ne] 3s2 3p4
ANSWER: Z) [Ne] 3s2 3p4
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 species that would be present in the net ionic equation of a neutralization reaction between nitric acid and sodium hydroxide 1) H+ 2) OH- 3) H2O
ANSWER: all
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A particle’s position as a function of time is given by the function s(t) = 2t3 – 3t2 + 8t – 5. In meters per second squared, what is the particle’s acceleration after 3 seconds?
ANSWER: 30
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice According to the first law of thermodynamics, which of the following pairs of movement of heat and work done guarantees a negative change in internal energy?
W) heat absorbed by system, work done on system
X) heat absorbed by system, work done by system
Y) heat released by system, work done on system
Z) heat released by system, work done by system
ANSWER: Z) heat released by system, work done by system
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Denoted by alternating red semicircles and blue triangles on opposite sides of a line, which of the following types of fronts is usually the mildest?
W) warm
X) cold
Y) stationary
Z) occluded
ANSWER: Y) stationary
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, order the following 4 eons in the geologic time from earliest to latest 1) Proterozoic 2) Phanerozoic 3) Hadean 4) Archaean
ANSWER: 3, 4, 1, 2
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Consider the statement: “If it rained yesterday, then it will be sunny today.” Which of the following statements must ALWAYS be true given that the initial statement is true?
W) If it is sunny today, then it rained yesterday
X) If it did not rain yesterday, then it is not sunny today
Y) If it is not sunny today, then it did not rain yesterday
Z) If it rained yesterday, then it is not sunny today
ANSWER: Y) If it is not sunny today, then it did not rain yesterday
5. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following statements describes the concavity of y = x2?
W) Concave up
X) Concave down
Y) Concave up and concave down
Z) No concavity
ANSWER: W) Concave up
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge are studying the behavior of electrons. What phenomenon, used in electron microscopes and modern integrated circuits, arises from the wave-particle duality of electrons and allows them to overcome a classically forbidden barrier?
ANSWER: quantum tunneling
6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying quantum chromodynamics. Which of the following particles mediates the interaction that holds hadrons together?
W) Gravitons
X) Mesons
Y) Photons
Z) Gluons
ANSWER: Z) Gluons
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer During aerobic cellular respiration, what diatomic molecule is the final electron acceptor?
ANSWER: OXYGEN (also accept: O2)
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 organelles that are part of the endomembrane system 1) Lysosomes 2) Vesicles 3) Golgi Apparatus
ANSWER: all
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following types of bonds is characterized by atoms arranged in a sea of delocalized electrons?
W) polar covalent
X) nonpolar covalent
Y) ionic
Z) metallic
ANSWER: Z) metallic
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following describes the work done in a bomb calorimeter?
W) zero because it is isobaric
X) zero because it is isochoric
Y) nonzero because it is isobaric
Z) nonzero because it is isochoric
ANSWER: X) zero because it is isochoric
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A calorie is based on which of the following properties of water?
W) density
X) thermal conductivity
Y) specific heat
Z) latent heat
ANSWER: Y) specific heat
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer A thin converging lens has a focal length of 10 centimeters. An object is placed 30 centimeters in front of the lens along the principal axis. In centimeters, what is the image distance from the lens?
ANSWER: 15
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of tides occur when the gravitational forces of the Moon and Sun act on Earth at right angles?
ANSWER: neap tides
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 stages of a Sun-like main sequence star in chronological order 1) T-Tauri 2) Red Giant 3) White Dwarf
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
11. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is the period of the function y = sin(2πx)?
W) 1/2
X) 1
Y) 2
Z) 4
ANSWER: X) 1
11. BONUS Math Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 trigonometric functions that are even 1) sin(x) 2) cos(x) 3) tan(x)
ANSWER: 2 only
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists are studying the high boiling points of glycerol compounds due to their ability to form hydrogen bonds. Identify all of the following 3 elements that can form hydrogen bonds 1) nitrogen 2) oxygen 3) fluorine
ANSWER: all
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are studying different types of fluid flow. What type of idealized fluid flow is characterized by smooth, parallel layers with no mixing between them?
ANSWER: laminar flow
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following three components of the digestive tract which food passes through from first to last 1) Stomach 2) Small intestine 3) Large intestine
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three structures that prokaryotes can possess 1) golgi apparatus 2) nucleoid 3) cell wall
ANSWER: 2 and 3
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In a hypothetical reaction C + U yields T, doubling the concentration of C quadruples the concentration of T and doubling the concentration of U doubles the concentration of T. What is the overall order of this reaction?
ANSWER: 3
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 3 types of bonds in increasing strength 1) sigma bond 2) pi bond 3) delta bond
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A resistor with resistance 4 ohms is connected across a potential difference of 8 volts. In watts, what is the power dissipated by the resistor?
ANSWER: 16
15. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice By what factor will the energy transported by a wave change if its amplitude is tripled?
W) 1/3
X) 1
Y) 3
Z) 9
ANSWER: Z) 9

W) top left
X) top right
Y) bottom left
Z) bottom right
ANSWER: Z) bottom right
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 types of volcanoes in increasing viscosity of magma 1) composite 2) cinder cone 3) shield

ANSWER: 25/102
17. BONUS Math Short Answer A right triangle has legs of length 5 and 12. If theta is the angle opposite the side of length 5, what is the co-secant of theta?
ANSWER: 13/5
18. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab are utilizing machine learning to predict energy usage for 2026. Which of the following types of machine learning involves algorithms learning patterns and structures from only unlabeled data?
W) supervised
X) unsupervised
Y) semi-supervised
Z) reinforcement
ANSWER: X) unsupervised
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are using UV-Vis spectroscopy to quantify dye concentrations in wastewater. What law states that absorbance is directly proportional to concentration?
ANSWER: Beer-Lambert law (also accept: beer’s law)
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A population of turtles are living on a small island. If an earthquake created a boundary that split the two populations, which of the following types of speciation would most likely occur?
W) Allopatric
X) Parapatric
Y) Peripatric
Z) Sympatric
ANSWER: W) Allopatric

ANSWER: Porifera

ANSWER: 20

W) visual
X) spectroscopic
Y) eclipsing

ANSWER: 13
