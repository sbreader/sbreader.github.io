1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Select ALL of the following plant cell types that are living at functional maturity 1) Sieve-tube element 2) Collenchyma 3) Tracheid
ANSWER: 1 and 2
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Students at Saratoga High School are investigating flowering patterns in plants. If a flower has a critical night length of 14 hours, under which of the following conditions would it flower?
W) Eight hours of night, a short flash of red light, and seven more hours of night.
X) Five hours of night, a short flash of far-red light, a short flash of red light, and ten more hours of night.
Y) Twelve uninterrupted hours of night.
Z) Seven hours of night, a short flash of red light, a short flash of far-red light, and seven more hours of night.
ANSWER: Z) Seven hours of night, a short flash of red light, a short flash of far-red light, and seven more hours of night.

20. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Despite their high surface areas, Jovian planets can retain very light gases such as hydrogen and helium. Which of the following best explains this?
