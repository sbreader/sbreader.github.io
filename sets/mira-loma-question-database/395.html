Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The distance from the Earth to the Sun is
W) 100 miles
X) 1 light-year
Y) 1 astronomical unit
Z) 10 astronomical units
ANSWER: Y) 1 astronomical unit
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer How many kilometers are in one astronomical unit? Express your answer to 2 significant figures.
ANSWER: 150 000 000 (150 million)
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice How many earthquakes, magnitude 4.0 -4.9, are estimated to occur annually?
W) 100
X) 25000
Y) 1200
Z) 13000
ANSWER: Z) 13000
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What would be the amplitude of an earthquake with 10 000 times the amplitude of an earthquake with an amplitude of 2.3?
ANSWER: 6.3
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice An aseismic fault is a fault on which
W) earthquakes occur every year
X) earthquakes never occur
Y) earthquakes occur every hour
Z) earthquakes happen as aftershocks of other earthquakes
ANSWER: X) earthquakes never occur
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Do earthquakes occur on seismic faults?
ANSWER: Yes
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice If an earthquake with a magnitude of 7.0 occurs, what would be a possible amplitude of an aftershock?
W) 6.0
X) 7.1
Y) 7.5
Z) 9.2
ANSWER: W) 6.0
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer If an aftershock occurs that is larger than the mainshock of an earthquake, then the aftershock becomes the new mainshock and the original mainshock becomes
ANSWER: the foreshock
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Earthquakes can occur along plate boundaries, within plates, and on
W) vaults
X) mountains
Y) lines
Z) faults
ANSWER: Z) faults
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The three types of faults are
ANSWER: normal (faults), thrust (faults), and strike-slip (faults)
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The distance that light travels in 1 year is called 1
W) mile
X) light-year
Y) light-day
Z) astronomical unit
ANSWER: X) light-year
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the diameter of the milky way galaxy?
ANSWER: 100 000 light years
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice About how far apart are the Milky Way and Andromeda Galaxies?
W) 20 million kilometers
X) 2 million astronomical units
Y) 2 million light-years
Z) 40 million light-years
ANSWER: Y) 2 million light-years
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer When is it estimated that the Milky Way and Andromeda Galaxies will collide?
ANSWER: in 4 billion years
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the average rate of motion across the San Andreas Fault
W) 12 inches/month
X) 34 centimeters/year
Y) 56 millimeters/year
Z) 0.92 feet/year
ANSWER: Y) 56 millimeters/year
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer If the San Andreas Fault continues to move at this rate, how many millions of years will it take until San Francisco is adjacent to Los Angeles?
ANSWER: 15
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Do earthquakes occur on the moon?
ANSWER: Yes
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the cause of earthquakes on the moon?
ANSWER: TIDAL STRESSES
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the cause of tidal waves
ANSWER: the gravitational interactions between the Sun, Moon, and Earth
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Himalaya-Karakoram Mountain Range is
W) the world's longest land mountain range
X) the world's lowest land mountain range
Y) the world's greatest land mountain range
Z) the world's least land mountain range
ANSWER: Y) the world's greatest land mountain range
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Of the 109 peaks in the world over 7,317 meters, how many are in the Himalaya-Karakoram?
ANSWER: 96
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is a measured value of an earthquake's size?
W) magnitude
X) amplitude
Y) intensity
Z) direction
ANSWER: W) magnitude
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Does the magnitude of an earthquake change by location?
ANSWER: No
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Each fault of the Wasatch Range has the capability to produce an earthquake of what magnitude?
ANSWER: 7.5
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice From the years 1975-1995, how many states in the United States of America did not have any earthquakes?
W) six
X) twenty
Y) two
Z) four
ANSWER: Z) four
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What four states in the United States of America did not have any earthquakes from the years 1975-1995?
ANSWER: Florida, Iowa, North Dakota, and Wisconsin
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Most earthquakes occur at depths of
W) less than 80 km from the Earth's surface
X) more than 20 km from the Earth's surface
Y) between 10 km and 125 km from the Earth's surface
Z) less than 5 km from the Earth's surface
ANSWER: W) less than 80 km from the Earth's surface
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Earthquakes that occur in the middle of plates and not at plate boundaries are called
ANSWER: intraplate (earthquakes)
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice About how long is the San Andreas Fault System?
W) 1,200 km
X) 1,300 km
Y) 800 km
Z) 10 km
ANSWER: X) 1,300 km
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Is the San Andreas Fault a single, continuous fault?
ANSWER: No
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice At what depths do the world's deepest earthquakes occur?
W) 120 km below the surface of the Earth
X) 560 km below the surface of the Earth
Y) 750 km below the surface of the Earth
Z) 1100 km below the surface of the Earth
ANSWER: Y) 750 km below the surface of the Earth
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Andes Mountain Range, in South America, is
W) the world's shortest land mountain range
X) the world's smallest land mountain range
Y) the world's longest land mountain range
Z) the world's biggest land mountain range
ANSWER: Y) the world's longest land mountain range
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What state in the United States of America is most earthquake-prone state
W) California
X) Texas
Y) Alaska
Z) New York
ANSWER: Y) Alaska
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer On average, how often does Alaska receive a magnitude 8 or higher earthquake
ANSWER: every 14 years
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice When the Chilean earthquake occurred in 1960, seismographs recorded seismic waves that traveled
W) through Chile
X) through South America
Y) all around the Pacific Ocean
Z) all around the Earth
ANSWER: Z) all around the Earth
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the phenomenon of seismic waves created by a single earthquake going all through the Earth called?
ANSWER: free oscillation (of the Earth)
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The first waves of an earthquake to be detected by seismographs are
W) S waves
X) P waves
Y) Love waves
Z) T waves
ANSWER: X) P waves
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer P waves are also called
ANSWER: compressional waves
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of plate boundaries are at mid-oceanic ridges?
W) divergent plate boundaries
X) subduction plate boundaries
Y) transform plate boundaries
Z) suction plate boundaries
ANSWER: W) divergent plate boundaries
24. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The mid-Atlantic Ridge is an example of a
ANSWER: mid-ocean(ic) ridge
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of volcanoes are formed at hot spots
W) stratovolcanoes
X) cinder cones
Y) shield volcanoes
Z) composite volcanoes
ANSWER: Y) shield volcanoes
25. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Shield volcanoes usually have what type of slopes?
ANSWER: gentle (slopes)
