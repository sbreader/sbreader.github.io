1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A 60-kilogram man holding a 20-kilogram box rides on a skateboard at a speed of positive 7 meters per second. He throws the box behind him, giving it a velocity of negative 5 meters per second, with respect to the ground. What is his velocity, in meters per second, after throwing the object?
W) 8
X) 9
Y) 10
Z) 11
ANSWER: Z) 11

W) Ampere’s Law
X) Faraday’s Law
Y) Gauss’ Law
Z) Ohm’s Law

2. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice The International Thermonuclear Experimental Reactor, commonly called ITER has been designed to produce 500 million watts of output power and is currently scheduled to begin plasma experiments in 2020. X) Inertial confinement

Y) Dendroclimatology

ANSWER: 2.5

ANSWER: 1, 2, AND 4

X) If not p, then q
Y) If not p, then not q
