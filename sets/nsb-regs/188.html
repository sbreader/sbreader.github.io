1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true about moments of inertia 1) Axes of rotation that pass through the center-of-mass minimize the moment of inertia 2) For planar objects, the perpendicular moment is dependent only on the in-plane moments 3) The moment of inertia increases as mass becomes more radially spread out.

9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice When radium-226 decays into radon-222, it emits an alpha particle as well as a gamma ray. Which of the following best describes this nuclear decay process?
