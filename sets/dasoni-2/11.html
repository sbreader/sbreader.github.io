1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Isostatic subsidence can result from a large glacier adding weight to the lithosphere, causing it to sink deeper in the mantle. What is the name for the nearby region that increases in elevation, instead of sinking?
ANSWER: FOREBULGE
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following compounds that are aromatic 1. cyclubutadiene 2. furan 3. cyclodecapentaene
ANSWER: 2 and 3 (also accept: all but 1)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three quantities that when increased, will increase the Q factor of a series RLC circuit. 1) Resistance 2) Capacitance 3) Inductance.
ANSWER: 3 only
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The real line is constructed by laying a countable number of unit line segments end-to-end. An extension of the real line is constructed from an uncountable number of unit line segments. What is the name of this construction?
ANSWER: The Long Line (Alexandroff line)
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What root-like extensions are produced by parasitic plants and fungi to absorb nutrients and water from host tissues?
ANSWER: Haustoria
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following would not be characteristic of a relatively warm vertical column of troposphere near the equator?
W) Strong vertical mixing
X) Abundant Precipitation
Y) High Tropopause
Z) Low Planetary Boundary Layer
ANSWER: Z) Low Planetary Boundary Layer
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice How many lattice points are contained in the conventional unit cell of a body-centered-cubic lattice?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 3
Z) 4
ANSWER: X) 2
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Yajur is studying crystals. What is the term for primitive unit cell of a crystal consisting of one lattice point and all points closer to that lattice point than any other lattice point in the crystal?
ANSWER: Wigner-Seitz cell
9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes Godel's incompleteness theorem?
W) Math is based on a logical inconsistency that cannot be resolved
X) Any system of math will have unprovable but true theorems
Y) Any system of math will have an inconsistency
Z) The axiom of choice is required for a consistent system of math
ANSWER: X) Any system of math will have unprovable but true theorems
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which phase in the recording of an electrocardiogram corresponds to ventricular depolarization?
W) P wave
X) QRS complex
Y) ST interval
Z) T wave
ANSWER: X) QRS complex
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the only natural satellite in the solar system that is not tidally locked to its host planet and is characterized by chaotic, irregular motion?
ANSWER: HYPERION
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In a second-order phase transition, identify all of the following quantities that are discontinuous at the phase boundary 1) Heat capacity 2) Gibbs free energy 3) Entropy
ANSWER: 1 and 3
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following that must be true for a process to be thermodynamically reversible 1) It must be quasistatic 2) It must decrease the entropy of the system 3) It must be endothermic
ANSWER: 1 only
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What term is used to describe a set that is both open and closed?
ANSWER: Clopen
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In vertebrate limb development, what posterior structure secretes Sonic hedgehog to establish the anterior-posterior axis?
ANSWER: Zone of polarizing activity (also accept: ZPA)
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 events in chronological order of when they occurred in the universe's history. 1) Neutrino decoupling 2) Dark Ages 3) Big Bang Nucleosynthesis
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In the Hartree-Fock method of approximating wave functions of quantum many-body systems, what specific mathematical tool is used to generate the fully antisymmetric wave function for fermions?
ANSWER: Slater determinant
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What force arises from the interaction of an accelerating charged particle with its own radiation?
ANSWER: Abraham-Lorentz force
19. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is a continuous function that is not differentiable anywhere along its domain?
W) sinc(x)
X) one-parameter Mittag-Leffler function
Y) Weierstrass function
Z) Hadamard's gamma function
ANSWER: Y) Weierstrass function
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Chymotrypsin is a proteolytic enzyme located in the small intestine secreted by the pancreas. Its proteolytic ability comes from the action of what canonical amino acid sequence, which can facilitate cooperative nucleophilic reactions?
ANSWER: Catalytic triad (also accept: serine, histidine, aspartate)
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Moulins are englacial channels that can bring supraglacial meltwater to the base of a glacier, increasing its velocity, a phenomenon known as basal sliding. What is the term for the effect that creates basal sliding through this mechanism?
ANSWER: ZWALLY EFFECT
22. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following categories of non-Newtonian fluids does oobleck belong to?
W) Rheopectic
X) Thixotropic
Y) Dilatant
Z) Pseudoplastic
ANSWER: Y) Dilatant
23. BONUS Physics Short Answer What general type of second-order phase transitions is exemplified by the superfluid transition of Helium 4 at low temperatures?
ANSWER: Lambda transition
24. BONUS Math Short Answer The Lucas numbers are a sequence starting with 2 and 1, where each consecutive term is generated by adding the previous two. The first five terms of this sequence are 2, 1, 3, 4, and 7. What is the last digit of the sum of the first 2025 Lucas numbers?
ANSWER: 2
25. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Lysozyme, which has an isoelectric point of 11, is commonly found in egg white. If Alan wants to separate lysozyme from egg white, which of the following experimental techniques should he use, assuming a low buffer pH?
W) Anion exchange chromatography
X) Cation exchange chromatography
Y) Size exclusion chromatography
Z) Affinity chromatography
ANSWER: X) Cation exchange chromatography

28. BONUS Physics Short Answer A physical pendulum is made from a stick with total mass M, length L, and mass density ρx = 2Mx/L2, where x is the distance from a point on the stick to the pivot at the top.
