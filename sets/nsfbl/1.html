Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three forces that are fictitious I. Coriolis force II. Centrifugal force III. Drag
ANSWER: I AND II
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer A newly discovered planet has a mass that is 5 times that of the Earth, but with a radius that is 20 times larger. Giving your answer in terms of ve, the escape velocity of Earth, what would be the escape velocity of that planet?
ANSWER: ve/2 (also accept: 1/2 ve; HALF OF ve)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Order the following bonds by increasing bond order 1) alkane C-C bond 2) alkene C-C bond 3) ozone O-O bond
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify ALL of the following 3 molecules that are correctly paired with their molecular geometry 1. Ammonia and Trigonal Pyramidal 2. Chlorine Trifluoride and Seesaw 3. Xenon Tetrafluoride and Square Planar
ANSWER: I AND III
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Labs are developing platinum catalysts to help break down plastics. The substrate plastics bind to what location on the catalyst to help initiate the reaction?
ANSWER: ACTIVE SITE
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility are using supercomputers to calculate the quark distribution in pions. Pions are included in what group of particles made of quark and antiquark pairs?
ANSWER: MESONS
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Every day, Adrian ate 25% of the jelly beans in the jar. If there are 27 jelly beans left at the end of the second day, how many jelly beans were in the jar in the beginning?
ANSWER: 48
4. BONUS Math Short Answer This competition is being hosted in 2022. Let S, C, and I be distinct positive integers such that S * C* I = 2022. What is the largest possible sum of S, C, and I?
ANSWER: 1014
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice After a polypeptide is assisted by a chaperonin, what is its level of structure?
W) Primary
X) Secondary
Y) Tertiary
Z) Quaternary
ANSWER: Y) Tertiary
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Select all of the following that are involved with the interaction between glutamic acid and histidine, given that the two amino acids interact at the tertiary structure level I. Salt Bridge II. Ionic Bond III. Peptide Bond
ANSWER: I AND II
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is TRUE regarding the relationship between the central pressure in a hurricane and the height of the storm surge?
W) As the pressure decreases, the storm surge decreases
X) As the pressure decreases, the storm surge increases
Y) As the pressure decreases, the storm surge remains constant
Z) As the pressure decreases, the storm surge decreases, then increases
ANSWER: X) As the pressure decreases, the storm surge increases
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice At which of the following elevations is the atmospheric temperature COLDER THAN the temperature at the tropopause, about 10 km above sea level?
W) 30 km
X) 60 km
Y) 90 km
Z) 120 km
ANSWER: Y) 90 km
7. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the maximum number of intersection points between a triangle and a circle?
W) 3
X) 4
Y) 5
Z) 6
ANSWER: Z) 6
7. BONUS Math Short Answer Evaluate the following expression: (52022 + 52021 + 52020)/(52022 − 52021 − 52020)
ANSWER: 31/19
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer If a sequence of DNA contains 35% Adenine, what percentage of the bases contain uracil?
ANSWER: 0
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is TRUE about splicing?
W) Spliceosomes are formed with snRNA and dicers
X) Alternative splicing occurs when additional exons are removed
Y) Splicing focuses on removing unnecessary exons
Z) The original mRNA molecule is lengthened as a result of splicing
ANSWER: X) Alternative splicing occurs when additional exons are removed
9. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Researchers at Idaho National Laboratory are developing new microreactors, which are smaller nuclear reactors that can maintain a specific part of the grid. These reactors are LEAST likely to use which of the following radioactive isotopes?
W) U-233
X) U-235
Y) U-238
Z) Pu-239
ANSWER: Y) U-238
9. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Idaho National Labs have developed the Portable Isotopic Neutron Spectroscopy (PINS) System to help determine the chemical composition of materials in sealed containers. To do this, they follow what product of neutron-nucleus collisions?
W) Positrons
X) Electrons
Y) Gamma Rays
Z) Radio Waves
ANSWER: Y) Gamma Rays
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The central mass of water in a large pot in the desert is primarily heated through what form of heat transfer?
W) Convection
X) Conduction
Y) Radiation
Z) All three equally contribute
ANSWER: W) Convection
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer 40 grams of metal heated to 130 C is added to a beaker with 100 mL of water at 25 C. What is the final temperature of the mixture, given that the specific heat of the metal is 0.5 J/(g·C) and the specific heat of water is 4 J/(g·C)?
ANSWER: 30 C
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following particles is a carrier of the weak force?
W) W boson
X) Electron
Y) Up quark
Z) Tau neutrino
ANSWER: W) W boson
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two plates in a vacuum can become attracted to each other due to what effect?
ANSWER: CASIMIR EFFECT
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following tephra in order of increasing size I. Lapilli II. Ash III. Volcanic Bombs
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What specific event occurs when the confining pressure on a rock is reduced by hot, solid rock rising to a point where the rock begins to melt?
ANSWER: DECOMPRESSION MELTING
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three ionic compounds that are soluble in water I. BaSO4 II. NaNO3 III. PbI
ANSWER: II ONLY
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Observe the endothermic reaction Ca(OH)2 (s) + HEAT ⇌ CaO (s) + H2O (g) Identify all of the following changes that would INCREASE the amount of calcium oxide present at equilibrium I. Crushing the calcium hydroxide into smaller pieces II. Adding Ar (g) III. Decreasing the temperature of the flask
ANSWER: NONE
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In which of the following lobes of the brain are audio signals primarily processed?
W) Frontal
X) Parietal
Y) Temporal
Z) Occipital
ANSWER: Y) Temporal
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What part of the brain serves as the relay center for all sensory information except for olfaction?
ANSWER: THALAMUS
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the product of 5-11i and its conjugate?
ANSWER: 146
15. BONUS Math Short Answer Find all complex roots of the equation x4 − 256 = 0.
ANSWER: 4, -4, 4i, -4i (also accept: PLUS AND MINUS FOUR I)
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What phenomenon causes the aurora borealis when it interacts with Earth’s magnetic field?
ANSWER: SOLAR WIND
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Large electrical discharges that occur above a thundercloud that often appear reddish-orange are called what?
ANSWER: SPRITES
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at SLAC National Laboratory have collaborated with Caltech scientists to uncover the protein structure of nuclear pore complexes. Nuclear pores are found on what membrane surrounding the nucleus, that also connects to the rough endoplasmic reticulum?
ANSWER: NUCLEAR ENVELOPE (also accept: NUCLEAR MEMBRANE) (do not accept: NUCLEAR LAMINA)
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the area of a circle whose circumference is 12?
ANSWER: 36/π
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are studying the characteristics of thin superconductors. All superconductors exhibit what effect, which causes them to repel magnetic fields?
ANSWER: MEISSNER EFFECT
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The position of a particle is given by the equation t2 + 3t + 1. What is the average velocity of the particle from 0 to 2 seconds?
ANSWER: 5
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer Three different objects are rolled down a ramp. Order the following three objects in order of FASTEST to SLOWEST time to roll down the ramp I. Hollow sphere II. Solid sphere III. Thin rod about center
ANSWER: III, II, I
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What structure connects the left and right hemispheres of the brain?
ANSWER: CORPUS CALLOSUM
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer In contrast to obligate anaerobes, which cannot survive in aerobic conditions, what name is given to creatures that can survive in either aerobic or anaerobic conditions?
ANSWER: FACULTATIVE ANAEROBES
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What specific shape generally takes the form of r = aθ?
ANSWER: SPIRAL OF ARCHIMEDES (also accept: ARITHMETIC SPIRAL) (do not accept: SPIRAL)

W) 2-Petal Rose Curve
X) 3-Petal Rose Curve
Y) 5-Petal Rose Curve
Z) Convex Limaçon
ANSWER: X) 3-Petal Rose Curve

W) Phosphorus
X) Chlorine
Y) Oxygen
Z) Sulfur
ANSWER: W) Phosphorus

ANSWER: I, II, III
22. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the velocity of a wave given that the wave has a period of 3 seconds and a wavelength of 6 m?
ANSWER: 2 m/s
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer Order the following materials from the slowest to fastest time it takes for sound to travel through them I. Water Vapor II. Ocean Water III. Freshwater
ANSWER: I, III, II

W) Top-left
X) Top-right
Y) Bottom-left
Z) Bottom-right
ANSWER: Y) Bottom-left

ANSWER: −cot(t)

ANSWER: HELIUM FLASH
