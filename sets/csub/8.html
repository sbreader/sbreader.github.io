Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What are the two components of velocity?
ANSWER: SPEED AND DIRECTION
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Neutrinos are named in which of the following ways:
W) after their successive discovery dates
X) based on the particles they are linked with
Y) based on the Greek letters that describe their relative energies
Z) based on their charges and masses
ANSWER: X) based on the particles they are linked with
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the systematic name for the compound N2O?
ANSWER: NITROUS OXIDE
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the common name for the following compound: a benzene ring with an OH group attached to carbon #1, a bromine attached to carbon #2, and a bromine attached to carbon #4
ANSWER: 2,4-DIBROMOPHENOL
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Transferal of immunity from breast milk to the infant is an example of:
W) passive immunity
X) active immunity
Y) cell mediated immunity
Z) paternal immunity
ANSWER: W) passive immunity
3. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The term pulmonic stenosis refers most specifically to:
W) pneumonia
X) an occluded coronary artery
Y) a blocked pulmonary valve
Z) hypertrophy of the pulmonary valves
ANSWER: Y) a blocked pulmonary valve
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the standard spectral classification letter for the Sun?
ANSWER: G
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Edwin Hubble devised a system, sometimes called a tuning fork classification, to differentiate what type of celestial bodies?
ANSWER: GALAXIES
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In the graph of a curve such as the cosecant curve, the straight vertical line to which the curve moves closer and closer is called what:
ANSWER: ASYMPTOTE
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the 10th term in the following arithmetic progression: 2, 7, 12…
ANSWER: 47
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice During the last 18,000 years, nearly all coasts have experienced:
W) submergence
X) emergence
Y) world wide decrease in sea level
Z) relatively unchanging sea levels
ANSWER: W) submergence
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to a circular or horseshoe-shaped organic reef growing on a submerged volcano, with an occasional small, low, coral sand island surrounding a shallow lagoon:
ANSWER: ATOLL
7. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following do archeologists and sociologists generally believe is the most important prerequisite to development of large cultural systems:
W) technology
X) writing
Y) agriculture
Z) a monetary system
ANSWER: Y) agriculture
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a simple fruit:
W) sour cherry
X) pineapple
Y) figs
Z) raspberries
ANSWER: W) sour cherry
8. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Secondary storage refers to:
W) processor cache
X) hard drives
Y) system memory
Z) a CPU
ANSWER: X) hard drives
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer What is the three-bit natural binary code equivalent of the decimal value 3/8?
ANSWER: 0.011
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What color would be produced when mixing magenta and yellow pigments?
ANSWER: RED
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the centripetal force required for an 8 kilogram body traveling at 2 meters per second in a perfect circle with a radius of 2 meters?
ANSWER: 16 NEWTONS
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is its characteristic geometrical arrangement of a molecule that has a hybrid orbital set of four identical sp3?
ANSWER: TETRAHEDRAL
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 4 gases found in the Earth’s atmosphere from the greatest percent by volume of dry air to the least: carbon dioxide; argon; nitrogen; oxygen
ANSWER: NITROGEN; OXYGEN; ARGON; CARBON DIOXIDE
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In which of the following areas of the human body is the zygomatic arch found:
W) hip
X) wrist
Y) shoulder
Z) head
ANSWER: Z) head
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Finger nails are mostly composed of what protein?
ANSWER: KERATIN
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the common name for the group of seven brightest stars in Ursa Major?
ANSWER: BIG DIPPER (also accept: PLOUGH)
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What millimeter-sized silicate metal spheres are the most abundant component of chondrites, making up between 50-80% by volume of this type of meteorite?
ANSWER: CHONDRULES
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A plane curve that is the locus of all points in the plane equidistant from a given point best describes what geometric figure?
ANSWER: CIRCLE
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Name two consecutive integers where three times the second plus the first equals 91:
ANSWER: 22 AND 23
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT classified as a high-level cloud:
W) altocumulus
X) cirrocumulus
Y) cirrostratus
Z) cirrus
ANSWER: W) altocumulus
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Namib desert of southwest Africa is an example of what type of desert:
W) continental
X) subtropical
Y) rainshadow
Z) coastal
ANSWER: Z) coastal
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Beekeepers typically use what substance to calm bees?
ANSWER: SMOKE
15. BONUS Energy Short Answer What common substance used by bacteriologists has a different melting point and freezing point?
ANSWER: AGAR AGAR (also accept: AGAR)
16. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice When doing a hardware setup procedure on a computer, the operator is effectively writing to:
W) ROM
X) RAM
Y) C-drive
Z) volatile memory
ANSWER: W) ROM
16. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice What is the name for the high-speed serial bus developed by Apple and Texas Instruments that allows for the connection of up to 63 multiple devices, supports hot-swapping, and transfers data at rates of up to 400 megabits per second:
W) USB port
X) ISO 9660
Y) FireWire
Z) Hot Port
ANSWER: Y) FireWire
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the most common term for the motion that is associated with an object that moves without rotating?
ANSWER: TRANSLATIONAL (also accept: TRANSLATIONAL MOTION)
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the total capacitance, in microfarads rounded to the second decimal place, for four capacitors connected in series if each capacitor is rated at 1 microfarad?
ANSWER: 0.25
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Marble statues around the world have been eaten away as a result of acid rain and the production of what aqueous acid?
ANSWER: SULFURIC ACID (also accept: H2SO4)
18. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The correct name for the compound CH3OCH3 is:
W) dimethyl ether
X) ethyl ether
Y) acetone
Z) dimethyloxide
ANSWER: W) dimethyl ether
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Aside from the four major blood types, what blood group in the USA is of the greatest clinical importance regarding agglutination reactions?
ANSWER: RH (also accept: RH FACTOR; RH GROUP; RH ANTIGENS)
19. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Multiplication is to differentiation as growth is to:
W) molting
X) division
Y) apoptosis
Z) development
ANSWER: Z) development
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the formula for the volume of a right circular cone?
ANSWER: (1/3)πr2h
20. BONUS Math Short Answer To the first decimal place, what is the approximate length, in inches, of the arc that is cut off by an angle of π/3 radians in a circle that has a radius of 4 inches:
ANSWER: 4.2
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The now dormant volcanoes at Yellowstone National Park are examples of which of the following types of volcanoes:
W) basaltic
X) rhyolitic
Y) andesitic
Z) basaltic and andesitic
ANSWER: X) rhyolitic
21. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is classified as an intrusive igneous rock:
W) gabbro
X) obsidian
Y) basalt
Z) phyllite
ANSWER: W) gabbro
22. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer In Freudian theory, what are the 3 divisions of the subconscious mind?
ANSWER: ID, EGO, SUPEREGO
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer Classical conditioning was first studied by what Russian Nobel laureate physiologist?
ANSWER: IVAN PAVLOV (also accept: PAVLOV)
23. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Work is a product of two quantities whose SI units are what?
ANSWER: METER AND NEWTON
23. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A 2.25 kilowatt engine is approximately the same as a:
W) 3 horsepower engine
X) 33 horsepower engine
Y) 155 horsepower engine
Z) 366 horsepower engine
ANSWER: W) 3 horsepower engine
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the general term for a solid crystalline substance made of just one element or compound, such as halite or calcite, that are the constituents of rocks that make up the Earth?
ANSWER: MINERAL
24. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for a long, calibrated glass tube with a tap at one end that is used vertically, with the tap at the bottom, and whose main use is as a reservoir for a chemical during titration?
ANSWER: BURETTE
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer If whole blood is allowed to clot, what is the fluid that is remaining once the clot is removed?
ANSWER: SERUM (do not accept: PLASMA)
25. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a monocot:
W) Acer rubrum
X) Quercus rubra
Y) Triticum aestivum
Z) Echinacea angustifolia
ANSWER: Y) Triticum aestivum
