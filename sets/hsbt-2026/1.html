1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of metamorphism is most likely to occur in the accretionary wedge of a subduction zone?
W) Burial
X) Dynamic
Y) Regional
Z) Impact
ANSWER: Y) Regional
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following glacial features is not primarily depositional in origin?
W) Drumlin
X) Lateral moraine
Y) Esker
Z) Cirque
ANSWER: Z) Cirque
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the smooth endoplasmic reticulum equivalent in muscle cells?
ANSWER: Sarcoplasmic Reticulum
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 structures that are membrane bound 1) Lysosome 2) Cell Wall 3) Ribosome.
ANSWER: 1 only
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three compounds that are soluble in water at room temperature 1) AgCl 2) NH4Cl 3) KCl.
ANSWER: 2, 3
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Travis is having fun shining lasers in places he isn’t supposed to. He notices that when he shines the laser through smoke, the path of the beam is visible from multiple directions. This observation is most directly due to which of the following phenomena?
W) Rayleigh scattering
X) Tyndall effect
Y) Rutherford effect
Z) Optical rotation
ANSWER: X) Tyndall effect
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many ways are there to rearrange the letters in the word HAWAII?
ANSWER: 180
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Simplify the fraction with numerator 25 and denominator 1/2 + 1/3.
ANSWER: 30
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Arman is biking at 10 meters per second and decelerates at a rate of 2 meters per second squared. In meters, how far will he travel before he stops?
ANSWER: 25
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three particles that experience the strong force 1) Proton 2) Electron 3) Pion.
ANSWER: 1, 3
6. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Travis is hiking the Ko'olau mountain range and notices transmission lines crossing the steep ridges. Which of the following best describes the conditions in which these transmission lines transport electricity from one side of the island to the other?
W) High voltage, direct current
X) High voltage, alternating current
Y) Low voltage, direct current
Z) Low voltage, alternating current
ANSWER: X) High voltage, alternating current
6. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Renewable energy refers to energy derived from sources that naturally replenish themselves over human timescales. Which of the following energy sources would be considered net-zero, but not renewable?
W) Geothermal
X) Biomass
Y) Nuclear
Z) Natural gas
ANSWER: Y) Nuclear
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following diagnostic properties is the least reliable indicator used to distinguish between unique minerals?
W) Color
X) Cleavage
Y) Hardness
Z) Streak
ANSWER: W) Color
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three marine provinces by increasing average slope 1) Abyssal plain 2) Oceanic trench 3) Continental rise.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following are, or are afflictions caused by DNA viruses?
W) Sars-CoV2
X) Measles
Y) HIV
Z) Adenovirus
ANSWER: Z) Adenovirus
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Select all of the following three types of hepatitis that are caused by positive sense RNA viruses. 1) Hepatitis A 2) Hepatitis B 3) Hepatitis C.
ANSWER: 1, 3
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following spectroscopic techniques would be best suited for analyzing molecular vibrations and identifying functional groups in organic compounds?
W) Microwave spectroscopy
X) Infrared spectroscopy
Y) UV-Vis spectroscopy
Z) NMR spectroscopy
ANSWER: X) Infrared spectroscopy
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Daniel loves to sunbathe at Ala Moana Beach Park. He brings graphite and tries to pressurize it with his body weight to turn it into diamond and get rich. Assuming he succeeds, what is the coordination number of each carbon atom in the resulting diamond lattice?
ANSWER: 4
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If an equilateral triangle has a perimeter of 12 cm, in cm2 and in simplest radical form, what is its area?
ANSWER: 4√3 cm2
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the value of 1 times 10 plus 2 times 9 plus 3 times 8, all the way to 10 times 1?
ANSWER: 220
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What angle is defined as the minimum angle of incidence for total internal reflection to occur?
ANSWER: Critical angle
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer The electric field 1 meter from an electric dipole is 10 volts per meter. In volts per meter, what is the electric field 2 meters from the dipole?
ANSWER: 1.25
12. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Travis is looking into the logistics behind fossil fuel energy generation. Rank the following three forms of fossil fuel energy by increasing average energy density by mass 1) Coal 2) Petroleum 3) Natural gas.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer Power outages in Hawaii are often caused by severe weather, falling trees, and renewable output capacity strain. What term is used to refer to a controlled drop in the electric voltage supply of Hawaii's grid system to protect a total blackout during peak stress?
ANSWER: Brownout
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Daniel is studying Kepler's 2nd Law, which states that planets in elliptical orbits sweep out equal areas in equal periods of time. Kepler's second law is a direct consequence of the conservation of what specific quantity?
ANSWER: Angular momentum
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three metamorphic rocks by increasing metamorphic grade 1) Schist 2) Slate 3) Gneiss.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice If you were to take a drug that inhibits proper microtubules assembly, which of the following cellular processes would be most affected?
W) Active transport of hydrogen ions
X) Cytoplasmic streaming in plant cells
Y) Cleavage furrow formation
Z) Separation of chromosomes
ANSWER: Z) Separation of chromosomes
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the shortest phase of mitosis, wherein microtubule polymers shorten by disassociating tubulin?
ANSWER: Anaphase
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Haru notices that the pKa of acetic acid is approximately 5 and prepares a 0.1 molar acetic acid solution. He then titrates the solution to the half-equivalence point. To the nearest whole number, what is the pH of the resulting solution?
ANSWER: 5
15. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Haru tries to synthesize the most acidic compound possible, but the only starting compound he has is acetic acid. Which of the following substituents would be most effective in increasing the acidity of his compound?
W) Fluorine
X) Chlorine
Y) Bromine
Z) Iodine
ANSWER: W) Fluorine
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Hong ran a 26-mile-long marathon with an average speed of 13 miles per hour. How many seconds did it take him to run the marathon?
ANSWER: 7200 s
16. BONUS Math Short Answer Julian falls asleep in his boat and floats down a river at a rate of 5 miles per hour. After sleeping for a certain number of hours, he wakes up and immediately begins paddling back upstream, travelling back at a speed of 8 miles per hour, and arrives back at his starting position after 3 hours of paddling. As a decimal, how many hours was Julian sleeping?
ANSWER: 4.8 hours
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice When Chris applies pressure to the top of a fluid, he notices that the pressure at the bottom increases by the same amount. Which of the following best explains why this is the case?
W) Archimedes' principle
X) Pascal's principle
Y) Bernoulli's principle
Z) Conservation of mass
ANSWER: X) Pascal's principle
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer To the nearest integer and in joules per mole kelvin, what is the constant volume heat capacity per mole of a monatomic gas?
ANSWER: 12
18. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Offshore wind is one potential renewable energy solution for Hawaii, often considered due to the restricted land area for large-scale onshore wind systems. Which of the following does not describe a major challenge to offshore wind generation?
W) Wind resource scarcity due to inconsistent trade winds
X) Susceptibility to high-energy swells and tropical cyclones
Y) Limited grid integration options
Z) Island bathymetry prevents fixed-bottom installation
ANSWER: W) Wind resource scarcity due to inconsistent trade winds
18. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Daniel wants to build a wind turbine on the North Shore of Oahu, and is looking for a location with sufficient wind speed for usable power generation. Which of the following ranges is closest to the minimum “cut-in” speed for a wind turbine?
W) 3-6 mph
X) 6-9 mph
Y) 9-12 mph
Z) 12-15 mph
ANSWER: X) 6-9 mph

X) Their membrane phospholipids have short fatty acid chains
Y) Their membrane phospholipids are saturated
Z) Their membrane includes an unusually high amount of cholesterol
