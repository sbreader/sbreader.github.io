1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true regarding Cnidaria?

2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the passing star hypothesis?

X) A passing star pulled matter out of the Sun to create the planets

Z) A passing star captured many solar system planetesimals
ANSWER: X) A passing star pulled matter out of the Sun to create the planets

3. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following most accurately defines prime knots?
