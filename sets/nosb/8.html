Packet 8 (2018 Regional Round 8) of National Ocean Sciences Bowl:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What gas law is commonly used to determine the amount of dissolved gas in sea water for a given partial pressure?
ANSWER: Henry's law
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the function of the fronds at the tip of the pneumatocyst?
W) Act as a buoy
X) Act as an anchor
Y) Gather nutrients and sunlight
Z) Reproductive structure
ANSWER: Y) Gather nutrients and sunlight
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Approximately what percent of the Earth’s atmosphere is carbon dioxide?
W) 10
X) 4
Y) 0.1
Z) 0.04
ANSWER: Z) 0.04
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following straits lies between Baffin Island and Greenland?
W) Hudson Strait
X) Strait of Karimata
Y) Strait of Belle Isle
Z) Davis Strait
ANSWER: Z) Davis Strait
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What forms if a meander to the coastal-water side of the Gulf Stream breaks off and forms a spinning circulation of water?
ANSWER: Warm-core ring (also accept: warm core eddy)
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer How does the predatory sea slug, Navanax, find its prey?
ANSWER: Follow the slime trail of its prey
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which is NOT an example of a coastal-plain estuary?
W) Pamlico Sound
X) Chesapeake Bay
Y) New York Harbor
Z) Delaware Bay
ANSWER: W) Pamlico Sound
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to a transition between vertically mixed and stratified water?
ANSWER: Tidal mixing front (also accept: Front)
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT one of the three major low salinity intermediate waters?
W) Antarctic Intermediate Water
X) Labrador Sea Water
Y) North Pacific Intermediate Water
Z) Mediterranean Water
ANSWER: Z) Mediterranean Water
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer In the same species, why might an intertidal individual have greater environmental tolerance than one that lives at a depth of 30 feet?
ANSWER: Exposure to greater environmental variability
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the device deployed by National Geographic to capture the "animal's eye view" in the water, in addition to collecting environmental data?
ANSWER: Crittercam
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which organism do you expect to have the highest acute physiological stress response?
W) A limpet in the presence of a sea star
X) A temperate fish in 18°C water
Y) A temperate fish in 13°C water
Z) A limpet during high tide
ANSWER: W) A limpet in the presence of a sea star
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Studies on the oldest identified zircons on earth suggest that the Earth’s ocean formed during what geologic eon?
ANSWER: Hadean
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following pollutants is most likely to biomagnify in the marine food web?
W) Benzene
X) Styrene
Y) Ethyl Acetate
Z) Arsenic
ANSWER: Z) Arsenic
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer After they find the river mouth, how do salmon identify their natal stream?
ANSWER: Smell (also accept: scent)
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true regarding hurricanes?
W) They occur primarily during the summer or fall
X) They commonly move westward in the tradewinds
Y) They form in high pressure zones
Z) They lose energy in areas with cold water
ANSWER: Y) They form in high pressure zones
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What two rivers in the United States carry the most fresh water to the sea from East of the Rocky Mountains and West of the Rocky Mountains?
ANSWER: The Mississippi and the Columbia
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How can CRISPR aid in marine conservation efforts?
W) Speed up evolution of traits
X) Increase true population size
Y) Artificially increase genetic diversity
Z) Increase species range
ANSWER: Y) Artificially increase genetic diversity
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice During the past million years the location of the eastern shoreline of the United States has been directly correlated to which of the following?
W) Extent of glacial ice
X) Frequency of earthquakes
Y) Volume of volcanic eruptions
Z) Number of submarine landslides
ANSWER: W) Extent of glacial ice
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the world's oldest and deepest freshwater lake?
ANSWER: Lake Baikal
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an important spawning area for striped bass along the east coast of the United States?
W) Roanoke River in North Carolina
X) Housatonic River in Connecticut
Y) Potomac River in Maryland
Z) Hudson River in New York
ANSWER: X) Housatonic River in Connecticut
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which non-climatic environmental variable can exacerbate heat stress in intertidal organisms?
ANSWER: Tidal height (also accept: timing of low tide)
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the maximum possible height to length ratio for an unbroken water wave?
W) 1 to 1
X) 1 to 2
Y) 1 to 7
Z) 1 to 10
ANSWER: Y) 1 to 7
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What microbial process involves the reaction of nitrite and ammonium to yield nitrogen gas and water?
ANSWER: Anammox (also accept: Anaerobic Ammonium Oxidation)
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice How is the marsh cordgrass especially adapted to brackish waters?
W) Greater photosynthetic capabilities
X) Deeper roots
Y) Roots filter out salts
Z) Shallower roots
ANSWER: Y) Roots filter out salts
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What term describes photons with wavelengths between 400 and 700 nanometers?
ANSWER: Photosynthetically Active Radiation (also accept: visible spectrum; PAR)
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which two variables would you expect to have roughly parallel depth profiles in the open ocean during the summer?
W) Temperature and nitrate
X) Oxygen and carbon dioxide
Y) Phosphate and pH
Z) Nitrate and phosphate
ANSWER: Z) Nitrate and phosphate
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The coastline of Northern Namibia is known by what other name?
ANSWER: Skeleton Coast
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Stokes Law is frequently used in oceanography to determine which of the following?
W) Density of seawater
X) Propagation rate of an internal wave
Y) Amplitude and periodicity of tides
Z) Settling rate of small particles in seawater
ANSWER: Z) Settling rate of small particles in seawater
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer The larvae of which class of flying animals are commonly found in streams?
ANSWER: Insecta (also accept: insects)
