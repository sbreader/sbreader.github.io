3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The genetic material of different viruses can be:
W) only double-stranded DNA
X) only single-stranded DNA or double-stranded DNA
Y) only double-stranded DNA or double-stranded RNA
Z) either single-stranded DNA and RNA or double-stranded DNA and RNA
ANSWER: Z) either single-stranded DNA and RNA or double-stranded DNA and RNA
