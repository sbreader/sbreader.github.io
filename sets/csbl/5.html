1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A typical precursor messenger RNA that is spliced contains regions of exons and introns. Which portion of spliced pre-mRNAs is excised out?
W) Exon
X) PolyA Tail
Y) 5’ Cap
Z) Intron
ANSWER: Z) Intron
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following that the spliceosome contains: I-RNA II – Proteins III-DNA IV – Carbohydrates
ANSWER: I and II
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Giving your answer as a power of 10, what is the wavelength of a gamma ray?
ANSWER: -12
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice A molecule which has an octahedral molecular geometry will have how many atoms attached to its central atom:
W) 4
X) 6
Y) 8
Z) 10
ANSWER: X) 6
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which fundamental force is responsible for friction?
W) Gravity
X) Electromagnetic force
Y) Strong nuclear force
Z) Weak nuclear force
ANSWER: X) Electromagnetic force
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Photons are the carriers of the electromagnetic force, what is the spin of a photon?
W) 0
X) 1/2
Y) 1
Z) 3/2
ANSWER: Y) 1
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify ALL of the following minerals that are stronger than Fluorite on the Mohs Hardness Scale I. Calcite II. Gypsum III. Orthoclase IV. Apatite
ANSWER: III AND IV
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals has NO planes of cleavage?
W) Hornblende
X) Olivine
Y) Biotite
Z) Muscovite
ANSWER: X) Olivine
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many degrees are in π/2 radians?
ANSWER: 90 DEGREES
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Scientists at SLAC National Accelerator Lab are studying the formation of semiconductors using 2D perovskites layered with other 2D materials. Which of the following is a good semiconductor?
W) Molybdenum
X) Vanadium
Y) Selenium
Z) Germanium
ANSWER: Z) Germanium
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Thomas Jefferson Lab are studying proton-neutron interactions in Calcium-48. Calcium-48 is unique because it undergoes what extremely rare radioactive decay process?
ANSWER: DOUBLE BETA DECAY (do not accept: BETA DECAY)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The ABO system of blood types concludes that there are the following blood types for individuals: A, B, AB, and O. What kind of inheritance is exhibited by ABO blood type determination?
W) Codominance
X) Dominant-Recessive
Y) Incomplete Dominance
Z) Sex-Linked
ANSWER: W) Codominance
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer A boy named Tommy is born with the blood type of B. If Tommy’s mother has an AB blood type, then which blood type, if any, can his father conclusively NOT have?
ANSWER: NONE

X) High Ionic Conductivity and Low Thermal Conductivity
Y) Low Ionic Conductivity and High Thermal Conductivity
Z) Low Ionic Conductivity and Low Thermal Conductivity
ANSWER: X) High Ionic Conductivity and Low Thermal Conductivity

W) 1.2%
X) 1.5%

17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer It takes Amy 20 minutes to paint a fence, and it takes Kyle 30 minutes to paint the same fence. Working together, how long does it take them to paint the fence?
ANSWER: 12 MINUTES

ANSWER: 46 MILES
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at the Pacific Northwest National Laboratory are trying to understand how a fundamental ubiquitous organic polymer is broken down by microbes. X) Amylopectin

Which of the following techniques would they have likely used to determine the protein identities?

20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Carbon dioxide has a boiling point of -57° Celsius, whereas sulfur dioxide has a boiling point of -10° Celsius. This difference is most likely due to the fact that sulfur dioxide exhibits which intermolecular force, while carbon dioxide does not?
ANSWER: DIPOLE-DIPOLE (also accept: DIPOLE FORCE)
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A car is travelling in the positive x-direction at a constant speed of 20 m/s, while a train accelerates in the positive x-direction uniformly from rest at 5 m/s2. Assuming the car and train start at the same location, how long will it take for the train to pass the car?
ANSWER: 8 s
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the work done on the train, which has a mass of 2,000,000 kg, during the first 8 seconds?
ANSWER: 1,600,000 kJ (also accept: 1,600,000,000 J; 1.6 × 109 J)
