1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A sloppy chemist forgets to label two beakers. One contains barium chloride solution, and the other contains magnesium chloride solution. The chemist decides to add a third solution to each beaker in order to distinguish the two. Which of the following reagents would form a precipitate in only one of the two beakers?
W) Sodium sulfate
X) Sodium carbonate
Y) Silver nitrate
Z) Ammonium chloride
ANSWER: W) Sodium sulfate
