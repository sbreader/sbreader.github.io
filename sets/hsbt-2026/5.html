1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Bay of Fundy in Nova Scotia has a wide tidal range due to what phenomenon caused by constructive interference of successive tidal patterns?
ANSWER: Tidal resonance
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Mr. Park is racing a seismic wave. Identify all of the following three quantities that, when increased in isolation, would increase the velocity of seismic waves 1) Temperature 2) Confining pressure 3) Density.
ANSWER: 2 only
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, order the following three phases of prophase I from earliest to latest in the cell cycle 1) Zygotene 2) Diplodene 3) Leptotene.
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer During zygotene, homologous chromosomes come together. These chromosomes form a protein aggregate known as what?
ANSWER: Synaptonemal Complex
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three aqueous species that would be classified as Lewis acids 1) AlCl3 2) BF3 3) NH3.
ANSWER: 1 and 2
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In which of the following compounds do the bonds have the greatest ionic character?
W) O2
X) NO2
Y) CO2
Z) OF2
ANSWER: Y) CO2
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer From a standard deck of 52 cards, Sola selects one card. Expressing your answer as a common fraction, what is the probability of Sola drawing a king or queen?
ANSWER: 2/13
4. BONUS Math Short Answer A triangle with coordinates of (0, 3), (0,9), and (3,6) is rotated around the y axis. Expressed in terms of pi, what is the volume of the resulting shape formed by this rotation?
ANSWER: 18π
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is FALSE about electromagnetic waves?
W) If the wave is in total internal reflection, an evanescent wave leaks past the material
X) The electric and magnetic portions of the wave have the same energies
Y) The path of an electromagnetic wave is sinusoidal
Z) The electric fields and magnetic fields of the wave are orthogonal to each other
ANSWER: Y) The path of an electromagnetic wave is sinusoidal
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer A uniform current flows through a wire with radius 10 cm. The magnetic field 4 cm from the center is B. In centimeters, what other distance from the center will the magnitude of the magnetic field be B?
ANSWER: 25
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term refers to the refining process whereby complex hydrocarbon molecules are broken down into lighter, high-value products such as gasoline and diesel?
ANSWER: Cracking
6. BONUS Energy Short Answer The Hawaii State Government is currently considering liquified natural gas, LNG, as a potential “bridge fuel” in its transition to clean energy generation. Order the following four steps in the production of LNG from first to last 1) Liquefaction 2) Purification 3) Shipping 4) Regasification.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3, 4

11. BONUS Physics Short Answer Kevin is singing an extremely high note at 6800 Hz. Taking the speed of sound to be 340 m/s, if Kevin is stationary and Arman is biking away from him at 90 m/s, then in hertz, what is the frequency that Arman hears?
ANSWER: 5000
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In solar photovoltaics, efficiency is dictated by the ability of semiconductor materials to absorb wavelengths of sunlight and generate an electric current. What term refers to the minimum energy required to excite an electron from the valence band in a semiconductor, allowing it to flow as electricity?
ANSWER: Band gap
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer Nuclear reactors use heat transfer fluids, also known as coolants, to pull thermal energy from the reactor core and turn it into usable power. Order the following three types of nuclear reactors by increasing specific heat capacity of their respective coolant 1) Heavy water reactors 2) Liquid metal fast reactors 3) Molten salt reactors.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
