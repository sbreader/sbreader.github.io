1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What law states that the union of two sets is equal to the sum of the sets without their intersection?
ANSWER: PRINCIPLE OF INCLUSION-EXCLUSION (also accept: PIE)
1. BONUS Math Short Answer Six boys and two girls line up to take a group picture. In the picture, all six boys must be next to each other. Furthermore, two of the boys are identical twins and therefore indistinguishable. If the line is randomly arranged, what is the probability that the arrangement is valid?
ANSWER: 3/28
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What law states that the entropy of an isolated system may never decrease?
W) Hess’s Law
X) First Law of Thermodynamics
Y) Second Law of Thermodynamics
Z) Rayleigh-Jeans Law
ANSWER: Y) Second Law of Thermodynamics
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer A system whose entropy is constant but is not in a state of equilibrium must be undergoing what type of process?
ANSWER: REVERSIBLE
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What equation is most commonly used to calculate the effect of temperature change on the vapor pressure of a substance?
ANSWER: CLAUSIUS-CLAPEYRON EQUATION

Y) ethanol, 58.9 torr
Z) ethane, 7095 torr
ANSWER: W) water, 1000 torr
