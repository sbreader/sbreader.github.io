Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements would have the lowest M+1 peak in a mass spectrum?
W) Boron
X) Carbon
Y) Nitrogen
Z) Fluorine
ANSWER: Z) Fluorine
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is observing the two electrons in the hydride anion and notes that in the ground state the two electrons must have opposite spin. This is due to what principle which states that no two electrons can share the same 4 quantum numbers?
ANSWER: Pauli Exclusion Principle
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following mixtures will deviate the least from Raoult's law?
W) Methanol and Ethanol
X) Ethanol and Propanol
Y) Propanol and Butanol
Z) Butanol and Pentanol
ANSWER: W) Methanol and Ethanol
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which set of principal, azimuthal, and magnetic quantum numbers is not possible?
W) 3,2,1
X) 2,1,-1
Y) 3,1,-2
Z) 4,1, -1
ANSWER: Y) 3,1,-2
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following 3 bases by increasing strength 1) Sodium Ethoxide 2) Sodium Amide 3) Sodium Hydride
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is studying the phosphate buffer system in the mitochondria. What equation may he use to calculate the pH of this buffer, using the concentrations of hydrogen phosphate and its conjugate acid?
ANSWER: Henderson-Hasselbalch Equation
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three compounds by increasing number of sigma bonds per molecule 1) dinitrogen 2) white phosphorus 3) ozone
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the amount of energy required to eject an electron from a metal?
ANSWER: work function
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer 1 mol of a standard carbohydrate of formula (CH2O)n is combusted with excess oxygen producing 88 g of carbon dioxide. How many total atoms are present in the carbohydrate?
ANSWER: 8
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What term is used to describe the final stage of a free-radical chain reaction where two free radicals collide and combine their unpaired electrons to form a stable, non-radical molecule?
ANSWER: termination
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer As opposed to phosphorescence, what short-lived phenomenon occurs when a substance absorbs light of one wavelength and almost instantly emits it back at a different, usually longer, wavelength?
ANSWER: Fluorescence
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is trying to calculate the pH of pure water at a certain temperature. To do this, he uses what equation which relates the equilibrium constant and temperature?
ANSWER: Van't Hoff Equation
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice UV-Vis Spectroscopy would be most useful in finding the concentration of which of the following ions?
W) Scandium (III)
X) Cobalt (II)
Y) Zinc (II)
Z) Vanadium (V)
ANSWER: X) Cobalt (II)
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Slowing down time, Abhinav observes that when he adds sodium hydroxide to an Iron (III) nitrate solution, originally a colloid is formed which then turns into a precipitate through the random motion of particles. What is the name for this random erratic movement of microscopic particles, which is named after a Scottish Botanist?
ANSWER: Brownian Motion
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Hexafluorobenzene and benzene form an adduct through what noncovalent attractive force between organic aromatic rings?
ANSWER: Pi-Pi Stacking
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three speeds of gas molecules following a Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution from lowest to highest 1) Most probable speed 2) Root-mean-square speed 3) Average speed
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three semiconductors by increasing conductivity 1) Pure Silicon 2) Silicon doped with 5 ppm Aluminum 3) Silicon Doped with 5 ppm Phosphorus
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Place the following three ions in order of increasing bond order between the central atom and oxygen 1) Nitrate 2) Sulfate 3) Phosphate
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Water is least likely to act as which of the following types of species?
W) Bronsted-Lowry Acid
X) Bronsted-Lowry Base
Y) Lewis Acid
Z) Lewis Base
ANSWER: Y) Lewis Acid
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three alkanes which would be liquids at room temperature 1) Butane 2) Pentane 3) Hexane
ANSWER: 2, 3
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three acids by increasing acidity 1) hypochlorous acid 2) hypobromous acid 3) hypoiodous acid
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice To honor the Amador Valley Scrim Server, Abhinav makes fireworks using the colors purple, blue, and green. Which of the following elements would Abhinav find least helpful for the making of these fireworks?
W) Barium
X) Copper
Y) Potassium
Z) Sodium
ANSWER: Z) Sodium
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Abhinav is working with samples of Phosphorus (V) Oxide and Arsenic (V) oxide and notices that the latter is more prone to thermal decomposition. This is because of what effect in which heavier elements are less stable in higher oxidation states?
ANSWER: Inert Pair Effect
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three options in which the use of a Thiele tube would be useful 1) boiling point analysis 2) melting point analysis 3) freezing point analysis
ANSWER: 1, 2
25. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three groups which would be considered ortho-para directors in electrophilic aromatic substitution 1) Methyl 2) Acyl 3) Amino
ANSWER: 1, 3
