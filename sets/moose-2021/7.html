1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of fermentation does Saccharomyces Cerevisiae undergo?
ANSWER: ALCOHOLIC FERMENTATION (also accept: ETHANOL FERMENTATION)
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In lactic acid fermentation, the pyruvate produced from glycolysis is converted into lactic acid by multiple chemical reactions. In alcoholic fermentation, however, the pyruvate is converted into an alcohol, releasing what other product in the process?
W) H2O
X) CO2
Y) C6H12O6
Z) CH3OH
ANSWER: X) CO2
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following that are true of index fossils 1) They have a distinctive appearance 2) The organism lived on land 3) They are widespread in geologic time 4) They are geographically widespread
ANSWER: 1 and 4
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory recently designed a small helicopter that can fly on the surface of Mars. This is difficult because Mars' atmosphere is approximately what percent as dense as Earth's?
W) 1
X) 10
Y) 100
Z) 1000
ANSWER: W) 1
3. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Curiosity and Perseverance, the only currently active rovers on Mars, both generate electricity using which of the following sources?
W) Solar panels
X) Hydrogen fuel cells
Y) Nuclear fission
Z) Radioactive decay
ANSWER: Z) Radioactive decay
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In 1991, the arrival of a massive typhoon coincided with the eruption of Mount Pinatubo in the Philippines. This event triggered large flows of water, boulders, and hot volcanic ash, typically called what?
ANSWER: LAHARS
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An isotope of what element was used as the fissile material in the first ever atomic bomb?
ANSWER: PLUTONIUM
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, order the following three electron transfers in the photosynthetic electron transport chain from first to last 1) Entry to Photosystem I 2) Reduction of plastoquinone 3) Entry to Photosystem II
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is 64 squared?
ANSWER: 4096
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 thermodynamic processes that are NOT present in the Carnot Cycle 1) Isochoric Heating 2) Isobaric Expansion 3) Isochoric Cooling
ANSWER: All
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a type of volcano?
W) Pyroclastic
X) Cinder cone
Y) Shield
Z) Stratovolcano
ANSWER: W) Pyroclastic
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab are studying bismuth vanadate, a compound that can be used to make fuel from sunlight. Which of the following is the correct chemical formula for bismuth vanadate?
W) BiVH2
X) BiVOH
Y) BiVO4
Z) BiV3
ANSWER: Y) BiVO4
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Light-dependent reactions in photosynthesis produce which two forms of chemical energy to be used in the Calvin Cycle?
W) NADP+ and ADP
X) NADP+ and ATP
Y) NADPH and ATP
Z) NADPH and ADP
ANSWER: Y) NADPH and ATP
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer If the nucleotide sequence of the template DNA strand used for mRNA synthesis is AGGCA, what is the nucleotide sequence of the complementary mRNA?
ANSWER: UCCGU
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 3 particles involved in a beta plus decay process 1) Positron 2) Electron Antineutrino 3) W-Boson
ANSWER: 1 only
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer 0.1 moles of gaseous methane undergo complete combustion, producing carbon dioxide. The same sample of methane could also undergo incomplete combustion, producing carbon monoxide. What is the ratio between the amount of oxygen required for the first reaction and the amount of oxygen required for the second reaction?
ANSWER: 4:3 (also accept: 4/3)
9. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Researchers at Ames Lab have recently discovered a process that can reuse waste that is produced when making paper. To make paper, cellulose and hemicellulose are removed and used, leaving behind what rigid polymer?
ANSWER: LIGNIN
9. BONUS Math Short Answer Mary has a box with 3 blue marbles, 3 red marbles, and 4 yellow marbles. What is the probability that an equal number of yellow and blue marbles will be picked after two draws, without replacement?
ANSWER: 1/3
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer By name or number, order the following 4 mathematical discoveries from earliest to latest 1) Euler discovers how to construct a 17 sided polygon 2) Euclid writes the Elements 3) Heron's formula is discovered 4) The golden ratio is discovered
ANSWER: 2, 3, 4, 1
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The moon Dione orbits around Saturn in a mean-motion resonance with what other moon, creating tidal forces that create geysers on its surface?
ANSWER: ENCELADUS
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three statements which are true about DNA ligase 1) DNA ligase is naturally present in many prokaryotic species 2) DNA ligase is commonly used in recombinant DNA experiments 3) DNA ligase adds nucleotides in the 3' to 5' direction on the lagging strand
ANSWER: 1 and 2
11. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Fermilab are studying the connections between fundamental forces. Specifically, they are studying the unification of what two forces?
W) Strong interaction and weak interaction
X) Weak interaction and electromagnetism
Y) Gravity and electromagnetism
Z) Strong interaction and gravity
ANSWER: X) Weak interaction and electromagnetism
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice At standard conditions, a chemical reaction has a positive change in enthalpy and a negative change in entropy. Which of the following best describes the reaction?
W) Nonspontaneous at all temperatures
X) Spontaneous at all temperatures
Y) Spontaneous at low temperatures and nonspontaneous at high temperatures
Z) Nonspontaneous at low temperatures and spontaneous at high temperatures
ANSWER: W) Nonspontaneous at all temperatures
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 metamorphic facies from first to last to form at a subduction zone 1) prehnite-pumpellyite 2) zeolite 3) blueschist
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What large moon is in a retrograde orbit about its planet and will break apart in about 3.5 billion years due to tidal effects?
W) Triton
X) Phobos
Y) Enceladus
Z) Io
ANSWER: W) Triton
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 92 lb impostor jumps into a vent that is recessed into the ground 2 meters. If the impostor somehow only jumps straight down with an initial speed of 2 m/s, what will his velocity be when he hits the bottom of the vent, taking up to be the positive direction? Give your answer as a radical in simplest terms, and assume g = 10 m/s2.
ANSWER: -2√11
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following elements is used to find evidence of asteroid impacts because, although it is rarely found in Earth's rocks, it is common in meteorites?
W) Iridium
X) Palladium
Y) Tungsten
Z) Niobium
ANSWER: W) Iridium
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer Most particle colliders, such as the one at SLAC, use superconducting magnets to accelerate and collide beams of what charged subatomic particles?
ANSWER: PROTONS
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A particle is undergoing uniform circular motion. Identify all of the following that would change if the particle's circular path radius changes 1) Pivot point 2) Frequency of motion 3) Centripetal acceleration
ANSWER: 2 and 3
15. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following changes must the secondary oocyte undergo after meiosis I and before fertilization?
W) Crossing Over
X) Independent Assortment
Y) Degeneration of the polar body
Z) DNA replication of the recombinant DNA
ANSWER: Y) Degeneration of the polar body
16. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In 2012, scientists at SLAC discovered the Higgs boson, a particle that gives other fundamental particles what property?
W) Spin
X) Momentum
Y) Mass
Z) Charge
ANSWER: Y) Mass
16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about phases of planets?
W) Jupiter will be at quarter phase when it is at quadrature
X) Mars is never at quarter phase
Y) Mercury is full at greatest eastern elongation
Z) Venus is never full
ANSWER: X) Mars is never at quarter phase
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In what phase of meiosis do homologous chromosomes come together to form a tetrad?
ANSWER: PROPHASE I (do not accept: PROPHASE)
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following that are true of chemical equilibrium 1) At equilibrium, the forward and back reactions cease to occur 2) At equilibrium, the concentrations of reactants and products are equal 3) A large value of K implies a reaction lies to the left
ANSWER: 0 only
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the event that occurs when a planet and the sun form a right angle with the Earth at the vertex?

ANSWER: RENAL CORTEX

ANSWER: LE CHATELIER'S PRINCIPLE
