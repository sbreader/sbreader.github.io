1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Non-storm wave action can move fine sand particles in depths of up to how many meters?
W) 10
X) 20
Y) 40
Z) 80
ANSWER: X) 20
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Paleocene-Eocene Thermal Maximum (PETM) was an event marked by major changes on Earth that occurred about 55 million years ago. Which of the following is NOT true of the PETM?
W) Global temperature rose by about 6°C
X) Carbonate deposits in the world's oceans dissolved
Y) Greenhouse gases such as CO2 were much lower
Z) Deep-sea extinctions of marine organisms occurred
ANSWER: Y) Greenhouse gases such as CO2 were much lower
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a process of plate tectonics?
W) Continental drift
X) Seafloor spreading
Y) Subduction
Z) Upwelling
ANSWER: Z) Upwelling
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Geological time charts use Latin or Greek for naming. What is the English translation of the Greek term "Mesozoic"?
ANSWER: MIDDLE LIFE (also accept: MEDIUM or BETWEEN for MIDDLE, ANIMALS or LIVING BEINGS for LIFE)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best classifies iodine?
W) Halogen
X) Inert gas
Y) Compound
Z) Alkali metal
ANSWER: W) Halogen
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer In the jungle, Curious George is swinging from a vine between two trees, like a pendulum, and in a surprisingly regular fashion. Given that the time he takes to swing from one tree to another is 1.5 seconds, assuming no energy is lost from air drag or friction, what is the period if we assume that George is a harmonic oscillator?
ANSWER: 3.0
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The sum of the ages of 5 people 5 years ago was 50. The sum of their ages 5 years from now will be how many years?
ANSWER: 100
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Triangle ABC is isosceles. If angle A has a measure of 40°, what are all of the possible degree measures of angle B?
ANSWER: 40, 70, 100
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following determines the sex of a turtle egg?
W) Temperature of the sand
X) Phase of the moon
Y) Season of fertilization
Z) How many eggs are laid
ANSWER: W) Temperature of the sand
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is least likely to be impacted by decreased environmental pH?
W) Pteropods
X) Diatoms
Y) Coccolithophorids
Z) Mussels
ANSWER: X) Diatoms
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Worldwide, over 70% of the total length of beaches has retreated at a rate of 10 centimeters per year. Which of the following is primarily responsible for this dramatic change?
W) Increase in shoreline construction
X) Decrease in the number of groins permitted
Y) Damming of the world's rivers
Z) Increases in ocean acidification
ANSWER: Y) Damming of the world's rivers
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Black holes are thought to eventually evaporate away due to what phenomenon?
ANSWER: HAWKING RADIATION (also accept: BLACK BODY RADIATION)
7. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Harmonics, an issue of power quality, causes which of the following effects?
W) Distorts the voltage and current waveforms of a circuit
X) Reduction in the source voltage
Y) Increase in the source voltage
Z) Power interruption for more than one minute
ANSWER: W) Distorts the voltage and current waveforms of a circuit
7. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice The heat engine utilized in a typical ocean thermal energy conversion system has an efficiency that lies in which of the following ranges?
W) 0-10%
X) 20-30%
Y) 40-50%
Z) 60-70%
ANSWER: W) 0-10%
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Today we often hear of politicians being given the litmus test to determine their character. However, in science, what chemical property does a litmus test measure?
ANSWER: ACIDITY (also accept: pH, ALKALINITY, ACIDS AND BASE, BASICITY)
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a product of the Krebs, or tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle?
W) ATP
X) NADH
Y) H2O
Z) CO2
ANSWER: Y) H2O

W) Nitrate
X) Sulfide
Y) Acetate
Z) Chloride
ANSWER: X) Sulfide
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer 10 grams of Substance A requires 500 Joules of heat to increase the temperature from 17°C to 167°C. What is the specific heat of Substance A in Joules per gram degrees Celsius, to two significant figures?
ANSWER: 0.33
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A parabola has x-intercept 3 and y-intercept 5. Which of the following can be determined?

X) The location of the vertex of the parabola
Y) The direction the parabola opens
Z) The equation representing the curve
ANSWER: W) Whether the parabola has values in quadrant I

ANSWER: 210

W) Chimaera

14. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Solar panels derive their power from sunlight. The maximum amount of solar irradiance received at any moment on the surface of the Earth is which of the following?

Y) Solar declination, 1,368 watts per meter squared
Z) Solar azimuth, 999 watts per meter squared

19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Some people joke that California is going to "break off" from the rest of the United States because of its seismic activity. ANSWER: CONVERGENT (also accept: DESTRUCTIVE PLATE BOUNDARY)

A secondary resistive circuit, identical to the first one, dissipates 180 watts of power. What is a possible reason the second circuit dissipates more power than the first one?

X) The voltage source from circuit #2 contains harmonics

Z) Parasitic losses are present in circuit #2
