1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the correct size range for which particles including viruses are said to be airborne?
W) 20-30 micrometers
X) 20-30 nanometers
Y) .5 micrometers-20 nanometers
Z) Less than .5 Micrometers
ANSWER: Z) Less than .5 Micrometers
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Relatively common in the world today, this sexually transmitted disease can lead to a sore throat, swollen lymph nodes, muscle aches and fatigue. If left untreated, the bacterium from which the disease arises, Treponema pallidum, can infect the Cerebrospinal Fluid of the brain and spinal cord, a rare development in the 21st century. Treatments range from antibiotics in the early stages of infection to daily penicillin injections. Which of the following accurately names this disease?
W) Chancroid
X) Syphilis
Y) Gonorrhea
Z) HIV
ANSWER: X) Syphilis
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What medically applied gas is produced by the decomposition of ammonium nitrate upon heating at 250 degrees Celsius? It is commonly referred to as laughing gas.
ANSWER: N2O aka DINITROGEN MONOXIDE
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Atmospheric nitrogen dioxide gas disproportionates to form acid rain. What strong acid is produced by the reaction between NO2 and water?
ANSWER: NITRIC ACID (also accept: HNO3)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer This expression of quantum mechanics was devised between 1925 and 1927 by Niels Bohr and Werner Heisenberg. What is the term for this interpretation, named after the Dutch city in which it was developed?
ANSWER: COPENHAGEN INTERPRETATION
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the quantum analogue of Newton’s second law?
ANSWER: SCHRÖDINGER EQUATION
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What are the period and phase shift respectively of y = 2sin(πx − 1) + 7?
ANSWER: 2; 1/π
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Sum the infinite geometric series with first term 1 and third term 0.5. Give your answer in simplified radical form.
ANSWER: 2 + √2
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What gemstone is made entirely out of microscopic glass spheres arranged like a crystal lattice?
ANSWER: OPAL
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The mineral corundum has a hardness of 9 on the Mohs scale, and is relatively common in its pure form. When impurities appear in corundum, which two gemstones can it be recognized as?
ANSWER: RUBY AND SAPPHIRE
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Laboratory are using what technique, which involves exciting electron spins, to study substances containing unpaired electron spins?
ANSWER: Electron Paramagnetic Resonance Spectroscopy
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Electron paramagnetic resonance spectroscopy is most commonly used to detect and identify what group of atoms or molecules?
ANSWER: Free radicals
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Contrary to its counterparts, this type of virus uses reverse transcriptase to convert its RNA to DNA and embed its genetic material into the host cell. Common examples of this virus include HIV and Corona Viruses. What is the name of this type of virus?
ANSWER: RETROVIRUS
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer An Intravenous medication is a type of medicine that must be administered directly into a vein and the bloodstream in order to take effect. One common method of administering an intravenous medication is through an IV drip. Which of the following, if any, is an Intravenous medication? 1) Hydromorphone 2) Amphotericin 3) Penicillin G

9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is true about inductors and capacitors?
W) Capacitors preserve voltage by storing energy in an electric field, whereas inductors preserve current by storing energy in a magnetic field.
X) Capacitors preserve voltage by storing energy in a magnetic field, whereas inductors preserve current by storing energy in an electric field.
Y) Capacitors preserve current by storing energy in an electric field, whereas inductors preserve voltage by storing energy in a magnetic field.
Z) Capacitors preserve current by storing energy in a magnetic field, whereas inductors preserve voltage by storing energy in an electric field.
ANSWER: W) Capacitors preserve voltage by storing energy in an electric field, whereas inductors preserve current by storing energy in a magnetic field.

12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Brookhaven National Laboratory are trying to recreate the state of the universe just after the Big Bang using the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider. According to quantum chromodynamics, only what two elementary particles were present after the Big Bang?
W) Gluons and quarks
X) W and Z Bosons
Y) Quarks and leptons
Z) Leptons and gluons
ANSWER: W) Gluons and quarks

ANSWER: NONE
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer The plant Primula produces a chemical called malvidin. The K gene controls the synthesis of this chemical, while inhibition is controlled by the D gene. Both genes are considered to be hereditarily dominant. However, if the dominant D allele is present, there will be no ge ne expression regardless of whether the K allele is present. What is this phenomenon called?
ANSWER: EPISTASIS (also accept: DOMINANT EPISTASIS; SIMPLE EPISTASIS)
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Yttrium oxysulfide is a common phosphor — a material that exhibits this property when impacted with electron or UV rays.
ANSWER: PHOSPHORESCENCE

15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A particle’s acceleration is given by a(t) = C*(tn) where C is a positive constant, n is a real number, and t is time. What is true about the jerk of the particle?
W) It is positive for any n
X) It is zero for any n
Y) It depends on only C
Z) It is positive as long as n is greater than 0
ANSWER: Z) It is positive as long as n is greater than 0
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer A particle’s acceleration is given by a(t) = C*(tn) where C is a positive constant, n is a real number, and t is time. Give the equation for velocity in terms of t, C, n, and v0.
ANSWER: v(t) = Ctn+1/(n+1) + v0
