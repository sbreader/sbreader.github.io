Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What acid, formed from the reaction of carbon dioxide and water, is the most common agent of chemical weathering on Earth’s surface?
ANSWER: CARBONIC ACID (also accept: H2CO3)
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three igneous rocks that are intrusive 1) Gabbro 2) Basalt 3) Rhyolite.
ANSWER: 1
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Argonne National Lab are studying the force that holds nuclei together. What is this force?
W) Gravity
X) Electromagnetic
Y) Weak
Z) Strong
ANSWER: Z) Strong
2. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice New computer simulations at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab have shown that adding a porous layer around a reservoir of liquid lithium could aid in heat absorption and transfer in a nuclear fusion reactor. This system could potentially be deployed in what toroidal device?
W) Tokamak
X) Spallator
Y) Solenoid
Z) Lawson device
ANSWER: W) Tokamak
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The least-squares regression line of a trade data set is y = 1.9x + 5.3, where x is the number of boxes shipped and y is the shipping time in minutes. According to this model, how many more minutes will it take the firm to ship one additional box?
ANSWER: 1.9 (also accept: 1 9/10)
3. BONUS Math Short Answer An arithmetic sequence has fourth term 7 and eighth term 31. What is the positive difference between consecutive terms for the sequence?
ANSWER: 6
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following equations describes the distribution of velocities among the particles of an ideal gas?
W) Planck’s law
X) Maxwell-Boltzmann equation
Y) Wien’s law
Z) Clausius-Clapeyron relation
ANSWER: X) Maxwell-Boltzmann equation
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer A current of 6 amperes is flowing through a loop of wire containing a resistor. In a span of one minute, how many coulombs of charge flow through the resistor?
ANSWER: 360
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the trophic status of a green and highlyproductive lake?
W) Eutrophic
X) Oligotrophic
Y) Mesotrophic
Z) Dystrophic
ANSWER: W) Eutrophic
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In eukaryotic cells, which of the following metabolic reactions does NOT occur within the mitochondrion?
W) Citric acid cycle
X) Glycolysis
Y) Pyruvate decarboxylation
Z) Oxidative phosphorylation
ANSWER: X) Glycolysis
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What state of matter has fixed volume and shape?
ANSWER: SOLID
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The energy required to make the n equals one to n equals infinity transition for a bound electron is also known as what?
ANSWER: IONIZATION ENERGY (also accept: IONIZATION)
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Generally speaking, the world’s largest earthquakes occur at what type of plate boundary?
ANSWER: CONVERGENT (also accept: SUBDUCTION ZONE; OCEAN-CONTINENT CONVERGENT)
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Ultramafic magma is slowly cooling. Which of the following minerals will crystalize out first?
W) Olivine
X) Pyroxene
Y) Biotite
Z) Quartz
ANSWER: W) Olivine
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Brookhaven National Lab are installing a new detector in their Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider. Which of the following is true regarding special relativity?
W) In every medium, the speed of light is the upper limit for the speed of an object in that medium
X) Quantum entanglement permits instantaneous communication
Y) Events can occur in different orders for different observers
Z) Neutrinos can travel faster than light in a vacuum
ANSWER: Y) Events can occur in different orders for different observers
8. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Brookhaven National Laboratory are studying collisions of polarized protons. Which of the following best describes linearly polarized light?
W) Electric field parallel to the direction of propagation
X) Magnetic field aligned in a single direction
Y) Parallel electric and magnetic fields
Z) Out-of-phase electric and magnetic fields
ANSWER: X) Magnetic field aligned in a single direction
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the discriminant of the quadratic function y = x2 – 6x + 7?
ANSWER: 8
9. BONUS Math Multiple Choice A 12-foot ladder leans against a vertical wall. If the height on the wall where the top of the ladder touches is known, what inverse trig function could most directly be used to find the angle that the ladder makes with the ground?
W) Inverse cosine
X) Inverse cotangent
Y) Inverse sine
Z) Inverse tangent
ANSWER: Y) Inverse sine
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three quantities that are vectors 1) Momentum 2) Impulse 3) Force.
ANSWER: ALL
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer What law states that a planetary orbit sweeps out area around its star at a constant rate?
ANSWER: KEPLER’S SECOND LAW (also accept: KEPLER’S SECOND) (do not accept: KEPLER’S)
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term for the phase of the cardiac cycle during which the ventricles are relaxing?
ANSWER: DIASTOLE (also accept: DIASTOLIC)
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer By mass, what three elements are the most abundant in humans?
ANSWER: CARBON, HYDROGEN, OXYGEN (also accept: in any order)
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What process separates volatiles by differences in boiling point?
W) Fractional distillation
X) Chromatography
Y) NMR spectroscopy
Z) Mass spectrometry
ANSWER: W) Fractional distillation
12. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the term for the ease of distortion of an electron cloud by an external entity?
W) Ionizability
X) Polarizability
Y) Hydrophilicity
Z) Hardness
ANSWER: X) Polarizability
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What Galilean moon is the closest to Jupiter, and subjected to intense tidal heating that drives prolific volcanism?
W) Callisto
X) Ganymede
Y) Titan
Z) Io
ANSWER: Z) Io
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer If the diameter of a telescope is multiplied by 5, by what factor is its light-gathering power multiplied?
ANSWER: 25
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Scientists at Argonne National Lab are using the third-most abundant gas in dry air as a scintillation medium. What is this gas?
W) Nitrogen
X) Neon
Y) Xenon
Z) Argon
ANSWER: Z) Argon
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Argonne National Lab are modeling the properties of hafnia, which has chemical formula HfO2. In this compound, what is the oxidation state of hafnium?
ANSWER: +4 (also accept: 4)
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If f of x is equal to 5x2 – 11x + 3, what is f of 1?
ANSWER: –3
15. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the integral from 1 to 4 of (x2 + 2) dx?
ANSWER: 27
16. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is true regarding resistors connected in parallel to a battery?
W) The resistances are added
X) Each resistor has the same current through it
Y) Each resistor has the same voltage across it
Z) There will be a short circuit unless the resistances are equal
ANSWER: Y) Each resistor has the same voltage across it
16. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is the term for the constant obtained by dividing the ideal gas constant by Avogadro’s constant?
W) Planck constant
X) Boltzmann constant
Y) Newton constant
Z) Fine structure constant
ANSWER: X) Boltzmann constant
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following metallic elements can be found in hemoglobin?
W) Magnesium
X) Iron
Y) Calcium
Z) Zinc
ANSWER: X) Iron
17. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In newly-saturated soils, which of the following processes consumes oxygen more rapidly than it can be replenished by diffusion from the atmosphere?
W) Denitrification
X) Methanogenesis
Y) Photosynthesis
Z) Respiration
ANSWER: Z) Respiration
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a homogeneous mixture?
W) Gasoline
X) Air
Y) Smoke
Z) Vinegar
ANSWER: Y) Smoke
18. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true for the reaction between silver nitrate and sodium chloride in solution?
W) Nitrate ion is not included in the net ionic equation
X) Silver ion is a spectator ion
Y) Silver chloride precipitates
Z) One mole of sodium nitrate is produced per mole of silver nitrate reacted
ANSWER: X) Silver ion is a spectator ion
