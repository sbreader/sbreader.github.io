Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A divergent plate boundary in the ocean is also known as
W) a mid-oceanic wall
X) a mid-oceanic mountain
Y) a mid-oceanic ridge
Z) a hot spot
ANSWER: Y) a mid-oceanic ridge
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What usually forms at a mid-oceanic ridge?
ANSWER: (an underwater) mountain chain
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A shield volcano usually has
W) a steep slope
X) a small base
Y) a gentle slope
Z) water
ANSWER: Y) a gentle slope
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What kind of volcanoes are usually found in the Hawaiian island chain?
ANSWER: shield (volcano)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice If lava is more than 63% silica, then it is called
W) felsic
X) silicous
Y) very silicous
Z) falsic
ANSWER: W) felsic
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Felsic lava is usually found in what type of volcanoes?
ANSWER: cinder cones and/or stratovolcanoes/composite volcanoes
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Mafic lava is usually found in what type of volcanoes?
ANSWER: shield volcanoes
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Volcanoes are considered extinct only if
W) they are not erupting
X) they have not erupted
Y) they are expected to erupt
Z) they are not erupting and are not considered likely to erupt in the future
ANSWER: Z) they are not erupting and are not considered likely to erupt in the future
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer If a volcano is currently erupting then it is considered
ANSWER: active
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of volcanoes can rise 8 000 feet above their bases?
W) none of them
X) shield volcanoes
Y) stratovolcanoes
Z) cinder cones
ANSWER: Y) stratovolcanoes
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Stratovolcanoes are also called
ANSWER: composite volcanoes
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The main "pipe" through which magma flows out of a volcano is called the
W) conduit
X) chamber
Y) crater
Z) branch
ANSWER: W) conduit
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The circular depression at the top of a volcano is called the
ANSWER: Crater
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Do S-waves travel faster than P-waves
ANSWER: No
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Using either numbers or words, denote which of the following are true of tephra 1: Tephra is anything, other than lava, ejected from a volcano during an eruption 2: Tephra is another word for lava 3: Tephra is always similar in appearance to a rock
ANSWER: 1
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Tephra smaller than 2 mm in diameter is called
ANSWER: ash
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Earth quakes usually occur
W) at plate boundaries
X) in the middle of plates
Y) on land
Z) in the ocean
ANSWER: W) at plate boundaries
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Earthquakes occur as a result of sudden releases of
ANSWER: energy
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice At what magnitude are earthquakes considered to cause serious damage?
W) magnitude 2
X) magnitude 4
Y) magnitude 6
Z) magnitude 7
ANSWER: Z) magnitude 7
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Under what magnitude are earthquakes usually imperceptible?
ANSWER: magnitude 3
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The most common type of fault is
W) an abnormal fault
X) a thrust fault
Y) a normal fault
Z) a strike-slip fault
ANSWER: Y) a normal fault
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Both normal and reverse faults are considered to be what type of faults?
ANSWER: dip-slip faults
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice When both dip-slip and strike-slip faults are part of an earthquake, it is called
W) a regular fault
X) a multi-part fault
Y) an oblique-slip fault
Z) a common fault
ANSWER: Y) an oblique-slip fault
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Are oblique-slip faults common?
ANSWER: Yes
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Earthquakes occurring within plates
W) never happen
X) happen very often
Y) are always harmless
Z) can be devastating
ANSWER: Z) can be devastating
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are earthquakes occurring within plates called?
ANSWER: intraplate (earthquakes)
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer If the aftershock is of a greater magnitude than the main shock, then the aftershock is redesignated as the
ANSWER: Main shock
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In an earthquake swarm, each consecutive earthquake
W) has an increasingly greater magnitude
X) has an increasingly lesser magnitude
Y) has a high magnitude
Z) has a relatively similar magnitude to the rest
ANSWER: Z) has a relatively similar magnitude to the rest
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Earthquake swarms strike a specific area in what kind of a period of time?
ANSWER: A short period of time
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice How many earthquakes occur per year, on average?
W) 10 000
X) 500 000
Y) 65 000
Z) 1 000 000
ANSWER: X) 500 000
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What percentage of earthquakes can be felt?
ANSWER: 20%
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What was the magnitude of the largest recorded earthquake in the world?
W) 10.0
X) 9.0
Y) 9.3
Z) 9.5
ANSWER: Z) 9.5
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What was the magnitude of the largest recorded earthquake in the United States?
W) 9.0
X) 8.7
Y) 9.2
Z) 9.4
ANSWER: Y) 9.2
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the average rate of motion of the San Andreas Fault, in cm/yr?
W) 10 cm/yr
X) 4.5 cm/year
Y) 7.2 cm/year
Z) 5.6 cm/year
ANSWER: Z) 5.6 cm/year
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer At the rate of 5.6 cm/year, how many years do scientists predict it will take before San Francisco and Los Angeles will be adjacent to each other?
ANSWER: 15 million (years)
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Using either number or words, denote which of the following is caused by an earthquake 1: a tidal wave 2: a tsunami
ANSWER: 2
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What causes a tidal wave?
ANSWER: interactions between the Earth and the Sun and /or Moon
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The epicenter of an earthquake is located
W) 100 m below the surface of the Earth
X) on the surface of the Earth
Y) 10 m below the surface of the Earth
Z) 25 m below the surface of the Earth
ANSWER: X) on the surface of the Earth
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The point directly below the epicenter of an earthquake, underneath the Earth's surface, where the rupture of the fault begins is called
ANSWER: the hypocenter (of an earthquake)
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice How many earthquakes occur in Southern California per year?
W) 100
X) 1 000
Y) 5 000
Z) 10 000
ANSWER: Z) 10 000
24. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer How many of the earthquakes occurring in Southern California each year are greater than magnitude 4.0
ANSWER: 15 (also accept: 20; 15 to 20)
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The magnitude of an earthquake
W) varies by location
X) can be the same in two locations
Y) is never the same in two locations
Z) is always the same in all locations
ANSWER: Z) is always the same in all locations
25. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the property of an earthquake that does vary by location?
ANSWER: the intensity (of an earthquake)
