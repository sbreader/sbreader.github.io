Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A planet is said to be at aphelion when it is:
W) closest to the sun
X) farthest from the sun
Y) at it's highest point above the ecliptic
Z) at it's lowest point below the ecliptic
ANSWER: X) farthest from the sun
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice At any time we may describe the position of an inferior planet by the angle it makes with the sun as seen from the earth. This angle is called the:
W) ecliptic
X) epicycle
Y) elongation
Z) proxima
ANSWER: Y) elongation
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Phobos and Deimos are moons that orbit which planet?
ANSWER: MARS
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The two main radiation belts that surround the earth are known by what name?
ANSWER: VAN ALLEN BELTS
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice On which of the following planets would the sun rise in the west?
W) Saturn
X) Pluto
Y) Mercury
Z) Venus
ANSWER: Z) Venus
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which planet seems to be turned on its side with an axis tilt of 98 degrees?
W) Uranus
X) Pluto
Y) Neptune
Z) Saturn
ANSWER: W) Uranus
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The planet Neptune was found by studying the deviations in another planet's orbit. Name the other planet discovered in this fashion.
ANSWER: URANUS
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The angle that the full moon takes up in the night sky is equal to which of the following values?
W) 1/8 degree
X) 1/2 degrees
Y) 1 degree
Z) 2 degrees
ANSWER: X) 1/2 degrees
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The period from one full moon to the next is:
W) 30.3 days
X) 30 days
Y) 29.5 days
Z) 28 days
ANSWER: Y) 29.5 days
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice When a superior planet is at opposition it is making an angle of how many degrees with the sun?
W) 0 degrees
X) 45 degrees
Y) 90 degrees
Z) 180 degrees
ANSWER: Z) 180 degrees
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The word Albedo refers to which of the following?
W) The wobbling motion of a planet
X) The amount of light a planet reflects
Y) The phase changes of a planet
Z) The brightness of a star
ANSWER: X) The amount of light a planet reflects
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Galileo discovered something about Venus with his telescope that shook the old theories. Which of the following was Galileo's discovery?
W) Venus was covered in clouds
X) Venus had phases like the moon
Y) Venus' surface was similar to the earth's
Z) Venus had retrograde motion
ANSWER: X) Venus had phases like the moon
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Cassini's division is described by which of the following?
W) A break in the rings of Saturn
X) A break in the clouds of Jupiter
Y) The distance between the first two moons of Jupiter
Z) The distance between the Van Allen belts
ANSWER: W) A break in the rings of Saturn
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The law that says that all planets orbit the sun in elliptical orbits is which of Kepler's three laws?
ANSWER: FIRST
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name the astronomer who discovered that Mars orbits the sun in an elliptical orbit.
ANSWER: KEPLER
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Name the phase that the moon is in for each type of eclipse, lunar and solar:
W) Full moon for both phases
X) New moon for both phases
Y) Full moon for lunar and new moon for solar
Z) New moon for lunar and full moon for solar
ANSWER: Y) Full moon for lunar and new moon for solar
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The orbital plane of the moon is how many degrees inclined from the ecliptic?
W) 15 degrees
X) 10 degrees
Y) 5 degrees
Z) 0 degrees
ANSWER: Y) 5 degrees
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which JOVIAN planet rotates slower than the earth?
ANSWER: NONE
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Neptune is closest in SIZE to which planet?
ANSWER: URANUS
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Is a sunspot hotter, cooler, or about the same temperature as the region around it?
ANSWER: COOLER
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In the lowest level of the photosphere of the Sun, the temperature is:
W) 1,000 degrees Kelvin
X) 6,000 degrees Kelvin
Y) 10,000 degrees Kelvin
Z) 13,000 degrees Kelvin
ANSWER: X) 6,000 degrees Kelvin
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A Galactic year is the length of time that it takes our sun to orbit the galaxy. In Earth years, how long is a Galactic year?
W) 100 million years
X) 230 million years
Y) 620 million years
Z) 940 million years
ANSWER: X) 230 million years
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A first magnitude star is how many times brighter than a second magnitude star?
W) 2.5
X) 7.3
Y) 10.0
Z) 12.0
ANSWER: W) 2.5
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the star that is the BRIGHTEST in the sky?
ANSWER: SIRIUS
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following constellations has more bright stars than any other constellation?
W) Big Dipper
X) Cassiopeia
Y) Orion
Z) Scorpion
ANSWER: Y) Orion
26. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A line through the three stars in Orion's belt points toward which one of the following stars?
W) Mizar
X) Polaris
Y) Sirius
Z) Rigel
ANSWER: Y) Sirius
27. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A pulsar is actually a:
W) black hole
X) white dwarf
Y) red giant
Z) neutron star
ANSWER: Z) neutron star
28. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In the Milky Way there are approximately
W) 2 million stars
X) 100 million stars
Y) 400 million stars
Z) 200 billion stars
ANSWER: Z) 200 billion stars
29. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following words best describes the shape of our galaxy?
W) spherical
X) elliptical
Y) spiral
Z) barred-spiral
ANSWER: Y) spiral
30. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the star CLOSEST to our sun?
ANSWER: PROXIMA CENTAURI
31. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice On February 9, 1991, ROSAT, an orbiting observatory, finished the first ever all-sky survey of:
W) radio waves.
X) infrared radiation.
Y) ultraviolet radiation.
Z) x-rays.
ANSWER: Z) x-rays.
32. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer On what planet does a "year" last only 88 days?
ANSWER: MERCURY
33. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Asteroid Belt is between the orbits of which two planets?
ANSWER: MARS AND JUPITER
34. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The ring nebula is an example of a planetary nebula. It is:
W) a ring of planets circling a star.
X) an expanding ring of gas released from a dying star.
Y) a contracting ring of gas and dust falling circling into a massive object.
Z) a ring of stars in a circular orbit.
ANSWER: X) an expanding ring of gas released from a dying star.
35. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer After what geophysical occurrence might you see Bishop's Ring around the Sun?
ANSWER: VOLCANIC ERUPTION
36. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A black hole with the mass of the earth would be the size of:
W) the Sun
X) the Moon
Y) a bowling ball
Z) a marble
ANSWER: Z) a marble
38. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer How many MANNED moon landings have there been?
ANSWER: SIX
39. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which planets are never visible at midnight?
ANSWER: MERCURY AND VENUS
40. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Whose paradox asks why the sky is not ablaze with starlight if the universe is infinite in extent and uniformly filled with stars?
W) Olber's
X) Greigheim's
Y) Schuller's
Z) Miller's
ANSWER: W) Olber's
41. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer On one of the moon landings astronauts left an object on the moon. Scientists periodically bounce a laser beam off of this object to measure the distance between the earth and the moon. What is this object?
ANSWER: MIRROR
42. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are Schmidt, Cassegrainian, and Galilean examples of?
ANSWER: TELESCOPES
43. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer How large of an angle is the tilt of the Earth's spin axis from the normal to the ecliptic plane?
ANSWER: 23 AND 1/2 DEGREES (also accept: 23.5; 23.4; 23 1/2)
44. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the two bright spots of light on opposite sides of a halo around the sun called?
ANSWER: SUN DOGS
46. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the Geminids, the Perseids, and the Leonids?
ANSWER: METEOR SHOWERS
47. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Laika, the first living creature to orbit the earth was what type of animal?
ANSWER: DOG
48. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Most stars are cooler than the sun. These stars, the planets, interstellar clouds and star-forming regions emit most of their radiant energy in the:
W) visible
X) x-ray region
Y) ultraviolet
Z) infrared
ANSWER: Z) infrared
49. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer If the DIAMETER of a reflector's objective mirror is doubled, how many times as great is its light-gathering power?
ANSWER: 4 TIMES
50. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Astronomers use cepheids principally as measures of what?
W) size
X) speed
Y) chemical composition
Z) distance
ANSWER: Z) distance
51. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the class that the andromeda Galaxy belongs to?
ANSWER: SPIRAL
52. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Where are most asteroids located?
W) Jupiter and Saturn
X) Mars and Venus
Y) Earth and Mars
Z) Mars and Jupiter
ANSWER: Z) Mars and Jupiter
53. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What was the year of the first lunar landing by man?
ANSWER: 1969
54. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the Latin name for the lunar seas?
ANSWER: MARIA
55. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Of the following phases of the moon, which is the one at which a spring tide occurs?
W) new
X) new gibbous
Y) first quarter
Z) new crescent
ANSWER: W) new
56. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer I am one of two stars known as the Pointers. My fellow star is Merak. Which star am I?
ANSWER: DUBHE
57. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer This is a question for you budding young astronomers. What is the sidereal period of the moon?
ANSWER: 27 AND 1/3 DAYS (also accept: 27.3; 27 1/3; 27.3 DAYS)
58. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer "The Swan" is the English name for this constellation. Give me the Latin name by which this constellation is known.
ANSWER: CYGNUS
59. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice A white dwarf has a mass of roughly one solar mass but a size of about:
W) a basketball
X) a car
Y) Lake Michigan
Z) the Earth
ANSWER: Z) the Earth
60. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Sirius was identified as a binary star long before its small companion was seen. What kind of star is the small companion of this binary system?
ANSWER: WHITE DWARF
61. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following can be used to see through Venus's clouds?
W) refracting telescopes
X) radar
Y) x-rays
Z) ultraviolet
ANSWER: X) radar
63. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the type of reflecting telescope in which the light focused by the primary mirror is intercepted short of focal point and refocused by a secondary mirror through a hole in the center of the primary mirror?
ANSWER: CASSEGRAIN (also accept: CASSEGRAINIAN)
64. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The only supernova explosion in modern times visible in the sky to the naked eye became visible in which year?
ANSWER: 1987
65. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer To within 5% what fraction, by mass, of the observable matter in our universe is helium?
ANSWER: 24% (also accept: 24; 25%; 25; 23%; 26%)
66. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the source of energy in a supernova explosion? (Name or describe the force).
ANSWER: GRAVITATION (also accept: GRAVITATIONAL COLLAPSE; GRAVITY)
67. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Give the year when Pluto was discovered.
ANSWER: 1930
68. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Primary cosmic radiation is characterized by:
W) an intensity which is essentially constant in time
X) isotropic in space
Y) very energetic particles
Z) all of the above
ANSWER: Z) all of the above
69. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Globular clusters in our galaxy are primarily found:
W) in the spiral arms.
X) distributed throughout the disk including regions between the spiral arms.
Y) in the bulge at the center of our galaxy.
Z) in the halo of our galaxy.
ANSWER: Z) in the halo of our galaxy.
70. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The phenomenon that causes the Moon's rotation about it's own axis to be equal to the Moon's period of revolution about the Earth is called:
W) Haner Effect
X) Landow Effect
Y) Orbital Synchronization
Z) Tidal Friction
ANSWER: Z) Tidal Friction
71. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Sun rotates about it's own axis approximately:
W) once every 60 minutes.
X) once every 24 hours.
Y) once every 365 days.
Z) varies with solar latitude.
ANSWER: Z) varies with solar latitude.
72. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice PRESENTLY, what is the farthest planet from the sun?
W) Pluto
X) Neptune
Y) Uranus
Z) None of the above
ANSWER: X) Neptune
73. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Andromeda, the nearest galaxy which is similar to the Milky Way, is how far from the Earth?
W) 200,000 light years
X) 2,000,000 light years
Y) 20,000,000 light years
Z) 200,000,000 light years

74. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The angular position of an object measured from a fixed point along the horizon is its:
W) azimuth
X) apogee
Y) zenith
Z) declination
ANSWER: W) azimuth
75. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The precession of the Earth refers to the:
W) change from night to day.
X) Earth's motion around the sun.
Y) change in orientation of the Earth's axis.
Z) effect of the moon on the Earth's orbit.
ANSWER: Y) change in orientation of the Earth's axis.
76. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Algol, the Demon star, is a binary star whose larger component revolves around and regularly eclipses the smaller, brighter star causing periodic changes in brightness. The constellation that Algol is in is also noted for meteor showers that appear annually in August. Name the constellation.

W) index of refraction

W) Copernicus
X) Tycho Brahe
