1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify ALL of the following three vitamins that are fat-soluble I. C II. D III. E

1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements correctly describe the beginning and propagation of action potentials in neurons?
W) They begin in dendrites and propagate towards axon terminals
X) They begin in dendrites and propagate towards axon hillocks
Y) They begin in axon hillocks and propagate towards axon terminals
Z) They begin in axon hillocks and propagate towards dendrites
ANSWER: Y) They begin in axon hillocks and propagate towards axon terminals

W) P
X) Cl
Y) Ge
Z) Se
ANSWER: W) P
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice In the redox reaction between silver and copper, where silver is reduced and copper is oxidized to the 2+ Oxidation state, which of the following statements is correct?
W) Increasing the copper ion concentration three-fold has the same effect on the cell voltage as increasing the silver ion concentration nine-fold.
X) Decreasing the copper ion concentration two-fold has the same effect on the cell voltage as decreasing the silver ion concentration two-fold.
Y) Increasing the copper ion concentration four-fold has the same effect on the cell voltage as decreasing the silver ion concentration two-fold.
Z) Increasing the copper ion concentration two-fold has the same effect on the cell voltage as decreasing the silver ion concentration four-fold.
ANSWER: Y) Increasing the copper ion concentration four-fold has the same effect on the cell voltage as decreasing the silver ion concentration two-fold.

6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at Argonne National Labs visualized the fracture of bones with a dynamic load at a quasi-static loading rate on different bones of the human body.
