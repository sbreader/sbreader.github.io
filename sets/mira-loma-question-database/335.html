Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the definition of biology
ANSWER: STUDY OF LIFE; LIVING ORGANISMS, ETC
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Who was the first person to make a biological observation through a microscope
W) Isaac Newton
X) Rene Descartes
Y) Galileo Galilei
Z) Gregor Johann Mendel
ANSWER: Y) Galileo Galilei
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following are antigens that have entered the body from the outside
W) Autoantigens
X) Tumor antigens
Y) Endogenous antigens
Z) Exogenous antigens
ANSWER: Z) Exogenous antigens
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following species are NOT considered eukaryotic
W) Protistans
X) Archaebacteria Eubacteria
Y) Animals
Z) Fungi
ANSWER: X) Archaebacteria Eubacteria
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the ONLY exclusive part of the cell that prokaryotic creatures possess, while none of the eukaryotic creatures do
ANSWER: BACTERIAL FLAGELLUM
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What does the cell theory state in its most basic form
ANSWER: THE CELL IS THE FUNDAMENTAL UNIT OF LIFE
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Who introduced the theory of evolution
ANSWER: CHARLES DARWIN
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the "driving force" of Darwin's theory and sums up most of his thoughts in two words
ANSWER: NATURAL SELECTION
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the definition of genetics
ANSWER: Science of heredity and variation in organisms
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What diagram represents the probability of different variations of offspring occurring
ANSWER: PUNNETT SQUARE
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Where does the majority of the energy needed for life come from
ANSWER: THE SUN
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the two most common processes in converting trapped energy into usable energy
ANSWER: METABOLISM AND CELLULAR RESPIRATION
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many major systems are there in the human body
ANSWER: 5 (nervous, respiratory, immune, endocrine, circulatory)
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which of the 5 systems in the human body handles processes associated with the brain
ANSWER: NERVOUS SYSTEM
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the traditional 5 kingdoms
ANSWER: MONERA, PROTISTA, FUNGI, ANIMALIA, PLANTAE
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are the 3 parts of the modern three-domain system
ANSWER: ARCHAEA, BACTERIA, EUKARYA
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many bones are there in the human body
ANSWER: 206
10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of these bones is part of the face
W) Ethmoid bone
X) Hyoid bone
Y) Vomer bone
Z) Incus
ANSWER: Y) Vomer bone
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many chambers of the heart are there in the human body
ANSWER: 4 (left atrium, right atrium, left ventricle, right ventricle)
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In general, which chamber is considered most important
W) Left ventricle
X) Right ventricle
Y) Left atrium
Z) Right atrium
ANSWER: W) Left ventricle
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Why do birds such as raptors have better vision than humans
ANSWER: DENSER PHOTORECEPTORS IN RETINA (anything similar)
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice About how many times sharper is a raptor's vision than that of a human
W) 2
X) 4
Y) 6
Z) 8
ANSWER: Z) 8
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What molecule is mostly always present in lipids
ANSWER: GLYCEROL
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are proteins made up of
ANSWER: AMINO ACIDS
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Who recommended the use of low temperatures to control bleeding and swelling
ANSWER: HIPPOCRATES
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer At what temperature, equivalent to that of a certain liquid substance, can cells be indefinitely suspended in
ANSWER: LIQUID NITROGEN TEMPERATURE (between 66 K and 77.2 K)
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Who first introduced bacteria
ANSWER: ANTON VON LEEUWENHOEK
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the three main parts of the brain
ANSWER: CEREBRUM, CEREBELLUM, BRAIN STEM
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which part of the brain controls balance of the human body
ANSWER: CEREBELLUM
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When does the heart stop to rest
ANSWER: NEVER; DEATH
17. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice About how many times does the heart beat in the average human lifetime
W) 1 million
X) 1.5 billion
Y) 2.5 billion
Z) 1 trillion
ANSWER: Y) 2.5 billion
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What branch of biology usually refers to genetic engineering
ANSWER: BIOTECHNOLOGY
18. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following does biotechnology NOT apply in
W) Health care
X) Crop production
Y) Agriculture
Z) Government
ANSWER: Z) Government
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the study of fungi
ANSWER: MYCOLOGY
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer What was the first fungus based medication
ANSWER: PENICILLIN
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the study of prehistoric life
ANSWER: PALEONTOLOGY
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Where are chromosomes found
ANSWER: CELLS
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer What are chromosomes made up of
ANSWER: DNA AND PROTEIN
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the study of reptiles and amphibians
ANSWER: HERPETOLOGY
22. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In what medicinal way has snake venom been used
W) Curing brain disease
X) Treating heart attack cases
Y) Treating broken bones
Z) Curing spinal issues
ANSWER: X) Treating heart attack cases
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the study of birds
ANSWER: ORNITHOLOGY
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer Why are the bones in birds' wings so much lighter than that of humans' arms
ANSWER: THE BONES IN THE WINGS ARE HOLLOW (also accept: close answers)
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What does DNA stand for
ANSWER: DEOXYRIBONUCLEIC ACID
24. BONUS Biology Short Answer How does DNA physically differ from RNA
ANSWER: DNA IS DOUBLE STRANDED; RNA IS SINGLE STRANDED
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the medical condition in which a person's spine is curved or rotated
ANSWER: SCOLIOSIS
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer What two letters of the alphabet does a spine that has scoliosis resemble
ANSWER: S AND C
