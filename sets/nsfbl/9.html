1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three proteins that can act as molecular motors I. Myosin II. Kinesin III. Dynein
ANSWER: ALL
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer A plant needs many different elements to survive, but if a plant is deficient in an element, a color change is often visible. If a plant’s leaves turn purple, it is indicative of the deficiency of what element?
ANSWER: PHOSPHORUS
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Adrian is trying to react nitrous oxide with oxygen gas to produce nitrogen dioxide. He knows beforehand that the standard enthalpy of formation of nitrous oxide is 90 kJ/mol. Given that the standard enthalpy of the reaction is -114 kJ/mol, what is the standard enthalpy of formation of nitrogen dioxide, in kilojoules/mole?
ANSWER: 33 kJ/mol
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following scenarios that will result in buffer solutions when mixed in equal volumes I. 0.1 M H3PO4 and 0.1 M Na3PO4 II. 0.3 M HBr and 0.3 M NaBr III. 0.4 M HCl and 0.8 M NH3
ANSWER: I AND III
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the ratio between the moments of inertia of a solid sphere and a thin spherical shell if both have the same mass and radius?
ANSWER: ⅗ (do not accept: 5/3)
3. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following quasiparticles does not result from spin-charge separation?
W) Holon
X) Spinon
Y) Exciton
Z) Orbiton
ANSWER: Y) Exciton
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Stars that have a collapsing core of mass greater than 1.4 solar masses overcome electron degeneracy pressure and form what two products from the combination of electrons and protons?
ANSWER: NEUTRON AND NEUTRINO
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What path do pre-main sequence stars with masses greater than 0.5 solar masses take on the Hertzprung-Russell diagram after the end of the Hayashi track?
ANSWER: HENYEY TRACK
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Evaluate the limit as x goes to infinity of x2/ex

ANSWER: 1/35
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Researchers at Argonne National Laboratory are developing neural networks to better estimate the masses, spin and other characteristics about black holes involved in a merger based on their gravitational waves. X) An isolated, spherical, rotating planet
Y) A pulsating, spherical star

ANSWER: Z) An asymmetric planetoid

8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A student gets fingerprints on a cuvette before using spectrophotometry to determine the concentration of a colored substance. Which of the following statements best describes its effect on the absorbance and reported concentration?

Y) Decreased Absorbance, Too Low Concentration
Z) Decreased Absorbance, Too High Concentration
