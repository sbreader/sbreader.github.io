1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Unlike the structure of a plant, this algal body lacks true roots, stems, and leaves. Rather, it uses holdfasts, stipes, and blades to promote its photosynthesis. What is the term for this plant-like protist body form that is familiar in seaweeds and kelps?
ANSWER: thallus
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following Protist phyla, which includes brown algae, golden algae, and diatoms, is a group of marine organisms that are some of the most important photosynthetic organisms on the planet, and is named after its "hairy" flagella?
W) Alveolates
X) Stramenopiles
Y) Excavates
Z) Rhizarians
ANSWER: X) Stramenopiles
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Symbolized usually by Z, this quantity is a complex number whose complex part is the reactance X and whose real part is the resistance R. As a parallel to DC's resistance, what is the term for the measure of opposition to AC?
ANSWER: Electrical Impedence
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, indicate which 2 of the following 4 statements are true about tornados: 1: The Saffir-Simpson scale is used to measure tornado intensity 2: Tornados originate from the mesocyclone of a thunderstorm 3: When a tornado has been sighted in the local area, a tornado watch is alerted 4: A descending tornado is known as a funnel cloud before hitting the ground
ANSWER: 2 and 4
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In a warm front, what is the term that describes warm air gliding up along a cold air mass?
ANSWER: overrunning
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 4 functional groups that contain atoms that have been sp2 hybridized: 1: aldehyde 2: amine 3: carboxylic acid 4: ester
ANSWER: 1,3, and 4
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What type of statistical error is the error of rejecting a null hypothesis when it is actually true, thus indicating a test of poor specificity, and is usually known as a false-positive?
ANSWER: Type I error
5. BONUS Math Short Answer What mathematical theorem, which allows one to calculate the probability of a false positive result, rests on the principle that the rate of false positives is not only dependent on the accuracy of the test, but the frequency of occurrence in the population?
ANSWER: Bayes' theorem
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What reformulation of classical mechanics combines the conservation of momentum with the conservation of energy, the integral of which in respect to time is action, and is based upon Hamilton's principle, also known as the principle of least action?
ANSWER: Lagrangian
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, indicate which 2 of the following 4 groups of organisms are more often haploid than diploid: 1: Apicomplexans 2: Coniferaphyta 3: Echinodermata 4: Bryophyta
ANSWER: 1 and 4
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the term for electromagnetic radiation that is similar to cyclotron radiation but is generated by the acceleration of ultrarelativistic charged particles through magnetic fields, and can be artificially generated or observed in active galatic nuclei?
ANSWER: synchrotron radiation
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the effect that describes the self-polarization of high-energy, relativistic electrons or positrons moving in a magnetic field to orientate the particles with spin opposite to the magnetic field, and which occurs through the emission of spin-flip synchrotron radiation?
ANSWER: Sokolov-Ternov effect
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is term for the faint, white glow seen in the night sky that extends from the vicinity of the sun along the ecliptic, and is caused by scattered sunlight off of interplanetary dust?
ANSWER: Zodiacal light
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, indicate all of the following 3 substances that play an integral role in maintaining acid-base homeostasis: 1: proteins 2: phosphate 3: bicarbonate
ANSWER: all of them
10. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice If blood pH shifts to 7.2, which of the following responses will most likely occur
W) phosphate ions are released from cells and bones
X) phosphate ions are absorbed into cells and bones
Y) hyperventilation expels CO2, reducing the amount of carbonic acid
Z) hypoventilation retains CO2, increasing the amount of carbonic acid
ANSWER: Y) hyperventilation expels CO2, reducing the amount of carbonic acid
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Find ei*pi/6 in the form a + bi.
ANSWER: √3/2 + i/2
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Also known as a protein immunoblot, what is an extremely useful analytical technique used to detect specific proteins via gel electrophoresis?
ANSWER: Western blot
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer As an extension to western blotting, eastern blots detect carbohydrate epitopes in order to analyze what step of protein biosynthesis?
ANSWER: post-translational modification
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What transformation in special relativity describes how two observers' varying measurements of space and time can be converted into each others' frames of reference?
ANSWER: Lorentz transformation
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following active galatic nuclei contains highly ionized gas that produce spectra containing only narrow absorption lines?
W) quasar
X) Seyfert Type I
Y) Seyfert Type II
Z) blazar
ANSWER: Y) Seyfert Type II
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What unification model joins Seyfert Type I and Seyfert Type II galaxies by proposing that a torus of obscuring material blocks the accretion disc in Type II Seyferts, thus blocking broad emission lines?
ANSWER: radio-quiet unification
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name of number, indicate which 2 of the following 4 applications are the major uses for sulfuric acid: 1: the production of phosphoric acid 2: a component of lead-acid batteries 3: in oil well acidification 4: as a secretion of the stomach in gastric acid
ANSWER: 1 and 2
17. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice In a binomial probability distribution, an event with probability 0.4 is performed 6 times. What is the mean and variance of this distribution?
W) mean is 4, variance is 1.44
X) mean is 4, variance is 1.2
Y) mean is 2.4, variance is 1.44
Z) mean is 2.4, variance is 1.2
ANSWER: Y) mean is 2.4, variance is 1.44
17. BONUS Math Short Answer In an office, an average of 6 calls come in per hour. What is the probability that 2 calls come in during a 30-minute period? You need not simplify.
ANSWER: 4.5*e-3
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In the construction of 1 molecule of G3P, how much ATP and NADPH must be consumed?
W) 3 ATP, 3 NADPH
X) 6 ATP, 3 NADPH
Y) 6 ATP, 6 NADPH
Z) 9 ATP, 6 NADPH
ANSWER: Z) 9 ATP, 6 NADPH
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer Since more ATP is required than NADPH to perform the Calvin Cycle, what alternative path of the light dependent reactions is often used to generate more ATP through feeding electrons from Ferridoxin back to the cytochrome complex?
ANSWER: cyclic electron flow
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the term for an optical phenomenon that creates a bright spot around the shadow of a viewer's head, caused by backscattering on the projected surface?
ANSWER: heiligenschein

X) 34 degrees

21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Most often seen in emerging plutons, such as Yosemite's Half Dome, what type of mass wasting is caused by fracturing of concentric slabs after the removal of overburden and the rebound of rock below it?
ANSWER: exfoliation
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following types of isomers have the same structural formula but have very different shapes due to rotations about one or more bonds, such as the chair and boat forms of cyclohexane?
W) cis-trans
X) conformational
Y) enantiomers
Z) tautomers
ANSWER: X) conformational
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What racemic drug, introduced as a sedative in the 1950s, had one enantiomer that treated morning sickness, but one enantiomer that was a teratogen?
ANSWER: thalidomide
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the term for the property of a set that measures the number of elements in the set? For example, for the set A = {1,2, 4}, this property equals 3.
ANSWER: cardinality
24. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Noting that the formation of a mole of glucose takes around 686 kcal, that each mole of ATP stores about 7.3 kcal, and that about 38 moles of ATP are produced for each mole of glucose, what is the approximate efficiency of cellular respiration?
W) 20%
X) 40%
Y) 60%
Z) 80%
ANSWER: X) 40%
24. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the process in which energy stored in the form of a hydrogen ion gradient across a cellular membrane is used to drive cellular work such as the synthesis of ATP?
ANSWER: chemiosmosis
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the protein subunit of microfilaments, which are 7nm intertwined strands that control changes in cell shape and muscle contraction?
ANSWER: actin
