ANSWER: -30

ANSWER: COLLAGEN

W) Skin
X) Liver
Y) Gallbladder
Z) Heart
ANSWER: W) Skin

W) Methane
X) Carbon dioxide
Y) Diatomic oxygen
Z) Water
ANSWER: W) Methane
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice As air rises, pressure decreases. This causes the air to expand, and as the air expands, it decreases in temperature. This is an example of which of the following cooling mechanisms?
W) Isothermal
X) Adiabatic
Y) Isochoric
Z) Isentropic
ANSWER: X) Adiabatic
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Carbon dioxide is typically produced during the reaction of hydrochloric acid with which mineral group that is typically defined with the formulaic CO32- unit?

W) Solid
X) Liquid

Z) Plasma
