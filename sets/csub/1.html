1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the most common name for the vector for Rocky Mountain Spotted Fever?
ANSWER: TICK
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Binomial nomenclature is classically made up of what two taxonomical terms?
ANSWER: GENUS AND SPECIES
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In the periodic table, the number found above each atomic symbol represents what value?
ANSWER: ATOMIC NUMBER (also accept: NUMBER OF PROTONS)
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many molecules of HCl are in 1 mole of HCl?
ANSWER: 6.022 × 1023 (also accept: 6.023 × 1023; AVOGADRO’S NUMBER)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What property of waves allows them to bend around the edges of an obstacle:
W) reflection
X) diffraction
Y) deflection
Z) dispersion
ANSWER: X) diffraction
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Considered by some as the forgotten father of electricity and technology, what person was the inventor of the alternating current motor and created a device in Colorado Springs that was able to produce bolts of lightning 130 feet long:
ANSWER: (NIKOLA) TESLA
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Taurids is to November as the Perseids is to:
W) July
X) August
Y) September
Z) October
ANSWER: X) August
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the main component of the atmosphere of Mars:
W) hydrogen
X) helium
Y) nitrogen
Z) carbon dioxide
ANSWER: Z) carbon dioxide
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is a rational number:
W) 2 + √5
X) √3
Y) √(4/9)
Z) √6
ANSWER: Y) √(4/9)
5. BONUS Math Short Answer If you count from 1 to 100, how many 9's will you pass on the way?
ANSWER: 20
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a vent in the surface of the Earth, from which magma, solid rock debris, and gases erupt?
ANSWER: VOLCANO
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Mount Rainier, Mount Baker and Mount Hood in the U.S. are all examples of:
W) stratovolcanoes
X) tephra cone volcanoes
Y) shield volcanoes
Z) basaltic lava volcanoes
ANSWER: W) stratovolcanoes
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Express the number 0.051 in proper scientific notation with the correct amount of significant figures:
ANSWER: 5.1 × 10-2
7. BONUS Energy Short Answer Rounded to the nearest decimal place, what is the Kelvin equivalent of -60°C?
ANSWER: 213
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The binary number 1010 is equivalent to what decimal number?
ANSWER: 10
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer What does the acronym RDRAM stand for?
ANSWER: RAMBUS DYNAMIC RANDOM ACCESS MEMORY
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A lack of the neurotransmitter dopamine in the brain of humans causes what disease:
ANSWER: PARKINSON’S
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT typically considered part of the immune system:
W) Kupffer cell
X) mast cell
Y) eosinophile
Z) fibroblast
ANSWER: Z) fibroblast
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The atomic number represents the number of what in an atom?
ANSWER: PROTONS (also accept: ELECTRONS) (do not accept: PROTONS PLUS ELECTRONS)
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer From the lowest to the highest atomic number, give the full names of the first 4 halogens:
ANSWER: FLUORINE; CHLORINE; BROMINE; IODINE
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A white-colored object illuminated by a yellow light will appear as what color to the human eye?
ANSWER: YELLOW
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the current, in amperes, through a 1200 watt heater running on a 120 volt power source:
ANSWER: 10
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which planet in our solar system has the strongest magnetic field?
ANSWER: JUPITER
12. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Andromeda Galaxy is which of the following types of galaxies:
W) elliptical
X) irregular

Z) barred spiral

ANSWER: JANUARY

ANSWER: 540°

24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is TRUE about sodium:
W) it is the 2nd most reactive of the alkali metals

25. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is the best reason that cigarette smoke is bluish when lit by sunlight:
W) scattering intensity is inversely related to the forth power of the incident wavelength
X) the light is diffracted preferentially in the blue region
Y) the carbon dioxide in the smoke absorbs all other frequencies except blue
Z) each smoke particle disperses the light like a rainbow and the blue region is easiest to see in daylight
ANSWER: W) scattering intensity is inversely related to the forth power of the incident wavelength
