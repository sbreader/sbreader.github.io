1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Supination and pronation are both movements of the forearm. Which of the following best describes the positions of the radius and the ulna during these movements?
W) During supination, the radius and ulna form an X-shape. During pronation, the radius and ulna are parallel.
X) During supination, the radius and ulna are parallel. During pronation, the radius and ulna form an X-shape.
Y) During both supination and pronation, the radius and ulna are parallel.
Z) During both supination and pronation, the radius and ulna form an X-shape.
ANSWER: X) During supination, the radius and ulna are parallel. During pronation, the radius and ulna form an X-shape.
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Answer the following questions. 1) What is the largest joint in the human body? 2) What bones articulate at this joint?
ANSWER: 1) Knee joint; 2) Femur, tibia and patella
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Vanadium (V) Oxide is commonly used as a catalyst in the contact process. It catalyzes the oxidation of sulfur dioxide in the presence of oxygen to give what common atmospheric pollutant?
ANSWER: SULFUR TRIOXIDE
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What phenomenon prevents colloid suspension like Iron III Hydroxide from settling out of solution? The small particles are constantly bombarded by solvent molecules due to this.
ANSWER: BROWNIAN MOTION
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the term for the reactive force to a centripetal force?
ANSWER: REACTIVE CENTRIFUGAL FORCE

ANSWER: LORENTZ FACTOR

7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Answer the following questions. 1) What is the name of this muscle, which originates from the sternum and clavicle and inserts into the temporal bone? ANSWER: 1) Yellow; 2) Green; 3) Blue
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In Lagrangian mechanics, what property of a system is equal to the sum of the kinetic energies of all the objects in the system minus the sum of all the potential energies of all the objects in the system?
W) Lagrangian
X) Euler-Lagrangian energy
Y) Euler energy
Z) D’Alembert’s Factor
ANSWER: W) Lagrangian
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer There is a star of mass n and a planet whose mass m is much less than that of the star. The planet and star interact through the gravitational potential V(r) =-Gnm/r, where r is the distance separating the star and planet. A: What is the Lagrangian of the planet? B: Is the planet’s angular momentum constant?
ANSWER: A: 1/2mv2 + 1/2mr2 ω2 B: YES
