Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Glucose does not contain which of the following groups?
W) Carbonyl
X) Hydroxyl
Y) Hemiacetal
Z) Carboxyl
ANSWER: Z) Carboxyl
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the unbranched form of the sugar digested by the enzyme amylase in the mouth and small intestine, and is stored by plants for energy?
ANSWER: AMYLOSE
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which technique, used for studying cell structure and function, takes cells apart and separates the major organelles and subcellular structures from one another using a centrifuge?
ANSWER: CELL FRACTIONATION
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Complete the sentence: In an ultracentrifuge, substances with the greatest (BLANK) would land at the bottom.
W) Density
X) Viscosity
Y) Mass
Z) Solubility
ANSWER: W) Density
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following parts of a bacterium from innermost to outermost: capsule, plasma membrane, cell wall.
ANSWER: PLASMA MEMBRANE, CELL WALL, CAPSULE
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Vesicles in transit from one part of the cell to another are called what?
ANSWER: TRANSPORT VESICLES
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a type of plastid?
W) Chromoplast
X) Erythroplast
Y) Rhodoplast
Z) Leucoplast
ANSWER: X) Erythroplast
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which motor proteins found in the flagellum are responsible for its bending movement and serve as cross-links between two microtubule doublets?
ANSWER: DYNEIN
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the thin layer of pectins found between the primary walls of adjacent plant cells?
ANSWER: MIDDLE LAMELLA
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following proteins are not part of the extracellular matrix?
W) Fibronectin
X) Integrins
Y) Collagen
Z) Proteoglycans
ANSWER: X) Integrins
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of animal intercellular junction is found between skin cells to make it watertight?
ANSWER: TIGHT JUNCTION
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a type of transport protein?
W) Channel protein
X) Aquaporin
Y) Transport protein
Z) Lipotransferin
ANSWER: Z) Lipotransferin
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which type of carrier protein facilitates the passage of water molecules through the cell membrane in certain cells?
ANSWER: AQUAPORIN
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which stage of cellular respiration produces two molecules of NADH, two molecules of ATP, and two molecules of pyruvate?
ANSWER: GLYCOLYSIS
9. BONUS Biology Short Answer The conversion of pyruvate to acetyl CoA is known as what?
ANSWER: PYRUVATE DECARBOXYLATION
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Using words or numbers, give all of the following nutritional modes the reverse Krebs Cycle, in which acetyl CoA is synthesized from the end-products of the Krebs Cycle, would most likely be found in: photoautotrophs, chemoautotrophs, photoheterotrophs, and chemoheterotrophs.
ANSWER: PHOTOAUTOTROPHS AND CHEMOAUTOTROPHS (1ST AND 2ND)
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name all of the following molecules which an electron from NADH will travel through: complex I, complex II, complex III, complex IV, ubiquinone, ATP synthase, oxygen.
ANSWER: COMPLEX I, COMPLEX III, COMPLEX IV, UBIQUINONE, OXYGEN (1st, 3rd, 4th, 5th, and 7th)
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Using words or numbers, name all of the following molecules that can serve as final electron acceptors in the electron transport chain: oxygen, fluorine, sulfur, nitrogen, hydrogen.
ANSWER: OXYGEN, SULFUR, NITROGEN (1st, 3rd, AND 4th)
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The protein complexes in the electron transport chain have increasing...
W) Molecular mass
X) Electronegativity
Y) Proton affinity
Z) Stability
ANSWER: X) Electronegativity
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a C3 plant?
W) Rice
X) Soybeans
Y) Wheat
Z) Corn
ANSWER: Z) Corn
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice CAM photosynthesis has evolved in many...
W) Carnivorous plants
X) Succulent plants
Y) Woody plants
Z) Herbaceous plants
ANSWER: X) Succulent plants
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which enzyme in the C4 pathway incorporates carbon dioxide into PEP to form oxaloacetate?
ANSWER: PEP CARBOXYLASE
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Complete the analogy: addition of phosphate groups is to protein kinase as removal of phosphate groups is to what?
ANSWER: PROTEIN PHOSPHATASE
14. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which ion is more widely used as a second messenger than cAMP, and has a greater extracellular than intracellular concentration? When certain channels are opened, these ions flow inwards and activate various proteins.
ANSWER: CALCIUM
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the two structures that have replicated by the onset of mitosis.
ANSWER: CENTRIOLES AND CHROMOSOMES
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Consider a chromosome in which locus T is in the telomere of the P arm, locus U is on the telomere of the Q arm, locus P is in the center of the P arm, locus Q is in the center of the Q arm, and locus C is in the centromere. Which loci will be closest to the equatorial plate during metaphase and during anaphase?
ANSWER: LOCUS C DURING METAPHASE AND LOCUS U DURING ANAPHASE
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following causes the elongation of the cell during anaphase?
W) Outward movement of centrioles
X) Lengthening of microtubules along the length of the cell
Y) Shortening of microtubules at the equatorial plane
Z) Inward fluctuation of the plasma membrane
ANSWER: X) Lengthening of microtubules along the length of the cell
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which complex is a protein structure that forms between two homologous chromosomes during meiosis and that is thought to mediate chromosome pairing, synapsis, and recombination?
ANSWER: SYNAPTONEMAL COMPLEX
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which genetic law states that when any individual produces gametes, the copies of a gene separate so that each gamete receives only one copy?
ANSWER: LAW OF SEGREGATION
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer In which genetic disorder are the neurons of a child unable to metabolize certain fats because of the inactivity of the enzyme hexosaminidase A?
ANSWER: TAY-SACHS DISEASE
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following are closest to modern land plants?
W) Red algae
X) Chlorophytes
Y) Charophytes
Z) Embryophytes
ANSWER: Y) Charophytes
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer Gametophytes contain gametangia, which are multicellular organs that produce gametes. What are the corresponding structures in sporophytes called, which produce spores?
ANSWER: SPORANGIA
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which three clades compose the nonvascular plants, or the bryophytes?
ANSWER: LIVERWORTS, HORNWORTS, AND MOSSES (PHYLA HEPATOPHYTA, ANTHOCEROPHYTA, AND BRYOPHYTA)
19. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following structures is not part of the moss sporophyte?
W) Capsule
X) Sorus
Y) Seta
Z) Foot
ANSWER: X) Sorus
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following proteins is not involved in the regulation of sister chromatid cohesion?
W) Cohesin
X) Separase
Y) Securin
Z) Geminin
ANSWER: Z) Geminin
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer The p53 protein senses DNA damage and can halt progression of the cell cycle in which phase?
ANSWER: G1
21. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The upper and lower epidermis of a plant is composed of what plant tissue type?
W) Parenchymal
X) Protective
Y) Sclerenchymal
Z) Chollenchymal
ANSWER: X) Protective
21. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name the two types of water-conducting cells in the xylem.
ANSWER: TRACHEIDS AND VESSEL ELEMENTS
22. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A scientist takes a cross-section of the stem of a woody plant. He identifies where the primary xylem and primary phloem lie. Which of the following is not a layer that would be found between them?
W) Pith
X) Vascular cambium
Y) Secondary xylem
Z) Secondary phloem
ANSWER: W) Pith
22. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following would not be found in the vascular cylinder of a young root?
W) Endodermis
X) Pericycle
Y) Cortex
Z) Xylem
ANSWER: Y) Cortex
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Using words or numbers, order the following four layers of the leaf from topmost to bottommost: spongy mesophyll, upper epidermis, lower epidermis, palisade mesophyll.
ANSWER: UPPER EPIDERMIS, PALISADE MESOPHYLL, SPONGY MESOPHYLL, LOWER EPIDERMIS
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer Within the stem of a woody plant, the cork, cork cambium, primary phloem, and secondary phloem comprise what layer?
ANSWER: BARK
24. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which plant hormones are present in all plant tissues and promote cell expansion and cell division in shoots, retard leaf abscission, and promote xylem differentiation?
ANSWER: BRASSINOSTEROIDS
24. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which plant hormone inhibits growth and stimulates responses to several stress situations?
ANSWER: ABSCISIC ACID
25. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice To sense gravity and maintain equilibrium, most invertebrates rely on sensory organs called what?
W) Statocyst
X) Ottocyst
Y) Utricle
Z) Saccule
ANSWER: W) Statocyst
25. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following layers of the kidney from outermost to innermost: renal cortex, calyx, renal medulla, papilla.
ANSWER: RENAL CORTEX, RENAL MEDULLA, PAPILLA, CALYX
