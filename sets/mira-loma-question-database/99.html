1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What are the most common types of histones in chromatin?
W) H2, H2B, H3, H4
X) H1A, H2A, H3, H4
Y) H1A, H1B, H2, H3
Z) H2A, H2B, H3, H4
ANSWER: Z) H2A, H2B, H3, H4
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer H2A, H2B, H3, and H4 are the 4 types of histones most critical to the DNA packaging. What fifth type of histone is involved in a further stage of DNA packaging?
ANSWER: H1

ANSWER: Pulvini

ANSWER: Red
