1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice An LC-circuit is most analogous to which of the following mechanical systems?
W) A barrel rolling down an incline
X) A fluid flowing without resistance
Y) A mass oscillating on a spring
Z) A ball bouncing on a table until it gradually comes to rest
ANSWER: Y) A mass oscillating on a spring
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A uniformly-charged insulating sphere of radius 5 is placed at the origin. At which of the following coordinates is the magnitude of the electric field the greatest?
W) (1, 0, 0)
X) (4, 3, 0)
Y) (5, 1, 1)
Z) (1, 2, 2)
ANSWER: X) (4, 3, 0)
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following aluminosilicate minerals forms at the highest temperature and lowest pressure?
W) Kyanite
X) Sillimanite
Y) Andalusite
Z) Granulite
ANSWER: W) Kyanite
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four minerals from highest to lowest crystallization temperature 1) Muscovite 2) Quartz 3) Pyroxene 4) Biotite.

15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The van’t Hoff factor for acetic acid dissolving in benzene is around 0.5, while the van’t Hoff factor for acetone under the same conditions is around 1. Which of the following best explains this observation?
