Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following nitrogenous bases is the most structurally similar to guanine?
W) Thymine
X) Uracil
Y) Adenine
Z) Cytosine
ANSWER: Y) Adenine
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a protist?
W) Cyanobacterium
X) Mosquito
Y) Mushroom
Z) Kelp
ANSWER: Z) Kelp
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If the intercepts of a line are (8, 0) and (0, –8), what is the slope-intercept equation of the line?
ANSWER: y=x-8
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Solve the following equation for x: x2+17x-480=0.
ANSWER: –32; 15
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A substance is sampled multiple times for some measurements. The mass is recorded on the y-axis and volume on the x-axis. What physical property of that substance will equal to the slope?
ANSWER: DENSITY
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following diatomic bonds is most polar?
W) Br-Br
X) C – H
Y) C – N
Z) Cl – H
ANSWER: Z) Cl – H
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the 3-kelvin radiation field that fills all of space?
ANSWER: CMB (also accept: COSMIC MICROWAVE BACKGROUND; CMBR; COSMIC MICROWAVE BACKGROUND RADIATION)
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer RR Lyrae and Cepheid stars are examples of what general type of stars?
ANSWER: VARIABLES (also accept: VARIABLE STARS)
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab are studying the genomes of fungi that are found living in a mutualistic relationship with algae. What is the term for this composite organism?
ANSWER: LICHEN
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Argonne National Lab researchers are studying how to biosynthesize gold nanoparticles within hypoxic tissues. The main organ they are studying accounts for 25% of the body’s oxygen demand but only 2% of the body’s mass. What organ is this?
ANSWER: BRAIN
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Mammals are named for their mammary glands, which produce what substance?
ANSWER: MILK
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of single-celled organisms 1) They are all prokaryotic 2) They can sexually reproduce 3) They include both autotrophs and heterotrophs.
ANSWER: 2 and 3
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Giving your answer as a decimal, decreasing a number by 54% is equivalent to multiplying it by what?
ANSWER: 0.46 (also accept: .46)
7. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the volume of a rectangular pyramid with base dimensions 13 and 9 and height 15?
ANSWER: 585
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice David has two ramps with the same height but at different angles to the horizontal. He places identical marbles at the tops of the ramps and allows them to roll down. Which of the following quantities will be the same for the two marbles?
W) Time taken to roll down the ramp
X) Velocity at the bottom of the ramp
Y) Acceleration along the ramp
Z) Position along the ramp after one second
ANSWER: X) Velocity at the bottom of the ramp
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three ionic compounds that are readily soluble in water 1) Lithium phosphate 2) Silver nitrate 3) Calcium hydroxide.
ANSWER: 2 only
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term is used to describe ground that is frozen for a period of at least two years?
ANSWER: PERMAFROST
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of felsic magma 1) It is more viscous than mafic magma 2) It has a higher melting temperature than mafic magma 3) It has a higher gas content than mafic magma.
ANSWER: 1 and 3
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Argonne National Lab are using copper-based photocatalysts to reduce carbon dioxide into useful compounds. To trace the movement of carbon atoms, they used carbon dioxide composed of a stable yet uncommon isotope of carbon. What isotope was this?
ANSWER: CARBON-13 (also accept: C-13)
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of rare earth elements 1) They are all metals 2) They are all actinides 3) Their total abundance in Earth’s crust is lower than gold.
ANSWER: 1 only
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Halophytes are plants that have adapted to which of the following environmental challenges?
W) Aridity
X) Salinity
Y) Long winters
Z) Flooded soil
ANSWER: X) Salinity
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer The medulla, pons, and midbrain collectively form what structure of the brain?
ANSWER: BRAINSTEM
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Simplify the following expression: 21 – 5 times 3
ANSWER: 6
12. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the largest prime factor of 2385?
ANSWER: 53
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A wave is generated by moving one end of a rope up and down, so that the displacement of the rope is perpendicular to the direction of travel of the wave. What is the term for this type of wave?
ANSWER: TRANSVERSE (do not accept: SINUSOIDAL)
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is primarily responsible for the presence of carbon-14 used in radiometric dating?
W) Volcanic gasses
X) Biomass burning
Y) Cosmic rays
Z) Lightning
ANSWER: Y) Cosmic rays
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals, when examined at a molecular level, would appear to be a stack of loosely interacting sheets?
W) Muscovite
X) Augite
Y) Olivine
Z) Quartz
ANSWER: W) Muscovite
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Brookhaven National Lab researchers are working on a reactor that can reduce a notable greenhouse gas into compounds such as formic acid, ethanol, and acetic acid. Which greenhouse gas are they attempting to reduce?
ANSWER: CARBON DIOXIDE (also accept: CO2)
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following four statements that are true of graphene 1) It is an excellent electrical conductor 2) It is an excellent heat conductor 3) It contains silicon atoms 4) It contains carbon atoms.
ANSWER: 1 and 2 and 4
30. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice According to VSEPR theory, what is the molecular geometry of water?
W) Bent
X) Linear
Y) Tetrahedral
Z) Trigonal planar
ANSWER: W) Bent
