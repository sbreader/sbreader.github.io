11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Lab have been studying the impacts of chymotrypsin in rumination digestion in order to better aid economical agriculture. Which of the following is NOT correct regarding chymotrypsin's mechanism of catalysis?
