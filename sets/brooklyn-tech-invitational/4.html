Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following five clades that did NOT come about during the Cambrian explosion. 1. Eutherians 2. Tunicates 3. Reptiles 4. Gastropods 5. Crustaceans
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term used to describe an organism with more than two sets of chromosomes, its polyploidy being derived from the asexual reproduction of a sterile hybrid?
ANSWER: ALLOPOLYPLOID (do not accept: autopolyploid)
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Cocaine has a neutral tertiary amine group with a pKa of 5.6. What is the pKa of cocaine's conjugate acid in an aqueous solution at 25° Celsius?
ANSWER: 8.4
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Calculate the molarity of a solution made by diluting 0.050 L of 0.10 M HCl solution to a volume of 1.0 L.
ANSWER: 0.005 M (also accept: 5.0 × 10-3 M)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A 4-kilogram object receives a force with respect to time according to the relationship F = 8t Newtons from time t = 0 seconds to t = 6 seconds. Giving your answer in kilogram meters per second, what is the impulse that the object experiences during this time interval?
ANSWER: 144
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Eric is sipping on a cup of water. Emma, who is mad, creates a small hole in the side of his cup using her psychic powers. Assuming the water is an ideal fluid and assuming the height of the water above the hole is h, and acceleration due to gravity is g, what is the speed of the water as it exits the hole in terms of h and g?
ANSWER: √(2gh)
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice An integer is formed by writing the integers from 1 to 30 side-by-side: 123...2930. What is the remainder when this number is divided by 9?
W) 2
X) 3
Y) 6
Z) 7
ANSWER: Y) 6
4. BONUS Math Short Answer A linear transformation defined by the 2x2 matrix with the first row [7, 6] and the second row [6, 7] is applied to a circle in the plane with an area of 9π. What is the product of the lengths of the major and minor axes of the resulting ellipse?
ANSWER: 468
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true about the Pacific Ocean during El Niño?
W) Trade winds strengthen along the ocean
X) The sea-surface temperature increases
Y) There is a decrease in the upwelling of water
Z) There is an increase in rainfall towards the east
ANSWER: W) Trade winds strengthen along the ocean
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following regions of the ocean is the deepest?
W) Abyssopelagic
X) Benthopelagic
Y) Bathypelagic
Z) Hadopelagic
ANSWER: Z) Hadopelagic
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following phytohormones is produced in response to drought and inhibits photosynthesis?
W) gibberellins
X) salicylic acid
Y) abscisic acid
Z) brassinosteroids
ANSWER: Y) abscisic acid
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer What term is used to describe the changing network of cytoplasm throughout a plant, composed of plasmodesmata that can open and close based on environmental factors?
ANSWER: symplast
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following 5 substances that are soluble in water. 1. NaCl 2. PbBr2 3. BaSO4 4. Ca3(PO4)2 5. BaCrO4
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In the chemical equation CH4 + 2O2 → CO2 + 2H2O, 16.0 g of Oxygen gas reacts with excess Methane. Rounding your answers to the nearest gram, how many grams of Carbon Dioxide and Water, respectively, are produced?
ANSWER: 11g CO2, 9g H2O (also accept: 11 and 9) (do not accept: 9 and 11)
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following types of particles are responsible for mediating the fundamental forces in the Standard Model?
W) Bosons
X) Baryons
Y) Fermions
Z) Mesons
ANSWER: W) Bosons
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer What principle explains why light bends when passing between materials with different refractive properties by stating that light chooses the path that minimizes the duration of travel?
ANSWER: Fermat’s principle (also accept: Principle of least time; Fermat’s principle of least time)
9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Given the roots of quadratic ax2+bx+c are reciprocals, which of the following MUST be true?
W) b = 0
X) a = c
Y) b2-4ac = 1
Z) b = ac
ANSWER: X) a = c
9. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the sum of the first 6 terms of the geometric sequence with first term 2 and second term 6?
ANSWER: 728
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Lunar rock samples from the Apollo missions contain evidence of many large craters formed between 4.1 and 3.8 billion years ago. This evidence supports the existence of what hypothesized period of intense asteroid impacts on the Earth and Moon?
ANSWER: LATE HEAVY BOMBARDMENT (also accept: LHB; LUNAR CATACLYSM) (do not accept: Late Great Bombardment)
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Star A has a temperature of 6700K, while Star B has a peak wavelength of 410 nm. Given that Wein's constant equals 2.9 × 10-3 meters Kelvin, which of the two stars will be hotter?
ANSWER: Star B
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the primary circulatory fluid in insects.
ANSWER: Hemolymph
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following structures found in insects performs the function most similar to that of the human pituitary gland?
W) Senilla
X) Fat body
Y) Corpora allata
Z) Malpighian tubules
ANSWER: Y) Corpora allata
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In which orbital, including both shell and subshell, is the element Gold located in?
ANSWER: 5d (do not accept: 5; d)
13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice When a material is stretched within its elastic limit, the ratio of stress to strain remains constant. Which of the following identifies this ratio?
W) Poisson’s ratio
X) Shear modulus
Y) Bulk modulus
Z) Young’s modulus
ANSWER: Z) Young’s modulus
13. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Yanzi is tending to her garden. To break into dirt, Yanzi points a shovel downwards and pushes on the shovelhead. What simple machine best describes the head?
W) Screw
X) Incline plane
Y) Lever
Z) Wedge
ANSWER: Z) Wedge
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Name all of the following three sequences that are considered harmonic. 1) 2, 1, ⅔,½… 2) ¼, 3/10, ⅜, ½… 3) ½, ¼, ⅛, 1/16, …
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
14. BONUS Math Multiple Choice In a school of 160 students, 88 students take algebra, 73 take geometry, 71 take trigonometry, 34 take trigonometry and algebra, 30 take trigonometry and geometry, 31 take algebra and geometry, and 19 take all three. Compute how many students take none of these courses.
W) 2
X) 4
Y) 8
Z) 16
ANSWER: X) 4
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name for all changes a sediment goes through before metamorphism?
W) Lithification
X) Orogenesis
Y) Diagenesis
Z) Authigenesis
ANSWER: Y) Diagenesis
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following four types of rock that are typically formed through evaporation. 1) Hematite 2) Dolomite 3) Magnesite 4) Calcite
ANSWER: 2, 3, 4
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name all of the following three structures that enable bacteria to form biofilms. 1. Fimbriae 2. Capsules 3. Glycocalyces
ANSWER: all
16. BONUS Biology Short Answer Bacteria form mating bridges to transfer what specific plasmid to other bacteria, allowing those bacteria to produce pili?
ANSWER: F factor (also accept: fertility factor; F factor plasmid; fertility factor plasmid; F plasmid)
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements has the lowest melting point?
W) Oxygen
X) Magnesium
Y) Xenon
Z) Bromine
ANSWER: W) Oxygen
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following four ions in order of increasing size for the dominant ionic form 1) Fluoride 2) Chloride 3) Sodium 4) Magnesium
ANSWER: 4, 3, 1, 2 (also accept: MAGNESIUM, SODIUM, FLUORIDE, CHLORIDE)
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Considered the first electric battery after its invention in 1800, what is the name associated with the set of individual Galvanic cells placed in series?
ANSWER: Voltaic pile
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer In an ideal gas, the average speed of the gas particles is proportional to what POWER of the absolute temperature?
ANSWER: ONE-HALF (also accept: 0.5; Root) (do not accept: Square root)
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In the proof that the limit of sin(t)/t as t approaches 0 is 1, what key theorem is necessary?
ANSWER: SQUEEZE THEOREM (also accept: SANDWICH THEOREM; PINCHING THEOREM; SANDWICH RULE; POLICE THEOREM; BETWEEN THEOREM; SQUEEZE LEMMA) (do not accept: L'Hôpital's Rule)
19. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the limit as t approaches 0 of (tan(t) − sin(t))/t3?
ANSWER: ½ (also accept: 0.5)
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four processes that form a glacier from first to last 1) Snowflakes are compressed and form granular ice 2) Increased weight forms glacial ice 3) Snow grains begin to fuse with each other 4) Bubbles compress for typically 2 years and become firn
ANSWER: 1, 3, 4, 2
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the shallow bowl-shaped depressions usually formed as a glacier undergoes ablation?
ANSWER: Suncups
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What order of mammals is the only mammalian group that is considered oviparous?
ANSWER: monotremes (also accept: MONOTREMATA)
21. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following brain structures, found in eutherians, is NOT found in marsupials nor monotremes?
W) visual cortex
X) cerebellum
Y) brainstem
Z) corpus callosum
ANSWER: Z) corpus callosum
22. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A capacitor of capacitance C is charged to voltage V and then is disconnected from its power supply. If the plates are pulled twice as far apart, what happens to the stored energy in the capacitor?
W) It stays the same
X) It doubles
Y) It quadruples
Z) It halves
ANSWER: X) It doubles
22. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Oliver and Milo ride a merry-go-round starting from rest with constant angular acceleration. Oliver sits at the edge, and Milo sits halfway between the center and the edge. During this interval, compared to Oliver’s angular and tangential accelerations, Milo’s angular and tangential accelerations are:
W) Both less
X) Greater angular acceleration and less tangential acceleration
Y) The same angular acceleration and less tangential acceleration
Z) The same angular acceleration and greater tangential acceleration
ANSWER: Y) The same angular acceleration and less tangential acceleration
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer When 1/7 is expanded as a decimal, what is the 50th digit to the right of the decimal point?
ANSWER: 4
23. BONUS Math Short Answer The lengths of the sides of a triangle are 13, 4, and 15. What is the area of the triangle?
ANSWER: 24
