Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The biomineralized exoskeletons of coral polyps are composed of what compound?
ANSWER: CaCO3 (also accept: Calcium carbonate)
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Embedded in the inner mitochondrial membrane, what enzyme complex harnesses the flow of protons down their gradient to phosphorylate ADP?
ANSWER: ATP SYNTHASE
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The intensity of a wave originating from a point source in three-dimensional space is proportional to which of the following powers of distance?
W) -2
X) -1
Y) 0
Z) 1
ANSWER: W) -2
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer Select all of the following three properties of light that remain constant when it enters a medium with a different index of refraction 1) Frequency 2) Wavelength 3) Speed
ANSWER: 1
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In his statistics test, Ken scores a 95. If the class' average score is 87 and its standard deviation is 5, what is Ken's z-score?
ANSWER: 1.6
3. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the median of the following list of 6 numbers: 7, 3, 6, 5, 8, 5
ANSWER: 5.5
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What element when burned produces a yellow flame?
ANSWER: Sodium
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When the same two elements form more than one compound, the differences in masses of one element with the mass of the other element held constant is in the ratio of small whole numbers. What law describes this phenomenon?
ANSWER: Law of Multiple Proportions
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following planets has the highest albedo?
W) Mercury
X) Venus
Y) Earth
Z) Mars
ANSWER: X) Venus
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Star Minghao has a luminosity of 4 solar luminosities and a temperature twice that of the sun. What is the ratio of the radius of star Minghao to the radius of the sun?
ANSWER: 1/2
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What nitrogenous waste product is recycled into the lower medullary space in order to maintain an osmotic gradient?
ANSWER: UREA
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The autoantibodies produced in Myasthenia gravis bind receptors for which of the following hormones?
W) Epinephrine
X) Norepinephrine
Y) Acetylcholine
Z) Histamine
ANSWER: Y) Acetylcholine
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the rotational analogue of mass?
ANSWER: Moment of Inertia (do not accept: Inertia)
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer Three identical resistors are connected in parallel to a 6V battery. If the current along the wire is 3 amperes, in Ohms, what is the resistance of the resistors?
ANSWER: 6
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Alex is buying bottles of water. If the local store only sells bottles of water in 3 Liter and 8 Liter bottles, in liters, what is the maximum volume of water that Alex cannot buy?
ANSWER: 13
8. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the sum of the digits of 67 base 10 in base 3?
ANSWER: 5
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What equation in electrochemistry calculates the cell potential of a galvanic cell under nonideal conditions?
ANSWER: Nernst equation
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The formation of C can be represented by the following equation: 3 A(g) + B(g) → C(g). If the Kc of this reaction is 10 at 300 K, then what is the value of Kp to one significant figure? Use R = 0.0821 L·atm/(mol·K).
ANSWER: 7 × 10-4 (also accept: 0.0007)
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Between June and October, in which of the following cardinal directions does the Intertropical Convergence Zone generally move?
W) North
X) East
Y) South
Z) West
ANSWER: Y) South
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three pieces of information about pulsars that support that they are neutron stars. 1) They are not highly luminous 2) The period of time between subsequent peaks of light is small 3) The period of time for which they peak in luminosity is small.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Daniel places a straw in a cup of water and notices that the straw appears to be bent at the surface of the water? What phenomenon best explains this?
ANSWER: Refraction
11. BONUS Physics Short Answer Chlorine-39 has a half life of approximately one hour. If Charles has 32 grams of Chlorine-39, after how many hours will he only have 1 gram of Chlorine-39 remaining?
ANSWER: 5
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Deciding to go for a walk after too many hours of math, Patrick hears a crunch after stepping on a beetle. Which nitrogen-containing polysaccharide, which forms arthropod exoskeletons, is responsible for this sound?
ANSWER: CHITIN
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What protein, found in the extracellular matrix, forms triple helices and provides tensile strength?
ANSWER: COLLAGEN
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer An object attached to an ideal spring is oscillating in one dimension. If the amplitude of the oscillations is doubled, by what factor does the object’s maximum speed increase?
ANSWER: 2
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer An object of mass 2 kilograms has an initial speed of 5 meters per second. A constant force is applied to the object such that its speed increases to 8 meters per second. In Joules, how much work did the force do on the object?
ANSWER: 39
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is sin(π/4) + cos(π/4) + tan(π/4)?
ANSWER: √2+1
14. BONUS Math Short Answer If sin(theta) = 3/5 and theta is in Quadrant 1, find sec(theta)?
ANSWER: 5/4
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many nearest neighbors surround a sodium ion in a crystal of sodium chloride?
ANSWER: 6
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Hriday's muscles are solid. Rank the following four ionic solids in order of increasing lattice energy 1) sodium fluoride 2) cesium chloride 3) beryllium oxide 4) barium selenide
ANSWER: 2, 1, 4, 3
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, order the following 3 planets by increasing rotational period 1) Earth 2) Venus 3) Jupiter
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The theoretical Planet 9 of our solar system is theorized to have a semi-major axis of 290 AU. If this is true, which of the following would be closest to the theorized orbital period of Planet 9?
W) 50 years
X) 500 years
Y) 5000 years
Z) 50000 years
ANSWER: Y) 5000 years
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The atherosclerotic plaques seen in heart disease are due to the buildup of what molecule, the building block of all steroids?
ANSWER: CHOLESTEROL
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer What term refers to a genetic inheritance pattern where both alleles of a gene are expressed together in a heterozygous organism?
ANSWER: CODOMINANCE
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Whenever Jake sees Nathaniel, he feels a conservative force of attraction proportional to 1/r5, where r is the distance between him and Nathaniel. To what power of r is the potential energy of this interaction proportional to?
ANSWER: -4
18. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following circuit elements stores energy in a magnetic field?
W) Capacitor
X) Resistor
Y) Inductor
Z) Transformer
ANSWER: Y) Inductor
19. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following cannot be written as the sum of two primes?
W) 55
X) 65
Y) 75
Z) 85
ANSWER: X) 65
19. BONUS Math Multiple Choice If n is an integer, which of the following is always divisible by 2?
W) n2
X) n2 + 1
Y) n2 + n
Z) 2n2 + n
ANSWER: Y) n2 + n
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the lightest element that does not have a stable isotope?
ANSWER: Technetium
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The combustion of a hydrocarbon produces what two compounds?
ANSWER: Water and CO2 (also accept: Water and carbon dioxide; H2O and CO2)
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What will happen if Connor adds two tenths of a solar mass to a one point one solar mass white dwarf, which of the following best describes what will happen?
W) It will get larger
X) It will get smaller
Y) It will remain the same size
Z) It will undergo a type Ia supernova
ANSWER: X) It will get smaller
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three air masses in order of increasing temperature 1) continental tropical 2) continental polar 3) continental arctic
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
22. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is the computer component that can be read and changed in any order, and is typically used to store data and machine-code that the CPU is actively using?
ANSWER: Random Access Memory (also accept: RAM)
22. BONUS Energy Short Answer In computer programming, what is the term given to the operation of joining sequential objects, such as character strings and lists end-to-end?
ANSWER: Concatenation
