1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following musical instruments can produce a pure tone:
W) violin
X) clarinet
Y) piano
Z) electronic synthesizer
ANSWER: Z) electronic synthesizer
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the centripetal force required for a 6 kilogram body traveling at 2 m/s in a perfect circle with a radius of 4 meters?
ANSWER: 6 NEWTONS
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The initial step in the preparation of recombinant DNA is to cut both DNA strands using enzymes known as:
W) snurps
X) restriction ligases
Y) restriction endonucleases
Z) restriction vectors
ANSWER: Y) restriction endonucleases
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Assuming brown eyes is dominant over blue eyes and there is no polygenesis, if a mother is blue eyed and her husband is brown eyed heterozygous, what are the chances that any of the children will be blue eyed?
ANSWER: 50%
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following does NOT describe alkanes:
W) alkanes with a carbon-carbon double bond are called straight-chain alkanes
X) all alkanes are saturated hydrocarbons
Y) the molecular formula C2H6 is one example of an alkane
Z) crude oil contains a wide variety of alkanes
ANSWER: W) alkanes with a carbon-carbon double bond are called straight-chain alkanes
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many molecules of diatomic hydrogen would have to be added to a molecule of benzene to produce cyclohexane?
ANSWER: 3
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The atmosphere of the Saturn's moon Titan is mostly composed of what gas:
W) carbon dioxide
X) methane
Y) hydrogen
Z) nitrogen
ANSWER: Z) nitrogen
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Which two planets have no known satellites:
ANSWER: MERCURY AND VENUS
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Matrix C is the product of Matrix A times Matrix B. Which of the following must be TRUE:
W) the number of rows of A = the number of rows of B
X) the number of columns of A = the number of columns of B
Y) the number of rows of A = the number of columns of B
Z) the number of columns of A = the number of rows of B
ANSWER: Z) the number of columns of A = the number of rows of B
5. BONUS Math Short Answer A rectangular family room is twice as long as it is wide and its perimeter is 84 feet. What are the dimensions of the room?
ANSWER: W = 14 and L = 28
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an example of a pluton:
W) cobble
X) laccolith
Y) volcanic pipe
Z) dike
ANSWER: W) cobble
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Earth's mantle is approximately how many miles thick:
W) 1000
X) 1200
Y) 1500
Z) 1800
ANSWER: Z) 1800
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A major function of a refractory is:
W) as a source of flux material for slag formation
X) the conduction of heat
Y) as a source of essential reactants for smelting
Z) for heat insulation
ANSWER: Z) for heat insulation
7. BONUS Energy Short Answer How many square inches are in 9 square feet?
ANSWER: 1296
8. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What kind of program was developed to allow the user to store, modify, organize and display data in a meaningful form:
W) firmware
X) database
Y) graphics
Z) word processing
ANSWER: X) database
8. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice How much data can be transmitted at once in typical parallel communications:
W) 1 bit
X) 4 bits
Y) 1 byte
Z) 1 kilobyte
ANSWER: Y) 1 byte
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following physical attributes makes aluminum unsuitable for jets traveling at speeds greater than Mach 2:
W) its weight
X) its level of resistance to corrosion
Y) its strength at different temperatures
Z) its cost
ANSWER: Y) its strength at different temperatures
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What scientist was the first to theorize that space and time were only generalizations created from observations, and that the properties of space did not exist independently?
ANSWER: ERNST MACH
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Bacteria that cannot grow in the presence of oxygen are:
W) aerobes
X) obligate anaerobes
Y) facultative anaerobes
Z) obligate aerobes
ANSWER: X) obligate anaerobes
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer The acromion process is part of what human bone?
ANSWER: SCAPULA
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following hydrocarbons is an example of an alkyne:
W) ethane
X) ethylene
Y) acetylene
Z) benzene
ANSWER: Y) acetylene
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the allotropic form of phosphorous that is a white waxy solid that reacts violently with air?
ANSWER: WHITE PHOSPHOROUS
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice At the North and South poles, which of the following correctly describes apparent motion of the stars:
W) they move in circles parallel with the horizon
X) they rise and set vertically
Y) they remain stationary
Z) they rise and set at an angle to the horizon
ANSWER: W) they move in circles parallel with the horizon
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the region around the Earth occupied by the Earth’s magnetic field:
ANSWER: MAGNETOSPHERE
13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Convert -720° into radians:
ANSWER: -4π (also accept: -4π rad; -4π radians)
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the x and y intersepts of the parabola y = 2x2 and its inverse:
ANSWER: (0, 0)
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice The first sedimentary rocks were laid down some 3.7 billion years ago in what era:
W) Precambrian
X) Cambrian
Y) Paleozoic
Z) Phenerozoic
ANSWER: W) Precambrian
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The supercontinent Pangea started to break apart into our present day continents approximately how many million years ago:
W) 100
X) 200
Y) 300
Z) 400
ANSWER: X) 200
15. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Bells are typically cast in which of the following:
W) in a bronze alloy of about 75% copper and 25% zinc
X) in a brass alloy of about 75% copper and 25% tin
Y) in a bronze alloy of about 75% copper and 25% tin
Z) in a brass alloy of about 75% copper and 25% zinc
ANSWER: Y) in a bronze alloy of about 75% copper and 25% tin
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Uranium-238, uranium-235, and thorium-232 all undergo decay to form isotopes of this one element of the carbon group:
ANSWER: LEAD
16. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer The term SQL, which is sometimes pronounced "sequel", stands for what language?
ANSWER: STRUCTURED QUERY LANGUAGE
16. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following shows a superclass-subclass relation:
W) holiday – Labor Day
X) Labor day – holiday
Y) car – wheel
Z) wheel – car
ANSWER: W) holiday – Labor Day
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice When marchers break step while crossing a bridge, they do so to avoid a possible bridge collapse, which is based mostly on this physical concept:
W) isostacy
X) inertia
Y) resonance
Z) chaos
ANSWER: Y) resonance
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer The electromagnetic force is carried by this familiar boson:
ANSWER: PHOTON
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In plants, stomata are made up of which of the following:
W) guard cells and collenchyma cells
X) collenchyma cells and parenchyma cells
Y) parenchyma cells and stomatal pores
Z) guard cells and stomatal pores
ANSWER: Z) guard cells and stomatal pores
18. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true:
W) sperm and egg contribute equal amounts of genetic information to the zygote
X) in the first meiotic division a polar body is formed
Y) in deuterastomes, the blastopore forms the anus
Z) most angiosperms and vertebrates are diploid
ANSWER: W) sperm and egg contribute equal amounts of genetic information to the zygote
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In the compound n-pentane, the n stands for:
W) normal
X) nano
Y) nitro
Z) non-isomer
ANSWER: W) normal
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Calculate the number of calories needed to raise the temperature of 20 grams of water from 20° C to 30° C:
ANSWER: 200 CALORIES
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When high speed electrons or protons from the solar wind collide with gases in the Earth’s upper atmosphere causing them to fluoresce, this results in what phenomena?
ANSWER: AURORA (also accept: NORTHERN LIGHTS; SOUTHERN LIGHTS; AURORA AUSTRALIS; AURORA BOREALIS)
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the astronomical term for the region of ionized hydrogen in interstellar space?
ANSWER: H II REGION
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a metamorphic rock:
W) schist
X) gneiss
Y) slate
Z) shale
ANSWER: Z) shale
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The theory that suggests that earthquakes result from the release of stored elastic energy by slippage on faults is called:
ANSWER: ELASTIC REBOUND THEORY
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer A satellite that orbits the equatorial plane of the Earth at a speed matching the Earth’s orbit is said to be in what kind of orbit?
ANSWER: GEOSYNCHRONOUS
22. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice After thorough cleaning and repeated applications of ammonium polysulfide followed by drying, the steel is then rubbed briskly with a cloth and sealed with linseed oil. This process describes which of the following:
W) bluing of steel
X) preparation of steel for cheap jewelry
Y) long term storage preparation of high grade steel
Z) preparation of steel for use in nuclear reactors
ANSWER: W) bluing of steel
23. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following enables the aviation designer to extrapolate from the model to the prototype:
W) Goddard equivalence
X) Reynold’s number
Y) Mach’s equivalence
Z) Bernoulli’s number
ANSWER: X) Reynold’s number
23. BONUS Physics Short Answer Three capacitors, each rated at 1 microfarad, are connected in series. What is the total capacitance, in microfarads, rounded to the second decimal place?
ANSWER: 0.33 MICROFARADS
24. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is TRUE about inactivated X chromosomes in humans:
W) they are the cause of Turner’s syndrome
X) they are lethal
Y) they are an expression of naked DNA
Z) they result in the human female being a genetic mosaic
ANSWER: Z) they result in the human female being a genetic mosaic
24. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Tight junctions, adherans junctions, and desmosomes are:
W) forms of intercellular attachment
X) forms of communication between cells
Y) all variations of gap junctions
Z) methods of active transport
ANSWER: W) forms of intercellular attachment
25. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The characteristic bad odor emitted in skunk spray is caused by which of the following:
W) thiols
X) aldehydes
Y) phenols
Z) alkenes
ANSWER: W) thiols
25. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A gas tank has a mixture of oxygen gas at 0.5 atmospheres, nitrogen gas at 380 millimeters mercury, and helium at 760 torr. Calculate, in atmospheres and to the nearest whole number, the total pressure in the tank:
ANSWER: 2 ATMOSPHERES
