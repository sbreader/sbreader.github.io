1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What cells produce testosterone in response to stimulation from luteinizing hormone?

1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify ALL of the following that are formed from tryptophan I. Auxin II. Melatonin III. Serotonin IV. Melanin

ANSWER: NONE
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice On a phase diagram, if the solid-liquid boundary line has a POSITIVE slope, which of the following statements is TRUE:
W) Its Liquid Phase is Denser than its Solid Phase
X) Its Solid Phase is Denser than its Liquid Phase
Y) It can never form a supercooled fluid
Z) It can never form a supercritical fluid
ANSWER: X) Its Solid Phase is Denser than its Liquid Phase

X) 2
Y) -1/2

X) 2C
Y) 4C

11. BONUS Math Short Answer Adrian runs at a constant speed of 21 miles per hour. He starts to run for one hour, and after this hour elapses, he turns around and runs for 30 minutes.
