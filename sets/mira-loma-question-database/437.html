Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is true regarding different types of folds?
W) Anticlines are formed by the downfolding of rock layers.
X) Synclines are formed by the upfolding of rock layers.
Y) Monoclines are results of the reactivating of steeply dipping fault zones.
Z) Anticlines and synclines are always found in association with each other.
ANSWER: Y) Monoclines are results of the reactivating of steeply dipping fault zones.
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What kind of folds are the most prominent feature of the Colorado Plateau?
ANSWER: MONOCLINES
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Horst and graben topography is most commonly associated with which kinds of faults?
W) reverse faults
X) thrust faults
Y) strike-slip faults
Z) normal faults
ANSWER: Z) normal faults
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Vertical displacements along dip-slip faults can produce long, low cliffs known as what?
ANSWER: FAULT SCARPS
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Which of the following three statements is true about joints? Joints are fractures along which much displacement has occurred. Most joints occur in near parallel groups. Most joints are produced when rocks in the outermost crust are deformed.
ANSWER: II AND III
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer When igneous rocks cool and develop shrinkage fractures that produce elongated columns, what kind of joint is formed?
ANSWER: COLUMNAR JOINT
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The name for the processes that collectively produce mountain belts is known as what?
ANSWER: OROGENESIS
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Most mountain building occurs at which kinds of plate boundaries?
ANSWER: CONVERGENT
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The East Coast of North America provides a present-day example of what kind of continental margin?
ANSWER: PASSIVE
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer During the development of a continental volcanic arc, sediment derived from the land and the subducting plate gets clumped together. This clump is known as what?
ANSWER: ACCRETIONARY WEDGE
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the oceanic crust?
W) Homogenous with an average composition of granordiorite
X) Homogenous with an average composition of basalt
Y) Heterogenous with an average composition of granordiorite
Z) Heterogenous with an average composition of basalt
ANSWER: X) Homogenous with an average composition of basalt
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the areas of shallow seabed close to the shores of the continental crust?
ANSWER: CONTINENTAL SHELVES
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of these is not a divergent boundary?
W) Mid-Atlantic Ridge
X) Gakkel Ridge
Y) East Pacific Rise
Z) Mariana Trench
ANSWER: Z) Mariana Trench
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What process is considered the driving force of plate tectonics?
ANSWER: CONVECTION
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The transitions between igneous, metamorphic and sedimentary rocks are part of which of Earth's cycle?
ANSWER: ROCK CYCLE
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of these processes is not part of the hydrologic cycle?
W) weathering
X) evaporation
Y) condensation
Z) precipitation
ANSWER: W) weathering
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Out of the 4 following statements, name all the true statements. Cirrus clouds are best described as sheets or layers that cover much of the sky. Cumulus clouds that undergo vertical development are called cumulonimbus clouds. Cumulus clouds are often associated with fair weather. Nimbostratus clouds are some of the highest clouds in the sky.
ANSWER: II and III
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The top of a cumulonimbus cloud is often referred to as what?
ANSWER: ANVIL HEAD
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Out of the following 4 statements, which 3 are true about minerals? Minerals are solid. Minerals have an orderly crystalline structure Minerals are always inorganic Minerals are naturally occurring
ANSWER: I, II and IV
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Consider the sedimentary rock limestone. Which mineral mainly composes this rock?
ANSWER: CALCITE
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Consider a situation in which galena was exposed to the atmosphere for 5 minutes. After the 5 minutes, what would its luster be classified as?
W) submetallic luster
X) metallic luster
Y) nonmetallic luster
Z) vitreous luster
ANSWER: W) submetallic luster
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What optical property of a mineral is considered the most inaccurate, due to the fact that even the slightest of impurities in the mineral can change it?
ANSWER: COLOR
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a habit of minerals?
W) prismatic
X) bladed
Y) botryoidal
Z) rounded
ANSWER: Z) rounded
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The mineral hematite is most commonly found with what kind of habit?
ANSWER: BOTRYOIDAL
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of these is not a term used to describe strength of a mineral?
W) tenacity
X) cleavage
Y) fracture
Z) streak
ANSWER: Z) streak
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Consider a mica crystal. A scientist cut some of it off from the whole by getting the knife blade to break weak atomic bonds in the crystal's structure. What property of minerals does this feat exhibit?
ANSWER: CLEAVAGE
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What shape of rocks exhibit 3 cleavage directions at 90 degrees?
ANSWER: CUBE
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Consider the fracture of quartz. What kind of fracture is this?
ANSWER: CONCHOIDAL FRACTURE
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What scale measures the relative hardness of minerals?
ANSWER: MOHS SCALE
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice All silicate minerals contain which two element?
W) silicon and sodium
X) silicon and aluminum
Y) silicon and oxygen
Z) silicon and magnesium
ANSWER: Y) silicon and oxygen
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer All silicates have the same fundamental building block. What is the name of this building block?
ANSWER: SILICON-OXYGEN TETRAHEDRON
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of these minerals is NOT a halide?
W) halite
X) fluorite
Y) sylvite
Z) dolomite
ANSWER: Z) dolomite
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the most plentiful group of silicate minerals?
ANSWER: FELDSPARS
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the only common mineral made completely of silicon and oxygen?
ANSWER: QUARTZ
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following gemstones is NOT precious?
W) ruby
X) opal
Y) sapphire
Z) amethyst
ANSWER: Z) amethyst
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a property that many gemstones exhibit that can be described as displaying flashes of color?
ANSWER: BRILLIANCE
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a direct result of lithification?
W) metamorphic rock
X) igneous rock
Y) sedimentary rock
Z) pillow lava
ANSWER: Y) sedimentary rock
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer According to the rock cycle, if any rock is weathered, it will become what?
ANSWER: SEDIMENT
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Consider an igneous rock. It first undergoes weathering. Then lithification. Finally, it is put under intense heat and pressure which changes its form. What kind of rock is it at the end of the process?
ANSWER: METAMORPHIC
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Texture describes things in an overall appearance based on three factors. Which of the following is not one of the three factors to texture?
W) size
X) structure
Y) shape
Z) arrangement
ANSWER: X) structure
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Igneous rocks that form rapidly at the surface of the upper crust are known to have what kind of texture?
ANSWER: FINE-GRAINED
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term used to describe igneous rocks that form when molten rock solidifies at the surface?
ANSWER: EXTRUSIVE (also accept: VOLCANIC)
24. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the other name for glassy textured igneous rock?
ANSWER: OBSIDIAN
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the arrangement of minerals that shows certain minerals crystallize first at higher temperatures, while other minerals crystallize at lower temperatures?
ANSWER: BOWEN'S REACTION SERIES
