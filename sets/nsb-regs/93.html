Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at Brookhaven National Lab are studying the replisome, which replicates DNA. What protein in the replisome is responsible for unwinding the DNA double helix?
ANSWER: HELICASE
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at SLAC National Accelerator Lab are analyzing data from the Fermi space telescope that performs astronomy in what part of the electromagnetic spectrum?
ANSWER: GAMMA RAY (also accept: GAMMA)
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the name for slow and gradual displacement of rock layers that occurs without the accumulation of significant strain?
W) Fault rupture
X) Fault break
Y) Fault creep
Z) Fault walk
ANSWER: Y) Fault creep
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three physical properties that are exhibited by both gypsum and halite 1) Glassy or vitreous luster 2) White streak 3) Three cleavage planes at right angles to one another.
ANSWER: 1, 2
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the distance between the points (–1, 4) and (–10, 6)?
ANSWER: √85
3. BONUS Math Short Answer A triangle with integer side lengths has longest side that is 4 times as long as a second side, and the third side has length 30. What is the greatest possible perimeter of the triangle?
ANSWER: 75
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A sphygmomanometer is a medical device that measures what vital sign?
ANSWER: BLOOD PRESSURE
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer In mRNA synthesis, what enzyme reads the DNA and synthesizes the strand of RNA?
ANSWER: RNA POLYMERASE
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the percent by mass of chloroform that is carbon?
W) 10
X) 16
Y) 20
Z) 25
ANSWER: W) 10
5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice A buffered aqueous system contains 0.02 molar ammonia and 0.2 molar ammonium. Which of the following is closest to the pH of this system?
W) 8.3
X) 9.3
Y) 10.3
Z) 12.3
ANSWER: W) 8.3
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In meters, how far does a 10-kilogram mass that is dropped from rest travel in 10 seconds?
ANSWER: 490
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer A positively charged particle is traveling in the positive direction along the line y = x in the presence of a constant B field pointing in the positive x direction. In what direction is the Lorentz force on the particle pointing?
ANSWER: NEGATIVE Z
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab use pulsed laser light to induce thermal stress. This enables them to study the behavior of what type of excitation that plays an important role in many of the physical properties of solids?
W) Photon
X) Phonon
Y) Neutron
Z) X-ray
ANSWER: X) Phonon
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are revisiting the BaBar collaboration to look for data pertaining to the material that is thought to compose 85% of the mass in the universe. What is the term for this material?
ANSWER: DARK MATTER
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What kind of trajectory will a comet follow if it has the minimum amount of energy needed to escape its star?
W) Circular
X) Elliptical
Y) Parabolic
Z) Hyperbolic
ANSWER: Y) Parabolic
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In what layer of the Earth’s atmosphere do auroras primarily occur?
ANSWER: THERMOSPHERE
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the chemical formula of ethylene, the simplest alkene?
ANSWER: C2H4
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the maximum number of stereoisomers possible for a molecule that contains 6 stereocenters?
ANSWER: 64
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Dinoflagellates have been identified as one of the main sources of what deadly marine phenomenon?
ANSWER: RED TIDE (also accept: HARMFUL ALGAL BLOOM)
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following four sections of the spine from the most superior to the most inferior 1) Lumbar 2) Cervical 3) Sacral 4) Thoracic.
ANSWER: 2, 4, 1, 3
11. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is equivalent to sine 47°cosine 11° – cosine 47°sine 11°?
W) sine 36°
X) cosine 36°
Y) sine 58°
Z) cosine 58°
ANSWER: W) sine 36°
11. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the remainder when 2x4 + 3x3-7x-3 is divided by x + 3?
ANSWER: 99
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Winds flowing from high to low pressures on the Earth appear to be deflected to the right in the Northern hemisphere because of what effect?
ANSWER: CORIOLIS
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer How much elastic energy, in joules, is stored in a spring that is displaced 10 centimeters from the equilibrium point, if its natural frequency of oscillation is 10 radians per second and the mass on its end is 1 kilogram?
ANSWER: 0.5 (also accept: 1/2)
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Livermore National Lab have devised an improved method for hydrogen production from water by using cheap transition-metal dichalcogenides. What is the name of this process for hydrogen production that involves splitting water into its components?
ANSWER: ELECTROLYSIS
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer Scientists at Princeton Plasma Physics Lab are building the world's largest stellarator, which is used to confine what phase of matter?
ANSWER: PLASMA
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals is not typically found in granite?
W) Olivine
X) Plagioclase
Y) Quartz
Z) Hornblende
ANSWER: W) Olivine
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Arrange the following three colors of light in order of increasing depth of penetration in average seawater 1) Violet 2) Red 3) Yellow.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If silicon is doped with boron, it becomes what type of semiconductor?
ANSWER: P-TYPE (also accept: P)
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer A rocket propels itself with 50 newtons of force directed completely horizontally. If its net acceleration is directed 30 degrees downward with respect to the horizontal, then to the nearest kilogram, what is the mass of the rocket?
ANSWER: 3
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of RNA carries amino acids to the ribosomes?
ANSWER: tRNA (also accept: TRANSFER RNA; TRANSFER)
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the reason for the similarity between the wing of a bird and the wing of an insect?
W) Convergent evolution
X) Divergent evolution
Y) Adaptive selection
Z) Stabilizing selection
ANSWER: W) Convergent evolution
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Six is one-third percent of what number?
ANSWER: 1800
17. BONUS Math Short Answer A student club sells tickets to a school play. They sell 683 tickets, averaging 14.8 tickets per member, rounded to the nearest tenth. How many members are in the club?
ANSWER: 46
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the ionic salt with chemical formula Fe(IO4)3?
ANSWER: IRON(III) PERIODATE (also accept: FERRIC PERIODATE)
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three sulfate salts that are soluble in water 1) Zinc sulfate 2) Aluminum sulfate 3) Iron(III) sulfate.
ANSWER: 1, 2, AND 3 (also accept: ALL)
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Edwin Purcell won the Nobel Prize in Physics for detecting the 21-centimeter spectral line originating from what element?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN
19. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What planet in our solar system is the only planet to have an orbital inclination to the ecliptic of greater than 4 degrees?
ANSWER: MERCURY
20. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What physical quantity is defined as force integrated over a time interval?
ANSWER: IMPULSE
20. BONUS Physics Short Answer Two capacitors, both with capacitance C, are initially charged such that they have voltages of 1.5 volts and 3.5 volts, respectively. If the positive plates are connected to each other, and the negative plates are also connected to each other, then, in volts, what is the total voltage across the system?
ANSWER: 2.5
21. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In the integers mod 8, what is 7 + 5?
ANSWER: 4
21. BONUS Math Short Answer If the line tangent to the graph of the differentiable function f of x at the point (–3, 5) passes through the point (4.5, –4), then what is f prime of x when x = –3?
ANSWER: –6/5 (also accept: –1 1/5; –1.2)
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Benzene demonstrates what special type of conjugation due to its 6 pi electrons?
ANSWER: AROMATICITY (also accept: AROMATIC)
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which of the electron's quantum numbers corresponds to the size and energy of the orbital?
ANSWER: PRINCIPAL (also accept: N)
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In female mammals, one of the two X chromosomes will be deactivated to form what structure?
ANSWER: BARR BODY
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer How many different human aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases are there to charge the 45 different tRNA molecules?
ANSWER: TWENTY
