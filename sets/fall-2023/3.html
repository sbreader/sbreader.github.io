Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following circuit components is characterized by its tendency to resist instantaneous changes in current?
W) Resistor
X) Capacitor
Y) Inductor
Z) Diode
ANSWER: Y) Inductor
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A box is placed on a frictionless inclined plane. The component of the net force on the box that is parallel to the plane is directly proportional to which of the following?"
W) The sine of the incline angle
X) The cosine of the incline angle
Y) The tangent of the incline angle
Z) The secant of the incline angle
ANSWER: W) The sine of the incline angle
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What law states that the recessional velocity of a galaxy is proportional to its proper distance?
ANSWER: HUBBLE’S LAW
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the type of sediment transport in which particles in a moving fluid bounce along a riverbed?
ANSWER: SALTATION
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Hansen is studying neutral carbon atoms using spectroscopic techniques and finds that some atoms have three electrons in their 2p subshell. Which of the following principles states this is NOT the ground state configuration for a neutral carbon atom?
W) Aufbau principle
X) Heisenberg uncertainty principle
Y) Pauli exclusion principle
Z) Hund’s rule
ANSWER: W) Aufbau principle
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Elemental forms of alkaline earth metals can often be obtained through their oxides via what general type of reaction?
ANSWER: REDUCTION (also accept: ELECTROLYSIS)
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What organ system is primarily affected by degenerative diseases such as Alzheimer's and Parkinson's disease?
ANSWER: NERVOUS SYSTEM (also accept: NEURAL)
4. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three products that are generated during the citric acid cycle in cellular respiration 1) Carbon dioxide 2) ATP 3) FADH2.
ANSWER: ALL
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers in the Schepartz Lab at UC Berkeley are interested in incorporating non-traditional amino acids into peptide chains. They are most likely studying enzymes found in what organelle?
ANSWER: RIBOSOME
5. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are developing specialized glaze coatings in order to make windows more energy-efficient. What common laboratory instrument might they use to characterize the absorption spectra of these materials?
ANSWER: SPECTROPHOTOMETER
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A box contains five Post-its labeled 1 through 5. If Eric draws two Post-its with replacement, what is the probability that the numbers differ by 2 or more?
ANSWER: 12/25
6. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the population standard deviation of the following list of four numbers: 3, 1, 7, and 5?
ANSWER: √5 (also accept: SQUARE ROOT OF 5)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at the Chang Lab at UC Berkeley tailor enzymes to convert plant biomass into fuel molecules. In nature, biomass-converting enzymes have the primary role of degrading cellulose into what general class of molecules?
ANSWER: SACCHARIDES (also accept: SUGARS)
7. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers from UC Berkeley and many other institutions collaborated at CERN in order to measure the gravitational acceleration of antimatter. They found that antimatter is affected by gravity in the same way as normal matter, verifying the predictions of what famous theory?
ANSWER: GENERAL RELATIVITY (do not accept: RELATIVITY; SPECIAL RELATIVITY)
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Coral reefs, clam shells, and other structures like cuttlebones are primarily made up of what mineral?
ANSWER: CALCIUM CARBONATE (also accept: CALCITE; ARAGONITE)
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A red-eyed tree frog and the leaves of the rainforest have what type of symbiotic relationship?
W) Competition
X) Mutualism
Y) Commensalism
Z) Parasitism
ANSWER: Y) Commensalism
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules has a non-zero standard enthalpy of formation?
W) Hydrogen gas
X) Bromine gas
Y) Oxygen gas
Z) Chlorine gas
ANSWER: X) Bromine gas
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three compounds in terms of increasing vapor pressure 1) Butane 2) Octane 3) Acetone.
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following laws describes the relationship between the circulation of a magnetic field around a closed loop and the electric current passing through it?
W) Gauss’s law
X) Faraday’s law
Y) Lenz’s law
Z) Ampere’s law
ANSWER: Z) Ampere’s law
10. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice If a new planet in the solar system is found to have an orbital period of 120 years, which of the following is closest to the radius of its orbit in astronomical units?
W) 11
X) 18
Y) 24
Z) 120
ANSWER: Y) 24
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a type of neutron star whose magnetic field decay causes periodic gamma ray bursts?
ANSWER: MAGNETAR
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three layers of the atmosphere in which temperature increases with altitude 1) Troposphere 2) Stratosphere 3) Mesosphere.
ANSWER: 2 ONLY
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Evaluate the following expression: i71.
ANSWER: –i
12. BONUS Math Short Answer Given that all of the following three triangles have the same perimeter, rank them in terms of increasing area 1) a 30-60-90 triangle 2) a 45-45-90 triangle 3) an equilateral triangle.
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What biological theory explains the evolution of eukaryotes from the engulfment of cyanobacteria?
ANSWER: ENDOSYMBIOSIS (also accept: ENDOSYMBIONT THEORY; SERIAL ENDOSYMBYOSIS)
13. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice When observing the roots of a bean plant, you see large nodules present at various intervals along the root. What process occurs within these root nodules?
W) Photosynthesis
X) Cellular respiration
Y) Gluconeogenesis
Z) Nitrogen fixation
ANSWER: Z) Nitrogen fixation
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of seismic wave does not pass through Earth’s outer core, and is the second wave that appears on a seismograph after an earthquake?
ANSWER: S-WAVE
14. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Dylan is analyzing a star that has very high temperature but very low luminosity. Which of the following would be the best classification of this star?
W) Red dwarf
X) White dwarf
Y) Brown dwarf
Z) Red giant
ANSWER: X) White dwarf
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Quartz is best categorized as which of the following types of solids?
W) Metallic
X) Molecular
Y) Network covalent
Z) Ionic
ANSWER: Y) Network covalent
15. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice While conducting burn tests, you realize that your sample of copper salt is incorrectly burning yellow. Which of the following salts has most likely contaminated your sample?
W) LiCl
X) NaCl
Y) KCl
Z) RbCl
ANSWER: X) NaCl
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In a DNA replication fork, what enzyme is responsible for unwinding and separation of the DNA strand?
ANSWER: DNA HELICASE (also accept: HELICASE)
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following terms best describes the pattern by which selectivity can emerge between the pollinator and flowering plants where they evolve to complement and only select for each other?
W) Convergent evolution
X) Divergent evolution
Y) Adaptive radiation
Z) Coevolution
ANSWER: Z) Coevolution
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A prism with 21 edges has how many vertices?
ANSWER: 14
17. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following functions of x grows the fastest?
W) x factorial
X) ex
Y) e2x
Z) 10x
ANSWER: W) x factorial
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What adjective is used to describe fluid flow that is chaotic and non-steady?
ANSWER: TURBULENT
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three properties that are considered extensive 1) Temperature 2) Pressure 3) Density.
ANSWER: NONE
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three blood vessels that transport oxygenated blood 1) Aorta 2) Pulmonary artery 3) Pulmonary vein.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer What cells are responsible for the production of myelin sheaths in the peripheral nervous system?
ANSWER: SCHWANN CELLS
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Indicating proper charge, what is the oxidation state of barium in the ionic solid barium phosphate?
ANSWER: 2+ (also accept: +2) (do not accept: 2)
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In grams per mole, what would be the molar mass of an atom that has de Broglie wavelength four times larger than that of titanium-48 traveling at the same speed?
ANSWER: 12
21. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the magnitude of the vector 3i + 4j + 5k?
ANSWER: 5√2 (also accept: 5 TIMES THE SQUARE ROOT OF 2)
21. BONUS Math Short Answer A 52-card deck contains the numbers 1 through 13, with each number appearing in exactly 4 different suits. How many distinct combinations of two cards from this deck add up to 21?
ANSWER: 48
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the global convection cell that is responsible for the trade winds in the tropics?
ANSWER: HADLEY CELL
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Biotite and muscovite exhibit what type of cleavage?
ANSWER: BASAL (also accept: PLANAR; PINACOIDAL)
23. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What term is given to the point on a stress-strain curve past which any strain would result in permanent deformation of a given material?
ANSWER: YIELD POINT (also accept: YIELD STRESS; YIELD STRENGTH)
23. BONUS Physics Short Answer Wei-Shan passes a soccer ball towards Sahand with a velocity vector of 5i + 3j. If Sahand applies a force such that the ball gains a velocity of –i + 8j, what is the magnitude of the velocity of the ball after Sahand kicks it towards the net?
ANSWER: √137 (also accept: THE SQUARE ROOT OF 137)
