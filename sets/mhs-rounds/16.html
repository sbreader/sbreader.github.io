3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements regarding nucleophilic carbonyl addition reactions is NOT true?
W) The Burgi Dunitz describes the optimal angle of attack on a carbonyl by a nucleophile.
X) When THF is used as a solvent for MgBr2 for a carbonyl with an ether attached to the alpha carbon, the alkyl attaches in a syn relationship.
