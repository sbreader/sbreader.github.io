Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What principle states that the laws of physics are same in all inertial reference frames?
ANSWER: Relativity principle
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer What theory is based on the two principles of the relativity principle and the constancy of the speed of light?
ANSWER: Special theory of relativity
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the rapid transmission of a nerve impulse along an axon, resulting from the action potential jumping from one node of Ranvier to another, skipping the myelin-sheathed regions of membrane?
ANSWER: Saltatory conduction
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What are the two types of glia that produce myelin sheaths and where are they located?
W) Oligodendrocytes in the PNS; Schwann cells in the CNS
X) Satellite cells in the PNS; Schwann cells in the CNS
Y) Satellite cells in the CNS; Oligodendrocytes in the PNS
Z) Schwann cells in the PNS; Oligodendrocytes in the CNS
ANSWER: Z) Schwann cells in the PNS; Oligodendrocytes in the CNS
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the thermoplastic polymer resin of the polyester family and is used in synthetic fibers and is sometimes known as polyethylene terephthalate?
ANSWER: Dacron
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In the Ptolemaic theory, what is the large circle around Earth along which the center of the eppicycle moved?
ANSWER: Deferent
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the point off-center in the deferent from which the center of the epicycle appears to move uniformly?
ANSWER: Equant
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the weight-density of water in pounds per cubic foot?
W) 64.4
X) 62.4
Y) 51.2
Z) 49.4
ANSWER: X) 62.4
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is a simple polyol compound that is a colorless, odorless, viscous liquid that is widely used in pharmaceutical formulations and has a weight-density of 51.2 pounds per cubic foot?
ANSWER: Glycerin (also accept: Glycerol; Glycerine)
6. BONUS Math Short Answer Convert the point (r, theta) = (2, pi) into rectangular coordinates.
ANSWER: (-2, 0)
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What does MVP stand for which represents the smallest population size at which a species is able to sustain its numbers and survive?
ANSWER: Minimum viable population
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer A meaningful estimate of MVP requires the researcher to determine this which is based on the potential of the population and helps find the right amount of population required to pass their alleles on to offspring.
ANSWER: Effective population size
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are highly magnetic neutron stars that emit X rays but spin slowly with periods of 5 to 10 seconds called?
ANSWER: Anomalous X-ray pulsars
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are neutron stars that emir irregular bursts of lower energy gamma-rays called?
ANSWER: Soft gamma-ray repeaters (SGRs)
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the fusion of the cytoplasm of cells from two individuals and occurs as one stage of syngamy?
ANSWER: Plasmogamy
10. BONUS Biology Short Answer These specialized hyphae are used by fungi to extract nutrients or exchange nutrients with their hosts.
ANSWER: Haustoria
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In a photovoltaic cell, the side of silicon mixed with Boron is known as what type of layer and has a surplus of what?
ANSWER: P-type layer; holes
11. BONUS Energy Short Answer The other side of the photovoltaic cell is mixed with what and has a deficit of electrons causing what phenomenon?
ANSWER: Boron; Electron holes
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the process of the charge flowing back and forth from one plate of the capacitor to the other, through the inductor, and continues to repeat itself called?
ANSWER: LC oscillations
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the term used when the maximum power is transmitted when the output impedance of one device equals the input impedance of the second?
ANSWER: Impedance matching
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the locking of the rotation of a body to its revolution around another body called?
ANSWER: Tidal coupling
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer These long, winding channels are often found near the edges of the maria and were evidently cut by flowing lava.
ANSWER: Sinuous rilles
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer This is both a specific compound and a class of compounds; it is the common name for Potassium Aluminum Sulfate dodecahydrate.
ANSWER: Alum
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term used for an animal whose body cavity is lined by tissue derived from both the mesoderm and the endoderm?
ANSWER: Pseudocoelomate
15. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice All animals except for sponges and a few other groups belong to this clade of animals with true tissues; what is the clade?
W) Clade of Metazoans
X) Clade of Deuterostomians
Y) Clade of Protostromians
Z) Clade of Eumetazoans
ANSWER: Z) Clade of Eumetazoans
16. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is a direct-bandgap material and has the highest efficiency among thin film materials for photovoltaic cells?
W) Copper indium gallium selenide
X) Light-absorbing dyes (DSSC)
Y) Gallium arsenide
Z) Cadmium telluride
ANSWER: Y) Gallium arsenide
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the SI unit for a magnetic field?
ANSWER: Tesla
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is another commonly used unit to specify magnetic field and is a cgs unit? How much Tesla units does 1 of these units equal?
ANSWER: Gauss; 10-4 T
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What it the term for a layered fossil formation caused by ancient mats of algae or bacteria that build up mineral deposits season after season called?
ANSWER: Stromatolite
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What experiment simulated the effects of lightning in Earth's early atmosphere by creating sparks through the use of an electric arc inside the apparatus? This experiment was performed in 1952.
ANSWER: Miller experiment
20. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term used to represent members of the vertebrate subgroup possessing jaws?
ANSWER: Gnathostome
20. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The common ancestors of all gnathostomes underwent an additional duplication of this type of genes.
W) Hox
X) Nox
Y) ACHE
Z) ACD

21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is term used to refer to an alloy of metal and mercury?
ANSWER: An amalgam

W) magnets
X) bearings

22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the doughnut-shaped cloud of ionized gas that encloses the orbit of Jupiter's moon Io called?
ANSWER: Io plasma torus
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In a system with two bodies orbiting each other, the volume of space dominated by the gravitation of one of the bodies is known as what?
ANSWER: Roche lobe
