2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at the Pacific Northwest National Laboratory are developing new computational methods to predict how amino acids will fold into a three-dimensional structure. What level of protein structure is maintained by hydrogen bonds between amide hydrogens and carbonyl oxygens of the peptide backbone?
ANSWER: SECONDARY STRUCTURE

Z) Microwaves
ANSWER: X) Radio Waves

ANSWER: MOLAR SOLUBILITY

Y) 2.6 × 10-10
Z) 1.8 × 10-10

6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the name for the region along the equator where trade winds from the Northern and Southern Hemispheres collide, creating an area of low pressure and few surface winds?

Y) Doldrums

7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers have developed a systems-level computational model named FoldME, which can accurately predict how E. coli responds to temperature changes and genetic mutations.
