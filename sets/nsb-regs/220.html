Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term used to describe a liquid’s resistance to flow?
ANSWER: VISCOSITY
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are assumptions made by the kinetic-molecular theory of gasses 1) Particles do not experience attractive or repulsive forces 2) Collisions are inelastic 3) Temperature is an average of the kinetic energy in a gas.
ANSWER: 1, 3
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A pair of 12-sided dice, each with the numbers one through 12 printed on the faces, is rolled. What is the probability that the two numbers rolled are the same?
ANSWER: 1/12
2. BONUS Math Short Answer In the expansion of the cube of the binomial 2a-3b, what is the coefficient of the ab2 term?
ANSWER: 54
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In the alternation of generations in plants, what is the term for the diploid form?
ANSWER: SPOROPHYTE
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of cytoskeletal fibers 1) Actin filaments are smaller in diameter than microtubules 2) Microtubules are hollow 3) Intermediate filaments are typically more stable than actin filaments.
ANSWER: ALL
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Feldspar has two planes of cleavage. What is the characteristic degree measure of the angle between these planes?
ANSWER: 90
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Rank the following three types of geologic mass movement in order of increasing speed 1) Earthflow 2) Pyro-clastic flow 3) Lahar.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Industrial applications of AC power often use three-phase power. In degrees, what is the smallest separation between the phases?
ANSWER: 120
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer A uniform solid cylinder is rolling about its principal axis without slipping. If its total kinetic energy is 60 joules, then, in joules, what is its rotational kinetic energy?
ANSWER: 20
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Lawrence Berkeley National Lab researchers are studying the oxygen-evolving complex in photosystem II. This complex creates oxygen gas by oxidizing what molecule?
ANSWER: WATER (also accept: H2O)
6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are accelerating electrons to energies of 7.8 giga-electron-volts at the BELLA center. Which of the following combinations of SI base units measures the same quantity as an electron-volt?
W) Kilogram-meter per second
X) Kilogram-meter per second squared
Y) Kilogram-meter squared per second squared
Z) Coulomb per second
ANSWER: Y) Kilogram-meter squared per second squared
7. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Consider the polynomial equation 6x3-14x + 15 = 0. According to the rational root theorem, which of the following is NOT a possible rational root of this equation?
W) –3
X) 1/3
Y) 3/5
Z) 3/2
ANSWER: Y) 3/5
7. BONUS Math Short Answer The perimeters of two squares differ by 12 inches and their areas differ by 42 square inches. What is the perimeter, in inches, of the larger square?
ANSWER: 34
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A positively-charged particle is traveling in the positive-x direction as it experiences a uniform magnetic field pointing in the positive-y direction. In which of the following directions will the particle initially be deflected?
W) Negative-y
X) Positive-y
Y) Negative-z
Z) Positive-z
ANSWER: Z) Positive-z
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 5-kilogram mass is undergoing simple harmonic motion at an angular frequency of 30 radians per second. In SI base units, what is the spring constant of the oscillator?
ANSWER: 4500 (also accept: 4500 KILOGRAMS PER SECOND SQUARED)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What Galilean moon is notable for its triple bands and many other dark lineae that cross its surface?
ANSWER: EUROPA
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the SI base units of the Hubble constant?
ANSWER: INVERSE SECONDS (also accept: ONE OVER SECONDS; RECIPROCAL SECONDS) (do not accept: SEC–1)
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for isomers that result from different orientations of substituents about a double bond?
ANSWER: GEOMETRIC ISOMERS (also accept: CIS/TRANS ISOMERS)
10. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Suppose you can know the exact position over time of a sample of randomly-colliding gas molecules. Which of the following types of plots would result in a straight line when averaged over the entire gas sample?
W) Displacement versus time squared
X) Displacement versus time
Y) Mean squared displacement versus square root of time
Z) Mean squared displacement versus time
ANSWER: Z) Mean squared displacement versus time
11. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Scientists at Jefferson Lab are part of a collaboration studying matter composed of what third-most-massive quark?
ANSWER: CHARM (also accept: C)
11. BONUS Energy Short Answer Lawrence Berkeley National Lab scientists are using machine learning techniques that employ neural networks to analyze health records for veterans. Identify all of the following three types of learning that neural networks can perform 1) Supervised 2) Semi-supervised 3) Unsupervised.
ANSWER: ALL
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Most trees can continue growing larger year after year without limit. What undifferentiated tissues do plants possess that can divide continually?
ANSWER: MERISTEMS
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three responses that would occur in the human body after eating a carbohydrate-rich meal 1) Formation of glycogen 2) Increase in glucagon secretion 3) Increase in insulin secretion.
ANSWER: 1, 3
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer On ocean shores, what is the term for currents that move in the opposite direction of breaking waves?
ANSWER: RIP CURRENT
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements about sediments and sedimentary rocks that are true 1) A rock composed of lithified pebbles is a conglomerate 2) Ripples in sedimentary layers can indicate that the sediment was deposited in moving water 3) Arkose is a feldspar-rich sandstone.
ANSWER: ALL
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the area of a triangle with vertices at the three points with coordinates (2, 5), (2, 11), and (9, 8)?
ANSWER: 21
14. BONUS Math Short Answer Four 12-sided dice, each with the numbers one through 12 printed on the faces, are rolled. What is the probability that at least two of the numbers rolled are the same?
ANSWER: 41/96
15. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes how the electric field strength varies with distance away from an infinite plane of electric charge?
W) It linearly increases
X) It decreases as the inverse
Y) It decreases as the inverse square
Z) It is constant
ANSWER: Z) It is constant
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three values that are possible values for the azimuthal quantum number 1) One-half 2) Zero 3) Negative one.
ANSWER: 2
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Pacific Northwest National Lab have recently reported the structure of the AMPA receptor. This is a receptor that plays an important role in what CNS cell type?
ANSWER: NEURON (also accept: NERVE CELL)
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at Ames Lab are studying the properties of twisted bilayer graphene. What is the hybridization of the carbon atoms in each of these layers?
ANSWER: sp2
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The myth that eating turkey makes a person sleepy originated from the fact that the amino acid tryptophan is a precursor to what neurotransmitter?
ANSWER: SEROTONIN (also accept: 5-HT; 5-HYDROXYTRYPTAMINE)
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three aspects of development that are characteristic of deuterostomes 1) Blastopore becomes the mouth 2) Coelom forms from pockets of the archenteron 3) Spiral cleavage.
ANSWER: 2
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the adjective for substances that can act as both acids and bases?
ANSWER: AMPHOTERIC
18. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice If you wanted to synthesize iso-butyl-benzene through a Friedel-Crafts alkylation reaction, what molecule would you react with benzene and aluminum chloride?
W) Iso-butyl-chloride
X) Iso-butane
Y) Butyl bromide
Z) Iso-butyl-aldehyde
ANSWER: W) Iso-butyl-chloride
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the partial derivative with respect to x of the expression x5 y8?
ANSWER: 5x4 y8
19. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the polynomial with least degree, real coefficients, and leading coefficient one that has –9 + 5i as a zero?
ANSWER: x2 + 18x + 106
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following hormones promotes bud formation?
W) Cytokinins
X) Oligosaccharins
Y) Ethylene
Z) Abscisic acid
ANSWER: W) Cytokinins
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the ecological term for the rate at which a population of a given species will increase when there are no environmental constraints on the rate of growth?
ANSWER: BIOTIC POTENTIAL
21. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A p-n junction, by itself, is best suited to be which of the following circuit components?
W) Transistor
X) Capacitor
Y) Inductor
Z) Diode
ANSWER: Z) Diode

ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In the triple alpha process, what element is the final product of the fusion reaction?
ANSWER: CARBON
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Place the following three dwarf planets in order of increasing mass 1) Ceres 2) Eris 3) Pluto.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the IUPAC name of the salt formed when benzoic acid is mixed with sodium hydroxide?
ANSWER: SODIUM BENZOATE

ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
