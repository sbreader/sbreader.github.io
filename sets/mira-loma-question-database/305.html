Packet 305 (SciBowl Qs #1 11th) of Mira Loma Question Database:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How is the rate of a reaction affected when the temperature is increased?
ANSWER: INCREASES
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What type of electrochemical cell has a negative Gibbs Free Energy?
ANSWER: VOLTAIC (also accept: GALVANIC)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the phase change by which a gas directly becomes a solid?
ANSWER: DEPOSITION
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer "The total enthalpy change of a reaction is the sum of the enthalpy changes of each step" is a statement of what law?
ANSWER: HESS'S LAW
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer If the pOH of an aqueous solution is 12, then what is the pH?
ANSWER: 2
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name given to the difference between the mass of the nucleus of an atom and the mass of its constituent protons and neutrons?
ANSWER: MASS DEFECT
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name given to the type of covalent bond where the shared electron pair comes from the lone pair of one atom?
ANSWER: COORDINATE COVALENT BOND (also accept: DIPOLAR BOND)
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the molecular geometry of AsH3(Arsine)?
ANSWER: TRIGONAL PYRAMIDAL
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the molecular geometry of an sp hybridized Carbon atom?
ANSWER: LINEAR
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Robert Millikan is credited with the measurement of what value?
ANSWER: CHARGE OF AN ELECTRON
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name given to the antiparticle of the electron?
ANSWER: POSITRON
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The amount of charge contained in one mole of electrons is equivalent to what unit?
ANSWER: FARADAY
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which principle states that no two electrons in the same atom can have identical quantum numbers?
ANSWER: PAULI EXCLUSION PRINCIPLE
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which subatomic particle is not subject to the Pauli Exclusion Principle, and can have identical particles occupying the same quantum state?
ANSWER: BOSON
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The change in energy when a gaseous atom in its ground state gains an electron is known as what chemical property?
ANSWER: ELECTRON AFFINITY
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer If temperature is held constant, and pressure is increased, what happens to the volume of an ideal gas?
ANSWER: DECREASES
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the effect of lowering the temperature on the solubility of a gas?
ANSWER: IT BECOMES MORE SOLUBLE
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What color does litmus paper become in an acidic solution?
ANSWER: RED
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name given to the organic reaction in which a Carbon-Carbon double bond is converted into a single bond?
ANSWER: ADDITION
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name given to the reaction where a bond is broken as a molecule absorbs a photon of light energy?
ANSWER: PHOTODISSOCIATION
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the heat of formation of N2 gas?
ANSWER: ZERO
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The idea that electrons orbit the nucleus in a true orbit was a part of which model of the atom?
ANSWER: BOHR MODEL
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which family of the periodic table contains elements that occur in all 3 states of matter?
ANSWER: HALOGENS
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What method of separation can be used due to the different boiling points of substances?
ANSWER: DISTILLATION
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Order "Colloids", "Solutions", and "Suspensions" in order of greatest particle size to smallest particle size in a solution.
ANSWER: SUSPENSIONS, COLLOIDS, SOLUTIONS
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name given to the temperature above which the liquid phase of a substance cannot exist?
ANSWER: CRITICAL TEMPERATURE
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many times as fast does one mole of H2 diffuse than one mole of O2?
ANSWER: 4 TIMES AS FAST
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name given to the random movement of particles suspended in a fluid?
ANSWER: BROWNIAN MOTION
21. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What are the two unsaturated series' of hydrocarbons?
ANSWER: ALKENES AND ALKYNES
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In a flame test, what is the color that pure copper burns?
ANSWER: BLUE
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In a galvanic cell, which type of ion is reduced?
ANSWER: CATION
23. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the device used to connect the oxidation and reduction half reactions of a galvanic cell?
ANSWER: SALT BRIDGE
24. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name given to the temperature at which normal Helium transitions into its super fluid state?
ANSWER: LAMBDA POINT
25. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name given to the organic compound used as an automotive antifreeze?
ANSWER: ETHYLENE GLYCOL
