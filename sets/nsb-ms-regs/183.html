1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following isotopes was responsible for the only known naturally occurring fission reactors, the Oklo Fossil Reactors?
W) Uranium-233
X) Plutonium-239
Y) Uranium-235
Z) Plutonium-238
ANSWER: Y) Uranium-235
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Bernoulli's equation describes which of the following?
W) Vorticity
X) Behavior of harmonic oscillators
Y) Behavior of real gases
Z) Fluid dynamics
ANSWER: Z) Fluid dynamics
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A tick sucks blood from a cow. Which of the following is this species interaction?
W) Competition
X) Mutualism
Y) Parasitism
Z) Predation
ANSWER: Y) Parasitism
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice If a DNA template has the sequence TAC-ACA-GGG, which one of the following would be the correct messenger RNA codon sequence?
W) ATG-TGT-CCC
X) ATG-UGU-GGG
Y) AUG-UGU-CCC
Z) UAC-UGU-CCC
ANSWER: Y) AUG-UGU-CCC
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is the underlying cause of most volcanoes on Jupiter's moon Io?
W) Meteor impacts
X) Tidal forces
Y) Plate tectonics
Z) Weather patterns
ANSWER: X) Tidal forces
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following bodies of water does an atoll usually enclose?
W) Lake
X) River
Y) Swamp
Z) Lagoon
ANSWER: Z) Lagoon
4. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What type of power plant only needs to be refueled once a year?
ANSWER: NUCLEAR
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Of the four major types of coal mined in the US, which has the lowest energy content?
ANSWER: LIGNITE
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What is the square root of one trillion?
W) Ten thousand
X) One hundred thousand
Y) One million
Z) Ten million
ANSWER: Y) One million
5. BONUS Math Short Answer A right circular cylinder has a radius of 2 and a height of 10. A second right circular cylinder has a radius of 3 and a height of 6. What is the larger lateral surface area of the two cylinders?
ANSWER: 40π
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In a population pyramid, what information does the width of the base give about the population?
ANSWER: BIRTH RATE
6. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of events occurred in the same decade?
W) Whitney invented the cotton gin and Jenner discovered a smallpox vaccine
X) A Jarvik artificial heart was successfully implanted in a human and the first satellite capable of data transmission went into orbit
Y) Dubois discovered Java man in Indonesia and Hall discovered the two moons of Mars
Z) Eastman invented the box camera and Curie discovered radium
ANSWER: W) Whitney invented the cotton gin and Jenner discovered a smallpox vaccine
7. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the term for a transfer of heat due to direct contact?
W) Conduction
X) Radiation
Y) Convection
Z) Induction
ANSWER: W) Conduction
7. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Hooke's law is most directly related to the behavior of what?
W) Inclined planes
X) Wedges
Y) Springs
Z) Projectiles
ANSWER: Y) Springs
8. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer How many strokes are in a complete cycle for typical small engines found in leaf blowers, hedge trimmers, and go-carts?
ANSWER: TWO
8. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice A low-e window will have greater performance in comparison to a standard home window in what regard?
W) It will allow more infrared energy into the home
X) It will allow less ultraviolet energy into the home
Y) It will emit less radiant energy from the home
Z) It will allow more visible light into the home
ANSWER: Y) It will emit less radiant energy from the home
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Based on genetic evidence, which two taxonomic domains are most closely related to one another?
ANSWER: ARCHAEA AND EUKARYA

W) Vacuoles
X) Mitochondria
Y) Chloroplasts

ANSWER: X) Mitochondria

17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of continental glaciers?

23. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a disadvantage of inbreeding?
W) Farmers get to choose what organisms to breed
X) The inbred organism is sterile

ANSWER: Y) The inbred organism may have genetic disorders due to similar alleles
