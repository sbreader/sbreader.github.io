Packet 10 (DE 5) of National Chemistry Bowl Fall 2026:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three elements by increasing work function in their most stable form at STP 1) Sulfur 2) Potassium 3) Silicon
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What type of elimination leads to the creation of carbenes, in which a hydrogen atom and a leaving group are removed from the same atom?
ANSWER: Alpha Elimination
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Ethene is reacted with Osmium Tetroxide, then sodium bisulfite, and then treated with Sodium meta-Periodate. What is the final product of this reaction?
ANSWER: Methanal (also accept: Formaldehyde)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for a series of compounds with the same crystal structure and fully occupied sites, where different atoms substitute for each other on specific sites, producing a continuous range of compositions and a smooth change in lattice parameters?
ANSWER: Solid Solution Series (also accept: Solid Solution)
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three types of defects by increasing occurrence 1) Point Defect 2) Extended Defect 3) Extrinsic Defect
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Consider a transition metal A which may exist in aqueous solutions as a +1 or a +3 ion. How will the pH of a 1 M solution of the +1 ion compare to that of a 1 M solution of the +3 ion?
W) higher
X) lower
Y) the same
Z) varies
ANSWER: W) higher
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three compounds which contain an equal number of sigma and pi bonds 1) C2N2 2) C3O2 3) XeO4
ANSWER: 2, 3
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The reaction between ammonium and nitrite ions to form nitrogen gas is an example of what kind of reaction in which two reactants containing the same element but in different oxidation states combine to form a single product with an intermediate oxidation state?
ANSWER: comproportionation (also accept: synproportionation)
5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The specific heat of water at its boiling point is equal to which of the following?
W) 4.184
X) 0
Y) Infinity
Z) Indeterminate
ANSWER: Y) Infinity
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three types of pericyclic reactions by increasing change in the number of sigma bonds 1) Cycloaddition 2) Electrocyclic ring closure 3) Sigmatropic Rearrangement
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
6. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice A Dean-Stark Trap is a piece of laboratory glassware that can be used to continuously remove water from a reaction mixture. Which of the following reactions would a Dean-Stark Trap be least useful for?
W) Aldol Condensation
X) Fischer Esterification
Y) Schiff Base Synthesis
Z) Williamson Ether Synthesis
ANSWER: Z) Williamson Ether Synthesis
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Thiocyanate and Azide are examples of what type of ions which are very similar to group 17 ions in terms of reactivity and behavior?
ANSWER: Pseudohalides
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the only metal which adopts a simple cubic packing system?
ANSWER: Polonium
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice How does adding an electron withdrawing group to the dienophile affect a Diels-Alder reaction?
W) Increase the energy of the HOMO
X) Decrease the energy of the HOMO
Y) Increase the energy of the LUMO
Z) Decrease the energy of the LUMO
ANSWER: Z) Decrease the energy of the LUMO
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, rank the following three reactions by increasing number of precipitates formed 1) Copper (II) Sulfate and Barium Hydroxide 2) Sodium Nitrate and Potassium Chloride 3) Magnesium Chloride and Silver Nitrate
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following species has a different electron geometry than the others?
W) SF4
X) ClF3
Y) I3-
Z) SOF2
ANSWER: Z) SOF2
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the product formed when a primary amine and secondary amine react with a carbonyl respectively?
W) imine, imine
X) imine, enamine
Y) enamine, imine
Z) enamine, enamine
ANSWER: X) imine, enamine
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 3 reactions which are generally considered to be reversible 1) Dehydration of an Alcohol 2) Fischer Esterification 3) Williamson Ether Synthesis
ANSWER: 1, 2
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What set of rules explains that no chemical bond is 100% ionic and predicts whether an ionic bond will have significant covalent character based on ion size and charge?
ANSWER: Fajans' rules
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds would undergo Berry pseudorotation at the fastest rate?
W) PF5
X) PCl5
Y) SbF5
Z) SbCl5
ANSWER: W) PF5
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the normality of a 0.010 M solution of H2SO4?
W) 0.005 N
X) 0.010 N
Y) 0.015 N
Z) 0.020 N
ANSWER: Z) 0.020 N
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The concentration of a reagent expressed as the total concentration of all forms of that reagent present in solution is called what?
ANSWER: Formality
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer MnO experiences what type of magnetism in which neighboring spins are locked into an opposing arrangement leading to a net zero magnetic moment?
ANSWER: antiferromagnetism
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In iodometric titrations, iodide reacts with thiosulfate to produce iodine and the tetrathionate anion. How many sigma and pi bonds respectively does the tetrathionate anion have?
ANSWER: 9, 4
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The Stork Enamine Synthesis is a method used to attach groups to the alpha carbon of an aldehyde or ketone. By name or number, order the following three steps of the Stork Enamine Synthesis from first to last 1) Hydrolysis 2) Enamine Formation 3) Michael Addition
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 3 types of transition states which will be observed in the Birch reduction 1) radical transition state 2) anion transition state 3) radical anion transition state
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the phenomenon by which an electron vacancy in an inner electron shell releases energy by ejecting another electron from an outer shell instead of emitting a photon?
ANSWER: Auger Effect
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following three statements that are true about the anions fulminate and cyanate, which have formulas CNO- and NCO- respectively 1) Fulminate is linear while cyanate is bent 2) Nitrogen has a neutral formal charge in both fulminate and cyanate 3) Cyanate is more stable than Fulminate
ANSWER: 3
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the functional group created when hydroxylamine is added to an aldehyde or a ketone?
ANSWER: Oxime
16. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following sets of terms describes standard broadband C-13 NMR?
W) Multiplicity is present, Integration is possible
X) Multiplicity is not present, Integration is possible
Y) Multiplicity is present, Integration is not possible
Z) Multiplicity is not present, Integration is not possible
ANSWER: Z) Multiplicity is not present, Integration is not possible
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The Mohr titration has a red endpoint caused by the reaction between silver cations and what anion?
ANSWER: Chromate (also accept: CrO42-)
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the laboratory technique used to determine the total nitrogen by using concentrated sulfuric acid and catalysts to break down organic matter and convert nitrogen into ammonium sulfate?
ANSWER: Kjeldahl Method
