1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is 500 Kelvin in degrees Fahrenheit?
ANSWER: 440.33 degrees
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer If Selenium has an atomic number of 34 and atomic weight of 79.0, how many protons, neutrons, and electrons does it have if the charge on the atom is -1?
ANSWER: 34 protons, 45 neutrons, 35 electrons
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the molar mass of two moles of sodium chloride, to the nearest whole number?
ANSWER: 117 grams
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is a T-shaped molecule?
W) BH3
X) H2CO
Y) ICl3
Z) CO32-
ANSWER: Y) ICl3
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the angle, to the nearest tenth, between two different atoms in a tetrahedral molecule?
ANSWER: 109.5
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the thiocyanate ion?
W) CN-
X) AsO43
Y) ClO4-
Z) SCN-
ANSWER: Z) SCN-
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the chemical formula and the stock system name of ferricyanide?
ANSWER: [Fe(CN)6]3− and HEXACYANOFERRATE (III)
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer At STP, how much volume, in milliliters, does 2 moles of hydrogen gas occupy?
ANSWER: 44,800
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If 2.00L of a gas measured at STP have a mass of 3.23g, what is the molar mass of the gas?
ANSWER: 36.2 g/mol
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the random motion of colloidal particles called?
ANSWER: Brownian movement
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer When a beam of light is visible and sharply outlined when it passes through a colloidal dispersion, what is this effect called?
ANSWER: TYNDALL EFFECT
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Who is element with 109 protons named after?
W) Lise Meitner
X) Glenn Seaborg
Y) William Roentgen
Z) Niels Bohr
ANSWER: W) Lise Meitner
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Name all nine elements that occur as polyatomic molecules.
ANSWER: Hydrogen, Oxygen, Nitrogen, Chlorine, Fluorine, Bromine, Iodine, Sulfur, Phosphorus
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the formula for calcium hydrogen sulfite?
ANSWER: Ca(HSO3)2
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The compound XY2 has a molar mass of 44g. Assuming X is carbon, what is the formula of the compound?
ANSWER: CO2
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer At what temperature is water the densest (in degrees Celsius), and how much water (in milliliters) weighs a kilogram at that temperature?
ANSWER: 4 degrees Celsius, 1000 milliliters
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How much heat, in calories, is required to change 1000 milliliters of water at 4 degrees Celsius to 100 degrees Celsius?
ANSWER: 96,000 calories
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the oxidation number of S8?
ANSWER: 0
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Determine the oxidation number for each atom in ferricyanide.
ANSWER: Fe +3, C +2, N -3
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the Lewis definition of a base?
W) A hydroxide-containing substance
X) A proton (H+) acceptor
Y) A species with an unshared pair of electrons
Z) A substance that produces hydroxide ions in aqueous solution
ANSWER: Y) A species with an unshared pair of electrons
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the process by which the ions of a salt separate as the salt dissolves?
ANSWER: DISSOCIATION
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many atoms of hydrogen are in 1 mole of H2SO4?
ANSWER: 1.2044 × 1024
12. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the percentage by mass of sulfur in H2SO4?
W) 16%
X) 33%
Y) 36%
Z) 42%
ANSWER: X) 33%
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many joules of heat are required to raise one gram of water one degrees Celsius?
ANSWER: 4.186
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If ten grams of liquid at 373 K are heated to 150 degrees Celsius. If the liquid absorbs 6 kcal, what is the specific heat of the liquid in calories per gram degrees Celsius?
ANSWER: 12
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of these molecules is polar?
W) C2H6
X) SF6
Y) CCl4
Z) NF3
ANSWER: Z) NF3
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Two molecules of sodium would form what type of bond?
ANSWER: Metallic
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A flask contains three times as many moles of H2 gas as it does O2 gas. What is the total pressure in the flask if the partial pressure due to oxygen is P?
W) 4P
X) 3P
Y) 4/3P
Z) 3/4P
ANSWER: W) 4P
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Who made the law of partial pressures that says that the pressure of a mixture of gases is equal to the sum of the pressures of all of the other gases?
ANSWER: John Dalton
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A person starts with 320 grams of a radioactive element and after 20 minutes he has 20 grams left. What is the half-life of this element?
ANSWER: 5 minutes
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What are the two types of artificial radiation?
ANSWER: POSITRON EMISSION AND ELECTRON CAPTURE
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which neutral atom is represented by the electron configuration 1s22s22p63s23p64s23d5?

17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer An atom with a sp3 hybridization forms what shape?
ANSWER: TETRAHEDRAL
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What feature of alkynes distinguishes them from alkanes?

18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Name the alkanes with one, two, three, and four carbons.
ANSWER: METHANE, ETHANE, PROPANE, BUTANE
