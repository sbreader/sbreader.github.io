Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Naman is an organism composed of cells, and suddenly learns that his existence must follow a set of rules, known as cell theory. Which of the following is not a basic tenet of cell theory?
W) All living things are made up of cells
X) The number of cells are unchanged
Y) All cells come from preexisting cells
Z) Cells are the basic unit of life
ANSWER: X) The number of cells are unchanged
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Naman also finds out that he is made of carbon, and that most other, if not all, living things are also made of carbon. Carbon is known as the backbone of life due to which of the following?
W) It can form strong hydrogen bonds
X) It can easily form metallic bonds
Y) It can form four stable covalent bonds
Z) It has high specific heat capacity
ANSWER: Y) It can form four stable covalent bonds
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Liam has been eating at his favorite restaurant, Taco Bell, lately, and releases a nasty fart. What phase of matter would a fart be classified as, which has no definite shape?
ANSWER: Fluid (also accept: Gas; Liquid)
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Liam runs chemical analysis on his fart, and determines that it is primarily made up of a noble gas that contains 10 protons. What noble gas, commonly used to illuminate signs in store windows, is his fart made of?
ANSWER: Neon
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Due to procrastinating too much for AP Physics C, Aaron is up at 2 am in the morning learning how to analyze a block on an inclined plane. What is the specific name of the diagram that he could make to find the forces acting on the block?
ANSWER: Free-body diagram (do not accept: Force diagram)
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Wanting to take a break from studying, Aaron walks 5 meters south, 6 meters east, 1 meter north, and 3 meters west. What is his final displacement in meters?
ANSWER: 5
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Liam has been calling Aaron “too small” recently, due to Aaron being 5 foot 3. To comfort himself, Aaron tells himself that celestial objects sometimes get called small too. Indeed, which astronomical object was reclassified in 2006 by the IAU as a dwarf planet, and is located in the Kuiper Belt?
ANSWER: Pluto
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following planets has a diameter closest to that of Venus?
W) Mercury
X) Earth
Y) Jupiter
Z) Neptune
ANSWER: X) Earth
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Evaluate the following expression: 10-4 * 2.
ANSWER: 2
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Larry is growing cells such that they double every hour. After 3 hours, he now has 64 cells. How many cells did he start with?
ANSWER: 8
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organs of the digestive system receives food first when eating?
W) Esophagus
X) Small intestine
Y) Stomach
Z) Mouth
ANSWER: Z) Mouth
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Naman the organism is back in business after suffering an identity crisis. However, he suddenly realizes that he is a prokaryote, and is actually a cell himself. Identify all of the following domains of life that are composed solely of prokaryotic organisms. 1. Bacteria 2. Archea 3. Eukarya
ANSWER: 1 and 2
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer After finishing his Taco Bell burrito, Liam moves on to eat five tacos in one sitting. This time, he releases another fart, which actually ionizes to a +1 charge when exposed to halogens. What is the name of this group of elements that usually form a +1 charge when bonding?
ANSWER: Alkali metals (also accept: Group 1) (do not accept: Alkaline Earth metals)
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What types of bonds primarily make up the compound sodium chloride?
ANSWER: Ionic bonds
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In Newtons, how much force is required to accelerate a 5 kilogram block from rest to 10 meters per second squared?
ANSWER: 50
8. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which scientist is most associated with developing quantum theory in the early 1900s?
W) Albert Einstein
X) Isaac Newton
Y) Stephen Hawking
Z) Max Planck
ANSWER: Z) Max Planck
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Aaron’s face is red from crying after being bullied for only being 5 foot 3. Thus, other people have started comparing him to which planet in our Solar System, which is commonly referred to as the Red Planet?
ANSWER: Mars
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three continents by increasing land area. 1. North America 2. Asia 3. Oceania
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the least common multiple of 4 and 6?
ANSWER: 12
10. BONUS Math Short Answer How many radians are in the unit circle?
ANSWER: 2π
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Lucas is a new plant, and is still learning about how to survive. He feels hungry and realizes that he wants to produce some glucose in order to make ATP in the future. What process in plants converts light energy into glucose?
W) Glycolysis
X) Calvin Cycle
Y) Cell Cycle
Z) Photosynthesis
ANSWER: Z) Photosynthesis
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the largest organ of the human body?
ANSWER: Skin
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the base SI unit for the mass of an object?
ANSWER: Kilogram
13. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes an ionic bond?
W) Electrons are shared between 2 atoms
X) Electrons are removed from 2 atoms
Y) Electrons are donated from one atom to another
Z) Electrons leave the atoms, creating a sea of electrons
ANSWER: Y) Electrons are donated from one atom to another
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Michelle is a superb physicist with 67 Nobel prizes who has been bullying Kiran in recent years. For some reason, she keeps calling him “dense”. This surprises many people, as she only knows Kiran’s mass. What other quantity would she need to know before determining his density?
ANSWER: Volume
14. BONUS Physics Short Answer In a graph where velocity is on the y-axis and time is on the x-axis, what quantity does the slope of the graph represent?
ANSWER: Acceleration
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Lucas has gone bald, and has been left with exactly 0 strands of hair. What natural satellite of our planet, like Lucas’s head, reflects the Sun's light and is visible in phases to observers on the Earth's surface?
ANSWER: The Moon
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a phase of the Moon?
W) Waxing crescent
X) First quarter
Y) Second quarter
Z) Third quarter
ANSWER: Y) Second quarter
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Kiran is struggling in math class, because the only number he knows is 67. To help him, the teacher tells him to practice identifying perfect squares. Select all of the following four numbers that are perfect squares. 1. 25 2. 50 3. 75 4. 100
ANSWER: 1 and 4
16. BONUS Math Short Answer Ayden is stuck in a snowstorm and now travels 40% slower than when the weather was good. If his normal commute to school was 15 minutes, what is his new commute time, in minutes?
ANSWER: 25
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In eukaryotic cells, which ATP-producing organelle is commonly called the powerhouse of the cell?
ANSWER: Mitochondria
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Chloroplasts generally appear green primarily due to the presence of what pigment?
ANSWER: Chlorophyll
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Michelle has become a communist, and decides that sharing is caring. Just like Michelle, some atoms are communist too, and share their electrons. What is the term for this type of bond between two atoms?
ANSWER: Covalent bond
18. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements does not exist as a gas in nature?
W) Carbon
X) Hydrogen
Y) Argon
Z) Oxygen
ANSWER: W) Carbon
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Lucas pushes Kiran for calling him bald, and in the process, Lucas feels himself being pushed backwards. Which physics law best encapsulates this scenario, which states that for every force, there is an equal and opposite reaction force?
ANSWER: Newton’s 3rd Law (do not accept: 3rd Law; Newton’s Law)
19. BONUS Physics Short Answer Which subatomic particle has a similar mass to a proton, but has no net charge?
ANSWER: Neutron
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which layer of the atmosphere is the lowest and is the one that we are standing in right now?
W) Stratosphere
X) Thermosphere
Y) Mesosphere
Z) Troposphere
ANSWER: Z) Troposphere
20. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the most common element by weight in the Earth's crust?
ANSWER: Oxygen
21. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the slope of the line that goes through the points (-1, 3) and (1, -1)?
ANSWER: -2
21. BONUS Math Short Answer People enter a concert at a rate of 5 people per second while 4 people leave every second. How many people will be in the concert after 10 seconds if the concert started with 80 people?
ANSWER: 90
