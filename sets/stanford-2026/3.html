1. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Annabeth has a secret base b, and she tells Percy that in base b, 3 base b is a divisor of 11 base b. Which of the following could be a possible value of b?

1. BONUS Math Short Answer Phil draws triangle ABC with area 25 and line DE of length 3 parallel to BC such that D lies on AB and E lies on AC. If the area of trapezoid DECB is 21, what is the length of BC?
ANSWER: 15/2
2. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Stanford’s Energy System Lab are studying the sustainability of renewable energy sources. Which of the following sources of energy is NOT considered renewable?
W) Nuclear
X) Geothermal
Y) Solar
Z) Hydroelectrical
ANSWER: W) Nuclear
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Scientists at Stanford’s Hadly Lab have determined that populations at carrying capacity oscillate according to the function sine of x times cosine of x. What is the derivative of this function at x equals pi?
ANSWER: 1
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In contrast to sills, what type of tabular igneous intrusion is described as discordant because it cuts across pre-existing rock layers?
ANSWER: Dikes
3. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not true regarding mountain streams and floodplain rivers?
W) Mountain streams have steeper gradients than floodplain rivers
X) Mountain streams are depositional, while floodplain rivers are erosional
Y) Mountain streams are generally straight, while floodplain rivers have large meanders
Z) Mountain streams have lower rates of discharge than floodplain rivers
ANSWER: X) Mountain streams are depositional, while floodplain rivers are erosional
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice When aluminum reacts with oxygen, electrons from aluminum’s 3p subshell are transferred to which of the following subshells on oxygen?
W) 2s
X) 3s
Y) 2p
Z) 3p
ANSWER: Y) 2p
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 3 compounds in order of lowest to highest oxidation state of manganese 1) MnSO4 2) KMnO4 3) MnO2.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A point located a perpendicular distance of 1 meter away from an infinite sheet of constant charge density has electric field E. What is the electric field a distance of 3 meters away from the sheet?
ANSWER: E
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Alistair is 160 centimeters tall. If he wants to buy a mirror that allows him to see his whole body, what is the minimum height, in centimeters, that he can buy?
ANSWER: 80
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The reintroduction of gray wolves to Yellowstone increased biodiversity of the ecosystem by keeping the elk population in check. What term best describes the role of these wolves in the ecosystem, exerting a disproportionately large effect relative to their abundance?
ANSWER: Keystone species
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of paracrine signaling? cells
W) Acetylcholine binding to a receptor on skeletal muscle
X) Glucagon binding to a receptor in liver cells
Y) Nitric oxide binding to a receptor on smooth muscle cells
Z) A helper T cell binding to MHC class II on a dendritic cell
ANSWER: Y) Nitric oxide binding to a receptor on smooth muscle cells
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer After heating a sample to increase solubility, Ben cools it gently so the dissolved solute does not immediately precipitate, even though the concentration now exceeds the equilibrium solubility at that temperature. What term best describes this solution?
ANSWER: Supersaturated
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 3 bonds by increasing length 1) Nitrogen-nitrogen bond in molecular nitrogen 2) Carbon-oxygen bond in dimethyl ether 3) Carbon-carbon bond in ethene.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the B soil horizon?
W) It is rich in minerals leached from the topsoil
X) It has the highest biological activity out of all soil horizons
Y) It is primarily composed of organic matter
Z) It is composed of unweathered parent rock
ANSWER: W) It is rich in minerals leached from the topsoil
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Stratified glacial drift is most directly deposited by which of the following sources?
W) Glacial terminus
X) Basal erosion
Y) Supraglacial gelifluction
Z) Meltwater streams
ANSWER: Z) Meltwater streams
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a limitation of the morphological definition of a species?
W) Cannot be applied to organisms that reproduce asexually
X) Misclassification due to analogous structures produced from convergent evolution
Y) Dependence on long-term observation of ecological niches
Z) Limited usefulness for classifying species from fossil remains
ANSWER: X) Misclassification due to analogous structures produced from convergent evolution
9. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Round seeds are dominant to wrinkled, and yellow seeds are dominant to green. A plant heterozygous for both traits is selfcrossed. Assuming Mendelian genetics, what fraction of the offspring will have wrinkled, yellow seeds?
W) 1/16
X) 3/16
Y) 7/16
Z) 9/16
ANSWER: X) 3/16

ANSWER: 11/16
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Scientists at UCLA’s Aguillon Lab are studying the diversification of bird species on separating islands. What type of speciation occurs when the same species becomes separated due to islands splitting off, causing populations to evolve independently over time?
W) Sympatric
X) Peripatric
Y) Parapatric
Z) Allopatric

ANSWER: Z) The fluid is incompressible
12. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A student measures the electric field at points on a sphere surrounding an object and finds that it points away from the object at every point. Which of the following is most directly implied by the student’s measurements?

Y) The object is a dipole with the positive side facing up
Z) The object is a magnet with the north side facing up
