Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What kind of collision conserves both kinetic energy and linear momentum?
ANSWER: ELASTIC (also accept: PERFECTLY ELASTIC)
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer An object in free-fall on the Earth will eventually stop accelerating. What is the term for the speed of the object when it reaches this point?
ANSWER: TERMINAL VELOCITY (also accept: TERMINAL SPEED)
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term is used to describe the fraction of light that is reflected by a surface?
ANSWER: ALBEDO
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice At which of the following locations is seafloor spreading occurring?
W) Marianas trench
X) African rift valley
Y) Mid-Atlantic ridge
Z) Tanna fault
ANSWER: Y) Mid-Atlantic ridge
3. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Ethanol in the US is primarily produced from what crop?
ANSWER: CORN
3. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the percentage of electricity produced in the US using renewable energy sources?
W) 80%
X) 50%
Y) 30%
Z) 10%
ANSWER: Z) 10%
4. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice To guard against sparks causing a fire, mechanics aboard submarines during World War II often used tools made from which of the following:
W) Chrome plated steel
X) Vanadium
Y) Cold rolled steel
Z) Bronze
ANSWER: Z) Bronze
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Life on Earth is based on building blocks of what element?
W) Oxygen
X) Carbon
Y) Fluorine
Z) Nitrogen
ANSWER: X) Carbon
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The exchange of gases between blood and the tissue occurs normally in:
W) Venules
X) Arterioles
Y) Veins
Z) Capillaries
ANSWER: Z) Capillaries
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer In what phylum are all vertebrate organisms?
ANSWER: CHORDATA
6. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice The county fair charges a $4 entrance fee. The cost of each ride is $2. Which of the following equations represents the cost (y) of getting into the fair and going on x rides?
W) y = 6 + x
X) y = 2 + 4x
Y) y = 4 + 2x
Z) y = 6x
ANSWER: Y) y = 4 + 2x
6. BONUS Math Short Answer If a circle has a circumference of 10 meters, what is its area, to the nearest whole square meter?
ANSWER: 8
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If a transformer has an input voltage of 440 volts and an output voltage of 110 volts, what is the ratio of the number of turns in the input coil to the number of turns in the output coil?
ANSWER: 4 to 1
7. BONUS Energy Short Answer What method is currently used to produce most of the world's renewable energy?
ANSWER: HYDROPOWER (also accept: HYDROELECTRIC)
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following provides a measure of the amount of matter in a substance?
W) Mass
X) Volume
Y) Density
Z) Weight
ANSWER: W) Mass
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer What derived SI unit is used to express power?
ANSWER: WATT
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Through which of the following mediums does sound travel the slowest?
W) Air
X) Water
Y) Copper
Z) Ice
ANSWER: W) Air
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Continental rifts and mid-ocean ridges are found at which type of plate boundary?
ANSWER: DIVERGENT
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of lava flows can be observed at the volcanic hot spots in Hawaii?
W) Felsic
X) Andesitic
Y) Basaltic
Z) Picritic
ANSWER: Y) Basaltic
10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of telescope uses convex lenses to bend light?
W) Radio telescope
X) Refracting telescope
Y) Reflecting telescope
Z) Hubble telescope
ANSWER: X) Refracting telescope
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms is known to only produce a small number of offspring at a time?
W) Frog
X) Oyster
Y) Fruit fly
Z) Chimpanzee
ANSWER: Z) Chimpanzee
11. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is an example of a change in the primary structure of a protein?
W) An amino acid substitution
X) A protein folding into a beta sheet
Y) A disulfide bond exchange
Z) Subunits of a protein dimerizing
ANSWER: W) An amino acid substitution
12. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Donna rolls two standard dice. What is the most likely sum of the results of the two dice?
ANSWER: 7
12. BONUS Math Short Answer You are given the following list of 9 numbers: 71, 80, 93, 101, 83, 19, 54, 80, 74. Calculate the difference between the median and mode.
ANSWER: 0
13. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Landfills are a potential source of what kind of biofuel?
ANSWER: BIOGAS (also accept: VEGETABLE OIL, COOKING OIL)
13. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three units that correspond to energy 1) Watts 2) Joules 3) Kilowatt-hours.
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many vertices does a pentagonal prism have?
ANSWER: 10
14. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Sherman's car holds 20 gallons of gas. He drove 375 miles, using 3/4 of a tank. How many miles per gallon does his car get?
W) 20
X) 25
Y) 30
Z) 35
ANSWER: X) 25
15. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In bean and corn seeds, the food supply that is used by the seed to germinate and grow is found in the:
W) Cotyledon
X) Root tip
Y) Abcission zone
Z) Petiole
ANSWER: W) Cotyledon
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following four choices that would not be visible under a standard laboratory light microscope when visualizing a plant cell 1) DNA 2) Ribosome 3) Cell wall 4) Chloroplast.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What layer of the mantle is found below the lithosphere?
ANSWER: ASTHENOSPHERE
16. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following four choices from lowest altitude to highest altitude 1) Weather balloon 2) Mesosphere 3) Contrails 4) Aurora Borealis.
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2, 4
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The movement of lithospheric plates over the asthenosphere is explained by which theory?
ANSWER: CONTINENTAL DRIFT
17. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice When crossing two heterozygous plants, what percentage of the offspring do you expect to be homozygous?
W) 0
X) 25
Y) 50
Z) 75
ANSWER: Y) 50
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A balanced chemical equation must possess which of the following?
W) The same number of molecules on both sides of the equation
X) The same kinds of molecules on both sides of the equation
Y) The same number of each kind of atom on both sides of the equation
Z) The same leading coefficient on both sides of the equation
ANSWER: Y) The same number of each kind of atom on both sides of the equation
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer What statistical law of thermodynamics states that entropy increases in a closed system?
ANSWER: SECOND LAW OF THERMODYNAMICS
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A nucleotide contains all except which of the following?

Z) Amino acid
ANSWER: Z) Amino acid

Y) Glaciers

Z) Siberia
