1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the radial acceleration, in meters per second squared, of an object undergoing uniform circular motion in a circle with radius 5 meters and speed 5 meters per second?
ANSWER: 5
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three conditions in which mechanical energy is conserved. I. An aircraft taking off II. A perfectly inelastic collision III. A launched ball bouncing off a wall at its peak and returning to its launch point;
ANSWER: III only
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following atoms would require the most energy to remove an electron from?
W) Gallium
X) Aluminum
Y) Silicon
Z) Carbon
ANSWER: Z) Carbon
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three orbitals in order of increasing energy. I. 3d II. 4s III. 4p.
ANSWER: II, I, III: 4s, 3d, 4p
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following four macromolecule classes that contain significant amounts of sulfur I. Proteins II. Carbohydrates III. Lipids IV. Nucleic Acids
ANSWER: I only
3. BONUS Biology Short Answer When joining two amino acids in a peptide bond to form a polymer, what molecule is produced?
ANSWER: WATER (also accept: H2O)
4. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What is 5 XOR 3?
ANSWER: 6
4. BONUS Energy Short Answer Often, data has patterns to it that allow us to create a version of it using less bits than the original data. What is the process of creating these versions called?
ANSWER: COMPRESSION
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the area surrounding a star in which liquid water can exist?
ANSWER: HABITABLE ZONE (also accept: GOLDILOCKS ZONE)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice One would expect the largest habitable zone to be found around what spectral class of star?
W) O
X) A
Y) F
Z) M
ANSWER: W) O
6. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A paper airplane is descending at a rate of 2 m/s. How long, in seconds, will it take the airplane to descend 54 meters?
ANSWER: 27 seconds
6. BONUS Math Short Answer Brennan is 4 years older than Hee. If the sum of their ages is 20, what is the sum of twice Brennan’s age and three times Hee’s age?
ANSWER: 48
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A string is wrapped around a pulley and is used to lift a 50 newton block at constant speed. In newtons, what is the force pulling on the pulley?
ANSWER: 100 NEWTONS
7. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice An adiabatic process is best defined in which there is zero of which of the following?
W) Heat exchanged with environment
X) Change in temperature
Y) Change in entropy|
Z) Change in pressure
ANSWER: W) Heat exchanged with environment
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three processes that decrease the entropy of a system? I. sublimation II. freezing III. separation.
ANSWER: II and III
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Compound A is undergoing a reaction which has a first-order rate law with respect to A. Initially, the concentration of A is 0.500 M. After 20 seconds, its concentration is 0.250 M. What will be the molar concentration of A after 40 seconds have passed?

X) 0.125
Y) 0.200

Z) Protista

W) Complete Graph
X) Tree
Y) Bipartite Graph

Having a dominant A allele yields red flowers, and homozygous recessive gene A flowers are white. However, if a flower is homozygous recessive for gene B, then no pigment will be produced and the flower will be white, regardless of the alleles in gene A. In the cross between two double heterozygous parents, what is the expected ratio of red to white flowers?
W) 12:4
X) 13:3
Y) 9:7
Z) 15:1
