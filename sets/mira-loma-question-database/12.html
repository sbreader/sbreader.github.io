Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which class of flatworms are free-living?
W) Monogenea
X) Trematoda
Y) Turbellaria
Z) Cestoda
ANSWER: Y) Turbellaria
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What are the sixth class of plant hormones that promote cell expansion and cell elongation along with auxin?
ANSWER: Brassinosteroids
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Aquatic osteichthyes have what protective bony flap that covers the chambers of the gills?
ANSWER: Operculum
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the systematic name for K3Fe(CN)6?
ANSWER: Potassium hexacyanoferrate (III)
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What equation gives the relationship between the cell potential and concentrations of the cell components?
ANSWER: Nernst equation
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the common name of C55H70O6N4Mg?
W) Chlorophyll a
X) Chlorophyll b
Y) Chlorophyll c
Z) Chlorophyll d
ANSWER: X) Chlorophyll b
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the electron configuration of the chromium atom?
ANSWER: [Ar] 4s13d5 (also accept: 1s22s22p63s23p64s13d5)
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If the length of a pendulum is 10 meters, assume g = 10 m/s2, what is the period of the pendulum?
ANSWER: 6.28 seconds
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is obtained when a planoconvex lens is placed on top of a flat glass surface?
ANSWER: Newton's rings
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What empirical diffraction limit is given when the images of two different points are just resolved when the principal diffraction maximum of one image coincides with the first minimum of the other?
ANSWER: Rayleigh criterion
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Suppose Young's experiment is carried out in air then in water. How does the distance between bright fringes change?
W) They move closer together.
X) They move farther apart.
Y) There is no change.
Z) The experiment cannot be performed underwater.
ANSWER: W) They move closer together.
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Photographs of the photosphere have a mottled appearance because of what phenomenon?
ANSWER: Granulation
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Where are Red dwarfs on the H-R diagram?
W) Top left
X) Top right
Y) Lower left
Z) Lower right
ANSWER: Z) Lower right
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Einstein added what constant to his equations that balances the gravitation in the universe?
ANSWER: Cosmological constant
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the primary nutrients in soil?
ANSWER: Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of carbon is used for water treatment and deodurisation?
W) Granular activated carbon
X) Powdered activated carbon
Y) Extruded activated carbon
Z) Impregnated carbon
ANSWER: W) Granular activated carbon
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the distance between star north and magnetic north?
ANSWER: Magnetic declination
18. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the integral of 2sin(x)cos(x)?
ANSWER: sin2(x) + C (also accept: -cos(2x)/2 + C; -cos2(x) + C)
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is equal to (e2x+1)/(e2x-1)
ANSWER: coth x
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What theorem states that a separation of a plane into a map can be colored so that no two adjacent regions have the same color?
ANSWER: Four color theorem
21. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is a pair (X,T) where X is a set and T is a subset of the power set of X?
ANSWER: Topological space
22. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A blue paper is lit by a red light passed through a green filter. What color does the paper appear to be?
W) Red
X) Green
Y) Blue
Z) Black
ANSWER: Z) Black
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the fear of prescribing pain medications for patients?
ANSWER: Opiophobia
24. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What fuel is produced by heating wood without the presence of oxygen?
ANSWER: Charcoal
25. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which trees provide resin for turpentine?
ANSWER: Pine trees
