1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice By how many times does the pH of the stroma differ from the pH of the thylakoid space in an illuminated chloroplast?
W) 3
X) 30
Y) 10
Z) 1000
ANSWER: Z) 1000
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many neutrons are in nitrogen-15?
ANSWER: 8
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A 5 kg ball is dropped at a height of 20 meters. Ideally, if no energy is lost as heat, what is the kinetic energy of the ball at 10 meters? Assume gravity is 10 m/ s2.
ANSWER: 500 Joules
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice An mP air mass is
W) dry and cold
X) humid and warm
Y) humid and cold
Z) dry and warm
ANSWER: Y) humid and cold
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer In the geometric sequence 2/3, 4/9… etc. what is the fifth term?
ANSWER: 32/243
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Which part of a mitochondria is synonymous with the thylakoid space in terms of the proton-motive force during respiration and photosynthesis?
ANSWER: Intermembrane space
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the charge of the silver ion?
ANSWER: 1+
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Giving your answer with 1 significant digit, what is the de Broglie wavelength in meters of an object at 800 kg moving at 8 m/s?
W) 53
X) 1 × 10-45
Y) 1 × 10-37
Z) 1 × 10-63
ANSWER: Y) 1 × 10-37

Y) 60
Z) 100

13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is false?
W) In Beta plus decay a positron and electron neutrino are emitted.
X) The atomic number decreases during electron capture.
Y) Beta decay doesn't change the number of nucleons.
Z) Beta minus decay causes the atomic number to decrease by 1.
ANSWER: Z) Beta minus decay causes the atomic number to decrease by 1.
