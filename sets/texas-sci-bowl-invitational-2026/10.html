1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice There are two regulatory mechanisms for the trp operon: one involving the trp repressor and the other involving the trpL sequence. Which of the following combinations of trp regulation occur when tryptophan levels are high?
W) trp repressor remains unbound; U-rich attenuator forms
X) trp repressor binds to operator; U-rich attenuator forms
Y) trp repressor remains unbound; U-rich attenuator does not form
Z) trp repressor binds to operator; U-rich attenuator does not form
ANSWER: X) trp repressor binds to operator; U-rich attenuator forms
