Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Four points are all equidistant from a fifth point. Which of the following types of figures must contain the four points?
W) Circle
X) Sphere
Y) Square
Z) Rectangle
ANSWER: X) Sphere
1. BONUS Math Short Answer If two coplanar concentric circles have radii of 5 and 11, respectively, what is the area of the region between them?
ANSWER: 96π
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In the tri-nomen Canis lupis familiaris, Canis refers to what taxonomic rank?
W) Family
X) Genus
Y) Species
Z) Subspecies
ANSWER: X) Genus
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer The two strands of DNA are held together by hydrogen bonds between what components of the DNA?
ANSWER: BASES (also accept: NITROGENOUS BASES)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What subatomic particles can be found in a cloud around the nucleus?
ANSWER: ELECTRONS
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The alloy brass is composed of copper and what other metal?
ANSWER: ZINC
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Argonne National Lab scientists are studying a class of mixtures in which one substance is uniformly distributed, but not dissolved, in another substance. What is the name for this general class of mixtures?
ANSWER: COLLOID
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer DOE scientists have been studying vanadium dioxide, a material that can conduct electricity without conducting heat. This material is also well-known for acting as a metallic conductor ONLY above 67 degrees Celsius. Below that temperature, it behaves as what type of electric material?
ANSWER: INSULATOR
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a mineral?
W) Gabbro
X) Muscovite
Y) Breccia
Z) Andesite
ANSWER: X) Muscovite
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Place the following three minerals in order of decreasing hardness 1) Quartz 2) Corundum 3) Apatite.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What effect occurs when the observer of a sound wave is moving relative to the source of the sound?
ANSWER: DOPPLER EFFECT (also accept: DOPPLER SHIFT)
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Protons and neutrons are made up of still smaller particles, called what?
ANSWER: QUARKS
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer DOE researchers are studying how global temperature increases affect the upper altitude limit of Engelmann spruce. What is the term for the upper altitude limit at which trees can grow?
ANSWER: TREE LINE
7. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers at SLAC are developing zeolite catalysts for methanol production. Which of the following statements best explains why zeolites are such effective catalysts?
W) They are very electron-poor
X) They are made from nonmetals
Y) They have a lot of pores
Z) They are very dense
ANSWER: Y) They have a lot of pores
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A disaccharide, such as lactose, is composed of how many simple sugar subunits?
ANSWER: 2
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer What chambers of the human heart are responsible for pumping blood out of the heart and into the rest of the body?
ANSWER: VENTRICLES (also accept: LEFT AND RIGHT VENTRICLES)
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer SN 1987A was what type of cosmic event?
ANSWER: SUPERNOVA (also accept: TYPE-TWO SUPERNOVA) (do not accept: TYPE-ONE SUPERNOVA)
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three planets that have an axial tilt greater than that of Earth 1) Mercury 2) Mars 3) Jupiter.
ANSWER: 2
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What type of bond is formed between sodium and chlorine in table salt?
ANSWER: IONIC
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three substances in increasing order of pH 1) Milk 2) Vinegar 3) Ammonia.
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the leading coefficient of the polynomial x5 – 2x3 – 4x + 6?
ANSWER: 1
11. BONUS Math Short Answer A squirrel runs 11 meters north, 7 meters west, and then 4 meters north. To the nearest whole meter, how far is the squirrel from its original starting point?
ANSWER: 17
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Some inhibitors, rather than competing with the substrate at the active site, bind to a distal site on the enzyme to inhibit activity. What type of inhibition is this?
W) Competitive
X) Co-factor
Y) Feedback
Z) Allosteric
ANSWER: Z) Allosteric
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer What phase of mitosis is marked by the sister chromatids traveling in opposite directions away from the metaphase plate?
ANSWER: ANAPHASE
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for the substance that is dissolved when forming a solution?
ANSWER: SOLUTE
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of the phases of matter 1) In solids, atoms vibrate in place 2) Boiling and evaporation are both types of vaporization 3) A two-liter glass of ice water has the same thermal energy as a one-liter glass of ice water.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Rounded to the nearest whole number, what is pi squared?
ANSWER: 10
14. BONUS Math Short Answer If two exterior angles of a triangle are 135 degrees and 120 degrees, what is the degree measure of the interior angle not adjacent to either of the given exterior angles?
ANSWER: 75
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Subduction zones are associated with what type of plate boundary?
ANSWER: CONVERGENT
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What are the two most abundant elements in Earth's crust?
ANSWER: OXYGEN AND SILICON (also accept: O AND Si)
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the square of the binomial x – 8?
ANSWER: x2 – 16x + 64
16. BONUS Math Short Answer The total volume of 3 identical tanks that are circular cylinders is 540π. If the height of one tank is 5, what is its radius?
ANSWER: 6
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In what part of the digestive tract are nutrients primarily absorbed into the bloodstream?
W) Stomach
X) Small intestine
Y) Large intestine
Z) Pancreas
ANSWER: X) Small intestine
17. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the largest terrestrial biome, characterized by large populations of coniferous trees?
W) Chaparral
X) Tundra
Y) Taiga
Z) Tropical rainforest
ANSWER: Y) Taiga
18. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Reverse osmosis, capacitive deionization, and forward osmosis are all examples of what process that makes seawater drinkable?
ANSWER: DESALINATION
18. BONUS Biology Short Answer DOE researchers have used advanced imaging methods to visualize how the structural fibers in the cytosol of a cell connect the nucleus to the extracellular matrix. What is the general term for these fibers that give structure to the inside of the cell?
ANSWER: CYTOSKELETON
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer An item is originally priced at 20 dollars. Discounts are based on the current marked price. In three consecutive sales, the price is reduced by 10%, then 10%, and then 50%. In dollars and cents, what is the final price of the item?
ANSWER: $8.10
19. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area of a right triangle with hypotenuse of length 9 and one leg of length 4?
ANSWER: 2√65
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Relative to the Sun, in which of the following directions is Sirius located on a Hertzsprung-Russell diagram?
W) Up and left
X) Up and right
Y) Down and left
Z) Down and right
ANSWER: W) Up and left
20. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the shape of the Milky Way galaxy?
W) Elliptical
X) Barred spiral
Y) Spiral
Z) Irregular
ANSWER: X) Barred spiral
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Amino acids all contain an amine group and what other group?
W) Hydroxyl
X) Ketone
Y) Aldehyde
Z) Carboxyl
ANSWER: Z) Carboxyl
21. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three compounds that consist of only carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen 1) Glucose 2) Ethanol 3) Acetic acid.
ANSWER: ALL
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What nerve in the human body is responsible for carrying signals from retinal photoreceptors to the brain?
ANSWER: OPTIC NERVE
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer Pyelonephritis, or inflammation of the kidney, can occur from an ascending urinary tract infection. Place the following three locations in chronological order of the route the bacteria takes 1) Kidney 2) Bladder 3) Urethra.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following would NOT contribute to sea level rise?
W) Land subsidence
X) Thermal expansion of seawater
Y) Melting of sea ice
Z) Melting of continental glaciers
ANSWER: Y) Melting of sea ice
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the three layers of the atmosphere in order of decreasing altitude 1) Stratosphere 2) Mesosphere 3) Thermosphere.
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
