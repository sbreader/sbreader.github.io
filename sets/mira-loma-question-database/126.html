1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice According to the activity series of metals, which metal would react most vigorously in a dilute acid solution?
W) Zinc
X) Iron
Y) Aluminum
Z) Magnesium
ANSWER: Z) Magnesium
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following metals from most reactive to the least reactive, according to the activity series: potassium, lead, calcium, gold.
ANSWER: POTASSIUM, CALCIUM, LEAD, GOLD
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice How many calories are required to heat 10 grams of water from 4 degrees Celsius to 14 degrees Celsius?
W) 10 calories
X) 14 calories
Y) 100 calories
Z) 200 calories
ANSWER: Y) 100 calories
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following substances from those with the largest specific heat capacity to the lowest: aluminum, magnesium, tungsten, liquid water
ANSWER: LIQUID WATER, MAGNESIUM, ALUMINUM, TUNGSTEN
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The point at which a titration is complete is called the
W) End point
X) Equilibrium point
Y) Completion point
Z) Saturated point
ANSWER: W) End point
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the concentration of acetic acid if 20 mL of 0.40 M NaOH is needed to titrate a 10 mL sample of the vinegar?
ANSWER: 0.8 MOLAR
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Of the following elements, which has the least tendency to gain electrons and form negative atoms?
W) Fluorine
X) Chlorine
Y) Bromine
Z) Iodine
ANSWER: Z) Iodine
4. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the process where electrolysis is used to coat a material with a layer of metal?
ANSWER: ELECTROPLATING
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What substance did Joseph Priestly heat, which led to the discovery of oxygen?
ANSWER: MERCURIC OXIDE (HgO)
5. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer This element was an attempt to explain processes such as combustion and rusting in the 17th century. What was the name of the "fire-like" element proposed by Johann Becher?
ANSWER: PHLOGISTON
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements about solubility is not true?
W) All hydroxides are insoluble
X) All chlorides are soluble except those of silver, mercury (I), and lead
Y) All nitrates are soluble
Z) Calcium sulfate is slightly soluble
ANSWER: W) All hydroxides are insoluble
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The statement that solubility varies in direct proportion to the pressure applied it is known as which law or principle?
ANSWER: HENRY'S LAW
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the balanced chemical equation for the Haber process?
ANSWER: N2 + 3H2 →2NH3
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In the Haber process, an increase in pressure would shift the equilibrium in which direction?
ANSWER: RIGHT (FORWARD)
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What quantity is defined as the product of the absolute temperature and the entropy of a system all subtracted from the internal energy of the system?
ANSWER: HELMHOLTZ ENERGY
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice A solution can be both
W) dilute and concentrated
X) saturated and dilute
Y) saturated and unsaturated
Z) supersaturated and saturated
ANSWER: X) saturated and dilute
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer As the temperature of a solution increases, what generally happens to its solubility?
ANSWER: INCREASES
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The bond between which of the following sets of atoms has the greatest energy?
W) Oxygen double bonded with oxygen
X) Hydrogen single bonded with hydrogen
Y) Carbon triple bonded with carbon
Z) Carbon single bonded with hydrogen
ANSWER: Y) Carbon triple bonded with carbon
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The property of being able to add enthalpies is based on
W) Law of Conservation of Heat
X) First Law of Thermodynamics
Y) Law of Multiple Proportions
Z) Ideal Gas Law
ANSWER: X) First Law of Thermodynamics
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In the reaction of hydrogen gas reacting with chlorine gas to make gaseous hydrogen chloride, what is the reaction's standard heat of formation, given that the bond between two hydrogen atoms has an energy of 432 kJ, the bond between two chlorine atoms has an energy of 239 kJ and the bond between a hydrogen atom and a chlorine atom has an energy of 427 kJ.
ANSWER: -91.5 kJ
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which indicator would cause a solution with a pH of 10 to appear yellow?
W) Phenolphthalein
X) Litmus
Y) Bromothymol blue
Z) Methyl orange
ANSWER: Z) Methyl orange
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which definition of an acid states that an acid is an electron-pair accepter?
ANSWER: LEWIS THEORY
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds would look white?
W) ZnS
X) CdS
Y) CuS
Z) PbS
ANSWER: W) ZnS
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following nitrogen compounds is a reddish brown gas, is very soluble in water, and has an extremely unpleasant odor?
W) Nitrous oxide
X) Nitric oxide
Y) Nitrogen dioxide
Z) Nitrogen tetraoxide
ANSWER: Y) Nitrogen dioxide
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In the past, this compound was prepared by distilling leather scraps, hoofs, and horns.
ANSWER: AMMONIA
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is an electrolytic method of preparing bromine?
W) Hall process
X) Ostwald process
Y) Hunter process
Z) Dow process
ANSWER: Z) Dow process
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In the Dow process, from what is the bromine extracted from?
ANSWER: BRINE
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Indicate using words or numbers which of the following statements are true 1. Atomic radii generally decrease from left to right across a period 2. Atomic radii decrease from top to bottom in a group Y. Electronegativity generally decreases from top to bottom in a group
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which element has the highest first ionization energy?
ANSWER: HELIUM
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Indicate using words or numbers which of the following statements are true 1. Acid-forming properties increase from right to left 2. Base-forming properties are low on the left side and increase to the right 3. First Nonmetallic properties are greatest on the left side and increase to the right.
ANSWER: NONE OF THEM
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is defined as the energy change that occurs when an electron is gained by a neutral atom?
ANSWER: ELECTRON AFFINITY
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A 1.5 gram sample of a substance is dissolved in 100 grams of water and produces a solution that boils at 100.51 degrees Celsius. What is the molecular mass of the substance?
ANSWER: 15 GRAMS
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer If 2 moles of a substance is added to 1000 grams of water, what would be the new freezing point of the solution?
ANSWER: -3.72 DEGREES CELSIUS
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Indicate using words or numbers which of the following statements are true 1. The melting point of an allow is usually higher than that of its components 2. An alloy is usually harder than the metals that compose it 3. An alloy that cools slowly usually has smaller crystalline particles
ANSWER: TWO ONLY
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Brass is an alloy of which two elements?
ANSWER: COPPER AND ZINC
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What property of a substance designates the highest temperature at which a substance can exist as a liquid?
ANSWER: CRITICAL TEMPERATURE
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following substances from highest to lowest critical temperatures: ammonia, argon, mercury, water
ANSWER: MERCURY, WATER, AMMONIA, ARGON
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Indicate using words or numbers which of the following statements about electrochemistry are true 1. In a galvanic cell, electricity is produced by a spontaneous chemical reaction. 2. The quantity of electricity carried by 1 mole of electrons is called a faraday. 3. Standard reduction potentials show the relative likelihood and spontaneity of half-cell reduction reactions
ANSWER: ALL OF THEM
21. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of is a film on the surface of bronze or similar metals produced by oxidation over a long period or by a chemical process?
ANSWER: PATINA
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is another name for 1,3-dimethylbenzene?
W) o-Xylene
X) p-Xylene
Y) m-Xylene
Z) q-Xylene
ANSWER: Y) m-Xylene
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which functional group has a formula C6H5?
ANSWER: PHENYL
23. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Decomposing water by an electric current will give a ratio of hydrogen to oxygen by volume equal to
W) 1 to 9
X) 1 to 8
Y) 1 to 2
Z) 2 to 1
ANSWER: Z) 2 to 1
23. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the most common anion used as an electrolyte in the electrolysis of water?
ANSWER: SULFATE ION
24. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Indicate by name or numbers which of the following statements are true 1. Each principal level of quantum number n has n + 1 subshells. 2. Each subshell of a quantum number l contains 2l orbitals 3. No more than two electrons can be placed in each orbital
ANSWER: JUST THREE

ANSWER: OSTWALD PROCESS
