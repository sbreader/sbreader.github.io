Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at the University of Southampton are studying chaotic physical phenomena, including turbulence. What dimensionless quantity quantifies the turbulence of a fluid?
ANSWER: REYNOLDS NUMBER
1. BONUS Math Short Answer Researchers at Los Alamos National Laboratory are studying the Quantum Butterfly effect. The butterfly effect is named after what quintessential demonstration of chaos theory?
ANSWER: LORENZ ATTRACTOR (also accept: LORENZ SYSTEM)
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice An orchid mantis that disguises itself as a bright flower to lure prey exhibits what kind of mimicry?
W) Batesian
X) Mullerian
Y) Peckhamian
Z) Self
ANSWER: Y) Peckhamian
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer What kind of symbiotic relationship is exhibited between the bread mold Penicillium and certain bacteria?
ANSWER: ANTIBIOSIS (also accept: ANTAGONISM; AMENSALISM)
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following 4 changes to a standard copper-zinc galvanic cell that would increase the measured cell potential 1) Increasing the concentration of copper (II) 2) Decreasing the concentration of copper (II) 3) Increasing the concentration of zinc (II) 4) Decreasing the concentration of zinc (II).
ANSWER: 1 AND 4
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer At 298 K, a concentration cell with two solutions of Ag+ has a measured cell potential of 0.075 V. To one significant figure, by what factor must the concentration of the more concentrated cell be multiplied by to double the measured cell potential?
ANSWER: 20
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Two particles have both charge and mass, and feel an attractive force of F to each other, which is a combination of the electrostatic force and the gravitational force. When the charge of one particle is doubled, the two particles feel an attractive force of 4F/3 towards each other. If the charges of the particles had remained at their original values, but the mass of one particle was doubled instead, then what would be the force the particles felt towards each other?
ANSWER: 5F/3
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer A car with mass 2,000 kg is driving up a ramp with a 30° incline. If the car exerts a force of 10,000 N, and it moves a vertical distance of 10 meters high in 2 seconds, what is the power exerted over those 2 seconds, in Watts?
ANSWER: 100,000
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer On Anurag's farm, there are chickens and cows. If there are 72 heads and 150 feet not including Anurag, how many chickens are there on Anurag’s farm?
ANSWER: 69
5. BONUS Math Short Answer The product of the factors of 124 can be written as 12n, where n is a positive integer. What is the value of n?
ANSWER: 90
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Billie the Blithe Blower blows into a closed pipe that is 2 meters long. Being blithe, Billie wants to know the wavelength of the second lowest note she could blow, in meters. What is it?
ANSWER: 8/3
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Barry the Big Bellower bellows a note at 100 decibels. His friend, Wally the Wimpy Whisperer, who is standing a negligible distance away, wants to join in, so he tries his best and yells a note at 80 decibels. To the nearest integer, what is the intensity, in decibels, of the sound the two produce in combination?
ANSWER: 100
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at the US Department of Energy have been engineering systems of RNA with CAD to study genetic expression in microorganisms. Which of the following is found in prokaryotes?
W) snRNA
X) miRNA
Y) RNase P
Z) snoRNA
ANSWER: Y) RNase P
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at the US Department of Energy have also been studying TATA box equivalents in prokaryotes. Disregarding 5 prime and 3 prime ends, what is the DNA sequence of that equivalent?
ANSWER: TATAAT
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What phylum of fungi do ectomycorrhizal fungi belong to?
ANSWER: GLOMEROMYCOTA
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements describing imperfect fungi is FALSE?
W) They are classified based on the morphological species concept
X) They are a polyphyletic group
Y) They include fungi that cause athlete’s foot
Z) They utilize budding processes to reproduce
ANSWER: Z) They utilize budding processes to reproduce
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements shows two near-equal peaks in mass spectrometry?
W) Fluorine
X) Chlorine
Y) Bromine
Z) Iodine
ANSWER: Y) Bromine
9. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The base peak in a mass spectrum of 3-pentanone has a mass to charge ratio closest to which of the following?
W) 43
X) 57
Y) 71
Z) 86
ANSWER: X) 57
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If an object of mass m falling through the air with a velocity v experiences a drag force of magnitude F, what is the magnitude of the drag force experienced by an object falling with mass 3m and velocity 4v?
ANSWER: 16F
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer If the air temperature is 70 degrees Fahrenheit, and water takes 5 minutes to cool from 170 degrees Fahrenheit to 120 degrees Fahrenheit, what will its temperature be in 5 more minutes, in degrees Fahrenheit?
ANSWER: 95
11. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice A brandohedron is a convex polyhedron with 100 vertices and 69 faces. How many edges does a brandohedron have?
W) 167
X) 168
Y) 169
Z) There is not enough information to determine the answer
ANSWER: W) 167
11. BONUS Math Short Answer There are 30 different complex numbers z such that z30 = 1. For how many of these is z5 a real number?
ANSWER: 10
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What period of rapid expansion immediately follows the Big Bang?
ANSWER: COSMIC INFLATION (also accept: INFLATION)
12. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, order the following four epochs in the history of the universe in chronological order 1) Grand unification 2) Planck 3) Quark 4) Inflationary.
ANSWER: 2,1,4,3
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory are studying the properties of chemical warfare agents in the gas phase. In the virial expansion, what does the second virial coefficient account for?
W) Size of the gas particles
X) dimerization of gaseous species
Y) Bimolecular attraction of particles
Z) Trimolecular repulsions of particles
ANSWER: Y) Bimolecular attraction of particles
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Idaho National Laboratory are studying vapor enriching methods to separate radioactive gases. When separating a 1:1 mixture of Bromine vapor (molar mass 160) and Argon (molar mass 40) via effusion, how many effusion steps are needed to obtain a 16:1 ratio of Argon to Bromine Vapor?
ANSWER: 4
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What kind of survivorship curve do squirrels follow?
ANSWER: TYPE II
14. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What kind of evolution is illustrated by Red Queen dynamics?
W) Disruptive selection
X) Fluctuating selection
Y) Kin selection
Z) Sexual dimorphism
ANSWER: X) Fluctuating selection
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following has the highest boiling point?
W) Chloromethane
X) Dichloromethane
Y) Trichloromethane
Z) Carbon tetrachloride
ANSWER: Z) Carbon tetrachloride
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If a material has an index of refraction of 10 million, to 2 significant figures, how many meters does light travel in the material in 1 minute?
ANSWER: 1800
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer A bar of metal with mass M lies on the x axis between x = 0 and x = 1, with the density at a point x being 6√(1 + 3x2). What is the x-coordinate of the center of mass of the bar, in terms of M?
ANSWER: 14/(3M)
17. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the geometric mean of the roots of x3-11x2 + 34x-27?
ANSWER: 3
17. BONUS Math Short Answer Let r1, r2, r3 be roots of the polynomial x3 + 5x2-4x + 9. What is the value of 1/r1 + 1/r2 + 1/r3?
ANSWER: 4/9
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What region around a rotating black hole drags spacetime and is a means of theoretically extracting energy from black holes?
ANSWER: ERGOSPHERE
18. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following four spacetime metrics which predict the existence of an ergosphere 1) Schwarzschild 2) Kerr 3) Reissner-Nordstrom 4) Kerr-Newman.
ANSWER: 2,4
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Ignoring spin-spin coupling, how many peaks would a H1 NMR spectrum of 2-butanol have?
ANSWER: 6
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer An organic compound with formula C4H8O2 shows a single peak in H1 NMR. What is this compound’s IUPAC name?
ANSWER: 1,4-DIOXANE (also accept: 1,4-DIOXACYCLOHEXANE)

W) Next-gen sequencing
X) Southern blotting
Y) Phage Induction
Z) Polymerase Chain Reaction

21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer During what era did Laurasia form?
ANSWER: MESOZOIC

ANSWER: ANTHROPOCENE
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer If the horizontal and the vertical component of the earth's magnetic field are equal, what is the resulting angle of dip?
ANSWER: 45 DEGREES
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the theory that explains how Earth gets its magnetic field from convection currents of the molten outer core and the solid inner core?
ANSWER: DYNAMO EFFECT

ANSWER: CANTOR SET
