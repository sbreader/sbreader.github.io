1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the point where groundwater leaves an aquifer to reach the surface?
ANSWER: Spring
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the Kelvin-Helmholtz timescale?
W) How long a protostar will be contracting
X) How long a star can sustain its total kinetic energy at its current luminosity
Y) How long after a galaxy formed for its nucleus to become inactive
Z) How long it takes for an asteroids orbit to become unstable
ANSWER: X) How long a star can sustain its total kinetic energy at its current luminosity
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A point charge +q is located at the origin, and a point charge -2q is located at x = a, where a is positive. Which of the following statements is true?
W) Close to the charges, the electric field can be zero off the x axis.
X) Close to the charges, the magnitude of the electric field can be a maximum off the x axis.
Y) The electric field can be zero between the charges.
Z) The electric field can be zero on the x axis at finite points not between the charges.
ANSWER: Z) The electric field can be zero on the x axis at finite points not between the charges.
2. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is true about the maximum transverse speed of a particle in a wave with wave speed v?
W) It is always equal to v
X) It is unrelated to v
Y) It is always greater than v
Z) It is always less than v
ANSWER: X) It is unrelated to v
