1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The reabsorption of bicarbonate in the kidneys can take place via the metabolism of what amino acid, which forms ammonium that is then excreted in the urine?
W) alanine
X) glutamine
Y) cysteine
Z) methionine
ANSWER: X) glutamine
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Name all of the following three hormones that are responsible for the degeneration of female reproductive ducts and the formation of male reproductive ducts. I. Anti-Müllerian hormone II. Luteinizing hormone III. Testosterone
ANSWER: I and III
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following ligands will cause the most d orbital splitting?
W) CO
X) H2O
Y) Cl-
Z) OH-
ANSWER: W) CO
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Name all of the following three properties of a solution that must remain additive when two non-ideal solutions are mixed 1) Molality 2) Entropy 3) Vapor pressure
ANSWER: 1
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A block with mass m lies at rest on an inclined plane angled at theta degrees above the horizontal and with coefficient of friction mu. Which of the following expressions best describes the magnitude of the frictional force on the block?
W) mg cosine theta
X) mg sine theta
Y) mu mg cosine theta
Z) mu mg sine theta
ANSWER: X) mg sine theta
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Consider a long segment of an axon, which can be modeled as a cylindrical shell of phospholipid bilayer. Assuming that the separation between the bilayers is much less than the radius of the cylinder, to what power of radius and what power of the thickness of the bilayer, respectively, is the capacitance of the axon proportional?
ANSWER: 1, −1
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Northern Russia and Canada are dominated by which of the following soil orders?
W) Alfisols
X) Gelisols
Y) Inceptisols
Z) Mollisols
ANSWER: X) Gelisols
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three protostar masses, in solar masses, that would follow the Hayashi track during their early evolution 1) 1 2) 2 3) 0.5.
ANSWER: All
5. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following must be true about abelian groups but is not true about all other groups?
W) The operation is commutative
X) The operation is associative
Y) The operation is distributive
Z) The operation has an identity element
ANSWER: W) The operation is commutative
5. BONUS Math Short Answer For how many distinct integers n is (x2-7x+11)x2-2x equal to 1?
ANSWER: 4
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three sex determination systems in which males and females have the same number of chromosomes 1) ZW 2) XO 3) haplodiploid.
ANSWER: 1 ONLY
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Adeline conducts a cross between an individual who is heterozygous for 3 genes and an individual who is homozygous recessive for all 3 genes. Giving your answer as a fraction in simplest form, what is the chance that the resulting offspring displays exactly 2 dominant phenotypes and 1 recessive phenotype?
ANSWER: 3/8
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the formation of the Hofmann product in elimination?

X) higher activation energy, lower final energy
Y) lower activation energy, higher final energy

X) increasing, constant
Y) constant, increasing
