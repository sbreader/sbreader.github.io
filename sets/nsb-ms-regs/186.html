1. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is 23% of 4?
ANSWER: 0.92 (also accept: 23/25)
1. BONUS Math Short Answer A ball is dropped from the top of a 45-meter-tall building. After each bounce, it reaches one-third of the height from which it previously fell. In meters, what is the total vertical distance the ball has traveled when it hits the ground for the 3rd time?
ANSWER: 85
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer How many seismograph locations are typically needed to locate the focus of an earthquake?
ANSWER: 3
2. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What is the ratio of iron to oxygen in the mineral hematite?
W) 1 to 2
X) 1 to 3
Y) 2 to 3
Z) 3 to 2
ANSWER: Y) 2 to 3
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer A cup of black coffee has a pH of 5, and a solution of ammonia has a pH of 11. How many times as acidic is the coffee compared to the ammonia?
ANSWER: 1,000,000
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three bonds in which the carbon atom will typically be the positive dipole 1) C-H 2) C-N 3) C-O.
ANSWER: 2, 3
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In order to indirectly measure the average kinetic energy of atoms in a given location, physicists at Oak Ridge National Lab have developed a device that will produce a nanometer-resolution map of what property of a crystal?
ANSWER: TEMPERATURE
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab at the IceCube experiment are studying high-energy neutrinos. Most high-energy neutrinos are produced by high-energy cosmic interactions, while low-energy neutrinos detected on Earth are primarily produced from nuclear reactors and what other major source?
ANSWER: SUN

ANSWER: OBLIGATE AEROBE

ANSWER: PROTOSTOME

ANSWER: THIRD
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What planet has a mean density about 70 percent that of Earth, and surface acceleration due to gravity about 40 percent that of Earth?
ANSWER: MARS
7. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is logically equivalent to "If T is a leopard, then T has spots"?
W) If T has spots, then T is a leopard.
X) If T is not a leopard, then T does not have spots.
Y) If T does not have spots, then T is not a leopard.
Z) If T does not have spots, then T is a leopard.
ANSWER: Y) If T does not have spots, then T is not a leopard.
7. BONUS Math Short Answer A pyramid with 36 edges has how many vertices?
ANSWER: 19

W) Violet
X) Orange

8. BONUS Physics Short Answer Suppose that a 50-kilogram cart and a 70-kilogram cart, both traveling at 5 meters per second in opposite directions, collide and stick together. In meters per second with one significant figure, what is the speed of the final composite object?
ANSWER: 0.8

X) Oxidizing agent
Y) Combustion catalyst
