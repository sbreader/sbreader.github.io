1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Ethan da’ Gee is looking at the Moon and is trying to determine whether it looks more like a female side profile or a rabbit pounding mochi. What is the name for the relatively young impact crater whose prominent ray system extends across the southern lunar highlands?
ANSWER: Tycho crater
1. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following conditions would be most conducive to the formation of a Fata Morgana?
W) High concentration of aerosols
X) High humidity
Y) Temperature inversion
Z) Radiative cooling
ANSWER: Y) Temperature inversion
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Tay-Sachs disease is very well known, with a genetic dysfunction of the HexA enzyme leading to GM2 ganglioside buildup. There actually exist two rarer, more lethal variants of this disease that are known. Give the name of both of these diseases.
ANSWER: Sandhoff’s disease, gangliosidosis AB
2. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice It seems to be a common misconception that Sandhoff’s disease only affects the HexB enzyme, and thus is similar in magnitude to Tay Sachs’s. This is not true, and Sandhoff’s is worse than Tay Sachs’s by some margin. Why is this the case?
W) Sandhoff’s disease induces mutations in both the HEXA and HEXB genes, deactivating both.
X) Deficiency in the HexB enzyme impacts the body more than HexA deficiency.
Y) HexB not only functions independently, but also potentiates HexA function.
Z) Mutation in the HEXB gene suppresses the synthesis of both the HexB and HexA enzymes.
ANSWER: Z) Mutation in the HEXB gene suppresses the synthesis of both the HexB and HexA enzymes.
