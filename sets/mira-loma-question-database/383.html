2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the study of the biology and systems of the Plant kingdom known as?
ANSWER: Botany
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The formula C2H3O2– refers to what ion?
ANSWER: Acetate
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The identity of an element is determined by which of the following?
W) the number of its protons
X) the number of its neutrons
Y) the number of its electrons
Z) the atomic mass
ANSWER: W) the number of its protons
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is Avogadro's number
W) 6.01 × 1022 mol
X) 6.2 × 1023 mol
Y) 6.02 × 1023 mol
Z) 6.22 × 1022 mol
ANSWER: Y) 6.02 × 1023 mol
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Granite is an example of a rock where
W) fossils are commonly found
X) fossils are not found at all
Y) fossils are rarely found
Z) certain processes need to occur for fossils to appear
ANSWER: X) fossils are not found at all
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer During an eclipse, what is known as the innermost and darkest part of a shadow, where the light source is completely blocked by the occluding body.
ANSWER: Umbra
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice According to which of the following astronomer's laws, the cube of the mean distance of a planet from the sun is proportional to the square of the period.
W) Johannes Kepler
X) Nicholas Copernicus
Y) Tycho Brahe
Z) Galileo Galilei
ANSWER: W) Johannes Kepler
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What planet in our solar system is most similar to Venus?
ANSWER: Earth
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Topaz is what number hardness on Moh's Hardness Scale?
W) 6
X) 8
Y) 3
Z) 7
ANSWER: X) 8
12. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What gas is natural gas mainly composed of?
ANSWER: Methane
14. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What determines whether crude oil is called sweet or sour?
ANSWER: Its sulfur content
15. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an example of renewable types of energy?
W) Biomass and Solar
X) Geothermal and Solar
Y) Solar and Hydro-Electric
Z) Coal and Gas
ANSWER: Z) Coal and Gas

ANSWER: Pascal
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Who are the two people credited with being the fathers of phsyics?
ANSWER: Albert Einstein and Sir Isaac Newton
18. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice When the volume of gas is increased, the pressure is..?
W) increases
X) decreases
Y) stays the same
Z) increases and decreases
ANSWER: X) decreases
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the fourth form of matter, besides solid, liquid and gas?
ANSWER: Plasma
