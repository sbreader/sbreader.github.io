1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice An increase in what pituitary hormone would most directly causes the follicle to rupture and the egg to be released down the fallopian tube?
W) Estrogen
X) Follicle-stimulating hormone
Y) Luteinizing hormone
Z) Progesterone
ANSWER: Y) Luteinizing hormone
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice In which of the following cells does carbon fixation take place in C3 plants?
W) Spongy mesophyll
X) Palisade mesophyll
Y) Bundle sheath cells
Z) Guard cells
ANSWER: X) Palisade mesophyll
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In a body-centered cubic unit cell, how many atoms are contained within the unit cell?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 4
Z) 6
ANSWER: X) 2
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer By name or number, order the following three oxyacids in order of increasing strength. 1) HNO2 2) HNO3 3) H2SO4
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A random radius between -100 and 100, and a random angle between −π and π are chosen to get the polar point A. If the product of the radius and the angle is a positive number, what is the expected value of the x-coordinate?
ANSWER: 0
3. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the cross product of i + j and k?
ANSWER: i − j
4. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The angular position of a point 50 centimeters away from the center of a rotating disc is given by θ(t) = t4, where t is time in seconds. What is the translational velocity, in meters per second, of the point at t = 2 seconds?
ANSWER: 16
4. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Under which of the following conditions is the work exerted by gas molecules to increase its volume is MAXIMIZED?
W) Under constant temperature
X) Under adiabatic conditions
Y) Under constant pressure
Z) Under free expansion
ANSWER: W) Under constant temperature
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of waves are formed when polar air moves towards the equator while tropical air moves toward the poles, typically associated with the jetstream?
W) Gravitational waves
X) Love waves
Y) Rossby waves
Z) Rosswell waves
ANSWER: Y) Rossby waves

ANSWER: 42

W) 1-bromopropane

Y) Tert-butanol

8. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice An event has a 1 in 10,000 probability of occuring any given year. Which of the following is closest to the probability that it occurs at least once over 10,000 years?
W) 1 − 2/e
X) 1 − 1/e
Y) 1 − 1/(2e)
Z) 1
ANSWER: X) 1 − 1/e

ANSWER: 20
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The opening of which oceanic gateway allowed the Antarctic Circumpolar Current to develop?
ANSWER: Drake Passage
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Emily finds a unique sequence of rocks during a hike. From top to bottom, it is made up of pelagic sediments, pillow basalts, sheeted dikes, layered gabbro, and finally peridotite. What is the name of this sequence?
ANSWER: Ophiolite sequence (also accept: Ophiolite)

13. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If there is a 99% chance that it does not rain on any particular day, what is the chance that it does not rain for an entire year, rounded to the nearest tenth?
ANSWER: 0.0
13. BONUS Math Short Answer Magikarp is taking a math class that is graded in 3 parts: homeworks are worth 20%, midterms are worth 40%, and the final is worth 40%. At the end of the semester, Magikarp has a 95% on the homeworks and a 83% on the midterms. What is the minimum integer percentage that Magikarp needs on the final to have at least a 90% in the class?
ANSWER: 95

15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Robert observes a star at culmination from Lafayette, Indiana, approximately 40° N 87° W. He notes that the declination of the star is 35° and the right ascension is 3 hours. Identify all of the true statements about the star. 1) The star is circumpolar 2) The current sidereal time is 3 hours 3) The star will lie on the meridian in 12 hours
