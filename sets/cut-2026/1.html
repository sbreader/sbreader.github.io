Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organelles does NOT have a double membrane?
W) mitochondrion
X) chloroplast
Y) nucleus
Z) lysosome
ANSWER: Z) lysosome
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following 3 organisms in chronological order of appearance during a primary ecological succession 1) oak trees 2) lichen 3) grass
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following describes the bonds present in a carbon dioxide molecule?
W) carbon-oxygen single bonds
X) carbon-carbon single bonds
Y) carbon-oxygen double bonds
Z) carbon-carbon double bonds
ANSWER: Y) carbon-oxygen double bonds
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following properties is intensive?
W) entropy
X) enthalpy
Y) energy
Z) temperature
ANSWER: Z) temperature
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A toy car starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 4 meters per second squared for 6 seconds. In meters per second, what is the final speed of the object?
ANSWER: 24
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Order the following 4 regions of the electromagnetic spectrum from lowest to highest frequency. 1) Ultraviolet 2) Radio 3) Visible 4) Infrared
ANSWER: 2, 4, 3, 1
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the location below the earth's surface where an earthquake originates?
ANSWER: hypocenter (also accept: focus)
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 stages in the water cycle in which water turns from liquid to gas 1) evaporation 2) precipitation 3) condensation
ANSWER: 1 only
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the value of x if log base x of 16 = 2?
ANSWER: 4
5. BONUS Math Short Answer A number is increased by 20% to get 60. What is the original number?
ANSWER: 50
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Researchers at the University of California, San Diego are examining how certain marine bacteria convert sunlight into usable energy using specialized pigments. These pigments allow the bacteria to perform what fundamental biological process?
ANSWER: Photosynthesis
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer DOE Scientists are utilizing beams of neutral particles to probe the atomic and magnetic structure of materials. What particle would they most likely be scattering?
ANSWER: neutrons
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In an ecosystem, primary producers contain 16,000 joules of energy. Approximately how much energy, in joules, is consumed by a secondary consumer?
W) 16,000
X) 1,600
Y) 160
Z) 16
ANSWER: Y) 160
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Order the following three taxonomic ranks from least to most specific 1) order 2) phylum 3) family
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer An alpha particle has the same structure as the nucleus of what element?
ANSWER: helium
8. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a chemical change?
W) corrosion
X) deposition
Y) combustion
Z) oxidation
ANSWER: X) deposition
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A 2 kilogram object moves in a straight line at a speed of 5 meters per second. In joules, what is the kinetic energy of the object?
ANSWER: 25
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What term describes a decay process in which an unstable nucleus splits into two fragments of comparable mass, often releasing additional neutrons and energy?
ANSWER: nuclear fission (also accept: fission)
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Proposed by Georges Lemaître, what theory states that the universe formed from an extremely hot, dense single point about 13.8 billion years ago and has been expanding and cooling ever since?
ANSWER: Big Bang theory
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 elements in increasing abundance in the universe 1) Hydrogen 2) Helium 3) Oxygen
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Three fair coins are flipped. What is the probability that 2 coins land on heads?
ANSWER: 3/8
11. BONUS Math Short Answer A box contains 12 pens, 4 of which are defective. If a pen is drawn at random, what is the probability it is defective?
ANSWER: 1/3
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying greenhouse gases. Melting of permafrost creates a positive feedback loop primarily by releasing what potent greenhouse gas?
ANSWER: methane
12. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Scientists at Brookhaven are using tokamaks to power nuclear fusion. Tokamaks confine which of the following states of matter?
W) solid
X) liquid
Y) gas
Z) plasma
ANSWER: Z) plasma
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer A sudden catastrophic event leaves only one percent of an elephant population surviving. This is an example of what type of genetic drift in which a population experiences a drastic reduction in size, leading to random changes in allele frequencies?
ANSWER: bottleneck effect
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the process in which a cell engulfs large particles by extending its membrane?
ANSWER: phagocytosis
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following atoms would most likely possess a +3 oxidation state when in a compound?
W) sodium
X) magnesium
Y) aluminum
Z) silicon
ANSWER: Y) aluminum
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Other than a salt, what other product is formed from a neutralization reaction?
ANSWER: water
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A circuit has a current of 3 amperes flowing through a resistor with a resistance of 5 ohms. In volts, what is the potential difference across the resistance?
ANSWER: 15
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following 3 quantities that are vectors 1) speed 2) velocity 3) force
ANSWER: 2 and 3
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Deep ocean trenches, subduction zones, and mountain belts are all features of what type of plate boundaries?
ANSWER: convergent
16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following soil horizons is characterized by an accumulation of leached materials?
W) O
X) A
Y) E
Z) B
ANSWER: Z) B
17. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice If the area of a rectangle is 36, which of the following is the maximum possible perimeter of the rectangle?
W) 18
X) 24
Y) 30
Z) 36
ANSWER: X) 24
17. BONUS Math Multiple Choice If a taxi charges a flat fee of $6 plus $0.60 per mile, which of the following is the cost of a 12 mile trip?
W) 12.60
X) 13.20
Y) 13.80
Z) 14.40
ANSWER: X) 13.20
18. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at the University of Michigan are studying how freshwater fish maintain salt balance by measuring ion transport across their gill membranes. The movement of ions from low concentration to high concentration requires which of the following cellular processes?
W) Passive diffusion
X) Active transport
Y) Osmosis
Z) Facilitated diffusion
ANSWER: X) Active transport
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying radioactive tracers used in metabolic imaging. A sample of an isotope decreases from 80 grams to 10 grams in 6 hours. If half-life is constant, how many hours are in one half-life of this isotope?
ANSWER: 2
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following three structural organizations in terms of increasing successive levels of organization 1) Organ system 2) Tissue 3) Organism
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer In ScienceBowlia, a mammoth population’s growth rate slowed as it approached a size of 1000 individuals, and the population has continued to hover around that number. What term is used for this stable population size of 1000?
ANSWER: carrying capacity
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Given that the pOH of a solution is 6.8, what is the pH of this solution?
ANSWER: 7.2
20. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following atomic subshells can contain the most number of electrons?
W) 4s
X) 4p
Y) 4d
Z) 4f
ANSWER: Z) 4f
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer In hertz, what is the beat frequency of two sound waves with frequencies of 440 hertz and 444 hertz?
ANSWER: 4
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer What term describes a process in which no heat is transferred between a system and its surroundings, even though work may be done on or by the system?
ANSWER: adiabatic
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer By observing whether light from a distant object is redshifted or blueshifted, astronomers can tell if it is moving away from or toward Earth. This is due to what effect, in which the observed wavelength of a wave is affected by the relative motion between the source and the observer?
ANSWER: Doppler effect
22. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Flocculent and Grand Design are subtypes of which of the following types of galaxies?
W) spiral
X) elliptical
Y) irregular
Z) lenticular
ANSWER: W) spiral
23. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The average of 8 numbers is 12. When a ninth number x is added, the average becomes 13. What is the value of x?
ANSWER: 21
23. BONUS Math Short Answer 3 fair six-sided dice are rolled. What is the expected value of the numbers shown?
ANSWER: 10.5
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Scientists at Oak Ridge National Lab are studying icy and rocky bodies in the Solar System. What term describes these objects, which develop glowing tails when they approach the Sun?
ANSWER: comets
24. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Scientists at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are studying how Earth’s surface absorbs solar radiation and then re-emits energy back into the atmosphere. In which of the following regions of the electromagnetic spectrum does Earth’s surface primarily re-emit energy?
W) radio
X) infrared
Y) visible light
Z) ultraviolet
ANSWER: X) infrared
