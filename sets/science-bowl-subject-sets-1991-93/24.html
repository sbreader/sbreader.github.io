Packet 24 (Physics 1993) of Science Bowl Subject Sets 1991–93:Back to all sets

Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following pair are vector quantities?
W) time and space
X) force and momentum
Y) energy and time
Z) momentum and mass
ANSWER: X) force and momentum
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A force of 15 newtons is acting on an object which has a mass of 3 kilograms. If this is the only force acting on the object, what is the acceleration of the object?
ANSWER: 5 METERS PER SECOND SQUARED (also accept: 5 M/S2; 5)
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice An object with a mass of 5 kilograms is a distance of 5 meters above the surface of the earth. If the acceleration due to gravity is 10 meters per second squared, the potential energy of the object relative to the surface of the earth is:
W) 10 joules
X) 20 joules
Y) 100 joules
Z) 250 joules
ANSWER: Z) 250 joules
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice For a body to be in equilibrium, which of the following conditions must exist?
W) no forces may be acting on the body.
X) the body must be accelerating.
Y) the velocity of the body must be zero.
Z) the sum of the forces acting on the body must be equal to zero.
ANSWER: Z) the sum of the forces acting on the body must be equal to zero.
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Newton's first law is often referred to as which of the following?
W) The law of straight-line motion
X) The law of momentum
Y) The law of inertia
Z) F = ma
ANSWER: Y) The law of inertia
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The statement, "In regions where fluid velocity is smaller, the pressure is higher, and vice versa", is known as the principle of:
W) Pascal
X) Bernoulli
Y) Torricelli
Z) Archimedes
ANSWER: X) Bernoulli
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Relative to its period on the earth, is the period of a pendulum on the moon shorter, longer, or unchanged?
ANSWER: LONGER
8. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Two bodies each have a mass 'M'. If the mass of each body is doubled, the force of attraction between the two bodies is:
W) twice the original force
X) 1/4 the original force
Y) 4 times the original force
Z) unchanged
ANSWER: Y) 4 times the original force
9. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice An object in equilibrium always has zero:
W) velocity
X) momentum
Y) mass
Z) acceleration
ANSWER: Z) acceleration
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an SI unit of measure?
W) watt
X) newton
Y) joule
Z) erg
ANSWER: Z) erg
11. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A body which is attached to a spring undergoes simple harmonic motion. The magnitude of the body's acceleration is:
W) constant
X) proportional to its displacement from its equilibrium position
Y) zero
Z) always increasing
ANSWER: X) proportional to its displacement from its equilibrium position
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice If the resultant force acting on a body of constant mass is zero, the body's acceleration is:
W) increasing
X) decreasing
Y) always zero
Z) negative
ANSWER: Y) always zero
13. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the name of the Scottish physicist who developed the mathematical explanation of the theory of light?
ANSWER: JAMES CLERK MAXWELL (also accept: MAXWELL)
14. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Who received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1923 for his experiment which measured the charge of the electron. The experiment consisted of allowing drops of oil which had become charged to fall in an electric field.
ANSWER: ROBERT MILLIKAN (also accept: MILLIKAN; ROBERT ANDREWS MILLIKAN)
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Who received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1922 for his theory which predicted the electron orbits of the Hydrogen atom?
ANSWER: NIELS BOHR (also accept: BOHR)
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Who was the first female to be awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics?
ANSWER: MARIE CURIE (also accept: MARIE SKLODOWSKA-CURIE; MADAME CURIE; CURIE)
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A current of 6 amperes flows through a 2 ohm resistor for 30 seconds. How many coulombs of charge have passed through the resistor?
ANSWER: 180 COULOMBS (also accept: 180)
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The potential difference across a 4 ohm resistor is 20 volts. What is the current in the resistor?
ANSWER: 5 AMPS (also accept: 5 AMPERES; 5)
19. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer At a point directly east of a negative point charge, the electric field points in what direction?
ANSWER: WEST (also accept: TOWARD THE CHARGE)
20. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The temperature of a gas is held constant while its volume is reduced. The pressure the gas exerts on the walls of its container increases because its molecules:
W) strike the container walls more often
X) strike the container walls with higher speeds
Y) strike the container walls with greater force
Z) all of the above
ANSWER: W) strike the container walls more often
21. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The electric field of a point charge varies:
W) linearly with distance and inversely with charge
X) linearly with distance and inversely with charge squared
Y) linearly with charge and inversely with distance squared
Z) linearly with charge and inversely with distance
ANSWER: Y) linearly with charge and inversely with distance squared
22. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A two farad and a four farad capacitor are connected in parallel. What single capacitance is "equivalent" to this combination?
ANSWER: 6 FARADS (also accept: 6)
23. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A current of 5 amps passes through a 10 ohm resistor. What power is dissipated by this resistor under these conditions?
ANSWER: 250 WATTS (also accept: 250)
24. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Is the filament resistance lower or higher in a 500 watt light bulb than in a 100 watt light? Assume that both bulbs have been designed to operate at 110 volts.
ANSWER: LOWER
25. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A five ohm and a ten ohm resistor are connected in parallel, what single resistance is "equivalent" to this combination?
ANSWER: 3 1/3 OHMS (also accept: 10/3 OHMS; 3.33 OHMS; 3.33)
26. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A beam of light is incident on a perfectly smooth body of water. Is the angle that the REFLECTED ray makes with the normal greater than, smaller than, or the same as the angle the incident ray makes with the normal?
ANSWER: THE SAME AS (also accept: THE SAME; SAME)
27. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A beam of light is incident on a large block of glass. The index of refraction of the glass is greater than one. Is the wavelength of the light in the glass longer than, shorter than, or the same as the wavelength of the light in the air?
ANSWER: SHORTER THAN (also accept: SHORTER)
28. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The focal length of a concave mirror is 2 meters. An object is positioned 8 meters in front of the mirror. Is the image of this object real or virtual?
ANSWER: REAL
29. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A converging thin lens has a focal length of 27 centimeters. An object is placed 9 centimeters from the lens. Is the image of the object real or virtual?
ANSWER: VIRTUAL
30. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Light has wavelength, frequency, and speed. Which, if any, of these quantities remains unchanged when light passes from a vacuum into a block of glass?
ANSWER: FREQUENCY
31. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which of the following waves is not a transverse wave: a wave traveling on a string, an electromagnetic wave, or a sound wave?
ANSWER: A SOUND WAVE (also accept: SOUND WAVE; SOUND)
32. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Assume that a ray of light passes from glass into air at an angle of incidence greater than zero degrees. Will the ray of light be bent toward or away from the normal as it passes into the air?
ANSWER: AWAY FROM THE NORMAL (also accept: AWAY FROM; AWAY)
33. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer If one looks at a point P on the bottom of a swimming pool, one sees an image point P prime. Is the image point, P prime, a real or a virtual image?
ANSWER: VIRTUAL
34. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A real image can be formed by which of the following single mirrors: a plane mirror, a concave spherical mirror, or a convex spherical mirror?
ANSWER: A CONCAVE SPHERICAL MIRROR (also accept: CONCAVE; CONCAVE MIRROR)
35. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice If two different gases have the same volume, temperature, and pressure and behave like ideal gases, they will also be identical in which one of the following ways?
W) average molecular velocity
X) total mass
Y) total molecular kinetic energy
Z) average momentum per molecule
ANSWER: Y) total molecular kinetic energy
36. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The frequency of a wave is 2/10 Hertz. What is the period of this wave?
ANSWER: 5 SECONDS (also accept: 5)
37. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer As a source of sound approaches a stationary listener, does the apparent wavelength increase, decrease, or stay the same?
ANSWER: DECREASES (also accept: DECREASE; IT DECREASES)
38. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The particle nature of light is demonstrated by which of the following?
W) the photoelectric effect
X) the various colors of light
Y) the speed of light
Z) diffraction
ANSWER: W) the photoelectric effect
39. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A wavelength is commonly measured in which one of the following units?
W) radians
X) angstroms
Y) electron volts
Z) seconds
ANSWER: X) angstroms
40. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Which of the following three nuclear radiations is the most penetrating: alpha, beta, or gamma?
ANSWER: GAMMA (also accept: GAMMA RADIATION; GAMMA RAYS)
41. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice An electrical current in a superconducting ring will theoretically flow unchanged for:
W) several milliseconds
X) a second
Y) several weeks
Z) forever
ANSWER: Z) forever
42. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What three colors form the picture in a color tv?
ANSWER: RED, GREEN, AND BLUE (also accept: RED GREEN BLUE; RGB)
43. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the name of the process whereby waves travel around corners and obstacles in their paths?
ANSWER: DIFFRACTION
44. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The physicist Chadwick is known for his association with:
W) the alpha particle
X) the neutron
Y) the cloud chamber
Z) the electron
ANSWER: X) the neutron
45. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A color with a wavelength longer than that of yellow is:
W) red
X) blue
Y) violet
Z) green
ANSWER: W) red
46. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The knot is a nautical measure of:
W) length
X) speed
Y) acceleration
Z) displacement
ANSWER: X) speed
47. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Assuming constant gravity and no air resistance, the path of a cannonball fired from a cannon is:
W) a semi-circle
X) an ellipse
Y) a parabola
Z) a hyperbola
ANSWER: Y) a parabola
48. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer As you go up a mountain top, does your weight increase slightly, decrease slightly, or remain exactly the same?
ANSWER: DECREASES SLIGHTLY (also accept: DECREASE; DECREASES)
49. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The shape of a magnetic field around a long straight current carrying wire is:
W) elliptical
X) square
Y) varies depending on the magnitude of the current
Z) circular
ANSWER: Z) circular
50. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What number represents the ratio of speed of an object to the speed of sound in the surrounding air?
ANSWER: MACH NUMBER (also accept: MACH)
51. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Of the following answers, which is the frequency closest to middle-C on a piano?
W) 20
X) 256
Y) 2520
Z) 25200
ANSWER: X) 256
52. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice The non-relativistic mass of an object
W) must be the same as its weight
X) depends on where you measure it
Y) does not change with increasing velocity
Z) is greatest at the poles
ANSWER: Y) does not change with increasing velocity
53. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A ball is thrown horizontally from the top of a cliff. Assuming no air resistance, the acceleration of the ball while it is in flight is:
W) in the vertical direction only
X) in the horizontal direction only
Y) in the vertical and horizontal directions
Z) zero
ANSWER: W) in the vertical direction only
54. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice While traveling in a straight line, a car uniformly changes its speed from 50 kilometers per hour to 110 kilometers per hour in 5 seconds. The acceleration of the car during this time is:
W) 5 kilometers per hour per second
X) 12 kilometers per hour per second
Y) 50 kilometers per hour per second
Z) 60 kilometers per hour per second
ANSWER: X) 12 kilometers per hour per second
55. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice If there is to be a change in velocity, there MUST be a change in
W) speed only
X) direction only
Y) either speed or direction
Z) both speed and direction
ANSWER: Y) either speed or direction
56. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Susan pushes against a 100 kilogram rock with a force of 5 Newtons, but the rock doesn't move. The force the rock exerts on Susan is
W) 0 Newtons
X) 5 Newtons
Y) 20 Newtons
Z) 100 Newtons
ANSWER: X) 5 Newtons
