Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following that are antigen presenting cells. 1-T cell, 2- Dendritic Cell, 3-B cell, 4-Macrophages
ANSWER: 2 AND 4
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Cytotoxic T cells are activated by binding to what specific molecules on the surface of infected cells?
ANSWER: CLASS 1 MHC
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the term for disubstituted benzene rings with substituents on the 1 and 3 positions?
ANSWER: META
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Answer the following questions about nucleophilic aromatic substitution. A) It requires what type of group in the ortho or para position relative to the leaving group? B) What is the name for its intermediate resonance-stabilized anionic complex?
ANSWER: EWG, MEISENHEIMER COMPLEX
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The quantization of angular momentum in fundamental particles was discovered by what experiment in 1922?
ANSWER: STERN-GERLACH EXPERIMENT
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Quantum physicists hypothesize that the exchange of the graviton gives rise to the force of gravity. What is the spin of the hypothetical graviton?
ANSWER: 2
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer 15 choose 7 plus 15 choose 8 is equal to n choose k. What is n+k?
ANSWER: 24
4. BONUS Math Short Answer How many terms does the trinomial expansion of (a+b+c)10 have?
ANSWER: 66
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the equatorial region where the trade winds meet?
ANSWER: ITCZ (also accept: INTERTROPICAL CONVERGENCE ZONE; DOLDRUMS)
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer El nifio and la nina are different phases of what overall cycle?
ANSWER: El-nifio Southern Oscillation
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory are developing methods to detect chromosomal defects in sperm cells. In the human male reproductive system, what cells, next to the seminiferous tubules, are activated by LH?
ANSWER: LEYDIG CELLS
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory are developing methods to detect chromosomal defects in sperm cells. In the human male reproductive system, what tube connects the vas deferens to the testes?
ANSWER: EPIDIDYMIS
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Oseltamivir, used to treat influenza, is a powerful antiviral that inhibits what important molecules on viral coatings?
ANSWER: NEURAMINIDASE
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer AIP in gram positive bacteria is an autoinducer for a signaling pathway that implicates in what effect observed in groups of bacteria such as Aliivibrio fischeri?
ANSWER: QUORUM SENSING
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer How many d electrons are in the permanganate anion?
ANSWER: 0
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer The copper (II) fluoride complex has peaks in its absorbance diagram at two distinct points. This is caused by what molecular effect?
ANSWER: JAHN-TELLER EFFECT (also accept: JAHN TELLER DISTORTION)
9. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Calculate the voltage gap across a capacitor in series with a 9 V battery and 3 Ohm resistor at the moment when the current in the circuit is 2 A.
ANSWER: 3 VOLTS
9. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the period of a series LC circuit with a 3 Henry inductor and 12 Farad capacitor. Give your answer as a decimal with three significant figures.
ANSWER: 37.7
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many distinct positive factors does 200 have?
ANSWER: 12
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the product of the greatest common divisor and least common multiple of 125 and 30?
ANSWER: 3750
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of neutron star has a magnetic field between 109 and 1011 Teslas and emits high energy EM radiation?
ANSWER: MAGNETAR
11. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name for the observation in astronomy of quick lived radio pulses that have been observed from magnetars?
ANSWER: FAST RADIO BURSTS
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are using neutron scattering to study the dynamics of proteins involved in cancer. What is the spin of a neutron?
ANSWER: 1/2
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer Researchers at Oak Ridge National Laboratory are using neutron scattering to study the dynamics of proteins involved in cancer. Answer the following questions about scattering. A) Electron scattering occurs by the exchange of virtual versions of what particles? B) What quantity, used in scattering, is the average distance that a particle travels between two collisions?
ANSWER: PHOTONS, MEAN FREE PATH
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Generation I antipsychotics such as Prochlorperazine are antagonists for the D2 receptors of what neurotransmitter?
ANSWER: DOPAMINE
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer What neurological disorder, believed to be caused by dopamine hyperactivity and glutamate reduction at NMDA receptors, causes hallucinations and delusions?
ANSWER: SCHIZOPHRENIA
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Gilman reagents are made with dialkyl types of what transition metal?
ANSWER: COPPER
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many equivalents of a Grignard reagent can react with an ester?
ANSWER: 2
15. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer The temperature of a gas can be related to its kinetic energy using what fundamental constant?
ANSWER: BOLTZMANN CONSTANT
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer How many degrees of freedom do monatomic ideal gases possess?
ANSWER: 3
16. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many positive integer triplets (x,y,z) with x y and z less than or equal to 8 and have distinct x,y,z?
ANSWER: 336
16. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the maximum value of xyz if x+y+z=30 and x,y,z are positive integers?
ANSWER: 1,000
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What precipitation occurs when supercooled water droplets freeze on the surface of already falling snowflakes outside of a cloud?
ANSWER: GRAUPEL
17. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer When large scale winds circulate around regions of high atmospheric pressure and rotate clockwise in the Northern hemisphere, what is formed?
ANSWER: ANTICYCLONE
18. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Laboratory have invented a new algorithm to quickly find high-entropy alloys. What is the entropy of a system with e10 microstates assuming that kb = 1.38.
ANSWER: 13.8
18. BONUS Energy Short Answer Researchers at Ames National Laboratory have invented a new algorithm to quickly find high-entropy alloys. Answer the following two questions about metals. A) Superconductivity in metals is hypothesized to be caused by the formation of what low temperature features? B) What is the most efficient crystal packing system?
ANSWER: COOPER PAIRS, FCC
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Species with extremely high birth rates are said to be selecting for what factor in population dynamics?
ANSWER: r
19. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the name of the rapid associative learning in which youth learn the characteristics of a stimulus from their parents. This effect is present in nidifugous birds.
ANSWER: IMPRINTING
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the common name for P(Ph)3O?
ANSWER: TRIPHENYLPHOSPHINE OXIDE
20. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Copper II iodide is unstable because it disproportionates to what two compounds?
ANSWER: COPPER (I) IODIDE AND I2
21. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What charged fundamental particle is released in beta minus decay?
ANSWER: ELECTRON
21. BONUS Physics Short Answer In beta minus decay, a neutron is converted to a proton by the emission of a boson as a quark changes flavor. Answer the following question. A) What is the flavor of the quark before and after the transformation respectively? B) What specific fundamental boson is emitted in the quark transformation that quickly decays?
ANSWER: A) down, up B) W- boson (do not accept: W; W+)
22. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the magnitude of 1i+2j+10k?
ANSWER: √105
22. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the cross product of 3i with i+j?
ANSWER: 3k
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Binary star systems exist because two stars are mutually orbiting around what center, a property of their system?
ANSWER: BARYCENTER
23. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The Type 1a “Champagne Supernova” was exceptional because it resulted from a binary system including a massive white dwarf that exceeded what limit?
ANSWER: CHANDRASEKHAR LIMIT
