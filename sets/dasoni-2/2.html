Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What element has the largest atomic radius?
ANSWER: francium
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a unit of power?
W) joule per second
X) electron volt per minute
Y) Newton meter Hertz
Z) kilogram meter per second
ANSWER: Z) kilogram meter per second
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following minerals is not a form of calcium sulfate?
W) gypsum
X) anhydrite
Y) calcite
Z) bassanite
ANSWER: Y) calcite
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following tissues most likely contributes to the formation of tree rings?
W) Primary xylem
X) Secondary xylem
Y) Primary phloem
Z) Secondary phloem
ANSWER: X) Secondary xylem
5. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Lucas, Shayan, and Ethan each pick a single-digit prime number, without replacement. What is the largest possible product of their numbers?
ANSWER: 105
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What equation in electrochemistry relates the EMF of a galvanic cell to the reaction quotient?
ANSWER: Nernst equation
7. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Lucas throws his pillow at an infinitely massive wall. If the collision is perfectly inelastic, what fraction of the pillow's initial kinetic energy is dissipated?
ANSWER: 1 (also accept: all of it, 100%)
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 3 Milankovitch cycles by increasing frequency. 1) Procession 2) Obliquity 3) Eccentricity
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following organisms is an ectoparasite?
W) Plasmodium
X) Tapeworm
Y) Hookworm
Z) Tick
ANSWER: Z) Tick
10. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Bourne has a set of numbers. If he adds 10 to every element in the set, which of the following will not change?
W) mean
X) median
Y) minimum
Z) variance
ANSWER: Z) variance
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements would have the highest fourth ionization energy?
W) Sodium
X) Magnesium
Y) Aluminum
Z) Silicon
ANSWER: Y) Aluminum
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following four particles that satisfy the Pauli exclusion principle 1) Electron 2) Proton 3) Neutron
ANSWER: All
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why a protostar becomes optically visible during the birthline phase?
W) Ignition of nuclear fusion in the core
X) Dissipation of the protostellar disk
Y) Mass ejections due to irregular variability
Z) Clearing of the dust envelope
ANSWER: Z) Clearing of the dust envelope
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Alex is looking at a bacterial population that is in a low-glucose environment. Which protein on the lac operon binds cyclic AMP, transcribing the genes for enzymes that process alternative carbon sources?
ANSWER: Catabolite activator protein (also accept: CAP; CRP)
15. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many complex solutions are there to the equation x4 = i?
ANSWER: 4
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Order the following interatomic forces in increasing strength 1) Hydrogen bonding 2) Dipole-dipole bonding 3) London dispersion
ANSWER: 3 2 1
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Julian is in an elliptical orbit around the sun. Identify all of the following quantities that are conserved for his orbit 1) Momentum 2) Angular momentum 3) Kinetic energy
ANSWER: 2 only
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Represented by lambda in the lambda-CDM model, which constant, first introduced by Albert Einstein, is used by astronomers to model the expansion of the universe?
ANSWER: COSMOLOGICAL CONSTANT
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice According to the Theory of Island Biogeography, which of the following environments would be expected to support the highest equilibrium number of species?
W) A small island, far from the mainland
X) A small island, close to the mainland
Y) A large island, far from the mainland
Z) A large island, close to the mainland
ANSWER: Z) A large island, close to the mainland
20. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is 73*83?
ANSWER: 6059
21. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following metals is not an Aufbau exception?
W) Chromium
X) Copper
Y) Molybdenum
Z) Platinum
ANSWER: Z) Platinum
22. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A ball is launched on a 37 degree inclined plane with an initial velocity of 2 meters per second perpendicular to the plane. Which of the following values, in seconds, is closest to the amount of time it takes for the ball to hit the plane again?
W) 0.2
X) 0.3
Y) 0.4
Z) 0.5
ANSWER: Z) 0.5
23. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Shreyas performs X-ray diffraction on a sample of opal, which has formula SiO2. The X-ray diffraction pattern produced would be most similar to the X-ray diffraction pattern produced by which of the following materials?
W) Quartz
X) Andesite
Y) Olivine
Z) Obsidian
ANSWER: Z) Obsidian
24. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Lucas knocks out gene X in some seeds of Arabidopsis thaliana. He places the seeds in normal growth conditions, but notices that they do not germinate. Which of the following hormones would gene X most likely code for?
W) Gibberellin
X) Auxin
Y) Abscisic acid
Z) Cytokinin
ANSWER: W) Gibberellin
25. BONUS Math Short Answer Julian wants to buy chicken wings. If his chicken wings come in packs of 8 and 9, what is the largest integer number of chicken wings that Julian cannot buy?
ANSWER: 55
26. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the oxidation state of zinc in the tetrachlorozincate anion, which has a formula of [ZnCl4]2-?
ANSWER: +2
27. BONUS Physics Short Answer A two kilogram ball travelling at 5 meters per second to the right collides elastically with a seventeen kilogram ball travelling at 3 meters per second to the left. What is the magnitude of the relative velocity between the two balls after the collision in meters per second?
ANSWER: 8
28. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer When warm, salty water overlies cool, freshwater, double diffusive instability may result. Since heat diffuses faster than salt, what feature is formed from this process?
ANSWER: SALT FINGERS
29. BONUS Biology Short Answer Kaison is on a field trip at the local graveyard and digs up a cadaver to dissect. Specifically, he's curious about the digestive system. Order the following parts of the alimentary canal from first to last. 1) Pyloric sphincter 2) Ileum 3) Jejunum
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
30. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following numbers is the largest?
W) 21/2
X) 31/3
Y) 41/4
Z) 51/5
ANSWER: X) 31/3
31. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following will lead to a single replacement reaction?
W) Sodium in water
X) Baking soda in vinegar
Y) Potassium iodide in lead nitrate
Z) Sulfuric acid in water
ANSWER: W) Sodium in water
32. BONUS Physics Short Answer Shayan puts a 2 microfarad capacitor in series with a 4 microfarad capacitor. In microfarads, what is the equivalent capacitance?
ANSWER: 4/3
33. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes Jupiter’s rings?
W) Long-lived primordial rings composed of material left over from Jupiter’s formation
X) Dense, stable icy rings maintained primarily by frequent particle–particle collisions
Y) Faint rings that are continuously replenished by debris from small moons within Jupiter’s Roche limit
Z) Gaseous rings confined by Jupiter’s magnetic field and sustained against gravitational collapse
ANSWER: Y) Faint rings that are continuously replenished by debris from small moons within Jupiter’s Roche limit
34. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following phyla is diploblastic?
W) Echinodermata
X) Arthropoda
Y) Ctenophora
Z) Porifera
ANSWER: Y) Ctenophora
35. BONUS Math Short Answer Lucas is counting sheep in bed and realizes that if he puts the sheep into groups of 5, there is 1 sheep left over, but if he puts the sheep into groups of 7, there are 3 sheep left over. What is the minimum number of sheep that Lucas could be counting?
ANSWER: 31
36. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many lone pairs of electrons are on the carbon and oxygen atom, respectively, in a molecule of carbon monoxide?
ANSWER: 1 and 1
37. BONUS Physics Short Answer In a series LR circuit, by what factor will the time constant be multiplied if both the inductance and resistance are doubled?
ANSWER: 1
38. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer A telescope has focal length 12 meters and focal ratio f/6. To two significant figures, what is the angular resolution of this telescope in radians at a wavelength of 500 nanometers?
ANSWER: 3 × 10-7
39. BONUS Biology Short Answer When two closely related species of Galapagos finches occur in sympatry, their beak sizes often diverge significantly more than when they exist in allopatry. What is the specific evolutionary term for this phenomenon, where trait differences among similar species are accentuated in regions where they co-occur to minimize interspecific competition?
ANSWER: character displacement
40. BONUS Math Short Answer If a number has 12 digits in hexadecimal, what is the difference between the maximum and minimum number of digits this number has in binary?
ANSWER: 3
