1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What enzyme breaks the hydrogen bonds between nucleobases to unwind the DNA double helix and create the replication fork?
ANSWER: Helicase
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes when the Calvin cycle and oxidative phosphorylation occur in plants, respectively?
W) Primarily during the day, primarily during the day
X) Primarily during the day, continuously through day and night
Y) Continuously through day and night, primarily during the day
Z) Continuously through day and night, continuously through day and night
ANSWER: X) Primarily during the day, continuously through day and night
2. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following numbers cannot be written as the sum of two distinct prime numbers?
W) 39
X) 49
Y) 59
Z) 69
ANSWER: Y) 59
2. BONUS Math Short Answer Alexander is trying to color a 2 by 2 square grid with 4 different colors: red, green, blue, and yellow. If two sections that share a side are not allowed to contain the same color, how many ways can he color this grid?
ANSWER: 84
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer The Born-Haber Cycle calculates lattice energy by summing the individual enthalpy changes for various chemical reactions. This cycle applies what law that states that the total enthalpy change of a reaction is independent of the reaction pathway?
ANSWER: Hess' law
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Carbon monoxide gas is reacted with hydrogen gas to form gaseous methanol in a sealed, rigid reaction vessel. Given that this reaction is exothermic, identify all of the following three changes that would shift equilibrium toward the products 1) Adding inert helium gas 2) Adding hydrogen gas 3) Decreasing the temperature
ANSWER: 2 and 3
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three lava types by increasing typical steepness of the volcanoes they produce 1) Rhyolitic 2) Andesitic 3) Basaltic
ANSWER: 3, 2, 1
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the formation of a fjord?
W) Glacial meltwater runoff erodes the ground
X) Glacial meltwater runoff fills a cirque
Y) Seawater floods a V-shaped valley
Z) Seawater floods a U-shaped valley
ANSWER: Z) Seawater floods a U-shaped valley
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A positively charged insulating rod is brought near, but does not touch, the right side of an isolated conducting metal ball. What is the distribution of charges on the left and right sides of the metal ball, respectively?
W) Neutral, neutral
X) Negative, positive
Y) Positive, negative
Z) Negative, negative
ANSWER: Y) Positive, negative
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer Taking gravitational acceleration as 10 meters per second squared, what is the maximum speed, in meters per second, that a car can drive around a curve of radius 5 meters and coefficient of friction 2?
ANSWER: 10
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Each individual sponge functions as both a male and a female in sexual reproduction, producing sperm and eggs. What term best describes such a system?
ANSWER: Hermaphrodite (also accept: Hermaphroditism)
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three organelles that would be found in the axons of neurons 1) Nucleus 2) Chloroplast 3) Golgi apparatus
ANSWER: None of them
7. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following equations, when graphed, would satisfy neither the vertical line test nor the horizontal line test?
W) x + y = 0
X) x – y = 0
Y) x2 + y2 = 0
Z) x2 – y2 = 0
ANSWER: Z) x2 – y2 = 0
7. BONUS Math Short Answer A random positive integer is chosen between 2 and 100, inclusive. What is the probability it is a perfect power, being a positive integer raised to the power of another positive integer that is not 1?
ANSWER: 4/33
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In a reaction between aqueous silver nitrate and sodium chloride, identify all of the following three ions that will be spectator ions 1) Ag+ 2) NO3- 3) Na+
ANSWER: 2 and 3
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three materials or compounds that can conduct electricity in the solid state 1) Graphene 2) Sodium chloride 3) Molecular nitrogen
ANSWER: 1 only
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Dalton and Maunder minima were two periods in which what temporary solar surface features appeared in amounts much lower than usual?
ANSWER: Sunspots
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why retreating continental glaciers commonly produce braided streams?
W) High meltwater discharge creates deeper channels
X) Low vegetation allows rivers to erode their banks more easily
Y) Large sediment loads from glacial outwash exceed the stream’s transport capacity
Z) Steep valley gradients prevent the formation of meanders
ANSWER: Y) Large sediment loads from glacial outwash exceed the stream’s transport capacity
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice An ideal gas undergoes a cyclic process and returns to its original state. Which of the following quantities must be zero over one complete cycle?
W) Net work done
X) Net heat absorbed
Y) Change in internal energy
Z) Maximum temperature
ANSWER: Y) Change in internal energy

13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Concentrated acids can be safely diluted by slowly adding the acid to water, whereas adding water to acid is highly unsafe. Which of the following best explains this?
W) Concentrated acids cause water to form highly flammable hydrogen gas
X) Concentrated acids dissociate when water is added, thus wasting acid
Y) Concentrated acids react exothermically with water, causing vigorous splattering
Z) Concentrated acids precipitate in the presence of water, contaminating the solution
ANSWER: Y) Concentrated acids react exothermically with water, causing vigorous splattering

ANSWER: 4, 2, 3, 1
17. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following drawn shapes is not correctly matched with its geometric loci?
W) Hyperbola; all points such that the difference between the distance to two foci is constant
X) Parabola; all points such that the distance between it and the focus is the same as it and a line
Y) Ellipse; all points such that the sum of the distance to two foci is constant
