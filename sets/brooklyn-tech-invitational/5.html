1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Red tide is a phenomenon in which certain organisms accumulate in the water, causing it to look red due to the presence of carotenoids and often killing off marine life. Name this group of organisms that we are obligated to include, since it’s brought up in these bio questions again and again.
ANSWER: DINOFLAGELLATES
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice The name “dinoflagellate” comes from the Greek word dinos, which means whirling. Dinoflagellates are known to spin in water due to which of the following characteristics?
W) A flagellum in the groove of its cellulose plates
X) Cilia that encircle its cellulose plates
Y) Uneven distribution of cellulose in its plates
Z) Three flagella that beat rhythmically below the cellulose plates
ANSWER: W) A flagellum in the groove of its cellulose plates
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What are the molecular geometries of Ammonia and Ammonium, respectively?
ANSWER: Trigonal Pyramidal and Tetrahedral (do not accept: Tetrahedral and Trigonal Pyramidal; Trigonal Planar and Tetrahedral)
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following elements would be able to replace Magnesium in MgCl2 during a single replacement reaction?
W) Manganese
X) Iron
Y) Calcium
Z) Copper
ANSWER: Y) Calcium
3. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Unpolarized light strikes a surface at a certain angle and the subsequent reflected light is completely polarized. What is this angle called?
W) Snell’s angle
X) Reflection angle
Y) Critical angle
Z) Brewster’s angle
ANSWER: Z) Brewster’s angle
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following changes that will increase the magnetic field strength within a solenoid 1. Inserting an aluminum rod 2. Increasing the current 3. Adding more turns to the coil
ANSWER: 2 and 3 (do not accept: 1-Aluminum is not ferromagnetic)
4. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following numbers is a Perfect Number?
W) 3
X) 6
Y) 12
Z) 24
ANSWER: X) 6
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Order the following four irrational numbers from least to greatest. 1. Golden ratio 2. √3 3. π/2 4. 1+ln(2)
ANSWER: 3,1,4,2
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What four spectral classes do brown dwarves typically occupy?
ANSWER: M, L, T, Y
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Name all of the following four options that can be used to differentiate brown dwarves from low-mass stars. 1) Lithium 2) Iron rain 3) Methane 4) Deuterium fusion
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3, 4 (also accept: ALL)
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify all of the following structures of a flower that facilitate the fertilization of a megagametophyte within the flower’s ovary. 1. Sepal 2. Style 3. Stigma 4. Filament
ANSWER: 2 AND 3
6. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is a characteristic of a eudicot that differentiates it from a monocot?
W) Eudicot seeds have an endosperm
X) Eudicots do not typically have a taproot system
Y) The eudicot clade includes a variety of food crops
Z) Eudicot pollen grains typically have multiple openings
ANSWER: Z) Eudicot pollen grains typically have multiple openings
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is NOT true?
W) Any foreign substance added to water will cause a freezing point drop
X) Methane has a higher boiling point than ethane
Y) Freezing point depression is a colligative property
Z) A nonvolatile solute raises the boiling point of a solvent
ANSWER: X) Methane has a higher boiling point than ethane
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Name the TWO organic functional groups that contain TWO oxygen atoms.
ANSWER: Organic Acid and Ester (also accept: carboxylic acid and ester)
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Karma Karma Karma Karma Karma Chameleon thankfully has mass due to the Higgs field. What phenomenon, besides a high energy event, could theoretically cause the Higgs field to transition from a false vacuum to a true vacuum, leading to vacuum decay?
ANSWER: Quantum Tunneling (also accept: QUANTUM MECHANICAL TUNNELING; BUBBLE NUCLEATION; TUNNELING; BARRIER PENETRATION)
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer Pikachu, with a mass of 6kg, stands on the left end of a uniform beam of negligible mass that is balanced on a fulcrum located 1m from this end. Psyduck stands 2 meters to the right of the fulcrum. Rounding your answer to the nearest tenth, how much must Psyduck weigh, in Newtons, for the beam to have a zero net torque, assuming g = 9.81m/s2?
ANSWER: 29.4 Newtons
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Name all of the following that can logically follow from “A implies B and C” 1) A and not B implies C 2) not B or not C implies not A 3) A implies B or C 4) not B implies not A
ANSWER: 2 and 3
9. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the ratio of the area of a regular hexagon inscribed in a circle to the area of a regular hexagon circumscribed about the same circle?
ANSWER: 3:4
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT typically considered to account for dark matter?
W) Phonons
X) Axions
Y) Primordial Black Holes
Z) Weakly Interacting Massive Particles
ANSWER: W) Phonons
10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following would happen if the mass-energy density of the universe equaled the critical density?
W) The universe would expand at a decreasing rate
X) The universe would expand at an increasing rate
Y) The universe would eventually collapse into a singularity
Z) The universe would expand linearly
ANSWER: W) The universe would expand at a decreasing rate
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of glial cells is known for maintaining the blood-brain barrier?
W) Astrocytes
X) Ependymal cells
Y) Oligodendrocytes
Z) Microglia
ANSWER: W) Astrocytes
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Yanzi is imaging an afferent neuron in the paw of a mouse. She sees a separate cell myelinating this neuron and wonders what type of cell it could be. What type of cell could Yanzi be looking at?
ANSWER: SCHWANN CELL (also accept: NEUROLEMMOCYTE)
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Carbon-14 undergoes which mode of nuclear decay?
ANSWER: Beta Decay (also accept: BETA; BETA MINUS)
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following four types of chemical reactions that a redox reaction could be. 1. Synthesis 2. Decomposition 3. Double Replacement 4. Single Replacement
ANSWER: 1, 2, and 4 (Synthesis, Decomposition, and Single Replacement)
13. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is the most DIRECT statement of Bernoulli’s principle?
W) Wings generate lift due to higher velocity air over the top of the wing
X) Air tends to follow the curved surface that it flows past
Y) Total energy of air will remain constant along a streamline
Z) Pressure is transmitted equally throughout a fluid
ANSWER: Y) Total energy of air will remain constant along a streamline
13. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following devices is primarily designed to confine hot plasma using magnetic fields for the purpose of nuclear fusion?
W) Cyclotron
X) Cloud chamber
Y) Fission reactor
Z) Tokamak
ANSWER: Z) Tokamak
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Compute the last two digits of 777
ANSWER: 43
14. BONUS Math Short Answer If sin(x) + cos(x) = 1/2, compute sin(2x).
ANSWER: -3/4

W) Parallel to the fold axis
X) Perpendicular to the dip
Y) Along the strike-slip fault
Z) Along the dip-slip fault
ANSWER: X) Perpendicular to the dip
15. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following types of folds contains overturned strata with a low-angle axial plane?
W) Slump
X) Ptygmatic
Y) Chevron
Z) Recumbent
ANSWER: Z) Recumbent
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, identify ALL of the following 4 cells that typically act as antigen-presenting cells. 1) Microglia 2) Erythrocytes 3) Natural killer cells 4) Langerhans cells
ANSWER: 1 and 4
16. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following options correctly identifies the location and immune cell type of Langerhans cells, respectively?
W) Epidermis; macrophage
X) Epidermis; dendritic cell
Y) Pancreas; macrophage
Z) Pancreas; dendritic cell
ANSWER: X) Epidermis; dendritic cell
17. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the 4 following substances that have different molecular and empirical formulas. 1. Water 2. Ammonia 3. Benzene 4. Carbon Dioxide
ANSWER: 3 ONLY
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A 5.0 L sample of gas at STP is compressed to 2.0 L and 3 atm. What is the new temperature of the gas rounded to the nearest degree Kelvin?
ANSWER: 328 K
18. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A converging lens with a focal length of 10 cm is used to form an image of an object placed 15 cm from the lens. Which of the following correctly describes the image formed?
W) The image is virtual, upright, and larger than the object
X) The image is real, inverted, and smaller than the object
Y) The image is virtual, upright, and smaller than the object
Z) The image is real, inverted, and larger than the object
ANSWER: Z) The image is real, inverted, and larger than the object
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer A car of mass 1200kg moves at 25 meters per second on a flat road and enters a circular curve of radius 50 meters. The coefficient of static friction between the tires and the road is 0.4 What is the minimum frictional force required to prevent the car from sliding?
ANSWER: 15,000 Newtons

W) 3
X) 7
Y) 1

ANSWER: 0.85 meters
22. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice A penguin is thrown vertically upward. Consider two equal intervals of DISPLACEMENT: one from position 0 to position 1, and one from position 1 to position 2, which is higher up. During which interval is the change in velocity the greatest?
W) The change in velocity during both intervals is the same
X) From position 0 to position 1
Y) From position 1 to position 2
Z) Depends on the initial velocity
ANSWER: Y) From position 1 to position 2

ANSWER: 7
