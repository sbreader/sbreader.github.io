1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice When an airplane's ailerons move in opposite directions they usually cause a plane to:
W) pitch
X) yaw
Y) roll
Z) stall
ANSWER: Y) roll
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Calcium, strontium, and barium are commonly called what kind of elements?
W) halides
X) inert
Y) alkali metals
Z) alkaline earth
ANSWER: Z) alkaline earth
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice In which of the geological time periods, did dinosaurs first appear:
W) Jurassic
X) Triassic
Y) Cretaceous
Z) Tertiary
ANSWER: X) Triassic
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer In what layer of the Earth's atmosphere does most of our weather occur?
ANSWER: TROPOSPHERE
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If 2 sides of a quadrilateral are parallel and the other sides are equal but NOT parallel, what is the quadrilateral known as?
ANSWER: TRAPEZOID
3. BONUS Math Short Answer Find the area of a triangle whose base is 8 and whose height is 6:
ANSWER: 24
4. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice A series of amino acids joined together will make a:
W) polysaccharide
X) DNA
Y) sugar
Z) protein
ANSWER: Z) protein
4. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice When a nerve message reaches the end of an axon it sends a message or impulse across the space between the nerves to continue its message to the next nerve by a substance best known as:
W) synaptic nodes
X) neuromotor stimluli
Y) neurotransmitters
Z) intersynaptic dosimeters
ANSWER: Y) neurotransmitters
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following moves in the form of a longitudinal compression wave:
W) ocean wave
X) sound wave
Y) light wave
Z) electromagnetic wave
ANSWER: X) sound wave

ANSWER: 15 OHMS
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Ergot, Amanita muscaris, Penicillium and smut are all organisms that belong to which one of the five classical Kingdoms of life?
ANSWER: FUNGI
6. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice When the moon appears through the night as a thin crescent, the suns light:
W) is being blocked by the earth
X) still illuminates about half the surface of the moon
Y) is only hitting the crescent region
Z) only shines during the day
ANSWER: X) still illuminates about half the surface of the moon
7. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice If a biker traveled at an average speed of 3 meters/second for 100 seconds, the length of the biker's path was about:
W) 33 meters
X) 300 meters
Y) 100 meters
Z) 1 kilometer
ANSWER: X) 300 meters
7. BONUS Math Short Answer If a perfect number is an integer equal to the sum of all its divisors, except the number itself, what is the lowest perfect number?
ANSWER: 6
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Most animal eukaryotic cells have:
W) a cell wall, nucleus, and cytoplasm
X) a plasma membrane, nucleus, and cytoplasm
Y) a cell wall, nucleus, and nucleolus
Z) a cell wall, nucleus, and mitochondrion
ANSWER: X) a plasma membrane, nucleus, and cytoplasm

ANSWER: NATURAL SELECTION

ANSWER: alpha, beta, gamma, delta, epsilon
9. BONUS Energy Short Answer Rounded to the nearest whole number, what is the temperature in Fahrenheit of a substance at -40°C?
ANSWER: -40
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Identify the quotient, dividend, and divisor for 15 ÷ 3:
ANSWER: QUOTIENT = 5; DIVIDEND = 15; DIVISOR = 3

ANSWER: x2 + 14 x + 49

W) above 20,000 kilohertz
X) below 20,000 kilohertz
