1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Monish is studying malaria, and finds that the causative agent of the disease, P. falciparum, is part of what group of parasitic organisms characterized by the presence of an apicoplast?
ANSWER: apicomplexans
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Birds and mice are both examples of endotherms, but their common ancestor, an early amniote, is an ectotherm. Based on the description, which of the following best characterizes endothermy in amniotes?
W) Pleisomorphy
X) Autapomorphy
Y) Synapomorphy
Z) Homoplasy
ANSWER: Z) Homoplasy
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Arjun is horsing around in the chemistry lab and accidentally spills some phenolphthalein in a beaker with a solution in it. The solution in the beaker immediately turns pink. Which of the following salts could have been in the solution?
W) NaF
X) NaCl
Y) KBr
Z) KI
ANSWER: W) NaF
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is the SI unit for activity when referring to a radioactive species?
W) Sievert
X) Reontgen
Y) Becquerel
Z) Gray
ANSWER: Y) Becquerel
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Streams can transport sediments in a variety of ways. Which of the following types of sediment load is primarily transported by saltation?
W) Dissolved load
X) Suspended load
Y) Bed load
Z) Channel load
ANSWER: Y) Bed load
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Calculate the discharge of a perfectly semicircular stream, in cubic meters per second, to the nearest ten, at a given point where the flow velocity is 7 m/s, and the depth of the channel is 2 meters?
ANSWER: 40
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many of the regular polygons can tile the plane in a non-intersecting tiling?
ANSWER: 3
4. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the sum of the first 2025 terms of the arithmetic sequence with first three terms-2025, −2023, −2021?
ANSWER: 2025
5. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Harshtha is listening to Taylor Swift on a spaceship traveling at 0.6 c. If Harshtha spends 4 minutes listening to the song in her frame, how long does she listen to the song for someone at rest?
ANSWER: 5 minutes
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer An object of mass 1 kg moves at 5 m/s and collides elastically with a 10,000 kg object. What is the speed of the 10,000 kg object after the collision to one significant figure?
ANSWER: 10-3 (also accept: 0.001 m/s)
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three substances that are secreted by parietal cells in the stomach. 1) HCl 2) Pepsinogen 3) Intrinsic Factor
ANSWER: 1,3
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer After performing an experiment on the limb bud of a chick embryo, Sanjay discovers that the limb has several abnormalities. The first abnormality is that it is much shorter than a normal limb, and the second is that the digits of the limb display a mirror image pattern where they have two ends which resemble the posterior end of a normal limb. Identify all of the following three procedures did Sanjay likely perform in his experiment? 1) destroyed the limbs bud’s apical ectodermal ridge 2) applied recombinant sonic hedgehog protein to both sides of the limb bud 3) destroyed the limb bud’s zone of polarizing activity
ANSWER: 1,2
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice When John Dalton was looking for evidence to convince him of the existence of atoms, he noted that elements like carbon and oxygen always combined together in small whole number ratios to form compounds. What law of stoichiometry does this illustrate?
W) Law of conservation of mass
X) Law of conservation of energy
Y) Law of definite proportions
Z) Law of multiple proportions
ANSWER: Z) Law of multiple proportions
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Carbon tetrachloride and hydrochloric acid are produced from the reaction between methane and chlorine gas. If the rate of formation of carbon tetrachloride is 0.80 M/min, then in M/min, what is the rate of disappearance of methane?
ANSWER: 0.80 M/min
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Praneel looks outside as an earthquake is happening, and notices that the ground is rippling like water. What type of earthquake wave is he observing?
ANSWER: Rayleigh wave
8. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following three stages of a coral reef in chronological order 1) Atoll 2) Barrier 3) Fringing
ANSWER: 3,2,1
9. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice For which of the following values of N does the last two digits of N2 equal the last two digits of N?
W) 111
X) 120
Y) 125
Z) 132
ANSWER: Y) 125
9. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the minimum value of the function (x2)/2 + 1/x over all positive real values of x?
ANSWER: 3/2.
10. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A tube closed on both ends has a length of 5 meters. What is the ratio of the frequency of the 2nd harmonic to the frequency of the 3rd harmonic?
ANSWER: 2/3
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer A disk has moment of inertia I and radius R. Keeping the density constant, what is the new moment of inertia if the disk has a radius of 2R?
ANSWER: 16 I
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What term refers to cell signalling pathways in which cells interact through direct contact, and is especially important for cell motility and embryonic development?
ANSWER: Juxtacrine Signalling
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer What term refers to an enzyme containing all components needed for active functioning, including an apoenzyme and its cofactors?
ANSWER: Holoenzyme (do not accept: Conjugated Enzyme)
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice For a given reaction, the line formed from graphing ln(Keq) vs 1/T is linear with positive slope. Which of the following statements must be true regarding this reaction?
W) ΔH is less than 0
X) ΔS is less than 0
Y) ΔH is greater than 0
Z) ΔS is greater than 0
ANSWER: W) ΔH is less than 0
12. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Reactions 1 and 2 have the same reaction rate at 100 Kelvin. However, Reaction 1 has a much higher activation energy than Reaction 2. Which of the following statements best compares the rates of Reaction 1 to Reaction 2 at 50 and 150 Kelvin respectively?
W) Slower, Slower
X) Slower, Faster
Y) Faster, Slower
Z) Faster, Faster
ANSWER: X) Slower, Faster
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following 4 options that contribute substantially to Mercury’s atmosphere 1) The Sun sublimates icy material off the surface of Mercury which then forms its atmosphere. 2) Impact vaporization from meteoroids produces transient atmospheric gases. 3) Mercury’s atmosphere is constantly replenished by outgassing from its interior. 4) Solar wind sputtering releases atoms from Mercury’s surface into the exosphere.

ANSWER: 16
15. BONUS Physics Short Answer A table is pushed against a wall. Heidi attaches a rope with a mass of 1 kg and length of 4 meters to the wall with Gorilla Glue and strings it over a pulley. Then, she attaches a 2 kg block to the rope. Assuming the system is still in equilibrium, what is the speed of a transverse wave through the rope to the nearest integer? Use g=10 m/s2.
ANSWER: 9 m/s
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In puppies, brown fur color (capital B) is dominant over blonde fur color (lowercase b), a medium length tail (capital M) is dominant over a short tail (lowercase m), and a solid coat (capitol S) is dominant over a spotted coat (lowercase s). If an individual with genotype BbMmSs is bred with an individual with genotype BBmmSs, what is the probability that one of its offspring will have brown fur, a short tail, and a spotted coat?
ANSWER: 1/8
