Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the type of cells, found between the seminiferous tubules, which produce testosterone and other androgens.
ANSWER: LEYDIG CELLS
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice After the ras oncogene is activated and tumor-suppressor gene DCC is deactivated in a human colon, which of the following types of growths is most likely to form?
W) Polyp
X) Adenoma
Y) Carcinoma
Z) Oncocytoma
ANSWER: X) Adenoma
3. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By number, state which of the following four statements is or are TRUE regarding genomes. 1: Gene density is higher in humans than in bacteria and archaea. 2: Eukaryotic genomes tend to be larger than prokaryotic genomes. 3: Nearly all human genes contain multiple exons. 4: Humans have 10,000 times as much noncoding DNA as bacteria.
ANSWER: 2, 3, and 4 (also accept: All but 1)
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Classify the following four situations as intrasexual selection or as intersexual selection. 1: A single male lion engages in ritualized displays that discourage would-be competitors. 2: A female peacock will choose a male with brighter plumage. 3: Lizards with handsomely colored heads have a higher chance of reproduction. 4: A pronghorn male will defend a fixed territory from other males, allowing only females.
ANSWER: INTRA, INTER, INTER, INTRA
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In the biosynthetic pathway for folic acid, sulfanilamide can attach to the active site of an enzyme, thereby disallowing para-aminobenzoic acid from binding to that site. What is the name for this kind of regulation?
W) Allosteric inhibition
X) Competitive inhibition
Y) Allosteric activation
Z) Mixed inhibition
ANSWER: X) Competitive inhibition
6. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The discovery of the five-eyed species Opabinia regalis made the largest single contribution to modern interest in this historic event. This event supports the theory of punctuated equilibrium, and most of the species that originated due to this event were coelomates like arthropods and annelids. For four points, name this evolutionary event that occurred between 535 and 525 million years ago.
ANSWER: CAMBRIAN EXPLOSION
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Within leukocytes, what is the name of the type of receptor which recognizes fragments of molecules characteristic of a set of pathogens?
ANSWER: TOLL-LIKE RECEPTOR
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Out of the following four animals, name the one that is NOT considered to be a lepidosaur.
W) Crocodiles
X) Lizards
Y) Snakes
Z) Tuatara
ANSWER: W) Crocodiles
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is false regarding plant responses to signals?
W) Flowering and other photoperiodic effects are controlled by day length.
X) Far-red light flashes can counter the effects of red light flashes.
Y) Florigen is the hypothetical signalling molecule for flowering and has not yet been discovered.
Z) If grafted together in short-day conditions, both long-day and short-day plants will flower.
ANSWER: W) Flowering and other photoperiodic effects are controlled by day length.
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Applications of this technology employ some DNA polymerase, such as the Taq polymerase. This biochemical technology was developed by Kary Mullis in 1983, and was awarded the Nobel Prize in Chemistry for it in 1993. It relies on thermal cycling of DNA. For four points, name this type of biochemical technology used to amplify DNA across several orders of magnitude.
ANSWER: POLYMERASE CHAIN REACTION (PCR)
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following classes of the phylum Cnidaria contains both marine and freshwater organisms, both of which exhibit polyp and medusa stages, and includes animals such as Portuguese man-of-wars, hydras, and some corals?
W) Anthozoa
X) Cubozoa
Y) Scyphozoa
Z) Hydrozoa
ANSWER: Z) Hydrozoa
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the electroreceptor organs found primarily in the cartilaginous fishes and which provide these fish with a sixth sense capable of detecting electromagnetic fields as well as temperature gradients.
ANSWER: AMPULLAE OF LORENZINI
13. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is FALSE regarding cellular respiration?
W) Obligate anaerobes cannot survive in the presence of oxygen.
X) Facultative anaerobes can undergo both respiration and fermentation.
Y) Glycosylation is the metabolism of fatty acids into two-carbon fragments.
Z) Alcohol and lactic acid fermentation both begin with pyruvate.
ANSWER: Y) Glycosylation is the metabolism of fatty acids into two-carbon fragments.
14. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, which of the following statements is or are TRUE regarding lakes? 1: Eutrophic lakes are nutrient-poor and oxygen-rich. 2: Oligotrophic lakes are nutrient-rich and oxygen-poor. 3: Rooted plants in lakes can only survive in the littoral zone. 4: The limnetic zone is inhabited by primarily phytoplankton and cyanobacteria.
ANSWER: 3 and 4
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following three events regarding G [protein-coupled receptors chronologically. 1: Receptor binds to inactive G protein, thereby activating it. 2: GTP on G protein hydrolyzed to GDP, thereby inactivating it. 3: G protein diffuses towards an enzyme and binds to it, releasing a cellular response.
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
16. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the adaptation, which literally means "life without water," that is employed by animals such as tardigrades in which the animal enters a dormant state in which their uptake of water is zero.
ANSWER: ANHYDROBIOSIS
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT considered one of the five largest mass extinction events in evolutionary history?
W) Ordovician-Silurian
X) Late Devonian
Y) Permian-Triassic
Z) Jurassic-Cretaceous
ANSWER: Z) Jurassic-Cretaceous
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The paternal type of this type of inheritance is expressed in the mouse IGF-2 gene, and the maternal type in the H19 gene. Defects in this gene may lead to human disorders like Angelman syndrome or Prader-Willi syndrome. For four points, what is the name of the phenomenon in which expression of an allele in offspring depends on whether the allele is inherited from the male or female parent?
ANSWER: GENOMIC IMPRINTING
19. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the name of the site or sites of gas exchange in bird lungs that function in the stead of alveoli in order to create a continuous passage of air through the system?
W) Parabronchi
X) Pleural cavity
Y) Vital cavity
Z) Diaphragm
ANSWER: W) Parabronchi
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Name the geological period in which diverse algae and soft-bodied invertebrate animals begin to appear.
W) Tonian
X) Cryogenian
Y) Ediacaran
Z) Cambrian
ANSWER: Y) Ediacaran
21. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer By name or number, which of the following is or are NOT conditions set by the Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium? 1: Genotype frequencies steady 2: No gene flow 3: No natural selection 4: Mutations are present
ANSWER: 1 and 4
22. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the signaling molecule that moves from the infection site on a plant to elicit systemic acquired resistance, a long-lasting systemic response that primes the plant for resisting a broad spectrum of pathogens.
ANSWER: METHYL SALICYLATE
23. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In what type of terrestrial biome would shrubs, small trees, deer, goats, and small mammals living in rainy winters and long, dry summers most likely occur?
W) Savanna
X) Chaparral
Y) Temperate broadleaf forest
Z) Temperate grassland
ANSWER: X) Chaparral
24. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the most likely type of plant given the fact that its sporophyte generation is dominant and its gametophyte generation is generally microscopic?
W) Moss
X) Fern
Y) Liverwort
Z) Pine
ANSWER: Z) Pine
25. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Name the protective bony flap found in aquatic osteichthyans that covers and protects the gills.
ANSWER: OPERCULUM
