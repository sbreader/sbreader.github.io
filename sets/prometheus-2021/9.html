Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer In reinforcement learning, an agent acts to maximize which property over its operation?
ANSWER: Expected cumulative reward
1. BONUS Energy Short Answer In some reinforcement learning problems, a system cannot fully predict which decisions will maximize expected cumulative reward. What is this property of problems called?
ANSWER: Partial observability
2. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Suppose the numbers x! and y! when written in base 13 both have 126 trailing zeroes. What is the greatest possible value of y − x?
ANSWER: 12
2. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the limit as x approaches 0 of (sin x − x)/x3?
ANSWER: −1/6
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three nuclei that are NMR active 1) Carbon-14 2) Nitrogen-14 3) Phosphorus-35.
ANSWER: 2 and 3
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the ratio of the number of rotational degrees of freedom to the number of vibrational degrees of freedom present in methane?
ANSWER: 1/3 (also accept: 1:3)
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In a large thunderstorm cloud, a net charge is created as rising snow collides with what form of precipitation, which is created when supercooled water freezes onto an ice crystal?
ANSWER: Graupel (do not accept: Hail)
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three seas that are located on a continental shelf 1) Sea of Japan 2) North Sea 3) Caspian Sea.
ANSWER: 2 only
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three elements whose deficiency in plants causes chlorosis 1) Iron 2) Magnesium 3) Nitrogen.
ANSWER: All
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer Bacterial RNA polymerase is a holoenzyme in its active form. The polymerase core associates with what additional protein that enables promoter recognition?
ANSWER: Sigma factor
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer By what factor is the resonant frequency of an RLC circuit multiplied if the resistance is multiplied by 3, the inductor has 4 times the turn density, and the capacitor has double the initial dielectric constant?
ANSWER: √2/8
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer Identify of the following three factors that would affect the terminal velocity of a ball thrown off of a plane 1) Temperature of the air 2) Mass of the ball 3) Density of air.
ANSWER: All
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer The phrase communicable disease originates from the use of what public health process that involves identifying persons exposed to a disease?
ANSWER: Contact tracing (do not accept: Quarantine)
7. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of these is NOT considered major evidence that the 1889 Russian Flu was caused by a zoonotic coronavirus?
W) Long-term neurological symptoms
X) A common cold coronavirus diverged from a bovine coronavirus around 1890
Y) Symptoms included loss of taste and smell
Z) It was less severe than typical influenza pandemics
ANSWER: Z) It was less severe than typical influenza pandemics
8. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Let x be the least real number greater than 1 such that cos(x) = cos(x2), where the arguments are in degrees. What is x rounded to the nearest integer?
W) 9
X) 14
Y) 18
Z) 20
ANSWER: Y) 18
8. BONUS Math Multiple Choice The graph with the equation −x2/4 − y2 = z − 3 is an example of what three-dimensional shape?
W) Cone
X) Ellipsoid
Y) Hyperboloid
Z) Elliptic paraboloid
ANSWER: Z) Elliptic paraboloid
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Delta and lambda isomerism occurs in molecules with what geometry?
ANSWER: Octahedral
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What are diastereomers that differ only at one stereocenter known as?
ANSWER: Epimers
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following components of the interstellar medium typically has the lowest temperature?
W) Molecular clouds
X) Intercloud medium
Y) Coronal gas
Z) H II regions
ANSWER: W) Molecular clouds
10. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of meteorites that are considered stony 1) Chondrites 2) Achondrites 3) Mesosiderites.
ANSWER: 1 and 2
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer During the summer, one may notice a bluish haze that hovers over mountains. This gas is actually emitted by plants to cope with heat by stabilizing photosynthetic membranes within the plant’s cells. What is this compound, which is also the building block of terpenes?
ANSWER: Isoprene
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Using the Frank-Starling law, identify all of the following three factors that would decrease with an increase in end diastolic volume 1) Stroke volume 2) Strength of ventricular contraction 3) Sarcomere length.
ANSWER: None
12. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What is the name of the effect that causes objects orbiting stars to lose their angular momentum due to the star’s radiation pressure?
W) Penrose process
X) Poynting -Robertson effect
Y) Thomson effect
Z) Cherenkov effect
ANSWER: X) Poynting -Robertson effect
12. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is not true about the Poynting-Robertson drag?
W) It primarily affects small particles
X) It causes particles to spiral towards the sun
Y) Its magnitude is proportional to the square of the particle’s velocity
Z) Its magnitude increases relative to gravity as the particle approaches the star
ANSWER: Y) Its magnitude is proportional to the square of the particle’s velocity
13. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What term describes an optimization algorithm optimized for a particular purpose by another optimization algorithm?
ANSWER: Mesa optimizer
13. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What term classifies the state where a mesa-optimizer’s objective is aligned with the base objective?
ANSWER: Inner alignment
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What principle explains the phenomenon where 80 percent of outcomes often come from 20 percent of related inputs?
ANSWER: Pareto Principle
14. BONUS Math Short Answer If f(x) = ex/x2, what is f′(x)?
ANSWER: ex (x − 2)/x3 (also accept: (xex − 2ex)/x3)
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What functional group is formed when a phenyl grignard reagent reacts with acetalydehyde?
W) Ketone
X) Primary alcohol
Y) Secondary alcohol
Z) Tertiary alcohol
ANSWER: Y) Secondary alcohol
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In the Tollens’ test of glucose, what species acts as the oxidizing agent?
ANSWER: Ag + (do not accept: Silver)
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following lakes would be least likely to form in a polar region?
W) Solution
X) Endorheic
Y) Pluvial
Z) Amictic
ANSWER: W) Solution
16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following metamorphic facies would most likely form in a Wadati-Benioff zone?
W) Amphibolite
X) Eclogite
Y) Granulite
Z) Sanidinite
ANSWER: X) Eclogite
17. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In Photosystem II, a water molecule in converted to oxygen through a cycle of 5 oxidation states of what transition metal?
ANSWER: Manganese
17. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which class of enzymes hydrolyze cell membrane phospholipids to form arachidonic acid, which act as a precursor to prostaglandins as a part of the inflammatory response?
ANSWER: Phospholipase A2
18. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is closest to the molar heat capacity of aluminum at room temperature, in joules per mole kelvin?
W) 2
X) 5
Y) 10
Z) 25
ANSWER: Z) 25
18. BONUS Physics Short Answer The thermodynamic beta of a system is defined as β = 1/kT where k is Boltzmann’s constant. In terms of SI base units, what are the units of the thermodynamic beta?
ANSWER: kg-1 m-2 s2
19. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Which type of electric generator relies on the inverse of the Peltier effect to generate electricity?
ANSWER: Thermoelectric generator (also accept: Seebeck effect generator)
19. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Thermoelectric generators have been suggested to be integrated with photovoltaic technology to take advantage of what advantage of thermoelectric generators?
W) Increased efficiency from infrared spectra
X) Increased compatibility with combined infrastructure
Y) Replacing electricity storage with thermal storage
Z) Greater scalability than steam generators
ANSWER: W) Increased efficiency from infrared spectra
20. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice What are the polar coordinates of the point (8, 15), with the angle measure to the nearest degree?
W) (17, 28)
X) (17, 37)
Y) (17, 53)
Z) (17, 62)
ANSWER: Z) (17, 62)
20. BONUS Math Short Answer In a best-of-seven series, Team A has home field for games 1, 2, 6, and 7 and Team B has home field for games 3, 4, and 5. The probability that Team A wins a home game is 0.6 and the probability that Team A wins an away game is 0.5. What is the probability that either team wins all of the first four games?
ANSWER: 0.13 (also accept: 13%)
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer When chemists use boron trichloride as a Lewis acid in chemical synthesis, the sample must be water-free. This is because boron trichloride will react with water to yield what boron-containing compound?
ANSWER: B(OH)3 (also accept: Boric acid; H3BO3)
21. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In sugars, the substituent on carbon 1 typically prefers an axial rather than an equatorial position. What is the name of the effect that dictates this?
ANSWER: Anomeric effect
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds has a spectral line whose peak is at the lowest temperature?
W) Hydrogen
X) Titanium oxide
Y) Ionized helium
Z) Ionized iron
ANSWER: X) Titanium oxide
22. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three stellar fusion processes in which the rate of fusion is inversely proportional to the star’s density 1) Proton-proton chain 2) CNO cycle 3) Triple alpha process.
ANSWER: None
23. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Pacemaker potentials in the heart are generated by HCN channels, which rhythmically depolarize pacemaker cells to generate a steady heart rate. In order to modulate their activity, they bind what signaling molecule that increases the likelihood of the channel opening?
ANSWER: Cyclic AMP (also accept: cAMP; cGMP; cCMP)
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer When a woody plant is injured, parenchymal cells in the heartwood produce outgrowth that partially block xylem vessels. What is the term for this process?
ANSWER: Tylosis

W) Beryllium
X) Oxygen

ANSWER: X) Oxygen
24. BONUS Physics Short Answer At higher temperatures, Einstein’s relation for heat capacity of solids approaches a constant value in accordance with which heat capacity relation?
ANSWER: Dulong -Petit Law
