1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Death of dopaminergenic neurons in the substantia nigra would result in what neurological condition?
W) Parkinson’s Disease
X) Alzheimer’s Disease
Y) Epilepsy
Z) Huntingon’s Disease
ANSWER: W) Parkinson’s Disease
1. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following types of plant cells are dead at maturity and provide structural support to the plant?
W) Parenchyma
X) Collenchyma
Y) Sclerenchyma
Z) Bundle sheath cells

2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the oxidation number of chromium in the compound K2Cr2O7?
ANSWER: +6 (also accept: 6) (do not accept: -6)
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following statements about polyatomic ions that are true 1) The sum of the oxidation numbers of each atom is equal to the overall charge 2) The sum of the formal charge of each atom is equal to the overall charge 3) In iodite, the oxidation state of iodine is +3
ANSWER: All
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A byte is the binary representation of any number from 0 to 255. What is the average value of the product of the digits of a byte?
ANSWER: 1/256
