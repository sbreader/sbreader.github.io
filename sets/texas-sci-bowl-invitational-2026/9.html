1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following properties of an island would make it most resistant to inbreeding depression and least likely for gene flow to occur?
W) Small area, far from mainland
X) Small area, close to the mainland
Y) Large area, far from mainland
Z) Large area, close to the mainland
ANSWER: Y) Large area, far from mainland
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer Some microbes, such as Trypansoma brucei, possess a distinctive structure within their mitochondria containing catenated rings of DNA compacted into maxicircles and minicircles. What is the name for the structure that contains this DNA?
ANSWER: Kinetoplast
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Order the following 3 molecules by increasing number of hydrogen donors. 1) Ammonia 2) Water 3) Hydrogen fluoride
ANSWER: 3,2,1
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A reaction has equilibrium constant, K = 0.01 at 298 Kelvin and K = 10 at 500 Kelvin. Which of the following state functions have signs that can be definitively determined at 400 Kelvin? 1) Delta G Standard 2) Delta H Standard 3) Delta S Standard
ANSWER: 2, 3
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If 25!/(23! + x!) = 24, what is the value of x?
ANSWER: 24
3. BONUS Math Short Answer A gelato shop has 2-scoop, 3-scoop, and 4-scoop cups. A cup can have multiple of the same flavor, and the order of the scoops doesn’t matter. How many unique cups exist if there are 6 total flavors?
ANSWER: 203
4. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice In an oblique, perfectly elastic collision between two identical masses where one is initially at rest, what is the angle between their velocity vectors after the collision?
W) 0°
X) 60°
Y) 90°
Z) 180°
ANSWER: Y) 90°
4. BONUS Physics Short Answer A flywheel with moment of inertia I = 0.50 kg · m2 rotates at 2.0 rev/s. A constant opposing torque of magnitude 1.0 N · m is applied until it stops. To two significant figures, how many revolutions does it make before stopping?

20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following best explains why carbon monoxide is an effective tracer of molecular hydrogen despite being far less abundant?
