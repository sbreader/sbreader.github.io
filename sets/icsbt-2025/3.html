1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which class of immunoglobulin is primarily involved in mediating allergic reactions?
W) IgG
X) IgA
Y) IgM
Z) IgE
ANSWER: Z) IgE
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer What plant hormone promotes shoot growth from a plant callus?
ANSWER: Cytokinin (also accept: s)
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of supernovae, also known as standard candles, are distinguishable from other supernovae by the presence of silicon in their spectral lines?
ANSWER: Type 1a
2. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Order the following 4 types of ice in terms of increasing air percentage. 1) Glacial Ice 2) Snow 3) Firn 4) Neve
ANSWER: 1,3,4,2
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the minimum number of colors required to color any planar graph?
ANSWER: 4
3. BONUS Math Short Answer A circle is inscribed in an equilateral triangle with side length 2. An equilateral triangle is inscribed in this circle. What is the area of the smaller equilateral triangle?
ANSWER: √3/4
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer In a buffer solution, the concentration of fluoride anions is 1 molar, while the concentration of hydrofluoric acid is 0.1 molar. If the pKa of hydrofluoric acid is 3.17, what is the pOH of the buffer to two significant figures?
ANSWER: 9.8
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Some chemists have theorized that cesium, rather than francium, is the element with the lowest ionization energy. This could be due to what phenomenon?
W) Lanthanide contraction
X) D orbital splitting
Y) Relativistic effects
Z) Inert pair effect
ANSWER: Y) Relativistic effects
5. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer K-Means is an example of what type of machine learning model that learns patterns from data without being given explicit instruction or labels?
ANSWER: Unsupervised
5. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice You are working on a model with a dataset that has a small number of rows. Which of the following training patterns would be best for this?
W) Hold-out cross-validation
X) k-fold cross-validation
Y) 80-20 train-test split
Z) 80-10-10 train-test-validation split
ANSWER: X) k-fold cross-validation
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer If a cyclic, planar molecule has 18 pi electrons, what rule states that it is considered aromatic?
ANSWER: Huckel’s Rule
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In the reaction between sulfuric acid and 3 liters of water to form hydrogen sulfate and hydronium, there are 2 mols of sulfuric acid, 3 mols of hydrogen sulfate, and 4 mols of hydronium in equilibrium. What is the concentration equilibrium constant for this reaction?
ANSWER: 0.5
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name given to small fragments of airborne molten material that solidify into tear-like droplets of glass?
ANSWER: Pele’s Tears
7. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a rift system that started to develop but did not evolve into a fully developed oceanic rift?
ANSWER: Aulacogen
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the general term for an object with emissivity less than 1 but non-zero?
ANSWER: Graybody
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer To the nearest integer, find the total energy in joules for a parabolic orbit with a focal length of 150 km and a latus rectum distance of 30,000 km.
ANSWER: 0
9. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer William is using ChatGPT to do his homework. What machine learning model, utilizing multi-head attention, does the ”T” in ChatGPT stand for?
ANSWER: Transformer
9. BONUS Energy Short Answer Chemistry researchers have recently started applying graph neural networks to chemical compounds. For which of the following four scenarios could graph neural networks be used well? 1) Generating compounds based on NMR spectra 2) Approximating macroscopic quantities for gases 3) Estimating environmental conditions based on a compounds concentration over time 4) Determining the stability of a crystal lattice
ANSWER: 1,2,4

17. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following describes the purpose of the BLAST algorithm in computational biology?
