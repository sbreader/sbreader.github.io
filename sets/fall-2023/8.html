1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Chaotropic agents such as guanidinium chloride are known to denature proteins and nucleic acids, mainly by disrupting what interactions?
ANSWER: HYDROGEN BONDING
1. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following molecular geometries most resembles the transition state of an SN2 reaction?
W) Trigonal pyramidal
X) Tetrahedral
Y) Trigonal bipyramidal
Z) Octahedral
ANSWER: Y) Trigonal bipyramidal
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Neo-endemism refers to a new species arising due to mutation specific to an environment. Which of the following ecosystems is characterized by its neo-endemic population?
W) Desert
X) Chaparral
Y) Serpentine
Z) Redwoods
ANSWER: Y) Serpentine
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three proteins that are essential for maintenance of histone modifications during DNA replication 1) Methyltransferase 2) Acetyltransferase 3) Deacetylase.
ANSWER: 1, 2
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for the streamlined, elongated hills left behind by a glacier, whose gentler slope points in the direction of flow?
ANSWER: DRUMLIN
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the term for a cloud of gas and dust found in the interstellar medium that is dense enough to block or absorb visible light from stars behind it?
ANSWER: DARK NEBULA (also accept: ABSORPTION NEBULA)
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the volume contained within an octahedron with all sides of length 2?
ANSWER: 8√2/3 (also accept: 8 TIMES THE SQUARE ROOT OF 2 OVER 3)
4. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the cross product of the two vectors i – 2j + k and 3i – 2j + 4k?
ANSWER: –6i – j + 4k
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Researchers at H2NEW of Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are investigating methods of generating hydrogen via electrolysis. During electrolysis, how many electrons are transferred for every two moles of water oxidized?
ANSWER: 4
5. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Researchers at the Space Sciences Laboratory at UC Berkeley are working on the ESCAPADE twin satellite mission to study the transfer of solar wind through the unique atmosphere of Mars. Identify all of the following three particles which would be found in solar wind 1) Protons 2) Electrons 3) Alpha particles.
ANSWER: ALL
6. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the term used to describe the shear stress required to create a given velocity gradient in a thin layer of fluid?
ANSWER: VISCOSITY (also accept: DYNAMIC VISCOSITY) (do not accept: KINEMATIC VISCOSITY)
6. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Consider two charged conducting spherical shells of equal radius, where sphere A has twice the charge of sphere B. If initially the surface of sphere A has electric potential V, then what is the electric potential at the center of sphere B after connecting the two spheres?
W) V
X) 4V/3
Y) 3V/2
Z) 2V
ANSWER: X) 4V/3
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Zeolites are a class of silicate minerals that are used in household products, medicine, and chemical reactions. In a zeolite, how many oxygen atoms does each silicate tetrahedron share with its neighboring tetrahedra?
ANSWER: 4
7. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT true of subduction zones?
W) They are characterized by high temperature and low pressure
X) They can occur without collision between two plates
Y) They cause the majority of high-magnitude earthquakes
Z) Older oceanic plates tend to subduct underneath younger oceanic plates
ANSWER: W) They are characterized by high temperature and low pressure
8. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In vegetative plants, which of the following colors in the visible electromagnetic spectrum promotes the most growth?
W) Red
X) Green
Y) Yellow
Z) Blue
ANSWER: Z) Blue
8. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice A polygenic trait is a phenotype that is affected by multiple genes. Which of the following methods is often used to determine corresponding genes?
W) Reporter assay
X) Linkage mapping
Y) Karyotyping
Z) Genome-wide association study
ANSWER: Z) Genome-wide association study
9. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers in the Hariharan Lab at UC Berkeley are interested in identifying genes that influence gene expression related to the winding of DNA strands in the chromosome. Which of the following functional groups is added to histone protein subunits to cause them to condense?
W) Methyl
X) Ester
Y) Carbonyl
Z) Alkyl
ANSWER: W) Methyl
9. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are studying fast-charging battery technologies. Which of the following properties is MOST responsible for the possibility of fast charging in a battery?
W) Energy density
X) Cycle life
Y) Critical current density
Z) Voltage
ANSWER: Y) Critical current density
10. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer How many ways are there to arrange 6 distinct people around a circular table where the seats are indistinguishable?
ANSWER: 120
10. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the definite integral from –1 to 1 of 1 over the quantity x2 + 1 dx?
ANSWER: π/2
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following compounds has the highest boiling point?
W) Hexane
X) 1-hexene
Y) Cyclopentane
Z) 1-pentyne
ANSWER: W) Hexane
11. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following statements must be true for the reversible, adiabatic expansion of an ideal gas?
W) Entropy change is zero
X) Energy change is zero
Y) Temperature is constant
Z) Pressure is constant
ANSWER: W) Entropy change is zero
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What is the equivalent capacitance, in farads, of a circuit containing a 10-farad capacitor in series with a 20-farad capacitor?
ANSWER: 20/3 (also accept: 6 2/3)
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer A car has a maximum acceleration and deceleration of 5 meters per second squared. If the car is to start from rest, travel forward, and come to rest exactly 20 meters from where it started, what is the shortest time, in seconds, required for this maneuver?
ANSWER: 4
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the process through which temperature regulation occurs in the pectoral fins of dolphins?
ANSWER: COUNTERCURRENT EXCHANGE
13. BONUS Biology Short Answer The early ancestors of the homo genus arose from Africa around 7 million years ago. Order the appearance of the following three early hominids from oldest to youngest 1) Ardipithecus 2) Neanderthals 3) Australopithecus
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
14. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following statements is true regarding p-values?
W) A p-value less than 5% indicates a failure to reject the null hypothesis
X) A p-value greater than 5% indicates statistical significance
Y) A p-value will be the same for one and two-tailed tests for all p-tests
Z) A p-value of less than 1% indicates high statistical significance
ANSWER: Z) A p-value of less than 1% indicates high statistical significance
14. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the area of an isosceles triangle with side lengths 18 and 41?
ANSWER: 360
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the hypothetical remnant of a low mass star which has radiated away all of its heat over quadrillions of years, and which our universe is not yet old enough to contain?
ANSWER: BLACK DWARF
15. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three ocean zones into which the Great Barrier Reef may be classified 1) Photic zone 2) Benthic zone 3) Neritic zone.
ANSWER: 1, 2
16. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice An object is placed 10 centimeters away from a concave mirror with a focal length of 8 centimeters. Which of the following best describes the image formed?
W) Inverted and real
X) Inverted and virtual
Y) Upright and real
Z) Upright and virtual
ANSWER: W) Inverted and real
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer You observe a fish swim past your face at half the speed of light. If you measure the length of the fish at that moment to be 1 meter, what is the rest length of the fish?
ANSWER: 2√3/3 (also accept: 2 TIMES THE SQUARE ROOT OF 3 OVER 3)
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Researchers working on Lawrence Berkeley National Lab’s LuSEE-Night experiment are landing a probe on the moon to detect incoming radio waves. Rank the following three types of waves in terms of increasing wavelength 1) Infrared waves 2) Radio waves 3) Gamma rays.
ANSWER: 3, 1, 2
17. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers at the Thermal Energy Group at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab are working on hydrogen generation via pyrolysis, the thermal decomposition of organic material. Generally, which of the following substances CANNOT be obtained via pyrolysis?
W) Ethylene
X) Carbon monoxide
Y) Coke fuel
Z) Oxygen
ANSWER: Z) Oxygen
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The reduced mass of an HCl molecule is closest to which of the following values in amu?
W) 1
X) 18
Y) 36
Z) 72
ANSWER: W) 1
18. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Consider the reaction A + B forms C. If the graph of 1 over the concentration of A is linear with time when B is in large excess, and the slope of the graph varies linearly with the concentration of B, what is this reaction’s overall reaction order?
ANSWER: 3
19. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the limit of the quantity sine 3x divided by x as x approaches zero?
ANSWER: 3
19. BONUS Math Short Answer Find all real roots of the function f(x) = x4 – 2x2 – 3.
ANSWER: √3 AND -√3 (also accept: SQUARE ROOT 3 AND NEGATIVE SQUARE ROOT 3)
20. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following molecules works alongside troponin to perform smooth and striated muscle contraction in humans?
W) Kinase
X) Dynein
Y) Tropomyosin
Z) Actin
ANSWER: Y) Tropomyosin
20. BONUS Biology Short Answer A gene enhanced by transcription factor II D must have what other protein for RNA polymerase II to form the transcription preinitiation complex?
ANSWER: TATA BOX BINDING PROTEIN (also accept: TBP)
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice At which of the following wavelengths would the amplitude of the cosmic background radiation be the largest?
W) 1 nanometer
X) 1 micrometer
Y) 1 millimeter
Z) 1 kilometer
ANSWER: Y) 1 millimeter
21. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three effects that are associated with a La Niña 1) Low pressure in the Western Pacific 2) Strong trade winds 3) Weak Peru current.
ANSWER: 1 AND 2
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Constantine places 20 grams of solid cadmium carbonate into a 1-liter beaker of water at standard temperature and pressure. If the Ksp of cadmium carbonate is 10-12, what is the concentration in moles per liter of the aqueous carbonate ion at equilibrium?
ANSWER: 10-6
22. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer According to molecular orbital theory, a neutral S2 molecule contains how many electrons in its pi star orbitals?
ANSWER: 2
23. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Identify all of the following three types of materials that tend to become more conductive as temperature rises 1) Semiconductors 2) Conductors 3) Insulators.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
23. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 4-meter long rod with a mass of 2 kilograms is fixed at one end, and a 5-kilogram point mass is attached to its other end. How much force in newtons must be applied at the midpoint of the bar to generate an angular acceleration of 3 radians per second squared?
ANSWER: 136
