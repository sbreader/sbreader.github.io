Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following amino acids contains an R group with two hydrogen bond donors?
W) Serine
X) Glutamate
Y) Arginine
Z) Cysteine
ANSWER: Y) Arginine
2. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Desmosomes and gap junctions are found within what junctions of cardiac tissue?
ANSWER: INTERCALATED DISCS
3. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following features would identify a bacteria as being gram negative instead of gram positive?
W) Peptidoglycan
X) Teichoic acid
Y) Lipopolysaccharides
Z) Capsule
ANSWER: Y) Lipopolysaccharides
4. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Pregnancy test-kits use monoclonal antibodies to detect the presence of what hormone, which the female human body begins to produce as soon as an embryo implants in the uterus?
ANSWER: HCG (also accept: HUMAN CHORIONIC GONADOTROPIN)
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following explains the evolutionary advantage of glycogen's extensive branching?
W) It allows localization to the aleurone layer
X) It increases its rate of breakdown
Y) It acts as a signal for gluconeogenesis
Z) It enables mutarotation
ANSWER: X) It increases its rate of breakdown
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice In positron emission tomography, a radioactive isotope of what element is used as a tracer for glucose?
W) Technetium
X) Xenon
Y) Iodine
Z) Fluorine
ANSWER: Z) Fluorine
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Mr. Gould discovers a new species of trypanosomes that are able to collectively change their behavior once their critical population reaches a certain level during an infection. Mr. Gould has discovered a eukaryotic analogue of what phenomena in prokaryotes?
ANSWER: QUORUM SENSING
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Given that they have the same common ancestor, rank the following three types of groups by increasing inclusiveness 1) Monophyletic 2) Polyphyletic 3) Paraphyletic
ANSWER: 2, 3, 1
9. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following 3 renal regions by increasing osmolarity 1) Cortex 2) Outer Medulla 3) Inner Medulla
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
10. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In the Warburg effect, elevated glycolysis is coupled with increased occurrence of what other pathway that produces NAD+?
ANSWER: FERMENTATION (also accept: LACTIC ACID FERMENTATION)
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Identify all of the following three barriers to reproduction that are pre-zygotic 1) Temporal isolation 2) Gametic isolation 3) Hybrid breakdown
ANSWER: 1 and 2
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Jeff is studying for the mcat and listening to music, after 30 minutes, he realizes he doesn't notice the music anymore. What is this an example of?
ANSWER: HABITUATION (also accept: SENSORY ADAPTATION)
13. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer In the evolution of land plants, what durable polymer found in pollen allows pollen grains to retain their moisture?
ANSWER: SPOROPOLLENIN
14. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Mitosomes and hydrogenosomes, commonly found in excavates, are evolutionary remnants of what organelles?
W) Chlorplasts
X) Mitochondria
Y) Golgi Apparatus
Z) Endoplasmic Reticulum
ANSWER: X) Mitochondria
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer An individual comes to the doctor complaining about a fatty hump on his neck and a round face. The doctor notices that his blood sugar is abnormally high and he has elevated levels of cortisol. What syndrome does the patient likely have?
ANSWER: CUSHINGS
16. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice The malfunction of which of the following organelles would most directly increase protein misfolding stress?
W) Rough ER
X) Smooth ER
Y) Golgi Apparatus
Z) Peroxisome
ANSWER: W) Rough ER
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Deafness is a recessive disease caused by a gene at one locus in dogs. If you wanted to find out what genotype a certain dog was, crossing it to which of the following genotypes of dogs would give you an unambiguous answer?
W) A dog that is deaf
X) A dog that is a carrier for being deaf
Y) A dog that is not deaf
Z) A dog whose parents were deaf but they are not deaf
ANSWER: W) A dog that is deaf
18. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Order the following three biomes by increasing average precipitation per year 1) Desert 2) Tropical rainforest 3) Savanna
ANSWER: 1, 3, 2
19. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Fei Sean is classifying a new species called williamus jungus. He observes that this species has three germ layers. Identify all of the following four phyla that Fei Sean could not classify williamus jungus as 1) Ctenophora 2) Echinodermata 3) Annelida 4) Cnidaria
ANSWER: 1 and 4
