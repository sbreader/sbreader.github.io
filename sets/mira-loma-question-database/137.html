1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice If an atom of Pu-239 undergoes alpha decay what will be the resulting atom
W) Thorium-235
X) Thorium-238
Y) Uranium-235
Z) Uranium-238
ANSWER: Y) Uranium-235
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In a replacement reaction, what is the term for the type of insoluble material produced from a reaction
ANSWER: PRECIPITATE
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the procedure used to find the unknown concentration of a known reactant
ANSWER: TITRATION
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the bond angle for the molecule NH3
W) 104.5o
X) 107o
Y) 109.5o
Z) 120o
ANSWER: X) 107o
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What term is infrequently used to refer to elements in group 6A
ANSWER: CHALCOGENS
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the theory used to model the predicted shapes of molecules
ANSWER: VSEPR THEORY
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice What theory states that electrons will occupy unused orbitals before moving to occupied orbital
W) Aufbau Principle
X) Madelung Rule
Y) Hund's Rule
Z) Pauli Exclusion Principle
ANSWER: Y) Hund's Rule
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What substance is defined as capable of receiving an electron pair
W) Arrhenius Base
X) Brønsted-Lowry Acid
Y) Lewis Base
Z) Lewis Acid
ANSWER: Z) Lewis Acid
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the short hand electron configuration for copper
ANSWER: [Ar] 4s1 3d10
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What thermodynamic potential measures the process initiating work from a system
ANSWER: GIBB'S FREE ENERGY
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The reaction of copper (II) sulfate and iron results in the copper undergoing what process
W) Oxidization
X) Reduction
Y) Ionization
Z) Deionization
ANSWER: X) Reduction
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What thermodynamic property can be calculated from internal energy plus pressure multiplied volume
ANSWER: ENTHALPY
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What principle states that the an energy change is independent of its path and only dependant on its final and initial states
ANSWER: HESS' LAW
14. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice For which of the following gases is the rate of effusion the greatest
W) Oxygen
X) Nitrogen
Y) Hydrogen
Z) Helium
ANSWER: Y) Hydrogen
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the acid containing the polyatomic ion nitrite with one less oxygen atom
ANSWER: HYPONITROUS ACID
17. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What law of gases state that ideal gases at the same temperature and pressure contain the same number of particles or molecules
W) Combined Gas Law
X) Charles's Law
Y) Gay-Lussac's Law
Z) Avogadro's Law
ANSWER: Z) Avogadro's Law
17. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What law of pressure states that at a constant temperature, as the pressure of a gas increases its volume decreases
ANSWER: BOYLE'S LAW
18. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer According to Charles's Law, if pressure is held constant, as temperature increases, what also increases proportionally
ANSWER: VOLUME
18. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following objects would feel hottest to the touch when left in the sun for 30 minutes
W) Gold
X) Iron
Y) Copper
Z) Silver
ANSWER: W) Gold
19. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What type of chemical reaction would occur between the two reactant copper (II) sulfate and iron (II) oxide
W) Single Replacement
X) Double Replacement
Y) Synthesis
Z) Decomposition
ANSWER: X) Double Replacement
19. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which atomic model gave a description including a small central nucleus with electrons orbiting in circular paths
ANSWER: THE BOHR MODEL
20. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which atomic model gave a description including a small dense nucleus surrounded by electrons orbiting like planets around the sun
ANSWER: THE RUTHERFORD MODEL
20. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which scientist was the first to successfully measure the charge of the electron
W) Robert Millikan
X) Ernest Rutherford
Y) William Prout
Z) James Chadwick
ANSWER: W) Robert Millikan
21. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which quantum number projects the orbital angular momentum
W) Principal Quantum Number
X) Azimuthal Quantum Number
Y) Magnetic Quantum Number
Z) Spin Quantum Number
ANSWER: Y) Magnetic Quantum Number

ANSWER: SODIUM HYPOSULFITE
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What state of matter would occur if a certain proportion of gaseous particles were ionized
