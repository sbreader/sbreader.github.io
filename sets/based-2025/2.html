1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of galaxy, characterized by its defined arm structures and active star formation, includes examples such as the Milky Way?
W) Spiral
X) Elliptical
Y) Lenticular
Z) Irregular
ANSWER: W) Spiral
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer While doing some astrophotography, Max notices that if he makes the exposure time too big, almost any star he tries to photograph will move and blur the image due to Earth's rotation. What star should he try to photograph so that he will not experience this problem?
ANSWER: POLARIS (also accept: NORTH STAR; POLE STAR)
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A block with mass 10 kilograms rests on a flat table. The coefficient of static friction between the block and the table is 0.5. A constant force of magnitude 20 newtons is then applied to the block for 2 seconds. In joules and to one significant figure, what is the work done on the block by the force?
W) 0
X) 3
Y) 10
Z) 20
ANSWER: W) 0
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer During a 100 meter race, a sprinter accelerates at a constant rate of 4.5 meters per second squared before hitting a top speed of 9 meters per second, running at a constant speed for the rest of the race. To the nearest tenth of a second, how long does it take the sprinter to complete the race?
ANSWER: 12.1
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer When amino acids bond with one another, a condensation reaction occurs. What type of bond results from this reaction?
ANSWER: PEPTIDE
3. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following anti-knocking agents was once added to gasoline, but has since been removed for its toxicity?
W) Dimethylmercury
X) Tetraethyl lead
Y) Lead arsenate
Z) Methyl tert-butyl ether
ANSWER: X) Tetraethyl lead
4. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Quinn is playing a modded Celeste level, in which a dream jump increases speed by 40 and an ultra increases speed by 20%. If his initial speed is 160 and he performs a dream jump then an ultra, what is his final speed?
ANSWER: 240
4. BONUS Math Short Answer Quinn continues his tech chain. He now has a starting speed of 240, and he continues by performing three dream jumps followed by two ultras. Recalling that a dream jump increases speed by 40 and an ultra increases speed by 20%, and to the nearest integer, what is Quinn’s final speed?
ANSWER: 518

W) Kuiper Belt

W) Lakes
X) Rivers
Y) Mountains

W) Reflecting angle
X) Critical angle
Y) Refracting angle
Z) Internal angle

W) 30 degrees
X) 37 degrees

Z) 60 degrees

7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Alex is investing in SHSTV stock. He buys 50 shares at 100 dollars each. If the value of SHSTV decreases by 20% then increases by 20%, what is the value, in dollars, of his stocks?
ANSWER: 4800
7. BONUS Math Short Answer Arjun purchased 12 tomatoes. Little did he know, the "tomato" item was actually in packs of 6, so he had actually purchased 72 tomatoes. If he follows the fibonacci sequence by eating one tomato on the first day, one on the second, two on the third, three on the fourth and so on, on what day will he eat his final tomato?
ANSWER: NINTH
8. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What type of alternative photosynthetic pathway, first discovered in plants of the genus Crassula, involves the temporal separation of different photosynthetic stages to minimize water loss?
ANSWER: CAM (also accept: CRASSULACEAN ACID METABOLISM)
8. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, order the following three types of photosynthesis in order of decreasing efficiency 1. C4 2. CAM 3. C3
ANSWER: 2, 1, 3 (also accept: CAM, C4, C3)
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer A mathematician rolls a standard six-sided die. What is the probability that he rolls a prime number?
ANSWER: 1/2
9. BONUS Math Multiple Choice The mathematician now rolls a pair of standard and fair six sided dice. What is the probability that the sum of values on the dice will be a prime number?
W) 5/12
X) 1/2
Y) 7/12
Z) 2/3
ANSWER: W) 5/12
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify the hybridization of the central carbon atom in hydrogen cyanide, HCN.
ANSWER: SP HYBRIDIZED
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What is the IUPAC systematic name for Ag2Cr2O7?
ANSWER: SILVER DICHROMATE (do not accept: SILVER (I) DICHROMATE)

W) Strong nuclear force
X) Weak nuclear force
Y) Electromagnetic force
Z) Gravity

X) Uranium-238
Y) Neptunium-232
Z) Neptunium-244
ANSWER: X) Uranium-238
12. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Students at Saratoga High are investigating the effects of longterm salad deprivation. While observing a subject who has reportedly never eaten a salad, they notice significantly lower levels of many vitamins, especially vitamin K. Which of the following body functions would be most affected by this deficiency?
W) Vision at night
X) Wound repair
Y) Long bone growth
Z) Blood clotting
ANSWER: Z) Blood clotting
12. BONUS Biology Short Answer In a blood clot, proteins join with platelets to stop bleeding. What protein, which polymerizes into a fibrous mesh, acts to mediate this process, as well as directly reinforce the clot?
ANSWER: FIBRIN (do not accept: FIBRINOGEN; THROMBIN)
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Max is a real gas. Unfortunately, the Ideal Gas Law does not do a very good job at approximating Max's pressure under certain conditions. What equation, which accounts for the volume of the gas particles and intermolecular forces, calculates a more accurate approximation for Max’s exerted pressure?
ANSWER: VAN DER WAALS EQUATION

ANSWER: 3, 1, 2

15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Students at Saratoga High School want to tour Europe, so they start by exploring London dispersion forces. Which of the following statements is false about London dispersion forces?
W) They arise due to instantaneous dipoles
X) They are present between all molecules
Y) Their strength decreases as molecular size increases
Z) They are the primary intermolecular force between noble gases
ANSWER: Y) Their strength decreases as molecular size increases
15. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following best describes the distinction between clean energy and green energy?
W) Clean energy is carbon-neutral, while green energy is carbon-negative
X) Clean energy involves biodegradable production methods, while green energy is strictly plant-based
Y) Clean energy produces no greenhouse gas emissions, while green energy explicitly results in no environmental harm
Z) Clean energy is always renewable, while green energy is sometimes renewable
ANSWER: Y) Clean energy produces no greenhouse gas emissions, while green energy explicitly results in no environmental harm
16. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What equation for fluid flow states that the amount of water flowing into a point must equal the amount of water flowing out of that point?
ANSWER: CONTINUITY EQUATION
16. BONUS Physics Short Answer A satellite orbits a certain planet at an altitude equal to the radius of the planet at a velocity v. If the planet's radius is doubled and so satellite's orbital altitude relative to the planet surface and planet's density remains the same, what is its new velocity, in terms of v?
ANSWER: 2v
17. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Max is doing a statistics project on how much students at Saratoga High School like certain flavors of ice cream. What is the name for such a procedure?

20. BONUS Biology Short Answer Science generally agrees that potatoes are living things. Potatoes belong to the kingdom Plantae, which includes all terrestrial and aquatic plants. What is the term for this type of evolution, characterized by the development of similar traits and morphologies from different ancestries?
ANSWER: CONVERGENT EVOLUTION
