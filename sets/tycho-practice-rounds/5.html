1. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following represents all the solutions, in degrees, of the equation 4sin2x = 1 in the open interval from 0° to 180°?
W) 30°
X) 30°, 60°
Y) 30°, 150°
Z) 60°, 120°
ANSWER: Y) 30°, 150°
2. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A spaceship moving at 0.9c relative to a stationary observer throws a ball forward. The observer records the ball's speed as 0.91c. Which of the following statements is TRUE of the ball's speed relative to the spaceship?
W) The speed of the ball is 0.01c
X) The speed of the ball is zero
Y) The speed of the ball is less than 0.01c but greater than zero
Z) The speed of the ball is greater than 0.01c
ANSWER: Z) The speed of the ball is greater than 0.01c
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following is a mild oxidizing solution that contains silver ions which precipitate out of solution when in contact with aldehydes?
W) Tollens' Reagent
X) Benedict's Reagent
Y) Markovnikov's Reagent
Z) Silver Reagent
ANSWER: W) Tollens' Reagent
3. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Potassium Chromate is slowly added to a solution contain 0.20 M AgNO3 and Ba(NO3)2. Which of the following statements is TRUE?
W) The BaCrO4 precipitates out of solution
X) The Ag2CrO4 precipitates first out of solution, and then the BaCrO4 precipitates
Y) Both BaCrO4 and Ag2CrO4 precipitate simultaneously out of solution
Z) Neither BaCrO4 nor Ag2CrO4 precipitates out of solution
ANSWER: X) The Ag2CrO4 precipitates first out of solution, and then the BaCrO4 precipitates
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer When approaching the planet Jupiter, comet Shoemaker Levi-9 fell within this limit, resulting in its disintegration due to tidal forces. What is the name for the radius at which this disintegration took place?
ANSWER: ROCHE LIMIT
7. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer If a particle has displacement in 1-dimension given the formula s(t) = t3 + 2t + 1 with t in seconds, when is its velocity equal to zero?
ANSWER: NOWHERE
7. BONUS Math Short Answer What point on the function f(x) = x3 has an instantaneous slope that equals the average slope of the function in the interval (0,3)?
ANSWER: (√3, 3√3)
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer What theorem in physics states that any differentiable symmetry of the action of a physical system has a corresponding conservation law?
ANSWER: Noether's Theorem
10. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice If a utility uses a real-time pricing structure, a residential customer could reduce consumption during the times of peak demand by upgrading all but which of the following?
W) Lighting
X) Air conditioning
Y) Heating
Z) Water heating
ANSWER: W) Lighting
13. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the only critical point of the function f(x) = x ln(x)?
ANSWER: (1/e, -1/e)
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When an alpine glacier near a coastal area disappears and the sea invades the resulting trough, a deep, steep-sided inlet is formed. What is the name of this inlet?
ANSWER: FJORD
22. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Most bactericidal antibiotics that act by inhibiting transpeptidases, such as penicillins and cephalosporins, are most active when bacteria are in which of the following phases?
W) Lag phase
X) Log phase
Y) Stationary phase
Z) Death phase
ANSWER: X) Log phase
22. BONUS Biology Short Answer What form of cancer is treatable by Gleevec, is slow-growing, and is a result of the Bcr-Abl fusion gene?
ANSWER: Chronic Myelogenous Leukemia (also accept: CML)
24. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What stars are evolved massive stars above 20 solar masses that lose mass rapidly through a stellar wind at a rate of approximately 10-5 solar masses per year?
ANSWER: Wolf-Rayet Stars
26. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Give, by name or number, all of the following statements that is TRUE of the application of Ampere's Circuital Law: The magnetic field a distance r from a wire is directly proportional to 1 over r The number of turns in a solenoid is independent of the strength of its magnetic field Two wires carrying a current in the same direction will attract each other
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
26. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice On a frictionless 2D surface, a block of mass M moving at speed v collides elastically with another block of the same mass M that is initially at rest. After the collision, the first block moves at an angle θ to its initial direction and has a speed v/2. The second block's speed after the collision is which of the following?
W) √3 v/4
X) v/4
Y) √3 v/2
Z) v/2
ANSWER: Y) √3 v/2
