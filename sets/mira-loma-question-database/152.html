Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What term is given to a tributary that flows parallel to a main stream because natural levees are present?
ANSWER: YAZOO TRIBUTARY
2. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Calculate the discharge of a river with a cross-section 10 meters wide, 15 meters deep, and a water velocity of 16 m/s.
ANSWER: 2400 m3/s
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In which spectral class of stars are the Balmer lines strongest?
ANSWER: TYPE A
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What two elements make up the alloys of kamacite and taenite, which are present in the Widmanstätten patterns of meteorites?
ANSWER: IRON AND NICKEL
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Asteroids whose orbits carry them near Earth are known as
W) Amor asteroids
X) blazars
Y) Apollo asteroids
Z) Herbig-Haro object
ANSWER: Y) Apollo asteroids
7. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT an ore mineral of copper?
W) chalcocite
X) bornite
Y) cassiterite
Z) chalcopyrite
ANSWER: Y) cassiterite
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following volcanic landforms would have the least viscosity?
W) composite cones
X) pyroclastic flows
Y) volcanic domes
Z) shield volcanoes
ANSWER: Z) shield volcanoes
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the process by which cold, dense air acts as a barrier over which warmer air rises?
ANSWER: FRONTAL WEDGING
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer In the United States, karst topography requires sufficient supply of groundwater and an area underlain by what rock?
ANSWER: LIMESTONE
11. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of lava flow have surfaces of rough, jagged blocks with dangerously sharp edges and spiny projections?
ANSWER: AA
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What two ultramafic minerals are the first to crystallize from a mass of cooling magma?
W) olivine and pyroxene
X) quartz and potassium feldspar
Y) olivine and plagioclase feldspar
Z) plagioclase feldspar and pyroxene
ANSWER: W) olivine and pyroxene
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is not a proposed idea regarding the origin of the Earth's moon?
W) fission hypothesis
X) large-impact theory
Y) condensation hypothesis
Z) inflationary theory
ANSWER: Z) inflationary theory
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following describes the first step in the CNO cycle of main-sequence stars 1.1 times the mass of the sun?
W) nitrogen-13 decays to become carbon-13
X) nitrogen-14 absorbs a proton to become oxygen-15
Y) carbon-12 absorbs a proton and becomes nitrogen-13
Z) nitrogen-15 absorbs a proton, ejects a helium nucleus, and becomes carbon-12
ANSWER: Y) carbon-12 absorbs a proton and becomes nitrogen-13
15. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the doughnut-shaped mass of ionized gas surrounding Io that is caused by the sweeping up of stray particles from Jupiter's wobbling magnetic field?
ANSWER: IO PLASMA TORUS (also accept: PLASMA TORUS)
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following sites on Earth are NOT involved with intraplate volcanism?
W) Canary Islands
X) Yellowstone
Y) Mount Kilauea
Z) Cascades
ANSWER: Z) Cascades
17. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What type of streamflow is exhibited in slow-moving streams where the particles movie in roughly straight-line paths?
ANSWER: LAMINAR FLOW (also accept: LAMINAR)
18. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What type of stream drainage would best be displayed in areas with uniform underlain rock?
W) dendritic drainage
X) trellis drainage
Y) radial drainage
Z) rectangular drainage
ANSWER: W) dendritic drainage
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is LEAST accurate regarding magnetic reversals?
W) rocks are said to have reverse polarity if their magnetism exhibits the opposite magnetism of the current magnetic field
X) there are two subchrons in the Matsuyama reversed chron period
Y) each chron in the magnetic time scale lasts roughly 500,000 years
Z) normally and reversely magnetized rocks of a given age in one location match the magnetism of rocks from other locations
ANSWER: Y) each chron in the magnetic time scale lasts roughly 500,000 years
20. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the phenomenon by which soils and other unconsolidated materials containing water are turned into fluid-like masses not capable of supporting structures?
ANSWER: LIQUEFACTION
21. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What satellite in the solar system is the closest to its parent, being only 5,500 kilometers away?
ANSWER: PHOBOS
22. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What effect, also a major problem that prevents manufacture of large lenses, occurs when light of different colors is focused at different places from the lens?
ANSWER: CHROMATIC ABERRATION
23. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the eclipse that occurs when the moon blocks light from the Sun, but is too distant to cover the Sun fully as viewed from the Earth?
ANSWER: ANNULAR ECLIPSE
24. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What arrangement of minerals was created to show the cooling temperatures of various magmatic components, proving that certain minerals crystallize before others?
ANSWER: BOWEN'S REACTION SERIES
25. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is least accurate regarding properties of minerals?
W) cleavage is the tendency of a mineral to break along planes of weak bonding
X) the Mohs scale of hardness is a relative ranking of the hardness of minerals
Y) streak colors vary as the color of minerals vary from sample to sample
Z) the word "habit" is used to describe the shape of a crystal of aggregate of crystals
ANSWER: Y) streak colors vary as the color of minerals vary from sample to sample
