ANSWER: Boiling-water reactor and pressurized-water reactor
6. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following is an advantage of pressurized-water reactors over boiling-water reactors.
W) PWRs are easier to use and control
X) PWRs have greater thermal efficiency

Z) PWRs produce dramatically less nuclear waste
