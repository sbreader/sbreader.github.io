1. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What component of a nuclear weapon is used to achieve supercriticality in an implosion-type weapon?
ANSWER: Explosive lens
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer In what shape must an explosive lens be constructed to deflect the spherically diverging wavefront of an explosive into a spherically converging one?
ANSWER: Paraboloid
2. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following is the solution to ∫01 0.5xe-x/2 dx?
W) 2 − e−1/2
X) 2 − 2e−1/2
Y) 2 − 3e−1/2
Z) 2 − 4e−1/2
ANSWER: Y) 2 − 3e−1/2
2. BONUS Math Short Answer For the system of two differential equations x1′ = 4x1 + 2x2 and x2′ = 3x1 − x2, what are the distinct real eigenvalues?
ANSWER: 5 and -2
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding the Wittig reaction 1) The reaction is entropically driven by the formation of a phosphine oxide byproduct 2) Unstabilized Wittig reagents favor the formation of the E alkene 3) Triphenylphosphine is the most common phosphorus nucleophile used to generate Wittig reagents.
ANSWER: 3 only
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer A certain atom is spn hybridized, where n is a real number but is not necessarily an integer. If the hybrid spn orbitals are separated by an angle of 150 degrees, then to two significant figures, what is n?
ANSWER: 1.2
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer When carbonate minerals are subducted, the carbon dioxide they carry is often released as they react with silica in the mantle. In this process, calcium carbonate typically reacts to release CO2 by forming what mineral?
ANSWER: Wollastonite
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice An air parcel passes through a forest region, where organic aerosols are released into the atmosphere. Which of the following is NOT a possible effect of these aerosols?
W) Cooling due to a decrease in albedo
X) Increased cloud formation
Y) Reduction of effective dew point
Z) Formation of ozone
ANSWER: W) Cooling due to a decrease in albedo
5. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following describes how Annexin A5 is used to quantify the number of cells undergoing apoptosis?
W) Annexin A5 binds to phosphatidylserine on the outer leaflet of the cell membrane
X) Annexin A5 binds to 3’ hydroxyl termini of double stranded DNA breaks
Y) Annexin A5 binds to Cytochrome C outside of the mitochondrion
Z) Annexin A5 binds to the Fas receptor on the cell membrane
ANSWER: W) Annexin A5 binds to phosphatidylserine on the outer leaflet of the cell membrane
5. BONUS Biology Short Answer One side effect of some non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs is reduced gastric mucus secretion. This is because they inhibit what class of enzymes that function in both inflammatory responses and mucus production?
ANSWER: Cyclooxygenases (also accept: COX)
6. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice What type of symmetry in quantum chromodynamics suggests that the same transformations can be applied on the two chiralities?
W) Vector symmetry
X) Chiral symmetry
Y) Axial symmetry
Z) Global symmetry
ANSWER: W) Vector symmetry
6. BONUS Physics Short Answer In a special spring, the restoring force is proportional to the cube of displacement. A ball oscillating with this special spring has an initial period of T. If its mass is doubled and the spring’s length is tripled, assuming constant k, what is the ball’s new oscillation period in terms of T?
ANSWER: T√2/3
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer Many pandemic risks originate from influenza viruses, coronaviruses, and which family of negative strand RNA viruses which includes mumps virus and RSV?
ANSWER: Paramyxoviruses (also accept: Paramyx-oviridae)
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer One of the first paramyxoviruses to pose a significant pandemic risk originated in fruit bats, and its first human fatality occurred in 1994, in a veterinarian attending to infected horses. What is the name of this virus?
ANSWER: Hendra virus
8. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following differential equations is not linear?
W) dy/dt = y2 + 10
X) dy/dt = t2 y + cos t
Y) dy/dt = (t + 3)y + 2
Z) dy/dt = (e2t)y − sin t
ANSWER: W) dy/dt = y2 + 10
8. BONUS Math Short Answer Identify all of the following four quantities that are vectors when applied to the real-valued function f(x, y, z) 1) Gradient 2) Divergence 3) Curl 4) Laplacian.
ANSWER: 1, 3, and 4
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice To which of the following point groups does benzene belong to?
W) C6v
X) D6h
Y) I6s
Z) C3
ANSWER: X) D6h
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three ions in order of increasing stability of their hexaamine complexes 1) Ni2+ 2) Fe2+ 3) Cu2+.
ANSWER: 2,1,3
10. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The Big Bang Theory predicts that when the universe was about a second old, the universe began expanding too quickly for some weak interactions to occur. This allowed certain particles to exist nearly unaffected by matter, creating what background radiation?
ANSWER: Cosmic neutrino background (also accept: Neutrino background; C ν B)
10. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Widmanstätten patterns cannot be replicated in a laboratory because their formation requires which of the following conditions?
W) Zero net gravitational field
X) Extremely slow cooling times
Y) Zero heat diffusion into crystals
Z) Abundance of crystal nuclei
ANSWER: X) Extremely slow cooling times
11. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What glycoprotein on the surface of the HIV envelope, which forms env with GP41, binds to CD4 to induce a series of conformational changes that allow the virus to enter the host cell?
ANSWER: GP120
11. BONUS Biology Short Answer Dolichol is a molecule which anchors oligosaccharides to the endoplasmic reticulum membrane, before they are attached to the side chain of what amino acid?
ANSWER: Asparagine
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer One precursor to modern quantum theory is a semiclassical method derived from setting the action of a system to integer multiples of Planck’s constant. What is the name of this method?
ANSWER: Bohr-Sommerfeld quantization
12. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Ball 1 of mass m and velocity v and ball 2 of mass 2m and velocity 2v collide at a right angle. What is the maximum possible fractional kinetic energy loss after the collision, δEloss/E0 where E0 is the kinetic energy of the system before collision?
W) 10/27
X) 2/9
Y) 3/8
Z) 7/22
ANSWER: W) 10/27
13. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Despite predictions to the contrary, Moore’s Law has proven resilient over time. However, a related principle, stating that a transistor’s power density remains constant for its area, broke down around 2006. What is this principle called?
ANSWER: Dennard scaling
13. BONUS Energy Short Answer In the future, Moore’s Law will eventually run into physical limits such as the minimum increase in entropy from an irreversible logic gate. What is this limit called?
ANSWER: Landauer limit
14. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The violation of the unrestricted comprehension principle by a set that contains only those elements which are not members of themselves is known as what paradox?
ANSWER: Russell’s Paradox
14. BONUS Math Short Answer What is the Jacobian determinant for the transformation with x = 2uv and y = u2 − v2?
ANSWER: −4u2 − 4v2

X) Serine
Y) Histidine
Z) Proline
ANSWER: Z) Proline
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true regarding the Walsh diagram for H2O as the angle between the two O-H bonds increases 1) The average energy of the lone pairs increases 2) The energy of the LUMO increases 3) The HOMO-LUMO gap increases.
ANSWER: All
16. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The oceanic mixed layer is typically deepest around 60°S latitude due to the formation of what water mass?
ANSWER: Antarctic Intermediate Water (also accept: AAIW)
16. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice A recent paper by Stoppa et al. described the challenges of converging classifications, where rocks formed in vastly different ways can have similar characteristics. Which of the following pairs of rocks would not be an example of converging classification?
W) Breccia and impactites
X) Limestone and carbonatites
Y) Migmatite and mylonite
Z) Tachylite and pseudotachylite
ANSWER: Y) Migmatite and mylonite
17. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice When a vascular cambium cell undergoes anticlinal cell division, what two cells are produced?

X) One cambial cell and one secondary cell
Y) Two secondary cells
Z) One secondary cell and one primary cell
ANSWER: W) Two cambial cells
17. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following channels is incorrectly matched to its location in the nephron?

18. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice A ball is released above an inclined plane and bounces elastically. What happens to its period and the displacement between consecutive bounces as time proceeds?

Z) Period is unchanged, displacement decreases
ANSWER: Y) Period is unchanged, displacement increases

ANSWER: Sadistic conclusion
