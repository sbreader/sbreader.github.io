1. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following is a true statement regarding electric fields and potentials?
W) If the electric field at a certain point is zero, the electric potential is also zero
X) If the electric potential at a certain point is zero, the electric field is also zero
Y) Field lines and equipotential surfaces are always mutually perpendicular
Z) The electric field must be constant over an equipotential surface
ANSWER: Y) Field lines and equipotential surfaces are always mutually perpendicular
2. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer By words or numbers, indicate which of the following three statements are true about superfluids. 1. They have no viscosity, so they can flow through a tube forever 2. Spinning a bucket of a superfluid would cause the surface of the superfluid to form a parabolic shape 3. A superfluid cannot hold a temperature gradient
ANSWER: 1 AND 3

The work a nonconservative force does on an object depends on the path taken
