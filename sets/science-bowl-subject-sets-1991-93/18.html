1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Who was known as "The Wizard of Menlo Park"?
ANSWER: THOMAS EDISON (also accept: EDISON)
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Plants get their nitrogen from:
W) rain
X) the soil
Y) the air
