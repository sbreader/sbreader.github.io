1. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Ortho-Hydroxybenzoic Acid, otherwise known as salicylic acid, is much more acidic than para-Hydroxybenzoic Acid. Which of the following best explains this observation?
W) Salicylic acid is much more polarizable
X) There is stronger resonance stabilization in salicylic acid
Y) Inductive Effects are much stronger for salicylic acid
Z) The conjugate base of Salicylic acid is able to form an intramolecular Hydrogen bond while para-Hydroxybenzoic Acid is not able to
ANSWER: Z) The conjugate base of Salicylic acid is able to form an intramolecular Hydrogen bond while para-Hydroxybenzoic Acid is not able to
1. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many sigma and pi bonds respectively are present in one molecule of P4O10?
ANSWER: 16,4
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following four alkanes that can experience gauche interactions 1. Methane 2. Ethane 3. Propane 4. Butane
ANSWER: 4 only
2. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following reagents is most likely to promote epoxide ring opening via nucleophilic attack at the more substituted carbon?
W) Phenyl magnesium bromide
X) Lithium dimethyl cuperate
Y) Hydrobromic acid
Z) Potassium cyanide
ANSWER: Y) Hydrobromic acid
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name of the electrically neutral quasiparticle formed by an electron and a positive hole in excited crystals, semiconductors, or organic materials, capable of transporting energy without transporting charge?
ANSWER: exciton
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Excitons are generally classified into two types based on the spatial extent of the electron-hole pair relative to the lattice constant. Name the type of exciton that is tightly bound, typically localized to a single unit cell, and common in molecular crystals or materials with high ionicity.
ANSWER: Frenkel exciton (also accept: Frenkel-Wannier exciton)
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What quantity represents the highest energy state occupied by electrons at absolute zero temperature?
ANSWER: Fermi Energy
4. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice The rate constant for the decomposition of a compound, X -> Y + Z, was measured at several temperatures. A plot of ln(k) versus 1/T (in Kelvin) yielded a straight line with slope -1.25 × 104 K. If the rate constant is 5 × 10-3 s-1 at 300 K, what is the value of the pre-exponential factor (A) in s-1.
W) 1.3 × 10-1
X) 4.5 × 103
Y) 1.2 × 105
Z) 3.1 × 108
ANSWER: Z) 3.1 × 108
5. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify which of the following 3 quantities are modified in the van der waals equation 1. Pressure 2. Temperature 3. Volume
ANSWER: 1,3
5. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following gases has the highest value of the van der Waals constant a?
W) He
X) Ne
Y) Ar
Z) Xe
ANSWER: Z) Xe
6. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Rank the following three compounds classes by increasing oxidation state of the sulfur atom 1) Sulfide 2) Sulfone 3) Sulfoxide
ANSWER: 1,3,2
6. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three monomers that add by anionic polymerization 1. CH2=CH–CN 2. CH2=CH–OCH3 3. CH2=CH–COOCH3
ANSWER: 1,3
7. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name given to the ions outside the coordination sphere which can often be exchanged for others without changing the bonding or ligands within the complex ion coordination sphere?
ANSWER: Counterions
7. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In a Frost diagram, a plot of nE° is plotted against what quantity?
ANSWER: oxidation number
8. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What is the name for the power series equation that is used to describe the thermodynamic behavior of real gases by accounting for interactions between particles?
ANSWER: Virial Expansion
8. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Another way in which real gases are measured is through what quantity, which is defined as the ratio of a gas's actual molar volume to its ideal molar volume?
ANSWER: Compressibility factor
9. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following accounts for the significantly smaller bond angle of HOF to H2O?
W) The high electronegativity of fluorine pulls bonding electron density away from oxygen, reducing bond pair repulsion
X) Fluorine increases steric hindrance around oxygen, forcing the bond angle to decrease
Y) The O–F bond is longer than the O–H bond, leading to decreased repulsion
Z) Oxygen forms fewer lone pairs in HOF than in H2O
ANSWER: W) The high electronegativity of fluorine pulls bonding electron density away from oxygen, reducing bond pair repulsion
9. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Hexafluorobenzene and benzene interact to form an adduct through what type of intermolecular interaction?
ANSWER: pi-pi stacking
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Which mechanistic step in organometallic chemistry would the reaction between an alkyl halide and an active palladium complex to form a singular palladium complex represent?
ANSWER: oxidative addition
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In the Cannizzaro Reaction between benzaldehyde and hydroxide, what is the order with respect to benzaldehyde and hydroxide respectively, at high concentrations of base?
ANSWER: 2nd, 2nd
11. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What theory is the combination of crystal field theory and molecular orbital theory?
ANSWER: Ligand Field Theory
11. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer What diagram plots the energies of d orbitals as a function of ligand field strength and is used to predict high-spin versus low-spin configurations?
ANSWER: Tanabe–Sugano diagram
12. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What quantum mechanical model describes a particle confined to a one-dimensional region with perfectly rigid boundaries and is commonly used as a first approximation for electrons in conjugated systems?
ANSWER: Particle in a Box
12. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In statistical mechanics, what is the quantity defined as the sum over all possible energy states, which encodes all thermodynamic information of a system?
ANSWER: Partition Function
13. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Reacting an aryl diazonium ion with HBF4 yields a fluorinated aryl compound. What is the name of this reaction?
ANSWER: Balz-Schiemann reaction (also accept: Schiemann reaction)
13. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Order the following three double bonds by increasing nucleophilicity 1. Carbon carbon double bond in an α,β-unsaturated carbonyl 2. Carbon-carbon double bond in an enamine 3. Carbon carbon double bond in an enolate
ANSWER: 1, 2, 3
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice In the context of a Latimer diagram, what quantity is represented by the product of the number of electrons transferred (n) and the standard reduction potential (Eo)
W) Standard Gibbs energy change
X) Negative standard Gibbs energy change divided by Faraday's constant
Y) Equilibrium Constant
Z) Overpotential
ANSWER: X) Negative standard Gibbs energy change divided by Faraday's constant
14. BONUS Chemistry Multiple Choice According to a Frost-Ebsworth diagram, which of the following is true for a species that lies above the line joining its two adjacent neighbors?
W) It is thermodynamically stable toward disproportionation.
X) It is a powerful oxidizing agent only.
Y) It is thermodynamically unstable toward disproportionation.
Z) It is in its highest possible oxidation state.
ANSWER: Y) It is thermodynamically unstable toward disproportionation.
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following organometallic reactions does not require a palladium catalyst?
W) Heck reaction
X) Simmons-Smith reaction
Y) Negishi reaction
Z) Stille reaction
ANSWER: X) Simmons-Smith reaction
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three reactions that can oxidize a primary alcohol into an aldehyde with high yield 1. Dess-Martin oxidation 2. Swern oxidation 3. Baeyer-Villiger oxidation
ANSWER: 1 and 2
16. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer Identify all of the following three compounds that will yield a positive result for the Tollen's Test 1) Formic Acid 2) Ethanal 3) acetone
ANSWER: 1,2
16. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer In the reaction of 2-methylhexene with Sodium periodate, identify all of the following three functional groups will be present in the final product 1) Ketone 2) Aldehyde 3) Alcohol
ANSWER: 1,2
