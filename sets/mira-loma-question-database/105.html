1. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer How many chromosomes are found in human somatic cells?
ANSWER: 46
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer The process by which mRNA is decoded at the ribosome is called what?
ANSWER: Translation
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The law of definite proportions was proposed by who?
W) John Dalton
X) Joseph Proust
Y) Robert Boyle
Z) Aristotle
ANSWER: X) Joseph Proust
2. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Along with the law of definite proportions, what other law helps form the basis for stoichiometry?
ANSWER: The Law of Multiple Proportions
3. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A six ohm resistor is connected in series with a nine ohm resistor. If this circuit is connected to a 15 volt battery, how many amps travel through the circuit?
ANSWER: 1 amp
3. BONUS Physics Short Answer Find the power generated by a circuit of 6 amps and .5 ohms.
ANSWER: 18 W

W) Betelgeuse
X) Sirius A
Y) Polaris
Z) Proxima Centauri
ANSWER: Z) Proxima Centauri
