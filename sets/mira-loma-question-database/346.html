Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Joule (J) is a derived unit of energy or work in the International System of Units. It is equal to the energy expended (or work done) in applying a force of what through a distance of one metre.
ANSWER: one Newton
1. BONUS Physics Short Answer Global energy supply is often expressed in Exajoules (EJ). How many Joules (J) is one Exajoule.
ANSWER: 1 EJ = 1.0 × 1018 Joules
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Hydrogen constitutes 92.1 percent of the atoms in the Sun. What is the next most abundant element and at what %?
ANSWER: Helium at 7.8%
4. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Solar cells have about what % efficiency in converting solar energy?
ANSWER: 15%
4. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The average received solar power on land is lower than in space. What is the average solar power received at surface in North America?
ANSWER: Between 125 and 375 W/m2, that is between 3 and 9 kWh per m2 per day
5. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Towards the end of it's life, the Sun will grow in to what?
ANSWER: Red Giant, expanding its diameter 100 fold
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Name of the type of thrmonuclear reaction that fuels the Sun?
ANSWER: Nuclear Fusion (where in Hydrogen is fused to produce heavier Helium)
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer The energy output of a 1 KW energy unit is roughly equivalent to the burning of how many pounds of coal and in turm release of how many pounds of carbon dioxide into the atmosphere
ANSWER: approximately 170 Ib and 300 lb
8. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Approximately what % of world energy generation comes from Conventional Thermal Energy source?
W) 35%
X) 45%
Y) 55%
Z) 65%
ANSWER: Z) 65%
8. BONUS Energy Short Answer The 3021 km Australian Darwin to Adelaide annual car race is significant for what?
ANSWER: It races solar powered cars
9. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Approximately what % of world energy generation comes from Nuclear Energy source?
W) 5%
X) 15%
Y) 25%
Z) 35%
ANSWER: X) 15%
11. BONUS Energy Short Answer With only 5% of world polution, what is approximate percentage of energy consumption in the United States?
ANSWER: Approximately 25%
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What type of current is generated from photo voltaic cells when the sunlight falls on it?
ANSWER: DC current
12. BONUS Energy Short Answer Name three major challenges of Nuclear Energy production.
ANSWER: Nuclear radiation accidents; Nuclear weapons proliferation; and Long-term storage of nuclear waste is difficult
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What is the most potent of the Green house gases?
ANSWER: Methene
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What are the three main contributors to global warming?
ANSWER: Human activity, burning fossil fuel and deforestation
16. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Most electrical power plant use
W) Chemical batteries
X) falling water
Y) solar panels
Z) steam turbines
ANSWER: Z) steam turbines
17. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Who invented the Electric Motor?
ANSWER: Michael Faraday
17. BONUS Physics Short Answer First US patent for an Electric Motor is issues to whom and hen?
ANSWER: Thomas Davenport in 1837
18. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Who built the first Fuel Cell?
ANSWER: William Robert Grove (1838)
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Geysers are examples of what type of energy?
W) Geothermal
X) Wind
Y) solar
Z) Tidal
ANSWER: W) Geothermal
19. BONUS Energy Short Answer PACE funding started in Berkeley, California in October of 2007. PACE acronym stands for?
ANSWER: Property Assessed Clean Energy
22. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer Most widely used material at US power plants is?
ANSWER: Coal
22. BONUS Energy Short Answer Biodiesel is made from?
ANSWER: vegetable oils, animal fats (also accept: recycled greases)
23. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Transformers are used in the transmission of what type of energy?
ANSWER: Electrical
