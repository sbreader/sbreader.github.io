Source packet

1. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer What radioactive isotope of carbon is prominently used in radiometric dating?
ANSWER: CARBON-14
1. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following scientific principles is most directly used in a stationary hand-held radar gun to determine the speed of a car?
W) Bernouilli's principle
X) The Doppler effect
Y) Newton's second law of motion
Z) The theory of relativity
ANSWER: X) The Doppler effect
2. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following pairs of metric volume units are NOT equal?
W) Milliliter and cubic centimeter
X) Centiliter and cubic millimeter
Y) Liter and cubic decimeter
Z) Kiloliter and cubic meter
ANSWER: X) Centiliter and cubic millimeter
2. BONUS Energy Short Answer To the nearest whole number, 100 kilometers per hour is how many meters per second?
ANSWER: 28
3. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the equation of the horizontal line that passes through the point with coordinates (5, 6)?
ANSWER: y = 6
3. BONUS Math Multiple Choice A rectangle is to be constructed using a given segment as one of its diagonals. How many different segments could possibly be the second diagonal of this rectangle?
W) 1
X) 2
Y) 4
Z) An infinite number
ANSWER: Z) An infinite number
4. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice Which of the following alternative energy sources provides the most energy in the US?
W) Wind
X) Hydropower
Y) Biomass
Z) Solar
ANSWER: X) Hydropower
5. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What regions of noncoding RNA are removed from a transcript before translation?
ANSWER: INTRONS
5. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is the MAJOR difference between Lamarck's and Darwin's theories of evolution?
W) Lamarck's theory could be applied to giraffes
X) Lamarck's theory involves changes to a population
Y) Darwin's theory involves changes to a population
Z) Darwin's theory involves changes to an individual
ANSWER: Y) Darwin's theory involves changes to a population
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following is a major component of marine snow?
W) Salt
X) Frozen water
Y) Calcium carbonate
Z) Fecal matter from zooplankton
ANSWER: Z) Fecal matter from zooplankton
6. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Edwin Hubble developed a four-type classification for galaxies that is often displayed in a "tuning fork" diagram. What are the four types?
ANSWER: ELLIPTICAL, SPIRAL, BARRED SPIRAL, IRREGULAR (also accept: ELLIPTICAL, SPIRAL, LENTICULAR, IRREGULAR)
7. TOSS-UP Energy Multiple Choice What is the number one tip the Department of Energy has for homeowners to save money on energy bills?
W) Lower the temperature on your water heater
X) Use a power strip for your electronic equipment
Y) Use natural lighting rather than artificial
Z) Install and set a programmable thermostat
ANSWER: Z) Install and set a programmable thermostat
7. BONUS Energy Short Answer While E10 fuel is known to reduce carbon monoxide emissions, it is also thought to increase emissions of what triatomic pollutant?
ANSWER: OZONE
8. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following phenomena is NOT related to burning fossil fuels?
W) Increased atmospheric CO2 levels
X) Magnetic reversals
Y) Acid rain
Z) Mercury pollution
ANSWER: X) Magnetic reversals
8. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice The Intertropical Convergence Zone is the low-pressure zone of rising, warm air found in what atmospheric circulation cell?
W) Ferrel
X) Hadley
Y) Polar
Z) Wegener
ANSWER: X) Hadley
9. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer The temperature at 9AM was 67 degrees Fahrenheit. If the temperature rose 4 degrees per hour, what was the temperature, in degrees Fahrenheit, at 2PM?
ANSWER: 87
9. BONUS Math Multiple Choice Which of the following translations changes the graph of y = x2 into that of y = (x + 3) squared plus 6?
W) 6 units down and 3 units to the left
X) 6 units down and 3 units to the right
Y) 6 units up and 3 units to the left
Z) 6 units up and 3 units to the right
ANSWER: Y) 6 units up and 3 units to the left
10. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice What is the name of the bone in the human chin?
W) Maxilla
X) Mandible
Y) Hyoid
Z) Palatine
ANSWER: X) Mandible
10. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice What is the largest part of the brain?
W) Cerebrum
X) Cerebellum
Y) Midbrain
Z) Pons
ANSWER: W) Cerebrum
11. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice If the widest point on a population pyramid is located at the middle-age range then population growth is:
W) Positive
X) Negative
Y) Neutral
Z) Indeterminate
ANSWER: X) Negative
11. BONUS Math Short Answer A data set includes a few points that are well out of the range of the rest of the points. What are these points called?
ANSWER: OUTLIERS
12. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer Kinetic energy of an object is determined by what two properties of the object?
ANSWER: SPEED and MASS (also accept: VELOCITY AND MASS) (do not accept: WEIGHT)
12. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the electric charge of the subatomic particle that carries electromagnetic force?
ANSWER: ZERO (also accept: NEUTRAL)
13. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer What does a tachometer measure?
ANSWER: ROTATIONAL MOTION (also accept: RPM, ROTATIONAL SPEED, REVOLUTIONS PER MINUTE)
13. BONUS Energy Short Answer What temperature in degrees Fahrenheit is equivalent to 40 degrees Celsius?
ANSWER: 104
14. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer Lahars are typically associated with eruptions of what type of volcanoes?
ANSWER: STRATOVOLCANOES (also accept: COMPOSITE VOLCANOES)
14. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify all of the following three statements that are true of oligotrophic lakes 1) They are nutrient-poor 2) They contain abundant algae 3) They typically have clear blue water.
ANSWER: 1 AND 3
15. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the term used to describe the embryonic leaf in a seed?
ANSWER: COTYLEDON
15. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the long bone of the upper arm of tetrapods?
ANSWER: HUMERUS
16. TOSS-UP Energy Short Answer The Ames Laboratory produced approximately 2 million pounds of uranium to contribute to what World War II effort?
ANSWER: THE MANHATTAN PROJECT
16. BONUS Energy Multiple Choice Light water reactors are an example of which of the following?
W) Fast neutron reactor
X) Fusion reactor
Y) Thermal neutron reactor
Z) Fast breeder reactor
ANSWER: Y) Thermal neutron reactor
17. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Two functions that are inverses of each other must be reflections of each other through which of the following?
W) The line y = x
X) The x-axis
Y) The y-axis
Z) The origin
ANSWER: W) The line y = x

ANSWER: -√7

X) Carbon monoxide

ANSWER: BROWNIAN MOTION
19. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Where can most deserts be found?
W) In the horse latitudes and on the lee side of mountains
X) At the equator and on the lee side of forests
Y) Near inland seas
Z) Wherever humans use too much water
ANSWER: W) In the horse latitudes and on the lee side of mountains

ANSWER: 2 AND 3

W) Viscosity
X) Surface tension
Y) Nucleation
Z) Adhesion
ANSWER: X) Surface tension

ANSWER: HEISENBERG UNCERTAINTY PRINCIPLE (also accept: UNCERTAINTY PRINCIPLE)

W) 0
X) 1

Z) 4
ANSWER: X) 1
21. BONUS Math Short Answer Jimmy must create a new password for his account using the following criteria: It must begin with 2 English letters, followed by 3 digits. The letters may not be repeated. The digits may be repeated but the password cannot end in zero or one. How many different passwords are possible?
ANSWER: 520,000
22. TOSS-UP Chemistry Short Answer What gas law states that the volume of a gas is directly proportional to its temperature in kelvins if the pressure and number of particles of gas are constant?
ANSWER: CHARLES'S LAW (also accept: IDEAL GAS LAW)
22. BONUS Physics Short Answer What type of materials will create an induced magnetic field opposite to an externally applied magnetic field?
ANSWER: DIAMAGNETIC

ANSWER: ZYGOTE
23. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the term for the flexible tissue that holds bones together?
ANSWER: LIGAMENT
