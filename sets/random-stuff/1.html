1. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following animals would have the longest alimentary canal relative to its body size?
W) Brown Bear
X) Human
Y) Tiger
Z) Koala
ANSWER: Z) Koala
1. BONUS Biology Short Answer By name or number, state all of the following hormones which are secreted by the anterior pituitary gland: 1: Luteinizing Hormone 2: Prolactin 3: Oxytocin 4: Vasopressin
ANSWER: 1 and 2
2. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Two constants are introduced into the Van der Waals equation in order to account for what two factors of real gas molecules?
W) Mass and Volume
X) Intermolecular Attraction and Rotational Energy
Y) Volume and Intermolecular Attraction
Z) Mass and Rotational Energy
ANSWER: Y) Volume and Intermolecular Attraction
2. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the ideal Carnot efficiency of an engine if the temperatures of its cold and hot reservoirs are 27° Celsius and 227° Celsius, respectively?
ANSWER: 40%
3. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Which of the following methods is most appropriately used for determining the distance to a galaxy which is Megaparsecs away from Earth?
W) Parallax
X) Type Ia Supernovae
Y) Cepheid Variables
Z) Radar
ANSWER: X) Type Ia Supernovae
3. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer If a Type Ia supernova observed in a far off galaxy has an apparent magnitude of −9.5 at its brightest, what is the distance to the galaxy in parsecs?
ANSWER: 1000 parsecs
4. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Which of the following functional groups is not paired with its appropriate IUPAC ending?
W) Aldehydes;-ol
X) Ketones;-one
Y) Amides;-amide
Z) Carboxylic Acids;-oic acid
ANSWER: W) Aldehydes;-ol

W) Circle
X) Elipse
Y) Parabola
Z) Hyperbola
ANSWER: Z) Hyperbola
5. BONUS Math Short Answer Calculate the length of the minor axis of an ellipse with vertices at the points (1, 1) and (7, 1) and one focus at (6, 1).
ANSWER: 2√5
6. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Researchers at the Oak Ridge National Laboratory are conducting a study on how the fungus Mortierella elongata affects the growth of Black cottonwood trees. Which of the following best describes the symbiotic relationship between the two organisms if the presence of the fungus encourages growth?
W) Commensalism
X) Ammensalism
Y) Mutualism
Z) Parasitism
ANSWER: Y) Mutualism
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory conducted a study of RNA viruses living in soils that can infect fungi. What is the term used to describe such viruses?
ANSWER: Mycoviruses
7. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice According to the ABC Hypothesis, what would the order of whorls be for flowers lacking the A gene, from outside to inside?
W) Sepal-Petal-Stamen-Carpel
X) Sepal-Sepal-Carpel-Carpel
Y) Sepal-Carpel-Carpel-Sepal
Z) Carpel-Stamen-Stamen-Carpel
ANSWER: Z) Carpel-Stamen-Stamen-Carpel
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer Ein mutants will fail to undergo the triple response even in the presence of what hormone?
ANSWER: Ethylene
8. TOSS-UP Physics Short Answer A force of 100 N is applied to stretch a spring 50 cm. What is the spring constant, in Newtons per meter?
ANSWER: 200
8. BONUS Physics Short Answer A 20 kilogram bowling ball is rolled toward a 5 kg bowling pin at 20 meters per second. Assuming a perfectly elastic collision, what is the velocity of the bowling pin after the collision, in meters per second?
ANSWER: 32
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Absolute stability in clouds occurs under what condition?
W) Environmental lapse rate is greater than the wet adiabatic rate
X) Environmental lapse rate is equal to the wet adiabatic rate
Y) Environmental lapse rate is less than the wet adiabatic rate
Z) Environmental lapse rate is less than the dry adiabatic rate
ANSWER: Y) Environmental lapse rate is less than the wet adiabatic rate
9. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer What is the name of the process that creates precipitation in clouds located in the tropics?
ANSWER: Collision-Coalescence Process
10. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What kind of colloid is shaving cream?
W) Gel
X) Sol
Y) Aerosol
Z) Foam
ANSWER: Z) Foam
10. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Put the following steps in the correct order in the process of making a supersaturated solution. 1: Heat solution to boiling point, then saturate 2: Saturate solution at room temperature 3: Slowly cool solution 4: Filter excess, undissolved solute crystals
ANSWER: 2,1,4,3
11. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer What is the sum of the first 15 odd integers?
ANSWER: 225

ANSWER: 60/31
12. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory investigated how the flow of solar neutrinos changes over time in the SNO+ experiment. What process in the sun is responsible for the creation of the majority of solar neutrinos?
W) Proton-Proton Chain Reaction
X) CNO Cycle
Y) Triple Alpha Process
Z) Sunspot Cycle
ANSWER: W) Proton-Proton Chain Reaction
12. BONUS Physics Multiple Choice What is the explanation for why neutrinos are so hard to detect?
W) They have an extremely small mass
X) They only interact with normal matter through the weak force and gravity
Y) They only interact with normal matter through the strong force and gravity
Z) They are electrically neutral
ANSWER: X) They only interact with normal matter through the weak force and gravity
