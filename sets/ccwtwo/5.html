1. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice What term describes the point in a planet’s orbit where it is closest to its sun?
W) Perihelion
X) Aphelion
Y) Perigee
Z) Apogee
ANSWER: W) Perihelion
1. BONUS Earth & Space Short Answer Identify which of the following three stellar spectral types are present in the Hertzsprung Gap? 1. A 2. F 3. G
ANSWER: 1, 2, and 3
2. TOSS-UP Biology Multiple Choice Which of the following is NOT a keystone species for their respective habitats?
W) Beavers in Wetlands
X) Gorillas in Rainforests
Y) Sharks in Pelagic Waters
Z) Giraffes in Savanna
ANSWER: Z) Giraffes in Savanna
2. BONUS Biology Short Answer Which of the following describe a R selected species 1) They only can reproduce at older ages 2) They die slowly 3) Produce small numbers of offspring 4) Population growth stays within carrying capacity
ANSWER: None of the above
3. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice What is the unit cell type for Zinc Sulfide?
W) Cubic and Simple
X) Cubic and Face Centered
Y) Cubic and Body Centered
Z) Tetragonal and Body Centered
ANSWER: X) Cubic and Face Centered
3. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer How many chiral centers does 2-bromo-3-chlorobutane have?
ANSWER: 2
4. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What smaller landform can eventually grow and build up to form a river delta?
ANSWER: Distributary
4. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice What happens to a barrier island in the summer?
W) The ocean beach is enlarged due to gentle waves
X) The ocean beach is shrunk due to gentle waves
Y) The ocean beach grows due to high energy waves
Z) the ocean beach is shrunk due to high energy waves
ANSWER: W) The ocean beach is enlarged due to gentle waves
5. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice Which of the following at is not considered a chaotic system?
W) Plume of Smoke
X) Ocean Turbulence
Y) Two-body Problem
Z) Double Pendulum
ANSWER: Y) Two-body Problem
5. BONUS Physics Short Answer There is a two planet system whose planets are perfectly spherical. Which of the following changes will result in a change only in the gravitational force between the two planets. 1) A planet suddenly doubles in mass 2) One of the planets becomes hollowed out, but retains its mass. 3) A third planet is introduced
ANSWER: 1 only
6. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Multiple Choice Researchers at FermiLab utilize the South Pole Telescope to measure cosmic microwave background, or CMB. The problem that describes the presence of isotropy in the temperature of CMB across the sky is known as what?
W) Coronal Heating Problem
X) Final Parsec Problem
Y) Horizon Problem
Z) CMB-Uncertainty Problem
ANSWER: Y) Horizon Problem
6. BONUS Biology Short Answer Researchers at Argonne National Laboratory are studying ways to track and treat Coronavirus while preserving its spread. Which subunit of the spike protein that gives Coronavirus mediates fusion between the viral and host membrane cells?
ANSWER: S2 subunit
7. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer When there are fluctuations in the blood pressure a group of receptors balance it out, what is the name for these receptors which can be found in the carotid sinus.
ANSWER: Baroreceptors (do not accept: stretch receptors)
7. BONUS Biology Short Answer What is the type of cartilage found in between the intervertebral disks?
ANSWER: FIBROCARTILAGE
8. TOSS-UP Math Short Answer Convert 21 into base 4.
ANSWER: 111
8. BONUS Math Short Answer A triangle has side lengths 5, 6, and 7. If the area is represented by √x, what is x?
ANSWER: 216
9. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer What phenomenon occurs between the three innermost Galilean moons in which their orbits around the sun become more elliptical?
ANSWER: Laplace Resonance
9. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice According to dynamo theory, which of the following reasons is attributed to Mars not having a global magnetic field?
W) A large impact prevented the formation of a magnetic field
X) The evaporation of oceans led to an overabundance of CO2
Y) The core solidified leading to the field disappearing
Z) A lack of metals in the Mars atmosphere
ANSWER: Y) The core solidified leading to the field disappearing
10. TOSS-UP Physics Multiple Choice If a circuit has a current of 10 amps and a resistance of 10 ohms, what is the power in watts?
W) 10
X) 100
Y) 1000
Z) 10000
ANSWER: Y) 1000
10. BONUS Physics Short Answer What is the resistance in ohms of 10 resistors connected in parallel, half with resistance 2 ohms and the other half with resistance 4 ohms?
ANSWER: 4/15
11. TOSS-UP Math Multiple Choice Which of the following statements at is false of the function y = |x|?
W) The function is continuous for all values of x.
X) The function is differentiable for all values of x.
Y) The integral from -1 to 1 in terms of x is 1.
Z) The derivative at x=1 is 1.
ANSWER: X) The function is differentiable for all values of x.
11. BONUS Math Short Answer How many local extrema does a function with derivative f '(x) = x3 +x2-x-1 have?
ANSWER: 1
12. TOSS-UP Biology Short Answer What is the name of the enzyme that removes RNA primers during DNA replication?
ANSWER: RNase (also accept: Ribonuclease)
12. BONUS Biology Multiple Choice Double positive T cells that have two glycoproteins also known as what?
W) CD4 and CDW
X) CD7 and CD 2
Y) Double positive proteins don’t exist
Z) CD4 and CD8
ANSWER: Z) CD4 and CD8
13. TOSS-UP Earth & Space Short Answer The concentration of waves onto a specific point due to wave refraction from what geographic feature?
ANSWER: Headland
13. BONUS Earth & Space Multiple Choice Beach starvation is caused by what process?
W) The building of erosion protection technology limiting the sand feeding.
X) The building of a dam along a river that feeds the beach causing less sand to be fed.
Y) The death of coral reefs causing loosening of sand and more backwash.
Z) A tsunami causing large amounts of water to displace large quantities of sand.
ANSWER: X) The building of a dam along a river that feeds the beach causing less sand to be fed.
14. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice Researchers in the Chemical Diagnostics and Engineering group at Los Alamos National Lab have been developing a characterization and digestion method for uranium nitride fuel for use in reactors and nuclear thermal propulsion. In their research, 47 analytes were labeled as potential impurities. What form of mass spectrometry did they most likely use to calculate percent recoveries for these analytes?
W) Atmospheric pressure chemical ionization mass spectrometry
X) Chemical ionization mass spectrometry
Y) Coupled plasma mass spectrometry
Z) DART mass spectrometry
ANSWER: Y) Coupled plasma mass spectrometry
14. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Scientists at the Los Alamos National Lab have uncovered methods of using D-wave quantum annealers to calculate the excited electronic states of molecular systems. In this research, what was most likely used as the basis set molecule?
ANSWER: Hydrogen molecule (also accept: H2; Hydrogen Gas)
15. TOSS-UP Chemistry Multiple Choice The equilibrium reaction, 2NO2 ⇌ N2O4, is occurring in a sealed container with a piston lid. If the piston is compressed to decrease pressure, which side of the reaction will be favored?
W) Left
X) Right
Y) Neither
Z) It is impossible to predict
ANSWER: W) Left
15. BONUS Chemistry Short Answer Which of the following are Bravais Lattices? 1) Triclinic 2) Uniclimic 3) Orthorhombic 4) Hexagonal 5) Pyramidal
ANSWER: 1) Triclinic, 3) Orthorhombic, AND 4) Hexagonal

ANSWER: NONE
